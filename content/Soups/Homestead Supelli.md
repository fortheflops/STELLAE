# Homestead Supelli

> *A hearty, traditional homestead-style soup featuring a rich tomato base, tender vegetables, and savory meats simmered to perfection. This rustic recipe hails from January 1979 by Frank Bregante.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Marinate
1. If using **stew meat**, marinate it in **wine** for several minutes until well coated.

### Step 2: Combine and Simmer
2. Combine the **1 can homestead supelli**, **2 large cans tomato sauce**, **1 beef bouillon cube**, **1 carrot**, **1 green pepper**, **1 mild chili pepper**, **1.5 cans ortega chiles**, **1 onion**, **2 cloves garlic**, **2-3 zucchini**, **1/2 pound mushrooms**, and **1 can tomatoes** in a large pot.
3. Bring the mixture to a gentle simmer and cook on low for **3-4 hours** until the vegetables are tender, the flavors are deeply melded, and the stew is rich and fragrant.
