# Marmitako

## Instructions

### Preparation & Cooking
1. In a large pot, heat the **6 tbsp olive oil** over medium heat. Sauté the **1 finely chopped onion** and **6 minced garlic cloves** until they are golden brown, soft, and highly fragrant.
2. Add the **1 lb fresh tuna**, **1 red pepper**, **3 green peppers**, **pinch of saffron**, **6 peeled & chopped tomatoes**, and **6 cubed potatoes** to the pot.
3. Pour in enough **water** to cover the ingredients and make six servings.
4. Season the mixture with **salt** and **pepper** to taste.
5. Bring to a boil, then lower the heat and simmer gently until the **potatoes** and other vegetables are cooked through and tender but not mushy, and the tuna is cooked.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Canned tuna can be used as a substitute if rinsed with boiling water first. Store leftovers in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 2 days.
