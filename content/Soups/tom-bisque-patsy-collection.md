# Tom Bisque

> 📜 **Collection:** Patsy Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Soups
> *A classic handwritten recipe for comforting tomato bisque, featuring options for fresh onions, canned tomatoes, milk, and thickening flour.*

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 30 mins | None | 4 servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Saucepan
* Blender

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | [[Onion]] | Chopped |
| 1/8 cup | [[Margarine]] | None |
| 2 cans | [[Tomatoes]] | Diced (29 oz) |
| 3 cups | [[Milk]] | None |
| 1/4-1/3 cup | [[Flour]] | None |
| 2 tsp | [[Sugar]] | None |
| 1/2 tsp | [[Salt]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Preparation and Cooking
1. Cook **1 chopped onion** in **1/8 cup margarine** until translucent.
2. Puree the cooked onion and **2 cans diced tomatoes** in a blender.
3. Heat the mixture in a saucepan until bubbling.
4. Add **1/4-1/3 cup flour** mixed with a little **milk** to thicken.
5. Add **3 cups milk** gradually until soup thickens to the desired consistency.
6. Stir in **2 tsp sugar** and **1/2 tsp salt** to taste.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftover soup in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days.
