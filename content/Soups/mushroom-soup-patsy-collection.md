# Mushroom Soup

> 📜 **Collection:** Patsy Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Pat Keene | 📂 **Category:** Soups
> *A creamy and flavorful homemade mushroom soup made with rich chicken stock, fresh herbs, and a touch of lemon juice. Perfectly balanced and can be served hot or cold.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 15 mins | None | 4-6 servings |

## Ingredients

### Soup Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 6 Tbs | [[Butter]] | Softened or sliced |
| 3 rounded Tbsp | [[Flour]] | All-purpose |
| 2 1/2 cups | [[Chicken Stock]] | Warm or room temperature |
| 1 1/4 cups | [[Milk]] | Whole milk preferred |
| 1 lb | [[Mushrooms]] | Chopped fine |
| 3 Tbsp | [[Parsley]] | Chopped |
| 1 | [[Lemon]] | Juiced |
| 2/3 cup | [[Heavy Cream]] | Or milk |
| to taste | [[Salt]] | Kosher salt |
| to taste | [[Black Pepper]] | Freshly ground |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Roux and Broth
1. Melt **6 Tbs butter** in a saucepan, add **3 rounded Tbsp flour** and cook for 3-4 minutes.
2. Add **2 1/2 cups chicken stock**, blend well and bring to a boil, stirring all the time.

### Step 2: Simmering and Finishing
3. Add **1 1/4 cups milk**, **1 lb mushrooms**, **3 Tbsp chopped parsley**, and juice of **1 lemon**.
4. Cook for 5 minutes.
5. Stir in **2/3 cup heavy cream** and season to taste with **salt** and **black pepper**.
6. Serve hot or cold.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days. Reheat gently on the stovetop without bringing to a hard boil.
