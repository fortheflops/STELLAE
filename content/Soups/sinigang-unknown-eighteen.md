# Sinigang

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Eighteen | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Soups
> *A classic Filipino sour soup featuring tender pork ribs and a medley of fresh vegetables in a savory tamarind broth. Traditionally served hot over a steaming bowl of white rice for a comforting meal.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 1 hour | None | 4 servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large pot
* Wooden spoon

| 1 packet | [[Tamarind Soup Base]] | Sinigang packet; use to taste |
| 1/2 head | [[Cabbage]] | chopped |
| 2 cups | [[Spinach]] | fresh leaves |
| 1 cup | [[Green Beans]] | trimmed |
| As needed | [[Water]] | enough to cover the meat |
| For serving | [[Rice]] | cooked |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Simmer the Meat
1. Place the **1.5 lbs pork ribs** in a large pot and pour in enough **water** to completely cover the meat.
2. Bring to a boil, then lower the heat to a gentle simmer. Cook until the **pork ribs** are fork-tender, which should take about 45 to 60 minutes.

### Step 2: Add Vegetables and Seasoning
3. Once the meat is tender, add the **1 cup green beans** first as they take longer to cook.
4. Add the remaining vegetables, including the **1/2 head cabbage** and **2 cups spinach**, and cook until they are tender-crisp and vibrant green.
5. Stir in the **1 packet tamarind soup base** (you may use a little of the packet or the whole packet depending on your preferred level of sourness) and stir well to dissolve.
6. Serve the hot soup and tender meat over a bed of freshly cooked **rice**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The sour broth develops deeper flavor when made a day ahead. Reheat gently on the stovetop and add fresh spinach just before serving to keep it vibrant green.
