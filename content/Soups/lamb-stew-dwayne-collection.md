# Lamb Stew

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Soups
> *A classic, slow-simmered lamb stew packed with tomatoes, potatoes, zucchini, okra, carrots, and green beans. Simple to prepare and deeply satisfying, this dish grows richer the longer it cooks.*

---

## Instructions

### Preparation & Cooking
1. In a large heavy pot or Dutch oven, brown the **1 to 2 lbs lamb stew meat** and **1 large onion** until nicely seared.
2. Add the **1 (8 oz) can tomato sauce** and **1 can stewed tomatoes**, then bring to a gentle simmer.
3. Stir in the prepared vegetables—**potatoes**, **zucchini**, **okra**, **carrots**, **green beans**—along with the **2 tbsp butter**.
4. Cook over low heat until all vegetables are fork-tender—the longer it simmers, the better the flavor. Add **water** as needed throughout to maintain desired stew consistency.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Stews taste even better the next day as the flavors deepen. Store covered in the refrigerator for up to 3 to 4 days, adding a splash of water when reheating.
