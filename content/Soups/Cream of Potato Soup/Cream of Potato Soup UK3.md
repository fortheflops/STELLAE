## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
| 4 Cups                | Water                      |
| 2 Cups                 | Potatoes, Raw, Diced              |
| 1/2 Cup              | Celery, Diced               |
| 1/4 Cup                | Onion, Chopped               |
| 3 Tablespoon          | Parsley, Chopped           |
| 1/2 Tablespoon          | Salt                      |
| 3 Tablespoon                 | Butter                       |
| 2 Tablespoon          | Flour              |
|2 Cups| Milk|
|To Taste| Salt|
|To Taste| Pepper|
|To Taste| Lee & Perrin Sauce|
___
### Instructions
1. Cook potatoes, celery, onion, parsley, and salt, covered in 4 cups water for 20 minutes.
2. When tender, press vegetables through a food mill.
3. Melt in pan, 3 tablespoon of butter.
4. Add and mix well, 2 tablespoons of flour.
5. Add flour and butter mixture to vegetables and the water that they were cooked in.
6. To this add 2 cups of milk.
7. Reheat and adjust seasoning with salt, pepper, and Lee & Perrin Sauce.
