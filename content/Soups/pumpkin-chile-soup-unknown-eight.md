# Pumpkin Chile Soup

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Eight | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Soups
> *A hearty and comforting soup featuring tender pumpkin, smoky green chiles, and savory ham simmered to smooth perfection. Perfect for warming up on a chilly autumn evening.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 2 hours | None | 6-8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large pot
* Knife
* Cutting board

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 to 2 1/2 lb. | [[Pumpkin]] | Peeled, seeded, and diced |
| 2 Tbsp | [[Oil]] | None |
| 1 large | [[Onion]] | Chopped |
| 2 cloves | [[Garlic]] | Minced |
| 4 c. | [[Chicken broth]] | None |
| 1/2 c. | [[Green chili]] | Chopped |
| 1/2 c. | [[Ham]] | Chopped |
| 1 tsp | [[Black pepper]] | None |
| 1 tsp | [[Rosemary]] | None |
| To taste | [[Salt]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Sauté
1. Peel the **2 to 2 1/2 lb. pumpkin**, remove seeds, and dice.
2. Put **2 Tbsp oil** in a large pot and brown the **1 large onion, chopped** and **2 cloves garlic, minced**.

### Step 2: Simmer
3. Add the diced pumpkin, **4 c. chicken broth**, **1/2 c. green chili, chopped**, **1/2 c. ham, chopped**, **1 tsp black pepper**, and **1 tsp rosemary** to the pot.
4. Cook over low heat for **2 hrs** or until the soup is smooth and thick.
5. Add **salt to taste** and serve hot.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftovers in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days; reheat gently on the stovetop.
