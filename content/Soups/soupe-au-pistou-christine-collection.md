# Soupe au Pistou

> 📜 **Collection:** Christine Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Christiane Dickey | 📂 **Category:** Soups
> *A traditional French Soupe au Pistou packed with fresh vegetables, potatoes, green beans, and a flavorful garlic-tomato paste, topped with freshly grated Gruyere cheese. This hearty and comforting soup serves a crowd with authentic rustic flavors.*

| 2 t | [[Chicken bouillon]] | Grains |

## Instructions

### Step 1: Soup Preparation
1. Cut **1 1/2 lb green beans** fine and chop **4 potatoes**. Scald **2 tomatoes**, cut in half, and seed by squeezing.
2. Put the vegetables in a pan, cover with **2 qts water**, and add **2 T salt** and pepper.
3. Fast boil until tender, about **35 min**, until vegetables reach fork-tender doneness.

### Step 2: Pistou Blend and Pasta
4. In another pan, saute chopped **1 onion** in **olive oil**, then remove from fire.
5. Put **2 T olive oil**, **4 tomatoes**, **2 cloves garlic**, **2 t chicken bouillon grains**, and the cooked onions into a blender and blend until smooth.
6. Cook **1/2 c vermicelle** and add to the soup when done.

### Step 3: Combining and Serving
7. Add the mix from the blender to the soup and cook for an additional **5 min**.
8. Add **100 gr Gruyere cheese** directly into individual soup bowls before ladling the hot soup over it.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the soup base and pistou blend separately in the refrigerator for up to 2 days; combine and heat through before serving with fresh cheese.
