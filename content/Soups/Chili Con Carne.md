# Chili Con Carne

> 📜 **Collection:** JC | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Soups
> *A straightforward and hearty recipe for classic Chili Con Carne, made with ground beef, kidney beans, and a rich tomato base. Best made a day ahead to let the flavors meld.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Brown the Meat and Aromatics
1. In a large pot or Dutch oven, brown the **2 pounds ground beef**, **3 medium onions** (chopped), and **2 cloves garlic** (crushed) over medium heat until the beef is fully browned and the onions are translucent.
2. Drain off any excess fat from the pot.

### Step 2: Simmer the Base
3. Stir in the remaining ingredients except for the beans: **3 (15-ounce) cans tomato sauce**, **3 (12-ounce) cans tomato paste**, **3 cups water**, **To Taste salt**, **1 teaspoon black pepper**, **1 tablespoon chili powder**, and **1 to 2 dashes Tabasco sauce**.
4. Bring the mixture to a gentle simmer and cook for **30-45 minutes**, stirring occasionally until rich, thick, and fragrant.

### Step 3: Add Beans and Finish
5. Stir in the **6 (15-ounce) cans kidney beans** and continue to heat for another **10 minutes** until thoroughly warmed through.
6. Taste and adjust seasoning if necessary, remembering that Tabasco gets hotter as it blends with the chili.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** For best results, let the chili cool and refrigerate it to serve the next day, as flavors meld significantly over 24 hours.
