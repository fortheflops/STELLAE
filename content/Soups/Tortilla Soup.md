## Instructions

### Step 1: Make the Soup Base
1. In a large pot, sauté the **white onions** and **garlic** in **canola oil** until translucent and fragrant.
2. Add the **red chili** along with the **pureed roasted tomato**.
3. Stir in a splash of **olive oil**, **salt**, **pepper**, **cilantro**, and **parsley**.
4. Cook this mixture down until it forms a thick, concentrated flavor base.

### Step 2: Finish and Serve
5. Use the prepared mixture as a rich foundation for the soup.
6. Add the shredded **chicken** and **chicken broth** to the pot.
7. Heat through until the soup is gently simmering and thoroughly combined.
8. Serve hot, garnished with crispy fried **tortilla strips**, a dollop of **sour cream**, and optional **corn** and **beans** until comforting and delicious.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The soup base can be made up to 2 days in advance and stored in an airtight container in the refrigerator.
