# Judy's Tomato Soup

## Instructions

### Step 1: Sauté and Simmer
1. In a large pot, sauté the **1 large onion, sliced**, **1/4 cup chopped celery**, and **2 tablespoons minced carrot** in **2 tablespoons butter** and **2 tablespoons chicken broth** (or water) until tender and translucent, but not browned.
2. Add the **6 large ripe tomatoes, peeled and chopped** and the remaining **6 cups water or chicken broth**. Cook for **15 minutes** over low heat until the tomatoes are completely softened.

### Step 2: Puree and Finish
3. Allow the soup to cool slightly, then transfer to a food processor and puree until smooth and velvety.
4. Return the pureed soup to the pot. Stir in the **2 tablespoons raw rice** and **1/2 teaspoon thyme**. Cook for **10 minutes** over medium-low heat, stirring occasionally, until the soup is thickened and the **rice** is soft.
5. Season with **salt** and **black pepper** to taste. Ladle into bowls and sprinkle with fresh **parsley**.
6. **Alternative Serving Suggestion:** Skip the **rice** entirely and top each bowl with crunchy **croutons** and a generous handful of melted, **shredded Swiss cheese**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This soup can be made ahead and stored in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days. Reheat gently on the stovetop, adding a splash of broth if the soup has thickened too much upon standing.
