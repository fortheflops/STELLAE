# Meaty Vegetable Bean Soup

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Soups
> *A hearty, traditional soup featuring meaty beef bones, navy beans, and barley simmered to perfection. This rustic recipe offers a deeply savory flavor profile that warms the soul.*

| 5 Cups | [[Water]] | For broth |
| 1 1/2 Teaspoons | [[Salt]] | For broth |

### Beans and Grains
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3/4 Cup | Navy [[Beans]] | Dry |
| 3 Cups | [[Water]] | Warm, for soaking beans |
| 2 Tablespoons | Pearled [[Barley]] | Uncooked |
| 1 Teaspoon | [[Salt]] | For beans and barley |

### Vegetables
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Onion]] | Sliced |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Carrot]] | Sliced |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Leeks]] | Sliced |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Soak
1. Soak the **3/4 cup navy beans** in **3 cups warm water** for at least 3 hours.

### Step 2: Simmer the Broth and Beans
2. In a large pot or Dutch oven, combine the **3 pounds meaty beef bones**, **5 cups water**, and **1 1/2 teaspoons salt**.
3. Add the soaked navy beans, **2 tablespoons pearled barley**, **1/2 cup sliced onion**, **1/2 cup sliced carrot**, and **1/2 cup sliced leeks** along with **1 teaspoon salt**.
4. Bring the mixture to a boil, then reduce heat, cover, and simmer for **2 hours** or until the beans are completely tender and the meat is falling off the bones.

### Step 3: Finishing
5. Remove any large bones, shredding desired meat back into the soup.
6. Simmer for a few final minutes until rich, aromatic, and piping hot.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store sealed in the refrigerator for up to 4 days, or freeze for up to 3 months. Flavors deepen significantly after resting overnight.
