# Cheddar Chowder

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Svarale | 📂 **Category:** Soups
> *A rich and hearty cheddar chowder loaded with new potatoes, carrots, celery, and savory chopped ham. This comforting vintage soup is thickened with a creamy homemade cheese sauce until smooth and velvety.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 25 mins | None | 6–8 Servings |

## Instructions

### Step 1: Simmer the Vegetables
1. In a large Dutch oven or pot, combine the **2 cups peeled new potatoes**, **1/2 cup diced carrots**, **1/2 cup sliced celery**, **1/4 cup chopped celery**, and **1 1/2 cups boiling water**.
2. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat, cover, and simmer for **15-20 minutes**, or until the vegetables are fork-tender. Do not drain.

### Step 2: Prepare the Cheese Sauce
3. In a separate saucepan, melt the **1/4 cup margarine** over medium heat.
4. Whisk in the **1/4 cup flour**, **2 teaspoons salt**, and **1/4 teaspoon pepper** until smooth.
5. Gradually stir in the **2 cups milk** until smooth.
6. Cook, stirring constantly, until the mixture thickens and comes to a gentle boil.
7. Reduce heat to low, add the **2 cups shredded cheddar cheese**, and stir until completely melted and smooth.

### Step 3: Combine and Heat Through
8. Pour the warm cheese sauce into the Dutch oven with the cooked vegetables.
9. Add the **1 cup chopped ham** and stir gently to combine.
10. Heat through thoroughly over low heat until piping hot, but do not allow it to boil.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftover chowder in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days; reheat gently on the stovetop without boiling to prevent separation.
