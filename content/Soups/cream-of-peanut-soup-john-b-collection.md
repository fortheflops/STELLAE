# Cream of Peanut Soup

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Soups
> *A rich and velvety traditional Cream of Peanut Soup featuring tender peanut purée combined with a creamy sauce. Serve warm garnished with whipped cream for a comforting and unique starter.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 30 mins | None | 4 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Saucepan
* Sieve
* Mixing bowl

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3/4 cup | [[Peanut meats]] | Raw or shelled |
| As needed | [[Boiling water]] | For cooking nuts |
| 1 1/2 cups | [[Milk]] | None |
| 1 1/2 tbsp | [[Flour]] | None |
| 1/2 tsp | [[Salt]] | None |
| Dash | [[Pepper]] | None |
| 1 1/2 tbsp | [[Butter]] | None |
| 1/2 cup | [[Cream]] | Whipped before adding |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparing the Peanuts
1. Cook the **3/4 cup peanut meats** in **boiling water** until tender, then press through a sieve; there should be **1 1/2 cups purée**.

### Step 2: Making the Soup Base
2. Have ready a sauce made of remaining ingredients except **1/2 cup cream** (using the **1 1/2 tbsp flour**, **1 1/2 tbsp butter**, **1/2 tsp salt**, **pepper**, and **1 1/2 cups milk**).

### Step 3: Combining and Serving
3. Just before serving, combine the peanut purée and sauce, then if necessary, reheat and stir in the **1/2 cup cream** (whipped).

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The soup base and peanut purée can be prepared ahead and combined, but fold in the whipped cream just before serving.
