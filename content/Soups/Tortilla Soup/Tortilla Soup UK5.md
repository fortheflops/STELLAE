## Instructions
**To Make the Soup Base:**
1.  In a pot, sauté the white onions and garlic in canola oil.
2.  Add the red chili along with the pureed roasted tomato.
3.  Add a splash of olive oil, salt, pepper, cilantro, and parsley.
4.  Cook this mixture down to create a concentrated flavor base.

**To Make the Soup:**
1.  Use the prepared mixture as a base for the soup.
2.  Add shredded chicken and chicken broth.
3.  Heat through until the soup is simmering.
4.  Serve hot, garnished with fried tortilla strips and a dollop of sour cream.
