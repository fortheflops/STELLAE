## Instructions

### Step 1: Sautéing
1. Heat **2 T olive oil**, stir in **3 T flour**, **1/4 t salt**, and **1/4 t pepper**, stirring for **2 min**.
2. Add **1# potatoes**, **1 1/2 c carrots**, **8 brussel sprouts**, onions, **2 stalks celery**, and **3 cloves garlic**. Cook for **5 min**.

### Step 2: Simmering and Serving
3. Stir in **14 oz. veg or chix broth** and **1 1/2 c apple cider**. Bring to a boil, reduce to a simmer, uncovered for **20 minutes** until potatoes are tender and done.
4. Cut **6 slices bread** from the **1 unsliced loaf bread**. Place on **6 plates**, divide the stew over bread, and top with **Cheddar or Swiss cheese**.
5. Serve with apple wedges.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftover stew in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days. Reheat gently on the stovetop before serving over freshly cut bread.
