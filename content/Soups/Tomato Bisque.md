## Instructions

### Step 1: Base Preparation
1. Cook **1 [[Onion]]** until translucent.
2. Puree the cooked **1 [[Onion]]** and **one can of Diced [[Tomatoes]]** in a blender until smooth.
3. Heat **[[Butter]]** in a pan.
4. Add **¼ - ⅓ cup [[Flour]]** and a little **[[Milk]]** to the melted butter.
5. Gradually add **[[Milk]]** until soup thickens to desired consistency.
6. Stir in **2 tsp [[Sugar]]** and **1 tsp [[Salt]]** until fully incorporated.

### Step 2: Alternative Method (OR)
7. Warm **[[Milk]]** and mix **[[Flour]]** into it until thoroughly blended.
8. Bring the mixture to a boil and add **1 tsp [[Baking Soda]]** (note that this will foam).
9. Simmer **[[Stewed Tomatoes]]** with **[[Salt]]**, **[[Pepper]]**, **Cloves**, **Allspice**, and **[[Sugar]]** for **10 minutes** until pasty.
10. Heat butter and whisk in flour. Add milk, simmer until thick, and combine with the tomato mixture.
11. Alternatively, combine **2 lbs [[Diced Tomatoes]]**, **1 teaspoon [[Sugar]]**, **3 tablespoons [[Butter]]**, **¼ cup [[Flour]]**, and **1 quart [[Milk]]** and cook until hot and creamy.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftover soup in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days. Reheat gently on the stovetop without bringing to a hard boil to prevent curdling.
