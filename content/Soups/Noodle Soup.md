# Noodle Soup

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Soups
> *A comforting vintage noodle soup featuring a rich meat broth, hearty tomatoes, and tender vegetables. Perfect for a cozy family dinner.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Simmer the Broth
1. In a large pot, combine the **1 soup bone with meat**, **salt**, **onion**, and **1 cube of garlic**. Cover with water and cook for **1.5 hours**.

### Step 2: Add Tomatoes and Vegetables
2. Remove the **1 soup bone** from the pot.
3. Add **1 can of solid pack tomatoes** (breaking them up with a spoon) and **1 can of tomato sauce** to the broth.
4. Finely chop the **2 carrots** and **2 stalks of celery** and add them to the soup. Cook until the vegetables are tender.

### Step 3: Cook the Noodles
5. Add the **noodles** to the pot and cook for **10 minutes**, or until tender according to package directions.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftover soup in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days. Reheat gently on the stovetop.
