# Iowa Corn Chowder

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Shirley Fry | 📂 **Category:** Soups
> *A comforting, hearty Iowa corn chowder loaded with sweet corn, savory bacon, tender potatoes, and creamy soup. This simple yet satisfying recipe comes together quickly for a cozy weeknight meal.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 20 mins | None | 5 1/2 cups |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Saucepan
* Knife
* Cutting board

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5 slices | [[Bacon]] | Sliced |
| 1 medium | [[Onion]] | Sliced and separated into rings |
| 2 1/2 cups | [[Milk]] | None |
| 2 cups | [[Frozen Corn]] | 1-10 oz package |
| 1-10 3/4 oz can | [[Cream of Mushroom Soup]] | None |
| 1 cup | [[Potatoes]] | Diced, cooked |
| To taste | [[Salt]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Cooking the Base
1. Cook the **5 slices bacon** until crisp. Remove and crumble, reserving **2-3 tablespoons bacon drippings** in a saucepan.
2. Cook the **1 medium onion** in the reserved drippings over medium heat until tender and translucent.

### Step 2: Simmering the Chowder
3. Stir in the **2 1/2 cups milk**, **2 cups frozen corn**, **1-10 3/4 oz can cream of mushroom soup**, and **1 cup potatoes**.
4. Bring the mixture to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer for **2-3 minutes** until heated through and bubbling.
5. Season with **salt** to taste.
6. Top each bowl with the crumbled bacon before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftover chowder in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days. Reheat gently on the stovetop over medium-low heat, stirring frequently.
