# Green Bean Soup

> 📜 **Collection:** Patsy Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Soups

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Boil Beans
1. Preheat your oven to **400°F** and grease a **10 x 1 1/2-inch pan**.
2. In a saucepan, combine the **~8 oz fresh green beans** and **1 inch of salted water**. Bring to a boil.
3. Cook uncovered for **5 minutes**, then cover and continue cooking for about **30 minutes** until the beans are tender and vibrant green.

### Step 2: Assemble and Blend
4. In the prepared baking dish, mix the cooked **~8 oz fresh green beans**, drained **1 can (4 oz) mushrooms**, **1/2 cup chopped onion**, **2 cloves crushed garlic**, and **1 cup shredded cheddar cheese**.
5. In a blender, combine the remaining ingredients: **1 1/2 cups milk**, **1/4 cup bacon bits**, **3 tbsp flour**, **1/4 tsp salt**, and **1/4 tsp pepper**. Blend for **15 seconds** until completely smooth.
6. Pour the blended mixture evenly over the vegetables and cheese in the baking dish.

### Step 3: Bake and Cool
7. Bake for **30 to 35 minutes** until the edges are bubbly and the top is lightly golden brown.
8. Cool for **5 minutes** before serving warm.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The vegetables and cheese can be prepped and arranged in the baking dish a day in advance. Blend and pour the milk mixture over the top just before baking.
