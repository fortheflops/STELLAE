# Black Bean and Green Chile Stew

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Soups
> *A hearty and robust stew featuring tender cubed beef, black beans, and fiery green chile simmered to perfection. This warming comfort dish melds rich savory spices with a tangy vinegar broth.*

| 4 Cups | [[Black Beans]] | cooked or canned, drained |
| 3 Cups | [[Green Chile]] | chopped |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Brown the Meat and Aromatics
1. In a large pot or Dutch oven, brown the **2 lb** cubed **[[Beef]]** in the **2 Tbsp.** **[[Oleo]]** over medium-high heat until deeply seared.
2. Add the **2 Cups** sliced **[[Onions]]** and cook until softened and translucent, about **5 minutes**.
3. Add the **4 cloves** minced **[[Garlic]]**, **2 tsp.** **[[Salt]]**, **1 tsp.** **[[Black Pepper]]**, and **1 tsp** **[[Thyme]]** to the pot. Stir well to combine until fragrant.

### Step 2: Simmer the Base
4. Add the **2** chopped **[[Tomatoes]]**, **2 Tbsp** **[[Parsley]]**, and **½ Cup** **[[Vinegar]]** to the pot.
5. Pour in the **1 Cup** **[[Boiling Water]]**, reduce the heat to low, cover, and simmer for at least **1 hour**, or until the beef is tender and fork-yielding.

### Step 3: Add Beans and Chiles
6. Add the **4 Cups** **[[Black Beans]]** and **3 Cups** chopped **[[Green Chile]]** to the stew.
7. Continue to simmer uncovered for at least **30 minutes**, or longer to allow the flavors to meld into a rich stew.
8. Serve hot and enjoy.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store sealed in the refrigerator for up to 4 days. Flavors often deepen and improve by the following day.
