## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
| 2 Cups                 | Peeled New Potatoes, Chopped|
| 1/2 Cup              | Diced Carrots               |
| 1/2 Cup              | Sliced Celery               |
| 1/4 Cup               | Chopped Celery              |
| 1 1/2 Cups            | Boiling Water               |
| 1/4 Cup            | Margarine               |
| 1/4 Cup            | Flour              |
| 2 Teaspoon          | Salt                      |
| 1/4 Teaspoon            | Pepper                     |
|2 Cups| Milk|
|2 Cups| Shredded Cheddar (8oz)|
|1 Cup| Chopped Ham|
___
### Instructions
1. Combine potatoes, carrots, sliced celery, chopped celery, and boiling water in a large Dutch oven or pot.
2. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat, cover, and simmer for 15-20 minutes, or until vegetables are tender. Do not drain.
3. In a separate saucepan, melt margarine over medium heat.
4. Whisk in flour, salt, and pepper until smooth.
5. Gradually stir in milk until smooth.
6. Cook, stirring constantly, until the mixture thickens and comes to a gentle boil.
7. Reduce heat to low. Add shredded cheddar cheese and stir until melted and smooth.
8. Pour the cheese sauce into the pot with the vegetables.
9. Add Chopped Ham
10. Stir to combine.
11. Heat through, but do not boil.
