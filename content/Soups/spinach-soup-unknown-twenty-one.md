# Spinach Soup

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty One | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Soups
> *A comforting and hearty spinach soup made with simple ingredients like chicken broth, carrots, and a hint of nutmeg. Perfect for warming up on a chilly day.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 30 mins | None | 4 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Soup pot
* Cutting board
* Knife

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | [[Onion]] | Chopped |
| 4 cups | [[Chix Broth]] | Or chicken broth |
| 2 | [[Carrots]] | Sliced |
| 1 pkg. | [[Frozen Spinach]] | Thawed and drained |
| 2 cups | [[Milk]] | Whole or low-fat |
| 1 pinch | [[Nutmeg]] | Ground |
| 1 strip | [[Orange rind]] | Fresh |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Simmering
1. Combine the **1 chopped onion**, **4 cups chix broth**, and **2 sliced carrots** in a soup pot.
2. Simmer until the vegetables are tender and fragrant.

### Step 2: Finishing
3. Add the **1 pkg. frozen spinach**, **2 cups milk**, **1 pinch nutmeg**, and **1 strip orange rind** to the pot.
4. Heat gently until hot throughout, stirring occasionally until well blended and simmering.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days. Reheat gently on the stovetop.
