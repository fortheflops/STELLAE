# Chile con Carne

> 📜 **Collection:** UK15 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Soups
> *A rich and hearty chili con carne featuring tender top sirloin, black beans, and aromatic spices simmered to perfection. This classic comfort dish is ideal for warming up on chilly evenings.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 2 hours | None | 4–6 Servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large pot
* Dutch oven
* Chef's knife
* Wooden spoon

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 pound | [[Top Sirloin]] | cut into small, bite-sized cubes |
| 3 tablespoons | [[Olive Oil]] | light |
| 2 cloves | [[Garlic]] | minced |
| 1 | [[White Onion]] | chopped |
| 3 tablespoons | [[Chili Powder]] | divided |
| 1/2 teaspoon | [[Cumin]] | ground |
| 1 tablespoon | [[Flour]] | all-purpose |
| 1 cup | [[Tomato Paste]] | None |
| 1 1/4 cups | [[Beef Stock]] | None |
| 1 cup | [[Black Beans]] | rinsed and drained |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Sear
1. Cut the **1 pound** [[Top Sirloin]] into small, bite-sized cubes.
2. In a large pot or Dutch oven, heat the **3 tablespoons** [[Olive Oil]] over medium-high heat.
3. Add the chopped **1** [[White Onion]] and cook until softened. Add the minced **2 cloves** [[Garlic]] and cook for another minute until fragrant.
4. Add the cubed [[Top Sirloin]] to the pot and brown on all sides until well-seared.

### Step 2: Simmer and Blend
5. Sprinkle the **1 tablespoon** [[Flour]], **3 tablespoons** [[Chili Powder]], and **1/2 teaspoon** [[Cumin]] over the meat. Stir to coat everything and cook for one minute.
6. Stir in the **1 cup** [[Tomato Paste]] and **1 1/4 cups** [[Beef Stock]].
7. Bring the mixture to a simmer, then reduce the heat to low, cover, and cook for at least **1-2 hours**, or until the beef is very tender and rich.

### Step 3: Finish and Serve
8. Stir in the **1 cup** [[Black Beans]] and cook for another **15-20 minutes** to heat through completely.
9. Ladle into bowls and serve hot.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store sealed in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 4 days; flavors often improve overnight.
