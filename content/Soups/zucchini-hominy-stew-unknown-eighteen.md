# Zucchini Hominy Stew

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Eighteen | ✍️ **Attribution:** Tom | 📂 **Category:** Soups
> *A hearty, rustic stew combining chunky zucchini, canned hominy, and spicy sausage with aromatic onions, garlic, and green chilis. Warmly spiced with oregano and cumin, this flavorful one-pot dish delivers a comforting, savory meal.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 30 mins | None | 4 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large pot
* Cutting board
* Chef's knife

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| As needed | [[Zucchini]] | Chunked |
| 1 can | [[Hominy]] | Canned |
| 1 lb | [[Hot Sausage]] | Sliced or crumbled |
| 1 | [[Onions]] | Chopped |
| 2 cloves | [[Garlic]] | Minced |
| 1/2 cup | [[Green Chili]] | Chopped |
| 1 tsp | [[Oregano]] | Dried |
| 1 tsp | [[Cumin]] | Ground |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Cooking
1. Chunk the **zucchini** and set aside.
2. Brown the **hot sausage** in a large pot over medium heat until fully cooked.
3. Add the chopped **onions** and minced **garlic** to the pot, sautéing until the onions are translucent and fragrant.
4. Stir in the **green chili**, **oregano**, and **cumin**, letting the spices bloom for a minute.
5. Add the chunked **zucchini** and canned **hominy** to the mixture, simmering until the zucchini is tender and the stew is piping hot and well combined.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This stew tastes even better the next day after the flavors have melded; store covered in the refrigerator for up to 3 days.
