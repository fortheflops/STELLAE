# Tomato Soup

> 📜 **Collection:** Georgia Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Soups
> *A quick and easy creamy tomato soup infused with picante sauce for a subtle kick, prepared entirely in the microwave. This comforting bowl comes together in minutes using convenient pantry staples.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5 mins | 4 mins | None | 2-3 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 4-cup glass measurer
* Microwave

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 can | [[Condensed tomato soup]] | Undiluted |
| 1/2 cup | [[Picante sauce]] | Mild or medium |
| 3/4 - 1 cup | [[Skim milk]] | Chilled |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Combine and Heat
1. In a **4-cup glass measurer**, combine **1 can condensed tomato soup**, **1/2 cup picante sauce**, and **3/4 to 1 cup skim milk**.
2. Microwave on high for **3 to 4 minutes**, stirring halfway through, until heated completely through and steaming hot.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Best enjoyed immediately after heating.
