## Instructions

### Step 1: Browning and Simmering
1. Fry **3 minced garlic cloves** in **2 tsp. vegetable oil**, brown **2 lbs. ground beef** and **1 large onion**.
2. Put into large kettle and add **1 can (small) chopped green chili**, **3 lb. cans tomatoes**, **4 1 lb. cans of mexican style pinto beans**, **2 6 oz. cans tomato paste**, **1/4 cup chili powder**, **1 tsp. white vinegar**, **3 dashes cayenne pepper**, **3 whole cloves**, **1 bay leaf**, and **salt & pepper to taste**.
3. Cook slowly for **2-3 hrs.** until rich and thick, making enough for about 12-14 people.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Chili tastes even better when made a day in advance; store covered in the refrigerator for up to 3 days.
