## Instructions

### Step 1: Brown Meat & Initial Simmer
1. In a large Dutch oven or heavy pot, heat **1/3 cup vegetable oil** over medium-high heat. Brown **2 lbs beef stew meat** on all sides.
2. Add **1 clove garlic** and **1 sliced yellow onion**, stirring until fragrant.
3. Season with **1/2 tsp dried oregano**, **1 tsp salt**, **1/4 cup fresh parsley**, and **1/4 tsp dried thyme**.
4. Pour in **1/2 cup red wine** and **1 1/2 cups water**. Cover and simmer gently for about 1 1/2 hours, or until the beef is tender.

### Step 2: Add Vegetables & Simmer
5. Add **1 cup green beans**, **1/2 lb pearl onions**, **2 large carrots**, **8 oz mushrooms**, optional **2 medium potatoes**, **extra red wine**, and **black pepper** to taste.
6. Continue simmering until all vegetables are tender and the gravy has thickened slightly. Serve piping hot.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Like most stews, this tastes even better the next day after flavors meld. Cool completely and store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days.
