# Nine Bean Soup

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Five | ✍️ **Attribution:** Sharon Bean | 📂 **Category:** Soups
> *A hearty, comforting classic Nine Bean Soup loaded with savory ham, onions, garlic, and tomatoes. Perfect for cold weather, this vintage recipe is slow-simmered to rich, flavorful perfection.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 2 hours | 12 hours | 6–8 Servings |

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |

| 2 quarts | [[Water]] | plus extra for soaking |
| 1 pound | [[Ham]] | diced |
| 1 large | [[Onion]] | chopped |
| 1 clove | [[Garlic]] | minced |
| 1/2 to 3/4 teaspoon | [[Salt]] | None |
| 1 (16-ounce) can | [[Tomatoes]] | chopped, undrained |
| 1 (10 ounce) can | [[Tomatoes and Green Chiles]] | undrained |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Soak the Beans
1. Sort and wash the **2 cups Nine Bean Soup Mix**. Place the beans in a **Dutch oven** and cover with **water** to 2 inches above the beans. Let them soak overnight until plump.

### Step 2: Simmer the Soup
2. The next day, drain the beans. Return the beans to the **Dutch oven** and add **2 quarts water**, the **1 pound diced ham**, **1 large chopped onion**, **1 clove minced garlic**, and **1/2 to 3/4 teaspoon salt**.
3. Cover the pot, bring to a boil, then reduce the heat and simmer for **1 1/2 hours**, or until the beans are tender and yielding.

### Step 3: Add Tomatoes and Finish
4. Add the remaining ingredients: **1 (16-ounce) can chopped tomatoes** and **1 (10 ounce) can tomatoes and green chiles**.
5. Simmer for an additional **30 minutes**, stirring occasionally, until the soup is rich, fragrant, and slightly thickened.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This soup tastes even better the next day. Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 4 days, or freeze for up to 3 months.
