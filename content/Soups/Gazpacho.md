# Gazpacho

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Linda Brown | 📂 **Category:** Soups
> *A refreshing and classic chilled soup featuring fresh tomatoes, cucumbers, and peppers blended with olive oil and wine vinegar. Perfect for warm days and enhanced when prepared a day in advance.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Blend Base Aromatics
1. In a blender, puree **1 garlic clove** and **1 med. chopped onion** in **3 T. olive oil** until smooth.

### Step 2: Liquefy Vegetables
2. Add **3 whole fresh tomatoes (chopped)**, **1 chopped green pepper**, **1 med. chopped cucumber**, **1 piece bread (broken in pieces)**, and **3 T. wine vinegar** to the blender and liquefy thoroughly.

### Step 3: Combine and Chill
3. Half this vegetable mixture before blending in **1 can beef consommé** along with the reserved onion and garlic mixture.
4. Pour all combined mixtures into a large container with a lid. For best flavor, store in the refrigerator the day before serving until well-chilled and integrated.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Make the soup the day before serving for better flavor development; store sealed in the refrigerator for up to 3 days.
