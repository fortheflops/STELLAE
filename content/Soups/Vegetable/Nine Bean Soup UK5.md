## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 2 cups                     | [[Nine Bean Soup Mix]]                    |
| 2 quarts                   | [[Water]]                                 |
| 1 pound                    | Diced [[Ham]]                             |
| 1 large                    | [[Onion]], chopped                        |
| 1 clove                    | [[Garlic]], minced                        |
| 1/2 to 3/4 teaspoon        | [[Salt]]                                  |
| 1 (16-ounce) can           | Chopped [[Tomatoes]], undrained           |
| 1 (10 ounce) can           | [[Tomatoes and Green Chiles]], undrained  |
___
## Instructions
1.  Sort and wash the 2 cups of bean mix.
2.  Place the beans in a Dutch oven and cover with water to 2 inches above the beans. Let them soak overnight.
3.  The next day, drain the beans.
4.  Return the beans to the Dutch oven and add 2 quarts of water, the diced ham, chopped onion, minced garlic, and salt.
5.  Cover the pot, bring to a boil, then reduce the heat and simmer for 1 1/2 hours, or until the beans are tender.
6.  Add the remaining ingredients (canned tomatoes and canned tomatoes with green chiles).
7.  Simmer for an additional 30 minutes, stirring occasionally.
