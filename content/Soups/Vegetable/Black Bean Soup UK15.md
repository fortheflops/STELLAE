## Instructions
(The following instructions are inferred as none were provided on the recipe card.)
1.  If using dried black beans, soak them overnight. If using canned, rinse and drain them.
2.  In a large pot, combine the black beans with enough water or vegetable broth to cover.
3.  Stir in the pureed garlic, cilantro, chili powder, tomato powder, and onion salt.
4.  Bring the soup to a boil, then reduce the heat and let it simmer for at least an hour (longer for dried beans) to allow the flavors to meld.
5.  For a creamier soup, you can use an immersion blender to partially or fully puree the soup.
6.  Serve hot.
