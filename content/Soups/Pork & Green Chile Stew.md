## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep the Chiles
1. Roast, steam, and peel the **green chiles**. Remove some of the seeds and chop them into chunks.

### Step 2: Brown the Meat
2. Cut the **pork** into chunks. Heat **fat for browning** in a skillet and brown the meat until golden and caramelized. Drain off any excess fat.
3. Toss in the chopped **onions** as the meat is browning, cooking until they are soft and translucent.

### Step 3: Simmer the Stew
4. Add the **1 can tomatoes** with their juice to the skillet or transfer everything to a larger pot. Bring to a simmer.
5. Stir in the chunked **green chiles** and optional **pinto beans** if using.
6. Cook over low heat until the pork is tender and the flavors are melded, seasoning with **salt** and **pepper** to taste.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This stew tastes even better the next day as the flavors meld. Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 4 days, or freeze for up to 3 months.
