# Chinese Chicken Broth

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Soups
> *A traditional, aromatic Chinese-style chicken broth enriched with savory ham, earthy mushrooms, and fresh ginger. This clear, flavorful base is perfect for elevating soups and stir-fry dishes.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 120 mins | None | 6 servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Stock pot
* Strainer
* Knife

---
## Ingredients

### Broth Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 whole | [[Chicken]] | cleaned |
| 2 slices | [[Ginger Root]] | fresh |
| 1 splash | [[Sherry]] | dry |
| 1 stalk | [[Scallion]] | trimmed |
| 1 heart | [[Chinese Cabbage]] | fresh |
| 1 slice | [[Virginia Ham]] | thinly sliced |
| 3-4 | [[Black Mushroom]] | dried, rehydrated |
| 1/2 cup | [[Bamboo Shoots]] | sliced |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Simmering
1. Place the **1 whole chicken** in a large stock pot with enough water to cover, adding the **2 slices ginger root**, **1 splash sherry**, and **1 stalk scallion**.
2. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to a gentle simmer until the chicken is tender and the broth is fragrant.

### Step 2: Finishing
3. Add the **1 heart Chinese cabbage**, **1 slice Virginia ham**, **3-4 black mushroom**, and **1/2 cup bamboo shoots** to the pot.
4. Simmer until the vegetables are tender and the flavors have melded, then strain and serve hot.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The broth can be strained and refrigerated for up to 3 days or frozen for up to 3 months.
