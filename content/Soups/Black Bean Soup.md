## Instructions

### Preparation and Cooking
1. If using dried **black beans**, soak them overnight for **8 hours**. If using canned, rinse and drain them thoroughly.
2. In a large pot, combine the **black beans** with enough water or vegetable broth to cover completely.
3. Stir in the **as needed pureed garlic**, **as needed cilantro**, **to taste chili powder**, **to taste tomato powder**, and **to taste onion salt**.
4. Bring the soup to a boil, then reduce the heat and let it simmer for at least **1 hour** (longer for dried beans) until the beans are tender, the flavors are deeply melded, and the kitchen is highly fragrant.
5. For a creamier soup, use an **immersion blender** to partially or fully puree the soup until it reaches a smooth, velvety consistency.
6. Serve hot and steaming, garnished with extra fresh cilantro if desired.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This soup tastes even better the next day as the spices meld. Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 5 days, or freeze for up to 3 months.
