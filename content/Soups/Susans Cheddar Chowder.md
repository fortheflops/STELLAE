# Susan's Cheddar Chowder

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Susan | 📂 **Category:** Soups
> *A comforting, hearty cheddar chowder packed with tender vegetables and savory ham. This creamy vintage soup is rich, flavorful, and easy to make.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 25 mins | None | 4-6 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Saucepan
* Large saucepan
* Whisk

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 cups | [[Potatoes]] | Peeled and chopped |
| 1/2 cup | [[Carrots]] | Sliced |
| 1/2 cup | [[Celery]] | Sliced |
| 1/4 cup | [[Onion]] | Chopped |
| 2 cups | [[Water]] | Boiling |
| 1/4 cup | [[Margarine]] | None |
| 1/4 cup | [[Flour]] | None |
| 1/2 teaspoon | [[Salt]] | None |
| 1/4 teaspoon | [[Pepper]] | None |
| 2 cups | [[Milk]] | None |
| 2 cups | [[Cheddar Cheese]] | Grated |
| 1 cup | [[Ham]] | Chopped |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Simmer the Vegetables
1. Mix the **2 cups potatoes**, **1/2 cup carrots**, **1/2 cup celery**, and **1/4 cup onion** with **2 cups boiling water** in a saucepan.
2. Simmer covered for **15 minutes** until tender, then set aside.

### Step 2: Prepare the Base and Finish
3. Melt **1/4 cup margarine** in a large saucepan.
4. Stir in **1/4 cup flour**, **1/2 teaspoon salt**, and **1/4 teaspoon pepper**.
5. Whisk in **2 cups milk** and cook until thickened, stirring constantly until smooth.
6. Stir in **2 cups cheddar cheese** until completely melted and smooth.
7. Add the cooked veggies with their cooking liquid along with **1 cup ham**.
8. Heat just to serving temperature, stirring occasionally, and ensure you do not boil.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftover chowder in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days. Reheat gently over low heat, taking care not to boil.
