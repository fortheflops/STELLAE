# Leber Spatzle (Liver Dumpling Soup)

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Eighteen | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Soups
> *Experience a traditional German comfort classic with this rich Leber Spatzle soup. Tender homemade liver dumplings simmer in a savory beef broth until light and done.*

| 2 | [[Eggs]] | none |

| as needed | [[Flour]] | none |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Making the Dumpling Mixture
1. Grind the **1 lb. beef liver**, **1 onion**, and **2 cloves garlic** together.
2. Add **1 1/2 cups bread crumbs**, **2 eggs**, and **salt and pepper to taste**, mixing well.
3. Gradually add enough **flour** to the mixture until it is thick enough to drop easily from a tablespoon without being too runny.

### Step 2: Cooking the Soup
4. Bring **2-3 qts. beef broth** to a rolling boil in a large soup pot.
5. Drop the liver mixture into the boiling broth by teaspoonfuls.
6. Boil for **15 min**, or until the dumplings float and are cooked through and tender.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The dumpling mixture can be prepared a few hours in advance and kept covered in the refrigerator until ready to drop into boiling broth.
