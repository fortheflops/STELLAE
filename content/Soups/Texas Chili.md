# Texas Chili

> 📜 **Collection:** UK5 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Soups
> *A classic hearty Texas chili featuring ground beef, beans, and rich tomatoes seasoned to perfection. Experience a comforting bowl of slow-simmered flavors that warms you up from the inside out.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 45 mins | None | 4–6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large skillet
* Heavy-bottomed pot or Dutch oven
* Wooden spoon
* Can opener

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 pound | [[Hamburger]] | ground |
| As needed | [[Onion]] | chopped |
| 1 or 2 cans | [[Chili Beans]] | drained or undrained per preference |
| 1 large can | [[Tomatoes]] | crushed or diced |
| A bunch | [[Cilantro]] | fresh, chopped |
| To taste | [[Garlic]] | minced |
| To taste | [[Salt]] |  |
| To taste | [[Pepper]] |  |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Brown the Meat and Aromatics
1. In a large skillet, brown the **1 pound hamburger** gently until cooked through but not too dark, stirring frequently.

### Step 2: Combine and Simmer
2. Add the **as needed chopped onion** to the meat, cooking until soft and translucent.
3. Carefully drain off any excess grease from the skillet.
4. Transfer the mixture to a pot and add **1 or 2 cans chili beans**, **1 large can tomatoes**, and **a bunch cilantro**.
5. Season with **to taste garlic**, **to taste salt**, and **to taste pepper**.
6. Simmer slowly over low heat until the flavors meld together into a rich, thick, and fragrant chili.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftover chili in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 4 days, or freeze for up to 3 months.
