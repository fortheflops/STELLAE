# Hopkind County Stew

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Soups
> *A hearty and traditional Hopkind County Stew featuring tender chicken, potatoes, and tomatoes. This slow-cooked classic is perfect for feeding a crowd and develops deep flavors over several hours.*

## Instructions

### Cooking Instructions
1. Fry **4 slices bacon** in a large kettle until crisp.
2. Add **2 tbs. butter or margarine**, **3 1/2 to 4 lbs. stewing chicken**, **6 med. onions**, **1 green pepper**, **3 cans tomatoes**, **2 cans kernel corn**, **3 lbs. potatoes**, **1 1/2 cups water**, **1 tbs. salt**, **1/4 tsp. paprika**, **1/4 tsp. curry powder**, and **1/4 tsp. pepper**.
3. Bring the mixture to a boil, then cover and simmer for at least 4 hours, stirring occasionally.
4. After about 1 hour of cooking, remove the **stewing chicken**, bone it, dice the meat, and return it to the kettle to finish cooking until tender.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This stew tastes even better the next day; store in an airtight container in the refrigerator and reheat gently on the stovetop.
