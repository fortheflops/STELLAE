# Inez's Soup with Dumplings

> 📜 **Collection:** Burcea Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Inez | 📂 **Category:** Soups
> *A comforting traditional soup featuring a rich bone broth and light, flavorful bread dumplings. This hearty dish simmers gently until the dumplings are tender and fluffy.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 25 mins | 40 mins | 15 mins | 6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Soup kettle
* Mixing bowl

---
## Ingredients

### Soup Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | [[Soup Bone]] | Shank, cooked |
| 2 | [[Onions]] | Diced |
| 2 to 3 cups | [[Celery]] | Cut fine |
| 1 tbsp | [[Nutmeg]] | Ground |
| To taste | [[Salt and pepper]] | None |

### Dumpling Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 loaf | [[White Bread]] | White part only, crumbled |
| 4 tbs | [[Marrow or butter]] | Melted |
| A little | [[Salt]] | None |
| 2 tsp | [[Nutmeg]] | Ground |
| 2 or 3 | [[Egg Whites]] | Well beaten |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Soup Base
1. Combine the cooked **1 shank soup bone**, **2 onions**, **2 to 3 c. celery**, **1 tbs. nutmeg**, and **salt and pepper** in a kettle and bring to a simmer.

### Step 2: Making Dumplings
2. Crumble the white part of **1 loaf state bread** into a bowl.
3. Pour over **4 tbs. melted marrow or butter**.
4. Add **a little salt** and **2 tsp. nutmeg**.
5. Add **2 or 3 egg whites** which have been well beaten, and mix lightly but well.
6. Form the mixture into balls the size of golf balls and let stand for **15 min**.

### Step 3: Cooking Dumplings
7. Drop the dumplings into the kettle of soup which must be at the lowest boiling point.
8. Simmer for **30-40 M.** until dumplings are cooked through and tender.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Soup base can be prepared a day in advance; shape and drop dumplings just before serving.
