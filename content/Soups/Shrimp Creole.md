## Instructions

### Step 1: Sauté Aromatics
1. In a large skillet, cook **1/2 cup chopped onion**, **1/2 cup chopped celery**, and **1 clove garlic** in **3 T. salad oil** until tender but not brown.

### Step 2: Simmer Base
2. Add **one 16 oz. can tomatoes**, **one 8 oz. can tomato sauce**, **1 T. Worcestershire sauce**, **1/2 t. salt**, **1/2 to 1 t. chili pepper**, and **1 dash bottled hot pepper sauce**.
3. Simmer, uncovered, for **45 minutes** until thickened and aromatic.

### Step 3: Thicken and Add Shrimp
4. In a small bowl, mix **2 t. cornstarch** with **1 T. cold water**; stir into the simmering sauce.
5. Cook, stirring constantly, until bubbly and slightly thickened.
6. Add **1 1/2 lbs. fresh or frozen shelled shrimp** and **1/2 cup chopped green pepper**.
7. Cover the skillet and simmer for **5 minutes** until the shrimp turn pink and opaque.
8. Serve hot over **cooked rice**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftover creole in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days. Reheat gently on the stovetop before serving over fresh rice.
