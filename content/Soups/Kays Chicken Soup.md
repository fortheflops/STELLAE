# Kay's Chicken Soup

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Kay | 📂 **Category:** Soups
> *A classic homemade chicken soup featuring a rich, clear broth made from chicken backs and necks, finished with tender wings and peas.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 2 hours 30 mins | None | 4–6 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large pot
* Cheesecloth
* Strainers

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | [[Chicken Back]] | and neck |
| 1 | [[Chicken Neck]] | paired with back |
| As Needed | [[Water]] | for covering |
| As Needed | [[Onions]] | chopped |
| To Taste | [[Salt]] | seasoning |
| As Needed | [[Chicken Wings]] | added later |
| Optional | [[Peas]] | for finishing |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Simmer the Base
1. Place the **1 chicken back** and **1 chicken neck** in a large pot and cover with **as needed water**.
2. Bring to a simmer and cook for **1.5 to 2 hours**.

### Step 2: Strain and Clarify
3. Add **as needed onions** and **to taste salt** to the pot.
4. Strain the entire contents of the pot through a cheesecloth to create a clear broth, then return the clear broth to the pot.

### Step 3: Finish the Soup
5. Add **as needed chicken wings** and **optional peas** to the broth.
6. Cook for another **30 minutes or more**, until the wings are tender and flavorful.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the cooled broth and soup in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days or freeze for up to 3 months.
