# Avocado Soup

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Soups
> *A refreshing and creamy cold soup featuring blended avocado, chicken broth, and garlic. Perfect as a quick and elegant starter for any meal.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 0 mins | None | 6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Blender
* Container

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 lg. | [[Avocado]] | Peeled and pitted |
| 1 1/2 c. | [[Chicken Broth]] | Chilled |
| 1 clove | [[Garlic]] | Whole or minced |
| 1/8 tsp. | [[Hot Pepper Flakes]] | Or Tabasco |
| 1/2 c. | [[Cracked Ice]] | Crushed |
| 1/2 c. | [[Cream]] | Or milk |
| As needed | [[Chives]] | Chopped, dill, or parsley for garnish |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Initial Blend
1. Into a container put **1 lg. avocado**, **1 1/2 c. chicken broth**, **1 clove garlic**, and **1/8 tsp. hot pepper flakes or Tabasco**.
2. Cover and blend at high speed for **15 seconds**.

### Step 2: Final Blend and Serve
3. Add **1/2 c. cracked ice** and **1/2 c. cream or milk**.
4. Cover and blend **10 seconds** longer until smooth.
5. If too thick, thin with more **chicken broth** or **cream**.
6. Serve cold, sprinkled with **chopped chives, dill, or parsley**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Best served immediately while cold and vibrant green, but can be kept in the refrigerator for a brief period before re-blending.
