## Instructions

### Slow Cooker Preparation
1. In a slow cooker, combine the **6 cups cubed peeled potatoes**, **5 cups water**, **2 cups chopped onion**, **1/2 cup chopped celery**, **1/2 cup thinly sliced carrots**, **1/4 cup butter or margarine**, **4 teaspoon chicken bouillon granules**, **2 teaspoon salt**, and **1/4 teaspoon pepper**.
2. Cover and cook on high for **7 hours** or until vegetables are tender.
3. Add the **1 can (12 ounces) evaporated milk** and **3 tablespoon chopped fresh parsley**, mix well.
4. Cover and cook for **30-60 minutes** longer until heated through.
5. Garnish with **snipped chives** if desired before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Leftovers can be stored in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days. Reheat gently on the stovetop.
