# Fried Meat Chili

## Instructions

### Step 1: Fry the Meat
1. Fry the **as needed meat** in **as needed lard or bacon grease** in a heavy pot until browned and fully cooked.

### Step 2: Make the Roux Base
2. Stir in **as needed flour** as if making a gravy, and cook until it bubbles and turns lightly golden and aromatic.

### Step 3: Spice and Simmer
3. Remove the pot from the heat and stir in **to taste chili powder**.
4. Gradually add **as needed water** to the flour mixture, then stir in **to taste garlic**, **to taste salt**, and **to taste oregano**.
5. Add **optional bouillon** if desired, return to heat, and simmer until the chili is rich and well-blended.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the cooled chili in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days, or freeze for up to 2 months.
