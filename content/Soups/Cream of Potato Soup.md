## Instructions

### Step 1: Simmer the Vegetables
1. In a covered saucepan, cook **2 cups potatoes**, **1/2 cup celery**, **1/4 cup onion**, **3 tablespoons parsley**, and **1/2 tablespoon salt** in **4 cups water** for **20 minutes** until tender and easily pierced with a fork.

### Step 2: Mill and Prepare Roux
2. When the vegetables are tender, press them through a food mill into a bowl and set aside with their cooking water.
3. In a separate pan, melt **3 tablespoons butter**.
4. Add **2 tablespoons flour** to the melted butter and mix well to form a smooth paste.

### Step 3: Combine and Finish
5. Add the flour and butter mixture to the milled vegetables and the water that they were cooked in.
6. Stir in **2 cups milk**.
7. Reheat the soup until hot and bubbling gently, then adjust seasoning to taste with **salt**, **pepper**, and **Lee & Perrin sauce** until flavorful and well-blended.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftover soup in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days. Reheat gently on the stovetop without letting it boil.
