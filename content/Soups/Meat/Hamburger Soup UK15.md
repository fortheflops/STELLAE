## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 pound                    | Lean [[Ground Beef]]                      |
| 1 medium to large          | [[Onion]], chopped                        |
| 1 medium can               | Chopped [[Tomatoes]] with juice           |
| 1 can                      | [[Tomato Soup]]                           |
| 3                          | [[Beef Bouillon Cubes]]                   |
| 1 large can                | [[Veg-All Mixed Vegetables]]              |
| 1 small can                | Drained [[Green Lima Beans]] (or corn/peas)|
| 4 to 5 tablespoons         | Long grain [[Rice]]                       |
| To Taste                   | [[Salt]] and [[Pepper]]                   |
___
## Instructions
1.  In a skillet, brown the ground beef and chopped onion.
2.  Transfer the browned meat and onion to a crockpot.
3.  Add all the remaining ingredients (tomatoes, tomato soup, bouillon cubes, mixed vegetables, drained lima beans, and rice) to the crockpot.
4.  Season with salt and pepper to taste.
5.  Cook on low all day or all night.
