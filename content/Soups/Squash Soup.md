# Squash Soup

> 📜 **Collection:** Jill Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Chef Yves Morrissette | 📂 **Category:** Soups
> *A velvety, spiced squash soup from Chef Yves Morrissette of Vermont's Inn on the Common. This comforting autumn classic features acorn squash, potato, fresh ginger, and a warm touch of curry and cinnamon.*

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Sauté the Aromatics
1. In a **large pot or Dutch oven**, heat the **oil or butter** over medium heat.
2. Sauté the **1 medium onion, chopped** and **1 tablespoon fresh ginger** until the onion is soft, fragrant, and translucent, about **10 minutes**.

### Step 2: Simmer the Soup
3. Add the **1 large potato**, **1 teaspoon curry powder**, **1 teaspoon cinnamon**, **2 pounds acorn squash**, and **10 cups chicken broth** to the pot.
4. Bring the mixture to a boil. Reduce the heat to low, cover, and simmer for about **30 minutes**, or until all the vegetables are very tender and easily pierced with a fork.

### Step 3: Puree and Season
5. Carefully puree the soup in a **blender** in batches, or use an **immersion blender** directly in the pot until completely smooth and velvety.
6. Taste and adjust seasonings with **salt and pepper** as needed.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This soup can be made ahead and stored in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 4 days, or frozen for up to 3 months. Reheat gently on the stovetop.
