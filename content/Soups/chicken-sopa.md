# Chicken Sopa

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Five | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Soups
> *A comforting and hearty multi-layered chicken soup and casserole dish featuring green chilies, savory broth, and rich melted cheese. This traditional recipe yields a generous portion, perfect for feeding a crowd with its robust flavor profile.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Sauce Preparation
1. Melt **2 tbsp oil** in a skillet.
2. Add **3-4 small cans chilies**, **3 chopped onions**, **2 cloves garlic**, **1 large can tomato juice**, and **1 1/2 cups chicken broth**.
3. Cook over low heat for **1 hr** until fragrant and well combined.

### Step 2: Layering and Baking
4. Add **oregano**, **salt**, and **pepper** to taste.
5. In a large casserole dish, layer **2-3 packages fried tortillas**, **2 hens**, and cheeses (**2 lbs jack cheese**, **1 1/2 lbs yellow cheese**) alternating with the sauce until repeated, ensuring no cheese is on the very top layer.
6. Bake at **300°** for **45 min**.
7. Remove, sprinkle with extra cheese, and return to the oven for **10-15 min** until bubbly and golden brown.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole ahead of time and refrigerate for up to 24 hours before baking, adding an extra 10 minutes to the initial bake time if chilled.
