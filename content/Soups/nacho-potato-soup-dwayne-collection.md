# Nacho Potato Soup

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Soups
> *A creamy and comforting potato soup infused with nacho flavors, featuring au gratin potatoes, corn, and melted American cheese. This easy-to-make soup is perfect for a cozy weeknight dinner.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 20 mins | None | 6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 3-quart saucepan

---
## Ingredients

### Soup Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 pkg (5-1/4 oz) | [[Au Gratin Potatoes]] |  |
| 1 can (11 oz) | [[Whole Kernel Corn]] | Drained |
| 1 can (10 oz) | [[Diced Tomatoes and Green Chilies]] | Undrained |
| 2 cups | [[Water]] |  |
| 2 cups | [[Milk]] |  |
| 2 cups | [[Process American Cheese]] | Cubed |
| Dash | [[Hot Pepper Sauce]] | Optional |
| As needed | [[Fresh Parsley]] | Minced, optional |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Simmering
1. In a **3-qt saucepan**, combine the contents of **potato pkg**, **corn**, **tomatoes and green chilies**, and **water**. Mix well.
2. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat. Cover and simmer for **15-18 minutes** or until the **potatoes** are tender.

### Step 2: Finishing
3. Add **milk**, **cubed process American cheese**, and **hot pepper sauce** if desired.
4. Cook and stir until the **cheese** is completely melted and the soup is smooth and hot.
5. Garnish with **minced fresh parsley** if desired before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftover soup in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days. Reheat gently on the stovetop over low heat.
