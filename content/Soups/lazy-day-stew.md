# Lazy Day Stew

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Soups
> *A comforting and hearty vintage stew featuring tender stew meat and garden vegetables. This effortless one-pot meal simmers slowly to rich, tender perfection.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 5 hours | None | 6 servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Roast Pan
* Heavy foil

## Ingredients

### Stew Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 lbs. | [[Stew Meat]] | Cut into pieces |
| 3 stalks | [[Celery]] | Chopped |
| 3 | [[Carrots]] | Chopped |
| 2 | [[Potatoes]] | Peelled and diced |
| 1 | [[Onion]] | Chopped |
| 1 | [[Green Pepper]] | Chopped |
| 8 oz. | [[Tomato Sauce]] | 1 can |
| 1 can | [[Water]] | Measured using the tomato sauce can |
| 2 tsp. | [[Tapioca]] | Quick-cooking |
| To taste | [[Salt and Pepper]] | Seasoning |

---

## Instructions

### Assembly and Baking
1. Arrange **2 lbs. stew meat** in a roast pan. Place the **3 stalks celery**, **3 carrots**, **2 potatoes**, **1 chopped onion**, and **1 green pepper** on top.
2. Pour **8 oz. can tomato sauce** and **1 can water** over all. Season with **salt and pepper** to taste. Sprinkle **2 tsp. tapioca** over the top.
3. Do not stir. Seal the roast pan tightly with heavy foil and cook in oven at 325 degrees for 4 to 5 hours until the meat is fork-tender and vegetables are soft.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the stew ingredients in the pan up to a day in advance, cover tightly, and refrigerate until ready to bake.
