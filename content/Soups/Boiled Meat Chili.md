# Boiled Meat Chili

> 📜 **Collection:** UK5 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Soups
> *A traditional, slow-simmered boiled meat chili featuring tender shredded pork or beef in a richly spiced broth. This rustic recipe relies on reduction for thickness rather than flour.*

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| As Needed | [[Pork]] or [[Beef Stewing Meat]] or [[Roast]] | cut into manageable pieces |
| As Needed | [[Water]] | for boiling |
| As Needed | [[Chili Powder]] | to taste |
| As Needed | [[Spices]] | salt, garlic, oregano |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Simmer the Meat
1. In a covered pot, combine the **as needed pork or beef stewing meat or roast** and **as needed water**.
2. Simmer for **about 2 hours** until the meat is thoroughly tender and fork-yielding.

### Step 2: Shred and Spice
3. Let the meat cool, then remove it from the broth and reserve the liquid.
4. Shred or chop the **as needed pork or beef stewing meat or roast**.
5. Take the reserved broth and stir in the **as needed chili powder** and **as needed spices**.
6. Add the shredded or chopped meat back into the spiced broth.
7. Simmer and keep cooking until the mixture reaches your desired consistency, ensuring you do not add flour if it seems too thin.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the chili in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days, or freeze for up to 3 months.
