# Lucia's Posole

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Lucia | 📂 **Category:** Soups
> *A traditional, hearty Mexican posole featuring tender cubed pork simmered with hominy, aromatic spices, and optional pigskin for rich depth. Perfect for feeding a crowd, this slow-cooked classic is deeply flavorful and comforting.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Soak
1. Place the **2 lb. pkg. posole** in a colander and rinse thoroughly 5 or 6 times.
2. Dump the rinsed **posole** into an **8-quart Dutch oven**, cover with water, and soak overnight.
3. If desired, add the **1 sq. ft. pigskin** cut into strips and **pigs feet** to the pot to enhance the flavor.

### Step 2: Initial Simmer
4. Bring the **posole** and water to a boil, then lower heat and let simmer for **2 to 3 hours**.
5. Meanwhile, chop the **3 to 4 medium white onions** and **4 cloves garlic**, and cube the **4 lbs. lean pork**.
6. Add the chopped **onions**, **garlic**, **1/8 to 1/4 cup salt**, and more water to the pot.

### Step 3: Brown the Pork
7. Place 1/4 of the cubed **pork** in a frying pan to brown, sprinkling about 1/4 of all remaining ingredients (**1 tbsp. cumin**, **1/4 to 1/2 cup red chili powder**, **1 tbsp. chilimole**, and **1 tbsp. oregano**) on the pork while it browns.
8. Once browned, dump the pork and spices into the posole pot.
9. Repeat this process in batches with the remaining **pork** and proportionate shares of the remaining spices until all the **pork** has been browned and added to the pot.

### Step 4: Final Simmer
10. Cover the pot (adding more water if needed) and simmer for **3 to 4 hours** until the meat is fork-tender and the posole corn-kernels have 'popped' and are fat and juicy.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Posole tastes even better the next day as the flavors meld. Store in the refrigerator for up to 3-4 days or freeze for up to 3 months.
