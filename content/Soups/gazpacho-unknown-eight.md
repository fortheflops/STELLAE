## Instructions

### Preparation & Blending
1. Roughly chop the **3 tomatoes**, **1 green pepper**, and **1 cucumber** to prepare them for blending.
2. In a high-powered blender, combine the chopped vegetables with the **1 clove garlic**, **1 sliced carrot**, **1/2 cup lemon juice**, **2 Tbsp chives**, **2 large sprigs parsley**, **1/4 cup fresh basil**, **1 1/2 tsp chervil**, **1/4 cup olive oil**, **3 cups chicken broth**, and **1 quartered onion**.
3. If desired, add **1 or 2 jalapeños** to the blender for an extra kick of heat.
4. Blend until completely smooth, creamy, and emulsified with no large chunks remaining.
5. Transfer to a container and chill in the refrigerator for at least **2 hours** before serving to allow the fresh flavors to meld beautifully.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3-4 days. Stir well before serving cold.
