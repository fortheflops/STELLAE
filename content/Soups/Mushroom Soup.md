## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Base
1. In a saucepan, melt **6 Tbsp butter**, add **3 rounded Tbsp flour**, and cook for **3-4 minutes** until smooth and slightly golden.
2. Gradually add **2 1/2 C chicken stock**; blend well and bring to a boil, stirring constantly until thickened and smooth.

### Step 2: Simmer and Finish
3. Stir in **1/4 C milk**, **~16 finely chopped mushrooms**, **3 Tbsp chopped parsley**, and **2-3 Tbsp lemon juice**.
4. Cook for **5 minutes** over medium heat until the mushrooms are tender and fragrant.
5. Stir in **1/3 cup heavy cream & milk** and season to taste with **salt** and **black pepper**. Serve hot or chilled.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Can be made ahead and stored in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days. Reheat gently on the stovetop, adding a splash of milk if needed to restore consistency.
