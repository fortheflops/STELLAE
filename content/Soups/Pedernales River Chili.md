# Pedernales River Chili

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Soups
> *A classic rustic chili featuring tender chopped beef, aromatic onions, and bold spices simmered to perfection. This hearty Texan-style recipe delivers rich, comforting flavors ideal for warming up on chilly evenings.*

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 4 Cups | [[Chopped Beef]] | Coarsely chopped |
| 3 Cups | [[Chopped Onion]] | Diced |
| 1 Tablespoon | [[Garlic]] | Finely minced |
| 1 Teaspoon | [[Oregano]] | Dried and crushed |
| 1 Teaspoon | [[Ground Cumin]] | Standard grind |
| 2 Tablespoons | [[Chili Powder]] | Or more to taste |
| 4 Cups | [[Canned Tomatoes]] | Crushed |
| To Taste | [[Salt]] | As needed |
| 2 Cups | [[Beef Broth]] | Or boiling water (instant beef bouillon can be used) |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Brown the Base
1. Combine the **4 cups chopped beef**, **3 cups chopped onion**, and **1 tablespoon garlic** in a heavy skillet over medium heat.
2. Cook until the **chopped beef** is no longer red (but not browned) and the **3 cups chopped onion** are soft and translucent.

### Step 2: Simmer the Chili
3. Add the **1 teaspoon oregano**, **1 teaspoon ground cumin**, **2 tablespoons chili powder**, **4 cups canned tomatoes**, and **2 cups beef broth** to the skillet.
4. Simmer gently for about **2 hours**—or longer—until the flavors meld into a rich, thick consistency and the meat is fork-tender.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftover chili in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 4 days, or freeze for up to 3 months.
