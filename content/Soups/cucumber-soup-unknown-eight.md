# Cucumber Soup

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Eight | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Soups
> *A refreshing chilled cucumber soup featuring potatoes, fresh herbs, and a tangy sour cream base. Perfect for warm weather dining.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 20 mins | 2 hours | 4 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Saucepan
* Blender

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 4 | [[potatoes]] | medium, peeled and diced |
| 1 bunch | [[celery]] | chopped |
| 1 bunch | [[scallions]] | chopped |
| 1 bunch | [[parsley]] | fresh |
| 1 bunch | [[mint]] | fresh |
| 1 | [[lime or lemon]] | juice of |
| 2 | [[cucumbers]] | peeled and sliced |
| as needed | [[chicken stock]] | or chicken bouillon |
| to taste | [[paprika]] | and pepper |
| 2 cups | [[sour cream]] | or substitute 1 cup sour cream and 1 cup yoghurt |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Sauté and Boil
1. Sauté the **4 potatoes**, **celery**, and **scallions** in **butter** until softened.
2. Add the **chicken stock** and boil for **20 minutes** until vegetables are tender, then chill.

### Step 2: Blend and Chill
3. In a blender, add the **sour cream**, **cucumbers**, **mint**, and **spices**.
4. Blend until smooth and chill thoroughly before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Keep refrigerated in an airtight container for up to 2 days before serving chilled.
