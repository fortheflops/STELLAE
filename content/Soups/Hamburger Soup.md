# Hamburger Soup

> 📜 **Collection:** UK15 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Soups
> *A comforting and hearty slow-cooked soup featuring lean ground beef, mixed vegetables, rice, and a rich tomato base. Perfect for effortless all-day simmering.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 6 hours | None | 6–8 Servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Skillet
* Crockpot
* Wooden spoon
* Can opener

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 pound | [[Ground Beef]] | Lean |
| 1 medium to large | [[Onion]] | Chopped |
| 1 medium can | [[Tomatoes]] | Chopped with juice |
| 1 can | [[Tomato Soup]] | Undiluted |
| 3 | [[Beef Bouillon Cubes]] | Uncrushed |
| 1 large can | [[Veg-All Mixed Vegetables]] | Un-drained |
| 1 small can | [[Green Lima Beans]] | Drained (or substitute corn/peas) |
| 4 to 5 tablespoons | [[Rice]] | Long grain, uncooked |
| To Taste | [[Salt]] and [[Pepper]] | As needed |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Brown the Meat
1. In a skillet over medium-high heat, brown the **1 pound lean ground beef** and **1 medium to large chopped onion** until the meat is fully cooked and the onions are translucent and fragrant.

### Step 2: Slow Cook
2. Transfer the browned **ground beef** and **onion** mixture to a crockpot.
3. Add the **1 medium can chopped tomatoes with juice**, **1 can tomato soup**, **3 beef bouillon cubes**, **1 large can Veg-All mixed vegetables**, **1 small can drained green lima beans**, and **4 to 5 tablespoons long grain rice** to the crockpot.
4. Season with **salt** and **pepper** to taste, stirring well to combine.
5. Cook on low all day or all night until the rice is tender and the flavors have melded into a rich, aromatic broth.
