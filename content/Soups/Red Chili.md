# Red Chili

> 📜 **Collection:** UK5 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Soups
> *A classic rustic red chili featuring tender meat, a rich gravy thickened with flour, and aromatic spices cooked low and slow. This traditional recipe captures deep, savory flavors reminiscent of vintage home-style cooking.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Brown the Meat
1. Brown the **hamburger, lean pork, or round steak** in a heavy pot. Add **Crisco** if needed to make a generous **2 tablespoons** of grease in the pan until the meat is fully cooked.

### Step 2: Make the Roux and Simmer
2. Add **flour** to thicken at a ratio of **1 tbsp flour** to **1 tbsp grease**.
3. Stir constantly and cook on very low heat, ensuring the mixture does not burn.
4. Add red **chili powder** to taste, then stir in **water** to create a rich gravy.
5. Cook and stir continuously, adding **garlic**, **salt**, **pepper**, and **oregano** to taste until fragrant and well-blended.
6. Simmer for a long time over low heat until the flavors meld into a rich, cohesive stew.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store sealed in the refrigerator for up to 4 days, or freeze for up to 3 months for best flavor development.
