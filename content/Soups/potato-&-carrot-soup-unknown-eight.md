# Potato & Carrot Soup

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Eight | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Soups
> *A hearty, comforting potato and carrot soup finished with crispy bacon and creamy milk. This classic rustic soup offers a rich flavor profile and tender vegetables in every warm spoonful.*

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |

| 1 | [[Onion]] | Diced |
| 3 cups | [[Potatoes]] | Diced |
| 3 cups | [[Water]] | None |
| 1 cup | [[Carrot]] | Grated |
| 2 tsps | [[Salt]] | None |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Pepper]] | None |
| 2 tbsps | [[Flour]] | None |
| 3 cups | [[Milk]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Cooking Base and Vegetables
1. Cook **2 slices bacon** in a soup pot until browned and set aside.
2. Add **1 onion** to the remaining fat in the pan and cook until golden.
3. Add **3 cups diced potatoes** and **3 cups water**, cover, and simmer for 20 minutes or until tender.
4. Add **1 cup grated carrot**, **2 tsps salt**, and **1/4 tsp pepper**.

### Step 2: Thickening and Finishing
5. Blend **2 tbsps flour** with a little of the **3 cups milk**, then stir into the hot mixture and cook until slightly thickened.
6. Add the remaining **3 cups milk** and heat through until hot.
7. Serve sprinkled with the crushed cooked **2 slices bacon**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftover soup in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days. Reheat gently on the stovetop over medium-low heat.
