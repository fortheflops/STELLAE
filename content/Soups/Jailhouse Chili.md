## Instructions

### Step 1: Sear the Beef
1. In a large pot, heat the **1/4 cup Wesson Oil** over high heat.
2. Add the **3 pounds diced lean Beef** and sear, stirring constantly, until the meat is gray but not yet browned.

### Step 2: Simmer
3. Add **1 quart water**, cover the pot, and cook over low heat for **1.5 to 2 hours** until the meat is tender and flavorful.
4. Add all the remaining seasoning ingredients including **8 chili pods** (or **6 tbsp chili powder**), **3 teaspoons salt**, **5 cloves garlic**, **1 teaspoon ground cumin**, **1 teaspoon marjoram**, **1 teaspoon red pepper**, **1 tablespoon white sugar**, and **3 tablespoons paprika**.

### Step 3: Thicken and Finish
5. If the meat is particularly fatty, skim the excess fat from the chili before proceeding.
6. In a separate bowl, prepare the thickener by whisking the **3 tablespoons flour**, **6 tablespoons white corn meal**, and **1 cup water** together until completely smooth.
7. Stir the thickening mixture into the chili.
8. Cook for **5 more minutes**, stirring as needed to prevent sticking until slightly thickened and bubbling. More water may be added if the chili becomes too thick.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the chili sealed in the refrigerator for up to 4 days, or freeze for up to 3 months. Flavors deepen significantly when made a day in advance.
