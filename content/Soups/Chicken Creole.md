## Instructions

### Step 1: Dredge and Fry the Chicken
1. In a bowl, mix together **1/2 cup flour**, **1 teaspoon salt**, and **1 1/2 teaspoon pepper**. Cut the **1 (1 1/2 to 3 lb.) frying chicken** into pieces and roll thoroughly in the flour mixture.
2. In a skillet, melt an equal mixture of **Crisco** and **butter**. Fry the chicken pieces until golden brown and fragrant, then transfer them to a casserole dish.

### Step 2: Prepare the Sauce and Bake
3. In a skillet, sauté **1/2 cup celery**, **1/2 cup green pepper**, and **1/2 cup onion** in **2 Tablespoons butter (for sauce)** until soft.
4. Add a little more butter, **1 cup catsup**, **2 Tablespoons Worcestershire sauce**, **1 cup water**, **2 Tablespoons brown sugar**, and additional **salt** and **pepper** to taste. Simmer briefly.
5. Pour the prepared sauce over the chicken in the casserole dish. Cover the casserole and bake at **325 degrees** for **1 hour** until bubbly and the chicken is tender. Yields **5 servings**. Serve hot over rice.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This dish can be prepared ahead of time and kept covered in the refrigerator before baking.
