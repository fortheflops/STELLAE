## Instructions

### Cooking the Posole and Pork
1. Put a **2 lb. package of posole** in a large pot with plenty of **water**, add some **salt**, **oregano**, a couple of **chopped onions**, and **garlic powder** to taste.
2. Cook about **4 hrs.** (till corn pops), watch to make sure it doesn't run out of water.
3. Cook **1 1/2 lbs. of pork** (either **pork shoulder roast** or **country spare ribs**, depending on how meaty you want it).
4. Use the same spices as above in cooking the **pork**.
5. Cook **pork** separately from the **posole**; when done shred into bite size pieces.
6. Drain **posole** (when done) and add it to the **pork** and broth.
7. You may add **red chili powder** or serve it separately.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Posole tastes even better the next day; store covered in the refrigerator for up to 3 days.
