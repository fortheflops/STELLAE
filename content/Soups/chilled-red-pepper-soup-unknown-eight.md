# Chilled Red Pepper Soup

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Eight | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Soups
> *This vibrant Chilled Red Pepper Soup combines sweet roasted red bell peppers, savory onions, and a touch of cayenne, all blended to silky perfection with plain yogurt. Refreshing and elegant, it makes an ideal starter or light lunch for warm summer days.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 25 mins | 2 hours | 4 servings |

* Large saucepan
* Food processor
* Slotted spoon

## Instructions

### Step 1: Sauté and Simmer
1. Heat **1 tbsp olive oil** in a large saucepan over medium heat. Add **1 small onion** and sauté until limp and translucent.
2. Add the **4 red bell peppers** and **1 qt chicken stock** to the saucepan. Bring to a simmer and cook until all vegetables are completely tender, about 15 to 20 minutes.

### Step 2: Puree and Chill
3. With a slotted spoon, transfer the tender **4 red bell peppers** and **1 small onion** to a food processor and puree. While the machine is running, gradually add the remaining **1 qt chicken stock** from the saucepan and process until completely smooth.
4. Season with a **dash of cayenne pepper**, and **salt** and **black pepper** to taste.
5. Stir in **1 cup plain yogurt** until fully incorporated and smooth.
6. Chill the soup in the refrigerator for at least 2 hours until cold. Serve chilled, garnished with fresh **cilantro leaves**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This soup can be prepared up to 2 days in advance and stored in an airtight container in the refrigerator. Stir well before serving chilled.
