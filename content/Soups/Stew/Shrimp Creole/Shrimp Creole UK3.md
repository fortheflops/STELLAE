## Recipe

### Instructions
1.  Thaw frozen shrimp.
2.  Cook 1/2 cup chopped onion, 1/2 cup chopped celery, 1 clove garlic, minced in 3 T. salad oil until tender but not brown.
3.  Add one 16 oz. can tomatoes, one 8 oz. can tomato sauce, 1 T. Worcestershire sauce, 1/2 t. salt, 1 t. to 1/2 t. chili pepper, and a dash of bottled hot pepper sauce.
4.  Simmer, uncovered, for 45 minutes.
5.  Mix 2 t. cornstarch with 1 T. cold water; stir into sauce.
6.  Cook, stirring, until bubbly.
7.  Add shrimp and 1/2 cup chopped green pepper.
8.  Cover, simmer 5 minutes.
9.  Serve over cooked rice.
