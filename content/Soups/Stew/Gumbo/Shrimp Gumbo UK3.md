## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
| 1/2 Cup                | [[Chopped Onion]]         |
| 2 Tablespoons          | [[Margarine]]             |
| 1 16 Ounce Can         | [[Tomatoes]], Cut Up      |
| 1 8 Ounce Can          | [[Tomato Sauce]]          |
| 1 13 3/4 Ounce Can     | [[Chicken Broth]]         |
| 1/2 Teaspoon           | [[Sugar]]                 |
| 1/8 Teaspoon           | [[Pepper]]                |
| 1                      | [[Bay Leaf]]              |
| 1 15 1/2 Ounce Can     | [[Cut Okra]], Drained     |
| 2 4 1/2 Ounce Cans     | [[Shrimp]], Drained       |
|                        | [[Hot Cooked Rice]] (for serving) |
___
### Instructions
1. In a 3-quart saucepan, cook onion in margarine until tender but not brown.
2. Add tomatoes, tomato sauce, chicken broth, sugar, pepper, and bay leaf.
3. Bring mixture to a boil. Reduce heat and simmer, uncovered, for about 30 minutes.
4. Remove bay leaf.
5. Add drained okra and drained shrimp; heat through.
6. Serve gumbo over hot cooked rice in soup plates.
___

>[!Note]
>Serves 6
