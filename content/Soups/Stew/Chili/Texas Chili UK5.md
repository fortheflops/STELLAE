## Instructions
1.  Brown 1 lb. of hamburger (not too dark).
2.  Add some chopped onion.
3.  Drain off the grease.
4.  Add 1 or 2 cans of chili beans, 1 large can of tomatoes, and a bunch of cilantro.
5.  Season with garlic, salt, and pepper to taste.
6.  Simmer slowly.
