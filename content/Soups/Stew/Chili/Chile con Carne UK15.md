## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 pound                    | [[Top Sirloin]]                           |
| 3 tablespoons              | Light [[Olive Oil]]                       |
| 2 cloves                   | [[Garlic]]                                |
| 1                          | [[White Onion]]                           |
| 3 tablespoons              | [[Chili Powder]]                          |
| 1/2 teaspoon               | [[Cumin]]                                 |
| 1 tablespoon               | [[Flour]]                                 |
| 1 cup                      | [[Tomato Paste]]                          |
| 1 1/4 cups                 | [[Beef Stock]]                            |
| 1 cup                      | [[Black Beans]]                           |

___
## Instructions
(The following instructions are inferred as none were provided on the recipe card.)
1.  Cut the top sirloin into small, bite-sized cubes.
2.  In a large pot or Dutch oven, heat the olive oil over medium-high heat.
3.  Add the chopped white onion and cook until softened. Add the minced garlic and cook for another minute until fragrant.
4.  Add the cubed sirloin to the pot and brown on all sides.
5.  Sprinkle the flour, chili powder, and cumin over the meat. Stir to coat everything and cook for one minute.
6.  Stir in the tomato paste and beef stock.
7.  Bring the mixture to a simmer, then reduce the heat to low, cover, and cook for at least 1-2 hours, or until the beef is very tender.
8.  Stir in the black beans (rinsed and drained, if using canned) and cook for another 15-20 minutes to heat through.
9.  Serve hot.
