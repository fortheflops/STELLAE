## Instructions
1.  Brown the hamburger, lean pork, or round steak. Add Crisco if needed to make a generous 2 tablespoons of grease in the pan.
2.  Add flour to thicken (1 tbsp flour to 1 tbsp grease).
3.  Stir and cook on very low heat (don't burn!).
4.  Add some red chili powder to taste.
5.  Stir in water to make a "gravy."
6.  Cook and stir, adding garlic, salt, pepper, and oregano to taste.
7.  Cook for a long time.
