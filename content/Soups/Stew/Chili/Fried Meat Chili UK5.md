## Instructions
1. Fry the meat in lard or bacon grease.
2. Stir in flour as if making a gravy, and cook until it bubbles.
3. Remove the pot from the heat and add chili powder to taste.
4. Add water to the flour mixture, then stir in the garlic, salt, and oregano.
5. You can add bouillon if desired.
