### Instructions
1.  Cut into pieces a frying chicken (1 1/2 to 3 lbs.) and roll in a mixture of 1/2 cup flour, 1 teaspoon salt, 1 1/2 teaspoon pepper.
2.  Fry in an equal mixture of Crisco & butter until golden brown.
3.  Transfer to casserole & pour following sauce over it.
4.  Saute 1/2 cup chopped celery, 1/2 cup diced green pepper, 1/2 cup sliced onion in 2 T. butter.
5.  Add a little more butter, 1 cup catsup, 2 T. Worcestershire sauce, 1 cup water, 2 T. brown sugar, salt & pepper.
6.  Cover casserole & bake 1 hr. at 325 degrees. Yields 5 servings. Serve over rice. This dish can be prepared ahead of time.
