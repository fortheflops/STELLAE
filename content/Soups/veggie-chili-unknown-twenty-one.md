# Veggie Chili

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty One | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Soups
> *A hearty and flavorful vegetarian chili packed with black beans, corn, zucchini, and melted cheddar. Ready in just a few simple steps for a comforting weeknight meal.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 15 mins | None | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large pot
* Serving bowls

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 28 oz. | [[Tomatoes]] | Undrained |
| 16 oz. | [[Salsa]] | Standard |
| 15 oz. | [[Black Beans]] | Rinsed and drained |
| 10 oz. | [[Frozen Corn]] | Frozen |
| 1 c. | [[Zucchini]] | Sliced into halves |
| 1 t. | [[Chili Powder]] | Standard |
| 8 oz. | [[Cheddar Cheese]] | Shredded |

---

## Instructions

### Cooking
1. Bring **28 oz. tomatoes**, **16 oz. salsa**, **15 oz. black beans**, **10 oz. frozen corn**, **1 c. sliced zucchini**, and **1 t. chili powder** to boil; reduce heat and simmer for 10 minutes until vegetables are tender and flavors meld.

### Assembly
2. Sprinkle **2 T. cheddar cheese** at the bottom of each serving bowl.
3. Top with hot chili and an additional **2 T. cheddar cheese**.
4. Garnish with hot red pepper slices, if desired.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the chili in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days. Reheat on the stove before serving with fresh cheese.
