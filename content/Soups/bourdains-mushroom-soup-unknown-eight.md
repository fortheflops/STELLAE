# Bourdain's Mushroom Soup

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Eight | ✍️ **Attribution:** Anthony Bourdain | 📂 **Category:** Soups
> *A rich and velvety mushroom soup adapted from Anthony Bourdain's classic recipe. Earthy button mushrooms are simmered in chicken stock, blended until smooth, and finished with a touch of sherry.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Sauté and Simmer
1. Over medium heat, melt **2 tbsp butter** in a saucepan. Cook **1 thinly sliced onion** until soft and translucent, but not brown.
2. Add the remaining **4 tbsp butter** to the pan, then add **12 oz halved button mushrooms**. Cook for **8 minutes** until the mushrooms are softened and have released their moisture.
3. Pour in **4 cups chicken stock**, add **1 sprig parsley**, and bring to a boil. Once bubbling, reduce the heat to a gentle simmer and cook uncovered for **1 hour** to concentrate the flavors.

### Step 2: Blend and Finish
4. Carefully pour the soup into a blender and process until completely smooth and velvety.
5. Return the blended soup to the saucepan and bring back to a simmer. Pour in **2 oz sherry** and season to taste with **salt** and **black pepper**.
6. Optional (Barbara's Variation): Sauté a few extra ounces of sliced mushrooms in butter and toss them into the finished soup just before serving for added texture.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This soup can be made 1-2 days in advance and stored in an airtight container in the refrigerator. Reheat gently on the stovetop, adding a splash of stock if it has thickened too much.
