## Instructions

### Preparation and Cooking
1. In a pot, combine **butter**, **water**, **oysters**, **clams**, **white sauce**, **paprika**, and **chili sauce**.
2. Cook the mixture over medium heat until the edges of the **oysters** begin to curl and look plump.
3. Stir in the **half-and-half** and **cream**.
4. Continue to cook until the stew is simmering and steaming hot. Do not boil, as this may curdle the dairy.
5. To serve, place a piece of **toast** in the bottom of each bowl and ladle the hot stew over it.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Best served immediately. Leftovers can be stored in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 1 day and reheated very gently over low heat.
