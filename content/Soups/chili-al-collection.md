## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Brown the Beef
1. Cut the **3/4 lb. beef (chuck roast)** into 1/2-inch cubes.
2. Lightly coat the beef cubes in **flour**.
3. In a Dutch oven or heavy pot, melt a small amount of **shortening** over medium-high heat. Add the floured beef cubes and brown them thoroughly on all sides until a deep golden crust forms.

### Step 2: Sauté Aromatics
4. Add the **1/2 medium chopped onion** and **1 stalk chopped celery** to the pot with the beef.
5. Sauté for a few minutes until the vegetables begin to soften and become fragrant.

### Step 3: Simmer and Serve
6. Add **1 tomato**, **1/2 cup tomato juice**, **2 Tbsp. green chili salsa**, **1/16 tsp. cumin**, **1/16 tsp. oregano**, **salt**, **pepper**, and **1/2 cup water** to the pot.
7. Stir well to combine, scraping up any browned bits from the bottom of the pot.
8. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for **1 hour** or more, until the beef is fork-tender and the chili has thickened to your liking.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This chili tastes even better the next day as the flavors meld. Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days, or freeze for up to 3 months.
