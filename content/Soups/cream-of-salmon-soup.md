# Cream of Salmon Soup

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Soups
> *A delicate and refined cream-based soup featuring pureed cooked salmon. This classic recipe relies on a smooth white sauce base seasoned with a hint of cayenne for depth.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 20 mins | None | 4 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Sieve
* Saucepan
* Whisk

## Ingredients

### Soup Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3/4 cup | [[Salmon]] | cooked |
| 4 cups | [[Milk]] | none |
| 1/4 cup | [[Flour]] | none |
| 1 tbsp | [[Salt]] | none |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Pepper]] | none |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Cayenne]] | none |
| 2 tbsp | [[Butter]] | none |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation
1. Rub the **3/4 cup cooked salmon** through a sieve to ensure a smooth texture.

### Step 2: White Sauce
2. Prepare a white sauce using the **4 cups milk**, **1/4 cup flour**, **1 tbsp salt**, **1/4 tsp pepper**, **1/4 tsp cayenne**, and **2 tbsp butter**.
3. Whisk constantly over medium heat until the sauce thickens and coats the back of a spoon.

### Step 3: Finishing
4. Just before serving, blend the prepared white sauce with the sieved **salmon**.
5. If necessary, reheat gently until hot throughout, being careful not to boil.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The white sauce can be made in advance and stored in the refrigerator; add the salmon and reheat just before serving.
