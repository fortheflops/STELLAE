# Cheesy Veggie Soup

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Sharon Fritson | 📂 **Category:** Soups
> *A comforting and creamy vegetable soup loaded with potatoes, celery, onions, and a frozen blend of broccoli, carrots, and cauliflower. Melted Velveeta and cream of chicken soup create a rich, cheesy base that is perfect for chilly days.*

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 package | [[Frozen Vegetables]] | cauliflower, broccoli, and carrots blend |
| 1 can | [[Cream of Chicken Soup]] | undiluted |
| 1 lb. | [[Velveeta Cheese]] | cubed for easy melting |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Cook the Base Vegetables
1. In a large soup pot, combine **1 qt. water**, **2 cups diced potatoes**, **1 cup diced onion**, and **1 cup sliced celery**.
2. Bring to a boil and cook for **10 minutes** until the potatoes begin to soften.

### Step 2: Add Frozen Vegetables
3. Add **1 package frozen cauliflower, broccoli, and carrots** to the pot.
4. Cook for an additional **10 minutes** until all the vegetables are tender.

### Step 3: Finish with Cheese and Simmer
5. Stir in **1 can cream of chicken soup** and **1 lb. Velveeta**.
6. Simmer gently over low heat, stirring frequently, until the cheese is completely melted, smooth, and the soup is heated through.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftovers in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days. Reheat gently on the stovetop over low heat, stirring frequently to prevent the cheese from separating.
