# Shrimp Gumbo

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Soups
> *A comforting southern-style seafood soup featuring shrimp, okra, and tomatoes simmered in a savory chicken broth. Perfectly served over a bed of warm cooked rice for a hearty meal.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Sauté the Aromatics
1. In a **3-quart saucepan**, cook **1/2 cup chopped onion** in **2 tablespoons margarine** until tender but not brown.

### Step 2: Simmer the Base
2. Add **1 16 ounce can tomatoes**, **1 8 ounce can tomato sauce**, **1 13 3/4 ounce can chicken broth**, **1/2 teaspoon sugar**, **1/8 teaspoon pepper**, and **1 bay leaf**.
3. Bring mixture to a boil. Reduce heat and simmer, uncovered, for about **30 minutes** until fragrant and well-blended.

### Step 3: Combine and Serve
4. Remove and discard **1 bay leaf**.
5. Add **1 15 1/2 ounce can cut okra** and **2 4 1/2 ounce cans shrimp**; heat through until hot.
6. Serve the gumbo over **hot cooked rice** in soup plates.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftover gumbo in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days. Reheat gently on the stovetop before serving over fresh rice.
