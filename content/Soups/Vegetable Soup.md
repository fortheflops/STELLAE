## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Broth
1. In a **pressure cooker or large stockpot**, combine the **1 knuckle bone**, **1 soup bone with meat (or beef ribs)**, **onion**, and **garlic**.
2. Cover with **water** and cook until a rich, deeply savory broth has formed and the meat is tender.
3. Remove the **bones** from the pot. Let the broth cool completely until the fat solidifies on top.
4. Once cool, skim the solidified fat from the surface of the broth.

### Step 2: Simmer the Soup
5. Return the skimmed broth to the pot and bring back to a gentle simmer.
6. Add the **1 large can solid pack tomatoes**, and the chopped **celery**, **cabbage**, and **carrots**. Season with **salt to taste**.
7. Add **a few noodles or macaroni** for flavoring.
8. Cook until all the vegetables are tender and translucent, and the pasta is cooked through.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The broth can be made 1-2 days in advance and kept in the refrigerator, which makes skimming the fat even easier. Store the finished soup in an airtight container for up to 4 days.
