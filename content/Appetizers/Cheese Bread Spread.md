# Cheese Bread Spread

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Appetizers
> *A deliciously simple vintage cheese and margarine spread that melts to perfection on toasted bread. Quick to prepare for unexpected guests or a cozy snack.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 5 mins | None | 6–8 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Baking sheet
* Oven

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 Jar | [[Sharp Cheddar Cheese]] | Softened |
| 1 Stick | [[Margarine]] | Softened |
| 1 Teaspoon | [[Mustard]] | Prepared |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mix and Spread
1. In a mixing bowl, blend together the **1 jar sharp cheddar cheese**, **1 stick margarine**, and **1 teaspoon mustard** until completely smooth and well combined.
2. Spread the cheese mixture evenly onto bread squares or sliced French bread.

### Step 2: Warm and Serve
3. Place the prepared bread on a baking sheet and warm in the oven until the topping is bubbly and melted until golden brown and fragrant.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The spread can be mixed ahead of time and stored in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 1 week before spreading on bread and warming.
