# Layered Taco Bean Dip

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Appetizers
> *A classic layered taco bean dip featuring seasoned beans, creamy sour cream and mayonnaise, topped with olives, cheese, lettuce, and tomatoes. Perfect for parties and gatherings, served cold with corn chips.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Assembly
1. Combine **2 10 1/2 oz. cans bean dip** and **1 1 1/4 oz. pkg. taco seasoning mix**.
2. Spread mixture on a 10" tray or in a 10" pie plate.
3. Sprinkle **6 green onions** (finely chopped) over the mixture.
4. Blend together **1 cup mayonnaise or salad dressing** and **1 cup dairy sour cream**.
5. Spread the creamy mixture over the onions, then sprinkle **1 4 1/2 oz. can sliced dark olives** (drained) over the top along with any optional chopped tomatoes, lettuce, avocado, grated cheese, or salsa if desired.

### Step 2: Chilling and Serving
6. Chill thoroughly in the refrigerator until set and cold.
7. Serve with **corn chips**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the dip a few hours in advance and keep covered in the refrigerator until ready to chill and serve.
