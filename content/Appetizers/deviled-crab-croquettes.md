# Deviled Crab Croquettes

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Appetizers
> *Crispy, golden-brown deviled crab croquettes made with a savory blend of crab meat, mustard, and Worcestershire sauce. These classic seafood bites are perfect for serving as an elegant appetizer.*

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

## Instructions

### Preparation and Frying
1. In a large bowl, combine the **1 lb crab meat**, **2 tbsp chopped onion**, **4 tbsp mayonnaise**, **2 tbsp prepared mustard**, **2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce**, **2 tbsp melted butter**, **3 beaten eggs**, and **salt and pepper to taste**.
2. Shape the mixture into 6-8 cakes or ovals.
3. Roll each cake in **cracker crumbs** until evenly coated.
4. Fry in deep fat until the exterior is golden brown and crispy.
5. Remove from the oil and drain on absorbent paper before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The crab mixture can be prepared and shaped up to 4 hours in advance; keep refrigerated until ready to bread and fry.
