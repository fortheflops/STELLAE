# Baked Brie with Almonds

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Appetizers
> *A deliciously simple baked brie appetizer featuring creamy cheese spread with soft butter and topped with crunchy sliced almonds. Perfect for serving warm and bubbly at your next gathering.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5 mins | 10 mins | None | 6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Baking dish
* Oven

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| As needed | [[Soft Butter]] | Softened |
| 1 wheel | [[Brie]] | Sliced |
| To taste | [[Sliced Almonds]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and Baking
1. Spread **as needed soft butter** over the **1 wheel brie**.
2. Cover with **to taste sliced almonds**.
3. Bake at **350 degrees** until the **1 wheel brie** melts completely and becomes bubbly.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the brie with butter and almonds ahead of time, cover, and refrigerate until ready to bake.
