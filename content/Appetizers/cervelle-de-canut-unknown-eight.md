# Cervelle De Canut

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Eight | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Appetizers
> *A traditional Lyon cheese spread made with fromage blanc, fresh herbs, garlic, and olive oil. Perfect served with crusty bread for dipping or spreading.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | None | 1 hour | 4 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Small bowl
* Whisk

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 cup | [[Fromage Blanc]] | Soft fresh cow's milk cheese |
| 3 tbsp | [[Fresh Herbs]] | Chopped, such as tarragon, parsley & chives (chives should dominate) |
| 1 tbsp | [[White Wine Vinegar]] |  |
| 2 tbsp | [[Olive Oil]] |  |
| 1 clove | [[Garlic]] | Minced (about 1 t.) |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Freshly Ground Pepper]] |  |
| 1/4 to 1/2 tsp | [[Salt]] |  |
| 1/4 cup | [[Half-and-Half]] | Optional |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing
1. Turn the **1 cup fromage blanc** into a small bowl.
2. Add the **3 tbsp chopped fresh herbs**, **1 tbsp white wine vinegar**, **2 tbsp olive oil**, **1 clove garlic**, **1/4 tsp freshly ground pepper**, and **1/4 to 1/2 tsp salt**, and beat with a whisk untill well-blended and smooth.
3. If a looser consistency is desired, stir in the optional **1/4 cup half-and-half**.

### Step 2: Chilling and Serving
4. Cover and set aside or refrigerate for **1/2 to 1 hr** until slightly chilled and flavors meld.
5. Adjust seasoning as desired before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Cover and refrigerate for up to 24 hours before serving. Adjust seasonings just before serving.
