# Mexican Dip

## Instructions

### Assembly
1. Spread **1 cup refried beans** evenly across a serving plate.
2. Spread **1 small carton sour cream** carefully over the layer of beans.
3. Spread **1 can guacamole dip** evenly across the sour cream layer.
4. Finely chop the **lettuce**, **onions**, **tomatoes**, and **black olives**, then layer these fresh vegetables over the top of the guacamole.
5. Sprinkle a generous amount of grated **cheddar cheese** and **mozzarella cheese** over the vegetable layer.
6. Drizzle **picante sauce** over the entire dip to finish.
7. Serve immediately or chill, accompanied by **Fritos chips** for dipping.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Can be assembled up to 4 hours in advance. Cover tightly with plastic wrap and refrigerate until ready to serve.
