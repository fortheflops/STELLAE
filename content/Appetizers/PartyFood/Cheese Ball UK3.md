## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
| 6 Ounce               | Diced Cheese                       |
| 2 5 Ounce Jars       | Processed Cheddar Cheese Spread   |
| 1 Teaspoon           | Grated Onion                     |
| 4 3 Ounce Package    | Cream Cheese                      |
| 1 Teaspoon           | Worcestershire Sauce            |
| 1 Teaspoon            | M.S.G.                           |
| 1 Cup                 | Ground Pecans                     |
| 1/2 Cup               | Finely Chopped Parsley           |
___
### Instructions
1. Have cheese at room temperature.
2. Mix cheese, onion, Worcestershire, & M.S.G. together until thoroughly blended.
3. Add 1/2 cup pecans, and 1/2 cup Parsley
4. Shape into a ball
5. Place in a bowl lined with wax paper or foil.
6. Chill overnight.
7. About 1 hour before serving, roll the ball in a mixture of remaining pecans + parsley.
8. Place on a platter and surround with crisp croutons.
