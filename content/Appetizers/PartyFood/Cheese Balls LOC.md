## Recipe
| Measurements           | Ingredients        |
| :--------------------- | :----------------- |

___
## Instructions
1.  Work into 1 lb. cream cheese: $\frac{3}{4}$ cup ranch dressing, 1 tablespoon chives, $\frac{1}{2}$ teaspoon paprika, $\frac{1}{2}$ teaspoon seasoning salt, and $\frac{1}{2}$ teaspoon onion salt (optional).
2.  Form into a ball.
3.  Roll in chopped walnuts or parsley.
4.  Chill. (The recipe notes: "Can be frozen.")
