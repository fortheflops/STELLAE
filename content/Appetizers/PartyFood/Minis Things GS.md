## Instructions
1.  Combine a little mayonnaise, a little bit of cheese, salt, and pepper.
2.  Spread the mixture on a base (e.g., crackers or small bread slices - this is implied as it's an appetizer to be put in the oven).
3.  Put in oven at 350°F - 375°F.
4.  Bake for just 3 minutes.
