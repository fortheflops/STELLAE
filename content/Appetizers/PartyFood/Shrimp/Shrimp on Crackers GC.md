## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |

| One (4 $\frac{1}{2}$ oz) Can     | [[Shrimp]] (or 60 cents worth of cooked shrimp - an old measure!) |
| Dash                             | [[Salt]]                                  |
| Dash                             | [[Pepper]]                                |
| $\frac{1}{3}$ Cup                | [[Mayonnaise]]                            |
| $\frac{1}{4}$ Cup                | Grated [[Cheddar Cheese]]                 |
| A Little (Optional)              | [[Onion]], Minced                         |
| As Needed                        | Ritz or other [[Crackers]]                |
___
## Instructions
1.  Use Ritz or other crackers as the base.
2.  Drain the shrimp. If using a can, mash the shrimp. If using cooked shrimp, chop them if necessary.
3.  In a bowl, combine the shrimp with $\frac{1}{3}$ cup of mayonnaise, a dash of salt, and a dash of pepper. (The card also mentions "a little onion," which can be finely minced and added here if desired).
4.  You can mix $\frac{1}{4}$ cup of grated cheddar cheese into the shrimp mixture now, or sprinkle it on top later.
5.  Spread the shrimp mixture onto the crackers.
6.  If you didn't mix the cheese in, sprinkle it on top of the shrimp mixture now.
7.  Broil or put in the oven until the cheese melts a little. (If baking, a moderate oven like 350-375°F for a few minutes should work).
8.  Makes about 2 dozen appetizers.
