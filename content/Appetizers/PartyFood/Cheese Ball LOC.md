## Recipe
| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
| 8 Ounces                  | [[Cream Cheese]]        |
| 3 Ounces                  | Soft [[Cheddar Cheese]] |
| 1 Ounce                   | [[Blue Cheese]]         |
| $\frac{1}{2}$ Cup         | Chopped [[Onions]]      |
| As Needed                 | Chopped [[Pecans]] (or other nuts) |
___
### Instructions
1.  Mix together 8 ounces cream cheese, 3 ounces soft cheddar cheese, 1 ounce blue cheese, and $\frac{1}{2}$ cup chopped onions.
2.  Form into a ball.
3.  Sprinkle with chopped pecans.
