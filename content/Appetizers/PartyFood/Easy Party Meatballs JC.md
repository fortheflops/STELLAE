## Recipe
| Measurements      | Ingredients                   |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| **For the Meatballs**|                                   |
| 2 pounds          | [[Ground Beef]] (hamburger)       |
| 1 large           | [[Egg]], slightly beaten          |
| 1 large           | [[Onion]], grated                 |
| **For the Sauce** |                                   |
| 1 (12-ounce) jar  | [[Chili Sauce]]                 |
| 1 (10-ounce) jar  | [[Grape Jelly]]                 |
| 1                 | [[Lemon]], juiced                 |
___
## Instructions
1.  **To make the sauce:** In a slow cooker or large pot, combine the chili sauce, grape jelly, and lemon juice. Heat over low heat, stirring occasionally, until the jelly melts and the sauce is smooth.
2.  **To make the meatballs:** In a large bowl, gently mix the ground beef, beaten egg, and grated onion with your hands until just combined.
3.  Shape the mixture into small, 1-inch party-sized meatballs.
4.  Carefully place the raw meatballs into the simmering sauce.
5.  Cover and cook on low until the meatballs are cooked through, about 4-6 hours in a slow cooker or 2-3 hours on the stovetop.
___

>[!Note]
>The original recipe card was very brief, only stating to "Mix and shape balls. Drop into sauce." The detailed instructions above were added based on standard cooking techniques for this dish to provide a complete guide. For a different texture, you can also brown the meatballs in a skillet before adding them to the sauce.
