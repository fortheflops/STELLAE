# Cheese Puffs

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Appetizers
> *A classic savory appetizer featuring sharp cheddar cheese dough wrapped around stuffed olives and baked to golden perfection. Perfect for parties, these warm cheese puffs can be prepped ahead and frozen until ready to bake.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 10 mins | 4 hours | 24 puffs |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Baking sheet
* Measuring cups
* Measuring spoons

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 cup | [[Sharp Cheddar Cheese]] | grated, natural |
| 1/4 cup | [[Butter]] | softened |
| 1/2 cup | [[All-Purpose Flour]] | sifted |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Salt]] |  |
| 1/2 tsp | [[Paprika]] |  |
| 24 | [[Stuffed Olives]] | approximate |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Dough
1. Cream the **1 cup grated natural sharp cheese** and **1/4 cup soft butter** together in a mixing bowl until smooth and well combined.
2. Add the next three ingredients: **1/2 cup sifted all purpose flour**, **1/4 tsp salt**, and **1/2 tsp paprika**. Mix well until a cohesive dough forms.

### Step 2: Assemble and Chill
3. Wrap approximately **1 tsp dough** around each of the **24 stuffed olives**, shaping it into a neat ball so the olive is completely enclosed.
4. Freeze the puffs, or refrigerate them for **4 to 5 hours** before they are ready to use.

### Step 3: Bake and Serve
5. Preheat your oven to **400°F**.
6. Bake the chilled or frozen puffs for **10 minutes** until the pastry is cooked through and lightly golden.
7. Serve warm.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** These cheese puffs can be prepared ahead and refrigerated for 4 to 5 hours, or frozen completely on a baking sheet and stored in a freezer bag until ready to bake.
