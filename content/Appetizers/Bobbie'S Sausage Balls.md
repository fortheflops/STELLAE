*A classic and easy three-ingredient appetizer from Bobbie, made with Bisquick, pork sausage, and sharp cheddar cheese. Simple to make and always a crowd-pleaser for any gathering.*

### Author
Bobbie

---

## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 3 cups | [[Bisquick]] baking mix |
| 1 pound | Raw [[Pork Sausage]] |
| 10 ounces | [[Sharp Cheddar Cheese]], shredded |

---

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 350°F.
2. In a large bowl, combine the **3 cups Bisquick baking mix**, **1 pound raw pork sausage**, and **10 ounces sharp cheddar cheese, shredded**.
3. Using your hands, mix the ingredients together thoroughly until everything is well combined. For easier mixing, allow the **raw pork sausage** and **sharp cheddar cheese** to come to room temperature before combining them with the baking mix.
4. Roll the mixture into small, 1-inch balls.
5. Place the sausage balls on an ungreased baking sheet.
6. Bake for about **15 minutes**, or until golden brown and cooked through.
7. Drain on paper towels before serving warm.
