# Bobbie'S Sausage Balls

> 📜 **Collection:** Jill's Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Bobbie | 📂 **Category:** Appetizers
> *A classic and easy three-ingredient appetizer from Bobbie, made with Bisquick, pork sausage, and sharp cheddar cheese. Simple to make and always a crowd-pleaser.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 15 mins | None | 48 sausage balls |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large mixing bowl
* Baking sheet
* Paper towels

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3 cups | [[Bisquick]] | baking mix |
| 1 pound | raw [[Pork Sausage]] | None |
| 10 ounces | [[Sharp Cheddar Cheese]] | shredded |

---

## Instructions

### Prepare the Mixture
1. Preheat oven to **350°F (175°C)**.
2. In a large bowl, combine the **3 cups Bisquick baking mix**, **1 pound raw pork sausage**, and **10 ounces shredded sharp cheddar cheese**.
3. Using your hands, mix the ingredients together thoroughly until everything is well combined and a cohesive dough forms.

### Shape and Bake
4. Roll the mixture into small, **1-inch balls**.
5. Place the sausage balls on an ungreased baking sheet.
6. Bake for about **15 minutes**, or until golden brown, fragrant, and cooked through.
7. Drain on paper towels before serving warm.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** For easier mixing, allow the sausage and cheese to come to room temperature before combining. Unbaked sausage balls can be frozen on a baking sheet and stored in a freezer bag for up to 3 months.
