# Spinach Dip For Veg

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Marilyn | 📂 **Category:** Appetizers
> *A creamy and flavorful spinach dip made with mayonnaise, sour cream, and aromatic herbs. Perfect for serving with fresh vegetables.*

---

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | None | 1 hour | 8 servings |

## Ingredients

### Dip Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 pkg. | [[Frozen chopped spinach]] | cooked 2-3 min and drained well |
| 1 C | [[Hellman mayonnaise]] | None |
| 1 C | [[Sour cream]] | None |
| 1/2 C | [[Small chopped onion]] | chopped |
| 1/4 C | [[Chopped parsley]] | dehydrated |
| 1 tsp | [[Dill weed]] | None |
| 1 tsp | [[Salt]] | None |
| 1 tsp | [[Lemon juice]] | None |
| 1 tsp | [[Beau monde]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing
1. In a mixing bowl, combine the cooked and well-drained **1 pkg. frozen chopped spinach**.
2. Stir in **1 C Hellman mayonnaise**, **1 C sour cream**, and the **1/2 C small chopped onion** until fully incorporated.
3. Add **1/4 C chopped parsley (dehydrated)**, **1 tsp dill weed**, **1 tsp salt**, **1 tsp lemon juice**, and **1 tsp Beau monde**.
4. Mix all ingredients thoroughly until smooth and well blended. Chill in the refrigerator for at least 1 hour before serving to let the flavors meld together.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Prepare the dip up to a day in advance and store covered in the refrigerator until ready to serve.
