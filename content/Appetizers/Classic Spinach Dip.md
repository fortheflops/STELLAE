# Classic Spinach Dip

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Appetizers
> *A simple and creamy classic spinach dip perfect for parties and gatherings. This vintage recipe combines fresh herbs and frozen spinach for a quick, crowd-pleasing appetizer.*

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Parsley]] | minced |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Green Onion]] | chopped, tops included |
| 1 Cup | [[Real Mayonnaise]] | room temperature |
| 1 Bag | [[Frozen Chopped Spinach]] | thawed and squeezed dry |
| To Taste | [[Salt]] & [[Pepper]] | freshly ground |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Combine and Chill
1. In a large mixing bowl, combine the **1 bag frozen chopped spinach**, **1 cup real mayonnaise**, **1/2 cup parsley**, and **1/2 cup green onion**.
2. Stir thoroughly until the mixture is uniform and creamy.
3. Season with **salt** and **pepper** to taste.
4. Cover and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes to allow the flavors to meld until chilled and fragrant.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This dip is best when made at least 2 hours in advance; store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days.
