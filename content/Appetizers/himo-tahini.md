# Himo Tahini

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Appetizers
> *A classic, large-batch homemade hummus recipe featuring a bright lemon profile and creamy tahini base. This versatile dip is easily adjusted to taste and blended until perfectly smooth.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 5 mins | None | 12 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Pot
* Potato masher
* Blender

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3 cans | [[Chickpeas]] | Progresso brand |
| 5 | [[Lemons]] | Juiced, or to taste |
| 2-3 cups | [[Tahini]] | Adjust for consistency |
| to taste | [[Salt]] | Fine sea salt |
| to taste | [[Garlic]] | Minced or crushed |
| as needed | [[Water]] | For thinning |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation
1. Boil the **3 cans chickpeas** for a few minutes until they soften.
2. Drain the **3 cans chickpeas** and mash thoroughly using a potato masher.

### Step 2: Mixing and Blending
3. Add the **juice of 5 lemons**, **2-3 cups tahini**, **salt**, and **garlic** to the mashed chickpeas.
4. Continue adding ingredients and mixing until the flavor profile is balanced to your preference.
5. Transfer the mixture to a blender and process until the texture is completely smooth.
6. Add small amounts of **water** if needed to reach your desired consistency.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 5 days; the flavors will continue to meld over time.
