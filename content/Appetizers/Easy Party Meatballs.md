## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Sauce
1. In a slow cooker or large pot, combine the **1 (12-ounce) jar chili sauce**, **1 (10-ounce) jar grape jelly**, and **1 juiced lemon**. Heat over low heat, stirring occasionally, until the jelly melts and the sauce is completely smooth and glossy.

### Step 2: Form the Meatballs
2. In a large bowl, gently mix the **2 pounds ground beef**, **1 large slightly beaten egg**, and **1 large grated onion** with your hands until just combined. Do not overmix to ensure the meatballs remain tender.
3. Shape the mixture into small, 1-inch party-sized meatballs.

### Step 3: Cook
4. Carefully place the raw meatballs into the simmering sauce.
5. Cover and cook on low until the meatballs are cooked through and have absorbed the sweet and tangy flavors, about **4-6 hours** in a slow cooker or **2-3 hours** on the stovetop.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** These meatballs can be made ahead and kept warm in a slow cooker for hours. Store leftovers in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days.
