# Vegetable Sandwiches

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Jan Batchelor | 📂 **Category:** Appetizers
> *A classic 1970s party spread featuring grated cucumbers, carrots, and celery bound with mayonnaise and stabilized with gelatin. Perfect for spreading on rye bread to make elegant tea sandwiches.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 5 mins | 2 hours | 20 sandwiches |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Grater
* Measuring cups
* Measuring spoons
* Small saucepan
* Pyrex measuring cup
* Mixing bowl
* Spatula

## Ingredients

### Vegetable Base
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 | [[Cucumbers]] | grated |
| 2 | [[Carrots]] | grated |
| 1 cup | [[Celery]] | grated or chopped fine |
| 1 small | [[Onion]] | about 1/2 the size of an egg, grated |

### Dressing & Binder
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 tsp | [[Lemon Juice]] |  |
| 1 tsp | [[Salt]] |  |
| 2 cups | [[Mayonnaise]] | preferably Hellmann's |
| 1 envelope | [[Gelatin]] | plain, unflavored |
| 1/4 cup | [[Water]] | cold, for softening gelatin |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Gelatin
1. In a **Pyrex measuring cup**, soften **1 envelope plain gelatin** in **1/4 cup cold water**.
2. Set the cup in a small pan of water over medium heat on the stove, stirring occasionally until the **gelatin** is completely dissolved and clear.

### Step 2: Mix and Chill
3. In a large mixing bowl, combine the grated **2 cucumbers**, grated **2 carrots**, **1 cup celery**, and grated **1 onion**.
4. Stir in **1 tsp lemon juice**, **1 tsp salt**, and **2 cups mayonnaise** until thoroughly mixed.
5. Pour the dissolved **gelatin** into the vegetable mixture and stir well to incorporate completely.
6. Cover and place in the refrigerator to chill and set up until firm, about **2 hours**.

### Step 3: Assembly
7. Spread the chilled mixture onto rye bread or your choice of bread to make about **20 sandwiches**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This vegetable sandwich spread keeps well in the refrigerator for up to 2 weeks.
