# Artichoke Nibbles

> 📜 **Collection:** Patsy Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** L. | 📂 **Category:** Appetizers
> *A savory, bite-sized baked appetizer featuring marinated artichoke hearts, shredded cheddar cheese, and fresh herbs. Prepared easily in the microwave, these warm squares are perfect for casual entertaining.*

---

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 13 mins | 10 mins | 24 bites |

* 7x11-inch baking dish
* Small mixing bowl

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep & Microwave Aromatics
1. Drain the marinade from **1 jar (6 oz) marinated artichoke hearts** into a 7x11-inch baking dish. Drain the second jar (reserving marinade for another use if desired) and coarsely chop all **2 jars (6 oz each) marinated artichoke hearts**.
2. Add **1 small finely chopped onion** and **1 clove minced garlic** to the marinade in the baking dish.
3. Cover the dish with wax paper and microwave on high power for **3 mins**, stirring after **2 mins**, until onions are tender and fragrant.

### Step 2: Mix & Microwave Nibbles
4. In a small mixing bowl, beat **4 large eggs** lightly.
5. Stir in **1/4 cup fine dry breadcrumbs**, **1/4 tsp salt**, **1/8 tsp black pepper**, **1/8 tsp dried oregano**, and **1/8 tsp Tabasco sauce**.
6. Fold in **2 cups shredded Cheddar cheese**, **2 tbsp minced fresh parsley**, and the chopped **2 jars (6 oz each) marinated artichoke hearts**.
7. Pour the egg mixture into the cooked onion mixture in the baking dish and stir until well blended.
8. Spread the mixture evenly in the baking dish and cover with wax paper.
9. Microwave on high power for **8 to 10 mins**, rotating the dish a 1/4 turn after **5 mins**, until fully set when lightly pressed in the center.

### Step 3: Rest & Serve
10. Let stand, covered with wax paper, for **10 mins** to finish setting.
11. Cut into 1-inch squares and serve warm.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Can be baked ahead and refrigerated; reheat individual pieces briefly in the microwave or oven before serving.
