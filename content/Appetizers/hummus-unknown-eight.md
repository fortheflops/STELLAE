# Hummus

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Eight | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Appetizers
> *A classic, creamy hummus recipe made with blended garbanzo beans, tahini, and lemon juice. Topped with a savory sprinkle of paprika and a drizzle of olive oil for the ultimate Mediterranean dip.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Blending
1. Combine **1 to 2 cloves garlic**, **2 cups drained garbanzos**, **4 tablespoons lemon juice**, **1/4 teaspoon salt**, **3 tablespoons tahini**, and **2 tablespoons cold water** in a blender or food processor.
2. Blend until completely smooth and creamy.

### Step 2: Garnish and Serve
3. Transfer the blended mixture to a serving dish.
4. Sprinkle **1/4 teaspoon paprika** over the mixture.
5. Dribble **2 tablespoons olive oil** over the top before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 to 4 days.
