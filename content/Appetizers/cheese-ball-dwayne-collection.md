# Cheese Ball

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mix and Chill
1. In a medium mixing bowl, combine **12 oz. Cream Cheese**, **4 oz. Blue Cheese**, **4 oz. Sharp Cheddar Cheese**, **1 clove Garlic**, **1 tbsp Onion**, **1 tbsp Worcestershire Sauce**, and a **dash** each of **Salt**, **Black Pepper**, and **Lawry's Seasoning Salt**.
2. Mix thoroughly using a hand mixer or sturdy spoon until the mixture is completely uniform and smooth.
3. Cover the bowl and allow to chill in the refrigerator for at least **2 hours** until firm enough to shape.

### Step 2: Shape and Coat
4. Divide the chilled mixture in half and form into two cohesive balls.
5. Either roll the balls in chopped **Pecans** until fully coated, or sprinkle generously with **Paprika** for color.
6. Serve chilled alongside crackers.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The cheese balls can be shaped and wrapped tightly in plastic wrap up to 3 days in advance. If using pecans, roll them in the nuts just before serving to ensure they stay crunchy.
