# Savory Chicken Almond Spread

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Appetizers
> *A rich and savory party spread featuring finely chopped chicken, salted almonds, and pimiento-stuffed olives folded into a whipped, seasoned butter base. Perfect for tea sandwiches or elegant holiday appetizers.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 0 mins | None | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Hand mixer
* Chef's knife
* Cutting board

---
## Ingredients

### Butter Base
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 cup | [[Butter]] | softened |
| 1 tbsp | [[Lemon Juice]] | fresh |
| 1 tsp | [[Worcestershire Sauce]] | None |
| 1 tsp | [[Prepared Mustard]] | None |

### Chicken Mixture
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 cups | [[Chicken]] | cooked, finely chopped (approximately 3 1/2 chicken breasts) |
| 1 cup | [[Almonds]] | salted, chopped |
| 4 tbsp | [[Pimiento-Stuffed Olives]] | chopped |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Whip the Butter Base
1. In a medium mixing bowl, combine **1 cup butter**, **1 tbsp lemon juice**, **1 tsp Worcestershire sauce**, and **1 tsp prepared mustard**.
2. Beat the mixture vigorously using a hand mixer until it is light, fluffy, and uniform in texture.

### Step 2: Prepare and Fold
3. Divide the whipped butter mixture into fourths.
4. Gently fold in the **2 cups finely chopped cooked chicken**, **1 cup chopped salted almonds**, and **4 tbsp chopped pimiento-stuffed olives** until thoroughly combined and a spreadable consistency is achieved.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Can be prepared up to 2 days in advance. Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator, and let soften slightly at room temperature before serving.
