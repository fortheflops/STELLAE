# Dill Dip For Veg

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Appetizers
> *A creamy and flavorful dill dip made with sour cream and mayonnaise, seasoned with dill weed, beau monde, onion salt, and parsley. Perfect for serving with fresh vegetables at any gathering.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | None | 1 hour | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Measuring cups
* Measuring spoons
* Spoon

---
## Ingredients

### Dip Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 cup | [[Sour cream]] | Standard |
| 1 cup | [[Hellmann's mayonnaise]] | Required, Miracle Whip won't work |
| 1 tsp | [[Dill weed]] | Dried or fresh |
| 1 tsp | [[Beau monde]] | Seasoning |
| 1 tsp | [[Onion salt]] | Fine |
| 1 Tbsp | [[Parsley flakes]] | Dried |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing and Chilling
1. In a mixing bowl, combine **1 cup sour cream**, **1 cup Hellmann's mayonnaise**, **1 tsp dill weed**, **1 tsp beau monde**, **1 tsp onion salt**, and **1 Tbsp parsley flakes**.
2. Mix thoroughly until completely combined and smooth.
3. Let set in the refrigerator for at least **one hour** to allow the flavors to meld before serving with fresh vegetables.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days before serving.
