# Green Chili Squares

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Five | ✍️ **Attribution:** Helen Williams | 📂 **Category:** Appetizers
> *Deliciously cheesy and savory Green Chili Squares featuring layered whole chiles and grated cheese baked in a fluffy egg custard. Perfect for serving warm as an appetizer or party snack.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 25 mins | None | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Baking dish
* Grater
* Mixing bowl

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 lb. | [[Cheese]] | Grated |
| 2 cans | [[Whole Chilies]] | Canned |
| 1 doz. (6-8) | [[Eggs]] | Well beaten |
| As needed | [[Salt]] | To taste |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Assembly and Baking
1. Layer the **2 cans whole chilies** and **2 lb. grated cheese** in a greased baking dish.
2. Pour the **1 doz. (6-8) well beaten eggs** over the layers.
3. Add **salt to taste**.
4. Bake at 350 degrees for **25 min** until set and golden.
5. Cut into squares and serve warm.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the dish ahead of time, cover, and refrigerate before pouring the eggs and baking.
