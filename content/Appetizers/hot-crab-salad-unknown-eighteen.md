# Hot Crab Salad

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Mix
1. Preheat oven to **325°F**.
2. In a large bowl, thoroughly mix the **1 can (6 3/4 oz) crab meat**, **1 can (4 1/2 oz) shrimp**, **1 cup diced celery**, **1/2 cup chopped green pepper**, **1 cup mayonnaise**, **1/2 cup chopped onion**, **2 tsp Worcestershire sauce**, **3 oz slivered almonds**, and **salt and pepper to taste**.

### Step 2: Assemble and Bake
3. Use half of the **1/4 lb margarine** to grease a casserole dish.
4. Transfer the seafood mixture into the greased casserole dish.
5. Mix the remaining half of the **1/4 lb margarine** with the **1/2 cup bread crumbs** and spread evenly over the top of the casserole.
6. Bake for about **40 minutes** at **325°F** until the topping is golden brown and the edges are bubbling hot.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the seafood mixture in the casserole dish up to 24 hours in advance and store covered in the refrigerator. Add the buttered breadcrumb topping just before baking.
