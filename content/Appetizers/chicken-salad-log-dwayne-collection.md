# Chicken Salad Log

> *A classic savory cheese and chicken spread shaped into a log or ball, coated with toasted sesame seeds. Perfect for entertaining and serving with crackers.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing and Shaping
1. Mix **8 oz cream cheese**, **1/4 cup mayonnaise**, **2 Tbsp lemon juice**, **1/2 tsp salt**, **1/4 tsp ground ginger**, and **1/4 tsp pepper** in a large bowl with a fork until smooth.
2. Fold in **2 cups white chicken**, **1/4 cup onion**, and **2 chopped hard boiled eggs** until well combined.
3. Shape mixture into a log or ball, cover, and refrigerate for **4 hrs** until firm.

### Step 2: Decorating
4. Decorate by rolling the chilled log in **sesame seeds** until fully coated.
5. Arrange **green pepper** rings around the log before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Can be prepared up to 24 hours in advance, keeping it refrigerated until just before rolling in sesame seeds and serving.
