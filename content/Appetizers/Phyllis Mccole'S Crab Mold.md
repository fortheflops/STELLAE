# Phyllis Mccole'S Crab Mold

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Phyllis McCole | 📂 **Category:** Appetizers
> *A classic, savory molded crab dip from a 1986 recipe. This elegant appetizer combines tender crab meat, rich cream cheese, and crisp vegetables in a smooth gelatin base, perfect for holiday gatherings.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 5 mins | 2 hours (Chilling) | 12–16 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large mixing bowl
* Small mixing bowl
* Small saucepan
* Decorative mold (approx. 4-5 cup capacity)
* Rubber spatula

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 (6-8 oz) can | [[Crab Meat]] | drained and flaked |
| 8 Ounces | [[Cream Cheese]] | softened |
| 1 Cup | [[Mayonnaise]] | room temperature |
| 1 (10.5 oz) can | Cream of [[Mushroom Soup]] | undiluted |
| 2 Packages | Knox [[Gelatin]] | unflavored |
| 2 Tablespoons | Cold [[Water]] | for blooming gelatin |
| 1 Cup | [[Celery]] | finely chopped |
| ½ Cup | [[Onion]] | finely chopped |
| As needed | [[Cooking Spray]] | non-stick, for the mold |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Base
1. In a large bowl, combine the softened **8 ounces cream cheese** and **1 cup mayonnaise**. Mix thoroughly until completely smooth and free of lumps.
2. Stir in the **1 cup finely chopped celery**, **½ cup finely chopped onion**, and the **1 (6-8 oz) can drained, flaked crab meat** until evenly distributed. Set aside.

### Step 2: Dissolve the Gelatin
3. In a small bowl, sprinkle the **2 packages Knox gelatin** over the **2 tablespoons cold water**. Let it stand for 1 to 2 minutes to soften and bloom.
4. In a small saucepan, gently heat the **1 (10.5 oz) can cream of mushroom soup** over low heat until warm and steaming, but do not let it boil.
5. Add the softened **gelatin** mixture to the warm **soup** and stir constantly until the gelatin is completely dissolved and the mixture is silky smooth.
6. Remove the soup from the heat and allow it to cool to room temperature.

### Step 3: Combine, Mold, and Chill
7. Pour the cooled **soup** mixture into the large bowl with the **cream cheese** mixture. Stir until everything is thoroughly blended and uniform in color.
8. Lightly coat a decorative mold with **non-stick cooking spray**.
9. Pour the final mixture into the prepared mold and refrigerate for at least **2 hours**, or until fully set, firm, and slightly springy to the touch.
10. To serve, carefully run a thin knife around the edges, unmold onto a serving platter, and accompany with crackers.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This mold can be prepared up to 24 hours in advance. Keep refrigerated in the mold until just before serving to maintain its shape.
