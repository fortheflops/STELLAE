## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 pound                    | [[Shrimp]], shelled and cooked            |
| 1 large bottle             | [[Ketchup]]                               |
| 1 small jar                | [[Sweet Pickle Relish]] (or chopped sweet pickles)|
| As Needed                  | Chopped [[Celery Hearts]]                 |
___
## Instructions
1.  Combine the cooked shrimp, ketchup, sweet pickle relish, and chopped celery hearts in a general bowl.
2.  Mix everything together.
3.  Let the mixture stand for at least 1 hour before serving to allow the flavors to meld.
