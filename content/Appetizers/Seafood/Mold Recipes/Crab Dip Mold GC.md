## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| One Cup                          | [[Mayonnaise]]                            |
| One (8 oz) Package               | [[Cream Cheese]], softened                |
| One Cup                          | Finely Chopped [[Celery]]                 |
| $\frac{1}{2}$ Cup                | Finely Chopped Green [[Onions]]           |
| One Can                          | Cream of [[Mushroom Soup]]                |
| Two Small Cans                   | [[Crab Meat]] (or fresh Crab, Tuna, or Shrimp) |
| Two Packages                     | Plain [[Gelatin]] (Unflavored)            |
| $\frac{1}{4}$ Cup                | Cold [[Water]] (implied, for gelatin)     |
___
## Instructions
1.  In a large bowl, soften the cream cheese.
2.  Add mayonnaise, finely chopped onion, finely chopped celery, and the crab meat (or tuna/shrimp). Mix well.
3.  In a small bowl, dissolve two packages of plain gelatin in $\frac{1}{4}$ cup of cold water.
4.  In a saucepan, warm the can of cream of mushroom soup.
5.  Add the gelatin mixture to the warm soup and stir until the gelatin is completely dissolved.
6.  Set the soup mixture aside to cool.
7.  When the soup mixture is cooled, add it to the cream cheese mixture and mix well.
8.  Spray a mold with Pam (or other non-stick spray).
9.  Pour the mixture into the prepared mold.
10. Refrigerate for several hours or until firm.
11. Unmold on a platter and serve with Ritz Crackers or Wheat Thins.
