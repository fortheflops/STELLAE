# Hot Shrimp And Cheese Puffs

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Appetizers
> *A quick and easy vintage appetizer featuring a savory mixture of shrimp, mayonnaise, and cheddar cheese spread on crackers. Baked or broiled until hot, bubbly, and golden brown.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 5 mins | None | 24 appetizers |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Small bowl
* Baking sheet
* Fork

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 (4.5 oz) can | Canned [[Shrimp]] | drained and mashed (or cooked shrimp, finely chopped) |
| 1/3 cup | [[Mayonnaise]] | None |
| 1/4 cup | Grated [[Cheddar Cheese]] | None |
| 1 tbsp | Minced [[Onion]] | optional |
| 1 dash | [[Salt]] | None |
| 1 dash | [[Pepper]] | None |
| Approx. 24 | Ritz or other [[Crackers]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Mixture
1. Preheat your oven to **375°F (190°C)** or set your broiler to low.
2. Drain the **1 (4.5 oz) can canned shrimp** well. If using canned shrimp, mash it with a fork. If using larger cooked shrimp, chop them finely.
3. In a small bowl, combine the **1 (4.5 oz) can canned shrimp**, **1/3 cup mayonnaise**, **1 tbsp minced onion** (if using), **1 dash salt**, and **1 dash pepper**.
4. You can either stir the **1/4 cup grated cheddar cheese** directly into the shrimp mixture now, or save it for topping.

### Step 2: Assemble and Bake
5. Arrange the **approx. 24 crackers** in a single layer on a baking sheet.
6. Spread a small amount of the shrimp mixture onto each cracker.
7. If you did not mix the **1/4 cup grated cheddar cheese** in earlier, sprinkle it over the top of each appetizer now.
8. Bake for **4-5 minutes** or broil for **1-2 minutes**, watching carefully, until the topping is hot and the cheese is melted, bubbly, and lightly browned.
9. Serve immediately.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The shrimp mixture can be prepared a few hours ahead and stored in the refrigerator. Assemble on crackers just before baking to prevent them from getting soggy.
