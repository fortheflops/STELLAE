## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 4 to 6                     | [[Avocados]]                              |
| Juice of 1/2               | [[Lime]]                                  |
| To Taste                   | [[Garlic Salt]]                           |
| 1 small can                | Chopped [[Green Chiles]]                  |
| As Needed                  | [[Pace Picante Sauce]] (medium hot)       |
___
## Instructions
1.  Mash the avocados in a bowl.
2.  Stir in the lime juice and garlic salt.
3.  Fold in the chopped green chiles and Pace Picante sauce to your desired taste and consistency.
4.  Serve immediately with tortilla chips.
