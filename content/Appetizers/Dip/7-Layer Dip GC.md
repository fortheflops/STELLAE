## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 (16 ounce) can           | [[Refried Beans]]                         |
| 2                          | [[Avocados]], mashed                      |
| 1 pint                     | [[Sour Cream]]                            |
| 1 jar                      | [[Salsa]] (mild or medium)                |
| 3                          | [[Green Onions]], chopped                 |
| As Needed                  | Grated [[Cheddar Cheese]]                 |
| 1 small can                | Chopped [[Olives]]                        |
___
## Instructions
1.  In a clear serving dish (like a glass pie plate or trifle bowl), spread the ingredients in the following order to create distinct layers:
2.  Layer 1: Refried Beans
3.  Layer 2: Mashed Avocados
4.  Layer 3: Sour Cream
5.  Layer 4: Salsa
6.  Layer 5: Chopped Green Onions
7.  Layer 6: Grated Cheddar Cheese
8.  Layer 7: Chopped Olives
9.  Cover and refrigerate until ready to serve.
___

>[!Note]
>Serve with regular or nacho-flavored tortilla chips.
