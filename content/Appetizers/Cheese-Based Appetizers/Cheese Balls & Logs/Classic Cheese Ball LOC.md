*A classic and savory cheese ball made with a blend of softened cream cheese, cheddar, and blue cheese, with finely chopped onions for flavor. It's rolled in crunchy pecans, making it a perfect appetizer for parties and gatherings.*

## Instructions
1.  In a medium bowl, combine the softened cream cheese, soft cheddar cheese, crumbled blue cheese, and finely chopped onions. Mix with a spatula or an electric mixer on low speed until thoroughly blended.
2.  Scrape the cheese mixture onto a piece of plastic wrap. Use the wrap to help shape the mixture into a uniform ball. Wrap tightly and refrigerate for at least 1 hour, or until firm. This step helps the flavors meld and makes the ball easier to handle.
3.  Spread the chopped pecans evenly on a plate or in a shallow dish. Unwrap the chilled cheese ball and roll it in the pecans, pressing gently to coat the entire surface.
4.  Serve immediately with your favorite crackers, pretzels, or vegetable sticks. The cheese ball can be made a day or two in advance and stored tightly wrapped in the refrigerator.
