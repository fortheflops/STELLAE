# Cheese Ball

> 📜 **Collection:** Elizabeth Knee Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Appetizers
> *A classic, crowd-pleasing party appetizer featuring a savory-sweet blend of cream cheese, crushed pineapple, chives, and bell pepper. Rolled in a generous coating of chopped pecans, it is the perfect centerpiece for any holiday spread.*

## Ingredients

### Cheese Ball Mixture
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 large bricks | [[Cream Cheese]] | softened |
| 1 small can | [[Crushed Pineapple]] | well drained |
| 1/4 cup | [[Chives]] | finely chopped |
| 1/4 cup | [[Bell Pepper]] | finely chopped |
| 1/2 cup | [[Pecans]] | chopped |
| 1/2 tsp. | [[Seasoned Salt]] | or seasonal seasoning |

### Coating
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 cup | [[Pecans]] | chopped, for rolling |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Combine the Ingredients
1. In a medium mixing bowl, beat the **2 large bricks cream cheese** until smooth and creamy.
2. Fold in the drained **1 small can crushed pineapple**, **1/4 cup chives**, **1/4 cup bell pepper**, **1/2 cup chopped pecans**, and **1/2 tsp. seasoned salt** until the mixture is uniformly combined.

### Step 2: Shape and Coat
3. Form the cream cheese mixture into a large, cohesive ball.
4. Spread the remaining **1 cup chopped pecans** onto a shallow plate or sheet of wax paper.
5. Roll the cheese ball gently in the **1 cup chopped pecans** until the entire surface is completely and evenly coated.

### Step 3: Chill and Serve
6. Wrap the coated cheese ball tightly in plastic wrap to help it hold its shape.
7. Refrigerate for at least **2 hours** (or overnight) until firm and chilled through. Serve alongside crackers or sliced baguette.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Can be made up to 2 days in advance. Keep tightly wrapped in plastic wrap in the refrigerator until ready to serve.
