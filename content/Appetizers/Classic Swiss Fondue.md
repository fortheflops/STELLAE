# Classic Swiss Fondue

> 📜 **Collection:** Patsy Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Appetizers
> *A traditional Swiss-style fondue featuring a blend of dry white wine and natural Swiss cheese. This rich, melted dip is perfect for communal dining and crusty bread dipping.*

---

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 15 mins | None | 4 Servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Fondue pot
* Wooden spoon
* Small mixing bowl

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 clove | [[Garlic]] | peeled |
| 3 Tbsp | [[Kirsch]] | divided |
| 2 Cups | [[Dry White Wine]] | room temperature |
| 3 Tbsp | [[Corn Starch]] | dissolved in kirsch |
| 1 LB | [[Natural Swiss Cheese]] | grated |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Baking Soda]] | none |
| to taste | [[White Pepper]] | none |
| to taste | [[Paprika]] | none |
| to taste | [[Nutmeg]] | none |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Pot
1. Rub the inside of the fondue pot thoroughly with the **1/2 clove garlic** to infuse the vessel with flavor.

### Step 2: Create the Base
2. In a small bowl, whisk together the **3 Tbsp corn starch** and **3 Tbsp kirsch** until smooth; set aside.
3. Pour the **2 cups dry white wine** into the pot and heat over medium heat until small bubbles begin to rise to the surface.

### Step 3: Melt and Emulsify
4. Gradually add the **1 lb natural swiss cheese** to the wine, stirring constantly until melted.
5. Once the cheese begins to bubble, stir in the **corn starch mixture** and continue stirring constantly until the texture is thick, smooth, and glossy.
6. Fold in the **1/4 tsp baking soda**, **white pepper**, **paprika**, and **nutmeg** until fully incorporated and fragrant.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Fondue is best served immediately; however, leftovers can be refrigerated and reheated gently over low heat with a splash of wine.
