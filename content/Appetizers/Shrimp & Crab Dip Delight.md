# Shrimp & Crab Dip Delight

> 📜 **Collection:** UK15 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Appetizers
> *A classic, creamy seafood dip featuring a savory blend of shrimp, crab, and onion soup mix. Perfect for gatherings and easily prepared ahead of time.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 0 mins | 1 hour | 8–10 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Fork
* Rubber spatula

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 16 Ounce | [[Sour Cream]] | 1 large carton |
| 1 Package | [[Lipton Dry Onion Soup]] | dry mix |
| 1 | [[Lemon]] | juiced |
| 1 Dash | [[Worcestershire Sauce]] | to taste |
| 6 Ounce | [[Deveined Shrimp]] | canned, mashed with a fork |
| 6 Ounce | [[Crab Meat]] | canned, drained |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Base
1. In a large mixing bowl, combine the **16 ounce sour cream**, **1 package Lipton dry onion soup**, **juice of 1 lemon**, and **1 dash Worcestershire sauce** until smooth and well incorporated.

### Step 2: Fold in Seafood
2. Add the **6 ounce deveined shrimp** (mashed) and **6 ounce crab meat** to the mixture.
3. Mix thoroughly until the seafood is evenly distributed and the dip is creamy and fragrant.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Will keep in the refrigerator for about 4 days; flavors meld best if chilled for at least one hour before serving.
