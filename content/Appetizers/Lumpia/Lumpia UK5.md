## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 pound                    | [[Ground Beef]]                           |
| 1 pound                    | [[Pork Sausage]]                          |
| As Needed                  | [[Egg(s)]]                                |
| As Needed                  | Chopped [[Celery]]                        |
| As Needed                  | Chopped [[Green Pepper]]                  |
| As Needed                  | Chopped [[Onion]]                         |
| As Needed                  | Chopped [[Carrots]]                       |
| As Needed                  | Finely chopped [[Ham]]                    |
| To Taste                   | [[Garlic Powder]] or [[Garlic Salt]]      |
| To Taste                   | [[Soy Sauce]]                             |
| As Needed                  | [[Shrimp]]                                |
| To Taste                   | [[Catsup]] (Ketchup)                      |
| To Taste                   | [[Pepper]]                                |
| 1 package                  | [[Lumpia Wrappers]]                       |
___
## Instructions
1.  In a large skillet, brown the ground beef and pork sausage. Drain off any excess fat.
2.  Add the other ingredients (egg, celery, green pepper, onion, carrots, ham, seasonings, shrimp, catsup) to the cooked meat to suit your personal preference.
3.  Place a portion of the filling onto a lumpia wrapper and roll it tightly.
4.  Fry the lumpia rolls in hot oil until they are golden brown.
___

>[!Note]
>A helpful tip is included: "I've found that if you dampen the edge to be sealed on the wrappers with water or egg whites they hold together better."
