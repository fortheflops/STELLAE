# Tahini with Himos

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Elaine James | 📂 **Category:** Appetizers
> *A classic homemade hummus recipe featuring boiled chickpeas blended with creamy tahini, garlic, and fresh lemon juice. Finished with a drizzle of olive oil, crushed pepper, and paprika for a flavorful dip.*

| 1 clove | [[Garlic]] | mashed (or coarse ground garlic) |

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep Chickpeas
1. In a saucepan, boil the **2 (#303) cans chickpeas** with a little **salt** for about 10 minutes until heated through and softened.
2. Drain the **chickpeas** thoroughly and mash or puree them in a blender.

### Step 2: Combine and Serve
3. In a mixing bowl or blender, stir the **1/2 cup tahini** while gradually adding **lemon juice** (and optional **water**) until it reaches the consistency of a thin batter.
4. Add the mashed **1 clove garlic** to the tahini mixture.
5. Combine the mashed **chickpeas** with the tahini mixture (using slightly more chickpeas relative to tahini) and blend until smooth. Make any taste adjustments as needed.
6. To serve, float a dash of **olive oil** on top along with **crushed pepper pods** and **paprika**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in a sealed container in the refrigerator for up to 5 days. Drizzle fresh olive oil over top before serving.
