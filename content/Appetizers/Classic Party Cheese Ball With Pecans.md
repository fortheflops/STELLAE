# Classic Party Cheese Ball With Pecans

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown 3 Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Appetizers
> *A classic party appetizer featuring a rich blend of cream cheese, processed cheddar spread, and sharp cheddar seasoned with Worcestershire and onion. Coated in ground pecans and fresh parsley for a retro crowd-pleaser.*

## Instructions

### Mix the Cheese Base
1. Ensure the **4 (3 oz) packages cream cheese**, **2 (5 oz) jars processed cheddar cheese spread**, and **6 oz sharp cheddar cheese** are fully at room temperature for easy blending.
2. In a large bowl, combine the **4 (3 oz) packages cream cheese**, **2 (5 oz) jars processed cheddar cheese spread**, **6 oz sharp cheddar cheese**, **1 tsp grated onion**, **1 tsp Worcestershire sauce**, and optional **1 tsp MSG**.
3. Using an electric mixer or sturdy spoon, beat the mixture thoroughly until completely smooth, uniform, and creamy.
4. Fold in **1/2 cup ground pecans** until evenly distributed throughout the cheese base.

### Shape & Chill
5. Gather the cheese mixture and shape it into a neat, smooth ball.
6. Transfer the cheese ball into a bowl lined with wax paper or foil, wrapping it securely.
7. Refrigerate overnight, or for at least **8 hours**, until firm enough to hold its shape easily.

### Coat & Serve
8. About 1 hour before serving, combine the remaining **1/2 cup ground pecans** and **1/2 cup finely chopped fresh parsley** on a shallow plate.
9. Unwrap the chilled cheese ball and roll it gently over the **pecans** and **parsley** mixture until completely and evenly coated.
10. Transfer to a serving platter and serve chilled alongside crisp crackers, croutons, or sliced baguette.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The cheese ball can be shaped and chilled up to 2 days in advance. For optimal texture and appearance, roll in the fresh parsley and pecan coating 1 hour before serving.
