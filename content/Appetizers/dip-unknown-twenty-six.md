# Dip

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Appetizers
> *A flavorful and creamy party dip combining tomatoes, chilies, and sour cream. Perfect for serving with chips or fresh vegetables.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | None | 2 hours | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Spoon

---
## Ingredients

### Main Dip
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 can | [[Stewed Tomatoes]] | Drain well, chopped |
| 1 pkg | [[Ranch Dip Mix]] | Dry |
| 1 can | [[Chilies]] | 7 oz, drain well |
| 1 pt | [[Sour Cream]] | 2 cups |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing and Chilling
1. Combine the drained and chopped **1 can stewed tomatoes**, **1 pkg ranch dip mix**, drained **1 can chilies**, and **1 pt sour cream** in a mixing bowl.
2. Stir thoroughly until well blended.
3. Chill in the refrigerator until thoroughly cold and set before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Cover and chill in the refrigerator for at least 2 hours or overnight before serving to allow flavors to meld.
