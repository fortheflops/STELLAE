# Parmesan Dip

> 📜 **Collection:** Georgia Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Appetizers
> *A quick and creamy Parmesan Dip made with Miracle Whip, milk, and a touch of chopped onion. Perfect for a fast party appetizer or a simple vegetable dip.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5 mins | None | None | 6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Measuring cups
* Measuring spoons
* Spoon

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 cup | [[Miracle Whip]] |  |
| 1/4 cup | [[Parmesan cheese]] | grated |
| 1/4 cup | [[Milk]] |  |
| 1 tbsp | [[Onion]] | chopped |

---

## Instructions

### Preparation
1. In a medium mixing bowl, combine the **1 cup Miracle Whip**, **1/4 cup parmesan cheese**, **1/4 cup milk**, and **1 tbsp chopped onion**.
2. Mix well with a spoon or whisk until the mixture is completely smooth, uniform, and well-integrated.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Can be made ahead and stored in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days. Stir well before serving.
