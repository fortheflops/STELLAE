# Sauerkraut Ball

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Appetizers
> *A festive and savory cheese ball featuring tangy sauerkraut, sharp cheddar, and pimentos. Perfect for parties and gatherings when chilled overnight.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | None | 6 hours | 10 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Serving platter

## Ingredients

### Ball Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 cups | [[Sauerkraut]] | well drained |
| 2-3 cups | [[Grated cheddar cheese]] | grated |
| 2 Tbsp. | [[Green onion]] | chopped |
| 2 Tbsp. | [[Green pepper]] | chopped |
| 2 Tbsp. | [[Pimentos]] | chopped |
| 1 | [[Hard boiled egg]] | chopped |
| 1/2 cup | [[Bread crumbs]] | dry |
| 1/2 cup | [[Mayonnaise]] | standard |
| 1/2 tsp. | [[Salt]] | none |
| 1 Tbsp. | [[Sugar]] | granulated |

### Spread & Garnish
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 - 8 oz. | [[Cream cheese]] | softened |
| 2 Tbsp. | [[Milk]] | none |
| As needed | [[Pimentos]] | for decoration |
| As needed | [[Green peppers]] | for decoration |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing and Shaping
1. Thoroughly mix all ball ingredients including the **2 cups sauerkraut**, **2-3 cups grated cheddar cheese**, **2 Tbsp. green onion**, **2 Tbsp. green pepper**, **2 Tbsp. pimentos**, **1 hard boiled egg**, **1/2 cup bread crumbs**, **1/2 cup mayonnaise**, **1/2 tsp. salt**, and **1 Tbsp. sugar** together in a large bowl.
2. Shape the combined mixture firmly into a large ball and place it on a serving platter.

### Step 2: Coating and Chilling
3. Whip together the **1 - 8 oz. cream cheese** and **2 Tbsp. milk** until smooth and spreadable.
4. Spread the whipped cream cheese mixture evenly over the entire exterior of the sauerkraut ball.
5. Decorate the outside with additional **pimentos** and **green peppers**, then refrigerate overnight or for at least **6 hours** until firm and well-chilled.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Can be prepared up to 24 hours in advance and kept refrigerated until ready to serve.
