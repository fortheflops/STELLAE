# Islander Cheese Ball

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Appetizers
> *Delight your guests with this classic Islander Cheese Ball, featuring a creamy blend infused with crushed pineapple, onion, and green pepper. Coated with finely chopped pecans, it makes for a fantastic party centerpiece.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | None | 2 hours | 1 large ball or 4 small ones |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Plastic wrap

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 (8 oz) | [[Cream Cheese]] | Softened |
| 1 (8 oz) | [[Crushed Pineapple]] | Drained |
| 2 tbsp | [[Onion]] | Finely chopped |
| 1/4 cup | [[Green Pepper]] | Finely chopped |
| 2 tsp | [[Lawry's Seasoned Salt]] | None |
| As needed | [[Pecans]] | Finely chopped for topping |

---

## Instructions

### Mixing and Shaping
1. Combine the **2 (8 oz) cream cheese**, **1 (8 oz) crushed pineapple**, **2 tbsp onion**, **1/4 cup green pepper**, and **2 tsp Lawry's seasoned salt** in a mixing bowl until thoroughly blended.
2. Note: You can add part of the **pecans** directly into the cheese mixture if desired.
3. Shape the mixture into 2 large balls or 4 small ones.
4. Roll the formed balls in the finely chopped **pecans** until fully coated, then wrap and chill until firm.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Can be prepared up to 2 days in advance and kept tightly wrapped in the refrigerator until ready to serve.
