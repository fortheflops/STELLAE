# Filipino Lumpia (Spring Rolls)

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Five | ✍️ **Attribution:** Jody Griffin | 📂 **Category:** Appetizers
> *A classic recipe for crispy Filipino lumpia, featuring a savory, customizable filling of ground beef, pork sausage, and finely chopped vegetables. Perfect as a crowd-pleasing appetizer served with sweet chili dipping sauce.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Filling
1. In a large skillet, cook the **1 pound ground beef** and **1 pound pork sausage** over medium-high heat until browned and no longer pink. Drain off excess fat.
2. Add the **1 medium chopped onion**, **1 cup chopped celery**, **1 cup shredded carrots**, and **1 chopped green pepper** to the skillet and sauté with the meat until tender-crisp and fragrant.
3. Stir in the **1 tsp garlic powder**, **2-3 tbsp soy sauce**, **1/2 tsp pepper**, and any optional ingredients like **1/2 cup chopped shrimp**, **1/2 cup finely chopped ham**, or **1-2 tbsp catsup**. Remove from heat and let the filling cool slightly.
4. Once cooled, stir in the **1 beaten egg** to help bind the mixture. Allow the filling to cool completely before wrapping to prevent the wrappers from becoming soggy.

### Step 2: Assemble the Lumpia
5. Place a **lumpia wrapper** on a flat surface in a diamond shape. Spoon about **2 tablespoons** of filling near the bottom corner. Fold the bottom corner up over the filling, fold in the left and right corners, then roll tightly towards the top corner.
6. Moisten the edge of the top corner with a dab of **water** or egg white to seal the roll securely. Repeat with the remaining filling and wrappers.

### Step 3: Fry and Serve
7. In a deep skillet or pot, heat 1-2 inches of **oil** to 350°F (175°C).
8. Fry the lumpia in batches, being careful not to overcrowd the pan. Cook for **3-5 minutes**, turning occasionally, until they are golden brown and crispy on all sides.
9. Remove with a slotted spoon and drain on paper towels. Serve hot with a sweet chili dipping sauce.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Lumpia can be rolled ahead of time and kept covered in the refrigerator for up to 24 hours, or frozen in a single layer and stored in a freezer bag for up to 3 months before frying directly from frozen.
