# Molded Crab Dip

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Sarah Hensley | 📂 **Category:** Appetizers
> *A classic retro-style party appetizer, this molded crab dip is a savory blend of crab, cream cheese, and crisp vegetables, set with gelatin into a beautiful shape. Perfect for serving with crackers at any gathering.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 5 mins | 4 hours (Chilling) | 16–20 Servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large bowl
* Hand mixer
* Small bowl
* Small saucepan
* 4- to 5-cup mold
* Serving platter

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 (6 oz) cans | [[Crab Meat]] | drained and flaked (or Tuna/Shrimp) |
| 1 (8 oz) package | [[Cream Cheese]] | softened |
| 1 cup | [[Mayonnaise]] |  |
| 1 (10.5 oz) can | Cream of [[Mushroom Soup]] |  |
| 2 packages | Plain [[Gelatin]] | unflavored |
| 1/4 cup | Cold [[Water]] |  |
| 1 cup | [[Celery]] | finely chopped |
| 1/2 cup | Green [[Onions]] | finely chopped |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Base
1. In a large bowl, beat the **1 (8 oz) package cream cheese** with a mixer until smooth and creamy. Add the **1 cup mayonnaise**, **2 (6 oz) cans crab meat**, **1 cup celery**, and **1/2 cup green onions**. Mix until well combined and evenly distributed.

### Step 2: Dissolve the Gelatin
2. In a small bowl, sprinkle the **2 packages gelatin** over the **1/4 cup cold water**. Let it stand for **1-2 minutes** to soften or 'bloom'.
3. In a small saucepan, heat the **1 (10.5 oz) can cream of mushroom soup** over low heat until warm but not boiling.
4. Add the softened **gelatin** mixture to the warm **cream of mushroom soup**. Stir constantly until the gelatin is completely dissolved and the mixture is smooth and uniform.

### Step 3: Combine and Mold
5. Remove the **soup** mixture from the heat and set it aside to cool to room temperature.
6. Once cooled, pour the **soup** mixture into the large bowl with the **cream cheese** mixture. Stir until everything is thoroughly blended and smooth.
7. Lightly coat a **4- to 5-cup mold** with non-stick cooking spray, then pour the final mixture into the prepared mold.

### Step 4: Chill and Serve
8. Refrigerate for at least **4 hours**, or overnight, until the dip is completely firm and holds its shape when touched.
9. To serve, briefly dip the bottom of the mold in warm water, then invert it onto a serving platter. Serve chilled with Ritz Crackers or Wheat Thins.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This dip can be made up to 24 hours in advance and kept refrigerated in its mold. Once unmolded, serve immediately or keep chilled until ready to present.
