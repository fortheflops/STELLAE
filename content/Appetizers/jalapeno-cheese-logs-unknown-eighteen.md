# Jalapeno Cheese Logs

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Eighteen | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Appetizers
> *A rich and savory party appetizer featuring a blend of melting cheeses, cream cheese, and smoked meats rolled into logs. Perfect for gatherings and easily customizable with your favorite coatings.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 25 mins | 0 mins | 20 mins | 12 servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large bowl
* Grater
* Freezer

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 - 8 oz. | [[Cream cheese]] | Softened |
| 10 oz. | [[Monterey Jack cheese]] | Block with jalapenos |
| 1 lb. | [[Velveeta cheese]] | Block with jalapenos |
| 8 oz. | [[Sharp cheddar cheese]] | Block |
| 1 pkg. | [[Smoked beef]] | Pastrami, bacon, etc. |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and Grating
1. Put **10 oz. Monterey Jack cheese** and **8 oz. sharp cheddar cheese** in the refrigerator.
2. Leave **1 lb. Velveeta cheese** and **2 - 8 oz. packages cream cheese** out at room temperature until soft.
3. Grate or grind **1 pkg. smoked beef**, pastrami, or bacon.

### Step 2: Mixing and Molding
4. In a large bowl, combine the softened **cream cheese** and **Velveeta** with the **grated cheddar** and **Monterey Jack**.
5. Add the grated or ground **smoked beef** and mix well.
6. Since the mixture will be too soft to mold, place it in the freezer for about **15 to 20 minutes** until it is firmer and easier to handle.
7. Roll the mixture into logs or balls.
8. Roll the shaped logs in nuts, sunflower seeds, parsley flakes, red chili powder, ground meat, or whatever coating you prefer.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the wrapped cheese logs in the refrigerator for up to 3 days before serving.
