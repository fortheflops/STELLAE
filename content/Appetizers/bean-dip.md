# Bean Dip

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Appetizers
> *A quick and flavorful layered bean dip featuring refried beans, extra sharp cheddar cheese, and green chilis. Perfect for parties and gatherings.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 20 mins | None | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Baking dish
* Can opener
* Knife

---
## Ingredients

### Dip Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 can | [[Refried Beans]] | with sausage |
| 1 whole piece | [[Extra Sharp Cheddar Cheese]] | sliced or grated |
| 1 to 2 cans | [[Chopped Green Chili]] | drained |
| To taste | [[Garlic Salt or Powder]] | none |
| To taste | [[Dried or Minced Onion]] | none |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Assembly and Baking
1. Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C). In a baking dish, spread the **1 can refried beans with sausage** evenly across the bottom.
2. Layer the **1 whole piece extra sharp cheddar cheese** and **1 to 2 cans chopped green chili** evenly over the beans.
3. Season with **garlic salt or powder** and **dried or minced onion** to taste.
4. Bake until the cheese is completely melted and bubbly, and the edges are lightly golden.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the dip in the baking dish ahead of time, cover, and refrigerate for up to 24 hours before baking.
