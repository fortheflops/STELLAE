## Instructions

### Step 1: Making and Browning Meatballs
1. Combine **3/4 lb ground beef**, **3/4 cup fine dry bread crumbs**, **1 1/2 tbsp minced onion**, **1/2 tsp horse radish**, **3 drops Tabasco sauce**, **2 eggs**, **3/4 tsp salt**, and **1/2 tsp pepper** in a mixing bowl.
2. Form the mixture into small balls.
3. Brown the meatballs in a skillet, then pour off any excess drippings until browned nicely.

### Step 2: Simmering in Sauce
4. In a separate bowl, mix **3/4 cup catsup**, **1/2 cup water**, **1/4 cup vinegar**, **1 tbsp minced onion**, **1 tbsp brown sugar**, **1 tsp dry mustard**, **1/4 tsp pepper**, **1 1/2 tsp salt**, **3 drops Tabasco sauce**, and **1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce**.
5. Pour the sauce mixture over the cooked meatballs.
6. Cover the skillet and cook for **10 mins** until heated through and bubbling.
7. Shake the pan gently and serve hot.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Meatballs can be formed and kept in the refrigerator for up to 24 hours before browning and simmering in the sauce.
