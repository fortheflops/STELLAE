# Mini Cheese Things

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail's Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Appetizers
> *An incredibly simple and fast recipe for warm, savory baked appetizers. A rich mixture of mayonnaise and cheese is spread onto crackers or bread and baked until hot and bubbly.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5 mins | 3 mins | None | 24 appetizers |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Small mixing bowl
* Baking sheet
* Spoon

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 cup | [[Mayo]] | Mayonnaise |
| 1/4 cup | [[Cheese]] | Grated or shredded, such as Parmesan or Cheddar |
| To taste | [[Salt]] and [[Black Pepper]] |  |
| Approx. 24 | [[Crackers]] | or small [[Bread Slices]] for the base |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Mix
1. Preheat your oven to **375°F (190°C)**.
2. In a small bowl, combine the **1/2 cup mayonnaise**, **1/4 cup cheese**, and a pinch of **salt** and **black pepper**. Stir until well mixed and smooth.

### Step 2: Assemble and Bake
3. Arrange your choice of base (**approx. 24 crackers** or **small bread slices**) in a single layer on a baking sheet.
4. Spoon a small amount of the **cheese mixture** onto each cracker or bread slice.
5. Bake for exactly **3 minutes**, or just until the topping is hot, bubbly, and lightly golden. Serve immediately.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The cheese spread can be mixed ahead of time and stored in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days. Assemble and bake just before serving.
