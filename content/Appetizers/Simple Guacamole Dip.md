# Simple Guacamole Dip

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Fifteen | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Appetizers
> *A quick and easy guacamole recipe made with fresh avocados, lime juice, green chiles, and picante sauce. A perfect, classic dip for any occasion.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 0 mins | None | 8–12 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Medium bowl
* Fork or potato masher

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 4 to 6 | [[Avocados]] | Ripe, pitted and scooped |
| Juice of 1/2 | [[Lime]] | Freshly squeezed |
| To taste | [[Garlic Salt]] |  |
| 1 (4 oz) can | [[Green Chiles]] | Chopped, drained |
| 1/4 - 1/2 cup | [[Pace Picante Sauce]] | Or to taste |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Mash
1. Cut the **4 to 6 ripe avocados** in half, remove the pits, and scoop the flesh into a medium bowl.
2. Using a fork or a potato masher, mash the **avocados** to your desired consistency (chunky or smooth).

### Step 2: Season and Mix
3. Squeeze the **juice of 1/2 lime** over the **avocados** and stir it in, along with **garlic salt** to taste.
4. Gently fold in the **1 (4 oz) can chopped green chiles** and **1/4 - 1/2 cup Pace Picante sauce**, adding more picante sauce if you prefer a thinner or spicier dip.
5. Taste and adjust seasonings if necessary. Serve immediately with tortilla chips to prevent browning and ensure a fresh, vibrant green appearance.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Best served immediately to prevent browning. If storing, press plastic wrap directly onto the surface of the guacamole and refrigerate for up to 1 day.
