# Tuna Cheese Ball

> 📜 **Collection:** Christine Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Appetizers
> *A delightful and savory Tuna Cheese Ball combining cream cheese, flaked tuna, capers, and chopped walnuts. Perfect for parties and gatherings.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | None | 2 hours | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Plastic wrap

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 - 8 oz pkgs | [[Cream cheese]] | Phil / softened |
| 1 - 9 1/2 oz | [[Tuna]] | Drained & flaked |
| 1 Tbsp | [[Lemon juice]] | Fresh |
| 2 Tbsp | [[Capers]] | Optional |
| 1/4 cup | [[Ripe olives]] | Finely chopped |
| 1/2 cup | [[Walnuts]] | Finely chopped |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Salt]] | None |
| 1/8 tsp | [[Pepper]] | None |
| 1 1/2 tsp | [[Dry mustard]] | None |
| 1/2 tsp | [[Thyme]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing
1. In a large mixing bowl, combine the **2 - 8 oz pkgs cream cheese**, **1 - 9 1/2 oz tuna**, **1 Tbsp lemon juice**, **2 Tbsp capers**, **1/4 cup ripe olives**, **1/4 tsp salt**, **1/8 tsp pepper**, **1 1/2 tsp dry mustard**, and **1/2 tsp thyme** until thoroughly blended and smooth.

### Step 2: Shaping and Chilling
2. Shape the mixture into a ball and roll it in the **1/2 cup walnuts** until evenly coated.
3. Wrap tightly in plastic wrap and chill in the refrigerator until firm, about 2 hours before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Can be prepared up to 24 hours in advance and kept refrigerated until ready to serve.
