# Vegetable Pizza

> 📜 **Collection:** Georgia Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Marlene Bashaw | 📂 **Category:** Appetizers
> *A classic cold appetizer featuring a crescent roll crust topped with a savory, seasoned cream cheese spread and a colorful assortment of fresh, crunchy vegetables. Perfect for parties, potlucks, or a light snack.*

---

| 20 mins | 15 mins | 30 mins | 8 servings |

## Instructions

### Step 1: Bake the Crust
1. Press **1 tube crescent rolls** dough into a pizza pan, carefully sealing all the seams to form a solid crust.
2. Bake for **15 to 20 minutes** until golden brown and cooked through. Let cool completely before adding toppings.

### Step 2: Mix and Assemble
3. In a mixing bowl, combine **8 oz. cream cheese**, **1 cup Miracle Whip**, **1/4 tsp. Tabasco sauce**, **2 tsp. horseradish**, **1/2 chopped onion**, and **1 pkg. ranch dressing mix** (or Italian dressing mix) together until smooth and well blended.
4. Spread the seasoned cream cheese mixture evenly over the cooled crescent crust.
5. Arrange your choice of sliced **assorted vegetables** (such as celery, carrots, cauliflower, broccoli, green pepper, red pepper, mushrooms, and cucumbers) attractively over the spread.
6. Top with grated **Cheddar cheese**.
7. Chill in the refrigerator for at least 30 minutes before slicing and serving cold.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Can be assembled a few hours ahead of time and kept chilled in the refrigerator. Best served the same day to keep the crescent crust crisp.
