# Easy California Blue Cheese & Onion Dip

> 📜 **Collection:** General Archive | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Appetizers
> *A quick and savory no-cook dip that elevates classic onion soup mix with rich sour cream, tangy blue cheese, and a splash of brandy. Perfect for effortless entertaining and vintage-inspired gatherings.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 0 mins | 1 hour (Chilling) | Approx. 3 cups |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Medium mixing bowl
* Rubber spatula
* Measuring spoons

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 4 oz. | [[Blue Cheese]] | crumbled |
| 1 pt. (2 cups) | [[Sour Cream]] | cold |
| 1 pkg. | [[Lipton Onion Soup Mix]] |  |
| 2 T. | [[Brandy]] |  |
| 2 T. | [[Parsley]] | chipped |
| To serve | Potato chips and/or assorted crackers | optional |

---

## Instructions

### Preparation and Chilling
1. In a medium bowl, combine the **4 oz. crumbled blue cheese**, **1 pt. sour cream**, **1 pkg. Lipton Onion Soup Mix**, **2 T. brandy**, and **2 T. chipped parsley**.
2. Stir well with a spatula until the mixture is thoroughly combined, smooth, and fragrant.
3. Cover the bowl and chill in the refrigerator for at least **1 hour** to allow the savory flavors to fully meld and deepen.
4. Serve cold alongside **potato chips** and/or **assorted crackers** for dipping.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This dip can be made up to 2 days in advance. Keep stored in an airtight container in the refrigerator until ready to serve.
