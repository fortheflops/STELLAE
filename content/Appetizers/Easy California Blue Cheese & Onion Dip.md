*A quick and savory no-cook dip. This recipe combines the classic Lipton onion soup mix with sour cream, crumbled blue cheese, parsley, and a splash of brandy.*

## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 4 oz. | [[Blue Cheese]], crumbled |
| 1 pt. | [[Sour Cream]] (2 cups) |
| 1 pkg. | [[Lipton Onion Soup Mix]] |
| 2 T. | [[Brandy]] |
| 2 T. | Chipped [[Parsley]] |

---

## Instructions
1. In a bowl, combine the **4 oz. crumbled blue cheese**, **1 pt. sour cream**, **1 pkg. Lipton Onion Soup Mix**, **2 T. brandy**, and **2 T. chipped parsley** and stir well.
2. Chill for at least **1 hour** to allow the flavors to meld.
3. Serve with **potato chips and/or assorted crackers**.
