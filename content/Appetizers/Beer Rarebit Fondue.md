# Beer Rarebit Fondue

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** The Green Pen | 📂 **Category:** Appetizers
> *A savory and tangy cheddar cheese fondue inspired by classic Welsh rarebit. Melted with beer, ginger ale, and warm spices, it is the ultimate crowd-pleasing dip for bread, vegetables, and meats.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Cheese Base
1. Ensure the **4 cups shredded sharp natural [[Cheddar Cheese]]** is at room temperature. In a medium bowl, use an electric mixer at low speed to blend the cheese until broken down.
2. Slowly pour in the **1/4 cup [[Beer]]** and **1/4 cup [[Ginger Ale]]** while mixing. Continue to beat until the mixture is fluffy and light.
3. Add the **2 tsp [[Worcestershire Sauce]]**, **1/2 tsp dry [[Mustard]]**, **1/8 tsp [[Garlic Powder]]**, and **1 dash hot [[Pepper Sauce]]**. Beat again until everything is thoroughly combined and uniform.

### Step 2: Melt and Thicken
4. Transfer the cheese mixture to a fondue pot or a medium saucepan over low-medium heat. Heat the mixture, stirring frequently, until it is completely melted and smooth.
5. In a small bowl, whisk together the **1 tbsp [[Cornstarch]]** and **2 tbsp cold [[Water]]** to create a smooth slurry.
6. Pour the slurry into the melted cheese mixture. Continue to cook and stir until the fondue thickens and begins to bubble gently, about **1-2 minutes**.
7. Serve immediately in the fondue pot to keep warm, alongside **French [[Bread Cubes]]**, **raw [[Vegetables]]**, and **cooked [[Meat]]** for dipping.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Best served immediately. Leftovers can be stored in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days and gently reheated over low heat, adding a splash of beer or water if needed to restore consistency.
