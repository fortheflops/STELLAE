# Frozen Guacamole Base

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Appetizers
> *A clever method for preserving fresh avocados by mashing them with lemon juice before freezing. Thaw later and add your favorite spices for instant, fresh-tasting guacamole.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | None | Freezing time | 4 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Fork
* Freezer bag

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 | [[Avocados]] | Ripe |
| 4 tbsp | [[Lemon Juice]] | Freshly squeezed (2 tablespoons per avocado) |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and Freezing
1. Mash **2 avocados** in a mixing bowl until smooth or desired chunkiness.
2. Add **4 tbsp lemon juice** (2 tablespoons for each avocado) and mix thoroughly to prevent browning.
3. Transfer to a freezer-safe container or bag and **freeze** until ready to use later.

### Step 2: Finishing
4. When ready to serve, thaw the mixture completely.
5. Add your favorite spices to the thawed **avocado** and **lemon juice** base to make delicious guacamole.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the mashed and lemon-treated avocado base in the freezer for up to 3-4 months until ready to thaw and season.
