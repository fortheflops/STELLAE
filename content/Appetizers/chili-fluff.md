# Chili Fluff

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Five | ✍️ **Attribution:** Mary Ann | 📂 **Category:** Appetizers
> *A delightful and fluffy baked appetizer featuring whole chilies and a generous layer of grated cheese smothered in a savory batter. Perfect for gatherings and easy to prepare ahead of time.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 1 hour | None | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Pyrex baking dish
* Grater
* Mixing bowl

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 large can | [[Whole Chilies]] | Drained |
| 1 lb. | [[Cheese]] | Grated |
| 1 1/2 cups | [[Bisquick]] | Baking mix |
| 6 | [[Eggs]] | Beaten |
| 4 cups (1 qt.) | [[Milk]] | Whole milk |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Assembly
1. Line a Pyrex baking dish with **1 large can whole chilies**.
2. Grate **1 lb. cheese** and spread evenly over the chilies.

### Step 2: Batter and Baking
3. In a mixing bowl, mix **1 1/2 cups Bisquick**, **6 eggs**, and **4 cups (1 qt.) milk** until combined.
4. Pour the batter mixture evenly over the chilies and cheese.
5. Bake at 350°F for **1 hour** until golden brown on top and set in the center.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the dish up to a day in advance, cover tightly, and refrigerate until ready to bake.
