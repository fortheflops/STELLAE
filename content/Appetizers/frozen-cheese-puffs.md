# Frozen Cheese Puffs

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Appetizers
> *Delightfully savory frozen cheese puffs made with Old English cheese and sliced white bread. Perfect for entertaining and easily baked straight from the freezer.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 10 mins | 2 hours | 27 squares |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixer
* Knife
* Baking sheet

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 part | [[Oleo]] | Softened |
| 1 part | [[Old English Cheese]] | Grated |
| To taste | [[Tabasco]] | None |
| As needed | [[White Bread]] | Sliced thin, not too fresh, crusts trimmed |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Making the Cheese Spread
1. Combine **equal parts oleo** and **grated old english cheese** in a mixer.
2. Beat until the mixture is light and fluffy.
3. Add **tabasco** to taste and mix well.

### Step 2: Assembly and Freezing
4. Trim the crusts from the **sliced thin white bread**.
5. Spread the **cheese mixture** on the bread slices and stack them 3 high.
6. Cut each stack into **9 squares**.
7. Frost each square completely on all sides with the remaining **cheese mixture** and place in the freezer until solid.

### Step 3: Baking
8. When ready to use, bake the **frozen cheese puffs** at 375° for **8 to 10 minutes** until the cheese will puff and turn golden.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the assembled puffs in the freezer for up to a month before baking directly from frozen.
