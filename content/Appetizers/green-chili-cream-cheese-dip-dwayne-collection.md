# Green Chili Cream Cheese Dip

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Appetizers
> *A simple and savory cream cheese dip spiked with green chilies and green onion. Perfect for making ahead and serving with salty corn chips.*

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 8 oz. | [[Cream Cheese]] | softened |
| 1 tbsp. | [[Green Onion]] | finely chopped |
| 1 tbsp. | [[Mayonnaise]] | regular |
| to taste | [[Lemon Juice]] | freshly squeezed |
| 1 to 3 oz. | [[Green Chilies]] | diced |
| for serving | [[Fritos]] | or other corn chips |

---

## Instructions

### Preparation
1. In a medium mixing bowl, combine the softened **8 oz. cream cheese**, **1 tbsp. green onion**, **1 tbsp. mayonnaise**, **lemon juice to taste**, and **1 to 3 oz. green chilies**.
2. Beat or stir vigorously until the mixture is completely smooth, uniform, and creamy.

### Chilling & Serving
3. Cover the bowl tightly and refrigerate to set for **at least 4 hours**, though **24 hours** is highly preferred to allow the flavors to fully meld.
4. Serve chilled alongside a bowl of salty **Fritos**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This dip is ideal for making ahead. Prepare it 24 hours in advance for the best flavor profile, and store covered in the refrigerator for up to 5 days.
