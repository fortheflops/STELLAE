# Spinach Dip

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Maxine | 📂 **Category:** Appetizers
> *A classic 1980s party favorite, this creamy spinach dip combines crunchy water chestnuts, green onions, and savory vegetable soup mix. Perfect for serving chilled with crackers, pumpernickel bread, or fresh vegetables.*

## Instructions

### Preparation
1. In a medium mixing bowl, combine the **10 oz cooked spinach** (ensuring it is squeezed completely dry), **1 can (8 oz) chopped water chestnuts**, **1 package dry regular vegetable soup mix**, **1/2 to 1 cup mayonnaise**, **1/2 to 1 cup sour cream**, and **1 bunch chopped green onions**.
2. Stir the mixture thoroughly until all ingredients are evenly distributed and the dip is smooth and creamy.
3. Cover the bowl tightly and chill in the refrigerator for at least 2 hours before serving to allow the flavors to meld and the dry soup mix to fully hydrate.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This dip is best made a few hours or a day ahead to let the flavors develop. Store covered in the refrigerator for up to 3 days.
