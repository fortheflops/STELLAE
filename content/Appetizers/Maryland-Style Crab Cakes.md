# Maryland-Style Crab Cakes

> 📜 **Collection:** General Archive | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Appetizers
> *A classic recipe for Maryland-style crab cakes, featuring flavorful crab claw meat with minimal filler. Seasoned with horseradish and Tabasco, these cakes are coated in cracker crumbs and fried to a perfect golden brown.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 10 mins | None | 8 crab cakes |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Medium mixing bowl
* Shallow dish or plate
* Large skillet
* Paper towels
* Rolling pin

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Crumbs and Mixture
1. Roll out crackers into **approx. 1 cup fine cracker crumbs**. Place the crumbs on a plate or shallow dish.
2. In a medium bowl, gently combine the **1 lb. crab claw meat**, **2 eggs** (lightly beaten), **2 tbsp mayonnaise**, **1 tbsp prepared horseradish**, **1 tbsp chopped parsley**, **1 dash Tabasco sauce**, **1/4 tsp salt**, and **1/8 tsp pepper**. Be careful not to overmix, as this can make the crab cakes tough.

### Step 2: Shape and Coat
3. Shape the crab mixture into cakes. The size is up to you, but they are typically about 1/2 to 3/4 inch thick and 2-3 inches in diameter.
4. Gently pat each crab cake into the **fine cracker crumbs**, coating both sides lightly.

### Step 3: Fry and Serve
5. Heat a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add enough **oil or butter** to coat the bottom of the pan. Once hot, carefully place the crab cakes in the skillet, leaving some space between them.
6. Fry for about 3-5 minutes per side, or until **golden brown and heated through** (the internal temperature should reach 145°F / 63°C).
7. Remove the crab cakes from the skillet and place them on a plate lined with paper towels to drain any excess **oil or butter**. Serve immediately.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Crab cakes can be shaped, coated, and refrigerated for up to 4 hours before frying. Cooked crab cakes are best enjoyed immediately but can be stored in the refrigerator for up to 2 days.
