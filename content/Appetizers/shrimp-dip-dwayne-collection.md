# Shrimp Dip

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Dip
1. In a mixing bowl, mash **1 can shrimp** (drained) with a fork.
2. Add **1/2 medium onion** (finely chopped), **a few dashes of Tabasco sauce**, and **cayenne pepper** to suit.
3. Stir in enough **Miracle Whip** to bind the mixture into a smooth paste.
4. Cover and refrigerate for **several hours** before serving to allow the flavors to meld.

### Step 2: Prepare the Toast Fingers
5. Preheat oven to **325°F**.
6. Butter both sides of the **bread fingers**.
7. Bake at **325°F** for **15 to 20 minutes**, then turn and bake for another **5 minutes** until golden brown and crispy.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This dip must be made several hours in advance and refrigerated to allow the flavors to fully develop. Serve chilled with warm toast fingers.
