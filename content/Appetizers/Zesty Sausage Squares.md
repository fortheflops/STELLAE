# Zesty Sausage Squares

> 📜 **Collection:** UnknownTwo | ✍️ **Attribution:** Ruth Nowalk | 📂 **Category:** Appetizers
> *A savory and satisfying layered appetizer featuring a Bisquick crust, a hearty sausage and onion filling, and a zesty cheese and green chili topping. Perfect for parties and potlucks.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 30 mins | 5 mins (Cooling) | 24 squares |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 9x13-inch glass baking pan
* Skillet
* Medium bowl
* Mixing bowl

---
## Ingredients

### For the Crust
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 cup | [[Bisquick]] | baking mix |
| ⅓ cup | [[Milk]] | None |
| 2 tbsp | [[Mayonnaise]] | None |

### For the Filling
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 lb. | [[Sausage]] | pork sausage |
| ½ cup | [[Onion]] | chopped |

### For the Topping
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 tbsp | [[Mayonnaise]] | divided |
| 1 | [[Egg]] | beaten |
| 2 cups | [[Cheese]] | grated (Cheddar or Monterey Jack) |
| 2 (4 oz) cans | [[Green Chilies]] | diced, drained |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Crust
1. Preheat your oven to **375°F (190°C)**. Grease a **9x13 inch glass baking pan**.
2. In a medium bowl, mix together the **1 cup Bisquick**, **⅓ cup milk**, and **2 tablespoons mayonnaise** to form a soft dough. Press this evenly into the bottom of the prepared pan to form the crust.

### Step 2: Cook the Filling
3. In a skillet, cook the **1 lb. sausage** and **½ cup chopped onion** over medium heat until the sausage is browned and the onion is soft and fragrant. Drain off any excess grease.
4. Spread the cooked sausage and onion mixture evenly over the **Bisquick crust**.

### Step 3: Add Topping and Bake
5. In a separate bowl, beat the **1 egg** with the remaining **2 tablespoons mayonnaise**. Stir in the **2 cups grated cheese** and the **2 (4 oz) cans drained green chilies**.
6. Carefully spread this cheese and egg mixture over the sausage layer.
7. Bake for **30 minutes**, or until the top is golden brown and bubbly and the center is set. Let cool for a few minutes before cutting into **24 squares** to serve.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This dish can be made ahead and reheated, or frozen and reheated later.
