# Margaret Freed's Broiled Cheese Rolls

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Margaret Freed | 📂 **Category:** Appetizers
> *A savory appetizer or snack made by broiling a mixture of sharp cheddar cheese, diced raw bacon, and green onions on English muffin halves until bubbly and golden.*

---

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 8 mins | None | 12 halves |

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |

| 2 Strips | [[Bacon]] | uncooked, finely diced |
| 5-6 | [[Onions]] | green, tops and all, chopped fine |
| 2 Tablespoons | [[Cooking Oil]] | none |
| 1/2 Teaspoon | [[Salt]] | none |
| 1/8 Teaspoon | [[Pepper]] | none |
| 1 Package (of 6) | [[English Muffins]] | split in half |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mix the Topping
1. Preheat your broiler. In a medium bowl, combine the **1/2 pound (8 oz) grated cheddar cheese**, **2 strips uncooked bacon**, **5-6 green onions**, **2 tablespoons cooking oil**, **1/2 teaspoon salt**, and **1/8 teaspoon pepper**. Mix well until fully incorporated.

### Step 2: Assemble and Broil
2. Split the **1 package (of 6) English muffins** in half and arrange the twelve halves on a broiler pan or baking sheet.
3. Spread the cheese mixture evenly over each of the muffin halves, covering all the way to the edges.
4. Broil about 4 inches from the heat for about **8 minutes**, or until the cheese topping is bubbly and browned and the bacon is cooked through.
5. Serve immediately while hot, or cut into wedges for smaller hors d'oeuvres.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The cheese and bacon mixture can be made ahead of time and kept sealed in the refrigerator until ready to assemble and broil.
