# Rice Croquettes

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Appetizers
> *Crispy golden rice croquettes bound with eggs and milk, seasoned with cayenne and parsley. Perfect as a savory appetizer or side dish served hot with white sauce.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 30 mins | 15 mins | 1 hour | 4 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Double boiler
* Deep fryer
* Mixing bowl

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 4 cups | [[Cooked rice]] | Prepared |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] | Beaten |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Cayenne]] | Ground |
| 1/4 cup | [[Milk]] | Whole |
| 1/4 cup | [[Butter]] | Softened |
| 1/2 tsp | [[Salt]] | Standard |
| 1/2 tsp | [[Pepper]] | Black, ground |
| 3 tbsp | [[Chopped parsley]] | Freshly chopped |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Cooking the Base
1. Cook all ingredients except **3 tbsp chopped parsley** in a double boiler until the **2 eggs** thickens.

### Step 2: Shaping and Frying
2. Stir in **3 tbsp chopped parsley** and cool the mixture.
3. When cold, shape the mixture, roll in crumbs, egg, and crumbs again, letting it stand if convenient.
4. Fry in deep fat until golden brown and crispy, then serve with white sauce.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Formed and crumbed croquettes can be refrigerated for up to 4 hours before frying in hot oil.
