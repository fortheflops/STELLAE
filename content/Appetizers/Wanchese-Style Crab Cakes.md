# Wanchese-Style Crab Cakes

> 📜 **Collection:** General Archive | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Appetizers
> *Classic pan-fried crab cakes with a cracker crumb binder and a zesty seasoning blend of mustard, Worcestershire, and hot sauce. Fried to a perfect golden brown and crispy texture.*

## Instructions

### Prepare the Patties
1. In a medium bowl, gently combine the **1 lb flaked crabmeat**, **1/4 cup cracker crumbs**, **1 beaten large egg**, **2 tbsp minced onion**, **2 tbsp mayonnaise**, **1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce**, **1 tbsp prepared mustard**, **3/4 to 1 1/2 tsp hot pepper sauce**, and **1 tsp salt**. Mix with a fork until just combined, being careful not to overwork the mixture.
2. Shape the mixture into **6 patties**, about 3 inches in diameter.
3. Place the additional **1 cup cracker crumbs** for coating on a plate or in a shallow dish.
4. Carefully coat each patty on both sides with the **cracker crumbs**.

### Fry the Crab Cakes
5. In a large skillet, heat about **1/4 inch of oil** over medium-high heat.
6. Carefully place the crab cakes in the hot oil and fry until golden brown and crispy, about **3 minutes per side**.
7. Remove the crab cakes from the skillet and drain them on a paper towel-lined plate. Serve hot.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Crab cakes can be shaped and coated ahead of time, then stored covered in the refrigerator for up to 4 hours before frying.
