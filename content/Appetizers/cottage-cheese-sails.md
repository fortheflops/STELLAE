# Cottage Cheese Sails

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Appetizers
> *A simple and refreshing appetizer featuring creamy cottage cheese seasoned with caraway seeds and onion. Served on crisp crackers and topped with thin cucumber slices for a light, crunchy finish.*

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 pkg (1/2 lb) | [[Cottage Cheese]] | Creamy |
| 1 tbsp | [[Caraway Seeds]] | None |
| 1 tsp | [[Minced Onion]] | None |
| To taste | [[Salt]] | None |
| To taste | [[Pepper]] | None |
| As needed | [[Crackers]] | None |
| 1 | [[Cucumber]] | Thinly sliced and quartered |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation
1. In a bowl, combine the **1/2 lb cottage cheese**, **1 tbsp caraway seeds**, and **1 tsp minced onion**.
2. Season the mixture to taste with **salt** and **pepper**.
3. Spread the mixture onto **crackers**.
4. Garnish each cracker with a **cucumber** slice that has been thinly sliced and cut into quarters.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The cottage cheese mixture can be prepared up to 2 hours in advance and kept refrigerated; assemble on crackers just before serving to maintain crispness.
