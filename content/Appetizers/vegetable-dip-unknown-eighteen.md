# Vegetable Dip

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Eighteen | ✍️ **Attribution:** Rosie | 📂 **Category:** Appetizers
> *A creamy, flavorful vegetable dip made with sour cream, miracle whip, and Italian dressing mix. Perfect for serving with fresh vegetables at your next gathering.*

## Ingredients

### Dip Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 cup | [[Sour Cream]] | None |
| 1/2 cup | [[Miracle Whip]] | None |
| 1 pkg | [[Good Seasons Italian Mix]] | Dry seasoning mix |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing and Chilling
1. In a mixing bowl, combine **1 cup sour cream**, **1/2 cup miracle whip**, and **1 pkg good seasons italian mix**.
2. Mix together thoroughly until well blended.
3. Let stand several hours before serving, preferably over night, to allow the flavors to meld into a rich and savory consistency.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Best prepared the night before to let the flavors develop fully in the refrigerator.
