# Cheese Ball

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Appetizers
> *A classic, savory cheese ball recipe featuring a blend of creamy and sharp cheeses with a hint of onion and garlic. Perfect for gatherings, this simple appetizer is finished with a crunchy coating of chopped pecans.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 0 mins | 1 hour | 8 servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Plastic wrap

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/4 lb | [[Blue Cheese]] | crumbled |
| 1 pkg (3 oz) | [[Cream Cheese]] | softened |
| 1/4 lb | [[Cheddar Cheese]] | shredded |
| 1 | [[Onion]] | minced |
| 1 tbsp | [[Garlic]] | minced |
| 1/4 lb | [[Pecans]] | coarsely chopped |

---

## Instructions

### Preparation
1. In a large mixing bowl, combine the **1/4 lb blue cheese**, **1 pkg (3 oz) cream cheese**, **1/4 lb cheddar cheese**, **1 minced onion**, and **1 tbsp garlic**.
2. Mix thoroughly until well combined and smooth.
3. Shape the mixture into a ball using your hands.
4. Roll the ball in the **1/4 lb coarsely chopped pecans** until evenly coated.
5. Wrap in plastic wrap and refrigerate for at least 1 hour until firm to the touch.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The cheese ball can be prepared up to 2 days in advance; keep refrigerated until ready to serve.
