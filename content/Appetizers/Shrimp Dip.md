# Shrimp Dip

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Appetizers
> *A classic, creamy shrimp dip featuring a savory blend of cream cheese, sour cream, and aromatic seasonings. Perfect for parties and gatherings, this vintage recipe is best served chilled with your favorite crackers.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 0 mins | 30 mins | 8 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Silicone spatula

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 8 Ounces | [[Cream Cheese]] | softened |
| 1/2 Pint | [[Sour Cream]] | full fat |
| 1 Can | [[Shrimp]] | cleaned, Blue Plate brand |
| 3-4 | [[Green Onions]] | finely chopped |
| 1 Tablespoon | [[Worcestershire Sauce]] | none |
| 1 1/2 Teaspoons | [[Garlic Salt]] | none |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Combine and Chill
1. In a large mixing bowl, combine the **8 ounces cream cheese**, **1/2 pint sour cream**, **1 can shrimp**, **3-4 green onions**, **1 tablespoon worcestershire sauce**, and **1 1/2 teaspoons garlic salt**.
2. Mix thoroughly until all ingredients are well incorporated and the texture is smooth.
3. Chill in the refrigerator for at least **30 minutes** to allow the flavors to meld before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This dip can be prepared up to 24 hours in advance; keep refrigerated in an airtight container.
