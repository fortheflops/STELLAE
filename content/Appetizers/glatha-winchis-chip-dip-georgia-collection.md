# Glatha Winchi's Chip Dip

> 📜 **Collection:** Georgia Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Glatha Winchi | 📂 **Category:** Appetizers
> *A creamy and flavorful homemade chip dip featuring cream cheese, garlic salt, and onion. Perfectly customizable in consistency and strength to suit your gathering's preferences.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | None | None | 6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Electric mixer

---
## Ingredients

### Dip Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 large pkg. | [[Cream cheese]] | Softened |
| 1 dash | [[Garlic salt]] | None |
| 1/8 tsp. | [[Salt]] | None |
| 1/4 cup | [[Milk]] | Adjust for consistency |
| 1 tbsp. | [[Onion]] | Minced |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing
1. In a mixing bowl, combine **1 large pkg. cream cheese**, **1 dash garlic salt**, **1/8 tsp. salt**, and **1 tbsp. onion**.
2. Gradually add **1/4 cup milk**, varying the amount until you reach a smooth, spreadable consistency that coats chips nicely.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Cover and refrigerate for at least 1 hour before serving to let the flavors meld.
