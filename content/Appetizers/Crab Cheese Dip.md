# Crab Cheese Dip

> 📜 **Collection:** The Loraine Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Appetizers
> *A creamy and savory crab dip featuring a blend of cream cheese and zesty seasonings. Perfect for serving with crackers or fresh vegetables at your next gathering.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 5 mins | None | 6–8 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Electric hand mixer
* Spatula

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 8 oz | [[Cream Cheese]] | Softened |
| 1/4 cup | [[Cream]] or [[Milk]] | Room temperature |
| 2 T. | [[Lemon Juice]] | Freshly squeezed |
| 1 1/2 t. | [[Worcestershire Sauce]] | None |
| 1 clove | [[Garlic]] | Minced |
| 1 dash | [[Salt]] & [[Pepper]] | To taste |
| 6.5–7.5 oz | [[Can Crab]] | Drained well |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Base
1. In a medium mixing bowl, beat the **8 oz cream cheese** until smooth and creamy.
2. Gradually add the **1/4 cup cream or milk** while mixing until fully incorporated and light.

### Step 2: Combine and Serve
3. Fold in the **2 T. lemon juice**, **1 1/2 t. worcestershire sauce**, **1 clove garlic**, and a **dash salt & pepper**.
4. Gently stir in the **6.5–7.5 oz can crab** until evenly distributed, ensuring the mixture is thick and well-combined.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Prepare up to 24 hours in advance and store in an airtight container in the refrigerator; stir well before serving.
