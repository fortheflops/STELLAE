# Classic Cocktail Party Trio

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Appetizers
> *An entertaining-ready trio of vintage party favorites featuring a savory Beau Monde Dip, smoky Bar-B-Qued Pecans, and a cheesy, spicy Jalapeño Fudge. Perfect for cocktail hour, these classic recipes can be prepared ahead of time for effortless hosting.*

## Instructions

### Beau Monde Dip Preparation
1. In a medium mixing bowl, combine **1 cup sour cream**, **1 cup mayonnaise**, **1 Tbsp grated fresh onion**, **1 Tbsp dill seed**, **1 Tbsp fresh parsley**, and **1 Tbsp beau monde spice**.
2. Mix together thoroughly. Cover and refrigerate for several hours or overnight for the best flavor development. Serve chilled with fresh vegetables or sliced jicama.

### Bar-B-Qued Pecans Preparation
3. Preheat oven to 300°F (150°C).
4. In a bowl, combine **2 Tbsp melted butter**, **1/4 cup Worcestershire sauce**, **1 Tbsp catsup**, and **1/8 tsp Tabasco sauce**.
5. Stir in **4 cups pecan halves** until they are well coated with the seasoning mixture.
6. Spread the pecans in a single layer in a shallow baking pan.
7. Bake at 300°F for **40 minutes**, stirring every 10 minutes to prevent burning. They should be fragrant and toasted.
8. Transfer the baked pecans to paper towels to drain, and sprinkle with **salt** if desired.

### Jalapeño Fudge Preparation
9. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C).
10. Grate **1 lb cheddar cheese** and **1 lb Monterey Jack cheese** directly into a 9x13-inch baking pan.
11. Add **3 dashes Tabasco sauce**, **8 chopped jalapeño peppers**, and **1 can chopped green chilies** to the cheese.
12. In a separate bowl, whisk **12 well beaten eggs** with **1/2 cup pancake mix** until smooth.
13. Pour the egg mixture over the cheese and mix well to distribute the ingredients evenly.
14. Bake at 350°F for **40 minutes** until the center is set and the top is lightly golden brown.
15. Allow to cool slightly, then cut into 1-inch squares. Serve warm or at room temperature.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The Beau Monde Dip is best made a day ahead. The Jalapeño Fudge can be frozen and reheated, making it excellent for stress-free cocktail parties.
