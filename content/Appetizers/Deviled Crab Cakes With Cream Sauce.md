# Deviled Crab Cakes With Cream Sauce

> 📜 **Collection:** General Archive | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Appetizers
> *Hearty, flavorful deviled crab cakes bound with a rich, thick cream sauce and seasoned with thyme and sautéed vegetables. Chilled, coated, and fried to a perfect golden brown.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Cream Sauce
1. In a small saucepan, melt **2 tablespoons butter** over medium heat. Whisk in **2 tablespoons flour** and cook for one minute, stirring constantly. Gradually whisk in **1 cup milk** until smooth. Bring to a simmer and cook, stirring, until the sauce becomes very thick, about 2-3 minutes. Season with a pinch of **salt** and **pepper**. Set aside.

### Step 2: Sauté the Vegetables
2. In a skillet, melt **1 tablespoon butter** over medium heat. Add the chopped **1 medium onion** and **1 small green bell pepper** and sauté until soft and transparent, about 5-7 minutes.

### Step 3: Mix and Chill
3. In a large bowl, combine the sautéed onion and pepper mixture, the prepared thick cream sauce, drained **1 (6 oz) can crab meat**, **1/2 teaspoon thyme**, **1 teaspoon minced parsley**, and **1 teaspoon celery flakes**. Season with additional **salt** and **pepper** to taste. Mix thoroughly.
4. Stir in enough **1/2 to 1 cup cracker meal** (starting with 1/2 cup) until the mixture reaches a consistency similar to mashed potatoes. Mix well.
5. Cover the bowl and refrigerate for several hours, or until the mixture is cold and firm.

### Step 4: Shape, Coat, and Fry
6. Shape the chilled mixture into small patties. In a shallow bowl, place the beaten **1 egg**. In another shallow dish, place additional **1 cup cracker meal**. Dip each patty first into the egg, then coat completely with cracker meal.
7. In a large skillet, heat a small amount of **fat or oil** over medium-high heat. Fry the crab cakes for 3-4 minutes per side, or until golden brown and cooked through. Drain on paper towels before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The crab cake mixture can be prepared and shaped ahead of time, then kept covered in the refrigerator for up to 24 hours before frying.
