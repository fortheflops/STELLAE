# Shrimp And Celery Cocktail Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Appetizers
> *A vintage-style shrimp cocktail salad featuring cooked shrimp, celery hearts, and sweet pickle relish mixed into a ketchup-based dressing. A simple, chilled appetizer perfect for retro-themed gatherings.*

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 pound | [[Shrimp]] | shelled, cooked, and chilled |
| 1 cup | [[Ketchup]] | or more, to taste |
| 1/2 cup | [[Sweet Pickle Relish]] |  |
| 1 cup | [[Celery Hearts]] | chopped |

---

## Instructions

### Preparation and Mixing
1. If using large **shrimp**, roughly chop the **1 pound cooked shrimp** into bite-sized pieces.
2. In a medium bowl, combine the chilled **1 pound cooked shrimp**, **1 cup ketchup**, **1/2 cup sweet pickle relish**, and **1 cup chopped celery hearts**.
3. Stir everything together until the **shrimp** and **celery** are evenly coated and the sauce is uniform.

### Chilling and Serving
4. Cover the bowl and refrigerate for at least **1 hour** until thoroughly chilled and the flavors are fully melded.
5. Serve chilled in small bowls or cocktail glasses.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Can be prepared up to 24 hours in advance. Keep tightly covered and refrigerated until ready to serve.
