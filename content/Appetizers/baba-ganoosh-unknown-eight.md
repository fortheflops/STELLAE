# Baba Ganoosh

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Eight | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Appetizers
> *A classic, smoky Mediterranean eggplant dip blended with creamy tahini, garlic, and fresh lemon juice. This simple, rustic spread is perfect served alongside warm pita bread or fresh vegetables.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Cook and Prep the Eggplant
1. Cook the **1 large eggplant** (either roast in a hot oven or char over a gas flame) until it is completely tender, collapsed, and smoky.
2. Allow the eggplant to cool enough to handle safely.
3. Gently slip off the charred skin and discard, leaving only the soft, cooked flesh.

### Step 2: Blend and Season
4. In a blender or food processor, combine the cooked eggplant flesh, **1/4 cup tahini**, **juice of 1 lemon**, **2 tablespoons olive oil**, **2 cloves garlic**, and **1/2 medium onion** (if using).
5. Blend all ingredients together until smooth and thoroughly combined.
6. Season with **salt** (or soy sauce) and **black pepper** to taste, blending briefly to incorporate.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Can be made ahead and stored in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 4 days. The flavors will deepen and meld after chilling for a few hours.
