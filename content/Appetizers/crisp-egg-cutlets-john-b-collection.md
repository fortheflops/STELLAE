# Crisp Egg Cutlets

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Appetizers
> *Discover a delightfully crispy egg cutlet recipe featuring hard-cooked eggs bound in a thick white sauce, chilled, crumbed, and pan-fried to golden perfection. This delightful retro appetizer offers a wonderfully creamy interior with a satisfying crunchy exterior.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 30 mins | 15 mins | 3 hours | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 8x8x2 pan
* Waxed paper
* Skillet
* Mixing bowl

## Ingredients

### Cutlet Mixture and Coating
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 cup | [[Thick white sauce]] | Prepared |
| 5 | [[Hard-cooked eggs]] | Chopped |
| 3/4 cup | [[Fine bread crumbs]] | Divided |
| 2 tsp | [[Parsley]] | Minced |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Paprika]] | None |
| 1 | [[Egg]] | Beaten |
| 1 tbsp | [[Water]] | For egg mixture |
| 4 tbsp | [[Fat]] | For frying |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing and Chilling
1. Combine the **1 cup thick white sauce**, **5 hard-cooked eggs**, **2 tsp parsley**, and **1/4 tsp paprika**. Cool slightly.
2. Line the bottom of an ungreased **8x8x2 pan** with waxed paper, then spoon the mixture evenly into the pan.
3. Chill for at least **3 hours** until set.

### Step 2: Shaping and Frying
4. Loosen the edges and turn the mixture out onto waxed paper. Cut into **8 portions**.
5. Roll in **3/4 cup fine bread crumbs** and form with fingers into cutlets.
6. Dip into the mixture of **1 beaten egg** and **1 tbsp water**, then coat well again in bread crumbs until completely covered.
7. Fry in a skillet with **4 tbsp fat** until golden brown and crispy.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The cutlet mixture can be prepared, pressed into the pan, and chilled up to 24 hours in advance before shaping and frying.
