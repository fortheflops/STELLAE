# Delightful Dip

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Appetizers
> *A creamy, savory appetizer dip featuring dried beef, cream cheese, and a toasted pecan topping. Baked until bubbly and served hot with crackers.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mix and Bake
1. Combine the **1 (8 oz) cream cheese**, **2 Tbsp milk**, **1 (2 1/2 oz) dried beef**, **1/4 cup green pepper**, **1/4 tsp garlic powder**, **1/4 tsp pepper**, **1 Tbsp onion**, and **1/2 cup sour cream** and mix until well blended.
2. Put into an **8" pie pan** or oven-proof dish of some capacity.

### Step 2: Topping and Baking
3. Sauté the **1/2 cup pecans** and **1/4 tsp salt** in **2 Tbsp butter** until nuts are lightly browned.
4. Sprinkle over the cheese-beef mixture.
5. Bake for **20 minutes** at **350°** until hot and bubbly.
6. Serve hot with low-salt crackers or melba toast.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the dip and topping in advance, store separately in the refrigerator, and bake just before serving.
