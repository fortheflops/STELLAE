# Spanakopita

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Appetizers
> *A classic Greek savory pastry featuring layers of crispy filo dough filled with a seasoned spinach and feta mixture. This elegant appetizer is perfect for entertaining and offers a delightful crunch in every bite.*

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 10-12 inch skillet
* 12-inch baking dish
* pastry brush
* rubber spatula
* scissors

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare Filling
1. In a heavy 10-12 inch skillet, heat **1/4 cup olive oil** over moderate heat until a light haze forms.
2. Add **1/2 cup onions** and **1/4 cup scallions**; cook for 5 minutes until transparent but not brown.
3. Stir in **2 pounds spinach**, cover, and cook for 5 minutes.
4. Add **1/4 cup dill**, **1/4 cup parsley**, **1/2 teaspoon salt**, and **black pepper**; cook uncovered for 10 minutes until liquid evaporates and spinach is sticky.
5. Transfer to a bowl, stir in **1/3 cup milk**, cool to room temperature, then beat in **4 eggs** and **1/2 pound feta cheese**.

### Step 2: Assembly and Baking
6. Preheat oven to 300°F. Brush a 12-inch baking dish with **1/2 pound melted butter**.
7. Layer **8 sheets of filo pastry** in the dish, brushing each sheet with **2-3 teaspoons butter** between layers.
8. Spread the spinach mixture evenly over the filo, then top with the remaining **8 sheets of filo**, brushing each with **butter**.
9. Trim excess pastry with scissors, brush the top with remaining **butter**, and bake for 1 hour until crisp and golden brown.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The spinach filling can be prepared in advance and kept refrigerated; assemble the filo layers just before baking to ensure the pastry remains crisp.
