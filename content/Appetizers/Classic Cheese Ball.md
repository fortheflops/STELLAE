# Classic Cheese Ball

> 📜 **Collection:** Louise Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Appetizers
> *A classic savory party appetizer made from a blend of cream cheese, soft cheddar, blue cheese, and finely chopped onions. Rolled in crunchy chopped pecans, it can easily be made ahead for entertaining.*

### 🔪 Key Equipment

* Spatula or electric mixer
* Plastic wrap
* Shallow dish or plate

| 1 Ounce | [[Blue Cheese]] | crumbled |

### For the Coating
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 Cup | [[Pecans]] | chopped (or other nuts) |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mix the Cheese Base
1. In a medium bowl, combine the **8 ounces cream cheese**, **3 ounces soft cheddar cheese**, **1 ounce blue cheese**, and **1/2 cup onions**. Mix with a spatula or an electric mixer on low speed until thoroughly blended and smooth.

### Step 2: Shape and Chill
2. Scrape the cheese mixture onto a piece of plastic wrap and use the wrap to help shape the mixture into a uniform ball. Wrap tightly and refrigerate for at least **1 hour**, or until firm to the touch.

### Step 3: Coat and Serve
3. Spread the **1 cup pecans** evenly on a plate or in a shallow dish. Unwrap the chilled cheese ball and roll it in the **pecans**, pressing gently until the entire surface is completely coated.
4. Serve immediately with your favorite crackers, pretzels, or fresh vegetable sticks.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The cheese ball can be prepared 1 to 2 days in advance and kept tightly wrapped in plastic wrap in the refrigerator. Roll in pecans shortly before serving.
