# Shrimp Zorka Skewers

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Mita | 📂 **Category:** Appetizers
> *A quick and flavorful recipe for broiled shrimp skewers. Large shrimp, lemon slices, and olives are marinated in a zesty Italian dressing with tarragon and garlic, then broiled to perfection.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 25 mins | 6 mins | 15–30 mins (Marinating) | 1–2 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Skewers (wooden or metal)
* Small bowl
* Broiler pan or baking sheet
* Foil
* Whisk

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 6 | [[Shrimp]] | shelled and deveined |
| 4 | [[Lemon Slices]] | thin, with peel |
| 6 | [[Olives]] | pimento-stuffed |
| 1 tbsp | [[Lemon Juice]] | None |
| 3 tbsp | Low-Calorie Italian [[Dressing]] | None |
| 1/2 tsp | Instant Granulated [[Garlic]] | None |
| 1/4 tsp | Dried [[Tarragon]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Marinade
1. In a small bowl, whisk together the **3 tablespoons low-calorie Italian dressing**, **1 tablespoon lemon juice**, **1/2 teaspoon instant granulated garlic**, and **1/4 teaspoon dried tarragon** to create the marinade.

### Step 2: Marinate
2. Add the **6 large shrimp**, **4 thin lemon slices**, and **6 pimento-stuffed olives** to the bowl. Stir gently to coat everything, then cover and let marinate in the refrigerator for at least **15-30 minutes**.

### Step 3: Assemble and Broil
3. Preheat your broiler. If using wooden skewers, soak them in water for **20 minutes** to prevent burning.
4. Thread the ingredients onto the skewers, alternating between **olives**, **shrimp**, and **lemon slices**. Reserve the leftover marinade for basting.
5. Place the skewers on a broiler pan or a baking sheet lined with foil.
6. Broil about 4-5 inches from the heat for **3 minutes** on one side.
7. Flip the skewers, baste with the reserved marinade, and broil for another **3 minutes**, or until the **shrimp** are pink, opaque, and cooked through.
8. Serve immediately.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The shrimp can be marinated in the refrigerator for up to 30 minutes before broiling. Do not over-marinate as the acid in the lemon juice will begin to cook the shrimp.
