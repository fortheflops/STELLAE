# Four-Cheese Party Log

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Appetizers
> *This savory Four-Cheese Party Log blends cream cheese, cheddar, Velveeta, and sharp cheddar with grated onion and Worcestershire sauce. Coated in crunchy pecans and chilled until firm, it makes a rich and satisfying classic appetizer for any gathering.*

## Ingredients

### For the Cheese Logs
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 8 oz | [[Cream Cheese]] | softened |
| 8 oz | [[Cheddar Cheese]] | shredded |
| 8 oz | [[Velveeta Cheese]] | softened and cubed |
| 8 oz | Sharp [[Cheddar Cheese]] | shredded |
| 1 small | [[Onion]] | grated |
| 1–3 tsp | [[Worcestershire Sauce]] | to taste |

### For the Coating
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| ½ cup | [[Pecans]] | finely chopped |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Blend the Cheeses
1. In a large bowl, combine **8 ounces softened cream cheese**, **8 ounces shredded cheddar cheese**, **8 ounces softened Velveeta cheese**, and **8 ounces shredded sharp cheddar cheese**.
2. Add **1 small grated onion** and **1 to 3 teaspoons Worcestershire sauce** to the cheeses.
3. Using an electric mixer, beat on low then medium speed until the mixture is completely smooth, creamy, and well-blended.

### Step 2: Shape and Coat
4. Divide the cheese mixture in half. On a sheet of wax paper, shape each portion into a smooth log.
5. Spread **½ cup finely chopped pecans** on a plate or sheet of wax paper.
6. Roll each log in the **pecans**, pressing gently until evenly coated on all sides.

### Step 3: Chill and Serve
7. Wrap each log tightly in wax paper or plastic wrap and refrigerate for at least **8 hours** or overnight until completely firm.
8. To serve, unwrap, slice into round portions, and serve chilled alongside crisp crackers.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The logs can be made ahead and stored tightly wrapped in the refrigerator for up to 1 week, or frozen for up to 1 month.
