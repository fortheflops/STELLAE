# Guacamole

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Helen Ferraro | 📂 **Category:** Appetizers
> *A classic homemade guacamole recipe featuring fresh avocados, lime juice, and a hint of spice. Perfect for serving with tortilla chips at your next gathering.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | None | None | 3 cups |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Knife
* Cutting board
* Fork or masher

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 4 | [[Avocados]] | Ripe, pitted and peeled |
| 1 1/2 tsp | [[Salt]] | None |
| 1/2 tsp | [[Season Salt]] | None |
| 2 tbsp | [[Lime Juice]] | Freshly squeezed |
| 1/2 tsp | [[Worcestershire Sauce]] | None |
| 1/8 tsp | [[Tabasco]] | Hot pepper sauce |
| To taste | [[Diced Onion]] | Diced |
| To taste | [[Ripe Tomatoes]] | Chopped fine |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing and Mashing
1. In a mixing bowl, combine the **4 avocados**, **1 1/2 tsp salt**, **1/2 tsp season salt**, **2 tbsp lime juice**, **1/2 tsp Worcestershire sauce**, and **1/8 tsp Tabasco**.
2. Mash the ingredients together until well blended and creamy yet retaining a little texture.
3. Gently fold in the **diced onion** and finely chopped **ripe tomatoes** until evenly distributed.
4. Serve immediately with chips until smooth and vibrant.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Press plastic wrap directly onto the surface of the guacamole to prevent browning and refrigerate for up to a few hours.
