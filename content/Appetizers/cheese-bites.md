# Cheese Bites

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Appetizers
> *Delightful bite-sized appetizers made with cream cheese, sharp cheddar, and paprika. Perfect for gatherings and quick party snacks.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 10 mins | None | 4 dozen |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Baking sheet

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3 oz | [[Cream Cheese]] | Softened |
| 1/4 cup | [[Butter]] | Softened |
| 1 cup | [[Sharp Cheddar Cheese]] | Shredded |
| 1/2 cup | [[Flour]] | All-purpose |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Salt]] | Standard |
| 1/8 tsp | [[Paprika]] | Ground |

---

## Instructions

### Preparation and Baking
1. Cream together the **3 oz cream cheese** and **1/4 cup butter** until smooth.
2. Mix in the **1 cup sharp cheddar cheese**, **1/2 cup flour**, **1/4 tsp salt**, and **1/8 tsp paprika** until thoroughly combined.
3. Roll the dough into small balls and place them on a baking sheet.
4. Bake at 350°F for **10 minutes** until golden and set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Unbaked dough balls can be refrigerated for up to 24 hours before baking.
