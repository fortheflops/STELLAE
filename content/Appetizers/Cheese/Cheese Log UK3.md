## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
| 8 Ounce               | Cream Cheese                 |
| 8 Ounce              | Cheddar Cheese              |
| 8 Ounce               | Velveeta Cheese             |
| 8 Ounce               | Sharp Cheese                |
| 0-3 Teaspoon          | Worcestershire              |
| 1 Small               | Onion, Grated                |
| 1/2 Cup               | Pecans, Chopped             |

___
### Instructions
1. Soften and blend cream cheese, cheddar cheese, Velveeta cheese, and sharp cheese.
2. Grate 1 small onion, mix with beater until smooth, with cheeses and worcestershire.
3. Chop 1/2 cup pecans.
4. Form cheese into log and roll on pecans.
5. Wrap in wax paper and chill 8 hours.
6. Slice and serve with crackers.
___

>[!Note]
>Makes 2 Logs
