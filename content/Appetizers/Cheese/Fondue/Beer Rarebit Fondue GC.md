## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| Four Cups                        | Shredded Sharp Natural [[Cheddar Cheese]] |
| $\frac{1}{4}$ Cup                | [[Beer]]                                  |
| $\frac{1}{4}$ Cup                | [[Ginger Ale]]                            |
| Two Teaspoons                    | [[Worcestershire Sauce]]                  |
| $\frac{1}{2}$ Teaspoon           | Dry [[Mustard]]                           |
| $\frac{1}{8}$ Teaspoon           | [[Garlic Powder]]                         |
| Dash                             | Hot [[Pepper Sauce]]                      |
| One Tablespoon                   | [[Cornstarch]]                            |
| Two Tablespoons                  | [[Water]]                                 |
| For Dipping                      | French [[Bread Cubes]], Raw [[Vegetables]], Cooked [[Meat]] |
___
## Instructions
1.  Have cheese at room temperature.
2.  Beat cheese at low speed until blended.
3.  Add beer and ginger ale SLOWLY.
4.  Beat until fluffy.
5.  Add Worcestershire sauce, dry mustard, garlic powder, and hot pepper sauce.
6.  Beat well.
7.  Melt mixture in a fondue pot on the range.
8.  Blend cornstarch and two tablespoons of water.
9.  Add the cornstarch mixture to the cheese mixture.
10. Cook and stir until thick and bubbly.
___

>[!Note]
>Serve with French bread cubes, raw vegetables, and cooked meat for dipping.
