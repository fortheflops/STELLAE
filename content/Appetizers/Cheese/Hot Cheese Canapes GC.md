## Recipe

___
## Instructions
1.  Cut white bread slices into small shapes (e.g., rounds, squares, triangles).
2.  Top each bread shape with a small slice of cold meat or a piece of shrimp.
3.  In a small bowl, combine one cup of mayonnaise and $\frac{1}{2}$ cup of shredded cheese.
4.  Cover each appetizer completely with the cheese and mayonnaise mixture.
5.  Broil 4 inches from the heat until brown, or bake in an oven (temperature and time not specified, but likely a moderate oven like 375-400°F for a few minutes until bubbly and browned).
