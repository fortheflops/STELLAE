## Instructions

### Making the Cheese Cakes
1. Grate **0.5 onion** and brown in **3 tbsp butter**.
2. Stir in **4 tbsp flour** to make a paste.
3. Add **1 cup milk**, **0.25 tsp salt**, **0.25 tsp pepper**, **0.25 tsp mustard**, and **0.125 tsp mustard**.
4. Cook for **5 minutes**, then remove from the stove.
5. Add **2 cups cheese**, **1.5 cups bread crumbs**, and **1 tbsp parsley**.
6. Stir until the **2 cups cheese** has completely melted.
7. Chill thoroughly until firm.

### Making the Egg Sauce
8. Hard cook **4 eggs** and chop fine.
9. Make a cream sauce using standard proportions seasoned with salt, pepper, and celery salt.
10. Add **2 tsp horseradish**.
11. Keep hot in a double boiler until ready to serve.

### Shaping and Cooking
12. When the cheese cake mixture is chilled, form and shape into flat patties.
13. Dust the patties with **flour** and brown on both sides in **melted fat** until golden and crisp.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The cheese cake mixture can be prepared and chilled up to 24 hours in advance before shaping and frying.
