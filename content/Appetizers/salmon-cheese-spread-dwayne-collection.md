# Salmon Cheese Spread

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Appetizers
> *A quick and savory salmon cheese spread featuring canned salmon, cream cheese, cheddar, and sweet pickle relish. Perfect for effortless entertaining when served alongside crisp crackers or crusty bread.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | None | None | 4 servings |

## Ingredients

### Spread Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 4 oz | [[Cream Cheese]] | softened |
| 1 can (7 1/2 oz) | [[Salmon]] | drained, bones and skin removed |
| 1/3 cup | [[Cheddar Cheese]] | shredded |
| 1/4 cup | [[Sweet Pickle Relish]] |  |

### For Serving
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| As needed | [[Bread]] or [[Crackers]] |  |

---

## Instructions

### Preparation
1. In a mixing bowl, combine the **4 oz cream cheese**, **1 can (7 1/2 oz) salmon**, **1/3 cup shredded cheddar cheese**, and **1/4 cup sweet pickle relish**.
2. Mix thoroughly until the ingredients are well combined and the spread is relatively smooth with small flecks of salmon and cheese throughout.
3. Use immediately as a spread, or serve on **bread** or **crackers**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Can be made up to 2 days in advance. Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator and stir gently before serving.
