# Party Chicken Sandwiches

> 📜 **Collection:** Georgia Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Appetizers
> *Delightful party-sized open-faced sandwiches topped with a creamy chicken, green chili, and cheddar mixture. Broiled until bubbly and lightly browned for a warm, savory bite.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 5 mins | None | 36 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Baking sheet
* Broiler

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 1/2 cups | [[Chicken]] | Finely chopped and cooked |
| 1 cup | [[Miracle Whip]] | None |
| 4 oz. | [[Green chilies]] | Chopped and drained |
| 3/4 cup | [[Cheddar cheese]] | Shredded |
| 1/4 cup | [[Onion]] | Finely chopped |
| 36 slices | [[Party rye or pumpernickel bread]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing and Assembling
1. Combine **1 1/2 cups cooked chicken**, **1 cup Miracle Whip**, **4 oz. green chilies**, **3/4 cup shredded cheddar**, and **1/4 cup onion** in a bowl. Mix lightly.
2. Spoon and cover the **36 slices party rye or pumpernickel bread** evenly with the chicken mixture.

### Step 2: Broiling
3. Broil about 5 inches from the heat source until the cheese is melted and bubbly and the tops are **lightly browned**.
4. Serve hot.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The chicken mixture can be prepared a few hours in advance and kept covered in the refrigerator until ready to assemble and broil.
