# Susan Ely'S Cheese Ball

> 📜 **Collection:** Patsy's Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Susan Ely | 📂 **Category:** Appetizers
> *A zesty party appetizer combining cream cheese, tangy blue cheese, and spicy cheddar spread seasoned with garlic and cayenne pepper. Perfect for holiday gatherings, this classic cheese ball can be prepared ahead of time and customized with your favorite coatings.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Combine and Blend
1. In the bowl of a mixer, combine **8 oz softened cream cheese**, **4 oz crumbled blue cheese**, **5 oz tube processed cheddar or jalapeño cheese spread**, **½ tbsp onion juice**, **2 cloves minced garlic**, and **½ tsp cayenne pepper**.
2. Blend on medium speed until all ingredients are thoroughly combined and the mixture is completely smooth.

### Step 2: Chill, Shape, and Coat
3. Cover the bowl and refrigerate for at least **1 hour**, or until the cheese mixture is firm enough to handle easily.
4. Once chilled, divide the mixture as desired and form into one large ball or several smaller balls.
5. If using optional coatings like **chopped nuts, parsley, paprika, or extra cayenne pepper**, spread them on a shallow plate and gently roll the cheese ball(s) until evenly coated across the surface.
6. Wrap the finished cheese ball(s) tightly in wax paper or plastic wrap and store in the refrigerator until ready to serve.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store wrapped tightly in the refrigerator for up to 5 days. For the best texture, roll in crunchy coatings like nuts just before serving.
