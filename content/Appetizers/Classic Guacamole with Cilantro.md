# Classic Guacamole with Cilantro

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Appetizers
> *A vibrant and traditional guacamole featuring fresh ripe avocados, tomatoes, red onion, and fragrant cilantro. Perfectly balanced with lemon juice and a hint of red chili for a delightful kick.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | None | None | 4–6 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Fork or potato masher
* Chef's knife
* Cutting board

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3 | [[Avocados]] | Ripe, peeled |
| 2 | [[Tomatoes]] | Coarsely chopped |
| 1 | [[Red Onion]] | Finely chopped |
| 1/2 Teaspoon | [[Cilantro]] | Coriander, finely chopped |
| Juice of 2 | [[Lemons]] | Freshly squeezed |
| To taste | [[Salt]] | As needed |
| To taste | [[Red Chili]] | As needed |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Mash
1. Peel the **3 ripe avocados** and place them into a mixing bowl; mash them gently with a fork until desired consistency is reached.

### Step 2: Combine and Season
2. Add the **2 coarsely chopped tomatoes**, **1 finely chopped red onion**, and **1/2 teaspoon finely chopped cilantro** to the mashed avocados.
3. Toss the mixture together lightly until well combined.
4. Sprinkle the **juice of 2 lemons** evenly over the top.
5. Season with **salt** and **red chili** to taste until savory and fragrant.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Press plastic wrap directly onto the surface of the guacamole to prevent browning and store in the refrigerator for up to 1 day.
