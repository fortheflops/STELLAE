# Hot Cheese And Mayo Canapés

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Appetizers
> *Simple, savory, and quick-to-make hot canapés. Small bites of bread are topped with meat or shrimp, covered in a cheesy mayonnaise mixture, and broiled until golden and bubbly.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 5 mins | None | 24 Canapés |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Baking sheet
* Small bowl
* Knife or cookie cutters
* Broiler

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 8–10 slices | White [[Bread Slices]] | crusts removed |
| 4 oz | sliced cold [[Meats]] (like ham or turkey) or cooked [[Shrimp]] |  |
| 1 cup | [[Mayonnaise]] |  |
| 1/2 cup | shredded [[Cheese]] | such as Cheddar or Parmesan |

---

## Instructions

### Preparation and Assembly
1. Position an oven rack about 4-5 inches from the broiler and preheat the broiler.
2. Remove the crusts from the **8–10 slices white bread slices**. Cut the bread into small decorative shapes, such as rounds, squares, or triangles, using a knife or cookie cutters.
3. Arrange the bread shapes on a baking sheet. Top each piece with a small slice of **4 oz cold meats** or a piece of **cooked shrimp**.
4. In a small bowl, thoroughly combine the **1 cup mayonnaise** and **1/2 cup shredded cheese**.
5. Spread a layer of the cheese and mayonnaise mixture over each canapé, making sure to cover the toppings completely to the edges of the bread.

### Broiling
6. Place the baking sheet under the preheated broiler. Watch very carefully and broil for **2–4 minutes**, or just until the topping is golden brown and bubbly. They can burn quickly!
7. Remove from the oven and serve immediately while hot.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** These canapés are best assembled and broiled just before serving. You can prep the bread shapes and cheese mixture a few hours ahead and store them separately in the refrigerator.
