# Four-Cheese Pimento Cheese

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Appetizers
> *A rich and creamy Southern-style spread featuring a blend of four distinct cheeses, sweet pimentos, and a tangy mayonnaise dressing. Perfect for sandwiches, crackers, or party platters.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | None | None | 12 servings |

* Mixing bowl

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 cup | [[American Cheese]] | grated |
| 1 cup | [[Cheddar Cheese]] | grated |
| 1 cup | [[Colby Cheese]] | grated |
| 1 cup | [[Velveeta Cheese]] | grated |
| 1 (4 oz) jar | [[Pimentos]] | chopped |
| 1 tbsp | [[Yellow Mustard]] |  |
| 2 tbsp | [[Sugar]] |  |
| 1 pint | [[Mayonnaise]] | preferably Hellmann's |
| 2 tbsp | [[Worcestershire Sauce]] |  |

---

## Instructions

### Preparation
1. Grate the **1 cup American cheese**, **1 cup Cheddar cheese**, **1 cup Colby cheese**, and **1 cup Velveeta cheese**, then toss them together in a large bowl until evenly combined.
2. In a separate bowl, combine the **1 tablespoon yellow mustard**, **2 tablespoons sugar**, **1 pint mayonnaise**, and **2 tablespoons Worcestershire sauce**. Stir until smooth and well blended, then fold in the **1 (4 oz) jar chopped pimentos**.
3. Add the tossed cheeses to the mayonnaise mixture and mix well until fully incorporated, creamy, and uniform in texture.
4. Transfer the mixture into clean storage jars, cover tightly, and refrigerate.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in airtight jars in the refrigerator. This spread keeps well for a long time and the flavors will deepen after chilling for a few hours.
