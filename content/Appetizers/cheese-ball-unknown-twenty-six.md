# Cheese Ball

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Appetizers
> *A classic party appetizer featuring a flavorful blend of sharp cheese and cream cheese. Formed into a ball and rolled for a stunning presentation.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | None | 1 hour | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Plastic wrap

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 jar | [[Old English Cheese]] | Spread |
| 1 - 8 oz pkg | [[Philadelphia Cream Cheese]] | Softened |
| to taste | [[Garlic Salt]] | None |
| as needed | [[Pecans]] | Optional |
| as needed | [[Chili Powder]] | For sprinkling |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing and Forming
1. Combine **1 jar old English cheese** and **1 - 8 oz pkg Phil. C. Cheese** in a mixing bowl.
2. Season with **garlic salt** to taste and mix well until smooth.
3. Stir in **pecans** if desired.
4. Form the mixture into a ball and sprinkle the outside with **chili powder** until coated.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Prepare the cheese ball a day in advance, wrap tightly in plastic wrap, and refrigerate until ready to serve.
