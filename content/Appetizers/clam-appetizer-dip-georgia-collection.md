# Clam Appetizer Dip

> 📜 **Collection:** Georgia Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Appetizers
> *A quick and creamy seafood dip featuring savory minced clams and zesty lemon. Perfect for serving with crackers or fresh vegetables at your next gathering.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 0 mins | 1 hour | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Electric mixer or spoon

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 8 oz | [[Minced Clams]] | drained, reserve 1/4 cup liquid |
| 8 oz | [[Cream Cheese]] | softened |
| 2 tsp | [[Lemon Juice]] | freshly squeezed |
| 1 1/2 tsp | [[Worcestershire Sauce]] | none |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Garlic Salt]] | none |
| 1 dash | [[Black Pepper]] | none |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation
1. In a mixing bowl, combine the **8 oz minced clams**, **1/4 cup clam liquid**, **8 oz cream cheese**, **2 tsp lemon juice**, **1 1/2 tsp worcestershire sauce**, **1/4 tsp garlic salt**, and **1 dash pepper**.
2. Blend the mixture well until smooth and creamy.
3. Chill in the refrigerator for at least one hour to allow flavors to meld before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This dip can be prepared up to 2 days in advance; keep refrigerated in an airtight container.
