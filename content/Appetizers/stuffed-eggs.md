# Stuffed Eggs

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Appetizers
> *A savory, protein-packed appetizer featuring hard-boiled eggs filled with a flavorful mixture of crab, walnuts, and celery. These elegant bites are served atop fresh tomato slices for a refreshing finish.*

## Ingredients

### Filling Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |

| 1 tsp | [[Mustard]] | Prepared |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Salt]] | Fine |
| 1 cup | [[Crab]] | Flaked |
| 1/2 cup | [[Celery]] | Chopped |
| 1/2 cup | [[Walnuts]] | Chopped |
| 2 tbsp | [[Green Pepper]] | Finely diced |
| To taste | [[Mayonnaise]] | As needed for consistency |

### Serving
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 6 slices | [[Tomato]] | Fresh |

---

## Instructions

### Preparation
1. Peel the **6 hard-boiled eggs** and slice them in half lengthwise.
2. Carefully remove the yolks and place them into a mixing bowl; rice or mash the yolks with a fork.
3. Add the **1 tsp mustard**, **1/4 tsp salt**, **1 cup flaked crab**, **1/2 cup chopped celery**, **1/2 cup chopped walnuts**, and **2 tbsp green pepper** to the yolks.
4. Gradually stir in enough **mayonnaise** until the mixture is moist and holds its shape.
5. Stuff the yolk mixture back into the egg whites.
6. Place each stuffed egg onto a fresh **slice of tomato** and serve chilled.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The egg filling can be prepared up to 24 hours in advance and stored in an airtight container in the refrigerator; assemble on tomato slices just before serving to prevent sogginess.
