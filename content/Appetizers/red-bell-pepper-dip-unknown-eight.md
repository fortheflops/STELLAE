# Red Bell Pepper Dip

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Eight | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Appetizers
> *A creamy and smoky red bell pepper dip seasoned with chili powder and fresh garlic. Perfect for serving with chips, crackers, or fresh vegetables at your next gathering.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | None | 3 hours | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Knife
* Cutting board
* Paper towels
* Spatula

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3 medium | [[Red Bell Peppers]] | roasted, peeled, and finely chopped |
| 16 oz | [[Sour Cream]] | at room temperature |
| 2 tablespoons | [[Chili Powder]] |  |
| 1 clove | [[Garlic]] | finely chopped |
| 1/2 teaspoon | [[Salt]] |  |
| 1/4 teaspoon | [[Black Pepper]] | freshly ground |

---

## Instructions

### Preparation and Mixing
1. After the **3 medium red bell peppers** are roasted and peeled, pat them dry with paper towels and chop them finely.
2. Pat the chopped **red bell peppers** dry again to remove excess moisture and keep the dip from becoming too soupy.
3. In a mixing bowl, combine the chopped peppers with **16 oz sour cream**, **2 tablespoons chili powder**, **1 clove garlic**, **1/2 teaspoon salt**, and **1/4 teaspoon black pepper**.
4. Mix all ingredients thoroughly until well combined and smooth.
5. Cover and chill in the refrigerator for several hours to allow the flavors to meld before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This dip is best made several hours or up to a day in advance to let the flavors fully develop. Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator.
