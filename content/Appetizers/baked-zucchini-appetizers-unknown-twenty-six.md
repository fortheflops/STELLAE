# Baked Zucchini Appetizers

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Appetizers
> *These savory Baked Zucchini Appetizers are packed with cheese, herbs, and garden-fresh zucchini. Cut into bite-sized squares, they make a wonderful and easy finger food for any gathering.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing and Baking
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees F. Grease a **9"x13" ovenproof pan**.
2. Mix **3 cups sliced zucchini, uncooked**, **1 cup prepared biscuit mix**, **1/2 cup finely chopped onion**, **1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese**, **2 T. snipped parsley or parsley flakes**, **1/2 t. salt**, **1/2 t. seasoned salt**, **1/2 t. oregano or marjoram**, dash salt, **1 clove crushed garlic**, **1/2 cup vegetable oil**, and **4 slightly beaten eggs** together well.
3. Spread the mixture evenly in the prepared pan.
4. Bake until **golden brown**, about **25 minutes**.

### Step 2: Serving
5. Cut into 2"x1" squares.
6. Serve hot.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Bake beforehand and reheat just before serving, or assemble the mixture and bake fresh.
