## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
| 1 Button               | Garlic                |
| 1                     | Hot Pepper            |
| 1 Tablespoon            | Dill Seed            |
| 1/2 Gallon            | Vinegar               |
| 1/2 Gallon            | Water                 |
| 1 Cup                 | Salt                  |
|                       | Okra                |
___
### Instructions
1. Mix vinegar, water, and salt.
2. Bring to a boil.
3. Pour over okra, garlic, hot pepper, and dill seed.
4. Seal for 6 weeks
