## Recipe
| Measurements      | Ingredients                   |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 can             | Sliced [[Beets]]                |
| 1/2 cup           | [[Sugar]]                       |
| 1/2 cup           | [[Vinegar]]                     |
| As Needed         | Sliced [[Onion]]                  |
___
## Instructions
1.  Drain the juice from the can of sliced beets.
2.  Place the drained beets in a jar.
3.  In a small bowl, stir the sugar and vinegar together until the sugar begins to dissolve. Pour this mixture over the beets.
4.  Add a few slices of onion.
5.  Seal the jar and keep it in the refrigerator. For best flavor, let them marinate for at least 24 hours.
___

>[!Note]
>This is a quick pickle recipe and is not intended for long-term shelf storage; always keep the pickles refrigerated.
