## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
| 1 Jar                | Sharp Cheddar Cheese         |
| 1 Stick              | Margarine                   |
| 1 Teaspoon           | Mustard                     |
___
### Instructions
1. Blend together the cheese, margarine and mustard
2. Spread mixture on bread squares or french bread.
3. Warm in Oven
