## Instructions
1. In a 10-ounce glass, combine two tablespoons of fresh lemon juice, two tablespoons of Grade B maple syrup, and $\frac{1}{10}$ teaspoon of cayenne pepper.
2.  Fill the remainder of the 10-ounce glass with water (as the card instructs "fill + drink").
3. Drink this mixture when hungry.
