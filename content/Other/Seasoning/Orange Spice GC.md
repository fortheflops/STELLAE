## Instructions
1.  Take a thick orange skin and peel as much of the white pith off the inside as possible.
2.  Cut the orange peel into $\frac{3}{4}$-inch "cog" shapes (likely meaning squares or decorative shapes).
3.  Put a whole clove in the center of each piece of orange peel.
4.  Let the spiced orange peels dry naturally.
5.  Use to spice up tea or as a sachet to scent a closet or drawer.
