# Ruth's Fresh Cranberry & Orange Relish

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Ruth | 📂 **Category:** Other
> *A vibrant and tangy uncooked relish made with fresh cranberries, whole oranges, and sugar, chilled to perfection. This make-ahead condiment adds a bright pop of flavor to poultry, pork, and holiday tables.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | None | 2 hours | 8 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Food processor
* Food chopper
* Storage container

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 lb | [[Cranberries]] | Fresh, washed |
| 2 whole | [[Oranges]] | Zested, juiced, and piths removed |
| 2 cups | [[Sugar]] | None |
| 1-2 tbsp | [[Lemon Juice]] | To taste |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare Ingredients
1. Wash the **1 lb cranberries** and **2 oranges** thoroughly. Use the zest and juice of the oranges, and remove their piths. Optionally, give all ingredients a rough chop before grinding to make the food processor's job easier.

### Step 2: Grind
2. Grind all ingredients together including the **1-2 tbsp lemon juice** and **2 cups sugar** in a food chopper or food processor until desired consistency is reached, ranging from a coarse relish to a finer, sauce-like texture.

### Step 3: Chill and Serve
3. Transfer the relish to a container, cover, and chill for at least **2 hours**, or preferably overnight, until the flavors meld and the relish thickens slightly. Serve cold as a condiment.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store sealed in the refrigerator for up to 1 week, or chill overnight before serving to allow flavors to fully develop.
