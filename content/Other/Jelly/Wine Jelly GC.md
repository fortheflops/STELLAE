## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| Two Cups                         | Fruit [[Wine]] (Blackberry, Apple, etc.)  |
| Three Cups                       | [[Sugar]]                                 |
| One Half Bottle                  | Certo (Liquid Pectin)                     |
| $\frac{1}{8}$ to $\frac{1}{4}$ Inch | Melted [[Paraffin Wax]] (for sealing)   |
___
## Instructions
1.  In a saucepan, dissolve three cups of sugar in two cups of fruit wine.
2.  Heat the mixture until it is near boiling, which should take about 5-10 minutes.
3.  Add one half of a bottle of Certo (liquid pectin).
4.  Stir the pectin in completely.
5.  Pour the jelly into sterilized jars.
6.  Top the jelly in each jar with $\frac{1}{8}$ to $\frac{1}{4}$ inches of melted paraffin wax to seal.
7.  Let cool and set.
___

>[!Note]
>The recipe notes it makes about 4 baby food jars per batch
