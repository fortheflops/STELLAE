## Instructions

### Mixing & Baking
1. In a large bowl, mix together **1/2 cup oil**, **1/2 cup honey or syrup**, **1 tsp vanilla**, **2 cups oats**, **2 cups whole wheat cereal flakes**, **1 cup sunflower seeds**, **1/4 cup sesame seeds**, **1 cup raisins**, **2 cups chopped nuts**, and **2 cups coconut** until evenly combined.
2. Spread the mixture evenly onto an ungreased baking sheet.
3. Bake at 350°F (175°C) for 15 to 20 minutes until golden brown, fragrant, and slightly toasted. Stir occasionally for even baking.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Allow granola to cool completely, then store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 2 weeks.
