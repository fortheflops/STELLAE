# 24 Hour Omelette

> 📜 **Collection:** JillCollection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Breakfast
> *A perfect make-ahead savory breakfast casserole featuring chunks of challah bread soaked overnight in a rich egg custard with Longhorn and cream cheeses. This comforting strata bakes into a golden, puffed masterpiece ideal for weekend brunch.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 1 hour | 8 hours | 10 to 12 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 9x13 inch casserole dish
* Mixing bowl
* Whisk

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 loaf | [[Egg Challah]] | Crusts removed |
| 1 stick (1/2 cup) | [[Butter]] | Melted |
| 3/4 pound | [[Longhorn Cheese]] | Grated |
| 8 ounces | [[Cream Cheese]] | Cubed |
| 8 large | [[Eggs]] | Beaten |
| 2 cups | [[Milk]] | None |
| 1/2 teaspoon | [[Dry Mustard]] | None |
| 1/2 teaspoon | [[Salt]] | None |
| 1 tablespoon | [[Chives]] | Chopped |
| 1 dash | [[Cayenne Pepper]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Layer the Base
1. Grease a large **9x13 inch casserole dish** well.
2. Tear the **1 loaf egg challah** into chunks and place them evenly in the dish.
3. Drizzle the **1 stick butter** evenly over the bread.
4. Sprinkle the **3/4 pound Longhorn cheese** over the bread and dot the top with small pieces of the **8 ounces cream cheese**.

### Step 2: Add Custard and Chill
5. In a separate mixing bowl, beat together the **8 large eggs**, **2 cups milk**, **1/2 teaspoon salt**, **1/2 teaspoon dry mustard**, **1 dash cayenne pepper**, and **1 tablespoon chives** until smooth.
6. Pour the egg mixture evenly over the bread and cheese in the casserole dish.
7. Cover the dish and refrigerate overnight or for at least 8 hours until fully absorbed.

### Step 3: Bake and Serve
8. When ready to bake, preheat the oven to **325°F (165°C)**.
9. Bake the casserole **covered** for **1 hour**, or until the center is set and puffed.
10. Uncover the dish for the last **10 minutes** of baking to brown the top until golden brown and bubbling.
11. Let stand for **10 minutes** before slicing and serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the entire casserole the night before, cover, and refrigerate overnight before baking as directed.
