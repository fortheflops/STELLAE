# Impossible Quiche

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Breakfast
> *A classic, effortless crustless quiche featuring savory bacon, Swiss cheese, and a simple Bisquick batter that magically forms its own crust. Perfect for an easy weekend brunch or a quick weeknight dinner.*

## Instructions

### Assembly and Baking
1. Heat oven to **350°F**. Lightly grease a **9 or 10-inch pie plate**.
2. Sprinkle **12 slices fried and crumbled bacon**, **1 cup shredded Swiss cheese**, and **1/3 cup finely chopped onion** evenly over the bottom of the prepared pie plate.
3. Place the remaining ingredients—**2 cups milk**, **1/2 cup Bisquick**, **4 eggs**, **1/4 tsp salt**, and **1/8 tsp pepper**—in a blender. Cover and blend at high speed for **1 minute**.
4. Pour the blended mixture into the pie plate over the bacon, cheese, and onion.
5. Bake for **50 to 55 minutes** until golden brown and a knife inserted in the center comes out clean.
6. Let stand **5 minutes** before cutting into **6 servings**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Can be baked ahead, refrigerated, and reheated gently in the oven before serving.
