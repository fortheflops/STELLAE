# Verlene's Apple Puffs

> 📜 **Collection:** Georgia Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Verlene | 📂 **Category:** Breakfast
> *Delicious apple-spiced muffins made with applesauce and Bisquick, finished with a sweet cinnamon-sugar coating. Perfect for a quick and comforting morning treat.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 12 mins | 5 mins | 12 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Muffin tin
* Cupcake liners

## Ingredients

### Puffs
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 cups | [[Bisquick]] | Dry baking mix |
| 1/2 cup | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 1 tsp | [[Cinnamon]] | Ground |
| 1 | [[Egg]] | Slightly beaten |
| 1/4 cup | [[Milk]] | None |
| 2 tbsp | [[Oil]] | None |
| 1 cup | [[Applesauce]] | None |
| 1 tsp | [[Vanilla]] | X-tra touch |

### Topping
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/4 cup | [[Margarine]] | Melted |
| 1/2 cup | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 1 tsp | [[Cinnamon]] | Ground |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Batter Preparation
1. Combine dry ingredients including **2 cups Bisquick**, **1/2 cup sugar**, and **1 t. cinnamon**.
2. Add remaining ingredients including **1 egg, slightly beaten**, **1/4 cup milk**, **2 T. oil**, **1 cup applesauce**, and **1 t. X-tra touch vanilla**, and beat well until smooth.

### Step 2: Baking and Topping
3. Fill cupcake papers **2/3 full** with the batter.
4. Bake for **12 min.** at **350°** until golden and set.
5. Cool slightly before handling.
6. Stir together **1/2 c. sugar** and **1 t. cinnamon** in a small bowl.
7. Dip tops of muffins in **1/4 c. margarine, melted** and then roll in the **sugar & cinnamon** mixture.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store baked puffs in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 2 days, or freeze without the topping and apply fresh after reheating.
