# Frittata

> *A flavorful stovetop frittata loaded with fresh zucchini, corn, green chiles, and a rich blend of melted cheeses. Perfectly finished under the broiler for a golden, bubbling top.*

## Instructions

### Sautéing Vegetables
1. Melt **2 tbsp butter** in a 10-inch oven-safe skillet over medium heat.
2. Add **1 zucchini** (quartered lengthwise and sliced 1/2-inch thick), **1/4 cup green onions**, and **1/3 to 1/2 cup green chiles**. Cook and stir for about 8 minutes until vegetable liquid has evaporated.
3. Stir in **1 cup corn** and remove the vegetable mixture from heat.

### Assembly & Cooking
4. In a mixing bowl, beat **6 eggs** with **1/2 tsp salt**.
5. Mix in the cooked zucchini mixture and half of the cheese (half of the **3/4 cup Monterey Jack Cheese** and half of the **1 cup sharp cheddar cheese**).
6. Pour the egg and vegetable mixture back into the 10-inch skillet.
7. Cover and cook over medium-low heat for about 10 minutes, or until the eggs are set.
8. Top with the remaining **3/4 cup Monterey Jack Cheese** and **1 cup sharp cheddar cheese**, then place under the broiler until the cheese is melted, golden, and bubbling.
