## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation
1. Preheat oven to **375°**. Paper line muffin cups.
2. Prepare topping by combining **1/2 c. sugar**, **1/3 c. flour**, **1/2 t. cinnamon**, and **1/4 c. cold margarine**. Stir until crumbly and set aside.

### Step 2: Batter and Baking
3. Cream **1/3 c. sugar** and **1/4 c. margarine** together. Add **1 egg** and mix well.
4. Combine **2 1/3 c. flour** and **1 T. + 1 t. baking powder**. Add dry ingredients alternately with **1 c. milk**.
5. Stir in **1 t. vanilla** and **1 t. blueberry flavor**.
6. Spoon the batter into the prepared muffin cups.
7. Sprinkle the crumbly topping over the batter.
8. Bake for **25-30 minutes** until golden brown and a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean. Remove from pan.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store baked muffins in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 3 days, or freeze for longer storage.
