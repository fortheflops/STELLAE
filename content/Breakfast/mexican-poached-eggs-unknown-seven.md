# Mexican Poached Eggs

> *A flavorful Tex-Mex style breakfast dish featuring layered corn tortillas, seasoned mashed avocado, homemade green chile tomato sauce, and perfectly poached eggs topped with melted Monterey Jack cheese.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Make the Tomato Sauce
1. In a saucepan, heat **3 tbsp olive oil** over medium heat. Add **1 chopped onion** and **2 cloves garlic**; cook until soft.
2. Add **3 peeled and chopped tomatoes**, **3 cans green chiles**, **2 tsp oregano**, **2 tsp coriander**, and **1 1/2 tsp salt**.
3. Simmer for 20 to 30 minutes until the sauce thickens and flavors meld.

### Step 2: Prepare the Avocado Mixture
4. In a bowl, combine **2 large mashed avocados**, **1/4 cup minced red onion**, **3 tbsp lime juice**, **3 tbsp olive oil**, and **2 tsp salt**.
5. Mix well to combine and set aside.

### Step 3: Assemble and Bake
6. Arrange **6 shallow 6-inch baking dishes** on a baking sheet. Preheat oven to 450°F.
7. Quickly dip **12 corn tortillas** into hot oil to soften.
8. Place 1 softened tortilla in the bottom of each dish. Spread each with a layer of the **avocado mixture** and add **2 tbsp tomato sauce**.
9. Layer a second softened tortilla on top and spread again with the **avocado mixture**.
10. In a pot of gently simmering water, poach **6 eggs** for 2 minutes until whites are set but yolks remain runny. Carefully place one poached egg on top of each layered dish.
11. Spoon the remaining **tomato sauce** over each dish and top generously with **Monterey Jack cheese**.
12. Bake at 450°F for 1 to 2 minutes until the cheese is melted and bubbling.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The tomato sauce and avocado mixture can be prepared several hours in advance. Assemble, poach eggs, and bake just before serving.
