# Muesli

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** New Age Health Foods + Granery | 📂 **Category:** Breakfast
> *A wholesome, fiber-rich traditional muesli blend featuring an assortment of rolled grains, crunchy nuts, and sweet dried fruits. Perfect for serving with cold milk or creamy yogurt for a hearty breakfast.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | None | None | 20–25 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Very large mixing bowl
* Airtight storage container

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 Pound | [[Oats]] | Rolled |
| 1/2 Pound | [[Rye Flakes]] | Dry |
| 1/2 Pound | [[Wheat Flakes]] | Dry |
| 1/2 Pound | [[Wheatgerm]] | Unroasted |
| 1 Pound | [[Honey-Almond Crunch]] | Crunchy style |
| 10 Ounces | [[Almonds]] | Sliced |
| 1/2 Pound | [[Cashews]] | Unsalted |
| 1/2 Pound | [[Walnuts]] | Shelled |
| 2 Pounds | [[Raisins]] | Dried |
| 1/4 Pound | [[Brown Sugar]] | To taste |
| 1 to 1 1/2 Pounds | [[Millet]] | Whole grain |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Combine and Mix
1. In a very large bowl or container, combine the **1/2 pound rolled oats**, **1/2 pound rye flakes**, **1/2 pound wheat flakes**, **1/2 pound unroasted wheatgerm**, **1 pound honey-almond crunch**, **10 ounces sliced almonds**, **1/2 pound unsalted cashews**, **1/2 pound shelled walnuts**, **2 pounds raisins**, **1/4 pound brown sugar**, and **1 to 1 1/2 pounds millet**.
2. Mix thoroughly until all ingredients are evenly distributed throughout the blend.

### Step 2: Store and Serve
3. Transfer the mixture into an airtight container for long-term storage.
4. Serve as a cold cereal with milk or yogurt until well incorporated and satisfying.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 3 months.
