# Jam Coffee Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** Georgia Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Dept Water / Power | 📂 **Category:** Breakfast
> *A quick and delightful 1950s coffee cake featuring a sweet jam-infused batter topped with a spiced sugar crumble. Baked in a jelly roll pan, this simple treat is ready to enjoy in just 10 minutes.*

## Instructions

### Preparation & Baking
1. Preheat oven to **450°F**.
2. Combine the batter ingredients: **2 cups biscuit mix**, **1 egg**, **1 cup milk**, and **1/2 cup jam** in a mixing bowl until well combined.
3. Pour the batter into a greased **10 1/2 x 15-inch jelly roll pan**.
4. In a separate bowl, blend the topping ingredients: **1/2 cup brown sugar**, **1/4 cup white sugar**, **1 tsp cinnamon**, and **1 tsp nutmeg**.
5. Sprinkle the spiced sugar topping evenly over the top of the batter.
6. Bake for **10 minutes** at **450°F** until the edges are golden brown and the center is set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Best served warm fresh from the oven. Leftovers can be stored in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 2 days and reheated briefly before serving.
