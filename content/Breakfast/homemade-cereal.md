# Homemade Cereal

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Breakfast
> *A wholesome and crunchy homemade cereal loaded with oats, wheat germ, coconut, and honey. Baked to golden perfection and tossed with sweet raisins for a delightful morning treat.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 30 mins | None | 10 servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Cookie sheet

## Ingredients

### Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 4 cups | [[Oatmeal]] | Dry |
| 1/2 cup | [[Wheat Germ]] | Dry |
| 1/2 cup | [[Bran Flakes]] | Dry |
| 1 cup | [[Coconut]] | Flaked or shredded |
| 1/2 cup | [[Water]] | Wet |
| 1/2 - 3/4 cup | [[Honey]] | Wet |
| 1/2 cup | [[Sesame Seeds]] or [[Sunflower Seeds]] | Seeds |
| 1/2 cup | [[Nuts]] | Chopped |
| 1/2 - 1 cup | [[Raisins]] | Added after baking |

---

## Instructions

### Baking Instructions
1. Mix **4 cups oatmeal**, **1/2 cup wheat germ**, **1/2 cup bran flakes**, **1 cup coconut**, **1/2 cup sesame seeds or sunflower seeds**, and **1/2 cup nuts** in a large bowl, keeping the **1/2 - 1 cup raisins** aside.
2. Combine **1/2 cup water** and **1/2 - 3/4 cup honey** to create the wet mixture, then add to the dry ingredients and mix well.
3. Spread the mixture evenly on **2 ungreased cookie sheets**.
4. Bake in a preheated oven at **350 degrees F** for **15-30 minutes**, stirring occasionally until golden and toasted.
5. Remove from the oven, let cool completely, and mix in the **1/2 - 1 cup raisins**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to two weeks.
