# Christmas Breakfast Casserole

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Breakfast
> *A delightful and hearty make-ahead breakfast casserole packed with bulk sausage, sharp cheese, and bread cubes. Perfect for holiday mornings and festive family gatherings.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 45 mins | 8 hours | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Skillet
* Paper towels
* Mixing bowl
* 9x13" Casserole dish
* Oven

---
## Ingredients

### Casserole Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 lb | [[Bulk sausage]] | Could use lean or hot |
| 2 cups | [[White bread cubes]] | Crusts cut off |
| 8 | [[Eggs]] | None |
| 1 tsp | [[Salt]] | None |
| 1/2 tsp | [[Pepper]] | None |
| 1 (13 oz) can | [[Evaporated milk]] | None |
| 1 cup | [[Milk]] | None |
| 2 cups | [[Sharp cheese]] | Grated |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and Assembly
1. Cook **1 lb bulk sausage** in a skillet and crumble; drain thoroughly on paper towels.
2. Beat **8 eggs**, **1 (13 oz) can evaporated milk**, and **1 cup milk** slightly in a mixing bowl. Add **2 cups white bread cubes**, **1 tsp salt**, **1/2 tsp pepper**, and **2 cups sharp cheese**.
3. Pour the mixture into a greased **9x13" casserole** dish.
4. Cover and refrigerate overnight (make Christmas Eve).

### Step 2: Baking
5. Preheat oven to **350°F**.
6. Bake for **45 mins** or until firm and golden brown.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole completely the night before, cover, and refrigerate overnight before baking in the morning.
