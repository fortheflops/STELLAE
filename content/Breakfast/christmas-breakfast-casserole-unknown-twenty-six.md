# Christmas Breakfast Casserole

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Breakfast
> *A delightful make-ahead Christmas breakfast casserole featuring savory sausage, crustless white bread, eggs, and sharp cheddar cheese. Perfect for holiday mornings, this comforting dish is assembled the night before and baked until golden and firm.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 45 mins | 8 hours | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Skillet
* Paper towels
* Mixing bowl
* 9x13" Casserole dish
* Baking dish

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 lb | [[Bulk sausage]] | Could use lean hot |
| 2 cups | [[White bread cubes]] | Crusts cut off |
| 8 | [[Eggs]] | Large |
| 1/2 tsp | [[Salt]] | None |
| 1/2 tsp | [[Pepper]] | None |
| 13 oz can | [[Evaporated milk]] | Plus additional milk |
| 1 cup | [[Milk]] | None |
| 2 cups | [[Sharp grated cheese]] | Sharp |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and Assembly
1. Cook the **1 lb bulk sausage** and crumble it, then drain on paper towels.
2. In a mixing bowl, beat the **8 eggs**, **13 oz can evaporated milk**, **1 cup milk**, **1/2 tsp salt**, and **1/2 tsp pepper** slightly.
3. Add the other ingredients including **2 cups white bread cubes** and **2 cups sharp grated cheese**, then pour into a **9x12" casserole** dish.
4. Cover and refrigerate overnight.

### Step 2: Baking
5. Preheat oven to 350°F.
6. Bake for **45 mins** or until firm and set in the center.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole completely the night before, cover, and refrigerate overnight before baking in the morning.
