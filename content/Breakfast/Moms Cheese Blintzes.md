# Mom's Cheese Blintzes

> 📜 **Collection:** JC | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Breakfast
> *A classic recipe for homemade cheese blintzes, featuring delicate crepes filled with a simple dry cottage cheese mixture. Fry them in butter until golden brown and serve immediately.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 25 mins | 20 mins | None | 12-15 blintzes |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Cheesecloth or fine-mesh sieve
* Mixing bowls
* Wire whisk
* Frying pan or crepe pan
* Skillet

## Ingredients

### For the Cheese Filling
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 (16 oz) package | [[Cottage Cheese]] | Dry, drained |
| 1 | [[Egg]] | Large |
| 2 tablespoons | [[Butter]] | Melted |
| 1/2 teaspoon | [[Salt]] | None |

### For the Crepes (Dough)
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] | Beaten |
| 1/2 cup+ | [[Water]] | Plus more if needed |
| 1/2 cup | [[All-Purpose Flour]] | None |
| 1 pinch | [[Salt]] | None |
| As needed | [[Butter]] | For frying |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Make the Filling and Batter
1. Place the **1 (16 oz) package dry [[Cottage Cheese]]** in a cheesecloth or fine-mesh sieve and press to remove any excess liquid.
2. In a bowl, mix the squeezed cheese, **1 [[Egg]]**, **2 tablespoons [[Butter]]**, and **1/2 teaspoon [[Salt]]** until very well combined. Set aside.
3. In another bowl, whisk together the **2 [[Eggs]]**, **1/2 cup+ [[Water]]**, **1/2 cup [[All-Purpose Flour]]**, and **1 pinch [[Salt]]** until you have the consistency of a thin paste.

### Step 2: Cook the Crepes
4. Heat a lightly buttered frying pan or crepe pan over medium heat.
5. Stir the batter, then pour about **1/4 cup** into the hot pan, swirling to create a thin, round crepe.
6. Cook for **1-2 minutes** until the top appears dry and the bottom is lightly browned (crepes are traditionally only cooked on one side).
7. Remove the crepe from the pan and stack on a plate. Repeat with the remaining batter, stirring the batter and re-buttering the pan for each crepe.

### Step 3: Assemble and Fry
8. Place a cooked crepe on a flat surface, cooked side up. Place about **1 tablespoon** of the cheese filling near one edge.
9. Fold in the sides, then roll up tightly like a small burrito.
10. In a clean skillet, melt additional **[[Butter]]** over medium heat. Fry the completed blintzes on both sides until golden brown and the filling is hot. Serve immediately.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Unfried assembled blintzes can be layered with parchment paper and stored in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 2 days or frozen for up to 1 month.
