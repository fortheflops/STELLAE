# Aspen Square Griddle Cakes

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty One | ✍️ **Attribution:** Aspen Square Condominium Hotel | 📂 **Category:** Breakfast
> *Enjoy the classic taste of the mountains with these light and fluffy griddle cakes from the Aspen Square Condominium Hotel. Made with a rich base of sour milk and sour cream, they offer a delightful tang and tender crumb.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 15 mins | None | 4 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Griddle
* Whisk

---
## Ingredients

### Batter Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 1/2 cups | [[Flour]] | Sifted |
| 1 1/4 tsp | [[Baking Soda]] | Standard |
| 2 cups | [[Sour Milk]] | Room temperature |
| 1 | [[Egg]] | Separated |
| As needed | [[Sour Cream]] | For richness |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing and Griddling
1. Beat the **1 egg** white until stiff peaks form and set aside.
2. Beat the **2 cups sour milk** and **sour cream** together in a large mixing bowl.
3. Combine the **1 1/2 cups flour** and **1 1/4 tsp soda**, then gently fold into the liquid mixture along with the beaten egg white until just combined.
4. Pour batter onto a hot, greased griddle and cook until bubbles form on the surface and edges look set, then flip and cook until golden brown.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Batter is best used immediately, but dry ingredients can be pre-measured ahead of time.
