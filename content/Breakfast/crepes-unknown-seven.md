## Instructions

### Preparation & Cooking
1. Heat a pan over medium heat.
2. Whisk together **2 cups flour**, **1/2 tsp salt**, **3 eggs**, **2 cups milk**, and **1 tsp oil**. Gradually add **water** until the batter reaches the thin consistency required for crepes.
3. Pour a thin layer of batter into the preheated pan. If the crepe sticks or burns onto the pan, cool the pan, scrape off any burnt residue, thin the batter slightly with **water**, and resume cooking.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Batter can be prepared and chilled in the refrigerator for up to 24 hours. Thin with a splash of water or milk before cooking if it thickens.
