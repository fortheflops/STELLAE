# Lazy Morning Breakfast

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Virginia Lujan | 📂 **Category:** Breakfast
> *A comforting, make-ahead breakfast casserole layered with seasoned croutons, sharp cheddar, savory sausage, and eggs. Perfect for lazy mornings when you want a hearty, delicious meal ready with minimal effort.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 1 hour 30 mins | 8 hours | 8 to 10 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 9x12 baking pan
* Mixing bowls

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3 cups | [[Herb Seasoned Croutons]] | None |
| 2 cups | [[Sharp Cheddar]] | grated |
| 1 1/2 lbs | [[Lean Sausage]] | Jimmy Dean, browned and drained |
| 4 | [[Eggs]] | slightly beaten |
| 3 cups | [[Milk]] | divided |
| 1 cup | [[Cream of Mushroom Soup]] | None |
| Optional | [[Green Chili]] | to taste |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Assembly
1. Put the first 3 ingredients (**3 cups herb seasoned croutons**, **2 cups sharp cheddar**, and **1 1/2 lbs lean sausage**) in layers in order given in a greased **9x12 pan**.
2. Mix the **4 slightly beaten eggs** in **2 1/2 cups milk** and pour over the other ingredients.

### Step 2: Overnight Storage & Baking
3. Refrigerate overnight.
4. Mix **1 cup cream of mushroom soup** with the remaining **1/2 cup milk** and pour over the top.
5. Bake covered for **1 1/2 hours at 350 degrees**, let set until firm and golden, then serve.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole completely and refrigerate overnight before baking the next morning.
