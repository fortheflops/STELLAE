# Egg Casserole

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty One | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Breakfast
> *A comforting make-ahead egg casserole layered with bread, ground ham, cheese, and vegetables. Perfect for a hearty family breakfast or brunch gathering.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 1 hour | 8 hours | 6-8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 9x13 baking dish
* Mixing bowl

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 8 slices | [[Bread]] | remove crusts |
| 2 cups | [[Ground ham]] | None |
| 1 cup | [[Grated cheese]] | None |
| 2 tbsp | [[Prepared mustard]] | None |
| 3 | [[Eggs]] | beaten |
| 2 cups | [[Milk]] | None |
| To taste | [[S&P & herbs]] | None |
| 1 | [[Onion]] | chopped |
| 1 | [[Green pepper]] | chopped |
| 1 cup | [[Mushrooms]] | chopped |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Assembly
1. In a **9 x 13 greased dish**, lay **8 slices bread** in the bottom.
2. Spread a mixture of **2 cups ground ham**, **1 cup grated cheese**, **2 tbsp prepared mustard**, **1 onion**, **1 green pepper**, and **mushrooms** over the bread.
3. Mix **3 beaten eggs**, **2 cups milk**, and **S&P & herbs**, then pour the mixture over the layers.

### Step 2: Baking
4. Refrigerate overnight.
5. Bake at **325°F** for **1 hour** until set and golden brown.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole completely and refrigerate overnight before baking the next morning.
