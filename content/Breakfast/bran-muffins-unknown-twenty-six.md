# Bran Muffins

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Jean Williams (Gwen Frame) | 📂 **Category:** Breakfast
> *A delightful recipe for wholesome bran muffins loaded with raisins and buttermilk. Perfect for preparing ahead of time with a batter that stores in the refrigerator for weeks.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing Base
1. Cream **1 cup sugar** and **1/2 cup shortening**. Add **2 eggs** one at a time.
2. Add **2 1/2 cups flour**, **2 1/2 tsp baking soda**, and **1/2 tsp salt**, then add **2 cups buttermilk**. Mix until smooth.

### Step 2: Soaking and Combining
3. Pour **1 cup boiling water** over **1 cup 100% bran**, let stand until absorbed and cooled.
4. Blend this mixture into batter. Add **3/4 cup raisins** and **2 cups All Bran**, and mix.

### Step 3: Baking
5. Refrigerate covered up to 5 weeks.
6. Scoop batter without stirring. Bake in greased tins for **20 minutes** at **400 degrees** until golden brown and cooked through.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The prepared batter can be stored covered in the refrigerator for up to 5 weeks.
