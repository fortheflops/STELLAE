# Codfish Omelet

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Breakfast
> *A savory and traditional codfish omelet featuring a delicate blend of shredded salt cod and tender potatoes. This light yet satisfying dish is folded with whipped egg whites for a fluffy texture.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 20 mins | None | 2 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Omelet pan
* Pot
* Mixing bowl

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 cup | [[Salt Cod]] | Finely shredded |
| 1/2 cup | [[Potatoes]] | Thinly sliced |
| 1 tbsp | [[Butter]] | For cooking |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Pepper]] | Ground |
| 3 | [[Egg Yolks]] | Separated |
| 3 | [[Egg Whites]] | Separated and whipped |
| As needed | [[Boiling Water]] | For boiling |

---

## Instructions

### Preparation and Cooking
1. Finely shred the **1/2 cup salt cod** and add the **1/2 cup potatoes** (thinly sliced).
2. Cook the mixture in **boiling water** until the potatoes are tender, then drain thoroughly.
3. Add the **1 tbsp butter** to the mixture and mash until combined.
4. Season with **1/4 tsp pepper**, mix in the **3 egg yolks**, and gently fold in the **3 egg whites**.
5. Turn the mixture into a greased omelet pan and cook over a slow fire until the eggs are set.
6. Fold the omelet, turn onto a hot platter, and serve at once.
