# Cottage Cheese Blintzes

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Breakfast
> *A traditional recipe for thick, pancake-style blintzes made with a rich cottage cheese batter. Each blintz is filled with fresh cottage cheese and served warm with sour cream and strawberries.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 20 mins | None | 10-12 blintzes |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large mixing bowl
* Whisk or electric mixer
* Non-stick skillet or griddle
* Spatula

---
## Ingredients

### For the Batter
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 4 | [[Eggs]] | Large |
| 1 cup | [[Cottage Cheese]] | Sieved or blended smooth |
| 1 cup | [[Sour Cream]] | Standard |
| 1 cup | [[Flour]] | All-purpose, sifted |
| 1 Tbsp | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| ½ tsp | [[Salt]] | Standard |
| As needed | [[Butter]] | For the griddle |

### For Filling & Topping
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 cup | [[Cottage Cheese]] | For filling |
| As needed | [[Sour Cream]] | For serving |
| As needed | [[Strawberries]] | Fresh, for serving |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Make the Batter
1. In a large bowl, beat the **4 [[Eggs]]** well.
2. Add **1 cup** of sieved or blended **[[Cottage Cheese]]** and **1 cup** of **[[Sour Cream]]**. Beat until completely combined.
3. Add the sifted **1 cup** of **[[Flour]]**, **1 Tbsp** of **[[Sugar]]**, and **½ tsp** of **[[Salt]]**. Beat the batter until it is smooth and thick.

### Step 2: Cook the Blintzes
4. Heat a griddle or non-stick skillet over medium heat and melt a small amount of **[[Butter]]**.
5. Pour the batter to create pancakes of your desired size. Cook for **2-3 minutes** per side, until golden brown and cooked through.

### Step 3: Fill and Serve
6. As soon as you remove each blintz from the griddle, while it is still hot, place a heaping teaspoon of **[[Cottage Cheese]]** in the center.
7. Roll it up and place on a serving plate.
8. Top the warm, filled blintzes with **[[Sour Cream]]** and fresh **[[Strawberries]]** to serve immediately.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Cooked blintzes without filling can be stored in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 2 days; reheat gently before filling.
