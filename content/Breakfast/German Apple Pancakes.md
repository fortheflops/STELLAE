# German Apple Pancakes

> *Tender, crepe-style pancakes filled with shredded apple and topped with a sweet cinnamon-sugar mixture.*

## Ingredients

### For the Batter
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 cup | [[Flour]] | Sifted |
| 1 ½ cups | [[Milk]] | None |
| 4 | [[Eggs]] | Large |
| 2 ½ cups | [[Apples]] | Coarsely shredded, from 3 large apples |
| ⅓ cup | [[Lemon Juice]] | None |
| 2 tsp | [[Sugar]] | For batter |
| ½ tsp | [[Salt]] | None |
| 2 to 3 Tbsp | [[Butter]] | Or oleo, for frying |

### For the Topping
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| ½ cup | [[Sugar]] | None |
| 1 ½ tsp | [[Cinnamon]] | Ground |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Make the Batter
1. In a large bowl, sift together the **1 cup flour**, **½ tsp salt**, and **2 tsp sugar**.
2. Gradually add the **1 ½ cups milk**, stirring constantly with a whisk to make a smooth batter.
3. Add the **4 eggs** one at a time, beating thoroughly after each addition.

### Step 2: Prepare Apples and Topping
4. In a separate bowl, toss the **2 ½ cups apples** with the **⅓ cup lemon juice** to prevent browning, then gently fold them into the batter.
5. In a small bowl, combine the **½ cup sugar** and **1 ½ tsp cinnamon** for the topping and set aside.

### Step 3: Cook and Serve
6. Heat some of the **2 to 3 Tbsp butter** in a heavy 6-inch skillet over low to medium-low heat.
7. Pour **⅓ cup** of the batter into the pan. Brown slowly on one side until golden brown; turn and brown the other side until cooked through. These will take longer to cook than regular pancakes.
8. Keep the finished pancakes hot in a warm oven while you cook the rest of the batter, adding more butter to the skillet as needed.
9. Sprinkle the hot pancakes generously with the **cinnamon-sugar mixture** before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftover cooked pancakes in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 2 days and reheat gently in a skillet.
