# Blueberry Streusel Coffee Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Claire Barnett | 📂 **Category:** Breakfast
> *A delightful blueberry streusel coffee cake featuring a spiced brown sugar crumb topping and a tender, moist crumb. Perfect for morning gatherings or an afternoon treat.*

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 35 mins | None | 9 servings |

* Mixing bowl

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation
1. Heat oven to **400°**. Grease an **8x8x2-inch pan**.
2. Drain the **blueberries** from the **1 pkg. Betty Crocker blueberry muffin mix**.

### Step 2: Make Streusel
3. Mix **1/3 c. brown sugar**, **1/3 c. flour**, **1/2 tsp. cinnamon**, and **1/4 c. butter or margarine** together until crumbly.

### Step 3: Batter and Baking
4. Mix **1/2 c. dairy sour cream** and **1 slightly beaten egg**, stir in **1/4 c. milk**.
5. Stir in **1 pkg. Betty Crocker blueberry muffin mix** just till moistened.
6. Spread half of the batter in the prepared pan, sprinkle with half of the streusel.
7. Spread remaining batter, add the drained blueberries to the batter last rather than sprinkling over the top, and sprinkle with remaining streusel.
8. Bake till golden brown, **30-35 mins**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Bake fresh for best results, or store tightly wrapped at room temperature for up to 2 days.
