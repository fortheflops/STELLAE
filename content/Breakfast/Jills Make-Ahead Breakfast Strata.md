# Jill's Make-Ahead Breakfast Strata

> 📜 **Collection:** JillCollection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Breakfast
> *A classic make-ahead breakfast or brunch casserole featuring cubed white bread and cheddar cheese soaked in an egg custard, then baked until puffed and golden. Perfect for effortless morning entertaining.*

---

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 1 hour | None | 6–8 servings |

* Knife

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 8 to 10 slices | [[White Bread]] | Crusts removed, cubed |
| 3/4 pound | [[Cheddar Cheese]] | Shredded or cubed |
| 6 large | [[Eggs]] | Beaten |
| 2 cups | [[Milk]] | None |
| 3/4 teaspoon | [[Salt]] | None |
| 1 dash | [[Pepper]] | None |
| As needed | [[Butter]] | Or shortening, for greasing |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Mix Custard
1. Preheat oven to **325°F**. Generously grease a casserole dish with **butter** or shortening.
2. Remove the crusts from the **8 to 10 slices white bread** and cut the bread into 1-inch cubes.
3. In a mixing bowl, beat the **6 large eggs** well. Whisk in the **2 cups milk**, **3/4 teaspoon salt**, and **1 dash pepper**.

### Step 2: Layer and Bake
4. Create alternating layers in the prepared dish: start with a layer of **white bread** cubes, followed by a layer of **3/4 pound cheddar cheese**, and pour some of the **egg mixture** over the top.
5. Repeat the layers until all ingredients are used, ending with a layer of cheese. Gently press down on the top to help the bread absorb the custard.
6. Bake for **1 hour**, or until the casserole is puffed, golden brown, and a knife inserted in the center comes out clean.
7. Serve immediately.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** For a richer flavor, you can assemble the casserole, cover it, and refrigerate for a few hours or overnight before baking.
