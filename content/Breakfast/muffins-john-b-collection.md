# Muffins

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Winifred Murray | 📂 **Category:** Breakfast
> *Delight in these classic homemade muffins courtesy of Winifred Murray's collection. Lightly sweet and perfectly fluffy, they make a wonderful morning treat.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 20 mins | None | 12 medium muffins |

## Ingredients

### Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 cup | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 1/4 cup | [[Butter]] | Softened |
| 1 | [[Egg]] | Whole |
| 2 cups | [[All Purpose Flour]] | Sifted |
| 4 t. | [[Baking Powder]] | Standard |
| 1 cup | [[Milk]] | Liquid |
| 1/2 t. | [[Salt]] | Fine |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and Baking
1. Cream the **1/4 cup butter** and **1 cup sugar**, then beat in the **1 egg**.
2. Sift together the **2 cups sifted all purpose flour**, **4 t. baking powder**, and **1/2 t. salt**, alternating addition with the **1 cup milk** to form a smooth batter.
3. Fill greased muffin pans **2/3 full**.
4. Bake at 425° oven for **20 minutes** until golden brown and a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Baked muffins can be stored in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 2 days or frozen for up to 3 months.
