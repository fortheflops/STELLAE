# Overnight Sticky Buns

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** N. Lake | 📂 **Category:** Breakfast
> *Indulge in sweet, gooey homemade cinnamon-style rolls prepared the night before for an easy morning treat. Topped with pecans, brown sugar, and melted butter, they bake to golden perfection.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 30 mins | 8 hours | 12 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Bundt pan
* Measuring cups
* Wax paper

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 pkg. | [[Frozen Yeast Rolls]] | 2 dozen Bridgeford Parker House rolls |
| 1 small pkg. | [[Vanilla Pudding]] | Not instant |
| 1/2 cup | [[Brown Sugar]] | None |
| 1 stick | [[Butter]] | Melted |
| 1/2 cup | [[Pecans]] | Chopped |

---

## Instructions

### Assembly and Overnight Rise
1. Place **1 pkg. frozen yeast rolls** in a greased bundt pan.
2. Mix dry ingredients (**1 small pkg. vanilla pudding**, **1/2 cup brown sugar**, and **1/2 cup chopped pecans**) and sprinkle over the rolls.
3. Drizzle **1 stick melted butter** over the top.
4. Set on the counter, cover with wax paper, and let set overnight.

### Baking and Serving
5. Bake at 350°F for **30 minutes** until golden brown and bubbly.
6. Let set for **3-5 minutes**, then carefully turn out onto a plate while still warm and gooey.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the rolls in the bundt pan completely the night before, cover with wax paper, and let rise overnight on the counter.
