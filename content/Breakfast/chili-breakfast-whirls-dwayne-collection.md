# Chili Breakfast Whirls

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Breakfast
> *A savory and spicy breakfast pastry featuring a rich biscuit dough rolled with hot and regular pork sausage and chopped green chilies. These convenient make-ahead whirls can be frozen and baked fresh for any morning gathering.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 45 mins | 20 mins | 1 hour 30 mins | 50-60 whirls |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Rolling pin
* Plastic wrap
* Baking sheet
* Knife

---
## Ingredients

### Dough
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 4 cups | [[Biscuit Baking Mix]] | such as Bisquick |
| 1/2 cup | [[Oleo]] | melted |
| 1 cup | [[Milk]] | None |

### Filling
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 lb | [[Hot Pork Sausage]] | None |
| 1 lb | [[Pork Sausage]] | regular |
| 1 1/2 cups | [[Green Chilies]] | chopped |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Dough and Filling
1. In a mixing bowl, combine the **4 cups biscuit baking mix**, **1/2 cup melted oleo**, and **1 cup milk**. Mix thoroughly, then cover and refrigerate for **1/2 hour**.
2. Meanwhile, mix the **1 lb hot pork sausage**, **1 lb regular pork sausage**, and **1 1/2 cups chopped green chilies** together in a separate bowl. Let the mixture stand to reach room temperature.

### Step 2: Assemble and Freeze
3. Divide the chilled dough in half. Roll out each piece into a large rectangle about **1/4 inch** thick.
4. Spread half of the sausage and chili mixture completely and evenly over each piece of dough.
5. Roll up each piece tightly, jelly-roll style. Wrap securely in plastic wrap and freeze for exactly **1 hour**.
6. Remove from the freezer and slice into rounds about **1/3 inch** thick. Freeze the slices until ready to use.

### Step 3: Baking
7. Preheat your oven to **400°F**.
8. Place the frozen slices onto a cookie sheet and bake for **20 minutes** until the pastry is golden brown and the sausage is cooked through and sizzling.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** These whirls are designed to be frozen after slicing. Keep them in an airtight container in the freezer, then bake directly from frozen at 400°F for 20 minutes.
