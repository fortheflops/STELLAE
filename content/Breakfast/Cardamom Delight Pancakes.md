# Cardamom Delight Pancakes

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Breakfast
> *A delightful recipe for fluffy pancakes with the warm, aromatic flavor of cardamom. Made with a rich batter of yogurt and buttermilk, these pancakes are a special treat for breakfast or brunch.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mix Dry and Wet Ingredients
1. In a large bowl, whisk together the dry ingredients: **2 cups all-purpose flour**, **2 tsps baking powder**, **1 tsp baking soda**, **1 tsp ground cardamom**, and **½ tsp salt**.
2. In a separate medium bowl, beat the **2 eggs**. Whisk in the **¼ cup honey** and **¼ cup oil**, followed by the **1 cup yoghurt** (or sour cream) and **1 cup buttermilk**.

### Step 2: Combine and Cook
3. Pour the wet ingredients into the bowl with the dry ingredients and stir just until everything is lightly mixed. A few lumps in the batter are okay.
4. Heat a lightly oiled griddle or frying pan over medium-high heat. Pour or scoop the batter onto the griddle, using approximately **¼ cup** for each pancake.
5. Cook until bubbles appear on the surface and the edges are dry, then flip and cook until golden brown on the other side.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftover pancakes in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days or freeze for up to 2 months.
