# Morning Glory Muffins

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Batter
1. Preheat your oven to **325°F** and line two 12-cup muffin tins with paper liners.
2. In a large bowl, whisk together the dry ingredients: **2 cups flour**, **1 tsp baking soda**, **1 tsp cinnamon**, **1/2 tsp cloves**, and **1 tsp salt**.
3. In a separate bowl, combine the wet ingredients and sugars: **2 large eggs** (lightly beaten), **3/4 cup sugar**, **1/2 cup vegetable oil** (or applesauce), and **2 tsp vanilla**.
4. Stir the **2 cups peeled, diced apple** and **2 cups grated carrot or zucchini** directly into the wet mixture.
5. Gently fold the dry ingredients into the wet ingredients until just combined, then fold in the **1 cup raisins**.

### Step 2: Baking
6. Fill the prepared muffin cups **2/3 full** with the batter.
7. Bake at **325°F** for **25 minutes** or until a toothpick inserted in the center of a muffin comes out clean and the tops are lightly golden and springy to the touch.
