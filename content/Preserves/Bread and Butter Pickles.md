# Bread and Butter Pickles

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Preserves
> *Crisp and tangy homemade bread and butter pickles featuring a sweet spiced brine. Perfect for preserving your summer garden harvest.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 30 mins | 15 mins | 2 hours | 32 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large pan
* Pot
* Straining colander
* Canning jars

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 8 Quarts | [[Cucumbers]] | Sliced |
| 6 | [[Peppers]] | Sliced |
| 6 | [[Onions]] | Sliced |
| 1 Cup | [[Salt]] | For soaking |
| 2 Quarts | [[Vinegar]] | Standard |
| 9 Cups | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 3 Tbsp | [[Turmeric]] | Ground |
| 1 Tbsp | [[Mustard Seed]] | Whole |
| 1 Tbsp | [[Celery Seed]] | Whole |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Soak the Vegetables
1. In a large pan, soak the **8 quarts cucumbers**, **6 peppers**, and **6 onions** with **1 cup salt** for **2 hours** to draw out excess moisture.

### Step 2: Prepare the Brine and Combine
2. In a separate pot, combine the **2 quarts vinegar**, **9 cups sugar**, **3 tbsp turmeric**, **1 tbsp mustard seed**, and **1 tbsp celery seed**.
3. Bring the vinegar mixture to a rolling boil until the sugar completely dissolves.
4. Add the drained and soaked **cucumbers**, **peppers**, and **onions** to the boiling vinegar mixture.
5. Cook until the color changes to a vibrant yellow and the vegetables are heated through, then pack into sterile jars.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store sealed jars in a cool, dark place for at least 2 weeks before opening to allow the flavors to fully develop.
