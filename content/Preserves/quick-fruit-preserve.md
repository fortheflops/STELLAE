# Quick Fruit Preserve

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Preserves
> *A simple, traditional method for creating fruit preserves using a quick-boil technique. This recipe yields a flavorful, syrupy fruit base perfect for topping desserts or breakfast items.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5 mins | 9 mins | 24 hours | 4 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Saucepan
* Wooden spoon

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 4 cups | [[Fruit]] | Fresh, prepared |
| 2 cups | [[Vinegar]] | Standard |
| 4 cups | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |

---

## Instructions

### Cooking and Setting
1. In a saucepan, combine **4 cups fruit** and **2 cups vinegar**; bring to a boil.
2. Once boiling, cook for 3 minutes, then stir in **4 cups sugar**.
3. Continue to boil for an additional 6 minutes until the mixture thickens.
4. Remove from heat and let stand for 24 hours, stirring occasionally to ensure even consistency.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The preserve must stand for 24 hours after cooking to reach the proper consistency before serving.
