# Strawberry Preserves

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Ruth Schafer | 📂 **Category:** Preserves
> *A classic, simple recipe for homemade strawberry preserves that results in a thick, flavorful spread. This versatile method can also be adapted for peaches, apricots, or red raspberries.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 9 mins | 24 hours | 4 cups |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large pot
* Mixing bowl
* Stirring spoon

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 4 cups | [[Strawberries]] | Fresh |
| 4 cups | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 2 tbsp | [[Vinegar]] or [[Lemon Juice]] | Acidifier |

---

## Instructions

### Cooking and Setting
1. Add **2 tbsp vinegar or lemon juice** to the **4 cups strawberries** and bring to a rolling boil.
2. Boil for 3 minutes, then add the **4 cups sugar**, stirring constantly.
3. Bring the mixture back to a rolling boil and boil for an additional six minutes.
4. Remove from heat and pour into a bowl; let sit uncovered for 24 hours, stirring occasionally.
5. Seal the preserves while cold.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The preserves must sit uncovered for 24 hours at room temperature to set properly before sealing.
