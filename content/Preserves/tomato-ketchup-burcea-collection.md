# Tomato Ketchup

> 📜 **Collection:** Burcea Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Preserves
> *A classic homemade tomato ketchup recipe crafted from fresh tomatoes and onions. Slow-cooked and seasoned with vinegar, sugar, mustard, and spices for a rich, flavorful condiment.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 3 hours 30 mins | None | 12 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large pot
* Sieve
* Stirring spoon

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 pk (15 lb.) | [[Tomatoes]] | Fresh |
| 2 | [[Onions]] | Chopped |
| 1 1/2 c. | [[Vinegar]] | None |
| 1 1/2 cup | [[Sugar]] | None |
| 2 tbs. | [[Mustard]] | None |
| 2 tbs. | [[Salt]] | None |
| 1 tsp. | [[Red Pepper]] | Ground |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Initial Cooking and Sieving
1. Combine the **1 pk (15 lb.) tomatoes** and **2 onions** in a large pot and cook until tender.
2. Sieve the cooked tomato and onion mixture to remove seeds and skins.

### Step 2: Simmering and Seasoning
3. Cook down the sieved mixture for about 3 hours.
4. Add the **1 1/2 c. vinegar**, **1 1/2 cup sugar**, **2 tbs. mustard**, **2 tbs. salt**, and **1 tsp. red pepper**.
5. Continue to cook down until it reaches the proper ketchup consistency, stirring occasionally.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in sterilized jars in the refrigerator for up to several weeks, or process for long-term pantry storage.
