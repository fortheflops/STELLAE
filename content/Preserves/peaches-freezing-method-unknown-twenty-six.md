# Peaches Freezing Method

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Preserves
> *A simple method for preserving fresh peaches in the freezer using sugar and fruit fresh. Perfect for enjoying summer flavor all year round.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | None | Freezing time | 1 quart |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Freezer container

---
## Ingredients

### Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 qt | [[Peaches]] | Sliced or prepared |
| 1/2 c | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 1 T | [[Fruit Fresh]] | Color protector |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation
1. Combine the **1 qt peaches**, **1/2 c sugar**, and **1 T fruit fresh** together in a bowl, mixing gently until the sugar is dissolved and the fruit is well coated.
2. Pack into freezer containers and freeze until solid.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Can be stored in the freezer for up to 8-12 months.
