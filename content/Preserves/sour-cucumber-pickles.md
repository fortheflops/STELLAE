# Sour Cucumber Pickles

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Preserves
> *A classic, simple recipe for crisp sour cucumber pickles that require no additional water-bath processing. This traditional method uses a brine of cider vinegar and salt to achieve the perfect tangy flavor.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 10 mins | None | 6 quarts |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Scrub brush
* Large pot
* Sterilized jars

---
## Ingredients

### Brine and Produce
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 6 quarts | [[Cucumbers]] | Small, washed and brushed |
| 1/3 gallon | [[Cider Vinegar]] | None |
| 1/2 gallon | [[Water]] | None |
| 3 tbsp | [[Salt]] | None |
| To taste | [[Spices]] | Optional |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and Brining
1. Wash the **6 quarts cucumbers** thoroughly with a brush and dry them.
2. In a large pot, heat together the **1/3 gallon cider vinegar**, **1/2 gallon water**, and **3 tbsp salt** until the mixture reaches a boil.
3. Drop the **6 quarts cucumbers** into the boiling liquid, remove from heat, and let stand for a few minutes, ensuring they do not boil further.

### Step 2: Packing and Sealing
4. Place the **6 quarts cucumbers** into hot sterilized jars.
5. Pour the hot **cider vinegar** brine over the cucumbers, add optional **spices** if desired, and seal the jars immediately.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in a cool, dark place; the pickles will continue to develop flavor over several weeks.
