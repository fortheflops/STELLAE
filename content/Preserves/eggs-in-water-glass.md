## Instructions

### Step 1: Preservation
1. Prepare a solution of **water glass** according to manufacturer instructions.
2. Carefully place the **6 dozen eggs** into a clean crock or jar.
3. Pour the **water glass** solution over the **eggs** until they are completely submerged, ensuring no air reaches the shells.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This is a long-term preservation method; eggs can be stored in the solution for several months in a cool, dark place.
