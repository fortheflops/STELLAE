# Crushed Strawberry Jam

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Preserves
> *Preserve the taste of summer with this traditional crushed strawberry jam recipe. Simple ingredients come together for a sweet, spreadable treat.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 30 mins | 1 min | 5 mins | 10 6-oz. glasses |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large saucepan
* Jelly glasses
* Coarse food chopper

---
## Ingredients

### Jam Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 4 cups (2 lbs) | [[Prepared strawberries]] | Coarsely chopped |
| 7 cups (3 lbs) | [[Granulated sugar]] | Granulated |
| 1/2 - 6-oz. bottle | [[Liquid pectin]] | Liquid |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and Boiling
1. Cut about **2 qts washed, fully ripe strawberries** through a coarse blade food chopper.
2. Measure **4 cups prepared fruit** and **7 cups gran. sugar** into a large saucepan.
3. Mix well, then bring to a full, rolling boil over high heat, stirring constantly before and while boiling.
4. Boil hard for **1 min**.

### Step 2: Pectin and Jarring
5. Remove from heat, stir in **1/2 - 6-oz. bottle liquid pectin**.
6. Stir and skim off foam for **5 min**.
7. Skim off any floating fruit, then pour quickly into clean jelly glasses to within 1/2 inch of top.
8. Cover top at once with a **1/8 layer of hot paraffin**.
9. When cool, cover with lids.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in a cool, dark place for up to one year. Once opened, keep refrigerated.
