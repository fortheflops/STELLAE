## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Soak
1. Remove seeds from the **3 large green peppers** and **1 red pepper**, then chop all peppers, **3 large tomatoes**, and **3 large onions**.
2. Cover the chopped vegetables with boiling water, let stand for **15 minutes**, and drain. Repeat this process 2 or 3 times to remove bitterness and soften the vegetables.

### Step 2: Simmer and Can
3. Mix together the drained vegetables, **3/4 cup vinegar**, **1 1/4 cups sugar**, a little **mustard seed**, a little **celery seed**, and **1 tbsp salt**.
4. Boil for **15 minutes** until well combined and slightly thickened, then pack into sterile jars and can.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store sealed canned jars in a cool, dark place for several months. Once opened, keep refrigerated.
