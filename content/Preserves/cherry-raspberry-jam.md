# Cherry-Raspberry Jam

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Preserves
> *A classic homemade fruit preserve combining sweet cherries and tart raspberries. This jam is cooked until thickened and sealed with paraffin for long-term storage.*

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3 cups | [[Raspberries]] | Crushed |
| 3 cups | [[Sweet Cherries]] | Pitted |
| 1/2 cup | [[Water]] | None |
| 2 tbsp | [[Lemon Juice]] | None |
| 5 cups | [[Sugar]] | None |
| As needed | [[Paraffin]] | Melted, for sealing |

---

## Instructions

### Cooking and Sealing
1. Combine **3 cups crushed raspberries** and **3 cups pitted sweet cherries** in a preserving kettle.
2. Add **1/2 cup water** and **2 tbsp lemon juice** to the kettle.
3. Cook the mixture until the **cherry skins** are tender.
4. Stir in **5 cups sugar** and continue to cook until the mixture thickens to a jam consistency.
5. Pour the hot jam into hot sterilized glasses and seal the tops with hot melted **paraffin**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store sealed glasses in a cool, dark place for up to one year.
