# Jalapeno Jelly

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Preserves
> *A classic sweet and spicy pepper jelly made with fresh jalapenos, green bell pepper, and Certo pectin. Perfect for serving over cream cheese with crackers or as a glaze for meats.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 10 mins | 2 hours | 6 half-pint jars |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Blender
* Large saucepan
* Measuring cups
* Canning jars with lids

## Ingredients

### Blender Mixture
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 cup | [[Jalapenos]] | chopped |
| 1 | [[Green Bell Pepper]] | chopped |
| 3/4 cup | [[White Vinegar]] |  |

### Jelly Base
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 cup | [[White Vinegar]] |  |
| 5 to 6 cups | [[Granulated Sugar]] |  |
| 6 oz | [[Certo Liquid Pectin]] |  |
| A few drops | [[Green Food Coloring]] | optional |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Blend the Peppers
1. Place the **1 cup chopped jalapenos**, **1 chopped green bell pepper**, and **3/4 cup white vinegar** into a **blender**.
2. Process until the mixture is very fine and uniform.

### Step 2: Boil and Set
3. In a large saucepan, combine the remaining **1 cup white vinegar** and **5 to 6 cups granulated sugar**.
4. Bring the mixture to a boil over medium-high heat.
5. Stir in the blended pepper mixture and boil rapidly for **3 to 5 minutes**, stirring occasionally.
6. Remove the saucepan from the heat ('fire').
7. Stir in the **6 oz Certo liquid pectin** and a **few drops of green food coloring** if a brighter green color is desired.
8. Pour the hot jelly immediately into sterilized **jars**, then allow to cool completely and seal.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store sealed jars in a cool, dark place for up to 1 year. Once opened, keep refrigerated and consume within 3 weeks.
