# Pickled Peaches or Pears

> 📜 **Collection:** GC | ✍️ **Attribution:** Ann Seranne | 📂 **Category:** Preserves
> *A classic home-preserving recipe for spiced pickled peaches or pears infused with cinnamon, cloves, and allspice. Perfect for adding a sweet and tangy touch to your pantry collection.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 30 mins | 10 mins | None | Standard Canning Yield |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large pot
* Spice bag
* Sterilized jars
* Fork

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 7 pounds | [[Peaches]] or [[Pears]] | Firm-ripe |
| As Needed | [[Cloves]] | Whole |
| 4 cups | [[Cider Vinegar]] | None |
| 6 cups | [[Sugar]] | None |
| 1 tablespoon | [[Stick Cinnamon]] | Broken |
| 1 tablespoon | [[Allspice]] | Whole |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep the Fruit and Spices
1. Peel the **7 pounds peaches or pears**. Stick **4 whole cloves** into each piece of fruit.
2. Tie the **1 tablespoon broken stick cinnamon** and **1 tablespoon whole allspice** in a spice bag.

### Step 2: Boil the Syrup
3. In a large pot, combine the **4 cups cider vinegar** and **6 cups sugar**.
4. Add the spice bag to the pot and bring the mixture to a boil.

### Step 3: Cook and Pack
5. Drop the fruit into the boiling syrup one piece at a time, ensuring the liquid continues to boil.
6. Cook for about **10 minutes**, or until the fruit can be pierced easily with a fork.
7. Discard the spice bag, then carefully pack the hot fruit into hot, sterilized jars.
8. Fill the jars to overflowing with the boiling syrup and seal the jars immediately until cool and secure.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store sealed jars in a cool, dark place for up to a year; refrigerate after opening.
