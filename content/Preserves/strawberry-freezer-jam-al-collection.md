# Strawberry Freezer Jam

> 📜 **Collection:** Al Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Preserves
> *A quick and easy no-cook strawberry freezer jam made with fresh strawberries and liquid pectin. Perfect for preserving the sweet taste of summer with minimal effort and no boiling required.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 0 mins | 24 hours | 4 cups |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Measuring cups
* Measuring spoons
* Mixing bowl
* Potato masher
* Storage containers with lids

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 pints | [[Strawberries]] | washed, stemmed, and crushed to yield exactly 2 cups of mash |
| 4 cups | [[Sugar]] | granulated |
| 1 pouch (3 oz) | [[Liquid Pectin]] | such as Certo brand |
| 2 Tbsp | [[Lemon Juice]] | freshly squeezed |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep the Fruit
1. Wash **2 pints strawberries**. Discard stems and crush the fruit using a potato masher. Measure out exactly **2 cups crushed strawberries** (do not use extra or the jam may not set properly).

### Step 2: Mix with Sugar
2. Stir **4 cups sugar** into the **2 cups crushed strawberries** in a large bowl. Mix well, then let stand for **10 minutes**, stirring occasionally to help dissolve the sugar.

### Step 3: Prepare Pectin and Combine
3. In a separate measuring cup, stir **1 pouch (3 oz) liquid pectin** into **2 Tbsp lemon juice**.
4. Stir the pectin mixture into the strawberry mixture. Stir constantly until the **4 cups sugar** is completely dissolved and no longer grainy, about **3 minutes**.

### Step 4: Jar and Set
5. Pour the jam into prepared containers, leaving **1/2 inch of headroom** at the top to allow for expansion during freezing. Cover with tight-fitting lids.
6. Let stand at room temperature for **24 hours** until set and firm.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in the refrigerator for up to 3 weeks, or freeze for up to 1 year. Thaw frozen jam in the refrigerator before using.
