# Chow Chow

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Preserves
> *A traditional southern-style chow chow relish featuring a crisp medley of green tomatoes, cabbage, and peppers. This heritage preserve offers the perfect balance of tangy and sweet flavors.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 45 mins | 30 mins | None | 4-5 quarts |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large preserving pan
* Spice bag or cheesecloth
* Canning jars

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1.5 qt | [[Green Tomatoes]] | Chopped |
| 1.5 qt | [[Cabbage]] | 3/4 large head, chopped |
| 1.5 qt | [[Onions]] | 6 large, chopped |
| 1/2 qt | [[Sweet Peppers]] | 2 extra large or 3, chopped |
| 4 | [[Hot Peppers]] | Minced |
| 1 Tbsp | [[Salt]] | Plus extra for drawing moisture if needed |
| As needed | [[Sugar]] | Equal parts with water and vinegar |
| As needed | [[Water]] | Equal parts with sugar and vinegar |
| 1.5 qt | [[Vinegar]] | Equal parts with sugar and water |
| 2 1/2 | [[Mixed Spices]] | Whole, tied in a spice bag |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Combine and Simmer
1. Mix the **1.5 qt green tomatoes**, **1.5 qt cabbage**, **1.5 qt onions**, **1/2 qt sweet peppers**, **4 hot peppers**, and **1 Tbsp salt** together in a large preserving pan.

### Step 2: Add Liquids and Spices
2. Cover the vegetable mixture with equal parts of **sugar**, **water**, and **vinegar** (using about **1 1/2 quarts of vinegar**).
3. Place the **2 1/2 whole mixed spices** into a cloth bag, tie securely, and submerge in the pot with the mixture.

### Step 3: Cook and Jar
4. Cook the mixture over medium heat until the vegetables are tender and well-infused.
5. Remove the spice bag when done, and pack the hot relish into sterilized jars until tender and fragrant.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store processed jars in a cool, dark place for several weeks to let the flavors mature before serving.
