# Eggplant Relish

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Joe Everett & Gail | 📂 **Category:** Preserves
> *A traditional marinated eggplant relish layered with garlic, parsley, peppers, and olive oil. Perfect for serving as a savory hors d'oeuvre after maturing in the refrigerator.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Salt and Press the Eggplant
1. Peel **five smallish eggplants or three large ones** and cut them into **one-inch slices**.
2. Put **lots of salt** on each slice as you put them in layers in a bowl. Let stand for about eight hours or so.
3. Mash each slice in the palm of your hand and squeeze until flat.

### Step 2: Boil and Drain
4. Combine **two cups of water** and **two cups of wine vinegar** and bring to a boil.
5. Drop in the **eggplant slices** and boil for **three to six minutes** until tender when tested with a fork; drain.

### Step 3: Layer and Marinate
6. Chop **six cloves of garlic** fine. Chop **parsley** fine. Cut **red or green pepper** into fine strips. If desired, include the **seed of a red chili pepper**.
7. Arrange the cooked **eggplant** in layers in plastic containers, placing the chopped **garlic**, **parsley**, **peppers**, and **chili seed** on each layer.
8. Cover each layer completely with **olive oil**.
9. Keep in the refrigerator and let stand for about two weeks or so for best results until fully infused and flavorful.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store sealed in the refrigerator and let stand for about two weeks for best results before serving.
