# Watermelon Rind Pickles

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Preserves
> *Traditional Southern-style pickled watermelon rinds spiced with cloves and cinnamon. These sweet and tangy preserves are a delightful way to reduce kitchen waste.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 30 mins | 45 mins | Overnight | 6–8 Pint Jars |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large container
* Large pot
* Spice bag
* Sterilized jars

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| As needed | [[Watermelon Rind]] | Green skin removed, cut into 1-inch cubes |
| 4 Tablespoons | [[Salt]] | For the brine |
| 1 Quart | [[Water]] | For the brine |
| 4 Cups | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 2 Cups | [[Vinegar]] | Standard pickling or white vinegar |
| 4 Teaspoons | [[Cloves]] | Whole |
| 8 Sticks | [[Cinnamon]] | Whole sticks |
| A little | [[Mustard Seed]] | To taste |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Soak
1. Peel the tough, outer green skin from the **[[Watermelon Rind]]**. Cut the remaining white rind into **one-inch cubes**.
2. In a large container, create a brine by dissolving **4 tablespoons** of **[[Salt]]** into **1 quart** of **[[Water]]**.
3. Soak the **[[Watermelon Rind]]** cubes in the salt water overnight until ready for the next step.

### Step 2: Boil the Rind
4. After soaking, drain the **[[Watermelon Rind]]** and transfer it to a large pot. Cover the rind with fresh water.
5. Bring the water to a boil and cook the rind until it is almost tender and yields slightly to pressure. Drain the water completely.

### Step 3: Make the Syrup and Pack
6. To make the pickling syrup, combine **4 cups** of **[[Sugar]]** and **2 cups** of **[[Vinegar]]** in a separate pot.
7. Place the **4 teaspoons** of whole **[[Cloves]]**, **8 sticks** of **[[Cinnamon]]**, and **a little** **[[Mustard Seed]]** into a spice bag and add it to the sugar and vinegar mixture.
8. Bring the syrup and spices to a boil, then let it set for **15 minutes** to infuse.
9. Add the drained **[[Watermelon Rind]]** to the syrup and cook until the rind becomes clear, transparent, and tender.
10. Carefully pack the hot pickles into sterilized jars and seal them immediately.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store sealed jars in a cool, dark place for up to a year; once opened, keep refrigerated and consume within one week.
