# Pickled Pears

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Ann Seranne | 📂 **Category:** Preserves
> *Delightful spiced pickled pears preserved in a sweet syrup, adapted from The Complete Book of Home Preserving. This vintage recipe yields tender, translucent fruit infused with warm spices.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 30 mins | 45 mins | Overnight | 6–8 Jars |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large pot
* Candy thermometer
* Spice bag
* Canning jars

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3 pounds | [[Pears]] | Firm, left whole |
| 4 cups | [[Sugar]] | None |
| 3 cups | [[Vinegar]] | None |
| 5 cups | [[Water]] | None |
| 1 piece | [[Ginger Root]] | None |
| 1 tablespoon | [[Whole Spices]] | Mixed |
| 1 stick | [[Cinnamon]] | Plus extra for jars |
| A few | [[Cloves]] | Whole |
| Grated rind of 1 | [[Lemon]] | Grated rind |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare Syrup and Spices
1. Peel the **3 pounds pears** but leave them whole.
2. In a large pot, make a syrup by combining the **4 cups sugar**, **3 cups vinegar**, and **5 cups water**.
3. Tie the **1 piece ginger root**, **1 tablespoon mixed whole spices**, **1 stick cinnamon**, and **a few whole cloves** in a spice bag. Add the spice bag to the syrup.

### Step 2: Cook and Steep
4. Bring the syrup to a boil, then add the whole pears and the **grated rind of 1 lemon**.
5. Cook until the pears are tender and clear, and the syrup registers 222°F on a candy thermometer.
6. Let the fruit stand in the syrup overnight until fully cooled and infused.

### Step 3: Pack and Seal
7. The next day, remove the spice bag.
8. Pack the pears into jars, adding an extra stick of fresh cinnamon to each jar.
9. Bring the syrup back to a boil, pour the hot syrup over the fruit in the jars, and seal immediately.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store sealed jars in a cool, dark place for several months; refrigerate after opening.
