# Chutney

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Preserves
> *A classic, robust homemade chutney featuring a blend of garden-fresh vegetables and fruits. This versatile preserve is cooked down to a thick, flavorful consistency perfect for pairing with meats or cheeses.*

### 🔪 Key Equipment

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3 lbs | [[Ripe Tomatoes]] | chopped |
| 3 lbs | [[Apples]] | peeled and chopped |
| 3 | [[Green Peppers]] | diced |
| 3 | [[Large Onions]] | chopped |
| 3 cups | [[Sugar]] | granulated |
| 2 tsp | [[Ginger]] | ground |
| 2 tsp | [[Mustard]] | ground |
| 1 tbsp | [[Salt]] | none |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Cayenne]] | ground |
| 1 1/2 cups | [[Raisins]] | none |
| 2 cups | [[Vinegar]] | cider or white |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Simmering
1. Combine the **3 lbs ripe tomatoes**, **3 lbs apples**, **3 green peppers**, and **3 large onions** in a large heavy-bottomed pot.
2. Stir in the **3 cups sugar**, **2 tsp ginger**, **2 tsp mustard**, **1 tbsp salt**, **1/4 tsp cayenne**, **1 1/2 cups raisins**, and **2 cups vinegar**.
3. Bring the mixture to a gentle boil, then reduce heat and simmer, stirring frequently to prevent scorching.
4. Continue to cook until the mixture reaches a thick, jam-like consistency and the liquid has reduced significantly.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in sterilized jars in a cool, dark place; flavor improves after aging for 2-4 weeks.
