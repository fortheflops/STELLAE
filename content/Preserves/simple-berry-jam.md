# Simple Berry Jam

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Preserves
> *A simple, rustic berry jam recipe that requires minimal ingredients and a slow setting process. Perfect for spreading on toast or pairing with morning pastries.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5 mins | 9 mins | 24 hours | 4 cups |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Saucepan
* Wooden spoon

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 4 cups | [[Berries]] | Fresh or frozen |
| 2 tbsp | [[Vinegar]] | Standard white or apple cider |
| 4 cups | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Initial Boil
1. Combine **4 cups berries** and **2 tbsp vinegar** in a saucepan.
2. Boil for **3 minutes** until the berries begin to break down.

### Step 2: Sweetening and Setting
3. Stir in **4 cups sugar** and bring the mixture to a boil.
4. Boil for **6 minutes** while stirring constantly.
5. Remove from heat and leave uncovered for **24 hours**, stirring occasionally to ensure proper set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This jam needs to sit uncovered for 24 hours to set properly before being transferred to jars for storage.
