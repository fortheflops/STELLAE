# Heinz Pickles

> 📜 **Collection:** Burcea Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Preserves
> *Discover a traditional recipe for homemade Heinz pickles cured in salt brine and spiced vinegar. This heirloom method creates exceptionally crisp and flavorful pickles perfect for storage.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Initial Brining
1. Put **100 pickles** in **salt brine** strong enough to hold up an egg.
2. Allow them to stand for **9 days**.
3. After **9 days**, put them in cold water for several hours.

### Step 2: Alum Boil and Soaking
4. Put the pickles in a kettle with **3 qts. of water** and **1 heaping tsp. powd. alum**, and let boil for **6 min**.
5. After that, put pickles through cold water so they get cooled off.

### Step 3: Vinegar Soak
6. Then put pickles in a stone jar again, cover pickles with **cold vinegar**, and let stand again for **9 days**.
7. After that, put them into fruit jars; in each jar put **1 dry red pepper** (from mixed spices).

### Step 4: Final Syrup and Bottling
8. To each cup of **sugar** add **1 c. vinegar**, **1/2 tsp. essence of cloves**, and **1/2 tsp. cinnamon**.
9. Boil, let stand to cool, before putting on pickles.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store sealed jars in a cool, dark place. Let mature for a few weeks before opening for best flavor.
