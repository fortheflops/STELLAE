# Pickled Okra

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Svarale | 📂 **Category:** Preserves
> *Discover a traditional vintage recipe for crisp pickled okra featuring garlic, hot pepper, and dill seed. Preserved to perfection after six weeks of curing.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 15 mins | 6 weeks | 1 batch |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large stockpot
* Canning jars with lids
* Measuring cups
* Measuring spoons

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 Button | [[Garlic]] | Peeled |
| 1 | [[Hot Pepper]] | Whole |
| 1 Tablespoon | [[Dill Seed]] | Whole |
| 1/2 Gallon | [[Vinegar]] | Standard |
| 1/2 Gallon | [[Water]] | Fresh |
| 1 Cup | [[Salt]] | Canning or pickling salt |
| As needed | [[Okra]] | Fresh pods, washed and trimmed |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Brine
1. In a large stockpot, mix the **1/2 gallon vinegar**, **1/2 gallon water**, and **1 cup salt**.

### Step 2: Boil and Pour
2. Bring the brine mixture to a boil until the salt is completely dissolved.
3. Pack the **okra**, **1 button garlic**, **1 hot pepper**, and **1 tablespoon dill seed** tightly into sterilized jars.
4. Carefully pour the hot brine over the **okra** and spices until fully submerged.

### Step 3: Seal and Cure
5. Seal the jars securely and store in a cool, dark place for **6 weeks** until fully pickled and deeply infused with flavor.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store sealed jars in a cool, dark place for at least 6 weeks before opening. Once opened, keep refrigerated.
