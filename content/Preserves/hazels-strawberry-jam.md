# Hazel's Strawberry Jam

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Hazel | 📂 **Category:** Preserves
> *A classic, simple recipe for homemade strawberry jam that requires no pectin. This traditional method results in a rich, sweet spread perfect for breakfast toast or biscuits.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 10 mins | 24 hours | 4 jars |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large pot
* Jars
* Strainer

---
## Ingredients

### Jam Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 qt | [[Strawberries]] | Fresh, cleaned |
| 4 cups | [[Sugar]] | Divided into two 2-cup portions |
| 1 tsp | [[Butter]] | Optional, to prevent foaming |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and Initial Boil
1. Pour boiling water over the **1 qt strawberries** and drain well.
2. Add **2 cups sugar** to the berries and boil for 2 minutes.
3. Remove from heat and stir in the remaining **2 cups sugar**.
4. Boil for an additional 5 to 8 minutes until the mixture thickens slightly.

### Step 2: Setting and Storage
5. Let the mixture set for 24 hours to reach the proper consistency.
6. If desired, add a **little butter** to the mixture to prevent foam from forming during the boil.
7. Transfer the jam into sterilized jars and seal tightly.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The jam must sit for 24 hours after cooking to properly set before it is ready to be sealed in jars.
