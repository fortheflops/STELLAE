# Kumquat Marmalade

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Bernice | 📂 **Category:** Preserves
> *A classic, bright citrus marmalade featuring sliced kumquats and lemons. This recipe utilizes an overnight soaking method to ensure the fruit is perfectly tender before reaching the jelly stage.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 45 mins | 24 hours | 4 jars |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large pot
* Glass jars
* Measuring cup

---
## Ingredients

### Fruit Base
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 cups | [[Kumquats]] | Sliced |
| 2 | [[Lemons]] | Cut into small pieces |
| 5 cups | [[Water]] | For soaking |
| Equal parts | [[Sugar]] | Measured by volume to fruit mixture |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Maceration
1. Combine **2 cups kumquats**, **2 lemons**, and **5 cups water** in a pot and let stand overnight.

### Step 2: Cooking
2. Cook the fruit mixture for 30 minutes until the fruit is tender.
3. Let the mixture stand overnight again.
4. Measure the fruit mixture and add an equal amount of **sugar**.
5. Cook the mixture over medium heat until it reaches the jelly stage (when it sheets off a spoon).
6. Pour the hot marmalade into **glass jars** and seal while hot.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This marmalade can be stored in a cool, dark place for up to one year; once opened, keep refrigerated.
