## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Soak
1. Wash the **1 peck cucumbers** thoroughly.
2. Soak the **cucumbers** overnight in a mixture of **4 quarts water** and **1/2 cup salt**.

### Step 2: Prepare Brine and Pack Jars
3. In a large pot, bring **3 quarts water**, **1 quart vinegar**, and **1 cup salt** to a rolling boil.
4. Pack the soaked **cucumbers** tightly into clean canning jars.
5. Add **dill**, **1 clove garlic** (or **onion**), and **1/4 tsp. alum** to each jar.
6. Pour the boiling hot **brine** over the **pickles** in the jars, leaving appropriate headspace, and seal immediately.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store sealed jars in a cool, dark place. Allow the pickles to cure for at least 3 to 4 weeks before opening to develop full flavor.
