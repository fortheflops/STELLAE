# Pickled Beets

> 📜 **Collection:** Jill Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Preserves
> *Discover an extremely simple and quick method for making sweet pickled beets using convenient canned sliced beets. These refrigerator pickles marinate overnight for a tangy, crisp bite that elevates any meal.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 0 mins | 24 hours | 4 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Small bowl
* Jar with lid
* Can opener

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 can | [[Beets]] | Sliced |
| 1/2 cup | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 1/2 cup | [[Vinegar]] | Standard |
| As Needed | [[Onion]] | Sliced |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and Jarring
1. Drain the juice from the **1 can sliced beets** thoroughly.
2. Place the drained **sliced beets** into a clean jar.

### Step 2: Mixing the Brine
3. In a small bowl, stir the **1/2 cup sugar** and **1/2 cup vinegar** together until the sugar begins to dissolve.
4. Pour this sweet vinegar mixture directly over the beets in the jar.
5. Add a few slices of **onion** into the jar.

### Step 3: Sealing and Marinating
6. Seal the jar tightly and keep it in the refrigerator.
7. For best flavor, let them marinate for at least **24 hours** until well-chilled and infused.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This is a quick pickle recipe and is not intended for long-term shelf storage; always keep the pickles refrigerated and consume within a couple of weeks.
