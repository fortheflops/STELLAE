# Wine Jelly

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Joni | 📂 **Category:** Preserves
> *A delightful homemade wine jelly crafted with fruit wine and liquid pectin, sealed with paraffin wax for long-lasting preservation. This vintage preserve recipe makes a charming addition to any pantry.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 10 mins | None | 4 baby food jars |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Saucepan
* Sterilized jars

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 Cups | Fruit [[Wine]] | Blackberry, Apple, etc. |
| 3 Cups | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 1/2 Bottle | Certo (Liquid Pectin) | Standard size bottle |
| 1/8 to 1/4 Inch | [[Paraffin Wax]] | Melted, for sealing |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Dissolve and Heat
1. In a saucepan, dissolve **3 cups sugar** in **2 cups fruit wine**.
2. Heat the mixture until it is near boiling, which should take about **5-10 minutes**.

### Step 2: Add Pectin and Jar
3. Add **1/2 of a bottle of Certo (liquid pectin)** to the hot mixture.
4. Stir the pectin in completely until fully incorporated.
5. Pour the hot jelly mixture carefully into sterilized jars.

### Step 3: Seal and Cool
6. Top the jelly in each jar with **1/8 to 1/4 inches of melted paraffin wax** to seal securely.
7. Let cool and set completely until firm to the touch.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store sealed jars in a cool, dark place for up to a year; once opened, keep refrigerated and consume within a few weeks.
