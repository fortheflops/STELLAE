# Peach Pickles

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Preserves
> *Traditional spiced peach pickles made with firm cling peaches simmered in a sweet cinnamon and clove-infused cider syrup. A wonderfully spiced vintage preserve that pairs exceptionally well with roasted meats.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 45 mins | 1 hour | None | 12–15 Pint Jars |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large saucepan
* Canning jars
* Slotted spoon
* Paring knife

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 Peck | [[Cling Peaches]] | Ripe but firm |
| 3 lbs | [[Brown Sugar]] | Light |
| 1 Quart | [[Cider Vinegar]] | Standard |
| 2 oz | [[Cinnamon]] | Stick |
| As needed | [[Whole Cloves]] | For studding |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep the Fruit
1. Select **1 peck cling peaches** that are ripe but firm. Scald the fruit in hot water and remove the skins completely.
2. Stick **3 whole cloves** firmly into each prepared peach.

### Step 2: Make the Syrup and Cook
3. In a large saucepan, combine the **3 lbs light brown sugar**, **1 quart cider vinegar**, and **2 oz stick cinnamon** to make a syrup.
4. Bring the syrup to a rolling boil. Add enough prepared peaches at a time to comfortably fill a quart jar, ensuring not to crowd the pot.
5. Cook gently until the peaches are tender but not broken, showing a soft yet intact texture.

### Step 3: Process and Repeat
6. Remove the cooked peaches and pack into warm jars. Repeat the batch cooking process with remaining fruit until all **peaches** and **syrup** are used, taking care not to cook all at once so the fruit stays in one piece.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the sealed jars in a cool, dark place for at least 2 weeks before serving to allow the spices to fully penetrate the fruit.
