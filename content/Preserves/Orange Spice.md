## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Cut the Peel
1. Take the **thick orange skin** and carefully peel as much of the white pith off the inside as possible until clean.

### Step 2: Shape and Stud
2. Cut the prepared **orange peel** into $\frac{3}{4}$-inch square or decorative shapes.
3. Push a **whole clove** firmly into the center of each piece of **orange peel**.

### Step 3: Dry and Store
4. Let the spiced **orange peels** dry naturally in a well-ventilated area until completely hardened and fragrant.
5. Use the finished pieces to spice up tea or place them in a sachet to scent a closet or drawer.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store fully dried orange spice pieces in an airtight glass container in a cool, dark place indefinitely.
