## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Brine
1. In a large saucepan, boil **2 quarts of water** with **1/2 cup of salt**.
2. Add **1 cup of vinegar** to the salted water and stir to combine.
3. Remove from heat and allow the brine to cool completely until lukewarm or room temperature.

### Step 2: Prepare the Jars
4. In each pickling jar, place **1 clove of garlic**, **1 teaspoon of peppercorns**, **dill seeds** to taste, **1 bay leaf**, and **1 1/2 teaspoons of spices** (adjust spice quantities if using smaller jars like half-pints).
5. Pack the **small green tomatoes** tightly into the prepared jars.
6. Pour the cooled brine over the **tomatoes** in the jars, ensuring they are completely submerged and covered.

### Step 3: Cure and Store
7. Seal the jars tightly.
8. Let stand in a cool, dark place for **three weeks** before using until the tomatoes change color and develop a tangy flavor.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store sealed pickled tomatoes in a cool, dark place for up to several months; let cure for at least three weeks before opening.
