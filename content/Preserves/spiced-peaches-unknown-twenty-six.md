# Spiced Peaches

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Preserves
> *Delightful spiced peaches simmered in aromatic cinnamon and cloves, then chilled to perfection. This classic preserve is easily prepared using canned peach halves and simple pantry spices.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 15 mins | Overnight | 6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large sauce pan

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3 1/2 c. | [[canned peach halves]] | save juice |
| 6 inches | [[stick cinnamon]] | None |
| 1 t. | [[cloves]] | None |
| 1 T. | [[vinegar]] | None |
| 1 1/2 T. | [[sugar]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Method
1. In **large sauce pan**, combine **3 1/2 c. canned peach halves** including juice, **6 inches stick cinnamon**, **1 t. cloves**, **1 T. vinegar**, and **1 1/2 T. sugar**.
2. Heat until mixture boils, then simmer for a few minutes until fragrant.
3. Chill overnight and drain before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Keep chilled overnight before serving, and drain thoroughly just prior to plating.
