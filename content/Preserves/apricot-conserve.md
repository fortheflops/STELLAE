# Apricot Conserve

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Preserves
> *A delightful vintage preserve combining fresh apricots, oranges, lemons, raisins, and blanched almonds. This thick and fruity conserve yields multiple glasses of homemade goodness.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 30 mins | 1 hour | None | 18 glasses |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Preserving kettle
* Sterilized glasses

---
## Ingredients

### Conserve Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3 | [[Oranges]] | Peeled and sectioned, membranes removed |
| 4 lbs | [[Fresh Apricots]] | Washed, cut into halves, pits removed |
| 1 | [[Orange]] | Grated rind |
| 1/2 | [[Lemon]] | Juiced |
| 1 cup | [[White Raisins]] | None |
| 3 lbs | [[Sugar]] | None |
| 1 cup | [[Blanched Almonds]] | Chopped |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Cooking
1. Combine the **3 oranges**, **4 lbs fresh apricots**, grated rind of **1 orange**, juice of **3 oranges**, juice of **1/2 lemon**, **1 cup white raisins**, and **3 lbs sugar** in a preserving kettle.
2. Cook until thick, adding **1 cup blanched almonds** in the last 5 minutes until fragrant and incorporated.

### Step 2: Canning
3. Pour the hot conserve into hot sterilized glasses and seal.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store sealed glasses in a cool, dark place for up to a year.
