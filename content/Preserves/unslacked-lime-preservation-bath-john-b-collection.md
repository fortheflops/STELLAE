# Unslacked Lime Preservation Bath

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Preserves
> *A historic pickling and egg preservation mixture utilizing quicklime, salt, and boiling water. This foundational lime bath creates a alkaline environment suitable for traditional food preservation.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5 mins | 15 mins | 2 hours | 3 gallons |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large heat-safe crock or bucket
* Wooden spoon

---
## Ingredients

### Lime Solution
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 pt | [[Unslacked Lime]] | quicklime |
| 1/2 pt | [[Salt]] | None |
| 3 gal | [[Water]] | boiling |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing the Solution
1. Carefully place **1 pt unslacked lime** and **1/2 pt salt** into a large, non-reactive, heat-safe vessel.
2. Pour **3 gal boiling water** slowly over the mixture, stirring carefully with a wooden spoon until dissolved and fully slaked.
3. Allow the solution to settle and cool completely until the liquid is clear before using for preservation.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The cooled lime water can be prepared ahead and kept covered in a non-reactive container until needed.
