# Cinnamon Sugar Candied Pecans

> 📜 **Collection:** General Archive | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Snacks
> *A classic and addictive recipe for crunchy, sweet, and spiced candied pecans. These are perfect for snacking, gifting, or adding a delightful crunch to salads.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 45 mins | None | Approx. 3 cups |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* medium bowl
* large zip-top bag
* shallow baking pan
* spatula

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | [[Egg White]] | large |
| 1 Tablespoon | [[Water]] | cold |
| 1 Cup | [[Sugar]] | granulated |
| 1 Teaspoon | [[Cinnamon]] | ground |
| 1 Teaspoon | [[Salt]] | fine |
| 1 (10 Ounce) Package | [[Pecans]] | raw |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and Coating
1. Preheat your oven to 300°F (150°C) and prepare a shallow baking pan.
2. In a medium bowl, beat the **1 egg white** and **1 tablespoon water** together until the mixture is light and frothy.
3. In a separate bowl or large zip-top bag, combine the **1 cup sugar**, **1 teaspoon cinnamon**, and **1 teaspoon salt**.
4. Add the **10 ounce package pecans** to the egg white mixture and toss until thoroughly coated.
5. Transfer the coated nuts into the sugar mixture and toss until they are evenly covered in the spice blend.

### Step 2: Baking and Cooling
6. Spread the **pecans** in a single layer across your prepared baking pan.
7. Bake for **45 minutes**, ensuring you stir the nuts every **15 minutes** to prevent burning and achieve a golden brown, fragrant finish.
8. Remove from the oven and let cool completely; the **pecans** will become crisp as they reach room temperature.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to two weeks, or freeze for up to three months.
