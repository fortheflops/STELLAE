# Caramel Popcorn Balls

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Snacks
> *A classic, sweet and salty treat featuring crunchy popcorn and peanuts bound together by rich, melted caramel. Perfect for autumn gatherings, Halloween, or a nostalgic family snack.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 10 mins | 15 mins | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Saucepan
* Large mixing bowl
* Wooden spoon
* Wax paper

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 quarts | [[Popcorn]] | popped, un-popped kernels removed |
| 1 lb | [[Caramel]] | unwrapped caramels |
| 1/4 cup | [[Water]] | None |
| 1/4 cup | [[Peanuts]] | salted or unsalted |

---

## Instructions

### Preparation
1. In a medium saucepan over low heat, combine the **1 lb caramel** and **1/4 cup water**. Stir constantly until the caramels are completely melted, smooth, and warm.
2. In a large mixing bowl, combine the **2 quarts popcorn** and **1/4 cup peanuts**.
3. Pour the warm caramel mixture over the popcorn and peanuts, stirring quickly with a wooden spoon until everything is evenly coated.
4. Working quickly while the mixture is still warm but safe to touch, use lightly buttered hands to press and shape the mixture into firm, round balls. Let them cool on wax paper until they are firm and set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store popcorn balls individually wrapped in plastic wrap or wax paper at room temperature for up to 5 days.
