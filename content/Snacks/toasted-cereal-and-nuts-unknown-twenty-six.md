# Toasted Cereal and Nuts

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Snacks
> *A delightful and crunchy mix of cereals, nuts, and pretzels coated in seasoned butter and Worcestershire sauce. Perfect for gathering and easy to bake low and slow.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 1 hour 15 mins | None | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Baking pan
* Mixing bowl

---
## Ingredients

### Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/4 cup | [[Butter]] | Melted |
| 1/2 tsp | [[Worcestershire sauce]] | None |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Garlic salt]] | None |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Celery salt]] | None |
| 1 1/2 cups | [[Rice Krispies]] | None |
| 1 cup | [[Cheerios]] | None |
| 1 cup | [[Wheat Chex]] | None |
| 1 cup | [[Rice Chex]] | None |
| 2 cups | [[Pretzel sticks]] | None |
| 1/2 cup | [[Nuts]] | None |
| To taste | [[Salt]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing and Baking
1. Combine the **1/4 cup butter**, **1/2 tsp Worcestershire sauce**, **1/4 tsp garlic salt**, and **1/4 tsp celery salt**.
2. Toss with the **1 1/2 cups Rice Krispies**, **1 cup Cheerios**, **1 cup Wheat Chex**, **1 cup Rice Chex**, **2 cups pretzel sticks**, and **1/2 cup nuts** until evenly coated. Season with **salt** to taste.
3. Bake at a low temperature, cooking for **1 hour 15 mins**, making sure to **stir every 15 mins** until toasted and fragrant.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to two weeks.
