# Toasted Cereal and Nut Mix

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Snacks
> *A delightful and savory toasted snack mix combining cereal, pretzels, and nuts with seasoned butter. Perfect for gatherings, parties, or an easy homemade crunchy treat.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 1 hour | None | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Baking pan
* Mixing bowl
* Measuring cups
* Measuring spoons

## Ingredients

### Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/4 cup | [[Butter]] | Melted |
| 2 tbsp | [[W. Sauce]] | Worcestershire sauce |
| 1 tsp | [[Garlic Salt]] | None |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Celery Salt]] | None |
| 1 1/2 cups | [[Mix]] | Cereal mix |
| 1 cup | [[Cherries]] | Dried |
| 1 cup | [[Wh. Chex]] | Wheat Chex cereal |
| 1 cup | [[Cheese Cracker]] | Cheese crackers |
| 2 cups | [[Pretzel Sticks]] | Broken or whole |
| 1/2 cup | [[Nuts]] | Assorted nuts |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Baking
1. Preheat your oven to **250°**.
2. In a large bowl, combine the **1/4 cup butter**, **2 tbsp W. sauce**, **1 tsp garlic salt**, and **1/4 tsp celery salt**.
3. Add the **1 1/2 cups mix**, **1 cup cherries**, **1 cup wh. chex**, **1 cup cheese cracker**, **2 cups pretzel sticks**, and **1/2 cup nuts**, tossing gently to coat evenly.
4. Bake for **1 hour**, stirring every **15 mins** until toasted and fragrant.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to two weeks.
