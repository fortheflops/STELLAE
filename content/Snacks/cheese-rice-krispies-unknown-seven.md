# Cheese Rice Krispies

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Seven | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Snacks
> *A delightful and crunchy cheese snack ball recipe made with sharp grated cheese and crispy cereal. Perfect for gatherings and easily baked to golden perfection.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 12 mins | None | 6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Baking sheet
* Fork

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3 cups | [[Cheese]] | Sharp, grated |
| 1 cup | [[Margarine]] | Softened |
| 2 cups | [[Flour]] | Plus extra for dipping fork |
| 2 cups | [[Rice Krispies]] | Crispy rice cereal |
| As needed | [[Salt]] | For sprinkling |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing and Shaping
1. Cream the **1 cup margarine** and **3 cups sharp grated cheese** together until smooth.
2. Add the **2 cups flour** and mix well.
3. Fold in the **2 cups Rice Krispies** until evenly distributed.
4. Form the mixture into small balls and place them on a baking sheet.

### Step 2: Baking
5. Dip a fork in **flour** and press down on each ball to flatten slightly and create a pattern.
6. Bake at 375°F for **12 minutes** until set and lightly golden.
7. Sprinkle with **salt** while warm and let cool until crisp.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Unbaked dough balls can be refrigerated in an airtight container for up to 24 hours before baking.
