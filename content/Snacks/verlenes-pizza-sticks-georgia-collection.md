# Verlene's Pizza Sticks

> 📜 **Collection:** Georgia Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Verlene | 📂 **Category:** Snacks
> *A quick and delicious snack featuring refrigerated pizza dough topped with a savory blend of provolone, Romano, garlic, and basil. Baked to golden perfection, these easy pizza sticks are perfect for game day or a quick family treat.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 15 mins | None | 24 sticks |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Cookie sheet
* Pastry brush
* Pizza cutter
* Small bowl

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 oz. can | [[Pizza Crust]] | refrigerated dough |
| 1 tbsp. | [[Margarine]] | melted |
| 1/2 cup | [[Provolone Cheese]] | shredded |
| 1 tbsp. | [[Romano Cheese]] | grated |
| 1/4 tsp. | [[Garlic Powder]] |  |
| 1/8 tsp. | [[Red Pepper]] | crushed flakes or ground |
| 1 tsp. | [[Dried Basil]] | leaves |
| To serve | [[Pizza Sauce]] | such as X-tra Touch brand, warmed |

---

## Instructions

### Preparation & Baking
1. Preheat your oven to **425°F**.
2. On a lightly greased cookie sheet, unroll the **10 oz. can pizza crust** dough to form a 9x12-inch rectangle.
3. Brush the dough evenly with the **1 tbsp. margarine**.
4. In a small bowl, combine the remaining dry ingredients: **1/2 cup shredded Provolone cheese**, **1 tbsp. grated Romano cheese**, **1/4 tsp. garlic powder**, **1/8 tsp. red pepper**, and **1 tsp. dried basil leaves**. Sprinkle this cheese and spice mixture evenly over the dough.
5. Cut the dough crosswise into 12 1-inch strips. Cut those strips in half to make **24 strips** total.
6. Bake at **425°F** for **10-15 minutes** or until the cheese is melted and the crust is a beautiful golden brown.
7. Serve warm alongside warmed **pizza sauce** for dipping.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** These are best served fresh and warm from the oven. You can prep the cheese and spice mixture ahead of time and store it in an airtight container.
