# Orange Glazed Popcorn

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Snacks
> *A delightful treat featuring freshly popped corn coated in a sweet orange sugar syrup. Perfect for parties, movie nights, or a sweet afternoon snack.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 15 mins | None | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 1 1/2-quart pan
* Candy thermometer
* Wooden spoon
* Waxed paper
* Forks

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 8 cups | [[Popped corn]] | Unsalted |
| 4 cups | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 2/3 cup | [[Hot water]] | Hot |
| 1/3 cup | [[Orange juice]] | Fresh or bottled |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Salt]] | Standard |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Making the Syrup
1. Mix **4 cups sugar**, **2/3 cup hot water**, **1/3 cup orange juice**, and **1/4 tsp salt** in a **1 1/2-quart pan**.
2. Bring to a rolling boil, stirring often, and continue to boil until the soft-ball stage reaches **234 degrees**.
3. Remove from heat.

### Step 2: Coating and Cooling
4. Pour the hot syrup over **8 cups popped corn** in a pan, taking care not to scrape.
5. Stir and fold the syrup over the corn using a **wooden spoon** until the corn is well mixed and loses its stickiness.
6. Pour out onto **waxed paper** and separate kernels with **2 forks**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 3 days.
