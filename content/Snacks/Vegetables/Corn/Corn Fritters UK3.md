## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
| 1 Cup             | Canned Corn            |
| 5/8 Cup           | Flour                   |
| 1/2 Teaspoon      | Baking Powder           |
| 1/2 Teaspoon      | Salt                    |
| Few Grains         | Paprika                 |
| 1                   | Egg                     |
___
### Instructions
1. Chop Corn, Drain, Add Dry Ingredients Mixed And Sifted.
2. Add Egg Yolk, Beaten Until Thick.
3. Fold In Egg White Beaten Until Stiff.
4. Drop By Spoonful's And Fry In Deep Fat (390 Degrees).
5. Drain On Paper.
