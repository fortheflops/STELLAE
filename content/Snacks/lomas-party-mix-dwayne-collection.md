# Loma's Party Mix

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Coat
1. Preheat your oven to **275°F**.
2. In a large roasting pan, combine the **1/2 box Wheat Chex**, **1/2 box Rice Chex**, **1/2 box Cheerios**, **1 box pretzel sticks**, **1 cup pecans**, and **1 can peanuts**.
3. In a small mixing bowl, whisk together the **1/2 cup melted bacon fat**, **1 stick melted butter**, **1 tsp garlic salt**, **1 tsp celery salt**, **1 tsp seasoned salt**, **2 tbsp Tabasco sauce**, and **2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce** until fully combined.
4. Pour the seasoned sauce mixture evenly over the cereal and nut mixture in the roasting pan, tossing gently to ensure everything is evenly coated.

### Step 2: Slow Roast
5. Toast the mixture in the preheated **275°F** oven for **1 1/2 to 2 hours**.
6. Stir the mixture thoroughly every **30 minutes** to ensure even browning and prevent burning. Bake until the mix is completely dry, crisp, and highly fragrant.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the cooled party mix in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 2 weeks. It can also be frozen in freezer-safe bags for up to 3 months.
