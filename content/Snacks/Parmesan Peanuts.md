# Parmesan Peanuts

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail's Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Snacks
> *Crispy roasted peanuts tossed in warm peanut oil, savory garlic salt, and freshly grated Parmesan cheese. An easy, savory party snack perfect for entertaining.*

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 tbsp | [[Peanut Oil]] |  |
| 1 lb | [[Peanuts]] | salted |
| 1 tsp | [[Garlic Salt]] |  |
| 2 tbsp | [[Parmesan Cheese]] | grated |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Warm the Oil and Coat the Peanuts
1. Preheat oven to 350°F.
2. Add **2 tablespoons peanut oil** to a flat baking pan and place in the preheated oven for **5 minutes** until hot.
3. Carefully remove the pan from the oven, add **1 pound salted peanuts**, and stir until fully coated with the hot oil.

### Step 2: Roast and Season
4. Return the pan to the oven for about **5 minutes** until the peanuts are warmed through and fragrant.
5. Remove from the oven and evenly sprinkle with **2 tablespoons grated Parmesan cheese** and **1 teaspoon garlic salt**.
6. Stir thoroughly until the cheese and salt mixture coats all the peanuts.
7. Allow the peanuts to cool completely on the baking pan until crisp before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store cooled peanuts in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 1 week.
