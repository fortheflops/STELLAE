# Artichoke Nibblers

> 📜 **Collection:** Patsy's Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Snacks
> *A savory microwave-baked appetizer packed with marinated artichoke hearts, sharp cheddar cheese, and aromatic spices. Cut into bite-sized squares, these easy nibblers are perfect for parties or quick entertaining.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 13 mins | 10 mins | 24–36 Bite-Sized Squares |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 7x11-inch microwave-safe baking dish
* Waxed paper
* Small mixing bowl
* Wire whisk
* Chef's knife

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 jars (6 oz each) | Marinated [[Artichoke Hearts]] | drained, 1 jar marinade reserved, chopped |
| 1 small | [[Onion]] | finely chopped |
| 1 clove | [[Garlic]] | minced |
| 4 | [[Eggs]] | lightly beaten |
| ¼ cup | Fine dry [[Bread Crumbs]] | None |
| ¼ tsp | [[Salt]] | None |
| ¼ tsp | [[Pepper]] | None |
| ¼ tsp | [[Oregano]] | None |
| ¼ tsp | [[Tabasco]] | None |
| 2 cups | [[Cheddar Cheese]] | shredded |
| 2 Tbsp | [[Parsley]] | minced |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep Aromatics
1. Drain marinade from **1 jar (6 oz)** of **marinated artichoke hearts** directly into a 7x11-inch microwave-safe baking dish. Drain the second jar, reserving the marinade for other uses if desired. Finely chop all **artichoke hearts** and set aside.
2. Add **1 small finely chopped onion** and **1 clove minced garlic** to the marinade in the baking dish. Cover with waxed paper and microwave on high for **3 minutes**, stirring after **2 minutes**, until onion is tender and fragrant.

### Step 2: Combine and Microwave
3. In a small mixing bowl, lightly beat **4 eggs**. Add **¼ cup fine dry bread crumbs**, **¼ tsp salt**, **¼ tsp pepper**, **¼ tsp oregano**, and **¼ tsp Tabasco**. Whisk to combine.
4. Stir in **2 cups shredded cheddar cheese**, **2 Tbsp minced parsley**, and the chopped **artichoke hearts**.
5. Pour the egg and cheese mixture into the baking dish with the cooked **onion** and **garlic**, stirring until well blended. Spread evenly and recover with waxed paper.
6. Microwave on high for **8 to 10 minutes**, rotating the dish a half turn after **5 minutes**, until set when lightly pressed in the center. Let stand, covered, for **10 minutes** to finish setting.
7. Cut into 1-inch squares and serve warm.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Can be baked, cooled, and stored in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days. Reheat individual squares briefly in the microwave before serving.
