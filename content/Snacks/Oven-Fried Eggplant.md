# Oven-Fried Eggplant

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Two | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Snacks
> *Crispy oven-fried eggplant slices coated in a savory mixture of crushed soda crackers and Parmesan cheese bound with mayonnaise. Baked at high heat until golden and tender for a quick, flavorful snack or side dish.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 20 mins | None | 4 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Cookie sheet
* Shallow bowl or dish
* Butter knife or offset spatula
* Peeler

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 Medium | [[Eggplant]] | Peeled and cut into ½-inch thick slices |
| ¾ Cup | [[Soda Crackers]] | Crushed |
| ¼ Cup | [[Mayonnaise]] | None |
| ⅓ Cup | [[Parmesan Cheese]] | Grated |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Dredge
1. Trim and peel the **1 medium eggplant**, then cut into **½-inch thick slices**.
2. In a shallow dish or plate, thoroughly combine the **¾ cup crushed soda crackers** and **⅓ cup Parmesan cheese**.
3. Lightly spread **¼ cup mayonnaise** on both sides of each eggplant slice, then dredge each piece in the crumb and cheese mixture, coating both sides completely.

### Step 2: Bake
4. Arrange the coated slices in a single layer on an ungreased cookie sheet.
5. Bake at 420°F (215°C) for about **15 minutes** until the tops are lightly browned and the eggplant is tender.
6. Turn each slice over and bake for an additional **3 to 5 minutes** until the undersides are golden brown and crisp.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Best enjoyed immediately while warm and crisp. Leftovers can be stored in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 2 days and reheated in a 400°F oven or toaster oven to restore crispness.
