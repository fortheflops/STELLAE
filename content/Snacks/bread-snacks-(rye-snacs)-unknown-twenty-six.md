# Bread Snacks (Rye Snacs)

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Snacks
> *Crispy sliced French bread spread with garlic butter, mayonnaise, and sharp cheddar cheese, then baked to golden perfection. These savory baked snacks make a wonderful complement to any warm bowl of soup.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 10 mins | None | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Cookie sheet
* Mixing bowl
* Knife

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 loaf | [[French bread]] | Cut into 1/3-inch slices |
| As needed | [[Garlic butter]] | Softened |
| 2 cups | [[Cheddar cheese]] | Shredded |
| 1 cup | [[Mayonnaise]] | Spread |
| To taste | [[Dill weed]] | For sprinkling |
| Optional | [[Olives]] | Sliced |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation
1. Cut **1 loaf French bread** into 1/3-inch slices.
2. Spread **garlic butter** over the bread slices.
3. Mix together **2 cups cheddar cheese** and **1 cup mayonnaise**, then spread the mixture evenly on the bread.
4. Place the prepared slices on a cookie sheet and sprinkle with **dill weed** (and optional **olives** if desired).

### Step 2: Baking
5. Bake at 350° for **10 mins** until the cheese is melted and bubbling and the edges are golden brown and crisp.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the slices on the cookie sheet ahead of time and refrigerate for up to a few hours before baking.
