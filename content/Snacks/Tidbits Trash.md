# Tidbits Trash

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Three Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Snacks
> *A classic savory party snack mix packed with cereals, pretzels, pecans, and smoked bacon fat seasoning. Slow-roasted at low heat until crispy, crunchy, and deeply flavorful.*

* Large plastic bag
* Roaster pan

* Spatula

| 1 Box | [[Cheerios]] | None |
| 1 Box | [[Wheat Chex]] | None |
| 1 Box | [[Rice Chex]] | None |
| 1 Box | [[Stick Pretzels]] | None |
| 1 Bag | [[Pretzel Goldfish]] | Or oyster crackers |
| 2 Pounds | [[Pecans]] | Whole or halves |

### Savory Coating
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |

| 2 Cups | [[Bacon Fat]] | Rendered and melted |
| 4 Tablespoons | [[Worcestershire Sauce]] | None |
| 3 Tablespoons | [[Garlic Salt]] | None |
| 4 Tablespoons | [[Liquid Smoke]] | None |
| 1 Tablespoon | [[Salt]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Combine Dry Ingredients
1. In a large, clean plastic bag, toss together the **1 box Cheerios**, **1 box Wheat Chex**, **1 box Rice Chex**, **1 box stick pretzels**, **1 bag pretzel Goldfish**, and **2 pounds pecans** until uniformly combined.

### Step 2: Mix Coating and Coat
2. In a separate bowl, whisk together the melted **2 sticks butter** and **2 cups bacon fat**. Stir in the **4 tablespoons Worcestershire sauce**, **3 tablespoons garlic salt**, **4 tablespoons liquid smoke**, and **1 tablespoon salt** until fully emulsified.
3. Pour the warm sauce mixture over the dry ingredients inside the plastic bag. Seal the bag tightly and toss thoroughly until all cereals, pretzels, and nuts are completely coated.

### Step 3: Slow Bake
4. Preheat the oven to 250°F and pour the coated mixture evenly into a large roaster pan.
5. Bake at 250°F for a total cook time of **2 hours**. Turn the mixture with a spatula after the first **20 minutes**, and continue turning every **15 minutes** thereafter until evenly toasted, crisp, and fragrant.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 2 weeks.
