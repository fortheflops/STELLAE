# Oven-Baked Caramel Popcorn

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Snacks
> *A classic recipe for perfectly crisp and crunchy caramel popcorn. Popcorn is coated in a simple, boiled brown sugar caramel sauce and then baked low and slow in the oven for an irresistible, non-sticky finish.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Caramel
1. Preheat your oven to **200°F** and prepare a large ungreased cookie sheet.
2. In a medium saucepan, melt the **1/2 cup butter/margarine**. Add the **1 cup brown sugar**, **1/4 cup corn syrup**, and **1/2 teaspoon salt**.
3. Bring the mixture to a boil over medium heat, stirring constantly. Continue to boil for **5 minutes** until the mixture is smooth and bubbling.
4. Remove the sauce from the heat and immediately stir in the **1/2 teaspoon baking soda**; the mixture will foam up significantly.

### Step 2: Bake and Crisp
5. Pour the hot caramel sauce over the **3 quarts popped popcorn** and stir well with a large spatula until every kernel is evenly coated.
6. Spread the coated popcorn onto the cookie sheet in a single layer.
7. Bake for **1 hour**, stirring the popcorn every **15 minutes** to ensure even crisping and prevent burning.
8. Let the popcorn cool completely on the cookie sheet until crisp and firm, then break apart any large clumps.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to one week to maintain crunch.
