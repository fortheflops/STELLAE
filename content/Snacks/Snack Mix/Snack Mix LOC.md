### Dry Ingredients

| :--------------------- | :----------------- |

| $2 \frac{2}{3}$ Cups | [[Corn Chex]]      |
| 1 Cup                  | [[Nuts]]           |
| 1 Cup                  | [[Pretzels]]       |
### Seasoning Mixture:
| Measurements          | Ingredients                 |
| :-------------------- | :-------------------------- |
| $1 \frac{1}{2}$ sticks | [[Melted Butter]] (12 Tbsp) |
| 2 Tbsp                | [[Worcestershire Sauce]]    |
| $\frac{1}{2}$ Tbsp   | [[Onion Powder]]            |
| $\frac{1}{2}$ Tbsp   | [[Garlic Powder]]           |
| 1 tsp                 | [[Tabasco Sauce]]           |
___
## Instructions
1.  Mix the above Dry Ingredients and pour the following Seasoning Mixture over it
2.  Put in 225 oven for an hour, stirring every 10 min.
