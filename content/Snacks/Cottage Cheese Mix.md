## Instructions

### Step 1: Combine and Serve
1. In a small mixing bowl, place the desired amount of **cottage cheese**.
2. Add the finely chopped **parsley** and finely chopped **onion** to taste.
3. Mix thoroughly until all ingredients are evenly distributed, creamy, and fragrant.
