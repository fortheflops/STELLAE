# Corn Fritters

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Three | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Snacks
> *Golden deep-fried corn fritters made with sweet canned corn and lightened with whipped egg whites. Crisp on the outside and fluffy inside, they make a wonderful vintage snack or side dish.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Corn Base
1. Chop and thoroughly drain the **1 cup canned corn**.
2. In a bowl, mix and sift together the **5/8 cup flour**, **1/2 teaspoon baking powder**, **1/2 teaspoon salt**, and a **few grains paprika**, then stir into the chopped corn.

### Step 2: Incorporate Egg
3. In a small bowl, beat the **1 egg yolk** until thick and light, then blend it into the corn batter.
4. In another bowl, beat the **1 egg white** until stiff peaks form, then gently fold into the mixture.

### Step 3: Fry to Golden
5. Heat deep oil or fat to **390°F**.
6. Drop the batter by spoonfuls into the hot fat and fry until cooked through, puffed, and golden brown.
7. Remove with a slotted spoon and drain on paper towels before serving hot.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Best served immediately after frying for maximum crispness. The dry ingredients and corn can be prepared ahead, but fold in the beaten egg whites just before frying.
