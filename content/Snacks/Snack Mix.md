# Snack Mix

> 📜 **Collection:** Louise Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Snacks
> *A classic savory party mix featuring crisp Chex cereal, nuts, and pretzels tossed in a seasoned butter glaze. Slow-baked to crunchy, golden perfection with hints of Worcestershire and spice.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Season
1. Preheat your oven to **225°F**.
2. In a large roasting pan or mixing bowl, combine the dry ingredients: **2 ⅔ cups Rice Chex**, **2 ⅔ cups Wheat Chex**, **2 ⅔ cups Corn Chex**, **1 cup nuts**, and **1 cup pretzels**.
3. In a separate bowl, whisk together the seasoning mixture: **1 ½ sticks melted butter**, **2 Tbsp Worcestershire sauce**, **½ Tbsp onion powder**, **½ Tbsp garlic powder**, and **1 tsp Tabasco sauce** until well blended.
4. Pour the seasoning mixture evenly over the dry ingredients, tossing gently until all pieces are thoroughly coated.

### Step 2: Bake
5. Place the mixture into the preheated oven at **225°F** and bake for **1 hour**.
6. Stir thoroughly every **10 minutes** to ensure even baking and to prevent scorching.
7. Remove from the oven when the mix is light golden brown, toasted, and fragrant. Allow to cool completely before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 2 weeks.
