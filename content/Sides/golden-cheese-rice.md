# Golden Cheese Rice

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A comforting, savory side dish featuring a golden blend of tender carrots, fluffy rice, and melted cheese. This simple casserole is perfect for family dinners and pairs beautifully with roasted meats.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 40 mins | None | 6 servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Baking dish

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 1/2 cups | [[Carrots]] | Ground |
| 2 cups | [[Rice]] | Cooked |
| 8 oz | [[Cheese]] | Any variety, shredded |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] | Beaten |
| 1/4 cup | [[Milk]] | None |
| 1 | [[Onion]] | Grated |
| 1 tsp | [[Salt]] | None |
| 1/8 tsp | [[Pepper]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Assembly and Baking
1. Preheat your oven to 350°F.
2. In a large mixing bowl, combine the **2 1/2 cups ground carrots**, **2 cups cooked rice**, **8 oz cheese**, **2 beaten eggs**, **1/4 cup milk**, **1 grated onion**, **1 tsp salt**, and **1/8 tsp pepper**.
3. Transfer the mixture into a baking dish and bake for 40 minutes, or until the top is golden brown and the center is set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The mixture can be assembled and kept refrigerated for up to 24 hours before baking; add 5-10 minutes to the bake time if starting from cold.
