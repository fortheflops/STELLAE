# Savory Mushroom and Celery Bake

> 📜 **Collection:** Elizabeth Knee Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A simple and savory baked side dish featuring mushrooms, celery, and onions slow-cooked in a rich, buttery chicken broth. Perfect as a flavorful accompaniment to roasted meats or as a base for stuffing.*

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |

| 2 small cans | [[Mushrooms]] |  |
| 1/2 cup | [[Celery]] | chopped |
| 1/2 cup | [[Onions]] | chopped |

---

## Instructions

### Prep and Bake
1. Preheat your oven to **300°F**.
2. In a baking dish, combine the **2/3 cup butter**, **2 cans chicken broth**, **2 small cans mushrooms**, **1/2 cup chopped celery**, and **1/2 cup chopped onions**.
3. Mix the ingredients thoroughly until well combined.
4. Bake uncovered at **300°F** for **1 1/2 hours** until the vegetables are completely tender, the broth is richly concentrated, and the top is lightly golden.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Can be assembled in the baking dish ahead of time and kept covered in the refrigerator. Bake just before serving.
