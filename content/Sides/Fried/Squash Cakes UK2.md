|                   | [[Crisco]]                  |
___
### Instructions
1. Boil squash, onion, and salt in a small amount of water until tender.
2. Cool slightly.
3. Add 2 eggs.
4. Mix well.
5. Add meal mix (or flour).
6. Mix well.
7. Spoon into frying pan.
8. Fry in a small amount of crisco till brown on both sides.
9. (Spoon bread dough consistency)
