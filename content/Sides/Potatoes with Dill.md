## Instructions

### Step 1: Boil and Peel
1. Scrub the **two or three per person potatoes** well and place them in a pot of **salted, boiling water**.
2. Cook them for about **20 minutes** until just tender.
3. Drain the **potatoes** and dry them completely in the empty pot for a few moments to remove excess moisture.
4. Remove the skins from the **potatoes** while they are still warm so they peel easily.

### Step 2: Sauté and Serve
5. Sauté the **potatoes** briefly in **butter** with **salt** and **pepper** to taste, along with **lots of chopped fresh dill weed**, until heated through and lightly coated.
6. Serve hot, garnished with **chopped parsley**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Boiled potatoes can be peeled ahead of time and stored in the refrigerator for up to 2 days before sautéing with butter and dill.
