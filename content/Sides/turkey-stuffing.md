# Turkey Stuffing

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A flavorful traditional turkey stuffing featuring seasoned lamb, rice, and pine nuts. Designed to generously fill a 13-pound holiday bird.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 15 mins | 30 mins | 13 pound bird |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Skillet

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 cups | [[Rice]] | Soaked and drained |
| 2 cups | [[Lamb]] | Finely chopped |
| 1 | [[Onion]] | Chopped |
| 3/4 cup | [[Pine Nuts]] | None |
| To taste | [[Salt]] | None |
| To taste | [[Pepper]] | None |
| To taste | [[Cinnamon]] | None |
| To taste | [[Allspice]] | None |
| To taste | [[Poultry Seasoning]] | None |
| As needed | [[Butter]] | For cooking |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Sautéing
1. Cook **2 cups finely chopped up lamb** and **1 chopped up onion** in **butter** in a skillet until brown.

### Step 2: Combining
2. Add rest of ingredients including **2 cups of rice**, **3/4 cup of pine nuts**, **salt and pepper**, **cinammon**, **Allspice**, and **Poultry seasoning** and cook for a few more minutes.
3. Do not overcook, rice will finish cooking inside of bird. Use a lot of seasonings as the stuffing carries it well. Enough for a 13 pound bird.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Prepare the mixture up to a day in advance and store covered in the refrigerator before stuffing the turkey.
