# Au Gratin Potatoes

> *A classic comfort food dish featuring tender [[Potatoes]] layered in a rich homemade cheese sauce and topped with crispy breadcrumbs. Baked until golden brown and bubbling.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Make the Cheese Sauce
1. Preheat oven to **350°F (175°C)**.
2. In a saucepan, melt **2 tablespoon butter** over medium heat.
3. Stir in **2 tablespoon flour** until smooth.
4. Gradually whisk in **1/2 cup milk** until smooth and thickened.
5. Remove from heat, then stir in **sharp American cheese**, **salt & pepper** until the cheese is completely melted and the sauce is smooth.

### Step 2: Assemble and Bake
6. Grease a baking dish with a little butter.
7. Layer thinly sliced **potatoes** in the prepared baking dish.
8. Pour the cheese sauce over the **potatoes**, ensuring that the slices are evenly coated.
9. Sprinkle the top generously with **breadcrumbs**.
10. Bake for **1 hour**, or until the **potatoes** are fork-tender and the top is golden brown and bubbling.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the dish a few hours in advance, cover tightly, and refrigerate until ready to bake; add 10-15 minutes to the total baking time if baking cold.
