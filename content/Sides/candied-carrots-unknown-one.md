# Candied Carrots

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown One | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A delightful recipe for tender candied carrots glazed in brown sugar and butter. Perfect as a sweet and savory side dish for any family meal.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 35 mins | None | 6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Small baking dish
* Saucepan

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 6 medium-size (about 2 lbs.) | [[Carrots]] | Washed, pared or scraped, whole |
| 3/4 cup | [[Brown sugar]] | Firmly packed |
| 6 tablespoons | [[Water]] | None |
| 3 tablespoons | [[Butter]] | Or margarine |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Cook Carrots
1. Set out a small baking dish.
2. Wash and pare or scrape **6 medium-size (about 2 lbs.) carrots**.
3. Cook whole **6 medium-size (about 2 lbs.) carrots** 15 to 25 minutes, or until just tender.

### Step 2: Make Glaze and Bake
4. Meanwhile, combine in a small saucepan **3/4 cup brown sugar**, **6 tablespoons water**, and **3 tablespoons butter**.
5. Stirring constantly, cook over medium heat until sugar is dissolved.
6. Drain **6 medium-size (about 2 lbs.) carrots** and place in baking dish.
7. Cover with sugar mixture.
8. Bake at 350°F about 10 min., or until **6 medium-size (about 2 lbs.) carrots** are completely glazed, baste occasionally.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Carrots can be boiled ahead of time and stored in the refrigerator until ready to assemble and bake.
