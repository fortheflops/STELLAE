# Cheezy Hash Browns

> 📜 **Collection:** Christine Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A deliciously cheesy and comforting hash brown casserole made with a rich blend of sour cream and Cheez Whiz. Perfect as a hearty side dish for family dinners or holiday gatherings.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 45 mins | None | 6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Baking dish

---
## Ingredients

### Casserole Base
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/4 cup | [[Butter]] | melted |
| 1 small jar | [[Cheez Whiz]] | None |
| 1 ctn | [[Sour Cream]] | None |
| 1 bag | [[Hash Browns]] | fresh or frozen |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing and Baking
1. Mix the **1/4 cup butter**, **1 small jar Cheez Whiz**, and **1 ctn sour cream** together in a large mixing bowl.
2. Stir in either fresh or frozen **1 bag hash browns** until fully combined.
3. Transfer the mixture to a greased baking dish and bake at 350 degrees until browned and bubbly on top.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole ahead of time, cover, and refrigerate for up to 24 hours before baking.
