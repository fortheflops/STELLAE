# Korean Bean Sprouts

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A refreshing and savory traditional Korean side dish featuring tender bean sprouts tossed in a flavorful sesame and garlic dressing. Perfect for serving chilled on warm days.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 5 mins | 15 mins (Cooling) | 4 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Medium saucepan
* Colander
* Mixing bowl

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 Pound | [[Bean Sprouts]] | Rinsed and trimmed |
| 1 Teaspoon | [[Salt]] | To taste |
| ¼ Teaspoon | [[Pepper]] | Ground black pepper |
| 1 Teaspoon | [[Sesame Seed]] | Toasted |
| 1 ½ Teaspoons | [[Vinegar]] | Standard white or rice vinegar |
| 1 Teaspoon | [[Sesame Oil]] | Toasted |
| ½ Teaspoon | [[Red Pepper]] | Crushed, hot |
| 1 | [[Green Onion]] | Chopped |
| ½ Clove | [[Garlic]] | Minced |
| 1 ½ Teaspoons | [[Soy Sauce]] | Standard |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Boil and Cool
1. Boil the **1 pound bean sprouts** until just tender, then drain thoroughly and squeeze out any excess water.
2. Rinse the sprouts immediately in **cold water** to stop the cooking process and preserve their crisp texture.

### Step 2: Toss and Serve
3. In a mixing bowl, combine the prepared sprouts with **1 teaspoon salt**, **¼ teaspoon pepper**, **1 teaspoon sesame seed**, **1 ½ teaspoons vinegar**, **1 teaspoon sesame oil**, **½ teaspoon crushed red pepper**, **1 chopped green onion**, **½ clove minced garlic**, and **1 ½ teaspoons soy sauce**.
4. Toss gently until all ingredients are evenly distributed and fragrant.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store sealed in the refrigerator for up to 2 days; this dish is especially refreshing when served chilled on hot days.
