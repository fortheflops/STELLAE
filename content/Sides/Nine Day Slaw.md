# Nine Day Slaw

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Three Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A crisp, classic refrigerator slaw tossed with celery, onion, peppers, and a boiled sweet-and-sour dressing. Designed to be made ahead, this tangy salad improves over time and stays fresh for up to nine days.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 5 mins | 24 hours (Chilling) | 8–10 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large mixing bowl
* 2-quart saucepan
* Wooden spoon
* Chef's knife

## Ingredients

### Vegetable Base
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 Medium | Head [[Cabbage]] | coarsely shredded (do not use processor or grater) |
| 4 Stalks | [[Celery]] | diced |
| 1 | [[Onion]] | diced |
| 1 | [[Green Bell Pepper]] | chopped |
| 1 Jar (4 Ounce) | Chopped [[Pimento]] | drained |

### Dressing
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 Cup | [[Sugar]] | divided |
| 1 Cup | [[Vegetable Oil]] | or salad oil |
| 1 Cup | [[Vinegar]] | white or apple cider |
| 2 Tablespoons | [[Salt]] | granulated |
| 1 Tablespoon | [[Dry Mustard]] | ground |
| 1 Tablespoon | [[Celery Seed]] | whole |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Toss the Vegetables
1. In a large mixing bowl, combine the **1 medium head coarsely shredded cabbage**, **4 stalks diced celery**, **1 diced onion**, **1 chopped green bell pepper**, **1 jar (4 ounce) drained pimento**, and **1 cup minus 2 tablespoons sugar** (reserving **2 tablespoons sugar** for the dressing). Toss thoroughly to distribute.

### Step 2: Cook the Dressing
2. In a saucepan over medium-high heat, combine the **1 cup oil**, **1 cup vinegar**, **2 tablespoons salt**, the remaining **2 tablespoons sugar**, **1 tablespoon dry mustard**, and **1 tablespoon celery seed**.
3. Bring the mixture to a rolling boil, stirring constantly until the sugar and salt are fully dissolved and fragrant.

### Step 3: Combine and Marinate
4. Pour the hot dressing mixture evenly over the prepared vegetable mixture and mix thoroughly until well coated.
5. Allow the slaw to cool to room temperature, then cover tightly and chill in the refrigerator for at least **24 hours** before serving to allow the flavors to meld.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This slaw gets better every day and keeps crisp and fresh in a sealed container in the refrigerator for up to 9 days.
