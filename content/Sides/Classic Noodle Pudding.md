# Classic Noodle Pudding

> 📜 **Collection:** JillCollection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A rich and comforting traditional noodle kugel featuring a decadent blend of farmer cheese, cottage cheese, and cream cheese. Baked until the center is set and the top turns a delightful golden brown.*

* Saucepan

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |

| 1 cup | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 2 cups | [[Milk]] | Whole milk preferred |
| 1 pound | [[Cottage Cheese]] | None |
| ¼ pound (4 oz) | [[Cream Cheese]] | Softened |
| ½ pound | [[Farmer Cheese]] | None |
| ½ pint (1 cup) | [[Sour Cream]] | None |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Vanilla Extract]] | None |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Salt]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Cook Noodles
1. Preheat your oven to **350°F**. Grease a **9x13 inch baking dish**.
2. Cook the **½ pound wide egg noodles** according to package directions, then drain well in a colander.

### Step 2: Mix the Custard and Noodles
3. In a large bowl, beat the **4 eggs**. Then, mix in the **1 cup sugar**, **2 cups milk**, **1 pound cottage cheese**, **¼ pound cream cheese**, **½ pound farmer cheese**, **½ pint sour cream**, **1 teaspoon vanilla extract**, and **1 teaspoon salt** until well combined and smooth.
4. Melt the **¼ pound butter** and toss it thoroughly with the hot, drained noodles.

### Step 3: Assemble and Bake
5. Stir the buttered noodle mixture gently into the cheese and egg mixture until evenly distributed.
6. Pour everything into the prepared **9x13 inch baking dish**.
7. Bake for **50-60 minutes**, or until the center is set and the top is golden brown and fragrant.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Leftovers can be stored covered in the refrigerator for up to 3 days and reheated before serving.
