# Sweet Potato Soufflé

> 📜 **Collection:** JC | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A rich and creamy sweet potato soufflé topped with a crunchy pecan and brown sugar streusel, perfect for holiday gatherings.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 45 mins | None | 4 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large bowl
* Food processor
* Baking pan
* Casserole dish
* Fork

---
## Ingredients

### For the Souffle
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3 to 4 cups | [[Sweet Potatoes]] | Mashed |
| 1 cup | [[Granulated Sugar]] | None |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] | None |
| 1 tablespoon | [[Vanilla Extract]] | None |
| 1/2 cup | [[Milk]] | None |
| 1/2 cup | [[Butter]] | Melted |

### For the Topping
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 cup | [[Pecans]] | Chopped |
| 1 cup | [[Brown Sugar]] | Packed |
| 1/3 cup | [[All-Purpose Flour]] | None |
| 1/3 cup | [[Butter]] | Softened |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Souffle Base
1. Preheat oven to **350°F**. Lightly grease a baking pan or casserole dish.
2. In a large bowl or a food processor, combine the **3 to 4 cups mashed [[Sweet Potatoes]]**, **1 cup [[Granulated Sugar]]**, **2 [[Eggs]]**, **1 tablespoon [[Vanilla Extract]]**, **1/2 cup [[Milk]]**, and **1/2 cup melted [[Butter]]**.
3. Mix until everything is well combined and the mixture is smooth and creamy, then spread evenly into the prepared baking pan.

### Step 2: Add Topping and Bake
4. In a separate bowl, mix together the **1 cup chopped [[Pecans]]**, **1 cup packed [[Brown Sugar]]**, **1/3 cup [[All-Purpose Flour]]**, and **1/3 cup softened [[Butter]]**. Use a fork or your fingers to create a crumbly streusel topping.
5. Sprinkle the topping evenly over the sweet potato mixture.
6. Bake for **30 to 45 minutes**, until the topping is golden brown and the soufflé is set and fragrant.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole ahead of time, cover, and refrigerate for up to 24 hours before baking.
