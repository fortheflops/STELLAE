# Lima Beans With Ham Bone Or Bacon

## Instructions

### Step 1: Soak and Prepare
1. Soak the **1 ½ cups lima beans** in water overnight, optionally adding **1 teaspoon soda** to help soften the beans.
2. Drain the soaking water thoroughly using a colander.

### Step 2: Cook with Ham Bone (Primary Method)
3. In a large pot or pressure cooker, combine the drained **lima beans**, **1 large white onion**, **a little celery**, and the **1 ham bone**.
4. Add **water** to cover (about halfway up the pot level).
5. Cook in a pressure cooker for **40 minutes to 1 hour**, or simmer gently on the stovetop.
6. Add **salt to taste**, and continue to simmer on mild heat, occasionally adding more water, until the beans are tender and creamy.

### Step 3: Alternate Preparation (Bacon Method)
7. If substituting the ham bone, rinse the **4 slices bacon** and cook them with the **onion** in **6 cups of water** for at least **1 hour**.
8. Stir in **1 cup tomato sauce**, **sugar to taste**, **1 pork love seasoning**, and a dash of **kitchen bouquet**.
9. Add the soaked and drained **lima beans** to the pot.
10. Simmer over low heat until the beans are completely tender and flavors are well-blended.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftover lima beans in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 4 days; flavors often improve by the next day.
