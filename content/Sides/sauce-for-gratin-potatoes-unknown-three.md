# Sauce For Gratin Potatoes

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Three | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A rich and creamy homemade white sauce designed specifically for gratin potatoes, featuring a hearty chunk of cheese. The versatile sauce can also be stored and used on any vegetable dish.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 20 mins | None | 6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Saucepan
* Casserole dish
* Knife

---
## Ingredients

### Sauce Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/4 stick | [[Butter]] | melted |
| 2 tablespoons | [[Flour]] | none |
| 2 cups | [[Milk]] | can use 1 cup thick canned milk and 1 cup water |
| 1 good-sized chunk | [[Cheese]] | none |
| to taste | [[Garlic Salt]] | added along the side |
| as needed | [[Potatoes]] | sliced and cooked until tender |
| to taste | [[Paprika]] | for sprinkling |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Making the Sauce
1. Melt **1/4 stick butter** and add **2 tablespoons flour**, heating until dissolved.
2. Add **2 cups milk** (using **1 cup thick canned milk** and **1 cup water**), along with a **good-sized chunk cheese** and **garlic salt** to taste.
3. Let boil until thick.

### Step 2: Assembling and Baking
4. Slice **potatoes** and cook until tender, then pour into a casserole dish.
5. Add the prepared **sauce** and sprinkle with **paprika**.
6. Set in the oven and heat through until bubbling and golden.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store any remaining sauce in a tight container in the ice box to use on any vegetable.
