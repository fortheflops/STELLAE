# Broccoli Bake

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty One | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A creamy and comforting broccoli bake featuring water chestnuts, pimentos, and celery soup. This classic casserole makes a delightful side dish for any family dinner.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 25 mins | None | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Casserole dish

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 pkg. | [[Broccoli]] | Cooked |
| 1 can | [[Cream of Celery Soup]] | Condensed |
| 1 can | [[Water Chestnuts]] | Sliced |
| 1 can | [[Pimentos]] | Diced |
| 1 cup | [[Sour Cream]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing and Baking
1. Mix the **2 pkg. broccoli**, **1 can cream of celery soup**, **1 can water chestnuts**, **1 can pimentos**, and **1 cup sour cream** together in a mixing bowl until well combined.
2. Transfer the mixture to a casserole dish and bake at 325°F for about **25 minutes** until bubbly and hot throughout.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole a few hours in advance and keep covered in the refrigerator until ready to bake.
