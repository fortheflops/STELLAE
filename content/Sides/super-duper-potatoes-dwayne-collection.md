# Super Duper Potatoes

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Barb | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A comforting holiday casserole featuring frozen hash browns baked in a creamy soup and sour cream mixture, topped with melted cheddar. Praised as wonderful by Barb in this delightful Easter recipe collection.*

### 🔪 Key Equipment

## Instructions

### Step 1: Assembly
1. Mix together the **1 - two lb. package partially thawed hash brown potatoes**, **1 can cream of celery soup**, **1 can cream of potato soup**, **1 carton sour cream**, and **1/2 cup chopped onion** in a large mixing bowl.
2. Pour the mixture into a **9" x 13" baking dish**.
3. Top with **2 cups shredded cheddar cheese**, **parsley flakes**, and **paprika**.

### Step 2: Baking
4. Cover the baking dish with foil and bake at 300 degrees for 60 to 90 minutes until hot, bubbly, and golden on top.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole in the baking dish ahead of time, cover tightly with foil, and refrigerate until ready to bake.
