# Broiled Tomatoes

> 📜 **Collection:** Louise Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A quick and savory side dish featuring fresh tomatoes topped with sharp cheese, sesame seeds, and dill weed. Broiled until bubbly and golden brown for a warm, comforting treat.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5 mins | 3-5 mins | None | 4 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Broiler-safe pan
* Baking sheet

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| As needed | [[Tomatoes]] | Sliced |
| As needed | [[Sharp Cheese]] | Shredded or thinly sliced |
| As needed | [[Sesame Seeds]] | None |
| As needed | [[Dill Weed]] | Dried |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Assemble
1. Preheat broiler.
2. Arrange the **tomatoes, sliced** on a broiler-safe pan or baking sheet.
3. Top the **tomatoes, sliced** with **sharp cheese**.

### Step 2: Broil and Serve
4. Sprinkle with **sesame seeds** and **dill weed (dried)**.
5. Broil for a few minutes, watching carefully, until the **sharp cheese** is melted and bubbly, and the **tomatoes, sliced** are heated through and tender. Serve immediately.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Best enjoyed immediately after broiling while the cheese is warm and bubbly.
