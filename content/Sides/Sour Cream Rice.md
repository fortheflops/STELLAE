# Sour Cream Rice

> 📜 **Collection:** Patsy's Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Emma Lou | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A creamy, comforting baked side dish made with converted rice, green chiles, jalapeño, and plenty of melted cheddar cheese. This vintage recipe from the Patsy Collection delivers a mild kick and rich texture.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 30 mins | None | 6–8 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Baking dish
* Saucepan

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| As directed | [[Converted Rice]] | Prepared according to package instructions |
| 1 whole | [[Jalapeno Pepper]] | Whole, for cooking in rice |
| 1 Can | [[Green Chiles]] | Chopped, divided |
| 1 Can | [[Corn]] | Optional |
| To taste | [[Black Pepper]] | Ground, lots of it |
| 1 Large Carton | [[Sour Cream]] | Room temperature |
| To top | [[Cheddar Cheese]] | Shredded |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Cook the Rice
1. Cook **Converted Rice** as directed with **1 jalapeno pepper** and **1 can chopped green chiles**.

### Step 2: Combine and Bake
2. Remove the **jalapeno pepper** from the cooked rice.
3. Stir in **1 can corn** (optional), more **green chiles**, lots of **ground black pepper**, and **1 large carton sour cream** until thoroughly combined.
4. Transfer the mixture to a baking dish and sprinkle lots of **shredded cheddar cheese** generously on top.
5. Bake at **350°F** for about **30 minutes** until bubbly, hot, and the cheese is melted and golden.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole ahead of time, cover tightly, and refrigerate for up to 24 hours before baking.
