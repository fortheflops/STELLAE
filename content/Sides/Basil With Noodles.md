# Basil With Noodles

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A classic vintage side dish featuring tender noodles tossed in a vibrant, herbaceous basil paste with melted margarine and oil. Ready in just 15 minutes for a quick and flavorful accompaniment.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5 mins | 10 mins | None | 4 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Saucepan
* Colander
* Small bowl
* Stirring spoon

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 package | [[Noodles]] | Standard package |
| 3 heaping tablespoons | [[Basil]] | Fresh or preserved |
| 1 tablespoon | [[Oil]] | None |
| 1/3 | [[Margarine]] | Unit not specified |
| As Needed | Hot water from cooking noodles | Reserved |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Cook the Noodles
1. Cook the **1 package** [[Noodles]] according to package directions. When they are done, drain them in a colander but reserve some of the hot cooking water.

### Step 2: Prepare the Basil Paste
2. In a small bowl, take **3 heaping tablespoons** [[Basil]] and add a small amount of the hot noodle water. Mash together until well combined to form a smooth paste.

### Step 3: Make the Sauce and Toss
3. In a saucepan, melt the **1/3** [[Margarine]] with **1 tablespoon** [[Oil]].
4. Add the basil paste to the melted margarine and oil.
5. Stir everything together, adding more hot noodle water as needed to reach your preferred sauce consistency.
6. Toss the fragrant herb sauce with the cooked noodles until thoroughly coated and serve immediately while hot.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Best served immediately, though leftover noodles can be stored in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 2 days.
