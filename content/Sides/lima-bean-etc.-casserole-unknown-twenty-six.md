# Lima Bean Etc. Casserole

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Darlene | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A comforting and creamy vegetable casserole featuring lima beans and broccoli topped with a crunchy cereal crust. Perfect for family gatherings and potlucks.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 30 mins | None | 6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Saucepan
* Colander
* Stove
* Baking dish

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 pkg. | [[Frozen Lima Beans]] | Frozen |
| 1 pkg. | [[Frozen Chopped Broccoli]] | Frozen |
| 1 can | [[Cream of Celery Soup]] | Condensed |
| 1 can | [[Cream of Mushroom Soup]] | Condensed |
| 1 cup | [[Crushed Rice Krispies or Chex]] | Crushed |
| 3/4 stick | [[Butter or Margarine]] | Melted |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Mix
1. Cook **1 pkg. frozen lima beans** and **1 pkg. frozen chopped broccoli** as directed and drain.
2. Mix the cooked vegetables with **1 can cream of celery soup** and **1 can cream of mushroom soup** and heat on top of the stove.

### Step 2: Bake
3. Melt **3/4 stick butter or margarine** and add to the **1 cup crushed rice Krispies or Chex**, adding some to the vegetable mixture and sprinkling the rest on top.
4. Bake at 375°F until brown and bubbly.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole in the baking dish ahead of time, cover, and refrigerate until ready to bake.
