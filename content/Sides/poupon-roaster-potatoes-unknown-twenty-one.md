# Poupon Roaster Potatoes

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty One | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *Crispy roasted red potatoes coated in a tangy Dijon mustard and garlic glaze. A simple yet flavorful side dish that pairs wonderfully with any roast.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 40 mins | None | 4 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Baking pan

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/3 cup | [[Poupon Dijon]] | None |
| 2 tbsp | [[Olive oil]] | None |
| 1 clove | [[Garlic]] | Chopped |
| 1/2 tsp | [[Italian seasoning]] | None |
| 6 potatoes (2 lbs) | [[Red potatoes]] | Cut in chunks |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and Baking
1. In a mixing bowl, combine **1/3 cup Poupon Dijon**, **2 tbsp olive oil**, **1 clove garlic**, and **1/2 tsp Italian seasoning**.
2. Add **6 potatoes** and toss thoroughly until the chunks are well coated.
3. Spread the coated potatoes onto a greased baking pan and bake at 425°F for **35-40 minutes** until golden brown and tender.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Potatoes can be chopped and stored in cold water in the refrigerator a few hours in advance; drain and pat completely dry before tossing with the sauce.
