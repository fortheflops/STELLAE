# Onion Roasted Potatoes

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty One | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A simple and flavorful recipe for onion roasted potatoes using savory onion soup mix. This versatile side dish pairs wonderfully with chicken, pork chops, or beef roast.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5 mins | 30 mins | None | 4 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Baking dish
* Mixing bowl

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 packet | [[Onion soup mix]] | Dry |
| 4 cups | [[Potatoes]] | Cubed or sliced |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and Baking
1. Mix the **4 cups potatoes** with the **1 packet onion soup mix** in a baking dish until thoroughly coated.
2. Bake until the **potatoes** are tender and golden brown with a crispy exterior.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Coat the potatoes in the onion soup mix ahead of time and store in a covered container in the refrigerator for up to a few hours before baking.
