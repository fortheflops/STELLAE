# Spicy Red Cabbage

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A classic sweet and savory side dish featuring shredded red cabbage simmered with apples, brown sugar, and vinegar. Perfect alongside roasted meats or holiday feasts.*

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |

| 3 Tbsp | [[Onion]] | chopped |
| 1 Tbsp | [[Bacon Drippings]] | or butter |
| 5 Tbsp | [[Brown Sugar]] |  |
| 3 Tbsp | [[Vinegar]] |  |
| 2 | [[Apples]] | cut into thin slices |

---

## Instructions

### Prep Work
1. Shred **6 cups red cabbage**, ensuring you remove and discard the hard core.
2. Cut **2 apples** into thin slices.

### Sauté and Simmer
3. In a large skillet, sauté **3 Tbsp chopped onion** in **1 Tbsp bacon dripping or butter** until the onion is soft and translucent.
4. Add **5 Tbsp brown sugar**, **3 Tbsp vinegar**, and the sliced **2 apples** to the skillet.
5. Stir in the shredded **6 cups red cabbage** and cook for **30 minutes**, stirring frequently, until the cabbage is tender and cooked through.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Can be made 1-2 days in advance and reheated gently on the stovetop; the flavors often improve overnight.
