# Savory Spinach

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty One | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A quick and flavorful side dish featuring tender spinach baked in a rich sour cream and parmesan dressing. Perfect as a simple weeknight accompaniment.*

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 cup | [[Sour Cream]] | None |
| 1 pkg. | [[Parmesan Salad Dressing]] | None |
| 1 pkg. | [[Spinach]] | defrosted |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and Baking
1. Combine **1/2 cup sour cream** and **1 pkg. parmesan salad dressing**.
2. Pour the mixture over **1 pkg. spinach**.
3. Bake at 375 degrees for **15 minutes** until hot and bubbling.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the dish ahead of time, cover, and refrigerate for up to a few hours before baking.
