# Broiled Tomatoes

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Dee | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A simple and savory side dish featuring thick tomato slices topped with a creamy herb and olive mixture. These broiled tomatoes are perfectly bubbly and make for a quick, flavorful addition to any meal.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 5 mins | None | 6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Broiler pan

---
## Ingredients

### Topping Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 cup | [[Mayonnaise]] | standard |
| 1/2 cup | [[Ripe Olives]] | chopped |
| 1/4 cup | [[Parsley]] | chopped |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Sweet Basil]] | dried |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Oregano]] | dried |

### Base
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 6 | [[Tomatoes]] | thickly sliced |

---

## Instructions

### Preparation and Broiling
1. In a small bowl, combine the **1/2 cup mayonnaise**, **1/2 cup ripe olives**, **1/4 cup parsley**, **1/4 tsp sweet basil**, and **1/4 tsp oregano** until well mixed.
2. Arrange the **6 tomatoes** on a broiler pan and spread the mixture evenly over the top of each slice.
3. Broil under high heat until the topping is golden brown and bubbly, indicating the tomatoes are heated through.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The topping mixture can be prepared up to 2 hours in advance and kept refrigerated, but assemble just before broiling to prevent the tomatoes from becoming watery.
