# Twice Baked Potatoes

> 📜 **Collection:** The Loraine Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** TOH Taste of the Country Cookbook 6th Edition | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A classic comfort food side dish featuring fluffy mashed baked potatoes mixed with butter, milk, onion, chives, and melted cheddar cheese, baked until golden and bubbling.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 1 hour 30 mins | None | 6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Baking sheet
* Mixing bowl
* Potato masher
* Knife
* Spoon

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 6 | [[Potatoes]] | Large baking potatoes |
| ½ cup | [[Butter]] | Or margarine, softened (or less to taste) |
| ⅗ cup | [[Milk]] | Or less to preference |
| 3 tbsp | [[Onion]] | Minced |
| 1 tbsp | [[Chives]] | Snipped |
| ½ tsp | [[Salt]] | Standard |
| Dash | [[Pepper]] | Ground black pepper |
| 1 ½ cups | [[Cheddar Cheese]] | Shredded, divided (about 6 oz) |
| As needed | [[Paprika]] | For sprinkling on top |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Initial Bake and Prep
1. Bake the **6 large baking potatoes** at **400°F** for **60 minutes**, or until soft and fork-tender.
2. Cut a lengthwise slice from the top of each of the warm **6 large baking potatoes**.
3. Carefully scoop out the potato pulp, leaving a thin shell, and place the pulp into a large mixing bowl.

### Step 2: Mixing the Filling
4. Mash the hot potato pulp together with the **½ cup butter** until smooth.
5. Add the **⅗ cup milk**, **3 tbsp minced onion**, **1 tbsp snipped chives**, **½ tsp salt**, **dash pepper**, and **1 cup of the cheddar cheese** (reserving the remaining ½ cup) to the bowl, mixing well until thoroughly combined.

### Step 3: Refilling and Final Bake
6. Refill the potato shells generously with the seasoned potato mixture.
7. Top the stuffed potatoes with the remaining **½ cup cheddar cheese** and lightly sprinkle each with **paprika**.
8. Bake at **375°F** for **25-30 minutes**, or until heated through, bubbly, and melted on top.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the twice-baked potatoes ahead of time, cover tightly, and refrigerate for up to 24 hours before baking.
