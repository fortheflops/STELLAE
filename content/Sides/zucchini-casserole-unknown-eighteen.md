# Zucchini Casserole

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Eighteen | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A savory, comforting zucchini casserole featuring crisp bacon, earthy mushrooms, and melted Swiss cheese. Baked to golden perfection with a crunchy saltine cracker crumb topping.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 40 mins | None | 6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Skillet
* Baking dish
* Mixing bowl

---
## Ingredients

### Casserole Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 slices | [[Bacon]] | Fries and crumbled |
| 1/2 cup | [[Mushrooms]] | Chopped |
| 1/4 cup | [[Onion]] | Minced |
| 1 dash | [[Garlic]] | Instant minced garlic |
| 1/2 cup | [[Butter]] | Melted |
| 2 medium | [[Zucchini]] | Grated |
| 1 1/4 cups | [[Cracker crumbs]] | Saltines, divided |
| 1 | [[Egg]] | Beaten |
| 1/2 tsp | [[Salt]] | None |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Pepper]] | None |
| 1/2 cup | [[Swiss cheese]] | Shredded |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and Cooking
1. Fry **2 slices bacon**, drain, and crumble.
2. Sauté **1/2 cup chopped mushrooms**, **1/4 cup minced onion**, and **1 dash instant minced garlic** in **1/2 cup melted butter** just until tender.
3. Combine all ingredients except **1/4 c. of cracker crumbs** and part of the cheese.

### Step 2: Baking
4. Pour into a greased casserole dish.
5. Sprinkle remaining cheese and **1/4 c. cracker crumbs** over the casserole.
6. Bake at **350°** for **40 minutes** until bubbly and golden brown.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole ahead of time, cover, and refrigerate before baking; add the topping just before putting it in the oven.
