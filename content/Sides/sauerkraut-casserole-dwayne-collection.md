# Sauerkraut Casserole

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A unique and comforting sweet-and-sour side dish featuring tangy sauerkraut, rich bacon, and sweet tomatoes. Baked to caramelized perfection, it pairs beautifully with roasted meats.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 1 hour | None | 6 servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Casserole dish
* Skillet
* Can opener
* Chef's knife
* Cutting board
* Wooden spoon

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 cans (14.5 oz each) | [[Tomatoes]] | peeled and drained |
| 1 can (14 oz) | [[Sauerkraut]] | drained |
| 12 oz | [[Bacon]] | browned and cut up into pieces |
| 1 cup | [[Sugar]] | granulated |
| 1 | [[Onion]] | chopped and browned in bacon drippings |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep the Bacon and Onion
1. Preheat your oven to **350°F**.
2. In a skillet over medium heat, cook the **12 oz bacon** until crispy and browned. Remove the bacon from the skillet, cut it up into bite-sized pieces, and set aside.
3. Reserve a portion of the bacon drippings in the skillet. Add the chopped **1 onion** to the hot drippings and sauté until beautifully browned and fragrant.

### Step 2: Assemble and Bake
4. In a medium casserole dish, combine the **2 cans tomatoes** (peeled and drained), **1 can sauerkraut**, the cooked **12 oz bacon** pieces, **1 cup sugar**, and the browned **1 onion**.
5. Mix all ingredients thoroughly directly in the casserole dish until well combined.
6. Bake uncovered at **350°F** for **1 hour** until the top is slightly caramelized and the edges are bubbling.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This casserole can be assembled up to 24 hours in advance, covered, and stored in the refrigerator. Bake uncovered as directed, adding 10-15 minutes to the baking time if starting from a cold refrigerator.
