# Macaroni Ring

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A delightful baked macaroni ring filled with cheese and breadcrumbs, traditionally served with creamed chicken or vegetables in the center. This classic side dish offers a comforting texture and elegant presentation.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 1 hour | None | 6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Ring mold
* Saucepan
* Mixing bowl

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 cup | [[Macaroni]] | Broken |
| 1 scant cup | [[Butter]] | Melted |
| 1 cup | [[Bread crumbs]] | Dry |
| 1 cup | [[Milk]] | Or cream |
| 2 | [[Pimentos]] | Cut fine |
| 1/2 tsp | [[Salt]] | None |
| 1 cup | [[Cheese]] | Grated |
| 3 | [[Eggs]] | Slightly beaten |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Mix
1. Cook **1 cup broken macaroni** in salted water, drain, and rinse.
2. Melt **1 scant cup butter**, put in **1 cup dry bread crumbs**, and pour over **1 cup milk** (or cream), **2 pimentos cut fine**, **1/2 tsp salt**, **1 cup grated cheese**, and **3 eggs slightly beaten**.
3. Mix everything thoroughly with the cooked [[Macaroni]].

### Step 2: Steam and Serve
4. Steam the mixture in a buttered ring mold for **1 hour** until set.
5. Un-mold carefully onto a serving platter and fill the center hole with creamed chicken, fish, or vegetables.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The ring mold can be prepared and assembled ahead of time, then steamed just before serving.
