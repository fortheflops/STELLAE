# Pinto Beans

> 📜 **Collection:** Patsy's Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A traditional homestyle side dish of tender pinto beans seasoned with garlic salt, black pepper, and oregano. Simple, comforting, and packed with flavor.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 1 hour | 30 mins | 4–6 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large pot or saucepan

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Fill pot to ½ to 1 inch depth | [[Pinto Beans]] | Dry beans |
| Cover with 4 inches | [[Water]] | Measured above the beans Level |
| To taste | [[Garlic Salt]] | None |
| To taste | [[Black Pepper]] | None |
| Generous amount | [[Oregano]] | Lots |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Cook and Simmer
1. Combine the **[[Pinto Beans]]**, **[[Water]]**, **[[Garlic Salt]]**, **[[Black Pepper]]**, and **[[Oregano]]** in a pot.
2. Cook for **1 hour** on medium heat until the beans are tender.
3. Turn off the heat and let sit undisturbed without opening the lid for **½ hour** to finish steaming.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftover cooked beans in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 4 days.
