# Gari's Rice Ortega Casserole

> 📜 **Collection:** UK5 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Gari | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A wonderfully creamy and cheesy rice casserole packed with green chiles, sour cream, and cottage cheese. This quick and easy side dish bakes to bubbling perfection in just twenty-five minutes.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 25 mins | None | 6-8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Casserole dish
* Small skillet
* Large mixing bowl

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2/3 cup | [[Minute Rice]] | Uncooked |
| 1/2 cup | [[Onion]] | Chopped |
| 1 tbsp | [[Butter]] | For sautéing |
| 1 can (4 oz) | [[Green Chiles]] | Chopped |
| 1 3/4 cups | [[Sour Cream]] | Standard |
| 1/2 pound (8 oz) | [[Cottage Cheese]] | Standard |
| 1/2 pound (2 cups) | [[Cheddar Cheese]] | Sharp, shredded |
| 1 clove | [[Garlic]] | Minced, or to taste |
| To taste | [[Salt]] | Standard |
| To taste | [[Pepper]] | Standard |
| 1 | [[Bay Leaf]] | Whole |
| As needed | [[Cheese]] | Grated, for topping |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Cook Rice
1. Preheat the oven to **375°F** (**190°C**). Grease a casserole dish.
2. Cook the **2/3 cup** of **[[Minute Rice]]** according to package directions.

### Step 2: Sauté Aromatics and Mix
3. While the rice is cooking, sauté the **1/2 cup** chopped **[[Onion]]** in **1 tbsp** of **[[Butter]]** in a small skillet until softened.
4. In a large bowl, combine the cooked rice, sautéed onion, **1 can** chopped **[[Green Chiles]]**, **1 3/4 cups** **[[Sour Cream]]**, **1/2 pound** **[[Cottage Cheese]]**, **1/2 pound** sharp **[[Cheddar Cheese]]**, minced **[[Garlic]]**, **[[Salt]]**, **[[Pepper]]**, and the **1** **[[Bay Leaf]]**. Mix well until fully incorporated.

### Step 3: Bake and Serve
5. Pour the mixture into the greased casserole dish and top with additional grated **[[Cheese]]**.
6. Bake for **25 minutes**, or until hot, bubbly, and lightly golden on top.
7. Remember to remove the **[[Bay Leaf]]** before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store sealed in the refrigerator for up to 3 days before baking.
