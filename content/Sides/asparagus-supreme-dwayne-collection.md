# Asparagus Supreme

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Sharon | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A comforting vintage asparagus casserole featuring a creamy shrimp soup base, sour cream, and a buttery herb-seasoned stuffing crust. Perfect as a savory side dish for holiday dinners or family gatherings.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 35 mins | None | 6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Saucepan
* Colander
* Mixing bowl
* 1-quart baking dish

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 4 cups | [[Asparagus]] | fresh, cut up (or two 8 oz packages frozen cut asparagus) |
| 1 can | [[Cream of Shrimp Soup]] | condensed |
| 1/2 cup | [[Sour Cream]] | dairy |
| 2 tbsp | [[Carrot]] | coarsely shredded |
| 1 tsp | [[Onion]] | grated |
| 1/8 tsp | [[Black Pepper]] |  |
| 1/2 cup | [[Stuffing Mix]] | herb-seasoned |
| 1 tbsp | [[Butter]] | melted (or margarine) |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Cook the Asparagus
1. Cook **4 cups fresh asparagus** in boiling water for **5-6 mins** or until crisp-tender. (If using **two 8 oz packages frozen cut asparagus**, cook according to package directions). Drain well.

### Step 2: Assemble the Casserole
2. In a mixing bowl, combine **1 can condensed cream of shrimp soup**, **1/2 cup dairy sour cream**, **2 tbsp coarsely shredded carrot**, **1 tsp grated onion**, and **1/8 tsp pepper**.
3. Gently fold the drained **asparagus** into the soup mixture.
4. Turn the mixture into an ungreased **1-quart baking dish**.

### Step 3: Add Topping and Bake
5. Combine **1/2 cup herb-seasoned stuffing mix** and **1 tbsp melted butter**.
6. Sprinkle the stuffing mixture around the edge of the **asparagus mixture**.
7. Bake uncovered in a **350°F** oven for **30-35 mins** until hot, bubbling, and the stuffing topping is golden brown.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Can be assembled up to a day ahead (without the stuffing topping), covered, and refrigerated. Add the buttered stuffing mix just before baking, adding 5-10 minutes to the baking time if cold.
