## Instructions

### Preparation and Service
1. Bake the **26 potatoes** until tender and fluffy.
2. Serve the baked potatoes alongside **2 - 12 oz sour cream** cartons.
3. Prepare the **6 lbs baked beans** from scratch, noting that slightly less quantity may suffice for future events.
4. Toss and serve the **2 bowls tossed salad** fresh.
5. Bake the **8 lbs canned ham** until heated through.
6. Arrange the assortment of **pickles**, including radish and apple varieties, on serving trays.
7. Prepare **2 angel food cake** desserts and top with whipped cream and strawberries.
8. Brew **30 c. coffee** and mix **3 cans lemonade**, along with alcoholic and soft beverages to taste.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Baking the ham and beans ahead of time allows for easier day-of assembly.
