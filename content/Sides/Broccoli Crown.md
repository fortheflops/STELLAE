# Broccoli Crown

> 📜 **Collection:** Patsy's Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Barbara Osborne | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *An elegant and savory baked broccoli crown served with herb-butter tomatoes, perfect for special family dinners. This vintage molded casserole features a rich sauce and a tender vegetable base.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Base and Sauce
1. Cook **2 lbs broccoli** until just crunchy; drain well.
2. Sauté **¼ cup chopped onion** in **4 tbsp butter**.
3. Stir in **4 tbsp flour** and **1 tsp seasoned salt**; cook until bubbly.
4. Stir in **1 cup milk** and cook until it thickens and boils one minute.

### Step 2: Combine and Bake
5. Beat **3 eggs** slightly.
6. Add a little sauce to mix with the **eggs**, then stir the **eggs** back into the sauce.
7. Cook over medium heat for about 3 minutes, until the mixture thickens again.
8. Stir in **½ cup mayonnaise** and **¼ cup parsley**.
9. Fold the sauce into the drained **broccoli**.
10. Spoon into a well-greased 5-cup mold, set the mold in a baking pan, and place the pan on an oven rack.
11. Pour boiling water into the baking pan to a depth of about one inch.
12. Bake at 350°F for 30 minutes or until set.

### Step 3: Unmold and Serve
13. Let stand a few minutes, loosen, then invert the **broccoli crown** onto a serving dish.
14. Prepare Herb-Butter Tomatoes: Melt **2 tbsp butter** with **½ tsp marjoram**, stir in **tomatoes**, and cook for 5 minutes or just until heated through.
15. Serve with Herb-Butter Tomatoes in the center.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The broccoli mixture can be assembled and spooned into the mold a few hours ahead before baking in the water bath just prior to serving.
