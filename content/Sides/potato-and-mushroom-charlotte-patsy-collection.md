# Potato and Mushroom Charlotte

> 📜 **Collection:** Patsy Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *An elegant layered potato and mushroom charlotte featuring earthy shiitake, oyster, and chanterelle mushrooms. Baked to golden perfection with Parmesan cheese and olive oil.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 30 mins | 45 mins | None | 6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Skillet
* Bowl
* Baking mold
* Paper towels
* Spatula

---
## Ingredients

### Mushrooms and Aromatics
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 tbsp | [[Unsalted Butter]] | Unsalted |
| 4 oz | [[Shiitake Mushrooms]] | Fresh, chopped |
| 4 oz | [[Oyster Mushrooms]] | Fresh, chopped |
| 4 oz | [[Chanterelle Mushrooms]] | Fresh, chopped |
| 1 tbsp | [[Chives]] | Chopped |
| To taste | [[Salt and Pepper]] | As needed |

### Potatoes and Assembly
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3 cups (2 lbs) | [[Russet Potatoes]] | Peeled and sliced 1/4-inch thick |
| 6 tbsp | [[Parmesan Cheese]] | Grated |
| 2 tbsp + 3/4 tsp | [[Olive Oil]] | Divided |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mushroom Filling
1. Melt **1 tbsp unsalted butter** in a skillet over medium heat.
2. Sauté **4 oz shiitake mushrooms**, **4 oz oyster mushrooms**, and **4 oz chanterelle mushrooms** until tender and liquid has evaporated, about 15 minutes.
3. Stir in **1 tbsp chives** and season with **salt and pepper**.

### Step 2: Potato Preparation and Layering
4. Blanch **3 cups russet potatoes** in boiling water for 3 minutes, then drain.
5. Arrange the potatoes in a single layer on paper towels and pat dry.
6. Cover the bottom of a mold with foil and coat the bottom with **1 tbsp mushroom mix** and **2 tsp parmesan cheese**.
7. Layer the potatoes, drizzle with **3/4 tsp olive oil**, season with **salt and pepper**, and repeat to create 8 total layers, ending with potatoes on top.
8. Drizzle the final layer with olive oil.

### Step 3: Baking
9. Bake at 400°F until golden brown, pressing down with a spatula every 15 minutes to compress the layers until tender and crisp.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the charlotte a few hours in advance, keeping it tightly covered before baking at 400°F.
