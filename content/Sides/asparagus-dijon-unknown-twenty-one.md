# Asparagus Dijon

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty One | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *Tender asparagus spears are paired with a creamy, tangy Dijon and sour cream sauce. This simple yet elegant side dish comes together in minutes.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 10 mins | None | 6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Saucepan
* Small mixing bowl

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 lbs | [[Asparagus]] | Fresh spears or 10 oz frozen |
| 1/3 cup | [[Sour Cream]] | None |
| 1/4 cup | [[Mayonnaise]] | None |
| 2 tbsp | [[Green Onions]] | Sliced |
| 1 tbsp | [[Dijon Mustard]] | None |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Lemon Peel]] | Grated |
| As needed | [[Milk]] | Optional, for thinning |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation
1. Cook the **2 lbs fresh asparagus** until tender-crisp, then drain and keep warm.

### Step 2: Sauce and Assembly
2. In a small bowl, mix the **1/3 cup sour cream**, **1/4 cup mayo**, **2 tbsp sliced green onions**, **1 tbsp Dijon**, and **1/4 tsp grated lemon peel**.
3. Thin the sauce with a little **Milk, optional** if it is too thick.
4. Spoon the prepared sauce over the warm asparagus until evenly coated.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The sauce can be prepared a day in advance and stored in the refrigerator until ready to use.
