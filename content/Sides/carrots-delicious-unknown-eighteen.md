# Carrots Delicious

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Eighteen | ✍️ **Attribution:** Ann Wagner | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A delightful and tangy marinated carrot and onion side dish that stores wonderfully in the refrigerator. Perfect for gatherings and easily prepared ahead of time.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 15 mins | Overnight | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Saucepan
* Colander
* Glass container

| 1 medium | [[Onion]] | Thinly sliced |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparing the Carrots
1. Cook **2 lb carrots** (cut up and sliced) in salted water. Bring to a boil, remove from heat, and let **carrots** sit in boiling water for **10 minutes**; drain and cool until tender-crisp.

### Step 2: Making the Dressing and Marinating
2. Combine **1 can tomato soup**, **1/2 cup oil**, **1 cup sugar**, **3/4 cup vinegar**, **1 tsp dry mustard**, **1 tsp Worcestershire sauce**, **1 tsp salt**, and **1/4 tsp pepper** to form the dressing.
3. Alternate layers of the cooked **carrots** and **1 medium-sized onion** (thin sliced) in a container.
4. Pour the dressing over the top and let stand overnight in a closed container in the refrigerator.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Will keep up to 2 weeks in the refrigerator when stored in a closed container.
