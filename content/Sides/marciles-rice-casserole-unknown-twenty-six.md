# Marcile's Rice Casserole

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Marcile | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A savory baked rice casserole featuring consomme, ripe olives, and parmesan cheese. This comforting side dish bakes uncovered until tender and golden.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 50 mins | None | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Casserole dish
* Skillet

---
## Ingredients

### Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 cup | [[Margarine]] | Melted |
| 1/3 cup | [[Onion]] | Chopped |
| 1/2 cup | [[Celery]] | Chopped |
| 1 cup | [[Rice]] | Regular raw rice |
| 1 cup | [[Consomme]] |  |
| 1 2/3 cups | [[Water]] |  |
| 1 cup | [[Ripe Olives]] |  |
| 1/2 cup | [[Parmesan Cheese]] |  |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation
1. Melt the **1/2 cup margarine**, add the **1/3 cup onion** and **1/2 cup celery**, and sauté until tender.
2. Add the **1 cup rice** and stir well.

### Step 2: Assembly and Baking
3. Put the mixture into a casserole dish.
4. Add the **1 2/3 cups water** to the **1 cup consomme**, then add to the casserole along with the **1 cup ripe olives** and **1/2 cup parmesan cheese**.
5. Bake uncovered at 350°F for **40 to 60 mins** until the rice is tender and the liquid is absorbed.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole ingredients ahead of time and refrigerate; bake just before serving.
