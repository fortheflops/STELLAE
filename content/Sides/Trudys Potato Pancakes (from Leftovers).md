# Trudy's Potato Pancakes (from Leftovers)

> 📜 **Collection:** LOC | ✍️ **Attribution:** Trudy | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A clever and delicious recipe from Trudy for turning leftover mashed potatoes into savory pancakes. Enriched with an egg yolk and a hint of nutmeg, they're fried in butter until golden and crisp.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 10 mins | None | 6-8 pancakes |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* medium bowl
* large skillet

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Approx. 2 cups | [[Leftover Mashed Potatoes]] | cold |
| 1 | [[Egg Yolk]] | large |
| 1 Tbsp | [[Flour]] | all-purpose |
| ¼ tsp | [[Nutmeg]] | ground |
| As needed | [[Butter]] | for frying |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mix and Shape
1. In a medium bowl, combine the **approx. 2 cups cold leftover mashed potatoes**, **1 egg yolk**, **¼ tsp ground nutmeg**, and **1 Tbsp flour**. Mix until just combined.
2. Form the mixture into small, flat patties.

### Step 2: Fry and Serve
3. In a large skillet, melt a tablespoon of **butter** over medium-high heat.
4. Carefully place the potato pancakes in the skillet and fry until golden brown and crisp, about **3-5 minutes** per side.
5. Serve hot.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Leftover cooked pancakes can be stored in the refrigerator for up to 2 days and reheated in a skillet until crisp.
