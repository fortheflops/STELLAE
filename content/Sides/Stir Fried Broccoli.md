# Stir Fried Broccoli

> *A vibrant and crisp stir-fried broccoli dish tossed with aromatic garlic, ginger, and a touch of sugar. Perfect as a quick and healthy side for any meal.*

### 🔪 Key Equipment

## Instructions

### Step 1: Blanching the Broccoli
1. To precook **3-4 cups broccoli**, drop into a large quantity of rapidly **boiling salted water** and cook for **3-4 minutes** until just tender when pierced with a fork.
2. Drain the **broccoli** and cool quickly with cold water, then drain again and set aside.

### Step 2: Stir-Frying
3. Before starting to cook, prepare all remaining ingredients and have them within reach of your range.
4. Heat a **10" or larger frying pan or wok** over high heat, then put in **approx. 1 tbsp salad oil**.
5. As soon as the oil is hot enough to ripple when the pan is tipped, add **½ - 1 tsp fresh ginger** (if used) and **1-2 cloves garlic**, quickly stir with a spatula until browning starts (about **30 seconds**).
6. Put in the **broccoli**, **1 tbsp water**, **1 tsp sugar**, and **½ tsp salt**.
7. Keep turning with a spatula until heated through and vibrant green (about **1-2 minutes**).
8. Turn out onto a warm serving dish and garnish with **toasted sesame seed** or **toasted chopped nuts**, if desired.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The broccoli can be blanched ahead of time and stored in the refrigerator for up to 24 hours before stir-frying.
