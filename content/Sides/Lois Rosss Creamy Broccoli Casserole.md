# Lois Ross's Creamy Broccoli Casserole

> 📜 **Collection:** General Archive | ✍️ **Attribution:** Lois Ross | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A classic and comforting broccoli casserole from Lois Ross, featuring tender broccoli, crunchy water chestnuts, and a rich, cheesy custard made with soup and mayonnaise.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 40 mins | None | 6-8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Oven
* Casserole dish
* Large mixing bowl
* Steamer basket

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 bunch | [[Broccoli]] | Cut up fine and steamed |
| 1 can | [[Water Chestnuts]] | Sliced, drained |
| ½ Medium | [[Onion]] | Chopped |
| 1 can | [[Cream of Mushroom Soup]] | 10.5 oz |
| ¾ cup | [[Mayonnaise]] | None |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] | Beaten |
| 1 cup | [[Cheddar Cheese]] | Grated |
| As needed | [[Bread Crumbs]] | Optional |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation
1. Preheat oven to **375°F**. Grease a baking dish.

### Step 2: Mixing the Casserole
2. In a large bowl, mix together the steamed **broccoli**, drained **water chestnuts**, **chopped onion**, **cream of mushroom soup**, **mayonnaise**, beaten **eggs**, and grated **cheddar cheese** until thoroughly combined.

### Step 3: Baking
3. Pour the mixture into the greased baking dish and sprinkle **bread crumbs** on top, if desired.
4. Bake for **30-45 minutes**, or until the casserole is hot, bubbly, and set in the center.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole up to a day in advance, cover tightly, and refrigerate until ready to bake, adding a few extra minutes to the baking time if chilled.
