# Baked Beans

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Nine | ✍️ **Attribution:** Cheri | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A classic homestyle baked beans recipe featuring pork and beans sweetened with brown sugar and ketchup, topped with crispy bacon. This hearty side dish bakes slowly until rich, thick, and bubbling.*

* Skillet

| 1 c. | [[Brown Sugar]] | packed |

---

## Instructions

### Preparation and Baking
1. Preheat your oven to **300°**.
2. In a mixing bowl, combine **3 - 28 oz. cans of pork and beans**, **1 tsp. minced onion**, **1 tsp. spicy brown mustard**, **1 c. packed brown sugar**, **3/4 c. + 1 Tbsp. ketchup**, and **5 slices of crispy cooked bacon - crumbled**.
3. Mix all ingredients thoroughly and place the mixture into a **3 qt. baking dish**.
4. Bake at **300°** for **2 hr.** until hot, bubbling, and thickened to your liking.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the bean mixture in the baking dish a day ahead, cover, and refrigerate until ready to bake; top with freshly crumbled bacon before cooking.
