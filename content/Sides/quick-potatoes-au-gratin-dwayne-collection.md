# Quick Potatoes Au Gratin

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A rich and creamy potato casserole made with convenient frozen hash browns, melted Velveeta, and a sharp cheese topping. Perfect for feeding a crowd at holiday gatherings or potlucks.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 1 hr | None | 12 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 9x13-inch baking dish
* Saucepan
* Measuring cups
* Measuring spoons

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 pt | [[Half & Half]] | referred to as cream |
| 1/2 lb. | [[Butter]] | or oleo |
| 2/3 lb. | [[Velveeta Cheese]] | cubed for easier melting |
| 4 (12 oz) pkgs | [[Hash Browns]] | frozen, thawed |
| 1 pkg | [[Sharp Cheddar Cheese]] | grated |
| To taste | [[Chives]] | chopped |
| Optional | [[Pimento]] | or green pepper, chopped |

---

## Instructions

### Preparation and Baking
1. Preheat oven to **350°F** and grease a **9x13-inch baking dish**.
2. In a saucepan over low heat, combine the **1 pt half & half**, **2/3 lb. Velveeta cheese**, and **1/2 lb. butter**. Heat, stirring frequently, until the cheese and butter are completely melted and the mixture is smooth and creamy.
3. Arrange the **4 (12 oz) pkgs thawed hash browns** evenly in the prepared **9x13-inch baking dish**. If desired, mix in the optional **pimento** or **green pepper**.
4. Pour the warm cheese and cream mixture evenly over the **hash browns**.
5. Sprinkle the top with **1 pkg grated sharp cheese** and **chives**.
6. Bake for **1 hour** at **350°F** until the potatoes are tender, the sauce is bubbling, and the cheese on top is melted and golden brown.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Can be assembled ahead of time, covered, and refrigerated overnight. Add 10-15 minutes to the baking time if baking straight from the refrigerator.
