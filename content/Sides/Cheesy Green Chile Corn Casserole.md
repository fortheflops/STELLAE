# Cheesy Green Chile Corn Casserole

> 📜 **Collection:** UK15 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A classic, savory corn casserole with a spoonbread-like texture, packed with creamed corn, cheddar cheese, and a mild kick from green chiles. This comforting side dish bakes up golden and set in the center.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 50 mins | None | 8-10 servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 11x7x1.5 inch baking dish
* Large bowl
* Small bowl
* Whisk

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 (16 ounce) cans | [[Cream Corn]] | Uncooked |
| 2 cups | [[Cheddar Cheese]] | Shredded |
| 1 (4 oz) can | [[Green Chiles]] | Chopped |
| 1/2 cup | [[Onion]] | Finely chopped |
| 1 cup | [[Milk]] | Standard |
| 2 large | [[Eggs]] | Lightly beaten |
| 1 cup | [[Corn Meal]] | Yellow |
| 1/2 teaspoon | [[Garlic Salt]] | Fine |
| 1/2 teaspoon | [[Soda]] | Baking soda |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Mix Wet Ingredients
1. Preheat the oven to **350°F**. Spray an **11x7x1.5 inch baking dish** with non-stick spray.
2. In a large bowl, combine the **2 (16 ounce) cans cream corn**, **2 cups shredded cheddar cheese**, **1 (4 oz) can chopped green chiles**, **1/2 cup finely chopped onion**, **1 cup milk**, and **2 large lightly beaten eggs**. Stir well until fully incorporated.

### Step 2: Combine and Bake
3. In a separate small bowl, whisk together the **1 cup yellow corn meal**, **1/2 teaspoon garlic salt**, and **1/2 teaspoon baking soda**.
4. Stir the dry corn meal mixture into the wet corn mixture until just combined and smooth.
5. Pour the batter evenly into the prepared baking dish.
6. Bake for **50 minutes**, or until the casserole is set in the center and a knife inserted into the middle comes out clean and golden.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole up to a few hours in advance, cover, and refrigerate until ready to bake, adding a few extra minutes to the baking time if necessary.
