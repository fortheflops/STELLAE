# Green Bean and Cheddar Casserole

> 📜 **Collection:** Patsy Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A delightful and savory casserole featuring fresh green beans, mushrooms, and sharp cheddar cheese. Perfectly baked until bubbly and tender with a golden finish.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 35 mins | 5 mins | 6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 10x1.5-inch baking pan
* Saucepan
* Blender
* Mixing bowl

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 8 oz | [[Fresh green beans]] | Cut length-wise into strips |
| 1 can (4 oz) | [[Mushrooms]] | Stems and pieces, drained |
| 1/2 cup | [[Onion]] | Chopped |
| 2 cloves | [[Garlic]] | Crushed |
| 1 cup | [[Cheddar cheese]] | Shredded |
| 1 1/2 cups | [[Milk]] | Liquid |
| 1/4 cup | [[Biscuit baking mix]] | Dry |
| 3 | [[Eggs]] | Whole |
| 1 tsp | [[Salt]] | Standard |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Black pepper]] | Ground |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Boil
1. Heat oven to **400°F**.
2. Grease a **10x1.5-inch** baking pan.
3. Heat **8 oz fresh green beans** and salted water, bring to a boil, and cook until tender.

### Step 2: Mixing and Blending
4. Mix **1 can mushrooms**, **1/2 cup chopped onion**, **2 cloves garlic**, and **1 cup shredded cheddar cheese** in a plate/bowl.
5. Beat remaining ingredients (**1 1/2 cups milk**, **1/4 cup biscuit baking mix**, **3 eggs**, **1 tsp salt**, and **1/4 tsp pepper**) until smooth using a blender.
6. Pour into plate/baking dish and combine with vegetables.

### Step 3: Baking
7. Bake for **30 to 35 minutes** until golden and set.
8. Cool for **5 minutes** before serving until firm and slightly settled.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole components a few hours in advance, cover, and refrigerate before baking.
