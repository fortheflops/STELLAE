# Spinach Cheese Casserole

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty One | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A comforting baked casserole featuring cottage cheese, cheddar, and spinach bound with eggs and flour. Baked until bubbly and set for a rich, savory side dish.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 1 hour | None | 4-6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Baking dish
* Mixing bowl
* Measuring cups
* Measuring spoons

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 pint | [[Cottage Cheese]] | None |
| 3 | [[Eggs]] | None |
| 1/4 cup | [[Oleo]] | Cut into pieces |
| 1/4 lb | [[American Or Cheddar Cheese]] | Cut into pieces |
| 1 pkg. | [[Spinach]] | Defrosted |
| 3 T | [[Flour]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing
1. Combine **1 pint cottage cheese** & **3 eggs**.
2. Cut **1/4 lb American or cheddar cheese** & **1/4 cup oleo** into pieces; add to cottage cheese.
3. Mix in **1 pkg. spinach**, add **3 T flour**, and stir til blended until smooth and cohesive.

### Step 2: Baking
4. Pour mixture into a greased baking dish and bake at 350 degrees for **1 hour** or til set, until the center is firm and the edges are lightly golden.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole ahead of time, cover, and refrigerate for up to 24 hours before baking.
