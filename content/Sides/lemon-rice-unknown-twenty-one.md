# Lemon Rice

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty One | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A bright and aromatic side dish featuring tender rice infused with fresh lemon juice and turmeric. This simple recipe provides a flavorful and colorful accompaniment to a variety of meals.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5 mins | 20 mins | None | 4 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Saucepan
* Microplane
* Citrus juicer

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | [[Lemon]] | Grated peel and squeezed juice |
| 1/4 t | [[Tumeric]] | Ground |
| 1 C | [[Rice]] | Uncooked |
| 2 1/4 c | [[Water]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Boiling and Cooking
1. Heat **juice**, **1/4 t tumeric** in **2 1/4 c water** to boil.
2. Stir in **1 C rice**, cover, and cook until done and tender.
3. When done, stir in the grated **peel** until fragrant and well combined.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftovers in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days. Reheat gently before serving.
