## Instructions

### Step 1: Fry the Rice and Vegetables
1. In a skillet, fry the **½ cup rice** in **bacon fat** until golden brown and fragrant.
2. Add the chopped **celery**, **bell pepper**, and **onion**, sautéing until softened.

### Step 2: Simmer and Serve
3. Stir in the **tomato sauce**, and season with **salt** and **pepper** to taste.
4. Fill the skillet with **water**, ensuring there is enough liquid to cook the rice and create a sauce.
5. Bring the mixture to a boil, then cover and simmer until the rice is tender and the liquid is fully absorbed.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftover Spanish rice in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days.
