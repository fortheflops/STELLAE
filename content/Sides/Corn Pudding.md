# Corn Pudding

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A classic comforting corn pudding baked to a tender, set perfection with a hint of nutmeg. This traditional vegetable casserole is a wonderful addition to any holiday table or family dinner.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 30 mins | None | 4–6 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Whisk
* Buttered baking dish

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 Cups | [[Corn]] | None |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] | Slightly beaten |
| 1 tsp | [[Salt]] | None |
| 2 Tbsp | [[Margarine]] | Melted |
| 2 Tbsp | [[Sugar]] | None |
| ¾ Cup | [[Milk]] | None |
| As needed | [[Nutmeg]] | For sprinkling |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mix and Bake
1. Beat the **2 eggs** slightly in a mixing bowl.
2. Add the **2 cups corn**, **1 tsp salt**, **2 Tbsp melted margarine**, **2 Tbsp sugar**, and **¾ cup milk**, mixing well to combine.
3. Pour the mixture into a buttered baking dish.
4. Bake at **325°F** for **25 to 30 minutes** until set, taking care not to overbake.
5. Test for doneness by inserting a knife blade; it should come out clean when the pudding is fully set.
6. Sprinkle the top lightly with **nutmeg** before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Cover and refrigerate unbaked or baked corn pudding for up to 1 day before reheating or baking.
