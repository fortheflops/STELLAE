# Creamed Corn

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Susan LaFreniere | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *Rich homemade creamed corn baked with a savory parmesan cheese crust until golden brown and bubbly. A comforting classic side dish from Susan LaFreniere's kitchen.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 20 mins | None | 6–8 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Small saucepan
* Skillet or saucepan
* Casserole dish
* Oven broiler

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 20-ounce bag | [[Frozen Corn]] | Thawed |
| 1 cup | [[Whip Cream]] | Unwhipped |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Salt]] | To taste |
| 1 tablespoon | [[Sugar]] | None |
| 2 tablespoons | [[Butter]] | None |
| 2 tablespoons | [[Flour]] | None |
| 1 small bag | [[Parmesan Cheese]] | Grated |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Simmer Corn and Cream
1. Thaw the **1 20-ounce bag frozen corn** completely.
2. In a saucepan, combine the thawed corn and **1 cup whip cream - unwhipped** and simmer for **5 minutes**.
3. Stir in the **1 teaspoon salt** and **1 tablespoon sugar**.

### Step 2: Prepare Roux and Thicken
4. In a separate pan, melt the **2 tablespoons butter** and stir in the **2 tablespoons flour** to form a smooth paste.
5. Add the flour and butter mixture to the corn, cooking and stirring continuously until the mixture is thick and bubbly.

### Step 3: Assemble and Broil
6. Transfer the creamed corn mixture into a casserole dish.
7. Top evenly with the **1 small bag parmesan cheese**.
8. Broil in the oven until the top is brown, bubbly, and fragrant.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole up to the baking step a few hours in advance, cover, and refrigerate until ready to top with cheese and broil.
