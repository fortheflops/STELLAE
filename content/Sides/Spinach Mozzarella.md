# Spinach Mozzarella

> 📜 **Collection:** Patsy's Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A delightful and comforting baked vegetable dish featuring savory spinach, onions, white sauce, and melted mozzarella cheese. This classic side dish is simple to prepare and bakes to golden, bubbly perfection.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 20 mins | None | 6 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large skillet
* Large baking dish
* Spoon or spatula

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 lb | [[Spinach]] | Cooked |
| ½ | [[White Onion]] | Chopped |
| ⅓ Tsp | [[Sage]] |  |
| ¼ Tsp | [[Oregano]] |  |
| 1 Tsp | [[Salt]] |  |
| 1 Tsp | [[Pepper]] |  |
| 1 Cup | [[White Sauce]] |  |
| ½ lb | [[Mozzarella Cheese]] | Sliced |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Sauté
1. Sauté the **½ white onion** and **1 lb cooked spinach** in a skillet until tender and fragrant.

### Step 2: Mix Seasonings
2. Mix the **1 cup white sauce** with the **⅓ tsp sage**, **¼ tsp oregano**, **1 tsp salt**, and **1 tsp pepper** until well combined.

### Step 3: Assemble and Bake
3. Put the sautéed **1 lb spinach** and **½ white onion** in a large baking dish.
4. Pour the seasoned **1 cup white sauce** evenly on top of the spinach mixture.
5. Place the **½ lb mozzarella cheese** slices over the top.
6. Place in a preheated **350°F** oven until the cheese melts and turns bubbly and golden brown.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole ahead of time, cover tightly, and refrigerate for up to 24 hours before baking.
