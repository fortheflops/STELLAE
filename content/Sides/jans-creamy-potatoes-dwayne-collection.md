# Jan's Creamy Potatoes

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Jan Batchelor | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A rich and savory potato casserole featuring a unique blend of cottage cheese and sour cream. This comforting side dish is finished with a golden, toasted almond topping.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 35 mins | None | 6-8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Baking dish
* Sieve or blender

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 4 cups | [[Potatoes]] | Cooked, mashed, about 6 medium |
| 2 tsp | [[Salt]] | For cooking potatoes |
| 2 1/2 tsp | [[Salt]] | Additional |
| 1/8 tsp | [[White Pepper]] | Black pepper may be substituted |
| 3 cups | [[Cottage Cheese]] | Creamed style |
| 3/4 cup | [[Sour Cream]] | None |
| 1 1/2 tbsp | [[Onion]] | Grated |
| To taste | [[Butter]] | Melted |
| 1/2 cup | [[Almonds]] | Toasted |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation
1. Thoroughly mash the **4 cups potatoes** without adding milk or butter.
2. Process the **3 cups cottage cheese** through a sieve or blender until smooth, or use directly from the carton for a chunkier texture.
3. Combine the warm **4 cups potatoes** and **3 cups cottage cheese** in a large bowl.

### Step 2: Assembly and Baking
4. Stir in the **3/4 cup sour cream**, **1 1/2 tbsp onion**, **2 1/2 tsp salt**, and **1/8 tsp white pepper** until well combined.
5. Transfer to a baking dish and brush the top with **melted butter**.
6. Bake at 350°F for 30 minutes.
7. Sprinkle with **1/2 cup almonds** and place under the broiler for a few minutes until the surface is lightly browned and fragrant.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The potato mixture can be prepared and assembled in the baking dish ahead of time; keep refrigerated until ready to bake.
