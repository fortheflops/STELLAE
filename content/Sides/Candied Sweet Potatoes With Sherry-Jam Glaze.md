# Candied Sweet Potatoes With Sherry-Jam Glaze

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Glaze
1. In a saucepan, combine the **1 cup cream sherry**, **1/2 cup apricot jam** (or **peach jam**), **1/2 cup honey**, **1 dash nutmeg** (or **cinnamon**), and **1 tablespoon cornstarch**.
2. Cook over low heat, stirring constantly, until the mixture has thickened into a glossy syrup.

### Step 2: Parboil and Prep Potatoes
3. Parboil the **sweet potatoes** for about **20 minutes**, or until they are just tender enough to be pierced with a fork.
4. Let the potatoes cool completely, then peel them and cut them in half lengthwise.
5. Place the **sweet potato** halves, flat side down, in a small, well-buttered baking pan.

### Step 3: Bake and Glaze
6. Spoon the prepared sherry-jam glaze over the **sweet potato** halves, ensuring they are well-coated.
7. Bake in a preheated oven at **350°F** for **20 minutes**.
8. After **20 minutes**, turn the **sweet potato** halves over and spoon more of the glaze on top. Continue baking until caramelized and fragrant.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The glaze can be prepared up to 2 days in advance and stored in the refrigerator; reheat slightly before using.
