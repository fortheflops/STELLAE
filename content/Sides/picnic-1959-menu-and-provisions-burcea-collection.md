## Instructions

### Preparation And Assembly
1. Organize the attendees including Vic, Jerry, Marlene, Pete, Cel Larsen, Briscoes, Rogers, Donna, and Baileys for a total headcount of **16** people.
2. Prepare the **33 brats** and **14 weiners** alongside **2 1/2 doz rolls** for the grill.
3. Portion out **5 lbs potato salad**, **4 cans beans**, and a **large bowl tossed salad** into serving dishes.
4. Arrange the relishes including **black olives**, **pickles**, **radishes**, and **carrots** on a platter.
5. Set out dressings including **sour cream**, **vinegar and oil**, and **French dressing**.
6. Serve the **Strawberry Nabisco Ice Box Cake** for dessert once chilled to a firm, sliceable consistency.
7. Pour **3 qts coffee** and **2 qts lemonade** for the drink department, adjusting cocktails with gin or whiskey as noted.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Prepare potato salad, tossed salad, and the ice box cake ahead of time and keep chilled in the ice box until ready to serve at the picnic.
