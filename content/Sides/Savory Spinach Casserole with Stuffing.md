# Savory Spinach Casserole with Stuffing

> 📜 **Collection:** General Archive | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A classic and comforting side dish, this savory spinach casserole combines spinach, cheese, and seasoned stuffing in a creamy base of either mushroom soup or cottage cheese.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 30 mins | None | 6-8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Casserole dish
* Large mixing bowl
* Measuring cups

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 (10 oz) pkgs. | [[Spinach]] | Frozen, thawed & squeezed dry |
| 1 Can | [[Cream of Mushroom Soup]] | Or substitute with cottage cheese |
| 1 Cup | [[Cottage Cheese]] | Alternative base option |
| 1 Small | [[Onion]] | Chopped |
| 1 Cup | [[Cheddar Cheese]] | Shredded |
| ½ Cup | [[Butter]] | Melted |
| 1 Cup | [[Seasoned Stuffing]] | Dry |
| 2-3 | [[Eggs]] | Beaten, optional |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation
1. Preheat oven to **350°F (175°C)**. Grease a casserole dish.

### Step 2: Mixing the Casserole
2. Thaw the **2 (10 oz) pkgs. spinach** and squeeze it to remove as much water as possible.
3. In a large bowl, combine the drained spinach, **1 can cream of mushroom soup** (or **1 cup cottage cheese**), **1 small onion**, **1 cup cheddar cheese**, **½ cup butter**, and **1 cup seasoned stuffing**.
4. If using, add the **2-3 eggs** and stir well until all ingredients are evenly combined.

### Step 3: Baking
5. Pour the mixture into the greased casserole dish and spread evenly.
6. Bake for **30 minutes**, or until heated through, bubbly around the edges, and golden brown and fragrant.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole ahead of time and store sealed in the refrigerator for up to 24 hours before baking.
