# Carol'S Potatoes

> 📜 **Collection:** LOC | ✍️ **Attribution:** Carol | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A comforting, vintage potato casserole featuring a rich blend of sour cream, cream of chicken soup, and sharp cheddar cheese. Baked to bubbly perfection, this makes an effortless and crowd-pleasing side dish.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 1 hour 30 mins | None | 6–8 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Baking dish
* Mixing bowl
* Measuring cups

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 lbs | [[Potatoes]] | Cubed |
| ½ Cup | [[Sour Cream]] | Standard |
| ½ Cup | [[Condensed Cream of Chicken Soup]] | Undiluted |
| ½ Cup | [[Green Onion]] | Sliced or chopped |
| 2 Cups | [[Cheddar Cheese]] | Shredded |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mix and Bake
1. Preheat your oven to **350°F**.
2. In a large mixing bowl, combine the **2 lbs potatoes**, **½ cup sour cream**, **½ cup condensed cream of chicken soup**, **½ cup green onion**, and **2 cups cheddar cheese**, stirring well to coat the [[Potatoes]].
3. Transfer the mixture to a greased baking dish and bake at **350°** for **1 ½ hours** until the potatoes are tender and the top is golden brown and bubbly.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole up to a day in advance, cover tightly, and store in the refrigerator until ready to bake.
