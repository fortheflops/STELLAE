# Glazed Apricots

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A simple and elegant side dish featuring whole apricots simmered in a sweet, buttery glaze. Perfect for pairing with roasted poultry for a sophisticated flavor contrast.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5 mins | 15 mins | None | 6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Skillet
* Spoon

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 cans | [[Apricots]] | Whole, unpeeled, drained |
| 1/2 cup | [[Granulated Sugar]] | None |
| 1/2 cup | [[Brown Sugar]] | None |
| 1/2 cup | [[Apricot Syrup]] | Reserved from cans |
| 1/4 cup | [[Butter]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Glazing
1. Place the **2 cans whole unpeeled apricots** into a skillet.
2. Add the **1/2 cup granulated sugar**, **1/2 cup brown sugar**, **1/2 cup apricot syrup**, and **1/4 cup butter**.
3. Cook over moderate heat, turning the **apricots** often with a spoon until they are well glazed and the sauce has thickened.
4. Serve hot alongside chicken.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** These are best served immediately while hot to maintain the glossy texture of the glaze.
