# Spinach Mozarella

> 📜 **Collection:** Patsy Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A comforting baked casserole featuring savory spinach layered with white sauce, aromatic herbs, and melted mozzarella cheese. Perfect as a rich side dish for your family dinners.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 25 mins | None | 6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Skillet
* Large baking dish

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 lb. | [[Spinach]] | Cooked |
| 1/2 | [[White Onion]] | Chopped |
| 1/3 tsp. | [[Sage]] | None |
| 1/4 tsp. | [[Oregano]] | None |
| 1 tsp. | [[Salt]] | None |
| 1 tsp. | [[Pepper]] | None |
| 1 c. | [[White Sauce]] | None |
| 1/2 lb. | [[Mozarella Cheese]] | Sliced |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and Assembly
1. Saute the **1/2 white onion** and **1 lb. cooked spinach**.
2. Mix the **1 c. white sauce** with **1/3 tsp. sage**, **1/4 tsp. oregano**, **1 tsp. salt**, and **1 tsp. pepper**.
3. Put the spinach and onion mixture into a large baking dish.
4. Pour the seasoned white sauce evenly on top.
5. Arrange the **1/2 lb. mozarella cheese** slices over the sauce.

### Step 2: Baking
6. Place in a preheated **350°** oven until the cheese melts and bubbles gently until golden.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole up to a day in advance, cover tightly, and refrigerate before baking.
