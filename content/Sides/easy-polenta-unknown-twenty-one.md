# Easy Polenta

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty One | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *Discover a foolproof method for making creamy baked polenta with minimal effort. This simple recipe requires just cornmeal, water, and a touch of oil or butter.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5 mins | 50 mins | None | 4 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Baking dish
* Mixing bowl

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 C | [[Corn Meal]] | Standard grind |
| 4 C | [[Water]] | Room temperature |
| 2 T | [[Olive Oil]] | Alternative: butter |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Initial Bake
1. Mix the **1 C corn meal** and **4 C water** together in a baking dish.
2. Bake at 350 degrees F for **45 min** until bubbling and starting to thicken.

### Step 2: Finish
3. Remove from the oven and add the **2 T olive oil** or butter, mixing well until fully incorporated.
4. Return to the oven for **4-5 minutes** until hot and fragrant.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Polenta can be mixed ahead of time in the baking dish and refrigerated before baking, though baking time may need a slight extension.
