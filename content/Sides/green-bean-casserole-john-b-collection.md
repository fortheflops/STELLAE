# Green Bean Casserole

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A classic, comforting green bean casserole featuring tender cut beans, creamy mushroom soup, and a crunchy topping. This timeless holiday favorite comes together quickly for a satisfying side dish.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 20 mins | None | 6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Casserole dish
* Mixing bowl

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 pkg | [[Frozen cut beans]] | Thawed or cooked |
| 1 tsp | [[Salt]] | To taste |
| 1/2 cup | [[Milk]] | None |
| 1 can | [[Cream of mushroom soup]] | Condensed |
| 1/8 tsp | [[Black pepper]] | Ground |
| 2 tbsp | [[Dried pimento]] | Optional |
| 1 cup (3 1/2 oz) | [[French fried onions]] | Divided |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Assembly and Baking
1. In a mixing bowl, combine the **2 pkg frozen cut beans**, **1 tsp salt**, **1/2 cup milk**, **1 can cream of mushroom soup**, **1/8 tsp black pepper**, **2 tbsp dried pimento**, and half of the **1 cup (3 1/2 oz) French fried onions**.
2. Transfer the mixture to a casserole dish and bake at **350 degrees F** for **20 minutes** until bubbly and hot, topping with the remaining onions during the last few minutes until golden and crisp.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole up to a day in advance without the onion topping, covering and refrigerating until ready to bake.
