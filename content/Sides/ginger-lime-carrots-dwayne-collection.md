# Ginger-Lime Carrots

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A vibrant and zesty side dish featuring tender carrots glazed in a sweet and tangy ginger-lime butter sauce. Perfect for holiday dinners or a bright weeknight side.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 17 mins | None | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large pan
* Small saucepan
* Colander
* Serving bowl

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3 pounds | [[Carrots]] | pared, cut diagonally into 1/4-inch slices |
| 3 cups | [[Water]] | None |
| 1 1/2 teaspoons | [[Salt]] | None |
| 3 tablespoons | [[Butter]] | None |
| 2 tablespoons | [[Honey]] | None |
| 1 tablespoon | fresh [[Ginger]] root | None |
| 2 tablespoons | fresh [[Lime Juice]] | None |
| 1 tablespoon | grated [[Lime Peel]] | None |
| As needed | [[Lime]] slices | for garnish |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Cook the Carrots
1. In a large pan, heat the **3 pounds carrots**, **3 cups water**, and **1 1/2 teaspoons salt** to boiling. Reduce heat and simmer uncovered until the **carrots** are crisp-tender, about **10 minutes**. Drain and return the **carrots** to the saucepan.

### Step 2: Prepare Glaze and Combine
2. While the **carrots** are cooking, heat the **3 tablespoons butter**, **2 tablespoons honey**, **2 tablespoons fresh lime juice**, **1 tablespoon grated lime peel**, and **1 tablespoon fresh ginger root** in a small saucepan over low heat until the **butter** melts and the mixture boils, about **5 minutes**.
3. Stir the **butter** mixture into the cooked **carrots**. Heat the **carrots** over low heat, stirring constantly, until they are beautifully glazed and shiny, about **2 minutes**.
4. Transfer to a serving bowl and garnish with **lime slices** before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Carrots can be pared and sliced up to 24 hours in advance and stored in cold water in the refrigerator. Drain thoroughly before cooking.
