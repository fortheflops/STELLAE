# Linda Brown's Green Chile Corn Casserole

> 📜 **Collection:** General Archive | ✍️ **Attribution:** Linda Brown | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A rich and savory corn casserole featuring both creamed and whole kernel corn, green chiles, and two types of cheese. This spoonbread-style dish is a perfect, hearty side with a zesty southwestern kick.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 45 mins | None | 8-10 servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 9x13 inch casserole dish
* Large mixing bowl
* Small mixing bowl
* Measuring cups
* Measuring spoons
* Mixing spoon

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |

| 1 Can | [[Whole Yellow Corn]] | approx. 15 oz, drained |
| 1 (4 Ounce) Can | [[Green Chilies]] | or 8 oz to preference |
| 1/4 Cup | [[Onion]] | chopped |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Corn Meal]] | yellow |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] | beaten |
| 1/2 Teaspoon | [[Garlic Powder]] | none |
| 1 Cup | [[Monterey Jack Cheese]] | shredded |
| 1 Cup | [[Sharp Cheddar Cheese]] | shredded |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Salad Oil]] | none |
| 1 Tablespoon | [[Sugar]] | none |
| 2 Teaspoon | [[Vinegar]] | none |
| 2 Cups | [[Evaporated Milk]] | none |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Mix
1. Preheat oven to **350°F**. Spray a **9x13 inch casserole dish** with non-stick spray.
2. In a large bowl, mix together the **2 cans yellow cream corn**, **1 can whole yellow corn**, **1 can green chilies**, **1/4 cup chopped onion**, **1/2 cup yellow corn meal**, **2 beaten eggs**, **1/2 teaspoon garlic powder**, **1/2 cup salad oil**, **1 tablespoon sugar**, **2 teaspoon vinegar**, and **2 cups evaporated milk** **except the cheeses**.
3. In a small bowl, toss the **1 cup Monterey Jack cheese** and **1 cup sharp cheddar cheese** together.

### Step 2: Assemble and Bake
4. Pour **half of the corn mixture** into the **9x13 inch casserole dish**.
5. Sprinkle **half of the cheese mixture** over this layer.
6. Pour the **other half of the corn mixture** on top.
7. Add the **remaining cheese** over the top.
8. Bake for about **45 minutes**, or until the casserole is set in the center and the cheese is golden and bubbly.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole a few hours in advance, cover tightly, and refrigerate until ready to bake; add a few extra minutes to the baking time if baking straight from the fridge.
