# Misty Chili with Sweet Potatoes

> 📜 **Collection:** UK15 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Moonlite | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A comforting vintage casserole featuring tender sweet potatoes baked in a sweet vanilla butter glaze and topped with toasted marshmallows. This classic side dish offers a delightful balance of rich and sweet flavors.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 20 mins | None | 4-6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Medium saucepan
* Baking dish
* Small bowl
* Colander

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 4 medium | [[Sweet Potatoes]] | Peeled and sliced |
| 1 cup | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Vanilla]] | Extract |
| 3 tablespoons | [[Butter]] | Melted |
| As Needed | [[Marshmallows]] | For topping |
| 1 cup (Optional) | [[Cream]] or [[Pet Milk]] | Can be used as a variation instead of marshmallows |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Boil
1. Peel and slice the **4 medium sweet potatoes**.

### Step 2: Cook and Assemble
2. Boil the potato slices in salted water until tender.
3. Drain the potatoes thoroughly and place them in a baking dish.

### Step 3: Mix and Bake
4. In a small bowl, mix the **1 cup sugar**, **1 teaspoon vanilla**, and **3 tablespoons melted butter**.
5. Pour this mixture evenly over the sweet potatoes in the baking dish.
6. Bake at **400°F** for **15 minutes** until hot and bubbling.

### Step 4: Topping and Finish
7. Cover the top generously with **marshmallows** and return to the oven to brown for about **5 more minutes** until golden brown and gooey.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** You can assemble the dish ahead of time up to the baking step and refrigerate it for up to 24 hours before baking.
