# Baked Pineapple

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** N. Lake | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A delightful and sweet baked pineapple side dish featuring a rich, creamy base thickened with cornstarch. Perfect when served alongside savory ham for holiday meals or family dinners.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 1 hr | None | 6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 8x8 inch baking pan
* Mixing bowl

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 (16 oz) | [[Pineapple]] | Crushed, not drained |
| 1/2 cup | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 1 | [[Egg]] | Whole |
| 2 tbsp | [[Cornstarch]] | Level |
| 1/4 cup | [[Milk]] | Standard |
| As needed | [[Butter]] | For dotting |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing and Baking
1. Cream the **1/2 cup sugar** and **1 egg** together.
2. Dissolve the **2 tbsp cornstarch** in the **1/4 cup milk**, then add to the sugar and egg mixture.
3. Add the **1 (16 oz) crushed pineapple** and mix well.
4. Place the mixture into an **8x8 pan**. Sprinkle with extra pineapple if desired and dot with **butter**.
5. Bake at 350° for 1 hour or until lightly browned. Do not cover. Watch for bubbles and a golden, lightly browned top.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the dish in the pan ahead of time, cover, and refrigerate for up to a few hours before baking at 350°.
