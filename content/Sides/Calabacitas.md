# Calabacitas

> 📜 **Collection:** UK4 Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Ana | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A classic southwestern vegetable side dish featuring sautéed yellow squash, zucchini, sweet corn, and mild green chiles. Melted cheese and a splash of cream bind the tender summer vegetables together before serving in warm flour tortillas.*

---

| 10 mins | 15 mins | None | 4 Servings |

| 2 medium | [[Yellow Squash]] | diced, equal parts with zucchini |
| 2 medium | [[Zucchini Squash]] | diced, equal parts with yellow squash |
| 1 Can | [[Corn]] | drained |
| 1 Can | [[Green Chiles]] | chopped |
| 1 Dash | [[Half and Half]] | or light cream |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Saute the Vegetables
1. In a large skillet over medium heat, melt **2 Tbsp butter**.
2. Add **1 chopped onion** and saute until soft and translucent, about 3 to 4 minutes.
3. Add equal parts diced **2 medium yellow squash** and **2 medium zucchini squash** to the skillet.

### Step 2: Add Corn, Chiles, and Cream
4. Add **1 can corn**, **1 can chopped green chiles**, and **1 dash half and half**.
5. Stir continuously until the squash softens slightly, about 5 minutes.

### Step 3: Melt Cheese and Serve
6. Sprinkle **1 cup grated cheese** evenly over the vegetable mixture.
7. Cover the skillet with a lid and simmer on low heat until the cheese is completely melted and bubbly and the squash is tender.
8. Spoon the warm calabacitas into warm **flour tortillas**, wrap tightly, and serve warm.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftover calabacitas mixture in a sealed container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days. Reheat gently on the stovetop before wrapping in fresh flour tortillas.
