# Cheezy Carrot Casserole

> 📜 **Collection:** Georgia Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Jan Stevenson | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A comforting and savory side dish featuring tender carrots layered with rich cheddar cheese, topped with sautéed onions and a buttery Ritz cracker crust. Perfect for family gatherings and holiday dinners.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 35 mins | None | 6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Baking dish
* Skillet
* Cheese grater

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5 cups | [[Carrots]] | cooked and sliced |
| 1 | [[Onion]] | diced |
| 1 cup | [[Margarine]] | divided |
| 2 cups | [[Cheddar Cheese]] | shredded |
| 1 cup | [[Ritz Crackers]] | crushed |

---

## Instructions

### Preparation and Baking
1. Preheat oven to **350°F** and grease a baking dish with cooking spray.
2. Dice **1 onion** and sauté in **1 cup margarine** in a skillet until tender.
3. Shred the **2 cups cheddar cheese**.
4. Layer the **5 cups cooked carrots** and the **2 cups cheddar cheese** in the sprayed baking dish.
5. Pour the sautéed **1 cup margarine** and **1 onion** mixture over the top of all layers.
6. Sprinkle **1 cup crushed Ritz crackers** on top.
7. Bake at **350°F** for **35 minutes** until the cheese is melted and bubbly and the cracker topping is golden brown.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This casserole can be assembled ahead of time, covered, and refrigerated. Add the crushed Ritz cracker topping just before baking to ensure it stays crispy.
