# Classic Asparagus Casserole

> 📜 **Collection:** General Archive | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A classic and simple side dish featuring tender canned asparagus covered in a rich, homemade cheese sauce and topped with crunchy, toasted breadcrumbs.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 10 mins | None | 4-6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* small saucepan
* wire whisk
* casserole dish

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 cans | [[Canned Asparagus]] | approx. 15 oz each, drained |
| 2 Tablespoons | [[Butter]] | none |
| 2 Tablespoons | [[Flour]] | none |
| 1 Cup | [[Milk]] | none |
| 1 cup | [[Cheese]] | grated, or to taste |
| 1/4 cup | [[Bread Crumbs]] | toasted |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Make the Cheese Sauce
1. In a small saucepan, melt the **2 tablespoons butter** over medium heat.
2. Whisk in the **2 tablespoons flour** and cook for 1 minute, stirring until fragrant.
3. Gradually whisk in the **1 cup milk**. Continue to cook, stirring constantly, until the sauce thickens and bubbles.
4. Remove the pan from the heat and stir in the **1 cup grated cheese** until it is completely melted and the sauce is smooth.

### Step 2: Assemble the Casserole
5. Gently arrange the drained **2 cans canned asparagus** in a serving dish or casserole.
6. Pour the hot cheese sauce evenly over the asparagus.
7. Sprinkle the **1/4 cup bread crumbs** over the top.
8. Serve immediately while warm, or place under a broiler for 1-2 minutes until golden brown and bubbling.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftover casserole in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days.
