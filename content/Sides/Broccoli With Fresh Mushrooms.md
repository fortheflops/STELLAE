# Broccoli With Fresh Mushrooms

> 📜 **Collection:** Louise Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A vibrant stir-fried side dish featuring tender broccoli florets and sliced mushrooms tossed with shallots and a hint of nutmeg. Perfect for pairing with your favorite Asian-inspired entrees.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Precook Broccoli
1. Drop **3-4 cups cut broccoli** into a large quantity of rapidly **boiling salted water** and cook until just tender when pierced with a fork (approx. **3-4 minutes**).
2. Drain the broccoli and cool quickly under cold running water. Drain again thoroughly and set aside.

### Step 2: Stir-Fry
3. Prepare the **up to ½ lb sliced mushrooms**, **2 chopped shallots or green onions**, **1 Tbsp water**, **1 tsp sugar**, and **½ tsp salt**, and have them within easy reach of your stove.
4. Heat a **10" or larger frying pan or wok** over high heat. Add the **2 Tbsp salad oil** to the hot pan.
5. Add the **up to ½ lb fresh mushrooms** to the hot oil first and stir fry them, tossing frequently, until they are browned and tender.
6. Add the **2 shallots or green onions** and the **dash of ground nutmeg** to the pan. Stir fry for about **30 seconds** until fragrant.
7. Add the **blanched broccoli**, **1 Tbsp water**, **1 tsp sugar**, and **½ tsp salt** to the pan.
8. Keep turning and tossing the ingredients with a spatula until the **broccoli** is heated through and tender-crisp (approx. **1-2 minutes**).
9. Turn the mixture out onto a warm serving dish. Garnish with **toasted sesame seeds** or **toasted chopped nuts**, if desired, and serve immediately.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The broccoli can be blanched ahead of time and stored in the refrigerator for up to 1 day before stir-frying.
