# New England Stuffed Sweet Potatoes

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A classic New England side dish featuring sweet potatoes stuffed with toasted pecans, dried cranberries, and a hint of ginger. Perfectly baked until tender and warm, this dish brings a cozy, sweet, and savory touch to any holiday table.*

## Instructions

### Baking and Stuffing
1. Preheat oven to **375°F**.
2. Wrap **6 sweet potatoes** in foil and bake at **375°F** for **1 hour** or until tender when pierced with a fork.
3. Unwrap **sweet potatoes**, split open each, and scoop out pulp, leaving a **1/4-inch-thick** shell.
4. In a bowl, combine the scooped pulp, **1/3 cup toasted pecans**, **1/4 cup dried cranberries**, **1/4 cup maple syrup**, **1 tbsp grated fresh ginger**, **1/2 tsp salt**, and **1/4 tsp ground red pepper**.
5. Stuff the mixture back into the **sweet potato** shells, place on a baking sheet, and bake at **375°F** for **10 minutes** or until hot.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The sweet potatoes can be baked, scooped, and stuffed up to 24 hours in advance. Store covered in the refrigerator and bake at 375°F for 15-20 minutes until heated through before serving.
