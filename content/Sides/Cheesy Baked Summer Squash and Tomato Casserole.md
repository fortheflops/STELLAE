# Cheesy Baked Summer Squash and Tomato Casserole

> 📜 **Collection:** General Archive | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A simple and comforting baked casserole featuring layers of sliced summer squash, tomatoes, and onions. Finished with a bubbly, melted sharp cheddar cheese topping.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 45 mins | None | 6-8 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Baking dish
* Cutting board
* Chef's knife

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 lbs. | [[Squash]] | yellow summer squash or zucchini, sliced |
| 3 | [[Tomatoes]] | sliced |
| 2 medium | [[Onions]] | sliced |
| 1 tsp | [[Salt]] | none |
| 1/8 tsp | [[Pepper]] | none |
| 2 T. | [[Butter]] or [[Margarine]] | none |
| 1/2 Cup | Sharp [[Cheddar Cheese]] | grated |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Layer the Casserole
1. Preheat your oven to 350°F and grease a baking dish.
2. Wash and slice the **2 lbs. [[Squash]]**, then arrange the slices in the prepared baking dish.
3. Cover the squash evenly with the **3 [[Tomatoes]]** and **2 medium [[Onions]]** slices.

### Step 2: Season and Bake
4. Sprinkle the layers evenly with **1 tsp [[Salt]]** and **1/8 tsp [[Pepper]]**.
5. Dot the top of the vegetables with **2 T. [[Butter]] or [[Margarine]]**, then generously sprinkle with **1/2 Cup sharp [[Cheddar Cheese]]**.
6. Bake at 350°F for about **45 minutes**, or until the vegetables are tender and the cheese is melted and bubbly.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole up to a day in advance and store covered in the refrigerator until ready to bake.
