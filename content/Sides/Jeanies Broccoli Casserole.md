# Jeanie's Broccoli Casserole

> 📜 **Collection:** GailCollection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Jeanie | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A classic and simple broccoli casserole from Jeanie, featuring tender broccoli layered with cheddar cheese and crushed crackers, all baked in a creamy celery soup sauce.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 20 mins | None | 6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Baking dish
* Small bowl
* Can opener

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 bunch | [[Broccoli]] | cooked and drained |
| 1/2 to 1 cup | [[Cheddar Cheese]] | shredded |
| 6 to 8 | [[Crackers]] | crushed |
| 1 can | [[Cream of Celery Soup]] | 10.5 oz |
| 1/2 can | [[Water]] | measured using soup can |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Layer
1. Preheat oven to 350°F. Grease a baking dish.
2. Cook the **1 bunch broccoli** until just barely tender-crisp. Drain well.
3. In the baking dish, create alternating layers of the cooked **broccoli**, shredded **1/2 to 1 cup cheddar cheese**, and crushed **6 to 8 crackers**.

### Step 2: Sauce and Bake
4. In a separate bowl, mix the **1 can cream of celery soup** with **1/2 can water** until smooth.
5. Pour the soup mixture evenly over the layered ingredients in the baking dish.
6. Bake for **20 minutes**, or until the casserole is hot and bubbly.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole ahead of time, cover tightly, and refrigerate for up to 24 hours before baking.
