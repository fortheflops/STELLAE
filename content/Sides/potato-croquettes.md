# Potato Croquettes

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *Crispy golden potato croquettes made with seasoned mashed potatoes, shaped and fried to perfection. A delightful side dish with a wonderful creamy interior and crunchy coating.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 30 mins | 15 mins | 30 mins | 4 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Deep fryer or deep skillet

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3 cups | [[Mashed Potatoes]] | Prepared |
| 2 tbsp | [[Butter]] | Softened |
| 1/8 tsp | [[Cayenne]] | Ground |
| To taste | [[Salt]] | None |
| 1 | [[Egg]] | Or 2 yolks |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Onion Juice]] | Fresh |
| 1 tbsp | [[Chopped Parsley]] | Fresh |
| To taste | [[Pepper]] | Ground |
| As needed | [[Bread Crumbs]] | For rolling |
| As needed | [[Deep Fat]] | For frying |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing and Shaping
1. Beat together **3 cups mashed potatoes**, **2 tbsp butter**, **1/8 tsp cayenne**, **salt to taste**, **1 egg or 2 yolks**, **1/4 tsp onion juice**, **1 tbsp chopped parsley**, and **pepper** until smooth.
2. Cool if necessary, then shape into croquettes.
3. Roll the shaped mixture in crumbs, dip in egg, and roll in crumbs again.

### Step 2: Frying
4. Fry the coated croquettes in **deep fat** until golden brown and crispy.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Croquettes can be shaped and crumbed ahead of time, then refrigerated for up to a few hours before frying.
