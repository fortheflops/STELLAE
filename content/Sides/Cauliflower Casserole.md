# Cauliflower Casserole

> 📜 **Collection:** Jill Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A simple baked cauliflower casserole featuring layers of tender cauliflower, a creamy homemade white sauce with peppers and mushrooms, and melted cheese, all topped with a sprinkle of paprika.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 25 mins | None | 6-8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 1 1/2-quart casserole dish
* Pot
* Saucepan
* Wire whisk

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 medium head | [[Cauliflower]] | broken into florets |
| 2 tablespoons | [[Butter]] | or margarine (oleo) |
| 1/2 cup | [[Green Pepper]] | chopped |
| 1/2 cup | [[Mushrooms]] | sliced |
| 2 tablespoons | [[All-Purpose Flour]] | None |
| 1 1/2 cups | [[Milk]] | None |
| 1 cup | [[Cheese]] | shredded (e.g., Cheddar) |
| To taste | [[Salt]] | None |
| For garnish | [[Paprika]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Cook the Cauliflower
1. Preheat oven to **400°F**. Grease a **1 1/2-quart casserole dish**.
2. In a pot, cook the **1 medium head cauliflower** florets in boiling salted water until tender-crisp. Drain well.

### Step 2: Make the Sauce
3. While the cauliflower is cooking, melt the **2 tablespoons butter** in a saucepan over medium heat.
4. Sauté the **1/2 cup green pepper** and **1/2 cup mushrooms** for about **5 minutes**.
5. Stir the **2 tablespoons all-purpose flour** into the vegetable mixture.
6. Gradually whisk in the **1 1/2 cups milk**. Continue cooking and stirring constantly until the sauce thickens and is smooth.

### Step 3: Assemble and Bake
7. Arrange half of the cooked cauliflower in the bottom of the prepared casserole dish.
8. Sprinkle with half of the **1 cup cheese**, then pour half of the sauce over the top.
9. Repeat with a second layer of the remaining cauliflower, cheese, and sauce.
10. Sprinkle **paprika** over the top.
11. Bake for **15 minutes**, or until the casserole is hot and bubbling.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole ahead of time, cover, and refrigerate for up to 24 hours before baking, adding a few extra minutes to the bake time if needed.
