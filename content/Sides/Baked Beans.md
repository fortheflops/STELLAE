# Baked Beans

> 📜 **Collection:** LouiseCollection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A delightful vintage side dish featuring baked beans combined with cubed luncheon meat, onions, and a surprising touch of canned peach slices. Quick to prepare on the stovetop, it offers a sweet and savory flavor profile.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 15 mins | None | 3–5 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Saucepan
* Wooden spoon

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 Cup | [[Luncheon Meat]] | Cubed |
| ¼ Cup | [[Onion]] | Chopped |
| 2 Tbsp | [[Butter]] or Margarine | Divided |
| 1 Tbsp | [[Ketchup]] | None |
| 1 can (21 oz) | [[Pork & Beans]] | Campbell's brand recommended |
| Generous Dash | [[Cinnamon]] | None |
| ½ Cup | [[Peach Slices]] | Canned, drained suggested |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Brown the Meat and Aromatics
1. In a saucepan, brown the **1 cup cubed luncheon meat** and cook the **¼ cup chopped onion** in **2 tbsp butter or margarine** until tender and translucent.

### Step 2: Simmer and Combine
2. Stir in the **1 tbsp ketchup**, **1 can (21 oz) Campbell's pork & beans**, **generous dash cinnamon**, and **½ cup canned peach slices**.
3. Heat thoroughly, stirring now and then until bubbling and heated through.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftovers in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days.
