# Spicy Beans & Rice

> 📜 **Collection:** Georgia Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A quick and flavorful side dish combining tender rice cooked with zesty picante sauce, cumin, and hearty beans. Perfect as a simple weeknight accompaniment or a base for a Mexican-inspired bowl.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5 mins | 20 mins | None | 4 servings |

* Saucepan
* Measuring cups

* Colander

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 cup | [[Rice]] | uncooked |
| 2/3 cup | [[Picante Sauce]] | used to replace 1/3 of the cooking water |
| 1 1/3 cups | [[Water]] |  |
| 1 tsp. | [[Cumin]] | ground |
| 1 to 2 cups | [[Beans]] | kidney, pinto, or black beans, rinsed and drained |

---

## Instructions

### Preparation
1. In a medium saucepan, combine the **1 cup rice**, **2/3 cup picante sauce**, **1 1/3 cups water**, and **1 tsp. cumin**.
2. Bring the mixture to a boil over medium-high heat, then immediately reduce the heat to low, cover with a tight-fitting lid, and cook as directed on the rice package (approximately 15 to 20 minutes) until the rice is tender and all liquid is fully absorbed.
3. Gently stir in the **1 to 2 cups beans** (kidney, pinto, or black beans). Cover and heat over low for an additional 3 to 5 minutes until the beans are thoroughly warmed through and steaming, then serve hot.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 4 days. Reheat on the stovetop with a splash of water or broth to restore moisture.
