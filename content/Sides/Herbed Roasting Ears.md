# Herbed Roasting Ears

> 📜 **Collection:** General Archive | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A simple and elegant way to prepare corn on the cob, wrapped in romaine leaves with a savory herb butter, and roasted in the oven.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 25 mins | None | 6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Small bowl
* Shallow baking dish
* Aluminum foil

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| ½ Cup | [[Butter]] | Soft, or margarine |
| 1 tsp. | [[Rosemary]] | Dried, crushed |
| ½ tsp. | [[Marjoram]] | Dried, crushed |
| 6 ears | [[Sweet Corn]] | Husked |
| 1 head | [[Romaine]] | Lettuce |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep the Herb Butter and Corn
1. Preheat oven to 450°F (230°C).
2. In a small bowl, blend the **½ cup soft butter** with the **1 tsp. rosemary** and **½ tsp. marjoram** until well combined.
3. Spread the herb butter evenly over all sides of each ear of **6 ears sweet corn**.

### Step 2: Wrap and Roast
4. Separate 12-18 large leaves from the **1 head romaine** lettuce. Wrap each buttered ear of corn in 2 or 3 of the romaine leaves.
5. Place the wrapped corn ears in a shallow baking dish and cover the baking dish tightly with foil.
6. Bake for **20 to 25 minutes**, or until the corn is tender and fragrant.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Corn can be buttered and wrapped in romaine leaves a few hours in advance; keep refrigerated until ready to bake.
