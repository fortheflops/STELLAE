# Darlene's Sour Cream Baked Potatoes

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Darlene | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *Discover a comforting and creamy side dish featuring hash browns, tangy sour cream, and melted cheese, topped with crunchy flakes. Perfect for large gatherings, this easy casserole bakes to golden perfection.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 1 hour | None | 12 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Baking dish
* Mixing bowl

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 lbs | [[Hash Browns]] | Thawed |
| 1 can | [[Cream of Chicken Soup]] |  |
| 1 pt | [[Sour Cream]] | 2 cups |
| 1 | [[Onion]] | Medium, chopped |
| 1 cup | [[Cheddar Cheese]] | Grated, or more to taste |
| 1/2 cup | [[Butter]] | Melted |
| 2 tsp | [[Salt]] |  |
| As needed | [[Chips or Corn Flakes]] | For topping |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Assembly
1. Combine the **2 lbs hash browns**, **1 can cream of chicken soup**, **1 pt sour cream**, **1 medium onion**, **1 cup grated cheddar cheese**, **1/2 cup melted butter**, and **2 tsp salt** in a large mixing bowl and mix well.
2. Transfer the mixture into a greased baking dish.

### Step 2: Baking
3. Top the mixture with crushed **chips or corn flakes**.
4. Bake uncovered at 350° for 1 hour until bubbly and golden brown on top with a slight crispness.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole a day in advance, cover, and refrigerate until ready to bake, adding the topping just before going into the oven.
