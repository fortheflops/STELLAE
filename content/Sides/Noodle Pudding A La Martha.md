# Noodle Pudding A La Martha

> 📜 **Collection:** Jill Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A classic baked noodle pudding featuring creamy cottage cheese, whipped egg whites, and a hint of lemon, baked until golden and bubbling.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 45 mins | None | 6–8 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Pyrex pan
* Large bowl
* Clean bowl
* Electric mixer
* Colander

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 pint | [[Cottage Cheese]] | None |
| 1 package | [[Noodles]] | Wide egg noodles recommended |
| 3 | [[Eggs]] | Separated into yolks and whites |
| 1 | [[Lemon]] | Zested for rind and juiced |
| To Taste | [[Sugar]] | None |
| To Taste | [[Cinnamon]] | None |
| 1 pinch | [[Salt]] | None |
| As needed | [[Butter]] | For greasing the pan |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Cook Noodles
1. Preheat your oven to between **375°F** and **400°F**.
2. Generously butter a **Pyrex pan** with **butter**.
3. Cook the **1 package noodles** according to package directions, then drain well.

### Step 2: Prepare the Cheese and Egg Mixture
4. Separate the **3 eggs** into yolks and whites.
5. In a large bowl, create the cheese preparation by mixing the **1 pint cottage cheese**, **1 lemon** juice and rind, **sugar** to taste, **cinnamon** to taste, and **1 pinch salt**.
6. Beat the **egg yolks** well and add them to the cheese preparation.
7. Stir the cooked **noodles** into the mixture.

### Step 3: Fold and Bake
8. In a clean, separate bowl, beat the **egg whites** until stiff peaks form.
9. Gently fold the beaten **egg whites** into the noodle and cheese mixture.
10. Pour the final mixture into the prepared buttered pan.
11. Bake until the top is browned and fragrant.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the pudding ahead of time and store covered in the refrigerator for up to 24 hours before baking.
