# New Mexico Chili Rice

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *Discover a flavorful New Mexico Chili Rice recipe featuring layers of fluffy rice, tangy sour cream, cottage cheese, and zesty whole chilies. Topped with a generous amount of melted cheddar cheese, this hearty side dish is perfect for complementing your favorite Mexican-inspired meals.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 30 mins | None | 8 servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Baking dish
* Saucepan
* Mixing bowl

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 cup | [[Onions]] | Chopped |
| 4 tbsp. | [[Butter]] | None |
| 4 cups | [[Rice]] | Cooked |
| 2 cups | [[Sour Cream]] | None |
| 1 cup | [[Cottage Cheese]] | None |
| 1 | [[Bay Leaf]] | None |
| To taste | [[Salt]] | None |
| To taste | [[Pepper]] | None |
| 2 (8 oz.) cans | [[Whole Chilies]] | Unseeded and cut into strips |
| 2 cups | [[Cheddar Cheese]] | Grated |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation
1. Sauté the **1 cup chopped onions** in **4 tbsp. butter** until translucent and tender.
2. Combine the cooked **4 cups rice**, **2 cups sour cream**, **1 cup cottage cheese**, and the sautéed onions in a large mixing bowl, seasoning with **Salt** and **Pepper** to taste along with the **1 bay leaf**.

### Step 2: Assembly and Baking
3. Layer the mixture in a baking dish, incorporating the **2-8 oz. cans whole chilies** unseeded and cut into strips.
4. Top the entire dish evenly with **2 cups grated cheddar cheese**.
5. Bake until bubbly, hot, and the cheddar cheese is completely melted and golden on top.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the dish in the baking dish a few hours ahead, cover, and refrigerate until ready to bake.
