# Sweet Potatoes with Orange Sauce

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *Tender sweet potatoes bathed in a glossy, citrus-infused orange sauce. A comforting and bright side dish that bakes to perfection.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 1 hour 15 mins | None | 6 servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Baking dish
* Saucepan
* Measuring cups
* Measuring spoons

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 6 medium | [[Sweet Potatoes]] | Cooked and frozen |
| 1 tbsp | [[Cornstarch]] | None |
| 1/2 cup | [[Sugar]] | None |
| 1 cup | [[Orange Juice]] | None |
| 1/8 tsp | [[Salt]] | None |
| 1 tbsp | [[Grated Orange Rind]] | Grated |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Sauce
1. Place **6 medium sweet potatoes** (unthawed) in a baking dish.
2. In a saucepan, mix **1 tbsp cornstarch**, **1/2 cup sugar**, and **1/8 tsp salt**.
3. Add **1 cup orange juice** and cook until thick and smooth.
4. Remove from heat and stir in **1 tbsp grated orange rind**.

### Step 2: Baking
5. Pour the warm orange sauce over the **6 medium sweet potatoes** in the baking dish.
6. Bake at 350° for 1 1/4 hours until bubbling and tender.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the dish ahead of time and refrigerate until ready to bake, adding a few extra minutes to the baking time if starting cold.
