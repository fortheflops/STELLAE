## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
| 1/2 Cup               | Quick Cooking Grits                    |
| 6 Cups                | Water                                   |
| 1 Stick               | Oleo                                    |
| 1 Stick               | Garlic Cheese                           |
| 1 Stick               | Jalapeno Cheese                         |
| Some                  | Velveeta                               |
| Dash                  | Paprika                                 |
| 3 Teaspoon          | Savory Salt                             |
| 3                     | Beaten Eggs                             |
___
### Instructions
1. Cook grits and water until done.
2. Add Oleo, Garlic Cheese, Jalapeno Cheese, Velveeta, Paprika, Savory Salt, and beaten eggs.
3. Bake at 200 degrees Fahrenheit for 1 Hour
