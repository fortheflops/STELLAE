# Wild Mushroom And Polenta Casserole

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Eight | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A flavorful and earthy casserole combining creamy polenta with savory wild mushrooms and melted cheese. Perfect as a comforting side dish or vegetarian main.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 25 mins | 15 mins | 6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Medium saucepan
* 8x8 inch or 6-cup casserole dish
* Saute pan

## Instructions

### Step 1: Making the Polenta
1. Bring **3 cups chicken stock** to a boil in a medium saucepan.
2. Stir in **1 cup polenta** and add **1 tsp salt**.
3. Lower heat and cook for 10 minutes until thickened.
4. Add **2 tsp red chile powder**, **2 tbsp Jack cheese**, and **2 tbsp butter**, and mix well.
5. Let rest 5 minutes off heat, then place in an oiled 8x8-inch or 6-cup casserole dish.

### Step 2: Preparing the Mushrooms and Baking
6. Wipe **1/2 lb exotic mushrooms** with a damp paper towel and slice thinly (if dry, reconstitute and rough chop).
7. Place **2 tbsp olive oil** in a saute pan over medium heat and saute mushrooms for 2 minutes.
8. Add Madera, salt, and pepper, and cook for another 2 minutes.
9. To serve, heat polenta for 10 minutes in a 350-degree oven and reheat mushrooms, placing them on top of the polenta until fragrant and hot.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the polenta and mushrooms separately, then reheat and combine just before serving.
