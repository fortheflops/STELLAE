# Picnic Menu And Quantities

> 📜 **Collection:** Burcea Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *An archival list of recommended quantities and provisions for a group picnic, including potato salad, bratwurst, and sides. Perfect for planning large outdoor gatherings and community events.*

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large bowls
* Grill
* Serving platters

## Ingredients

### Picnic Provisions
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5 lbs | [[Potatoes]] | For potato salad, split 1/2 german style and 1/2 mayonnaise |
| 2 qts | [[Beans]] | Prepared |
| 10 lbs | [[Bratwurst]] | 2 lbs left over noted |
| 4 doz | [[Buns]] | 2 doz left over noted |
| 1 large | [[Tossed Salad]] | Bowl full, just right amount |
| As needed | [[Olives]] | Assorted |
| As needed | [[Radishes]] | Fresh |
| As needed | [[Celery]] | Carrot sticks |
| As needed | [[Pickles]] | Sliced or whole |
| 2 | [[Cakes]] | 2 dozen cupcakes, 1 sponge cake |
| 1 can | [[Beer]] | Plus extra bottles left over |
| 1 can | [[Soda]] | Plus extra bottles left over |

---

## Instructions

### Preparation And Assembly
1. Prepare the **5 lbs potatoes** and divide them into half German-style and half mayonnaise-based potato salad.
2. Heat the **2 qts beans** until piping hot and transfer to a portable serving container.
3. Grill the **10 lbs bratwurst** over medium heat until fully cooked through and nicely browned on the outside.
4. Arrange the **4 doz buns**, **1 large tossed salad**, **olives**, **radishes**, **celery and carrot sticks**, and **pickles** on the serving table.
5. Display the **2 cakes**, cupcakes, and sponge cake alongside the chilled **beer** and **soda** for dessert and refreshments.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Salads and baked goods can be prepared the day before and kept refrigerated until the picnic.
