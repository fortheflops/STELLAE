# Kidney Beans

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A hearty vintage side dish featuring slow-simmered kidney beans, ground meat, and robust tomatoes. This savory classic tastes even better when prepared a day in advance.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Brown the Meat and Aromatics
1. Fry **one pound ground meat** in **oil** with **one chopped onion** and **two cloves garlic**. Break the meat into small pieces as it cooks until fully browned and aromatic. Season with **salt** and **pepper** to taste.

### Step 2: Simmer with Tomatoes
2. Add **one large can solid pack tomatoes**. Cut the tomatoes into small pieces right in the pot and cook along with the meat for **20 minutes**.

### Step 3: Combine Beans and Finish
3. Add **two tall cans kidney beans** with their juice and let the mixture cook together slowly for **20 minutes** until well blended and hot. Stir in **½ teaspoon cayenne pepper**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This dish tastes significantly better if it stands all day or overnight in the refrigerator before serving.
