# Spanish Rice

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A comforting vintage casserole featuring savory [[Hamburger Meat]], [[Rice]], and melted [[Grated Cheese]]. Baked to perfection in a slow oven for an easy family side dish.*

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 45 mins | None | 4–6 Servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Skillet
* Casserole dish
* Oven

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| ½ | [[Bell Pepper]] | Brown in butter |
| ½ large | [[Onion]] | Brown in butter |
| 1 lb. | [[Hamburger Meat]] | Ground |
| 1 cup | [[Water]] | Add more if needed |
| 1 cup | [[Rice]] | Uncooked |
| 1 can | [[Tomatoes]] | Canned |
| As needed | [[Grated Cheese]] | For topping |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Mix
1. Brown the **½ bell pepper** and **½ large onion** in butter along with the **1 lb. hamburger meat**.
2. Mix the browned meat mixture with **1 cup water**, **1 cup rice**, and **1 can tomatoes** (add more **water** if needed).

### Step 2: Bake
3. Place the mixture in a casserole dish and bake in the oven at **250 degrees** for **30 minutes**.
4. Add the **grated cheese** on top and cook for **15 more minutes** until bubbly and golden, then mix before serving.
