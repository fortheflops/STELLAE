# Hush Puppies

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *Crispy golden-brown southern hush puppies made with cornmeal, onion, and red pepper. Perfectly fried for a delicious savory side dish.*

### 🔪 Key Equipment

* Whisk

| ⅓ Cup | [[Flour]] | All-purpose |
| 1 ½ tsp | [[Salt]] | None |
| ½ tsp | [[Red Pepper]] | None |
| 1 | [[Onion]] | Finely chopped |
| 1 | [[Egg]] | Whole |
| Pinch | [[Baking Powder]] | None |
| As needed | [[Sweet Milk]] | For proper batter consistency |
| As needed | [[Oil]] | For frying |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mix the Batter
1. In a large bowl, whisk together **1 cup cornmeal**, **⅓ cup flour**, **1 ½ tsp salt**, **½ tsp red pepper**, and **pinch baking powder**.
2. Add the **1 finely chopped onion** and **1 egg** to the dry ingredients.
3. Gradually stir in **sweet milk** until the batter will almost drip from a spoon, ensuring the consistency is thinner than cornbread batter but not watery.

### Step 2: Fry and Serve
4. In a deep fryer or large, heavy pot, heat about **2-3 inches of oil** to **350°F (175°C)** until it sizzles.
5. Carefully drop rounded tablespoons of batter into the hot oil, being careful not to overcrowd the pot.
6. Fry for **2-3 minutes**, turning occasionally, until the **hush puppies** are golden brown and cooked through.
7. Remove the cooked **hush puppies** with a slotted spoon and place them on a plate lined with paper towels to drain excess oil before serving hot.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Best served immediately while hot and crispy, though leftover batter can be covered and refrigerated for a few hours.
