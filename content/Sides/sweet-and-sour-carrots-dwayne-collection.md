# Sweet and Sour Carrots

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A classic marinated side dish featuring tender carrots, crisp green peppers, and onions tossed in a sweet and tangy tomato-based dressing. Perfect for making ahead, this dish gets better as it sits and chills.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 13 mins | None | 12 servings |

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 lb. pkg | [[Carrots]] | sliced |
| 1 | [[Green Pepper]] | thinly sliced |
| 1 | [[Onion]] | thinly sliced |
| 1 can | [[Tomato Soup]] | None |
| 3/4 cup | [[Vinegar]] | None |
| 1/2 cup | [[Oil]] | None |
| 1 cup | [[Sugar]] | None |
| 1 tsp. | [[Dry Mustard]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep the Vegetables
1. Cook the **2 lb. pkg carrots** in salted water for **8 minutes** until they are tender-crisp, then drain them thoroughly.
2. Thinly slice and cut up the **1 green pepper** and **1 onion**, then add them to the warm, drained **carrots** in a large bowl.

### Step 2: Make the Dressing & Combine
3. In a saucepan, combine the **1 can tomato soup**, **3/4 cup vinegar**, **1/2 cup oil**, **1 cup sugar**, and **1 tsp. dry mustard**.
4. Heat the mixture for **5 minutes** until the sugar is completely dissolved and the sauce is hot.
5. Pour the warm sauce over the **carrots**, **onion**, and **pepper** while still warm, tossing gently to ensure everything is evenly coated.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Cover and refrigerate for at least 4 hours, or preferably overnight, to allow the sweet and sour flavors to fully marinate and develop.
