# Pantry Cole Slaw

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A classic, creamy pantry-style coleslaw featuring finely shredded cabbage tossed in a sweet and tangy seasoned dressing. Perfect as a make-ahead chilled side dish for barbecues and family meals.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Dressing
1. In a medium mixing bowl, whisk together **¾ cup + 1 Tbsp mayonnaise**, **3 Tbsp + ½ tsp sugar**, **1½ Tbsp + ¼ tsp wine vinegar**, and **⅜ cup + ½ Tbsp oil** until emulsified.
2. Add **1/16 tsp garlic powder**, **1/16 tsp onion powder**, **1/16 tsp mustard powder**, **1/16 tsp celery powder**, **1 dash black pepper**, **½ Tbsp + 1 tsp lemon juice**, **⅜ cup + ½ Tbsp half & half**, and **¼ tsp salt**.
3. Stir continuously until the mixture is completely smooth, uniform, and velvety.

### Step 2: Combine and Chill
4. Place **1 head finely shredded cabbage** into a large serving bowl.
5. Pour the mixed dressing over the **1 head finely shredded cabbage** and toss thoroughly until every strand is well coated.
6. Cover tightly and refrigerate for at least 1 hour until chilled and cabbage has softened slightly.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Will keep for several days when stored in a tightly covered container in the refrigerator.
