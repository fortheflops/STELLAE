# Oriental Marinated Asparagus

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Eight | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A crisp and flavorful marinated asparagus dish featuring sesame oil, rice wine vinegar, and water chestnuts. Perfect as a refreshing side dish for any meal.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5 mins | 5 mins | 1 hr | 4 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Saucepan
* Colander
* Shallow non-metallic dish

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 lb. | [[Asparagus]] | Trimmed |
| 1/4 C. | [[Rice wine vinegar]] | None |
| 1 T. | [[Sesame oil]] | None |
| 1/2 C. | [[Water chestnuts]] | Sliced |
| 1 T. | [[Sesame seeds]] | Toasted |
| 4 strips | [[Pimento or red bell pepper]] | Optional |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Cooking and Marinating
1. Cook **1 lb. asparagus** 2-5 minutes in boiling water until tender-crisp.
2. Rinse in cold water and drain well.
3. Combine **1/4 C. rice wine vinegar**, **1 T. sesame oil**, and **1/2 C. sliced water chestnuts** in a shallow non-metallic dish.
4. Add **1 lb. asparagus**, turning to coat evenly.
5. Cover and refrigerate for 1 hour to blend flavors.
6. Sprinkle with **1 T. toasted sesame seeds** and garnish with **4 strips pimento or red bell pepper**, if desired.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Cover and refrigerate up to 24 hours before serving to let the flavors fully develop.
