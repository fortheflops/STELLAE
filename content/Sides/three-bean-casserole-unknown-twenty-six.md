# Three Bean Casserole

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A hearty, classic three bean casserole featuring ground beef, brown sugar, and a tangy mustard-vinegar sauce. Perfect for potlucks and family gatherings.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 1 hour | None | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Skillet
* Casserole dish
* Loaf pan

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 lb | [[Ground Beef]] | Browned |
| 1 can | [[Kidney Beans]] | 15 oz, or pinto beans |
| 1 can | [[Butter Beans]] | Or use frozen limas |
| 1 can | [[Pork and Beans]] | Medium size |
| 1/2 cup | [[Catsup]] |  |
| 3/4 cup | [[Brown Sugar]] |  |
| 1/2 cup | [[Onion]] | Chopped |
| 1 tsp | [[Dry Mustard]] |  |
| 2 tbsp | [[Vinegar]] |  |
| 1 tsp | [[Salt]] |  |
| To taste | [[Black Pepper]] |  |
| A few slices | [[Bacon]] | Fried, for topping |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and BROWNING
1. Brown the **1 lb ground beef** in a skillet and season with **1 tsp salt** and **black pepper**.

### Step 2: Mixing and Baking
2. Combine the browned beef with **1 can kidney beans**, **1 can butter beans**, **1 can pork and beans**, **1/2 cup catsup**, **3/4 cup brown sugar**, **1/2 cup onion**, **1 tsp dry mustard**, and **2 tbsp vinegar**.
3. Transfer mixture to a casserole dish and bake at 350°F for **1 hr** (or 300°F for 2 hours, or all day in a crock pot until bubbly and thick).
4. Top with **fried bacon slices** before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Can be assembled ahead of time and refrigerated up to 24 hours before baking.
