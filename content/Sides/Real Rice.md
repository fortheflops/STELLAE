## Instructions

### Step 1: Boil the Rice
1. In a special pot, bring **4 to 5 cups water** and **1 heaping teaspoon salt** to a rolling boil.
2. Add **1 cup dried rice** and boil fast and hard with the lid off for **20 minutes** until tender.
3. Taste the **1 cup dried rice**; if it is not soft enough, pour hot water over the rice in a strainer and drain well.

### Step 2: Steam for Fluffy Texture
4. Put a small amount of hot water in the bottom of the pot and bring it back to a boil.
5. Place the drained **1 cup dried rice** in a strainer and set the strainer on top of the pot of boiling water.
6. Take a clean, folded white cloth and place it directly on top of the **1 cup dried rice**.
7. Cover the pot, reduce heat to low, and let the **1 cup dried rice** steam until light and fluffy.

### Step 3: Optional Variations
8. For Spanish Rice: Sauté **chopped onion** and **tomato sauce** together until tender, then warm the cooked **1 cup dried rice** up in the sauce.
9. For Scrambled with Eggs: Scramble **eggs** with the cooked **1 cup dried rice** until set and piping hot.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Steamed rice keeps well for up to 2 hours when covered with a cloth and kept warm.
