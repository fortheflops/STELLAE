# Oven Fried Potatoes

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *Enjoy these crispy oven-fried potatoes seasoned with savory parmesan, garlic, and paprika. Perfect as a golden, comforting side dish for any family meal.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 45 mins | None | 4-6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Cookie sheet
* Mixing bowl

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 cup | [[Margarine]] | Melted |
| 2 tbsp | [[Parmesan cheese]] | Grated |
| 1 tsp | [[Salt]] | None |
| 1/2 tsp | [[Garlic powder]] | None |
| 1/2 tsp | [[Paprika]] | None |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Pepper]] | None |
| 8 lg | [[Potatoes]] | Cut in wedges |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation
1. Melt the **1/2 cup margarine** and combine it with **2 tbsp Parmesan cheese**, **1 tsp salt**, **1/2 tsp garlic powder**, **1/2 tsp paprika**, and **1/4 tsp pepper**.
2. Pour the mixture over a cookie sheet, then add the **8 lg potatoes** (cut in wedges) and toss to coat.

### Step 2: Baking
3. Bake at 375 degrees for **45 mins**, turning the potatoes once at half time until golden brown and tender.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Potatoes can be cut and kept in cold water for a few hours before draining and coating, but for best results, bake immediately after tossing in the seasoned margarine mixture.
