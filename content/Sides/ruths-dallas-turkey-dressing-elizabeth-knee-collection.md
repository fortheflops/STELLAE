# Ruth's Dallas Turkey Dressing

> 📜 **Collection:** Elizabeth Knee Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Ruth | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A classic Southern-style turkey dressing featuring a combination of cornmeal mush and torn bread, seasoned with sage and grated onions. Perfect for holiday feasts, this recipe yields a moist, flavorful side dish baked to golden perfection.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 30 mins | 1 hour | 30 mins | 10 servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large pot
* Mixing bowl
* Grater
* Baking dish

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Cornmeal Mush
1. Stir the **3 cups yellow cornmeal** into boiling **water** as if making mush. Set aside to continue cooking and cool completely.

### Step 2: Combine and Moisten
2. In a large mixing bowl, combine the cooled cornmeal mush with **1 large loaf bread** (torn into pieces), **2 to 3 large grated onions** (about **2 cups grated onions**), **1 tbsp. black pepper**, **1 tbsp. level salt**, **1 tbsp. rounded sugar**, and **1 tsp. sage**.
3. Moisten the mixture with **turkey juice** and added **water** until it reaches the consistency of oatmeal.
4. Just before placing in the oven, fold in the **4 lightly beaten eggs** until fully incorporated.

### Step 3: Bake
5. Transfer the mixture to a greased baking dish and bake in a preheated **350°F or 400°F** oven for **at least 1 hour** until set and golden brown on top.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The cornmeal mush can be prepared and cooled a day in advance. Store the assembled dressing without the eggs in the refrigerator, then stir in the beaten eggs just before baking.
