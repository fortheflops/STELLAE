# Miriam Fox's Broccoli Casserole

> 📜 **Collection:** GailCollection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Miriam Fox | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A classic and comforting broccoli casserole featuring a rich, homemade Cheez Whiz sauce and a crunchy cracker crumb topping. The beaten eggs give it a light, soufflé-like texture.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 50 mins | None | 6–8 servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Saucepan
* Large bowl
* Casserole dish

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 (10 oz) Packages | [[Broccoli]] | Chopped, thawed and squeezed dry |
| 1/4 Cup | [[Onion]] | Chopped |
| 6 Tablespoons | [[Butter]] | Divided |
| 2 Teaspoons | [[Flour]] | Standard |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Water]] | Standard |
| 1 (8 oz) Jar | [[Cheez Whiz]] | Standard |
| 3 | [[Eggs]] | Well beaten |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Cracker Crumbs]] | Standard |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Base and Sauce
1. Preheat oven to **350°F (175°C)** and grease a casserole dish.
2. Thaw the **2 packages chopped broccoli** and squeeze out as much excess water as possible using a clean towel.
3. In a saucepan, sauté the **1/4 cup chopped onion** in **4 tablespoons of butter** until tender.
4. Stir in the **2 teaspoons flour** to form a paste. Gradually whisk in the **1/2 cup water** to create a smooth sauce, and cook until thickened.
5. Add the **1 jar Cheez Whiz** to the saucepan and stir over low heat until completely melted and smooth.

### Step 2: Assemble and Bake
6. In a large bowl, combine the well-drained **broccoli** with the prepared cheese sauce. Stir in the **3 well-beaten eggs** until fully incorporated.
7. Pour the entire mixture into the greased casserole dish.
8. Melt the remaining **2 tablespoons of butter** and toss them with the **1/2 cup cracker crumbs** until moistened.
9. Sprinkle the buttered crumbs evenly over the top of the casserole.
10. Bake for **45 to 50 minutes** at **350°F**, or until the casserole is set in the center and the topping is golden brown and fragrant.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole up to 24 hours in advance, cover, and refrigerate before adding the cracker crumb topping just prior to baking.
