# Chili Rice

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Maxine | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A comforting casserole featuring layers of cooked rice, Monterey Jack cheese, and diced green chilis bound with sour cream. Finished with a golden crown of melted cheddar cheese for a bubbly, savory side dish.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 45 mins | None | 6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Baking dish
* Mixing bowl
* Knife

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3/4 lb | [[Monterey Jack cheese]] | Cut into strips |
| 1 pint | [[Sour cream]] | None |
| 1 can | [[Diced green chilis]] | Drained |
| 4 cups | [[Cooked rice]] | Prepared |
| To taste | [[Salt and pepper]] | None |
| 1/2 cup | [[Grated cheddar cheese]] | Grated |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and Assembly
1. Preheat the oven to 350 degrees F.
2. Mix the **1 can diced green chilis** and **1 pint sour cream** together in a bowl.
3. Cut the **3/4 lb Monterey Jack cheese** into strips.
4. Layer the **4 cups cooked rice**, **Monterey Jack cheese**, sour cream and chili mixture in a baking dish.
5. Season with **salt and pepper** to taste.

### Step 2: Baking
6. Bake at 350 degrees F for **30 mins** until hot and bubbling.
7. Sprinkle the **1/2 cup grated cheddar cheese** over the top and bake for another **15 mins** until the cheese is completely melted and lightly golden.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole up to a day ahead, cover tightly, and refrigerate before baking.
