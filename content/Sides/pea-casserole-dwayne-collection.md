# Pea Casserole

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A classic comforting side dish combining green peas, crunchy water chestnuts, creamy mushroom soup, and crispy fried onions under a layer of melted cheddar cheese. Easy to assemble and bake for family dinners or potlucks.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 25 mins | None | 10 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Casserole dish
* Mixing bowl
* Can opener
* Cutting board
* Chef's knife

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 cans | [[Peas]] | drained |
| 2 cans | [[Fried Onion Rings]] | French-fried style onions |
| 2 cans | [[Cream of Mushroom Soup]] | condensed |
| 1 can | [[Water Chestnuts]] | drained and sliced |
| 1 cup | [[Cheddar Cheese]] | grated |

---

## Instructions

### Assembly
1. Drain the **2 cans peas** and slice the drained **1 can water chestnuts**.
2. In a large bowl, combine the **2 cans peas**, **2 cans fried onion rings**, **2 cans cream of mushroom soup**, sliced **1 can water chestnuts**, and most of the **1 cup grated cheddar cheese**, reserving a small portion of cheese to sprinkle on top.
3. Transfer the mixture to a baking dish and top with the reserved **1 cup grated cheddar cheese**.

### Baking
4. Bake at 365°F for **25 minutes** until heated through, bubbling around the edges, and the top cheese layer is melted and golden.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole up to 24 hours in advance, cover tightly, and refrigerate. Add 5 to 10 minutes to the bake time if baking cold from the refrigerator.
