# Joe's Special (Creamy Spinach Casserole)

> 📜 **Collection:** UK5 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Joe | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A classic and comforting 'Joe's Special' spinach casserole featuring cooked rice, sharp cheese, and a savory egg custard. This dish bakes into a golden, bubbling side that perfectly complements any meal.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 30 mins | None | 6-8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 1.5 or 2-quart casserole dish
* Large mixing bowl
* Colander

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 package (10 oz) | [[Spinach]] | frozen chopped, cooked and well drained |
| 2 large | [[Eggs]] | beaten |
| 1/4 cup | [[Onion]] | chopped |
| 1 cup | [[Milk]] | cold or room temperature |
| 1 cup | [[Cheese]] | sharp, shredded |
| 1 cup | [[Rice]] | cooked |
| To taste | [[Seasoning]] | Salt and Pepper |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Mix
1. Preheat the oven to **350°F**. Grease a **1.5 or 2-quart casserole dish** with butter or cooking spray.
2. Cook the **1 package (10 oz) spinach** according to package directions. Drain the **spinach** very well, squeezing out as much excess water as possible.
3. In a large bowl, combine the drained **spinach**, **2 large eggs**, **1/4 cup onion**, **1 cup milk**, **1 cup cheese**, and **1 cup rice**.
4. Season the mixture to taste with **salt and pepper** and stir well until fully incorporated.

### Step 2: Bake
5. Pour the mixture into the greased casserole dish and spread into an even layer.
6. Bake for **30 minutes**, or until the casserole is set in the center and lightly browned on top.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole up to 24 hours in advance, cover, and store in the refrigerator until ready to bake; add a few extra minutes to the baking time if chilled.
