## Recipe

| Three Packages                   | Frozen [[Spinach]]                        |
| One Small Carton                 | [[Sour Cream]]                            |
| One Package                      | Lipton [[Onion Soup Mix]]                 |
| One Small Package                | Frozen [[Onion Rings]]                    |
___
## Instructions
1.  Cook and drain spinach.
2.  Stir sour cream and onion soup mix together.
3.  Stir the sour cream mixture into the spinach.
4.  Put the spinach mixture into a casserole dish.
5.  Place onion rings on top.
6.  Heat for about 20 minutes (presumably in a preheated oven, e.g., 350°F, though temperature is not specified on the card).
