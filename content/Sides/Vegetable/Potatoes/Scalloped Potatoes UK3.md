## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
| 3 or 4 Medium        | Potatoes                                        |
| To Taste             | Salt                                         |
| To Taste               | Pepper            |
| To Taste          | Grated Cheese |
| If desired      |Grated Onion          |
| Enough              | Milk                 |
___
### Instructions
1. Peel And Slice (Crossways) About 3 Or 4 Medium Potatoes.
2. Arrange In Alternate Layers With Salt And Pepper, Grated Cheese, And, If Desired, Grated Onion.
3. Have Topping Of Cheese.
4. Pour Enough Milk In To Come To About 1 Inch From Top.
5. Bake At 350 Degrees For About 45 Minutes To An Hour.
