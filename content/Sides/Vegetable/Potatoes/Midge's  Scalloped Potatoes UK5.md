## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 2                          | [[Potatoes]], peeled and thinly sliced    |
| As Needed                  | [[Flour]]                                 |
| To Taste                   | [[Salt]] and [[Pepper]]                   |
| 1 tablespoon per layer     | [[Butter]]                                |
| As Needed                  | [[Milk]]                                  |
___
## Instructions
1.  Preheat the oven to 350°F - 375°F.
2.  In a well-buttered baking pan, place a single layer of thinly sliced potatoes.
3.  Lightly sprinkle the layer with flour, salt, and pepper.
4.  Dot the layer with butter (about 1 tablespoon, placed in each corner).
5.  Continue creating layers of potatoes and seasonings.
6.  **Do not flour the top layer.**
7.  Pour milk over the potatoes until the dish is about 2/3 full.
8.  Bake for less than one hour, or until the potatoes are tender and the top is golden brown.
