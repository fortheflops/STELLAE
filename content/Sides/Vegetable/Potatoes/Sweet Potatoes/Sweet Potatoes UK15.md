## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 4 medium                   | [[Sweet Potatoes]]                        |
| 1 cup                      | [[Sugar]]                                 |
| 1 teaspoon                 | [[Vanilla]]                               |
| 3 tablespoons              | Melted [[Butter]]                         |
| As Needed                  | [[Marshmallows]]                          |
| Optional                   | 1 cup [[Cream]] or [[Pet Milk]]           |

___
## Instructions
1.  Peel and slice the 4 medium sweet potatoes.
2.  Boil the potato slices in salted water until tender.
3.  Drain the potatoes and place them in a baking dish.
4.  In a small bowl, mix the sugar, vanilla, and melted butter.
5.  Pour this mixture over the sweet potatoes in the baking dish.
6.  Bake at 400°F for 15 minutes.
7.  Cover the top with marshmallows and return to the oven to brown for about 5 more minutes.
___

>[!Note]
>For a variation without marshmallows, you can leave them off and instead pour 1 cup of cream or Pet milk over the potatoes and brown them at 400°F.
