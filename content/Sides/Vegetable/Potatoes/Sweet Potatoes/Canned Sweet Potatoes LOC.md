## Recipe
| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
| 1 (23 oz) can     | Sweet Potatoes (Yams) *(Implied by instructions)* |
| $\frac{1}{4}$ Cup | Syrup from can (of sweet potatoes)       |
| $\frac{1}{3}$ Cup | Sugar                                      |
| 3 Tbsp            | Butter                                     |
___
### Instructions
1.  Bring to boil in saucepan: $\frac{1}{4}$ C syrup from can, $\frac{1}{3}$ C sugar, 3 T butter.
2.  Pour over yams and bake for 30 min. with ham or at about 350°.
