## Instructions
1.  Choose small, uniform-sized new potatoes (two or three per person, depending on their size).
2.  Scrub the potatoes well and put them in a pot of salted, boiling water.
3.  Cook them until just tender (about 20 minutes).
4.  Drain the potatoes and dry them completely in the empty pot for a few moments (this helps remove excess moisture).
5.  Remove the skins from the potatoes (they should peel easily if cooked until just tender).
6.  Sauté the potatoes briefly in butter with salt and pepper to taste, and lots of chopped fresh dill weed, until heated through and lightly coated.
7.  Serve hot, garnished with chopped parsley.
