## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
| 1 Medium              | Head Cabbage, Coarsely Shredded          |
|                       | (Do not use processor or grater)          |
| 4 Stalks             | Celery, Diced                            |
| 1                     | Onion, Diced                             |
| 1                     | Green Bell Pepper, Chopped                |
| 1 Jar (4 Ounce)        | Chopped Pimento, Drained                 |
| 1 Cup                 | Sugar                                     |
| 1 Cup                 | Oil                                       |
| 1 Cup                 | Vinegar                                   |
| 2 Tablespoon          | Salt                                      |
| 1 Tablespoon          | Dry Mustard                             |
| 1 Tablespoon          | Celery Seed                             |
___
### Instructions
1. In a large bowl, mix cabbage, celery, onion, pepper, pimento, and 1 cup (minus 2 tablespoons) of the sugar.
2. Combine oil, vinegar, salt, remaining sugar, mustard, and celery seed in a saucepan.
3. Bring to a boil, stirring constantly.
4. Pour hot mixture over cabbage and mix.
5. Cool, cover, and chill for 24 hours.
___

>[!Note]
>This gets better every day and is still good after nine days, if it lasts that long.
