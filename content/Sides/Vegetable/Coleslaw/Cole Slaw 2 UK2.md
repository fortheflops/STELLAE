## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients                                      |
| :----------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| 1                  | [[Cabbage]]         |
| 2                  | [[Apples]]          |
| 2                  | [[Carrots]]         |
| 1/2 Cup            | [[Sour Cream]]      |
| 1 Small             | [[Onion]]           |
|                    | [[Mayonnaise]]      |
| 1/4 Teaspoon       | [[Sugar]]           |
___
### Instructions
   1.  Shred cabbage.
   2.  Dice apples.
   3.  Grate carrots.
   4.  Combine and moisten with mayonnaise.
   5.  Add sugar and sour cream.
   6.  Mix thoroughly.
