### Instructions

2.  Arrange tomato slices on a broiler-safe pan or baking sheet.
3.  Top the tomato slices with sharp cheese (shredded or thinly sliced).
4.  Sprinkle with sesame seeds and dill weed.
5.  Broil for a few minutes, watching carefully, until the cheese is melted and bubbly, and the tomatoes are heated through. Serve immediately.
