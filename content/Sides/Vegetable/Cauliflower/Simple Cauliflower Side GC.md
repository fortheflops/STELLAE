## Instructions
1.  Cut the cauliflower into florets.
2.  Blanch the florets in salted, boiling water for 5 minutes.
3.  Drain the cauliflower thoroughly.
4.  In a bowl, toss the drained cauliflower with a dusting of olive oil, garlic salt, and any other seasonings you desire ("The Works").
5.  Serve warm.
