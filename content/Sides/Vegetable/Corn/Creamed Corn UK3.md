## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
| 1 20 Ounce Bag       | Frozen Corn                  |
| 1 Cup               | Whip Cream - Unwhipped        |
| 1 Teaspoon          | Salt                          |
| 1 Tablespoon        | Sugar                         |
| 2 Tablespoons        | Butter                         |
| 2 Tablespoons        | Flour              |
| 1 Small Bag          | Parmesan Cheese      |
___
### Instructions
1. Thaw Corn.
2. Add Cream And Simmer For 5 Minutes.
3. Stir In Salt And Sugar.
4. Melt Butter And Stir In Flour (Separate Pan).
5. Add To Corn And Cook Until Thick.
6. Put In Casserole Dish, Top With Parmesan Cheese; Broil In Oven Until Top Is Brown.
