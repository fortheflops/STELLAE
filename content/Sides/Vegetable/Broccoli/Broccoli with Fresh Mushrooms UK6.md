## Recipe
| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
| 3-4 Cups                 | Cut Broccoli *(florets and tender stems)* |
| As needed                | Boiling Salted Water *(for blanching broccoli)* |
| Up to $\frac{1}{2}$ lb  | Fresh Mushrooms, trimmed & cut in $\frac{1}{4}$" slices |
| 2 Tbsp                   | Salad Oil                                        |
| 2                        | Shallots or Green Onions, chopped                |
| Dash                     | Ground Nutmeg                                    |
| 1 Tbsp                   | Water                                            |
| 1 tsp                    | Sugar                                            |
| $\frac{1}{2}$ tsp       | Salt                                             |
### Optional Garnish
| Ingredients                                        |
| :------------------------------------------------- |
| Toasted Sesame Seed                                |
| Toasted Chopped Nuts (Almonds, Cashews, Filberts) |

## Instructions
1.  **Precook Broccoli:** Drop cut broccoli into a large quantity of rapidly boiling salted water & cook until just tender when pierced with a fork (approx. 3-4 min.)
2.  Drain broccoli & cool quickly under cold running water. Drain again thoroughly & set aside.
3.  Prepare the sliced mushrooms, chopped shallots (or green onions), water, sugar, and salt, and have them within easy reach of your stove.
4.  Heat a 10" or larger frying pan or wok over high heat. Add the 2 Tbsp oil to the hot pan.
5.  Add the mushrooms to the hot oil first & stir fry them, tossing or stirring frequently, until they are browned and tender.
6.  Add the 2 chopped shallots (or green onions) and the dash of ground nutmeg to the pan. Stir fry for about 30 seconds until fragrant.
7.  Add the blanched broccoli, 1 Tbsp water, 1 tsp sugar, and $\frac{1}{2}$ tsp salt to the pan.
8.  Keep turning and tossing the ingredients with a spatula until the broccoli is heated through (approx. 1-2 min.).
9.  Turn the mixture out onto a warm serving dish.
10. Garnish with toasted sesame seeds or toasted chopped nuts, if desired. Serve immediately.
