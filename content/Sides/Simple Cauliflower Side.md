# Simple Cauliflower Side

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A quick and versatile cauliflower side dish blanched to crisp-tender perfection and seasoned with olive oil, garlic salt, and spices. An easy, light addition to any weeknight dinner.*

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large pot
* Colander
* Mixing bowl

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 head | [[Cauliflower]] | Cut into florets |
| As needed | [[Salt]] | For blanching water |
| As needed | [[Olive Oil]] | None |
| As needed | [[Garlic Salt]] | None |
| As needed | Seasoning Blend | "The Works" or preferred seasonings |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Blanch
1. Cut the **1 head cauliflower** into even florets.
2. Blanch the florets in a large pot of **salted** boiling water for **5 minutes** until crisp-tender.
3. Drain the **cauliflower** thoroughly in a colander.

### Step 2: Season and Serve
4. In a mixing bowl, toss the drained **cauliflower** with a light dusting of **olive oil**, **garlic salt**, and **seasoning blend** until evenly coated.
5. Serve warm.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Blanch and drain the cauliflower ahead of time and refrigerate in a sealed container for up to 3 days. Toss with oil and seasonings just before reheating and serving.
