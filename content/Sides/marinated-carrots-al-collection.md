# Marinated Carrots

> 📜 **Collection:** Al Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A classic sweet and tangy marinated carrot salad tossed with onions and green bell peppers in a rich tomato-based vinaigrette. Perfect as a make-ahead side dish that gets better as it sits.*

| 1/4 teaspoon | [[Black Pepper]] | to taste |

## Instructions

### Step 1: Cook the Carrots
1. In a large pot, cook the **5 cups carrots** just until tender, but not mushy. Drain well.

### Step 2: Prepare the Marinade
2. While the carrots are cooking, mix together the **1 can tomato soup** (or V-8/tomato juice), **1/2 cup oil**, **1 cup sugar**, **3/4 cup vinegar**, **1 teaspoon prepared mustard**, **1 teaspoon worcestershire sauce**, **1 teaspoon salt**, and **1/4 teaspoon pepper** in a saucepan.
3. Bring the mixture to a boil and boil for exactly **one minute**.

### Step 3: Combine and Marinate
4. Pour the hot marinade over the **carrots** while they are still hot.
5. Let the mixture cool for **5 to 10 minutes**.
6. Add the **1 onion** (chopped or in rings) and **1 bell pepper** (chopped in rings) to the carrots and stir gently to combine.
7. Cover and refrigerate. For best flavor, make a day or so before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This dish will keep well in the refrigerator for several weeks. It is highly recommended to make it a day or two before serving to allow the marinade to fully penetrate the vegetables.
