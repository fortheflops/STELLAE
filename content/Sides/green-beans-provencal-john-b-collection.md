# Green Beans Provencal

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A delightful side dish featuring tender green beans tossed with savory herbs, crisp bread cubes, and parmesan cheese. This classic Provencal preparation offers a wonderful blend of textures and bright flavors.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 15 mins | None | 4 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large skillet
* Saucepan

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 lb | [[Green Beans]] | Trimmed |
| 2 T | [[Butter]] | Divided |
| 1/4 t | [[Dried Savory Leaves]] | Crushed |
| 1/8 t | [[Salt]] | None |
| 1 cup | [[Bread Cubes]] | Soft, from 3 slices |
| 1 T | [[Butter]] | For bread cubes |
| 1 T | [[Vinegar]] | None |
| 1/8 t | [[Onion Juice]] | None |
| 1/4 t | [[Salt]] | For sauce |
| 1/4 t | [[Savory Leaves]] | Additional |
| 1 T | [[Parmesan Cheese]] | Grated |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Cook Beans
1. Cook **1/2 lb green beans** in boiling water until tender; drain well.
2. Melt **2 T butter** in a large skillet, add **1/4 t dried savory leaves**, **1/8 t salt**, and **1 cup bread cubes**.

### Step 2: Croutons and Combination
3. Cook the bread cubes until lightly browned, stirring constantly. Remove cubes.
4. Add **1 T butter**, **1 T vinegar**, **1/8 t onion juice**, **1/4 t salt**, **1/4 t savory leaves**, and the cooked green beans to the skillet.
5. Cook for 3-5 minutes to combine flavors, then add the croutons and **1 T parmesan cheese** to combine before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Best served immediately while the croutons are crisp, though beans can be boiled ahead of time.
