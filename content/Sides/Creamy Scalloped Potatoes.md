# Creamy Scalloped Potatoes

> 📜 **Collection:** Jill Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A comforting and easy scalloped potatoes recipe featuring a rich, creamy sauce made from canned cheese and mushroom, chicken, or celery soup. Baked to bubbly perfection, it is the ultimate hearty side dish for any family meal.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 30 mins | None | 6–8 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large pot
* Mixing bowl
* Casserole dish
* Knife
* Cutting board

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3 to 4 pounds | [[Potatoes]] | Peeled and sliced 1/4-inch thick |
| 1 can | [[Cheddar Cheese Soup]] | Undiluted |
| 1 can | Cream of [[Mushroom Soup]], Cream of [[Chicken Soup]], or Cream of [[Celery Soup]] | Undiluted |
| As needed | Extra shredded [[Cheese]] | For garnish |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Boil
1. Preheat oven to **350°F**. Grease a casserole dish.
2. Place the **3 to 4 pounds potatoes** in a large pot of water and boil them until just tender. Drain well.

### Step 2: Assemble and Bake
3. In a separate bowl, mix the **1 can cheddar cheese soup** and **1 can cream of mushroom soup, cream of chicken soup, or cream of celery soup** together.
4. Spread a thin layer of the soup mixture on the bottom of the prepared casserole dish.
5. Add a layer of the drained, cooked **potatoes**.
6. Continue creating alternating layers of soup and potatoes, making sure to end with a final layer of the soup mixture on top.
7. Sprinkle the **extra shredded cheese** for garnish over the top layer.
8. Bake for approximately **30 minutes**, or until the casserole is hot and bubbly.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole ahead of time and refrigerate for up to 24 hours before baking; add an extra 10-15 minutes to the baking time if baking straight from the fridge.
