# Crock Pot Ratatouille

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Eight | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A wholesome and flavorful slow-cooker ratatouille layered with fresh vegetables and herbs. Tenderly simmered to perfection for an effortless side dish.*

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 7-9 hours | None | 6 servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Crock pot

* Cutting board

## Ingredients

### Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 large | [[Onions]] | Sliced |
| 1 large | [[Eggplant]] | Sliced |
| 4 small | [[Zucchini]] | Sliced |
| 3 cloves | [[Garlic]] | Minced |
| 2 | [[Bell peppers]] | 1 red and 1 green, seeded & cut into thin strips |
| 6 large | [[Tomatos]] | Cut into 1/2" wedges |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Basil]] | None |
| 2 teaspoons | [[Salt]] | Note from author: I didn't put anywhere near that much in!! |
| 1/4 teaspoon | [[Pepper]] | None |
| 2 Tablespoons | [[Parsley]] | Chopped |
| 1/4 cup | [[Olive oil]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Layering
1. Layer half the veggies in the crockpot in the following order: **2 large onions**, **1 large eggplant**, **4 small zucchini**, **3 cloves garlic**, **2 bell peppers**, and **6 large tomatos**.
2. Repeat the layers.
3. Sprinkle **1 teaspoon basil**, **2 teaspoons salt**, **1/4 teaspoon pepper**, and **2 Tablespoons parsley** on top.
4. Drizzle with **1/4 cup olive oil**.

### Step 2: Cooking
5. Cook on low for **7-9 hours**.
6. Cool and chop coarsely (after **9 hrs**, author note: I didn't have to chop!).

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Veggie layers can be assembled in the crock insert, covered, and refrigerated overnight before cooking.
