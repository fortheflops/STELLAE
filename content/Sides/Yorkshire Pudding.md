# Yorkshire Pudding

> 📜 **Collection:** UK Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A classic British side dish baked in rich rendered beef fat until dramatically puffed, light, and golden brown. Designed to bake underneath roast beef so savory juices flavor the crisp pudding.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Batter
1. In a mixing bowl, combine **1 cup flour** and **1/4 teaspoon salt**.
2. Add **1/2 cup milk**, **1/2 cup water**, and **3 well-beaten eggs**.
3. Beat the entire mixture vigorously for several minutes with a rotary beater or electric mixer until smooth and thoroughly aerated.

### Step 2: Prepare the Pan
4. Remove cooked roast beef from its roasting pan. Pour into a saucepan enough **beef drippings** to make gravy, leaving the remaining **beef drippings** in the pan and running it up the sides for greasing (or use greased, hissing-hot muffin pans).
5. Pour the batter into the greased pan until it is about 1/2 inch thick.
6. Place the roast beef on a wire rack directly over the pudding pan so the cooking juices run down onto the pudding during baking.

### Step 3: Bake and Serve
7. Bake in a preheated oven at 425°F for **15 minutes**.
8. Reduce the oven heat to 375°F and continue baking for **30 minutes** until the pudding is dramatically puffed, deeply golden brown, and crisp along the edges.
9. Serve immediately while hot.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Yorkshire pudding must be served immediately out of the oven while hot and crisp. It is not suitable for make-ahead storage.
