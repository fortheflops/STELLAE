# Marcis's Zucchini Casserole

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Marcis | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A comforting and creamy zucchini casserole topped with crunchy croutons. Perfect as a savory side dish for family dinners.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 20 mins | None | 6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Saucepan
* Baking dish

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 4 cups | [[Zucchini]] | Unpeeled and cubed |
| 1 stick | [[Margarine]] | Melted over hot zucchini |
| 1 cup | [[Cream of Chicken Soup]] | Condensed |
| 1 cup | [[Sour Cream]] | Standard |
| 1 1/2 cups | [[Plain Croutons]] | Divided (1 cup for mixing, 1/2 cup for topping) |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Boil
1. Boil the cubed **4 cups zucchini** (don't peel) in salt water until tender. Drain well.

### Step 2: Mix and Bake
2. Place the **1 stick margarine** over the hot drained zucchini.
3. Mix in the **1 cup cream of chicken soup**, **1 cup sour cream**, and **1 cup plain croutons** in a baking dish.
4. Pour the remaining **1/2 cup plain croutons** on top.
5. Bake in a **350° oven** for **20 minutes** until bubbly and golden.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole in the baking dish ahead of time, cover, and refrigerate; add the top layer of croutons right before baking.
