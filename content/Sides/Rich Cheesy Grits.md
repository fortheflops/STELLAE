# Rich Cheesy Grits

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A rich and savory baked grits casserole loaded with garlic cheese, jalapeño cheese, and Velveeta. Perfect as a comforting side dish for any hearty meal.*

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |

| 6 Cups | [[Water]] | For cooking grits |

| 1 Stick | [[Garlic Cheese]] | Processed cheese stick or equivalent |
| 1 Stick | [[Jalapeno Cheese]] | Spicy cheese stick or equivalent |
| Some | [[Velveeta]] | Cut into cubes |
| Dash | [[Paprika]] | For dusting |
| 3 Teaspoons | [[Savory Salt]] | Seasoned salt blend |
| 3 | [[Beaten Eggs]] | Whisked until smooth |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Cook the Grits
1. In a saucepan, combine **6 cups water** and **½ cup quick cooking grits**. Cook according to package instructions until thick and done.

### Step 2: Combine Cheeses and Eggs
2. To the hot cooked grits, stir in **1 stick oleo**, **1 stick garlic cheese**, **1 stick jalapeno cheese**, **some Velveeta**, **dash paprika**, and **3 teaspoons savory salt** until melted and smooth.
3. Allow to cool slightly, then stir in **3 beaten eggs** until fully incorporated.

### Step 3: Bake
4. Pour the mixture into a greased baking dish and bake at **200 degrees Fahrenheit** for **1 hour** until set and bubbling gently around the edges.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole up to a day in advance, cover, and refrigerate before baking.
