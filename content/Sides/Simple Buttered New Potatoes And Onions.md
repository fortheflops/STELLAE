# Simple Buttered New Potatoes And Onions

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A classic vintage side dish featuring tender new potatoes cooked with onions and tossed in rich butter. Perfectly seasoned with salt and pepper for a comforting addition to any meal.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 20 mins | None | 4 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Medium saucepan
* Colander
* Chef's knife
* Cutting board

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5-6 | [[New Potatoes]] | With skin on |
| 1 large | [[Onion]] | Cut |
| As needed | [[Butter]] | Amount not stated |
| To taste | [[Salt]] | None |
| To taste | [[Pepper]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Boil and Sauté
1. Cook the **5-6 new potatoes** with skin on in boiling water until tender when pierced with a fork.
2. Add the **1 large onion** and cook until clear and translucent.

### Step 2: Finish and Serve
3. Drain the **5-6 new potatoes** and **1 large onion** thoroughly in a colander.
4. Add the **butter**, **salt**, and **pepper** to taste, tossing gently until the butter is melted and the potatoes are well coated.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Best served immediately while hot, though leftovers can be stored in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 2 days.
