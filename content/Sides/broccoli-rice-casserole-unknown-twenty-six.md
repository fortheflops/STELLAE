# Broccoli Rice Casserole

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Martha | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A classic baked casserole featuring chopped broccoli, tender rice, and a rich combination of soups and melted cheese. Perfectly comforting and easy to prepare ahead for family gatherings.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 30 mins | None | 12 servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Skillet
* Saucepan
* Casserole dish
* Mixing bowl

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 cup | [[Celery]] | Chopped |
| 1/2 cup | [[Onion]] | Chopped |
| 1 tbsp | [[Margarine]] | None |
| 1 pkg | [[Broccoli]] | Chopped (2 or 3) |
| 1 cup | [[Rice]] | Regular, cooked separately |
| 1 cup | [[Mushroom Soup]] | 2 jars mushroom soup |
| 1 cup | [[Cream of Celery Soup]] | None |
| 8 oz | [[Jalapeno Cheese]] | Whiz or sharp cheddar cheese |
| to taste | [[Salt and Pepper]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Sauté
1. Sauté **1/2 cup celery** and **1/2 cup onion** in **1 tbsp margarine** until tender.
2. Cook the **1 cup rice** separately according to package directions.

### Step 2: Combine and Bake
3. Add **1 cup mushroom soup** and **1 cup cream of celery soup** to the sautéed celery and onions.
4. Mix in the cooked **1 cup rice**, chopped **1 pkg broccoli**, and **8 oz jalapeno cheese** (or sharp cheddar), seasoning with **salt and pepper** to taste.
5. Transfer to a 3-quart casserole dish.
6. Bake at 350°F for **30 mins**, or alternatively at 375°F for **20 mins**, until bubbling hot and melted throughout.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole in the baking dish, cover tightly, and refrigerate up to 24 hours before baking.
