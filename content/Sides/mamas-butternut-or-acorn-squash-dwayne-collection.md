# Mama's Butternut Or Acorn Squash

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A delightful layered casserole featuring spiced mashed butternut or acorn squash combined with tender sliced apples and a crunchy cornflake pecan topping. This comforting side dish offers a perfect balance of savory and sweet flavors for any family gathering.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 25 mins | 15 mins | None | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Skillet
* 3 qt. Casserole dish
* Mixing bowl

---
## Ingredients

### Squash and Apple Filling
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3 cups | [[Butternut Squash]] | Mashed, cooked (or acorn squash) |
| 1/4 c. | [[Butter]] | For seasoning squash |
| 1 T | [[Brown Sugar]] | For seasoning squash |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Salt]] | Plus dash of white pepper |
| 1 1/2 T | [[Butter]] | For skillet |
| 6 cups | [[Apples]] | Sliced, pared, Jonathan preferred |
| 1/4 c. | [[Granulated Sugar]] | For apples |

### Topping
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 1/2 c. | [[Cornflakes]] | Coarsely crushed |
| 1/2 c. | [[Pecans]] | Chopped |
| 1/2 c. | [[Brown Sugar]] | For topping |
| 2 T | [[Melted Butter]] | For topping |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep Squash and Apples
1. Season **3 cups mashed cooked butternut squash** with **1/4 c. butter**, **1 T brown sugar**, and **1/4 tsp salt & pepper**.
2. Heat **1 1/2 T butter** in skillet; add **6 cups sliced pared apples**, sprinkle with **1/4 c. granulated sugar**, cover and simmer over low heat until barely tender.

### Step 2: Assemble and Bake
3. Spread the tender apples in a **3 qt. casserole**; spoon the seasoned mashed squash evenly over the apples.
4. Mix **1 1/2 c. cornflakes**, **1/2 c. chopped pecans**, **1/2 c. brown sugar**, and **2 T melted butter** together.
5. Sprinkle the cornflake mixture evenly over the squash layer.
6. Bake in a **350°** oven for **15 mins** until the topping is golden and bubbling.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole up to a point before baking, cover, and refrigerate; add the crunchy topping just before baking.
