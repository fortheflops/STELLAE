# Noodles Romanoff

> 📜 **Collection:** Patsy's Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A comforting side dish featuring wide egg noodles tossed in a rich, creamy blend of sour cream, ranch dressing, and Parmesan cheese. This quick and easy recipe is a crowd-pleasing classic for any family dinner.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 10 mins | None | 4–6 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large pot
* Colander
* Mixing bowl
* Whisk

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 (8 oz) pkg | [[Wide Egg Noodles]] | Uncooked |
| ¼ cup | [[Sour Cream]] | None |
| 1 ½ cups | [[Prepared Hidden Valley Ranch Original Ranch Salad Dressing]] | None |
| ¼ cup | [[Parmesan Cheese]] | Grated |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Cook the Noodles
1. Cook the **1 (8 oz) pkg wide egg noodles** according to package directions until tender. Drain well and keep hot.

### Step 2: Prepare the Sauce and Combine
2. In a mixing bowl, blend the **¼ cup sour cream**, **1 ½ cups prepared hidden valley ranch original ranch salad dressing**, and **¼ cup parmesan cheese** until smooth.
3. Toss the dressing mixture with the hot noodles until evenly coated and heated through.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Best served warm immediately, but leftovers can be stored in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days.
