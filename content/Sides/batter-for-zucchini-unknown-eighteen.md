# Batter for Zucchini

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Eighteen | ✍️ **Attribution:** Jill Buchholz | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A crispy and flavorful batter for zucchini sticks, featuring a blend of cornmeal, flour, and spices. Perfect for deep frying into golden, crunchy bites.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 10 mins | None | 4 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Deep fryer or skillet

---
## Ingredients

### Batter Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3 | [[Zucchini]] | Large, sliced or stick cut |
| 1 cup | [[Flour]] | Standard |
| 1/2 cup | [[Cornmeal]] | Standard |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] | Beaten |
| 1/2 cup plus | [[Milk]] | As needed |
| 1/2 tsp | [[Paprika]] | Standard |
| To taste | [[Salt]] | Standard |
| To taste | [[Pepper]] | Standard |
| 1 tsp | [[Sugar]] | Standard |

---

## Instructions

### Preparation and Frying
1. Stir and mix **1 cup flour**, **1/2 cup cornmeal**, **2 eggs**, **1/2 cup plus milk**, **1/2 tsp paprika**, **salt**, **pepper**, and **1 tsp sugar** all together in a mixing bowl until smooth.
2. Dip **3 large zucchini** (sliced or stick) in batter.
3. Deep fry in **Crisco** until golden brown and crispy.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Best prepared fresh just before frying.
