# Bouillon Pilaf

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A classic and comforting rice pilaf cooked in seasoned bouillon broth. Baked to fluffy perfection with a golden finish.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 40 mins | None | 6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Skillet
* Baking dish
* Oven

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/4 cup | [[Butter]] | Or margarine |
| 1 1/2 cups | [[Rice]] | Uncooked |
| 3 cups | [[Broth]] | Hot, well seasoned chicken, beef, or lamb broth (or Campbell's beef bullion soup with water added to make 3 cups total) |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Sauté
1. Melt **1/4 cup butter or marg.** in a skillet and add **1 1/2 cup uncooked rice**, cook stirring until **butter** begins to bubble.

### Step 2: Bake
2. Add **3 cups hot well seasoned chicken, beef or lamb broth** (or beef bullion soup and water to make 3 cups total).
3. Cover tightly & bake in hot oven **400°** for **30 minutes**.
4. Remove cover, stir and cook bake **10 minutes** more until liquid is absorbed and rice is tender.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftover pilaf in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days.
