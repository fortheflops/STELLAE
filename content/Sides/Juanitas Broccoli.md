# Juanita's Broccoli

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Juanita | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *Discover Juanita's Broccoli, a comforting vintage side dish featuring tender broccoli bathed in a rich garlic cheese and mushroom soup sauce. Topped with bread crumbs and baked to golden perfection, it is a timeless family favorite.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 30 mins | None | 6–8 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Skillet
* Baking dish
* Saucepan

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 Stick | [[Butter]] | or Oleo |
| As needed | [[Onion]] | Chopped |
| To taste | [[Garlic]] | Minced |
| 1 Roll | [[Kraft Garlic Cheese]] | Sliced |
| 1 Can | [[Mushroom Soup]] | Condensed |
| 1/2 Can | [[Water]] | Measured in soup can |
| 1 Bunch | [[Broccoli]] | Cooked and separated |
| As needed | [[Bread Crumbs]] | For topping |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Make the Cheese Sauce
1. In a skillet, melt **1 stick of butter** over medium heat.
2. Add the chopped **onion** and **garlic**, cooking until translucent and fragrant.
3. Add **1 roll of Kraft garlic cheese**, **1 can of mushroom soup**, and **1/2 can of water**. Stir continuously until the cheese is completely melted and the sauce is smooth.

### Step 2: Assemble and Bake
4. Cook and separate the **broccoli** into florets, then drain well.
5. Place the drained **broccoli** into a greased baking dish.
6. Pour the warm cheese mixture evenly over the **broccoli**.
7. Sprinkle generously with **bread crumbs**.
8. Bake at **325°F** for **30 minutes** until bubbly, golden brown, and fragrant.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole ahead of time, cover tightly, and refrigerate for up to 24 hours before baking.
