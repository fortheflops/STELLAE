# Macaroni and Cheese

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Eighteen | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A classic, comforting baked macaroni and cheese featuring layers of tender pasta and grated aged cheese, moistened with scalded milk and seasoned with a touch of mustard. An optional topping of buttered breadcrumbs adds a delightful crunch.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Cook the Macaroni
1. Cook the **2 cups broken macaroni** in **8 cups boiling water** seasoned with **2 teaspoons salt** for **15 minutes** until tender.
2. Drain the macaroni and rinse it thoroughly in hot water.

### Step 2: Assemble and Bake
3. Place the cooked macaroni in layers in a buttered baking dish, sprinkling each layer with the **2 cups grated aged cheese**, **1 teaspoon mustard**, **1/4 teaspoon salt**, and a **dash pepper**.
4. Pour the **1 1/4 cups scalded milk** around the edges of the baking dish.
5. If desired, sprinkle **buttered crumbs** evenly over the top layer.
6. Bake in a moderate oven preheated to **350°F** for **30 minutes** until the cheese is melted, bubbling, and the top is beautifully golden brown.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This dish can be assembled ahead of time, covered, and refrigerated. If baking straight from the refrigerator, add 5 to 10 minutes to the baking time.
