# Cole Slaw

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Two Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A crisp, refreshing vintage cole slaw elevated with sweet diced apples, grated carrots, and shredded cabbage. Tossed with a quick creamy dressing of sour cream, mayonnaise, and a pinch of sugar.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 0 mins | 30 mins (Chilling) | 4–6 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Chef's knife
* Box grater
* Large mixing bowl
* Rubber spatula

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 Head | [[Cabbage]] | Shredded |
| 2 | [[Apples]] | Diced |
| 2 | [[Carrots]] | Grated |
| 1 Small | [[Onion]] | Finely diced |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Sour Cream]] |  |
| 1/4 Cup (or to taste) | [[Mayonnaise]] |  |
| 1/4 Teaspoon | [[Sugar]] |  |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep the Produce
1. Shred the **1 head cabbage**, dice the **2 apples**, finely dice the **1 small onion**, and grate the **2 carrots**.

### Step 2: Combine and Dressing
2. In a large bowl, combine the shredded **cabbage**, **apples**, **carrots**, and **onion**.
3. Add the **1/2 cup sour cream**, **1/4 cup mayonnaise**, and **1/4 teaspoon sugar**.
4. Mix thoroughly until everything is evenly coated, glossy, and creamy. Chill in the refrigerator before serving for best flavor.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Cover and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes before serving to let the flavors meld. Store sealed in the refrigerator for up to 3 days.
