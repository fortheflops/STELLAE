# Stuffed Tomatoes

> 📜 **Collection:** Georgia Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *Flavorful medium tomatoes stuffed with savory brown rice, celery, onion, and Parmesan cheese. Baked to perfection for a delightful vegetable side dish.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 25 mins | 45 mins | None | 6-8 servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Saucepan
* 9-inch pie pan
* Custard cups

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Filling
1. Cut off the top of each **6-8 medium tomatoes**. Save the top and scoop out the centers. Chop the pulp and save it. Place the **6-8 medium tomatoes** shells upside down to drain.
2. Heat oven to 350°F. Heat **2 T. oil** in a saucepan. Sauté **1/3 c. chopped celery** and **2 T. chopped onion**. Remove and add to the tomato pulp, **2 c. cooked brown rice**, **1/4 c. Parmesan**, **1 T. snipped fresh parsley**, **1 t. dried basil leaves**, **1/8 t. pepper**, and **1/8 t. garlic powder**. Mix well.
3. Fill the **6-8 medium tomatoes** with the rice mixture. Replace the tops.

### Step 2: Baking
4. Oil a 9-inch pie pan. Place the **6-8 medium tomatoes** in the dish. Cover with foil and bake for **30-45 minutes** until tender and heated through. Can also bake in individual oiled custard cups.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Tomatoes can be stuffed ahead of time and refrigerated for up to a few hours before baking.
