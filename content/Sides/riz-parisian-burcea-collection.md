# Riz Parisian

> 📜 **Collection:** Burcea Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A flavorful savory rice dish prepared with onion soup and mushrooms. It makes a comforting side that pairs well with various main courses.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5 mins | 15 mins | 20 mins | 4-5 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Saucepan
* Measuring cups

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 package | [[Onion Soup Mix]] | Dry |
| 1 3/4 to 2 cups | [[Water]] | None |
| 2 tbsp | [[Butter]] | None |
| 1 small can | [[Mushrooms]] | Canned |
| 1 3/4 cups | [[Minute Rice]] | Uncooked |
| to taste | [[Salt]] | Optional |
| to taste | [[Pepper]] | Optional |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Cooking the Base
1. Make up a pack of **onion soup** with **1 3/4 to 2 c. water**.

### Step 2: Combining Ingredients
2. When this is ready, add **2 Tbls. butter**, **1 small can mushrooms**, and **1 3/4 c. minute rice**.
3. Cover, turn off heat, and let minute rice cook.

### Step 3: Resting and Serving
4. Stir now and then.
5. Taste to see if it needs more **salt** or **pepper**.
6. Let set about 20 minutes, turn heat on again to serve hot.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Can be prepared up to the resting stage and reheated gently on the stove before serving.
