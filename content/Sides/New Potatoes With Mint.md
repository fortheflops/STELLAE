## Instructions

### Step 1: Boil the Potatoes
1. Choose **small, even-sized new potatoes** with tender skins.
2. Place the **potatoes** in a pot and add **boiling water** to cover, seasoned with **salt** and a good deal of **fresh mint sprigs**.
3. Cook for about **15 minutes**, or until the **potatoes** are fork-tender.

### Step 2: Finish and Serve
4. Drain the **potatoes** using a colander. Optionally, set them over a saucepan with a little water to keep them warm and gently steam.
5. Rub off the tender skins with your fingers if desired, or leave them on.
6. Serve the **potatoes** hot, tossed with **butter** and garnished with **chopped parsley**.

### Variation: New Peas with New Potatoes in Cream Sauce
7. Prepare the **new potatoes** as described above by boiling with **mint**.
8. Cook the **new peas** separately until tender.
9. Prepare a simple **cream sauce** by melting **butter**, whisking in flour, gradually adding milk, and cooking until thickened.
10. Combine the cooked **new potatoes** and **peas** with the **cream sauce** and serve hot.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Potatoes can be boiled ahead of time and gently reheated in butter before serving.
