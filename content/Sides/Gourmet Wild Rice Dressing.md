# Gourmet Wild Rice Dressing

> *A versatile wild rice side dish that can be prepared on the stovetop or baked into a firm casserole with gourmet add-ins like mushrooms, sausage, and toasted nuts.*

### Base
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |

### Gourmet Add-ins (To taste)
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| As desired | [[Herbs]] | Fresh or dried |
| As desired | [[Mushrooms]] | Sautéed |
| As desired | [[Sausage]] | Cooked and crumbled |
| As desired | [[Chicken Livers]] or [[Oysters]] | Minced |
| As desired | [[Nuts]] | Toasted |
| As desired | [[Pimiento]] | Chopped |
| As desired | [[Parsley]] | Freshly chopped |
| As desired | [[Olives]] | Sliced |
| As desired | [[Orange]] or [[Lemon]] Rind | Grated |
| As desired | [[Wine]] | Optional splash |

### For Baked Version
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | [[Egg]] | Beaten |

---

## Instructions

### Stovetop Method
1. Cook the **6 oz. [[Uncle Ben's Wild Rice]]** according to package directions in a saucepan until tender.
2. About **5 minutes** before the end of cooking, fold in your choice of gourmet add-ins such as **[[Mushrooms]]**, **[[Nuts]]**, **[[Parsley]]**, or **[[Wine]]** to taste.
3. Continue cooking for the final **5 minutes** until hot and fragrant, then serve immediately.

### Baked "Firm Set" Dressing Variation
4. Preheat your oven to **350°F** and lightly grease a casserole dish.
5. Cook the **6 oz. [[Uncle Ben's Wild Rice]]** and desired add-ins using the stovetop method until tender.
6. Remove the cooked rice from the heat and gently fold in **1 [[Egg]]** (beaten) until thoroughly combined.
7. Pour the mixture evenly into the greased casserole dish.
8. Bake at **350°F** for **15 to 20 minutes** until set and golden on top.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the baked version ahead of time in the casserole dish, cover tightly, and refrigerate for up to 24 hours before baking.
