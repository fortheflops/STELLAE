# Party Spinach

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing and Baking
1. Combine **4 10oz. pkgs. frozen spinach** with **2 3/4 oz dry onion soup**, **1 pt. sour half & half**, and **1 c seasoned bread crumbs**.
2. Mix; blend in **4 eggs, beaten**.
3. Pour mixture into a greased **2 1/2 qt. casserole** dish.
4. Top with **Swiss cheese, shredded**.
5. Bake at 350 degrees F for **45 minutes** until bubbly and golden on top.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole up to a day in advance, cover, and refrigerate until ready to bake.
