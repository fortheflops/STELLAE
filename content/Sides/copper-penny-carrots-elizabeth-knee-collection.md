# Copper Penny Carrots

> 📜 **Collection:** Elizabeth Knee Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Estelle | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A classic marinated carrot salad featuring tender sliced carrots, crisp onions, and green peppers tossed in a sweet and tangy tomato-based vinaigrette. This colorful side dish is perfect for potlucks and gets better as it marinates overnight.*

---

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 10 mins | 12 hours | 6 servings |

* Mixing bowl
* Whisk
* Airtight container

## Ingredients

### Vegetables
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 lb | [[Carrots]] | cooked and sliced |
| 1 | [[Onion]] | chopped |
| 1 | [[Green Pepper]] | chopped |

### Dressing
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 can (10 3/4 oz.) | [[Tomato Soup]] | condensed |
| 3/4 cup | [[Sugar]] | can be decreased slightly to prevent being too soupy |
| 1/2 cup | [[White Vinegar]] | can be decreased slightly to prevent being too soupy |
| 1/4 cup | [[Oil]] | can be decreased slightly to prevent being too soupy |
| 1 tsp | [[Worcestershire Sauce]] |  |
| 1 tsp | [[Prepared Mustard]] |  |
| 1 dash | [[Hot Sauce]] | such as Tabasco |
| To taste | [[Salt]] and [[Black Pepper]] |  |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep the Vegetables
1. In a large bowl, combine the **1 lb carrots**, **1 onion**, and **1 green pepper**.

### Step 2: Mix and Pour Dressing
2. In a separate bowl, whisk together the **1 can tomato soup**, **3/4 cup sugar**, **1/2 cup white vinegar**, **1/4 cup oil**, **1 tsp Worcestershire sauce**, **1 tsp prepared mustard**, **1 dash hot sauce**, and **salt and black pepper to taste** until smooth and well blended. *Note: You may decrease the sugar, vinegar, and oil slightly if you prefer a less soupy consistency.*
3. Pour the dressing mixture over the **carrots**, **onion**, and **green pepper** and toss gently to coat evenly.

### Step 3: Marinate
4. Cover and refrigerate overnight to allow the flavors to meld and the carrots to become thoroughly chilled and marinated. The salad keeps well in the refrigerator for several days.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This dish must be made ahead and refrigerated overnight. It keeps well in the refrigerator for several days, with the flavor improving over time.
