# Green Apples Stuffed With Sweet Potatoes

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *Delightful tart green apples are hollowed out and generously filled with spiced sweet potatoes and marshmallows. Baked until tender, they make a comforting and unique holiday side dish.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 30 mins | 30 mins | None | 8-10 servings |

## Ingredients

### Filling & Shells
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 8-10 | [[Green apples]] | Large, firm |
| 1-20 oz. can | [[Sweet potatoes]] | Canned |
| 1/4 cup | [[Dark Karo syrup]] | None |
| 1/4 cup | [[Butter]] | None |
| 1 tsp. | [[Salt]] | None |
| 1/4 tsp. | [[Cinnamon]] | None |
| 16 | [[Mini marshmallows]] | None |
| 1 tbsp. | [[Lemon juice]] | None |
| 1/4 cup | [[Melted butter]] | Melted |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation
1. Prepare apple shells by scooping out fruit till wall of **8-10 green apples** is 1/2 inch thick.
2. Mix other ingredients (**1-20 oz. can sweet potatoes**, **1/4 cup dark Karo syrup**, **1/4 cup butter**, **1 tsp. salt**, **1/4 tsp. cinnamon**, **16 mini marshmallows**, **1 tbsp. lemon juice**, and **1/4 cup melted butter**) and stuff **8-10 green apples** generously.

### Step 2: Baking
3. Place stuffed **8-10 green apples** in 350° oven.
4. Bake 1/2 hour, till soft, but not mushy.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Apples can be hollowed and stuffed ahead of time, then refrigerated for a few hours before baking.
