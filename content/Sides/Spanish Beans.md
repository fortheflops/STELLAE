# Spanish Beans

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A traditional slow-baked Spanish bean dish featuring savory lima beans infused with aromatic vegetables, white wine, and paprika. Comforting and rich, this vintage side pairs wonderfully with roasted meats.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Soak and Simmer
1. Soak the **2 cups dry [[Lima Beans]]** overnight in water.
2. Drain the beans, then add them to a saucepan with the **1 [[Carrot]]**, **2 stalks [[Celery]]**, and **3 cups water**.
3. Season with **salt** and **pepper** to taste.
4. Cover and cook slowly, starting in an oven set at **225°F** until tender.

### Step 2: Strain and Assemble
5. Strain off the stock from the cooked beans and save **2 cups** of the liquid to use later on.
6. Pour the drained beans into a **3-quart casserole** dish.
7. Add the **2 Tbsp [[Butter]]**, **2 Tbsp [[Salad Oil]]**, **1 finely chopped [[Onion]]**, **1 clove finely chopped [[Garlic]]**, **2 skinned and finely chopped [[Tomatoes]]**, a pinch of **[[Basil]] or [[Thyme]]**, **1 tsp dry [[Mustard]]**, **1 tsp [[Paprika]]**, and additional **salt** and **pepper**.

### Step 3: Bake
8. Pour in the reserved **2 cups bean stock** along with the **1/4 cup [[White Wine]]**.
9. Cover the casserole dish and bake in the oven for **2 hours** until bubbling, tender, and richly aromatic.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** These beans can be assembled a day ahead and refrigerated until ready to slow-bake; leftovers store sealed in the refrigerator for up to 4 days.
