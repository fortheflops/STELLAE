# Hush Puppies

## Instructions

### Preparation and Frying
1. Mix **2 cups white corn meal**, **1 egg**, **1 tbls salt**, **1/2 tbls sugar**, **1/4 teas soda**, and **1 cup buttermilk** together in a mixing bowl.
2. Add **water** gradually until a proper batter consistency is reached.
3. Drop the batter by spoonfuls into **deep hot fat or oil**.
4. Cook until golden brown and crispy.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Best served immediately while hot and crispy. The dry ingredients can be pre-mixed ahead of time.
