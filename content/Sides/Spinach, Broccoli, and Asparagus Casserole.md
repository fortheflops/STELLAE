# Spinach, Broccoli, and Asparagus Casserole

> 📜 **Collection:** Patsy's Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A simple and savory vegetable casserole combining spinach, broccoli, and asparagus in a creamy cheese base topped with fresh tomatoes.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 30 mins | 15 mins | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 9x13 inch baking dish
* Large mixing bowl

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 pkg | [[Spinach]], [[Broccoli]], & [[Asparagus]] | frozen, approx. 12-16 oz, thawed and drained |
| 1 Cup | [[Sour Cream]] | none |
| 1 Cup | [[Cottage Cheese]] | none |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Bisquick]] | or as needed |
| 1 | [[Tomato]] | sliced |
| As needed | [[Parmesan Cheese]] | grated |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Mix
1. **Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C).** Lightly grease a 9x13 inch baking dish.
2. Thaw the frozen **Spinach, Broccoli, & Asparagus** and drain them thoroughly, squeezing out as much excess moisture as possible.
3. In a large bowl, mix together the thawed vegetables, **1 cup sour cream**, and **1 cup cottage cheese**.
4. Gradually add about **1/2 cup Bisquick** to the vegetable mixture, mixing until just combined to a thick, moist consistency.

### Step 2: Assemble and Bake
5. Pour the mixture into the prepared baking dish.
6. Arrange the sliced **1 tomato** over the top and sprinkle generously with grated **Parmesan cheese**.
7. Bake for **25-30 minutes** until heated through, the cheese is melted, and the top is lightly browned and bubbly.
8. Let the casserole cool for **10-15 minutes** before serving to allow it to set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftover casserole sealed in the refrigerator for up to 3 days. Reheat individual portions in the microwave or oven until warm.
