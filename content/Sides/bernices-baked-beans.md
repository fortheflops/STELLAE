# Bernice's Baked Beans

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Bernice | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A classic, hearty recipe for traditional baked beans prepared in a pressure cooker for deep, savory flavor. This dish features tender navy beans slow-cooked with salt pork and brown sugar for the perfect balance of sweet and salty.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 12 hours | 60 minutes | 12 hours | 6-8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Pressure cooker
* Colander

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 lb | [[Navy beans]] | Small, white, dried |
| 1 large | [[White onion]] | Diced |
| 1/2 lb | [[Salt pork]] | Sliced |
| 5-6 tbsp | [[Brown sugar]] | Packed |
| To taste | [[Salt]] | Optional |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation
1. Soak the **1 lb navy beans** overnight in water, then drain and wash thoroughly.

### Step 2: Cooking
2. Place the beans in the pressure cooker and add the **1 large white onion**, **1/2 lb salt pork**, **5-6 tbsp brown sugar**, and **salt** to taste.
3. Cover with water until it reaches about 1/2 inch above the beans.
4. Secure the lid and bring to a boil until steam escapes in an even flow.
5. Bring the pressure up to 15 pounds and cook for 60 minutes.
6. Allow the pressure to drop naturally before opening the cooker.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** These beans improve in flavor after sitting in the refrigerator for 24 hours; reheat gently on the stovetop.
