# Lois' Cole Slaw

> *A crisp, tangy vintage coleslaw featuring shredded cabbage, sliced onions, and green pepper tossed in a simple vinegar and oil dressing. A simple classic side dish preserved from Lois's kitchen collection.*

| As Needed | [[Onions]] | sliced into thin rings |
| 1 Whole | [[Green Pepper]] | sliced into thin rings |

### Dressing
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| ½ Cup | [[Vinegar]] | None |
| ½ Cup | [[Salad oil]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Prepare the Vegetables
1. Shred the **cabbage** very finely. Transfer to a bowl and season heavily with **salt** to soften.
2. Slice the **onions** and **1 green pepper** into thin rings.

### Make Dressing & Serve
3. In a small bowl or jar, combine the **½ cup vinegar** and **½ cup salad oil**, whisking thoroughly until blended.
4. Pour the dressing over the **cabbage**, **onions**, and **1 green pepper**, tossing until all vegetables are evenly coated and crisp.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Can be prepared up to 1 day ahead; keep covered and chilled in the refrigerator to allow the flavors to meld.
