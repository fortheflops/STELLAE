# Baked Beans With Barbecued Meat Balls

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare and Brown Meatballs
1. Combine **1 lb ground pork**, **1 tsp salt**, and **⅛ tsp pepper**, and form into balls **1 ½ inches** in diameter.
2. Brown the meatballs in **1 Tbsp fat** in a skillet over medium heat until fully cooked through.

### Step 2: Glaze and Simmer
3. In a small bowl, combine **¼ cup catsup**, **1 Tbsp brown sugar**, **1 Tbsp cider vinegar**, **1 Tbsp Worcestershire sauce**, and **1 Tbsp prepared brown mustard**.
4. Pour the sauce mixture over the meatballs, cover the skillet, and simmer for about **5 minutes** or until the sauce is thick and glossy.

### Step 3: Heat Beans and Assemble
5. In a separate saucepan, heat the **19 oz can Heinz Oven Baked Beans, Boston Style** until piping hot.
6. Pour the heated beans onto a hot serving platter and arrange the glazed meatballs over the top.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Meatballs can be formed ahead of time and refrigerated for up to 24 hours before cooking.
