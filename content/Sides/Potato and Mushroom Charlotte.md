# Potato and Mushroom Charlotte

> 📜 **Collection:** Patsy Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *An elegant layered potato and wild mushroom charlette baked to golden perfection. This vintage side dish features a rich mushroom filling encased in thinly sliced russet potatoes and parmesan cheese.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 30 mins | 45 mins | None | 6–8 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Sauté pan
* Charlotte mold or springform pan
* Kitchen towels
* Spatula
* Baking knife

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 4 oz | [[Shitakee Mushrooms]] | Chopped |
| 4 oz | [[Chanterelle Mushrooms]] | Cleaned |
| 4 oz | [[Oyster Mushrooms]] | Cleaned |
| 1 Tbsp | [[Chives]] | Chopped |
| As needed | [[Salt]] | To taste |
| As needed | [[Pepper]] | To taste |
| 3 cups | [[Russet Potatoes]] | Peeled, 1/8" thick slices |
| 6 Tbsp | [[Parmesan Cheese]] | Grated, divided |
| 2 tsp | [[Olive Oil]] | Plus extra for drizzling |
| As needed | [[Butter]] | For the pan and roux |
| As needed | [[Flour]] | For the roux |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Mushroom Mix
1. Melt **butter** in a pan, then add a little **flour** to create a light roux.
2. Add all the **4 oz fresh [[Shitakee Mushrooms]]**, **4 oz [[Chanterelle Mushrooms]]**, and **4 oz [[Oyster Mushrooms]]** and cook until tender and fragrant.
3. Season the mixture with **salt**, **pepper**, and **1 tbsp chopped [[Chives]]**.

### Step 2: Prepare the Potatoes
4. Blanch the **3 cups [[Russet Potatoes]]** in a bowl of hot water, then drain thoroughly.
5. Mix the drained potatoes with **2 tsp [[Parmesan cheese]]** and **2 tsp [[olive oil]]**.
6. Dry the potato slices completely on kitchen towels.

### Step 3: Assembly
7. Grease a Charlotte mold or a springform pan with **butter**.
8. In 8 total layers, build the charlotte: cover the bottom of the dish with a medium layer of the **potatoes**, add the **mushroom mixture** on top, and sprinkle with **parmesan cheese**.
9. Continue layering until all ingredients are used, ending with a final layer of **potatoes** on top.
10. Drizzle a little more **olive oil** evenly across the top layer.

### Step 4: Baking
11. Bake at **400°F** until the top is golden brown and bubbling.
12. Press down firmly on the layers with a spatula periodically while baking to compact the charlotte.
13. Test for doneness by inserting a small knife; it should pierce through all the layers easily.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the charlotte a few hours in advance and keep refrigerated until ready to bake.
