# Creamy Shoepeg Corn Casserole

> 📜 **Collection:** UK15 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A wonderfully simple and creamy corn casserole featuring sweet shoepeg corn, cream cheese, and a mild kick of green chiles. This easy 4-ingredient side dish is a perfect, quick addition to any meal.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 20 mins | None | 6–8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Oven
* Small casserole dish
* Medium mixing bowl
* Mixer

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/3 cup | [[Butter]] | melted |
| 8 ounces | [[Cream Cheese]] | softened |
| 1 (12 ounce) can | [[Shoe Peg Corn]] | drained |
| 1 (4 ounce) can | [[Green Chiles]] | diced |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Mixture
1. Preheat the oven to **350°F**. Grease a small casserole dish.
2. In a medium mixing bowl, combine the **1/3 cup butter**, melted, and the **8 ounces cream cheese**, softened. Mix until smooth and creamy.

### Step 2: Combine and Bake
3. Stir in the **1 (12 ounce) can shoe peg corn**, drained, and the **1 (4 ounce) can green chiles**, diced, until everything is well combined.
4. Transfer the mixture to the prepared baking dish.
5. Bake for **20 minutes**, or until heated through and bubbly around the edges.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole in advance and store covered in the refrigerator for up to 24 hours before baking.
