# Broccoli and Shrimp Soup Casserole

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A comforting vegetable casserole featuring tender broccoli spears baked in a rich, creamy shrimp and sour cream sauce, topped with toasted Devonshire breadcrumbs. Perfect as a savory side dish for family gatherings.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 1 hr | None | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Baking dish
* Saucepan
* Mixing bowl

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 4 packages | [[Broccoli]] | spears, cooked as directed |
| 2 cans | [[Shrimp Soup]] | condensed cream of shrimp soup |
| 1 pint | [[Sour Cream]] |  |
| 1/4 lb | [[Butter]] | melted |
| 1 cup | [[Breadcrumbs]] | Devonshire style preferred |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Assembly
1. Cook the **4 packages broccoli** according to package directions.
2. In a mixing bowl, combine the **2 cans shrimp soup** and **1 pint sour cream**; mix together thoroughly.
3. Arrange the cooked **4 packages broccoli** spears evenly in a baking pan.
4. Pour the soup and sour cream mixture over the broccoli spears in the pan.

### Step 2: Topping and Baking
5. In a saucepan, melt **1/4 lb butter**, then mix in **1 cup breadcrumbs** and cook until lightly browned.
6. Cover all of the broccoli and sauce with the browned **1 cup breadcrumbs**.
7. Bake at **350°F** for **1 hour** until hot, bubbly, and the crumb topping is golden brown.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Can be assembled up to a day ahead (without the breadcrumb topping), covered, and refrigerated. Add the buttered breadcrumbs just before baking.
