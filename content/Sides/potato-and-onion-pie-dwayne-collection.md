# Potato and Onion Pie

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A comforting, rustic savory pie layering tender sliced potatoes and onions seasoned with garlic salt, baked in a rich pastry crust with evaporated milk until bubbling and golden brown.*

## Instructions

### Assembly and Baking
1. Preheat oven to **350°F** (**175°C**). Line a pie dish with half of the **rich pastry**.
2. Layer the **4 large potatoes** and **1 small onion** alternately in the pastry-lined dish, seasoning each layer with **garlic salt**, **salt**, and **black pepper** to taste.
3. After layering, pour **1 small can evaporated milk** evenly over all the potato and onion layers.
4. Roll out the remaining **rich pastry** and place it over the top of the pie. Seal and crimp the edges, then brush the top crust with a little **milk**.
5. Bake at **350°F** for approximately **50 minutes** until the pastry is **golden brown** and the potatoes are tender when pierced with a fork.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Can be assembled a few hours in advance and kept refrigerated before baking. Leftovers reheat well in a 350°F oven to restore crust crispness.
