# M. Everett's Creamy Spinach Casserole

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** M. Everett | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A classic and incredibly easy spinach casserole from M. Everett's collection. This simple side dish features a creamy, savory base of spinach, sour cream, and onion soup mix, all topped with crunchy French-fried onions.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 40 mins | None | 6–8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Casserole dish
* Medium mixing bowl
* Colander

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3 packages (10 oz each) | [[Frozen Spinach]] | frozen |
| 1 small container (8 oz) | [[Sour Cream]] | chilled |
| 1 package (1 oz) | [[Onion Soup Mix]] | dry |
| 1 small can (2.8 oz) | [[French-Fried Onion Rings]] | crispy |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Mix
1. Preheat your oven to **375°F (190°C)**. Lightly grease a casserole dish.
2. Cook the **3 packages frozen spinach** according to package directions.
3. Drain the spinach **thoroughly**, squeezing out as much excess moisture as possible until completely dry.
4. In a medium bowl, combine the drained spinach, the **1 small container sour cream**, and the **1 package dry onion soup mix**, stirring well until fully incorporated.
5. Transfer the mixture into the prepared casserole dish, spreading it out evenly.

### Step 2: Bake and Top
6. Bake for **20-25 minutes**, or until the casserole is heated through and bubbling gently around the edges.
7. Remove from the oven and sprinkle the **1 small can French-fried onion rings** evenly over the top.
8. Return to the oven and bake for an additional **10-15 minutes**, until the onions are golden brown and fragrant.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole up to a day in advance without the onion topping, cover, and refrigerate until ready to bake.
