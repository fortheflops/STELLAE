## Instructions

### Step 1: Dressing & Salad Preparation
1. In a small bowl, whisk together **4 Tbsp sugar**, **1/4 tsp pepper**, **1 tsp salt**, **1/2 cup oil**, and **2-4 Tbsp vinegar** until well combined.
2. In a large salad bowl, combine the chopped **1 head lettuce**, **6 slices bacon**, **4 Tbsp sesame seeds**, **3/4 cup almonds**, **4 green onions**, and **1/2 (3 oz) can chow mein noodles**.
3. Toss the salad with the dressing just before serving.

### Step 2: Mashed Potatoes Preparation
4. Boil water in a large pot, add the **10 medium Idaho potatoes**, and cook for about **15 minutes** until soft; drain thoroughly.
5. Beat the potatoes until smooth, then mix in **1 cup milk**, **1/2 cup butter**, **1 tsp salt**, **1 Tbsp grated onion**, and **1 (8 oz) cream cheese** until well incorporated, adding more milk if needed.
6. Spread the mixture into a 9x13-inch baking dish and sprinkle the top with **paprika**.

### Step 3: Baking
7. Refrigerate overnight. Take out of the refrigerator **1 1/2 hours** before baking.
8. Bake uncovered at **350°F** for **45 minutes** until heated through and golden.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The mashed potatoes can be fully assembled and refrigerated overnight before baking.
