# Turkey Dressing

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Rick & Joy Gay | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A hearty, traditional turkey dressing featuring savory sausage, aromatic vegetables, and a unique potato-bread base. This classic recipe is perfect for stuffing poultry or serving as a rich side dish for pork.*

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 4 large | [[Onions]] | chopped |
| 2 pounds | [[Sausage]] | bulk |
| 6-8 large stalks | [[Celery]] | chopped |
| 1/2 medium | [[Potatoes]] | cooked and chopped |
| Equal to potatoes | [[Dry Bread]] | cubed |
| To taste | [[Sage]] | equal parts with seasoning |
| To taste | [[Poultry Seasoning]] | equal parts with sage |
| To taste | [[Salt]] | None |
| To taste | [[Pepper]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Sauté the Base
1. In a large skillet, cook the **2 pounds sausage**, **6-8 large stalks celery**, and **4 large onions** until the **sausage** is browned and the vegetables are tender.

### Step 2: Combine and Season
2. Do not drain the grease; add the **1/2 medium potatoes** and **equal amount dry bread** to the skillet.
3. Season with **sage**, **poultry seasoning**, **salt**, and **pepper** to taste, ensuring equal parts of **sage** and **poultry seasoning** are used.
4. Mix everything well until thoroughly combined and fragrant.

### Step 3: Cool
5. Allow the dressing to cool completely before stuffing a turkey or serving alongside pork.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The dressing can be prepared and cooled completely before stuffing; store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 2 days before use.
