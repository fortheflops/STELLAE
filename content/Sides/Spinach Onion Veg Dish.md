# Spinach Onion Veg Dish

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A comforting vintage side dish featuring frozen spinach bound with creamy sour cream and savory Lipton onion soup mix, topped with crispy onion rings. This easy-to-make casserole is wonderfully rich and tastes even better when made ahead.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 20 mins | None | 6–8 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Saucepan
* Colander
* Mixing bowl
* Spoon
* Casserole dish
* Oven

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3 Packages | [[Spinach]] | Frozen, cooked and drained |
| 1 Small Carton | [[Sour Cream]] | Standard small container |
| 1 Package | [[Onion Soup Mix]] | Lipton brand |
| 1 Small Package | [[Onion Rings]] | Frozen |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Spinach Base
1. Cook the **three packages frozen spinach** according to package directions, then thoroughly drain in a colander.

### Step 2: Mix the Creamy Filling
2. In a mixing bowl, stir together the **one small carton sour cream** and **one package Lipton onion soup mix** until well combined.
3. Stir the prepared sour cream mixture gently into the drained **spinach** until evenly incorporated.

### Step 3: Assemble and Bake
4. Transfer the **spinach mixture** into a greased casserole dish and spread it out evenly.
5. Arrange the **one small package frozen onion rings** in a single layer on top of the spinach.
6. Bake in a preheated oven at **350°F** for about **20 minutes** until heated through and the onion rings are golden and crispy.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** It is good left-over and keeps awhile; store covered in the refrigerator for up to 3 days.
