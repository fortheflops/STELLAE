### Instructions
**Stovetop Method**
1.  Cook rice according to package directions.
2.  About 5 minutes before the end of cooking, fold in "to your taste" any of the ingredients listed above.
3.  Serves 4-6.

**Baked "Firm Set" Dressing (Variation)**
1.  After cooking the rice as directed above, fold in one beaten egg.
2.  Pour the mixture into a greased casserole dish.
3.  Bake for 15 to 20 minutes in a 350°F oven.
