### Instructions
1.  Sauté onion & mushrooms in butter.
2.  Add water, catsup, and Kitchen Bouquet.
3.  Stir in contents of pkg. of wild rice.
4.  Bring to boil, cover, boil gently 22 to 25 minutes or until rice is tender.
5.  Remove from heat and fold in 1/2 Cup dairy sour cream just before serving.
