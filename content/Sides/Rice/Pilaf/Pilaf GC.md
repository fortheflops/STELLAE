## Instructions
1.  Melt two tablespoons of butter in a skillet.
2.  Add one cup of uncooked wheat bulgur and one tablespoon of minced onion.
3.  Cook until the bulgur is golden.
4.  Add two cups of chicken broth and seasonings ($\frac{1}{4}$ teaspoon oregano, $\frac{1}{2}$ teaspoon salt, a few grains of pepper).
5.  Cover and bring the mixture to a boil.
6.  Reduce the heat and simmer for 15 minutes, or until the liquid is absorbed and the bulgur is tender.
7.  Serve with chicken, lamb, or pork.
