# Mac and Cheese

> 📜 **Collection:** Al Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A comforting, creamy baked macaroni and cheese casserole (charmingly titled 'Mac + Chuse' in the original manuscript) made with sharp Cheddar, cream of mushroom soup, and a touch of mayonnaise. An optional dash of sherry adds a sophisticated depth of flavor to this classic family favorite.*

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 2 1/2-quart casserole dish
* Mixing bowl
* Measuring cups
* Measuring spoons
* Spatula

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 8 oz. (approx. 2 cups dry) | [[Elbow Macaroni]] | cooked |
| 8 oz. | [[Cheddar Cheese]] | shredded sharp (or part Swiss) |
| 10 3/4 oz. | [[Cream of Mushroom Soup]] | 1 can |
| 1/2 cup | [[Mayonnaise]] | None |
| 1/2 cup | [[Milk]] | None |
| To taste | [[Salt]] and [[Black Pepper]] | None |
| 1 dash | [[Sherry]] | optional |

---

## Instructions

### Preparation
1. Preheat your oven to **375°F** and grease a **2 1/2-quart casserole dish**.
2. In a large mixing bowl, combine the **8 oz. cooked elbow macaroni**, **8 oz. shredded sharp Cheddar cheese**, **10 3/4 oz. cream of mushroom soup**, **1/2 cup mayonnaise**, and **1/2 cup milk**.
3. Season with **salt and black pepper to taste**, and stir in an optional **dash of sherry** if desired. Mix all ingredients thoroughly until the pasta is evenly coated and the mixture is creamy.

### Baking
4. Pour the mixture into the prepared **2 1/2-quart casserole dish**, smoothing the top with a spatula.
5. Bake at **375°F** for **25 minutes** until the casserole is hot, bubbling around the edges, and the top is lightly golden.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This casserole can be assembled, covered, and refrigerated up to 24 hours before baking. If baking directly from the refrigerator, add 5 to 10 minutes to the baking time to ensure it is heated through.
