# French-Style Green Beans

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A classic side dish of julienned fresh green beans steamed until crisp-tender, tossed in garlicky butter, and finished with fresh parsley and grated Parmesan cheese.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 25 mins | None | 6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Vegetable steamer
* Saucepan or saute pan
* Chef's knife
* Cutting board

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 lbs. | [[Green Beans]] | fresh, julienned |
| 1 sm. | [[Garlic]] | clove, crushed |
| 1/4 cup | [[Butter]] |  |
| 2 tsp. | [[Parsley]] | finely chopped |
| 2 tsp. | [[Salt]] |  |
| 1/8 tsp. | [[Black Pepper]] | fresh ground |
| To taste | [[Parmesan Cheese]] | grated |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Steam
1. Julienne your **2 lbs. fresh green beans**. Wash thoroughly and place in the top of a vegetable steamer.
2. Cook for about 20 minutes or until **green beans** are crisp-tender.
3. Remove **green beans** from top of steamer pan, discard water from bottom pan, and turn beans into the base pan.

### Step 2: Sauté and Serve
4. In the pan, saute **1 sm. clove crushed garlic** in **1/4 cup butter** for about 1-2 minutes.
5. Season with **2 tsp. salt**, **1/8 tsp. fresh ground pepper**, and **2 tsp. finely chopped parsley**, then pour melted garlic butter over the **green beans**.
6. Re-heat **green beans** for several minutes, then sprinkle with a touch of grated **Parmesan cheese**. Shake contents to coat evenly and serve.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The green beans can be julienned and steamed a day in advance. Store covered in the refrigerator and finish sauteing with garlic butter and Parmesan right before serving.
