# Green Orzo

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Eight | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *Green Orzo is a vibrant and tender pasta side dish featuring fresh spinach, garlic, and herbs. It is easily prepared in a saucepan and can be served hot or at room temperature.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 20 mins | None | 2 or 3 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Small heavy-bottomed saucepan
* Small pan

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 T | [[Butter]] | None |
| 1/4 C | [[Onion]] | Diced |
| 3/4 C | [[Orzo]] | None |
| 2 C | [[Chicken Broth]] | None |
| 1 T | [[Olive Oil]] | None |
| 1 T | [[Garlic]] | Minced |
| 2 T | [[Parsley]] | Minced |
| 2 T | [[Green Onion]] | Minced |
| 1 C | [[Fresh Spinach Leaves]] | Packed roughly chopped |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Cooking the Orzo
1. Melt the **1 T butter** with the **1/4 C onion** in a small heavy-bottomed saucepan. Cook over medium heat until the onion softens, about 2-3 mins.
2. Add the **3/4 C orzo** and stir to coat the grains.
3. Add the **2 C chicken broth**, bring to a boil, then lower heat to a simmer and cook the orzo, partially covered, until just tender, about 15 mins.

### Step 2: Preparing the Green Mixture
4. Meanwhile, heat the **1 T olive oil** in a small pan.
5. Add **1 T garlic**, **2 T parsley**, and **2 T green onion** and stir only until the green onion wilts.
6. Add **1 C packed roughly chopped fresh spinach leaves** and stir until it barely wilts.

### Step 3: Combining and Serving
7. Just before serving, fold the spinach mixture into the orzo.
8. Season with salt and pepper, and serve hot or at room temperature.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftovers in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 2 days; bring to room temperature before serving.
