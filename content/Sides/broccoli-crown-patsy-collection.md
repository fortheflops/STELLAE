# Broccoli Crown

> 📜 **Collection:** Patsy Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Barbara Osborne | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A elegant vintage-style broccoli mold cooked in a water bath with a savory egg and mayonnaise sauce. Served with warm herb-butter tomatoes spooned into the center.*

## Instructions

### Prep & Base Sauce
1. Cook **2 lbs. broccoli** until just crunchy; drain well.
2. In a saucepan, sauté **1/4 cup chopped onion** in **4 Tbsp. butter**.
3. Stir in **4 Tbsp. flour** and **1 tsp. seasoned salt**; cook until bobbly.
4. Stir in **1 cup milk** and cook until mixture thickens and boils for 1 minute.

### Assembly & Baking
5. Beat **3 eggs** slightly in a small bowl. Add a little hot sauce to temper the **3 eggs**, then stir the egg mixture back into the main sauce.
6. Cook over medium heat for about 3 minutes until the sauce thickens again.
7. Stir in **1/2 cup mayonnaise** and **1/4 cup parsley**.
8. Fold the sauce into the drained **2 lbs. broccoli** and spoon into a well-greased 5-cup mold.
9. Set the mold in a baking pan and place on an oven shelf. Pour boiling water into the baking pan to a depth of about 1 inch.
10. Bake at 350°F for 30 minutes or until firmly set.
11. Let stand for a few minutes, loosen edges, and invert onto a serving dish.

### Garnish & Serving
12. For the Herb-Butter Tomatoes, melt **2 Tbsp. butter** with **1/2 tsp. marjoram** in a skillet.
13. Stir in **1 to 2 cups tomatoes** and cook for 5 minutes or just until heated through.
14. Spoon the warm tomatoes into the center ring of the inverted **broccoli crown** and serve.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The broccoli mold can be cooked, cooled, and refrigerated prior to serving. Reheat gently and fill center with freshly prepared herb-butter tomatoes right before serving.
