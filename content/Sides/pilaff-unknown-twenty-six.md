# Pilaff

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A classic baked rice pilaff cooked in a seasoned broth until tender and fluffy. This savory side dish pairs wonderfully with a variety of main courses.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 40 mins | None | 6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Skillet
* Baking dish with cover

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/4 cup | [[Butter or Margarine]] | Melted |
| 1 1/2 cups | [[Uncooked Rice]] | Uncooked |
| 3 cups | [[Hot Well Seasoned Chicken, Beef or Lamb Broth]] | Or use Campbell's canned beef bouillon/broth with added water to make 3 cups total |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Bake
1. Melt **1/4 cup butter or marg.** in skillet, & add **1 1/2 cups uncooked rice**. Cook, stirring until butter begins to bubble.
2. Add **3 cups hot well seasoned chicken, beef or lamb broth** (I use the Campbells canned beef bouillon (broth) add water to make 3 cups total).
3. Cover tightly & bake in hot oven 400° for **30 min**.
4. Remove cover, stir rice & bake **10 more minutes** until liquid is absorbed and rice is tender.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Best served fresh, but leftovers can be stored in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days.
