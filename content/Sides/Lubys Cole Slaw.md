# Luby's Cole Slaw

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Two | ✍️ **Attribution:** Luby | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A classic, creamy coleslaw recipe featuring chopped cabbage and finely chopped spinach tossed in a sweet and tangy mayonnaise dressing. Perfect as a refreshing side dish for barbecues, picnics, and family gatherings.*

---

## Instructions

### Preparation
1. In a large mixing bowl, combine the **1 medium chopped cabbage**, **1 cup firmly packed, finely chopped spinach leaves**, **1 cup mayonnaise**, **1 Tbsp sugar**, **½ tsp white vinegar**, and a **dash of salt & pepper**.
2. Toss all ingredients thoroughly until the cabbage and spinach are evenly coated with the dressing and the mixture is creamy.

### Chilling
3. Cover the bowl and refrigerate for at least **2 hours** until thoroughly chilled and the flavors are fully melded.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 to 4 days. Stir well before serving as liquid may settle at the bottom.
