# Squash Casserole

> 📜 **Collection:** Al Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A comforting southern-style squash casserole featuring tender yellow squash, savory onions, and a buttery cracker crumb topping. Baked to golden perfection, it's the ultimate side dish for family gatherings.*

| 1 cup | [[Cracker Crumbs]] | divided |
| 1 | [[Egg]] | beaten |
| To taste | [[Salt]] |  |
| To taste | [[Pepper]] |  |
| To taste | [[Garlic Salt]] |  |
| 1/2 cup | [[Cheese]] | shredded |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Boil
1. Boil the **4-5 medium yellow squash** with the **1 small onion** in a saucepan until tender.
2. Drain well in a colander to remove excess moisture.

### Step 2: Mix and Season
3. In a mixing bowl, add **1/2 stick butter**, half of the **1 cup cracker crumbs**, and **1 egg**. Mix well.
4. Season to taste with **salt**, **pepper**, and **garlic salt**.

### Step 3: Assemble and Bake
5. Transfer the mixture to a greased baking dish.
6. Cover the top with the remaining **crumbled cracker crumbs** and **1/2 cup cheese**.
7. Bake at **350°F** for **30 minutes** until the cheese is melted and bubbly, and the top is golden brown.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This casserole can be assembled, covered, and refrigerated up to 24 hours before baking. Add 5-10 minutes to the baking time if cooking straight from the refrigerator.
