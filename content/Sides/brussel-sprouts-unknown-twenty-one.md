# Brussel Sprouts

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty One | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A quick and easy microwave-cooked shredded brussels sprouts side dish featuring fresh dill, red pepper, and Dijon mustard. Perfectly tender and flavorful in under 10 minutes.*

---

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 lb | [[Brussel sprouts]] | Shredded raw |
| 1/2 cup | [[Red pepper]] | Chopped |
| 1/4 cup | [[Fresh dill]] | Chopped |
| 1/3 cup | [[Chicken broth]] | Or water |
| 1 tbsp | [[Dijon mustard]] | None |
| 2 tsp | [[Butter]] | None |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Black pepper]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Microwave Cooking
1. Toss **1 lb shredded raw brussel sprouts**, **1/2 c chopped red pepper**, **1/4 c chopped fresh dill**, **1/3 c chix broth or water**, **1 T Dijon**, **2 t butter**, and **1/4 t bl. pepper** together in a microwave-safe dish.
2. Microwave for **5-7 minutes** until tender and piping hot.
