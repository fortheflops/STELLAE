# Savory Mushroom & Wild Rice Stroganoff

> 📜 **Collection:** General Archive | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A savory side dish of wild rice cooked with mushrooms, onions, and Kitchen Bouquet, finished with a rich fold of sour cream.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 30 mins | None | 4-6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Saucepan with lid
* Wooden spoon
* Can opener

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| ½ medium | [[Onion]] | Sliced |
| 1-2 oz. can | [[Mushrooms]] | Drained |
| 1 T. | [[Butter]] | None |
| 2 ¼ c. | [[Water]] | None |
| 1 T. | [[Catsup]] | None |
| ½ tsp. | [[Kitchen Bouquet]] | None |
| 1 (6oz.) pkg. | [[Wild Rice]] | None |
| ½ C. | [[Sour Cream]] | Dairy |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Sauté and Simmer
1. In a saucepan, sauté the **½ medium onion** and **1-2 oz. can mushrooms** in **1 T. butter** until translucent and fragrant.
2. Add the **2 ¼ c. water**, **1 T. catsup**, and **½ tsp. Kitchen Bouquet**. Stir well.
3. Stir in the contents of the **1 (6oz.) pkg. wild rice**.

### Step 2: Cook and Finish
4. Bring the mixture to a boil, then cover with a tight-fitting lid and reduce heat.
5. Boil gently for **22 to 25 minutes**, or until the rice is tender and the liquid is absorbed.
6. Remove from heat and gently fold in the **½ C. sour cream** just before serving for a rich, creamy finish.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftover wild rice stroganoff in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days.
