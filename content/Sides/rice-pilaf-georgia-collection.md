# Rice Pilaf

> 📜 **Collection:** Georgia Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A rich and creamy twist on classic rice pilaf, featuring tender long-grain rice, carrots, and green onions simmered in a savory beef broth. Finished with cream cheese and mayonnaise, this comforting side dish is exceptionally velvety and flavorful.*

---

## Instructions

### Preparation and Cooking
1. Dissolve the **2 beef bouillon cubes** in **2 1/4 cups boiling water** in a medium saucepan.
2. Add the **1 cup long grain rice**, **1 cup carrot slices**, **2 tbsp sliced green onion**, **1 tbsp margarine**, and **1/2 tsp dill weed** to the saucepan.
3. Cover with a tight-fitting lid and simmer for **20 minutes** until the rice is tender and the liquid is fully absorbed.
4. Remove from heat. Add the **8 oz cubed cream cheese** and **2 tbsp mayonnaise**, stirring constantly until the cheese is completely melted and the mixture is smooth, creamy, and evenly combined.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Best served immediately while warm and creamy. Leftovers can be stored in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days and gently reheated with a splash of milk or water to restore its velvety texture.
