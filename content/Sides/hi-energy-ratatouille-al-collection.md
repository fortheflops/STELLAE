# Hi-Energy Ratatouille

> 📜 **Collection:** Al Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A vibrant and hearty twist on the classic French vegetable stew, featuring fresh eggplant, zucchini, and a unique addition of sweet orange segments. Baked to perfection with a savory Parmesan crust, this dish makes a nutritious side or main.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 45 mins | None | 6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large skillet
* Baking dish (11 3/4 x 7 1/2 x 1 3/4 inch)
* Aluminum foil

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 medium | [[Eggplant]] | cubed |
| 3 medium | [[Zucchini]] | sliced |
| 2 medium | [[Onions]] | sliced |
| 1 medium | [[Green Pepper]] | diced |
| 3 tbsp | [[Olive Oil]] |  |
| 2 cloves | [[Garlic]] | minced |
| 1 (15 oz) can | [[Tomatoes]] | strained and chopped |
| 1 | [[Orange]] | peeled and segmented |
| 1/2 cup | [[Fresh Parsley]] | chopped |
| 1 tbsp | [[Fresh Basil]] | chopped |
| 1/2 cup | [[Parmesan Cheese]] |  |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Sauté
1. Preheat oven to **450°F**.
2. Sauté the **1 medium cubed eggplant**, **3 medium sliced zucchini**, **2 medium sliced onions**, and **1 medium diced green pepper** in the **3 tbsp olive oil** and **2 cloves minced garlic** in a large skillet or saucepan until the vegetables are barely tender, about **10 minutes**.
3. Add the **1 (15 oz) can strained and chopped tomatoes**, **1 peeled and segmented orange**, **1/2 cup chopped fresh parsley**, and **1 tbsp chopped fresh basil**. Stir well to blend. Cook for **5 minutes** over medium heat to allow the excess liquid to evaporate.

### Step 2: Bake
4. Place the mixture in an **11 3/4 x 7 1/2 x 1 3/4-inch baking dish**.
5. Sprinkle evenly with the **1/2 cup Parmesan cheese**.
6. Cover the dish with **aluminum foil** and bake in the hot oven at **450°F** for **30 minutes** until the vegetables are tender and bubbling.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The vegetable mixture can be sautéed and assembled in the baking dish up to 24 hours in advance. Cover, refrigerate, and add 5-10 minutes to the baking time if cooking straight from the fridge.
