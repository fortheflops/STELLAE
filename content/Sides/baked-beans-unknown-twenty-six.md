# Baked Beans

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A rich and hearty three-bean casserole loaded with savory ground beef and topped with crispy bacon. This crowd-pleasing side dish can be slow-baked in the oven or simmered all day in a crockpot.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 2 hours | None | 8 servings |

* Skillet
* Baking dish
* Crockpot

| 1 can | [[Pork and Beans]] |  |

| 1 tsp | [[Salt]] |  |
| 4 to 6 slices | [[Bacon]] | fried and crumbled |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep the Meats
1. In a skillet, fry the **4 to 6 slices bacon** until crispy. Remove, drain on paper towels, crumble into pieces, and set aside.
2. In the same skillet, brown the **1 lb hamburger** with the **1/2 cup onion** until the meat is fully cooked and the onion is translucent and tender.

### Step 2: Combine and Cook
3. In a large bowl, mix the browned **hamburger** and **onion** with the **1 can butter beans**, **1 can pinto beans**, **1 can pork & beans**, **1/2 cup brown sugar**, **1/2 cup ketchup**, **2 tbsp vinegar**, **1 to 2 tbsp mustard**, and **1 tsp salt** until thoroughly combined.
4. Transfer the mixture to a baking dish or a crockpot.
5. Top the mixture evenly with the fried **bacon** pieces.
6. To bake: Cook uncovered in a preheated oven at **300°F** for **2 hours** until bubbling and caramelized. To slow cook: Cover and cook on low 'all day' in a **crockpot**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Can be assembled a day ahead and kept covered in the refrigerator before cooking. Add the bacon topping just before baking to ensure it stays crisp.
