# Sweet Potato Casserole

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A classic, comforting sweet potato casserole made with butter, cinnamon, and a crunchy buttered crumb topping. Perfect for holiday dinners or family gatherings.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 25 mins | 20 mins | None | 6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Saucepan
* Colander
* Mixing bowl
* Casserole dish

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 6 | [[Sweet potatoes]] | Medium, unpeeled |
| 1/2 tsp | [[Salt]] | None |
| 4 tbsp | [[Butter]] | None |
| 1/2 tsp | [[Cinnamon]] | None |
| As needed | [[Buttered crumbs]] | For topping |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation
1. Boil **6 medium sweet potatoes** in their jackets until tender when pierced with a fork.
2. Peel the potatoes and push them through a colander to achieve a smooth texture.

### Step 2: Assembly and Baking
3. Mix the pureed potatoes with **1/2 tsp salt**, **4 tbsp butter**, and **1/2 tsp cinnamon** until well combined.
4. Place the mixture into a buttered casserole dish and top with **buttered crumbs**.
5. Bake in a preheated **350°F** oven for **20 mins** until the top is golden and the casserole is heated through.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole up to a day in advance, cover, and refrigerate before adding the crumb topping and baking.
