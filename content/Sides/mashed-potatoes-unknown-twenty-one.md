# Mashed Potatoes

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty One | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A creamy and simple mashed potato recipe featuring plain yogurt and a hint of black pepper. Quick to prepare using a hand beater or fork until smooth.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 20 mins | None | 4 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Pot
* Hand beater
* Fork
* Mixing bowl

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 24 oz. | [[Potatoes]] | Cooked |
| 3/4 cup | [[Plain Yogurt]] | Plain |
| to taste | [[Black Pepper]] | Ground |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mash
1. Combine the cooked **24 oz. potatoes**, **3/4 cup plain yogurt**, and **black pepper** in a bowl.
2. Mash the mixture using a beater or fork until smooth and fluffy.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 2 days; reheat thoroughly before serving.
