## Instructions

### Step 1: Combine and Simmer
1. In a saucepan or pot, combine **2 (20 oz) cans kidney beans** (undrained), **3/4 cup brown sugar**, **6 tablespoons finely cut bacon**, **1/4 teaspoon cinnamon**, **1/4 teaspoon nutmeg**, **4 tablespoons vinegar**, **salt**, and **pepper**.
2. Simmer the mixture over low heat, stirring occasionally, until the sauce has thickened to your liking and the beans are tender, about **30 to 45 minutes** until rich, glossy, and fragrant.
