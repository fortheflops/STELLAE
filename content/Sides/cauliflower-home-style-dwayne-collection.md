# Cauliflower Home-Style

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A comforting home-style side dish featuring tender cauliflower florets tossed with toasted breadcrumbs, eggs, sour cream, and shredded mozzarella cheese. Baked until set and lightly golden brown for a warm, savory accompaniment.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 30 mins | None | 4 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 2-quart baking dish
* Large pot
* Colander
* Non-stick skillet
* Large mixing bowl
* Whisk

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 head | [[Cauliflower]] | cut into 1-inch florets |
| 2 tbsp | [[Breadcrumbs]] | fresh |
| 1 tbsp | [[Unsalted Butter]] |  |
| 2 large | [[Eggs]] |  |
| 2 tbsp | [[Sour Cream]] |  |
| 1 cup (about 4 oz) | [[Mozzarella Cheese]] | shredded |

---

## Instructions

### Prep & Parboil
1. Preheat oven to 375°F. Butter a 2-quart baking dish.
2. Cook **1 head cauliflower** (cut into 1-inch florets) in salted boiling water for 3 minutes. Drain well.

### Sauté & Combine
3. In a non-stick skillet over medium heat, sauté **2 tbsp fresh breadcrumbs** in **1 tbsp unsalted butter** until golden. Stir in the cooked **cauliflower**, then remove from heat.
4. In a large bowl, whisk together **2 large eggs**, **2 tbsp sour cream**, and **1 cup shredded mozzarella**.
5. Add the **cauliflower mixture** to the bowl, stirring well to combine thoroughly.

### Baking
6. Transfer the mixture to the buttered 2-quart baking dish.
7. Bake for 25 minutes, or until the top is set and pale golden.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the unbaked casserole up to 24 hours in advance, cover tightly, and refrigerate. Add 5 minutes to the baking time when cooking straight from the refrigerator.
