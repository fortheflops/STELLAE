# Susan Shadoff's Broccoli-Corn Casserole

> 📜 **Collection:** JillCollection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Susan Shadoff | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A classic and comforting side dish from Susan Shadoff featuring cream-style corn and chopped broccoli baked in a savory custard with a buttery cracker crumb topping.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 40 mins | None | 8-10 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Casserole dish
* Large bowl
* Small bowl

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 (16-ounce) can | [[Cream-Style Corn]] | Unopened can |
| 1 (10-ounce) pkg | [[Frozen Chopped Broccoli]] | thawed and drained |
| 1 large | [[Egg]] | beaten |
| 1/2 cup | [[Ritz Crackers]] | crushed, or plain bread crumbs |
| 1 tablespoon | [[Onion]] | chopped |
| 2 tablespoons | [[Butter]] | melted |
| 1/2 teaspoon | [[Salt]] | standard |
| To Taste | [[Black Pepper]] | freshly ground |

### For Topping
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 cup | [[Ritz Crackers]] | additional crushed, or bread crumbs |
| 1-2 tablespoons | [[Butter]] | melted |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Mix
1. Preheat oven to **350°F**. Grease a casserole dish with butter or cooking spray.
2. In a large bowl, combine the **1 (16-ounce) can cream-style corn**, thawed and drained **1 (10-ounce) pkg frozen chopped broccoli**, beaten **1 large egg**, **1/2 cup of Ritz crackers**, **1 tablespoon onion**, **2 tablespoons butter**, **1/2 teaspoon salt**, and **black pepper**. Stir well until thoroughly incorporated.

### Step 2: Assemble and Bake
3. Pour the mixture into the prepared casserole dish.
4. In a small bowl, combine the additional **1/2 cup of Ritz crackers** with **1-2 tablespoons butter** to moisten them.
5. Sprinkle the buttered crumbs evenly over the top of the casserole.
6. Bake for **35 to 40 minutes**, or until the casserole is set and the topping is golden brown and fragrant.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole ahead of time and refrigerate unbaked for up to 24 hours before adding the topping and baking.
