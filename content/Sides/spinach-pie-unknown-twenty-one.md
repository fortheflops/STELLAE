# Spinach Pie

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty One | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A creamy baked spinach pie featuring mushrooms, nutmeg, and slivered almonds. Perfect as a comforting side dish for any family meal.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 20 mins | None | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Baking dish
* Mixing bowl

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 oz | [[Spinach]] | Fresh or frozen, chopped, cooked, and drained |
| 10 1/2 oz | [[Cream of Mushroom Soup]] | Condensed |
| Generous dash | [[Nutmeg]] | Ground |
| 1/3 cup | [[Almonds]] | Slivered and blanched |
| To taste | [[Salt and Pepper]] | S&P |
| To taste | [[Garlic]] | Minced or powder |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and Assembly
1. Blend the **20 oz spinach**, seasonings ([[Salt and Pepper]], [[Garlic]], and **generous dash of gr. nutmeg**), and **half of the 1/3 c slivered blanched almonds** directly into the **10 1/2 oz cr. of mushroom soup**.
2. Place the mixture into a greased baking dish, and sprinkle the remaining **almonds** on top.

### Step 2: Baking
3. Bake at 350°F for **20 minutes** until bubbly and golden on top.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the dish ahead of time, cover, and refrigerate for up to 24 hours before baking.
