# Potato Pancakes

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A traditional recipe for crispy potato pancakes made with grated potatoes and onions. Pan-fried until golden brown for a comforting, savory side dish.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 15 mins | None | 4 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Grater
* Mixing bowl
* Skillet

---
## Ingredients

### Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 1/2 tablespoons | [[Onions]] | grated, or 1/4 cup leeks |
| 1 cup | [[Potatoes]] | grated |
| 2 tablespoons | [[Flour]] | none |
| 1/2 teaspoon | [[Salt]] | none |
| 1 | [[Egg]] | lightly beaten |
| to taste | [[Pepper]] | fresh ground |
| as needed | [[Oil]] | for cooking |

---

## Instructions

### Cooking Instructions
1. Combine the **2 1/2 tablespoons grated onions**, **1 cup grated potatoes**, **2 tablespoons flour**, **1/2 teaspoon salt**, **1 egg**, and **pepper to taste** in a mixing bowl.
2. Heat **oil** in a skillet.
3. Drop the mixture by tablespoonfuls into the hot **oil** and fry on both sides until golden brown and crispy.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Best served immediately while hot and crispy.
