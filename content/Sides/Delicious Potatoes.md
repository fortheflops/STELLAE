# Delicious Potatoes

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Miriam Fox | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A slow-baked vintage casserole featuring grated potatoes, peppers, and green onions bathed in rich milk and butter. This comforting side dish bakes low and slow for hours until tender and flavorful.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 25 mins | 4-6 hours | None | 6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Saucepan
* Grater or grinder
* 1.5-quart casserole dish

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 ½ cups | [[Milk]] | plus extra if needed during baking |
| 1 stick (½ cup) | [[Butter]] | melted |
| 6 | [[Potatoes]] | medium sized, raw |
| ¼ | [[Pepper]] | green pepper |
| ¼ | [[Pimento]] | sweet red pepper or pimento |
| 5 | [[Onions]] | green onions with about 3 inches of green |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Salt]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Melt
1. Preheat oven to **200°F to 250°F**.
2. In a saucepan, melt the **one stick butter** in the **one & half cups milk**. Do not let it boil.

### Step 2: Grate and Assemble
3. Grate or grind the raw **six potatoes**, **quarter green pepper**, **quarter sweet red pepper (or pimento)**, and **five green onions**.
4. Put the grated vegetable mixture into a **1 & half quart casserole dish**.
5. Pour the warm milk and butter mixture over the vegetables. Add the **one teaspoon salt** and stir gently until well combined.

### Step 3: Bake
6. Bake the casserole for **4-6 hours** until tender and bubbling.
7. Add more **milk** if it seems necessary during baking to prevent dryness.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole ahead of time, cover, and refrigerate for up to a few hours before baking low and slow.
