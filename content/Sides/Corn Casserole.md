# Corn Casserole

> 📜 **Collection:** Patsy's Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Opal | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A comforting and creamy corn casserole featuring sweet corn and green chiles melted together with rich cream cheese. Perfect as a quick and easy side dish for any weeknight meal or gathering.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 25 mins | None | 4–6 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Casserole dish
* Saucepan
* Measuring cups

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 pkg or 2 Cans | [[Frozen Corn]] | Thawed if frozen |
| 1 Can | [[Green Chiles]] | Drained |
| 8 oz | [[Cream Cheese]] | Softened |
| ¼ to ½ Cup | [[Milk]] | As needed for consistency |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Melt and Combine
1. Melt the **8 oz cream cheese** with the **¼ to ½ cup milk** over low heat until smooth and creamy.
2. Add the **1 pkg or 2 cans frozen corn** and **1 can green chiles** to the mixture, stirring well to combine all ingredients.

### Step 2: Cook
3. Transfer to a casserole dish and bake at 350°F until bubbly and hot, or alternatively, simmer on top of the stove until heated through and thoroughly combined.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole ahead of time and store covered in the refrigerator for up to 24 hours before baking.
