## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 package                  | [[Noodles]]                               |
| 3 heaping tablespoons      | [[Basil]]                                 |
| 1 tablespoon               | [[Oil]]                                   |
| 1/3                        | [[Margarine]] (unit not specified)        |
| As Needed                  | Hot water from cooking noodles            |
___
## Instructions
1.  Cook the noodles according to package directions. When they are done, drain them but reserve some of the hot cooking water.
2.  In a small bowl, take 3 heaping tablespoons of basil and add a small amount of the hot noodle water. Mash together to form a paste.
3.  In a saucepan, melt the 1/3 unit of margarine with 1 tablespoon of oil.
4.  Add the basil paste to the melted margarine and oil.
5.  Stir everything together, adding more hot noodle water if desired to reach your preferred sauce consistency.
6.  Toss the sauce with the cooked noodles and serve immediately.
___

>[!Note]
>Cooking time about 15 minutes
