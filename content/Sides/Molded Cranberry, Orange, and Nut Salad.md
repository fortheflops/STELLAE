# Molded Cranberry, Orange, and Nut Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A classic holiday side dish featuring ground fresh cranberries and a whole orange, with a delightful crunch from celery and nuts. This refreshing molded salad is set with gelatin for a perfect festive presentation.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 10 mins | 4 hours | 8–10 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* food processor
* saucepan
* small bowl
* mold or serving dish

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 quart | [[Cranberries]] | fresh or frozen |
| 1 whole | [[Orange]] | quartered |
| 2 Cups | [[Sugar]] | granulated |
| 2 Tbsp | [[Gelatin]] | unflavored |
| 1 Cup | [[Water]] | cold |
| 1 Cup | [[Nuts]] | chopped pecans or walnuts |
| 1 Cup | [[Celery]] | diced |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Fruit Base
1. Using a food processor, coarsely grind the **1 quart cranberries** and the **1 whole orange** (including the peel) until finely chopped.

### Step 2: Cook the Mixture
2. Transfer the fruit to a saucepan, stir in **2 cups sugar**, and bring to a boil over medium heat until the **sugar** is fully dissolved.

### Step 3: Bloom Gelatin and Combine
3. In a small bowl, sprinkle **2 tbsp gelatin** over **1 cup cold water** and let stand for 5 minutes.
4. Remove the **cranberry mixture** from heat and stir in the softened **gelatin** until completely dissolved.

### Step 4: Cool and Set
5. Allow the mixture to cool at room temperature, then stir in **1 cup chopped nuts** and **1 cup diced celery**.
6. Pour into a mold and refrigerate for several hours until the salad is completely firm and set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This salad is best made the day before to ensure it is completely firm; keep refrigerated until ready to serve.
