# Creamed Corn

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Three | ✍️ **Attribution:** Susan LaFreniere | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *Rich and creamy homemade creamed corn topped with melted parmesan cheese and browned to perfection. A comforting side dish recipe courtesy of Susan LaFreniere.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 15 mins | None | 4 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Saucepan
* Separate pan
* Casserole dish
* Oven

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 (20 oz) bag | [[Frozen Corn]] | Thawed |
| 1 cup | [[Whip Cream]] | Unwhipped |
| 1 tsp | [[Salt]] | None |
| 1 tbsp | [[Sugar]] | None |
| 2 tbsp | [[Butter]] | None |
| 2 tbsp | [[Flour]] | None |
| 1 small bag | [[Parmesan Cheese]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Simmering Corn
1. Thaw the **1 (20 oz) bag frozen corn**.
2. Add the **1 cup whip cream** to the corn and simmer for **5 minutes**.
3. Stir in the **1 tsp salt** and **1 tbsp sugar**.

### Step 2: Making the Roux
4. In a separate pan, melt the **2 tbsp butter** and stir in the **2 tbsp flour**.
5. Add this mixture to the corn and cook until thick and bubbly.

### Step 3: Baking and Broiling
6. Transfer the mixture into a casserole dish.
7. Top with the **1 small bag parmesan cheese**.
8. Broil in the oven until the top is golden brown and bubbly.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole up to a day in advance, cover, refrigerate, and broil just before serving.
