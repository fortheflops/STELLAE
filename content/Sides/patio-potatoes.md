# Patio Potatoes

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** David Wade | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A rich and creamy potato casserole featuring mild cheddar and zesty green chilis. This comforting side dish is perfect for gatherings and pairs beautifully with grilled meats.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 20 mins | None | 6-8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large skillet
* Baking dish

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 cups | [[Creamy White Sauce]] | Thick |
| 2 cups | [[Mild Cheddar Cheese]] | Grated |
| 1 tuber | [[Garlic]] | Crushed |
| 1 can | [[Green Chili]] | Chopped |
| 2 1/2 lbs | [[New Potatoes]] | Boiled and sliced |
| To taste | [[Salt]] | None |
| To taste | [[Pepper]] | None |
| As needed | [[Bread Crumbs]] | For topping |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Sauce Preparation
1. In a large skillet, combine the **2 cups creamy white sauce** and **2 cups mild cheddar cheese**.
2. Heat the mixture over medium heat just long enough to melt the cheese.
3. Stir in the **1 tuber crushed garlic**, **1 can chopped green chili**, and **salt and pepper** to taste.

### Step 2: Assembly and Baking
4. Place the **2 1/2 lbs boiled and sliced new potatoes** into a baking dish.
5. Pour the prepared cheese sauce over the **potatoes**.
6. Sprinkle the top with **bread crumbs**.
7. Bake uncovered at 350°F for 20 minutes, or until the sauce is bubbly and golden.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** You can assemble the dish up to a day in advance; keep refrigerated and add the bread crumbs just before baking.
