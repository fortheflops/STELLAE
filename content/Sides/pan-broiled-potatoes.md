# Pan Broiled Potatoes

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and Cooking
1. Wash and grate the **3 medium-sized potatoes** using a medium grater.
2. Spread the grated **potatoes** into a well-greased skillet to a depth of 1/4 inch.
3. Cook covered over medium heat until the bottom is golden brown.
4. Turn the mixture and brown the other side until crispy.
5. Season with **salt** to taste and serve hot. Optionally, add **grated onion** for extra flavor.
