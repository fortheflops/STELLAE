# Scalloped Potatoes

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A classic, comforting vintage recipe featuring layered potatoes baked in milk with savory cheese and onion. Perfectly tender with a rich, golden cheese topping.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 45-60 mins | None | 4 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Casserole dish
* Knife
* Peeler

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3 or 4 | [[Potatoes]] | Medium, peeled and sliced crossways |
| To taste | [[Salt]] | None |
| To taste | [[Pepper]] | None |
| To taste | [[Grated Cheese]] | Divided for layers and topping |
| As desired | [[Grated Onion]] | Optional |
| Enough | [[Milk]] | To come to about 1 inch from the top |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Layering
1. Peel and slice crossways about **3 or 4 medium potatoes**.
2. Arrange the sliced **potatoes** in alternate layers with **salt**, **pepper**, **grated cheese**, and, if desired, **grated onion** in a casserole dish.
3. Finish with a final topping layer of **grated cheese**.

### Step 2: Baking
4. Pour enough **milk** into the dish so it comes up to about **1 inch** from the top.
5. Bake at **350 degrees** for about **45 minutes to an hour** until the **potatoes** are tender and the top is bubbly and golden brown.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the dish a few hours ahead and keep refrigerated; add milk just before baking.
