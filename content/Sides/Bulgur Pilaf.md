# Bulgur Pilaf

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Harriet Boston | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A savory and traditional wheat bulgur pilaf lightly seasoned with oregano and cooked in rich chicken broth. Perfect for pairing with chicken, lamb, or pork.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 15 mins | None | 4 Servings |

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 Cup | [[Bulgur]] | Uncooked wheat bulgur |
| 2 Tablespoons | [[Butter]] | Unsalted or salted |
| 1 Tablespoon | [[Onion]] | Minced |
| 2 Cups | [[Chicken Broth]] | Warm or room temperature |
| 1/4 Teaspoon | [[Oregano]] | Dried |
| 1/2 Teaspoon | [[Salt]] | To taste |
| A Few Grains | [[Pepper]] | Freshly ground |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Toast and Sauté
1. Melt **2 tablespoons of butter** in a skillet over medium heat.
2. Add **1 cup of uncooked wheat bulgur** and **1 tablespoon of minced onion**.
3. Cook, stirring frequently, until the **bulgur** is golden and fragrant.

### Step 2: Simmer and Serve
4. Add **2 cups of chicken broth**, **1/4 teaspoon of oregano**, **1/2 teaspoon of salt**, and a **few grains of pepper**.
5. Cover the skillet and bring the mixture to a boil.
6. Reduce the heat and simmer for **15 minutes**, or until the liquid is absorbed and the **bulgur** is tender.
7. Serve warm alongside chicken, lamb, or pork.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Leftover pilaf can be stored in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days and reheated gently with a splash of broth.
