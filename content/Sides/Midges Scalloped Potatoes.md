# Midge's Scalloped Potatoes

> 📜 **Collection:** UK5 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Midge Robertson | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A classic, comforting vintage recipe for scalloped potatoes layered with butter, flour, and milk until tender and golden brown. Perfect as a rich and creamy side dish for any hearty family meal.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 1 hour | None | 6–8 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Baking pan
* Knife
* Peeler

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 | [[Potatoes]] | peeled and thinly sliced |
| As Needed | [[Flour]] | for dusting layers |
| To Taste | [[Salt]] | divided |
| To Taste | [[Pepper]] | divided |
| 1 tablespoon per layer | [[Butter]] | cut into pieces |
| As Needed | [[Milk]] | for pouring |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Layering the Potatoes
1. Preheat the oven to **350°F to 375°F**.
2. In a well-buttered baking pan, place a single layer of **thinly sliced potatoes**.
3. Lightly sprinkle the layer with **flour**, **salt**, and **pepper**.
4. Dot the layer with **butter** (about **1 tablespoon**, placed in each corner).
5. Continue creating layers of **potatoes**, **flour**, **salt**, **pepper**, and **butter**.
6. Do not flour the top layer of **potatoes**.

### Step 2: Baking
7. Pour **milk** over the **potatoes** until the dish is about **2/3 full**.
8. Bake for less than **one hour**, or until the **potatoes** are tender and the top is golden brown and bubbling.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Leftovers can be covered and refrigerated for up to 3 days; reheat gently in the oven before serving.
