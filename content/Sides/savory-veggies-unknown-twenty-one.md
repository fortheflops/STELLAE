# Savory Veggies

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty One | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A quick and flavorful side dish featuring a mix of tender vegetables simmered in chicken broth. Perfect as an easy accompaniment to any weeknight meal.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5 mins | 5 mins | None | 4 servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Saucepan

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 C | [[Chicken broth]] | chix broth |
| 3 C | [[Mixed vegetables]] | broccoli, cauliflower, carrots, celery |

---

## Instructions

### Cooking
1. In saucepan, combine **1 C chicken broth** and **3 C mixed vegetables**. Heat over medium heat to bring to a boil.
2. Cover, reduce heat to low, and cook for **5 minutes** or until vegetables are crisp-tender. Drain before serving.
