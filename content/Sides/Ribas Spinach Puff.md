# Riba's Spinach Puff

> 📜 **Collection:** General Archive | ✍️ **Attribution:** Riba | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A classic and savory side dish featuring a light, fluffy spinach and cream cheese base layered with crunchy crackers and topped with cheddar cheese. This delightful casserole bakes up golden and puffed for any family dinner.*

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 (10 oz) Packages | [[Spinach]] | Frozen |
| 4 Ounce Package | [[Cream Cheese]] | Softened |
| 3 | [[Eggs]] | Beaten |
| 4 Tablespoons | [[Butter]] | Melted |
| 3/4 Cup | [[Milk]] | Room temperature |
| 1/4 Teaspoon | [[Salt]] | None |
| Dash | [[White Pepper]] | None |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Harvest Wheat Crackers]] | Crumbled, divided |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Cheddar Cheese]] | Shredded |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Spinach Base
1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Grease a square baking pan.
2. Cook the **2 packages frozen spinach** according to package directions. Drain **very well**, squeezing out as much excess moisture as possible.
3. In a pan over low heat, combine the hot, drained **spinach** with the softened **4 ounce package cream cheese**. Stir until the cheese is melted and the mixture is smooth.

### Step 2: Mix the Custard Base
4. Remove the pan from the heat. In a large bowl, combine the spinach-cheese mixture with the **3 beaten eggs**, melted **4 tablespoons butter**, **3/4 cup milk**, **1/4 teaspoon salt**, and dash of **white pepper**. Mix well.

### Step 3: Assemble and Bake
5. Pour **half of the spinach mixture** into the greased pan. Sprinkle evenly with **1/4 cup harvest wheat crackers** (half of the crumbs).
6. Pour the **remaining spinach mixture** over the crumbs, then top with the shredded **1/2 cup cheddar cheese** and the **remaining 1/4 cup harvest wheat crackers**.
7. Bake for **30 minutes**, or until the casserole is puffed, set in the center, and golden brown on top.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store sealed in the refrigerator for up to 3 days. Reheats well in the oven or microwave.
