# Dijon Carrots

> 📜 **Collection:** Patsy's Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *Tender sliced carrots cooked to perfection and tossed in a sweet and tangy butter, brown sugar, and Dijon glaze. A simple yet elegant vegetable side dish suitable for both weeknight dinners and special occasions.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 12 mins | None | 4 Servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Vegetable peeler
* Chef's knife
* Small saucepan with lid
* Colander
* Serving dish

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 Pound | [[Carrots]] | Peeled and cut into ¼-inch diagonal slices |
| ½ Cup | Water | For simmering |
| 1 Tbsp | [[Butter]] |  |
| 1 Tbsp | [[Brown Sugar]] |  |
| 1 Tbsp | [[Dijon Mustard]] |  |
| To taste | [[Parsley]] | Snipped, for garnish |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Simmer the Carrots
1. In a small saucepan, bring **½ cup water** to a boil over medium-high heat.
2. Add the prepared **1 pound carrots**, cover with a lid, and cook until barely tender, about **10 minutes**.
3. Drain the **carrots** thoroughly in a colander.

### Step 2: Glaze and Serve
4. Return the drained **carrots** to the pan and stir in **1 tbsp butter**, **1 tbsp brown sugar**, and **1 tbsp Dijon mustard**.
5. Cook and stir over medium heat until the sugar dissolves and the carrots are coated in a shiny, smooth glaze, about **1 to 2 minutes**.
6. Transfer to a warm serving dish and sprinkle with fresh snipped **parsley**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Carrots can be peeled and sliced a day in advance and stored in the refrigerator. Store leftover cooked carrots in an airtight container for up to 3 days and gently reheat on the stovetop.
