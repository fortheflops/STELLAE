## Recipe
| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
| 1 Cup               | Cubed Luncheon Meat                     |
| $\frac{1}{4}$ Cup  | Chopped Onion                           |
| 2 Tbsp              | Butter or Margarine                     |
| 1 Tbsp              | Ketchup                                 |
| 1 can (21 oz)       | Campbell's Pork & Beans                 |
| Generous Dash       | Cinnamon                                |
| $\frac{1}{2}$ Cup  | Canned Peach Slices *(Drained suggested)* |
___
### Instructions
1.  In saucepan, brown meat and cook onion in butter until tender.
2.  Stir in remaining ingredients.
3.  Heat; stir now and then.
___

>[!Note]
>Serves 3-5
