## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 Pound               | [[Bean Sprouts]] (boil)         |
| 1 Teaspoon            | [[Salt]]                        |
| 1/4 Teaspoon          | [[Pepper]]                      |
| 1 Teaspoon            | [[Sesame Seed]]                 |
| 1 1/2 Teaspoons       | [[Vinegar]]                     |
| 1 Teaspoon            | [[Sesame Oil]]                  |
| 1/2 Teaspoon          | Crushed [[Red Pepper]] (hot)    |
| 1                     | [[Green Onion]], chopped        |
| 1/2 Clove             | [[Garlic]], minced              |
| 1 1/2 Teaspoons       | [[Soy Sauce]]                   |
___
## Instructions
1. Boil bean sprouts and squeeze out excess water.
2. Rinse in cold water.
3. Mix with remaining ingredients (salt, pepper, sesame seed, vinegar, sesame oil, crushed red pepper, chopped green onion, minced garlic, soy sauce)
___

>[!Note]
>This is a good side dish for hot days.
