# Carrots & Rutabega

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty One | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *Discover a comforting side dish featuring boiled and coarsely mashed carrots and rutabega enriched with butter. This simple root vegetable mash offers a wholesome, flavorful addition to any hearty meal.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 30 mins | None | 6-8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Saucepan
* Potato masher
* Colander
* Knife
* Cutting board

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 lbs | [[Carrots]] | cut in 1" pieces |
| 1 lb | [[Rutabega]] | cut in 1" pieces |
| 3 tbsp | [[Butter]] | or oleo |
| 1 pinch | [[Sugar]] | optional |
| to taste | [[Salt and Pepper]] | S&P |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Boiling and Mashing
1. Boil the **2 lbs carrots** and **1 lb rutabega** for **30 minutes** or until tender.
2. Drain the veggies, then mash coarse and add the **3 tbsp butter or oleo**, **1 pinch sugar**, and **salt and pepper** to taste until well incorporated.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftover mashed root vegetables in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days. Reheat gently on the stovetop or in the microwave with a splash of milk or a pat of butter.
