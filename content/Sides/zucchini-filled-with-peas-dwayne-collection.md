# Zucchini Filled with Peas

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *Tender zucchini halves scooped into shells and filled with a savory mixture of bacon, creamed peas, and herbs. A comforting and vibrant vintage vegetable side dish.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 20 mins | None | 4 servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large pot
* Skillet
* Spoon
* Chef's knife

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 4 whole | [[Zucchini]] | washed |
| 2 slices | [[Bacon]] | cut into small pieces |
| 1 tbsp | [[Onion]] | finely chopped |
| 1 tsp | [[Flour]] | None |
| 2 cups | [[Creamed Peas]] | cooked (frozen, canned, or fresh) |
| To taste | [[Salt]] | None |
| To taste | [[Black Pepper]] | freshly ground |
| To taste | [[Parsley]] | chopped, for garnish |

---

## Instructions

### Prep Zucchini Shells
1. Drop **4 whole zucchini** into boiling **salted water** and cook until tender, taking care not to overcook.
2. Drain well, split each zucchini in half lengthwise, and scoop out approximately **1 tbsp zucchini pulp** from the center to create hollowed shells.

### Prepare Filling & Serve
3. In a skillet, sauté **2 slices bacon** (cut into small pieces) with **1 tbsp finely chopped onion** until crisp.
4. Add **1 tsp flour** to thicken the drippings.
5. Add **2 cups cooked creamed peas** to the skillet.
6. Season to taste with **salt** and **freshly ground black pepper**, then mix well.
7. Pile the pea mixture into the **zucchini** shells and sprinkle with **chopped parsley** before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The zucchini shells can be boiled and hollowed a day in advance; store covered in the refrigerator and warm briefly before filling.
