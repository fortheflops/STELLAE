# Wild Rice And Yellow Rice Dish

> 📜 **Collection:** Burcea Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sides
> *A traditional archival recipe featuring a flavorful combination of wild rice with water chestnuts and chicken broth, paired alongside yellow rice with chicken, onion, and celery. This comforting side dish offers a delightful interplay of textures and savory aromas suitable for family gatherings.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 45 mins | None | 6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Saucepan
* Mixing spoon
* Casserole dish

---
## Ingredients

### Wild Rice Section
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 cup | [[Wild Rice]] | Cooked |
| 1 can | [[Water Chestnuts]] | Sliced |
| 1 can | [[Cream of Chicken Soup]] | None |
| to taste | [[Mushrooms]] | Optional |

### Yellow Rice Section
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 cup | [[Yellow Rice]] | Prepared |
| 1/2 cup | [[Chicken]] | Cooked and diced |
| 1/4 cup | [[Onion]] | Diced |
| 1/4 cup | [[Celery]] | Diced |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation
1. Combine the cooked **1 cup wild rice**, sliced **1 can water chestnuts**, and **1 can cream of chicken soup** in a mixing bowl until thoroughly incorporated.

### Step 2: Assembly and Baking
2. In a separate dish, mix the prepared **1 cup yellow rice** with the diced **1/2 cup chicken**, **1/4 cup onion**, and **1/4 cup celery**.
3. Layer or combine the mixtures as desired in a baking dish and bake until hot, bubbly, and fragrant.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the rice mixtures in the baking dish a day ahead, cover tightly, and refrigerate until ready to bake.
