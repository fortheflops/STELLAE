## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
| Enough to make 2 Cups| Fresh Spinach (Or Frozen)                      |
| 1/2 Cup          | Chopped Onion                                  |
| 2                   | Eggs                                           |
| 1/2 Cup              | Milk             |
| 1/2 Cup    | Grated Cheese |
| To Taste         | Buttered Bread Crumbs|
___
### Instructions
1. Drain Fresh Spinach (To Make 2 Cups Cooked) In Salted Boiling Water, (Or Frozen).
2. Chop Onion. (1/2 Cup).
3. In Blender, Add Eggs, 1/2 Cup Milk, And Drained Spinach And Chop.
4. Pour Into Buttered Dish, Add 1/2 Cup Grated Cheese, And Top With Buttered Bread Crumbs.
5. Bake 20 Minutes At 350 Degrees.
