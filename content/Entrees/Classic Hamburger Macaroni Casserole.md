# Classic Hamburger Macaroni Casserole

> 📜 **Collection:** UK5 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A classic, hearty comfort food casserole featuring seasoned ground beef, tender macaroni, and a rich tomato-Velveeta cheese sauce. This easy dish is a perfect weeknight family meal.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 40 mins | None | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 9x13 inch casserole dish
* Large skillet
* Large bowl
* Colander

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 pound | [[Hamburger]] | Ground beef |
| 1 package | [[Macaroni]] | 16 oz, cooked and drained |
| As Needed | [[Tomatoes]] | And/or tomato paste |
| 1 pound | [[Velveeta]] | Cubed, "lots" of cheese |
| 1 | [[Onion]] | Chopped |
| As Needed | [[Seasonings]] | Salt, pepper, etc. to taste |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Cook Pasta
1. Preheat oven to **350°F (175°C)**. Grease a large casserole dish.
2. Cook the **1 package macaroni** according to package directions. Drain well.

### Step 2: Brown the Meat
3. In a large skillet, brown the **1 pound hamburger** with the **1 chopped onion** and **as needed seasonings**. Drain off any excess grease.

### Step 3: Combine and Bake
4. In a large bowl, combine the cooked macaroni, the meat mixture, **as needed tomatoes**, and most of the **1 pound Velveeta cheese**.
5. Pour the mixture into the prepared casserole dish and top with the remaining cheese until evenly covered.
6. Bake for **30-40 minutes** until hot, bubbly, and the cheese is completely melted.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole ahead of time and store sealed in the refrigerator for up to 24 hours before baking.
