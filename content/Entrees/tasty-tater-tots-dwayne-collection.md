## Instructions

### Tasty Tater Tot Casserole Assembly & Baking
1. Spread **1 package frozen mixed vegetables** evenly in the bottom of a casserole dish.
2. Place **1 lb. browned ground chuck** directly on top of the vegetable layer.
3. Spread **1 can cream of mushroom soup** evenly over the meat, then pour **1/4 cup milk** over the top.
4. Arrange **1 package frozen tater tots** in neat rows across the top of the casserole.
5. Bake uncovered at **375°F** for **30 minutes** until the tater tots are golden brown, crispy, and the edges are bubbling hot.

### Chuck Roast Marinade Preparation
6. Combine **1 1/2 cups salad oil**, **3/4 cup soy sauce**, **1/4 cup Worcestershire sauce**, **2 tbsp dry mustard**, **2 1/2 tsp salt**, **1 tbsp coarse black pepper**, **1/2 cup red wine vinegar**, **1 1/2 tsp dried parsley flakes**, **2 cloves garlic**, and **1/3 cup fresh lemon juice** in a medium bowl.
7. Mix well until thoroughly emulsified. This makes about **3 1/2 cups marinade**.
8. Pour over chuck roast or steaks to marinate. The marinade can be drained from the steaks and saved for a second use before broiling.

### Chow Mein Casserole Variation
9. Brown **1 lb. ground beef** in a skillet and drain excess fat.
10. In a baking dish, mix the browned meat with **1/2 cup rice**, **2 cans cream of chicken soup**, **1/4 cup soy sauce**, and **1 1/2 cups water**.
11. Bake for **30 minutes**.
12. Top with **1 can chow mein noodles** and cook for an additional **30 minutes** until the noodles are toasted and the rice is tender.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The Tater Tot Casserole can be assembled up to 24 hours in advance, covered, and stored in the refrigerator. Add 5 to 10 minutes to the baking time if cooking directly from the fridge.
