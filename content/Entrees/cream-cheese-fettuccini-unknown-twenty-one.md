# Cream Cheese Fettuccini

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty One | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *Discover a quick and creamy Cream Cheese Fettuccini recipe featuring a savory garlic and herb cheese sauce. This lighter pasta dish comes together in minutes for a comforting weeknight meal.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 15 mins | None | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Pot
* Colander
* Saucepan

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 16 oz. | [[Fettuccini]] | or other pasta of choice |
| 1 T | [[Olive oil]] | or butter substitute |
| 1/4-1/2 c | [[Skim milk]] | none |
| 6 oz | [[Alpine Lace fat free cream cheese w/garlic & herbs]] | none |

---

## Instructions

### Cooking Instructions
1. Cook **16 oz. fettuccini** and coat with **1 T olive oil**.
2. Melt **6 oz Alpine Lace fat free cream cheese w/garlic & herbs** & **1/4-1/2 c skim milk** til blended.
3. Toss together until the pasta is evenly coated and steaming hot.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Best served fresh, but leftovers can be stored in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 2 days.
