# Chinese Casserole

> 📜 **Collection:** Christine Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A comforting and savory baked casserole featuring seasoned ground beef, rice, and mixed vegetables combined with creamy soups. Perfect for a hearty weeknight family dinner.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 1 hour | None | 6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Skillet
* Large baking dish
* Can opener

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 lbs | [[Hamburger]] | Ground beef |
| 1 | [[Onion]] | Medium, chopped |
| 1 can | [[Cream of Mushroom Soup]] | Condensed |
| 1 can | [[Cream of Chicken Soup]] | Condensed |
| 1 can | [[Chinese Vegetables]] | Drained |
| 1 can | [[Brussels Sprouts]] | Drained |
| 3/4 cup | [[Raw Rice]] | Uncooked |
| 2 tbsp | [[Soy Sauce]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and Cooking
1. Brown the **2 lbs hamburger** and **1 medium onion** in a skillet, then drain well.
2. In a large baking dish, mix the cooked hamburger, **1 can cream of mushroom soup**, **1 can cream of chicken soup**, **1 can Chinese vegetables**, and **1 can Brussels sprouts**.
3. Stir in **3/4 cup raw rice** and **2 tbsp soy sauce** until well combined.

### Step 2: Baking
4. Bake at 350°F for **45 minutes to 1 hour**, until bubbling and the rice is tender and done.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole up to a day in advance, cover tightly, and refrigerate before baking; add an extra 15 minutes to the baking time if baking straight from the fridge.
