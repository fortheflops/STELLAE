# Beef Bourguignon

> 📜 **Collection:** Al Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Ina Garten | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *An exquisite, slow-cooked French classic featuring tender beef chuck, smoky bacon, and earthy mushrooms simmered in a rich red wine sauce. This comforting, crowd-pleasing stew is perfect for elegant dinner parties or cozy family gatherings.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep & Sear
1. Preheat the oven to **250 degrees F**.
2. Heat the **1 tablespoon olive oil** in a large Dutch oven. Add the **16 ounces bacon** and cook over medium heat for **10 minutes**, stirring occasionally, until the bacon is lightly browned. Remove the bacon with a slotted spoon to a large plate.
3. Dry the **5 pounds beef cubes** with paper towels and then sprinkle them with **kosher salt** and **black pepper**. In batches in single layers, sear the beef in the hot oil for **3 to 5 minutes**, turning to brown on all sides. Remove the seared cubes to the plate with the bacon and continue searing until all the beef is browned. Set aside.

### Step 2: Simmer & Bake
4. Toss the **2 pounds carrots**, **4 yellow onions**, **1 tablespoon salt**, and **2 teaspoons pepper** in the fat in the pan and cook for **10 to 15 minutes**, stirring occasionally, until the onions are lightly browned. Add the **2 teaspoons garlic** and cook for **1 more minute**.
5. Put the meat and bacon back into the pot with the juices. Add the **1 bottle red wine** plus enough **beef broth** (up to **2 cups**) to almost cover the meat. Add the **1 tablespoon tomato paste** and **1 teaspoon thyme**. Bring to a simmer, cover the pot with a tight-fitting lid and place it in the oven for about **1 1/4 hours** or until the meat and vegetables are very tender when pierced with a fork.

### Step 3: Thicken & Finish
6. Combine **2 tablespoons butter** and the **3 tablespoons flour** with a fork and stir into the stew. Add the **1 pound frozen pearl onions**. Saute the **1 pound mushrooms** in the remaining **2 tablespoons butter** for **10 minutes** until lightly browned and then add to the stew. Bring the stew to a boil on top of the stove, then lower the heat and simmer for **15 minutes**. Season to taste.

### Step 4: Serve
7. To serve, toast the **sourdough bread** in the toaster or oven. Rub each slice on **1 side** with a cut clove of **garlic**. For each serving, spoon the stew over a slice of bread and sprinkle with **1/2 cup parsley**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This stew tastes even better the next day. Store covered in the refrigerator for up to 3 days and gently reheat on the stovetop before serving.
