# Baked Enchiladas

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Five | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *Discover a comforting classic with these layered baked enchiladas, featuring savory ground beef, melted sharp cheese, and rich tomato sauce. Perfect for feeding a crowd with hearty, traditional flavors.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 25 mins | 1 hour 15 mins | None | 6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Pan
* 2 qt. Casserole
* Grater

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparing the Meat Mixture
1. Crumble **1 lb. ground beef** into a small amount of **shortening** in a pan and brown thoroughly until fully cooked.
2. Add **1 large or 2 med. onions (chopped)**, **2 cans tomato sauce**, **1 can water**, **garlic**, **green chili**, **salt**, and **pepper** to taste. Simmer until flavors blend.

### Step 2: Layering the Casserole
3. Grate **1/2 lb. sharp cheese** and break **1 pkg. tortillas** into small pieces.
4. In a greased **2 qt. casserole**, place alternating layers of **tortillas**, **meat mixture**, and sprinkle with **cheese** until full and all ingredients are used, ensuring **meat & cheese** are on the very last top layer.

### Step 3: Baking
5. Bake at **350°** for **1 hour & 15 min** until bubbly and golden brown on top with melted, bubbling cheese.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole completely in advance, cover tightly, and refrigerate for up to 24 hours before baking.
