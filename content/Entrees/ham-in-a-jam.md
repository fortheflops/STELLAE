# Ham in a Jam

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A unique, sweet-and-savory ham dish featuring thin slices rolled with orange marmalade and glazed to perfection. This classic recipe offers a delightful twist on traditional baked ham.*

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Toothpicks
* Paper towels

* Ovenproof platter

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 slices | [[Ham]] | Boiled or smoked |
| To taste | [[Orange Marmalade]] | Spread |
| As needed | [[Sugar]] | For glazing |

---

## Instructions

### Preparation and Baking
1. Spread each **2 slices ham** with **orange marmalade**, roll tightly, and secure with **toothpicks**.
2. Wrap each roll in heavy wet paper towels, folding edges tightly.
3. Place in a pan with a tight cover and bake for 20 minutes at 425°F.
4. Unwrap, place on an ovenproof platter, pour pan juices over the top, and sprinkle with **sugar**.
5. Glaze in a hot oven until bubbly, then remove **toothpicks** before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The ham rolls can be assembled and kept refrigerated for a few hours before the initial baking step.
