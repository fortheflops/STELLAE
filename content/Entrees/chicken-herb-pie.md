# Chicken Herb Pie

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A comforting, savory chicken pie featuring a medley of vegetables and mushrooms, topped with golden, poppy seed-dusted biscuits. This classic recipe is perfect for a hearty family meal and can be prepared ahead for convenience.*

## Instructions

### Preparation
1. Place **2 cups cooked chicken** in a deep 9-inch pie pan.
2. Sauté **1 bunch baby onions**, **1/2 cup diced celery**, and **1 can whole mushrooms** in a portion of the **1/4 cup butter** until golden.
3. Remove vegetables from the saucepan and spread them evenly over the **2 cups cooked chicken** along with the **1 pkg frozen carrots and peas**.

### Sauce and Baking
4. In the saucepan, blend **1 tbsp all-purpose flour** with the remaining **1/4 cup butter**, stirring until smooth.
5. Gradually add **1 cup chicken broth** and **1/3 cup cream**, cooking until thickened while stirring constantly.
6. Add **1/8 tsp marjoram** and **salt and pepper** to taste, then pour the sauce over the chicken mixture.
7. To bake, preheat oven to 350°F and bake for 25 minutes.
8. Increase heat to 425°F, top with **biscuits**, sprinkle with **poppy seeds**, and bake for an additional 15 to 18 minutes until the biscuits are golden brown.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The assembled pie can be wrapped and frozen before baking. To serve, remove the wrap and bake as directed.
