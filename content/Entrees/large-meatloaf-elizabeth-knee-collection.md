# Large Meatloaf

## Instructions

### Preparation and Baking
1. Preheat your oven to **350°F**.
2. In a large mixing bowl, thoroughly combine the **1 1/2 lbs ground round**, **2 grated onions**, **4 or 5 minced garlic cloves**, **1/2 cup salsa**, **1 cup catsup**, **1 1/2 chopped green peppers**, **1/3 cup prepared mustard**, **1 tbsp horseradish**, **1/2 tsp sugar**, **salt**, and **black pepper**.
3. Incorporate the **2 eggs** and **2/3 cup oatmeal** into the mixture, blending gently until just combined to keep the texture light.
4. Shape the mixture into a cohesive loaf and place it into a greased baking dish.
5. Bake in the preheated **350°F** oven for **1 hour** until the meatloaf is firm to the touch and reaches an internal temperature of 160°F.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The meatloaf mixture can be shaped and kept covered in the refrigerator for up to 24 hours before baking.
