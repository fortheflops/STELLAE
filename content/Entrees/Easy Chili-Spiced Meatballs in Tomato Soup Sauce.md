# Easy Chili-Spiced Meatballs in Tomato Soup Sauce

> 📜 **Collection:** UK15 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A quick and easy retro recipe for ground beef meatballs, browned and then baked in a simple, savory sauce made from tomato soup and chili powder. Perfect for a cozy weeknight dinner.*

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |

| 1 pound | [[Ground Beef]] | Standard |
| 1/3 cup | [[Bread Crumbs]] | Fine dry |
| 2 tablespoons | [[Onion]] | Minced |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Salt]] | Standard |
| 3/4 teaspoon | [[Pepper]] | Standard |
| 1 | [[Egg]] | Slightly beaten |
| 1 can | [[Campbell's Tomato Soup]] | Divided for mixture and sauce |
| 2 tablespoons | [[Shortening]] | For browning |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Chili Powder]] | For sauce |
| 1/4 cup | [[Water]] | For sauce |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Form the Meatballs
1. Preheat your oven to 350°F.
2. In a large bowl, combine **1 pound** of **[[Ground Beef]]**, **1/3 cup** of **[[Bread Crumbs]]**, **2 tablespoons** of **[[Onion]]**, **1 teaspoon** of **[[Salt]]**, **3/4 teaspoon** of **[[Pepper]]**, **1** **[[Egg]]**, and **1/4 cup** of the **[[Campbell's Tomato Soup]]**.
3. Shape the mixture firmly into uniform 1-inch meatballs until all mixture is used.

### Step 2: Brown and Bake
4. In a large skillet, melt **2 tablespoons** of **[[Shortening]]** over medium heat and brown the **[[Ground Beef]]** meatballs on all sides until nicely seared and golden brown.
5. In a separate bowl, mix the remaining **[[Campbell's Tomato Soup]]**, **1 teaspoon** of **[[Chili Powder]]**, and **1/4 cup** of **[[Water]]** to create a smooth sauce.
6. Pour the chili-tomato sauce evenly over the browned meatballs in the skillet.
7. Cover the skillet or transfer to a baking dish, and bake in the 350°F oven for about **30 minutes** until bubbling, thickened, and cooked through.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Unbaked assembled meatballs can be refrigerated for up to 24 hours before browning, or fully cooked meatballs can be stored in an airtight container for up to 3 days.
