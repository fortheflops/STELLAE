## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Cornmeal Mush
1. In a saucepan, combine **3 1/2 cups water**, **1 cup cornmeal**, **1 tsp salt**, **2 tbsp olive oil**, and **1 tbsp chili powder**.
2. Cook over medium heat, stirring constantly, until the mixture becomes very thick and holds its shape.
3. Spread **2/3 of the cornmeal mush** evenly into the bottom of a greased casserole dish. Save the remaining **1/3 of the cornmeal mush** to use later for the top layer.

### Step 2: Prepare the Meat Filling
4. Chop **1 green pepper** and **1 onion**. Pour **1/4 cup boiling water** over them, then brown them in **1/4 cup oil** in a large skillet over medium heat.
5. Add **1 lb ground beef**, **1 tsp salt**, **2 tsp chili powder**, **1 tsp cumin**, and **garlic** to taste. Cook, breaking up the meat, until the meat is almost done and no longer pink.
6. Stir in **1 large can of tomatoes** and **3/4 cup chopped olives**. Cook down until almost all the liquid has evaporated and the mixture is thick.

### Step 3: Assemble and Bake
7. Pour the prepared meat filling over the bottom layer of mush in the casserole dish.
8. Spread the remaining **1/3 of the cornmeal mush** evenly over the top of the meat layer.
9. Sprinkle **1/2 cup grated cheese** over the top.
10. Bake at **350°F** for **30 minutes** until the casserole is bubbling hot and the cheese is melted and lightly golden.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The cornmeal mush and meat filling can be prepared and assembled in the casserole dish up to 24 hours in advance. Cover and refrigerate, then add 10-15 minutes to the baking time if cooking straight from the fridge.
