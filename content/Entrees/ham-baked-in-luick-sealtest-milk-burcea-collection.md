# Ham Baked in Luick Sealtest Milk

> 📜 **Collection:** Burcea Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A classic savory recipe featuring a thick-cut ham baked in milk and seasonings until tender, creating its own rich gravy. Accompanied by a traditional homemade egg noodle recipe.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Ham Preparation
1. Unless you know the **1 slice ham** is not too salty, cover the slice with **cold water** and heat to boiling in a skillet.
2. If the skillet is much larger than the slice, transfer the **1 slice ham** to a casserole or smaller baking dish for baking.
3. Spread the top with a mixture of **brown sugar** and **dry mustard**; rub the seasonings well into the **1 slice ham**.
4. Just cover the **1 slice ham** with **milk** in which **baking soda** has been dissolved (only **1/16 teaspoon** to a **cup of milk**).

### Step 2: Baking
5. Bake in a moderate oven at **375 degrees F.** for **1 hour** until done and bubbly.
6. The **milk** will form a gravy which can be served with the **1 slice ham**.

### Step 3: Making Noodles
7. Mix **eggs**, **1 tbsp water** per egg, **pinch salt**, and enough **flour** to form a soft dough.
8. Roll out to a thin sheet using plenty of **flour** on the board, then allow to dry.
9. Fold and cut into thin strips, dry thoroughly, and cook in **salted water** until tender.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Noodles can be fully dried and stored in an airtight container for weeks before cooking.
