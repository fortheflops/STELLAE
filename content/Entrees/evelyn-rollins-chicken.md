# Evelyn Rollins Chicken

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Evelyn Rollins | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A classic, comforting chicken casserole featuring a savory mushroom sauce and a crunchy almond topping. This simple one-dish meal is perfect for busy weeknights.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 45 mins | None | 4 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Casserole dish
* Skillet

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 lb | [[Chicken]] | cut into pieces |
| 1/2 cup | [[Flour]] | for dredging |
| 1 can | [[Cream of Mushroom Soup]] | undiluted |
| 1/2 cup | [[Water]] | divided |
| 1 cup | [[Fresh Mushrooms]] | sliced |
| 1/2 cup | [[Pitted Olives]] | sliced |
| 1/4 cup | [[Slivered Almonds]] | toasted |
| to taste | [[Salt and Pepper]] | seasoning |

---

## Instructions

### Preparation and Baking
1. Dredge the **chicken** in **flour** and brown in a skillet with a little water.
2. Place the browned **chicken** into a casserole dish.
3. Top with **cream of mushroom soup**, **fresh mushrooms**, **pitted olives**, and **slivered almonds**.
4. Season with **salt and pepper** and add a splash of **water**.
5. Cover the dish and bake in the oven until the **chicken** is tender and the juices run clear.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole up to 8 hours in advance and keep refrigerated until ready to bake.
