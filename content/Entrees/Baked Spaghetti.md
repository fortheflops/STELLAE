# Baked Spaghetti

> 📜 **Collection:** Jill Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A simple, old-fashioned baked spaghetti casserole featuring a comforting creamy tomato and cheese sauce. This classic family favorite bakes up warm, bubbly, and golden on top.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 30 mins | None | 6–8 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large pot
* Skillet
* Casserole dish
* Colander
* Wooden spoon

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 package | [[Spaghetti]] | Uncooked |
| 1 can | [[Whole Tomatoes]] | Canned |
| 1 can | [[Tomato Soup]] | Canned |
| 1 | [[Onion]] | Chopped |
| 1/2 cup | [[Green Pepper]] | Finely chopped |
| As Needed | [[Mushrooms]] | Optional, sliced |
| A small chunk | [[Melting Cheese]] | e.g., Velveeta or cheddar |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Cook Pasta and Sauté Vegetables
1. Preheat your oven to **350°F**. Cook the **1 package spaghetti** in a large pot of boiling water according to package directions, then drain using a colander.
2. While the spaghetti cooks, fry the **1 onion**, **1/2 cup green pepper**, and optional **as needed mushrooms** in a skillet over medium heat until tender and browned.

### Step 2: Combine and Bake
3. Add the **a small chunk melting cheese** to the skillet with the vegetables and stir continuously until the cheese is completely melted, creating a rich sauce.
4. In a large bowl or back in the spaghetti pot, combine the drained **1 package spaghetti** with the **1 can whole tomatoes** (breaking them up gently with a spoon) and **1 can tomato soup**.
5. Pour the warm cheese and vegetable mixture into the spaghetti and mix everything well until thoroughly combined.
6. Transfer the mixture to a casserole dish and bake in the preheated oven until bubbly and golden brown on top.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole in advance and store covered in the refrigerator for up to 24 hours before baking.
