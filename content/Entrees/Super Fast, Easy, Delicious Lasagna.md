# Super Fast, Easy, Delicious Lasagna

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A remarkably quick and easy skillet lasagna featuring layered noodles, ground meat, and rich cheeses. This simplified recipe delivers classic Italian flavors in a fraction of the traditional cooking time.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 35 mins | 10 mins | 6-8 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large skillet with lid
* Measuring cups
* Measuring spoons
* Spatula

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 Pound | [[Cooked, Drained Ground Meat]] | ground turkey is good |
| 1 Small Package | [[Spaghetti Mix (dry)]] | divided |
| 1 Pint Carton | [[Small Curd Cottage Cheese]] | chilled |
| 3 Cups | [[Dry Wide Noodles]] | un-cooked |
| 1 Small Package | [[Spaghetti Mix (2nd package)]] | divided |
| 3 Cups | [[Canned Tomatoes with Juice]] | undrained |
| 1 Cup | [[Water]] | room temperature |
| 1 Tablespoon | [[Parsley Flakes]] | dried |
| 1 Tablespoon | [[Sweet Basil]] | dried |
| 1 Big Package | [[Grated Jack or Mozzarella Cheese]] | shredded |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Layering the Skillet
1. In a big skillet, layer ingredients in the following order: First, add the **1 pound cooked, drained ground meat**. Sprinkle **1 small package spaghetti mix (dry)** over the meat.
2. Next, add the **1 pint carton small curd cottage cheese**, followed by the **3 cups dry wide noodles**.
3. Sprinkle the **1 small package spaghetti mix (2nd package)** over the noodles.
4. Pour the **3 cups canned tomatoes with their juice** over these layers, and finally, add **1 cup water**.
5. Gently poke holes through the layers so liquid gets to the bottom and prevents the meat from scorching.

### Step 2: Seasoning and Simmering
6. Sprinkle the **1 tablespoon parsley flakes** and **1 tablespoon sweet basil** over the top of the assembled layers.
7. Bring the mixture to a boil. Cover the skillet and reduce the heat to a simmer.
8. Cook for **35 minutes**, or until the noodles are tender and cooked through.

### Step 3: Melting the Cheese
9. Remove the skillet from the heat. Cover the entire top of the lasagna with the **1 big package grated Jack or Mozzarella cheese**.
10. Cover the skillet again and let it sit for **10 minutes** until the cheese is completely melted and gooey.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftover lasagna in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days. Reheat individual portions in the microwave.
