# Pirozhkis

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Dough
1. In a large mixing bowl, combine the **1/2 cup butter**, **1/2 pint sour cream**, and whole **1 egg** until smooth.
2. In a separate bowl, sift together enough **flour** (to thicken the mixture so that it can be rolled very thin), **1 tsp baking powder**, and a pinch of **salt**.
3. Gradually incorporate the dry ingredients into the wet mixture until a manageable dough forms.

### Step 2: Assemble and Bake
4. Roll the dough out very thin on a lightly floured surface. Cut into rings using a **2 1/2-inch** diameter cutter.
5. Place a teaspoon of your chosen filling (**meat**, **apples**, **fruit conserve**, or **fruits**) onto the center of each dough circle.
6. Fold the dough over the filling to create a half-moon shape and pinch the edges tightly together to seal.
7. Place the assembled pies on a well-greased baking sheet, paint the tops lightly with the beaten **1 egg**, and bake at **425°F** until golden brown and fragrant.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Unbaked assembled pirozhkis can be frozen on a tray before transferring to a bag for up to 1 month; bake directly from frozen, adding a few extra minutes to the bake time.
