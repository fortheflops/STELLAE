# Tuna Casserole

> 📜 **Collection:** Elizabeth Knee Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Carol Carpenter | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A classic, comforting Tuna Casserole recipe from the kitchen of Carol Carpenter. Quick to assemble with egg noodles, condensed mushroom soup, and a crunchy potato chip topping.*

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |

| 1 can | [[Tuna]] | drained |
| A little | [[Water]] | to loosen the soup |

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| To taste | [[Potato Chips]] | crushed, or crackers |

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Mix
1. Cook **1 package egg noodles** in boiling water for about **5 minutes**, then drain.
2. In a mixing bowl, combine the **1 can tuna**, **1 can mushroom soup**, and **a little water**.
3. Mix the cooked noodles and the tuna mixture together, then transfer all into a baking dish.

### Step 2: Bake
4. Top the mixture with crushed **potato chips** or crackers.
5. Bake at **350°** for **20 minutes** or until the edges bubble well and the topping is golden brown.
6. Optionally, **peas** may be mixed into the casserole before baking for color, or served on the side. Serve hot with a fresh salad.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the noodle and tuna mixture in the baking dish, cover, and refrigerate up to 24 hours ahead. Add the potato chip topping just before baking.
