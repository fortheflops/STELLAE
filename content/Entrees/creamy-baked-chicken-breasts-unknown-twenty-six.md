# Creamy Baked Chicken Breasts

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** M. Lake | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *Tender chicken breasts layered with Swiss cheese, mushroom soup, and a buttery stuffing crust. This comforting baked dish serves eight and is perfect for family gatherings.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 55 mins | None | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 13x9x2 baking dish
* Mixing bowl
* Measuring cups

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 4 | [[Chicken Breasts]] | Whole, split, skinned and boned |
| 8 slices | [[Swiss Cheese]] | Approx 4x4" slices |
| 1 (10 1/2 oz) can | [[Cream of Mushroom Soup]] | Undiluted |
| 1/4 cup | [[Water]] | For thinning soup |
| 1 cup | [[Herb-Seasoned Stuffing Mix]] | Crushed |
| 1/4 cup | [[Margarine]] | Melted |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Assembly
1. Arrange the **4 whole chicken breasts** in a lightly greased 13x9x2 baking dish.
2. Top the chicken with the **8 slices Swiss cheese**.
3. Combine the **1 can cream of mushroom soup** with **1/4 cup water**, stirring well until smooth.
4. Spoon the sauce evenly over the **chicken** and **cheese**.
5. Sprinkle with the **1 cup herb-seasoned stuffing mix**, then drizzle the **1/4 cup margarine** over the crumbs.

### Step 2: Baking
6. Bake at 350°F for **45 to 55 minutes** until the chicken is cooked through and the topping is golden brown and bubbling.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the dish up to a few hours in advance, cover, and refrigerate until ready to bake; add 5-10 minutes to the baking time if baking straight from the fridge.
