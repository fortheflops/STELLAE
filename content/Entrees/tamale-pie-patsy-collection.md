## Instructions

### Step 1: Cook the Tomato Base
1. In a saucepan, combine **1 (#2 1/2) can tomatoes**, **1 cup pitted black olives**, **1 small bay leaf**, **1/2 lb butter**, **1 tsp sugar**, and **1 dash salt**.
2. Cook for 20 minutes over medium heat, then set aside to cool.

### Step 2: Prepare the Cornmeal Mixture
3. In a bowl, mix **1 cup cornmeal** with just enough cold water to make damp.
4. Dip spoon by spoon into **1 cup boiling water** and cook for a few minutes until thickened.
5. Add **1 can creamed corn** and let cool.
6. Add **2 eggs** and **1 pinch salt**, then stir well.

### Step 3: Combine and Bake
7. Add the cooled tomato mixture to the cornmeal mixture and stir until combined.
8. Transfer to a baking dish and bake for 1 hour at 350°F until firmly set and hot throughout.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The tomato sauce and cooked cornmeal base can be prepared up to 24 hours in advance and refrigerated before assembling and baking.
