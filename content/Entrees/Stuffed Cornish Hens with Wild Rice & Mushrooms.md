# Stuffed Cornish Hens with Wild Rice & Mushrooms

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *An elegant dish perfect for guests, featuring Cornish game hens stuffed with a savory mixture of wild rice, sautéed mushrooms, onions, and walnuts, then roasted until golden.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 30 mins | 1 hour | None | 4 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Roasting pan
* Large mixing bowl
* Skillet
* Instant-read thermometer

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 package | [[MJB White and Wild Rice Mix]] | prepared according to package directions |
| 3/4 pound | [[Mushrooms]] | fresh, chopped |
| As needed | [[Onion]] | chopped, about 1/2 cup |
| As needed | [[Butter]] | for sautéing and brushing, about 2-3 tablespoons |
| As needed | [[Walnuts]] | chopped, about 1/2 cup |
| 4 | [[Cornish Game Hens]] | thawed and patted dry |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Rice
1. Preheat your oven to **400°F (200°C)**.
2. Thaw the **4 Cornish game hens** and pat them completely dry.
3. Prepare the **1 package MJB White and Wild Rice Mix** according to the package directions.

### Step 2: Sauté the Filling
4. While the rice cooks, melt some **butter** in a skillet and sauté the **3/4 pound fresh mushrooms** and chopped **onion** until tender and fragrant.
5. In a large bowl, combine the prepared **MJB White and Wild Rice Mix** with the sautéed **mushrooms** and **onions**.
6. Stir in the chopped **walnuts** until well incorporated.

### Step 3: Stuff and Roast
7. Loosely stuff the cavity of each **Cornish game hen** with the prepared rice and mushroom mixture.
8. Place the stuffed **hens** in a roasting pan, brush them with melted **butter**, and season with salt and pepper if desired.
9. Bake at **400°F (200°C)** for **50 minutes to 1 hour**, or until the juices run clear and an internal thermometer inserted into the thickest part of the thigh reads **165°F (74°C)** until golden brown and cooked through.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The rice and mushroom stuffing can be prepared a day in advance and stored in the refrigerator until ready to stuff and roast the hens.
