# Southwest Swiss Steak

> 📜 **Collection:** Georgia Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A hearty and flavorful Southwest Swiss Steak featuring tenderized round steak simmered with onions, carrots, tomatoes, and picante sauce. Finished with fresh cilantro, this comforting dish is perfect for a cozy family dinner.*

## Instructions

### Preparation and Cooking
1. Cut **1 1/2 lbs. boneless round or chuck steak** into serving pieces, then sprinkle with **1/2 tsp. salt** and **1/4 tsp. pepper**.
2. Pound all of the **1/4 cup flour** into the meat using a meat mallet until well incorporated.
3. Heat **2 tbsp. oil** in a large, heavy skillet over medium-high heat, and brown the meat on both sides until a nice crust forms.
4. Arrange the **2 large onions**, **2 cups carrots**, and **2 minced garlic cloves** over the browned meat.
5. Pour the **16 oz. canned tomatoes**, **1/2 cup picante sauce**, and **1/2 cup beef broth** over everything.
6. Cover tightly and simmer for **50 to 60 minutes** until the meat is tender.
7. Remove the meat to a serving platter. Cook the remaining sauce over high heat until it is reduced and thickened, about **5 to 7 minutes**.
8. Pour the thickened sauce over the meat and sprinkle with **2 tbsp. chopped cilantro** before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This dish can be made a day ahead and gently reheated on the stovetop; the flavors will deepen overnight. Store leftovers in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days.
