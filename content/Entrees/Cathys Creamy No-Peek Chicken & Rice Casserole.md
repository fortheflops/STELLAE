# Cathy's Creamy No-Peek Chicken & Rice Casserole

> 📜 **Collection:** General Archive | ✍️ **Attribution:** Cathy | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A classic and easy casserole featuring raw rice and chicken breasts layered with a creamy, savory sauce made from three kinds of soup and sherry, then topped with cheese and almonds and baked until tender.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 2 hours | 5-10 mins resting | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 9x13 inch casserole dish
* Small mixing bowl
* Aluminum foil

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 8 | [[Chicken Breasts]] | boned |
| 1 Cup | [[Raw Rice]] | uncooked |
| 1 Tablespoon | [[Butter]] | melted |
| 1 Can (10 3/4 oz.) | [[Cream of Mushroom Soup]] | divided |
| 1 Can (10 3/4 oz.) | [[Cream of Celery Soup]] | divided |
| 1 Can (10 3/4 oz.) | [[Cream of Chicken Soup]] | divided |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Sherry]] | or substitute [[Vermouth]] |
| As needed | [[Grated Cheddar Cheese]] | to taste |
| As needed | [[Sliced Almonds]] | to taste |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Layer Base
1. Preheat oven to **350°F (175°C)**. Choose a casserole dish large enough to hold the ingredients (e.g., 9x13 inch).
2. Spread the **1 cup raw rice** evenly in the bottom of the casserole dish.
3. Drizzle the **1 tbsp butter** over the rice and stir briefly to coat.

### Step 2: Add First Sauce Layer and Chicken
4. In a small bowl, combine **half** of the **1 can cream of mushroom soup**, **half** of the **1 can cream of celery soup**, and **half** of the **1 can cream of chicken soup**. Mix lightly and pour this half-mixture evenly over the rice layer.
5. Arrange the **8 chicken breasts** in a single layer on top of the rice and soup mixture.

### Step 3: Add Top Sauce and Garnishes
6. In the same small bowl, combine the **remaining half** of each of the three soups. Stir in the **1/2 cup sherry** (or vermouth) until well mixed.
7. Pour this sherry-soup mixture evenly over the **chicken breasts**.
8. Sprinkle the top generously with **grated cheddar cheese** and **sliced almonds**, covering the chicken and soup mixture.

### Step 4: Bake and Rest
9. Cover the casserole dish tightly with a lid or heavy-duty aluminum foil.
10. Bake in the preheated oven for **1 1/2 hours (90 minutes)**.
11. After **1 1/2 hours**, carefully remove the lid or foil. Return the casserole dish to the oven and continue baking **uncovered** for an additional **1/2 hour (30 minutes)** until the rice is cooked, the chicken is tender, and the topping is golden brown and bubbly.
12. Let the casserole rest for **5-10 minutes** before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole up to a step ahead and store covered in the refrigerator for up to 24 hours before baking, adding a few extra minutes to the covered bake time if baking cold.
