# Cook Lamb Shanks

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *Tender lamb shanks simmered to perfection in a rich and savory blend of cream of celery and mushroom soups. A classic vintage comfort food that yields a delicious gravy for potatoes or rice.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 2 hours | None | 4 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large pot or Dutch oven

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| As Needed | [[Lamb Shanks]] | Fresh |
| As Needed | [[Oil]] | For browning |
| 1 Can | Cream of [[Celery Soup]] | Undiluted |
| 1 Can | Cream of [[Mushroom Soup]] | Undiluted |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Brown the Meat
1. In a large pot or Dutch oven, brown the **as needed** **[[Lamb Shanks]]** in **as needed** **[[Oil]]** over medium-high heat until golden brown on all sides.
2. Carefully drain off all the excess oil from the pot.

### Step 2: Simmer and Braise
3. Add **1 can** of Cream of **[[Celery Soup]]** and **1 can** of Cream of **[[Mushroom Soup]]** to the pot, stirring gently to combine.
4. Cover the pot with a tight-fitting lid, reduce heat to low, and simmer until the lamb is fall-off-the-bone tender, taking approximately **1.5 to 2.5 hours** depending on the size of the shanks.

### Step 3: Serve
5. Serve the **[[Lamb Shanks]]** hot, spooning the savory gravy from the pot over the meat and alongside potatoes or rice.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Leftovers can be stored in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days and reheated gently.
