# Classic Layered Chicken & Stuffing Casserole

> 📜 **Collection:** General Archive | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *The ultimate comfort food. This classic casserole features layers of tender cooked chicken and creamy chicken soup, topped with a savory herb-seasoned stuffing and baked until golden brown and bubbly.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 1 hour | None | 6–8 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Oblong baking pan
* Small bowl
* Large bowl
* Aluminum foil

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 large | [[Chicken]] | cooked, boned, and shredded (or turkey) |
| 2 cans | [[Cream of Chicken Soup]] |  |
| 4 cups | [[Pepperidge Farm Herb Seasoned Stuffing]] |  |
| 1 stick | [[Butter]] | melted |
| 3 1/2 cups plus 6 Tablespoons | [[Chicken Broth]] | divided |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Layer
1. Preheat your oven to **350°F**. Lightly grease an **oblong baking pan**.
2. Spread the **1 large cooked and boned chicken** evenly across the bottom of the prepared pan.
3. In a small bowl, dilute the **2 cans cream of chicken soup** with **6 tablespoons chicken broth**. Spread this mixture evenly over the chicken layer.

### Step 2: Stuffing and Assembly
4. In a large bowl, mix together the **4 cups Pepperidge Farm herb seasoned stuffing**, **2 1/4 cups chicken broth**, and **1 stick melted butter** until the stuffing is evenly moistened.
5. Spread the stuffing mixture in an even layer over the soup layer.
6. Pour the remaining **1 cup chicken broth** evenly over the entire mixture in the pan to ensure moisture during baking.

### Step 3: Bake
7. Cover the pan tightly with **aluminum foil** and bake at **350°F** for **45 minutes**.
8. Remove the foil and bake uncovered for another **15 minutes**, or until the stuffing top is golden brown, crisp, and the casserole is bubbling hot around the edges.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole ahead of time, cover tightly, and refrigerate for up to 24 hours before baking. Add 5 to 10 minutes to the covered baking time if baking straight from the refrigerator.
