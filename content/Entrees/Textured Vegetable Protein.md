# Textured Vegetable Protein

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *Learn how to properly hydrate and prepare Textured Vegetable Protein (TVP) as a versatile meat substitute for burgers and other savory dishes. This simple archival guide ensures a perfect texture every time.*

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 Cup | [[Dry TVP (Textured Vegetable Protein)]] | Dry, pre-cooked |
| 7/8 Cup | [[Boiling Water]] | Hot |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Hydrate the TVP
1. Add the **7/8 cup boiling water** to the **1 cup dry TVP (Textured Vegetable Protein)**.

### Step 2: Soak and Rest
2. Stir the mixture and let it soak for **10 minutes** until the liquid is fully absorbed and the TVP is soft.

### Step 3: Cooking and Shaping
3. When browning the hydrated **1 cup dry TVP (Textured Vegetable Protein)**, stir continuously to avoid sticking until hot and fragrant.
4. When forming the hydrated **1 cup dry TVP (Textured Vegetable Protein)** into balls, keep your hands wet to prevent sticking.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Hydrated TVP can be stored in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days before using in meatless dishes.
