# Chili Rellenos Casserole

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A comforting, cheesy baked casserole featuring whole roasted green chilies layered with sharp cheddar and Monterey Jack cheeses, bound together with a simple egg and evaporated milk custard.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 45 mins | None | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 13x9-inch baking dish
* Paper towels
* Mixing bowl
* Whisk

---
## Ingredients

### Casserole Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3 cans (4 oz each) | [[Green Chilies]] | whole roasted |
| 1 lb. | [[Sharp Cheddar Cheese]] | grated |
| 1 lb. | [[Monterey Jack Cheese]] | grated |
| 3 | [[Eggs]] | beaten |
| 1 can (13 oz) | [[Evaporated Milk]] |  |
| As needed | [[Butter]] | for greasing the baking dish |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep the Chilies and Dish
1. Wash and split the **3 cans (4 oz each) green chilies**, removing all seeds and membranes. Dry them thoroughly on paper towels.
2. Generously grease a 13x9-inch baking dish with **butter**.

### Step 2: Assemble the Casserole
3. In a bowl, combine the **1 lb. sharp cheddar cheese** and **1 lb. Monterey Jack cheese**.
4. Place a layer of half of the mixed **cheeses** in the bottom of the prepared dish, layer all of the prepped **green chilies** on top, and cover with the remaining half of the **cheeses**.

### Step 3: Add Custard and Bake
5. Whisk together the **3 eggs** and **1 can (13 oz) evaporated milk** until well blended, then pour the mixture evenly over the **green chilies** and **cheeses**.
6. Bake at 350°F for **40-45 mins** until the custard is set, the cheese is melted and bubbly, and the top is beautifully golden brown.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole, cover tightly, and refrigerate overnight. Bake directly from the fridge, adding 5-10 minutes to the baking time if needed.
