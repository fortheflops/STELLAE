# Sunny Citrus Chicken

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty One | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *Brighten up dinner with Sunny Citrus Chicken, featuring tender breast halves baked in a vibrant orange and lemon glaze. Infused with aromatic curry and garlic, this easy dish is finished with fresh watercress.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 40 mins | None | 2 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Baking dish
* Mixing bowl

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 | [[Chicken Breast Halves]] | Halves |
| 1/2 tsp | [[Curry Powder]] | None |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Garlic Powder]] | None |
| 1/2 tsp | [[Orange Rind]] | Grated |
| 1 | [[Orange]] | Juiced |
| 2 tbsp | [[Lemon Juice]] | None |
| As needed | [[Watercress]] | For garnish |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and Baking
1. Place **2 chicken breast halves** in a baking dish.
2. Combine the **1/2 tsp curry powder**, **1/4 tsp garlic powder**, **1/2 tsp orange rind**, **1 orange juiced**, and **2 tbsp lemon juice**, then pour the mixture over the chicken.
3. Cover the dish and bake at 350°F for **30 min**.
4. Uncover and bake for **10 min** more, until the chicken is tender.

### Step 2: Serving
5. Serve on a platter with additional slices of orange and lemon, and garnish with **watercress**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Chicken can be marinated in the citrus and spice mixture, covered, and refrigerated for up to 4 hours before baking.
