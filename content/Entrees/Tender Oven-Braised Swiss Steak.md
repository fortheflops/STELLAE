# Tender Oven-Braised Swiss Steak

> 📜 **Collection:** UK5 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Midge Robertson | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A classic comfort food recipe featuring tender round steak slow-braised in the oven with stewed tomatoes, vegetables, and melted cheese. Perfect for a cozy weeknight dinner.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 2 hours 10 mins | None | 2 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Skillet
* Shallow baking dish
* Measuring cups
* Measuring spoons

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 to 3/4 pound | [[Round Steak]] | Cut into two portions |
| 2 tablespoons | [[Flour]] | Divided for coating and gravy |
| To Taste | [[Salt]] and [[Pepper]] | For seasoning |
| 1 (8 ounce) can | Stewed [[Tomatoes]] | Undrained |
| 1/4 cup | Chopped [[Celery]] | Finely chopped |
| 1/4 cup | Chopped [[Carrot]] | Finely chopped |
| 1 tablespoon | Chopped [[Onion]] | Finely chopped |
| 1/4 teaspoon | [[Worcestershire Sauce]] | None |
| 2 tablespoons | Shredded [[Cheese]] | For topping |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Brown the Meat
1. Cut the **1/2 to 3/4 pound [[Round Steak]]** into two portions.
2. Coat the meat thoroughly with **salt and pepper** and a portion of the **2 tablespoons [[Flour]]**.
3. In a skillet, brown the meat in hot oil until golden brown on both sides, then place the browned meat in a shallow baking dish.

### Step 2: Prepare the Sauce and Braise
4. Blend any remaining **2 tablespoons [[Flour]]** with the pan drippings left in the skillet.
5. Add the **1 (8 ounce) can stewed tomatoes**, **1/4 cup chopped celery**, **1/4 cup chopped carrot**, **1 tablespoon chopped onion**, and **1/4 teaspoon [[Worcestershire sauce]]** to the skillet.
6. Cook, stirring constantly, until the mixture boils.
7. Pour the hot sauce mixture evenly over the meat in the baking dish.
8. Cover and bake at 325°F - 350°F for **2 hours**, or until the meat is fork-tender and yields easily.

### Step 3: Melt the Cheese and Serve
9. Sprinkle the **2 tablespoons shredded [[Cheese]]** over the tender meat.
10. Return the baking dish to the oven just until the cheese melts and is bubbly.
