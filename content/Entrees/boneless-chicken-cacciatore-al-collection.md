# Boneless Chicken Cacciatore

> 📜 **Collection:** Al Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A classic, comforting Italian-style boneless chicken cacciatore simmered in a rich tomato and white wine sauce. This rustic family recipe yields tender chicken infused with aromatic herbs, peppers, and onions.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep the Chicken
1. Boil the **1 broiler fryer chicken** in water until fully cooked and tender. Drain, reserving **2 cups chicken stock**, and set aside to cool slightly. Once cool enough to handle, debone the **chicken** and tear or cut into bite-sized pieces.

### Step 2: Simmer the Sauce
2. In a large pot, combine the **2 cans tomato sauce**, **1 tsp oregano**, **1/2 cup dry white wine**, **1 medium onion**, **1 large green pepper**, **1 clove minced garlic**, **1/2 tsp salt**, **1/4 tsp pepper**, and the reserved **2 cups chicken stock**.
3. Bring the mixture to a gentle boil, then reduce heat and simmer uncovered for about **30 minutes** until the vegetables are tender and the sauce begins to thicken and develop a rich aroma.

### Step 3: Combine and Finish
4. Add the deboned **chicken** pieces back into the simmering sauce.
5. Cook for another **30 minutes** until the chicken is thoroughly heated through, tender, and has absorbed the rich flavors of the cacciatore sauce.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This dish tastes even better the next day as the flavors meld. Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days, or freeze for up to 3 months.
