## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Fry
1. Boil the **2 pkgs spaghetti** according to package instructions until tender.
2. In a skillet, fry the **1 chopped onion** and **1 chopped green pepper** in **butter** until softened.

### Step 2: Combine and Bake
3. Mix in **1 can tomato soup**, **1 small can corn**, and **2 cups minced cooked chicken** sautéed with pepper and onion.
4. Season with **Worcestershire sauce**, **cayenne**, **salt**, and **black pepper**.
5. Fold in **cheese**, **olives**, **pimentos**, and **mushrooms**, moistening the mixture with enough **chicken stock**.
6. Transfer to a baking dish and bake for **1/2 to 1 hour** until bubbly and golden on top.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the goulash in the baking dish ahead of time, cover, and refrigerate for up to 24 hours before baking.
