# Lena's Hearty 3-Cheese Chicken & Noodle Bake

> 📜 **Collection:** General Archive | ✍️ **Attribution:** Lena | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A rich, crowd-pleasing casserole featuring diced chicken and noodles baked in a creamy three-cheese sauce with mushrooms, pimentos, and basil.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 1 hour | None | 12–15 Servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Skillet
* Large mixing bowl
* 13" x 9" x 2" baking pan

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Sauce and Mix
1. Preheat oven to **350°F**. Grease a **13" x 9" x 2" pan**.
2. In a skillet, saute **½ cup chopped onion** and **½ cup chopped green pepper** in **3 tbsp butter** until tender.
3. Stir in **1 can condensed cream of chicken soup**, **1 can drained sliced mushrooms**, **1 (2oz) jar drained pimentos**, and **½ tsp dried basil**. Set this sauce mixture aside.
4. In a large bowl, combine **1 pkg (8 oz) noodles**, **3 cups diced cooked chicken**, **2 cups shredded cheddar cheese**, **2 cups ricotta**, and **½ cup grated parmesan cheese**.
5. Add the prepared sauce mixture to the large bowl and mix well until thoroughly combined.
6. Pour everything into the greased **13" x 9" x 2" pan**.

### Step 2: Bake the Casserole
7. Bake uncovered at **350°F** for **40 to 45 minutes**.
8. Sprinkle **¼ cup buttered bread crumbs** evenly over the top and bake for an additional **15 minutes**, or until golden and bubbly.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole a day in advance, cover, and refrigerate until ready to bake, adding an extra 10–15 minutes to the initial baking time if chilled.
