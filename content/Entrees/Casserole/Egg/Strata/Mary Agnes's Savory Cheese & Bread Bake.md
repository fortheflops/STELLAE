*A classic, savory bread and cheese casserole, also known as a strata. Layers of buttered bread and grated cheese are baked in a rich, seasoned egg and milk custard. A perfect brunch or side dish.*

### Instructions
1.  Preheat oven to 325°F.
2.  Spread bread with butter and cut each slice into 4 pieces.
3.  Alternate layers of cheese and bread in a baking dish, making sure that the cheese is on top.
4.  In a separate bowl, combine milk, eggs (slightly beaten), and seasonings (salt, dry mustard).
5.  Pour this mixture over the cheese and bread.
6.  Bake in a slow oven (about 325°F) for approximately 40 minutes.
