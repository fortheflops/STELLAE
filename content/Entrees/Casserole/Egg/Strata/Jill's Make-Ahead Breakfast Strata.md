*This classic breakfast casserole, also known as a strata, gets its "soufflé" name from the way it puffs up in the oven. It's a perfect make-ahead dish for brunch.*

## Recipe
| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 8 to 10 slices | [[White Bread]] |
| 3/4 pound | [[Cheddar Cheese]], shredded or cubed |
| 6 large | [[Eggs]] |
| 2 cups | [[Milk]] |
| 3/4 teaspoon | [[Salt]] |
| 1 dash | [[Pepper]] |
| For the dish | [[Butter]] or [[Shortening]] |
___
## Instructions
1.  Preheat oven to 325°F. Grease a casserole dish.
2.  Remove the crusts from the **bread** slices and cut the bread into cubes.
3.  In a mixing bowl, beat the **eggs** well. Stir in the **milk**, **salt**, and **pepper**.
4.  Create layers in the prepared dish: start with a layer of bread cubes, followed by a layer of **cheese**, and then pour some of the egg mixture over it.
5.  Repeat the layers until all ingredients are used.
6.  Bake for 1 hour, or until puffed and golden.
7.  Serve immediately.
___

>[!Note]
>For a richer flavor, you can assemble the casserole, cover it, and refrigerate for a few hours or overnight before baking. This allows the bread to fully absorb the egg mixture.
