*A classic make-ahead breakfast or brunch casserole, also known as a strata. Cubed bread and cheddar cheese are layered and soaked in a simple egg custard, then baked until puffed and golden.*
### Author
Unknown
___
## Recipe
| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 8 slices | [[White Bread]], crusts removed |
| 3/4 pound | Mild [[Cheddar Cheese]], cut in small cubes |
| 6 large | [[Eggs]], well beaten |
| 2 cups | [[Milk]] |
| 3/4 teaspoon | [[Salt]] |
| 1 dash | [[Black Pepper]] |
___
## Instructions
1.  Grease a casserole dish.
2.  Remove the crusts from the **bread** and cut it into small cubes.
3.  In a separate bowl, whisk together the well-beaten **eggs**, **salt**, **pepper**, and **milk**.
4.  To assemble, create alternating layers in the prepared casserole dish: place a layer of bread cubes, then a layer of cubed **cheese**, and pour some of the egg mixture over the top.
5.  Repeat the layers until all ingredients have been used.
6.  Cover the dish and place it in the refrigerator overnight.
7.  When ready to bake, preheat the oven to 325°F.
8.  Bake the casserole **uncovered** for 1 hour, or until the center is set and the top is golden brown.
9.  Serve immediately.
___

>[!Note]
>While the recipe suggests refrigerating overnight for best results, the author notes, "You can mix this up and bake immediately if you so desire."
