## Recipe
| Measurements      | Ingredients                   |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 8 to 10 slices    | [[White Bread]]                   |
| 3/4 pound         | [[Cheddar Cheese]], shredded or cubed |
| 6 large           | [[Eggs]]                        |
| 2 cups            | [[Milk]]                        |
| 3/4 teaspoon      | [[Salt]]                        |
| 1 dash            | [[Pepper]]                      |
| For the dish      | [[Butter]] or [[Shortening]]      |
___
## Instructions
1.  Preheat oven to 325°F.
2.  Grease an uncovered casserole dish.
3.  Remove the crusts from the bread slices and cut the bread into cubes.
4.  In a mixing bowl, beat the eggs well. Stir in the milk, salt, and pepper.
5.  Create layers in the prepared dish: start with a layer of bread cubes, followed by a layer of cheese, and then pour some of the egg mixture over it.
6.  Repeat the layers until all ingredients are used.
7.  Bake for 1 hour.
8.  Serve immediately.
___

>[!Note]
>For a richer flavor, you can assemble the casserole, cover it, and refrigerate for a few hours or overnight before baking. This allows the bread to fully absorb the egg mixture.
