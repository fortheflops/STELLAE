## Recipe
| Measurements      | Ingredients                   |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 (16-ounce) can  | [[Cream-Style Corn]]              |
| 1 (10-ounce) pkg  | [[Frozen Chopped Broccoli]], thawed |
| 1 large           | [[Egg]], beaten                   |
| 1/2 cup           | Crushed [[Ritz Crackers]] or [[Plain Bread Crumbs]] |
| 1 tablespoon      | [[Onion]], chopped                |
| 2 tablespoons     | [[Butter]], melted                |
| 1/2 teaspoon      | [[Salt]]                        |
| To Taste          | [[Black Pepper]]                  |
| **For Topping** |                                   |
| 1/2 cup           | Additional [[Ritz Crackers]] or [[Bread Crumbs]] |
| To Moisten        | [[Butter]], melted                |
___
## Instructions
1.  Preheat oven to 350°F.
2.  Grease a casserole dish.
3.  In a bowl, combine the cream-style corn, thawed broccoli, beaten egg, 1/2 cup of cracker crumbs, chopped onion, 2 tablespoons of melted butter, salt, and pepper.
4.  Pour the mixture into the prepared casserole dish.
5.  In a small bowl, combine the additional 1/2 cup of cracker crumbs with enough melted butter to moisten them.
6.  Sprinkle the buttered crumbs evenly over the top of the casserole.
7.  Bake for 35 to 40 minutes.
___

>[!Note]
>The recipe author notes that this "serves lots." You can use an equivalent amount of egg beaters instead of a fresh egg.
