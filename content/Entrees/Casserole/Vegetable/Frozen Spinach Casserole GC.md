## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 3 packages                 | [[Frozen Spinach]]                        |
| 1 small container          | [[Sour Cream]]                            |
| 1 package                  | Dry [[Onion Soup Mix]]                    |
| 1 small can                | [[French-Fried Onion Rings]]              |
___
## Instructions
1.  Cook the frozen spinach according to the package directions.
2.  Drain the spinach thoroughly.
3.  In a bowl, mix the drained spinach with the sour cream and the dry onion soup mix.
4.  Transfer the mixture to a baking pan.
5.  When ready to bake, heat the casserole in a 375°F oven until hot.
6.  Top with the small can of onion rings.
7.  Return to the oven and cook for another 10-20 minutes.
