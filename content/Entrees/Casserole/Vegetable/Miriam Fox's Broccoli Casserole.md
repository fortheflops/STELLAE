*A classic and comforting side dish, this broccoli casserole gets a light, soufflé-like texture from the beaten eggs and a rich, savory flavor from the homemade Cheez Whiz sauce.*
### Author
Miriam Fox
___
## Recipe
| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 2 Packages | Chopped [[Broccoli]], thawed and squeezed dry |
| 1/4 Cup | Chopped [[Onion]] |
| 6 Tablespoons | [[Butter]] (divided) |
| 2 Teaspoons | [[Flour]] |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Water]] |
| 1 (8 oz) Jar | [[Cheez Whiz]] |
| 3 | [[Eggs]], Well Beaten |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Cracker Crumbs]] |
___
## Instructions
1.  Preheat oven to 350°F. Grease a casserole dish.
2.  Thaw the **broccoli** and squeeze it in a towel to get it as dry as possible.
3.  **Make the Sauce:** In a saucepan, sauté the **chopped onion** in **four tablespoons of butter** until tender.
4.  Stir in the **flour**. Gradually add the **water**, stirring to create a smooth, thick sauce.
5.  Add the **Cheez Whiz** and stir over low heat until melted.
6.  **Assemble:** In a large bowl, combine the cheese sauce, the well-drained **broccoli**, and the **well-beaten eggs**.
7.  Pour the mixture into the greased casserole dish.
8.  **Make the Topping:** Melt the **remaining two tablespoons of butter** and toss with the **cracker crumbs**.
9.  Sprinkle the buttered crumbs over the top of the casserole.
10. Bake for 45 to 50 minutes at 350°F, or until set.
