## Recipe
| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 1 (8 ounce) can | [[Green Chiles]] |
| 1 pound | Grated [[Monterey Jack Cheese]] |
| 12 | [[Eggs]] |
| 1 (16 ounce) container| Dairy [[Sour Cream]] |
___
## Instructions
1.  Preheat the oven to 350°F. Grease a 13"x9"x2" baking dish.
2.  Scrape the seeds from the **green chiles**. Drain and pat them dry.
3.  Create alternating layers of the **green chiles** and the grated **Monterey Jack cheese** in the baking dish.
4.  In a separate bowl, combine the **eggs** and **sour cream**. Beat well until smooth.
5.  Pour the egg and sour cream mixture over the layered chiles and cheese.
6.  Bake for 45-50 minutes, or until the casserole puffs up and is set in the center.
