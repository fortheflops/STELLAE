## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
| 2 Tablespoon          | Flour                       |
| 2 Tablespoon          | Butter                       |
| 1 Cup                 | Milk                        |
| To Taste                     | Melted Grated Cheese     |
|                       | Canned Asparagus             |
| To Taste                      | Toasted Bread Crumbs     |
___
### Instructions
#### Sauce
1. Melt Butter
2. Add flour and mix
3. Add Milk, mix.
4. Cook until thickened
5. Melt Cheese in Sauce
#### Assembly
1. Pour thin white sauce with melted grated cheese over canned asparagus. Top with toasted breadcrumbs.
