*A classic, simple, and comforting broccoli casserole layered with cheese and crackers.*
### Author
Jeanie
___
## Recipe
| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 1 bunch | [[Broccoli]], cooked and drained |
| 1/2 to 1 cup | Shredded [[Cheddar Cheese]] |
| 6 to 8 | [[Crackers]], crushed |
| 1 can | [[Cream of Celery Soup]] |
| 1/2 can | [[Water]] (use soup can) |
___
## Instructions
1.  Preheat oven to 350°F. Grease a baking dish.
2.  Cook the **broccoli** until just barely tender. Drain well.
3.  In the baking dish, create alternating layers of the cooked **broccoli**, shredded **cheddar cheese**, and crushed **crackers**.
4.  In a separate bowl, mix the **cream of celery soup** with **1/2 can of water**.
5.  Pour the soup mixture evenly over the layered ingredients.
6.  Bake for 20 minutes, or until hot and bubbly.
