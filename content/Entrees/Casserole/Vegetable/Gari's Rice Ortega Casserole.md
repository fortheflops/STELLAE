*A wonderfully creamy and cheesy green chile rice casserole. This is a quick and easy side dish that's perfect for a potluck.*

Gari

## Recipe
| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 2/3 cup | [[Minute Rice]] |
| 1/2 cup | Chopped [[Onion]] |
| 1 tbsp | [[Butter]] (for sautéing) |
| 1 can | Chopped [[Green Chiles]] (4 oz) |
| 1 3/4 cups | [[Sour Cream]] |
| 1/2 pound | [[Cottage Cheese]] (8 oz) |
| 1/2 pound | Sharp [[Cheddar Cheese]], shredded (2 cups) |
| To Taste | [[Garlic]], minced |
| To Taste | [[Salt]] and [[Pepper]] |
| 1 | [[Bay Leaf]] |
| As Needed | Grated [[Cheese]] (for topping) |
___
## Instructions
1.  Preheat the oven to 375°F. Grease a casserole dish.
2.  Cook the **Minute Rice** according to package directions.
3.  In a small skillet, sauté the **chopped onion** in **butter** until soft.
4.  In a large bowl, combine the cooked rice, sautéed onion, **green chiles**, **sour cream**, **cottage cheese**, **1/2 lb. of cheddar cheese**, **garlic**, **salt**, **pepper**, and the **bay leaf**. Mix well.
5.  Pour the mixture into the greased dish.
6.  Top with additional grated **cheese**.
7.  Bake for 25 minutes.
___

>[!Note]
>Remember to remove the bay leaf before serving. The recipe card also mentions "(17 oz pk)" next to the Minute Rice, which is likely an error as 2/3 cup of instant rice is much less than 17 ounces. Following the 2/3 cup measurement is the correct interpretation.
