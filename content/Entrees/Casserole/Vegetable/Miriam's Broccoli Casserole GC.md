## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| $\frac{1}{4}$ Cup                | Chopped [[Onion]]                         |
| Six Tablespoons                  | [[Butter]] (divided)                      |
| Two Teaspoons                    | [[Flour]]                                 |
| $\frac{1}{2}$ Cup                | [[Water]]                                 |
| One (8 oz) Jar                   | [[Cheez Whiz]]                            |
| Two Packages                     | Chopped [[Broccoli]], thawed and squeezed very dry |
| Three                            | [[Eggs]], Well Beaten                     |
| As Needed                        | [[Cracker Crumbs]]                        |
___
## Instructions
1.  Preheat oven to 350°F. Grease a casserole dish.
2.  In a saucepan, fry the $\frac{1}{4}$ cup of chopped onion in four tablespoons of butter until tender.
3.  Stir in two teaspoons of flour. Gradually add $\frac{1}{2}$ cup of water, stirring to create a smooth sauce.
4.  When the sauce is thick, add the 8 oz jar of Cheez Whiz and stir until melted and smooth.
5.  In a large bowl, combine the cheese sauce with the two packages of thawed and well-squeezed chopped broccoli.
6.  Stir in the three well-beaten eggs.
7.  Pour the mixture into the greased casserole dish.
8.  Melt the remaining two tablespoons of butter and toss with cracker crumbs.
9.  Top the casserole with the buttered crumbs.
10. Bake for 45 to 50 minutes at 350°F.
