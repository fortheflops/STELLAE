## Recipe
| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 1 medium head | [[Cauliflower]], broken into florets |
| 2 tablespoons | [[Butter]] or [[Margarine]] (oleo) |
| 1/2 cup | Chopped [[Green Pepper]] |
| 1/2 cup | Sliced [[Mushrooms]] |
| 2 tablespoons | [[All-Purpose Flour]] |
| 1 1/2 cups | [[Milk]] |
| 1 cup | Shredded [[Cheese]] (e.g., Cheddar) |
| To Taste | [[Salt]] |
| For Garnish | [[Paprika]] |
___
## Instructions
1.  Preheat oven to 400°F. Grease a 1 1/2-quart casserole dish.
2.  In a pot, cook the **cauliflower** florets in boiling salted water until tender-crisp. Drain well.
3.  While the cauliflower is cooking, melt the **butter** in a saucepan over medium heat. Sauté the **green pepper** and **mushrooms** for about 5 minutes.
4.  Stir the **flour** into the vegetable mixture.
5.  Gradually whisk in the **milk**. Continue cooking and stirring constantly until the sauce thickens and is smooth.
6.  Arrange half of the cooked cauliflower in the prepared casserole dish. Sprinkle with half of the **cheese**, then pour half of the sauce over the top.
7.  Repeat with a second layer of the remaining cauliflower, cheese, and sauce.
8.  Sprinkle **paprika** over the top.
9.  Bake for 15 minutes, or until the casserole is hot and bubbling.
