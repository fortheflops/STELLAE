## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
| 2 Cans                | Yellow Cream Corn OR                             |
| 1 Can             | Whole Yellow Corn                                |
| 1 4 Ounce Can              | Green Chilies (My family likes 8oz)                     |
| 1/4 Cup               | Chopped Onion                                   |
| 1/2 Cup               | Yellow Corn Meal                                |
| 2                     | Beaten Eggs                                      |
| 1/2 Teaspoon          | Garlic Powder                                    |
| 2 Cups                 | (Monterey Jack 1c & 1c Sharp Cheese), Shredded             |
| 1/2 Cup               | Salad Oil                 |
| 1 Tablespoon            | Sugar                                    |
| 2 Teaspoon                 | Vinegar                        |
| 2 Cups                 | Evaporated Milk                           |
___
### Instructions
1. (Spray container with Pam before adding ingredients.)
2. Mix together all ingredients except cheese.
3. Pour 1/2 of ingredients mixture into casserole.
4. Spread 1/2 of cheese over this layer.
5. Pour other 1/2 of ingredients.
6. Add other 1/2 of cheese over top.
7. Bake at 350 degrees Farenheit for about 45 minutes.
