## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 package                  | [[Spinach]] (likely frozen, chopped)      |
| 2                          | [[Eggs]]                                  |
| Some                       | [[Onion]], chopped                        |
| 1 cup                      | [[Milk]]                                  |
| 1 cup                      | Sharp [[Cheese]], shredded                |
| 1 cup                      | Cooked [[Rice]]                           |
| To Taste                   | [[Seasoning]] (e.g., salt and pepper)     |
___
## Instructions
1.  Preheat the oven to 350°F. Grease a casserole dish.
2.  Cook the package of spinach according to the directions, then drain it very well.
3.  In a large bowl, combine the drained spinach, eggs, chopped onion, milk, sharp cheese, and cooked rice.
4.  Season the mixture to your taste.
5.  Pour the mixture into the greased casserole dish.
6.  Bake for approximately 30 minutes, or until the casserole is set.
