| Measurements | Ingredients |

| 1/4 cup | [[Onion]], chopped |
| 1 cup | [[Milk]] |

| 1 cup | Cooked [[Rice]] |
| To Taste | [[Seasoning]] (Salt and Pepper) |
___
## Instructions
1.  Preheat the oven to 350°F. Grease a casserole dish.
2.  Cook the **spinach** according to package directions. Drain the spinach very well, squeezing out excess water.
3.  In a large bowl, combine the drained spinach, beaten **eggs**, chopped **onion**, **milk**, shredded **cheese**, and cooked **rice**.
4.  Season the mixture to taste with **salt and pepper**.
5.  Pour the mixture into the greased casserole dish.
6.  Bake for 30 minutes, or until the casserole is set.
