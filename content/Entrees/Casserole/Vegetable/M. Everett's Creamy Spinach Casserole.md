## Recipe
| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 3 packages | [[Frozen Spinach]] |
| 1 small container| [[Sour Cream]] (8 oz) |
| 1 package | Dry [[Onion Soup Mix]] |
| 1 small can | [[French-Fried Onion Rings]] |
___
## Instructions
1.  Preheat oven to 375°F. Grease a casserole dish.
2.  Cook the **frozen spinach** according to package directions.
3.  Drain the spinach **thoroughly**, squeezing out all excess moisture.
4.  In a bowl, mix the drained spinach with the **sour cream** and the dry **onion soup mix**.
5.  Transfer the mixture to the prepared baking dish.
6.  Bake for 20-25 minutes, or until hot.
7.  Top with the **French-fried onion rings**.
8.  Return to the oven and bake for another 10-15 minutes, until the onions are golden.
