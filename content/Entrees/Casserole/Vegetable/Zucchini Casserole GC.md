## Recipe
| Measurements              | Ingredients                               |
| :------------------------ | :---------------------------------------- |
| 1 1/2 Pounds                   | [[Zucchini]], cut in 1/4 inch rounds                |
| 1 Pound                        | [[Ground Beef]]                                     |
| 1 Medium                         | [[Onion]], chopped                                  |
| 1 Cup                          | [[Instant Rice]]                                    |
| 1 Teaspoon                       | [[Seasoned Salt]] or [[Garlic Salt]]                |
| 1 Teaspoon                       | [[Oregano]]                                         |
| 2 Cups (1 Pint)                | Small Curd [[Cottage Cheese]] (optional)            |
| 1 Cup (10 1/2 ounces)          | [[Cream of Mushroom Soup]]                          |
| 1 Cup                          | Grated Sharp [[Cheddar Cheese]]                     |
___
## Instructions
1. Cook zucchini in boiling, salted water until barely tender. Drain well.
2. Sauté beef with onion until meat is lightly browned.
3. Add rice and seasonings (seasoned or garlic salt, oregano).
4. Place half of the sliced zucchini in bottom of 2 1/2 quart shallow casserole.
5. Cover with beef mixture and spoon over the cottage cheese.
6. Add remaining zucchini.
7. Then spread soup all over.
8. Sprinkle with grated cheese.
9. Bake uncovered at 350° for 35-40 minutes or until bubbly hot.
10. Makes 8 servings.
