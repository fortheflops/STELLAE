___
## Instructions
1.  Preheat the oven to 375°F. Grease a casserole dish.
2.  Cook 2/3 cup of Minute Rice according to package directions.
3.  Sauté the 1/2 cup of chopped onion in butter.
4.  In a large bowl, combine the cooked rice, sautéed onion, chopped green chiles, sour cream, cottage cheese, 1/2 lb. of sharp cheddar, garlic, salt, pepper, and the bay leaf. Mix well.
5.  Pour the mixture into the greased dish.
6.  Top with additional grated cheese.
7.  Bake for 25 minutes.
___

>[!Note]
>Remember to remove the bay leaf before serving. The recipe card also mentions "(17 oz pk)" next to the Minute Rice, which is likely an error as 2/3 cup of instant rice is much less than 17 ounces. Following the 2/3 cup measurement is the correct interpretation.
