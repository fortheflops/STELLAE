*A classic, creamy, and cheesy broccoli casserole that's a perfect side dish for a potluck or holiday meal.*
### Author
Lois Ross
___
## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 1 bunch | Fresh [[Broccoli]], Cut Up Fine & Steamed |
| 1 can | Sliced [[Water Chestnuts]], Drained |
| ½ Medium | [[Onion]], Chopped |
| 1 can | [[Cream of Mushroom Soup]] |
| ¾ cup | [[Mayonnaise]] |
| 2 | [[Eggs]], Beaten |
| 1 cup | Grated [[Cheddar Cheese]] |
| As needed | [[Bread Crumbs]] (Optional) |
___
### Instructions
1.  Preheat oven to 375°F. Grease a baking dish.
2.  In a large bowl, mix together the steamed **broccoli**, drained **water chestnuts**, **chopped onion**, **cream of mushroom soup**, **mayonnaise**, beaten **eggs**, and grated **cheddar cheese**.
3.  Pour the mixture into the greased baking dish.
4.  Sprinkle **bread crumbs** on top, if desired.
5.  Bake for 30-45 minutes, or until hot and set.
