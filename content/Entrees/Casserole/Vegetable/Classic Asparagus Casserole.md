## Recipe
| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 2 cans | [[Canned Asparagus]], drained |
| 2 Tablespoon | [[Butter]] |
| 2 Tablespoon | [[Flour]] |
| 1 Cup | [[Milk]] |
| 1 cup (or to taste) | Grated [[Cheese]] |
| To Taste | Toasted [[Bread Crumbs]] |
___
## Instructions
### Sauce
1.  In a saucepan, melt the **butter** over medium heat.
2.  Whisk in the **flour** and cook for 1 minute.
3.  Gradually whisk in the **milk**. Cook, stirring constantly, until the sauce thickens and bubbles.
4.  Remove from heat and stir in the grated **cheese** until melted.

### Assembly
1.  Arrange the drained **canned asparagus** in a serving dish.
2.  Pour the hot cheese sauce over the asparagus.
3.  Top with toasted **bread crumbs** and serve immediately.
