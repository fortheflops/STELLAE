___
## Recipe
| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 1/3 cup | [[Butter]], melted |
| 8 ounces | [[Cream Cheese]], softened |
| 1 (12 ounce) can | [[Shoe Peg Corn]], drained |
| 1 (4 ounce) can | [[Green Chiles]], diced |
___
## Instructions
1.  Preheat the oven to 350°F. Grease a small casserole dish.
2.  In a mixing bowl, combine the melted **butter** and the softened **cream cheese**. Mix until smooth.
3.  Stir in the drained **shoe peg corn** and the diced **green chiles** until everything is well combined.
4.  Transfer the mixture to the prepared baking dish.
5.  Bake for 20 minutes, or until heated through and bubbly.
