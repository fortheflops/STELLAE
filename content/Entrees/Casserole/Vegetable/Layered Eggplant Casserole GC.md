## Instructions
1.  In a baking dish, create layers with the first three ingredients: spaghetti sauce, sliced eggplant, and mozzarella cheese.
2.  Sprinkle the top layer with Parmesan cheese.
3.  Bake in a preheated oven at 350°F.
