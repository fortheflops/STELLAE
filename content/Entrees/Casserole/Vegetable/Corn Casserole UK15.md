## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 2 (16 ounce) cans          | [[Cream Corn]]                            |
| 2 cups                     | Shredded [[Cheddar Cheese]]               |
| 1 tiny can                 | Chopped [[Green Chiles]]                  |
| 1/2 cup                    | Finely chopped [[Onion]]                  |
| 1 cup                      | [[Milk]]                                  |
| 2 large                    | [[Eggs]], lightly beaten                  |
| 1 cup                      | Yellow [[Corn Meal]]                      |
| 1/2 teaspoon               | [[Garlic Salt]]                           |
| 1/2 teaspoon               | Baking [[Soda]]                           |
___
## Instructions
1.  Preheat the oven to 350°F. Spray a 11x7x1.5 inch baking dish with non-stick spray (like Pam).
2.  In a large bowl, combine the first 6 ingredients (cream corn, cheddar cheese, green chiles, onion, milk, and beaten eggs).
3.  In a separate bowl, combine the corn meal and the remaining ingredients (garlic salt and baking soda).
4.  Stir the dry corn meal mixture into the wet corn mixture.
5.  Pour the entire batter into the prepared baking dish.
6.  Bake for 50 minutes, or until a knife inserted in the center comes out clean.
