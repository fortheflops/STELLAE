## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 1/3 cup                    | [[Butter]], melted                        |
| 8 ounces                   | [[Cream Cheese]], softened                |
| 1 (12 ounce) can           | [[Shoe Peg Corn]], drained                |
| 1 (4 ounce) can            | [[Green Chiles]], diced                   |
___
## Instructions
1.  Preheat the oven to 350°F.
2.  In a mixing bowl, combine the melted butter and the softened cream cheese.
3.  Stir in the drained shoe peg corn.
4.  Add the diced green chiles and mix everything together until well combined.
5.  Transfer the mixture to a baking dish.
6.  Bake for 20 minutes, or until heated through and bubbly.
