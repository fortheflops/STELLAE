## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 bunch                    | [[Broccoli]]                              |
| 1/2 to 1 cup               | Shredded [[Cheddar Cheese]]               |
| 6 to 8                     | [[Crackers]], crushed                     |
| 1 can                      | [[Cream of Celery Soup]]                  |
| 1/2 can                    | [[Water]]                                 |
___
## Instructions
1.  Cook the bunch of broccoli until just barely tender (you should be able to pierce the stems with a fork). Drain well.
2.  In a greased baking dish, create alternating layers of the cooked broccoli, shredded cheddar cheese, and crushed crackers.
3.  In a separate bowl, mix the can of cream of celery soup with 1/2 can of water to create the cream sauce.
4.  Pour the cream sauce evenly over the layered ingredients in the baking dish.
5.  Bake in a preheated oven at 350°F for 20 minutes.
