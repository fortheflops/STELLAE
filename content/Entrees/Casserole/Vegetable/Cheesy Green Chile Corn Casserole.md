## Instructions
1.  Preheat the oven to 350°F. Spray an 11x7x1.5 inch baking dish with non-stick spray.
2.  In a large bowl, combine the **cream corn**, **cheddar cheese**, **green chiles**, **onion**, **milk**, and beaten **eggs**.
3.  In a separate small bowl, combine the **corn meal**, **garlic salt**, and baking **soda**.
4.  Stir the dry corn meal mixture into the wet corn mixture.
5.  Pour the batter into the prepared baking dish.
6.  Bake for 50 minutes, or until a knife inserted in the center comes out clean.
