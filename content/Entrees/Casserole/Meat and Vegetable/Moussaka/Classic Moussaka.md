## Instructions
1.  **Prep Eggplant:** Peel **eggplants**, slice into $\frac{1}{2}$-inch rounds. Sprinkle with **salt** and let rest in a colander for 30 minutes. Pat dry.
2.  **Make Meat Sauce:** Brown the **ground meat** and **onion**. Drain fat. Stir in **red wine**, **water**, **parsley**, **tomato paste**, **1 tsp salt**, and **pepper**. Simmer ~4 minutes until liquid is nearly gone. Cool slightly.
3.  Stir in **half** the **bread crumbs** ($\frac{3}{8}$ Cup), **half** the **cheese** ($\frac{1}{4}$ Cup), the **2 beaten eggs**, and **cinnamon**. Set aside.
4.  **Make Béchamel:** In a saucepan, melt **margarine**, stir in **flour**, **$\frac{1}{2}$ tsp salt**, **nutmeg**, and **pepper**. Cook for 1 minute. Add **milk** all at once, whisking constantly until thick and bubbly.
5.  Temper the **1 beaten egg** by slowly whisking in some of the hot béchamel. Return the egg mixture to the saucepan and cook for 2 more minutes, stirring. Do not boil. Remove from heat.
6.  **Cook Eggplant:** In a large skillet, heat **oil**. Brown the eggplant slices in batches on both sides until tender. Drain on paper towels.
7.  **Assemble Moussaka:** Preheat oven to 350°F. Sprinkle the **remaining bread crumbs** in the bottom of a 2-qt baking dish.
8.  Layer **half** of the browned eggplant slices.
9.  Spread all the meat sauce over the eggplant.
10. Layer the **remaining** eggplant slices on top.
11. Pour the **Béchamel sauce** evenly over the top.
12. Sprinkle with the **remaining cheese**.
13. **Bake:** Bake **uncovered** at 350°F for 45 minutes, or until golden brown and bubbly. Let stand 10-15 minutes before serving.
