### Instructions
1.  Preheat oven to 300°F.
2.  Lightly grease pan with oil.
3.  Line bottom of pan with raw rice.
4.  Dip salted chicken in Italian dressing & place over rice.
5.  Mix soups (Onion Soup Mix + Cream of Celery Soup) & 2 cans of water.
6.  Pour over chicken.
7.  Sprinkle with Accent.
8.  Cover dish.
9.  Bake 2 hrs, at 300°.
