## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 2 tablespoons              | [[Butter]]                                |
| 1                          | [[Onion]], chopped                        |
| 1 can                      | [[Cream of Chicken Soup]]                 |
| 1 can                      | [[Cream of Mushroom Soup]]                |
| 2                          | [[Chicken Bouillon Cubes]]                |
| 1 cup                      | Hot [[Water]]                             |
| 1 can (2-3 pounds)         | Boned [[Chicken]]                         |
| 1 can                      | Chopped [[Green Chile]]                   |
| As Needed                  | [[Corn Tortillas]]                        |
| 1 pound                    | Grated [[Cheese]]                         |
___
## Instructions
1.  Preheat the oven to 350°F.
2.  In a skillet, brown the chopped onion in 2 tablespoons of butter.
3.  In a large bowl, mix together the sautéed onion, cream of chicken soup, cream of mushroom soup, chicken bouillon cubes dissolved in hot water, boned chicken, and chopped green chile.
4.  In a 10x13 inch pan, create layers starting with tortillas, then the chicken mixture, then grated cheese.
5.  Repeat the layers once more ("Layer twice").
6.  Bake for at least 35 minutes.
