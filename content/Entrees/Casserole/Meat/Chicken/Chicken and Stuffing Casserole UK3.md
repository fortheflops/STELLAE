## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
| 1 large| Chicken Fryer |
|1 large|[[Chicken (Cooked and Boned)]]|
|2 cans|[[Cream of Chicken Soup]]|
|6 Tablespoons|[[Broth (for diluting soup)]]|
|4 cups|[[Pepperidge Farm Herb Seasoned Stuffing]]|
|2 1/4 cups|[[Broth (for stuffing)]]|
|1 stick|[[Butter (Melted)]]|
|1 cup|[[Broth (for pouring over mixture)]]|
___
### Instructions
1.  Spread cooked and boned chicken (or turkey) in an oblong pan.
2.  Spread the soup, diluted with 6 tablespoons of broth, over the meat.
3.  Mix the stuffing, 2 1/4 cups of broth, and melted butter.
4.  Spread over the soup.
5.  Pour 1 cup of broth over the mixture in the pan.
6.  Cover the pan with foil and bake at 350 degrees for 45 minutes.
7.  Uncover.
8.  Bake another 15 minutes.
