*A comforting and easy recipe from Mrs. Mulligan. Cooked chicken and cheddar cheese are rolled up in crescent dough and baked in a simple, creamy chicken soup sauce.*

Mrs. Mulligan

## Recipe
| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 1 | [[Chicken]], cooked and deboned |
| As needed | Grated [[Cheddar Cheese]] |
| 1 can | [[Cream of Chicken Soup]] |
| 1 cup | [[Milk]] |
| 1 can | [[Crescent Rolls]] |
___
### Instructions
1.  Boil a chicken - debone. Cut meat into pieces.
2.  Preheat oven to 350°F.
3.  In a large shallow pan, unroll crescent rolls.
4.  Fill each triangle with grated cheese & some chicken pieces.
5.  Roll each one up and place in the pan.
6.  While you are doing this - Heat 1 cup of milk & 1 can of cream of chicken soup. Add any extra cheese or chicken you have left.
7.  Pour this sauce over the filled crescent rolls.
8.  Bake at 350°F for 40-45 minutes - until brown.
