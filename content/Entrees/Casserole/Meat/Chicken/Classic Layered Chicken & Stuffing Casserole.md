___
### Instructions
1.  Preheat oven to 350°F.
2.  Spread cooked and boned chicken (or turkey) in an oblong pan.
3.  Spread the soup, diluted with 6 tablespoons of broth, over the meat.
4.  Mix the stuffing, 2 1/4 cups of broth, and melted butter.
5.  Spread this mixture over the soup.
6.  Pour 1 cup of broth over the mixture in the pan.
7.  Cover the pan with foil and bake at 350°F for 45 minutes.
8.  Uncover.
9.  Bake another 15 minutes.
