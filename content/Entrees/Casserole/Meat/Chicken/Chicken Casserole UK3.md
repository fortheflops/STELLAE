## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
| 8             | [[Chicken Breasts]], boned              |
| 1 Cup             | [[Raw Rice]]         |
| 1 Tablespoon                   | [[Butter]], melted          |
| 1 Can (10 3/4 oz.)                   | [[Cream of Mushroom Soup]] |
| 1 Can (10 3/4 oz.)        | [[Cream of Celery Soup]]          |
| 1 Can (10 3/4 oz.)      |[[Cream of Chicken Soup]]|
| 1/2 Cup | [[Sherry]] (substitute [[Vermouth]])|
||[[Grated Cheddar Cheese]]|
||[[Sliced Almonds]]|
___
### Instructions
1. Bone Chicken breasts. Set aside.
2. Melt butter, put rice in casserole dish and stir in butter.
3. Pour 1/2 can of each of the 3 soups over rice.
4. Place Chicken over top of rice & soup.
