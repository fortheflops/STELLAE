## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| As Needed                  | Cooked, [[Shredded Chicken]]              |
| 12                         | [[Corn Tortillas]]                        |
| 1 can                      | [[Cream of Mushroom Soup]]                |
| 1 can                      | [[Cream of Chicken Soup]]                 |
| 1 can                      | [[Green Chile Salsa]]                     |
| 1 (8 ounce) container      | [[Sour Cream]]                            |
| Optional                   | [[Onions]], chopped                       |
___
## Instructions
1. In a large bowl, mix together the cream of mushroom soup, cream of chicken soup, green chile salsa, and sour cream. If using, stir in the chopped onions.
2. In a greased crockpot or baking dish, begin layering the ingredients. Start with a layer of tortillas, followed by a layer of shredded chicken, and then a layer of the soup mixture.
3. Repeat the layers until you run out of ingredients, ending with a layer of the soup mixture on top.
4. Cook using one of the two methods below:
    * **Crockpot:** Cook on High for 4 hours or on Low for 8 hours.
    * **Oven:** Bake in a preheated oven at 375°F for 1 hour.
