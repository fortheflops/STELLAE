### Instructions
1.  In a large pot or Dutch oven, combine the cut-up chicken, cream of mushroom soup, cheddar cheese soup, and small onions.
2.  Cook for about 1 hour, or until the chicken is tender.
3.  Serve over rice.
___

>[!Note]
>The recipe card suggests using Oregon Brand Rice and notes that you can "cut recipe in half for small group." It also suggests serving the meal with a vegetable, salad, bread, and dessert.
