## Instructions
1.  Preheat oven to 375°F.
2.  Cook the mixed vegetables and drain.
3.  In a casserole dish, create layers using the tuna (or chicken), cooked mixed vegetables, chopped green onions, and Chinese noodles.
4.  In a separate bowl, prepare the sauce by mixing together the cream of celery soup, milk, soy sauce, garlic salt, marjoram, and pepper.
5.  Just before baking, pour the sauce mixture evenly over the layers in the casserole dish.
6.  Top the casserole with the chopped boiled eggs.
7.  Bake at 375°F for 30 minutes.
