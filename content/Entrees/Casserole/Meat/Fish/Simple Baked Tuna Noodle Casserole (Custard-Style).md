## Instructions
1.  Preheat the oven to 350°F.
2.  Grease a casserole dish with butter.
3.  In a bowl, whisk together the milk and eggs.
4.  In the greased casserole dish, combine the cooked noodles and tuna.
5.  Pour the milk and egg mixture over the noodles and tuna and mix to combine.
6.  Top the casserole with crushed potato chips.
7.  Bake for 30 minutes.
