## Instructions
### Instructions
#### Prepare the Tomato Sauce
1.  Place the stewing beef in a large pot and brown it, stirring often.
2.  Pour in the tomato puree, tomato paste, and tomato sauce.
3.  Add seasonings like basil and parsley as desired.
4.  Cook slowly, stirring occasionally, for about 2.5 to 3 hours, until the meat is tender. (The author notes you can remove garlic cloves, if used).
#### Prepare the Casserole Components
1.  **Meatballs:** The day before assembling, combine the ground round, bread crumbs, egg, garlic, basil, and salt. Mix until you are able to roll the mixture properly. Form into small meatballs, fry until browned, then drain and store.
2.  **Eggs:** Hard-boil 4-5 eggs, let them cool, and slice them.
3.  **Rice:** Boil the rice to your desired softness, then drain.

#### Assemble and Bake the Casserole
1.  Preheat the oven to 350°F.
2.  In a saucepan, mix the cooked rice with just enough of the prepared tomato sauce to color it.
3.  Spread about 5 tablespoons of the tomato sauce on the bottom of a baking dish.
4.  Begin layering: a layer of the sauce-colored rice, a layer of Swiss cheese, a layer of sliced hard-boiled eggs, and a layer of meatballs.
5.  Continue alternating the layers of rice, cheese, eggs, meatballs, and sauce until the dish is almost full.
6.  Cover the top lightly with sauce.
7.  Bake at 350°F until the cheese is melted and the casserole is heated through.
