## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
|1 medium size|[[Onion (Chopped)]]|
|1 lb.|[[Ground Meat]]|
|1 can|[[Mushroom Soup]]|
|1/2 pkg.|[[Spaghetti]]|
|2 cups|[[Grated Cheese]]|
| |[[Salt (to taste)]]|
| |[[Pepper (to taste)]]|
| |[[Garlic Salt (to taste)]]|
___
### Instructions
1.  Brown chopped onion and ground meat.
2.  Cook spaghetti.
3.  Add mushroom soup to it.
4.  Add meat mixture.
5.  Spread a layer of meat mixture then cheese alternately with cheese on top.
6.  Bake in a 350-degree oven for 20 minutes.
