## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
|1 can|[[Cream of Mushroom Soup]]|
|1 can|[[Cream of Chicken Soup]]|
|1 can|[[Taco Sauce (Small)]]|
|1 can|[[Chopped Green Chilies (Small)]]|
|1/2 cup|[[Milk]]|
|1 1/2 lbs.|[[Ground Beef]]|
|1|[[Medium Onion (Chopped)]]|
|1/2 lb.|[[Grated Cheddar Cheese (or more)]]|
|1 dozen|[[Soft Corn Tortillas (Torn in 1/4's)]]|
___
### Instructions
1.  Mix cream of mushroom soup, cream of chicken soup, taco sauce (small), chopped green chilies (small), and milk in a saucepan and warm.
2.  Brown 1 1/2 lbs. ground beef and 1 medium chopped onion.
3.  In a greased 11x13 baking dish, layer:
	* Tortillas
	* Sauce
	* Meat
	* Sauce
4.  Will form 2 layers (end with cheese).
5.  Bake at 325 degrees for 1 hour or until hot.
