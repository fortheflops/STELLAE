## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 1/2 pounds               | [[Ground Beef]]                           |
| 1 (15 ounce) can           | [[Tomato Sauce]]                          |
| 6 ounces                   | [[Noodles]]                               |
| 1 medium                   | [[Onion]], diced                          |
| 1 medium                   | [[Bell Pepper]], diced                    |
| 1 (16 ounce) can           | Small [[Peas]], drained                   |
| 1 (11 ounce) can           | [[Corn Niblets]], drained                 |
| 2 to 4 tablespoons         | [[Chili Powder]]                          |
| To Taste                   | [[Salt]] and [[Pepper]]                   |
| As Needed                  | Grated [[Cheddar Cheese]]                 |
___
## Instructions
1.  Preheat the oven to 350°F.
2.  Cook the noodles according to package directions and drain.
3.  In a large skillet, fry the ground beef until browned. Drain off any excess grease.
4.  In a large bowl, combine the cooked ground beef, cooked noodles, and all other ingredients except for the cheddar cheese.
5.  Pour the mixture into a baking dish.
6.  Cover the top with plenty of grated cheddar cheese.
7.  Bake for 25 minutes.
