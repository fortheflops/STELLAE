## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
| 8 Ounces             | [[Uncooked Noodles]]              |
| 1 Pound             | [[Ground Beef]]         |
| 1 Tablespoon                   | [[Fat]]          |
| 2 Cans (8 Ounces each) | [[Tomato Sauce]]          |
| 1/2 Pound      |[[Cottage Cheese]]|
| 1 Package (8 Ounces) | [[Cream Cheese]]|
| 1/4 Cup | [[Commercial Sour Cream]]|
| 1/3 Cup | [[Minced Onion]]|
| 1 Tablespoon | [[Minced Green Pepper]]|
| 2 Tablespoons | [[Melted Butter]]|
___
### Instructions
1.  Cook & drain noodles according to package directions.
2.  Brown ground beef in fat; drain excess fat. Stir in tomato sauce.
3.  Combine cottage cheese, cream cheese, sour cream, minced onion, and minced green pepper. Mix well.
4.  In a casserole dish, layer the ingredients (typically noodles, meat sauce, cheese mixture - repeat if desired, or just one layer of each).
5.  Pour melted butter over the top.
6.  Refrigerate (optional, if making ahead) then bake at 350° for 45-50 minutes, or until heated through and bubbly.

___

>[!Note]
>Serves 8
