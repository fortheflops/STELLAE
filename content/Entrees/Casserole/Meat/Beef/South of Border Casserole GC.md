## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 1/2 pounds               | [[Ground Beef]]                           |
| 1                          | [[Onion]], chopped                        |
| 1 large can                | [[Whole Kernel Corn]]                     |
| 1 large can                | [[Tomato Juice]]                          |
| 1 dozen                    | [[Corn Tortillas]]                        |
| 2 heaping tablespoons      | [[Chili Powder]]                          |
| To Taste                   | [[Salt]]                                  |
| To Taste                   | [[Garlic Salt]]                           |
| As Needed                  | [[Cheddar Cheese]], shredded              |
___
## Instructions
1.  In a large skillet, brown the ground beef and onion.
2.  Stir in the can of corn and the tomato juice.
3.  Season the mixture with salt, garlic salt, and the chili powder.
4.  In a casserole dish, create alternating layers of the meat mixture and the corn tortillas.
5.  Sprinkle the top with shredded cheddar cheese.
___

>[!Note]
>The recipe does not specify a baking time or temperature. A common instruction for a similar casserole would be to bake at 350°F (175°C) for 20-30 minutes, or until heated through and the cheese is bubbly
