### Instructions
1.  Preheat oven to 325°F.
2.  Brown meat in a skillet. Add garlic, salt, pepper, & 1 tsp. sugar to taste.
3.  Add tomato sauce & let simmer about 20 minutes.
4.  While sauce simmers, cook noodles according to package directions. Drain.
5.  In a separate bowl, mix the sour cream, softened cream cheese, and chopped spring onion tops.
6.  Layer in a baking dish: noodles, then the sour cream sauce, then the meat sauce.
7.  Sprinkle with grated cheese.
8.  Bake for 20 minutes at 325°F.
