## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |

| 1 Teaspoon                   | [[Salt]]          |

| 1                   | [[Chopped Onion]]          |

| 1 Can               | [[Tomato Sauce]]          |

___
### Instructions
1. Brown ground meat in a skillet.
2. Add chili powder, salt, black pepper, and chopped onion.
3. Cook meat mixture in skillet 30-45 minutes.
4. Heat salad oil in another pan. Dip tortillas in hot oil briefly (about a minute, or until softened).
5. Fill softened tortillas with the meat mixture and roll them up.
6. Place rolled tortillas seam-side down in a baking dish.
7. Heat 1 can tomato sauce to boiling. Add 1 can water and mix.
8. Pour the tomato sauce mixture over the tortillas in the baking dish.
9. Top with grated cheese.
10. Bake at 375° for 30 minutes.
