*A classic "hot dish" casserole that brings all the flavors of pizza to a hearty noodle bake.*
### Author
Dorthy
___
## Recipe
| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 3 cups | Uncooked [[Noodles]] |
| 1 pound | [[Hamburger]] |
| 1 small | [[Onion]], diced |
| 1 stick | [[Pepperoni]], sliced |
| 1 (10.5 oz) can | [[Pizza Sauce]] |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Oregano]] |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Garlic Salt]] |
| 1/2 cup | [[Milk]] |
| 1-2 cups | [[Mozzarella Cheese]], shredded |
___
## Instructions
1.  Preheat oven to 375°F. Grease a casserole dish.
2.  Cook the **noodles** according to package directions. Drain.
3.  In a skillet, brown the **hamburger** with the diced **onion**. Drain off excess grease.
4.  In a large bowl, combine the cooked noodles, the browned hamburger mixture, sliced **pepperoni**, **pizza sauce**, **oregano**, **garlic salt**, **milk**, and a generous amount of **mozzarella cheese**.
5.  Mix all ingredients together thoroughly and pour into the casserole dish.
6.  Top with extra cheese if desired.
7.  Bake for 45 minutes.
___

>[!Note]
>The recipe card suggests using either a 10.5 oz can of pizza sauce or a 15.5 oz jar of Ragu Spaghetti Sauce with mushrooms for variation.
