## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients |

| 12 | [[Tortillas]] |
| 1 Can | [[Tomato Sauce]] (approx. 15 oz) |

___
### Instructions
1.  Preheat oven to 375°F. Grease a baking dish.
2.  In a skillet, brown the **ground meat**. Add the **chili powder**, **salt**, **black pepper**, and **chopped onion**. Cook until the meat is done and the onion is soft. Drain excess fat.
3.  Heat **salad oil** in another pan. Briefly dip the **tortillas** in the hot oil, one by one, to soften them (about 30-60 seconds). Drain on paper towels.
4.  Fill each softened tortilla with the meat mixture and roll it up.
5.  Place the rolled tortillas seam-side down in the baking dish.
6.  In a small saucepan, heat the **tomato sauce** to boiling. Add 1 can of **water** and mix.
7.  Pour the tomato sauce mixture over the rolled tortillas.
8.  Top with **grated cheese**.
9.  Bake for 30 minutes.
