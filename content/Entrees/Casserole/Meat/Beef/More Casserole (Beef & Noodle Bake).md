## Recipe
| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 1 1/2 pounds | [[Ground Beef]] |
| 6 ounces | [[Noodles]] (uncooked) |
| 1 medium | [[Onion]], diced |
| 1 medium | [[Bell Pepper]], diced |
| 1 (15 ounce) can | [[Tomato Sauce]] |
| 1 (16 ounce) can | Small [[Peas]], drained |
| 1 (11 ounce) can | [[Corn Niblets]], drained |
| 2 to 4 tablespoons | [[Chili Powder]] |
| To Taste | [[Salt]] and [[Pepper]] |
| As Needed | Grated [[Cheddar Cheese]] |
___
## Instructions
1.  Preheat the oven to 350°F. Grease a large baking dish.
2.  Cook the **noodles** according to package directions. Drain.
3.  In a large skillet, fry the **ground beef**, diced **onion**, and diced **bell pepper** until the meat is browned. Drain off any excess grease.
4.  In a large bowl, combine the cooked meat mixture, cooked noodles, **tomato sauce**, drained **peas**, drained **corn**, **chili powder**, **salt**, and **pepper**. Stir well.
5.  Pour the mixture into the prepared baking dish.
6.  Cover the top with plenty of grated **cheddar cheese**.
7.  Bake for 25 minutes, or until hot and bubbly.
