## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 3 cups                     | Uncooked [[Noodles]]                      |
| 1 pound                    | [[Hamburger]]                             |
| 1 small                    | [[Onion]], diced                          |
| 1 stick                    | [[Pepperoni]], sliced                     |
| 1 (10.5 ounce) can         | [[Pizza Sauce]]                           |
| 1 teaspoon                 | [[Oregano]]                               |
| 1 teaspoon                 | [[Garlic Salt]]                           |
| As Needed                  | [[Mozzarella Cheese]], shredded           |
| 1/2 cup                    | [[Milk]]                                  |
___
## Instructions
1.  Cook the noodles according to package directions. Drain.
2.  In a skillet, brown the hamburger with the diced onion. Drain off any excess grease.
3.  In a large bowl or directly in a casserole dish, combine the cooked noodles, the browned hamburger mixture, sliced pepperoni, pizza sauce, oregano, garlic salt, milk, and a generous amount of mozzarella cheese.
4.  Mix all ingredients together thoroughly.
5.  Bake in a preheated oven at 375°F for 45 minutes.
___

>[!Note]
>The recipe card suggests using either a 10.5 oz can of pizza sauce or a 15.5 oz jar of Ragu Spaghetti Sauce with mushrooms.
