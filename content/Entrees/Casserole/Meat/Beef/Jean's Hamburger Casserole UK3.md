## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
| 1 Pound             | [[Ground Meat]]              |
| 1                   | [[Bell Pepper]]         |
| 1                   | [[Onion]]          |
| 1 Cup | [[Ripe Pitted Olives]] (Chopped)          |
| 1 Cup      |[[Corn]]|
| 1 Can | [[Tomatoes]]|
| 1/2 Cup | [[Macaroni]]|
| 1/2 Pound | [[Cheese]]|
___
### Instructions
1. Brown pepper & onion. Add meat & cook.
2. Cook macaroni according to package directions.
3. Mix olives, corn, tomatoes in with cooked macaroni.
4. Thin with olive juice or tomato juice if needed.
5. Combine meat mixture and macaroni mixture in a casserole dish.
6. Top with cheese.
7. Bake at 350° for 45 minutes.
