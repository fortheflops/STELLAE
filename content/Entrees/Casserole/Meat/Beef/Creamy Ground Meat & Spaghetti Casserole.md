### Instructions (Clarified Method)
1.  Preheat oven to 350°F. Grease a casserole dish.
2.  Cook the **spaghetti** according to package directions. Drain.
3.  In a skillet, brown the **chopped onion** and **ground meat**. Drain excess fat.
4.  Stir the **mushroom soup** into the meat mixture. Season with **salt**, **pepper**, and **garlic salt** to taste.
5.  In the casserole dish, create layers: start with a layer of the meat mixture, then a layer of spaghetti, then a layer of **cheese**.
6.  Repeat the layers, ending with a final layer of cheese on top.
7.  Bake for 20 minutes, or until hot and bubbly.
