### Instructions
1. Brown 2 pounds ground meat lean, chopped onion, 1 clove garlic, 1 green pepper, Salt & pepper to taste, pimentos.
2. Add: 1 Can cream of chicken soup, 1 Can cream of mushroom soup, 1 tall can whole kernal corn, 3 Cups cooked rice or noodles, 1 Cup sour cream.
3. Mix these well.
4. Sprinkle 1 can chow mein noodles.
5. Bake 350° 45-1 hr.
___

>[!Note]
>Serves 10
