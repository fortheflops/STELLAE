*A classic layered "Company Casserole" that's perfect for making ahead. It features a rich meat sauce and a creamy cheese filling between layers of noodles.*

## Recipe
| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 8 Ounces | [[Uncooked Noodles]] |
| 1 Pound | [[Ground Beef]] |
| 1 Tablespoon | [[Fat]] or Oil |
| 2 Cans (8 Ounces each) | [[Tomato Sauce]] |
| 1/2 Pound | [[Cottage Cheese]] |
| 1 Package (8 Ounces) | [[Cream Cheese]], softened |
| 1/4 Cup | [[Commercial Sour Cream]] |
| 1/3 Cup | [[Minced Onion]] |
| 1 Tablespoon | [[Minced Green Pepper]] |
| 2 Tablespoons | [[Melted Butter]] |
___
### Instructions
1.  Preheat oven to 350°F. Grease a casserole dish.
2.  Cook & drain **noodles** according to package directions.
3.  In a skillet, brown the **ground beef** in **fat**; drain. Stir in the **tomato sauce**.
4.  In a separate bowl, combine the **cottage cheese**, softened **cream cheese**, **sour cream**, **minced onion**, and **minced green pepper**. Mix well.
5.  In the casserole dish, create layers:
    * Half of the noodles.
    * Half of the meat sauce.
    * All of the cheese mixture.
    * The remaining noodles.
    * The remaining meat sauce.
6.  Drizzle the melted **butter** over the top.
7.  Bake at 350° for 45-50 minutes, or until heated through and bubbly.
___

>[!Note]
>Serves 8. This casserole can be assembled ahead of time and refrigerated before baking.
