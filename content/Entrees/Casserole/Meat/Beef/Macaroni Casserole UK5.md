## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 pound                    | [[Hamburger]]                             |
| 1 package                  | Cooked [[Macaroni]]                       |
| As Needed                  | [[Tomatoes]] and/or [[Tomato Paste]]      |
| "Lots"                     | [[Velveeta]] cheese                       |
| As Needed                  | [[Seasonings]]                            |
| As Needed                  | Chopped [[Onion]]                         |
___
## Instructions
1.  Brown 1 lb. of hamburger with chopped onion and seasonings. Drain off the grease.
2.  Combine the meat with 1 package of cooked macaroni.
3.  Add tomatoes and/or tomato paste, lots of Velveeta, and more seasoning.
4.  Bake slowly.
___

>[!Note]
>The card suggests several variations:
>* **White version:** Use cream of mushroom soup instead of tomato.
>* **Flavor additions:** Add chili powder, celery, or bell pepper as a flavor or filler.
