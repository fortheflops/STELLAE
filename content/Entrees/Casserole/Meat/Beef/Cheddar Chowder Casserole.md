### Instructions
1.  Preheat oven to 350°F. Grease a large casserole dish.
2.  In a large skillet, brown the **ground meat**, **chopped onion**, **garlic**, and **green pepper**. Drain excess fat.
3.  Stir in the **salt**, **pepper**, and drained **pimentos**.
4.  Add the **cream of chicken soup**, **cream of mushroom soup**, drained **corn**, cooked **rice** (or noodles), and **sour cream**. Mix well.
5.  Pour the mixture into the casserole dish.
6.  Sprinkle the **chow mein noodles** evenly over the top.
7.  Bake for 45 minutes to 1 hour, or until hot and bubbly.
___

>[!Note]
>Despite the title "Cheddar Chowder," this classic recipe does not actually contain any cheddar cheese. It is a savory ground beef and chow mein noodle casserole. It serves 10.
