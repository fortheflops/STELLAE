## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 1/2 pound                  | [[Ground Beef]]                           |
| 1 teaspoon                 | [[Salt]]                                  |
| 1/8 teaspoon               | [[Lawry's Seasoned Salt]]                 |
| 1/8 teaspoon               | [[Black Pepper]]                          |
| 1/4 teaspoon               | All-purpose Seasoning                     |
| 1 pound package            | Frozen [[French Fries]]                   |
| 2 cans                     | [[Cream of Mushroom Soup]]                |
| 2/3 can                    | [[Milk]]                                  |
| Optional                   | [[Onions]] and [[Peas]]                   |
___
## Instructions
1. Lightly spread the raw ground beef in a flat 8x11 inch well-buttered baking dish.
2. In a small bowl, combine the salt, seasoned salt, black pepper, and all-purpose seasoning. Sprinkle this mixture evenly over the ground beef.
3. Layer the frozen French fries on top of the seasoned meat.
4. In a separate bowl, blend the cream of mushroom soup and milk until thoroughly mixed.
5. Pour the soup mixture evenly over the top of the French fries.
6. Bake at 375°F for 45 minutes.
