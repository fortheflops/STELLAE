## Recipe
| Measurements              | INGREDIENTS             |
| :------------------------ | :---------------------- |
| 1 Envelope                | Lipton Beef Flavor [[Mushroom Mix]] |
| 1 Pound                   | [[Ground Beef]]         |
| $\frac{1}{2}$ Cup         | Soft [[Bread Crumbs]]   |
| $\frac{1}{4}$ Cup         | [[Ketchup]]             |
| $\frac{1}{4}$ Cup         | [[Water]]               |
| 1                         | [[Egg]]                 |
| 2-3 Cups                  | Hot [[Mashed Potatoes]] |
___
## Instructions
1.  Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C).
2.  In a medium bowl, combine Lipton mix, ground beef, ketchup, water, and egg; mix thoroughly.
3.  Press meat mixture into a 9-inch pie pan.
4.  Bake 35-40 minutes; drain.
5.  Top with mashed potatoes
___

>[!Note]
>Makes about 4 servings
