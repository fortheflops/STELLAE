*A wonderfully simple and hearty casserole, this "E-Z Casserole" bakes raw ground beef, seasonings, and frozen french fries in a creamy mushroom sauce.*
### Author
Paulette (9/10/84)
___
## Recipe
| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 pound | [[Ground Beef]] (raw) |
| 1 pound package | Frozen [[French Fries]] |
| 2 cans | [[Cream of Mushroom Soup]] |
| 2/3 can | [[Milk]] |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Salt]] |
| 1/8 teaspoon | [[Lawry's Seasoned Salt]] |
| 1/8 teaspoon | [[Black Pepper]] |
| 1/4 teaspoon | All-purpose Seasoning |
| Optional | [[Onions]] and [[Peas]] |
___
## Instructions
1.  Preheat oven to 375°F. Butter an 8x11 inch baking dish.
2.  Lightly spread the raw **ground beef** in the bottom of the dish.
3.  In a small bowl, combine all the **seasonings** (Salt, Seasoned Salt, Pepper, All-purpose Seasoning). Sprinkle this mixture evenly over the ground beef.
4.  Layer the frozen **French fries** on top of the meat. (Add optional **onions** and **peas** here, if using).
5.  In a separate bowl, blend the **cream of mushroom soup** and **milk** until smooth.
6.  Pour the soup mixture evenly over the French fries.
7.  Bake for 45 minutes.
