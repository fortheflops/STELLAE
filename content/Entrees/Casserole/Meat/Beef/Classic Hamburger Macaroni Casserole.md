## Instructions (Clarified Method)
1.  Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Grease a large casserole dish.
2.  Cook the **macaroni** according to package directions. Drain.
3.  In a large skillet, brown the **hamburger** with the **chopped onion** and **seasonings**. Drain off the grease.
4.  In a large bowl, combine the cooked macaroni, the meat mixture, **tomatoes** (and/or tomato paste), and most of the **Velveeta** cheese.
5.  Pour the mixture into the casserole dish and top with the remaining cheese.
6.  Bake for 30-40 minutes ("Bake slowly") until hot and bubbly.
