*A classic and comforting main course, this "Easy As Pie" is a simple Shepherd's (or Cottage) Pie with a savory meatloaf-like base and a mashed potato topping.*

## Recipe
| Measurements | INGREDIENTS |
| :--- | :--- |
| 1 Envelope | Lipton Beef Flavor [[Mushroom Mix]] |
| 1 Pound | [[Ground Beef]] |
| $\frac{1}{2}$ Cup | Soft [[Bread Crumbs]] |
| $\frac{1}{4}$ Cup | [[Ketchup]] |
| $\frac{1}{4}$ Cup | [[Water]] |
| 1 | [[Egg]] |
| 2-3 Cups | Hot [[Mashed Potatoes]] |
___
## Instructions
1.  Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C).
2.  In a medium bowl, combine the Lipton **mushroom mix**, **ground beef**, **bread crumbs**, **ketchup**, **water**, and **egg**. Mix thoroughly.
3.  Press the meat mixture into the bottom and up the sides of a 9-inch pie pan to form a crust.
4.  Bake for 35-40 minutes.
5.  Carefully drain any excess fat from the pan.
6.  Top the hot meat pie with the hot **mashed potatoes** and serve.
___

>[!Note]
>Makes about 4 servings.
