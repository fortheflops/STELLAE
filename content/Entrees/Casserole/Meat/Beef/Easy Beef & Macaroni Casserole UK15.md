## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 pound                    | [[Ground Beef]]                           |
| 1/2 cup                    | [[Miracle Whip]]                          |
| 1 (30 ounce) jar           | [[Spaghetti Sauce]]                       |
| 1 (7 ounce) package        | [[Macaroni]]                              |
| As Needed                  | [[Cheese]]                                |
___
## Instructions
1.  Cook the macaroni according to package directions. Drain.
2.  While the macaroni is cooking, brown the ground beef in a large skillet or Dutch oven. Drain off any excess grease.
3.  Stir the Miracle Whip and spaghetti sauce into the browned ground beef.
4.  Add the cooked macaroni to the meat sauce and stir to combine.
5.  Heat through on the stovetop.
6.  Top with cheese before serving.
