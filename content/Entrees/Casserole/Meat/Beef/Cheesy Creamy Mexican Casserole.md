### Layers
| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 1 1/2 lbs. | [[Ground Beef]] |
| 1 | [[Medium Onion (Chopped)]] |
| 1 dozen | [[Soft Corn Tortillas (Torn in 1/4's)]] |
| 1/2 lb. | [[Grated Cheddar Cheese (or more)]] |
___
### Instructions
1.  Preheat oven to 325°F. Grease an 11x13 inch baking dish.
2.  **For the Sauce:** In a saucepan, mix the **cream of mushroom soup**, **cream of chicken soup**, **taco sauce**, **chopped green chilies**, and **milk**. Warm over low heat, stirring until smooth.
3.  **For the Meat:** In a separate skillet, brown the **ground beef** and **chopped onion**. Drain any excess fat.
4.  **To Assemble:** Layer the ingredients in the greased dish as follows:
    * A thin layer of sauce.
    * Half of the torn **tortillas**.
    * Half of the meat mixture.
    * Half of the remaining sauce.
    * The rest of the tortillas.
    * The rest of the meat mixture.
    * The rest of the sauce.
5.  Sprinkle the **grated cheddar cheese** evenly over the top.
6.  Bake for 1 hour, or until hot and bubbly.
