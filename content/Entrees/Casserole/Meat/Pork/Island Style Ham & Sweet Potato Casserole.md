### Instructions
1.  Cook, mash potatoes - add butter, salt, pepper, nutmeg & milk to whip.
2.  Saute ham in 2 T margarine, stirring until golden.
3.  Add green pepper & pineapple chunks - cook 2-3 min.
4.  Stir in combined br. sugar & cornstarch then juice & vinegar.
5.  Cook stirring until clear & thick.
6.  Pour mixture into 9" pie plate, drop spoonfuls of potato on top.
7.  Bake until bubbling hot.
