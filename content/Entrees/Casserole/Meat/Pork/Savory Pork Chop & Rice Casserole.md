### Instructions
1.  Preheat oven to 350°F.
2.  Place 6 T raw rice in casserole.
3.  Place on top 4 browned pork chops.
4.  Add drippings from chops.
5.  Put on a slice of onion, tomato and ring of green pepper on each chop.
6.  Salt & pepper each layer.
7.  Pour on 1 can consommé and sprinkle with a couple pinches each of marjoram and thyme.
8.  Bake 350° for 1 hr.
