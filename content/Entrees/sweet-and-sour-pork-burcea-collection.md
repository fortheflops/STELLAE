# Sweet And Sour Pork

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Fry Meat
1. First of all, cube your **2 lbs pork**. Then dip in batter made of **2 eggs**, **1/2 tsp salt**, **3/4 cup flour**, and **2-3 tbsp water**. Batter will be thick.
2. Dip **2 lbs pork** and fry til golden brown.

### Step 2: Make Sauce and Combine
3. Mix the **1 cup vinegar**, **1 cup water**, **1 cup sugar**, **3 tbsp corn starch**, **1 tsp salt**, **1/2 tsp pepper**, **1 1/2 tsp accent**, **1 pinch ginger**, and **3 tbsp ketchup** together.
4. Stir and cook til clear—a pretty reddish color only transparent. It will get a little thick.
5. Then add cooked **2 lbs pork** also **2 green peppers**, **2 tomatoes**, and **1 can pineapple**.
6. Cook for about 3 minutes.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The sauce and meat can be prepared ahead separately, but combine and cook with the fresh vegetables right before serving for the best texture.
