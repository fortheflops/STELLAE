# Simple Baked Tuna Noodle Casserole

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A classic, simple tuna casserole from the Gail Collection. This version features a homemade custard of milk and eggs poured over noodles and tuna, topped with crunchy crushed potato chips.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 30 mins | None | 6 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Casserole dish
* Large bowl
* Wire whisk

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 8 oz. | [[Noodles]] | cooked and drained |
| 2 (6 oz) cans | [[Tuna]] | drained |
| 1 1/2 cups | [[Milk]] |  |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] |  |
| 1 cup | [[Potato Chips]] | crushed |
| 1 Tbsp | [[Butter]] | for greasing pan |

---

## Instructions

### Preparation and Baking
1. Preheat the oven to **350°F**. Grease a casserole dish with **1 Tbsp butter**.
2. In a large bowl, whisk together the **1 1/2 cups milk** and **2 eggs** until smooth.
3. Add the cooked **8 oz. noodles** and **2 cans drained tuna** to the greased casserole dish.
4. Pour the **milk** and **egg** mixture over the **noodles** and **tuna**, and stir gently to combine.
5. Top the casserole evenly with the **1 cup crushed potato chips**.
6. Bake at **350°F** for **30 minutes**, or until the custard is set and the top is golden brown and bubbling.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole (without the potato chips) up to a day in advance, cover, and refrigerate. Add the potato chips just before baking.
