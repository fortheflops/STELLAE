# Wilma's Chili Relleno Casserole

> 📜 **Collection:** PatsyCollection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Wilma Lachmann | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A classic, hearty Chili Relleno Casserole featuring layers of whole green chiles and two kinds of cheese, all baked in a light, fluffy egg soufflé topping and finished with tomato sauce. This comforting Tex-Mex dish bakes up golden brown and bubbly.*

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 9x13 inch casserole dish
* Medium mixing bowl
* Clean mixing bowl
* Electric mixer or whisk

## Ingredients

### Casserole Layers
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 6 (3.5 oz) Cans | [[Chili Peppers]] | Whole, de-seeded and drained |
| 1 lb | [[Cheddar Cheese]] | Tillamook or Longhorn, grated |
| 1 lb | [[Monterey Jack Cheese]] | Grated |

### Soufflé Topping
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 4 | [[Eggs]] | Separated |
| 1 (12 oz) Can | [[Evaporated Milk]] | Or 1.5 cups plain milk |
| 3 Tbsp | [[Flour]] | None |
| To Taste | [[Salt]] & [[Pepper]] | None |

### Final Topping
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 (8 oz) Cans | [[Tomato Sauce]] | Small cans |

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Layer
1. Preheat your oven to **325°F**. Use an ungreased **9x13 inch pan**.
2. Drain, de-seeded, and slit the **6 (3.5 oz) cans whole [[Chili Peppers]]**, patting them dry.
3. Lay out **half** of the **chiles** to cover the bottom of the pan.
4. Cover the chiles with all of the grated **1 lb Cheddar Cheese**.
5. Layer the **remaining chiles** on top of the cheese.
6. Add a final layer of all of the grated **1 lb Monterey Jack Cheese**.

### Step 2: Make the Soufflé Topping
7. Separate the **4 [[Eggs]]**. In a medium bowl, beat the **4 egg yolks**.
8. Add the **1 (12 oz) can evaporated milk**, **3 tbsp flour**, **salt**, and **pepper** to the yolks and whisk until smooth.
9. In a separate clean bowl, beat the **4 egg whites** until stiff peaks form.
10. Gently fold the beaten **egg whites** into the yolk mixture until just combined.
11. Pour this egg soufflé mixture evenly over the cheese and chiles in the pan.

### Step 3: Bake and Serve
12. Bake uncovered for **1 hour**, or until the egg mixture is set, puffed, and lightly golden.
13. Remove from the oven and pour the **2 (8 oz) cans tomato sauce** evenly over the top.
14. Return to the oven and bake for another **30 minutes** until bubbling and hot.
15. Let stand for **10 minutes** before slicing and serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftovers sealed in the refrigerator for up to 3-4 days. Reheat gently in the oven or microwave before serving.
