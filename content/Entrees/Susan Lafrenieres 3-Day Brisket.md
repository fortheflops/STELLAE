# Susan Lafreniere's 3-Day Brisket

> 📜 **Collection:** General Archive | ✍️ **Attribution:** Susan Lafreniere | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A classic, multi-day recipe from Susan Lafreniere featuring a slow-roasted beef brisket that is chilled, sliced, and reheated in homemade BBQ sauce.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Make the BBQ Sauce
1. In a small saucepan, combine **1/2 tablespoon brown sugar**, **1/2 cup catsup**, **1/4 cup water**, **dash of pepper**, **1/2 teaspoon salt**, **1 teaspoon celery salt**, **3 tablespoons butter**, **1/2 teaspoon dry mustard**, **2 tablespoons worcestershire**, and **1 tablespoon liquid smoke**.
2. Heat slowly, stirring continuously, until the **butter** is melted and the sauce is fully combined. Set aside to cool completely.

### Step 2: Day 1 - Season & Rest
3. Place the **1 (3-5 lb.) brisket** in a large piece of heavy-duty aluminum foil.
4. Baste the **brisket** generously with **2-3 tbsp liquid smoke** and **1 tbsp salt**.
5. Wrap tightly in the foil and refrigerate overnight.

### Step 3: Day 2 - Slow Roast & Chill
6. Preheat oven to **275°F (135°C)**.
7. Open the foil and rub the **brisket** thoroughly with **1 tbsp dried onion** and **1 tbsp garlic powder**.
8. Rewrap tightly in the foil, place in a roasting pan, and bake for **10 hours** without opening the foil.
9. After **10 hours**, remove from the oven, let cool slightly, and refrigerate overnight in the foil until thoroughly chilled.

### Step 4: Day 3 - Slice, Sauce & Reheat
10. Preheat oven to **325°F (165°C)**.
11. Remove the cold **brisket** from the foil and scrape off any congealed fat. Slice the **brisket** thinly against the grain.
12. Place the sliced **brisket** back in the foil or a clean baking dish.
13. Cover the **brisket** slices completely with the cooled BBQ sauce and rewrap or cover tightly with foil.
14. Bake for **1 hour**, or until the sauce is hot and bubbly and the meat is fork-tender.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This recipe is designed to be made over 3 days, with the brisket resting and chilling overnight at multiple stages before its final bake.
