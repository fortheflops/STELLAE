# Stuffed Manicotti Marinara

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *Delight in this comforting baked manicotti featuring a savory spinach and ricotta filling, topped with rich marinara and melted mozzarella. Perfectly portioned and baked until bubbling and golden.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 20 mins | None | 2 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Spoon or piping bag
* 2 individual shallow baking dishes

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 Cup | [[Spinach]] | cooked, chopped, and well drained (frozen is best) |
| ⅔ Cup | [[Ricotta Cheese]] | fresh |
| ¼ tsp | [[Garlic Powder]] | none |
| ¼ tsp | [[Ground Nutmeg]] | none |
| ½ tsp | [[Salt]] | none |
| ⅛ tsp | [[Pepper]] | none |
| 4 | [[Manicotti Noodles]] | cooked and drained |
| ½ Cup | [[Marinara Sauce]] | divided |
| 2 oz | [[Mozzarella Cheese]] | grated, divided |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Filling
1. In a mixing bowl, combine the **1 cup spinach** and **⅔ cup ricotta cheese**. Add the **¼ tsp garlic powder**, **¼ tsp ground nutmeg**, **½ tsp salt**, and **⅛ tsp pepper**, mixing thoroughly until well incorporated.

### Step 2: Assemble and Bake
2. Stuff the cheese and spinach mixture equally into the **4 manicotti noodles**.
3. Divide the stuffed noodles evenly into **2 individual shallow baking dishes**.
4. Spoon **2 Tbsp.** of **½ cup marinara sauce** over each noodle, then top each with **1oz.** of **2 oz grated mozzarella cheese**.
5. Bake uncovered at 350° for **20 minutes** until the cheese is bubbly and lightly golden.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the dishes ahead of time, cover tightly, and refrigerate for up to 24 hours before baking as directed.
