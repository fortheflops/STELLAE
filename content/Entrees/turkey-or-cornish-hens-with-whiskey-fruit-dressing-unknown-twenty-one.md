## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and Dressing
1. Thoroughly rub the **1 whole turkey or Cornish hens** with a combined mixture of **Dijon mustard** and **molasses**.
2. In a mixing bowl, combine **2 cups bread crumbs**, **1/2 cup onion**, **1/2 cup celery**, **1 cup apple**, **1/2 cup nuts**, **1/4 cup prunes**, and **1/4 cup raisins** that have been previously soaked in **1/4 cup whiskey**.
3. Mix the dressing thoroughly, adding **turkey broth** or **chicken broth** as needed to achieve a moist consistency.

### Step 2: Stuffing and Baking
4. Stuff the dressing loosely into the cavity of the prepared **1 whole turkey or Cornish hens** or place in a baking dish alongside the bird.
5. Bake in a preheated oven until the skin is deeply browned and crisp, and the meat reaches safe internal doneness temperatures with juices running clear.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The dressing can be prepared up to one day in advance and refrigerated separately before stuffing and baking.
