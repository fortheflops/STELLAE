# Chix Picante

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty One | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *Discover a quick and flavorful weeknight dinner with this easy Chix Picante recipe, featuring tender chicken breasts baked in a tangy picante and brown sugar glaze. Serve alongside warm cooked rice for a satisfying and simple meal.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 20 mins | None | 4 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Bake dish
* Small mixing bowl

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 4 | [[Chix breasts]] | boneless, skinless |
| 1 1/2 c | [[Picante sauce]] | None |
| 3 T | [[Brown sugar]] | None |
| 1 T | [[Dijon mustard]] | None |
| 3 C | [[Rice]] | cooked |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and Baking
1. In a bake dish, place **4** **[[Chix breasts]]**.
2. In a small bowl, mix **1 1/2 c** **[[Picante sauce]]**, **3 T** **[[Brown sugar]]**, and **1 T** **[[Dijon mustard]]**; pour mixture over the **[[Chix breasts]]**.
3. Bake at 400°F for **20 minutes** or until done and the chicken is cooked through.
4. Serve warm with **3 C** **[[Rice]]**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the chicken and sauce in the baking dish a few hours in advance, cover, and refrigerate until ready to bake.
