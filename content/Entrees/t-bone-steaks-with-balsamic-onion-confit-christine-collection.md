# T-Bone Steaks with Balsamic Onion Confit

> 📜 **Collection:** Christine Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *Thick, juicy T-bone steaks seasoned simply and paired with a rich, slow-cooked balsamic onion confit. This elegant entree delivers a perfect balance of savory steak and sweet, tangy caramelized onions.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Onion Confit
1. To make the confit, melt **1 Tbsp unsalted butter** in a large non-stick skillet over medium heat.
2. Add the **3 large onions**, a **pinch of salt**, and **1 cup water**. Cook for 20 minutes, stirring often, until the water evaporates and the onions are soft and translucent.
3. Stir in another **1 cup water** and cook for an additional 20 minutes, stirring often, until the water evaporates again and the onions are deeply browned and tender.
4. Add **3 Tbsp balsamic vinegar** and **3 Tbsp honey**. Cook for 10 minutes, stirring often, until the onions are caramelized, sweet, and jammy.

### Step 2: Cook the Steaks
5. Season **4 (14 oz) T-Bone steaks** evenly on both sides with **1/2 tsp salt** and **1/2 tsp freshly ground black pepper**.
6. Cook the seasoned steaks in a hot cast-iron skillet or on a grill over medium-high heat until they reach your desired level of doneness (about 4 to 5 minutes per side for medium-rare, or an internal temperature of 135°F).
7. Let the steaks rest for 5 minutes to lock in the juices, then serve topped generously with the warm balsamic onion confit.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The balsamic onion confit can be prepared up to 3 days in advance and stored in an airtight container in the refrigerator. Reheat gently on the stovetop before serving over freshly cooked steaks.
