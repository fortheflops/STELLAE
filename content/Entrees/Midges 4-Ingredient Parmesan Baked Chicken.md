# Midge's 4-Ingredient Parmesan Baked Chicken

> 📜 **Collection:** UK5 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Midge Robertson | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *An incredibly simple and quick recipe featuring skinned chicken breasts dipped in melted butter and Parmesan cheese, then baked until golden and tender.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 50 mins | None | 2 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Oven
* Shallow pie plate or baking dish

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 medium | [[Chicken Breasts]] | skinned |
| 1 tablespoon | [[Margarine]] or [[Butter]] | melted |
| 1/3 cup | [[Parmesan Cheese]] | grated |
| 2 tablespoons | [[Margarine]] | for the pan |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation
1. Preheat the oven to **400°F**.

### Step 2: Coating and Baking
2. Dip the **2 medium chicken breasts** first in the **1 tablespoon melted margarine or butter**, then dip them in the **1/3 cup grated Parmesan cheese** to coat thoroughly.
3. Melt the remaining **2 tablespoons margarine** in a shallow pie plate or baking dish in the oven.
4. Place the cheese-coated chicken breasts in the hot dish with the melted margarine.
5. Bake for about **50 minutes**, or until the chicken is tender and golden brown.
6. Spoon the pan drippings over the chicken once or twice during baking.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Leftover baked chicken can be stored in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 to 4 days.
