# Chicken Enchiladas

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Jean W. | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A delightful layered chicken enchilada casserole baked with creamy soups, green chilis, and melted cheddar cheese until bubbly and golden. Perfect for a hearty family dinner serving up to eight.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 45 mins | 5 mins | 6-8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Skillet
* 9x13 baking pan
* Spoon

---
## Ingredients

### Filling and Assembly
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 whole | [[Chicken]] | Cooked and boned |
| 1 pkg. | [[Tortillas]] | Standard |
| 1/2 lb. | [[Cheddar Cheese]] | Grated |
| 1 lg. | [[Onion]] | Chopped |
| 6 lg. | [[Green Chilis]] | Chopped |
| 1 can | [[Cream of Mushroom Soup]] | Condensed |
| 1 can | [[Cream of Chicken Soup]] | Condensed |
| 1 can | [[Chicken Broth]] | Standard |
| 1 tbsp | [[Butter]] | For sautéing |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Sauce Preparation
1. Sauté the **1 lg. onion** in **1 tbsp butter** in a skillet until translucent.
2. Add the **6 lg. green chilis**, **1 can cream of mushroom soup**, **1 can cream of chicken soup**, and **1 can chicken broth**.
3. Simmer for **5 mins** until heated through and well blended.

### Step 2: Layering and Baking
4. In a **lg. baking pan (9x13)**, layer the **1 pkg. tortillas**, **1 whole chicken**, the prepared **sauce**, and **1/2 lb. cheddar cheese**.
5. Bake at **350°** for **about 45 mins** until bubbling around the edges and the cheese is melted and lightly golden.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole in the baking pan ahead of time, cover tightly, and refrigerate for up to 24 hours before baking.
