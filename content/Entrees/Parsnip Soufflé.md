# Parsnip Soufflé

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *An elegant vintage parsnip soufflé baked in a water bath until golden and set. This delicate side dish pairs wonderfully with roast beef.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 30 mins | 45 mins | None | 6 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Saucepan
* Sieve or blender
* Clean mixing bowl
* 1 1/2 quart soufflé dish
* Shallow baking pan for water bath

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 Pound | [[Parsnips]] | 4 medium, unpeeled |
| 3 Tablespoons | [[Butter]] | or margarine |
| 3 Tablespoons | [[Flour]] | all-purpose |
| 1/2 Teaspoon | [[Salt]] | plus more for boiling |
| 2/3 Cup | [[Cream]] | light cream |
| 1/3 Cup | [[Parsnip Water]] | reserved from boiling |
| 1/4 Teaspoon | [[Sugar]] | granulated |
| 1/8 Teaspoon | [[Nutmeg]] | ground |
| Pinch | [[White Pepper]] | ground |
| 4 | [[Egg Yolks]] | well-beaten |
| 5 | [[Egg Whites]] | room temperature |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare and Puree Parsnips
1. Preheat your oven to **350°F**.
2. Cook **1 pound parsnips** in salted water for **20 minutes**, or until tender.
3. Drain the parsnips, reserving **1/3 cup** of the drained water.
4. Run cold water over the cooked parsnips and slip the skins off.
5. Puree the parsnips by pushing them through a sieve or using a blender until you have approximately **1 cup of parsnip puree**.

### Step 2: Make the White Sauce Base
6. In a saucepan, melt **3 tablespoons butter**.
7. Blend in **3 tablespoons flour**, **1/2 teaspoon salt**, **2/3 cup light cream**, and the **1/3 cup reserved parsnip water**.
8. Simmer for **3 to 5 minutes**, stirring constantly, until the sauce is thick. Remove from heat and let it cool.

### Step 3: Mix and Bake the Soufflé
9. Combine the cooled white sauce with the **1 cup parsnip puree**, **1/4 teaspoon sugar**, **1/8 teaspoon nutmeg**, and a **pinch of white pepper**.
10. Blend in the **4 well-beaten egg yolks**.
11. In a separate clean bowl, beat the **5 egg whites** until soft peaks are formed.
12. Gently fold the beaten egg whites into the parsnip mixture in two stages.
13. Carefully pour the mixture into a **1 1/2 quart greased soufflé dish**.
14. Place the soufflé dish in a shallow pan filled with **1/2 inch of hot water** to create a water bath.
15. Bake for **45 minutes**, or until the soufflé is golden on top and set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Soufflés are best served immediately upon baking as they will deflate shortly after removal from the oven.
