# Mexican Chicken

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Marty Edwards | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A comforting baked casserole featuring tender chicken, melted cheddar cheese, and soft tortillas simmered in savory green chile soup. This delicious family-style dish bakes until bubbly and hot.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 30 mins | 35 mins | None | 6–8 Servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Baking dish
* Saucepan
* Knife
* Cutting board

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | [[Fryer Chicken]] | Boiled and cut into bite-size pieces |
| 1 | [[Onion]] | Chopped |
| 1/2 lb. | [[Cheddar Cheese]] | Grated |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Garlic Salt]] | None |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Chili Powder]] | None |
| 1 can | [[Tomatoes & Green Chilies]] | Mashed |
| 1 pkg. | [[Soft Tortillas]] | None |
| 2 cans | [[Cream of Chicken Soup]] | Divided use |
| As needed | [[Chicken Stock]] | For softening tortillas |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep the Ingredients
1. Boil the **1 [[Fryer Chicken]]** and cut it into bite-size pieces.
2. In a bowl, mix the **bite-size chicken pieces**, chopped **1 [[Onion]]**, **1/2 lb. [[Cheddar Cheese]]** (grated), **1 teaspoon [[Garlic Salt]]**, **1 teaspoon [[Chili Powder]]**, **1 can [[Tomatoes & Green Chilies]]** (mashed), **1 pkg. [[Soft Tortillas]]**, and **2 cans [[Cream of Chicken Soup]]**.

### Step 2: Assemble and Bake
3. Soften the **soft tortillas** in boiling **[[Chicken Stock]]**.
4. Line the softened **tortillas** in a baking dish.
5. Add the prepared **chicken mixture** on top of the tortillas.
6. Top the dish with an extra portion of **cream of chicken soup**.
7. Bake in the oven at **350 degrees** for about **35 minutes** until bubbly and hot.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole ahead of time and store covered in the refrigerator for up to 24 hours before baking.
