# Classic Homemade Sweet and Sour Pork

> 📜 **Collection:** General Archive | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A timeless sweet and sour pork recipe featuring tender braised pork strips in a tangy homemade sauce of pineapple, brown sugar, and soy, tossed with crisp green peppers and onions.*

| 2 T. | [[Cornstarch]] | None |

| 1 cup | [[Onion]] | Thinly sliced |
| As needed | Hot [[Fat]] | For browning |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Water]] | None |

## Instructions

### Step 1: Braise the Pork
1. Brown **1 1/2 lb. lean pork** strips in a small amount of **hot fat** in a large skillet or pot until golden brown on all sides.
2. Add **1/2 cup water**, cover, and simmer for **about 1 hour** until the pork is tender.

### Step 2: Prepare the Sauce
3. Drain the **pineapple chunks**, making sure to reserve all the syrup.
4. In a small bowl, combine the **1/4 cup brown sugar** and **2 T. cornstarch**.
5. Stir in the reserved pineapple syrup, **1/2 cup wine vinegar**, **4 T. soy sauce**, and **1/2 tsp. salt** to create the sauce mixture.

### Step 3: Simmer and Serve
6. Pour the sauce mixture over the tender pork in the skillet, and cook and stir until the gravy thickens.
7. Add the drained **pineapple chunks**, **1 small green pepper** strips, and **1 cup onion** slices.
8. Cook for **2 to 3 more minutes**, stirring constantly, until the vegetables are tender-crisp.
9. Serve hot over fluffy rice or chow mein noodles.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftover sweet and sour pork in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days.
