# Chili Relleno Casserole

> 📜 **Collection:** Patsy Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Wilma Lachmann | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A hearty, layered Chili Relleno Casserole featuring mild green chilies, Tillamook and Monterey Jack cheeses, and a fluffy egg topping. This comforting dish is perfect for family dinners and can be easily adapted for smaller gatherings.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 90 mins | None | 8-10 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 9x13 baking dish
* Mixing bowls
* Electric mixer or whisk

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 6 | [[Chili Peppers]] | 3.5 oz cans, whole, washed and de-seeded |
| 1 lb | [[Tillamook Cheese]] | Grated |
| 1 lb | [[Monterey Jack Cheese]] | Grated |
| 4 | [[Eggs]] | Separated |
| 1 can | [[Evaporated Milk]] | Tall can |
| 2 small cans | [[Tomato Sauce]] | None |
| 3 Tbsp | [[Flour]] | None |
| To taste | [[Salt]] and [[Pepper]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Assembly
1. Slit and lay half of the **6 chili peppers** in an ungreased 9x13 casserole dish to cover the bottom.
2. Add a layer of **1 lb Tillamook cheese**, followed by a layer of the remaining **chili peppers**.
3. Top with a layer of **1 lb Monterey Jack cheese**.

### Step 2: The Egg Mixture
4. Separate the **4 eggs**. Beat the yolks, then add **1 can evaporated milk**, **3 Tbsp flour**, **salt**, and **pepper** to the yolks.
5. In a separate bowl, beat the **4 egg whites** until stiff peaks form, then gently fold them into the yolk mixture.
6. Pour the egg mixture over the layered **chili peppers** and **cheeses**.

### Step 3: Baking
7. Bake at 325°F for 1 hour uncovered.
8. Pour **2 small cans tomato sauce** over the top and bake for an additional 30 minutes, or until the center is set and firm.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** You can assemble the casserole layers in advance and refrigerate; add the egg mixture and bake just before serving.
