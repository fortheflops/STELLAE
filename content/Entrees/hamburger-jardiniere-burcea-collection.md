# Hamburger Jardiniere

> 📜 **Collection:** Burcea Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A classic savory hamburger loaf baked with tomatoes and peas in a rich pan gravy. Served surrounded by a fluffy border of mashed potatoes.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 1 hour | None | 6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Skillet
* Pancake turner
* Baking pan
* Hot platter

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 1/2 lbs. | [[Hamburger]] | Ground beef |
| to taste | [[Salt]] | None |
| to taste | [[Pepper]] | None |
| 1 can | [[Peas]] | Drained |
| 1/4 c. | [[Flour]] | None |
| 1 can | [[Tomatoes]] | Whole or crushed |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Brown
1. Season the **1 1/2 lbs. hamburger** with **salt** and **pepper** and pat into a round flat loaf.
2. Brown in hot fat, turning carefully with a pancake turner so the loaf will not break.

### Step 2: Bake
3. Add the **1/4 c. flour** to the fat and mix.
4. Add the **1 can tomatoes** and **1 can peas**, cover the pan, and cook in a moderate oven (350° F) for one hour until bubbling and tender.
5. Remove to a hot platter and surround with a border of mashed potatoes.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The meat loaf can be shaped ahead of time and refrigerated for up to 24 hours before cooking.
