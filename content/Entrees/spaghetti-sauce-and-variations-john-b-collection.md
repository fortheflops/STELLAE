## Instructions

### Step 1: Sautéing and Simmering
1. Take the **2 tbsp. bacon grease**, **2 tbsp. oil**, and **2 tbsp. butter** and sauté the **2 large onions** and **1 1/2 pods garlic** until tender.
2. Add the **2 lbs. ground round** and **2 links italian sausage**. Cook until brown with all the spices (**3 whole cloves**, **2 bay leaves**, **3 shakes of nutmeg**, **1/2 tsp. rosemary leaves**, and **1/4 tsp. oregano leaves**).
3. Add **1/2 cup wine** and boil briefly.
4. Add **4 fresh mushrooms**, **1 can tomato sauce** (plus **1 can water**), and **1/2 can tomato paste** (plus **1/2 can water**).
5. Simmer for **3 hours** until rich and thickened to a deep, aromatic doneness.
6. Serve over cooked **spaghetti** and top generously with **parmesan cheese**.

### Step 2: Leftover Variations
7. **Pizza:** Make **pizza dough** and spread top with leftover mixture. Top with **mozzarella cheese** and **pepperoni sausage** and bake until bubbly.
8. **Rice-A-Roni:** Add **1 cup rice** to leftover mixture and keep adding **bouillon** as needed for juice till the **rice** is done. Top with **parmesan cheese**.
9. **Lasagne:** Use leftover mixture and add **lasagne noodles**. Sprinkle top with **parmesan cheese** and bake.
10. **Goulash:** Add a little **water** to leftover mixture and **noodles**. Top with **parmesan cheese**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This sauce tastes even better the next day and can be refrigerated for up to 4 days or frozen for up to 3 months.
