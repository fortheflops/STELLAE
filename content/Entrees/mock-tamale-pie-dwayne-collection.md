# Mock Tamale Pie

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A comforting and easy-to-make Mock Tamale Pie featuring layers of hominy, seasoned ground beef, chili, onions, and melted cheddar cheese. Baked to bubbly perfection, it's a quick and hearty weeknight favorite.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Cook the Beef
1. In a skillet, sauté **3/4 lb. ground beef** until browned and fully cooked. Season with **salt**, **black pepper**, **garlic powder**, **chili powder**, and **ground cumin** to taste.
2. Add **1 can chili without beans** to the seasoned beef and heat through until bubbling.

### Step 2: Assemble and Bake
3. Drain **1 can hominy** and place half of it in the bottom of a casserole dish.
4. Cover the hominy with half of the **beef and chili mixture**, then sprinkle with half of the **1 chopped onion** and half of the **1 cup shredded cheddar cheese**.
5. Repeat the layering process once more with the remaining ingredients, ending with a layer of cheese on top.
6. Bake at **350°F** for **1/2 hour** until the cheese is melted, bubbly, and golden brown around the edges.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Can be assembled up to 24 hours in advance, covered, and stored in the refrigerator. Add 5-10 minutes to the baking time if baking directly from the fridge.
