# Easy Chicken & Cheese Crescent Roll Casserole

> 📜 **Collection:** General Archive | ✍️ **Attribution:** Mrs. Mulligan | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A comforting and easy vintage recipe from Mrs. Mulligan featuring tender chicken and cheddar cheese rolled in crescent dough. Baked in a creamy chicken soup sauce, it's a quick family favorite.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 45 mins | None | 4–6 Servings |

* Measuring cup

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | [[Chicken]] | cooked, deboned, and cut into pieces |
| As needed | [[Cheddar Cheese]] | grated |
| 1 can | [[Cream of Chicken Soup]] | None |
| 1 cup | [[Milk]] | None |
| 1 can | [[Crescent Rolls]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Assemble
1. Preheat oven to **350°F** and grease a large shallow baking pan.
2. Prepare the **1 cooked and deboned chicken** by cutting the meat into small pieces.
3. Unroll the **1 can crescent rolls** and separate into triangles.
4. On each triangle, place a small amount of **grated cheddar cheese** and some of the **cooked chicken** pieces.
5. Roll each crescent up, starting from the wide end, and place it in the prepared baking pan.

### Step 2: Sauce and Bake
6. In a saucepan, heat the **1 cup milk** and **1 can cream of chicken soup**. Add any extra leftover **cheddar cheese** or **chicken** you have, stirring until warm and combined.
7. Pour the soup sauce evenly over the filled crescent rolls in the pan.
8. Bake at **350°F** for **40-45 minutes**, or until the rolls are golden brown and the sauce is bubbly.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the crescent rolls and sauce ahead of time, cover, and refrigerate for up to 24 hours before baking. Add 5-10 minutes to the baking time if baking straight from the fridge.
