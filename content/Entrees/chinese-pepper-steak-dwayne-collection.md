# Chinese Pepper Steak

## Instructions

### Step 1: Brown the Beef
1. Melt **2 tbsp. lard or drippings** in a skillet and add **1 lb. beef chuck strips**. Brown lightly until no longer pink.

### Step 2: Add Vegetables & Simmer
2. Add **2 tbsp. minced onion**, **1 clove minced garlic**, **1/2 cup sliced celery**, **2 large green peppers**, and **2 tbsp. chopped pimento** to the skillet. Pour in **1/2 cup consommé or stock** and season with **salt** and **black pepper** to taste. Cover and simmer for **20 minutes** until the vegetables are tender.

### Step 3: Thicken & Serve
3. In a small bowl, blend **2 tsp. cornstarch** with **2 tbsp. water** and **1 tsp. soy sauce** until smooth. Stir this mixture into the skillet to thicken the sauce. Simmer for an additional **5 minutes** until the sauce is glossy, bubbling, and thickened. Serve hot over **3 cups cooked rice**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The beef and vegetables can be prepped ahead of time and stored separately in the refrigerator. Leftovers keep well in an airtight container for up to 3 days.
