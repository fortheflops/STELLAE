# Alma Roeder's Mushroom Quiche

> 📜 **Collection:** Patsy Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Alma Roeder | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A classic and savory mushroom quiche from Alma Roeder, featuring sautéed mushrooms, green onions, and Gruyère cheese baked in a rich, tarragon-spiced egg custard. Perfect for brunch or a light dinner, this vintage recipe is both elegant and comforting.*

## Instructions

### Preparation and Baking
1. Preheat oven to **425°F**. Prepare the **1 unbaked 10-inch pastry shell** in a **10-inch quiche dish**.
2. In a skillet, sauté the **1/2 lb sliced mushrooms** and **3 chopped green onions** in **3 Tbsp of the butter** just until glazed, tender, and fragrant.
3. In a large bowl, beat the **4 eggs** until light and frothy. Whisk in the **1 cup milk**, **1/2 tsp salt**, and **1/2 tsp crumbled dried tarragon**.
4. Stir the sautéed vegetables and the **1 cup shredded Gruyere or Swiss cheese** into the egg mixture.
5. Pour the filling into the prepared **pastry shell**.
6. Dot the top with the remaining **1 Tbsp of butter** and sprinkle evenly with **3 Tbsp grated Parmesan cheese**.
7. Bake at **425°F** for **10 minutes**.
8. Reduce the oven heat to **350°F** and bake for **30 minutes** longer, or until the center is set and a knife inserted near the center comes out clean.
9. Let the quiche cool for **10 minutes** to allow the custard to set before cutting into wedges to serve.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This quiche can be baked ahead of time, cooled completely, and stored in the refrigerator for up to 3 days. Reheat in a 325°F oven for 15-20 minutes until warmed through.
