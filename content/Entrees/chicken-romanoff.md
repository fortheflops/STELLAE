# Chicken Romanoff

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A comforting vintage casserole featuring tender chicken baked with Romanoff noodles and a rich, creamy soup sauce. Perfect for feeding a crowd, this classic recipe yields enough to fill both a 9x13 pan and an 8x8 pan.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 1 hour | None | 12 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 9x13 baking pan
* 8x8 baking pan
* Mixing bowl

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3 pkgs | [[Romanoff Noodles]] | Prepared according to package directions |
| 1 cup | [[Cream of Celery Soup]] | Canned |
| 1 cup | [[Cream of Mushroom Soup]] | Canned |
| 1/3 can | [[Cooking Wine]] | Measured using empty soup can |
| 2/3 can | [[Milk]] | Measured using empty soup can |
| 2 lbs | [[Cooked Chicken]] | Boned and cooked |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Assembly and Baking
1. Follow package directions to prepare the **3 pkgs Romanoff noodles**.
2. In a large mixing bowl, combine the prepared noodles, **1 cup cream of celery soup**, **1 cup cream of mushroom soup**, **1/3 can cooking wine**, **2/3 can milk**, and **2 lbs cooked chicken**, mixing thoroughly until well blended.
3. Transfer the mixture into one 9x13 pan and one 8x8 pan.
4. Bake at 350°F for one hour until hot, bubbly, and lightly golden on top.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Freezes well. Assemble the casseroles in the pans, cover tightly, and freeze before baking.
