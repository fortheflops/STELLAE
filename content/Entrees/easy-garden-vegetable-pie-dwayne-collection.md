# Easy Garden Vegetable Pie

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Audrey | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A classic, savory crustless garden vegetable pie packed with broccoli, cauliflower, green pepper, and sharp cheddar cheese bound in a light Bisquick batter. Simple to prepare and baked until golden brown, this comforting dish makes a delightful main or hearty side.*

## Instructions

### Prep & Vegetables
1. Heat oven to 400°F. Lightly grease a 10 x 1 1/2-inch pie plate or round cake pan (a deep cake pan works best).
2. Bring 1 inch of salted water (using **1/2 teaspoon salt** to **1 cup water**) to a boil in a saucepan. Add the **2 cups chopped fresh broccoli or cauliflower florets** and cook until almost tender; drain thoroughly. (Note: If using frozen broccoli or cauliflower, do not cook first).

### Assembly & Baking
3. In the greased pan, mix the drained **2 cups chopped fresh broccoli or cauliflower florets**, **1/2 cup chopped onion**, **1/2 cup chopped green pepper**, and **1 cup shredded Cheddar cheese**.
4. In a blender, combine **1 1/2 cups milk**, **3/4 cup Bisquick baking mix**, **3 eggs**, **1 teaspoon salt**, and **1/4 teaspoon black pepper**. Blend on high for 15 seconds (or beat for 1 minute with a hand beater) until completely smooth.
5. Pour the batter evenly over the vegetable and cheese mixture in the pie plate.
6. Bake at 400°F for 35 to 40 minutes until golden brown and a knife inserted into the center comes out clean.

### Serving
7. Let stand 5 minutes before cutting. Garnish as desired with **tomato slices** and fresh **parsley**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Refrigerate any remaining pie covered for up to 3-4 days; reheat individual slices in the oven or microwave before serving.
