# Tuna Puffs

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A classic, comforting recipe for individual tuna puffs, perfect for a light lunch or dinner. These savory bites are baked in a water bath to ensure a delicate, custard-like texture.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 50 mins | 2 hours | 6-8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Individual baking cups
* Baking pan

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 4 | [[Eggs]] | Lightly beaten |
| 2 cups | [[Bread crumbs]] | Soft |
| 1 tsp | [[Salt]] | None |
| 1 tbsp | [[Prepared mustard]] | None |
| 1 tbsp | [[Onion]] | Minced |
| 2 cups | [[Milk]] | None |
| 1 can | [[Tuna]] | Chunk style |
| 1 cup | [[Mushroom soup]] | Condensed |
| 2 tbsp | [[Butter]] | Melted |

---

## Instructions

### Preparation
1. Early in the day, combine the **4 eggs**, **2 cups bread crumbs**, **1 tsp salt**, **1 tbsp prepared mustard**, **1 tbsp onion**, **2 cups milk**, **1 can tuna**, **1 cup mushroom soup**, and **2 tbsp butter** in a large bowl.
2. Pour the mixture into 6 or 8 individual cups and chill in the refrigerator.

### Baking
3. 50 minutes before serving, preheat the oven.
4. Set the cups in a pan of warm water.
5. Bake until the puffs are set and firm to the touch.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The mixture can be prepared early in the day and chilled in the individual cups until ready to bake.
