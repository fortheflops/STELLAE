# Chicken Spaghetti

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Beree Meyers | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A classic comforting baked chicken spaghetti casserole featuring tender hen thighs, mushrooms, pimentos, and a rich cheese topping. Prepared by baking from a cold oven until golden brown and bubbling.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 1 hour | None | 6–8 Servings |

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Mix
1. Cook the **1 1/4 C. cooked spaghetti**, drain well, and set aside.
2. In a **3-quart casserole**, mix the **2 C. cooked hen (thighs)**, **1/2 C. chopped onion**, **1/4 C. diced pimentos**, **1/4 C. chopped green peppers**, and the prepared **1 1/4 C. cooked spaghetti**.

### Step 2: Combine and Top
3. Pour in the **1 can mushroom soup**, **1/2 C. chicken stock**, **1/2 t. salt**, pepper to taste, and **1/4 C. grated longhorn cheese** (reserving the remaining cheese). Mix thoroughly.
4. Top the mixture evenly with the remaining **1 1/4 C. grated longhorn cheese**.

### Step 3: Bake
5. Place the casserole in a cold oven, then set the temperature to **325-350°F** and bake for **1 hour** until bubbling and the cheese on top is melted and golden brown.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole up to 24 hours in advance, cover, and store in the refrigerator until ready to bake in a cold oven.
