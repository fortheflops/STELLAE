# Simple 2-Ingredient Baked Ham in Milk

> 📜 **Collection:** JohnBCollection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A surprisingly simple, old-fashioned method for creating a tender, moist baked ham. A thick-cut ham steak is simmered in milk in a covered casserole for 2 hours.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 2 hours | None | 4–6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Granite pan or casserole dish
* Cover for casserole

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 slice | [[Ham]] | thick-cut, 2 to 2 1/2 inches thick, rind removed |
| As needed | [[Milk]] | or a mixture of milk and water, to cover |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and Assembly
1. Preheat your oven to a moderate temperature of **350°F (175°C)**.
2. Remove the rind from the **1 slice ham** and wipe the surface with a damp cloth.
3. Place the prepared **1 slice ham** into a granite pan or casserole dish.
4. Pour in enough **milk** (or milk and **water**) to completely cover the **1 slice ham**.
5. Secure a tight-fitting cover on the pan or casserole.

### Step 2: Baking
6. Bake in the preheated oven for about **2 hours**, or until the **1 slice ham** is fork-tender and thoroughly cooked.
7. If the **1 slice ham** is not sufficiently browned at the end of the cooking time, remove the cover and let it bake for a few more minutes until golden brown and fragrant.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftover baked ham in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 to 4 days.
