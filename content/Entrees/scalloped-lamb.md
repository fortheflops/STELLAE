# Scalloped Lamb

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A hearty and comforting baked casserole featuring tender lamb and macaroni in a savory tomato sauce. This simple dish is topped with buttery crumbs for a perfect golden finish.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 30 mins | None | 4 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Baking dish

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 cups | [[Lamb]] | Cooked, cubed |
| 1 cup | [[Macaroni]] | Cooked |
| 1 1/2 cups | [[Tomato sauce]] | None |
| 1/2 cup | [[Bread crumbs]] | Buttered |

---

## Instructions

### Assembly and Baking
1. Arrange the **2 cups cooked lamb cubes** and **1 cup cooked macaroni** in alternating layers within a buttered baking dish.
2. Pour the **1 1/2 cups tomato sauce** evenly over the layers.
3. Cover the top with the **1/2 cup buttered bread crumbs**.
4. Bake until the dish is bubbling hot and the crumbs are golden brown.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the dish ahead of time and refrigerate; add the buttered crumbs just before baking to ensure they stay crisp.
