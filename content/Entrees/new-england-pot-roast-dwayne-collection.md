# New England Pot Roast

> *A classic New England pot roast featuring a rump roast slow-cooked with horseradish, potatoes, carrots, and onions. This comforting, hearty dish delivers tender beef and savory vegetables in a rich, simple broth.*

## Instructions

### Preparation and Cooking
1. Season the **2 lb rump roast** with **1/2 tsp salt** and **1/2 tsp black pepper**. In a large Dutch oven, brown all sides of the roast for **5 minutes** until a deep golden crust forms.
2. Spread the **1/2 cup horseradish** evenly on top of the browned roast, then add **1/2 cup water** to the pot.
3. Cover and cook on low heat for **90 minutes**.
4. Bring the liquid back to a boil, then add the **4 halved potatoes**, **4 carrots**, and **4 small onions**. Cover and cook for **1 hour** longer, or until the vegetables are fork-tender and the beef pulls apart easily.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This pot roast tastes even better the next day. Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days and reheat gently on the stovetop.
