# Glazed Canned Ham with Pineapple & Brown Sugar

> 📜 **Collection:** Jill Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A classic, easy method for dressing up a pre-cooked canned ham from the Jill Collection. The ham is trimmed, packed with brown sugar, and baked with pineapple slices and juice for a sweet, savory glaze.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 1 hour | None | 4-6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Baking pan
* Toothpicks
* Oven

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 can | [[Hormel Pre-baked Ham]] | Pre-cooked |
| To Cover | [[Brown Sugar]] | Packed heavily |
| As Needed | [[Pineapple Juice]] | For pouring over top |
| 1 can | [[Pineapple Slices]] | Drained |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation
1. Preheat oven to **350°F**.
2. Remove the **1 can [[Hormel Pre-baked Ham]]** from the can. Cut off any excess fat and remove the gelatin.

### Step 2: Glazing and Baking
3. Place the ham in a baking pan.
4. Pack the ham down heavily with **to cover [[Brown Sugar]]**, covering the entire surface.
5. Pour **as needed [[Pineapple Juice]]** over the top.
6. Arrange **1 can [[Pineapple Slices]]** on top of the ham, securing them with toothpicks.
7. Bake at **350°F** for approximately **1 hour**, or until heated through and the glaze is bubbly and golden brown.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftover glazed ham tightly sealed in the refrigerator for up to 4 days.
