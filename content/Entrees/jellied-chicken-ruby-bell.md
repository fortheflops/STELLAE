## Instructions

### Preparation
1. Heat **1 pint stock** to boiling point, pour over **2 tbsp gelatine** (soaked in **0.5 cup cold water**), and stir until dissolved.
2. When the mixture begins to jell, add the **2 cups chicken**, **1 tbsp green pepper**, **1 tbsp pimento**, **0.5 cup celery**, **1 tbsp onion pulp**, and **salt**.
3. Pour half of the meat mixture into a mold, place the **4 hard-cooked eggs** inside, then cover with the remaining meat mixture.
4. Chill until firm. Slice and serve on lettuce with the prepared dressing.

### Dressing
5. Whip the **0.5 cup whipping cream** until stiff peaks form, then fold in **1 cup mayonnaise**, **2 tsp horseradish**, and **1 tbsp vinegar**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The mold must be prepared at least 4 hours in advance to ensure it sets firmly in the refrigerator.
