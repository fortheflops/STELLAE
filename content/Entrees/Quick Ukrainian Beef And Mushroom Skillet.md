# Quick Ukrainian Beef And Mushroom Skillet

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A fast and flavorful way to use leftover beef. This one-skillet dish features diced beef and mushrooms simmered in a savory tomato, paprika, and sour cream sauce.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Build the Base
1. Melt **¼ cup table fat** in a skillet, fry **1 large onion** and **sliver garlic** lightly until translucent and fragrant; add **1 cup tomatoes**.

### Step 2: Simmer the Sauce
2. Simmer for **10 minutes** until the tomatoes break down and thicken.
3. Add **2 cups diced leftover beef**, **1 cup beef stock**, and **1 teaspoon paprika** and let simmer.

### Step 3: Saute Mushrooms
4. In a separate pan, saute **½ lb mushrooms** until golden brown, then add them to the meat mixture.

### Step 4: Thicken and Finish
5. Make a paste of **1 tablespoon flour** and **1 tablespoon water**, then add to the mixture, stirring constantly until smooth.
6. Add **½ teaspoon salt**, **dash pepper**, and **¼ cup thick sour cream**.
7. Heat through gently until hot, but do not boil to prevent curdling.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftover beef skillet in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days. Reheat gently over low heat without boiling.
