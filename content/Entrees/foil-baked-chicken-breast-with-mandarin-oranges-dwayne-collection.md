# Foil Baked Chicken Breast with Mandarin Oranges

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A delightful and simple foil-packet recipe featuring chicken breast baked with mandarin oranges, sliced peppers, onions, and mushrooms. Infused with a touch of lemon juice for a bright and savory flavor profile.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 30 mins | None | 1 serving |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Aluminum foil
* Baking sheet

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | [[Chicken Breast]] | whole |
| As needed | [[Mushrooms]] | sliced |
| As needed | [[Sliced Pepper]] | sliced |
| As needed | [[Mandarin Oranges]] | drained |
| As needed | [[Onion]] | sliced |
| 1 tbsp | [[Lemon Juice]] | fresh |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Assembly and Baking
1. Place the **1 chicken breast** on a sheet of aluminum foil.
2. Squeeze **1 tbsp lemon juice** over the top of the **1 chicken breast**.
3. Top the **1 chicken breast** with **mandarin oranges**, **sliced pepper**, **onion**, and **mushrooms**.
4. Fold the foil over the top in a double fold to seal tightly.
5. Bake at 350°F for **30 mins** until the **1 chicken breast** is cooked through and tender.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the foil packet ahead of time and store in the refrigerator for up to a few hours before baking.
