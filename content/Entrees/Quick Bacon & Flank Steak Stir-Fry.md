# Quick Bacon & Flank Steak Stir-Fry

> 📜 **Collection:** LouiseCollection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A unique and speedy stir-fry featuring tender marinated flank steak strips, flash-fried with bacon and tossed with crisp lettuce, green onions, and lemon slices.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 40 mins | 15 mins | 30 mins | 4 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large skillet
* Mixing bowl
* Cutting board
* Chef's knife

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 1/2 lbs | [[Flank Steak]] | Thinly sliced across the grain |
| 4 tsp | [[Cornstarch]] | None |
| 4 tsp | [[Vinegar]] | None |
| 1 Tbsp | [[Sugar]] | None |
| 1/2 tsp | [[Salt]] | None |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Pepper]] | Freshly ground |
| 4 slices | [[Bacon]] | Cut into thin strips |
| 1 large head | [[Iceberg Lettuce]] | Or Romaine Lettuce, cut into 1/2-inch thick slices |
| 2 | [[Green Onions]] | With green tops, thinly sliced |
| 8 very thin slices | [[Lemon]] | Fresh, with peel, cut into quarters |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Marinate
1. Slice the **1 1/2 lbs flank steak** thinly across the grain into strips no thicker than 1/8 inch (it slices best when partially frozen).
2. In a bowl, toss the steak strips with **4 tsp cornstarch**, **4 tsp vinegar**, **1 Tbsp sugar**, **1/2 tsp salt**, and **1/4 tsp freshly ground pepper**. Allow to stand for **30 minutes**.

### Step 2: Cook Bacon and Beef
3. In a large skillet, slowly cook **4 slices bacon** until almost crisp. Remove bacon from the pan and set aside.
4. Remove any bacon fat from the skillet in excess of **4 tablespoons**. Heat the remaining bacon fat in the skillet until very hot.
5. Add the marinated beef strips to the very hot skillet and cook over very high heat for just a few minutes, tossing lightly, just until browned and sizzling. Remove the meat from the pan and set aside.

### Step 3: Toss Vegetables and Finish
6. Cut the **1 large head iceberg lettuce** (or romaine) across the head into 1/2-inch thick slices, thinly slice the **2 green onions**, and cut the **8 very thin slices fresh lemon** into quarters.
7. Add the lettuce, onions, and lemon quarters to the pan. Cook, tossing frequently, for a minute or two, until barely wilted and tender-crisp.
8. Return the cooked beef and bacon to the skillet and toss just until heated through.
9. Garnish with additional sliced green onions if desired and serve immediately.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Best served immediately upon preparation; ingredients can be prepped and portioned ahead of time and stored separately in the refrigerator.
