# Susan's Meatloaf

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Susan | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A classic vintage meatloaf recipe featuring a savory blend of ground beef, green peppers, onions, and a rich tomato-bacon topping. Perfectly seasoned and baked until tender and juicy.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 1 hour | 5 mins | 4-6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large bowl
* Loaf pan or baking dish
* Measuring cups and spoons

## Ingredients

### Meatloaf
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 lb | [[Ground Beef]] | Regular or lean |
| 1/4 | [[Green Pepper]] | Finely chopped |
| 1/2 | [[Onion]] | Finely chopped |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Cracker Crumbs]] | Crushed |
| 1 | [[Egg]] | Lightly beaten |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Catsup]] | Also known as ketchup |
| 1/4 Cup | [[Worcestershire Sauce]] | Divided use |

### Sauce
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| As needed | [[Worcestershire Sauce]] | For topping |
| As needed | [[Bacon Slices]] | To cover top |
| 1 can | [[Tomato Sauce]] | 8 oz size |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and Mixing
1. Preheat oven to **350°F** (**175°C**). Lightly grease a loaf pan or baking dish.
2. In a large bowl, combine the **1 lb ground beef**, **1/4 green pepper**, **1/2 onion**, **1/2 cup cracker crumbs**, **1 egg**, **1/2 cup catsup**, and **1/4 cup Worcestershire sauce**.
3. Mix these ingredients together gently with your hands until just combined, being careful not to overmix.

### Step 2: Shaping and Baking
4. Shape the **ground beef** mixture into a loaf and place it in the prepared pan.
5. Top with the **1 can tomato sauce**, additional **Worcestershire sauce**, and **bacon slices**.
6. Bake for **1 hour**, or until cooked through and the internal temperature reaches **160°F** (**71°C**).
7. Let rest for a few minutes until firm before slicing and serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Unbaked meatloaf can be assembled, covered, and stored in the refrigerator for up to 24 hours before baking, or frozen for up to 3 months.
