## Instructions

### Step 1: Poach the Chicken
1. Preheat oven to **350°F**. Well-grease a casserole dish.
2. In a large pot, combine **2 (3-4 lb) chickens**, **1 cup water**, **1 cup dry sherry**, **1/2 teaspoon salt**, **1 tablespoon curry powder**, **1 medium onion**, and **1/2 cup celery**. Cover tightly and cook until the chicken is tender and cooked through.
3. Remove the **chicken** from the pot and set aside to cool. Save the broth.
4. Once cool, cut the **chicken** into bite-size pieces.

### Step 2: Cook the Rice and Mushrooms
5. Use the reserved chicken broth to cook **1 package wild rice** according to the package directions. When done, stir in **1/2 cup butter** (1/4 pound) until melted and rich.
6. While the rice cooks, slash **1 cup fresh mushrooms** and sauté them in the remaining **1/4 cup butter** until tender and lightly browned.

### Step 3: Assemble and Bake
7. In a small bowl, mix **1 cup sour cream** with **1 can cream of mushroom soup** until smooth.
8. In a large bowl, toss the bite-size **chicken**, the cooked **wild rice**, the sautéed **mushrooms**, and the **soup/sour cream mixture** until thoroughly combined.
9. Pour the mixture into the well-greased casserole dish.
10. Bake at **350°F** for **1 hour** until bubbling and golden brown on top.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This dish freezes exceptionally well. Assemble the casserole fully, cover tightly, and freeze; bake from thawed, adding 10-15 minutes to the baking time if cold.
