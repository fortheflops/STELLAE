# Chicken Divan

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Five | ✍️ **Attribution:** Barbara Spurr | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A classic homestyle casserole featuring tender chicken and broccoli baked in a rich mushroom and curry sauce. Topped with melted cheese and buttery bread crumbs for a comforting, flavorful finish.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Assembly
1. Cook **2 pkgs frozen broccoli** for a few minutes (5 minutes).
2. Butter the bottom of a casserole dish.
3. Lay the cooked **2 pkgs frozen broccoli** on the bottom.
4. Lay **2 cups cooked chicken** over the broccoli.

### Step 2: Sauce and Baking
5. Mix **1 can cream of mushroom soup**, **1 cup (sparse) mayo**, **1 tbsp lemon juice**, and **1 tsp curry powder** together.
6. Pour the mixture over the chicken and broccoli.
7. Sprinkle **1 cup shredded cheese** over the soup layer.
8. Top with **buttered bread crumbs**.
9. Bake at 350° for **30 minutes** until bubbly and golden brown on top.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole up to a day in advance, cover, and refrigerate until ready to bake; add a few extra minutes to the baking time if chilled.
