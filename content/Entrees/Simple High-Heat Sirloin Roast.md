# Simple High-Heat Sirloin Roast

> 📜 **Collection:** JillCollection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A straightforward and effective method for a sirloin tip roast. This recipe uses a high-heat 500°F oven sear in a preheated Pyrex dish with chicken fat, followed by a long, slow roast with onions.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 2 hours 30 mins | None | 6-8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Pyrex baking dish
* Oven

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3 pounds or less | [[Sirloin Tip Roast]] | center cut |
| A bit | [[Chicken Fat]] | trimmings |
| 3 | [[Onions]] | whole or quartered |
| To taste | [[Salt]] | none |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: High-Heat Sear
1. Preheat the oven to **500°F** with a Pyrex baking dish inside to heat up.
2. Place a bit of **chicken fat** trimmings in the bottom of the hot Pyrex dish and let it render in the oven.
3. Place the **3 pounds or less sirloin tip roast** in the hot pan and brown it well on each side until nicely seared in the **500°F** oven.
4. Season the roast with **salt**.

### Step 2: Slow Roast
5. Turn the oven temperature down to **350°F**.
6. About **2 hours** after the initial browning, add the **3 onions** to the pan.
7. Continue to roast until the desired doneness is reached and the meat is tender and juicy.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftover roasted beef wrapped tightly in the refrigerator for up to 3-4 days.
