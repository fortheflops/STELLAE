# Sweet and Sour Spareribs

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *Tender spareribs simmered in savory soy sauce, then glazed with a classic sweet and sour sauce. This traditional recipe delivers a perfect balance of tangy and sweet flavors.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Simmer the Ribs
1. In a large pan, combine **2 cups water**, **2 1/2 pounds spareribs**, **4 tablespoons soy sauce**, and **1 teaspoon salt**.

### Step 2: Reduce and Glaze
2. Bring the mixture to a boil, then turn the heat to low and simmer for **1 hour** until tender.
3. Add **1 teaspoon sugar** and **1 tablespoon sherry**.
4. Turn heat to high and continue cooking until most of the liquid evaporates.
5. Take out as much excess oil as possible from the pan.

### Step 3: Add Sauce
6. In a small bowl, blend the sauce ingredients: **3 tablespoons sugar**, **vinegar**, **cornstarch**, and **1/2 cup water**.
7. Pour the sauce slowly over the **spareribs**.
8. Cook for **2 minutes** until the sauce thickens to a glossy finish and is bubbling hot.
