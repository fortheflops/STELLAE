# Veal Steak with Ham & Mushrooms

> 📜 **Collection:** JohnBCollection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A flavorful stovetop veal dish featuring a tender large veal steak simmered with garlic spread. It is finished with a savory sauce of ham, fresh mushrooms, and bright lemon.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 50 mins | None | 6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large skillet
* Measuring cup
* Heated platter

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 T | [[Salad Oil]] | None |
| 1 (1 1/2 lb) | [[Veal Steak]] | Large |
| 1/2 t | [[Salt]] | None |
| 1/2 t | [[Pepper]] | None |
| 1 1/2 t | [[Garlic Spread]] | Bottled |
| 1 c | [[Ham]] | Cooked, cubed |
| 2 c | [[Mushrooms]] | Fresh, sliced |
| 1 T | [[Lemon Juice]] | None |
| 1 t | [[Lemon Peel]] | Grated |
| 2 T | [[Water]] | For simmering |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Season and Sauté
1. Sprinkle the **1 (1 1/2 lb) veal steak** with **1/2 t salt** and **1/2 t pepper**.
2. Heat the **1 T salad oil** in a large skillet and sauté the **1 (1 1/2 lb) veal steak** until browned.

### Step 2: Simmer the Veal
3. Spread the **1 1/2 t bottled garlic spread** over the top of the **1 (1 1/2 lb) veal steak**.
4. Add **2 T water** to the skillet, cover tightly, and simmer over low heat for **45 minutes** until the **1 (1 1/2 lb) veal steak** is fork-tender.

### Step 3: Prepare the Sauce and Finish
5. Uncover the **1 (1 1/2 lb) veal steak**, pour off the drippings into a measuring cup, and add enough water to make **2/3 c** of liquid; return this liquid to the skillet.
6. Add the **1 c cooked ham cubes** and **2 c fresh mushrooms** to the skillet, and cook for **5 minutes**, turning occasionally until heated through.
7. Remove the **1 (1 1/2 lb) veal steak** to a heated platter.
8. Add the **1 T lemon juice** to the mixture remaining in the skillet, spoon the sauce mixture over the **1 (1 1/2 lb) veal steak**, and sprinkle with the **1 t grated lemon peel**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftovers in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days. Reheat gently on the stovetop over low heat.
