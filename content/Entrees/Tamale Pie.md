## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Meat and Aromatics
1. Cook the **1 pound ground round meat** first in a skillet; drain any excess fat and set aside.
2. In a large pot or skillet, cook the **1 cup onion** in **2 tablespoons oil** until softened.

### Step 2: Simmer the Filling
3. Add the cooked meat, **1 large can whole kernel corn**, **1/2 green pepper**, **1 large can mashed up tomatoes**, **1 cup water**, **2 teaspoons salt**, and **1/8 teaspoon cayenne pepper** to the pot.
4. Bring the mixture to a boil.
5. Gradually stir in the **1 cup yellow cornmeal**, stirring constantly to prevent lumps.
6. Reduce heat to low, cover, and cook for **20 minutes**, stirring frequently until thickened and fragrant. Towards the end of this cooking time, stir in the **1 can ripe olives**.

### Step 3: Bake
7. Pour the mixture into a greased casserole dish.
8. Bake at 350°F for **45 minutes** until bubbling and golden brown.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole ahead of time and store covered in the refrigerator for up to 24 hours before baking.
