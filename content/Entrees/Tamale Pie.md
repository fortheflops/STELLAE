# Tamale Pie

> 📜 **Collection:** Patsy Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A rustic vintage Tamale Pie featuring a savory blend of tomatoes, olives, cornmeal, and creamed corn baked to golden perfection. This comforting casserole brings traditional flavors together in a unique, easy-to-bake dish.*

| 2 Tbsp | [[Butter]] | Unsalted |
| 1 small | [[Bay leaf]] | Dried |
| 1 tsp | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 1 dash | [[Salt]] | Plus extra a pinch |
| 1 Cup | [[Cornmeal]] | Yellow or white |
| 1 Can | [[Creamed Corn]] | Standard size |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Make the Olive and Tomato Filling
1. In a skillet, sauté **1 cup pitted olives**, **1 small bay leaf**, and **2 tbsp butter**. Cook for **20 minutes** until fragrant, then set aside to cool.

### Step 2: Prepare the Cornmeal Base
2. Mix **1 cup cornmeal** with cold water to make a damp paste.
3. Drop the cornmeal mixture by the spoonful into **1 cup** of boiling water.
4. Cook for a few minutes until thickened, then add **1 can creamed corn** to the cornmeal mixture and let cool.

### Step 3: Combine and Bake
5. Add **2 eggs** to the cooled corn mixture and stir well.
6. Add **1 dash salt** and **1 tsp sugar** along with the tomato-olive mixture, combining thoroughly.
7. Transfer the mixture to a baking dish and bake for **one hour** at **350°F** until set and golden brown.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the unbaked pie a day in advance and store covered in the refrigerator until ready to bake.
