# Skillet Meat Loaf

> 📜 **Collection:** UK25 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *Enjoy a comforting, quick-cooking skillet meat loaf topped with savory kidney beans and mushroom spaghetti sauce. This easy one-skillet meal is perfect for busy weeknights and serves six.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare and Shape the Meat Loaf
1. In a large bowl, combine the **1 1/2 pounds ground beef**, **1 tablespoon minced onion**, **1/2 cup quick-cooking oats**, **1/2 cup milk**, **1 beaten egg**, **2 teaspoons salt**, and **1/4 teaspoon pepper**, mixing thoroughly.
2. Press the meat mixture evenly into a **10-inch skillet** and score the top into neat wedges.

### Step 2: Make the Sauce and Cook
3. In a separate bowl, combine the **1 (8 ounce) can spaghetti sauce with mushrooms** and the **1 (15 ounce) can kidney beans** with their liquid.
4. Pour the sauce and bean mixture evenly over the meat wedges in the skillet.
5. Cook slowly, uncovered, for **30 minutes**, or until the meat is thoroughly cooked through, browned, and fragrant.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftover meatloaf sealed in the refrigerator for up to 3 to 4 days.
