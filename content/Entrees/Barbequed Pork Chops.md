# Barbequed Pork Chops

## Instructions

### Step 1: Initial Roast
1. Place **10 pork chops** in a baking pan.
2. Put a slice of **1 large onion** and **1 lemon** on each chop.
3. Roast for **30 minutes** at **450°F** until lightly browned.

### Step 2: Sauce and Braise
4. In a mixing bowl, combine **1 cup catsup**, **1 cup water**, **3 T. Worcestershire Sauce**, **1 t. chili powder**, **1 t. salt**, and **2 dashes Tabasco**.
5. Pour the sauce mixture over the chops.
6. Reduce heat to **350°F**.
7. Bake for **1 hour more**, basting occasionally, until the pork is tender and the sauce is thick and fragrant.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftover pork chops in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days.
