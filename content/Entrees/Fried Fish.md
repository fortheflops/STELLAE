# Fried Fish

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *Enjoy a classic, crispy fried fish fillet coated in seasoned flour and pan-fried to golden perfection. This quick and simple recipe delivers a delightful crunch and tender, flaky seafood in minutes.*

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Deep skillet
* Tongs or spatula

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| As Needed | [[Fish Fillets]] | rinsed and patted dry |
| As Needed | [[Flour]] | seasoned with salt and pepper |
| 1 inch deep | [[Olive Oil]] | or other frying oil |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Coat and Fry
1. Dip the **as needed fish fillets** in **as needed flour**, coating them completely and shaking off any excess.
2. Heat about **1 inch deep** of **olive oil** in a deep skillet or pan over medium-high heat until hot and shimmering.
3. Carefully place the floured **as needed fish fillets** into the hot **olive oil**.
4. Fry until golden brown and crispy on both sides and cooked through, yielding a flaky texture.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Best enjoyed immediately while hot and crispy. Leftovers can be stored in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 2 days.
