# Party Chicken

> 📜 **Collection:** Patsy Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** GA | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A classic retro casserole featuring chicken breasts wrapped in bacon, layered over dried chipped beef, and slow-baked in a velvety mushroom and sour cream sauce. Serve over a bed of rice to enjoy every drop of the rich, savory gravy.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 2 hours 30 mins | None | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 9x11-inch baking dish
* Mixing bowl
* Spoon

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 4 | [[Chicken Breasts]] | halved |
| 8 strips | [[Bacon]] |  |
| 1 (4 oz) pkg | [[Chipped Beef]] | dried |
| 1 (10.5 oz) can | [[Cream of Mushroom Soup]] |  |
| 1 (8 oz) carton | [[Sour Cream]] |  |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Assembly
1. Wrap **2 strips bacon** securely around each of the **4 chicken breasts**.
2. Scatter the **1 (4 oz) pkg dried chipped beef** evenly across the bottom of a 2 1/2-quart or 9x11-inch baking dish.
3. Arrange the bacon-wrapped **chicken breasts** in a single layer on top of the **chipped beef**.
4. In a bowl, mix together the **1 (10.5 oz) can cream of mushroom soup** and **1 (8 oz) carton sour cream** until smooth, then spread the mixture over the **chicken breasts**.

### Step 2: Baking & Serving
5. Bake uncovered at 275°F for 2 1/2 to 3 hours until the chicken is tender and thoroughly cooked.
6. Serve hot with prepared Rice-A-Roni or rice, spooning the pan sauce generously over top.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the chicken and sauce in the baking dish, cover tightly, and refrigerate overnight before baking.
