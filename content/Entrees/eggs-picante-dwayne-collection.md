# Eggs Picante

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A comforting and flavorful dish featuring hard-boiled eggs and tender potatoes bathed in a creamy, mildly spicy cheese sauce. Perfect for a hearty brunch or a unique family dinner.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 20 mins | None | 4 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Saucepan
* Strainer
* Mixing bowl
* Knife
* Cutting board

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 slices | [[White Bread]] | cut into small pieces |
| 1 cup | [[Milk]] | boiled |
| 2 level soup spoons | [[Yellow Pepper Powder]] | yellow picante |
| 1/2 cup | [[Vegetable Oil]] |  |
| 2 | [[Onions]] | chopped small |
| To taste | [[Salt]] |  |
| 1/2 lb. | [[Muenster Cheese]] |  |
| 6 | [[Eggs]] | hard-boiled and chopped |
| 3 | [[Potatoes]] | boiled and chopped (optional) |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Soak
1. In a small bowl, put the **2 slices white bread** to soak in the **1 cup boiled milk**.
2. Mix the **2 level soup spoons yellow pepper powder** in a little water to form a smooth paste.

### Step 2: Cook the Base
3. Heat the **1/2 cup vegetable oil** in a saucepan over medium heat. Fry the pepper paste, then add the **2 onions** and **salt** to taste.
4. Pass the soaked **2 slices white bread** and **1 cup boiled milk** mixture through a strainer to ensure a smooth texture, then add it to the saucepan.
5. Cook for a few minutes, stirring constantly, then add the **1/2 lb. Muenster cheese**.

### Step 3: Combine and Serve
6. When the **1/2 lb. Muenster cheese** begins to melt and the sauce is creamy, gently fold in the **6 eggs** (hard-boiled and chopped) and **3 potatoes** (boiled and chopped). Note: the **3 potatoes** may be omitted if desired.
7. Heat through gently until hot and bubbly, then serve immediately.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The spicy cheese sauce base can be prepared a day in advance and stored in the refrigerator. Reheat gently on the stovetop, adding a splash of milk if too thick, before folding in the freshly boiled eggs and potatoes.
