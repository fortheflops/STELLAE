# Deviled Hamburgers

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Antoinette Pope School | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *Flavorful deviled hamburgers spiced with chili sauce, prepared mustard, horseradish, and Worcestershire sauce. Cooked to juicy perfection and served on toasted buttered hamburger buns.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 10 mins | None | 4 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Griddle or frying pan
* Broiler

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 pound | [[Ground Round]] | Lean |
| 1/3 cup | [[Chili Sauce]] | None |
| 2 tsps | [[Prepared Mustard]] | None |
| 1 tsp | [[Horse Radish]] | None |
| 1 | [[Garlic Clove]] | Very small, minced |
| 1 tsp | [[Worcestershire Sauce]] | None |
| 1 tsp | [[Salt]] | None |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Pepper]] | None |
| 2 tsps | [[Grated Onion]] | None |
| 4 | [[Hamburger Buns]] | None |
| As needed | [[Butter]] | For frying and toasting |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing and Shaping
1. Combine **1 pound ground round**, **1/3 cup chili sauce**, **2 tsps prepared mustard**, **1 tsp horse radish**, **1 garlic clove**, **1 tsp Worcestershire sauce**, **1 tsp salt**, **1/4 tsp pepper**, and **2 tsps grated onion** in a bowl. Blend well.
2. Divide the mixture into **4** portions and flatten into **5-inch patties**.

### Step 2: Cooking and Serving
3. Fry patties on a hot griddle or in a hot frying pan over medium flame in a small amount of **butter**.
4. Cook **2 minutes** on each side for medium, or **3 minutes** each side for well done. Do not turn patties over until browned on one side.
5. Brush **4 hamburger buns** with **butter** prior to toasting, and toast in the broiler until golden.
6. Serve hamburgers on the toasted buns at once.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Patties can be shaped and refrigerated for a few hours before cooking; bring slightly closer to room temperature before frying.
