# Crock Pot Chile Verde

> 📜 **Collection:** Al Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Julie Cook | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A comforting slow-cooker beef chile verde featuring tender cubed beef simmered with green chiles, tomatoes, and warm spices. Perfect for serving over rice, warm tortillas, or macaroni.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Slow Cooking
1. In a **crock pot**, combine the **1 1/2 lbs cubed lean beef**, **1 can tomatoes**, **1/2 cup beef consommé**, **2 tbsp lemon juice**, **1 clove crushed garlic**, **1/4 tsp ground cloves**, **1 pint bag of frozen green chiles**, and **1/3 cup parsley**.
2. Cover and cook on low for **6 to 8 hours** (or on high for **4 hours**) until the beef is fork-tender and the flavors are fully melded.

### Step 2: Serving
3. Serve hot over warm **rice**, **tortillas**, or **macaroni**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Can be assembled in the slow cooker insert the night before, covered, and stored in the refrigerator. Leftovers freeze beautifully for up to 3 months.
