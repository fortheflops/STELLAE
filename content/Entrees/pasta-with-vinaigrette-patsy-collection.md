# Pasta with Vinaigrette

> 📜 **Collection:** Patsy Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A fresh and easy penne pasta dish tossed in a flavorful herb and lemon vinaigrette with coarsely chopped fresh tomatoes, garlic, shallots, and Parmesan cheese. Perfect for a quick, light weeknight entree or summer pasta salad.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 10 mins | 30 mins | 4 servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Whisk
* Mixing bowl
* Large stockpot
* Colander

## Ingredients

### Vinaigrette & Tomato Base
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 Tbsp | [[Herb Oil]] | rosemary or basil infused |
| 2 Tbsp | [[Olive Oil]] | None |
| 2 Tbsp | [[Lemon Juice]] | freshly squeezed |
| 1 Tbsp | [[Shallot]] | minced |
| 2 tsp | [[Garlic]] | minced |
| 4 medium | [[Tomatoes]] | cored and coarsely chopped |
| 2 Tbsp | [[Parsley]] | minced |
| 1/2 tsp | [[Salt]] | None |
| To taste | [[Black Pepper]] | freshly ground |

### Pasta & Finishing
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 cups | [[Penne Pasta]] | uncooked |
| 4 Tbsp | [[Parmesan Cheese]] | grated |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Make the Vinaigrette & Base
1. In a mixing bowl, whisk together **2 Tbsp herb oil**, **2 Tbsp olive oil**, and **2 Tbsp lemon juice** until thoroughly combined.
2. Add **1 Tbsp minced shallot**, **2 tsp minced garlic**, **4 medium cored and coarsely chopped tomatoes**, **2 Tbsp minced parsley**, **1/2 tsp salt**, and **black pepper**.
3. Set the tomato mixture aside at room temperature for **30 minutes** to allow the flavors to meld.

### Step 2: Cook Pasta & Toss
4. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil and cook **2 cups penne pasta** according to package directions until tender yet al dente.
5. Drain the cooked pasta and immediately add the room-temperature tomato vinaigrette sauce and **4 Tbsp Parmesan cheese**, tossing gently to combine.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The tomato vinaigrette base can be mixed and left to marinate at room temperature for up to 30 minutes before tossing with freshly boiled pasta.
