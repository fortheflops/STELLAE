# Fried Rice

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A classic homemade fried rice cooked with bacon drippings, tender veggies, cubed meat, sliced scrambled eggs, and mushrooms. Simmered slowly to allow all savory flavors to meld into a comforting main dish.*

## Instructions

### Preparation & Cooking
1. In a large skillet over medium heat, fry **2 to 3 slices of bacon** until crisp. Remove bacon and chop into pieces, leaving bacon drippings in skillet.
2. Sauté chopped **3 green onions**, **3 pieces celery**, and **1 medium green pepper** in the bacon drippings along with the **1 cup cubed meat** until vegetables are tender-crisp.
3. Remove the sautéed vegetables and meat from the skillet. Add **4 cups cooked rice** to the remaining drippings in the skillet and cook until lightly browned.
4. After rice has browned, return the cooked **3 pieces celery**, **3 green onions**, **1 medium green pepper**, and **1 cup cubed meat** to the pan along with **1 can mushrooms** and **2 eggs** (scrambled and cut into strips). Add **to taste soy sauce**.
5. Cook slowly on low heat for **45 minutes to 1 hour** until heated through and flavors are well combined.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftovers in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days. Reheat in a skillet over low heat with a splash of soy sauce or water.
