# Artichokes and Chicken a la Cream

> 📜 **Collection:** GailCollection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Gail Babineau | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A rich and elegant vintage entree featuring tender chicken breasts and fresh artichokes simmered in a creamy white wine sauce seasoned with curry and lemon. Perfect for serving over rice with a crisp Riesling.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 25 mins | 55 mins | None | 4 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large skillet or Dutch oven
* Knife
* Cutting board

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3 Tablespoons | [[Butter]] | divided |
| 1 1/2 cups | [[Onions]] | thinly sliced |
| 2 large | [[Chicken Breasts]] | cut in half |
| 3/4 teaspoon | [[Salt]] | none |
| 1/4 teaspoon | [[White Pepper]] | none |
| 1/4 teaspoon | [[Curry Powder]] | none |
| 4 medium | [[Artichokes]] | fresh or frozen |
| 3 Tablespoons | [[Lemon Juice]] | divided |
| 3/4 cup | [[White Table Wine]] | none |
| 3 cups | [[Cream]] | or cream of mushroom soup, divided |
| As needed | [[Fresh Parsley]] | for garnish |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Sauté Aromatics and Chicken
1. Melt **3 tablespoons butter** in a large skillet or Dutch oven over medium-low heat.
2. Add **1 1/2 cups thinly sliced onions**, stir, cover, and cook over low heat for **5 minutes**, or until tender.

### Step 2: Brown the Chicken and Season
3. Add the **2 large chicken breasts** to the skillet and turn to coat them with butter.
4. Cover and cook over low heat for **10 minutes**.
5. Add **3/4 teaspoon salt**, **1/4 teaspoon white pepper**, and **1/4 teaspoon curry powder**. Stir to combine.

### Step 3: Prepare and Add Artichokes
6. Meanwhile, wash the **4 medium artichokes**, cut off stems, and remove the bottom leaves. Quarter them and remove the chokes.
7. Rub all cut edges with **2 tablespoons lemon juice** to prevent browning.
8. Add the prepared artichokes to the chicken in the skillet.

### Step 4: Simmer with Wine and Cream
9. Pour in **3/4 cup white table wine**. Bring to a boil and cook rapidly until about two-thirds of the liquid has evaporated.
10. Pour in **2 3/4 cups hot cream** (or cream of mushroom soup), ensuring the cream was brought to a boil separately beforehand.
11. Cover and simmer gently for **35 to 45 minutes**, or until the chicken and artichokes are tender.

### Step 5: Finish the Sauce and Serve
12. Transfer the chicken and artichokes to a warm platter and cover to keep warm.
13. Remove the skillet from the heat and stir in the remaining **1 tablespoon lemon juice**.
14. Gradually beat the remaining **1/4 cup cream** into the skillet to enrich the sauce.
15. Pour the finished sauce over the chicken and artichokes, garnish with **fresh parsley**, and serve.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftovers in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days. Reheat gently on the stovetop over low heat.
