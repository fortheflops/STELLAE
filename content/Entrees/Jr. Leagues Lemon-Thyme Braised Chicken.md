# Jr. League's Lemon-Thyme Braised Chicken

> 📜 **Collection:** Junior League | ✍️ **Attribution:** Jr. League | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A simple and zesty one-pot chicken dish featuring cut-up chicken browned and simmered in a savory lemon-butter-thyme sauce. Delicious served hot over fluffy rice.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 45 mins | None | 4 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Deep heavy pot
* Small bowl

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |

| 1/4 t | [[Thyme]] | None |
| 1/4 t | [[Pepper]] | None |
| 1/4 C | [[Lemon Juice]] | None |
| 1/4 C | [[Butter]] | None |
| 1 T | [[Onion]] | Chopped |
| 1/2 Clove | [[Garlic]] | Minced |
| As needed | [[Rice]] | Hot cooked, for serving |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Season
1. Cut the **1 [[Fryer]]** into pieces, and season evenly with **1/4 t [[Salt]]** and **1/4 t [[Pepper]]**.

### Step 2: Brown the Chicken
2. In a deep, heavy pot, melt the **1/4 C [[Butter]]**. Brown the chicken pieces in the butter until golden brown on all sides.

### Step 3: Mix Sauce and Simmer
3. In a small bowl, combine the **2 T [[Salad Oil]]**, **1/4 t [[Thyme]]**, **1/4 C [[Lemon Juice]]**, **1 T chopped [[Onion]]**, and **1/2 clove minced [[Garlic]]**.
4. Pour the lemon juice mixture over the chicken in the pot.
5. Cover and cook slowly over low heat until the chicken is tender and cooked through, about 30 to 40 minutes.

### Step 4: Serve
6. Serve warm over hot cooked **[[Rice]]**.

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Leftovers can be stored in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days.
