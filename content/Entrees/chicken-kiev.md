# Chicken Kiev

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A classic preparation of tender chicken breasts stuffed with a savory herb butter mixture. These breaded cutlets are fried until golden brown and crisp.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 40 mins | 15 mins | 30 mins | 4 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Frying pan
* Meat mallet

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 4 | [[Chicken Breasts]] | medium, boneless and skinless |
| to taste | [[Salt]] | none |
| 1/2 cup | [[Green Onion]] | chopped, or shallots |
| 1/2 cup | [[Parsley]] | chopped |
| 1/4 lb | [[Butter]] | or margarine, softened |
| as needed | [[Flour]] | for dredging |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] | beaten |
| 1 cup | [[Bread Crumbs]] | none |

---

## Instructions

### Preparation
1. Pound the **4 chicken breasts** until thin. Mix the **1/4 lb butter**, **1/2 cup green onion**, and **1/2 cup parsley** together, then place a portion in the center of each breast.
2. Roll the chicken tightly to enclose the butter, securing with toothpicks if necessary.
3. Dredge each roll in **flour**, dip into the **2 eggs**, and coat thoroughly with **1 cup bread crumbs**.
4. Fry in hot oil until the coating is golden brown and the chicken is cooked through, ensuring the butter is melted inside.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The stuffed chicken rolls can be prepared and refrigerated for up to 2 hours before breading and frying to help them hold their shape.
