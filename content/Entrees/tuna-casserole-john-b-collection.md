# Tuna Casserole

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A classic baked tuna casserole featuring a rich white sauce, hard-cooked eggs, peas, and a crunchy potato chip topping. Perfect for a cozy weeknight family dinner.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 30 mins | None | 6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Casserole dish
* Mixing bowl

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1-6 1/2 oz. | [[Tuna]] | Grated |
| 2 cups | [[White Sauce]] | Medium |
| 1 cup | [[Peas]] | Cooked |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] | Hard-cooked and diced |
| 1 tsp. | [[Celery Salt]] | None |
| 1/2 tsp. | [[Salt]] | None |
| 1/2 cup | [[American Cheese]] | Grated |
| 1/2 cup | [[Potato Chips]] | Crushed |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Assembly
1. Combine **1-6 1/2 oz. tuna** with **2 cups white sauce**.
2. Mix in **1 cup peas**, **2 diced eggs**, **1 tsp. celery salt**, and **1/2 tsp. salt**.
3. Pour the mixture into a buttered casserole dish.
4. Top with **1/2 cup grated American cheese** and **1/2 cup crushed potato chips**.

### Step 2: Baking
5. Bake in a moderate oven at 350°F for about 30 minutes, or until bubbly and golden brown on top.
6. Serve hot.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole ahead of time, cover, and refrigerate for up to a few hours before adding the potato chip topping and baking.
