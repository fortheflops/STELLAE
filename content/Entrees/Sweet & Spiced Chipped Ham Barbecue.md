# Sweet & Spiced 'Chipped' Ham Barbecue

> 📜 **Collection:** LouiseCollection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A vintage recipe for chipped ham in a sweet and spiced tomato-based barbecue sauce. The ham is simmered and then slow-baked to allow the flavors to meld.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 50 mins | None | 6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large saucepan
* Stove
* Deep baking dish
* Oven

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 Cup | [[Ketchup]] | Base for the sauce |
| 1/3 Cup | [[Brown Sugar]] | Packed |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Water]] | None |
| 2 Tablespoons | [[Vinegar]] | None |
| 1/2 Tablespoon | [[Mustard]] | Dry |
| 1/2 Tablespoon | [[Nutmeg]] | Ground |
| 1 1/2 Pounds | [[Ham]] | Chipped, boiled |
| As Needed | [[Hamburger Buns]] | For serving |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare and Mix
1. Preheat the oven to **250°F (120°C)**.
2. In a large saucepan, whisk together the **1 cup [[Ketchup]]**, **1/3 cup [[Brown Sugar]]**, **1/2 cup [[Water]]**, **2 tablespoons [[Vinegar]]**, **1/2 tablespoon dry [[Mustard]]**, and **1/2 tablespoon [[Nutmeg]]** until well combined.

### Step 2: Simmer and Bake
3. Stir the **1 1/2 pounds chipped, boiled [[Ham]]** into the sauce mixture.
4. Bring the mixture to a boil on the stovetop.
5. Pour the mixture into a deep baking dish.
6. Bake uncovered for **45 minutes** until hot, bubbling, and aromatic.
7. Serve warm on **as needed [[Hamburger Buns]]**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftover ham barbecue in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3-4 days, and reheat gently before serving.
