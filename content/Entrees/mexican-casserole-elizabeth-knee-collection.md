# Mexican Casserole

> 📜 **Collection:** Elizabeth Knee Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A simple and comforting Mexican Casserole layered with corn tortillas, seasoned ground beef, longhorn cheese, and a creamy green chili sauce. This quick-prep vintage recipe is perfect for an easy weeknight family dinner.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 30 mins | None | 3-4 servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Baking dish
* Skillet
* Saucepan
* Cheese grater

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 pack | [[Corn Tortillas]] | None |
| 1 cup | [[Green Chili]] | canned, divided to taste |
| 1 lb. | [[Ground Beef]] | referred to as hamburger |
| 1/2 package | [[Longhorn Cheese]] | grated |
| 1 small | [[Onion]] | chopped |
| 1 can | [[Cream of Chicken Soup]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Sauce
1. Chop the **1 small onion** and grate the **1/2 package longhorn cheese**.
2. In a skillet, brown the **1 lb. ground beef** until fully cooked and no longer pink, then drain excess fat.
3. In a saucepan, mix the **1 can cream of chicken soup** with **1/2 cup to 1 cup green chili** (use half or the whole can depending on how hot you want it) and heat until warm and well combined.

### Step 2: Assembly and Baking
4. Spread a little of the warm **cream of chicken soup** and **green chili** mixture in the bottom of a baking dish.
5. Add one **corn tortilla**, then sprinkle with the grated **longhorn cheese**, chopped **onion**, and browned **ground beef**.
6. Drizzle a little more of the **cream of chicken soup** and **green chili** mixture over the layer.
7. Continue layering with the remaining **corn tortillas**, **longhorn cheese**, **onion**, **ground beef**, and **cream of chicken soup** mixture until the sauce runs out or the dish is filled.
8. Bake at 350°F (175°C) for 20 to 25 minutes until the cheese is melted, bubbly, and slightly golden brown on top.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Can be assembled ahead of time, covered, and refrigerated for up to 24 hours before baking. Add 5-10 minutes to the baking time if cooking straight from the fridge.
