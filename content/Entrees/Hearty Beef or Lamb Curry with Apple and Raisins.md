## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Combine Ingredients
1. Dice the **2 tart apples** and the **1 large onion**.
2. Place **2 pounds lean beef or lamb, cut in 1" cubes** in a large skillet or stew kettle.
3. Add all other ingredients: **1 cup water**, **2 bouillon cubes**, **1 can tomato paste**, **1 1/2 teaspoons ginger**, **1 diced clove garlic**, **1 teaspoon salt**, **1/2 teaspoon pepper**, **1 teaspoon Worcestershire sauce**, **1 1/2 to 2 teaspoons curry powder**, **1/2 cup grated coconut**, **1/2 cup seedless raisins**, the diced **tart apples**, the diced **large onion**, and **1 1/2 teaspoons coriander** (optional).

### Step 2: Simmer and Serve
4. Cover the skillet or kettle with a lid.
5. When the mixture comes to a steaming point, reduce heat to a gentle simmer.
6. Stir occasionally, and if more liquid is required, add either **water** or **tomato juice** to ensure the mixture remains a thick sauce rather than a thin liquid.
7. Cook for approximately **2 hours**, or until the **beef or lamb** is tender.
8. Serve hot over **hot rice**.
