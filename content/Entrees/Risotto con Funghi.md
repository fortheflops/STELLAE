# Risotto con Funghi

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Rick & Joy Gay | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A classic Italian comfort dish featuring creamy Arborio rice simmered with earthy mushrooms and savory beef stock. Finished with rich margarine and generous amounts of grated Parmesan cheese.*

---

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 30 mins | None | 4–6 Servings |

* Wooden spoon

* Measuring spoons

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |

| 1 Large | [[Onion]] | Finely Chopped |
| 1 1/2 Cups | [[Mushrooms]] | Sliced |
| 2 Cups | [[Rice]] | Uncooked Arborio or similar risotto rice recommended |
| 3 Cups | [[Beef Stock]] | Or 3-4 beef bouillon cubes + 3 cups water, hot |
| 1/2 Cube (4 Tablespoons) | [[Margarine]] | Melted, for finishing |
| Generous Amount | [[Parmesan Cheese]] | Grated, for finishing |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Sauté Aromatics and Mushrooms
1. Melt **8 tablespoons margarine** in a large pot or Dutch oven over medium heat.
2. Add **1 large onion** and cook until the **onion** becomes translucent and tender.
3. Add **1 1/2 cups mushrooms** and cook for **5 minutes** longer until slightly softened and fragrant.

### Step 2: Toast the Rice and Simmer
4. Add **2 cups rice** to the pot. Stir constantly, cooking until the **rice** takes on color and becomes slightly translucent or toasted.
5. Pour in **3 cups beef stock** (ensuring the stock is hot) and mix well.
6. Bring the mixture to a boil.
7. Cover the pot and simmer the **risotto** for **20 to 25 minutes**, or until the liquid is absorbed and the **rice** is al dente with a slight bite.

### Step 3: Finish and Serve
8. Just before serving, pour **4 tablespoons melted margarine** over the **risotto**.
9. Sprinkle generously with **grated parmesan cheese**, stir gently, and serve hot.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Risotto is best served immediately, but leftovers can be stored in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days and reheated with a splash of broth.
