# Mary Francis's All Day Stew

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Mary Francis | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A comforting all-day stew featuring tender beef, root vegetables, and a rich broth cooked low and slow. Perfect for a hearty family dinner with minimal active prep.*

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

## Instructions

### Preparation and Slow Cooking
1. Combine the **3/4 pkg little onions**, **3 lb stew meat**, **1 #2 can tomatoes**, **1 can peas**, **1 1/2 tsp salt**, **3 carrots**, **pepper**, **1/4 c white wine**, **1/2 c consomme**, and **3 T tapioca** in a roaster.
2. Cook in roaster at **250 degrees** all day until meat is fork-tender and broth is thickened.
3. Add the **potatoes** about 2 hours before you want to eat.
4. Sprinkle with **1/4 c bread crumbs** towards the end of cooking until golden and set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble all ingredients in the roaster vessel the night before, cover, and refrigerate until ready to slow-cook the next morning.
