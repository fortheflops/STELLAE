# Slow-Cooked Taco Casserole

> 📜 **Collection:** JillCollection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Georgia | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A layered taco casserole from Georgia, made in the slow cooker with ground sirloin, beans, corn, and tortillas, all baked in a savory enchilada sauce.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Brown Meat
1. Brush the inside of a **4-quart slow cooker** with oil or spray with nonstick cooking spray.
2. In a large skillet over medium-high heat, brown the **1 1/2 pounds ground sirloin** with the **1 small onion** and **1 clove garlic** for 8 to 10 minutes, or until thoroughly cooked and no longer pink. Drain off any excess fat.
3. Stir the **1 (1.25-oz) pkg taco seasoning mix**, **1 teaspoon salt**, and **1 1/2 teaspoons pepper** into the cooked beef mixture until well combined.

### Step 2: Assemble the Casserole
4. Place **3 tortillas** in the bottom of the slow cooker.
5. Top with the seasoned beef mixture, **1/2 cup chicken broth**, **1/2 cup tomato sauce**, and **1 (10-oz) can Old El Paso Enchilada sauce**. Sprinkle with **1/2 cup of cheddar cheese**.
6. Layer with **3 more tortillas**.
7. Top with the **2 (15-oz) cans pinto beans, black beans, or kidney beans**, **1 (11-oz) can mexicorn**, **1 (4.5-oz) can green chiles**, half of the **1 (2.25-oz) can black olives**, and another **1/2 cup of cheddar cheese**.
8. Top with the remaining **3 tortillas**. Sprinkle with the remaining **1/2 cup of cheddar cheese** and the rest of the **black olives**.

### Step 3: Cook and Serve
9. Cover and cook on high for **2.5 to 3 hours** or on low for **6 to 7 hours** until hot and bubbly.
10. Uncover the slow cooker for the last **30 minutes** of cooking time to help the sauce thicken to a rich consistency.
11. Serve hot, topped with **sour cream** if desired.
