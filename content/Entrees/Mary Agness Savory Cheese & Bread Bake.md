# Mary Agnes's Savory Cheese & Bread Bake

> 📜 **Collection:** General Archive | ✍️ **Attribution:** Mary Agnes | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A classic savory bread and cheese casserole, also known as a strata, featuring layers of buttered bread and grated cheese baked in a rich seasoned custard. This comforting dish makes a wonderful centerpiece for brunch or a hearty side.*

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |

| 4 | [[Eggs]] | Slightly beaten |
| 1/3 c. | [[Butter]] | Softened |
| 3 c. | [[Milk]] | None |
| 1 1/2 t. | [[Salt]] | None |
| 1/8 t. | [[Dry Mustard]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Layer
1. Preheat oven to **325°F** and grease a baking dish.
2. Spread the **8 slices white bread** with **1/3 c. butter**, then cut each slice into **4 pieces**.
3. Alternate layers of the prepared bread pieces and **2 c. grated cheese** in the baking dish, ensuring the top layer consists of cheese.

### Step 2: Mix and Bake
4. In a separate bowl, combine **3 c. milk**, **4 eggs**, **1 1/2 t. salt**, and **1/8 t. dry mustard**.
5. Pour the milk and egg mixture evenly over the layered cheese and bread.
6. Bake in the **325°F** oven for approximately **40 minutes**, until puffed, golden brown, and set in the center.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole a few hours in advance, cover, and refrigerate before baking, adding a few extra minutes to the bake time if needed.
