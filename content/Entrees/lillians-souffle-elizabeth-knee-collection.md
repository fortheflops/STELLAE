# Lillian's Souffle

> 📜 **Collection:** Elizabeth Knee Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Lillian | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A delightfully simple and healthy spinach and cheese souffle baked directly in a pie plate. Featuring cottage cheese, Swiss cheese, and spinach, it makes for a perfect light brunch or side dish.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 1 hour | 10 mins | 6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 10-inch pie plate
* Mixing bowl
* Measuring spoons

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 16 oz | [[Cottage Cheese]] | small curd, fat-free |
| 3 | [[Eggs]] | dropped in one at a time, unbeaten |
| 3 tbsp | [[Flour]] | mixed in |
| 10 oz | [[Spinach]] | thawed, chopped, and squeezed dry |
| 3 tbsp | [[Olive Oil]] |  |
| 6 slices (about 1 cup) | [[Swiss Cheese]] | fat-free, grated |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Mix
1. Preheat oven to **350°F** and spray a **10-inch pie plate** with cooking spray.
2. In a mixing bowl, combine the **16 oz cottage cheese**, **3 eggs** (adding them one at a time without beating), **3 tbsp flour**, thawed and squeezed **spinach**, **3 tbsp olive oil**, and **6 slices Swiss cheese** (grated, about **1 cup**). Mix all ingredients together thoroughly.

### Step 2: Bake and Serve
3. Pour the mixture into the prepared **10-inch pie plate**.
4. Bake at **350°F** for **1 hour** until the souffle is set and lightly golden on top.
5. Cool for **10 minutes** before serving to allow the souffle to set properly.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Can be assembled a few hours in advance and kept covered in the refrigerator before baking. Leftovers reheat well in a low oven.
