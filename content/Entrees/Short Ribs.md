## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Season
1. Preheat your oven to approximately **450°F**.
2. Trim all the fat off the **as needed short ribs**.
3. Wash the **as needed short ribs**, then season them thoroughly with **to taste salt** and **to taste seasonings**.

### Step 2: Bake and Roast
4. Place the seasoned **as needed short ribs** in a lidded Pyrex dish and put it in the preheated oven.
5. When the meat is nice and brown, add the **optional potatoes** and **optional onions** to the dish.
6. Bake for a total of about **2 to 3 hours**, or until the meat and vegetables are tender and fragrant.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftover cooked short ribs in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 to 4 days.
