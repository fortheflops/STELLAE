# Texas Pot Roast

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A savory, slow-braised Texas-style rump roast cooked to fork-tender perfection with green chilies, chili sauce, and beef bouillon. This comforting classic is baked low and slow for a rich, deeply flavorful gravy.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Sear
1. Preheat oven to **325°F**.
2. In a Dutch oven or heavy oven-safe pot, heat a **small amount of vegetable oil** over medium-high heat. Fry **2 cloves garlic** until fragrant and golden, then discard the garlic.
3. Season the **rump roast** with **salt** and **black pepper**, then dust lightly with **all-purpose flour**. Sear the roast well on all sides in the hot oil until a deep brown crust forms.

### Step 2: Assemble and Bake
4. Add **2 1/2 cups canned beef bouillon** and **1 chopped medium onion** to the pot around the roast.
5. On top of the roast, spread **1 small can chopped green chili** and **1 cup bottled chili sauce**.
6. Cover tightly with a lid or foil and bake in the preheated **325°F** oven for at least **4 hours**, turning the roast over after **2 1/2 hours** of cooking. The roast is done when it is fork-tender and pulls apart easily with a fork.
7. Note: If the roast is over **6 lbs**, use more chili.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This pot roast tastes even better the next day. Store in the refrigerator in its juices for up to 3 days, and gently reheat on the stovetop or in a 325°F oven until warmed through.
