## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Mix Meatloaf
1. Preheat the oven to **350°F**. Lightly grease a loaf pan.
2. In a large bowl, combine the **1 1/2 pounds ground beef**, **1/2 cup fresh bread crumbs**, **1 chopped onion**, **1 beaten egg**, **1 1/2 teaspoons salt**, **1/4 teaspoon pepper**, and **1 cup tomato sauce** (half of the total tomato sauce).
3. Mix well until fully incorporated and place the mixture into the prepared loaf pan, shaping it evenly.

### Step 2: Prepare Topping and Bake
4. In a separate bowl, combine the **1 cup tomato sauce** (remaining) with the **1/3 cup water**, **3 tablespoons vinegar**, **3 tablespoons brown sugar**, **2 tablespoons prepared mustard**, and **2 teaspoons Worcestershire sauce** to create the barbecue topping.
5. Pour the prepared barbecue topping sauce evenly over the meatloaf in the pan until fully coated.
6. Bake for **1 hour** until cooked through, bubbly, and fragrant.
