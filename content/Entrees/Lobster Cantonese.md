# Lobster Cantonese

> 📜 **Collection:** GC | ✍️ **Attribution:** May Belle | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A classic, savory recipe for Lobster Cantonese featuring tender lobster pieces stir-fried with pork, garlic, and a rich egg-thickened gravy. Perfect for a special seafood dinner at home.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Sear the Aromatics and Meat
1. Heat **3 tablespoons salad oil** in a large skillet. Add **1 clove garlic**, crushed, and **1/2 pound pork butt** (or steak). Stir-fry until the pork loses its pink color.

### Step 2: Build the Sauce and Cook Lobster
2. Stir in **1/2 tablespoon soy sauce**, **2 cups chicken broth**, and **1 teaspoon sugar**. Bring the mixture to a boil.
3. Add **2 lobsters** (or lobster tails), cracked and cut into pieces. Cover the skillet and cook for **five to seven minutes**, or until the lobster turns bright red and is cooked through.

### Step 3: Thicken and Finish
4. In a small bowl, mix **2 tablespoons cornstarch** and **2 tablespoons water** together to create a smooth paste. Add this paste to the skillet with the lobster. Stir continuously until the gravy thickens and glossy.
5. Stir in **2 green onions**, chopped.
6. Pour **1 egg**, slightly beaten, over the lobster mixture. Stir gently just until the egg is set and ribboned throughout. Serve immediately.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Best served immediately for optimal seafood texture and fresh egg ribbons.
