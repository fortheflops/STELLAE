# Chile Pie

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Seven | ✍️ **Attribution:** Ruth Radtke | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A classic Tex-Mex style casserole featuring seasoned ground beef, green chiles, corn, and tomato sauce layered with tortillas and melted longhorn cheese. Easy to assemble and baked until hot, bubbly, and cheesy.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare Hamburger Sauce
1. Sauté **1 chopped onion** in a large skillet. Add **1 1/2 lbs. hamburger**, **salt**, and **black pepper**, cooking until browned.
2. Stir in **2 tsp. chile powder**, **1 can green chiles**, **2 cans tomato sauce**, and **1 can whole kernel corn** (with liquid).
3. Cook and simmer for **15 minutes** to allow flavors to meld.

### Step 2: Assemble and Bake
4. In an ungreased casserole dish, place a bottom layer of **corn tortillas**.
5. Spoon a layer of the prepared **hamburger sauce** over the tortillas, followed by a layer of **1 1/2 lbs. longhorn cheese** (grated).
6. Repeat the process to make 2 full layers, ending with a generous top layer of **grated longhorn cheese**.
7. Bake at **325°F** for **30 minutes** until hot, bubbly, and the cheese is fully melted.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The pie can be assembled up to 24 hours in advance, covered, and stored in the refrigerator before baking. Add 5 to 10 extra minutes to the bake time if coming directly from the cold fridge.
