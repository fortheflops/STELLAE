# Fish Balls

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A classic, hearty recipe for traditional fish balls made with salt cod and potatoes. These savory bites are mashed until creamy and fried to golden perfection.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 25 mins | None | 4 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Pot
* Colander
* Mixing bowl
* Fork
* Deep fryer or heavy skillet

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 cup | [[Salt Cod]] | Shredded |
| 2 1/2 cups | [[Potatoes]] | Pared and cubed or sliced |
| 1 | [[Egg]] | Beaten |
| 1/2 tsp | [[Butter]] | Softened |
| 1/8 tsp | [[Pepper]] | Ground |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and Boiling
1. Wash the **1 cup salt cod** in cold water, then cut or pull into fine shreds.
2. Pare and cut the **2 1/2 cups potatoes** into cubes or slices.
3. Place the **salt cod** and **potatoes** in a pot, cover with water, and boil until the **potatoes** are tender.

### Step 2: Mixing and Frying
4. Drain the mixture well and mash thoroughly.
5. Add the **1/2 tsp butter**, **1 egg**, and **1/8 tsp pepper**, then beat with a fork until the mixture is creamy.
6. Drop rounding tablespoons of the mixture into deep hot fat and fry until golden brown.
7. Remove and drain on paper towels before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The mashed mixture can be prepared and refrigerated for up to 12 hours before frying.
