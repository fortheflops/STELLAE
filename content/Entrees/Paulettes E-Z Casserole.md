# Paulette's E-Z Casserole

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Paulette | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A wonderfully simple and hearty casserole from Paulette. This 'E-Z' recipe layers raw ground beef, seasonings, and frozen French fries, then bakes it all in a creamy mushroom soup sauce.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 45 mins | None | 4-6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 8x11 inch baking dish
* Small mixing bowl
* Medium mixing bowl

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 pound | [[Ground Beef]] | raw |
| 1 pound package | [[French Fries]] | frozen |
| 2 cans | [[Cream of Mushroom Soup]] | unprepared |
| 2/3 can | [[Milk]] | measured using the soup can |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Salt]] | none |
| 1/8 teaspoon | [[Lawry's Seasoned Salt]] | none |
| 1/8 teaspoon | [[Black Pepper]] | none |
| 1/4 teaspoon | All-purpose Seasoning | none |
| Optional | [[Onions]] and [[Peas]] | optional addition |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Layer
1. Preheat oven to **375°F**. Generously butter an **8x11 inch baking dish**.
2. Lightly spread the **1/2 pound ground beef** in the bottom of the prepared dish.

### Step 2: Season and Add Fries
3. In a small bowl, combine the **1 teaspoon salt**, **1/8 teaspoon Lawry's Seasoned Salt**, **1/8 teaspoon black pepper**, and **1/4 teaspoon all-purpose seasoning**. Sprinkle this mixture evenly over the **ground beef**.
4. Layer the **1 pound package frozen French fries** on top of the seasoned meat. (If using, add optional **onions** and **peas** here).

### Step 3: Mix Sauce and Bake
5. In a separate bowl, blend the **2 cans cream of mushroom soup** and **2/3 can milk** until smooth. Pour the soup mixture evenly over the top of the **French fries**.
6. Bake for **45 minutes**, or until the casserole is bubbly, golden brown, and the beef is cooked through.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftover casserole sealed in the refrigerator for up to 3-4 days.
