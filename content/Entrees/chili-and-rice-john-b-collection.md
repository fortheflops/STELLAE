# Chili and Rice

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A comforting baked casserole featuring fluffy rice combined with spicy green chilis and rich cheddar cheese. Perfect for a cozy weeknight dinner.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 50 mins | None | 4-6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 2 quart casserole
* Saucepan
* Mixing bowl

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3/4 cup | [[Rice]] | Uncooked |
| 2 cups | [[Water]] | For cooking rice |
| 1 can | [[Green Chilis]] | Diced |
| 2 cups | [[Cheddar Cheese]] | Grated |
| 1 | [[Egg]] | Beaten |
| 2 cups | [[Milk]] | Whole or 2% |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Bake
1. Combine **3/4 cup rice** and **2 cups water** in a saucepan, cook until tender, then let cool.
2. In a mixing bowl, mix together **1 can green chilis**, **2 cups cheddar cheese**, **1 egg**, and **2 cups milk**.
3. Add the mixture to the cooked **3/4 cup rice** and stir well.
4. Pour into a greased 2 quart casserole and bake for **50 min at 350** degrees until bubbling and golden on top.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole ahead of time, cover, and refrigerate for up to 24 hours before baking.
