## Instructions

### Step 1: Browning and Simmering
1. In a skillet, brown **2 lb. chicken parts** in **2 tbsp. shortening** until golden on all sides.
2. Stir in **1 can tomato soup**, **1/3 c. celery**, **1/3 c. onions**, **1 small clove garlic**, **2 tbsp. brown sugar**, **2 tbsp. Worcestershire sauce**, **2 tsp. lemon juice**, **2 tsp. mustard**, and **2 to 4 drops Tabasco**.
3. Cover the skillet and simmer for **45 min.**, or until the chicken is tender, stirring now and then until the sauce thickens and coats the chicken.
4. For an extra touch, garnish with **lemon slices** before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Can be prepared a few hours in advance and gently reheated on the stovetop before serving.
