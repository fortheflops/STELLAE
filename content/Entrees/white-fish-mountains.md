# White Fish Mountains

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A classic layered casserole featuring white fish, sliced tomatoes, and hard-boiled eggs topped with a rich sharp cheese sauce. This hearty dish is baked until golden and bubbling for a comforting meal.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 20 mins | None | 4 servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Baking dish

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 4 rounds | [[Toast]] | Sturdy |
| 2 large | [[Tomatoes]] | Sliced |
| 1 lb | [[White Fish]] | Cooked and flaked |
| 2 | [[Hard Boiled Eggs]] | Sliced |
| 1 cup | [[Sharp Cheese Sauce]] | Prepared |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Assembly and Baking
1. Create a sandwich base using the **4 rounds toast**.
2. Layer the **2 sliced tomatoes**, **1 lb cooked white fish**, and **2 sliced hard boiled eggs** on top of the toast.
3. Arrange the stacks in a baking dish and pour the **1 cup sharp cheese sauce** over the tops.
4. Place into a hot oven and bake for 20 minutes until the sauce is bubbling and golden.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the stacks in the baking dish ahead of time, but add the cheese sauce just before baking to prevent the toast from becoming soggy.
