# Chicken Hot Dish

> 📜 **Collection:** Georgia Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A comforting classic chicken hot dish layered with tender noodles, creamy sour cream, mushrooms, and a crunchy pecan topping. Baked to bubbling perfection, this hearty casserole is sure to please the whole family.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 45 mins | None | 6 servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Baking dish
* Mixing bowl

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 8 oz pkg | [[Noodles]] | cooked |
| 8 oz | [[Sour Cream]] | None |
| 4 cups | [[Chicken]] | cooked and cut up |
| 1 can | [[Mushroom Soup]] | None |
| 1/2 cup | [[Parmesan]] | None |
| 1 can | [[Black Olives]] | halved |
| 1 cup | [[Pecans]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Assembly and Baking
1. Preheat your oven to **350°F**.
2. Add **8 oz pkg noodles**, **8 oz sour cream**, **4 cups chicken**, **1 can mushroom soup**, **1/2 cup Parmesan**, and **1 can black olives** together, mixing well.
3. Transfer the mixture to a baking dish and top with **1 cup pecans**.
4. Bake at **350°** for **45 minutes** until bubbly and golden brown.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the hot dish ahead of time, cover, and refrigerate for up to 24 hours before baking.
