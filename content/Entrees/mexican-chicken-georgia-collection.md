# Mexican Chicken

> 📜 **Collection:** Georgia Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Kaylene Dyke | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A delightful layered Mexican chicken casserole featuring crushed chips and creamy soups. Perfect for a comforting family dinner.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 1 hr | None | 6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Baking dish
* Mixing bowl

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| At least 2 cups | [[Boned Chicken]] | Boned and cooked |
| 1 cup | [[Onion]] | Chopped |
| 1 pkg. | [[Doritos]] | Crushed |
| 2 cups | [[Cheddar Cheese]] | Shredded |
| 1 can | [[Cream of Mushroom Soup]] | Condensed |
| 1 can | [[Cream of Chicken Soup]] | Condensed |
| 1 can | [[Rotel Tomatoes]] | Diced tomatoes with chilies |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Assembly and Baking
1. In a sprayed baking dish, layer **at least 2 cups boned chicken**, **1 cup chopped onion**, **1 pkg. crushed Doritos**, and **2 cups shredded cheddar cheese**.
2. In a mixing bowl, mix well together **1 can cream of mushroom soup**, **1 can cream of chicken soup**, and **1 can rotel tomatoes**.
3. Pour the soup and tomato mixture over the top of the **at least 2 cups boned chicken** and other layers.
4. Bake at 350°F for **1 hr.** until bubbling hot and golden on top.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole up to a few hours ahead, keeping the chips separate until right before baking if you prefer extra crunch.
