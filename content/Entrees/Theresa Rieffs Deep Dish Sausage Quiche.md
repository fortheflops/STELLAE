# Theresa Rieff's Deep Dish Sausage Quiche

> 📜 **Collection:** General Archive | ✍️ **Attribution:** Theresa Rieff | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A hearty, deep-dish quiche from Theresa Rieff, packed with sausage, onions, peppers, and cheddar cheese, all baked in a rich and savory egg custard. It makes a satisfying and flavorful main course for any meal of the day.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare Filling
1. Preheat oven to **375°F**.
2. In a skillet, fry the **1 pound mild or hot sausage**, **1/2 cup chopped onion**, and **1/3 cup chopped green pepper** together until the meat is browned and vegetables are tender. Drain off excess grease.

### Step 2: Assemble Crust and Custard
3. In a small bowl, toss the grated **1/2 cup cheddar cheese** with the **1 tablespoon flour**.
4. Spread the cooked sausage mixture in the bottom of the unbaked **1 deep dish unbaked pie crust**.
5. Sprinkle the cheese and flour mixture evenly over the sausage layer.
6. In a separate bowl, mix the beaten **2 eggs**, **1 cup evaporated milk**, **1 tablespoon parsley flakes**, **1 1/2 teaspoon season salt**, **1/4 teaspoon garlic salt**, and **1/4 teaspoon black pepper**.
7. Carefully pour the egg mixture over the sausage and cheese.

### Step 3: Bake and Serve
8. Bake for **45-55 minutes**, or until golden brown, puffed, and a knife inserted in the center comes out clean.
9. Let the quiche cool for **10-15 minutes** before slicing and serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftover quiche tightly sealed in the refrigerator for up to 3-4 days. Reheat gently in the oven or microwave before serving.
