# Chop Suey

> 📜 **Collection:** Jill Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A classic American-style Chop Suey recipe featuring thinly sliced meat, celery, onion, and canned chop suey vegetables served hot over rice.*

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 40 mins | None | 4–6 Servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large skillet or wok
* Small bowl
* Spoon or spatula

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 pound | [[Chop Suey Meat]] | pork or beef, thinly sliced |
| 2 cups | [[Celery]] | diced |
| 1 cup | [[Onion]] | diced |
| 1 can | [[Mixed Vegetables]] | Canned Chop Suey, drained with juice reserved |
| 1 small can | [[Mushrooms]] | Sliced |
| 2 tablespoons | [[Cornstarch]] | divided for slurry |
| To Taste | [[Chop Suey Sauce]] or [[Soy Sauce]] | adjusted for flavor |
| For Serving | [[Rice]] or crispy chow mein noodles | cooked |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Brown the Meat and Vegetables
1. In a large skillet or wok, brown the **1 pound chop suey meat** until fully cooked.
2. Add the **2 cups celery** (diced) and **1 cup onion** (diced) to the skillet with the meat.
3. Drain the **1 can mixed vegetables**, reserving the juice. Add **2 tablespoons** of the reserved juice to the skillet.
4. Cook slowly for **20 minutes** until the vegetables soften and are fragrant.

### Step 2: Add Vegetables and Thicken
5. Add the drained **1 can mixed vegetables** and the **1 small can mushrooms** (sliced) into the skillet.
6. In a small bowl, mix the **2 tablespoons cornstarch** with a few tablespoons of cold water to make a smooth slurry.
7. Stir the cornstarch slurry into the skillet. Add more **chop suey sauce** or **soy sauce** to taste.
8. Bring the mixture to a simmer, stirring continuously until the sauce thickens and coats the ingredients. Cook for another **20 minutes** until bubbling and savory.
9. Serve hot over **cooked rice** or crispy noodles.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Leftovers can be stored in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days and reheated on the stovetop.
