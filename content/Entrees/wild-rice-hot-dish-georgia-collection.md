# Wild Rice Hot Dish

> 📜 **Collection:** Georgia Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A classic Midwestern wild rice hot dish baked to comforting perfection with cream soups, mushrooms, and peppers. This versatile casserole can be served as a hearty side or turned into a full meal with added chicken.*

---

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 1 hr 15 mins | None | 6 servings |

| 1 1/2 cups | [[Water]] | for cooking rice |
| 1/2 cup | [[Green Onions]] | chopped |
| 1/4 cup | [[Green Bell Pepper]] | chopped |
| 1 cup | [[Mushrooms]] | sliced |
| 1/2 tsp | [[Salad Oil]] | or vegetable oil |
| 1 can | [[Cream of Mushroom Soup]] | undiluted |
| 1 can | [[Cream of Chicken Soup]] | undiluted |
| to taste | [[Salt]] | None |
| to taste | [[Black Pepper]] | None |

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |

## Instructions

### Step 1: Cook the Rice
1. Cook **3/4 cup wild rice** in **1 1/2 cups water** in a covered saucepan until the water is completely gone and the rice grains are tender and split open. Rinse well in a colander.

### Step 2: Sauté and Combine
2. In a skillet, sauté **1/2 cup green onions**, **1/4 cup green bell pepper**, and **1 cup mushrooms** in **1/2 tsp salad oil** until softened and fragrant.
3. In a large bowl, combine the cooked wild rice, sautéed vegetables, **1 can cream of mushroom soup**, **1 can cream of chicken soup**, **to taste salt**, and **to taste black pepper**. If desired, stir in cooked **chicken breast** pieces.

### Step 3: Bake
4. Place the mixture into a sprayed casserole dish. Cover and bake at **350°F** for **1 hour** until hot, bubbling, and golden around the edges.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This casserole can be assembled, covered, and refrigerated up to 24 hours before baking. Add 10-15 minutes to the baking time if baking straight from the refrigerator.
