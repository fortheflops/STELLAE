# Baked Chicken Breasts with Pepperidge Farm Dressing

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *Tender chicken breasts dipped in butter, coated with crunched Pepperidge Farm dressing, and baked to perfection. A comforting and flavorful main dish from the John B. Collection.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 30 mins | None | 4 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Baking dish
* Blender

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 4 | [[Chicken Breasts]] | Deboned |
| as needed | [[Butter]] | Melted |
| to taste | [[Salt]] | None |
| to taste | [[Pepper]] | None |
| 1 package | [[Pepperidge Farm Dressing]] | Dry, crunched in blender |
| to taste | [[Parsley Flakes]] | None |
| to taste | [[Parmesan Cheese]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and Coating
1. Dip the **deboned chicken breasts** in **melted butter**.
2. Season the chicken with **salt** and **pepper** to taste.
3. Pour on top the **dry Pepperidge Farm dressing**, which has been crunched in a blender.

### Step 2: Topping and Baking
4. Top with **parsley flakes** and **Parmesan cheese**.
5. Dribble additional **butter** on top.
6. Cook in oven for **1/2 hour or less**, until golden and cooked through.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the chicken with the crumb coating in the baking dish and refrigerate for a few hours before baking.
