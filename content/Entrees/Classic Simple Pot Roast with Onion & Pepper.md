# Classic Simple Pot Roast with Onion & Pepper

> 📜 **Collection:** JillCollection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A very simple, classic pot roast recipe where a chuck or rump roast is browned and then slow-cooked with a whole onion and green pepper until tender.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 3 hours | None | 6–8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Heavy iron skillet
* Heavy-bottomed pot with lid

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 (3 to 5-pound) | [[Chuck Roast]] or [[Rump Roast]] | Trimmed |
| 1 whole | [[Onion]] | Left whole |
| 1 whole | [[Green Pepper]] | Left whole |
| As needed | [[Fat]] | Optional, e.g., oil or bacon grease |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Sear and Braise
1. In a heavy iron skillet or a heavy-bottomed pot, brown the **1 (3 to 5-pound) chuck roast or rump roast** well on all sides over medium-high heat. If your pot is not non-stick, add a little **as needed fat** if necessary to prevent burning until deeply browned and fragrant.
2. Once the meat is browned, add the **1 whole onion** and **1 whole green pepper** to the pot.
3. Cover the pot, reduce the heat to low, and let it cook slowly until tender.
4. The meat will release its own juices, but it must be watched carefully to ensure the liquid doesn't boil away and cause the meat to burn.
5. Cook for approximately **3 hours**, or until the roast is fork-tender and easily pulls apart.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftover pot roast in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 4 days.
