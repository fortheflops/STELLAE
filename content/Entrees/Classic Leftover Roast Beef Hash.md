# Classic Leftover Roast Beef Hash

> 📜 **Collection:** Jill Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *Transform yesterday's dinner into a savory breakfast favorite with this resourceful classic skillet hash. Chopped roast beef, tender potatoes, and onions are fried to crisp, golden perfection with savory gravy.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 20 mins | None | 2-4 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Skillet
* Chef's knife
* Cutting board
* Spatula

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| As Needed | Leftover cooked [[Meat]] (e.g., [[Roast Beef]]) | chopped |
| As Needed | Leftover cooked [[Potatoes]] | chopped |
| As Needed | [[Onions]] | chopped |
| As Needed | [[Butter]] | softened or sliced |
| As Needed | Leftover [[Gravy]] | any type |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Sauté Aromatics and Gravy
1. In a skillet, melt **butter** over medium heat and brown the chopped **onions** first until translucent and fragrant.

### Step 2: Combine and Crisp
2. Add any available **gravy** to the skillet and heat through.
3. Add the chopped leftover **meat** and cooked **potatoes**.
4. Cook, turning and pressing down occasionally, until the hash is heated through and well browned to your liking with a crispy exterior.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** For the best crisp texture, prepare and cook fresh from leftover components, pressing the mixture firmly into the pan to achieve a golden-brown crust.
