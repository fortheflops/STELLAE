# Jay's Creamy Layered Beef & Noodle Casserole

> *A comforting and savory ground beef casserole featuring layers of egg noodles, a rich sour cream and cream cheese mixture, and a seasoned tomato-beef sauce baked until bubbly.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Meat and Sauce
1. Preheat your oven to **325°F**.
2. Brown the **1 lb. ground beef** in a skillet.
3. Add the **1-2 cloves garlic**, **salt**, **pepper**, and **1 tsp. sugar** to taste.
4. Stir in the **2 small cans tomato sauce** and let simmer for about **20 minutes** until rich and fragrant.

### Step 2: Cook Noodles and Cream Mixture
5. While the sauce simmers, cook the **1 pkg. egg noodles** according to package directions, then drain.
6. In a separate bowl, mix the **1 carton sour cream**, **1 small pkg. cream cheese**, and chopped **1 bunch spring onions** tops until well blended.

### Step 3: Assemble and Bake
7. In a casserole dish, create layers: start with a layer of **egg noodles**, then a layer of the **sour cream sauce**, and then a layer of the **meat sauce**. Repeat if possible.
8. Sprinkle the top with grated **cheese**.
9. Bake at **325°F** for **20 minutes**, or until hot, bubbly, and melted.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole ahead of time and store covered in the refrigerator for up to 24 hours before baking.
