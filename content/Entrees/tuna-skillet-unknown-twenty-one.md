# Tuna Skillet

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty One | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A quick and comforting skillet meal featuring flaky tuna and tender peas in a creamy shrimp soup sauce. Served hot over crisp chow mein noodles or rice for a satisfying weeknight dinner.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 15 mins | None | 4 servings |

* Stove

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |

| 1 c | [[Peas]] |  |
| 1 can | [[Tuna]] |  |
| dash | [[Pepper]] |  |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Cooking
1. Cook **1 med diced onion** in **1 T oleo**.
2. Add **1 can frozen cream of shrimp soup**, **1/2 c milk**, and **1 c peas**.
3. Heat just to boiling, then add **1 can tuna** and **dash pepper**, and heat through until piping hot.
4. Serve over **chow mein noodles** or **rice**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Best served immediately, but leftovers can be stored in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 2 days.
