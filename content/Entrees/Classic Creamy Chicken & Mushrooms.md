# Classic Creamy Chicken & Mushrooms

> 📜 **Collection:** General Archive | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A quick and classic comfort food featuring diced cooked chicken and sautéed mushrooms in a rich homemade cream sauce. Served piping hot over crunchy chow mein noodles.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 15 mins | None | 4 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large saucepan
* Wire whisk
* Measuring cups
* Measuring spoons

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Butter]] or [[Margarine]] | Unsalted preferred |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Sliced Mushrooms]] | Fresh |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Flour]] | All-purpose |
| 1/2 Teaspoon | [[Salt]] | None |
| Dash | [[Pepper]] | Freshly ground |
| 1 1/4 Cups | [[Chicken Broth]] | Warm or room temperature |
| 3/4 Cup | [[Half & Half]] | Or cream |
| 2 Cups | [[Chicken]] | Cut up, cooked |
| For Serving | [[Chow Mein Noodles]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Sauté the Base
1. In a large saucepan, melt the **1/2 cup butter or margarine** and add the **1/2 cup sliced mushrooms**, sautéing until tender and lightly browned.

### Step 2: Make the Roux
2. Stir in the **1/2 cup flour**, **1/2 teaspoon salt**, and **dash pepper**. Cook for **1 minute**, stirring constantly, until smooth, bubbly, and fragrant.

### Step 3: Simmer and Thicken
3. Gradually whisk in the **1 1/4 cups chicken broth** and **3/4 cup half & half**. Continue to stir constantly over medium heat until the sauce thickens, bubbles, and coats the back of a spoon.

### Step 4: Combine and Serve
4. Add the **2 cups chicken** and heat well until warmed through. Serve hot over a bed of **chow mein noodles**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the creamed chicken mixture in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days. Reheat gently on the stovetop before serving over fresh chow mein noodles.
