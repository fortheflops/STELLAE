# Jean's Hamburger Casserole

> 📜 **Collection:** General Archive | ✍️ **Attribution:** Jean | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A hearty, all-in-one casserole from Jean featuring seasoned ground beef, macaroni, vegetables, and a bubbly cheesy topping. This classic weeknight dinner delivers comforting flavors in every bite.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 45 mins | None | 6–8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Casserole dish
* Large skillet
* Large saucepan
* Mixing bowl
* Colander

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 Pound | [[Ground Meat]] | Beef |
| 1 | [[Bell Pepper]] | Chopped |
| 1 | [[Onion]] | Chopped |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Macaroni]] | Uncooked |
| 1 Cup | [[Ripe Pitted Olives]] | Chopped |
| 1 Cup | [[Corn]] | Canned or frozen, thawed |
| 1 Can | [[Tomatoes]] | Approx. 15 oz, undrained |
| 1/2 Pound | [[Cheese]] | 2 cups, grated |
| As needed | [[Olive Juice]] or [[Tomato Juice]] | Optional, for thinning |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Cook Macaroni
1. Preheat oven to **350°F** (**175°C**). Grease a casserole dish.
2. Cook the **1/2 cup macaroni** according to package directions. Drain and set aside.

### Step 2: Brown the Meat and Vegetables
3. In a large skillet, sauté the chopped **1 bell pepper** and **1 onion** until softened.
4. Add the **1 pound ground meat** to the skillet and cook until completely browned, breaking it apart. Drain any excess fat.

### Step 3: Combine and Bake
5. In a large bowl, combine the cooked macaroni, chopped **1 cup ripe pitted olives**, **1 cup corn**, and **1 can tomatoes** with their juice.
6. Add the cooked meat mixture to the macaroni bowl and stir everything to combine. If the mixture seems too dry, thin it with a splash of **olive juice** or **tomato juice**.
7. Pour the entire mixture into the prepared casserole dish and spread evenly.
8. Top the mixture generously with the **1/2 pound cheese**.
9. Bake for **45 minutes**, or until the casserole is hot throughout and the cheese is melted, bubbly, and golden brown.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole up to 24 hours in advance, cover tightly, and refrigerate before baking; add an extra 10 minutes to the baking time if baking straight from the fridge.
