# Stuffed Summer Squash

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Filling and Squash
1. In a medium mixing bowl, thoroughly combine the **2 cups cooked rice**, **1/2 pound hamburger**, and **salt** and **pepper** to taste.
2. Peel the **6 small squash** unless they are very young and tender. Carefully slice off the tops and scoop out the seeds from the top cavity to create a hollow space for the filling.
3. Stuff each hollowed squash generously with the prepared **rice** and **hamburger** mixture.

### Step 2: Bake and Finish
4. Arrange the stuffed squash upright in a baking pan and pour **1 cup water** into the bottom of the pan.
5. Bake at 350°F (175°C) until the squash is tender when pierced with a fork, basting frequently with the pan liquids to keep them moist.
6. Spoon **1 cup sour cream** and **1 cup tomato pulp** evenly over the top of the squash, then return to the oven to brown until bubbling and golden.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The squash can be stuffed up to 24 hours in advance and kept covered in the refrigerator. Bake as directed, adding 5-10 minutes to the baking time if starting from cold.
