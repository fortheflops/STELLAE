# Creamy Chicken Rice Casserole

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A classic, comforting creamy chicken and wild rice casserole featuring mushrooms, pimentos, and a crunchy almond topping. This hearty dish is perfect for family gatherings and easy to prepare ahead.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 40 mins | None | 6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 2-quart casserole dish
* saucepan
* skillet

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 cup | [[Wild Rice]] | prepared per package directions |
| 3 cups | [[Chicken]] | diced |
| 1/2 cup | [[Onion]] | chopped |
| 1/4 cup | [[Pimentos]] | diced |
| 1/2 cup | [[Butter]] | melted |
| 2 tbsp | [[Parsley]] | snipped |
| 1/4 cup | [[Enriched Flour]] | all-purpose |
| 1 1/2 tsp | [[Salt]] | none |
| 6 oz | [[Mushrooms]] | canned, sliced, drained with liquid reserved |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Black Pepper]] | none |
| As needed | [[Chicken Broth]] | combined with mushroom liquid to equal 1 1/2 cups |
| 1 1/2 cups | [[Light Cream]] | none |
| 1/3 cup | [[Almonds]] | slivered, blanched |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Base Preparation
1. Prepare the **1 cup wild rice** according to package directions.
2. In a skillet, cook the **1/2 cup onion** in **1/2 cup butter** until tender but not brown; remove from heat.

### Step 2: Sauce and Assembly
3. Stir the **1/4 cup enriched flour** into the onion mixture.
4. Combine the reserved mushroom liquid with enough **chicken broth** to measure 1 1/2 cups, then gradually stir into the flour mixture.
5. Add the **1 1/2 cups light cream** and cook, stirring constantly, until the mixture thickens.
6. Stir in the **1 cup prepared wild rice**, **6 oz mushrooms**, **3 cups diced chicken**, **1/4 cup pimentos**, **2 tbsp parsley**, **1 1/2 tsp salt**, and **1/4 tsp black pepper**.
7. Transfer the mixture to a 2-quart casserole dish and sprinkle with **1/3 cup slivered almonds**.
8. Bake at 350°F until the casserole is hot and bubbling.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole completely, cover, and refrigerate for up to 24 hours before baking.
