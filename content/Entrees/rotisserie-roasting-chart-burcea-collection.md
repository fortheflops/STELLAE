# Rotisserie Roasting Chart

> 📜 **Collection:** Burcea Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A handwritten historical reference guide detailing cooking times and weights for various rotisserie beef roasts. Perfect for achieving the ideal level of doneness for family gatherings.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Rotisserie
* Meat thermometer

## Ingredients

### Meats
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5-6 oz | [[Beef tenderloin]] | Trimmed |
| 8 lbs | [[Rolled rib roast]] | Rolled |
| 4 1/2 lbs | [[Rolled rib roast]] | Rolled |
| 3 1/2 lbs | [[Beef tenderloin tip]] | Trimmed |
| 4 1/2 to 5 lbs | [[Rolled sirloin butt]] | Rolled |
| 4 lbs 4 oz | [[Rolled sirloin tip]] | Rolled |

---

## Instructions

### Cooking Times
1. Roast the **5-6 oz beef tenderloin** for **1 hr 10 min** until fine.
2. Roast the **8 lbs rolled rib roast** for **2 hrs** for just right results.
3. Roast the **4 1/2 lbs rolled rib roast** for **1 hr 25 min** for some medium-rare and some well done.
4. Roast the **3 1/2 lbs beef tenderloin tip** for **1 hr 25 min** (noted as too much done).
5. Roast the **4 1/2 to 5 lbs rolled sirloin butt** for **1 hr 35 min** for fairly rare, just right results.
6. Roast the **4 lbs 4 oz rolled sirloin tip** for **1 hr 45 min** at **325° oven** temperature, using a meat thermometer in pan.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Ensure meat is fully thawed and trussed before mounting on the rotisserie spit.
