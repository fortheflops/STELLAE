## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep Seafood
1. Cut **1 cup fresh or frozen scallops** into bite-size pieces and simmer for about **5 minutes** in **1 cup salted water**.
2. Drain and save liquid, adding **2 chicken-bouillon cubes** to the reserved liquid.
3. Heat **1 pint fresh or frozen oysters, thawed** until the edges curl, then set aside and add the oyster liquid to the scallop liquid.

### Step 2: Make Sauce
4. Have **1 cup cooked, shelled and deveined shrimp**, **2 cups cooked chicken meat, cut into chunks**, and **1 cup cooked lobster meat** ready.
5. Sauté **1 cup sliced mushrooms** in **6 tablespoons butter** until golden brown.
6. Grate **1/2 medium onion** over the mushrooms, sprinkle with **3 tablespoons chopped green pepper** and **3 tablespoons pimiento**.
7. Add **6 tablespoons flour** and blend until smooth.
8. Then add the **milk and fish broth** to make **2 cups**. Stir gradually into the butter, flour, and mushrooms in the skillet, making a thick cream sauce.
9. Season with **1 teaspoon Worcestershire sauce** and **1/4 teaspoon prepared mustard**.
10. Stir in **1 cup light cream** and heat a few minutes longer. Season to taste with **salt and pepper**.

### Step 3: Assemble and Bake
11. Arrange the fish and chicken in separate layers in a **2-quart casserole**.
12. Pour the sauce over and bake in a slow oven, **300° F.**, **45 minutes to 1 hour**, or until bubbly.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The casserole may be stored in the refrigerator until 1 hour before baking.
