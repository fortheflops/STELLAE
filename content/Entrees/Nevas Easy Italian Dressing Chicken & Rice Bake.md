# Neva's Easy Italian Dressing Chicken & Rice Bake

> 📜 **Collection:** General Archive | ✍️ **Attribution:** Neva | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A simple and savory 'no-peek' casserole from Neva. A whole fryer chicken, salted and dipped in Italian dressing, is baked over raw rice with a rich sauce made from onion soup and cream of celery soup.*

---

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Assemble
1. Preheat oven to **300°F**. Lightly grease a casserole pan with **oil**.
2. Line the bottom of the pan evenly with **1 ¼ cup raw rice**.
3. Season the **fryer chicken** pieces with **salt**, then dip them thoroughly in **Italian salad dressing**. Place the coated chicken pieces directly on top of the rice.
4. In a mixing bowl, combine the **1 pkg dry onion soup mix**, **1 can cream of celery soup**, and **2 cans water**.
5. Pour this soup mixture evenly over the chicken and rice, then sprinkle with **Accent seasoning** to taste.

### Step 2: Bake
6. Cover the dish tightly with foil or a lid and bake at **300°F** for **2 hours** without peeking, until the chicken is tender and cooked through, the rice is soft, and the sauce is bubbly and fragrant.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This casserole is best enjoyed fresh so the rice maintains its texture. Leftovers can be stored in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days.
