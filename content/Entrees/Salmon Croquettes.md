# Salmon Croquettes

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *Crispy golden salmon croquettes made with flaked red salmon, buttermilk, and a seasoned cracker crumb coating. Perfectly pan-fried until deliciously crunchy on the outside and tender within.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 10 mins | None | 4–6 servings |

* Mixing bowl
* Skillet
* Measuring cups

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 can (16 oz) | [[Red Salmon]] | Drained, with large bones and skin removed |
| ½ C | [[Buttermilk]] | Cold |
| ½ C | [[Flour]] | All-purpose |
| 2 | [[Beaten Eggs]] | Whisked |
| ¼ t | [[Baking Soda]] | None |
| 1 | [[Onion]] | Small, chopped fine |
| To Taste | [[Salt]] | None |
| To Taste | [[Pepper]] | None |
| 1 C | [[Cracker Crumbs]] | Plus additional for rolling |
| As Needed | [[Grease]] / [[Oil]] | Hot, for frying |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare and Mix
1. Remove large bones and skin from the **1 can (16 oz) red salmon**.
2. Place the salmon in a mixing bowl and flake well.
3. Add the **½ c buttermilk**, **½ c flour**, **2 beaten eggs**, **¼ t baking soda**, **1 small onion**, **salt**, **pepper**, and **1 c cracker crumbs** to the bowl. Mix thoroughly until combined.

### Step 2: Shape and Fry
4. Shape the mixture into small croquettes.
5. Roll each croquette in additional **cracker crumbs** until evenly coated.
6. Fry the croquettes in **hot grease / oil** until golden brown and crispy on the outside.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Formed uncooked croquettes can be refrigerated for up to 24 hours before frying.
