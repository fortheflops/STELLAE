# Peachy Chicken

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A comforting vintage casserole featuring savory chicken baked atop seasoned rice and sweet sliced peaches. The chicken drippings create a savory balance against the sweet peach syrup infused in the rice.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 45 mins | None | 4 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Saucepan
* Shallow baking pan
* Measuring cups

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Amount as needed | [[Rice]] | Cooked |
| 1 can/jar | Sliced [[Peaches]] | Drained, reserve syrup |
| To taste | [[Salt]] | Divided |
| To taste | [[Pepper]] | Divided |
| A little | [[Peach Syrup]] | Reserved from peaches |
| 1/2 or pieces | [[Chicken]] | Prepared for baking |
| Amount as needed | [[Butter]] | Softened or melted |
| Other desired | [[Seasonings]] | Optional |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Rice Bed
1. Cook the **amount as needed rice** until done.
2. Place the **cooked rice** in the bottom of a shallow baking pan.
3. Mix in the **1 can/jar drained sliced peaches**, **to taste salt**, **to taste pepper**, and other **desired seasonings** with the rice.
4. Add a **little peach syrup** to the rice mixture, being careful not to add too much so the rice does not become overly sweet.

### Step 2: Assemble and Bake
5. Place the **1/2 or pieces chicken** on top of the rice mixture.
6. Season the **chicken** with **to taste salt**, **to taste pepper**, **amount as needed butter**, and any other **desired seasonings**.
7. Bake at **350°F** until the chicken is cooked through, golden brown, and the juices run clear.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the baking dish ahead of time, cover tightly, and refrigerate for up to 24 hours before baking at 350°F until cooked through.
