# Stuffed Zucchinis

> 📜 **Collection:** Christine Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A simple and rustic recipe for baked stuffed zucchinis featuring fresh tomatoes, red wine, and dill. Tender zucchini boats are hollowed out and roasted until tender and flavorful.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 35 mins | None | 2-4 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Baking dish
* Knife
* Spoon

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3 | [[Zucchini]] | Cut in half, hollowed out center |
| As needed | [[Chopped tomatoes]] | Chopped |
| Splash | [[Red wine]] | None |
| To taste | [[Dill]] | Fresh or dried |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Assembly and Baking
1. Cut **3 zucchini** in half and hollow out the center.
2. Stuff the hollowed zucchini with **chopped tomatoes**, a splash of **red wine**, and **dill**.
3. Bake at **400°** for **35 minutes** until the zucchini is tender and lightly browned.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Can be stuffed ahead of time and refrigerated for up to a few hours before baking.
