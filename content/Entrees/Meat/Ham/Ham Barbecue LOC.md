### Instructions
1.  In a saucepan, mix 1 cup ketchup, $\frac{1}{3}$ cup brown sugar, $\frac{1}{2}$ cup water, 2 tablespoons vinegar, $\frac{1}{2}$ tablespoon dry mustard, and $\frac{1}{2}$ tablespoon nutmeg.
2.  Stir well.
3.  Add 1 $\frac{1}{2}$ lbs chipped, boiled ham to the sauce mixture in the saucepan.
4.  Bring the mixture to a boil on top of the stove.
5.  Transfer the mixture to a deep baking dish.
6.  Bake uncovered at 250°F (120°C) for 45 minutes.
7.  The meat is served on hamburger buns.
8.  It is good served with french fries and a green salad.
