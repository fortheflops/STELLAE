### Instructions
1.  Dice the tart apples and the large onion.
2.  Place meat (2 lbs lean beef or lamb, cut in 1" cubes) in a large skillet or stew kettle.
3.  Add all other ingredients: 1 cup water, 2 bouillon cubes, 1 can tomato paste, 1 $\frac{1}{2}$ teaspoons ginger, 1 diced clove garlic, 1 teaspoon salt, $\frac{1}{2}$ teaspoon pepper, 1 teaspoon Worcestershire sauce, 1 $\frac{1}{2}$ to 2 teaspoons curry powder, $\frac{1}{2}$ cup grated coconut, $\frac{1}{2}$ cup seedless raisins, the diced tart apples, the diced large onion, and 1 $\frac{1}{2}$ teaspoons coriander (optional).
4.  Cover the skillet or kettle.
5.  When the mixture comes to a steaming point, reduce heat to simmer.
6.  Stir occasionally.
7.  Should more liquid be required, add either water or tomato juice. The mixture should be a thick sauce, not a thin mixture.
8.  Cook for approximately 2 hours, or until the meat is tender.
9.  Serve over hot rice.
___

>[!Note]
>Serves 4-6
