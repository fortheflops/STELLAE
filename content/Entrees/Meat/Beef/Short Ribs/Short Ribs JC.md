## Instructions
1.  Preheat oven to approximately 450°F.
2.  Trim all the fat off the short ribs.
3.  Wash the ribs, then season them with salt and other seasonings of your choice.
4.  Place the ribs in a lidded Pyrex dish and put it in the oven.
5.  When the meat is nice and brown, add the potatoes and onions to the dish.
6.  Bake for a total of about 2 to 3 hours, or until the meat and vegetables are tender.
___

>[!Note]
>The author wrote a side note: "I did brown later so they wasn't brown!" This suggests you may get better results by searing the ribs in a pan on the stovetop for browning before placing them in the oven to bake.
