## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 2 large                    | Cubed [[Steaks]], seasoned                |
| 1 tablespoon               | [[Cooking Oil]]                           |
| 1 (6 ounce) can            | [[Corn]]                                  |
| 2 heaping tablespoons      | [[Green Chili]]                           |
| 1 cup                      | Grated [[Jack Cheese]] or [[Cheddar Cheese]]|
| 1 (8 ounce) can            | [[Tomato Sauce]]                          |
___
## Instructions
1.  Preheat the oven to 350°F.
2.  In a skillet, brown the seasoned steaks on both sides in the cooking oil.
3.  In a small bowl, combine the corn, green chili, and **half** of the grated cheese.
4.  Spoon half of this corn mixture into the center of each browned steak.
5.  Fold or roll the steaks and secure them with a toothpick or two.
6.  Place the steak rolls in a baking dish.
7.  Cover the rolls with the tomato sauce.
8.  Top with the remaining cheese.
9.  Bake for 20-25 minutes.
