## Recipe
| Measurements             | Ingredients                                           |
| :----------------------- | :---------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 lb                     | [[Lean Ground Beef]]                                  |
| 3 med.                   | [[Onions]], Chopped coarse ($1 \frac{1}{2}$ C)       |
| 1 large                  | [[Green Pepper]], diced coarse (1 C)                  |
| 1 t                      | [[Salt]]                                              |
| 1 t                      | [[Basil]] (or $\frac{1}{3}$ C [[Fresh Basil]]) |
| $\frac{1}{2}$ t         | [[Ground Pepper]]                                     |
| $\frac{1}{4}$ t         | [[Sugar]]                                             |
| $\frac{1}{4}$ t         | [[Oregano]]                                           |
| $\frac{1}{8}$ t         | [[Crushed Red Pepper]]                                |
| 3 large cloves           | [[Garlic]], crushed                                   |
| 2 T                      | [[Chopped Parsley]]                                   |
| 1 sm. can (8 oz.)        | [[Red Kidney Beans]], drained (reserve liquid)        |
| 1 sm. can (8 oz.)        | [[Golden Hominy]], drained                            |
| Optional Garnish         | Fresh [[Parsley]] sprigs or [[Basil]] sprigs   
___
### Instructions
1.  In large heavy skillet over medium heat brown beef, stirring to break up.
2.  Add onions & green pepper. Sauté, stirring occasionally, just until vegetables are tender.
3.  Stir in salt, basil, ground pepper, sugar, oregano, red pepper, garlic, chopped parsley, beans & hominy.
4.  Simmer uncovered, gradually adding about $\frac{1}{2}$ C reserved bean liquid & stirring occasionally, 10 minutes or until flavors are blended. *(If using fresh basil, stir in just before serving & heat through)*.
5.  Garnish with parsley sprigs.
___

>[!Note]
>Serve with a green salad, hot garlic-buttered Fr. bread, a relatively dry red table wine & sliced fresh oranges
