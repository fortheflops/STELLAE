## Instructions
1.  Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C).
2.  Season the **brisket** with **salt** and **pepper**.
3.  Brown the brisket on all sides in a heavy pan with hot **fat** or **oil**.
4.  Transfer the browned meat to a roaster with a secure lid.
5.  Cook, covered, for approximately 2 hours (half an hour for each pound of meat), or until fork-tender.
___

>[!Note]
>The original recipe is very brief and notes that "Brisket takes a lot of cooking," which suggests you should check for tenderness and be prepared to cook it longer than the "half an hour per pound" estimate if needed. For more flavor, you could brown onions in the pan after the meat and add them to the roaster with a small amount of beef broth.
