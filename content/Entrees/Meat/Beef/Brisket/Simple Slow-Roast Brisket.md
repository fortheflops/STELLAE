*A classic, simple method for a tender, slow-roasted brisket, marinated overnight in a savory, smoky blend.*

Unknown
___
## Recipe
| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 1 (4-5 lb) | [[Beef Brisket]] |
| 2 Tablespoons | [[Liquid Smoke]] |
| 1 Teaspoon | [[Garlic Salt]] |
| 1 Teaspoon | [[Onion Salt]] |
| 1 Teaspoon | [[Celery Salt]] |
| 2 Teaspoons | [[Worcestershire Sauce]] |
___
### Instructions
1.  **Marinate:** In a small bowl, mix the **liquid smoke**, **garlic salt**, **onion salt**, **celery salt**, and **Worcestershire sauce**. Pour this over the **brisket** in a large dish, cover, and marinate overnight in the refrigerator.
2.  **Roast:** Preheat the oven to 275°F. Place the brisket and its marinade in a roasting pan and cover tightly with foil.
3.  Bake for 4-5 hours, or until tender.
4.  **Baste:** Remove the foil for the last hour of cooking and baste the brisket with the pan drippings.
5.  Let rest for 15-20 minutes before slicing against the grain.
