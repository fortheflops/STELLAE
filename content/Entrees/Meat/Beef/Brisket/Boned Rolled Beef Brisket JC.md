## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 3 1/2 pounds               | Boned, Rolled [[Beef Brisket]]            |
| A little                   | [[Butter]]                                |
| As Needed                  | [[Onions]]                                |
| To Taste                   | [[Salt]]                                  |
| 1/2 cup                    | [[Water]]                                 |
___
## Instructions
1.  In an open pressure cooker (lid off), brown the brisket on all sides with a little butter and some onions for about 25 minutes.
2.  Season the brisket with salt.
3.  Add 1/2 cup of water, secure the lid, and bring the cooker up to 15 lbs of pressure.
4.  After 30 minutes, check the meat and turn it over.
5.  Continue cooking under pressure.
___

>[!Note]
>The total cooking time should be about 1.5 hours.
