*A classic and simple recipe for a tender, pressure-cooked beef brisket.*

## Recipe
| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 3 1/2 pounds | Boned, Rolled [[Beef Brisket]] |
| 1 Tbsp | [[Butter]] |
| 1-2 | [[Onions]], sliced |
| To Taste | [[Salt]] |
| 1/2 cup | [[Water]] |
___
## Instructions
1.  In an open pressure cooker (lid off), brown the **brisket** on all sides with a little **butter** and the sliced **onions** for about 25 minutes.
2.  Season the brisket with **salt**.
3.  Add 1/2 cup of **water**, secure the lid, and bring the cooker up to 15 lbs of pressure.
4.  Cook for 30 minutes.
5.  Release pressure, open the lid, and turn the meat over.
6.  Secure the lid again, bring back to 15 lbs of pressure, and cook for another 60 minutes.
___

>[!Note]
>The total cooking time under pressure should be about 1.5 hours (90 minutes).
