## Instructions
1.  Brown the brisket on all sides in a heavy pan, just as you would for a roast beef.
2.  Transfer the browned meat to a roaster with a secure lid.
3.  Cook, covered, for approximately half an hour for each pound of meat.
___

>[!Note]
>The original recipe is very brief and notes that "Brisket takes a lot of cooking." For more flavor, you could brown onions and other aromatics in the pan after the meat and add them to the roaster.
