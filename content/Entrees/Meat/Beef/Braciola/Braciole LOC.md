## Instructions
1.  Heat oil in a large skillet.
2.  Sauté Braciole in oil until well browned on all sides.
3.  Add Tully's spaghetti sauce, cover, and simmer for one hour.
4.  Take meat out of sauce and set aside to cool for one-half hour.
5.  Remove string (if used to secure the Braciole).
6.  Slice and serve in tomato sauce over pasta or rice.
