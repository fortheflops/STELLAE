## Instructions
1.  Heat **oil** in a large skillet.
2.  Sauté the **Braciole** in the oil until well browned on all sides.
3.  Add **Tully's spaghetti sauce** to the skillet, cover, and simmer for one hour.
4.  Remove the meat from the sauce and let it cool for at least 15-30 minutes.
5.  Remove any string used to secure the Braciole.
6.  Slice the rolls and serve them in the tomato sauce, over **pasta** or **rice** if desired.
