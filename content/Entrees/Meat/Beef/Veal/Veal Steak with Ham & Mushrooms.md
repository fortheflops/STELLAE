### Instructions
1.  Sprinkle steak with salt & pepper.
2.  Sauté in hot oil.
3.  Spread garlic spread over top of veal.
4.  Add 2 T water to skillet, cover tightly, then simmer over low heat 45m or until veal is tender.
5.  Uncover veal, pour off drippings - add water to make 2/3 c. return to skillet.
6.  Add ham cubes & mushroom slices, then cook 5m, turning occasionally.
7.  Remove steak to heated platter.
8.  To mixture in skillet add lemon juice then spoon it over steak.
9.  Sprinkle with grated peel.
