## Instructions
1. Requires approximately 2 packages of lean short ribs.
2. To cut shortribs as thin as possible, slice when partly frozen.
3. Tenderize the sliced pieces with knife, cutting meat along the bone.
4. Marinate 1/2 hour in this sauce: (Soy sauce, Sugar, Accent, Salad oil, Mashed garlic, Salt + pepper, Sesame seed, Chopped green onion).
5. Cook meat over hibachi or barbeque.
6. Cook a couple seconds each side.
7. Eat hot.
8. Can also use flank steak.
