## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 pound                    | [[Ground Beef]]                           |
| 1/3 cup                    | Fine dry [[Bread Crumbs]]                 |
| 2 tablespoons              | Minced [[Onion]]                          |
| 1 teaspoon                 | [[Salt]]                                  |
| 3/4 teaspoon               | [[Pepper]]                                |
| 1                          | [[Egg]], slightly beaten                  |
| 1 can                      | [[Campbell's Tomato Soup]]                |
| 2 tablespoons              | [[Shortening]]                            |
| 1 teaspoon                 | [[Chili Powder]]                          |
| 1/4 cup                    | [[Water]]                                 |
___
## Instructions
1.  In a large bowl, combine the ground beef, bread crumbs, minced onion, salt, pepper, beaten egg, and **1/4 cup** of the Campbell's Tomato Soup.
2.  Shape the mixture into 1-inch meatballs.
3.  In a large skillet, brown the meatballs in the shortening.
4.  In a separate bowl, mix the **remaining** tomato soup, the chili powder, and the 1/4 cup of water to create a sauce.
5.  Pour the sauce over the browned meatballs in the skillet.
6.  Cover and bake in a 350°F oven for about 30 minutes.
___

>[!Note]
>This recipe serves 4-5 people.
