### Instructions
1.  Soak bread in milk, then squeeze nearly dry.
2.  Mix with meat, chives, onion, and seasoning.
3.  Shape into balls.
4.  Fry in bacon drippings until browned on all sides.
5.  Add 1/2 cup sour cream.
6._ Cover and simmer for 1/2 hour.
7.  Add remaining sour cream, heat thoroughly, and serve.
