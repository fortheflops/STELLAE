## Recipe
| Measurements              | INGREDIENTS             |
| :------------------------ | :---------------------- |
| 1 Pound                   | [[Ground Beef]]         |
| 1 (16 Ounce) Can          | [[Tomatoes]], Cut Up    |
| 1 Teaspoon                | [[Chili Powder]]        |
| 1 Teaspoon                | [[Worcestershire Sauce]]|
| $\frac{3}{4}$ Teaspoon    | [[Garlic Salt]]         |
| $\frac{1}{2}$ Teaspoon    | [[Sugar]]               |
| $\frac{1}{4}$ Teaspoon    | [[Dry Mustard]]         |
| 8                         | [[Hamburger Buns]], Split & Toasted |
| 2 Cups                    | Shredded [[Lettuce]]    |
| 1 Cup (4 Ounces)          | Shredded [[American Cheese]] |
___
## Instructions
1.  In a skillet, brown the meat; drain off fat.
2.  Add undrained tomatoes, chili powder, Worcestershire sauce, garlic salt, sugar, and dry mustard.
3.  Stir well, breaking up large pieces of tomato.
4.  Bring to boiling; reducei  heat.
5.  Boil gently, uncovered, until thick, about 15-20 minutes.
6.  Spoon onto toasted buns.
7.  Sprinkle each of the burgers with lettuce and shredded cheese.
___

>[!Note]
>Serves 8
