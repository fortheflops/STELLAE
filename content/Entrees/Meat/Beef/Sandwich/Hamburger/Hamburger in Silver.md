*A classic and simple "foil packet" meal, perfect for an easy dinner with minimal cleanup. This recipe makes two large, stuffed hamburger patties.*

| To Taste | [[Tarragon]] |

## Instructions
1.  Preheat the oven to 350°F.
2.  For each packet, lay out a large sheet of aluminum foil ("silver").
3.  Place one **hamburger patty** on the foil.
4.  Top the patty with half of the drained **mushrooms**, then one slice of **cheese**.
5.  Season to taste with **onion salt**, **soy sauce**, **celery salt**, and **tarragon**.
6.  Place another **hamburger patty** on top of the cheese.
7.  Wrap the aluminum foil tightly around the entire stack, sealing the edges.
8.  Repeat for the second packet.
9.  Place the foil packets on a baking sheet and bake for 40 minutes, or until the hamburger is cooked through.
___

>[!Note]
>The recipe says it serves two. The instruction to "broil for 40 minutes" is likely a typo for "bake," as broiling for that long would burn the hamburger.
