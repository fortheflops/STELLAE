## Instructions
1. Preheat the oven to 350°F.
2. For each packet, lay out a large sheet of aluminum foil.
3. Place one hamburger patty on the foil.
4. Top the patty with mushrooms, then a slice of cheese.
5. Season to taste with onion salt, soy sauce, celery salt, and tarragon.
6. Place another hamburger patty on top of the cheese.
7. Wrap the aluminum foil tightly around the entire stack, sealing the edges to create a packet.
8. Repeat for the second packet.
9. The recipe card says to "broil for 40 minutes." This is likely a typo for "bake." Place the foil packets on a baking sheet and bake for 40 minutes, or until the hamburger is cooked through.
___

>[!Note]
>The recipe says it serves two. It also lists several seasonings at the bottom: "onion salt, soy sauce, celery salt, tarragon." The instruction to "broil for 40 minutes" might be a typo for "bake," as broiling for that long would likely burn the hamburger.
