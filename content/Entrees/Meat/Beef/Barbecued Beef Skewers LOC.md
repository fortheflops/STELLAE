## Recipe
| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
| 1 $\frac{1}{2}$ - 2 Pounds   | [[Flank Steak]]             |
| 1 Cup                        | Finely Chopped, [[Onion]]   |
| 1 $\frac{1}{2}$ Teaspoons    | Minced, [[Garlic]]          |
| 3 Tablespoons                | [[Vegetable Oil]]           |
| 1 Cup                        | [[Water]]                   |
| $\frac{1}{4}$ Cup            | [[Brown Sugar]]             |
| 3 Tablespoons                | [[Soy Sauce]]               |
| 2 Tablespoons                | [[Peanut Butter]]           |
| $\frac{1}{2}$ Teaspoon       | [[Ground Ginger]]           |
| $\frac{1}{2}$ Teaspoon       | Grated, [[Lemon Peel]]      |
| Dash                         | [[Hot Pepper]]              |
___
### Instructions
1.  Cut steak across the grain into $\frac{1}{4}$ inch slices.
2.  Sauté onion and garlic in oil.
3.  Stir in remaining ingredients and cook until slightly thickened.
4.  Thread meat on skewers; brush with sauce.
5.  Grill for 8-10 minutes, basting with sauce.
