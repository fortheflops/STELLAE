## Instructions
*(No instructions were provided on the recipe card. The following is an inferred method.)*
1.  In a saucepan, combine the vinegar, tomato puree, soy sauce, margarine, minced onion, and brown sugar.
2.  Bring the sauce to a simmer over medium heat, stirring until the sugar is dissolved and the margarine is melted.
3.  Stir in the drained tuna or the diced Spam.
4.  Heat through for several minutes.
5.  Serve warm on hamburger buns.
