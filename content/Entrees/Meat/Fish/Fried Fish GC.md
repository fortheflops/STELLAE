## Instructions
1.  Dip the fish fillets in seasoned flour, coating them completely.
2.  Heat about 1 inch of olive oil in a deep skillet or pan over medium-high heat.
3.  Carefully place the floured fish into the hot oil.
4.  Fry until golden brown on both sides and cooked through.
