## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
|1 - 6 ounce box|[[Long Grain Rice]]|
|1 - 10 ounce can|[[Cream of Mushroom Soup]]|
|1 - 16 ounce can|[[Chinese Vegetables (Drained)]]|
|6|[[Lean Pork Chops]]|
| |[[Pepper (to taste)]]|
|1 1/4 cup|[[Water]]|
___
### Instructions
1.  Combine rice, soup, and 1 1/4 cups of water, and vegetables in a large baking dish.
2.  Arrange pork chops on top.
3.  Sprinkle with pepper.
4.  Bake covered at 350 degrees for 1 hour and 20 minutes.
5.  Add additional water if needed.
