### Instructions
1.  Preheat oven to **350°F (175°C)**.
2.  Cut chicken in 8 pieces & roll in flour.
3.  Fry in an oven-safe saucepan (Dutch oven) with butter, ham, mushrooms, white onions until golden brown.
4.  Add wine, bay leaf, cloves, crushed garlic, salt & pepper.
5.  Cover the pan & stew in moderate oven for at least 45 minutes.
6.  Add a little water during cooking if necessary.
7.  When done place chicken, mushrooms, onions & ham on serving dish.
8.  Add cream to gravy & bring to a boil, stirring constantly till reduced to a rich, smooth gravy.
9.  Strain through a fine strainer over the chicken.
10. Fry the bread in butter till brown and garnish the dish with it.
