### Instructions
1.  Cut chicken into pieces, season with salt and pepper.
2.  Brown in butter (use a deep, heavy pot, as this splatters).
3.  Combine other ingredients (salad oil, thyme, lemon juice, onion, garlic).
4.  Pour over chicken.
5.  Cover and cook slowly until tender, about 30 or 40 min.
6.  Good served over rice.
