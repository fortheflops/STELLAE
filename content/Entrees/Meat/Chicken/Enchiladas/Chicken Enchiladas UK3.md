## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
|1 pkg.|[[Chicken Breast]]|
|1 pkg.|[[Tortillas]]|
|1 can|[[Rotel]]|
|1 can|[[Cream of Chicken Soup]]|
|1 can|[[Cream of Mushroom Soup]]|
|1 8 ounce carton|[[Sour Cream]]|
|1/2 teaspoon|[[Cumin]]|
| |[[Longhorn Cheddar Cheese]]|
| |[[Monterey Jack Cheese]]|
___
### Instructions
1.  Lay tortillas in the bottom of a dish.
2.  Chop chicken into little bits & spread 1/2 of it on top of tortillas.
3.  Mix both cans of soup & Rotel in a saucepan & simmer until hot & let cool, then add cumin & sour cream.
4.  Pour this mixture over chicken, then do another layer & pour more sauce.
5.  Grate cheese, put on the bottom layer & on the cheddar cheese.
6.  Put in oven on 325 degrees & let cook until bubbly and hot.
