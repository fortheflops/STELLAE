## Instructions
1.  Cook rice until done.
2.  Place the cooked rice in the bottom of a shallow baking pan.
3.  Mix in the drained peach slices and seasonings (salt, pepper, etc.) with the rice.
4.  Add a little of the reserved peach syrup to the rice mixture. Be careful not to add too much, or the rice will be too sweet.
5.  Place the 1/2 chicken or chicken pieces on top of the rice mixture.
6.  Season the chicken with salt, pepper, butter, and any other desired seasonings.
7.  Bake at 350°F until the chicken is cooked through. The drippings from the chicken will flavor the rice and help to cut down on the sweetness from the peaches.
