## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| Two to Three Pounds              | [[Chicken]], cut up                       |
| $\frac{1}{2}$ Cup                | [[Cornstarch]]                            |
| To Taste                         | [[Salt]]                                  |
| $\frac{1}{2}$ Cup                | [[Salad Oil]]                             |
| One Large Can                    | [[Pineapple Chunks]] (reserve juice)      |
| One Small Bunch                  | [[Onions]], cut up (tops too)             |
| One Cup                          | [[Celery]], cut in medium chunks          |
| Two                              | Bell [[Peppers]], seeded and cut-up       |
| $\frac{1}{4}$ Cup                | [[Brown Sugar]]                           |
| Three Tablespoons                | [[Soy Sauce]]                             |
| One Teaspoon                     | Powdered or Fresh [[Ginger]]              |
___
## Instructions
1.  Preheat oven to 350°F (implied, a moderate oven temperature is appropriate).
2.  In a bag, shake the chicken pieces with salt and cornstarch until coated.
3.  Fry the coated chicken pieces in salad oil until medium brown.
4.  Place the browned chicken pieces in a baking dish.
5.  Put the chopped vegetables (onions, celery, bell peppers) and the drained pineapple chunks over the chicken pieces.
6.  Make the sauce: Mix the oil from frying the chicken (be sure it's not more than $\frac{1}{3}$ cup), $\frac{1}{4}$ cup of brown sugar, the reserved pineapple juice from the can, three tablespoons of soy sauce, and one teaspoon of ginger.
7.  Pour the sauce over everything in the baking dish.
8.  Cover the dish with foil.
9.  Bake for 45 minutes, or until the chicken is tender and done. (The card notes a very small chicken would take less time).
10. Serve with boiled rice.
