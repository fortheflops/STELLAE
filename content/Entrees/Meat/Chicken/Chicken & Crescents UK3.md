|1 can|[[Crescent Rolls]]|
___
### Instructions
1.  Boil a chicken - debone.
2.  Cut meat into pieces.
3.  In a large shallow pan, unroll crescent rolls.
4.  Fill each with grated cheese & some chicken pieces.
5.  Roll each one up.
6.  While you are doing this - Heat 1 cup of milk & cream of chicken soup. Add any extra cheese or chicken you have left.
7.  Pour this sauce over the filled crescent rolls.
8.  Bake at 350 degrees for 40-45 minutes - until brown.
