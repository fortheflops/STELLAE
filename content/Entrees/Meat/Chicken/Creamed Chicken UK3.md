## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
| 1/2 Cup             | [[Butter]] or [[Margarine]]              |
| 1/2 Cup             | [[Sliced Mushrooms]]         |
| 1/2 Cup                   | [[Flour]]          |
| 1/2 Teaspoon | [[Salt]]          |
| Dash      |[[Pepper]]|
| 1 1/4 Cups | [[Chicken Broth]]|
| 3/4 Cup| [[Half & Half]]|
|2 Cups| [[Chicken]] - cut up|
___
### Instructions
1. Melt [[butter]], sauteed [[mushrooms]].
2. Stir in [[Flour]], [[salt]] & [[pepper]] til smooth.
3. Add [[broth]] & [[cream]]. Stir constantly until thick.
4. Add meat & heat well.
5. Serve over [[Chow Mein Noodles]].
