### Instructions
1.  Boil and cut chicken into bite-size pieces.
2.  Mix chicken, chopped onion, 1/2 lb. grated cheddar cheese, 1 teaspoon garlic salt, 1 teaspoon chili powder, 1 can mashed tomatoes & green chilies, 1 package soft tortillas, and 2 cans cream of chicken soup.
3.  Soften tortillas in boiling chicken stock.
4.  Line tortillas in baking dish.
5.  Add chicken mixture.
6.  Top with 2 cans chicken soup.
7.  Bake in oven at 350 degrees about 35 minutes or until bubbly and hot.
