## RECIPE
Recipe Notes: A simple, flavorful meatloaf with a Southwestern twist.

| Measurements               | Ingredients                               |
| :------------------------- | :---------------------------------------- |
| 1 1/2 pounds               | [[Ground Beef]]                           |
| 1 cup                      | Soft [[Bread Crumbs]]                     |
| 1 (10 ounce) can           | [[Tomatoes and Green Chiles]] (e.g., Ro-Tel)|
| 3 tablespoons              | Dry [[Onion Flakes]]                      |
| 1 1/4 teaspoons            | [[Salt]]                                  |
| 1/4 teaspoon               | [[Garlic Salt]]                           |

## Instructions
1.  Preheat the oven to 375°F.
2.  In a large bowl, mix all ingredients together thoroughly.
3.  With moistened hands, shape the mixture into a loaf.
4.  Place the loaf in a shallow baking dish.
5.  Bake for 1 hour.
___

>[!Note]
>This recipe serves 4.
