## Recipe
### Meatloaf
| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
| 2 lb                | Ground Meat                  |
| $\frac{1}{2}$ Cup  | Onion, finely chopped        |
| $\frac{3}{4}$ Cup  | Bread Crumbs                 |
| 1                   | Egg, lightly beaten          |
| $\frac{1}{2}$ Cup  | Tomato Sauce                 |
| $\frac{1}{2}$ tsp  | Black Pepper                 |
| 2 tsp               | Salt                         |
| $\frac{1}{2}$ tsp  | Worcestershire Sauce         |
### Glaze
| Measurements        | Ingredients                  |
| :------------------ | :--------------------------- |
| $\frac{1}{2}$ Cup  | Tomato Sauce                 |
| $\frac{1}{2}$ Cup  | Water                        |
| 2 Tbsp              | Brown Sugar                  |
| 2 Tbsp              | Vinegar                      |
| 1 tsp               | Worcestershire Sauce         |
| 2 tsp               | Dry Mustard                  |
___
## Instructions
1.  Preheat oven to $350^{\circ}F$ ($175^{\circ}C$). Lightly grease a standard loaf pan (approx. 9x5 inches).
2.  In a large bowl, combine all the **Meatloaf Ingredients**: ground meat, chopped onion, bread crumbs, beaten egg, $\frac{1}{2}$ cup tomato sauce, black pepper, salt, and $\frac{1}{2}$ tsp Worcestershire sauce.
3.  Mix the ingredients thoroughly but gently using your hands until just combined. Avoid overmixing.
4.  Press the meat mixture evenly into the prepared loaf pan.
5.  **Prepare Glaze:** In a small bowl, whisk together all the **Glaze Ingredients**: $\frac{1}{2}$ cup tomato sauce, water, brown sugar, vinegar, 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce, and dry mustard until smooth.
6.  Pour the prepared glaze evenly over the top of the raw meatloaf in the pan.
7.  Bake for 1 hour to 1 hour 15 minutes, or until the meatloaf is cooked through and the internal temperature reaches $160^{\circ}F$ ($71^{\circ}C$) on a meat thermometer.
8.  Let the meatloaf rest in the pan for about 10 minutes before slicing and serving.
