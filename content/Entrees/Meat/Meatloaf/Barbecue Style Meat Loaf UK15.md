## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| **For the Meatloaf:** |                                           |
| 1 1/2 pounds               | [[Ground Beef]]                           |
| 1/2 cup                    | Fresh [[Bread Crumbs]]                    |
| 1                          | [[Onion]], chopped                        |
| 1                          | [[Egg]], beaten                           |
| 1 1/2 teaspoons            | [[Salt]]                                  |
| 1/4 teaspoon               | [[Pepper]]                                |
| 2 cups                     | [[Tomato Sauce]] (divided)                |
| **For the Barbecue Topping:** |                                   |
| 1/3 cup                    | [[Water]]                                 |
| 3 tablespoons              | [[Vinegar]]                               |
| 3 tablespoons              | [[Brown Sugar]]                           |
| 2 tablespoons              | Prepared [[Mustard]]                      |
| 2 teaspoons                | [[Worcestershire Sauce]]                  |
___
## Instructions
1.  Preheat the oven to 350°F. Lightly grease a loaf pan.
2.  In a large bowl, combine the ground beef, bread crumbs, chopped onion, beaten egg, salt, pepper, and **half** of the tomato sauce (approximately 1 cup).
3.  Mix well and place the mixture into the prepared loaf pan.
4.  In a separate bowl, combine the **remaining** tomato sauce with the water, vinegar, brown sugar, prepared mustard, and Worcestershire sauce to create the topping.
5.  Pour the topping sauce over the meatloaf in the pan.
6.  Bake for 1 hour.
