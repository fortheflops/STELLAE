## Recipe
### Meatloaf
| Measurements        | Ingredients                   |
| :------------------ | :---------------------------- |
| 1 lb                | Ground Beef                   |
| $\frac{1}{4}$      | Green Pepper, finely chopped  |
| $\frac{1}{2}$      | Onion, finely chopped         |
| $\frac{1}{2}$ Cup  | Cracker Crumbs                |
| 1                   | Egg, lightly beaten           |
| $\frac{1}{2}$ Cup  | Catsup (Ketchup)              |
| $\frac{1}{4}$ Cup  | Worcestershire Sauce          |
### Sauce
| Measurements      | Ingredients                   |
| :---------------- | :---------------------------- |
| Amount not stated | Worcestershire Sauce          |

## Instructions
1.  Preheat oven to $350^{\circ}F$ ($175^{\circ}C$). Lightly grease a loaf pan or baking dish.
2.  In a large bowl, combine the **Meatloaf Ingredients**: ground beef, chopped green pepper, chopped onion, cracker crumbs, beaten egg, catsup, and $\frac{1}{4}$ cup Worcestershire sauce.
3.  Mix these ingredients together gently with your hands until just combined. Do not overmix.
4.  Shape the mixture into a loaf and place it in the prepared pan.
5.  Bake for 1 hour, or until cooked through (internal temperature reaches $160^{\circ}F$ / $71^{\circ}C$).
6.  Let rest for a few minutes before slicing.
