## Recipe
| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
| $\frac{1}{2}$ Cup  | [[Evaporated Milk]] (reg. milk o.k.)    |
| $\frac{1}{4}$ Cup  | [[Bread Crumbs]]                        |
| 1                   | [[Egg]]                                 |
| 1 tsp               | [[Salt]]                                |
| 1 tsp               | [[Dry Mustard]]                         |
| $\frac{1}{4}$ tsp  | [[Pepper]]                              |
| $\frac{1}{3}$ Cup  | [[Minced Onion]]                        |
| 1 lb                | [[Ground Beef]]                         |
| Amount not stated | [[Cheese Slices]]                       |
| Amount not stated | [[Mashed Potatoes]] *(for topping)* |
| Amount not stated | [[Butter]] *(for dotting)* |
___
### Instructions
1.  Adjust quantities to your needs
2.  Mix milk with bread crumbs.
3.  Beat in next 5 ingredients.
4.  Mix in ground beef with fork.
5.  Turn into 9" pie plate.
6.  Bake in moderate oven (350°F.) 30 min.
7.  Remove. Cover hot meat with cheese slices.
8.  Swirl mashed potatoes on top; dot with butter.
9.  Place under broiler to melt butter and lightly brown.
10. Don't forget to make mashed potatoes while meat is cooking.
