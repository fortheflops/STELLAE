## Instructions
1.  Preheat the oven to 250°F (120°C).
2.  In a large saucepan, whisk together the ketchup, brown sugar, water, vinegar, dry mustard, and nutmeg.
3.  Stir the chipped ham into the sauce mixture.
4.  Bring the mixture to a boil on the stovetop.
5.  Pour the mixture into a deep baking dish.
6.  Bake uncovered for 45 minutes.
7.  Serve warm on hamburger buns.
