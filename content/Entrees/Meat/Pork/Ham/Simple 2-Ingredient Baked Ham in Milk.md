### Instructions
1.  Preheat oven to moderate (350°F).
2.  Remove rind from ham. Wipe with a damp cloth.
3.  Place ham in a granite pan or casserole dish.
4.  Cover the ham with milk (or milk and water).
5.  Place a cover on the pan or casserole.
6.  Bake in the moderate oven for about 2 hours.
7.  If the ham is not brown, remove the cover and let it bake for a few more minutes to brown.
