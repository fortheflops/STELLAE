___
## Instructions
1.  Preheat oven to 350°F.
2.  Remove the ham from the can. Cut off any excess fat and remove the gelatin.
3.  Place the ham in a baking pan.
4.  Pack the ham down heavily with brown sugar.
5.  Pour pineapple juice over the top.
6.  Arrange pineapple slices on top of the ham, securing them with toothpicks.
7.  Bake until heated through and the glaze is bubbly.
___

>[!Note]
>The recipe card notes "350° / hr.". As this type of ham is already cooked, you are heating it through. Bake for approximately 15-20 minutes per pound, or until an internal temperature of 140°F is reached.
