## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 3 cans                     | [[Beans]] (Pork & Beans recommended)      |
| 1 1/2 to 2 cans            | [[Chili]]                                 |
| 1 can                      | [[Spam]], diced                           |
| 1 small                    | [[Onion]], chopped                        |
| 1/4 to 1/2 cup             | [[Water]]                                 |
| To Taste                   | [[Salt]]                                  |
| To Taste                   | [[Bacon Drippings]]                       |
| As Needed                  | Canned Fried [[Onion Rings]]              |

___
## Instructions
1.  Open the cans of beans and place them in a large saucepan over medium heat.
2.  Add 1/4 to 1/2 cup of water, salt to taste, and bacon drippings to taste.
3.  Mix well and continue heating for about 10 minutes, stirring occasionally.
4.  While the beans are heating, dice the can of Spam.
5.  After ten minutes, add the diced Spam and the chili to the beans.
6.  Mix and heat for five more minutes.
7.  Place everything in a serving bowl and sprinkle with canned fried onion rings.
8.  Serve immediately.
