### Instructions
1.  Brown pork in a small amount of hot fat.
2.  Add 1/2 Cup water, cover, and simmer until tender (about 1 hour).
3.  Drain pineapple, reserving the syrup.
4.  Combine sugar and cornstarch in a small bowl.
5.  Add the reserved pineapple syrup, vinegar, soy sauce, and salt.
6.  Add this mixture to the pork. Cook and stir until the gravy thickens.
7.  Add the pineapple, green pepper, and onion.
8.  Cook for 2 to 3 minutes, until vegetables are tender-crisp.
9.  Serve over hot fluffy rice or chow mein noodles.
