## Recipe
### BBQ Sauce

| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
| 1/2 Tablespoon             | [[Brown Sugar]]              |
| 1/2 Cup             | [[Catsup]]         |
| 1/4 Cup                   | [[Water]]          |
| Dash                   | [[Pepper]] |
| 1/2 Teaspoon      |[[Salt]]|
| 1 Teaspoon | [[Celery Salt]] or [[Celery Seed]]|
|3 Tablespoons| [[Butter]]|
|1/2 Teaspoon|[[Dry Mustard]]|
|2 Tablespoons|[[Worcestershire]]|
|1 Tablespoon|[[Liquid Smoke]]|
___
### Instructions
#### BBQ Sauce 
1. Combine all in a saucepan, heat slowly, Cool.
#### Brisket 
1.  **Day 1:** Baste [[brisket]] w/ [[liquid smoke]] & [[salt]]. Wrap in foil & refrigerate overnight.
2.  **Day 2:** Rub [[brisket]] w/ [[onion]] (dried) & [[garlic powder]]. Rewrap in foil & bake at 275° for 10 hours. Refrigerate overnight.
3.  **Day 3:** Slice [[brisket]] thin. Cover with sauce. Rewrap in foil & bake 325° for 1 hr.
___

>[!Note]
>3-5 lb.
