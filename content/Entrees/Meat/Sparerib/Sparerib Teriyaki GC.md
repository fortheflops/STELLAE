## Recipe
| Measurements              | Ingredients                               |
| :------------------------ | :---------------------------------------- |
| 1/2 Cup               | [[Soy Sauce]]                   |
| 1/2 Cup               | [[Sugar]]                       |
| 2 Teaspoons           | [[Worcestershire Sauce]]        |
| 1 Tablespoon          | [[Vinegar]]                     |
| 3 Tablespoons         | [[Catsup]]                      |
| 1 Teaspoon            | [[Dry Mustard]]                 |
|                       | [[Cut Up Spareribs]]            |
___
## Instructions
1. Mix ingredients (Soy Sauce, Sugar, Worcestershire Sauce, Vinegar, Catsup, Dry Mustard) in saucepan and bring to a boil.
2. Then add cut up spareribs in sauce for 20 minutes.
3. Stir spareribs occasionally.
4. Drain and roast in 325-350 degree oven until browned, about 20-30 minutes.
5. Use excess sauce for basting.
