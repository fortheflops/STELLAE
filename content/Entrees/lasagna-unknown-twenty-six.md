## Instructions

### Step 1: Make the Sauce
1. Sauté **1/4 lb hot sausage** and **1 lb ground beef** with chopped onion, then drain.
2. Stir in **1 clove garlic**, **1 tbsp parsley**, **1 tbsp basil**, **1 1/2 tsp salt**, **1 lb can tomatoes**, and **1 6 oz can tomato paste**.
3. Simmer the sauce for **30 minutes** until thickened and aromatic.

### Step 2: Prepare the Cheese Mixture
4. In a mixing bowl, combine **3 cups cottage cheese**, **2 beaten eggs**, **2 tsp salt**, **1/2 tsp pepper**, **2 tbsp parsley flakes**, and **1/2 cup grated parmesan cheese**.

### Step 3: Assemble and Bake
5. Layer the meat sauce, **10 oz dry lasagna noodles**, the cottage cheese mixture, and **1 lb sliced mozzarella cheese** in a baking dish.
6. Bake at **375°F** for **1 hour** until bubbling and the cheese is melted and lightly browned.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the lasagna a day in advance, cover tightly, and refrigerate until ready to bake, adding a few extra minutes to the baking time if needed.
