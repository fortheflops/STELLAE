## Instructions

### Step 1: Making the Sauce
1. Brown the **1/4 lb pork sausage** and **1 lb hamburger** in a skillet with the minced **1 clove garlic**.
2. Add the **1 T parsley**, **1 T basil**, **1 1/2 tsp salt**, **1-1 lb can tomatoes**, and **1-12 oz tomato paste**. Simmer until the sauce is thick and fragrant.

### Step 2: Assembly and Baking
3. Boil the **10 oz box lasagna noodles** in a pot of salted water until tender, then drain.
4. Mix the **3 c cottage cheese** with the **2 beaten eggs**.
5. Layer the noodles, meat sauce, and the cottage cheese mixture in a baking dish.
6. Bake until bubbly and golden brown on top, with the cheese fully melted and bubbling at the edges.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the lasagna a day in advance, cover tightly, and refrigerate until ready to bake. Add an extra 10-15 minutes to the baking time if baking straight from the refrigerator.
