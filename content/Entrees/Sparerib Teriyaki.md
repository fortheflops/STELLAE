## Instructions

### Step 1: Make the Teriyaki Sauce and Simmer
1. In a saucepan, mix the **1/2 cup soy sauce**, **1/2 cup sugar**, **2 teaspoons Worcestershire sauce**, **1 tablespoon vinegar**, **3 tablespoons catsup**, and **1 teaspoon dry mustard**. Bring the mixture to a rolling boil.

### Step 2: Simmer the Spareribs
2. Add the **cut up spareribs** to the boiling sauce and let them simmer for **20 minutes**, stirring occasionally until the meat begins to absorb the flavors.

### Step 3: Roast to Finish
3. Drain the spareribs, reserving the excess sauce, and place them in a roasting pan. Roast in a preheated oven at **325°F to 350°F** until browned and tender, about **20 to 30 minutes**, using the reserved excess sauce for basting.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Spareribs can be simmered in the sauce up to one day in advance, then refrigerated before roasting.
