# Party Chicken Casserole

> 📜 **Collection:** Patsy Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A classic and savory casserole featuring bacon-wrapped chicken breasts baked low and slow on a bed of dried beef. Smothered in a rich, creamy mushroom and sour cream sauce, it is an effortless crowd-pleaser.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 3 hours | None | 8 Servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 9x13-inch baking dish
* Small mixing bowl

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 4 large | [[Chicken Breasts]] | halved to make 8 pieces |
| 8 strips | [[Bacon Strips]] | None |
| 1 (4 oz) pkg | [[Dried Chopped Beef]] | None |
| 1 (10.5 oz) can | [[Mushroom Soup]] | condensed |
| 1 (8 oz) carton | [[Sour Cream]] | None |
| For Serving | [[Cooked Rice]] | hot |

---

## Instructions

### Preparation and Assembly
1. Preheat your oven to **275°F (135°C)**.
2. If your **4 large chicken breasts** are thick, cut each one in half lengthwise to create **8 thinner pieces**.
3. Wrap **1 strip of bacon** securely around each piece of **chicken**.
4. Spread the **1 (4 oz) package dried chopped beef** evenly over the bottom of a **9x13-inch baking dish**.
5. Arrange the bacon-wrapped **chicken** pieces in a single layer directly on top of the **dried chopped beef**.

### Sauce and Baking
6. In a small bowl, combine the **1 (10.5 oz) can condensed cream of mushroom soup** and the **1 (8 oz) carton sour cream**, stirring until the mixture is smooth and fully blended.
7. Pour the sauce mixture evenly over the **chicken**, ensuring each piece is completely covered to keep it moist during the long bake.
8. Bake, uncovered, for **2.5 to 3 hours** until the chicken is incredibly tender, the bacon is cooked through, and the sauce is bubbly and golden brown around the edges.
9. Serve hot over a bed of **cooked rice**, spooning the rich, savory sauce from the dish over both the chicken and the rice as a gravy.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This casserole can be assembled up to 24 hours in advance, covered, and stored in the refrigerator before baking. Leftovers keep well in an airtight container for up to 3 days.
