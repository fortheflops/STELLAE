# Steak In The Crock Pot

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** N. Lake | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A tender slow-cooker steak recipe featuring a rich onion soup gravy. Perfect for serving over a bed of creamy mashed potatoes.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 6-8 hours | None | 4-6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Crock Pot
* Knife
* Cutting board

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 lbs | [[Rd. St.]] | cut into 1-1/2" strips |
| 1 can | [[M.R. Soup]] | mushroom soup |
| 1 pkg | [[Dry Onion Soup]] | soup mix |
| 1/4 cup | [[Water]] | none |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Assembly and Cooking
1. Pour **1/4 cup water** into the bottom of the **C. Pot**.
2. Layer the **1-1/2" strips of Rd. St.**, **M.R. Soup**, and **dry onion soup** alternately in layers.
3. Cook on medium temperature for **6-8 hrs** until the meat is tender and yields a delicious gravy.
4. Serve warm with **mash potatoes**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the ingredients in the crock pot insert the night before, cover, and refrigerate until ready to cook the next day.
