## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep & Brown
1. Cook **1 package (8 oz) noodles** in boiling water for **20 minutes** until tender, then drain.
2. Cut **2 lbs lean pork** into small pieces, roll them in flour, and brown in a skillet over medium-high heat until golden brown on all sides.
3. Chop **1 green pepper** and **2 medium onions**.

### Step 2: Assemble & Bake
4. In a large casserole dish, combine the browned **2 lbs lean pork**, cooked **1 package (8 oz) noodles**, chopped **1 green pepper**, chopped **2 medium onions**, **1/2 lb American cheese**, **1 cup tomato soup**, **1 cup water**, and **1 cup mushrooms**.
5. Mix well to ensure all ingredients are evenly distributed.
6. Cover and bake slowly at 300°F (150°C) for **2 hours** until the pork is fork-tender and the casserole is bubbling and hot throughout.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This casserole can be assembled a day ahead, covered, and refrigerated. Increase baking time by 15 minutes if baking straight from the refrigerator.
