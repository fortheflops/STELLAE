# Souper Tuna Crunch

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty One | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A delightful and crunchy tuna casserole made with cream of mushroom soup and topped with crispy Chinese noodles. This comforting dish combines savory tuna with nuts and vegetables for a satisfying meal.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 35 mins | None | 4 1/2 cups |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 1 1/2 qt. casserole dish
* Measuring cups
* Can opener

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3 oz | [[Chinese noodles]] | Divided |
| 1 can | [[mushroom soup]] | Cream of |
| 3/4 c | [[water]] | None |
| 6 1/2 oz | [[tuna]] | Drained, broken in chunks |
| 1 c | [[cashews]] | Or peanuts |
| 1 c | [[celery]] | Chopped |
| 1/2 c | [[onion]] | Chopped |
| 1/2 c | [[peas]] | Cooked |
| to taste | [[soy sauce]] | For serving |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing and Baking
1. Reserve **1/2 c Chinese noodles** for later use.
2. In a **1 1/2 qt. casserole**, combine the remaining **Chinese noodles**, **mushroom soup**, **water**, **tuna**, **cashews**, **celery**, **onion**, and **peas**.
3. Bake at 350°F for **30 minutes**, then stir until hot and bubbling.

### Step 2: Topping and Serving
4. Sprinkle the reserved **Chinese noodles** around the edge of the casserole.
5. Bake for an additional **5 minutes** until the topping is golden and crisp.
6. Serve warm with **soy sauce** on the side.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole up to a point before baking, cover, and refrigerate for up to 24 hours. Add the reserved noodles just before the final baking step.
