# Chicken Enchilada Pie

> 📜 **Collection:** JC | ✍️ **Attribution:** Glenda Jones | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A comforting, layered casserole from Glenda Jones featuring cooked chicken, corn tortillas, and Longhorn cheese baked in a creamy sauce.*

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 30 mins | None | 6–8 Servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Medium bowl
* Casserole dish
* Oven

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 whole | [[Chicken]] | cooked and diced |
| 1 large | [[Onion]] | chopped and fried |
| 1/2 pound | [[Longhorn Cheese]] | grated |
| 1 can | Cream of [[Mushroom Soup]] | undiluted |
| 1 can | Cream of [[Chicken Soup]] | undiluted |
| 1 soup can | [[Chicken Broth]] | measured using empty soup can |
| 1 small can | Diced [[Green Chiles]] | drained |
| 1 package | Corn [[Tortillas]] | standard |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Sauce
1. Preheat oven to **350°F**. Grease a casserole dish.
2. In a medium bowl, combine the **1 large onion**, **1 can cream of mushroom soup**, **1 can cream of chicken soup**, and **1 small can diced green chiles** to create the sauce.

### Step 2: Assemble the Pie
3. Place half of the **1 package corn tortillas** in the bottom of the casserole dish.
4. Add a layer of the **1 whole chicken**.
5. Pour half of the soup mixture over the chicken.
6. Sprinkle with half of the **1/2 pound Longhorn cheese**.
7. Repeat with a second layer of tortillas, chicken, soup mixture, and remaining cheese.

### Step 3: Bake
8. Bake for **30 minutes**, or until hot and bubbly.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole ahead of time, cover tightly, and refrigerate for up to 24 hours before baking.
