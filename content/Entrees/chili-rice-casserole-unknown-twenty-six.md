# Chili-Rice Casserole

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Mrs. Ben A. (Dellie) Lanford | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A comforting cheesy chili-rice casserole made with layers of seasoned rice, sour cream, green chiles, and melted Monterey Jack and cheddar cheeses. This hearty baked dish serves a crowd and brings rich, savory flavors to the table.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 30 mins | None | 8-10 servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Baking dish
* Mixing bowl

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 4 cups | [[Cooked rice]] | Uncle Ben's preferred |
| 4 cups | [[Sour cream]] | None |
| 3 (4 oz.) cans | [[Chopped green chiles]] | Chopped |
| 3/4 lb. | [[Monterey Jack cheese]] | Cut in strips |
| 1/2 cup | [[Cheddar cheese]] | Grated |
| To taste | [[Salt]] | None |
| To taste | [[Pepper]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Assembly and Baking
1. Mix **4 cups cooked rice**, **4 cups sour cream**, and **3 4oz. cans chopped green chiles**. Season with **salt** and **pepper** to taste.
2. Put in layers in baking dish with **2 layers of the Jack cheese** (cut in strips), ending with the rice mixture on top.
3. Bake **30 minutes** in a **350°** oven until hot and bubbling.

### Step 2: Finishing
4. About **5 minutes** before done, sprinkle **1/2 cup grated cheddar cheese** on top and put back in oven until the cheese is completely melted and bubbling.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole in the baking dish ahead of time, cover, and refrigerate until ready to bake, adding a few extra minutes to the baking time if cold.
