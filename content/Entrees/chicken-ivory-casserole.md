# Chicken Ivory Casserole

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A classic, hearty chicken casserole featuring a creamy sauce, tender peas, and a crunchy potato chip topping. This comforting dish is perfect for feeding a crowd with its rich flavors and satisfying textures.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 30 mins | 60 mins | None | 12 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large kettle
* Saucepan
* Shallow baking dish

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3 | [[Broilers]] | 1 1/2 lbs each |
| 2 tsp | [[Salt]] | Divided |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Pepper]] | Divided |
| 1 tsp | [[Curry Powder]] | None |
| 1/2 cup | [[Margarine]] | None |
| 1/2 cup | [[Flour]] | None |
| 2 cups | [[Mushrooms]] | Sliced |
| 1 pkg | [[Frozen Peas]] | Thawed |
| 2 cups | [[Potato Chips]] | Crumbled |
| 1 jar | [[Pimentos]] | For garnish |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Poach
1. Place **3 broilers** in a large kettle with water and cook until the meat is tender.
2. Drain the chicken, reserving 4 cups of the broth, then dice the chicken meat.

### Step 2: Sauce and Assembly
3. Prepare a sauce using the **1/2 cup margarine**, **1/2 cup flour**, and reserved broth, seasoning with **salt**, **pepper**, and **1 tsp curry powder**.
4. Combine the sauce with the **diced chicken**, **2 cups mushrooms**, and **1 pkg frozen peas**.
5. Pour the mixture into a shallow baking dish and sprinkle the top with **2 cups crumbled potato chips**.

### Step 3: Bake
6. Bake at 400°F for 20 minutes until the top is golden brown and the sauce is bubbling.
7. Garnish with cut **pimentos** before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The chicken can be poached and diced up to one day in advance; store in the refrigerator until ready to assemble.
