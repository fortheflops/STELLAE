## Instructions

### Preparation & Cooking
1. In a large skillet over medium-high heat, melt the **1/4 cup oleo**.
2. Add the **3 deboned chicken breasts** and cook until lightly browned and opaque throughout.
3. Dissolve **1 bouillon cube** into **1/2 cup hot water**, then pour the broth into the skillet.
4. Season with **1/2 tsp Accent**, **1 tsp salt**, and **1/4 cup sugar**, stirring well to combine.
5. Add **1 cup sliced celery** and **3 chopped green onions** to the skillet, simmering until the vegetables are tender-crisp.
6. Fold in **8 oz snow peas** last, cooking for an additional 2 to 3 minutes until bright green and cooked through.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Refrigerate leftovers in an airtight container for up to 3 days. Reheat gently in a skillet over medium heat, adding a splash of water if needed to restore moisture.
