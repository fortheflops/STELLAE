## Instructions

### Preparation and Baking
1. In a large mixing bowl, thoroughly combine the **2 lbs. meatloaf mixture**, **1 cup fine dry bread crumbs**, **1/2 cup dairy sour cream**, **1/2 cup ketchup**, **2 beaten eggs**, **3 oz. can chopped mushrooms**, **2 Tbs. Parmesan cheese**, **1 Tbs. chopped onion**, **2 tsp. salt**, and **1/4 tsp. leaf thyme**.
2. Shape the mixture into a cohesive loaf and place it into a baking dish.
3. Bake at 350 degrees F for **1 hr.** until the meatloaf is nicely browned on top, firm to the touch, and cooked through to an internal temperature of 160°F.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The meatloaf can be shaped and kept covered in the refrigerator for up to 24 hours before baking. Leftovers store well in an airtight container for up to 3 days.
