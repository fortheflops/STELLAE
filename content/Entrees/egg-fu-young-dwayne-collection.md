# Egg Fu Young

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A classic, comforting recipe for Egg Fu Young featuring a savory blend of sautéed green peppers, onions, bean sprouts, and water chestnuts folded into fluffy beaten eggs. Pan-fried to golden perfection, these patties are quick, easy, and delicious.*

| 5 | [[Eggs]] |  |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Sauté the Vegetables
1. In a skillet, heat **1 tbsp. salad oil**.
2. Add the chopped **1 medium green pepper**, chopped **1 medium onion**, rinsed and drained **1 can bean sprouts**, and rinsed and sliced **1 can water chestnuts**.
3. Cook and stir the vegetables until they are tender, then remove the skillet from the heat.

### Step 2: Mix and Fry the Patties
4. In a bowl, beat the **5 eggs** until they are thick and lemony.
5. Stir the cooked vegetable mixture and **2-3 tbsp. soy sauce** into the beaten eggs.
6. In the skillet, heat the remaining **1 tbsp. salad oil**.
7. Pour the egg mixture into the hot skillet, forming patties about **5 inches** in diameter while pouring.
8. When the patties are set on the top side, turn them over to cook the bottom side until golden brown and cooked through.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Best served immediately while hot. Leftovers can be stored in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 2 days and gently reheated in a skillet.
