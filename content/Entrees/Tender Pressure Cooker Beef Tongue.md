| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |

| 1 small | [[Onion]] | Whole |
| As needed | [[Water]] | According to pressure cooker directions |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Pressure Cooking
1. Place the **1 beef tongue** and **1 small onion** in a pressure cooker with **water** according to the manufacturer's directions.
2. Cook at **15 lbs. of pressure** for **1 hour** until tender.

### Step 2: Peeling and Slicing
3. After cooking, cool the tongue enough to handle so the thick outer skin can be easily peeled off and discarded before slicing the meat.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store cooked and peeled beef tongue sealed in the refrigerator for up to 3-4 days.
