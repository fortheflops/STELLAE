# Rich, Tender Italian Wine-Braised Beef

## Instructions

### Step 1: Brining and Freezing
1. Soak the **as needed meat** in **as needed water** and **as needed salt** in the refrigerator for **one day**.
2. After soaking, transfer the **as needed meat** to the freezer to freeze thoroughly.

### Step 2: Preparation and Braising
3. When ready to prepare, soak the frozen **as needed meat** in **as needed wine**.
4. Remove from wine and cut the **as needed meat** as desired.
5. In a heavy pot or Dutch oven, heat the **as needed oil** and sauté the **as needed meat** until lightly browned.
6. Pour off the excess **as needed oil** from the pot.
7. Season the meat by adding **to taste Italian herbs**, **to taste salt**, and **to taste pepper**.
8. Pour additional **as needed wine** into the pot.
9. Cover and cook in the oven at **300°F** for **1 to 1.5 hours**, until the meat is fork-tender and fragrant.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The meat can be brined and frozen in advance; once cooked, leftovers store well in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days.
