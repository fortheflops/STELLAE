# Turkey Meat Loaf

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty One | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A wholesome and savory turkey meat loaf packed with spinach, carrots, and oatmeal. This flavorful main dish is simple to prepare and bakes to a tender perfection.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 50 mins | 5 mins | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* 13x9 pan
* Broiler pan

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 1/2 lbs | [[Ground Turkey]] | Or lean beef |
| 10 oz pkg | [[Spinach]] | Frozen, chopped, thawed, and drained |
| 1/2 cup | [[Red Pepper]] | Chopped green or red pepper (alternative to spinach) |
| 1 cup | [[Oatmeal]] | Uncooked |
| 1/2 cup | [[Onion]] | Chopped |
| 1/2 cup | [[Carrots]] | Shredded |
| 2 | [[Egg Whites]] | Or 1 whole egg, slightly beaten |
| 1/3 cup | [[Skim Milk]] | None |
| 1 1/2 tsp | [[Italian Seasoning]] | None |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Black Pepper]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and Baking
1. Combine all ingredients (**1 1/2 lbs ground turkey**, **10 oz pkg frozen chopped spinach**, **1 c oatmeal**, **1/2 c chopped onion**, **1/2 c shredded carrots**, **2 egg whites**, **1/3 c skim milk**, **1 1/2 t Italian seasoning**, and **1/4 t black pepper**) in a mixing bowl.
2. Place the mixture in a **13x9 pan** or on the rack of a broiler pan.
3. Shape the mixture into a loaf.
4. Bake at **350°F** for **45-50 minutes** until cooked through and golden brown.
5. Let stand for **5 minutes** before slicing.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The meat loaf can be shaped and kept covered in the refrigerator for up to 24 hours before baking.
