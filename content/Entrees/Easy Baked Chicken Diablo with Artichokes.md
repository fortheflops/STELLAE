# Easy Baked Chicken Diablo with Artichokes

> 📜 **Collection:** General Archive | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *Enjoy a quick and easy baked chicken dish featuring boneless chicken breasts baked in a creamy, savory salsa-mushroom soup sauce with artichoke hearts, black olives, and cumin. Perfect for a flavorful weeknight dinner.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 40 mins | None | 6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 9x13 baking dish
* Mixing bowl

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3/4 cup | [[Salsa]] | Standard |
| 1/4 teaspoon | [[Cumin]] | Ground |
| 6 | [[Boneless Chicken Breasts]] | Whole |
| 1 (14 oz.) can | [[Artichoke Hearts]] | Drained & Quartered |
| 1 (2 1/4 oz.) can | [[Sliced Black Olives]] | Drained |
| 1 can | [[Healthy Choice Cream of Mushroom with Cracked Pepper & Herbs Condensed Soup]] | Condensed |
| As needed | [[Cilantro]] | For sprinkling |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Initial Bake
1. Preheat oven to **350°F**. Grease a **9x13 baking dish**.
2. In a bowl, mix the **1 can healthy choice cream of mushroom with cracked pepper & herbs condensed soup**, **3/4 cup salsa**, and **1/4 teaspoon cumin**. Set aside.
3. Arrange the **6 boneless chicken breasts** in the prepared baking dish. Bake at **350°F** for **10 minutes**.

### Step 2: Assemble and Final Bake
4. Remove from oven and place the **1 (14 oz.) can artichoke hearts** around the **6 boneless chicken breasts**.
5. Pour the prepared soup mixture evenly over the top.
6. Sprinkle with the **1 (2 1/4 oz.) can sliced black olives**.
7. Bake for an additional **25 to 30 minutes**, or until the **6 boneless chicken breasts** are no longer pink in the center and the sauce is bubbling.
8. Sprinkle with fresh **cilantro** before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftover chicken in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days.
