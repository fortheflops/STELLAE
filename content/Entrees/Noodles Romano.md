# Noodles Romano

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Betty Yandell | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A rich and creamy vintage pasta sauce featuring soft cream cheese and delicate herbs. Toss this warm, velvety sauce over your favorite noodles for a comforting meal.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 5 mins | None | 4 Servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Electric mixer or whisk
* Saucepan

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/4 Cup | [[Soft Margarine]] | Softened |
| 1 8-Ounce Package | [[Cream Cheese]] | Softened |
| 2 Tablespoons | [[Parsley Flakes]] | None |
| 1 Teaspoon | [[Crushed Basil]] | Dried |
| 1/4 Teaspoon | [[Salt]] | None |
| 1/2 Teaspoon | [[Pepper]] | Ground |
| 2/3 Cup | [[Boiling Water]] | Hot |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare Cream Cheese Sauce
1. Combine the **1/4 cup soft marg.**, **1 8-ounce package cream cheese, softened**, **2 tablespoons parsley flakes**, **1 teaspoon crushed basil**, **1/4 teaspoon salt**, and **1/2 teaspoon pepper** in a mixing bowl.
2. Pour in the **2/3 cup boiling water**.
3. Blend all ingredients together until completely smooth and creamy.

### Step 2: Serve
4. Serve the warm sauce immediately over freshly cooked noodles until well coated and fragrant.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftover sauce in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days; gently reheat on the stovetop before tossing with fresh noodles.
