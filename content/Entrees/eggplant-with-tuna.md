# Eggplant With Tuna

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *Discover a unique and savory casserole featuring hollowed eggplant baked with a creamy tuna filling and whipped egg whites. This comforting retro dish combines tender eggplant with savory tuna and a rich white sauce.*

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 30 mins | 1 hour | None | 4 servings |

| 1 cup | [[Milk]] | None |
| 1 1/2 cups | [[Bread Crumbs]] | Soft |
| 1 Tbsp | [[Onion]] | Grated |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] | Separated |
| 2 Tbsp | [[Butter]] | None |
| 2 Tbsp | [[Flour]] | None |
| 7 oz can | [[Tuna]] | None |
| 1 Tbsp | [[Catsup]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Cook Eggplant
1. Wash the **1 large eggplant**, cut lengthwise in half, and scrape out the center, leaving a 1/2-inch shell.
2. Cook the removed **1 large eggplant** in salted water until tender, then drain and mash.

### Step 2: Make Sauce and Combine
3. Melt **2 Tbsp butter**, blend in **2 Tbsp flour**, add **1 cup milk**, and cook until thickened.
4. Add the mashed **1 large eggplant**, **1 1/2 cups soft bread crumbs**, **7 oz can tuna**, **1 Tbsp grated onion**, **1 Tbsp catsup**, and **2 egg yolks**. Season to taste.

### Step 3: Bake
5. Fold in the beaten **2 egg whites** from the separated eggs and fill the reserved **1 large eggplant** shells.
6. Bake at 350°F for **1 hr** until set and golden.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the stuffed eggplant shells ahead of time and refrigerate for up to a few hours before baking.
