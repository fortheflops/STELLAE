# Pork Chops Dijon

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty One | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *Tender pork chops coated in a flavorful blend of Italian dressing and Dijon mustard, cooked with sweet onions. Serve atop a bed of crisp lettuce for a delightful presentation.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 30 mins | None | 4 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Skillet
* Platter
* Mixing bowl

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 4 | [[Pork Chops]] | 1/2 - 3/4" thick |
| 2 T | [[Italian Salad Dressing]] | not oil free |
| 2 T | [[Dijon Mustard]] | None |
| 1/4 t | [[Pepper]] | None |
| 1 | [[Onion]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Season
1. Trim fat from **4 chops**.
2. Stir together **2 T Italian salad dressing**, **2 T Dijon mustard**, and **1/4 t pepper**; brush both sides of **chops** with the mixture.
3. Slice **1 onion**.

### Step 2: Cooking
4. Spray skillet w/nonstick; cook **chops** and **1 onion**, covered on medium-low for 20 minutes until tender.
5. Turn **chops**; cook covered 5-10 minutes more until fully cooked through.

### Step 3: Plating
6. Line platter w/lettuce leaves. Top w/**chops**, top **chops** w/**onions**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Chops can be marinated in the dressing and mustard mixture up to 4 hours in advance before cooking.
