## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and Warming
1. Preheat your oven to 400 degrees. Wrap the **corn tortillas** tightly in aluminum foil and place them in the oven to warm until soft and pliable.
2. In a mixing bowl, combine the **1 can black beans**, **1 tsp oil**, **1 tsp garlic**, **1 tbsp white wine vinegar**, chopped **red & yellow peppers**, and chopped **jalapeno pepper**. Mix thoroughly.

### Step 2: Assembly and Serving
3. Spread the bean and pepper mixture evenly onto the warm **corn tortillas**, then carefully roll them up.
4. Serve immediately with **rice**, **onion**, and **jack cheese** on the side.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The filling can be mixed ahead of time and stored in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 2 days.
