# More Casserole (Beef and Noodle Bake)

> 📜 **Collection:** UK15 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A hearty, all-in-one casserole packed with ground beef, noodles, peas, and corn in a zesty, chili-powder-seasoned tomato sauce, topped with melted cheddar cheese.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Cook Bases
1. Preheat the oven to **350°F**. Grease a large baking dish.
2. Cook the **6 ounces noodles** according to package directions. Drain thoroughly.

### Step 2: Brown the Meat
3. In a large skillet, fry the **1 1/2 pounds ground beef**, diced **1 medium onion**, and diced **1 medium bell pepper** until the meat is browned. Drain off any excess grease.

### Step 3: Assemble and Bake
4. In a large bowl, combine the cooked meat mixture, cooked noodles, **1 (15 ounce) can tomato sauce**, drained **1 (16 ounce) can peas**, drained **1 (11 ounce) can corn niblets**, **2 to 4 tablespoons chili powder**, **salt**, and **pepper**. Stir well until thoroughly combined.
5. Pour the mixture into the prepared baking dish and spread evenly.
6. Cover the top with plenty of grated **cheddar cheese**.
7. Bake for **25 minutes**, or until hot, bubbly, and the cheese is melted to a golden perfection.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole in advance and store covered in the refrigerator for up to 24 hours before baking, adding a few extra minutes to the bake time if cold.
