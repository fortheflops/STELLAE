# Easy Baked Cheesy Green Chili Steak Rolls

> 📜 **Collection:** UK5 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Midge Robertson | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *Tender cubed steaks are browned, then rolled up with a savory filling of corn, green chili, and cheese. The rolls are baked in tomato sauce and topped with more melted cheese for an easy, flavorful meal.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 35 mins | None | 2 steak rolls |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Skillet
* Small bowl
* Baking dish
* Toothpicks

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 large | [[Steaks]] | Cubed, seasoned |
| 1 tablespoon | [[Cooking Oil]] | None |
| 1 (6 ounce) can | [[Corn]] | None |
| 2 heaping tablespoons | [[Green Chili]] | None |
| 1 cup | [[Jack Cheese]] or [[Cheddar Cheese]] | Grated, divided |
| 1 (8 ounce) can | [[Tomato Sauce]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Brown the Steaks
1. Preheat the oven to **350°F**.
2. In a skillet, brown the **2 large seasoned steaks** on both sides in **1 tablespoon cooking oil** until golden brown.

### Step 2: Assemble and Bake
3. In a small bowl, combine the **1 (6 ounce) can corn**, **2 heaping tablespoons green chili**, and **half** of the **1 cup grated jack cheese or cheddar cheese**.
4. Spoon half of this corn mixture into the center of each browned steak.
5. Fold or roll the steaks and secure them with a toothpick or two.
6. Place the steak rolls in a baking dish.
7. Cover the rolls with the **1 (8 ounce) can tomato sauce**.
8. Top with the remaining **cheese**.
9. Bake for **20-25 minutes** until bubbly and golden brown and fragrant.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the rolls ahead of time and keep covered in the refrigerator for up to 24 hours before baking.
