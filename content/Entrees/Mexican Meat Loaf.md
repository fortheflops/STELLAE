# Mexican Meat Loaf

> 📜 **Collection:** UK15 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A simple and flavorful Southwestern-twisted meatloaf featuring ground beef, soft bread crumbs, and zesty tomatoes with green chiles. Baked to juicy perfection, this easy weeknight entree delivers bold flavor with minimal effort.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 1 hour | None | 4 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large bowl
* Shallow baking dish

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 1/2 pounds | [[Ground Beef]] | None |
| 1 cup | [[Bread Crumbs]] | Soft |
| 1 (10 ounce) can | [[Tomatoes and Green Chiles]] | e.g., Ro-Tel |
| 3 tablespoons | [[Onion Flakes]] | Dry |
| 1 1/4 teaspoons | [[Salt]] | None |
| 1/4 teaspoon | [[Garlic Salt]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mix and Shape
1. Preheat the oven to **375°F**.
2. In a large bowl, thoroughly combine the **1 1/2 pounds ground beef**, **1 cup soft bread crumbs**, **1 (10 ounce) can tomatoes and green chiles**, **3 tablespoons dry onion flakes**, **1 1/4 teaspoons salt**, and **1/4 teaspoon garlic salt**.
3. With moistened hands, shape the mixture into a firm loaf.

### Step 2: Bake
4. Place the shaped loaf into a shallow baking dish.
5. Bake for **1 hour** until completely cooked through and nicely browned on top.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftover meatloaf tightly wrapped in the refrigerator for up to 3 days.
