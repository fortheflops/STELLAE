# Skillet Southwest Chili Poorboys

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty One | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A quick and flavorful skillet chili featuring ground beef, tomatoes, beans, and green chilies. Perfect for serving hot over buns and garnishing with your favorite toppings.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 10 mins | None | 4 servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Skillet
* Can opener

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 lb | [[Ground Beef]] | ground |
| 1 Tbsp | [[Chili Powder]] |  |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Garlic Powder]] |  |
| 15 oz | [[Tomatoes]] | Del Monte ClassicCrush |
| 15 oz | [[Beans]] | black or kidney beans, drained |
| 4 oz | [[Green Chilies]] | diced |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Cooking
1. Brown **1 lb ground beef** in a skillet, then drain the excess fat.
2. Add **1 Tbsp chili powder**, **1/4 tsp garlic powder**, **15 oz tomatoes**, **15 oz beans**, and **4 oz green chilies** to the skillet.
3. Simmer uncovered for 10 minutes, stirring occasionally until bubbling and thickened.
4. Garnish with grated cheese, green onions, or cilantro, if desired, and serve over buns.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Chili can be prepared ahead of time and reheated on the stove before serving over warm buns.
