# Snow Cap Pot Roast

> 📜 **Collection:** Burcea Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A comforting classic pot roast braised with red burgundy, spices, and topped with a creamy sour cream finish. This tender, slow-cooked beef dish serves 6 to 8 people.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 3 hours | None | 6 to 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Dutch oven
* Roasting pan

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 4 lb | [[Beef Roast]] | With bone |
| 1 clove | [[Garlic]] | Minced |
| 1/2 tsp | [[Dried Marjoram]] | Crushed |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Ground Nutmeg]] | Ground |
| 1 dash | [[Salt]] | To taste |
| 1 dash | [[Pepper]] | To taste |
| 1 cup | [[Red Burgundy Wine]] | Red |
| 1/2 cup | [[Sour Cream]] | For topping |
| 2 tbsp | [[Green Onion]] | Chopped |
| 1 dash | [[Paprika]] | For garnish |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Braising
1. Brown one **4 lb. beef roast** over high heat with **1 clove garlic, minced**.
2. Add **1/2 tsp. dried marjoram, crushed**, **1/4 tsp. ground nutmeg**, and a **dash each salt & pepper**.
3. Pour **1 cup red burgundy** over meat.
4. Cook, covered, in slow oven (325°) for 2 1/2 to 3 hrs until the meat is fork-tender.

### Step 2: Serving
5. To serve, remove bone & excess fat. Top meat with **1/2 cup sour cream**, sprinkle with **2 tbs. chopped gr. onion** & **dash paprika** — make gravy if desired.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Can be cooked a day ahead and gently reheated before adding the sour cream topping.
