# Enchilada Casserole

> 📜 **Collection:** Georgia Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A comforting, classic Enchilada Casserole featuring seasoned ground beef, black olives, and melted cheddar cheese rolled in tortillas. Baked to bubbly perfection in a savory, slightly diluted enchilada sauce.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Filling and Sauce
1. In a skillet, brown the **1 lb. ground beef**; drain excess fat if necessary. Add the chopped **1 onion**, **2 cloves garlic**, **1 pinch oregano**, and **1 tsp chili powder**. Cook for **5 to 10 minutes** until the onion is soft and translucent.
2. In a separate pan, dilute the **1 can enchilada sauce** with **1/2 can water**. Heat slightly until warm.

### Step 2: Assemble and Bake
3. Soak the **1 pkg. tortillas** briefly in the warm **enchilada sauce** to soften them.
4. Spread each soaked tortilla with a portion of the cooked **ground beef** mixture, some of the **1 lb. grated cheddar cheese**, and the chopped **1 small can black olives**. Roll them up and place them seam-side down in a square baking dish.
5. Sprinkle the remaining **1 lb. grated cheddar cheese** over the rolled tortillas, then pour the remaining **enchilada sauce** evenly over the top.
6. Bake for **12 to 20 minutes** in a preheated **400°F** oven until the cheese is melted, bubbly, and slightly golden brown at the edges.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The casserole can be fully assembled, covered tightly, and refrigerated up to 24 hours before baking. Add 5 to 10 minutes to the baking time if cooking directly from the refrigerator.
