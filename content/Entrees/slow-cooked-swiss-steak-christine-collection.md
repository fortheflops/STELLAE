# Slow Cooked Swiss Steak

> 📜 **Collection:** Christine Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *Experience tender beef simmered to perfection in a rich celery and onion sauce. This comforting slow cooker dish makes weeknight dinners effortless.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and Bounding
1. In a shallow bowl, combine **3/4 c. flour**, **1 tsp pepper**, and **1/4 tsp salt**.
2. Cut **1 round steak** into serving pieces; dredge in the flour mixture.
3. In a skillet, brown the steak in **2 tbsp margarine** (or butter), then transfer to a slow cooker.

### Step 2: Slow Cooking
4. Combine remaining ingredients including **1 can cream of celery soup**, **1 1/3 c. water**, **1 c. sliced celery**, **1/2 c. chopped onion**, **1 minced garlic clove**, and **1-3 tsp bouillon granules**, and pour over the steak.
5. Cover and cook on low for **8-9 hours** or until the meat is tender and easily pierced with a fork.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the meat and vegetables in the slow cooker insert the night before and refrigerate; cook on low the following day.
