# Chicken Tetrazzini

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Sauce
1. Cook the **1/2 cup onions (chopped)** in **1 tablespoon fat** in a skillet until tender.

### Step 2: Combine Ingredients
2. Add **1 can mushroom soup**, **1 - 6 ounce can evaporated milk**, and **1/3 cup parmesan cheese (grated)** to the skillet; heat and stir until well combined and warm.
3. Stir in **1 cup chicken (cooked, cubed)** along with the remaining ingredients: **1 - 3 ounce can mushrooms (sliced, drained)**, **1/2 cup ripe olives (sliced)**, **2 tablespoons parsley (chopped)**, **2 teaspoons lemon juice**, and **6 ounces noodles or spaghetti**.

### Step 3: Bake
4. Pour the mixture into a greased casserole dish.
5. Sprinkle with additional **parmesan cheese (grated)**.
6. Bake at **370 degrees** for **20-25 minutes** until bubbly and golden brown.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole ahead of time, cover tightly, and refrigerate for up to 24 hours before baking.
