# Simple Grilled Korean BBQ Beef

> 📜 **Collection:** GailCollection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A quick and easy Korean BBQ recipe featuring thin-sliced short ribs or flank steak marinated in a sweet and savory soy-garlic-sesame sauce and grilled hot and fast.*

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 45 mins | 10 mins | 30 mins | Varies (approx. 2 packages) |

* Mixing bowl
* Knife
* Cutting board

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Marinate the Meat
1. To cut the **approximately 2 packages lean short ribs** as thin as possible, slice when partly frozen.
2. Tenderize the sliced pieces with a knife, cutting meat along the bone (if using short ribs). Alternatively, you can also use flank steak.
3. In a bowl, combine the marinade ingredients: **1 cup soy sauce**, **10 tablespoons sugar**, **1 teaspoon accent**, **1/2 tablespoon salad oil**, **1-2 cloves mashed garlic**, **dash salt + pepper**, **1 tablespoon sesame seed**, and chopped **green onion**.
4. Add the prepared meat to the marinade and let sit for **1/2 hour**.

### Step 2: Grill and Serve
5. Preheat a hibachi or barbecue grill to high heat.
6. Cook the **lean short ribs** for a couple of seconds per side until caramelized and done.
7. Remove from heat and eat hot.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Marinated meat can be covered and refrigerated for up to 24 hours before grilling.
