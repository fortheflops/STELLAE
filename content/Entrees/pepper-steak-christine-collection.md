# Pepper Steak

> 📜 **Collection:** Christine Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A classic home-style pepper steak recipe featuring tender beef, crisp onions, and green peppers simmered in a savory tomato sauce. Perfect for a hearty weeknight dinner served hot.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 30 mins | None | 4 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Skillet
* Knife
* Cutting board

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 lbs | [[Thin Steak]] | Sliced |
| 2 large | [[Onions]] | Sliced |
| 2 | [[Green Peppers]] | Sliced |
| 1 large can | [[Tomato Sauce]] | None |
| 3 | [[Beef Bouillon Cubes]] | None |
| 2 tbsp | [[Sugar]] | None |
| As needed | [[Butter]] | For browning |
| To taste | [[Salt]] | None |
| To taste | [[Pepper]] | None |
| To taste | [[Garlic Salt]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Cooking Instructions
1. Brown the **2 lbs thin steak** in **butter**, **salt**, **pepper**, and **garlic salt** until browned and cooked through.
2. Sear the **2 large onions** and **2 green peppers** in the pan, then add the **1 large can tomato sauce** and **3 beef bouillon cubes** along with **2 tbsp sugar**.
3. Simmer until done, until the meat is tender and the sauce has thickened to a rich consistency.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Can be prepared ahead and stored in the refrigerator for up to 2 days; reheat gently on the stovetop before serving.
