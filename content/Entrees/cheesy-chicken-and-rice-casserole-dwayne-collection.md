# Cheesy Chicken and Rice Casserole

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A comforting and easy family casserole featuring tender chicken breasts baked over seasoned rice and vegetables, topped with melted cheddar cheese. This hearty one-dish meal comes together quickly for a satisfying weeknight dinner.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 45 mins | None | 4 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 12x8 inch baking dish
* Mixing spoon

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 can (10 3/4 oz) | [[Campbell's Cream of Chicken Soup]] | undiluted |
| 1/3 cup | [[Water]] | none |
| 3/4 cup | [[Uncooked Long Grain White Rice]] | uncooked |
| 2 cups | [[Fresh or Frozen Vegetables]] | peas and carrots recommended |
| 1/2 tsp | [[Onion Powder]] | none |
| 4 | [[Skinless Chicken Breasts]] | none |
| 1/2 cup | [[Shredded Cheddar Cheese]] | shredded |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Assembly
1. Stir **1 can (10 3/4 oz) Campbell's Cream of Chicken Soup**, **1/3 cup water**, **3/4 cup uncooked long grain white rice**, **2 cups fresh or frozen vegetables**, and **1/2 tsp onion powder** together in a **12x8 baking dish**.
2. Top the rice mixture with **4 skinless chicken breasts**, seasoning the chicken as desired.

### Step 2: Baking
3. Bake at 375°F for **45 min** or until the chicken is cooked through, the rice is tender, and the mixture is bubbling.
4. Top with **1/2 cup shredded cheddar cheese** during the last few minutes until melted and bubbly.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole in the baking dish, cover tightly with foil, and refrigerate up to 24 hours before baking; add a few extra minutes to the baking time if baking straight from the refrigerator.
