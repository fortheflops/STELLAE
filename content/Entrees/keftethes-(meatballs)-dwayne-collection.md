# Keftethes (Meatballs)

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A classic Greek recipe for Keftethes, savory herb-infused meatballs flattened into fritters and pan-fried to golden perfection. Flavored with fresh mint, garlic, and white wine, they make a perfect appetizer or main course.*

## Instructions

### Preparation and Frying
1. Place the **1 pound ground beef** and **1 pound ground veal or pork** in a large mixing bowl. Add the **2 eggs**, and combine with the **1 large chopped onion**, **1 tablespoon crushed mint leaves**, **2 tablespoons minced parsley**, **2 minced garlic cloves**, **2 tablespoons tomato sauce**, **1 cup soaked and squeezed saltine crackers**, **1/2 cup white wine**, and **salt and pepper** to taste.
2. Knead the mixture thoroughly with your hands until all ingredients are well mixed and cohesive.
3. On a floured board, shape the meat mixture into balls, then flatten them gently with your palms into fritter shapes. Dust each fritter lightly with **flour**.
4. Heat **cooking oil** in a skillet over medium heat. Fry the fritters until they are beautifully browned on both sides and cooked through to your desired doneness.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The meat mixture can be shaped into fritters and stored covered in the refrigerator for up to 24 hours before frying.
