# Jennie's Chili Egg Puff

> 📜 **Collection:** General Archive | ✍️ **Attribution:** Jennie | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A light and fluffy baked egg casserole loaded with green chiles, cottage cheese, and Monterey Jack cheese. Blended together for an easy, delightful crustless quiche or soufflé.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 35 mins | 10 mins | 8-10 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 9x13 inch baking dish
* Blender

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 | [[Eggs]] | Whole |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Flour]] | All-purpose |
| 1 Teaspoon | [[Baking Powder]] | None |
| 1/2 Teaspoon | [[Salt]] | None |
| 1 Pint (16 oz) | [[Cottage Cheese]] | Small curd |
| 1 Pound | [[Monterey Jack Cheese]] | Grated |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Butter]] | Melted |
| 2 Cans (4-7 oz each) | [[Chopped Green Chiles]] | Drained |
| Optional | [[Tomatoes]] | Sliced for garnish |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Blend the Base
1. Preheat your oven to **350°F (175°C)** and generously grease a **9x13 inch baking dish**.
2. Place the **10 eggs** in a blender. Add the **1/2 cup flour**, **1 teaspoon baking powder**, **1/2 teaspoon salt**, **1 pint cottage cheese**, **1 pound Monterey Jack cheese**, and **1/2 cup melted butter**.
3. Blend on medium speed until all the ingredients are well combined and the mixture is completely smooth.

### Step 2: Bake and Serve
4. Stir in the drained **2 cans chopped green chiles** by hand or pulse once in the blender.
5. Pour the **egg mixture** into the prepared baking dish.
6. If desired, arrange sliced **tomatoes** on top of the mixture before baking.
7. Bake for **35 minutes**, or until the center is set, the edges are golden, and the casserole has puffed up.
8. Let stand for **5-10 minutes** before cutting into squares and serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Leftovers can be stored covered in the refrigerator for up to 3-4 days and reheated gently.
