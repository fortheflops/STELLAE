## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Coat the Veal
1. In a shallow dish, combine the **1/3 cup all-purpose flour**, **1 teaspoon salt**, **1/4 teaspoon black pepper**, **1/2 teaspoon basil**, and **1/4 teaspoon thyme**. Dredge the **2 1/2 pounds veal** cubes in the flour mixture to coat all sides evenly.

### Step 2: Brown the Veal and Sauté Vegetables
2. In a large Dutch oven over low heat, melt the **6 tablespoons margarine** with the **3 teaspoons olive oil**. Add the coated **veal** and cook gently, stirring just until the outside is no longer raw but not browned. Remove the **veal** from the pan and set aside.
3. Add the **1/2 cup onion**, **3 green peppers**, **1 clove garlic**, and **2 cups mushrooms** to the Dutch oven. Season with a little more **salt** and **black pepper** and cook for **15 minutes**, or until the vegetables are softened.

### Step 3: Simmer or Bake
4. Return the **veal** to the pot. Add the **1 can chicken broth**, **3/8 cup white wine**, **1 bay leaf**, **3 tablespoons lemon juice**, **2 tablespoons parsley**, and optional **1 can tomato sauce**. Stir everything together.
5. **Stovetop Method:** Bring the mixture to a simmer, then reduce heat to low, cover, and cook for **1.5 to 2 hours**, until the veal is tender.
6. **Oven Method:** Alternatively, pour the entire mixture into a casserole dish, cover, and bake in a preheated **325°F (165°C)** oven for **1.5 hours**, or until tender and fragrant.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftovers in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days. Reheat gently on the stovetop before serving.
