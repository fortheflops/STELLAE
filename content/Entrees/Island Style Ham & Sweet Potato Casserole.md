# Island Style Ham & Sweet Potato Casserole

> 📜 **Collection:** John B Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *Discover a delightful sweet and savory casserole featuring golden sautéed ham, pineapple, and green peppers in a tangy glaze, topped with creamy mashed sweet potatoes and baked until bubbly.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 25 mins | 20 mins | None | 4-6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Saucepan
* Potato masher
* Skillet
* Small bowl
* 9-inch pie plate

---
## Ingredients

### Potatoes
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 6 med | [[Sweet Potatoes]] | Peeled and cubed for boiling |
| 1 T | [[Oleo]] | Plus extra butter for mashing |
| To Taste | [[Salt]] & [[Pepper]] | For seasoning |
| Pinch | [[Nutmeg]] | Ground |
| As Needed | [[Milk]] | To whip potatoes |

### Filling
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 c | [[Ham]] | Cut up, cooked |
| 2 T | [[Margarine]] | For sautéing |
| 1/2 c | [[Green Pepper]] | Or [[Pimento]] strips |
| 1 can (#2) | [[Pineapple Chunks]] | Drained |
| 1 T | [[Cornstarch]] | None |
| 2 T | [[Brown Sugar]] | None |
| 3/4 c | [[Pineapple Juice]] | None |
| 2 T | [[Vinegar]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Sweet Potatoes
1. Cook and mash the **6 med sweet potatoes**. Add **1 T oleo**, **salt & pepper**, **pinch nutmeg**, and **milk** to whip until light and fluffy.

### Step 2: Sauté the Ham and Filling
2. In a skillet, melt **2 T margarine** and sauté the **2 c cut up cooked ham**, stirring constantly until golden brown.
3. Add the **1/2 c green pepper** (or pimento) strips and **1 can (#2) drained pineapple chunks**, cooking for **2-3 minutes** until slightly softened.

### Step 3: Make the Glaze and Combine
4. In a small bowl, combine the **2 T brown sugar** and **1 T cornstarch**, then stir in the **3/4 c pineapple juice** and **2 T vinegar**.
5. Add the liquid mixture to the skillet and cook, stirring until the sauce is clear and thick.

### Step 4: Assemble and Bake
6. Pour the ham and pineapple mixture into a **9-inch pie plate**.
7. Drop spoonfuls of the whipped sweet potatoes on top of the ham mixture.
8. Bake at **350°F** for **15-20 minutes** until bubbling hot and golden on top.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole up to 24 hours in advance, cover tightly, and store in the refrigerator until ready to bake.
