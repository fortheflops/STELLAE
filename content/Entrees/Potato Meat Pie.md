# Potato Meat Pie

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A classic layered savory pie featuring thinly sliced potatoes, onions, and savory ham baked inside a flaky double pastry crust. Finished with a rich infusion of warm heavy cream poured right before serving.*

| Several sprigs | [[Parsley]] | Chopped |
| 1 Slice | [[Ham]] | ½" thick, cut into pieces |
| 3 Tbsp | [[Butter]] or [[Margarine]] | Divided for dotting |
| 1 ¼ Cups | [[Heavy Cream]] | Warm |
| 1 | [[Double Pastry]] | Prepared |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Assembly
1. Slice the **6-7 medium potatoes** and **2 medium onions** very thin.
2. Mix the sliced vegetables with **1 ½ tsp salt**, **pepper**, and **several sprigs chopped parsley**.
3. Preheat oven to **375 degrees**.
4. Line a casserole dish with half of the **double pastry**.
5. Pile in half of the seasoned potato mixture.
6. Cut the **1 slice ham** into pieces and cover the potato layer.
7. Add the rest of the potatoes and dot the top with **3 tbsp butter or margarine**.
8. Cover with the unpunctured top crust and seal the edges securely.

### Step 2: Bake and Finish
9. Bake for **1 hour** until the crust is golden brown and the filling is tender.
10. Remove pie from the oven, and cut a small circle in the crust about the size of a half-dollar.
11. Carefully pour the **1 ¼ cups warm heavy cream** into the hole, then replace the pastry cutout.
12. Let the pie stand for **10 to 15 minutes** before slicing and serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the pie up to a few hours in advance, cover tightly, and refrigerate before baking. Let the pie stand for 10-15 minutes after baking for best slicing consistency.
