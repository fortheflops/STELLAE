# Lazy Clam Linguini

> 📜 **Collection:** UK5 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Ms Lisa Mc Farland | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A quick and easy weeknight entree featuring canned white clam sauce enriched with white wine and creme fraiche, tossed over al dente linguini. Finished with a drizzle of chili oil and fresh sprouts for a burst of flavor.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5 mins | 10 mins | None | 2–4 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large pot
* Colander
* Saucepan
* Wooden spoon

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| As Needed | [[Linguini]] | or flavored pasta like Vesuvio |
| 1 can | [[Progresso White Clam Sauce]] | undrained |
| To Taste | [[Chili Oil]] | for serving |
| As Needed | [[Creme Fraiche]] | to enrich sauce |
| As Needed | [[White Wine]] | a splash |
| Optional | [[Sprouts]] | fresh |
| To Taste | Seasoning | as desired |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Cook the Pasta
1. Cook your chosen **[[Linguini]]** in a large pot of boiling salted water until it is "al dente." Drain well using a colander.

### Step 2: Prepare the Clam Sauce
2. While the pasta is cooking, gently heat the **1 can** of **[[Progresso White Clam Sauce]]** in a saucepan over medium-low heat.
3. Stir in a splash of **[[White Wine]]** and some **[[Creme Fraiche]]** to enrich the sauce.
4. Season the sauce to your liking with **Seasoning**, and add a drizzle of **[[Chili Oil]]** for some heat until warm and aromatic.

### Step 3: Combine and Serve
5. Toss the cooked, drained pasta with the prepared clam sauce until well coated.
6. If using, gently fold in some fresh **[[Sprouts]]** at the end.
7. Serve immediately while hot.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Best served immediately upon preparation; the sauce can be prepared a few minutes ahead and kept warm on low heat.
