# Snapper Florentine

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A delightful baked snapper Florentine featuring tender fish fillets over wilted spinach, topped with a creamy dill and lemon sauce. This quick and elegant dish comes together in under 30 minutes for a healthy weeknight dinner.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 10 mins | None | 4 Servings |

* Wire whip
* Frying pan
* Platter

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Bake the Fish
1. Arrange **1 pound fish fillets** in a shallow baking dish (9x13).
2. Season the fish with **salt** and **pepper** to taste.
3. With a wire whip, blend **3/4 cup sour cream**, **1/3 cup mayonnaise**, **2 tablespoons flour**, **2 tablespoons lemon juice**, and **1/4 teaspoon dill weed**.
4. Spread the creamy mixture evenly over the **fish fillets**.
5. Bake at 400 degrees Fahrenheit until flaky and opaque — about **10 minutes**.

### Step 2: Wilt Spinach and Assemble
6. Meanwhile, put **2 pounds spinach** in a frying pan with **water** clinging to the leaves.
7. Cover and cook over medium heat until the **spinach** wilts down.
8. Drain the **spinach** thoroughly and arrange it on a serving platter.
9. Place the baked **fish fillets** directly on top of the wilted **spinach** and serve immediately.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The creamy sauce can be mixed a few hours in advance and stored in the refrigerator until ready to assemble and bake.
