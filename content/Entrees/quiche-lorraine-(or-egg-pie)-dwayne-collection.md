# Quiche Lorraine (Or Egg Pie)

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Emme | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A classic rustic Quiche Lorraine recipe featuring a rich egg and half-and-half custard, savory bacon, and Swiss cheese in a flaky crust. Originally from Emme's personal kitchen archives.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Filling
1. Preheat your oven to **450°F**.
2. Prepare a **10-inch pie crust** and keep it refrigerated until ready to use.
3. Grate **1 cup Swiss cheese** and fry **10 slices bacon** until crisp, then crumble (or chop **1 cup leftover ham** very small).
4. Sprinkle the **crumbled bacon** and **grated Swiss cheese** evenly across the bottom of the prepared **pie crust**.

### Step 2: Mixing the Custard
5. In a mixing bowl, combine **4 eggs**, **2 cups half-and-half**, a **pinch sugar**, a **pinch nutmeg**, **3/4 tsp salt**, **1/2 tsp pepper**, and a **dash red pepper**.
6. Mix together thoroughly and pour the **custard mixture** directly over the **bacon** and **cheese** in the **pie crust**.

### Step 3: Baking and Resting
7. Bake at **450°F** for **12 minutes**, then reduce the oven heat to **325°F** and continue to cook for **25 to 30 more minutes**.
8. Test for doneness with a knife like a custard; the center should be set and the knife should come out clean.
9. Let the quiche set out for about **half an hour** after baking before cutting for cleanest slices.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** You can do all the prep ahead of time, including mixing the custard part, and put everything together at the last minute before baking.
