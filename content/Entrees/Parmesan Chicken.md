# Parmesan Chicken

> 📜 **Collection:** Jill Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A simple recipe for pan-fried Parmesan Chicken cutlets, coated in a seasoned fresh breadcrumb and cheese mixture.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 6 mins | None | 4 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Shallow dish
* Large skillet
* Paper towels

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 4 | [[Chicken Breasts]] | boneless and skinless |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] | beaten |
| 2 tablespoons | [[Water]] | none |
| 1 1/2 cups | [[Bread Crumbs]] | fresh |
| 1/2 cup | [[Parmesan Cheese]] | freshly grated |
| 3/4 teaspoon | [[Thyme]] | dried |
| 1/2 teaspoon+ | [[Salt]] | divided |
| 1 dash+ | [[Black Pepper]] | divided |
| 3 tablespoons | [[Olive Oil]] or [[Butter]] | none |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Coat
1. Prepare the **4 [[Chicken Breasts]]** by cutting them in half horizontally to create thinner cutlets. Season the cutlets lightly on both sides with **1/2 teaspoon+ [[Salt]]** and **1 dash+ [[Black Pepper]]**.
2. In a shallow dish, combine the **1 1/2 cups fresh [[Bread Crumbs]]**, **1/2 cup freshly grated [[Parmesan Cheese]]**, **3/4 teaspoon dried [[Thyme]]**, **1/2 teaspoon+ [[Salt]]**, and **1 dash+ [[Black Pepper]]**.
3. In a second shallow dish, beat the **2 [[Eggs]]** with the **2 tablespoons [[Water]]**.
4. Dip each chicken cutlet first into the egg mixture, allowing the excess to drip off. Then, press the cutlet firmly into the bread crumb mixture to coat both sides.

### Step 2: Sauté and Serve
5. In a large skillet, heat the **3 tablespoons [[Olive Oil]] or [[Butter]]** over medium-high heat.
6. Sauté the **4 [[Chicken Breasts]]** for about **3 minutes** per side, until golden brown and cooked through.
7. Remove the chicken from the skillet and drain on paper towels. Serve warm.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftover chicken in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days.
