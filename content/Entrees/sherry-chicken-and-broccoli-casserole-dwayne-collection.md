# Sherry Chicken and Broccoli Casserole

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A comforting, classic mid-century casserole featuring tender chicken breasts and broccoli baked in a rich sherry-infused mushroom sauce. Topped with crispy canned onion rings and melted grated cheese for a perfect family dinner.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 1 hr | None | 4 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Skillet
* Baking dish
* Mixing bowl

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 4 | [[Chicken Breasts]] | deboned after cooking |
| 2 tbsp | [[Butter]] | for pan-frying |
| 1 head | [[Broccoli]] | cooked as directed |
| 1 can (10.5 oz) | [[Cream of Mushroom Soup]] |  |
| 2 tbsp | [[Sherry]] | dry sherry preferred |
| 2 tbsp | [[Milk]] | small amount to thin the soup |
| 1 cup | [[Canned Onion Rings]] | such as French's crispy fried onions |
| 1/2 cup | [[Grated Cheese]] | such as Cheddar or Swiss |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep the Chicken and Broccoli
1. In a skillet, pan-fry the **4 chicken breasts** in **2 tbsp butter** until cooked through and golden. Let cool, then debone and cut into bite-sized pieces.
2. Cook the **1 head broccoli** as directed on the package or steam until tender-crisp. Place the cooked **broccoli** in the bottom of a greased baking dish.
3. Layer the cooked, deboned **chicken breasts** evenly on top of the **broccoli**.

### Step 2: Sauce and Assembly
4. In a mixing bowl, mix **1 can cream of mushroom soup** with **2 tbsp sherry** and a **small amount of milk** (about **2 tbsp**) until smooth.
5. Pour the soup mixture evenly over the **chicken** and **broccoli**.
6. Sprinkle **1 cup canned onion rings** over all.

### Step 3: Baking
7. Bake in a preheated oven at **350°F** for **50 minutes** until the sauce is hot and bubbling.
8. Sprinkle **1/2 cup grated cheese** over the top during the last **10 minutes** of baking, until the cheese is melted, bubbly, and lightly browned.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole (without the onion rings and cheese) up to 24 hours in advance, cover, and refrigerate. Add the onion rings and cheese during the designated baking times.
