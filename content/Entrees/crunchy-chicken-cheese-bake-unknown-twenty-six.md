# Crunchy Chicken-Cheese Bake

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A delightful and comforting baked chicken dish topped with swiss cheese, cream of chicken soup, and crunchy herb-seasoned stuffing. Perfect for a cozy weeknight family dinner.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 25 mins | None | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 3 qt oblong baking dish
* Mixing bowl

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 8 | [[Chicken breast halves]] | Skinless, boneless |
| 4 slices | [[Swiss cheese]] | Cut in half |
| 1 (10 3/4 oz) | [[Cream of chicken soup]] | Condensed |
| 8 | [[Tomato slices]] | Thin |
| 2 T. | [[Margarine]] | Melted |
| 1/2 cup | [[Herb-seasoned stuffing]] | Pepperidge Farm, crushed |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Assembly and Baking
1. In a **3 qt oblong baking dish**, place **8 skinless boneless chicken breast halves**.
2. Top each with **4 slices Swiss cheese**.
3. Stir **1 (10 3/4 oz) cream of chicken soup** and spread over **4 slices Swiss cheese**.
4. Top with **8 thin tomato slices**.
5. Combine **2 T. melted margarine** and **1/2 cup herb-seasoned stuffing**, then sprinkle over **8 thin tomato slices**.
6. Bake at **400°** for **25 mins** until **8 skinless boneless chicken breast halves** are no longer pink inside.
7. Garnish with **oregano** before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the dish up to a point before adding the crunchy stuffing topping, cover, and refrigerate until ready to bake.
