# Simple Spaghetti

> 📜 **Collection:** UK15 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A quick and classic weeknight entree featuring simple boiled [[Spaghetti]] topped with warm [[Garden Harvest Sauce]]. Perfect for a fast and comforting family dinner.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5 mins | 10 mins | None | 4 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large stockpot
* Colander
* Saucepan
* Wooden spoon

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 pound (8 ounces) | [[Spaghetti]] | dry |
| 1 (27.5 ounce) jar | [[Garden Harvest Sauce]] | Ragu Lite |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Cook the Pasta and Sauce
1. Cook the **1/2 pound (8 ounces) spaghetti** in a large pot of boiling water according to the package directions until al dente.
2. While the **spaghetti** is cooking, heat the **1 (27.5 ounce) jar garden harvest sauce** in a saucepan over medium heat until warm and bubbling gently.
3. Drain the cooked **spaghetti** in a colander and serve immediately topped with the heated **garden harvest sauce**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftover pasta and sauce separately in airtight containers in the refrigerator for up to 3 days.
