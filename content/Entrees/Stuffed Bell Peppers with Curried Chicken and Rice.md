# Stuffed Bell Peppers with Curried Chicken and Rice

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Sauté
1. Preheat oven to **375°F (190°C)**.
2. Cut the **four to six large bell peppers** in half lengthwise and remove the seeds and membranes. Place them cut-side up in a baking dish.
3. In a skillet, heat the **one tablespoon olive oil** over medium heat. Add the **one medium onion** and cook until softened, about **5 minutes**. Add the **two cloves garlic** and cook for another minute until fragrant.

### Step 2: Mix Filling and Fill Peppers
4. In a large bowl, combine the **one pound boneless, skinless chicken**, **one cup cooked rice**, and the sautéed onion and garlic mixture.
5. Add the **one tablespoon curry powder**, **1/2 cup mayonnaise**, and **1/4 cup chopped fresh cilantro**. Season with **salt** and **black pepper** to taste. Mix everything together until well combined.
6. Fill each bell pepper half with the curried chicken and rice mixture.

### Step 3: Bake and Serve
7. Pour about **1/4 inch** of water into the bottom of the baking dish to help steam the peppers.
8. Cover the baking dish with foil and bake for **30-35 minutes**, or until the peppers are tender.
9. If desired, remove the foil for the last **5-10 minutes** of baking and top with **shredded cheese** until it is melted and bubbly.
10. Let stand for a few minutes before serving until set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the stuffed peppers a day ahead, cover, and refrigerate until ready to bake. Add an extra 5 minutes to the baking time if cooking straight from the fridge.
