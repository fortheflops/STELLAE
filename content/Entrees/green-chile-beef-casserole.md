# Green Chile Beef Casserole

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Five | ✍️ **Attribution:** Dorothy Kinsel | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A comforting layered casserole featuring seasoned beef, corn tortillas, green chiles, and melted Longhorn cheese. This hearty dish bakes to bubbly perfection for a comforting family meal.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 1 hour | None | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Skillet
* 9x13 Casserole pan
* Aluminum foil

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 lbs | [[Hamburger meat]] | Ground |
| 1 medium | [[Onion]] | Diced |
| To taste | [[Salt and pepper]] | None |
| 1 tsp | [[Garlic salt]] | None |
| 1 can | [[Chicken mushroom soup]] | None |
| 1 can | [[Celery soup]] | None |
| 1 can | [[Enchilada sauce]] | Ashley brand |
| 1 can | [[Green chile]] | El Paso brand, chopped |
| As needed | [[Longhorn cheese]] | Grated, enough to cover |
| 1 pkg | [[Thin tortillas]] | Corn |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Layer
1. Brown **2 lbs hamburger meat** with **1 medium onion**, **salt, pepper & garlic**, and **1 tsp garlic salt** in a skillet until fully cooked.
2. Line a long **9x13 casserole pan** with **thin tortillas** cut into quarters.
3. Put **1/2 meat mixture** on top of the tortillas.
4. Repeat the tortillas and meat layers.

### Step 2: Sauce and Bake
5. Mix **1 can chicken mushroom soup** heated with **1 can celery soup**, and spread over the meat mixture.
6. Add **1 can enchilada sauce** and **1 can green chile**.
7. Top generously with **grated Longhorn cheese**.
8. Cover the pan with **aluminum foil** and bake at 350° for 1 hour until bubbly and golden. Serve hot.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole ahead of time, cover, and refrigerate for up to 24 hours before baking as directed.
