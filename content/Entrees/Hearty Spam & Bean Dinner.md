# Hearty Spam & Bean Dinner

> 📜 **Collection:** UK15 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A comforting, vintage skillet dinner featuring savory pork and beans, canned chili, and diced spam. Topped with crispy fried onion rings, this hearty meal comes together in just twenty minutes.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5 mins | 15 mins | None | 4-6 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large saucepan
* Can opener
* Wooden spoon
* Serving bowl

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3 cans | [[Beans]] | Pork & Beans recommended |
| 1 1/2 to 2 cans | [[Chili]] | canned |
| 1 can | [[Spam]] | diced |
| 1 small | [[Onion]] | chopped |
| 1/4 to 1/2 cup | [[Water]] | as needed |
| To Taste | [[Salt]] | optional |
| To Taste | [[Bacon Drippings]] | for flavor |
| As Needed | [[Onion Rings]] | Canned fried |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Simmer the Beans
1. Open the **3 cans beans** and place them in a large saucepan over medium heat.
2. Add **1/4 to 1/2 cup water**, **salt** to taste, and **bacon drippings** to taste.
3. Mix well and continue heating for about **10 minutes**, stirring occasionally until bubbling gently.

### Step 2: Combine and Finish
4. While the beans are heating, dice the **1 can spam**.
5. After ten minutes, add the diced **1 can spam**, chopped **1 small onion**, and the **1 1/2 to 2 cans chili** to the beans.
6. Mix thoroughly and heat for **5 more minutes** until piping hot throughout.
7. Place everything in a serving bowl and generously sprinkle with canned fried **onion rings**.
8. Serve immediately while hot and crispy.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftovers in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days; reheat on the stovetop or in the microwave.
