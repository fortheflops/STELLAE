# Never Fail Brisket of Beef

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** N. Sahn | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A foolproof, tender braised beef brisket flavored with onion soup mix and brown sugar. Perfect for preparing ahead and slicing easily the next day.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 3.5 hours | Overnight | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Heavy duty aluminum foil
* Roasting pan with lid
* Knife
* Cutting board

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 4-5 lbs | [[Beef Brisket]] | Whole |
| 1 clove | [[Garlic]] | Peeled and halved |
| 1 pinch | [[Accent Seasoning]] | Monosodium glutamate |
| 1/2 cup | [[Brown Sugar]] | Packed |
| 1 pkg | [[Onion Soup Mix]] | Dry |
| 1 pinch | [[Oregano]] | Dried |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Braise
1. Rub the **4-5 lbs beef brisket** with **1 clove garlic** and sprinkle with **1 pinch Accent seasoning**.
2. Line a roasting pan with heavy duty aluminum foil in the bottom.
3. Sprinkle half of the **1 pkg onion soup mix** and **1 pinch oregano** on the foil, place the **4-5 lbs beef brisket** on top, and cover with the rest of the **1 pkg onion soup mix**.
4. Seal tightly with the foil, cover with a lid, and bake at 325°F for **2.5 hours** until fork tender.

### Step 2: Finish and Serve
5. Remove from oven and open the foil. Sprinkle the **1/2 cup brown sugar** on top of the **4-5 lbs beef brisket**.
6. Rest and cool, then refrigerate overnight.
7. The next day, slice the **4-5 lbs beef brisket** thin, remove any excess fat, and replace in the broth without foil and cover.
8. Cook about **1 hour** until hot, tender, and bubbling.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Cook the brisket completely through the initial baking step, add the brown sugar, and refrigerate overnight before slicing and reheating the next day.
