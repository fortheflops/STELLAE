# Never-Fail Brisket Of Beef

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** N. Lake | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A tender and flavorful never-fail beef brisket recipe featuring a savory onion soup mix and brown sugar glaze. Perfectly slow-roasted and designed for effortless make-ahead preparation.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 3 hours 30 mins | Overnight | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Roasting pan
* Heavy duty aluminum foil
* Knife

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 4-5 lb | [[Beef Brisket]] | Whole cut |
| 1 clove | [[Garlic]] | Minced or halved |
| To taste | [[Accent Seasoning]] | Optional flavor enhancer |
| 1/2 cup | [[Brown Sugar]] | For topping |
| 1 pkg | [[Onion Soup Mix]] | Dry soup mix |
| To taste | [[Oregano]] | Dried |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and Initial Baking
1. Rub the **4-5 lb beef brisket** with **1 clove garlic** and sprinkle with **Accent Seasoning**.
2. Line a roasting pan with heavy-duty aluminum foil, then sprinkle half of the **1 pkg onion soup mix** and a pinch of **Oregano** on the foil.
3. Place the **4-5 lb beef brisket** on top of this and add the rest of the **1 pkg onion soup mix**.
4. Cover with foil, seal tightly, and cover the roaster with a lid. Bake in the oven at **325°** for **2 1/2 hrs**.
5. Open the foil and sprinkle **1/2 cup brown sugar** on top of the meat. Remove and allow to cool, then close the foil.

### Step 2: Refrigeration and Finishing
6. Refrigerate the brisket overnight.
7. Slice the **4-5 lb beef brisket** into thin slices and remove any congealed fat.
8. Replace the meat in the broth without foil and cover.
9. Cook about **1 hr** until the meat is hot and tender.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Refrigerate the cooked brisket overnight, slice, remove congealed fat, and reheat in broth for 1 hour before serving.
