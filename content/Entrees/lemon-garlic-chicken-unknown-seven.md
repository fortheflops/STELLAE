# Lemon Garlic Chicken

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Seven | ✍️ **Attribution:** Sande | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A quick and flavorful pan-sautéed chicken dish featuring tender breasts coated in seasoned flour, cooked in garlic-infused butter, and finished with bright lemon juice.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 15 mins | None | 3 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Skillet
* Tongs

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3 | [[Chicken Breasts]] | skinned and boned |
| 1 tsp | [[Salt]] | None |
| 1/2 tsp | [[Black Pepper]] | None |
| 1 cup | [[Flour]] | None |
| 4 tbsp | [[Butter]] | None |
| 2 cloves | [[Garlic]] | sliced |
| 1 cup | [[Lemon Juice]] | None |
| 1 | [[Lemon]] | sliced |

---

## Instructions

### Preparation & Coating
1. Wash and pat dry the **3 chicken breasts**. In a shallow bowl, blend the **1 tsp salt**, **1/2 tsp pepper**, and **1 cup flour**.
2. Dip the chicken breasts into the flour mixture until evenly coated on all sides.

### Cooking & Serving
3. Melt the **4 tbsp butter** in a skillet over medium heat. Add **2 cloves garlic, sliced** and sauté until golden, then discard the garlic.
4. Add the flour-coated chicken breasts to the garlic-infused butter and sauté for 6 to 8 minutes until golden brown and cooked through.
5. Pour in **1 cup lemon juice** and cook for 1 minute.
6. Garnish with **1 lemon, sliced** and serve immediately.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Best enjoyed fresh, but leftovers can be stored in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days and gently reheated.
