# Green Chile & Egg Soufflé Casserole

> 📜 **Collection:** UK15 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A classic, simple, light, and fluffy green chile and egg casserole. This dish puffs up like a soufflé as it bakes, featuring layers of green chiles and Monterey Jack cheese in a rich sour cream and egg custard.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 50 mins | 10 mins | 8-10 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 13x9x2 inch baking dish
* Large bowl
* Wire whisk

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 (8 ounce) can | [[Green Chiles]] | Drained, seeds removed, and patted dry |
| 1 pound | [[Monterey Jack Cheese]] | Grated |
| 12 | [[Eggs]] | Whole |
| 1 (16 ounce) container | [[Sour Cream]] | Dairy |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Layer
1. Preheat the oven to **350°F**. Grease a **13"x9"x2" baking dish**.
2. Scrape the seeds from the **1 (8 ounce) can green chiles**. Drain and pat them dry.
3. Create alternating layers of the **1 (8 ounce) can green chiles** and the grated **1 pound Monterey Jack cheese** in the prepared baking dish.

### Step 2: Mix and Bake
4. In a separate large bowl, combine the **12 eggs** and **1 (16 ounce) container sour cream**. Beat well with a whisk until smooth and fully combined.
5. Pour the egg and sour cream mixture evenly over the layered chiles and cheese.
6. Bake for **45-50 minutes**, or until the casserole is puffed up like a soufflé and set in the center until a knife inserted comes out clean.
7. Let stand for **5-10 minutes** before slicing and serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Leftovers can be stored sealed in the refrigerator for up to 3 days and reheated gently.
