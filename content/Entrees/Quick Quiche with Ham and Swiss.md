# Quick Quiche with Ham and Swiss

> 📜 **Collection:** General Archive | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A classic and quick quiche featuring diced ham and Swiss cheese in a rich, simple custard of heavy cream and eggs. Perfect for a cozy brunch or light dinner.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 40 mins | 10 mins | One 9-inch quiche |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 9-inch pie shell
* Baking sheet
* Medium bowl
* Wire whisk
* Paring knife

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | [[9-inch Pie Shell]] | Unbaked |
| 1 cup | [[Swiss Cheese]] | Grated |
| 1 cup | [[Ham]] | Cooked and diced, or other meat |
| 3 | [[Eggs]] | Whole |
| 1/2 pint (1 cup) | [[Heavy Cream]] | Cold |
| 1/2 teaspoon | [[Salt]] | None |
| 1/4 teaspoon | [[Pepper]] | To taste |
| 1/8 teaspoon | [[Nutmeg]] | To taste |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Layer
1. Preheat oven to **450°F**. Place the **1 unbaked 9-inch pie shell** on a baking sheet.
2. Sprinkle the **1 cup grated Swiss cheese** and **1 cup cooked and diced ham** evenly across the bottom of the pie shell.

### Step 2: Mix the Custard
3. In a medium bowl, beat the **3 eggs**. Whisk in the **1/2 pint heavy cream**, **1/2 teaspoon salt**, **1/4 teaspoon pepper**, and **1/8 teaspoon nutmeg** until completely smooth and well combined.
4. Carefully pour the egg mixture directly over the cheese and ham in the pie shell.

### Step 3: Bake and Serve
5. Bake in the upper third of the oven at **450°F for 8 to 10 minutes**.
6. Reduce the oven temperature to **350°F** and continue to bake for **30 to 40 minutes**, until the custard is set and a knife inserted near the center comes out clean and golden.
7. Let the quiche cool for **10 minutes** before slicing and serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftover quiche covered in the refrigerator for up to 3 days and reheat gently before serving.
