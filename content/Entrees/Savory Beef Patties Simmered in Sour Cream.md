# Savory Beef Patties Simmered in Sour Cream

> 📜 **Collection:** General Archive | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A comforting, old-world recipe featuring ground beef patties browned in bacon drippings and slow-simmered in a rich, tangy sour cream gravy. Perfect for a hearty family dinner.*

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 40 mins | None | 4 servings |

| 1 Small | [[Onion]] | chopped fine |
| 2 Teaspoons | [[Chives]] | chopped |
| As Needed | [[Bacon Drippings]] | for frying |
| 1 Cup | [[Sour Cream]] | divided |
| As Needed | [[Milk]] | for soaking bread |
| To Taste | [[Salt]] | none |
| To Taste | [[Pepper]] | none |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Shape Patties
1. Soak the **6 slices bread** in **milk**, then squeeze nearly dry.
2. Mix the soaked bread with the **1 pound ground beef**, **2 teaspoons chives**, **1 small onion**, **salt**, and **pepper** until well combined.
3. Shape the meat mixture into balls or patties.

### Step 2: Brown and Simmer
4. Fry the patties in **bacon drippings** in a skillet until golden brown on all sides.
5. Add **1/2 cup sour cream** to the skillet.
6. Cover and simmer for **1/2 hour** until the patties are cooked through and tender.
7. Stir in the remaining sour cream, heat thoroughly until hot and bubbly, and serve immediately.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftover patties and gravy in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days.
