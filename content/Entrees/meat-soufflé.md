# Meat Soufflé

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A classic, airy meat soufflé recipe utilizing leftover cooked meat and pantry staples. This elegant dish is perfect for transforming simple ingredients into a sophisticated meal.*

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

## Ingredients

### Base Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/3 cup | [[Butter]] | melted |
| 2 tbsp | [[Flour]] | all-purpose |
| 2 cups | [[Milk]] | whole |
| 1 tsp | [[Salt]] | fine |
| 1/8 tsp | [[Pepper]] | ground |
| 1/2 cup | [[Bread Crumbs]] | stale |

### Filling and Binding
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 cups | [[Cooked Meat]] | finely chopped, cold |
| 3 | [[Eggs]] | yolks and whites separated, whites beaten |
| 1 tbsp | [[Parsley]] | chopped |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Base
1. In a saucepan, melt the **1/3 cup butter**, then stir in the **2 tbsp flour**.
2. Gradually whisk in the **2 cups milk**, **1 tsp salt**, **1/8 tsp pepper**, and **1/2 cup bread crumbs**.
3. Cook for 2 minutes until thickened, then remove from heat.

### Step 2: Combine and Bake
4. Stir in the **2 cups cooked meat**, **3 egg yolks**, and **1 tbsp parsley**.
5. Gently fold in the **3 beaten egg whites** until just combined.
6. Pour into a baking dish and bake for 35 minutes until the top is golden brown and the center is set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The base can be prepared up to 2 hours in advance, but the egg whites should be folded in immediately before baking to ensure proper rise.
