## Recipe
| Measurements      | Ingredients                   |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| **For the Souffle**|                                   |
| 3 to 4 cups       | Mashed [[Sweet Potatoes]]         |
| 1 cup             | [[Granulated Sugar]]              |
| 2                 | [[Eggs]]                        |
| 1 tablespoon      | [[Vanilla Extract]]             |
| 1/2 cup           | [[Milk]]                        |
| 1/2 cup           | [[Butter]], melted                |
| **For the Topping**|                                   |
| 1 cup             | Chopped [[Pecans]]                |
| 1 cup             | Packed [[Brown Sugar]]            |
| 1/3 cup           | [[All-Purpose Flour]]           |
| 1/3 cup           | [[Butter]], softened              |
___
## Instructions
1.  Preheat oven to 350°F. Lightly grease a baking pan or casserole dish.
2.  **To make the souffle:** In a large bowl or a food processor, combine the mashed sweet potatoes, granulated sugar, eggs, vanilla, milk, and melted butter.
3.  Mix until everything is well combined and the mixture is smooth and creamy.
4.  Spread the sweet potato mixture evenly into the prepared baking pan.
5.  **To make the topping:** In a separate bowl, mix together the chopped pecans, packed brown sugar, flour, and softened butter. Use a fork or your fingers to create a crumbly streusel topping.
6.  Sprinkle the topping evenly over the sweet potato mixture.
7.  Bake for 30 to 45 minutes, until the topping is golden brown and the souffle is set.
