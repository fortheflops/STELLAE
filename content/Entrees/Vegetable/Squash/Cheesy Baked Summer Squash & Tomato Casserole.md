### Instructions
1.  Preheat oven to 350°F.
2.  Wash and slice squash and place in a greased baking dish.
3.  Cover with tomato and onion slices.
4.  Sprinkle with salt & pepper.
5.  Dot with the butter or margarine.
6.  Sprinkle with cheese.
7.  Bake at 350°F for about 45 minutes.
