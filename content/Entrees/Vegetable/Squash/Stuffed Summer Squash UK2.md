## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
| 2 Cups            | Cooked [[Rice]]           |
| 1/2 Pound        | [[Hamburger]]             |
| 6 Small            | [[Squash]]               |
| 1 Cup            | [[Sour Cream]]            |
| 1 Cup            | [[Water]]                 |
| 1 Cup            | [[Tomato Pulp]]           |
|                   | [[Salt]]                 |
|                    | [[Pepper]]          |
___
### Instructions
1. Combine rice, meat, salt, and pepper.
2. Peel squash unless very young.
3. Remove seeds from top.
4. Stuff with rice and meat.
5. Place in pan with water.
6. Bake until tender, basting frequently.
7. Cover with cream and tomato pulp.
8. Brown in oven.
