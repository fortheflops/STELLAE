# Mom's Pizza

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Mom | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A classic homemade pizza featuring a from-scratch hot roll mix crust, rich meat sauce, and plenty of grated mozzarella cheese. Perfectly customizable with your favorite toppings.*

* Skillet

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Sauce
1. Make a spaghetti sauce by cooking the **2 cups (or more) hamburger** with **as needed solid pack tomatoes**, **as needed chopped onions**, **as needed minced garlic**, **to taste salt**, and **to taste pepper**.
2. Simmer until flavors are well blended. (Day before recommended).

### Step 2: Prepare the Dough
3. Follow directions on the **1 box Betty Crocker hot roll mix** package to prepare the pizza dough.
4. Let it rise in a bowl for an hour or more until puffed up.

### Step 3: Assemble the Pizza
5. Divide the dough in half to make two pizzas.
6. Place each half on an oiled cookie sheet and flatten it out until the sheet is covered evenly.
7. Poke the dough with a fork to make holes.
8. Spread the **2 cups (or more) hamburger** sauce over the dough.
9. Add your desired optional toppings such as **optional anchovies**, **optional mushrooms**, and **optional strips of onion**.
10. Grate the **2 round mozzarella cheeses** over the top.

### Step 4: Bake
11. Place the assembled pizza in a preheated **400°F** oven.
12. Bake for **6-10 minutes** until golden brown and bubbling, watching closely.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The sauce can be made the day before, and the assembled pizza can rest for 3 hours ahead of baking.
