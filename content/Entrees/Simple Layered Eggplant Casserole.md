# Simple Layered Eggplant Casserole

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A classic and incredibly simple layered eggplant casserole, featuring delicious layers of eggplant, spaghetti sauce, and melted mozzarella.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 40 mins | 30 mins | 6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 9x13 inch baking dish
* Colander
* Paper towels

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 large | [[Eggplant]] | sliced into 1/4-inch rounds |
| 1 jar (24 oz) | [[Spaghetti Sauce]] | divided |
| 2 cups | [[Mozzarella Cheese]] | shredded |
| 1/2 cup | [[Parmesan Cheese]] | grated |
| As needed | [[Salt]] | for drawing out moisture |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep the Eggplant
1. Preheat the oven to **350°F**. Grease a **9x13 inch baking dish**.
2. Slice the **1 large eggplant** into 1/4-inch rounds, sprinkle with **as needed salt**, and let sit in a colander for **20-30 minutes** to draw out excess moisture before patting dry with a paper towel.

### Step 2: Assemble and Bake
3. Spread a thin layer of **1 jar spaghetti sauce** in the bottom of the prepared baking dish.
4. Arrange a single layer of **eggplant slices** over the sauce, then top with a layer of **2 cups mozzarella cheese**.
5. Repeat the layers until all ingredients are used, ending with a final layer of sauce.
6. Sprinkle **1/2 cup Parmesan cheese** evenly over the top.
7. Bake for **35-45 minutes** until the eggplant is tender and the cheese is melted, golden brown, and bubbly.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole up to a day in advance, cover tightly, and refrigerate until ready to bake, adding a few extra minutes to the bake time if needed.
