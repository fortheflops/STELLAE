# Cathy's Classic Baked Chicken Parmigiana

> 📜 **Collection:** JC | ✍️ **Attribution:** Cathy | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A straightforward and classic recipe for Chicken Parmigiana from Cathy, featuring breaded chicken breasts baked with Ragu sauce and a generous amount of mozzarella and Parmesan cheese.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 40 mins | None | 6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Small bowl
* Shallow dish
* Large skillet
* 13x9 inch baking dish

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 pounds | [[Chicken Breasts]] | Boneless, skinless |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] | Beaten |
| 3/4 cup | [[Bread Crumbs]] | Seasoned |
| 1/4 cup | [[Olive Oil]] | None |
| 6 ounces | [[Mozzarella Cheese]] | Shredded |
| 2 tablespoons+ | [[Parmesan Cheese]] | Grated |
| 1 jar | [[Ragu]] | Italian cooking sauce |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Bread
1. Preheat oven to **350°F**.
2. In a small bowl, beat the **2 eggs**. In a separate shallow dish, place the **3/4 cup seasoned bread crumbs**.
3. Dip each piece of **2 pounds boneless, skinless chicken breasts** first in the egg, then press it into the bread crumbs to coat both sides completely.

### Step 2: Brown and Assemble
4. In a large skillet, heat the **1/4 cup olive oil** over medium-high heat.
5. Brown the breaded chicken well on both sides until golden brown.
6. Arrange the browned chicken pieces in a single layer in a **13x9 inch baking dish**.
7. Sprinkle the **6 ounces shredded mozzarella cheese** and **2 tablespoons grated Parmesan cheese** over the chicken.
8. Pour the **1 jar Ragu Italian cooking sauce** evenly over the top.
9. Sprinkle with more Parmesan cheese to your liking.

### Step 3: Bake
10. Bake uncovered for **30 minutes**, or until the sauce is bubbly and the cheese is melted and golden.
