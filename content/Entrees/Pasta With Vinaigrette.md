# Pasta With Vinaigrette

> 📜 **Collection:** Patsy Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A vibrant pasta dish tossed in a zesty herb-infused oil and lemon vinaigrette, topped with fresh tomatoes and parmesan cheese. Perfect for a light and flavorful lunch or dinner.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 10 mins | 30 mins | 4 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Wire whisk
* Large pot
* Colander

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 Tbsp | [[Herb Oil]] | Rosemary or basil |
| 2 Tbsp | [[Olive Oil]] | Standard |
| 2 Tbsp | [[Lemon Juice]] | Freshly squeezed |
| 1 Tbsp | [[Minced Shallot]] | Minced |
| 2 Tsp | [[Minced garlic]] | Minced |
| 4 Med | [[Tomatoes]] | Cored & coarsely chopped |
| 2 Tbsp | [[Parsley]] | Minced |
| 1/2 Tsp | [[Salt]] | To taste |
| As needed | [[Black Pepper]] | Freshly ground |
| 2 cups | [[Penne pasta]] | Dry |
| 4 Tbsp | [[Parmesan Cheese]] | Grated |
| 1/4 cup | [[Vinegar]] | Optional extra emulsion |
| 1/2 cup | [[Oil]] | Optional extra emulsion |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Make the Vinaigrette
1. In a mixing bowl, whisk together the **2 tbsp herb oil**, **2 tbsp olive oil**, and **2 tbsp lemon juice** until well combined.

### Step 2: Marinate the Flavors
2. Add the **1 tbsp minced shallot**, **2 tsp minced garlic**, **4 med tomatoes**, **2 tbsp parsley**, **1/2 tsp salt**, and **black pepper** to the bowl. Set aside at room temperature for **30 minutes** to let the flavors meld.

### Step 3: Cook the Pasta
3. Cook the **2 cups penne pasta** in a large pot of boiling salted water until al dente, then drain using a colander.

### Step 4: Combine and Serve
4. Toss the cooked pasta with the marinated sauce and **4 tbsp parmesan cheese** until evenly coated and fragrant.
5. If desired, separately combine the **1/4 cup vinegar** and **1/2 cup oil** to mix well, then drizzle onto the pasta before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftover pasta in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 2 days.
