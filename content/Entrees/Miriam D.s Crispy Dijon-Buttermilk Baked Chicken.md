# Miriam D.'s Crispy Dijon-Buttermilk Baked Chicken

> 📜 **Collection:** GailCollection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Miriam D. | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A delightful and lighter diet recipe featuring chicken legs and thighs coated in tangy Dijon mustard, buttermilk, and crispy breadcrumbs, then baked to golden perfection.*

| To taste | [[Salt]] | For seasoning |
| To taste | [[Pepper]] | Freshly ground |
| As needed | [[Buttermilk]] | For dipping |
| As needed | [[Breadcrumbs]] | Fine dry variety |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Coat Chicken
1. Preheat your oven to **400°F**.
2. Spread **6 chicken legs** and **6 chicken thighs** lightly with **Dijon-style mustard**.
3. Sprinkle the chicken pieces generously with **salt** and **pepper**.

### Step 2: Bread and Bake
4. Pour **buttermilk** and **fine dry breadcrumbs** into two separate shallow dishes.
5. Dip each piece of chicken first in the **buttermilk**, letting any excess drip off.
6. Coat each piece thoroughly with **fine dry breadcrumbs**.
7. Place the coated chicken pieces on a baking sheet and cover the baking sheet with a sheet of foil.
8. Bake at **400°F** for **30 minutes**.
9. Remove the foil, increase the oven heat to **450°F**, and bake for an additional **15 to 20 minutes** longer, or until the chicken is golden brown and tender when tested with a fork.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftover baked chicken in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3-4 days; reheat in a 375°F oven to restore crispness.
