# Chicken 'n Rice Hot Dish

> 📜 **Collection:** Burcea Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A classic mid-century comfort food casserole featuring tender chicken baked over a savory onion and mushroom rice base. Topped with a generous layer of Parmesan cheese, this simple hot dish bakes up beautifully golden and bubbling.*

| 1 can | [[Mushroom Soup]] | condensed |
| 1 cup | [[Water]] |  |
| Assorted | [[Chicken]] | pieces, lightly seasoned |
| To taste | [[Parmesan Cheese]] | grated, for generous topping |

---

## Instructions

### Preparation and Assembly
1. Preheat your oven to **350°F**.
2. In a mixing bowl, combine the **1 cup minute rice**, **1 package dry onion soup mix**, **1 can mushroom soup**, and **1 cup water** until well blended.
3. Pour the rice and soup mixture into a shallow baking pan.
4. Place the lightly seasoned **chicken pieces** in a single layer on top of the rice mixture.
5. Cover the top generously with **Parmesan cheese**.

### Baking
6. Cover the pan tightly with **aluminum foil** and bake at **350°F** for **1 hour**.
7. Remove the foil and continue to bake uncovered for **1/2 hour** (30 minutes) until the **chicken** is cooked through, tender, and the **Parmesan cheese** is beautifully browned and bubbling.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The rice and soup base can be mixed and spread in the pan up to 4 hours ahead. Add the chicken and cheese just before baking, adding 5-10 minutes to the covered bake time if starting from a cold refrigerator.
