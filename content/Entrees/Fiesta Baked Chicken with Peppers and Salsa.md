# Fiesta Baked Chicken with Peppers and Salsa

> 📜 **Collection:** UK15 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A colorful and easy weeknight meal featuring tender chicken breasts topped with sautéed bell peppers, onions, picante sauce, and melted cheddar cheese. This Mexican-American inspired dish bakes to bubbling perfection for a crowd-pleasing dinner.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 1 hour 10 mins | None | 6-8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large baking pan
* Skillet

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 6 to 8 | [[Chicken Breasts]] | bone-in or boneless |
| 1 | [[Green Bell Pepper]] | sliced |
| 1 | [[Yellow Bell Pepper]] | sliced |
| 1 | [[Red Bell Pepper]] | sliced |
| As needed | [[Purple Onion]] | sliced |
| 1 jar | [[Picante Sauce]] | standard size |
| 1 cup | [[Cheddar Cheese]] | shredded |
| To taste | [[Salt]] | for seasoning |
| As needed | [[Butter]] | for greasing pan |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Season
1. Preheat oven to **375°F (190°C)**. Grease a large baking pan with **butter**.

### Step 2: Assemble Chicken and Vegetables
2. Place the **6 to 8 chicken breasts** in the buttered baking pan.
3. Season the chicken lightly with **salt** and let it set for a few minutes.
4. In a skillet, sauté the **1 green bell pepper**, **1 yellow bell pepper**, and **1 red bell pepper**. Add some sliced **purple onion** and continue to sauté until tender-crisp.
5. Spread the pepper and onion mixture evenly over the **chicken breasts**.
6. Cover everything thoroughly with the **1 jar picante sauce**.

### Step 3: Bake and Melt
7. Bake at **375°F** for approximately **1 hour**, or until the chicken is cooked through and juices run clear.
8. Top the dish with **1 cup cheddar cheese** and return to the oven for **5 to 10 minutes**, until the cheese is completely melted and bubbly.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftover baked chicken in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 to 4 days.
