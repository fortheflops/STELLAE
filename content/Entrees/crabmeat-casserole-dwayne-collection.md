# Crabmeat Casserole

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Mary Emma Knapp | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A classic, comforting seafood bake featuring tender crabmeat and fine-cut noodles folded into a creamy shrimp soup base. Topped with melted cheddar cheese, this dish is a simple yet satisfying family favorite.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 35 mins | None | 4 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Casserole dish
* Mixing bowl
* Can opener
* Measuring cups

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 (6 oz) pkg | [[Crabmeat]] | frozen, thawed, drained, and broken into pieces |
| 1 can | [[Shrimp Soup]] | condensed |
| 2/3 cup | [[Milk]] | None |
| 1/3 cup | [[Mayonnaise]] | None |
| 2 cups | [[Noodles]] | fine cut, uncooked |
| 1/2 cup | [[Cheddar Cheese]] | grated |

---

## Instructions

### Preparation & Assembly
1. Drain the **1 (6 oz) pkg frozen crabmeat** and break it gently into bite-sized pieces.
2. In a medium mixing bowl, thoroughly blend together **1 can shrimp soup**, **2/3 cup milk**, and **1/3 cup mayonnaise** until smooth.
3. Stir the **2 cups uncooked fine cut noodles** and the prepared **crabmeat** into the soup mixture.
4. Turn the mixture into a buttered casserole dish and top evenly with **1/2 cup grated cheddar cheese**.

### Baking
5. Bake at **350°F** for **35 minutes** until the casserole is bubbling hot throughout and the cheese on top is melted and golden brown.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This casserole can be assembled, covered, and refrigerated up to 24 hours before baking. Add 5 to 10 minutes to the baking time if baking directly from the refrigerator.
