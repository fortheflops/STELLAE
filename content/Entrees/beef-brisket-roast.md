# Beef Brisket Roast

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Five | ✍️ **Attribution:** Florin Young | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A classic slow-roasted beef brisket infused with savory seasonings and liquid smoke. Tender, melt-in-your-mouth perfection achieved through overnight marinating and low-temperature baking.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 3 hours | 8 hours | 6-8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Heavy foil
* Baking pan
* Refrigerator

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | [[Beef Brisket]] | Whole cut |
| As needed | [[Tenderizer]] | Optional |
| To taste | [[Seasoned Salt]] |  |
| To taste | [[Pepper]] | Ground |
| To taste | [[Salt]] | If necessary |
| 1 dash | [[Worcestershire Sauce]] |  |
| 1 dash | [[Liquid Smoke]] |  |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Marinate
1. Lay the **1 [[Beef Brisket]]** on heavy foil.
2. Sprinkle with **tenderizer**, **seasoned salt**, **pepper**, **salt**, **worcestershire sauce**, and a dash of **liquid smoke**.
3. Seal tightly in the foil and put in refrigerator to marinate overnight.

### Step 2: Baking
4. Next day, place the wrapped brisket in a baking pan.
5. Bake for **2 1/2 to 3 hrs** at **250 degrees**, or optionally at **300 degrees**, until the meat is fork-tender and aromatic.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Prep the brisket and let it marinate wrapped in the refrigerator overnight before baking the next day.
