# Mary Lamb Berlin Meat Balls

> 📜 **Collection:** Burcea Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Mary Lamb | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *Discover Mary Lamb's traditional recipe for Berlin Meat Balls, a hearty combination of ground veal and pork mixed with grated carrots, onions, and breadcrumbs. Baked to tender perfection, this savory dish is a classic comfort meal.*

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 1 hour 30 mins | None | 6 servings |

## Ingredients

### Meat and Vegetables
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 lbs | [[Veal]] | Ground |
| 1 lb | [[Pork]] | Ground |
| 3 | [[Carrots]] | Raw, grated |
| 1 | [[Onion]] | Medium, egg size, chopped |

### Seasoning and Binders
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 tbsp | [[Salt]] | None |
| 1 pinch | [[Pepper]] | To taste |
| 1/2 cup | [[Gelatin]] | Savory |
| 2 cups | [[Breadcrumbs]] | None |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] | Beaten |
| 1 1/2 cups | [[Milk]] | None |
| 1/3 cup | [[Bacon]] | Sliced, for topping |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing
1. Grind together the **2 lbs veal** and **1 lb pork**.
2. Mix in the **3 raw carrots** (grated), **1 medium onion**, **1 tbsp salt**, **1 pinch pepper**, and **1/2 cup savory gelatin**.
3. Add the **2 cups breadcrumbs**, **2 beaten eggs**, and **1 1/2 cups milk**, mixing until thoroughly combined.

### Step 2: Shaping and Baking
4. Shape the mixture into about 16 balls and form into a loaf shape.
5. Top the loaf with **1/3 cup sliced bacon**.
6. Bake at 400 degrees for 1 1/2 hours (or at 325 degrees for 2 hours) until browned and fully cooked through.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Shape the meat mixture and cover tightly, refrigerating for up to 24 hours before baking.
