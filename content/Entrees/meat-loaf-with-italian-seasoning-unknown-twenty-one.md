# Meat Loaf With Italian Seasoning

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty One | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A simple and flavorful meat loaf seasoned with Italian dressing mix for a quick weeknight dinner. With just two ingredients, this savory dish comes together effortlessly.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 1 hour | None | 6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Loaf pan

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 lbs | [[Ground meat]] | Beef, pork, or a blend |
| 1 envelope | [[Italian dressing mix]] | Dry seasoning packet |

---

## Instructions

### Preparation and Baking
1. In a mixing bowl, thoroughly combine the **2 lbs ground meat** and **1 envelope Italian dressing mix** until fully incorporated.
2. Shape the mixture into a loaf and place it into a baking pan. Bake until cooked through and golden brown on top, with an internal temperature reaching 160°F.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Unbaked meat loaf can be assembled, covered, and refrigerated up to 24 hours before baking.
