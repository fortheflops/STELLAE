# Simple Brisket

> 📜 **Collection:** Jill Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A very simple, concise method for cooking a tender brisket in a roaster. Enjoy this classic comfort food roasted to perfection.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 2 hours | 15 mins | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large heavy pan
* Roaster with a secure lid
* Oven

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 (4-pound) | [[Brisket]] | Trimmed |
| As needed | [[Fat]] or [[Oil]] | For browning |
| To taste | [[Salt]] and [[Pepper]] | For seasoning |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Brown
1. Preheat oven to **350°F (175°C)**.
2. Season the **1 (4-pound) brisket** with **salt** and **pepper** to taste.
3. Heat the **fat or oil** in a large, heavy pan over medium-high heat and brown the brisket on all sides until nicely seared, about **20-25 minutes total**.

### Step 2: Roast and Rest
4. Transfer the browned meat to a roaster with a secure lid.
5. Cook, covered, for approximately **2 hours** (about half an hour per pound), or until the meat is fork-tender and yields easily.
6. Let the **brisket** rest for **15-20 minutes** before slicing against the grain.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftover sliced brisket in an airtight container with its juices in the refrigerator for up to 4 days.
