# Boned Rolled Beef Brisket (Pressure Cooker)

> 📜 **Collection:** Jill Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A simple, classic recipe from Jill's collection for a tender, boned and rolled beef brisket, cooked quickly and easily in a pressure cooker with onions.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 30 mins | 1 hour 30 mins | None | 6-8 servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Pressure cooker
* Knife
* Cutting board

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3 1/2 pounds | [[Beef Brisket]] | Boned, Rolled |
| 1 tablespoon | [[Butter]] | None |
| 1-2 | [[Onions]] | Sliced |
| To taste | [[Salt]] | None |
| 1/2 cup | [[Water]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Brown the Meat
1. In an open pressure cooker pot with the lid off, melt the **1 tablespoon butter** over medium-high heat. Add the **3 1/2 pounds boned, rolled beef brisket** and **1-2 onions**.
2. Brown the brisket on all sides, along with the onions, for about **25 minutes** until nicely browned and fragrant.

### Step 2: Pressure Cooking
3. Season the brisket with **salt** to taste. Add the **1/2 cup water** to the pot.
4. Secure the lid and bring the cooker up to **15 lbs of pressure**. Cook for **30 minutes**.
5. Release the pressure according to your manufacturer's directions. Carefully open the lid, turn the meat over, and re-secure the lid.
6. Bring the cooker back up to **15 lbs of pressure** and continue cooking for another **60 minutes**, for a total of **1.5 hours** of cooking time under pressure, until the meat is fork-tender.
7. Let the pressure release naturally before opening. Serve the brisket sliced, with the onions and pan juices.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftover brisket sliced in its juices in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 4 days.
