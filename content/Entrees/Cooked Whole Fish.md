# Cooked Whole Fish

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A classic vintage recipe for a whole baked fish stuffed with onions and margarine, then basted in a savory lemon and tomato juice sauce until flaky and tender.*

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 50 mins | None | 4–6 Servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | [[Fish]] | Whole, frozen or fresh, scaled and gutted |
| To Taste | [[Salt]] | Divided |
| 1 Stick | [[Oleo]] | Margarine, sliced |
| As needed | [[Onions]] | Sliced |
| As needed | [[Butter]] | For greasing pan |
| As needed | [[Flour]] | For dusting pan |
| A little | [[Oil]] | For basting mixture |
| Several | [[Lemon Slices]] | Fresh |
| As needed | [[Tomato Juice]] | For basting |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep the Fish and Pan
1. Preheat your oven to **350°F**.
2. Scale the **1 whole fish**. Gut and wash it thoroughly, then pat dry with paper towels.
3. Season the **fish** inside and out with **salt** to your preference.
4. Grease a baking pan with **butter** and lightly dust it with **flour**. Lay the prepared **fish** in the pan.

### Step 2: Add Fillings and Basting Sauce
5. Place slices of **oleo** and sliced **onions** inside the **fish** cavity or arrange them around the **fish** in the baking pan.
6. Prepare a basting sauce by combining a little **oil**, **lemon slices**, a pinch of **salt**, and some **tomato juice**.
7. Pour the basting mixture over the **fish** before baking, or reserve it to baste during the baking process.

### Step 3: Bake and Serve
8. Bake at **350°F** for **45-50 minutes**.
9. Check for doneness by trying to lift the skin; the flesh should pull away easily from the bones and flake gently.
10. When cooked, carefully lift the **fish** out of the pan and serve immediately.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The fish can be cleaned, scaled, and stuffed ahead of time, then covered and refrigerated for up to 24 hours before baking.
