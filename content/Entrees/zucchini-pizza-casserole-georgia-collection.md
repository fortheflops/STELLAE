# Zucchini Pizza Casserole

> 📜 **Collection:** Georgia Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A delightful and cheesy twist on pizza night featuring a baked shredded zucchini crust topped with ground beef, savory tomato sauce, and plenty of melted cheese. Perfect for a comforting family dinner that sneaks in extra vegetables.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 40 mins | 10 mins | 6-8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Strainer
* 13"x9"x2" baking pan
* Skillet

---
## Ingredients

### Casserole Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 4 cups | [[Zucchini]] | Shredded, unpeeled |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] | Beaten |
| 1/2 tsp. | [[Salt]] | Plus extra for draining |
| 1/2 cup | [[Parmesan Cheese]] | Grated |
| 1 cup | [[Cheddar Cheese]] | Grated, divided |
| 2 cups | [[Mozzarella Cheese]] | Grated, divided |
| 1 pound | [[Ground Beef]] | Cooked and drained |
| 1/2 cup | [[Onion]] | Chopped |
| 1 can (15 oz.) | [[Tomato Sauce]] | Italian flavor or plain tomato sauce |
| 1/4 tsp. | [[Oregano]] | Used if plain tomato sauce is substituted |
| 1/4 tsp. | [[Basil]] | Used if plain tomato sauce is substituted |
| 1 medium | [[Green Pepper]] | Chopped |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Crust Preparation
1. Place **4 cups shredded unpeeled zucchini** in a strainer and sprinkle with **1/2 tsp. salt**. Let drain for **10 min**, then squeeze out as much moisture as possible.
2. Combine the drained zucchini with **2 eggs**, **1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese**, and half of the **1 cup cheddar cheese** and **2 cups mozzarella cheese**.
3. Press the mixture firmly into a greased **13"x9"x2" baking pan**.
4. Bake at **400°** for **20 minutes** until set and lightly golden.

### Step 2: Meat Sauce and Assembly
5. Meanwhile, brown **1 pound ground beef** with **1/2 cup chopped onion** in a skillet. Drain excess grease.
6. Add **1 can (15 oz.) tomato sauce** (mixed with **1/4 tsp. oregano** and **1/4 tsp. basil** if using plain sauce) to the beef and onions.
7. Spoon the meat mixture evenly over the baked zucchini crust.
8. Top with the remaining cheddar and mozzarella cheeses, then sprinkle with **1 medium green pepper**, chopped.
9. Bake for **20 minutes more** until the cheese is melted, bubbly, and lightly browned on top.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** You can assemble the casserole up to the baking step, cover tightly, and refrigerate for up to 24 hours before baking.
