# Milk Can Supper

> *A classic rustic one-pot meal featuring browned bratwurst simmered with red potatoes, cabbage, corn, carrots, and aromatics in beer.*

| 2 | [[Bay Leaves]] |  |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep & Sauté
1. Brown the **8 bratwurst** in a large pan until browned on all sides, then remove from the pan and cut into **1-inch pieces**.
2. Sauté the **4 cloves garlic** briefly in the pan, then remove.

### Step 2: Layer & Simmer
3. In a large Dutch oven or pot, layer the **8 bratwurst** pieces, **2 lbs red potatoes**, **1 head cabbage** wedges, **3 ears corn**, **1 lb carrots**, **1 red onion** wedges, sautéed **4 cloves garlic**, **10 sprigs thyme**, and **2 bay leaves**.
4. Pour **1/2 cup beer** over the layered ingredients.
5. Cover tightly and simmer until all vegetables are fork-tender.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Ingredients can be washed, chopped, and prepped ahead of time; assemble and simmer just before serving.
