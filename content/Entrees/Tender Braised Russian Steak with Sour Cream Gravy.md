# Tender Braised Russian Steak with Sour Cream Gravy

> 📜 **Collection:** General Archive | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A classic comfort dish where round steak is pounded, floured, and browned, then slow-simmered with golden onions in a rich and tangy sour cream gravy until fall-apart tender.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 1 hour 45 mins | None | 4–6 servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Heavy skillet
* Meat mallet
* Wooden spoon
* Measuring cups

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 lbs | [[Round Steak]] | Pounded and cut into 3-inch squares |
| As needed | [[Flour]] | For dredging and thickening |
| 2 cups | [[Sliced Onion]] | Sliced |
| To taste | [[Salt]] | None |
| To taste | [[Pepper]] | None |
| 4 tbsp | [[Butter]] or [[Fat]] | For frying |
| 1 cup | [[Water]] | None |
| 1 cup | [[Sour Cream]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Brown the Aromatics
1. Pound the **2 lbs round steak** well, dredge with **flour**, and cut into pieces about 3 inches square.
2. Simmer the **2 cups sliced onion** in **4 tbsp butter or fat** until golden but not brown.
3. Remove onions from the skillet and set aside.

### Step 2: Brown the Meat
4. In the same fat left in the skillet, brown the **round steak** pieces until a rich crust forms.
5. When well browned, sprinkle with **flour**, **salt**, and **pepper**. Turn and repeat on the other side until seasoned and golden.

### Step 3: Braise in Sour Cream Gravy
6. Add the reserved onions, **1 cup water**, and **1 cup sour cream** to the skillet.
7. Cook very slowly over low heat until the meat is fall-apart tender and the gravy is rich, about **1½ hours**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftovers in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days; gently reheat on the stovetop over low heat.
