# Chili Rice Wraps

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty One | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *These quick and flavorful Chili Rice Wraps combine savory beans and seasoned rice wrapped in soft tortillas. Topped with fresh tomatoes and cheese, they make a comforting weeknight meal.*

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

## Instructions

### Step 1: Cooking the Filling
1. Bring **15 oz. chili with beans** and **1/2 cup water** to a boil, then stir in **1 cup minute rice**. Cook for **5 minutes** until the rice is tender and absorbs the liquid.

### Step 2: Assembling the Wraps
2. Spoon the mixture over **6 flour tortillas**. Top with **shredded cheddar cheese**, **chopped tomato**, and **green onions**. Fold the tortillas and secure each with a toothpick.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The filling can be prepared ahead of time and reheated before assembling the wraps.
