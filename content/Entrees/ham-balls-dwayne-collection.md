## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Ham Balls
1. Preheat your oven to **350°F**.
2. In a large mixing bowl, combine the **2 1/2 lbs. ground ham**, **2 lbs. ground pork**, **1 lb. ground beef**, **3 eggs**, **3 cups graham cracker crumbs**, and **2 cups milk**. Mix well until thoroughly combined.
3. Shape the mixture into golf-ball-sized portions and place them in a shallow baking dish.

### Step 2: Sauce and Bake
4. In a separate bowl, whisk together the **2 cups tomato sauce**, **3/4 cup vinegar**, **2 1/2 cups brown sugar**, and **2 tsp. dry mustard** until smooth.
5. Pour the sauce evenly over the ham balls in the baking dish.
6. Cover the dish with foil and bake in the preheated oven for the first part of cooking. Uncover for the remaining time, baking for a total of **1 hour** until the ham balls are cooked through, caramelized on top, and the sauce is bubbling.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The ham balls can be shaped and placed in the baking dish a day ahead; cover and refrigerate, then add the sauce just before baking. Leftovers freeze beautifully in the sauce.
