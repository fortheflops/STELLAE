# Molly Mc Butter Primavera

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty One | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A light and flavorful pasta primavera tossed with crisp vegetables and Molly McButter. This quick entree brings together fresh tomatoes, peppers, and zucchini for an easy meal.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 10 mins | None | 4 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large bowl
* Pot

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| As needed | [[Fettuccine]] | Cooked |
| 8 | [[Cherry tomatoes]] | Cut in wedges |
| 1/4 | [[Red pepper]] | Cut in strips |
| 1/8 | [[Green pepper]] | Cut in strips |
| 1/4 | [[Zucchini]] | Cut in strips, seeds removed |
| 2-3 T | [[Molly McButter sprinkles]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation
1. Cook the **Fettuccine** until al dente.
2. In a large bowl, add the **8 cherry tomatoes**, **1/4 red pepper**, **1/8 green pepper**, and **1/4 zucchini**.
3. Add the hot pasta and **2-3 T Molly McButter sprinkles**, then toss well until combined and aromatic.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Best served immediately while the pasta is hot and the vegetables are crisp-tender.
