## Instructions

### Step 1: Toasting Hazelnuts & Frying Chicken
1. Place **2 oz hazelnuts** in a baking dish in the oven at 450°F, oven door open slightly, until you smell it toasted.
2. Dip the **2 chicken breasts** in **flour** seasoned with **salt and pepper**.
3. Beat **1 egg yolk** with **1 tbsp oil** and spread it over the **chicken breasts**, then dip in **flour** again.
4. Fry over medium heat in about **1 tbsp butter** for about 3 minutes on each side until golden brown and cooked through.

### Step 2: Preparing Sauce and Serving
5. Mix **2 tsp arrowroot** with a small amount of **chicken stock** together.
6. Put about **1/2 pound of chicken sauce** on to boil and add the arrowroot mixture along with **20g butter**.
7. Put the toasted nuts in a blender and add **3 tsp of the hazelnuts** to the butter, then add the sauce, **some lemon juice**, **some cream**, **salt**, **pepper**, and **1/3 oz brandy**.
8. Put the **chicken breasts** on a heatproof plate and pour the sauce over it until sizzling and aromatic.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The hazelnuts can be toasted and ground ahead of time, but the chicken is best prepared fresh for optimal texture.
