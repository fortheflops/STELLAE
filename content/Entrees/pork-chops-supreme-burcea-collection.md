# Pork Chops Supreme

> 📜 **Collection:** Burcea Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *Tender braised pork chops simmered with celery and onion soup, finished with a rich mushroom and evaporated milk gravy. This comforting homestyle dish serves six.*

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 6 | [[Pork Chops]] | trimmed |
| To taste | [[Salt]] | optional |
| To taste | [[Pepper]] | optional |
| To taste | [[Paprika]] | optional |
| 1 cup | [[Water]] | divided |
| 1/2 cup | [[Celery]] | finely chopped |
| 1/4 cup | [[Dry Onion Soup Mix]] | from a 1/2 envelope |
| 3 tbsp | [[Flour]] | all-purpose |
| 1 tbsp | [[Parsley Flakes]] | dry |
| 1/4 cup | [[Cold Water]] | for blending |
| 2/3 cup | [[Evaporated Milk]] | from a 6 oz can |
| 3 oz | [[Mushrooms]] | canned, drained |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Basting and Braising
1. Season the **6 pork chops** with **salt**, **pepper**, and **paprika**.
2. In a skillet, brown the **6 pork chops** slowly on both sides over medium heat.
3. Drain off excess fat from the skillet.
4. Add **1 cup water**, the **1/2 cup celery**, and **1/4 cup dry onion soup mix** to the skillet.
5. Cover and cook over low heat for 20 minutes.
6. Turn the **6 pork chops** and continue cooking for another 15 to 20 minutes, until the **6 pork chops** are tender and cooked through.
7. Remove the **6 pork chops** from the skillet, keeping them warm.

### Step 2: Making the Gravy
8. Combine the **3 tbsp flour**, **1 tbsp dry parsley flakes**, and **1/4 cup cold water** with the pan juices in the skillet, blending until smooth.
9. Stir in the **2/3 cup evaporated milk** and the **3 oz mushrooms**.
10. Cook over low heat, stirring constantly, until thickened and boiling.
11. Serve the hot mushroom gravy over the **6 pork chops**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Leftovers can be stored in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days and reheated gently on the stovetop.
