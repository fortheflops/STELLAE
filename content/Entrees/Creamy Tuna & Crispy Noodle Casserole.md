# Creamy Tuna & Crispy Noodle Casserole

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Layer
1. Preheat oven to **375°F**. Grease a casserole dish.
2. Cook the **frozen mixed vegetables** according to package directions and drain well.
3. In the casserole dish, create layers using the **1 can tuna (or chicken)**, cooked **frozen mixed vegetables**, **3 chopped green onions**, and **1 can Chinese noodles**.

### Step 2: Sauce and Bake
4. In a separate bowl, prepare the sauce by mixing together the **1 can cream of celery soup**, **3/4 cup milk**, **scant tablespoon soy sauce**, **1/4 teaspoon garlic salt**, **1/4 teaspoon marjoram**, and **pepper to taste**.
5. Just before baking, pour the sauce mixture evenly over the layers in the casserole dish.
6. Top the casserole with the **2 chopped boiled eggs**.
7. Bake at **375°F** for **30 minutes** until hot, bubbly, and the noodles are golden brown and crispy.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole layers and sauce ahead of time, storing them separately in the refrigerator. Pour the sauce and top with eggs just before baking.
