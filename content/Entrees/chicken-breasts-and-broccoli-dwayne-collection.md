# Chicken Breasts And Broccoli

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A comforting baked casserole featuring tender chicken breasts and broccoli smothered in mushroom soup, sherry, and melted cheese. This hearty classic is topped with crunchy onion rings for a delightful texture contrast.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 1 hour 10 mins | None | 4 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Skillet
* Saucepan
* Shallow baking pan

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2-4 | [[Chicken Breasts]] | Browned |
| 1 bunch | [[Broccoli]] | Tenderized by boiling |
| 1/2 cup | [[Butter]] | For browning chicken |
| 1 can | [[Cream of Mushroom Soup]] | Diluted |
| 1 splash | [[Milk]] | To dilute soup |
| 1 splash | [[Sherry]] | To dilute soup |
| 1 can | [[Canned Onion Rings]] | Crispy |
| 1/2 cup | [[Grated Cheese]] | For topping |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Bake
1. Brown the **2-4 chicken breasts** in **1/2 cup butter**. Boil the **1 bunch broccoli** until tender.
2. Place the broccoli in a shallow baking pan and put the **2-4 chicken breasts** on top.
3. Pour **1 can cream of mushroom soup** diluted with a little **1 splash milk** and **1 splash sherry** over the chicken and broccoli.
4. Sprinkle with **1 can canned onion rings**.
5. Bake for **1 hr** at **350°F**, then sprinkle with **1/2 cup grated cheese** and bake for an additional **10 minutes** until bubbly and golden.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the dish ahead of time up to the baking step, cover, and refrigerate for a few hours before popping it into the oven.
