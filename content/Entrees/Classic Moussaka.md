# Classic Moussaka

> 📜 **Collection:** General Archive | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A classic, hearty Moussaka featuring layers of tender fried eggplant, a rich and savory spiced meat sauce, and a creamy béchamel topping, all baked to golden perfection. This traditional Greek comfort food is an absolute masterpiece of layered flavors.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Cook Eggplant
1. Peel the **2 medium eggplants** and slice them into 1/2-inch rounds. Sprinkle generously with **salt** and set in a colander to drain for **30 minutes**, then pat dry thoroughly.
2. In a large skillet, heat a small amount of **1/4 cup+ oil**. Working in batches, lightly brown the **eggplant slices** on both sides until tender. Drain on paper towels.

### Step 2: Make the Meat Sauce
3. In a large skillet, brown the **1 lb ground meat** and **1 cup chopped onion**. Drain off excess fat.
4. Stir in the **1/4 cup dry red wine**, **1/4 cup water**, **2 Tbsp snipped fresh parsley**, **1 Tbsp tomato paste**, **1 tsp salt**, and a **dash black pepper**. Simmer for about **4 minutes** until the liquid has nearly evaporated. Remove from heat and cool slightly.
5. In the bowl with the meat mixture, stir in half of the **3/8 cup soft bread crumbs**, half of the **1/4 cup shredded cheese**, the **2 beaten eggs**, and **1/4 tsp ground cinnamon**. Set aside.

### Step 3: Make the Béchamel Sauce
6. In a saucepan, melt the **3 Tbsp margarine**. Stir in the **3 Tbsp all-purpose flour**, **1/2 tsp salt**, **1/8 tsp ground nutmeg**, and a **dash black pepper**. Cook, stirring, for **1 minute**.
7. Gradually add the **1 1/2 cups milk** all at once, whisking constantly until the sauce thickens and bubbles. Reduce heat to low.
8. Temper the **1 beaten egg** by slowly whisking about **1/2 cup** of the hot béchamel into it. Pour the tempered egg mixture back into the saucepan, whisking constantly. Cook and stir over low heat for **2 more minutes** (do not boil). Remove from heat.

### Step 4: Assemble and Bake
9. Preheat your oven to **350°F (175°C)**. Sprinkle the bottom of a **2-quart baking dish** with the remaining **bread crumbs**.
10. Arrange half of the **browned eggplant slices** in the dish. Spoon the **meat sauce mixture** evenly over the eggplant, then arrange the remaining **eggplant slices** on top.
11. Pour the **béchamel sauce** evenly over the top, and sprinkle with the remaining **shredded cheese**.
12. Bake uncovered at **350°F** for **45 minutes**, or until the top is golden brown and bubbly. Let stand for **10-15 minutes** before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Moussaka can be assembled a day ahead and kept covered in the refrigerator before baking, or baked and stored for up to 3 days.
