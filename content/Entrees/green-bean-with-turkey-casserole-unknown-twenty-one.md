# Green Bean With Turkey Casserole

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty One | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A comforting microwave casserole featuring tender turkey or chicken, green beans, and mushroom soup, topped with mashed potatoes and crispy onion rings. This quick and hearty dish comes together effortlessly for a satisfying weeknight meal.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing and Heating
1. In a **2 qt. casserole**, combine **1 1/2-2 C cooked turkey or chicken**, **10 3/4 oz. cream of mushroom soup**, **14.5 oz. cut green beans**, **4 oz. Cheddar cheese**, and **1/3 c milk**.
2. Microwave for **6 minutes** or until the mixture is hot and bubbly, stirring once halfway through.

### Step 2: Topping and Final Cook
3. Top the hot mixture with **6 servings mashed potatoes** and sprinkle with **1/2 of 2.8 oz. can French fried onion rings**.
4. Microwave for an additional **2-4 minutes** until heated through and onion rings are fragrant.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the base mixture in the casserole dish ahead of time, cover, and refrigerate up to 24 hours before heating and adding the potato and onion topping.
