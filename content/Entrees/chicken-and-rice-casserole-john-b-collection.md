# Chicken And Rice Casserole

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A comforting and easy-to-make chicken and rice casserole baked with creamy soup, cheese, peas, and pimentos. Perfect for a quick family weeknight dinner.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 25 mins | None | 6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 1 1/2 quart casserole dish
* Mixing spoon
* Oven

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 can | [[Cream of Chicken Soup]] | Undiluted |
| 1/2 cup | [[Milk]] | None |
| 5 oz | [[Swanson Boned Chicken]] | Canned |
| 3 cups | [[Cooked Rice]] | None |
| 1 cup | [[Shredded Process Cheese]] | None |
| 1 cup | [[Cooked Peas]] | None |
| 1/4 cup | [[Chopped Pimentos]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing and Baking
1. In a **1 1/2 quart casserole** blend **1 can cream of chicken soup** and **1/2 cup milk**.
2. Stir in **5 oz boned chicken**, **3 cups cooked rice**, **1 cup shredded process cheese**, **1 cup cooked peas**, and **1/4 cup chopped pimentos**.
3. Bake in a moderate oven at **375 degrees** for about **25 minutes** until bubbly and hot throughout.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole in advance, cover, and refrigerate up to 24 hours before baking.
