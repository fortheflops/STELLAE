# Salmon With Green Sauce

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty One | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A delightful broiled salmon recipe topped with a vibrant and herbaceous green sauce. Perfect for a quick and flavorful weeknight seafood dinner.*

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

## Ingredients

### Salmon
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 fillet | [[Salmon]] | Fresh |
| 1 bunch | [[Fresh dill]] | Chopped |
| 1 | [[Lemon]] | Juiced |
| To taste | [[Olive oil]] | None |

### Green Sauce
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 bunch | [[Green onion]] | Chopped |
| To taste | [[Pepper]] | None |
| 1 handful | [[Spinach]] | Fresh |
| A splash | [[Water]] | Very little |
| 1/2 cup | [[Mayo]] | None |
| 2 tbsp | [[Lemon juice]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Broil Salmon
1. Mix **fresh dill**, juice of **1 lemon**, and **olive oil** together in a bowl.
2. Spread the mixture evenly over the **salmon**.
3. Broil until the fish is cooked through and flakes easily with a fork.

### Step 2: Make Green Sauce
4. Combine **green onion**, **pepper**, **spinach**, and a very little **water** in a blender; blend until smooth.
5. Add **1/2 c mayo** and **2 T lemon juice** to the blender and pulse until combined into a creamy sauce.
6. Pour the green sauce over the cooked **fish** and serve immediately.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The green sauce can be prepared up to a day in advance and stored in an airtight container in the refrigerator.
