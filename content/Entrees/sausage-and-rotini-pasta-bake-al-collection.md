# Sausage and Rotini Pasta Bake

> 📜 **Collection:** Al Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A comforting and creamy pasta bake featuring savory Italian sausage, chive and onion cream cheese, and tender spinach. Perfect for an easy weeknight family dinner that comes together in minutes.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 40 mins | None | 6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large bowl
* Baking dish

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 lb | [[Italian Sausage]] | browned (such as Jimmy Dean or Owens) |
| 1 tub | [[Cream Cheese]] | chive and onion flavor |
| 1 can | [[Diced Tomatoes]] | Italian-style |
| 1 package (10 oz) | [[Spinach]] | chopped, thawed and drained |
| To taste | [[Salt]] and [[Black Pepper]] |  |
| 16 oz | [[Rotini Pasta]] | cooked |
| 1 cup | [[Mozzarella Cheese]] | shredded, for topping |
| As needed | [[Cooking Spray]] | such as Pam |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Combine Ingredients
1. In a large bowl, combine the **1 lb** **Italian sausage**, **1 tub** **cream cheese**, **1 can** **diced tomatoes**, and **1 package** **spinach**.
2. Season with **salt** and **black pepper** to taste, then fold in the **16 oz** **rotini pasta** until well incorporated.

### Step 2: Assemble and Bake
3. Transfer the mixture into a baking dish coated with **cooking spray**.
4. Top with **1 cup** **mozzarella cheese**.
5. Cover and bake at 350°F for **30 minutes**.
6. Uncover and bake for an additional **5 to 10 minutes** until the **mozzarella cheese** is melted, bubbly, and slightly browned.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Can be assembled ahead of time, covered, and refrigerated. Add 5 to 10 minutes to the covered baking time if baking directly from the refrigerator.
