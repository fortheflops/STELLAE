# Sweet and Sour Pork

## Instructions

### Step 1: Making the Sauce
1. Combine **3/4 cup less 2 Tbs. dark vinegar**, **1 3/4 cups sugar**, **1 1/2 cups water**, **3 Tbs. soy sauce**, and **4 1/2 Tbs. corn starch** in a saucepan. Bring to a boil, stirring constantly until thickened, then set aside.

### Step 2: Cooking Vegetables and Preparing Pork
2. In a frying pan, use a small amount of **oil** to cook **2 or 3 cut-up onions** and **1 or 2 cans drained chunk pineapple**. Cover and cook until the **onions** are tender.
3. Cut **2 lbs. pork tenderloin** into small cubes.
4. In a bowl, mix the **pork tenderloin** with **1 or 2 Tbs. white wine** (optional), **1 tsp. salt**, **2 Tbs. soy sauce**, **2 beaten eggs**, and add **1 cup corn starch** to coat the meat.

### Step 3: Frying and Assembly
5. Drop the coated **pork tenderloin** by single pieces into hot fat. Cook for **3 to 4 minutes** until golden and crispy, then remove and drain.
6. Put the cooked **pork tenderloin** on a platter, and add the cooked **onions** and **pineapple**.
7. Spoon the hot **sauce** generously over the top and serve immediately.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The sauce can be prepared a day in advance and reheated before serving. For best results, fry the pork just before serving to maintain crispiness.
