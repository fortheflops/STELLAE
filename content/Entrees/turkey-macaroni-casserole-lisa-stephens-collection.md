# Turkey-Macaroni Casserole

> 📜 **Collection:** Lisa Stephens Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A quick and comforting microwave casserole featuring tender cubed turkey, elbow macaroni, green chiles, and a creamy cheddar sauce. Perfect for a fast weeknight dinner or using up leftover holiday turkey.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 15 mins | None | 4 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large saucepan
* Mixing bowl
* Microwave-safe casserole dish with lid

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 8 oz | [[Macaroni]] | uncooked |
| 2 cups | [[Turkey]] | cooked and cubed |
| 1 cup | [[Cheddar Cheese]] | shredded |
| 1/2 cup | [[Green Onions]] | sliced |
| 1 can (10.5 oz) | [[Cream Soup]] | such as cream of chicken or mushroom |
| 1/2 cup | [[Sour Cream]] | plain |
| 1 can (4 oz) | [[Green Chiles]] | diced |

---

## Instructions

### Boil Pasta & Prep
1. Boil **8 oz macaroni** in salted water until tender; drain and toss in the **1/2 cup green onions**.

### Assemble & Microwave
2. Meanwhile, combine the **2 cups turkey**, **1 cup cheddar cheese**, **1 can cream soup**, **1/2 cup sour cream**, and **1 can green chiles** in a mixing bowl until uniform.
3. Mix the cheese mixture with the cooked **8 oz macaroni** and **1/2 cup green onions**.
4. Transfer to a microwave-safe casserole dish, cover, and cook in the microwave until heated through, cheese is melted, and the casserole is bubbling.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole in advance and store covered in the refrigerator for up to 24 hours before microwaving until hot.
