# Mrs. Frumkes' Classic Chicken Parmigiana

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and Breading
1. Preheat oven to **350°F**.
2. Place **boneless, skinless chicken breasts** between two sheets of plastic wrap and pound them into thin, even cutlets.
3. Dip each chicken cutlet first into the **beaten eggs**, then press firmly into the **Italian bread crumbs**, coating both sides thoroughly.

### Step 2: Frying and Assembly
4. In a large skillet, heat a shallow layer of **oil** over medium-high heat.
5. Fry the breaded cutlets until golden brown and crispy on each side.
6. Arrange the fried **chicken** in a single layer in a baking dish.
7. Top each piece of chicken with **sliced mozzarella cheese**.
8. Cover generously with **marinara sauce**.

### Step 3: Baking
9. Bake for **30 minutes**, or until the sauce is bubbly and the **mozzarella cheese** is completely melted and lightly golden.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the dish ahead of time in the baking dish, cover tightly, and refrigerate for up to 24 hours before baking.
