# Slow Roasted Lamb

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty One | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A slow-roasted lamb recipe infused with garlic, rosemary, and red wine. Tender and rich after a long, low bake.*

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 7 hours | None | 6-8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Roasting pan
* Aluminum foil

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 whole | [[Lamb]] | Roast |
| As needed | [[Garlic]] | Cloves, minced or sliced |
| As needed | [[Rosemary]] | Fresh springs |
| 1 cup | [[Red Wine]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and Cooking
1. Preheat the oven to **200 degrees**.
2. Place the **1 whole lamb** in a roasting pan and season with **garlic** and **rosemary**.
3. Pour **1 cup red wine** over the meat.
4. Cover tightly and bake for **7 hours** until the meat is fall-apart tender and fragrant.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Can be marinated with the garlic, rosemary, and red wine a few hours in advance before slow roasting.
