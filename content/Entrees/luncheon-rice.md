# Luncheon Rice

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Marie | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A simple and comforting baked rice casserole featuring cheese and pimento. This classic dish is perfect for a quick and satisfying midday meal.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 60 mins | None | 4 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Baking dish
* Mixing bowl

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 1/2 cups | [[Rice]] | Cooked |
| 1 cup | [[Cheese]] | Grated |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] | Beaten |
| 1 cup | [[Milk]] | Whole |
| 1 small can | [[Pimento]] | Drained |
| to taste | [[Salt]] and [[Pepper]] | Seasoning |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Assembly and Baking
1. Preheat oven to 350°F.
2. In a mixing bowl, combine the **1 1/2 cups cooked rice**, **1 cup cheese**, **2 beaten eggs**, **1 cup milk**, and **1 small can pimento**.
3. Season the mixture with **salt and pepper** to taste.
4. Pour into a greased baking dish and bake for 1 hour until the center is set and the top is lightly golden.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The mixture can be prepared and kept in the refrigerator for up to 12 hours before baking.
