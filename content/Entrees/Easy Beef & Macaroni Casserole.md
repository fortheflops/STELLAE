# Easy Beef & Macaroni Casserole

> 📜 **Collection:** UK15 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A classic, super-simple beef and macaroni one-pot meal, made extra creamy and tangy with the addition of Miracle Whip. A perfect weeknight dinner that's ready in minutes.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 15 mins | None | 4-6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large skillet
* Dutch oven
* Colander
* Saucepan

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 pound | [[Ground Beef]] | Browned and drained |
| 1 (7 ounce) package | [[Macaroni]] | Cooked and drained |
| 1 (30 ounce) jar | [[Spaghetti Sauce]] | Standard jar |
| 1/2 cup | [[Miracle Whip]] | For creamy tanginess |
| As Needed | [[Cheese]] | Shredded, for topping |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Cook Pasta and Beef
1. Cook the **1 (7 ounce) package macaroni** according to package directions until al dente, then drain well.
2. While the macaroni is cooking, brown the **1 pound ground beef** in a large skillet or Dutch oven until fully cooked, then drain off any excess grease.

### Step 2: Combine and Heat
3. Stir the **1/2 cup Miracle Whip** and **1 (30 ounce) jar spaghetti sauce** into the browned ground beef and heat until the sauce is warm and bubbling gently.
4. Add the cooked, drained **macaroni** to the meat sauce and stir thoroughly until well combined and heated through on the stovetop until piping hot.

### Step 3: Serve
5. Serve the casserole in bowls, topped with a generous sprinkle of **cheese** until slightly melted.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftovers in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days. Reheat gently on the stovetop or in the microwave with a splash of water to restore moisture.
