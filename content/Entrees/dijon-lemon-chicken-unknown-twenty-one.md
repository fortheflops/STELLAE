# Dijon Lemon Chicken

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty One | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A simple and tangy baked chicken dish coated in a sweet-and-savory glaze of Dijon mustard, honey, and fresh lemon juice. Tender, juicy chicken breasts baked to perfection.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 35 mins | None | 4 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Baking dish
* Small mixing bowl

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 4 | [[Chicken Breasts]] | Boneless, skinless |
| 1/2 | [[Lemon]] | Juiced |
| 2 T | [[Dijon Mustard]] | None |
| 1 T | [[Honey]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Assembly and Baking
1. Place **4 chicken breasts** in a lightly oiled baking dish.
2. In a small bowl, mix together the **1/2 lemon juice**, **2 T Dijon mustard**, and **1 T honey**, then spread the mixture evenly over the chicken.
3. Bake for **30-40 minutes** or until the chicken is tender and cooked through.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the chicken and glaze in the baking dish, cover, and refrigerate up to 4 hours before baking.
