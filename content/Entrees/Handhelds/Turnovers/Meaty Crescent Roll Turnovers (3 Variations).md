___
### Filling Options
**Ham Filling**
* Ground [[Ham]]
* [[Sweet Pickle]]
* [[Mayonnaise]]
* 1 T [[Milk]]
* [[Horseradish]]

**Chicken Filling**
* Cooked, chopped [[Chicken]]
* 1/2 c [[Sour Cream]]
* 3 T chopped [[Pimento]]
* [[Salt]]

**Tuna Filling**
* 1 can Flaked [[Tuna]]
* 1/2 c Shredded [[Cheese]]
* 1/4 c chopped [[Celery]]
* 1/4 c [[Mayonnaise]]
* 1 T [[Lemon Juice]]
* [[Salt]]

___
### Instructions
1.  Preheat oven to 375°.
2.  Separate dough into triangles & place 4 triangles on greased cookie sheets.
3.  Mound & spread filling about 1/4" from edge of triangle.
4.  Beat egg with 1 T water, use to moisten edges of triangles.
5.  Top with remaining triangles, pinch edges together firmly.
6.  Brush tops with egg mixture.
7.  Bake 20-25 m until browned.
8.  Slice in half & serve hot.
