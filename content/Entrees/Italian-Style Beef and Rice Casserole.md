# Italian-Style Beef and Rice Casserole

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A rich, hearty, and layered casserole featuring a slow-cooked beef and tomato ragu baked with rice, meatballs, hard-boiled eggs, and Swiss cheese. This true company casserole brings together classic Italian-American comfort flavors.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Tomato Sauce
1. In a large pot or Dutch oven, brown the **2 pounds stewing beef**.
2. Add the **1 large can (28 oz) tomato puree**, **1 can (6 oz) tomato paste**, **1 can (15 oz) tomato sauce**, **1-2 cloves garlic**, **1 tsp dried basil**, and **1 tsp dried parsley**.
3. Bring to a simmer, then reduce heat to low, cover, and cook slowly for **2.5 to 3 hours**, stirring occasionally, until the meat is tender and rich. Remove and discard the whole garlic cloves.

### Step 2: Prepare Casserole Components
4. While the sauce simmers, prepare the meatballs: In a bowl, combine the **1 pound ground round**, **1/2 cup bread crumbs**, **1 egg**, **1 clove garlic**, **1/2 tsp dried basil**, and **salt and pepper**. Mix well, form into small meatballs, fry in a skillet until browned, and drain on paper towels.
5. Cook the **2 cups uncooked rice** according to package directions and drain.
6. Hard-boil the **4-5 eggs**, let them cool completely, peel, and slice them.

### Step 3: Assemble and Bake
7. Preheat the oven to **350°F (175°C)** and grease a large baking dish.
8. In a bowl, mix the cooked **rice** with just enough of the prepared tomato-beef sauce to color and moisten it.
9. Spread about **5 tablespoons of the plain tomato sauce** on the bottom of the baking dish.
10. Create the layers: start with a layer of the sauce-colored **rice**, then add a layer of sliced **1/4 pound Swiss cheese**, a layer of sliced **4-5 eggs**, and a layer of the cooked **meatballs**.
11. Continue alternating the layers of **rice**, **Swiss cheese**, **eggs**, **meatballs**, and sauce until the dish is almost full, and cover the top lightly with a final layer of sauce.
12. Bake at **350°F** for **30-40 minutes**, or until the cheese is melted and the casserole is heated through, bubbly, and fragrant.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The tomato sauce and meatballs can be prepared the day before and stored separately in the refrigerator until ready to assemble and bake.
