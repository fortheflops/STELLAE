# Company Baked Pork Chops

> 📜 **Collection:** Georgia Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *Hearty baked pork chops layered with potatoes, onions, cheddar cheese, and cream of mushroom soup. A comforting and easy all-in-one casserole dish.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 2 hours | None | 4 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 9x13 pan
* Aluminum foil

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 4 med. | [[Pork Chops]] | Uncooked |
| To taste | [[Salt]] & [[Pepper]] | For seasoning |
| 1 cup | [[Cheddar]] | Grated, divided |
| 2 med. | [[Onions]] | Sliced |
| 4 med. | [[Potatoes]] | Sliced |
| 1 cup | [[Cream of Mushroom Soup]] | Undiluted |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Assembly
1. Place **4 med. pork chops** in a **9x13 pan** and season with **salt & pepper**.
2. Sprinkle **1/2 cup cheese** over the **pork chops**.
3. Top with layers of **2 med. onions** and **4 med. potatoes**. Season again.
4. Pour **1 cup cream of mushroom soup** over all, and sprinkle with the remaining **1/2 cup cheese**.

### Step 2: Baking
5. Cover the pan and bake at 350° for **2 hours**, or until the **potatoes** are fork-tender and the **pork chops** are cooked through with a golden, bubbly cheese top.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole up to a few hours in advance, cover, and refrigerate until ready to bake; add 15-20 minutes to the baking time if baking straight from the fridge.
