# Parmesan Chicken Breasts

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty One | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *Crispy Parmesan-coated chicken breasts baked to golden perfection with a seasoned bread crumb crust. A quick and flavorful main dish that is ready in under 30 minutes.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 20 mins | None | 6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* 15x10 baking dish

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 6 | [[Chicken breasts]] | Boneless skinless |
| 1/2 cup | [[Parmesan cheese]] | Grated |
| 1/4 cup | [[Bread crumbs]] | Seasoned |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Paprika]] | None |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Black pepper]] | Ground |
| 3 tbsp | [[Oleo]] | Melted |
| Optional | [[Red and yellow tomato wedges, zucchini curls, and fresh basil leaves]] | For garnish |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and Coating
1. Mix the **1/2 cup Parmesan cheese**, **1/4 cup bread crumbs**, **1/4 tsp paprika**, and **1/4 tsp black pepper** together in a bowl.
2. Dip each of the **6 chicken breasts** in the **3 tbsp oleo**, then coat thoroughly with the cheese mixture.
3. Place the coated chicken into a **15x10 baking dish**.

### Step 2: Baking and Serving
4. Bake at 400°F for **20 minutes** or until cooked through and juices run clear.
5. Garnish with **red and yellow tomato wedges**, **zucchini curls**, and **fresh basil leaves**, if desired.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Chicken can be coated ahead of time and refrigerated for up to 4 hours before baking.
