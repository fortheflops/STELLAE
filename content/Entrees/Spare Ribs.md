# Spare Ribs

> 📜 **Collection:** JillCollection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A classic vintage recipe for slow-roasted spare ribs, seasoned simply and baked until tender and golden brown. Perfect for a hearty family dinner with homemade gravy from the savory drippings.*

| As Needed | [[Water]] | for washing and gravy |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Season
1. Preheat oven to **450°F**.
2. Trim off as much fat as possible from the **1 rack (approx) spare ribs**.
3. Wash the meat with **water** and pat dry to remove excess moisture.
4. Season the [[Spare Ribs]] well with **salt** to taste.

### Step 2: Roast and Brown
5. Place the seasoned [[Spare Ribs]] in a roasting pan.
6. Bake for **2 to 2 1/2 hours**, changing the position of the ribs periodically during baking so they get evenly brown all over until golden brown and tender.
7. Remove the lid during the final stages for further browning if needed.
8. When making gravy from the drippings, remove excess fat and add **water** if needed.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Leftover spare ribs can be stored in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 to 4 days.
