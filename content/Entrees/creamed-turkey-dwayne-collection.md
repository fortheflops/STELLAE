# Creamed Turkey

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Gravy Base
1. Chop the **1 red pepper** and **1 green pepper** into small pieces.
2. In a skillet, sauté the chopped peppers in about **1/4 cup vegetable oil** over medium heat until they are softened and fragrant.
3. Stir in **1 cup flour** to coat the peppers, then gradually whisk in **whole milk** until a smooth, thick gravy forms.

### Step 2: Assemble and Bake
4. Cut the cooked **turkey** into bite-sized pieces and place them into a casserole dish.
5. Add the **5 oz jar pimientos** to the **turkey** in the dish.
6. Pour the prepared gravy over the **turkey** and **pimientos**, stirring thoroughly to combine.
7. Bake in a preheated oven at **350 degrees** for **1 1/2 hours** until the casserole is bubbling hot and lightly browned on top.

### Step 3: Serve
8. Spoon the hot creamed turkey generously over warm **mashed potatoes** and split **buttermilk biscuits**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This casserole can be assembled up to 24 hours in advance and stored covered in the refrigerator. Increase the baking time by 10-15 minutes if baking directly from the fridge.
