# Hamburger in Silver

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A simple and savory 'Hamburger in Silver' recipe featuring two hamburger patties stuffed with cheese and mushrooms, seasoned, and baked in a foil packet.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 40 mins | None | 2 large stuffed patties |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Aluminum foil
* Baking sheet
* Oven

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 4 | [[Hamburger Patties]] | approx. 1 lb total |
| 2 slices | [[Cheese]] | Cheddar or Swiss |
| 1 small can | [[Mushrooms]] | 4 oz, sliced, drained |
| To Taste | [[Onion Salt]] | None |
| To Taste | [[Soy Sauce]] | None |
| To Taste | [[Celery Salt]] | None |
| To Taste | [[Tarragon]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Assemble Packets
1. Preheat the oven to **350°F**.
2. For each packet, lay out a large sheet of aluminum foil.
3. Place one **hamburger patty** on the foil.
4. Top the patty with half of the drained **mushrooms**, then one slice of **cheese**.
5. Season to taste with **onion salt**, **soy sauce**, **celery salt**, and **tarragon**.
6. Place another **hamburger patty** on top of the cheese to create a stuffed patty.
7. Wrap the aluminum foil tightly around the entire stack, sealing the edges completely, and repeat for the second packet.

### Step 2: Bake and Serve
8. Place the foil packets on a baking sheet and bake for **40 minutes**, or until the hamburger is cooked through and savory aromas fill the kitchen.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Packets can be assembled a few hours in advance and stored in the refrigerator until ready to bake.
