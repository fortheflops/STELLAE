# Creamy Chicken & Brussels Sprout Bake

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A comforting and hearty casserole featuring diced cooked chicken and tender Brussels sprouts baked in a simple, homemade seasoned cream sauce.*

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 40 Ounces (2.5 lbs) | Frozen [[Brussels Sprouts]] | Frozen |
| 2.5 Pounds | Cooked [[Chicken]] | Diced |
| 1/4 Cup | [[Pimento]] | Chopped |
| 4 Tablespoons | [[Butter]] | Unsalted or salted |
| 4 Tablespoons | [[Flour]] | For roux |
| 2 Cups | [[Chicken Broth]] | Or milk |
| 1 Cup | Reconstituted Nonfat Dry [[Milk]] | Or 1 can Cream of Chicken/Mushroom Soup |
| 2 Teaspoons | [[Paprika]] | Standard |
| 2 Teaspoons | [[Salt]] | To taste |
| 1 Teaspoon | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Sauce
1. Preheat your oven to **350°F**.
2. Cook the **40 ounces frozen brussels sprouts** according to the directions on the box. Drain them well.
3. In a large saucepan, melt the **4 tablespoons butter** and stir in the **4 tablespoons flour** to make a roux, cooking for **1-2 minutes** until smooth and fragrant.
4. Gradually whisk in the **2 cups chicken broth** and the **1 cup reconstituted nonfat dry milk**. Stir constantly until the sauce thickens and bubbles.

### Step 2: Assemble and Bake
5. Add the **2 teaspoons paprika**, **2 teaspoons salt**, **1 teaspoon sugar**, **1/4 cup pimento**, and **2.5 pounds cooked chicken** to the sauce. Stir until thoroughly combined.
6. Arrange the cooked and drained **brussels sprouts** in a large casserole dish.
7. Pour the creamed chicken mixture evenly over the sprouts.
8. Bake at **350°F** for **25-30 minutes**, or until heated through and lightly browned and bubbly on top.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole ahead of time, cover tightly, and refrigerate for up to 24 hours before baking, adding a few extra minutes to the bake time if cold.
