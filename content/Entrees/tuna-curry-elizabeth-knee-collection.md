## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Base
1. In a medium saucepan over medium-low heat, combine the **1 recipe white sauce (or 1 can celery soup with milk)** and **1 can tuna**.
2. Simmer the mixture gently for a few minutes until it is heated through and bubbling slightly around the edges.

### Step 2: Fold in Fresh Ingredients & Serve
3. Remove the saucepan from the heat. Stir in the **1/2 cup chopped celery**, **1/4 chopped bell pepper**, **1 tbsp grated or finely chopped onions**, **2 tsp curry powder**, and **2 chopped hard-boiled eggs**.
4. Do not cook these final ingredients; simply fold them into the warm tuna and sauce mixture so they retain their crisp texture and fresh flavor.
5. Serve immediately over warm **rice** accompanied by **chutney** and **coconut**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The tuna and white sauce base can be prepared ahead and kept in the refrigerator for up to 2 days. Reheat gently before folding in the fresh celery, bell pepper, onions, and hard-boiled eggs just before serving to maintain their crunch.
