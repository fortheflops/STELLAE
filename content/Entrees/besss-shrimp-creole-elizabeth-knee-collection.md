# Bess's Shrimp Creole

> 📜 **Collection:** Elizabeth Knee Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Bess | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A classic, comforting Southern-style Shrimp Creole recipe dated May 9, 1977. Tender shrimp are simmered in a savory, spiced tomato sauce with a traditional aromatic base of onions, green peppers, and celery, served over hot rice.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Sauté Aromatics
1. In a large skillet, melt the **3 tbsp butter** over medium heat. Sauté the **3/4 cup chopped green pepper**, **1 cup chopped onion**, **1 clove finely cut garlic**, and **1/2 cup diced celery** until they are softened and fragrant.

### Step 2: Simmer Sauce
2. Add the **1 tsp salt**, **1/4 tsp black pepper**, **pinch of paprika**, **pinch of thyme**, **1 small bay leaf**, **1/2 tsp dried parsley**, **1 tbsp sugar**, optional **red pepper**, and **2 cups canned tomatoes** to the skillet.
3. Bring the mixture to a boil, then reduce the heat and simmer gently for **15-18 minutes** until the sauce has thickened slightly and the flavors are well blended.

### Step 3: Add Shrimp and Serve
4. Stir in the **1 lb large cooked shrimp** and heat thoroughly until the shrimp is hot and fully warmed through.
5. Serve immediately over **3 cups hot cooked rice**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The Creole tomato sauce base can be prepared up to 24 hours in advance and stored in the refrigerator. Reheat the sauce gently and stir in the cooked shrimp just before serving to prevent them from becoming rubbery.
