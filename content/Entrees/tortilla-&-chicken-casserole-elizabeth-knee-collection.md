# Tortilla & Chicken Casserole

> 📜 **Collection:** Elizabeth Knee Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A comforting, layered Southwestern-style casserole featuring tender chicken, green chiles, and tortillas baked under a bubbly blanket of melted cheese. Perfect for an easy weeknight family dinner.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 30 mins | None | 6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 9x13-inch baking pan
* Mixing bowl

---
## Ingredients

### Casserole Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 package (12 count) | [[Tortillas]] | divided |
| 1 can | [[Cream of Chicken Soup]] | undiluted |
| 1 can | [[Green Chiles]] | chopped |
| 1 | [[Onion]] | chopped |
| 2 to 3 | [[Chicken Breasts]] | cut into chunks |
| To taste | [[Cheese]] | grated |

---

## Instructions

### Preparation and Assembly
1. Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C).
2. In a mixing bowl, thoroughly mix together the **1 can cream of chicken soup**, **chunks of chicken (2 or 3 breasts)**, **1 can chopped green chiles**, and **1 chopped onion**.
3. In a **9x13-inch baking pan**, arrange **6 tortillas** evenly across the bottom.
4. Spread half of the chicken and soup mixture over the bottom layer of tortillas.
5. Place **6 more tortillas** on top, then layer the remaining chicken and soup mixture evenly over them.

### Baking
6. Generously sprinkle **grated cheese** over the top layer.
7. Bake for **30 minutes** until the **grated cheese** melts well, is bubbly, and starts to turn slightly golden brown at the edges.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This casserole can be assembled, covered tightly with foil, and refrigerated up to 24 hours before baking. If baking directly from the refrigerator, add an extra 5 to 10 minutes to the baking time.
