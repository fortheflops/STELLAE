# Sweet & Savory Hawaiian Baked Chicken

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A classic baked chicken dish featuring cornstarch-coated fried chicken baked with pineapple, peppers, and onions. Smothered in a sweet and tangy sauce made from pan drippings, pineapple juice, and soy sauce.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 1 hour | None | 4-6 servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large baking dish
* Bag for coating
* Large skillet
* Small bowl
* Aluminum foil

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 to 3 pounds | [[Chicken]] | cut up |
| 1/2 cup | [[Cornstarch]] | none |
| To taste | [[Salt]] | none |
| 1/2 cup | [[Salad Oil]] | for frying |
| 1 large can | [[Pineapple Chunks]] | reserve juice |
| 1 small bunch | [[Onions]] | cut up, tops too |
| 1 cup | [[Celery]] | cut in medium chunks |
| 2 | Bell [[Peppers]] | seeded and cut up |
| 1/4 cup | [[Brown Sugar]] | none |
| 3 tablespoons | [[Soy Sauce]] | none |
| 1 teaspoon | Powdered or Fresh [[Ginger]] | none |
| For serving | Boiled [[Rice]] | optional accompaniment |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Fry the Chicken
1. Preheat your oven to **350°F** and grease a large baking dish.
2. In a bag, shake the **2 to 3 pounds chicken** pieces with **salt** and **1/2 cup cornstarch** until evenly coated.
3. In a large skillet, heat the **1/2 cup salad oil**. Fry the coated chicken pieces until they are medium brown and golden on all sides, then place them into the prepared baking dish.

### Step 2: Assemble and Bake
4. Arrange the chopped **1 small bunch onions**, **1 cup celery**, **2 bell peppers**, and the drained **1 large can pineapple chunks** directly over and around the chicken pieces.
5. Make the sauce: Measure out **1/3 cup** of the remaining oil from the frying pan. In a small bowl, combine this oil with the **1/4 cup brown sugar**, the reserved **pineapple juice**, **3 tablespoons soy sauce**, and **1 teaspoon ginger**, mixing well until fully incorporated.
6. Pour the sweet and savory sauce evenly over everything in the baking dish.
7. Cover the baking dish tightly with aluminum foil and bake at **350°F** for **45 minutes**, or until the chicken is fork-tender and done.

### Step 3: Serve
8. Serve hot alongside a bed of freshly boiled **rice**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Leftovers can be stored in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days and reheated thoroughly.
