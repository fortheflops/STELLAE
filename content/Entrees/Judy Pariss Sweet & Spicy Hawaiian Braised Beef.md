# Judy Paris's Sweet & Spicy Hawaiian Braised Beef

> 📜 **Collection:** General Archive | ✍️ **Attribution:** Judy Paris | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A show-stopping pot roast from Judy Paris featuring a 24-hour sweet and spiced wine marinade. Slow-braised in a Dutch oven until fall-apart tender and served over hot rice.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Marinate the Beef
1. In a large bowl, combine the **1 C. brown sugar**, **1 clove garlic**, **1/2 C. red wine vinegar**, **1/2 C. burgundy wine**, **1/8 tsp. nutmeg**, **1/4 tsp. powdered ginger**, **1/2 tsp. cinnamon**, **1 bay leaf**, **2 med. onions**, and **1 tsp. seasoned salt**.
2. Place the **3-4 lbs. beef chuck** in the large bowl and pour the marinade over it, ensuring the meat is completely covered.
3. Marinate the meat in the refrigerator for **24 hours**, turning frequently.

### Step 2: Braise and Serve
4. When ready to cook, preheat the oven to **325°F**.
5. Transfer the **beef roast** and all of its marinade into a Dutch oven.
6. Cook covered in the oven at **325°F** for **2 1/2 to 3 hours**, or until the meat is fork-tender.
7. Remove the **beef roast** to a hot serving platter.
8. Serve with **rice**, pouring the flavorful juices from the Dutch oven over each helping until well coated.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The beef can be marinated for up to 24 hours in the refrigerator before cooking.
