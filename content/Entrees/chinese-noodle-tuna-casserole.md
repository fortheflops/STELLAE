# Chinese Noodle Tuna Casserole

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A simple, layered casserole featuring Chinese noodles, tuna, and crunchy cashews baked in a creamy mushroom sauce. This classic comfort dish is perfect for a quick weeknight meal.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 40 mins | None | 4 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Casserole dish

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 can | [[Chinese noodles]] | Standard size |
| 1 can | [[Tuna]] | Drained |
| To taste | [[Cashew nuts]] | Chopped |
| 1 can | [[Mushroom soup]] | Condensed |
| 2 tbsp | [[Cooking wine]] | Dry |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Assembly
1. Layer the **1 can Chinese noodles** and **1 can tuna** in a casserole dish.
2. Sprinkle the **cashew nuts** over the layers and repeat the process.
3. Cover the mixture with the **1 can mushroom soup** and drizzle with **2 tbsp cooking wine**.

### Step 2: Baking
4. Bake in a slow oven at 325°F for 40 minutes until the top is golden and the sauce is bubbling.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** You can assemble the casserole ahead of time and refrigerate; add 10 minutes to the baking time if cooking from cold.
