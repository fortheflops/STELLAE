# Fettuccine Alfredo

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *Rich and comforting classic Fettuccine Alfredo featuring tender pasta tossed in a creamy butter and parmesan sauce. A simple yet indulgent weeknight dinner favorite from the archive.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5 mins | 15 mins | None | 4 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large pot
* Colander
* Saucepan or serving pot

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| ½ Stick | [[Butter]] | softened |
| 1 lb | [[Fettuccine Noodles]] | uncooked |
| 1 Cup | Grated [[Parmesan Cheese]] | plus extra for serving |
| ½ Cup | [[Heavy Cream]] | room temperature |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Boil the Noodles
1. Boil the **1 lb fettuccine noodles** in a large pot of salted water for **10 minutes** until al dente, then drain well.

### Step 2: Combine and Thicken
2. Melt the **½ stick butter** in the pot; add the drained **1 lb fettuccine noodles**, **½ cup heavy cream**, and **1 cup grated parmesan cheese**.
3. Cook over low heat, stirring gently, until the sauce coats the noodles and thickens into a rich, velvety consistency.

### Step 3: Serve
4. Serve immediately while hot, topped with additional grated **parmesan cheese**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Best served fresh, as the pasta will absorb the cream sauce as it sits.
