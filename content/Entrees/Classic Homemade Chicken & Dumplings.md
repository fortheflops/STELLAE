# Classic Homemade Chicken & Dumplings

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A classic, from-scratch recipe featuring a whole chicken simmered into a rich broth, chilled to collect the fat, and used to create a simple, dense 'slick' dumpling dough.*

## Instructions

### Cooking the Chicken
1. Place **One cut-up fryer chicken** with extra legs in a large pot. Add **As needed water** to cover.
2. Add **One (Small) onion**, **As needed carrot**, **As needed diced garlic**, **As needed celery**, **One bay leaf**, and **One teaspoon salt & pepper**.
3. Bring the pot to a boil, then reduce the heat and simmer. Test the chicken for tenderness with a fork after about **30 minutes** until it is tender but not falling off the bone.
4. Once cooked, remove the pot from the heat and let the chicken and broth cool completely.
5. Put the pot in the refrigerator until the **As needed skimmed chicken grease** settles on top and solidifies.

### Preparing the Broth and Dough
6. Skim the cold, solidified **As needed skimmed chicken grease** off the top of the broth and set aside.
7. Remove the chicken meat from the bones and set it aside.
8. In a bowl, combine the **Two cups flour** and **One teaspoon salt**.
9. Add the skimmed chicken grease to the flour mixture. Work the grease in with your hands until you can squeeze the dough together.
10. Roll out the dough and cut it into big squares.

### Cooking the Dumplings
11. Bring the defatted chicken broth back to a rolling boil, adding extra water if needed to ensure plenty of boiling liquid.
12. Drop the **Dumplings** one by one into the boiling broth.
13. Cover the pot. When it returns to a boil, turn the heat down to a slow, gentle boil.
14. Cook for about **12 minutes** until the dumplings are tender and cooked through.
15. Add the deboned chicken meat back to the pot and heat through for a few minutes until hot and fragrant before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftover chicken and dumplings sealed in the refrigerator for up to 3 days.
