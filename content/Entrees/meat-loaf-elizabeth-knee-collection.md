# Meat Loaf

> 📜 **Collection:** Elizabeth Knee Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** C. Carpenter | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A classic, forgiving family-style meatloaf recipe from the kitchen of C. Carpenter. Features a customizable blend of ground beef, ketchup, mustard, and optional cheese stuffing.*

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 lb or more | [[Hamburger]] | Ground beef |
| 4 Tbl. | [[Ketchup]] | None |
| 1 | [[Mustard]] | Prepared |
| 1 small | [[Egg]] | Beaten |
| about 5 | [[Crackers]] | Crushed, optional |
| to taste | [[Onions]] | Chopped, optional |
| as needed | [[Longhorn or American Cheese]] | Optional center filling |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing and Shaping
1. Place **1 lb or more hamburger** in a bowl.
2. Add **4 Tbl. ketsup**, **1 mustard**, and **1 small egg**.
3. Mix together with hands, incorporating **about 5 crackers** and **onions** if desired, adjusting amounts until you can form a loaf that holds together.

### Step 2: Baking
4. If desired, place **longhorn or American cheese** in the center of the loaf and fold the meat over it.
5. Bake at 350° for **30-40 min.**, depending on the size and thickness of the loaf, until cooked through and browned.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The meatloaf can be shaped and kept covered in the refrigerator for up to 24 hours before baking.
