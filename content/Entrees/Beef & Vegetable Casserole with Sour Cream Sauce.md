# Beef & Vegetable Casserole with Sour Cream Sauce

> *A hearty, old-fashioned casserole featuring tender cubes of round steak layered with onions, mushrooms, peas, and carrots, all baked in a rich and tangy sour cream sauce.*

| 2 cups | [[Sour Cream]] | Rich and tangy |

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Layer
1. Preheat oven to **375°F**. Grease a baking dish.
2. Cut the **1½ lbs round steak** into 2-inch cubes. Pound well with a mallet to tenderize.
3. Season the meat with **salt** and **pepper**, then brown in a skillet with **1-2 tbsp fat** over medium-high heat until nicely seared.
4. In the prepared baking dish, create a layer using half of the **onions**, **mushrooms**, **peas**, **carrots**, and **capers**.
5. Add a layer of the browned meat, then top with the remaining vegetables. Ensure the top layer is meat.

### Step 2: Make the Sauce and Bake
6. **Make the Sauce:** In a saucepan, melt **3 tbsp fat** over medium heat. Whisk in the **1 tbsp flour** and cook for **1 minute** to form a roux.
7. Gradually whisk in the **2 cups sour cream**. Add the **2 bay leaves** and heat gently until warm and smooth, ensuring it does not boil.
8. Pour the warm sauce evenly over the layers in the baking dish.
9. Cover the dish tightly with a lid or aluminum foil and bake for **1.5 to 2 hours**, or until the meat is completely tender and fork-pierced.
