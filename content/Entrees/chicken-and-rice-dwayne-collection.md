# Chicken and Rice

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A comforting, slow-baked casserole featuring tender chicken breasts, seasoned rice, and a rich blend of mushroom, chicken, and celery soups. Topped with sliced almonds and baked low and slow until perfectly tender and golden.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 3 hours | None | 6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Baking pan
* Mixing bowl

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 1/2 cups | [[Rice]] | Uncooked, not instant |
| 1 can | [[Mushroom Soup]] | Condensed |
| 1 can | [[Chicken Soup]] | Condensed |
| 1 can | [[Celery Soup]] | Condensed |
| 1 1/2 cans | [[Water]] | Measured using soup cans |
| 1 can | [[Mushrooms]] | Sliced |
| 1/2 cup | [[Almonds]] | Slivered |
| 2 | [[Chicken Breasts]] | Halves |
| 1 stick | [[Butter]] | Melted (plus extra for buttering the pan) |
| To taste | [[Salt]] | None |
| To taste | [[Pepper]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Assembly and Baking
1. Place **1 1/2 cups rice** in a buttered baking pan.
2. In a bowl, mix **1 can mushroom soup**, **1 can chicken soup**, **1 can celery soup**, **1 1/2 cans water**, **1 can mushrooms**, and **1/2 cup almonds**, then pour the mixture evenly over the rice.
3. Season **2 chicken breasts** with **salt** and **pepper**, dip them in **1 stick butter**, and place them on top of the rice.
4. Bake at 250°F for 3 hours until the rice is tender and the chicken is fully cooked through.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole up to a few hours in advance, cover tightly with foil, and refrigerate before baking.
