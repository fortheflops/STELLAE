## Instructions

### Step 1: Poach the Fish
1. Dice and slice the **1/4 pound scallops**. Cut the **1/4 pound haddock (fresh or frozen, no skin)** into small pieces and remove any skin and bone.
2. Place the fish in a pan with the **2/3 cup milk or dry white wine**, **1/2 onion, chopped**, and **4 sprigs parsley, chopped**.
3. Bring the mixture to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer for **5 minutes** until the fish is cooked through.
4. Drain the fish, reserving the cooking liquid on one side.

### Step 2: Prepare the Sauce
5. In a separate pan, melt the **4 tablespoons butter**. Add the **6 tablespoons flour** and cook for **1 minute**, stirring constantly.
6. Gradually stir in the reserved fish cooking liquid to the butter and flour mixture. Bring to a boil, stirring continuously until the sauce thickens and coats the back of a spoon. If more liquid is needed for the sauce, use part of the **2 cups milk**.

### Step 3: Assemble and Serve
7. Add the **1/2 cup grated Monterey Jack cheese**, the cooked scallops, and haddock to the sauce. Season well with **salt & pepper to taste**.
8. Divide the fish and sauce mixture between **4-6** scallop shells or individual ovenproof dishes.
9. Pipe the **mashed potatoes** in a border around the edge of each dish until picturesque and golden brown when baked.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the filled shells ahead of time and store covered in the refrigerator for up to 24 hours before baking.
