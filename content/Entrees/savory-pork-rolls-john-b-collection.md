# Savory Pork Rolls

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Stuff
1. Trim fat and remove any bone from **6 pork shoulder steaks**. Pound slices with a mallet or a meat hammer to thin them.
2. Season the steaks with **1 tsp salt** and **1/4 tsp pepper**.
3. Combine **1 egg**, **1/4 cup milk**, and **2 cups cubed soft bread**. Add **1/4 cup chopped onion**, **1/2 lb ground beef**, **1 tsp prepared mustard**, **1/4 tsp poultry seasoning**, and **1/2 tsp salt**. Mix well.
4. Spoon stuffing onto the **pork shoulder steaks**, roll up jelly-roll fashion, and fasten with wooden picks or a string.

### Step 2: Brown and Bake
5. Dust the rolls with **2 tbsp flour**. Brown lightly with **1 tbsp fat or salad oil** in a skillet.
6. Remove meat to a **1.5-quart casserole dish**. Pour off excess fat in the skillet, add **1/2 cup water** to the brown drippings, stir, and pour over the rolls in the casserole.
7. Cover and bake in a moderate 350° oven for **1 hour** until tender and cooked through.

### Step 3: Mushroom Sauce and Serving
8. Serve with mushroom sauce made by combining **1 can condensed mushroom soup** and **1/2 cup milk**. Simmer for **2 minutes** until hot and bubbling.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Rolls can be assembled and placed in the casserole dish a few hours ahead of time, covered and refrigerated until ready to brown and bake.
