# Classic Mexican Casserole

> 📜 **Collection:** General Archive | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A classic and simple layered enchilada-style casserole featuring seasoned ground beef rolled in lightly fried tortillas, topped with tomato sauce and melted cheese.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 30 mins | None | 6-8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large skillet
* Smaller frying pan
* Small saucepan
* 9x13 inch baking dish
* Paper towels

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 Pound | [[Ground Beef]] | raw |
| 1 Teaspoon | [[Chili Powder]] | none |
| 1 Teaspoon | [[Salt]] | none |
| To taste | [[Black Pepper]] | none |
| 1 | [[Chopped Onion]] | chopped |
| As needed | [[Salad Oil]] | for frying |
| 12 | [[Tortillas]] | corn |
| 1 Can (15 oz) | [[Tomato Sauce]] | none |
| 1 Can (15 oz) | [[Water]] | using the same can |
| 1-2 cups | [[Grated Cheese]] | grated |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Cook the Filling and Prepare Tortillas
1. Preheat oven to **375°F (190°C)**. Grease a **9x13 inch baking dish**.
2. In a large skillet, brown the **1 pound ground beef** with the **1 chopped onion**, **1 teaspoon chili powder**, **1 teaspoon salt**, and **black pepper** until the meat is cooked through and onions are soft. Drain any excess fat.
3. In a separate, smaller pan, heat about **1/4 inch of salad oil** over medium-high heat. One at a time, dip each **12 corn tortillas** into the hot oil for about **30-60 seconds**, just until softened and pliable. Drain on paper towels.

### Step 2: Assemble and Bake
4. Fill each softened tortilla with a spoonful of the meat mixture and roll it up. Place the rolled tortillas seam-side down in the prepared **baking dish**.
5. In a small saucepan, heat the **1 can tomato sauce** to boiling. Add **1 can water** and stir to combine. Pour this sauce mixture evenly over the rolled tortillas in the dish.
6. Top generously with **1-2 cups grated cheese**.
7. Bake at **375°F** for **30 minutes**, or until the casserole is hot and the cheese is melted, bubbly, and golden brown.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole ahead of time, cover tightly, and refrigerate for up to 24 hours before baking.
