# Chicken Supreme

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep Ingredients
1. Cut the cooked **1 large chicken** into fairly small, bite-sized pieces.
2. Cook the **4 oz egg noodles** in boiling salted water until tender, then drain thoroughly.

### Step 2: Make the Sauce
3. In a saucepan over medium heat, melt the **6 tbsp butter**, add the **6 tbsp flour**, and stir to blend into a smooth paste.
4. Stir in the **1 1/2 tsp salt**, **1/2 tsp black pepper**, **1/2 tsp celery salt**, **Beau Monde seasoning**, and **marjoram**.
5. Gradually pour in the **2 cups chicken broth** and cook over low heat, stirring constantly, until the sauce becomes thick and smooth.

### Step 3: Assemble and Bake
6. Remove the sauce from the heat and gently stir in the **2 cups scalded heavy cream**, **2 tbsp sherry wine**, **1 large can mushrooms**, **1/3 cup toasted slivered almonds**, **3 tbsp minced parsley**, the cooked **4 oz egg noodles**, and the diced **1 large chicken**.
7. Pour the mixture into a buttered **2-quart casserole dish** and top evenly with the **1/2 cup grated cheddar cheese**.
8. Bake uncovered in a preheated **350°F** oven for **35-40 minutes** until bubbling hot and the cheese is melted and golden brown.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This casserole can be assembled, covered, and refrigerated up to 24 hours before baking. Add 5 to 10 minutes to the baking time if cooking straight from the refrigerator.
