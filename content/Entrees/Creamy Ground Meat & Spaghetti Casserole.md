# Creamy Ground Meat & Spaghetti Casserole

> 📜 **Collection:** General Archive | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A classic, comforting casserole with layers of spaghetti, seasoned ground beef in a creamy mushroom sauce, and plenty of melted cheese. A perfect weeknight dinner.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 20 mins | None | 6–8 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Casserole dish
* Large skillet
* Saucepan

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 pkg. | [[Spaghetti]] | 8 oz, cooked and drained |
| 1 lb. | [[Ground Meat]] | or ground beef |
| 1 | [[Onion]] | medium size, chopped |
| 1 can | [[Mushroom Soup]] | 10.5 oz, cream of mushroom |
| 2 cups | [[Grated Cheese]] | divided |
| To taste | [[Salt]] | None |
| To taste | [[Pepper]] | None |
| To taste | [[Garlic Salt]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Cook Base
1. Preheat oven to **350°F**. Grease a casserole dish.
2. Cook the **1/2 pkg. spaghetti** according to package directions. Drain well.
3. In a large skillet, brown the **1 medium size onion (chopped)** and **1 lb. ground meat**. Drain any excess fat until the meat is fully cooked and the onions are translucent.
4. Stir the **1 can mushroom soup** into the meat mixture. Season with **salt**, **pepper**, and **garlic salt** to taste until well combined.

### Step 2: Assemble and Bake
5. In the prepared casserole dish, spread a layer of the meat mixture, top with a layer of cooked **spaghetti**, then a layer of **2 cups grated cheese**.
6. Repeat the layers, ending with a final layer of cheese on top.
7. Bake for **20 minutes**, or until the casserole is heated through and the cheese is melted, bubbly, and lightly golden.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole ahead of time and store covered in the refrigerator for up to 24 hours before baking, adding a few extra minutes to the bake time if cold.
