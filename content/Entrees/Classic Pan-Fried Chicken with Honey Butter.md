## Instructions

### Step 1: Coat the Chicken
1. Wash the cut-up **One broiler-fryer chicken** pieces and pat them dry.
2. In a paper or plastic bag, combine **1/2 cup flour**, **two teaspoons salt**, **1/4 teaspoon pepper**, and **one teaspoon paprika**.
3. Shake a few pieces of chicken at a time in the bag until they are well coated.

### Step 2: Fry the Chicken
4. In a large skillet, heat about one inch of **shortening** or cooking **oil** over medium-high heat.
5. Place the chicken pieces, skin-side down, in the hot oil. Be careful not to overcrowd the skillet.
6. Brown the chicken on all sides until golden and crisp.
7. Reduce the heat, cover the skillet tightly, and cook for **30 to 45 minutes**, or until the chicken is tender and cooked through. Uncover for the last 5-10 minutes to re-crisp the skin.

### Step 3: Prepare Honey Butter and Serve
8. To make the Honey Butter: While the chicken is cooking, heat **1/2 cup honey** and **1/4 cup butter** together until the butter is melted and warm.
9. Serve the hot **honey butter** drizzled over the fried **chicken**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Leftover fried chicken can be stored in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days, though best enjoyed fresh and crispy.
