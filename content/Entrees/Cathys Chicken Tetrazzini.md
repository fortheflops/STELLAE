# Cathy's Chicken Tetrazzini

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Cathy Jote | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A comforting baked pasta casserole featuring tender chicken, mushrooms, and a rich creamy sauce topped with melted cheese. Perfect for feeding a hungry family with classic mid-century appeal.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 30 mins | None | 6–8 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 12x7x2 inch baking dish
* Mixing bowl
* Measuring cups

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 cans | [[Creamy Chicken Mushroom Soup]] | Undiluted |
| 1 can | [[Cream of Mushroom Soup]] | Undiluted |
| 1 can | [[Cream of Chicken Soup]] | Undiluted |
| 1 cup | [[Chicken Broth]] | Standard |
| 2 cups | [[Shredded Processed Cheese Spread]] | Shredded |
| 4 cups | [[Cooked, Diced Chicken]] | Diced |
| 1-12 oz. pkg. | [[Spaghetti, Cooked & Drained]] | Cooked and drained |
| 1-4 oz. can | [[Mushrooms, Drained]] | Drained |
| 1/2 cup | [[Grated Parmesan Cheese]] | Grated |
| To taste | [[Paprika]] | For color |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mix the Casserole Base
1. In a large mixing bowl, combine the **2 cans creamy chicken mushroom soup**, **1 can cream of mushroom soup**, **1 can cream of chicken soup**, and **1 cup chicken broth**.

### Step 2: Assemble and Bake
2. Stir in the **2 cups shredded processed cheese spread**, **4 cups cooked, diced chicken**, **1-12 oz. pkg. spaghetti, cooked & drained**, and **1-4 oz. can mushrooms, drained** until thoroughly mixed.
3. Pour the mixture into a greased **12x7x2 inch baking dish**.
4. Sprinkle the top with **1/2 cup grated parmesan cheese** and dust lightly with **paprika** to add color.
5. Bake at **350°** for **25 to 30 minutes** until bubbly and golden brown.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole a day in advance, cover tightly, and refrigerate until ready to bake, adding an extra 10 minutes to the baking time.
