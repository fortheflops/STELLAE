# Turkey Italiano

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty One | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A delightful Italian-style turkey dish coated in crispy breadcrumbs, lightly pan-fried, and topped with warm spaghetti sauce and melted mozzarella. Perfect for a quick and savory weeknight meal.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 5 mins | None | 4 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Skillet
* Platter
* Mixing bowl

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 lb | [[Turkey breast slices]] | Sliced |
| 1/4 cup | [[Italian bread crumbs]] | None |
| 1/4 cup | [[Parmesan]] | Grated |
| 1-2 tsp | [[Oil]] | None |
| 1 1/2 cups | [[Spaghetti sauce]] | Hot |
| 1/2 cup | [[Mozzarella]] | Shredded |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Coating
1. Combine **1/4 cup Italian bread crumbs** and **1/4 cup Parmesan** cheese in a shallow dish.
2. Brush **1 lb turkey breast slices** with a small amount of water; coat thoroughly with the crumb mixture.

### Step 2: Cooking and Assembly
3. Heat **1-2 tsp oil** in a skillet, add **1 lb turkey breast slices**, and cook for **1 1/2 to 2 minutes** on each side until lightly browned.
4. Place the cooked turkey on a platter and top with **1 1/2 cups spaghetti sauce** and **1/2 cup mozzarella**. Serve with pasta if desired.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the coated turkey slices ahead of time and store them covered in the refrigerator for up to a few hours before cooking.
