# Ham and Asparagus Rice Casserole

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A comforting mid-century style casserole combining savory cubed ham, tender rice, and a rich cream of asparagus sauce. Topped with a buttery, crispy corn flake crust and garnished with elegant asparagus spears.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 25 mins | None | 6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 1 1/2-quart casserole dish
* Mixing bowl
* Measuring cups
* Measuring spoons

---
## Ingredients

### Casserole Base
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 cups | [[Ham]] | cubed |
| 2 cups | [[Rice]] | cooked |
| 1/2 cup | [[American Cheese]] | grated |
| 1/2 cup | [[Evaporated Milk]] | or light cream |
| 1 cup | [[Cream of Asparagus Soup]] | condensed |
| 2 to 4 tbsp | [[Onion]] | chopped |

### Topping & Garnish
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3/4 cup | [[Corn Flakes]] | slightly crushed |
| 3 tbsp | [[Butter]] | melted |
| As needed | [[Asparagus]] | spears, for garnish |

---

## Instructions

### Assembly and Baking
1. Preheat oven to **370°F** and grease a **1 1/2-quart casserole dish**.
2. In a large mixing bowl, thoroughly combine the **2 cups cubed ham**, **2 cups cooked rice**, **1/2 cup grated American cheese**, **1/2 cup evaporated milk**, **1 cup cream of asparagus soup**, and **2 to 4 tablespoons chopped onion**.
3. Transfer the mixture into the prepared **1 1/2-quart casserole dish**, smoothing the top.
4. In a small bowl, toss the **3/4 cup slightly crushed corn flakes** with the **3 tablespoons melted butter** until evenly coated, then sprinkle over the casserole.
5. Garnish the top of the casserole with **asparagus spears**.
6. Bake uncovered at **370°F** for **20 to 25 minutes** until the edges are bubbling and the corn flake topping is golden brown and crispy.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole base ahead of time, cover, and refrigerate. Add the corn flake topping and butter just before baking to keep it crispy.
