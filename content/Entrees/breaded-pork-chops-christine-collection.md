# Breaded Pork Chops

> 📜 **Collection:** Christine Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *Crispy baked pork chops coated in ranch dressing, Italian bread crumbs, and Parmesan cheese. A simple and flavorful main dish that comes together quickly.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 25 mins | None | 6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Shallow dish
* 13x9x2 pan

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 cup | [[Italian-seasoned dry bread crumbs]] | Dry |
| 2 Tbsp. | [[Grated Parmesan cheese]] | Grated |
| 1/3 bottle | [[Ranch salad dressing]] | Liquid |
| 6 | [[Pork chops]] | 1 1/2 in. thick |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Coating
1. Combine **1 cup Italian-seasoned dry bread crumbs** and **2 Tbsp. grated Parmesan cheese** in a shallow dish.
2. Dip **6 pork chops** in **1/3 bottle ranch salad dressing**, then coat thoroughly in the crumb mixture.
3. Place the coated chops in an ungreased 13x9x2 pan.

### Step 2: Baking
4. Bake, uncovered, at 375°F for **25 mins** or until pork chops are done and golden brown.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Pork chops can be coated ahead of time and refrigerated for up to 2 hours before baking.
