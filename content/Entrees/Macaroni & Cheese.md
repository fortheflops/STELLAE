# Macaroni & Cheese

> 📜 **Collection:** Patsy Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A comforting baked macaroni and cheese featuring a rich blend of mozzarella and cheddar cheeses. This vintage-style casserole bakes up bubbly and golden brown for a hearty family-favorite dinner.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 1 hour | None | 6–8 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large pot
* Mixing bowl
* Baking dish
* Aluminum foil

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5 cups | [[Macaroni]] | Uncooked |
| 5 Tbsp. | [[Butter]] | Melted or softened |
| 1 | [[Eggs]] | Beaten |
| 1/4 tsp. | [[Salt]] | None |
| 1/2 tsp. | [[Pepper]] | None |
| 3 cups | [[Milk]] | None |
| 2 cups | [[Mozzarella]] | Shredded |
| 4 cups | [[Cheddar Cheese]] | Shredded, divided |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Combine
1. Cook the **5 cups macaroni** according to package instructions until al dente, then drain.
2. In a large bowl, combine the **5 Tbsp. butter**, beaten **1 egg**, **1/4 tsp. salt**, **1/2 tsp. pepper**, **3 cups milk**, **2 cups mozzarella**, and all but **1 cup** of the **4 cups cheddar cheese**.

### Step 2: Bake
3. Transfer the mixture to a baking dish and cover tightly with aluminum foil.
4. Bake for **45 minutes** at **350°F** until hot and bubbling.
5. Remove the foil, sprinkle the remaining **1 cup** of **[[Cheddar Cheese]]** over the top, and bake for **15 more minutes** until golden brown and bubbly.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole in advance and store it covered in the refrigerator for up to 24 hours before baking.
