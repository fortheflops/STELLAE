# Country-Style Herbed Chicken Kiev Bake

> 📜 **Collection:** General Archive | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A simple, country-style take on Chicken Kiev featuring chicken breasts dipped in butter and a savory herb-parmesan breading, baked until golden, and finished with a rich green onion and white wine butter sauce.*

---

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Breading
1. Preheat oven to **375°F**.
2. In a heavy saucepan, melt **1/3 cup butter**.
3. On a piece of wax paper, combine **1/2 cup fine dry bread crumbs**, **2 T. grated Parmesan cheese**, **1 t. basil**, **1 t. oregano**, **1/2 t. garlic**, and **1/4 t. salt**.
4. Dip each **2 chicken breasts, split (about 1 1/2 lbs.)** piece first in the melted butter, then roll in the crumb mixture to coat.
5. Place the coated chicken skin side up in an ungreased 9-inch pan.

### Step 2: Baking and Sauce
6. Bake at **375°F** for **50-60 minutes**, or until golden brown and chicken is tender.
7. Meanwhile, add **1/4 cup dry white wine or apple juice**, **1/4 cup chopped green onion**, and **1/4 cup chopped parsley** to the saucepan with the remaining melted butter. Heat gently.
8. When the chicken is done, pour the butter-sauce mixture around and over the chicken.
9. Return to the oven for **3-5 minutes**, or until the sauce is hot and bubbly.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the chicken and breading ahead of time, cover, and refrigerate for up to 2 hours before baking.
