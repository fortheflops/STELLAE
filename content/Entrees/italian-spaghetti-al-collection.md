## Instructions

### Step 1: Brown the Meat and Aromatics
1. In a large skillet, heat **1/2 cup olive oil or butter** over medium heat.
2. Add **1/3 cup sliced onion**, **1 clove chopped garlic**, **1/2 cup chopped green pepper**, **1/2 cup mushrooms**, and **1 1/2 lbs. ground beef**. Season with **1 tsp. salt**.
3. Cook until the ground beef is thoroughly browned and the vegetables are tender.

### Step 2: Simmer the Sauce
4. Add **2 (6 oz.) cans tomato paste** and **1 (No. 2) can tomatoes** to the skillet.
5. Cook and simmer for **40 to 60 minutes**, adding a little water if necessary to keep the sauce from sticking or becoming too thick.
6. Stir in **2 tsp. Worcestershire sauce** and continue to cook for another **15 to 20 minutes** until the sauce is rich and deeply flavorful.

### Step 3: Cook the Pasta & Serve
7. While the sauce finishes simmering, cook **10 oz. spaghetti** in a large pot of boiling salted water according to package directions until al dente.
8. Drain the spaghetti in a colander and serve hot, topped generously with the meat sauce.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The meat sauce can be made 1-2 days in advance and stored in the refrigerator; the flavor improves as it sits. Reheat gently on the stovetop before serving over freshly cooked spaghetti.
