# Chili Rellenos Casserole

> 📜 **Collection:** UK15 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A classic and hearty Chili Rellenos Casserole featuring layers of seasoned ground beef, green chiles, and cheddar cheese, all baked in a fluffy, savory egg custard. This comforting Tex-Mex dish captures traditional flavors in an easy-to-make layered casserole format.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Cook Meat
1. Preheat the oven to **350°F**. Grease a **10x6 inch baking dish**.
2. In a skillet, brown the **1 pound ground beef** and **1/2 cup onion**. Drain off excess grease. Stir in **1/2 teaspoon salt** and **1/4 teaspoon pepper** until well combined.

### Step 2: Assemble Casserole Layers
3. In the prepared baking dish, arrange half of the **2 (4 ounce) cans green chiles** in a single layer.
4. Sprinkle all of the **1 1/2 cups cheddar cheese** evenly over the chiles.
5. Top the cheese with the cooked **ground beef** mixture, spreading it into an even layer.
6. Arrange the remaining **green chiles** on top of the meat.

### Step 3: Make Custard, Bake, and Serve
7. In a separate bowl, combine the **1 1/2 cups milk**, **1/4 cup flour**, **4 eggs**, the second **1/2 teaspoon salt**, and **hot sauce**. Beat with a whisk until smooth.
8. Pour the **egg mixture** evenly over the layers in the casserole dish.
9. Bake for **50 to 60 minutes**, or until the custard is set and a knife inserted in the center comes out clean.
10. Let the casserole cool for **5 minutes** before cutting into squares and serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftover casserole sealed in the refrigerator for up to 3-4 days; reheat gently before serving.
