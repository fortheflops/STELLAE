# Meat Pie

> 📜 **Collection:** LOC | ✍️ **Attribution:** F. Woodland | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A classic vintage meat pie featuring seasoned ground beef, a layer of melted cheese, and a fluffy swirled mashed potato topping. Baked to bubbling perfection and browned under the broiler.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Meat Mixture
1. Mix **1/2 cup evaporated milk** with **1/4 cup bread crumbs** in a mixing bowl.
2. Beat in the next **5 ingredients** (**1 egg**, **1 tsp salt**, **1 tsp dry mustard**, **1/4 tsp pepper**, and **1/3 cup minced onion**).
3. Mix in **1 lb ground beef** thoroughly using a fork.
4. Turn the mixture evenly into a **9" pie plate**.

### Step 2: Bake and Top
5. Bake in a moderate oven at **350°F** for **30 minutes** until cooked through.
6. Remove from the oven and cover the hot meat with **cheese slices**.
7. Swirl **mashed potatoes** on top of the cheese; dot generously with **butter**.
8. Place under the broiler until the butter is melted and the potato peaks are lightly browned and fragrant.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the meat base ahead of time and refrigerate unbaked for up to 24 hours before baking and topping.
