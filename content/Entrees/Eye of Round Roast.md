## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | [[Eye of Round Roast]] | Size not specified |
| 1 pkg | [[Dry Onion Soup Mix]] | ~1 oz size |
| 1 Cup | [[Red Wine]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Roast
1. Preheat your oven to **325°F**.
2. Place the **1 eye of round roast** in a roasting pan or baking dish.
3. Sprinkle the **1 pkg dry onion soup mix** over the roast and pour the **1 cup red wine** into the pan.
4. Bake at **325°F** for **4 hours**, or until tender and flavorful.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftover roasted beef in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3-4 days.
