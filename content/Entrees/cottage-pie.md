# Cottage Pie

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A classic, hearty Cottage Pie featuring tender beef cubes simmered in a rich, thickened gravy and topped with a golden layer of seasoned mashed potatoes. This traditional comfort dish is perfect for a warming family dinner.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 60 mins | None | 4 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Saucepan
* Baking dish

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 4 cups | [[Beef]] | Cooked and cubed |
| As needed | [[Flour]] | To thicken gravy |
| To taste | [[Salt]] | None |
| To taste | [[Pepper]] | None |
| To taste | [[Cayenne]] | None |
| 2 cups | [[Mashed Potatoes]] | Seasoned and prepared |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation
1. Cut the **4 cups beef** into cubes and cover with boiling water.
2. Simmer until the **beef** is tender.
3. Stir in **flour** to thicken the gravy, then season with **salt**, **pepper**, and **cayenne** to taste.

### Step 2: Assembly and Baking
4. Pour the hot mixture into a baking dish.
5. Cover with a thick layer of **seasoned mashed potatoes**.
6. Bake until the top is golden brown and bubbling.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The beef filling can be prepared a day in advance and stored in the refrigerator; assemble with fresh mashed potatoes before baking.
