# Stuffed Bell Peppers

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Nine | ✍️ **Attribution:** Cheri | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A classic home-style stuffed bell pepper recipe featuring tender bell pepper shells packed with a savory filling of ground beef, rice, garlic, and tomato sauce, topped with melted mozzarella cheese. Baked until perfectly tender and bubbly.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 27 mins | None | 4 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 4-quart Dutch oven
* Square glass baking pan
* Aluminum foil
* Skillet

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 4 large | [[Bell Peppers]] | any color, tops and seeds removed, rinsed |
| 1 lb | [[Ground Beef]] |  |
| 2 tbsp | [[Onion]] | chopped |
| 1 cup | [[Rice]] | cooked |
| 1 tsp | [[Salt]] |  |
| 1 clove | [[Garlic]] | finely chopped |
| 1 can (15 oz) | [[Tomato Sauce]] | divided |
| 3/4 cup | [[Mozzarella Cheese]] | shredded |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep Peppers
1. Remove top stem from each of the **4 large bell peppers** and slice off the top. Remove seeds and membranes, then rinse clean. If necessary, trim a thin slice from the bottom of each pepper so they stand up straight.
2. In a 4-quart Dutch oven, add enough water to cover the peppers and bring to a boil. Add the peppers, cook for **2 mins**, then drain.

### Step 2: Make Filling
3. In a skillet over medium heat, cook the **1 lb ground beef** and **2 tbsp chopped onion** until browned. Drain excess grease.
4. Stir in the **1 cup cooked rice**, **1 tsp salt**, **1 clove finely chopped garlic**, and **1 cup tomato sauce**. Cook until heated through.

### Step 3: Stuff and Bake
5. Stuff the **4 large bell peppers** with the **ground beef mixture**. Stand them upright in a square glass baking pan.
6. Pour the remaining **tomato sauce** over the top of the peppers.
7. Cover tightly with foil and bake at **350°F** for **10 mins**.
8. Uncover and bake for another **15 mins** or until peppers are tender. Sprinkle with **3/4 cup shredded mozzarella cheese** and let melt before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Peppers can be parboiled and stuffed up to 24 hours in advance; keep covered in the refrigerator until ready to bake, adding 5–10 extra minutes to the covered baking time.
