# Chicken Enchilada Casserole

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A comforting, layered Southwestern-style casserole featuring tender chicken, mild green chilies, and corn tortillas smothered in a savory enchilada broth. Perfect for busy weeknights and family gatherings.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 40 mins | None | 6 servings |

* Casserole dish
* Mixing bowl
* Measuring cups
* Chef's knife

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 packages | [[Chicken Breast]] | frozen, cooked and cut up |
| 1 package | [[Cheese]] | Cracker Barrel mellow, shredded |
| 1 medium | [[Onion]] | minced |
| 1 small can | [[Green Chilies]] | Ortega's brand, diced |
| 1 package | [[Tortillas]] | corn tortillas preferred |
| 2 1/2 cups | [[Chicken Broth]] |  |
| 1 can | [[Enchilada Sauce]] |  |
| 1 small can | [[Milk]] | evaporated milk |

---

## Instructions

### Assembly
1. Preheat your oven to 350°F.
2. Use **half of the package of tortillas** to cover the bottom of a greased casserole dish, tearing them slightly if needed to fit.
3. Cover the tortilla layer evenly with the **2 packages of cooked, cut-up chicken breast**, **1 package of Cracker Barrel cheese**, **1 minced medium onion**, and **1 small can of Ortega's green chilies**.
4. Cover the entire mixture with the **remaining half of the package of tortillas**.

### Sauce and Baking
5. In a mixing bowl, combine **1 small can of milk**, **1 can of enchilada sauce**, and **2 1/2 cups of chicken broth**.
6. Pour the liquid mixture evenly over the top layer of **tortillas**, ensuring they are well-saturated.
7. Bake for **35 to 40 minutes** at 350°F until the casserole is bubbling vigorously at the edges, the liquid has absorbed slightly, and the top is lightly golden brown.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This casserole can be assembled, covered, and refrigerated up to 24 hours before baking. If baking straight from the refrigerator, add an extra 10 minutes to the baking time.
