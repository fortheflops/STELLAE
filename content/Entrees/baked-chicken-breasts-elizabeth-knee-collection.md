# Baked Chicken Breasts

> 📜 **Collection:** Elizabeth Knee Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A simple and classic recipe for tender baked chicken breasts prepared in a cast-iron skillet. Seasoned with flour, salt, pepper, and a touch of paprika, then dotted with butter for a golden finish.*

| 2 tbsp | [[Butter]] | sliced into small pieces |
| A pinch | [[Paprika]] | optional, for color |

---

## Instructions

### Preparation and Baking
1. Preheat your oven to **350°F**.
2. Dredge and season the **4 chicken breasts** with **flour**, **salt**, and **black pepper** to taste.
3. Place the seasoned chicken breasts into an **iron skillet**.
4. Dot the top of the chicken with **2 tbsp butter** and sprinkle with **a pinch of paprika** if desired for color.
5. Bake uncovered in the oven at **350°F** for **at least 40 minutes**, or until the chicken is cooked through, juices run clear, and the top is beautifully golden brown.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Leftovers can be stored in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days and reheated gently in the oven or microwave.
