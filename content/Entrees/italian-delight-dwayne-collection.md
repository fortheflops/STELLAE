# Italian Delight

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A comforting and savory baked casserole featuring seasoned ground beef, bell peppers, mushrooms, and noodles layered with melted cheese. This hearty Italian Delight is perfect for feeding a crowd on busy weeknights.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 45 mins | None | 8 servings |

* Skillet
* Casserole dish
* Pot

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1-1/2 lbs | [[Ground Beef]] | Hamburger |
| 1 | [[Onion]] | Large, chopped |
| 1 | [[Bell Pepper]] | Chopped |
| 1 can | [[Mushrooms]] | Canned |
| 1 can | [[Tomatoes]] | Canned |
| 1 can | [[Olives]] | Chopped |
| 1 pkg | [[Frozen Noodles]] | Prepared by package directions |
| To taste | [[Italian Seasoning]] | Optional |
| To taste | [[Salt and Pepper]] | None |
| As needed | [[Shredded Cheese]] | For layering |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparing the Sauce
1. Cook the **1-1/2 lbs ground beef**, **1 onion**, **1 bell pepper**, along with any garlic if desired, in a skillet until the meat is browned and vegetables are tender.
2. Season with **salt and pepper** to taste.
3. Add the **1 can tomatoes**, **1 can mushrooms**, and **1 can olives** to the skillet, stirring to combine.
4. Stir in the prepared **1 pkg frozen noodles** and mix well.

### Step 2: Baking
5. Place the mixture into a greased 9x13 casserole dish.
6. Layer the mixture with **shredded cheese** a couple of times.
7. Bake at 325 degrees F for **35-45 min** until the cheese is melted and bubbling.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole in the baking dish ahead of time, cover, and refrigerate for up to 24 hours before baking.
