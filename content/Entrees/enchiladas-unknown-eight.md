# Enchiladas

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Eight | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A comforting casserole-style enchilada dish layered with shredded chicken, corn tortillas, green chilies, and melted cheddar cheese. Baked until bubbly and hot, it makes for a hearty family dinner.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 30 mins | 35 mins | None | 6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Pot
* Skillet
* 9x13-inch pan
* Knife
* Cutting board

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3-5 | [[Boneless chicken breasts]] | Boiled and shredded |
| 2 cans (10 oz each) | [[Cream of chicken soup]] | None |
| 1 medium | [[Onion]] | Diced |
| 1 3/4 cups | [[Green chili]] | Chopped |
| 3 Tbsp. | [[Margarine]] | None |
| 1 dozen | [[Corn tortillas]] | Divided |
| 1 lb. | [[Grated cheddar]] | Shredded |
| 1/2 cup | [[Saved broth from chicken]] | Reserved from boiling chicken |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Sauce
1. Boil the **3-5 boneless chicken breasts**, cool, and shred, making sure to save **1/2 cup saved broth from chicken**.
2. Preheat your oven to 325°F.
3. Sauté the **1 medium onion** in **3 Tbsp. margarine** until slightly soft.
4. Combine the sautéed onion with **2 cans (10 oz) cream of chicken soup**, **1/2 cup saved broth from chicken**, and **1 3/4 cups green chili**, then stir well.

### Step 2: Layering and Baking
5. Tear **6 corn tortillas** into small pieces and cover the bottom of a 9x13-inch pan.
6. Spread **1/2 of the chicken** over the tortilla pieces, then layer **1/2 of the sauce** and **1/2 of the grated cheddar**.
7. Repeat the layers with the remaining ingredients.
8. Bake for 30-40 minutes, until very hot, bubbly, and lightly browned.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the enchiladas in the pan, cover tightly, and refrigerate for up to 24 hours before baking.
