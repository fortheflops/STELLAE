# Company Casserole (Layered Beef Noodle Bake)

> 📜 **Collection:** General Archive | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A classic and comforting 'Company Casserole' featuring layers of tender egg noodles, a rich ground beef and tomato sauce, and a creamy, tangy filling of cottage cheese, cream cheese, and sour cream.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 50 mins | None | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 9x13 inch casserole dish
* Large skillet
* Medium bowl
* Colander

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 8 Ounces | [[Uncooked Noodles]] | Egg noodles |
| 1 Pound | [[Ground Beef]] | None |
| 1 Tablespoon | [[Fat]] | Or oil |
| 2 Cans (8 Ounces each) | [[Tomato Sauce]] | None |
| 1/2 Pound | [[Cottage Cheese]] | None |
| 1 Package (8 Ounces) | [[Cream Cheese]] | Softened |
| 1/4 Cup | [[Commercial Sour Cream]] | None |
| 1/3 Cup | [[Minced Onion]] | Minced |
| 1 Tablespoon | [[Minced Green Pepper]] | Minced |
| 2 Tablespoons | [[Melted Butter]] | Melted |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Sauce
1. Preheat oven to **350°F**. Grease a **9x13 inch casserole dish**.
2. Cook and drain the **8 ounces uncooked noodles** according to package directions.
3. In a large skillet, brown the **1 pound ground beef** in **1 tablespoon fat**; drain any excess grease. Stir in the **2 cans tomato sauce** and heat through.

### Step 2: Cheese Mixture and Assembly
4. In a medium bowl, combine the **1/2 pound cottage cheese**, softened **1 package cream cheese**, **1/4 cup commercial sour cream**, **1/3 cup minced onion**, and **1 tablespoon minced green pepper**. Mix well until combined.
5. In the prepared casserole dish, spread half of the cooked **uncooked noodles**, layer with half of the meat sauce, then all of the cheese mixture. Top with the remaining **uncooked noodles**, and then the remaining meat sauce.
6. Drizzle the **2 tablespoons melted butter** over the top until evenly distributed.

### Step 3: Bake
7. Bake at **350°F** for **45-50 minutes**, or until heated through and bubbly around the edges.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This casserole can be assembled ahead of time, covered, and refrigerated before baking.
