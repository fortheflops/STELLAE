# Hot Chicken Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Dee | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A classic, comforting hot chicken salad featuring a crunchy potato chip topping and savory cream soup base. This retro favorite is perfect for potlucks and easy weeknight family dinners.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 30 mins | None | 6-8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Casserole dish
* Mixing bowl

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 4-5 | [[Chicken Breasts]] | Cooked and cut up |
| 2 cans | [[Cream of Chicken or Celery Soup]] | Thickened with a little flour |
| 2 cans | [[Water Chestnuts]] | Sliced |
| 1 pkg | [[Potato Chips]] | Crushed |
| 2 cups | [[Celery]] | Chopped |
| 4 tsp | [[Onion]] | Minced |
| 4 tbsp | [[Mayonnaise]] | None |
| to taste | [[Slivered Almonds]] | For topping |

---

## Instructions

### Assembly and Baking
1. Preheat oven to 450°F.
2. In a large bowl, combine the **4-5 chicken breasts**, **2 cans cream of chicken or celery soup** (thickened with flour), **2 cans water chestnuts**, **2 cups celery**, **4 tsp onion**, and **4 tbsp mayonnaise**.
3. Transfer the mixture to a casserole dish and top with the **1 pkg crushed potato chips** and **slivered almonds**.
4. Bake for 30 minutes until the top is golden brown and the center is bubbling.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the mixture in the baking dish and refrigerate; add the potato chip topping just before baking to ensure maximum crunch.
