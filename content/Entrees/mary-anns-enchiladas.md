## Instructions

### Step 1: Filling
1. Sauté **1 lb meat** with **1 clove garlic**, **1 tbsp chili powder**, **1 tbsp red wine**, and **1 1/2 tsp salt** until browned.
2. Stir in **1 can (5 oz) kidney beans**, bring to a boil, reduce heat, and simmer for **5 min** until warmed through.

### Step 2: Sauce
3. Sauté **1/2 cup onions** and **1 clove garlic** in **2 tbsp oil** for **5 min**.
4. Remove from heat, stir in **1 tbsp flour** to blend, then add **1 can tomato puree**, **1 cup water**, **1 beef bouillon cube**, **1 tbsp green chiles**, **2 tsp sugar**, **1 tsp chili powder**, **1/2 tsp salt**, **1 dash pepper**, and **1 dash cumin**.
5. Simmer while stirring for **5 min** until thickened.

### Step 3: Assembly and Baking
6. Soften **tortillas** in warm water, fill with the meat mixture, roll up, and secure with toothpicks.
7. Pour half of the **sauce** into a baking dish, arrange the rolled **tortillas**, pour the rest of the **sauce** on top, and sprinkle with **cheese**.
8. Bake at 350°F for 20-25 minutes until the cheese is melted and bubbly.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the enchiladas in the baking dish a few hours ahead, cover tightly, and refrigerate until ready to bake.
