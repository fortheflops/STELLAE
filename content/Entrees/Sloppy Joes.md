## Instructions

### Step 1: Brown the Meat and Aromatics
1. In a large skillet or Dutch oven, cook the **2 lb hamburger (ground beef)** and **1/2 cup onion** over medium-high heat until the meat is browned and the onion is softened. Break up the meat as it cooks until fully browned and aromatic.

### Step 2: Simmer the Sauce
2. Drain off any excess fat from the skillet.
3. Add all the remaining ingredients except the buns: **7 oz catsup (ketchup)**, **1/2 cup water**, **1/4 cup green pepper**, **1/4 cup celery**, **1/8 cup lemon juice**, **1 Tbsp brown sugar**, **1 Tbsp Worcestershire sauce**, **1 1/2 tsp salt**, **1 Tbsp vinegar**, **3/4 tsp prepared mustard**, **black pepper**, and **1 Tbsp MSG** if using.
4. Stir everything together thoroughly to combine.
5. Bring the mixture to a simmer. Once simmering, reduce the heat to low, cover the skillet, and let it simmer for **30 minutes**, stirring occasionally until rich and thickened.

### Step 3: Serve
6. Taste and adjust seasoning with additional **salt** and **black pepper** if necessary.
7. Serve the hot Sloppy Joe mixture generously spooned onto **hamburger buns**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The sloppy joe mixture can be made ahead and stored in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 4 days, or frozen for up to 3 months.
