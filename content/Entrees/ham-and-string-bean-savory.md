# Ham and String Bean Savory

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A hearty, comforting dish featuring savory ham and tender string beans served over warm, fresh cornbread. This classic recipe creates a creamy, satisfying meal perfect for family dinners.*

---

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Double boiler
* Saucepan
* Baking pan

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 lb | [[American Cheese]] | cubed |
| 2/3 cup | [[Milk]] | whole |
| 1 1/2 cups | [[Ham]] | slivered and cooked |
| 1 1/2 cups | [[String Beans]] | cooked |
| 1/2 tsp | [[Worcestershire Sauce]] | none |
| As needed | [[Cornbread]] | baked and hot |

---

## Instructions

### Preparation
1. Prepare and bake your favorite **cornbread** recipe until golden brown.
2. In the top of a double boiler, melt the **1/2 lb American cheese**.
3. Gradually add the **2/3 cup milk** to the cheese, stirring constantly until the mixture is smooth and creamy.
4. Fold in the **1 1/2 cups slivered cooked ham**, **1 1/2 cups string beans**, and **1/2 tsp Worcestershire sauce**.
5. Heat the mixture thoroughly until bubbling and well-combined.
6. Serve the savory mixture over warm squares of **hot cornbread**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The cheese sauce can be prepared in advance and reheated gently on the stovetop; add the ham and beans just before serving.
