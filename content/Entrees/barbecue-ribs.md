# Barbecue Ribs

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Ruth | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A classic, hearty recipe for tender oven-baked spare ribs smothered in a savory, homemade vegetable-infused barbecue sauce. This simple preparation yields fall-off-the-bone results perfect for a family dinner.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 90 mins | None | 4 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Oven
* Baking dish
* Food grinder or processor

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 4 lbs | [[Spare-ribs]] | raw |
| 1 | [[Green pepper]] | ground |
| 1 | [[Onion]] | ground |
| 1 bottle | [[Ketchup]] | standard size |
| 1/2 bottle | [[Water]] | measured using empty ketchup bottle |
| 2 level tsp | [[Chili powder]] | none |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Initial Bake
1. Place **4 lbs spare-ribs** in a baking dish and bake in the oven for 45 minutes without a lid.

### Step 2: Sauce and Simmer
2. Grind the **1 green pepper** and **1 onion** together.
3. Combine the ground vegetables with **1 bottle ketchup**, **1/2 bottle water**, and **2 level tsp chili powder**.
4. Pour the sauce over the ribs, cover with a lid, and simmer at 350°F for 45 minutes or longer until the meat is tender and pulls away from the bone.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The sauce can be prepared a day in advance and stored in the refrigerator until ready to use.
