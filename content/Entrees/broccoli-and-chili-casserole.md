# Broccoli and Chili Casserole

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A hearty and comforting casserole featuring tender broccoli, rice, and zesty green chilies in a creamy cheese sauce. This simple baked dish is perfect for a quick weeknight dinner.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 30 mins | None | 4-6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Casserole dish
* Pot
* Mixing bowl

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 pkg | [[Broccoli]] | Chopped |
| 1 can | [[Mushroom Soup]] | Condensed |
| 1 pkg | [[Cheese Sauce Mix]] | Prepared |
| 1 can | [[Green Chilies]] | Diced |
| 1/2 cup | [[Rice]] | Uncooked |
| 1/2 cup | [[Cheese]] | Grated, for topping |

---

## Instructions

### Preparation and Baking
1. Cook the **1 pkg broccoli** and **1/2 cup rice** according to package directions until tender.
2. In a mixing bowl, combine the cooked broccoli and rice with **1 can mushroom soup**, **1 pkg cheese sauce mix**, and **1 can green chilies**.
3. Transfer the mixture to a casserole dish and top with **1/2 cup grated cheese**.
4. Bake until the cheese is bubbly and golden brown.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole in the dish and refrigerate covered for up to 12 hours; add the cheese topping just before baking.
