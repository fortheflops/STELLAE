# Nanny's Chicken Enchiladas

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Eighteen | ✍️ **Attribution:** Nanny | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A classic, comforting recipe for shredded chicken enchiladas rolled in corn tortillas and baked in a savory homemade chili sauce. Perfect for a cozy family dinner, these enchiladas are topped with melted cheddar cheese and baked until bubbling.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep Tortillas
1. In a frying pan, heat **1/4 inch** of **oil** on medium heat. Fry **corn tortillas** for a few seconds until pliable but not crisp, then let drain on paper towels.

### Step 2: Make Chili Sauce
2. Put **2 tbsp (or more)** of **chili powder** in **2 cups** of very hot **water** to soak.
3. Drain off most of the hot **oil** from the pan. Add **1 heaping tbsp** of **flour** to the remaining **oil** in the pan. Stir constantly until it turns a light brown color.
4. Carefully add the **chili water** and keep stirring to remove any lumps. Add **1 clove** of **garlic, mashed**, lower the heat, and let simmer until the sauce thickens slightly.

### Step 3: Assemble and Bake
5. In each **corn tortilla**, place some **cooked chicken**, **onion**, and **cheddar cheese**.
6. Roll tightly into a tube and place seam-side down in a baking dish.
7. Pour the prepared **chili sauce** evenly over the rolled **corn tortillas**.
8. Bake at 350°F (175°C) until the sauce is boiling and bubbling at the edges and the cheese is completely melted.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the enchiladas in the baking dish, cover, and refrigerate up to 24 hours before baking. Add an extra 5-10 minutes to the baking time if cooking straight from the refrigerator.
