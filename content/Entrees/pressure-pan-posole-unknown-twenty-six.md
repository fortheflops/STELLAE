# Pressure Pan Posole

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A traditional and hearty pressure cooker posole featuring tender pork and hominy, perfectly spiced with oregano, cumin, and dried red chilies. This comforting one-pot meal yields a rich and flavorful broth that warms you from the inside out.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 1 hr | None | 8-12 servings |

## Instructions

### Step 1: Pressure Cooking
1. Combine the **3 lb pork in lg pieces**, **5 c water**, **1 lb posole**, **10-12 c water**, **2 T salt**, **1 med onion**, **2 cloves garlic**, **1/4 t oregano**, **1 t gr. camino**, and **3-6 dried red chili pods** into the pressure cooker.
2. Cook at 15 lb pressure for **1 hour**, until the pork is fork-tender and the posole is fully bloomed and soft.

---
