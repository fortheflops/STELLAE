# Chicken Enchiladas

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparing the Sauce and Filling
1. Melt **1 stick ole** in a pan, add **1 large onion**, and simmer until soft but not brown.
2. Add **1 can crm. of chicken soup**, **1 can crm. of mushroom soup**, and the prepared **1 bouillon cube** 1 at a time, stirring until smooth.
3. Add **1 can chopped green chilies** and **1 can boned chicken**, mix well, and set this aside.

### Step 2: Assembly and Baking
4. Roll the **1 pkg tortillas**, and place them in a line in a greased baking dish.
5. Pour on **1/2 of the soup mixture** and top with **1/2 of the cheese**, then repeat the layers.
6. Bake for **30 min** at **400°** until bubbly and golden brown.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the enchiladas in the baking dish ahead of time, cover tightly, and refrigerate for up to 24 hours before baking.
