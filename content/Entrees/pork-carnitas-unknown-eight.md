## Instructions

### Step 1: Braising
1. Combine the **3 1/2 lb pork shoulder**, **3 tbsp tomato paste**, **4-5 garlic cloves**, **1 tsp oregano**, **1 tbsp cumin**, **1 onion coarsely cut**, **1 quart chicken stock**, **salt**, **pepper**, **water to cover**, and **1 bay leaf** in a pot as braising liquid.
2. Simmer and braise until tender, about **3 hrs**. Remove **pork** and let cool slightly.

### Step 2: Roasting and Serving
3. Pull the **pork shoulder** apart into **3-4 in pieces** and put on a baking sheet.
4. Season with **salt**, **cayenne**—whatever you wish.
5. Put into a very hot oven at **450°** for about **20-25 min** until the fat in the meat renders and crisps the **carnitas** to golden perfection.
6. Chop and serve on **tortillas** with **salsa**, **onion**, **lime**, **cilantro**, avocado, or whatever you like.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The pork can be fully braised, shredded, and stored in the refrigerator for up to 3 days before crisping in the oven.
