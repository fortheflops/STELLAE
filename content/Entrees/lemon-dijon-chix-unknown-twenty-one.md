# Lemon Dijon Chix

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty One | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A quick and flavorful weeknight chicken dish coated in a tangy lemon and Dijon mustard glaze. Baked to golden perfection with simple pantry staples.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 40 mins | None | 4 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Baking dish

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/4 C | [[Dijon]] | Mustard |
| 2 T | [[Lemon Juice]] | Freshly squeezed |
| 1 T | [[Olive Oil]] | Standard |
| As needed | [[Chix]] | Chicken pieces |
| To taste | [[S&P]] | Salt and pepper |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and Baking
1. Mix the **1/4 C Dijon**, **2 T Lemon Juice**, and **1 T Olive Oil** together in a small bowl.
2. Spread the mixture evenly over the **Chix** and season with **S&P** to taste.
3. Bake at 425° for **40 Minutes** until the chicken is cooked through and golden.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble and coat the chicken up to 4 hours in advance, keeping it covered in the refrigerator before baking.
