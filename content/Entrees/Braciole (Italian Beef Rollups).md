# Braciole (Italian Beef Rollups)

> 📜 **Collection:** Louise Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A classic Italian-American dish featuring tender beef rollups browned to perfection and simmered in rich tomato sauce until melt-in-your-mouth tender. Serve over pasta or rice for a comforting and hearty meal.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Brown the Beef
1. Heat a few tablespoons of **oil** in a large skillet or Dutch oven over medium-high heat.
2. Sauté the **Braciole** in the hot **oil**, turning them until they are well browned on all sides.

### Step 2: Simmer in Sauce
3. Pour in enough **Tully's spaghetti sauce** to cover the meat. Bring to a simmer, then reduce the heat to low, cover the pan, and simmer for **one hour**, or until the beef is fork-tender.

### Step 3: Rest and Slice
4. Carefully remove the **Braciole** from the sauce and let them rest on a cutting board for about **10-15 minutes**.
5. Carefully remove any kitchen string from the rolls.
6. Slice the **Braciole** into 1/2-inch to 1-inch thick pinwheels and serve them covered in the hot tomato sauce, alongside **pasta** or **rice** if desired.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftover braciole submerged in the tomato sauce in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 4 days.
