# Chef Andrés Sunshine Paella

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Eight | ✍️ **Attribution:** Chef Andrés | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A vibrant and hearty traditional-style paella loaded with savory sausage, tender chicken, and fragrant saffron rice. This satisfying dish feeds a crowd and brings warm, aromatic flavors to your dinner table.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 25 mins | 45 mins | None | 6-8 servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Paella pan
* Large skillet
* Baking dish
* Chef's knife

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 lbs | [[Chicken]] | Cut small |
| 1 lb | [[Sweet Italian Sausage]] | Uncooked |
| 1 ea | [[Red Bell Pepper]] | Diced |
| 1 ea | [[Green Bell Pepper]] | Diced |
| To taste | [[Salt, Pepper & Sage Mixture]] | Blended |
| 1 tbsp | [[Chile Pequeñ]] | Whole or crushed |
| 3 leafy stalks | [[Celery]] | Diced |
| 1 bunch | [[Scallions]] | Diced |
| 2 medium | [[Zucchini]] | Diced |
| 1 tsp | [[Garlic]] | Gusmashed |
| 1/4 c | [[Olive Oil]] | divided |
| 1 1/2 lbs | [[Uncooked Rice]] | Standard long-grain |
| 1/4 c | [[Parsley]] | Chopped |
| 2 quarts | [[Chicken Stock]] | Warmed |
| 1 tsp | [[Saffron]] | Boiled in 1 c stock |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Sear
1. Cut the **2 lbs chicken** into bite-sized pieces and season liberally with **salt, pepper & sage mix**.
2. Heat **1/4 c olive oil** in a pan to smoking and sear the **chicken** till nicely browned, adding more oil if necessary.

### Step 2: Sauté and Combine
3. Sauté the diced **red & green bell peppers**, **scallions**, **celery**, **zucchini**, **garlic**, and **chile pequen** with the meat.
4. Add the **1 1/2 lbs uncooked rice** and **1 lb sweet Italian sausage**, continuing to cook over medium heat, stirring constantly until the **rice** browns slightly.

### Step 3: Bake
5. Add the remainder of ingredients including **2 quarts chicken stock**, **saffron mixture**, and **1/4 c chopped parsley**, then bring to a boil.
6. Cover with a tightly fitting lid and bake for **35 to 45 min** at **350°** until all **chicken stock** has been absorbed or **rice** is done, noting more liquid may need to be added depending on the **rice** type.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Best served immediately for optimal texture, but leftovers can be stored in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days.
