# Baked Fish with Lemon Butter

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A delicate and flaky baked fish dish brushed with a savory lemon butter and onion mixture. Perfectly baked for a quick and flavorful seafood dinner.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 25 mins | None | 4 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Small bowl
* Baking dish
* Pastry brush

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| As Needed | [[Fish Fillets]] | Fresh or thawed |
| 1/4 cup | [[Butter]] | Melted |
| 1 tablespoon | [[Lemon Juice]] | Freshly squeezed |
| To Taste | [[Onion]] | Minced |
| To Taste | [[Fish Seasoning]] | Favorite blend |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Mix
1. Preheat your oven to **350°F**.
2. In a small bowl, combine the **1/4 cup butter**, **1 tablespoon lemon juice**, and **onion** to create the lemon butter mixture.

### Step 2: Season and Bake
3. Season the **fish fillets** to your liking with the **fish seasoning**.
4. Place the seasoned **fish fillets** in a baking dish and brush generously with the lemon butter mixture, coating it well.
5. Bake for **25-30 minutes**, or until the fish flakes easily with a fork but is still moist.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble and coat the fish just before baking for the best texture, as raw fish does not hold well when pre-coated in butter and citrus.
