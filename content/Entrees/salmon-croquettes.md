# Salmon Croquettes

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Five | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *Crispy golden salmon croquettes made with simple pantry staples like cracker crumbs and canned salmon. Perfectly pan-fried to a savory, tender finish.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 10 mins | 30 mins | 4 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Skillet

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | [[Egg]] | Beaten |
| As needed | [[Cracker Crumbs]] | Divided for mixing and coating |
| 1 can | [[Salmon]] | Drained and flaked |
| Small amount | [[Oil]] | For frying |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing and Shaping
1. Stir **cracker crumbs** in **beaten egg** until absorbed.
2. Mix in the **salmon**, shape into croquettes, and cool in the icebox.

### Step 2: Frying
3. Roll the chilled croquettes in additional **cracker crumbs**.
4. Fry in a small amount of **oil** until golden brown and crispy.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Shaped croquettes can be refrigerated in the icebox for up to 24 hours before frying.
