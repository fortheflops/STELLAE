# Honey Mustard Chicken and Green Beans

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty One | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A quick and flavorful foil-packet dinner featuring tender chicken breasts coated in a tangy honey mustard glaze, baked alongside fresh green beans. This easy one-pan meal delivers a delicious, hassle-free weeknight supper with minimal cleanup.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 22 mins | None | 4 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Aluminum foil
* Cookie sheet

---
## Ingredients

### Sauce
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/3 cup | [[Dijon]] | Mustard |
| 3 tbsp | [[Honey]] | None |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Pepper]] | None |

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 4 | [[Chicken Breast]] | Skinless, boneless halves |
| 2 cups | [[Green Beans]] | Fresh or frozen |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and Baking
1. Mix the **1/3 cup Dijon**, **3 tbsp honey**, and **1/4 tsp pepper** together in a small bowl.
2. Place the **4 skinless, boneless chicken breast halves** on a 12x18-inch piece of aluminum foil.
3. Pour the mustard mixture over the **4 skinless, boneless chicken breast halves**, then top with the **2 cups fresh or frozen green beans**.
4. Wrap the foil tightly around the ingredients and place it on a cookie sheet.
5. Bake at 450°F for **18-22 minutes** until the chicken is cooked through and opaque.
6. Open the packet carefully and spoon the **2 cups fresh or frozen green beans** and sauce over the **4 skinless, boneless chicken breast halves** before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the foil packets up to a few hours in advance, keep refrigerated, and bake just before serving.
