# Rolled Chicken Washington

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Seven | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A classic recipe for tender chicken breasts rolled with a savory mushroom and cheddar filling. These rolls are breaded and fried before being finished in the oven for a crispy, golden texture.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare Filling
1. Melt **1/4 cube butter** in a skillet and add **1/2 cup mushrooms**; cook for 5 minutes.
2. Stir in **2 tbsp flour**, **1/2 cup canned milk**, **salt**, and **pepper**.
3. Add **1/2 cup sharp cheddar cheese** and stir until melted, then remove from heat and chill.

### Step 2: Assemble and Coat
4. Pound **6 chicken breasts** until thin.
5. Place cheese strips inside the chicken, roll up tightly, and secure.
6. Dust the rolls in **flour**, dip in **egg**, and roll in **bread crumbs**; chill before frying.

### Step 3: Cook
7. Deep fry the chicken rolls until golden brown.
8. Transfer to a baking dish and bake for 30 minutes at 350°F until the chicken is cooked through and juices run clear.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The filling and the breaded chicken rolls can be prepared and chilled in the refrigerator for up to 2 hours before frying and baking.
