# Simple Lemon-Marinated Baked Chicken

> 📜 **Collection:** GailCollection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A very simple and classic baked chicken recipe featuring a cut-up fryer marinated in a tangy lemon and oil blend. Baked until golden brown and tender, it makes for an effortless weeknight dinner.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 1 hour | 2 hours | 4-6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Shallow baking dish
* Paper towels
* Baking pan

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/4 Cup | [[Lemon Juice]] | Freshly squeezed |
| 1/4 Cup | [[Salad Oil]] | Vegetable or canola |
| 1/2 Teaspoon | [[Salt]] | Standard table salt |
| 1/4 Teaspoon | [[Pepper]] | Freshly ground |
| One | [[Chicken]] | Cut-up frying chicken |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Marinate the Chicken
1. In a bowl or shallow dish, combine the **1/4 cup lemon juice**, **1/4 cup salad oil**, **1/2 teaspoon salt**, and **1/4 teaspoon pepper**, then mix well.
2. Wash the **chicken** pieces and pat them dry with a paper towel.
3. Put the **chicken** in a shallow baking dish and pour the marinade over it.
4. Refrigerate for **2 hours**, turning the **chicken** pieces occasionally to ensure they are evenly coated.

### Step 2: Bake
5. Preheat the oven to **350°F**.
6. Remove the **chicken** pieces from the marinade and place them on a cooking sheet or in a baking pan.
7. Bake for **1 hour**, or until the **chicken** is cooked through and golden brown and fragrant.
8. Baste the **chicken** with the remaining marinade periodically while it bakes.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The chicken can be left to marinate in the refrigerator for up to 4 hours before baking.
