# Taco Burgers

> 📜 **Collection:** LouiseCollection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *Seasoned ground beef in a thick tomato-chili sauce, served on toasted buns with lettuce and cheese. A quick and easy weeknight meal featuring a taco-inspired twist on a classic sloppy-joe style burger.*

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 25 mins | None | 8 burgers |

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 Pound | [[Ground Beef]] | Browned and drained |
| 1 (16 Ounce) Can | [[Tomatoes]] | Cut up, undrained |
| 1 Teaspoon | [[Chili Powder]] | None |
| 1 Teaspoon | [[Worcestershire Sauce]] | None |
| 3/4 Teaspoon | [[Garlic Salt]] | None |
| 1/2 Teaspoon | [[Sugar]] | None |
| 1/4 Teaspoon | [[Dry Mustard]] | None |
| 8 | [[Hamburger Buns]] | Split & toasted |
| 2 Cups | [[Lettuce]] | Shredded |
| 1 Cup (4 Ounces) | [[American Cheese]] | Shredded |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Brown the Beef and Simmer Sauce
1. In a skillet, brown the **1 pound ground beef**; drain off fat.
2. Add the **1 (16 ounce) can tomatoes**, **1 teaspoon chili powder**, **1 teaspoon Worcestershire sauce**, **3/4 teaspoon garlic salt**, **1/2 teaspoon sugar**, and **1/4 teaspoon dry mustard**.
3. Stir well, breaking up large pieces of tomato.
4. Bring to a boil; reduce heat.
5. Boil gently, uncovered, until thick and reduced, about **15-20 minutes**.

### Step 2: Assemble and Serve
6. Spoon the hot meat mixture onto the **8 hamburger buns**, split & toasted.
7. Sprinkle each of the burgers with **2 cups shredded lettuce** and **1 cup shredded American cheese** until melted and ready to serve.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The seasoned taco meat mixture can be prepared ahead of time and stored in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days; reheat gently before serving on freshly toasted buns.
