# Chicken Corn Bread Pie

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A comforting chicken and vegetable filling topped with a ring of cornbread muffin batter. Baked until golden for an easy and satisfying weeknight meal.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 25 mins | None | 4 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 9" Pie Pan
* Skillet

---
## Ingredients

### Filling and Batter
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 cup | [[Onion]] | Chopped |
| 2 tbsp | [[Shortening]] | None |
| 1 can | [[Cream of Mushroom Soup]] | None |
| 1/4 cup | [[Milk]] | None |
| 5 oz | [[Boned Chicken]] | Canned |
| 1/2 cup | [[Green Beans]] | Cooked |
| 1 package (12 oz) | [[Corn Muffin Mix]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Filling and Assembly
1. Cook **1/2 cup chopped onion** in **2 tbsp shortening**.
2. Add **1 can cream of mushroom soup**, **1/4 cup milk**, **5 oz boned chicken**, and **1/2 cup cooked green beans**.
3. Pour the mixture into a **9" pie pan**.
4. Mix **1 12 oz package of corn muffin mix** according to package directions to make the batter.
5. Spoon **1/2 batter** on top of pie along edge, and use rest for muffins.

### Step 2: Baking
6. Bake at 400° for **25 minutes** until the cornbread is golden brown and the filling is bubbly.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the filling in the pie pan and cover tightly; store in the refrigerator for up to 24 hours before adding the fresh batter and baking.
