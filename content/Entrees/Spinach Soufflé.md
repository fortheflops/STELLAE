# Spinach Soufflé

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Jual Mercis | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A delightful and airy spinach soufflé recipe by Jual Mercis, featuring fresh spinach, onion, and a cheesy breadcrumb topping. Perfect for a cozy vegetarian meal.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 20 mins | None | 2 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Blender
* Baking dish
* Saucepan

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Enough to make 2 Cups | [[Fresh Spinach]] | or frozen, cooked |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Chopped Onion]] | chopped |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] | whole |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Milk]] | liquid |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Grated Cheese]] | grated |
| To Taste | [[Buttered Bread Crumbs]] | for topping |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Blend
1. Drain the **enough to make 2 cups fresh spinach** cooked in salted boiling water (or frozen).
2. Chop the **1/2 cup chopped onion**.
3. In a blender, add the **2 eggs**, **1/2 cup milk**, and drained **fresh spinach** and chop until smooth.

### Step 2: Assemble and Bake
4. Pour the mixture into a buttered baking dish, add the **1/2 cup grated cheese**, and top with **buttered bread crumbs**.
5. Bake for **20 minutes** at **350 degrees** until golden brown and set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Best served fresh out of the oven for maximum rise and fluffiness.
