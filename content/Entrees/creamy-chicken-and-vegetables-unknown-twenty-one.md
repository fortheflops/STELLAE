# Creamy Chicken and Vegetables

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty One | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A comforting one-skillet dinner featuring tender chicken breasts and mixed vegetables in a creamy mushroom sauce. Perfect for a quick and satisfying weeknight meal.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Brown the Chicken
1. Sprinkle **4 chicken breast halves** with **1/8 tsp garlic powder**.
2. Spray a skillet with vegetable spray, heat over medium-high heat for **1 min**.
3. Cook **4 chicken breast halves** for **10 min** or until browned; set aside.

### Step 2: Simmer and Serve
4. Add **10 3/4 oz cream of mushroom soup**, **1/2 cup milk**, **1 tbsp lemon juice**, **1/4 tsp dried basil leaves**, and **16 oz frozen vegetables combination** to the skillet.
5. Heat to boil, then return **4 chicken breast halves** to the pan.
6. Cover, cook low for **10 min** or until the chicken is done and cooked through.
