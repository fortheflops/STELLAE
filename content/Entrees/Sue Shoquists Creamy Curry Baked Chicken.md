# Sue Shoquist's Creamy Curry Baked Chicken

> 📜 **Collection:** JillCollection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Sue Shoquist | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A simple and comforting baked chicken dish from Sue Shoquist, featuring chicken baked in a creamy curry sauce with apples and onions.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 1 hour 30 mins | None | 4-6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Shallow 9x12 inch baking dish
* Skillet
* Wooden spoon or spatula

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3 pounds | [[Chicken]] parts | fryer or breasts |
| 2 tablespoons | [[Butter]] | softened or sliced |
| 1 | [[Onion]] | chopped |
| 1 | [[Apple]] | chopped |
| 3 teaspoons | [[Curry Powder]] | none |
| 1 can | [[Mushroom Soup]] | cream of |
| 1 cup | [[Half-and-Half]] | none |
| To taste | [[Salt]] and [[Pepper]] | none |
| To taste | [[Paprika]] | none |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Season
1. Preheat oven to **350°F**. Butter a shallow 9x12 inch baking dish.
2. Season the **3 pounds chicken parts** with **salt**, **pepper**, and **paprika**, then arrange them in a single layer in the prepared dish.

### Step 2: Make the Curry Sauce
3. In a skillet over medium heat, melt the **2 tablespoons butter**. Add the chopped **1 onion**, chopped **1 apple**, and **3 teaspoons curry powder**. Sauté until the onion is transparent and fragrant.
4. Stir in the **1 can cream of mushroom soup** and the **1 cup half-and-half**. Heat the sauce through, stirring until smooth and well combined.

### Step 3: Bake
5. Pour the warm sauce evenly over the **chicken**.
6. Bake uncovered for **1 1/2 hours**, or until the chicken is cooked through and the sauce is bubbly and golden.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftover chicken and sauce sealed in the refrigerator for up to 3 days.
