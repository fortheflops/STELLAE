## Recipe
| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
|3|[[Eggs]]|
|1/2 pint|[[Heavy Cream]]|
|1/2 teaspoon|[[Salt]]|
| |[[Pepper]]|
| |[[Nutmeg]]|
|1 cup|[[Grated Swiss Cheese]]|
|1|[[9 inch Pie Shell (Unbaked)]]|
| |[[Meat (Cooked, diced Ham suggested)]]|
___
### Instructions
1.  Beat eggs, cream, and seasonings (salt, pepper, nutmeg).
2.  Add cheese, diced ham, and mix.
3.  Pour into unbaked pie shell.
4.  Bake in the upper 1/3 of the oven at 350 degrees for 40 minutes, or until set.
5.  Bake at 450 degrees for 8 minutes.
