## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
| 1/2 Cup               | Grated Cheddar Cheese               |
| 1/2 Cup             | Chopped Onion                         |
| 1 Tablespoon          | Flour                               |
| 2                     | Eggs, Beaten                        |
| 1 Cup                 | Evaporated Milk                     |
| 1/4 Teaspoon            | Black Pepper                         |
| 1 Pound      | Mild or Hot Sausage |
| 1/3 Cup | Chopped Green Pepper|
|1| Deep Dish Unbaked Pie Crust |
| 1 Tablespoon| Parsley Flakes|
|1 1/2 Teaspoon| Season Salt|
|1/4 Teaspoon| Garlic Salt |
___
### Instructions
1.  Fry sausage, onion, and pepper together. Drain off grease.
2.  Combine Cheese and Flour. Pour over sausage mixture, and pour it to unbaked crust.
3. Mix remaining ingredients, pour over sausage mixture.
4.  Bake at 375 degrees Fahrenheit for 45-55 minutes, until golden brown.
___

>[!note] 
>Makes one deep dish pie!
