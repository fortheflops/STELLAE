*A classic and hearty deep-dish sausage quiche with a rich, savory custard and cheese filling.*
### Author
Theresa Rieff
___
## Recipe
### Filling
| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 1 Pound | Mild or Hot [[Sausage]] |
| 1/2 Cup | Chopped [[Onion]] |
| 1/3 Cup | Chopped [[Green Pepper]] |
| 1/2 Cup | Grated [[Cheddar Cheese]] |
| 1 Tablespoon | [[Flour]] |
| 1 | Deep Dish Unbaked [[Pie Crust]] |
### Custard
| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 2 | [[Eggs]], Beaten |
| 1 Cup | [[Evaporated Milk]] |
| 1 Tablespoon | [[Parsley Flakes]] |
| 1 1/2 Teaspoon| [[Season Salt]] |
| 1/4 Teaspoon | [[Garlic Salt]] |
| 1/4 Teaspoon | [[Black Pepper]] |
___
### Instructions
1.  Preheat oven to 375°F.
2.  In a skillet, fry the **sausage**, **onion**, and **green pepper** together until the meat is browned. Drain off excess grease.
3.  In a small bowl, toss the grated **cheddar cheese** with the **flour**.
4.  Spread the cooked sausage mixture in the bottom of the unbaked **pie crust**.
5.  Sprinkle the cheese/flour mixture over the sausage.
6.  In a separate bowl, mix the beaten **eggs**, **evaporated milk**, **parsley flakes**, **season salt**, **garlic salt**, and **black pepper**.
7.  Pour the egg mixture over the sausage and cheese.
8.  Bake for 45-55 minutes, or until golden brown and a knife inserted in the center comes out clean.
___

>[!note]
>Makes one deep dish pie!
