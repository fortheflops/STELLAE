## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 1 | [[9 inch Pie Shell (Unbaked)]] |
| 1 cup | [[Grated Swiss Cheese]] |
| 1 cup | [[Meat (Cooked, diced Ham suggested)]] |
| 3 | [[Eggs]] |
| 1/2 pint (1 cup)| [[Heavy Cream]] |
| 1/2 teaspoon | [[Salt]] |
| To taste | [[Pepper]] |
| To taste | [[Nutmeg]] |
___
### Instructions
1.  Preheat oven to **450°F**.
2.  Sprinkle the **grated Swiss cheese** and diced **ham** into the bottom of the **unbaked pie shell**.
3.  In a bowl, beat the **eggs**, **heavy cream**, **salt**, **pepper**, and **nutmeg** together.
4.  Pour the egg mixture over the cheese and ham.
5.  Bake in the upper 1/3 of the oven at **450°F for 8-10 minutes**.
6.  Reduce the oven temperature to **350°F** and continue to bake for 30-40 minutes, or until the quiche is set and a knife inserted in the center comes out clean.
7.  Let cool for 10 minutes before serving.
___

>[!Note]
>The baking instructions on the original card are listed backward. The method above (a hot start, then a lower temperature) is the standard technique for baking a quiche in an unbaked crust and will yield the best results.
