## Recipe
| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
| 2 Packages             | Frozen Spinach                        |
| 1/2 Cup               | Harvest Wheat Crackers, Crumbled                 |
| 3                     | Eggs, Beaten                            |
| 4 Ounce Package      | Cream Cheese                           |
| 4 Tablespoon          | Butter                                  |
| 3/4 Cup               | Milk                                    |
| 1/4 Teaspoon          | Salt                                    |
| 1/2 Cup               | Cheddar Cheese, Shredded               |
| Dash                  | White Pepper                            |
___
### Instructions
1.  Cook spinach according to package directions.
2.  Drain and add cream cheese.
3. Stir over low heat until cheese is melted (looks like cream sauce).
4.  Add all other ingredients except wheat crumbs and cheese.
5. Place 1/2 spinach mixture in a greased, square pan.
6. Top with 1/2 wheat crumbs.
7. Pour the rest of the spinach mixture over the crumbs.
8. Top with the cheese and remainder of the crumbs.
9. Bake at 350°F for 30 minutes until puffed and golden.
___

>[!Note]
>prep time: 30 minutes 
>servings: 6
>Reheats well
