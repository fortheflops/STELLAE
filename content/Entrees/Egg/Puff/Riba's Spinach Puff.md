*A classic, savory spinach side dish with a light, fluffy texture from the eggs and a crunchy cracker topping.*
### Author
Riba
___
## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 2 Packages | Frozen [[Spinach]] |
| 4 Ounce Package | [[Cream Cheese]], softened |
| 3 | [[Eggs]], Beaten |
| 4 Tablespoon | [[Butter]], melted |
| 3/4 Cup | [[Milk]] |
| 1/4 Teaspoon | [[Salt]] |
| Dash | [[White Pepper]] |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Harvest Wheat Crackers]], Crumbled |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Cheddar Cheese]], Shredded |
___
### Instructions
1.  Preheat oven to 350°F. Grease a square baking pan.
2.  Cook **spinach** according to package directions. Drain **very well**, squeezing out all excess moisture.
3.  In a pan over low heat, combine the hot, drained spinach with the softened **cream cheese**. Stir until the cheese is melted and the mixture is smooth.
4.  Remove from heat. In a large bowl, combine the spinach-cheese mixture with the beaten **eggs**, melted **butter**, **milk**, **salt**, and **white pepper**.
5.  Pour **half** of the spinach mixture into the greased pan.
6.  Top with **half** (1/4 cup) of the **cracker crumbs**.
7.  Pour the **rest** of the spinach mixture on top.
8.  Top with the shredded **cheddar cheese** and the **remainder of the crumbs**.
9.  Bake for 30 minutes, or until puffed and golden.
___

>[!Note]
>Prep time: 30 minutes. Servings: 6. Reheats well.
