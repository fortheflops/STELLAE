## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
| 10                    | [[Eggs]]                                      |
| 1/2 Cup        | [[Flour]]                                     |
| 1 Teaspoon           | [[Baking Powder]]                             |
| 1/2 Teaspoon         | [[Salt]]                                      |
| 1 Pint               | [[Cottage Cheese]], small curd               |
| 1 Pound              | [[Monterey Jack Cheese]], grated              |
| 1/2 Cup               | [[Butter]], melted                            |
| 2 Cans                 | [[Chili]], or similar amount frozen           |
|                       | (Optional) Sliced [[Tomatoes]]             |
___
### Instructions
1. Dump eggs in blender.
2. Add flour, baking powder, salt, cottage cheese, Monterey Jack cheese, and melted butter.
3. Blend.
4. Add chili or similar amount frozen.
5. Pour into greased pan.
6. (Optional) Place sliced tomatoes on top before baking.
7. Bake at 350 degrees Fahrenheit for 35 minutes.
