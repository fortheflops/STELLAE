# Entrée Crepes

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *Delicate and thin French-style crêpes ideal for savory fillings like creamed seafood or spinach. This classic recipe yields a light batter that requires a brief resting period before swirling into a hot pan.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 20 mins | 2 hours | 6–8 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowls
* Whisk
* Crepe pan or skillet
* Paper towels or wax paper

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 cup | [[Flour]] | Sifted |
| 1/2 tsp | [[Salt]] | None |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] | Beaten |
| 2 | [[Egg Yolks]] | Beaten |
| 1 3/4 cups | [[Milk]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Batter
1. Mix until smooth **1 cup sifted flour** and **1/2 teaspoon salt**.
2. In a separate bowl, beat **2 eggs** and **2 egg yolks**.
3. Gradually add **1 3/4 cups milk** to the beaten eggs.
4. Combine the wet and dry ingredients and mix until completely smooth.
5. Let the batter stand for **2 hours**. It will be thin.

### Step 2: Cook the Crepes
6. The batter must be thin enough so just a little can be swirled to cover a buttered pan. Use about **2 tablespoons** of batter per crepe.
7. Heat a lightly buttered crepe pan or skillet over medium heat. Pour out any excess butter.
8. Swirl the batter to cover the pan. Cook for about **1 minute**, until the batter sets and is delicately brown.
9. Flip the crepe and cook for about **1 more minute** on the other side.

### Step 3: Cool and Store
10. Cool crepes separately and then stack with paper towels or wax paper between each layer.
11. To serve, heat filled crepes in a buttered pan to serving temperature until hot and fragrant.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Crepes can be covered and refrigerated for several days or frozen for longer storage until ready to fill and reheat.
