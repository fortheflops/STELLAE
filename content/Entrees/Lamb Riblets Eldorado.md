# Lamb Riblets Eldorado

## Instructions

### Step 1: Brown the Lamb
1. Brown **1 1/2 pounds [[Lamb Breast]]** in a skillet for about **30 minutes**, turning frequently to melt the fat and pouring off the excess fat as it accumulates.
2. Sprinkle the lamb with **salt**, **pepper**, and **garlic salt** to taste.

### Step 2: Combine and Simmer
3. When the lamb is browned, pour off most of the remaining fat.
4. Add **1 small [[Onion]]** (chopped) to the skillet and brown it slightly.
5. Stir in **1/4 cup [[Bell Pepper]]**, **1 can [[Red Kidney Beans]]**, **1 can [[Tomato Soup]]**, and **1/2 teaspoon [[Chili Powder]]**, mixing well.
6. Place the lamb pieces over the surface of the bean and vegetable mixture.
7. Cover the skillet and cook slowly until the meat is fork-tender, about **45 minutes** (alternatively, bake in a **350°F** oven for **1 hour**).
8. Garnish with **[[Parsley]]** before serving, if desired, until fragrant and piping hot.
