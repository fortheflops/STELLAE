## Instructions

### Step 1: Simmering the Filling
1. Boil **1 chicken**, **1 onion**, and **2 cloves garlic** in **2 cups chicken broth** for 10 minutes.
2. Add **1 large can tomatoes**, **1 can tomato sauce**, **1 cup ripe olives**, **1/4 cup margarine**, **1/2 cup oil**, **1 Tbsp chili powder**, and **1 can corn**. Simmer until heated through.

### Step 2: Preparing the Cornmeal and Assembling
3. Mix together **2 cups corn meal**, **2 cups milk**, **3 beaten eggs**, and **1 tsp salt**.
4. Cook until thickened, stirring well as it cooks until the mixture is smooth and thick.
5. Add the tomato mixture and the cooked **1 chicken** pieces into the cornmeal mixture, stirring to combine.
6. Place into a sprayed casserole dish and bake for 1 hour until golden and bubbling around the edges.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole up to a day in advance, cover tightly, and refrigerate until ready to bake; add 10-15 minutes to the baking time if baking straight from the fridge.
