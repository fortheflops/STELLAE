# South of Border Casserole

> 📜 **Collection:** UK15 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A classic and hearty South of the Border casserole featuring seasoned ground beef, corn, and tortillas in a rich tomato-chili sauce. Topped with melted cheddar cheese, it makes for a satisfying and easy family dinner.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 30 mins | None | 8-10 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large skillet
* Dutch oven
* Large casserole dish (9x13 inch)

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 1/2 pounds | [[Ground Beef]] | None |
| 1 | [[Onion]] | Chopped |
| 1 large can | [[Whole Kernel Corn]] | Undrained |
| 1 large can | [[Tomato Juice]] | Approx. 46 oz |
| 1 dozen | [[Corn Tortillas]] | None |
| 2 heaping tbsp | [[Chili Powder]] | None |
| To taste | [[Salt]] | None |
| To taste | [[Garlic Salt]] | None |
| 1-2 cups | [[Cheddar Cheese]] | Grated or shredded |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Sauce
1. Preheat the oven to **350°F**. Grease a large casserole dish.
2. In a large skillet or Dutch oven, brown the **1 1/2 pounds ground beef** and **1 chopped onion** until the meat is cooked through. Drain any excess fat.
3. Stir in the **1 large can whole kernel corn** (undrained), **1 large can tomato juice**, **2 heaping tbsp chili powder**, **salt**, and **garlic salt**. Bring the mixture to a simmer until fragrant.
4. Tear or cut the **1 dozen corn tortillas** into pieces or strips.

### Step 2: Assemble and Bake
5. In the prepared casserole dish, create alternating layers of the meat mixture and the **corn tortillas**, ending with a final layer of the meat mixture on top.
6. Sprinkle the top evenly with **1-2 cups grated cheddar cheese**.
7. Bake for **20-30 minutes**, or until the casserole is hot and the **cheddar cheese** is completely melted and bubbly.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole up to a day in advance, cover tightly, and store in the refrigerator until ready to bake. Add a few extra minutes to the baking time if baking straight from the fridge.
