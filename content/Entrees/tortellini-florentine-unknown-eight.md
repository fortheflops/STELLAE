# Tortellini Florentine

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Eight | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A quick and comforting pasta dish featuring layers of tender cheese tortellini, warm spaghetti sauce, and vibrant spinach. Perfect for a fast weeknight meal topped with freshly grated parmesan cheese.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 15 mins | None | 4 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Saucepan
* Pot
* Slotted spoon
* Serving bowls

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 (10-oz) package | [[Spinach]] | frozen, chopped |
| 1 (9-oz) package | [[Cheese Tortellini]] | frozen |
| 1 (16-oz) jar | [[Spaghetti Sauce]] | such as Celebrity brand |
| To taste | [[Parmesan Cheese]] | freshly grated |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Cook
1. Cook the **1 (10-oz) package frozen chopped spinach** according to package directions. Use a slotted spoon to drain the **spinach** thoroughly.
2. Cook the **1 (9-oz) package frozen cheese tortellini** according to package directions until tender and cooked through.
3. Heat the **1 (16-oz) jar spaghetti sauce** in a small saucepan until hot and bubbling.

### Step 2: Assembly
4. Divide the ingredients into **4 portions** by layering the drained **spinach** on the bottom of each serving dish.
5. Top the spinach with the cooked **cheese tortellini**, then spoon the warm **spaghetti sauce** over the pasta.
6. Finish each portion with a generous sprinkle of **freshly grated parmesan cheese** before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This dish is best enjoyed fresh, but leftovers can be stored in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days and reheated in the microwave.
