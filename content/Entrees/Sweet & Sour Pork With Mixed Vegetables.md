# Sweet & Sour Pork With Mixed Vegetables

> *A classic sweet and sour pork recipe featuring crispy battered meat, vibrant vegetables, and a tangy homemade sauce. Perfect for a flavorful dinner paired with steamed rice.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Marinate and Batter the Pork
1. In a bowl, combine **1 tablespoon wine**, **2 tablespoons soy sauce**, **minced ginger**, **mashed garlic**, and **1/2 teaspoon Accent** for the marinade.
2. Add **1 pound pork loin** cubes to the marinade and ensure they are well coated. Soak for **2-3 hours or overnight**.
3. In a separate bowl, mix the **2 egg yolks or 1 egg** with **4 tablespoons cornstarch** to make a stiff batter, adding extra cornstarch if necessary.
4. Remove the pork from the marinade and dip each piece well into the batter until fully coated.

### Step 2: Fry the Pork and Prepare the Sauce
5. Heat **oil** for deep frying in a suitable pot or deep fryer to **420°F**.
6. Deep fry the battered pork until crisp and golden brown, then turn out onto a platter.
7. In a saucepan, combine **6 tablespoons sugar**, **2 tablespoons soy sauce**, **1 tablespoon wine**, **3 tablespoons vinegar**, **1/2 cup pineapple juice**, and **3 tablespoons catsup**. Bring to a boil.
8. In a small bowl, mix **2 tablespoons cornstarch** with **1/2 cup water**, then add to the boiling sauce and stir continuously until the sauce thickens.

### Step 3: Cook Vegetables and Assemble
9. Heat **6 tablespoons oil** in a large pan or wok to **420°F**.
10. Add the **1/2 cup green pepper**, **1/2 cup carrots**, **1/2 cup bamboo shoots**, **1/2 cup pineapple**, and **1/2 cup onion** to the hot oil.
11. Fry for a few minutes until the vegetables take on a translucent appearance, then add **1/4 teaspoon Accent** and **1/4 teaspoon salt**.
12. Add the prepared sauce and the cooked meat to the pan with the vegetables, combine well until hot and fragrant, and serve.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The pork can be marinated overnight in the refrigerator. Leftovers are best consumed immediately but can be stored separately in airtight containers for up to 2 days.
