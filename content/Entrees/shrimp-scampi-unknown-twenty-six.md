# Shrimp Scampi

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Randy | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A classic and quick shrimp scampi recipe loaded with garlic, butter, and white wine. Perfect for serving with crusty French bread to soak up the flavorful broth.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 5 mins | None | 2-3 servings |

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3/4 lb | [[Shrimp]] | Medium, peeled and deveined |
| 3 tbsp | [[Butter]] | Unsalted |
| 2 tbsp | [[Olive Oil]] | None |
| 1 tbsp | [[Green Onion]] | Chopped |
| 4-5 cloves | [[Garlic]] | Minced |
| 2 tsp | [[Lemon Juice]] | Freshly squeezed |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Salt]] | None |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Lemon Peel]] | Grated |
| 2 tbsp | [[Parsley]] | Minced |
| Dash | [[Hot Pepper]] | Seasoning, red pepper flakes or hot sauce |
| 1/2 cup | [[White Wine]] | Dry |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Sautéing Aromatics
1. Heat a wok over medium heat and let it get hot.
2. Add **3 tbsp butter** and **2 tbsp olive oil**.
3. When melted, add **1 tbsp green onion**, **4-5 cloves garlic**, **2 tsp lemon juice**, and **1/4 tsp salt**.
4. Stir fry until the onion is limp, about 30 seconds.

### Step 2: Cooking Shrimp and Finishing
5. Turn heat to high, add **1/2 cup white wine**, and add **3/4 lb medium shrimp**.
6. Stir fry until the **3/4 lb medium shrimp** turns pink, about 2 minutes.
7. Stir in **1/4 tsp grated lemon peel**, **2 tbsp parsley**, and a dash of **hot pepper** seasoning.
8. Serve hot with lemon wedges and enjoy with French bread to dip in the broth.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Best enjoyed immediately after cooking while the shrimp is tender and the sauce is hot.
