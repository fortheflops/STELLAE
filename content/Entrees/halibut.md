# Halibut

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Five | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A delicious baked halibut steak recipe topped with a savory sour cream, green onion, and Parmesan cheese sauce. Perfect for a comforting and easy seafood dinner.*

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 lbs | [[Halibut]] | Fresh or frozen, steak, thawed, skin and bones removed, cut in 6 portions |
| 1 cup | [[Sour cream]] | None |
| 1/2 cup | [[Green onion]] | Sliced |
| 1 tsp | [[Salt]] | None |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Basil]] | None |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Pepper]] | None |
| 1/4 cup | [[Parmesan cheese]] | Grated |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and Baking
1. Thaw **2 lbs halibut steak**, remove skin and bones, and cut into 6 portions.
2. Place fish in a greased 12x8 inch baking dish.
3. Combine **1 cup sour cream**, **1/2 cup sliced green onion**, **1 tsp salt**, **1/4 tsp basil**, and **1/4 tsp pepper** together in a bowl.
4. Pour sauce over **2 lbs halibut**, then sprinkle with **1/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese**.
5. Bake at 350°F for **25 to 30 minutes** until fish flakes easily with a fork.
6. To brown the top, place under the broiler for **4 to 5 minutes** until golden.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the dish with the sauce and cheese, cover, and refrigerate up to a few hours before baking.
