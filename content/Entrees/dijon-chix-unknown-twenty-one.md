# Dijon Chix

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty One | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *Succulent chicken breasts coated in a tangy sour cream and Dijon mustard sauce, then rolled in bread crumbs and baked until tender. This versatile dish can be served hot or cold for any gathering.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 1 hour | None | 12 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Shallow baking pan
* Mixing bowls

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 6 | [[Chicken breasts]] | Whole, halved |
| 1 C | [[Sour cream]] | None |
| 1 C | [[Dijon mustard]] | None |
| 2 C | [[Italian bread crumbs]] | None |
| 1/4 t | [[Garlic powder]] | None |
| To taste | [[Pepper]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and Coating
1. Season the **6 chicken breasts** with **1/4 t garlic powder** and **pepper to taste**.
2. Combine the **1 C sour cream** and **1 C Dijon mustard** in a mixing bowl.
3. Dip the seasoned **chicken breasts** into the sour cream and mustard mixture, then roll thoroughly in **2 C Italian bread crumbs**.

### Step 2: Baking
4. Place the coated chicken in a shallow pan and bake at 350 degrees for **1 hour** or until tender and golden brown.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the chicken up to a few hours ahead, cover and refrigerate before baking.
