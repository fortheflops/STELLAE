# Pork or Beef Teriyaki-Style

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A flavorful and savory Asian-inspired recipe featuring thinly sliced pork or beef marinated in a rich soy-ginger sauce and deep-fried to crispy perfection. Perfect for serving alongside steamed rice and vegetables.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 35 mins | 10 mins | 30 mins | 4 Servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Deep pot or wok
* Slotted spoon
* Paper towels

| 2 Cloves | [[Garlic]] | Minced |
| 1 Teaspoon | [[Ginger]] | Grated |
| As Needed | [[Oil]] | For deep frying |

## Instructions

### Step 1: Marinate the Meat
1. In a bowl, combine the marinade ingredients: **1/2 cup soy sauce**, **1/4 cup brown sugar or honey**, **1 tablespoon sesame oil**, **2 cloves minced garlic**, and **1 teaspoon grated ginger**.
2. Add the **1 pound pork or beef** to the marinade, ensuring it is well coated. Let it marinate for at least **30 minutes** until the flavors penetrate.

### Step 2: Deep Fry
3. Heat a sufficient amount of **oil for deep frying** in a deep pot or wok to **350-375°F (175-190°C)**.
4. Carefully remove the meat from the marinade, allowing excess to drip off.
5. Deep fry the **pork or beef** in batches for **1-2 minutes**, or until cooked through and slightly crisped. Do not overcrowd the pot.
6. Remove the cooked meat with a slotted spoon and drain on paper towels.

### Step 3: Serve
7. Serve immediately while hot, for example, with rice and steamed vegetables.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The meat can be marinated up to 4 hours in advance in the refrigerator before deep frying.
