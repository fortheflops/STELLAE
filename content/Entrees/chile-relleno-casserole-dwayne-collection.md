# Chile Relleno Casserole

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A delightful and cheesy layered casserole featuring green chilies and thick white sauce. Baked to bubbly perfection and lightly browned for a comforting meal.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 30 mins | 10 mins | 6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Casserole dish
* Saucepan

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 batch | [[White sauce]] | Thick |
| As needed | [[Canned green chilies]] | Whole or sliced |
| As needed | [[American cheese]] | Sliced |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Assembly and Baking
1. Put alternate layers of **thick white sauce**, **canned green chilies**, and **thick American cheese** in a casserole dish.
2. Bake at 375°F till bubbly and **cheese** slightly browned.
3. Let set about 10 minutes before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole ahead of time, cover, and refrigerate until ready to bake.
