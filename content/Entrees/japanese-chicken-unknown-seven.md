# Japanese Chicken

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Seven | ✍️ **Attribution:** Betty | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A quick and savory stir-fry of diced chicken breast and frozen Japanese-style vegetables simmered in a light wine and soy sauce mixture. Served over hot rice, this classic recipe makes a complete weeknight meal in under 30 minutes.*

## Instructions

### Preparation & Cooking
1. Cut **1 package (4 halves) chicken breasts** into bite-sized cubes.
2. Heat **1-2 tbsp cooking oil** in a skillet or wok until hot. Add the chicken cubes and cook until slightly browned.
3. Add **1 large package frozen Japanese-style vegetables**, **1/4 cup white wine**, **1/4 cup water** whisked with **2 tbsp cornstarch**, **soy sauce to taste**, and **1 pinch ginger**.
4. Cook until the vegetables are tender and the sauce thickens to a glossy consistency. Serve immediately over warm **rice**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftovers in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days. Reheat gently in a skillet over medium heat.
