# Ham Loaf

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A classic, hearty ham loaf made from a savory blend of ground ham and pork, bound with eggs and cracker crumbs. Topped with a tangy vinegar glaze, this comforting main dish bakes to golden perfection.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 1.5 hours | None | 8-10 servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Loaf pan
* Measuring cups

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5 lbs | [[Meat]] | 3 lbs ham, 2 lbs pork, have butcher grind well together |
| 4 | [[Eggs]] | Beaten |
| 2 cups | [[Cracker Crumbs]] | Crushed in blender |
| 1 to 1 1/2 cups | [[Milk]] | As needed for moisture |
| 1 | [[Green Pepper]] | Chopped |

### Glaze
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5 tbsp | [[White Vinegar]] | Measured into cup and filled with water |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing and Shaping
1. Combine the **5 lbs meat** (3 lbs ham and 2 lbs pork), **4 eggs**, **2 cups cracker crumbs**, **1 to 1 1/2 cups milk**, and chopped **1 green pepper**.
2. Work with hands until thoroughly combined, then shape into **2 ham loaves** and place in loaf pans.

### Step 2: Baking and Glazing
3. Bake the **2 ham loaves** covered for **1 hour** at 350 degrees F.
4. Uncover and bake for an additional **1/2 hour**.
5. Combine the **5 tbsp white vinegar** with enough water to fill a measuring cup, pour over the top, and baste with it while the loaves finish baking until cooked through and golden brown.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The loaves can be shaped ahead of time and refrigerated for up to 24 hours before baking.
