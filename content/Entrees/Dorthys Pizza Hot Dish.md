# Dorthy's Pizza Hot Dish

> 📜 **Collection:** UK15 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Dorthy | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A classic and easy 'hot dish' casserole from Dorthy, this recipe combines all the flavors of pizza with hamburger and noodles for a hearty family meal.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 minutes | 45 minutes | None | 6–8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 9x13 inch casserole dish
* Large skillet
* Large bowl
* Colander

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3 cups | [[Noodles]] | Uncooked, e.g., egg noodles |
| 1 pound | [[Hamburger]] | Ground beef |
| 1 small | [[Onion]] | Diced |
| 1 stick | [[Pepperoni]] | Sliced |
| 1 (10.5 oz) can | [[Pizza Sauce]] | Or substitute with 15.5 oz jar of mushroom spaghetti sauce |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Oregano]] | None |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Garlic Salt]] | None |
| 1/2 cup | [[Milk]] | None |
| 1-2 cups | [[Mozzarella Cheese]] | Shredded, divided |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Bake
1. Preheat your oven to **375°F**. Grease a **9x13 inch casserole dish**.
2. Cook the **3 cups uncooked noodles** according to package directions, then drain well.
3. In a large skillet, brown the **1 pound hamburger** with the **1 small diced onion**. Drain off any excess grease.
4. In a large bowl, combine the cooked noodles, the browned hamburger mixture, **1 stick sliced pepperoni**, **1 (10.5 oz) can pizza sauce**, **1 teaspoon oregano**, **1 teaspoon garlic salt**, **1/2 cup milk**, and a generous amount of the **1-2 cups mozzarella cheese**.
5. Mix all ingredients together thoroughly and pour the mixture into the prepared casserole dish.
6. Top the casserole with any remaining mozzarella cheese.
7. Bake for **45 minutes**, or until heated through and the cheese is melted, golden brown, and bubbly.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole ahead of time and store covered in the refrigerator for up to 24 hours before baking.
