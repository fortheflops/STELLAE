# Yemistes Domates kai Pepperyies (Stuffed Tomatoes)

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A classic Greek recipe for stuffed tomatoes and green peppers filled with a savory mixture of ground beef, rice, and fresh herbs. Baked to tender perfection, this traditional dish is best served alongside Greek cheeses, olives, and a chilled white retsina.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep the Vegetables
1. Cut off tops of **8 large tomatoes** and save them. Scoop out the insides, then chop and save the pulp.
2. Cut off the tops of **6 large green peppers** and discard. Scoop out the insides and discard.
3. Place the tomato and pepper shells in a baking dish. Sprinkle **salt and pepper** generously on the insides and outsides.

### Step 2: Prepare the Filling
4. In a skillet, sauté **2 chopped onions**, **1 minced garlic clove**, and **1 pound ground beef** until the beef is browned and the onions are soft.
5. Add **1/2 can tomato paste**, 1 cup of the saved tomato pulp, **2 tbsp chopped parsley**, **2 tbsp chopped mint leaves**, and **2 tbsp dill weed**. Stir well to combine.
6. Add **1 cup raw rice** and a few teaspoons of **sugar**. Cook (simmer) for 20 minutes, adding a little **water (or wine)** if needed to keep the mixture moist.

### Step 3: Stuff and Bake
7. Stuff the tomatoes and peppers with the rice mixture and cover the tomatoes with their saved tops.
8. Squeeze the rest of the tomato pulp over the tomatoes. (Any leftover rice mixture may be baked alongside the tomatoes in the same pan).
9. Pour a little **olive oil** over the tomatoes and peppers.
10. Bake at 350°F for 35 minutes, basting occasionally, until the vegetables are tender and lightly browned.

### Step 4: Serve
11. Serve warm with assorted Greek cheeses, olives, sliced cucumbers, bread, and a chilled white retsina.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The vegetables can be stuffed a few hours in advance and kept refrigerated before baking. Leftovers reheat beautifully and taste even better the next day.
