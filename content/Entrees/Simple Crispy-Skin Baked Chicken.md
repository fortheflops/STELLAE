# Simple Crispy-Skin Baked Chicken

> 📜 **Collection:** GailCollection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A simple and effective method for crispy-skin chicken. Pieces are heavily seasoned, drizzled with butter, and broiled on both sides until crisp, then finished by baking at 325°F until tender.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Season
1. Preheat the broiler. Set the oven rack about **6 inches** from the broiler element.
2. Cut the **[[Chicken]]** into your desired pieces.
3. In a bowl, season the **[[Chicken]]** pieces to your taste with **[[Salt]]**, **[[Pepper]]**, **[[Oregano]]**, **[[Powdered Garlic]]**, and **[[Paprika]]**. Mix well to coat.

### Step 2: Broil
4. Arrange the seasoned **[[Chicken]]** pieces on a baking tray.
5. Melt the **[[Butter]]** and pour it evenly over the **[[Chicken]]**.
6. Place the tray under the broiler and broil until the top of the **[[Chicken]]** is golden brown and crisp.
7. Carefully turn each piece of **[[Chicken]]** over.
8. Return to the broiler and broil the other side until it is also golden brown and crisp.

### Step 3: Bake
9. Remove from the broiler and change the oven setting to Bake at **325°F**.
10. Bake the **[[Chicken]]** for an additional **45 minutes**, or until cooked through and tender.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftover baked chicken in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 to 4 days.
