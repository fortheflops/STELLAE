# Campbell's Beef Stroganoff

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Brown
1. Cut **1 1/2 lbs sliced round steak** into thin strips. Dust with **1/4 cup flour**, **pepper**, and **salt**.
2. Melt **3 tbsp butter** in a skillet, add **1 can sliced mushrooms**, **1/2 cup chopped onion**, and **1 clove minced garlic**. Brown lightly.

### Step 2: Simmer and Finish
3. Stir in **1 can beef broth**. Cover and cook for **1 hr** until meat is tender, stirring now and then.
4. Gradually stir in **1 cup sour cream**. Cook over low heat for **5 min**.
5. Serve over **3 cups noodles**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The meat sauce can be prepared a day ahead up to the point of adding sour cream; reheat gently and stir in the sour cream just before serving.
