# Filet of Beef Benedict Hawaii

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Galloping Gourmet | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *Experience a tropical twist on a brunch classic with Filet of Beef Benedict Hawaii. Tender chunks of beef and poached eggs are elevated with a rich sauce and a touch of horseradish.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparing the Beef and Sauce
1. Melt **butter** in a **skillet**, add the **tenderloin of beef chunks**, and fry the meat until browned. Season with **salt** and **pepper**.
2. In a **double boiler** with simmering water, combine **egg yolks**, a squeeze of **lemon**, and **butter**, stirring until a smooth sauce forms.

### Step 2: Poaching Eggs and Assembling
3. Puncture an **egg** and lower it into boiling water for 10 seconds, then remove.
4. Alternatively, put **butter** in a frying pan, pour in boiling water, and break in the **egg** to poach. When the **egg** is poached, put it in cold water.
5. Place a circle of **ham** on a hot cross bun, place a **steak** chunk on each one, and top with a poached **egg**.
6. Add **2 tsp of horseradish** to the sauce, stir well, and pour the sauce over the whole assembly. Serve immediately with **asparagus**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The sauce and beef can be prepared shortly before assembly, but the eggs should be poached fresh for the best texture.
