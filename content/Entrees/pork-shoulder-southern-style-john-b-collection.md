# Pork Shoulder Southern Style

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A traditional southern-style baked pork shoulder recipe featuring a unique blend of flour, spices, sugar, and whole cloves. Slow-cooked to tender perfection with a rich caramelized crust.*

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |

| As needed | [[Flour]] | For coating |
| To taste | [[Salt]] | None |
| To taste | [[Pepper]] | None |
| A pinch | [[Ground cinnamon]] | None |
| A sprinkle | [[Sugar]] | None |
| As needed | [[Whole cloves]] | For studding |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and Initial Bake
1. Take the **1 shoulder pork**, wet it, and cover with **as needed flour**, **to taste salt**, and **to taste pepper**.
2. Sprinkle **a pinch ground cinnamon** and **a sprinkle sugar** on top.
3. Bake in a moderate oven until it starts to brown.

### Step 2: Finishing
4. When nearly done, baste and press in **as needed whole cloves** 1" apart.
5. Let cook in a slow oven until done and tender, with a rich aromatic crust.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Can be seasoned and prepared up to a day in advance, covered and kept in the refrigerator before baking.
