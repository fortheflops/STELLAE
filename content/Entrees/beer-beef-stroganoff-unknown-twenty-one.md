# Beer Beef Stroganoff

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty One | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *Indulge in a rich and savory classic with this Beer Beef Stroganoff, featuring tender strips of steak simmered in a bubbly beer sauce with mushrooms and onions. Finished with creamy sour cream and served over hot, buttered noodles, it is the ultimate comfort meal for any night of the week.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 20 mins | None | 6-8 servings |

## Ingredients

| 2 | [[Onions]] | Sliced |
| 3 oz. | [[Canned Sliced Mushrooms]] | Undrained |
| 2 T | [[Flour]] | None |
| 1/2 t | [[Paprika]] | None |
| 12 oz | [[Beer]] | 1 1/2 c |
| 1 t | [[Worcestershire]] | None |
| 1 c | [[Sour Cream]] | None |
| As needed | [[Hot, Buttered Noodles]] | For serving |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Browning and Simmering
1. Brown **2 # steak** cut in strips 1/3 at a time in a skillet. Season with **1/8 t pepper**, remove from skillet, and keep warm.
2. Cook **2 onions, sliced** and **3 oz. canned sliced mushrooms, undrained** for 3-4 minutes until tender, then push to the side.
3. Blend **2 T flour** and **1/2 t paprika** in the pan.
4. Add **12 oz beer** and **1 t worcestershire**, and cook until thickened and bubbly.
5. Return beef to the pan and stir.

### Step 2: Finishing
6. Stir in **1 c sour cream**, cook until heated through and bubbling gently.
7. Serve over **hot, buttered noodles**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The beef and sauce mixture can be prepared ahead of time, but add the sour cream just before heating through to prevent curdling.
