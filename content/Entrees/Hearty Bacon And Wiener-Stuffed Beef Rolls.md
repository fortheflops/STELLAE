# Hearty Bacon And Wiener-Stuffed Beef Rolls

> 📜 **Collection:** General Archive | ✍️ **Attribution:** Linda Brown | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A unique and savory dish featuring thin round steak stuffed with bacon and wieners, simmered until tender in a rich cream gravy. Perfect for a hearty family dinner.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 30 mins | 2 hours 15 mins | None | 8 rolls |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Heavy skillet
* Electric skillet
* Toothpicks

| As needed | [[Flour]] | For rolling |

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Assemble
1. Pound the **2 lbs. round steak** thin and flat, then cut into strips about 4" x 1 1/2" or into 8 equal portions.
2. Sprinkle both sides of the meat with **salt** and **pepper**.
3. Spread **prepared mustard** over one side of each piece of meat.
4. Top with a **1/2 slice of raw bacon** and a wiener from the **8 wieners**.
5. Roll up tightly and secure with **2 or 3 toothpicks**, then roll each in **flour**.

### Step 2: Brown and Simmer
6. Melt **margarine** in a heavy or electric skillet over medium heat.
7. Heat the skillet and brown the rolls on all surfaces until golden brown and fragrant, adjusting toothpicks as needed to allow easier browning.
8. Dissolve the **1 or 2 bouillon cubes** in the **1/2 c. water**, pour off any extra grease from the skillet, and add the bouillon water.
9. Cover and simmer the meat until fork-tender, cooking for **1 to 3 hours** depending on available time.

### Step 3: Make the Gravy and Serve
10. Lift the beef rolls from the skillet, remove the toothpicks, and keep them warm in a low oven.
11. Add the **1/4 c. cream or canned milk** to the skillet and bring to a boil until slightly thickened.
12. Return the beef rolls to the skillet and spoon the rich gravy over them until piping hot and ready to serve.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftover beef rolls and gravy in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days. Reheat gently on the stovetop before serving.
