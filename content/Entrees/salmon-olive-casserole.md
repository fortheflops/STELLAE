# Salmon Olive Casserole

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A classic, comforting casserole featuring tender salmon, savory stuffed olives, and a rich cheese sauce layered with rice. This versatile dish can easily be adapted by substituting the salmon with tuna for a quick weeknight meal.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 30 mins | None | 4 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Double boiler
* Casserole dish

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 1/3 cups | [[Rice]] | Cooked |
| 1/2 cup | [[Milk]] | None |
| 1/2 lb | [[Cheese]] | Thinly sliced |
| 3/4 tsp | [[Salt]] | None |
| 1/2 tsp | [[Pepper]] | None |
| 7 3/4 oz | [[Salmon]] | Can substitute tuna |
| 1/4 cup | [[Stuffed Olives]] | Chopped |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare Sauce
1. Combine **1/2 cup milk**, **1/2 lb cheese**, **3/4 tsp salt**, and **1/2 tsp pepper** in a double boiler.
2. Heat the mixture over simmering water until the sauce is completely smooth.

### Step 2: Assemble and Bake
3. In a casserole dish, add alternate layers of **1 1/3 cups cooked rice**, **7 3/4 oz salmon**, **1/4 cup chopped stuffed olives**, and the prepared cheese sauce.
4. Bake in the oven until the casserole is bubbling and heated through.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The cheese sauce can be prepared in advance and stored in the refrigerator for up to 2 days before assembling the casserole.
