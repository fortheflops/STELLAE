# Meat and Rice Chartreuse

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A savory, molded dish featuring minced meat and rice, steamed to perfection and served with a rich tomato sauce. This classic recipe offers a comforting and elegant way to repurpose cooked meats.*

| 15 mins | 45 mins | None | 4-6 servings |

| 1 tsp | [[Salt]] | None |

| 1-2 tbsp | [[Worcestershire Sauce]] | None |
| As needed | [[Gravy]], [[Stock]], or [[Water]] | To moisten |
| 1/2 tbsp | [[Onion]] | Diced |
| 1 | [[Egg]] | Beaten |
| 3 cups | [[Cooked Rice]] | Divided |
| To taste | [[Tomato Sauce]] | For serving |

---

## Instructions

### Assembly and Steaming
1. Combine the **2 cups minced cooked meat**, **1 tsp salt**, **1/2 tsp pepper**, **1-2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce**, **gravy/stock/water**, **1/2 tbsp onion**, **1 beaten egg**, and a portion of the **3 cups cooked rice** in a mixing bowl.
2. Turn the mixture into a buttered mold lined with a portion of the **3 cups cooked rice**, then cover the top with the remainder of the **3 cups cooked rice**.
3. Steam for **45 minutes** until the mold is firm and heated through.
4. Turn the mold onto a hot platter, surround with **tomato sauce**, and serve with additional **tomato sauce** on the side.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The meat mixture can be prepared and kept chilled, but the molding and steaming should be done just before serving for the best texture.
