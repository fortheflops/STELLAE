## Instructions

### Preparation and Baking
1. Preheat oven to 375°F.
2. In a large bowl, combine **1 lb ground beef**, **3/4 cup stuffing mix**, **1 egg**, **1 tbsp onion**, **2 tbsp sweet pickle relish**, **2 tbsp worcestershire sauce**, **1 tbsp catsup**, **1 tbsp barbecue sauce**, and **1/4 tsp each of salt, pepper, celery salt, and garlic salt**.
3. Mix thoroughly and shape into a loaf or ball, then place in a baking dish.
4. Cover the top with **barbecue sauce** and lay **bacon strips** across the surface.
5. Bake for 1 hour until the meat is firm and the bacon is crispy.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The meatloaf can be shaped and refrigerated up to 12 hours before baking; add the topping just before placing in the oven.
