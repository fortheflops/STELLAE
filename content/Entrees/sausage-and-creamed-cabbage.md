# Sausage and Creamed Cabbage

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A hearty and comforting skillet meal featuring savory sausage links simmered in a creamy celery sauce with tender shredded cabbage. This simple one-pan dish is perfect for a quick weeknight dinner.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 15 mins | None | 4 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Skillet

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 package | [[Sausage Links]] | Brown-and-serve style |
| 1 can | [[Cream of Celery Soup]] | Condensed |
| 2 tsp | [[Mustard]] | Prepared |
| 4 cups | [[Cabbage]] | Shredded |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Cooking
1. Brown the **1 package sausage links** in a skillet over medium heat.
2. Add the **1 can cream of celery soup**, **2 tsp mustard**, and **4 cups cabbage** to the skillet.
3. Stir to combine all ingredients thoroughly.
4. Cover the skillet and cook for 10 minutes, or until the **cabbage** is tender and the mixture is heated through.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This dish can be stored in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days; reheat gently on the stovetop.
