# Corned Beef

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A classic, slow-simmered corned beef brisket infused with garlic, whole cloves, black peppercorns, and bay leaves. This traditional preparation yields an incredibly tender and flavorful main course perfect for any family gathering.*

## Instructions

### Preparation and Cooking
1. Wipe the **4-5 lbs corned beef brisket** with damp paper towels.
2. Place the brisket in a large kettle and cover completely with cold water.
3. Add the **1 clove garlic**, **2 whole cloves**, **10 whole black peppercorns**, and **2 bay leaves** to the kettle.
4. Bring the liquid to a rolling boil, then reduce the heat and simmer for **5 mins**.
5. Skim any foam or residue from the surface using a slotted spoon.
6. Cover the kettle with a tight-fitting lid and simmer gently for **3-4 hours** or until the meat is fork-tender.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Can be cooked a day ahead, cooled in its cooking broth, and refrigerated. Slice cold and reheat gently in the broth before serving to keep the meat moist.
