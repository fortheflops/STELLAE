# Korean Green Beans & Beef

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *Experience the vibrant flavors of tender beef and crisp green beans tossed in a savory soy and oyster sauce. This quick and easy stir-fry comes together in minutes for a satisfying weeknight meal.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 15 mins | None | 4 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Wok
* Saucepan
* Knife
* Cutting board

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 lb | [[Green Beans]] | Fresh, cut into 2" pieces |
| 2 Tablespoons | [[Peanut Oil]] | For cooking |
| ½ lb | [[Beef]] | Lean, chopped fine |
| 2 Tablespoons | [[Sesame Seeds]] | Whole |
| 2 | [[Green Onions]] | Chopped |
| 2 Tablespoons | [[Soy Sauce]] | Standard |
| 1 Tablespoon | [[Oyster Sauce]] | Standard |
| 1½ Teaspoons | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Parboil the Beans
1. Parboil the **1 lb fresh green beans** in a small amount of water for about **5 minutes**; drain and set aside.

### Step 2: Stir-Fry the Meat and Aromatics
2. Heat the **2 tablespoons peanut oil** in a wok.
3. Add the **½ lb lean beef**, **2 tablespoons sesame seeds**, and **2 green onions**.
4. Stir fry for a few minutes until the meat is done and lightly browned.

### Step 3: Combine and Simmer
5. Add the parboiled beans, **2 tablespoons soy sauce**, **1 tablespoon oyster sauce**, and **1½ teaspoons sugar**; stir well to combine.
6. Cover the wok, lower the heat, and simmer until the beans are barely tender—about **5 minutes**.
7. Do not overcook, keeping the beans crisp-tender, then serve with rice or ramen noodles.
