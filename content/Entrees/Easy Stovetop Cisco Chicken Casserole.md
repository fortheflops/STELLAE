# Easy Stovetop 'Cisco Chicken' Casserole

## Instructions

### Step 1: Combine and Simmer
1. In a large pot or Dutch oven, combine the **2 cut-up chickens**, **2 cans cream of mushroom soup**, **2 cans cheddar cheese soup**, and **2 or more cans small onions**.
2. Stir to combine thoroughly, then bring to a simmer over medium-low heat.
3. Cover and cook for about **1 hour**, until the chicken is tender, cooked through, and the sauce is bubbling and fragrant.

### Step 2: Serve
4. Serve hot over **hot cooked rice**.
