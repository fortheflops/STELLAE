## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep & Sauce
1. Cook the **5 oz. noodles** in boiling salted water until al dente. Drain well and set aside.
2. In a saucepan over medium heat, melt **2 tbsp. shortening**. Stir in **2 tbsp. flour**, **1/2 tsp. salt**, and a pinch of **black pepper** to form a smooth paste.
3. Gradually pour in **2 cups milk**, whisking constantly to prevent lumps. Cook until the sauce thickens and bubbles gently.

### Step 2: Assemble & Bake
4. Gently fold the cooked **5 oz. noodles**, the prepared white sauce, **1 (7 oz.) can tuna fish**, **1 chopped pimiento**, and **1 cup peas** together until evenly coated.
5. Transfer the mixture into a greased baking dish.
6. Evenly scatter **2 cups buttered crumbs** over the top.
7. Bake at 350°F (175°C) for 20 to 25 minutes until the casserole is bubbling hot at the edges and the crumb topping is beautifully golden brown.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Can be assembled ahead of time, covered, and refrigerated. Add the buttered crumbs just before baking.
