# Vegetable and Cheese Sandwich

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A refreshing and crunchy vegetarian sandwich featuring a flavorful blend of grated cheese, alfalfa sprouts, and assorted seeds bound with mayonnaise. Perfect for a quick and satisfying lunch.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | None | None | 3–4 Sandwiches |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Spoon or spatula
* Bread knife

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 1/2 Cups | [[Cheese]] | Grated, any variety like Jack, Cheddar, Swiss, or mixed |
| 1 Cup | [[Alfalfa Sprouts]] | Fresh |
| Dash | [[Onion Powder]] | To taste |
| Dash | [[Salt]] | To taste |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Seeds]] | Assorted, such as sunflower seeds, peanuts, and soy nuts |
| To Moisten | [[Mayonnaise]] | As needed for spreading consistency |
| 6 to 8 Slices | [[Bread]] | Whole wheat |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mix the Filling
1. In a mixing bowl, combine the **1 1/2 cups grated cheese**, **1 cup alfalfa sprouts**, **dash of onion powder**, and **dash of salt**.

### Step 2: Add Seeds and Binder
2. Stir in the **1/2 cup assorted seeds**, then add just enough **mayonnaise** to give enough moisture to easily spread the mixture until thoroughly combined and creamy.

### Step 3: Assemble and Serve
3. Spoon the mixture evenly between the **6 to 8 slices whole wheat bread** to assemble **3 to 4 sandwiches**, slice, and serve immediately.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The cheese and seed spread can be prepared a day in advance and stored in an airtight container in the refrigerator; assemble on bread just before serving.
