# Pastitso

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A traditional Greek lasagna featuring layered macaroni, spiced ground meat, and a rich cream sauce. Baked until golden brown and bubbling with melted cheese.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Pasta and Meat Preparation
1. Boil **1 lb. macaroni** in salted water until tender, then drain and return to pan.
2. Pour a portion of the **1 1/2 lbs. butter** (melted) over the **1 lb. macaroni**, also add most of the **1 lb. grated cheese** and **3** slightly beaten **eggs**. Mix well.
3. In a separate skillet, fry **3 small chopped onions** in butter, add **2 lbs. ground lamb or beef**, **1/2 can tomato paste**, **1/2 cup wine**, **1 tsp. cinnamon**, and **1 tsp. clove**. Cook till the **2 lbs. ground lamb or beef** is done, adding more wine if necessary.

### Step 2: Cream Sauce
4. Over low heat, place a portion of the **1 1/2 lbs. butter** (melted) in a pan. Add **5 tbsp. cornstarch**, **1 qt. milk**, and **1/3 tsp. salt**. Stir till thickened.
5. Cool slightly. Add a little of the hot mixture to **2** beaten **eggs** and then temper the **2** beaten **eggs** mixture back into the main **1 qt. milk** sauce. Mix well.
6. Stir in **1/2 of original melted butter** into the finished cream sauce.

### Step 3: Assembly and Baking
7. Pour a little **cream sauce** in the bottom of a deep lasagne pan.
8. Spread half of the **1 lb. macaroni** mixture, cover with the seasoned **2 lbs. ground lamb or beef** meat sauce.
9. Spread the rest of the **1 lb. macaroni** and cover with the remaining **cream sauce**. Sprinkle the top with remaining **1 lb. grated cheese**.
10. Bake for **40 minutes** at **350 degrees**. Let it set awhile until firm before serving. Cut into squares and serve.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the pastitso completely in the pan, cover tightly, and refrigerate for up to 24 hours before baking.
