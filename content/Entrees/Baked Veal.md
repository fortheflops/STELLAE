# Baked Veal

> *Discover a vintage recipe for Baked Veal marinated in wine vinegar, roasted with thick-sliced onions and tomatoes, and simmered to tenderness. This classic dish offers a deeply savory flavor profile perfect for a comforting family dinner.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Marinate and Brown
1. Place the **1 1/2 pounds veal cutlet** in a deep bowl; season with **salt** and **pepper**.
2. Marinate in **wine vinegar** for **1 hour**.
3. Remove the **1 1/2 pounds veal cutlet** from the vinegar, pat dry, and place in a greased roaster.
4. Place in a hot oven preheated to **450 degrees Fahrenheit** and brown until golden brown and fragrant.

### Step 2: Fry Vegetables and Bake
5. Cut the **3 onions** and **4 tomatoes** into thick slices and fry in a skillet.
6. Add the fried **3 onions** and **4 tomatoes** to the **1 1/2 pounds veal cutlet** in the roaster.
7. Reduce the oven temperature to **300 degrees Fahrenheit**.
8. Add **stock** or **water** to cover the contents.
9. Continue to bake for **30 to 40 minutes** until the meat is fork-tender and the sauce is rich.
