# Easy As Pie (Cottage Pie)

> 📜 **Collection:** Louise Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A classic and simple Cottage Pie featuring a savory seasoned ground beef base baked in a pie pan and topped with fluffy mashed potatoes. This comforting dish brings together familiar flavors for an easy family-friendly dinner.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 40 mins | None | 4 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Medium bowl
* 9-inch pie pan
* Oven

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 Envelope | Lipton Beef Flavor [[Mushroom Mix]] | Dry |
| 1 Pound | [[Ground Beef]] | Raw |
| 1/2 Cup | Soft [[Bread Crumbs]] | Soft |
| 1/4 Cup | [[Ketchup]] | Standard |
| 1/4 Cup | [[Water]] | None |
| 1 | [[Egg]] | Beaten or whole |
| 2-3 Cups | Hot [[Mashed Potatoes]] | Hot and freshly prepared |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Mix
1. Preheat oven to **350°F (175°C)**.
2. In a medium bowl, combine the **1 envelope Lipton mushroom mix**, **1 pound ground beef**, **1/2 cup soft bread crumbs**, **1/4 cup ketchup**, **1/4 cup water**, and **1 egg**. Mix thoroughly until well incorporated.

### Step 2: Bake the Base
3. Press the meat mixture firmly into the bottom and up the sides of a **9-inch pie pan** to form a savory crust.
4. Bake for **35-40 minutes**, or until the meat is cooked through and bubbling.
5. Carefully drain any excess fat from the pan.

### Step 3: Top and Serve
6. Top the hot meat base with the **2-3 cups hot mashed potatoes**, spreading evenly to the edges until the pie is fully covered and golden brown and fragrant.
7. Serve immediately.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the meat base ahead of time and store covered in the refrigerator for up to 24 hours before baking.
