## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Meat Filling
1. In a skillet, brown **1 1/2 lb ground beef** and **1 chopped onion** until the meat is fully cooked and no longer pink.
2. Season with **salt**, **black pepper**, and **chili powder** to taste.
3. Stir in **2 cups tomato sauce** and **1 can corn**. Mix well and let simmer briefly.

### Step 2: Assemble and Bake
4. Preheat oven to 325°F.
5. In a baking dish, place a layer of **tortillas**, then cover with a portion of the meat mixture, followed by a layer of **grated cheese** and **chopped black olives**.
6. Continue layering in this order, ending with a generous layer of **grated cheese** and **chopped black olives** on top.
7. Bake at 325°F for **30 minutes** until the cheese is melted, bubbly, and golden brown around the edges.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Can be assembled a day in advance, covered, and refrigerated. Add 5-10 minutes to the baking time if baking straight from the refrigerator.
