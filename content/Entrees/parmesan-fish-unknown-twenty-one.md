# Parmesan Fish

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty One | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A quick and flavorful broiled fish recipe topped with savory parmesan and a dusting of paprika. Perfect for a simple weeknight seafood dinner.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5 mins | 15 mins | None | 4 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Baking sheet
* Broiler

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 lb | [[Fish]] | Fillets |
| 2 tbsp | [[Oleo]] | Softened or melted |
| 1/4 cup | [[Parmesan]] | Grated |
| 1 tsp | [[Paprika]] | As needed |
| 1 pinch | [[Oregano]] | Optional |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Broil
1. Spread the **2 tbsp oleo** evenly over the **1 lb fish**.
2. Sprinkle the **1/4 cup parmesan** and **1 tsp paprika** over the fish, adding **1 pinch oregano** if desired.
3. Broil on the 3rd rack for **15 minutes** without turning until cooked through and golden brown on top.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Best prepared fresh, but fish can be seasoned ahead and refrigerated for up to 2 hours before broiling.
