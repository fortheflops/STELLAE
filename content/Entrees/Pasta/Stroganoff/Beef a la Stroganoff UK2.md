## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
| 1½ lbs               | [[Tenderloin of Beef]], cut in shoestring strips |
| To Taste           | [[Salt]]                               |
| To Taste           | [[Pepper]]                             |
| 2 Tablespoons               | [[Butter]]                             |
| 1 Tablespoon            | [[Flour]]                              |
| 2 Cups              | [[Stock]]                              |
| 1 Tablespoon            | [[Tomato Paste]]                       |
| 1 Tablespoon            | Chopped [[Onion]]                     |
| ½ lb                | [[Mushrooms]]                          |
| 2 Tablespoons               | [[Sour Cream]]                         |
___
### Instructions
1.  Season meat well with salt & pepper, set stand for 2 hours.
2.  Brown flour in butter, work to a smooth paste, stir in stock gradually, and bring to a boil.
3.  Stir in tomato paste.
4.  Add mushrooms.
5.  Sear meat in butter with chopped onion.
6.  When brown, put meat in the sauce and simmer 5 min.
7.  Add sour cream; heat and serve.
