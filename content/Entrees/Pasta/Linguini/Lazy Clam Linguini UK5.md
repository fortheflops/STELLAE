## Instructions
1.  Cook your chosen pasta until it is "al dente."
2.  While the pasta is cooking, gently heat the canned white clam sauce in a saucepan.
3.  Stir in a splash of white wine and some creme fraiche to enrich the sauce.
4.  Season the sauce to your liking, and add a drizzle of chili oil for some heat.
5.  Toss the cooked, drained pasta with the prepared sauce.
6.  If using, toss in some fresh sprouts at the end.
7.  Serve immediately.
