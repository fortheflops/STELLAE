## Recipe
| Measurements      | Ingredients                   |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 package         | [[Spaghetti]]                   |
| 1 can             | [[Whole Tomatoes]]              |
| 1 can             | [[Tomato Soup]]                 |
| 1                 | [[Onion]], chopped                |
| 1/2 cup           | Finely chopped [[Green Pepper]]   |
| As Needed         | [[Mushrooms]] (optional)          |
| A small chunk     | [[Melting Cheese]] (e.g., Velveeta or cheddar) |
___
## Instructions
1.  Preheat oven to 350°F. Cook the spaghetti according to package directions.
2.  While the spaghetti cooks, fry the onions, green peppers, and mushrooms (if using) in a skillet until tender and browned.
3.  Add the chunk of melting cheese to the skillet and stir until the cheese is melted into the vegetables, creating a sauce.
4.  Drain the cooked spaghetti. In a large bowl or the spaghetti pot, combine the spaghetti with the can of whole tomatoes (break them up with a spoon) and the can of tomato soup.
5.  Pour the cheese and vegetable mixture into the spaghetti and mix everything well.
6.  Transfer the mixture to a casserole dish and bake until browned and bubbly on top.
