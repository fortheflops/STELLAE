## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
|1 1/4 C.|[[Cooked Spaghetti]]|
|2 C.|[[Cooked Hen (Thighs)]]|
|1/4 C.|[[Diced Pimentos]]|
|1/4 C.|[[Chopped Green Peppers]]|
|1 can|[[Mushroom Soup]]|
|1/2 C.|[[Chicken Stock]]|
|1/2 t.|[[Salt]]|
|1 3/4 C.|[[Grated Longhorn Cheese]]|
|1/2 C.|[[Chopped Onion]]|
|1 jar|[[Mushrooms]]|
___
### Instructions
1. Cook spaghetti, drain, and set aside.
2. Mix chicken, onion, pimentos, green peppers, and spaghetti in a 3-quart casserole.
3. Pour in soup, stock, salt, pepper, and 1/4 C. cheese. Mix well.
4. Top with remaining 1 1/4 C. cheese.
5. Cook at 325-350° for 1 hour. Start in a cold oven.
