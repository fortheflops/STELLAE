## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
|2 cans|[[Creamy Chicken Mushroom Soup]]|
|1 can|[[Cream of Mushroom Soup]]|
|1 can|[[Cream of Chicken Soup]]|
|1 cup|[[Chicken Broth]]|
|2 cups|[[Shredded Processed Cheese Spread]]|
|4 cups|[[Cooked, Diced Chicken]]|
|1-12 oz. pkg.|[[Spaghetti, Cooked & Drained]]|
|1-4 oz. can|[[Mushrooms, Drained]]|
|1/2 cup|[[Grated Parmesan Cheese]]|
| |[[Paprika]]|
___
### Instructions
1. Combine soups and broth.
2. Stir in shredded cheese, chicken, spaghetti, and mushrooms.
3. Pour into 12x7x2 inch greased baking dish.
4. Sprinkle with Parmesan cheese and use paprika to add color.
5. Bake at 350° for 25 to 30 minutes.
6. Serves 6-8.
