# Sweet and Sour Chicken

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A classic baked sweet and sour chicken featuring a savory homemade glaze brushed repeatedly for maximum flavor. Tender, juicy, and perfectly balanced with pineapple.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 1 hour | None | 4–6 Servings |

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Glaze
1. In a small pan, combine the **1 tablespoon cornstarch**, **1 tablespoon cold water**, **1/4 cup sugar**, **1/4 cup soy sauce**, **1/4 cup vinegar**, **a dash garlic salt**, **1/8 teaspoon Accent**, **1/8 teaspoon ginger**, and **1/8 teaspoon coarse ground pepper**.
2. Bring the mixture to a boil. As soon as it thickens and becomes glossy, remove from fire.

### Step 2: Bake and Glaze the Chicken
3. Place **2 to 2 1/2 pounds chicken pieces** in a baking pan.
4. Season the **chicken pieces** with **salt** to your desired taste.
5. Brush the chicken with the prepared glaze. Continue to brush with glaze every **10 minutes** for a total of **30 minutes**.
6. After **30 minutes**, turn the chicken pieces over.
7. Brush the other side with the remaining glaze until well coated.
8. If using, add the **4 1/2 ounce can pineapple spears** to the baking pan during the last **10 minutes** of cooking.
9. Bake until the chicken is thoroughly cooked, golden, and done within an hour.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Glaze can be mixed ahead of time, but it is best prepared fresh just before brushing on the baking chicken.
