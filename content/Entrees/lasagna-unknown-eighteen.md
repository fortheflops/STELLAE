## Instructions

### Making the Sauce
1. Combine in a large bowl and stir together well: **1 - 14 1/2 oz. can tomatoes**, **1 large can tomato sauce**, **2 small cans tomato paste**, **1/2 tsp minced garlic**, **1 - 1 1/2 tsp oregano leaves**, **1 - 1 1/2 tsp Italian seasoning**, and **salt and pepper**.
2. Fry **1 - 1 1/2 lb. hamburger** in a skillet and drain well. Add to the tomato sauce and mix well.
3. Simmer sauce over low heat for **1/2 hour** until flavors meld.

### Preparing the Cheese and Pasta
4. Combine in a bowl: **1 carton cottage cheese**, **3 or 4 oz. romano-parmesan cheese**, **1 egg**, **salt and white pepper**, **1 tbls parsley flakes**, and **1/2 tsp oregano leaves**. Stir well until the mixture is difficult to stir; it should not be too creamy. If it is creamy, add more romano-parmesan cheese.
5. Cook at least **9 lasagna noodles** in boiling salted water to which **2 tbls olive oil** has been added. Remove from water, drain well, and rinse in cold water.

### Baking the Lasagna
6. In a baking dish, layer in the following order: First, **sauce** to cover the bottom of the pan.
7. Add **3 or 4 pasta noodles** in a single layer.
8. Spread the **cottage cheese mixture**.
9. Add another layer of **3 or 4 pasta**.
10. Layer **1 - 1 1/2 lb. mozzarella cheese**.
11. Add another layer of **3 or 4 pasta**.
12. Pour the **remaining sauce**, ensuring all pasta is covered with sauce.
13. Sprinkle lightly with **romano-parmesan cheese**.
14. Bake for one-half to three-quarter hour in a **375° oven**. Remove 15 minutes before serving to set cheeses until bubbly and golden brown.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the lasagna ahead of time, cover, and refrigerate for up to 24 hours before baking, adding a few extra minutes to the baking time if chilled.
