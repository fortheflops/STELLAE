# Beef Noodle Casserole

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A comforting, savory casserole combining ground beef, noodles, mushrooms, and almonds topped with crispy fried onion rings. This hearty baked dish serves four and comes together easily for a satisfying weeknight meal.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 30 mins | None | 4 servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Skillet
* Casserole dish

## Ingredients

### Casserole Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 lb | [[Ground Beef]] | Ground chuck |
| As needed | [[Butter]] | For sautéing |
| 1 | [[Onion]] | Chopped fine |
| 1 (8 oz) | [[Tomato Soup]] | Canned |
| 1 (8 oz) | [[Noodles]] | Cooked |
| 1 cup | [[Whole Kernel Corn]] | Canned or frozen |
| 1 cup | [[Stuffed Olives]] | Sliced |
| 1 (4 oz) | [[Sauted Mushrooms]] | Canned |
| 1 cup | [[Almonds]] | Sliced |
| 1 can | [[Fried Onion Rings]] | Crispy |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and Assembly
1. Sauté **1 lb ground beef** in a little **butter** alongside **1 chopped onion** until browned and fragrant.
2. Add the **1 (8 oz) can tomato soup**, **1 (8 oz) pkg cooked noodles**, **1 cup whole kernel corn**, **1 cup sliced, stuffed olives**, **1-4 oz can sauted mushrooms**, and **1 cup sliced almonds**, mixing everything together thoroughly.
3. Pour the mixture into a buttered casserole dish.
4. Sprinkle **1 can fried onion rings** (crumbled) evenly over the top.

### Step 2: Baking
5. Bake in a **350° oven** until heated through and bubbling, then serve hot to four people.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole in the dish up to a point before adding the onion rings, cover, and refrigerate for up to 24 hours. Add the fried onion rings just before baking.
