# Savory Pork Chop & Rice Casserole

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A hearty, vintage one-dish meal featuring browned pork chops baked over a bed of rice with fresh vegetables and savory consommé. Perfect for a comforting and effortless family dinner.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 1 hour | None | 4 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Casserole dish
* Skillet

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 6 Tablespoons | [[Rice]] | raw |
| 4 | [[Pork Chops]] | browned |
| As Needed | Drippings | from chops |
| 4 Slices | [[Onion]] | one per chop |
| 4 Slices | [[Tomato]] | one per chop |
| 4 Rings | [[Green Pepper]] | one per chop |
| To Taste | [[Salt]] | divided |
| To Taste | [[Pepper]] | divided |
| 1 Can | [[Consommé]] | undiluted |
| A Couple Pinches | [[Marjoram]] | dried |
| A Couple Pinches | [[Thyme]] | dried |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Assemble the Casserole
1. Preheat your oven to **350°F**.
2. Spread **6 tablespoons raw rice** evenly across the bottom of a casserole dish.
3. Arrange the **4 browned pork chops** on top of the **rice**, then pour the **drippings** from the browning pan over the chops.
4. Place **1 slice onion**, **1 slice tomato**, and **1 ring green pepper** on top of each **pork chop**.
5. Season each layer lightly with **salt** and **pepper** to taste.
6. Pour **1 can consommé** over the entire assembly, then sprinkle with **a couple pinches marjoram** and **a couple pinches thyme**.

### Step 2: Bake
7. Bake uncovered at **350°F** for **1 hour** until the pork chops are tender, the rice is fully cooked, and the liquid is absorbed into a rich, savory gravy.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This casserole can be assembled a few hours in advance and kept covered in the refrigerator before baking. Leftovers store well in an airtight container for up to 3 days.
