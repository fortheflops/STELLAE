# Lasagne Pie

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *Delight your family with Lasagne Pie, a creative and savory main dish featuring seasoned ground beef and sour cream encased in a flaky crescent roll crust. Topped with melted mozzarella cheese, this comforting bake comes together easily for a satisfying weeknight meal.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation
1. In skillet, brown **1 lb. ground beef**; drain.
2. Add **1 can (6 oz.) tomato paste**, **1 tablespoon instant minced onion**, **1 teaspoon salt**, **1/2 teaspoon Italian seasoning**, and **1/4 teaspoon pepper**; blend well.

### Step 2: Assembly and Baking
3. Separate **1 can (8 oz.) Pillsbury Refrigerated Quick Crescent Dinner Rolls** dough into 8 triangles. Place in ungreased 9-inch pie pan, pressing pieces together to form a crust.
4. Turn meat mixture into crust; spread with **1/2 cup dairy sour cream** and top with **4 slices Kraft Mozzarella Cheese** slices.
5. Bake at 375° for 25 to 30 minutes until bubbling and golden. Serve hot.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the pie up to a few hours in advance in the pie pan, cover, and refrigerate until ready to bake.
