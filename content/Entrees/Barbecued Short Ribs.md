# Barbecued Short Ribs

> 📜 **Collection:** General Archive | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *Tender, fall-off-the-bone short ribs braised in a tangy and savory barbecue-style sauce with onions, curry, and paprika. This classic comfort dish delivers rich, aromatic flavors after a slow bake in the oven.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Brown
1. Preheat your oven to **350°F**.
2. Chop up **1 clove garlic** fine.
3. Heat the **3 Tbsp fat** in a heavy skillet over medium-high heat.
4. Add the **1 clove garlic** and **3 lb short ribs** to the skillet and brown well on both sides until nicely seared and aromatic.
5. Transfer the meat and accumulated drippings to a large casserole dish or Dutch oven.

### Step 2: Braise and Serve
6. Slice up the **3 medium onions** and add them directly to the meat in the Dutch oven.
7. In a mixing bowl, combine **1 cup vinegar**, **1 tsp curry powder**, **1 tsp paprika**, **¼ tsp chili powder**, **1 Tbsp brown sugar**, **1 cup beef stock**, **1 tsp salt**, **½ tsp pepper**, and **½ tsp dry mustard**, then pour the mixture evenly over the beef and onions.
8. Cover the Dutch oven very tightly with a lid and bake in the preheated oven for **1½ hours** until fork-tender, rich, and fragrant.
9. To serve, carefully place the tender short ribs onto a warm serving platter and generously pour the savory sauce over them.

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Leftovers can be stored in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days, and actually taste even better as the flavors meld overnight.
