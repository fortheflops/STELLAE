# E. Wilson's Creamy Chicken Romanoff Casserole

> 📜 **Collection:** LouiseCollection | ✍️ **Attribution:** E. Wilson | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A comforting vintage casserole featuring baked chicken, broccoli, and Noodle-Roni Romanoff baked in a creamy mushroom soup sauce. Perfect for a cozy family dinner.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 1 hour 45 mins | 15 mins | 6–8 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Casserole dish
* Mixing bowl
* Baking sheet
* Saucepan

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3–4 | [[Chicken Breasts]] | whole |
| 1 Package | [[Noodle-Roni Romanoff]] | uncooked |
| Amount Not Specified | [[Broccoli Spears]] | cooked or frozen |
| 1 Can | Cream of [[Mushroom Soup]] | undiluted |
| 3/4 Cup | [[Milk]] | cold |
| 2 1/2 Tablespoons | [[Butter]] | melted |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Bake Chicken
1. Preheat oven to **375°F (190°C)**. Lightly grease a **casserole dish**.
2. Place **3-4 chicken breasts** on a baking sheet and bake at **375°F (190°C)** for **1 1/4 hours** until cooked through and juices run clear.
3. Let the chicken cool slightly for **15 minutes**, then skin, bone, and cut into bite-sized pieces.

### Step 2: Prepare Sauce and Noodles
4. Cook **1 package Noodle-Roni Romanoff** according to package instructions, using the **2 1/2 tablespoons butter**.
5. In a separate mixing bowl, whisk together **1 can cream of mushroom soup** and **3/4 cup milk** until smooth and combined.

### Step 3: Assemble and Bake
6. In the greased casserole dish, gently combine the cooked chicken pieces, cooked Noodle-Roni, **broccoli spears**, and the soup mixture.
7. Bake at **375°F (190°C)** for **30 minutes** until bubbly, hot, and fragrant.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole ahead of time, cover tightly, and refrigerate for up to 24 hours before baking. Add 10-15 minutes to the baking time if baking straight from the fridge.
