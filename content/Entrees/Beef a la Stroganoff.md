# Beef a la Stroganoff

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A classic savory beef stroganoff featuring tender strips of beef, earthy mushrooms, and a rich sour cream sauce. This traditional recipe delivers deep umami flavor and a comforting texture.*

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1½ lbs | [[Tenderloin of Beef]] | cut in shoestring strips |
| To Taste | [[Salt]] | divided |
| To Taste | [[Pepper]] | divided |
| 2 Tablespoons | [[Butter]] | divided |
| 1 Tablespoon | [[Flour]] | none |
| 2 Cups | [[Stock]] | none |
| 1 Tablespoon | [[Tomato Paste]] | none |
| 1 Tablespoon | [[Onion]] | chopped |
| ½ lb | [[Mushrooms]] | sliced |
| 2 Tablespoons | [[Sour Cream]] | none |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Season
1. Season the **1½ lbs tenderloin of beef** well with **salt** and **pepper** to taste, and let it stand for **2 hours**.

### Step 2: Prepare the Sauce
2. Brown the **1 tablespoon flour** in **1 tablespoon butter**, work to a smooth paste, stir in **2 cups stock** gradually, and bring to a boil.
3. Stir in **1 tablespoon tomato paste** and add the **½ lb mushrooms**.

### Step 3: Sear and Combine
4. In a separate pan, sear the seasoned meat in **1 tablespoon butter** with **1 tablespoon chopped onion** until brown.
5. When brown, transfer the meat into the prepared sauce and simmer for **5 minutes** until heated through and fragrant.
6. Stir in **2 tablespoons sour cream**, heat gently, and serve immediately.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Best served fresh, but the sauce and meat mixture can be prepared a day ahead without the sour cream, then reheated and finished with sour cream just before serving.
