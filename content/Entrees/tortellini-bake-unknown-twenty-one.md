# Tortellini Bake

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty One | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A quick and comforting microwave tortellini bake layered with ground beef, zucchini, chunky spaghetti sauce, and Parmesan cheese. Perfect for a fast weeknight dinner with easy make-ahead and freezing options.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 25 mins | None | 4 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Saucepan
* Skillet
* Microwave-safe dish

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 12 oz | [[Cheese Tortellini]] | Or ravioli |
| 1/2 # | [[Ground Beef]] | Gr. |
| 1/2 med. | [[Onion]] | Chopped |
| 2 cloves | [[Garlic]] | Minced |
| 1/2 t | [[Oregano]] |  |
| 26 oz. | [[Spaghetti Sauce]] | Chunky with garlic & herb |
| 2 | [[Zucchini]] | Sliced |
| 1 1/2 oz. | [[Parmesan]] | Cheese |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Sauce
1. Cook the **12 oz cheese tortellini or ravioli** according to package directions and drain.
2. Brown the **1/2 # gr. beef** with the **1/2 med. onion**, **2 cloves garlic**, and **1/2 t oregano**, then drain. Season if desired.
3. Add the **26 oz. chunky spaghetti sauce w/garlic & herb** and **2 zucchini** to the beef mixture.
4. Cook for **15 min** or until thickened, stirring occasionally.

### Step 2: Layering and Baking
5. In a microwave-safe dish, arrange half of the cooked pasta.
6. Top with half each of the meat sauce and cheese, then repeat the layers ending with the remaining cheese; cover.
7. Microwave for **8-10 minutes** or until heated through, rotating the dish halfway through the cooking time until bubbling.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Double the recipe and freeze half for later, or assemble and refrigerate the dish to microwave later.
