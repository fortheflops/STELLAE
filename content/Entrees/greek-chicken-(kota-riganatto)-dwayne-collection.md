## Instructions

### Preparation & Baking
1. Wash **1 fryer chicken** and pat dry. Brush with **butter** and season with **salt** and **black pepper** to taste, then place into a baking pan.
2. Squeeze **1 fresh lemon** juice over the **fryer chicken**. Sprinkle with **oregano**.
3. If desired, brush peeled and cut up **potatoes** with **butter** and arrange them in the same baking pan to roast alongside the **fryer chicken** so they absorb the delicious lemon and chicken flavors.
4. Bake at 325°F for **1 hour** or until the chicken is cooked through, golden brown, and the juices run clear.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Leftovers can be stored in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days. Reheat in the oven at 350°F to maintain a crisp skin.
