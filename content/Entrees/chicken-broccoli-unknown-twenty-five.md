# Chicken Broccoli

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Five | ✍️ **Attribution:** Lyn Green | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A comforting baked casserole featuring tender chicken and broccoli layered with a rich soup mixture and melted cheese. This hearty dish is simple to assemble and bakes to golden perfection.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 1 hr | None | 6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Baking dish
* Mixing bowl

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3 cups | [[Broccoli]] | Cooked frozen |
| 1 | [[Chicken]] | Boiled and boned |
| 2 cans | [[Cream of Chicken Soup]] | Condensed |
| 2 tbsp | [[Milk]] | None |
| 1 cup | [[Sour Cream]] | None |
| 1 tsp | [[Garlic Salt]] | None |
| to taste | [[Salt]] | None |
| to taste | [[Pepper]] | None |
| as needed | [[Grated Cheese]] | For topping |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and Assembly
1. In a mixing bowl, combine the **2 cans cream of chicken soup**, **2 tbsp milk**, **1 cup sour cream**, **1 tsp garlic salt**, **salt**, and **pepper** to taste, mixing until smooth.
2. Layer the cooked **3 cups broccoli** and boned **1 chicken** in a greased baking dish.
3. Pour the soup mixture evenly over the chicken and broccoli layers.
4. Top the casserole with a generous layer of **grated cheese**.

### Step 2: Baking
5. Bake at 350°F for **1 hr** until the cheese is melted, bubbly, and golden brown on top.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole up to a day in advance, cover tightly, and refrigerate until ready to bake.
