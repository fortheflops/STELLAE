# Crunchy Chicken with Cheese

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty One | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A comforting, retro-style baked chicken casserole featuring tender chicken breasts topped with melted cheese, savory cream of chicken soup, and a delightfully crunchy herb stuffing crust.*

| 15 mins | 25 mins | None | 8 servings |

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 8 | [[Chicken Breasts]] | skinless |
| 4 slices | [[Swiss Cheese]] or [[American Cheese]] | cut in half |
| 10 3/4 oz. | [[Cream of Chicken Soup]] | canned |
| 8 | [[Tomato]] | sliced |
| 2 tbsp | [[Oleo]] | melted (margarine) |
| 1/2 cup | [[Herb Stuffing Mix]] | dry |

### Optional Variations
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |

## Instructions

### Assembly
1. Preheat your oven to **400°F**.
2. Place the **8 skinless chicken breasts** in a single layer in a greased baking dish, then top each breast with the halved **4 slices Swiss or American cheese**.
3. Stir the **10 3/4 oz. cream of chicken soup** until smooth, then spread it evenly over the cheese-topped chicken. (If you prefer to omit the tomatoes, combine the soup with **1/4 cup milk** before spreading).
4. Top the soup layer with the **8 tomato slices**.
5. In a small bowl, combine the **2 tbsp melted oleo** and **1/2 cup herb stuffing**, stirring until the stuffing is evenly moistened. Sprinkle this mixture over the tomato slices.

### Baking
6. Bake at **400°F** for **25 minutes** until the chicken is cooked through, the sauce is bubbling at the edges, and the stuffing topping is beautifully golden brown and crunchy.
7. Serve hot over a bed of warm rice.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This dish can be assembled up to 8 hours in advance, covered tightly, and stored in the refrigerator. Add 5 to 10 minutes to the baking time if cooking directly from the refrigerator.
