# Salisbury Steak

> 📜 **Collection:** Georgia Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A classic, hearty Salisbury steak recipe featuring seasoned ground beef patties coated in crunchy corn flakes. This dish is finished in a rich, savory gravy for a tender and flavorful meal.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 25 mins | None | 4 servings |

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 lb | [[Ground Beef]] | raw |
| 1 | [[Egg]] | large |
| 2 tbsp | [[Onion]] | chopped |
| 1 tbsp | [[Oleo]] | margarine or butter |
| 1 tsp | [[Salt]] | none |
| 1/2 tsp | [[Pepper]] | none |
| 2 tbsp | [[Parsley]] | chopped |
| 1/2 cup | [[Corn Flakes]] | crushed |
| 2 tbsp | [[Shortening]] | for frying |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation
1. In a bowl, mix the **1 lb ground beef**, **1 egg**, **2 tbsp chopped onion**, **1 tsp salt**, **1/2 tsp pepper**, and **2 tbsp chopped parsley** until well combined.
2. Form the mixture into patties and dredge them in the **1/2 cup crushed corn flakes**.

### Step 2: Cooking
3. Melt the **2 tbsp shortening** in a skillet over medium heat.
4. Brown the patties in the **2 tbsp shortening** until a deep golden crust forms.
5. Remove the patties from the pan, prepare gravy in the drippings, then return the patties to the gravy and continue cooking until the meat is cooked through and tender.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Patties can be shaped and breaded ahead of time and refrigerated for up to 4 hours before browning.
