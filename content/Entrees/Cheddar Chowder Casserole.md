# Cheddar Chowder Casserole

> 📜 **Collection:** General Archive | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A hearty and savory ground beef casserole featuring rice or noodles in a creamy double-soup base with corn and vegetables, all baked under a crunchy chow mein noodle topping. Despite the name, this recipe does not contain cheddar cheese.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 50 mins | None | 10 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large skillet
* Dutch oven
* 9x13 inch casserole dish

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Brown
1. Preheat oven to **350°F**. Grease a large casserole dish.
2. In a large skillet, brown the **2 pounds ground meat**, **1 chopped onion**, **1 clove garlic**, and **1 green pepper**. Drain any excess fat until the meat is fully browned and aromatic.

### Step 2: Mix the Casserole
3. Stir in the **salt**, **pepper**, and drained **1 can pimentos**.
4. Add the **1 can cream of chicken soup**, **1 can cream of mushroom soup**, drained **1 tall can whole kernel corn**, cooked **3 cups rice or noodles**, and **1 cup sour cream**. Mix well until thoroughly combined.

### Step 3: Bake and Serve
5. Pour the mixture into the prepared casserole dish and spread evenly.
6. Sprinkle the **1 can chow mein noodles** evenly over the top of the casserole.
7. Bake for **45 minutes to 1 hour**, or until hot, bubbly, and the noodles on top are golden brown and fragrant.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store sealed in the refrigerator for up to 3-4 days; assemble ahead of time and add crunchy noodles just before baking.
