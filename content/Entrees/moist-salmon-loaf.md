# Moist Salmon Loaf

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A classic handwritten recipe for a tender and savory salmon loaf bound with breadcrumbs and evaporated milk. Infused with fresh lemon juice, grated lemon rind, and minced onion, it bakes up golden and flavorful.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and Baking
1. Preheat oven to 350°F and grease a loaf pan.
2. In a mixing bowl, combine **1 egg**, **2/3 cup undiluted evaporated milk**, **2 cups salmon**, **1 cup soft bread crumbs**, **1 small minced onion**, **1 tsp grated lemon rind**, **1/2 tsp salt**, **1/8 tsp pepper**, **2 tbsp melted shortening**, and **2 tbsp lemon juice**.
3. Mix thoroughly until well incorporated, then pour the mixture into the prepared loaf pan.
4. Bake at 350° for **50 minutes** until set and golden brown on top.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the loaf in the pan and refrigerate, covered, for up to 4 hours before baking.
