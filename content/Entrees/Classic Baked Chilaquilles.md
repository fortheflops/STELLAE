# Classic Baked Chilaquilles

> 📜 **Collection:** General Archive | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A classic and hearty baked dish featuring layers of lightly fried corn tortillas, a savory homemade tomato sauce with green chiles, and generous amounts of melted Monterey Jack cheese, all topped with sour cream.*

---

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 25 mins | 30 mins | None | 8-10 servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 9x13 inch baking dish
* Large pot or Dutch oven
* Skillet
* Slotted spoon
* Paper towels

## Ingredients

| 1 clove | [[Garlic]] | Minced |

| 2 large cans | [[Tomatoes]] | Approx. 28 oz each, crushed or whole |
| To taste | [[Salt]] | None |
| To taste | [[Pepper]] | None |
| 1 can | [[Green Chili Peppers]] | 4-7 oz, mild, cut in strips |
| 1 dozen | [[Corn Tortillas]] | Cut in fourths |
| 2 lb. | [[Monterey Jack Cheese]] | Grated |
| 1 pint | [[Sour Cream]] | For topping |

## Instructions

### Step 1: Make the Sauce
1. In a large pot or Dutch oven, heat **2-3 tablespoons of fat**. Sauté the **2 chopped onions** and **1 clove minced garlic** until clear and softened.
2. Add the **2 large cans tomatoes** (breaking them up with a spoon if whole), **salt**, and **pepper**. Simmer for **15-20 minutes**, or until the sauce has thickened slightly.
3. Stir in the **1 can green chili pepper strips**.

### Step 2: Prepare the Tortillas
4. While the sauce simmers, heat about **1/2 inch of fat** in a separate skillet. Cut the **1 dozen corn tortillas** into fourths (triangles).
5. Working in batches, fry the tortilla quarters in the hot fat for **30-60 seconds per side**, just until they are softened and lightly crisped. Remove immediately with a slotted spoon and drain on paper towels.

### Step 3: Assemble and Bake
6. Preheat oven to **350°F (175°C)**. Grease a large **9x13 inch baking dish**.
7. Spread a thin layer of sauce in the bottom of the greased baking dish. Create layers of fried **corn tortillas**, sauce, and **2 lb. grated Monterey Jack cheese**. Repeat these layers until the dish is full.
8. Top the dish with a final generous layer of grated cheese.
9. Bake at **350°F** for **20-30 minutes**, or until the dish is heated through and the cheese is melted and bubbly until golden and bubbling.
10. Remove from the oven and serve hot, topped with dollops of **1 pint sour cream**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store assembled unbaked or leftover baked chilaquilles sealed in the refrigerator for up to 3 days.
