# Quick & Tangy Barbecue Tuna (or Spam) Sandwiches

> 📜 **Collection:** UK15 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A quick and tangy recipe for a hot barbecue sandwich filling featuring a simple stovetop sauce.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5 mins | 10 mins | None | 4-6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Saucepan
* Stovetop
* Wooden spoon

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 cup | [[Vinegar]] | none |
| 1/2 cup | [[Tomato Puree]] | none |
| 1 tablespoon | [[Soy Sauce]] | none |
| 1/4 cup | [[Margarine]] | none |
| 3 tablespoons | [[Onion]] | minced |
| 2 tablespoons | [[Brown Sugar]] | none |
| 2 (7 ounce) cans | [[Tuna]] | drained |
| 1 can | [[Spam]] | diced, optional alternative to tuna |
| As needed | [[Hamburger Buns]] | for serving |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Simmer the Sauce
1. In a saucepan, combine the **1/2 cup vinegar**, **1/2 cup tomato puree**, **1 tablespoon soy sauce**, **1/4 cup margarine**, **3 tablespoons minced onion**, and **2 tablespoons brown sugar**.

### Step 2: Melt and Combine
2. Bring the sauce to a simmer over medium heat, stirring until the **2 tablespoons brown sugar** is dissolved and the **1/4 cup margarine** is melted until smooth and fragrant.

### Step 3: Add Protein and Serve
3. Stir in the **2 (7 ounce) cans tuna** (or **1 can spam**) and heat through for several minutes until bubbling hot.
4. Serve warm spooned generously onto **hamburger buns**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Leftover barbecue filling can be stored in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days and reheated on the stovetop.
