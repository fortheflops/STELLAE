# Sauerbraten

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Seven | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A classic German sauerbraten featuring top round steak larded with bacon and marinated in spiced wine vinegar for several days. Slow-simmered in a Dutch oven with red wine and finished with a rich sour cream gravy.*

## Instructions

### Preparation & Marinating
1. Lard the **4-6 lbs top round steak** by cutting 6 gashes across the meat and inserting the **4 slices bacon** into the gashes.
2. In a saucepan, combine **1 1/2 cups California wine vinegar**, **2 tsp salt**, **10 whole peppercorns**, **3 cloves**, **2 bay leaves**, **2 chopped onions**, and **2 sliced carrots**. Bring to a boil, then remove from heat and cool for 1/2 hour.
3. Place the meat in a glass or porcelain bowl and pour the cooled vinegar mixture over it. Cover and marinate for 3 to 5 days in the refrigerator, turning the meat several times.

### Browning & Simmering
4. Drain the meat, reserving the marinade liquid and vegetables, and blot the meat dry with paper towels.
5. Melt **3 tbsp butter** in a Dutch oven over medium heat. Add the meat and brown deeply on all sides. Pour in the reserved marinade and **1/2 cup boiling water**.
6. Cover tightly and simmer gently for 2 1/2 hours until fork-tender.
7. Add **1 cup Burgundy wine** to the pot and simmer for an additional 30 minutes.
8. Stir in **1 cup sour cream**, stirring constantly to incorporate into the pan liquid. Simmer gently for 15 minutes until creamy and hot. Serve immediately.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Requires 3 to 5 days of refrigeration to marinate before cooking.
