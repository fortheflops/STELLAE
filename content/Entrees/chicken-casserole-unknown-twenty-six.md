# Chicken Casserole

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Wanda | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A comforting, classic baked chicken casserole layered with rice, vegetables, and topped with crunchy potato chips and melted cheese. Perfect for a hearty weeknight family dinner.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 1 hour | None | 6-8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 9"x13" baking dish
* Mixing bowl
* Knife
* Cutting board

---
## Ingredients

### Casserole Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3 c. | [[Chicken]] | Cooked, chopped or diced |
| 1 1/2 c. | [[Rice]] | Cooked |
| 2 c. | [[Celery]] | Sliced |
| 2 t. | [[Onion]] | Grated |
| 1 | [[Green Bell Pepper]] | Diced |
| 3 | [[Eggs]] | Hard boiled, chopped |
| 1 can | [[Cream of Chicken Soup]] | Condensed |
| 1 c. | [[Mayonnaise]] | Standard |
| 1 T. | [[Lemon Juice]] | Fresh |
| 1/2 t. | [[Salt]] | To taste |
| 1 c. | [[Cheese]] | Grated |
| 2 c. | [[Potato Chips]] | Crushed |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Assembly
1. Combine all ingredients except **cheese** and **potato chips** in a large mixing bowl and mix well.
2. Place the mixture into a greased **9"x13" baking dish**.

### Step 2: Baking
3. Sprinkle the top with **grated cheese** and **crushed potato chips**.
4. Bake uncovered at 350°F for **1 hour**, until bubbling and golden. If the top browns too much, cover loosely with foil.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole up to a day in advance, cover, and refrigerate; add the potato chip topping just before baking to maintain crunch.
