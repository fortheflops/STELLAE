# Easy Enchilada Casserole

> 📜 **Collection:** General Archive | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A classic, super-easy 5-ingredient enchilada casserole. This dump-and-bake recipe layers canned beef enchiladas, beans, and tomatoes, all topped with cheddar cheese for a quick and hearty meal.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 30 mins | None | 6–8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 9x13 inch casserole dish
* Medium mixing bowl

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 #2 can (approx. 20 oz) | [[Beef Enchiladas]] | Canned |
| 1 #2 can (approx. 20 oz) | [[Pinto or Kidney Beans]] | Drained |
| 1 #303 can (approx. 16 oz) | [[Tomatoes]] | Undrained |
| 1 medium | [[Onion]] | Chopped |
| 1 cup | [[Sharp Cheddar Cheese]] | Grated |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Layer
1. Preheat oven to **350°F**. Grease a casserole dish.
2. Arrange the **1 #2 can beef enchiladas** in a single layer in the bottom of the prepared dish.

### Step 2: Mix and Top
3. In a medium bowl, combine the drained **1 #2 can pinto or kidney beans**, the undrained **1 #303 can tomatoes**, and the chopped **1 medium onion**. Stir to mix.
4. Pour the bean and tomato mixture evenly over the top of the enchiladas.
5. Sprinkle the grated **1 cup sharp cheddar cheese** all over the top.

### Step 3: Bake
6. Bake for **30 minutes**, or until the casserole is hot and the cheese is bubbly and melted until golden brown and fragrant.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftovers sealed in the refrigerator for up to 3-4 days.
