## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparing the Sausage Sauce
1. Put **2-3 lbs Italian mild sausage** in a pot and cover with water.
2. Boil until half of the water is gone, keeping burner on medium heat.
3. Add **2 cans tomato puree**, **garlic salt**, **salt**, **pepper**, **oregano**, and **Italian seasoning** to the pot.

### Step 2: Making and Adding Meatballs
4. Make meatballs using **2-3 lbs ground meat**, **parmesan cheese**, **1 egg**, **milk**, **bread crumbs**, **salt**, **pepper**, and **garlic salt**.
5. Drop balls into the simmering sauce.
6. Cook (simmer) for **4 hours** until the sauce is deeply rich and flavorful.

### Step 3: Serving
7. Serve the finished meat and sauce over hot cooked **spaghetti**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This sauce tastes even better when made a day in advance and reheated slowly on the stovetop.
