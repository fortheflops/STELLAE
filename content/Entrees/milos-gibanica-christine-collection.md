# Milo's Gibanica

> 📜 **Collection:** Christine Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Milo | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A traditional Serbian cheese pie featuring layers of crumpled phyllo dough dipped in a rich mixture of cottage cheese, Muenster, sour cream, and eggs. Baked until golden brown and puffed, it makes for a comforting and savory dish.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Batter
1. Preheat oven to **350°F** and grease a deep baking dish with some of the **1/2 lb melted butter**.
2. In a large bowl, mix the **1/2 lb grated Muenster cheese**, **6 egg yolks**, **2 tbsp flour**, **1/2 lb melted butter**, **1 large pkg cottage cheese**, **8 oz sour cream**, **salt**, and **pepper** together.
3. Stir in enough **milk** to make the mixture the consistency of pancake batter.
4. In a separate clean bowl, beat the **6 egg whites** until stiff peaks form, then gently fold them into the cheese batter.

### Step 2: Assemble and Bake
5. Take a sheet of **1 pkg phyllo dough** and crumple it loosely like a napkin.
6. Dip the crumpled sheet into the cheese batter, then place it into the prepared deep buttered baking dish. Repeat to make **2 layers** of dipped phyllo.
7. Pour any unused cheese filling over the top of the layers.
8. Bake at **350°F** for **1 hour** until puffed, golden brown, and set in the center.
9. *(Alternative Method)*: Try putting the **1 pkg phyllo dough** in flat layers, brushing each sheet with **1/2 lb melted butter**, and then spreading the cheese filling, repeating for many layers.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Can be assembled a few hours ahead and kept refrigerated before baking. Leftovers reheat well in a 350°F oven to restore crispness.
