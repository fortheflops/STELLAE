# Hearty Zucchini and Beef Casserole

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail's Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A classic, hearty casserole featuring layers of tender zucchini, seasoned ground beef and rice, cottage cheese, and a creamy mushroom soup topping, all baked under a layer of cheddar.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 40 mins | None | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 2 1/2-quart shallow casserole dish
* Large skillet
* Saucepan
* Colander

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 1/2 Pounds | [[Zucchini]] | cut in 1/4 inch rounds |
| 1 Pound | [[Ground Beef]] | fresh |
| 1 Medium | [[Onion]] | chopped |
| 1 Cup | [[Instant Rice]] | uncooked |
| 1 Teaspoon | [[Seasoned Salt]] or [[Garlic Salt]] | to taste |
| 1 Teaspoon | [[Oregano]] | dried |
| 2 Cups (1 Pint) | [[Cottage Cheese]] | Small Curd, optional |
| 1 Can (10.5 oz) | [[Cream of Mushroom Soup]] | undiluted |
| 1 Cup | [[Cheddar Cheese]] | Grated Sharp |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Cook Base
1. Preheat oven to **350°F (175°C)**. Grease a **2 1/2-quart shallow casserole dish**.
2. Cook the **1 1/2 pounds zucchini** in boiling, salted water for about **5 minutes**, or until barely tender. Drain very well.
3. While the zucchini is cooking, sauté the **1 pound ground beef** with the **1 medium onion** in a large skillet until the meat is lightly browned and aromatic. Drain any excess fat.
4. Stir the **1 cup instant rice**, **1 teaspoon seasoned salt or garlic salt**, and **1 teaspoon oregano** into the beef mixture until well combined.

### Step 2: Assemble and Bake
5. Place half of the drained **1 1/2 pounds zucchini** slices in the bottom of the casserole dish. Cover with the beef and rice mixture.
6. Spoon the **2 cups (1 pint) cottage cheese** (if using) evenly over the beef layer, then arrange the remaining zucchini slices on top.
7. Spread the **1 can (10.5 oz) cream of mushroom soup** evenly over the top, like a frosting, and sprinkle with the **1 cup cheddar cheese**.
8. Bake uncovered at **350°F** for **35-40 minutes**, or until bubbly and hot, and the cheese is melted and golden brown.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftovers sealed in the refrigerator for up to 3-4 days; reheat thoroughly before serving.
