# Chop Suey

## Instructions

### Step 1: Simmering Meats
1. Cut **1 1/2 lbs lean pork** and **1 1/2 lbs beef** into cubes.
2. Cook in a covered dish and let simmer until it draws out juice of meat.
3. Then add enough **boiling water** to cover and cook until nearly done.

### Step 2: Adding Vegetables and Thickening
4. Add **3 cups celery**, **6 tablespoons black molasses**, and **salt** to taste.
5. Add **3 to 5 onions** and cook all until tender, keeping it covered with **water**.
6. Add **2 cans bean sprouts**, thicken as any stew, and serve with **rice**.
