# Diabetic Tuna Buns

> 📜 **Collection:** Georgia Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A classic, diabetic-friendly warm tuna sandwich baked in foil. These savory tuna buns are loaded with cheddar, hard-cooked eggs, and green pepper for a satisfying meal.*

## Instructions

### Preparation and Baking
1. Preheat your oven to **350°F**.
2. In a medium mixing bowl, combine the **4 oz. grated cheddar**, **7 oz. can water packed tuna**, **1/4 cup minced green pepper**, **1 tsp. dried onion**, **3 diced hard cooked eggs**, **3 tbsp. dill pickle relish**, and **4 tbsp. low-fat salad dressing** until thoroughly mixed.
3. Fill each of the **10 hamburger buns** evenly with the tuna mixture.
4. Wrap each filled bun individually in **aluminum foil** to seal in the moisture.
5. Place the wrapped buns on a baking sheet and heat in the preheated oven for **20 to 25 minutes** until the cheese is completely melted and the buns are hot and soft.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Buns can be assembled and wrapped in foil ahead of time, then refrigerated. Increase baking time by 5 to 10 minutes if baking directly from the refrigerator.
