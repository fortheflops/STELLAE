# Simple Slow-Roast Brisket

> 📜 **Collection:** General Archive | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A simple, classic recipe for a tender, slow-roasted brisket, marinated overnight in a savory blend of liquid smoke and seasoned salts, then baked for hours until fall-apart tender.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 5 hours | 8 hours | 8-12 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Small bowl
* Wire whisk
* Large dish or zip-top bag
* Roasting pan
* Heavy-duty aluminum foil

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 (4-5 lb) | [[Beef Brisket]] | Whole |
| 2 Tablespoons | [[Liquid Smoke]] | None |
| 1 Teaspoon | [[Garlic Salt]] | None |
| 1 Teaspoon | [[Onion Salt]] | None |
| 1 Teaspoon | [[Celery Salt]] | None |
| 2 Teaspoons | [[Worcestershire Sauce]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Marinate
1. In a small bowl, whisk together the **2 tablespoons liquid smoke**, **1 teaspoon garlic salt**, **1 teaspoon onion salt**, **1 teaspoon celery salt**, and **2 teaspoons Worcestershire sauce** to create the marinade.
2. Place the **1 (4-5 lb) beef brisket** in a large dish or a zip-top bag, pour the marinade over it, turn to coat, cover, and refrigerate overnight.

### Step 2: Roast
3. When ready to cook, preheat the oven to 275°F (135°C).
4. Place the **1 (4-5 lb) beef brisket** and all of its marinade in a roasting pan and cover the pan tightly with heavy-duty aluminum foil.
5. Bake for **4-5 hours**, or until the brisket is fork-tender.

### Step 3: Baste and Serve
6. For the last hour of cooking, remove the foil and baste the **1 (4-5 lb) beef brisket** with its own pan drippings every **20 minutes** to create a browned crust until deeply golden and fragrant.
7. Let the **1 (4-5 lb) beef brisket** rest for **15-20 minutes** before slicing against the grain to serve.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftover brisket in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 4 days or freeze for up to 3 months.
