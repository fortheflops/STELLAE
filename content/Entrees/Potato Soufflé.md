# Potato Soufflé

> 📜 **Collection:** Jill Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Delores Foley | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A creamy and fluffy mashed potato soufflé enriched with cream cheese and eggs, then baked with a crispy fried onion topping until heated through.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 35 mins | None | 6 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Baking dish
* Electric mixer
* Mixing bowl

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 cups | [[Potatoes]] | Mashed |
| As Needed | [[Half-and-Half]] or [[Milk]] | For mashing |
| 8 ounces | [[Cream Cheese]] | Softened |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] | Whole |
| 1 small | [[Onion]] | Diced, optional |
| 2 tablespoons | [[All-Purpose Flour]] | Standard |
| To Taste | [[Salt]] and [[Pepper]] | Seasoning |
| 1 small can | [[Fried Onions]] | French style, approximately 2.8 oz |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Mix
1. Preheat oven to **300°F**. Grease a baking dish.
2. Prepare **2 cups** of mashed [[Potatoes]] to your liking, using [[Half-and-Half]] or [[Milk]].
3. To the warm mashed [[Potatoes]], add the softened **8 ounces** [[Cream Cheese]], **2** [[Eggs]], and **2 tablespoons** [[All-Purpose Flour]].
4. Using an electric mixer, beat the mixture on low speed to combine, then switch to high speed and beat until the mixture is very light and fluffy.
5. Stir in the diced **1 small** [[Onion]] (if using) and season with [[Salt]] and [[Pepper]] to taste.

### Step 2: Bake
6. Pour the potato mixture into the prepared baking dish.
7. Sprinkle the **1 small can** French [[Fried Onions]] evenly over the top.
8. Bake for **35 minutes**, or until heated through and golden.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the unbaked soufflé, cover tightly, and refrigerate for up to 24 hours before baking.
