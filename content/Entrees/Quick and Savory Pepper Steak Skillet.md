# Quick and Savory Pepper Steak Skillet

> 📜 **Collection:** UK5 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Midge Robertson | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A fast and flavorful weeknight meal featuring tender strips of round steak simmered with onions, green peppers, and stewed tomatoes in a savory oregano-spiced sauce. Perfect for serving over a bed of warm rice.*

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 pound | [[Round Steak]] | cut in 1/2" by 1" strips |
| To taste | [[Salt]] and [[Pepper]] | as needed |
| 1/2 cup | [[Onion]] | chopped |
| 1 (8 ounce) can | [[Tomatoes]] | stewed |
| 1 medium | [[Green Pepper]] | seeded and cut in strips (or 1 medium [[Zucchini]], cut in bite-size pieces) |
| 1/2 teaspoon | [[Oregano]] | dried |
| 1 tablespoon | [[Steak Sauce]] or [[Worcestershire Sauce]] | flavor enhancer |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Brown
1. Season the **1/2 pound round steak** with **salt** and **pepper** to taste.
2. Brown the meat in hot oil or its own fat in a skillet until seared.

### Step 2: Simmer and Combine
3. Lightly sauté the **1/2 cup chopped onion** and **1 medium green pepper** (or zucchini). You can do this in the same pan by just pushing the meat to one side.
4. Add the sautéed vegetables to the meat, then incorporate the **1 (8 ounce) can stewed tomatoes**, **1/2 teaspoon oregano**, and **1 tablespoon steak sauce or Worcestershire sauce**.
5. Cook slowly on top of the range for **45 minutes to 1 hour**, or until the meat is tender and fork-pierceable.
6. Serve warm over rice.
