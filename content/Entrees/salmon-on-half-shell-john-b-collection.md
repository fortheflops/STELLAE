# Salmon On Half Shell

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A delightful baked potato shell filled with whipped potatoes and savory canned salmon, topped with grated cheese. Perfect for a comforting and hearty meal.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 1 hr 10 mins | None | 4 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Baking sheet
* Mixing bowl
* Potato masher

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 4 | [[Potatoes]] | Large, unpeeled |
| 1 | [[Egg]] | Beaten |
| 1/3 cup | [[Milk]] | None |
| 2 tsp | [[Onion]] | Grated |
| 3/4 tsp | [[Salt]] | None |
| 3/4 cup (1/2 lb) | [[Canned Salmon]] | With liquor |
| To taste | [[Pepper]] | Desired |
| 1/4 cup | [[Cheese]] | Grated |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Baking and Mashing Potatoes
1. Bake the **4 large potatoes** at 400 degrees for about 1 hour until tender.
2. When done, cut each one lengthwise and carefully scoop out the insides into a bowl, keeping the skins intact.
3. Mash the potato insides until smooth.

### Step 2: Mixing Filling and Refilling
4. Combine the mashed potatoes with **1 beaten egg**, **1/3 cup milk**, **2 tsp grated onion**, **3/4 tsp salt**, **3/4 cup (1/2 lb) canned salmon** with liquor, and **pepper if desired**.
5. Mix together gently until smooth and fluffy.
6. Refill each potato half with the mixture without pressing down.

### Step 3: Final Bake
7. Sprinkle over the top: **1/4 cup grated cheese**.
8. Return to oven and bake at 450 degrees until brown, about 10 minutes.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The potato shells can be stuffed ahead of time and refrigerated for up to a few hours before adding the cheese and performing the final bake.
