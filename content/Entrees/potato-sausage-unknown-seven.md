# Potato Sausage

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Seven | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A traditional homemade potato sausage recipe blending ground beef, fat pork, and equal parts potatoes seasoned with black pepper, thyme, and red chile.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 45 mins | 30 mins | None | 12 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Meat grinder
* Large mixing bowl
* Sausage stuffer

---
## Ingredients

### Sausage Mixture
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 lbs | [[Beef]] | ground |
| 2 lbs | [[Fat Pork]] | ground |
| 1 | [[Onion]] | small, finely minced or ground |
| 4 lbs | [[Potatoes]] | peeled and finely ground (equal amount to meat) |
| 1/3 cup | [[Black Pepper]] | coarse ground |
| 1/8 Tbsp | [[Thyme]] | ground |
| 1 Tbsp | [[Red Chile]] | ground or flakes |
| 3/4 cup | [[Salt]] | or to taste |

---

## Instructions

### Preparation & Assembly
1. Grind the **2 lbs beef**, **2 lbs fat pork**, and **1 small onion** together using a coarse plate on a meat grinder.
2. Grind **4 lbs potatoes** (an amount equal to the total meat weight) and thoroughly drain any excess liquid.
3. In a large mixing bowl, combine the ground meat, ground potatoes, **1/3 cup coarse ground black pepper**, **1/8 Tbsp ground thyme**, **1 Tbsp red chile**, and **3/4 cup salt**.
4. Mix everything thoroughly until the seasonings and ingredients are evenly distributed throughout the sausage mixture.
5. Stuff into prepared casings or form into patties and cook until browned and fully cooked through to an internal temperature of 160°F.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Uncooked sausage mixture can be stuffed into casings and frozen for up to 3 months.
