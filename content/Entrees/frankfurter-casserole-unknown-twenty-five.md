# Frankfurter Casserole

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Five | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A comforting and hearty mid-century casserole featuring sliced potatoes, green beans, and split weiners baked in a creamy mushroom sauce. Topped with crispy bacon and cheese for a satisfying family dinner.*

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Stovetop Preparation
1. Cook **2 slices bacon** in a skillet until crisp, then remove and reserve.
2. Cook **1/2 cup chopped onion** in the bacon drippings until tender.
3. Stir in **1 can cream of mushroom soup**, **1/2 cup water**, **1/2 tsp salt**, and **1 dash pepper**.

### Step 2: Assembly and Baking
4. Add **3 cups cooked, sliced potatoes** and **1 cup cut green beans** into the mixture and stir well.
5. Pour the mixture into a casserole dish and stand **1/2 lb split and halved weiners** around the edges.
6. Bake at 350° for **30 minutes** until bubbly and hot.
7. Top with the reserved **bacon** and **cheese cubes** if desired before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole up to a day in advance, cover, and refrigerate until ready to bake at 350° for 35 minutes.
