# Oven Baked Chicken Mozzarella

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty One | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *Discover a quick and easy recipe for oven-baked chicken smothered in rich pasta sauce and melted mozzarella cheese. Perfect for a comforting weeknight dinner served alongside your favorite pasta.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 30 mins | None | 4 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Baking dish
* Shallow bowls

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 4 | [[Chicken Breasts]] | Boneless, skinless |
| 1 | [[Egg]] | Beaten |
| 3/4 cup | [[Italian Bread Crumbs]] | Dry |
| 26-28 oz | [[Pasta Sauce]] | Marinara or preferred variety |
| 1 cup | [[Mozzarella Cheese]] | Shredded |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Bread and Bake
1. Dip each **4 chicken breasts** in the **1 beaten egg**, then coat thoroughly in **3/4 cup Italian bread crumbs**.
2. Bake at 400 degrees F for **20 minutes** until the coating is golden and crisp.

### Step 2: Sauce, Cheese, and Finish
3. Pour **26-28 oz pasta sauce** evenly over the baked chicken and top generously with **1 cup mozzarella cheese**.
4. Bake for an additional **10 minutes** until the cheese is melted, bubbly, and lightly browned.
5. Serve with **pasta** if desired.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the breaded chicken in the baking dish ahead of time and refrigerate for up to 4 hours before baking.
