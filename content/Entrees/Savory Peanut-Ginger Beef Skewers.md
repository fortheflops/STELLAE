## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep the Meat
1. Cut **1 1/2 - 2 pounds flank steak** across the grain into **1/4 inch** slices.

### Step 2: Make the Sauce
2. Sauté **1 cup onion** and **1 1/2 teaspoons garlic** in **3 tablespoons vegetable oil** until fragrant and translucent.
3. Stir in **1 cup water**, **1/4 cup brown sugar**, **3 tablespoons soy sauce**, **2 tablespoons peanut butter**, **1/2 teaspoon ground ginger**, **1/2 teaspoon lemon peel**, and **dash hot pepper**, and cook until slightly thickened and aromatic.

### Step 3: Grill the Skewers
4. Thread the sliced meat onto skewers; brush generously with the prepared sauce.
5. Grill for **8-10 minutes**, basting continuously with sauce until browned and cooked to desired doneness.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the skewers and store them covered in the refrigerator for up to 24 hours before grilling.
