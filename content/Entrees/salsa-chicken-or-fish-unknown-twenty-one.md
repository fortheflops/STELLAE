# Salsa Chicken or Fish

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty One | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A quick and flavorful glaze made with salsa, mayo, mustard, and honey, perfect for topping chicken or fish. Simply mix the ingredients, pour over your protein, and bake until done.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5 mins | 20 mins | None | 4 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Baking dish

---
## Ingredients

### Sauce Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 cup | [[Salsa]] | Standard |
| 2 tbsp | [[Mayo]] | Mayonnaise |
| 2 tbsp | [[Mustard]] | Prepared |
| 2 tbsp | [[Honey]] | Liquid |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Bake
1. In a mixing bowl, combine **1/2 cup salsa**, **2 tbsp mayo**, **2 tbsp mustard**, and **2 tbsp honey** and mix well.
2. Pour the mixture over chicken or fish in a baking dish and bake until the protein is cooked through and tender.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The sauce can be mixed a day ahead and stored in the refrigerator until ready to pour over chicken or fish.
