# Wanda's Layered Green Chile Chicken Casserole

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Five | ✍️ **Attribution:** Wanda | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A hearty, vintage layered casserole featuring tender chicken, green chiles, and corn tortillas baked in a rich, creamy soup-based sauce. Perfect for a comforting family dinner that comes together with minimal effort.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 35 mins | None | 8–10 Servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 10x13-inch baking pan
* Skillet
* Large mixing bowl

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Sauté
1. Preheat the oven to **350°F**. Grease a **10x13-inch baking pan**.
2. In a skillet over medium heat, brown the **1 chopped onion** in **2 tablespoons butter** until soft, translucent, and fragrant.

### Step 2: Mix the Sauce
3. In a large mixing bowl, dissolve the **2 chicken bouillon cubes** in **1 cup hot water**.
4. To the bowl, add the sautéed **onion**, **1 can cream of chicken soup**, **1 can cream of mushroom soup**, **1 can boned chicken**, and **1 can chopped green chile**. Mix well until thoroughly combined and creamy.

### Step 3: Assemble and Bake
5. In the prepared **10x13-inch baking pan**, create the first layer starting with **corn tortillas**, followed by a layer of the **chicken mixture**, and then **1 pound grated cheese**.
6. Repeat the layers once more, ending with a generous layer of **grated cheese** on top.
7. Bake at **350°F** for at least **35 minutes**, or until the casserole is hot and bubbly and the cheese is completely melted and golden brown around the edges.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This casserole can be assembled up to 24 hours in advance, covered, and stored in the refrigerator before baking. Leftovers keep well in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 4 days.
