# Oregano Sword Fish

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty One | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A simple and flavorful recipe for grilled swordfish seasoned with crushed oregano and black pepper. Perfect for a quick outdoor barbecue and served with fresh lemon wedges.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 10 mins | None | 4 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Pie plate
* Grill
* Tongs

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| To taste | [[Oregano]] | Crushed |
| To taste | [[Black pepper]] | Ground |
| 4 | [[Swordfish]] | 2 inches thick |
| 1 | [[Lemon]] | Cut into wedges |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and Grilling
1. Mix **crushed oregano** and **black pepper** in a pie plate.
2. Dredge the **2" thick fish** thoroughly in the spice mixture.
3. BBQ over medium-high heat, turning once, until cooked through and slightly charred on the outside.
4. Serve hot with fresh **lemon wedges**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Fish can be dredged in the seasonings up to 1 hour before grilling, kept covered in the refrigerator.
