## Instructions

### Base & Simmer
1. In a large Dutch oven or pot, melt **1/4 lb butter** over medium heat.
2. Add **2 small chopped onions**, **1 chopped green pepper**, and **3 chopped garlic cloves**; sauté until soft and fragrant.
3. Stir in **5 Tbsp flour** to coat the vegetables and create a roux base.
4. Add **2 #2 cans tomatoes**, **1 can tomato puree**, **1 cup water**, **1 pinch chili powder**, **1 dash Tabasco sauce**, **about 1 Tbsp sugar**, **1 bay leaf**, **1 dash red pepper**, and **salt and pepper**.
5. Bring the mixture to a gentle boil, then turn down the heat and simmer uncovered for 1 hour and 30 minutes, stirring occasionally until deep in color and thickened.

### Finishing the Creole
6. Add the **2 lbs shrimp** into the simmering tomato sauce for the last 30 minutes of cooking.
7. Add the **1 peeled lemon** into the pot for the last 10 minutes of cooking to infuse bright citrus flavor.
8. Remove the **1 bay leaf** and **1 peeled lemon** before serving.
9. Serve hot spooned over warm cooked white rice with additional **lemon juice** at the table.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The tomato Creole sauce base can be made up to 2 days in advance and stored covered in the refrigerator. Reheat the sauce thoroughly before adding the raw shrimp and lemon for the final 30 minutes of simmer time.
