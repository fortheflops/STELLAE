# Barbecued Beef Cutlets

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation
1. Mix together **1/2 cup tomato ketchup**, **3 tbsp prepared brown mustard**, **3 tbsp Worcestershire sauce**, **1/2 tsp salt**, and **1/4 cup water** in a bowl.
2. Dip **1 lb beef slices** into the mixture, then dip slices in **1 cup bread crumbs** to coat well.

### Step 2: Frying
3. Fry the coated cutlets in hot fat until crispy and golden brown on both sides.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Coat the cutlets up to a few hours in advance and keep refrigerated until ready to fry.
