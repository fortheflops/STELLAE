## Instructions

### Step 1: Make the Sauce
1. In a skillet, brown **1/4 lb pork sausage**, **1 lb ground beef**, **1 chopped onion**, and **1 clove garlic**. Drain excess fat.
2. Stir in **2 T. olive oil**, **1 (1-lb) can tomatoes**, **1 (6-oz) tomato paste**, **1 T. parsley**, **1 T. basil**, and **1 1/2 tsp. salt**.
3. Simmer the ingredients for **30 minutes** uncovered until slightly thickened and aromatic.

### Step 2: Prepare Cheese Mixture and Assemble
4. In a mixing bowl, mix together **3 cups cottage cheese**, **2 beaten eggs**, **2 tsp. salt**, **1/3 tsp. pepper**, **2 T. parsley flakes**, and **1/2 cup grated parmesan cheese**.
5. Layer the **10 oz lasagne noodles**, meat sauce, cheese mixture, and **1 lb mozzarella cheese** in a baking dish.

### Step 3: Bake
6. Bake at **375 degrees** for **30 minutes** until the cheese is melted, bubbly, and lightly golden brown.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the lasagne ahead of time, cover tightly, and refrigerate for up to 24 hours before baking.
