## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Broil Eggplant
1. Brush **2 med eggplant** slices with **oil** and place in broiler till lightly browned. Remove.

### Step 2: Make Meat Sauce
2. Brown **1 lb. ground lamb or beef** and **1/2 c. chopped onion**.
3. Add **8 oz. can tomato sauce**, **salt and pepper**, **2 Tablespoons parsley**, and **1 teaspoon nutmeg**.
4. Cook and mix till juice is absorbed.

### Step 3: Make Cream Sauce
5. Heat **2 cups milk**, add **2 beaten eggs**, **1/4 c. flour**, and **1/2 tsp. salt**.
6. Cook over moderate heat and stir constantly until it boils and thickens.

### Step 4: Assembly and Baking
7. Butter pan with **butter**. Sprinkle with **grated cheese** and **1 cup bread crumbs**.
8. Arrange 1/2 of **2 med eggplant** slices. Spread 1/2 of the cream sauce. Spread all meat sauce. Arrange the rest of the eggplant slices.
9. Sprinkle with **grated cheese** and **bread crumbs**. Spread the rest of the cream sauce. Sprinkle with **grated cheese**.
10. Bake 30 minutes at 350 degrees, then 15 minutes at 400 degrees until bubbly and golden brown.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole ahead of time, cover, and refrigerate for up to 24 hours before baking.
