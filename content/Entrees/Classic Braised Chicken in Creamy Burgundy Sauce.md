# Classic Braised Chicken in Creamy Burgundy Sauce

> 📜 **Collection:** General Archive | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *Experience a classic French Coq in Burgundy featuring a roasting chicken braised with ham, mushrooms, and onions. Finished with a rich cream gravy and crispy croutons, this dish offers deep, savory flavors.*

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Brown
1. Preheat your oven to **350°F (175°C)**.
2. Cut the **3 ½ lb roasting chicken** into 8 pieces and roll them thoroughly in the **2 tablespoons flour**.
3. In a large, oven-safe saucepan or Dutch oven, melt the **¼ cup butter**. Add the **3 ½ lb roasting chicken**, **½ cup diced ham**, **1 cup mushrooms**, and **10 small white onions**. Fry until the **3 ½ lb roasting chicken** is golden brown and fragrant.

### Step 2: Braise in Oven
4. Add the **1 pint (2 cups) burgundy wine**, **1 bay leaf**, **2 cloves**, **2 cloves crushed garlic**, **1 teaspoon salt**, and **1 pinch black pepper** to the saucepan.
5. Cover the pan and stew in the oven for at least **45 minutes**, or until the **3 ½ lb roasting chicken** is tender and yields easily to a fork. (Add a little water during cooking if necessary to maintain liquid levels).

### Step 3: Make Gravy and Garnish
6. When done, remove the **3 ½ lb roasting chicken**, **1 cup mushrooms**, **10 small white onions**, and **½ cup diced ham** to a warm serving dish.
7. Place the saucepan with the remaining gravy on the stovetop. Add the **1 cup cream** and bring to a boil, stirring constantly, until it reduces to a rich, smooth gravy.
8. Strain the gravy through a fine strainer, pouring it evenly over the **3 ½ lb roasting chicken**.
9. In a separate small pan, fry the **1 slice white bread, diced** in **butter** until golden brown and crisp. Garnish the dish with the resulting croutons.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftover chicken and sauce in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days. Reheat gently on the stovetop over low heat.
