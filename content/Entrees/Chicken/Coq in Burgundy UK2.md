## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
| 3½ lb               | [[Roasting Chicken]]                     |
| ½ Cup               | Diced [[Ham]]                           |
| 1 Cup               | [[Mushrooms]]                           |
| 10                  | Small [[White Onions]]                   |
| 1 Slice             | [[White Bread]], diced                  |
| ¼ Cup               | [[Butter]]                              |
| 1 Teaspoon            | [[Salt]]                                |
| 1 Pinch             | [[Black Pepper]]                        |
| 2 Tablespoons               | [[Flour]]                               |
| 1                   | [[Bay Leaf]]                            |
| 2                   | [[Cloves]]                              |
| 2 Cloves            | Crushed [[Garlic]]                    |
| 1 Pint              | [[Burgundy Wine]]                       |
| 1 Cup               | [[Cream]]                               |
___
### Instructions
1.  Cut chicken in 8 pieces & roll in flour.
2.  Fry in saucepan with butter, ham, mushrooms, white onions until golden brown.
3.  Add wine, bay leaf, cloves, crushed garlic, salt & pepper.
4.  Cover the pan & stew in moderate oven for at least 45 minutes.
5.  Add a little water during cooking if necessary.
6.  When done place chicken, mushrooms, onions & ham on serving dish.
7.  Add cream to gravy & bring to a boil, stirring constantly till reduced to a rich, smooth gravy.
8.  Strain through a fine strainer over the chicken.
9.  Fry the bread in butter till brown and garnish the dish with it.
