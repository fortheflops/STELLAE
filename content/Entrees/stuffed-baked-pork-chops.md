# Stuffed Baked Pork Chops

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *Thick-cut loin pork chops stuffed with a savory breadcrumb, onion, and celery filling, then baked until tender and golden brown. This classic comfort food dish is seasoned with sage and poultry seasoning for rich flavor.*

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 25 mins | 1 hr 30 mins | None | 4 servings |

* Mixing bowl

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 4 | [[Loin Pork Chops]] | 1 1/2 to 2" thick, unboned or meaty |
| 1 tbsp | [[Chopped Onion]] | Chopped |
| 1/4 cup | [[Diced Celery]] | Diced |
| 2 tbsp | [[Fat]] | For cooking |
| 2 cups | [[Soft Bread Crumbs]] | Use day old bread |
| 1/2 tsp | [[Salt]] | Plus extra for seasoning chops |
| 1/8 tsp | [[Pepper]] | Plus extra for seasoning chops |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Sage]] | None |
| 1/8 tsp | [[Poultry Seasoning]] | None |
| 1 | [[Beef Bouillon Cube]] | None |
| 1/2 cup | [[Hot Water]] | For dissolving bouillon |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Stuffing
1. Slit a pocket lengthwise in the side of each **4 Loin Pork Chops**.
2. Prepare stuffing by browning **1 tbsp chopped onion** and **1/4 c diced celery** in **2 tbsp fat**.
3. Dissolve **1 beef bouillon cube** in **1/2 c hot water**, then add rest of ingredients except breadcrumbs and stir to combine.
4. Add **2 c soft bread crumbs** to the mixture and stir well.

### Step 2: Baking
5. Stuff each chop with **1/4 of this mixture**, then season the outside of the chops with **salt & pepper**.
6. Place in a baking dish, cover, and bake in moderate oven at 350 degrees for 1 hour.
7. Uncover and continue baking for 30 minutes until golden brown and tender.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Chops can be stuffed a few hours ahead and kept covered in the refrigerator until ready to bake.
