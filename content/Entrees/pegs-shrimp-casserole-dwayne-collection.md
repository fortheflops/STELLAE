# Peg's Shrimp Casserole

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Peg | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A classic, comforting seafood casserole featuring tender shrimp, rice, sautéed aromatics, and Swiss cheese bound in cream of mushroom soup. Baked to perfection with a light dusting of paprika.*

---

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 35 mins | None | 6 servings |

## Instructions

### Preparation & Baking
1. Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C) and grease a baking casserole dish.
2. In a skillet, sauté the **1 cup chopped green pepper**, **4 tbsp chopped celery**, **4 tbsp chopped onion**, and **1 clove minced garlic** in a portion of the **4 tbsp melted butter** until softened and fragrant.
3. In a large mixing bowl, combine the sautéed vegetables with the **2 1/2 lbs shrimp**, **3 cups cooked rice**, **2 tsp snipped parsley**, **1/2 cup grated Swiss cheese**, **1/2 tsp salt**, **1 tsp black pepper**, **2 cans mushroom soup**, and **juice of 1 large lemon**.
4. Transfer the mixture into the prepared casserole dish. Drizzle with the remaining **4 tbsp melted butter** and sprinkle **paprika** evenly on top.
5. Bake at 350°F for approximately 35 minutes, until hot, bubbly, and cooked through.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole ahead of time, cover tightly, and refrigerate for up to 24 hours before baking.
