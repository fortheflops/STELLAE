# Tofu Burgers

> 📜 **Collection:** Patsy Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A wholesome and savory vegetarian burger recipe featuring firm tofu, grated zucchini, and a blend of aromatic spices. These pan-seared patties offer a hearty and nutritious meal option.*

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 15 mins | None | 4 Servings |

* Sauté pan
* Box grater
* Mixing bowl
* Spatula

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Sauté and Prep
1. Sauté the **1 large onion** in a pan until translucent and fragrant.
2. Grate the **1 large zucchini** using a box grater.

### Step 2: Mix the Burger Base
3. In a large mixing bowl, combine the **1 lb firm tofu**, sautéed **1 large onion**, grated **1 large zucchini**, **whole wheat flour**, **garlic**, **3 egg whites**, **1 egg yolk**, **tamari**, **fresh parsley**, **black pepper**, **cumin**, **½ cup wheat germ**, and **nutritional yeast** until a cohesive mixture forms.

### Step 3: Cook the Burgers
4. Plop the mixture, keeping it not too thick, onto a pan on low heat and cook until golden brown and firm on both sides.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Uncooked patties can be formed and stored in the refrigerator for up to 2 days or frozen between layers of parchment paper for up to 1 month.
