# Emily Pineda's Green Chile Chicken Enchilada Casserole

> 📜 **Collection:** General Archive | ✍️ **Attribution:** Emily Pineda | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A comforting, layered chicken enchilada casserole featuring tender chicken, cheese, and onions in a creamy green chile and mushroom soup sauce. Layered with lightly fried tortilla quarters and baked to bubbly perfection.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 45 mins | None | 6–8 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Casserole dish
* Large mixing bowl
* Saucepan
* Skillet
* Paper towels
* Whisk

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2–3 Cups | [[Chicken]] | cooked and shredded |
| 2 Cans | [[Green Chile]] | None |
| 1 ½ Cups | [[Cheese]] | grated, plus more for topping |
| 1 Can | [[Mushroom Soup]] | None |
| ¼ Cup | [[Onion]] | chopped |
| 1 pkg | [[Tortillas]] | cut into quarters |
| 1 ½ Cups | [[Chicken Broth]] | None |
| As needed | [[Fat]] | for frying |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Filling and Sauce
1. Preheat oven to **350°F** and grease a casserole dish. In a large bowl, combine the **2–3 cups cooked chicken**, **2 cans green chile**, **1/4 cup chopped onion**, and **1 1/2 cups grated cheese**.
2. In a saucepan, heat **1 1/2 cups chicken broth** and **1 can mushroom soup**. Whisk and mix well until the sauce is completely smooth, hot, and steaming.
3. Pour the hot soup mixture into the bowl with the **chicken** and **cheese** mixture, blending thoroughly until well combined.

### Step 2: Fry Tortillas and Assemble
4. Cut **1 package tortillas** into quarters. In a separate skillet, quickly fry the tortilla quarters in **hot fat** for just a few seconds until lightly crisped but still pliable. Drain them on paper towels.
5. In the greased casserole dish, create alternate layers of the fried **tortillas** and the **chicken and sauce mixture**.
6. Top the final layer generously with additional **grated cheese**.

### Step 3: Bake
7. Cover the dish tightly with foil and bake at **350°F** for **40 minutes**.
8. Uncover and bake for the final **5 minutes** until the **cheese** is completely melted, bubbly, and golden brown around the edges.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This casserole can be assembled a day in advance, covered, and stored in the refrigerator. Bake as directed, adding 5 to 10 minutes to the covered baking time if baking straight from the fridge.
