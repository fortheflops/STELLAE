# Beef Stroganoff

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A classic savory beef stroganoff featuring tender round steak browned in butter, simmered with onions, garlic, and beef broth, and finished with a rich sour cream sauce. Perfect for serving over your favorite pasta or noodles for a comforting family dinner.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 45 mins | None | 4–6 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Skillet or heavy-bottomed pan
* Wooden spoon
* Measuring cups and spoons

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1½ lb | [[Round Steak]] | Cubed & Rolled in [[Flour]] |
| 2 Tbsp | [[Butter]] | Unsalted or salted |
| ½ cup | [[Onion]] | Chopped |
| 1 Clove | [[Garlic]] | Minced |
| To taste | [[Salt]] | As needed |
| To taste | [[Pepper]] | As needed |
| 1 Can | [[Beef Broth]] | Or 2 [[Bouillon Cubes]] dissolved in water |
| 1 cup | [[Sour Cream]] | Room temperature recommended |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Brown the Beef
1. Melt the **2 Tbsp butter** in a skillet over medium-high heat. Add the **1½ lb round steak** (cubed and rolled in flour) and brown well on all sides until nicely seared and golden.

### Step 2: Simmer the Sauce
2. Add the **½ cup chopped onion**, **1 clove minced garlic**, **salt**, **pepper**, and **1 can beef broth or 2 bouillon cubes** to the skillet. Bring the mixture to a gentle bubble, then cover and simmer until the meat is fork-tender.

### Step 3: Finish with Sour Cream
3. Stir in the **1 cup sour cream** and simmer gently for **5 minutes** until heated through and thickened into a rich, creamy sauce.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftovers in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days; reheat gently over low heat without boiling to prevent the sour cream from separating.
