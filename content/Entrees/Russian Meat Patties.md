## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Mixture and Patties
1. Grind the **1/2 pound beef**, **1/2 pound veal**, and **1/2 pound pork** together.
2. Add the **1 egg** yolk (reserving the white), the **1 cup bread crumbs** that have been soaked in milk, the grated **1 onion**, **salt**, and **pepper**, then mix thoroughly.
3. Form the meat mixture into individual patties.

### Step 2: Coat and Sauté
4. Beat the reserved **1 egg** white until frothy.
5. Dip the patties first in the beaten egg white, then roll them in the dry **bread crumbs** to coat evenly.
6. Sauté the coated patties in **butter or fat** in a skillet until golden brown and cooked through.

### Step 3: Finish and Serve
7. When the patties are brown, add the **1/2 cup sour cream** to the skillet and simmer only until the cream is hot.
8. Serve immediately with new potatoes.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Formed uncooked patties can be refrigerated for up to 24 hours before breading and cooking.
