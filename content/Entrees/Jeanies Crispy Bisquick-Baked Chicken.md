# Jeanie's Crispy Bisquick-Baked Chicken

> 📜 **Collection:** GailCollection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Jeanie | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A classic oven-fried chicken recipe from Jeanie featuring cut-up chicken seasoned with herbs, coated in Bisquick, and baked in a rich margarine and oil mixture until crispy and golden brown.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Season
1. Preheat your oven to **350°F**.
2. Place **1/2 cup margarine** and **1/2 cup oil** into a baking dish and place it in the hot oven until melted.
3. Sprinkle all sides of the **1 whole chicken** pieces with **salt**, **pepper**, **rosemary**, **oregano**, and **garlic salt** to taste.

### Step 2: Coat and Bake
4. Place **Bisquick baking mix** inside a bag, add the seasoned **chicken**, and shake to coat evenly.
5. Place the coated **chicken** pieces into the hot baking dish with the melted **1/2 cup margarine** and **1/2 cup oil**, skin-side down.
6. Bake at **350°F** for about **30 minutes**.

### Step 3: Finish and Serve
7. Turn the **chicken** pieces over so they are skin-side up.
8. Continue to bake for another **30 minutes**, or until the **chicken** is tender, cooked through, and golden brown.
9. If the **chicken** is browning too quickly, cover it loosely with aluminum foil for the remainder of the cooking time.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftover baked chicken in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3-4 days.
