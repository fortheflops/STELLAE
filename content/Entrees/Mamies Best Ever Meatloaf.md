# Mamie's Best Ever Meatloaf

> 📜 **Collection:** UK15 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A classic, comforting double-loaf meatloaf recipe featuring a savory blend of ground beef and sausage, topped with bacon and ketchup, served with a rich homemade pan-juice sauce.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 25 mins | 1 hour | 10 mins | 8–10 Servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large mixing bowl
* Two 8"x4" loaf pans
* Saucepan
* Wire whisk
* Warm serving platter

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Mix
1. Preheat the oven to **400°F**.
2. In a large bowl, soak the **1 1/2 cups bread pieces** in the **1 cup evaporated milk** until soft and fluffy.
3. Add the **2 large eggs**, **2 1/2 pounds ground beef**, **1/2 pound sausage**, **1/2 cup carrot**, **1/2 cup parsley**, **1 teaspoon garlic salt**, **1 teaspoon marjoram**, **1/2 teaspoon thyme**, **1/2 teaspoon basil**, **1/2 teaspoon Italian seasoning**, and **1/2 teaspoon black pepper**. Mix well to combine.

### Step 2: Bake the Meatloaves
4. Divide the mixture evenly between two **8"x4" loaf pans**.
5. Top each loaf with **1 strip bacon** and **2 tablespoons ketchup**.
6. Place the pans in the preheated oven and immediately lower the temperature to **325°F**.
7. Bake for **1 hour** until fully cooked through and browned.
8. Remove the meatloaves from the pans and place them on a warm platter. Let them stand for **10 minutes** before slicing.

### Step 3: Make the Sauce
9. To make the sauce, pour the pan juices into a saucepan.
10. Whisk in the **2 tablespoons flour** until smooth.
11. Add the **1 (10.75 ounce) can beef broth** and bring the sauce to a simmer while whisking.
12. Continue to cook until the sauce heats and thickens to a rich, smooth consistency. Thin with a little water if needed.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Leftover meatloaf can be stored in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 to 4 days.
