# Creamy Eggplant and Crab Casserole

> 📜 **Collection:** General Archive | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A unique and savory casserole featuring mashed eggplant and crabmeat baked in a rich, creamy shrimp and cheddar cheese sauce. Topped with crushed croutons, this golden-brown dish makes a comforting and flavorful entree.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 50 mins | 10 mins | 4 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 3-quart saucepan
* Casserole dish
* Large mixing bowl
* Small mixing bowl
* Whisk

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 medium | [[Eggplant]] | Peel and cubed |
| 1 1/2 Cups | [[Water]] | Divided for soaking and boiling |
| 1/3 Cup | [[Onion]] | Chopped |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Celery]] | Chopped |
| 1 can (10.5 oz) | [[Shrimp Soup]] | Cream of shrimp |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Milk]] | Room temperature |
| 1/3 Cup | [[Bell Pepper]] | Chopped |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Cheddar Cheese]] | Grated |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Croutons]] | Crushed |
| 1 tsp | [[Salt]] | Standard |
| 6 sticks | [[Imitation Crab Sticks]] | Or 2 small cans of crabmeat, drained |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Boil
1. Preheat oven to **350°F (175°C)**. Grease a casserole dish.
2. Peel and cube the **[[Eggplant]]**. Place it in a bowl of cold water for **10 minutes** to prevent browning.
3. Drain the **[[Eggplant]]**. In a saucepan, combine the cubed **[[Eggplant]]**, chopped **[[Onion]]**, and **[[Celery]]** with **1 1/2 cups of water**. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer until the vegetables are tender. Drain all water very well.

### Step 2: Mix and Bake
4. In a large bowl, mash the cooked vegetable mixture.
5. In a separate small bowl, whisk the **[[Shrimp Soup]]** and **[[Milk]]** together until smooth, then pour this into the mashed eggplant mixture.
6. Stir in the chopped **[[Bell Pepper]]**, grated **[[Cheddar Cheese]]**, crushed **[[Croutons]]**, and **[[Salt]]** until fully combined.
7. Slice the **[[Imitation Crab Sticks]]** into 1/2-inch pieces (or drain the canned **[[Crabmeat]]**) and gently fold them into the mixture.
8. Pour the entire mixture into the greased casserole dish and bake uncovered for **35 minutes**, or until hot, bubbly, and slightly golden brown.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole up to a day in advance, cover tightly, and store in the refrigerator until ready to bake.
