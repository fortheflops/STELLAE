# Tex's Hearty Argentinean Beef & Hominy Skillet

> *A flavorful one-skillet meal featuring ground beef, kidney beans, and hominy sautéed with onions, peppers, and savory herbs.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Brown the Beef
1. In a large heavy skillet over medium heat, brown the **1 lb lean ground beef**, stirring to break up until no longer pink.

### Step 2: Sauté Vegetables
2. Add the **3 med. onions** and **1 large green pepper** to the skillet. Sauté, stirring occasionally, just until the vegetables are tender.

### Step 3: Combine Ingredients
3. Stir in the **1 t salt**, **1 t basil**, **1/2 t ground pepper**, **1/4 t sugar**, **1/4 t oregano**, **1/8 t crushed red pepper**, **3 large cloves garlic**, **2 T chopped parsley**, **1 sm. can (8 oz.) red kidney beans**, and **1 sm. can (8 oz.) golden hominy**.

### Step 4: Simmer
4. Simmer uncovered, gradually adding about **1/2 C reserved bean liquid** and stirring occasionally, for **10 minutes** or until the flavors are fully blended and rich. (If using fresh basil, stir in just before serving and heat through).

### Step 5: Garnish and Serve
5. Garnish with **fresh parsley sprigs** and serve warm.
