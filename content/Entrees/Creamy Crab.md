# Creamy Crab

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *Indulge in this vintage Creamy Crab recipe featuring tender crab meat simmered in a rich sherry and cream sauce. Perfect for serving over toasted points or fragrant saffron rice.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 15 mins | None | 4-6 Servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Saucepan
* Wooden spoon
* Chafing dish

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 Cans | [[Crab Meat]] | drained |
| 1/4 Cup | [[Flour]] | unrefined |
| 1/4 Cup | Dry [[Sherry]] | optional |
| 1 1/4 Cup | Light [[Cream]] | room temperature |
| 1/4 Cup | [[Butter]] | unsalted |
| Several drops | [[Hot Sauce]] | to taste |
| 2 Tbsp | Sliced [[Green Onions]] | fresh |
| 1/8 tsp | [[Pepper]] | ground |
| 2 Tbsp | Chopped [[Green Pepper]] | minced |
| 1/4 Cup | Sliced Stuffed [[Olives]] | pimento-stuffed |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Sauté
1. Place **2 cans crab meat** in a mixing bowl and sprinkle with **1/4 cup dry sherry (opt.)**.
2. Melt **1/4 cup butter** in a saucepan over medium heat.
3. Add **2 tbsp sliced green onions** and **2 tbsp chopped green pepper**, then sauté until tender and fragrant.

### Step 2: Create the Sauce
4. Add **1/4 cup flour** to the vegetable mixture, blending thoroughly until smooth.
5. Gradually pour in **1 1/4 cup light cream**, stirring continuously until completely smooth.
6. Cook over medium heat, stirring constantly until the mixture is smooth and thickened.

### Step 3: Combine and Simmer
7. Remove the saucepan from the heat.
8. Stir in **several drops hot sauce**, **1/8 tsp pepper**, the prepared **2 cans crab meat**, and **1/4 cup sliced stuffed olives**.
9. Transfer the mixture to a chafing dish and simmer for **5 minutes** until heated through.
10. Serve warm over toast points or saffron rice.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftover creamy crab in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 2 days, reheating gently on the stovetop.
