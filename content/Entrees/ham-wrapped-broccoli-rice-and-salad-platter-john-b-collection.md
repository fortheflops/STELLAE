# Ham Wrapped Broccoli Rice And Salad Platter

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A classic party platter featuring ham slices wrapped around broccoli-rice with melted cheese, served alongside a colorful array of fruit salad, olives, muffins, and strawberry shortcake. This comforting retro combination makes for a complete and visually appealing spread.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 30 mins | 15 mins | None | 6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Baking dish
* Mixing bowl
* Saucepan

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 6 slices | [[Ham]] | Sliced |
| 2 cups | [[Broccoli]] | Chopped |
| 1 cup | [[Rice]] | Cooked |
| 1/2 cup | [[Cheese]] | Melted |
| 1 | [[Pineapple]] | Sliced |
| 1 | [[Peach]] | Fresh |
| 1 | [[Pear]] | Fresh |
| 1 | [[Apricot]] | Fresh |
| 1/2 cup | [[Cherries]] | Pitted |
| 1/2 cup | [[Olives]] | Whole |
| 6 | [[Muffins]] | Baked |
| 1 | [[Strawberry Shortcake]] | Prepared |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Assembly
1. Wrap each **6 slices ham** around the cooked **2 cups broccoli** and **1 cup rice** mixture.
2. Top with **1/2 cup cheese** and melt until bubbly and golden.
3. Serve alongside the **1 pineapple**, **1 peach**, **1 pear**, **1 apricot**, **1/2 cup cherries**, **1/2 cup olives**, **6 muffins**, and **1 strawberry shortcake** on a large platter.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the ham and broccoli rolls ahead of time and refrigerate until ready to melt the cheese and serve.
