# Sautéeed Chicken

> 📜 **Collection:** Burcea Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A classic savory recipe featuring tender chicken sautéed with bacon, mushrooms, onions, and green peppers in a rich gravy. Perfect for serving alongside warm potatoes for a hearty family dinner.*

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3 1/2 to 4 lbs | [[Chicken]] | Cut in pieces, browned |
| 2 tbsp | [[Butter]] | None |
| 5 slices | [[Bacon]] | Cut in pieces, browned |
| 1/2 lb | [[Fresh Mushrooms]] | Alternative: 1 can mushrooms |
| 1 | [[Onion]] | Sliced |
| 1/4 | [[Green Pepper]] | Sliced |

### Gravy Paste
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 cup | [[Cold Water]] | None |
| 2 tbsp | [[Flour]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Browning the Meats and Vegetables
1. Brown the **3 1/2 to 4 lbs chicken** in **2 tbsp butter**.
2. Brown the **5 slices bacon** cut in pieces.
3. Sauté **1/2 lb fresh mushrooms** (or **1 can mushrooms**), **1 onion** (sliced), and **1/4 green pepper** (sliced) until they reach a golden brown color. Season to taste.

### Step 2: Simmering
4. Mix **1 cup cold water** and **2 tbsp flour** together to make a paste.
5. Put the **chicken** in a covered skillet and cover with the other ingredients.
6. Pour the paste in, add a little extra water, cover, and simmer slowly for **1 1/2 hours** until tender and aromatic.
7. Serve warm with potatoes.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Can be prepared up to a day ahead and gently reheated on the stove over low heat before serving.
