# Baked Hen And Spaghetti

> *A comforting baked casserole featuring tender hen, spaghetti, and a rich vegetable tomato sauce. Perfect for a hearty family dinner.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Cook Chicken
1. Cook the **3 lb. hen** in **3 cups chicken stock** until real tender so you can pull it off the bone and cut it up.

### Step 2: Pasta and Assembly
2. Boil the **1 small package spaghetti** for ten minutes and drain (it isn't necessary to break, but if you do, don't break into small pieces).
3. Mix **1 onion**, **1 bunch celery**, **1 bell pepper**, **1 T. Worcestershire sauce**, **1 small can mushrooms**, **salt & pepper to taste**, and **pimentos for color** together with the cooked chicken and spaghetti.

### Step 3: Baking
4. Bake everything together in a baking dish in a moderate oven about 350° for about 1 hour (maybe longer), stirring two or three times while baking until piping hot and bubbling.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole in the baking dish ahead of time, cover, and refrigerate for up to 24 hours before baking.
