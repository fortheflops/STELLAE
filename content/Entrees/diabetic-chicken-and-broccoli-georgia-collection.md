# Diabetic Chicken and Broccoli

> 📜 **Collection:** Georgia Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A comforting and hearty casserole featuring tender chicken, broccoli, and mushrooms topped with stuffing and cheese. Perfect for a balanced family dinner.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 45 mins | None | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 9x13 casserole dish
* Mixing bowl
* Oven

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 pkgs. | [[Broccoli]] | Cooked to tender |
| 12 oz. | [[Boned chicken]] | Cooked and ready |
| 4 oz. can | [[Mushrooms]] | Drained |
| 2 cans | [[Cream of mushroom soup]] | Condensed |
| 1/2 pkg. | [[Stuffing cubes]] | Dry |
| As needed | [[Chicken broth]] | For moistening dressing |
| To taste | [[Shredded cheese]] | For topping |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Assembly
1. Place the **2 pkgs. broccoli** in a **9x13 casserole** dish.
2. Top with the **12 oz. boned chicken** and **4 oz. can mushrooms**.
3. Cover the mixture evenly with the **2 cans cream of mushroom soup**.
4. Moisten the **1/2 pkg. stuffing cubes** with **chicken broth** and put them on top of the casserole.
5. Add a generous layer of **shredded cheese** over the stuffing.

### Step 2: Baking
6. Bake uncovered at 350°F for **30 to 45 minutes** until the cheese is melted, bubbly, and golden brown on top.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole up to a day in advance, cover, and refrigerate until ready to bake; add a few extra minutes to the baking time if baking cold.
