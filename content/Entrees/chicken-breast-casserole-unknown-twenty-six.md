# Chicken Breast Casserole

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Marcile | 📂 **Category:** Entrees
> *A comforting baked chicken casserole featuring layers of skinned and boned chicken breasts, Swiss cheese, and creamy celery soup with a touch of vermouth. Topped with crushed garlic and onion croutons, this hearty dish is baked until bubbly and golden.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 1 hour | None | 6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 9x13 baking dish
* Mixing bowl

---
## Ingredients

### Casserole Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| As needed | [[Chicken breast]] | Skinned and boned |
| To taste | [[Salt]] | None |
| To taste | [[Pepper]] | None |
| Approx. 3/4 lb | [[Swiss cheese]] | Sliced |
| 2 cans | [[Celery soup]] | None |
| 3/4 cup | [[Vermouth]] | None |
| 1 1/2 boxes | [[Croutons]] | Garlic & onion flavor, crushed |
| As needed | [[Butter]] | Dotted on top |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Assembly
1. Preheat your oven to 350°F and prepare a **9x13** baking dish.
2. Arrange the **skinned and boned chicken breast** in the bottom of the baking dish.
3. Season the chicken with **salt** and **pepper** to taste.
4. Layer approximately **3/4 lb Swiss cheese** over the chicken.
5. In a bowl, mix the **2 cans celery soup** with the **3/4 cup vermouth**, then pour this mixture evenly over the cheese.
6. Scatter the **1 1/2 boxes dry crouton (garlic & onion)** (crushed) on top of the casserole and spot with pieces of **butter**.

### Step 2: Baking
7. Bake at 350°F for **1 hour** until bubbling and golden brown on top with melted cheese.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole a few hours in advance, cover, and refrigerate until ready to bake.
