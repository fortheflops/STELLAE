# Classic Coconut Layer Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A delightful from-scratch, three-layer white butter cake made with beaten egg whites for a light texture. This makes a tender, delicate base for any classic coconut cake.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 25 mins | 35 mins | 10 mins | 1 3-layer 9-inch cake |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Three 9-inch round cake pans
* Medium bowl
* Large bowl
* Clean bowl
* Wire rack
* Electric mixer

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 cup | [[Butter]] | Softened |
| 2 cups | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 4 | [[Egg Yolks]] | Large |
| 4 | [[Egg Whites]] | Large |
| 1 1/2 teaspoons | [[Vanilla]] | Extract |
| 2 2/3 cups | [[Cake Flour]] | Sifted |
| 1/2 teaspoon | [[Baking Powder]] | Sifted |
| 1/2 teaspoon | [[Salt]] | Sifted for batter |
| 1/4 teaspoon | [[Salt]] | For egg whites |
| 1 cup | [[Milk]] | Room temperature |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and Dry Ingredients
1. Preheat oven to **350°F**. Grease and flour three **9-inch round cake pans**.
2. In a separate bowl, sift together the **2 2/3 cups cake flour**, **1/2 teaspoon baking powder**, and **1/2 teaspoon salt**. Set aside.

### Step 2: Mixing the Batter
3. In a large bowl, cream the **1 cup butter**. Gradually add the **2 cups sugar** and beat until the mixture is light and fluffy.
4. Add the **4 egg yolks** one at a time, beating well after each addition. Stir in the **1 1/2 teaspoons vanilla**.
5. Add the dry ingredients to the creamed mixture alternately with the **1 cup milk**, beginning and ending with the flour mixture. Mix on low speed until just combined.

### Step 3: Whipping and Folding Egg Whites
6. In a separate, clean bowl, beat the **4 egg whites** with the **1/4 teaspoon salt** until stiff, glossy peaks form.
7. Gently fold the beaten egg whites into the cake batter in three additions until no white streaks remain.

### Step 4: Baking and Cooling
8. Divide the batter evenly among the three prepared pans.
9. Bake for **30 to 35 minutes**, or until a toothpick inserted into the center of the cakes comes out clean.
10. Cool in the pans on a wire rack for **10 minutes** before inverting them to cool completely until ready to frost.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the cooled cake layers wrapped tightly in plastic wrap at room temperature for up to 2 days, or freeze for up to 1 month before frosting.
