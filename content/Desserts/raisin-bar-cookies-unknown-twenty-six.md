# Raisin Bar Cookies

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Bessie | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *Delicious old-fashioned raisin bar cookies baked in a sheet pan and topped with sweet frosting. These tender cake-like treats are easy to slice and perfect for sharing.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 25 mins | None | 24 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Saucepan
* Mixing bowls
* Jelly roll pans

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 cup | [[Raisins]] | Simmered |
| 1 cup | [[Water]] | None |
| 1 1/2 tsp | [[Baking soda]] | None |
| 1 1/2 cups | [[Sugar]] | None |
| 1 cup | [[Shortening]] | None |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] | Beaten well |
| 3 cups | [[Flour]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Simmer Raisins
1. Simmer **1/2 cup raisins** and **1 cup water** for 10 minutes, then add **1 1/2 tsp baking soda** and set aside.

### Step 2: Mix Dough
2. Cream together **1 1/2 cups sugar** and **1 cup shortening**.
3. Add **2 eggs**, beaten well, to the creamed mixture.
4. Combine both mixtures and add **3 cups flour**, mixing well.

### Step 3: Bake and Frost
5. Pour into 2 greased jelly roll pans or cookie sheets, ensuring the batter is spread thin so the cookies will look like pieces of cake.
6. Bake at 325 degrees F for 20 to 30 minutes until golden and cooked through.
7. When cool, frost cookies and cut into squares.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store baked and frosted bars in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 3 days.
