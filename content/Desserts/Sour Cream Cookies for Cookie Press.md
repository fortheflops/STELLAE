# Sour Cream Cookies for Cookie Press

> 📜 **Collection:** General Archive | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic, large-batch recipe for tender and tangy cookie press spritz cookies. This dough is enriched with sour cream and warmly spiced with nutmeg, creating dozens of festive treats.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 12 mins | None | 10 dozen cookies |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* electric mixer
* large mixing bowl
* cookie press
* cookie sheet

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |

| 1 Cup | [[Sugar]] | granulated |
| 2 | [[Egg Yolks]] | beaten |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Sour Cream]] | thick |
| 1 tsp | [[Vanilla]] | extract |
| 4 Cups | [[Flour]] | sifted all-purpose |
| 1 tsp | [[Nutmeg]] | ground |
| 1/2 tsp | [[Baking Soda]] | none |
| 1/2 tsp | [[Salt]] | none |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Dough
1. Preheat your oven to 375°F.
2. In a large bowl, cream the **1 cup shortening** and **1 cup sugar** together with an electric mixer until well combined.
3. Beat in the **2 egg yolks**, **1/2 cup sour cream**, and **1 tsp vanilla**.
4. In a separate bowl, sift together the **4 cups flour**, **1/2 tsp salt**, **1 tsp nutmeg**, and **1/2 tsp baking soda**.
5. Gradually add the dry ingredients to the creamed mixture, mixing on low speed until a soft dough forms.

### Step 2: Press and Bake
6. Fill your cookie press with the dough and fit it with your desired disk.
7. Press the cookies onto an ungreased cookie sheet.
8. Bake for **10-12 minutes** until the edges are set but not browned.
9. Let the cookies cool on the baking sheet for a minute before transferring them to a wire rack to cool completely.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The dough can be prepared in advance and refrigerated for up to 2 days; bring to room temperature before using in the cookie press.
