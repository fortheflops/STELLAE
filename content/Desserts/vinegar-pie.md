# Vinegar Pie

## Ingredients

### Pie Filling
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 cup | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 1/2 cup | [[Molasses]] | Standard |
| 1/4 cup | [[Vinegar]] | White or cider |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] | Beaten |
| 1/2 cup | [[Milk]] | Whole |
| 1 tbsp | [[Butter]] | Melted |
| 2 tbsp | [[Cracker Crumbs]] | Fine |
| 1 | [[Pie Crust]] | Unbaked |

---

## Instructions

### Preparation
1. Combine the **1 cup sugar**, **1/2 cup molasses**, **2 eggs**, **1/2 cup milk**, **1 tbsp butter**, and **2 tbsp cracker crumbs** in a large mixing bowl.
2. Stir in the **1/4 cup vinegar** last, mixing until the batter is smooth and well-incorporated.
3. Pour the mixture into the **1 pie crust**.
4. Bake until the center is set and the crust is golden brown, indicating the pie is done.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The filling can be mixed ahead of time, but pour into the crust and bake immediately before serving for the best texture.
