# Mississippi Mud Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Jan Batchelor | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A rich, decadent chocolate cake layered with gooey marshmallow creme and a velvety chocolate fudge icing. This classic southern dessert develops its best flavor and texture after resting for a full day.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare and Bake the Cake
1. Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C).
2. Cream together **2 cups sugar** and **1 cup vegetable shortening** in a large bowl until light and fluffy.
3. Add **4 eggs** to the creamed mixture and beat thoroughly.
4. Sift together **1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour**, **1/3 cup cocoa powder**, and **1/2 teaspoon salt**.
5. Add the sifted dry ingredients to the creamed mixture and mix until just combined.
6. Gently fold in **3 teaspoons vanilla extract** and **1 cup chopped nuts**.
7. Pour the batter into a greased 9x13-inch baking pan and bake at 350°F for **30 minutes** until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.

### Step 2: Apply Toppings and Icing
8. Immediately after removing the cake from the oven, spread **1 (9 oz) jar marshmallow creme** evenly over the hot cake.
9. In a separate bowl, combine **2 sticks melted butter**, **1 lb powdered sugar**, **1/2 cup cocoa powder**, **1/2 cup evaporated milk**, and **1 teaspoon vanilla extract**. Mix until smooth and pourable.
10. Pour this chocolate icing mixture over the marshmallow layer on the cake, spreading gently to cover completely if needed.
11. Sprinkle the top generously with additional **chopped nuts**.
12. Let the cake stand at room temperature for at least **24 hours** before serving to allow the layers to set and the flavors to fully develop.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This cake must be made ahead. Let it stand at room temperature for at least 24 hours before slicing to allow the marshmallow and fudge layers to set properly.
