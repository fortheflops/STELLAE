# Cream Caramel

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic hand-written recipe for creamy caramel candy made with sugar, Karo syrup, and rich cream. Cooked to the hard-ball stage and beaten until set before pouring into a loaf pan.*

## Instructions

### Cooking and Mixing
1. Combine **3 1/2 cups sugar**, **1 cup Karo**, and **2 cups cream** (or use the alternate large batch proportions) in a heavy saucepan.
2. Cook the mixture to the hard-ball stage until a small amount dropped into cold water forms a stiff, pliable ball.
3. Remove from heat, let cool slightly, add nuts if desired, and beat vigorously until thickened and smooth.
4. Pour the mixture into a prepared loaf pan and allow to cool completely until set before slicing.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container with parchment paper between layers at room temperature for up to 1 week.
