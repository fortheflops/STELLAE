# Peach Cobbler

> 📜 **Collection:** Burcea Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A delightful homemade peach cobbler featuring a buttery crust and fresh fruit. Baked to golden perfection with a warm touch of cinnamon.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 45 mins | None | 6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Baking dish
* Mixing bowl
* Measuring cups
* Oven

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 stick | [[Butter]] | Melted |
| As needed | [[Peaches]] | Cut up |
| 1 cup | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 1 cup | [[Flour]] | All-purpose |
| 1 cup | [[Milk]] | Whole or 2% |
| To taste | [[Cinnamon]] | For sprinkling |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and Assembly
1. Melt **1 stick butter** directly in a baking dish.
2. Put in the cut up **[[Peaches]]** over the melted butter.
3. In a mixing bowl, combine **1 cup sugar**, **1 cup flour**, and **1 cup milk**.
4. Pour the batter mixture evenly over the peaches.
5. Sprinkle the top with **cinnamon** to taste.

### Step 2: Baking
6. Bake at 350 degrees until the top is golden brown and bubbling.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the cobbler just before baking for the best texture and crispness.
