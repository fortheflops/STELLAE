## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Bake the Cake
1. Preheat your oven to **350°F**. Grease and flour three **9-inch round cake pans**.
2. In a large bowl, cream together the **½ cup butter** and **1 cup sugar** until light and fluffy. Beat in the **4 egg yolks** one at a time, then stir in the **½ cup ribbon cane syrup**.
3. In a separate bowl, whisk together the **2 cups flour**, **½ tsp baking soda**, **1 tsp cinnamon**, **2 tsp nutmeg**, **1 tsp cloves**, and **1 tsp allspice**.
4. Alternately add the dry ingredients and the **⅔ cup buttermilk** to the creamed mixture, beginning and ending with the dry ingredients. Mix until just combined.
5. By hand, stir in the **1 cup raisins**, **1 cup nuts**, **1 large chopped apples**, **1 cup coconut**, and **½ cup drained crushed pineapple**.
6. Divide the batter evenly among the prepared pans.
7. Bake for **30-35 minutes**, or until a wooden skewer inserted into the center comes out clean and the edges pull slightly away from the pan.
8. Let the cakes cool in the pans for **10 minutes** before inverting them onto a wire rack to cool completely.

### Step 2: Make the Filling and Assemble
9. In a saucepan, combine all the filling ingredients: **2 cups sugar**, **1 cup milk**, **2 tbsp butter**, **1 cup nuts**, **1 cup dates**, **1 cup coconut**, and **½ cup drained crushed pineapple**.
10. Cook over medium heat, stirring constantly, until the mixture thickens to a spreading consistency and appears glossy.
11. Once the cake layers are completely cool, spread the warm filling between the layers and on top of the cake until evenly coated.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the assembled cake in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 2 days or refrigerate for up to 5 days.
