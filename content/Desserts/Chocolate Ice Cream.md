# Chocolate Ice Cream

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A simple, three-ingredient no-cook chocolate ice cream that delivers a rich, creamy texture. This classic recipe is perfect for quick preparation using a standard ice cream maker.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5 mins | 25 mins | 4 hours | 1 Gallon |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large mixing bowl
* Wire whisk
* 1-gallon ice cream maker
* Freezer-safe container

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 Gallon | [[Chocolate Milk]] | Cold |
| 1 (9-12 Ounce) Carton | [[Refrigerated Whipped Cream Topping]] | Thawed |
| 1 Can | [[Condensed Milk]] | Sweetened |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Combine Base
1. In a large bowl, whisk together the **1/2 gallon chocolate milk** and **1 can condensed milk** until the mixture is completely smooth and uniform.

### Step 2: Incorporate Topping
2. Gently fold in the **1 carton refrigerated whipped cream topping** until just combined, being careful not to deflate the mixture.

### Step 3: Churn and Freeze
3. Pour the mixture into the canister of a **1-gallon ice cream maker** and churn according to the manufacturer's directions until thick and frozen.
4. Transfer the ice cream to a freezer-safe container and freeze for at least **4 hours** to ripen until firm and scoopable.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This ice cream can be stored in a freezer-safe container for up to 2 weeks; for best texture, let it sit at room temperature for 5 minutes before scooping.
