# Pecan Pie

> 📜 **Collection:** Elizabeth Knee Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic Southern pecan pie featuring a rich, caramel-like filling made with dark brown sugar and light corn syrup, topped with beautiful pecan halves. Baked to golden perfection in shallow nine-inch pie shells.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Boil
1. Preheat oven to **350°F**.
2. In a saucepan, combine **1 cup dark brown sugar** and **1 3/4 cups light corn syrup**. Bring to a boil and boil exactly **2 minutes**.

### Step 2: Mix Filling
3. In a mixing bowl, beat **4 eggs** until they are light and lemon-colored.
4. Slowly pour the hot syrup mixture into the beaten **4 eggs** while beating constantly. Mix well.
5. Add **2 tablespoons butter**, **1 teaspoon vanilla**, and **2 cups pecan pieces** to the mixture and mix thoroughly.

### Step 3: Assemble and Bake
6. Pour the filling evenly into the **two 9-inch pie shells**.
7. Lay **pecan halves** decoratively on top of the filling.
8. Bake at **350°F** for **35 minutes** until the filling is set with a slight, gelatinous jiggle in the center and the crust is golden brown.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Can be baked up to 1 day in advance. Store covered at room temperature or in the refrigerator.
