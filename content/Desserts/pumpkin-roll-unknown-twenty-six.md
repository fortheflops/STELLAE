## Instructions

### Step 1: Making the Batter
1. Beat **3 eggs** for **5 mins**. Gradually add **1 cup sugar**.
2. Then add everything except **1/2 cup nuts** and mix well.

### Step 2: Baking
3. Line a cookie sheet (jelly roll pan, **15 1/2 x 10**) with **wax paper**. Grease and flour top of wax paper.
4. Pour batter, sprinkle top with **1/2 cup nuts** (push nuts into batter).
5. Bake at **350** for **15 mins** until golden and springy to the touch.

### Step 3: Rolling and Filling
6. As soon as done, sprinkle **powdered sugar** on dish towel. Turn cake onto towel.
7. Roll it up like a jelly roll. Let cool.
8. Then unroll and spread with filling (mix filling in mixer and spread on roll).
9. Re-roll and chill. Can be frozen. (Nuts to outside).

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Can be frozen after rolling. Thaw slightly before slicing and serving.
