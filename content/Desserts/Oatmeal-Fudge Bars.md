## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Base
1. Preheat oven to 350°F and grease a 9x9 inch pan.
2. In a medium bowl, beat **1/2 cup shortening or butter** and **1 cup brown sugar** until fluffy. Beat in **1 egg** and **1/2 teaspoon vanilla**.
3. Whisk together **3/4 cup flour**, **1/2 teaspoon soda**, and **1/2 teaspoon salt**. Add to the creamed mixture.
4. Stir in **2 cups oats** and **1/2 cup walnuts**. Reserve **1 cup** of this mixture, then press the remainder into the pan.

### Step 2: Prepare the Fudge Filling
5. In a small saucepan over low heat, combine **6 ounces chocolate pieces**, **1 tablespoon butter or margarine**, **1/3 cup milk**, and **1/4 teaspoon salt**.
6. Stir frequently until the mixture is melted and smooth. Remove from heat and stir in **1/2 cup walnuts** and **1 teaspoon vanilla**.

### Step 3: Assemble and Bake
7. Spread the fudge mixture over the oatmeal crust. Crumble the reserved **1 cup oat mixture** over the top.
8. Bake for **25 minutes** until the surface is lightly browned. Cool completely on a wire rack before cutting.
