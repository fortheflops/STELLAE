# Cheese Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Seven | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic homemade baked cheesecake flavored with lemon juice, vanilla, and orange extract over a graham cracker crust. Topped with fresh, sweet strawberries for a light fruity finish.*

## Instructions

### Baking & Assembly
1. Preheat oven to 350°F.
2. Prepare the **1 graham cracker crust** in your baking dish.
3. In a mixing bowl, beat together **2 (8 oz) packages cream cheese**, **4 eggs**, **2 tbsp lemon juice**, **1 tsp vanilla extract**, **1/2 tsp orange extract**, **3/4 cup sugar**, **2 tbsp flour**, and **3/4 tsp salt** until smooth and fully incorporated.
4. Pour the cheesecake batter into the prepared crust.
5. Bake at 350°F for **40 minutes** or until the center is set.
6. Allow to cool completely, then top with **1-2 pints strawberries** before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Chill the baked cheesecake in the refrigerator for at least 4 hours or overnight before adding the fresh strawberry topping.
