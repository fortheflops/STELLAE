## Recipe
| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
| 3 Bars (3 Ounces Each) | [[Triangular Shaped Chocolate (Toblerone)]]                                   |
| 1/2 Cup            | [[Heavy Cream]]                                                                |
| 2 Tablespoons      | [[Brandy, Cointreau, Coffee Liqueur or Peppermint Liqueur, or 1 Teaspoon Instant Coffee]] |
|                    | [[Bite-Size Cubes of Sponge or Angel Food Cake]]                               |
|                    | [[Marshmallows]]                                                               |
|                    | [[Grapes]]                                                                     |
|                    | [[Pineapple Chunks]]                                                           |
|                    | [[Dried Apricots]]                                                             |
|                    | [[Banana Chunks, Etc.]]                                                        |
___
### Instructions
1. Break chocolate into pieces. Combine with cream in a small saucepan over low heat on the range or over a warmer.
2. Stir until melted & smooth.
3. Stir in brandy. Keep warm.
4. Spear pieces of cake or fruit on forks & dip. (Serves 8)
