# Cherry Ginger Ale Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic and refreshing molded Jello salad featuring cherry gelatin, fruit cocktail, and the fizzy, subtle spice of ginger ale for a delightful twist. This vintage dessert is perfect for festive gatherings and light, sweet finishes.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 5 mins | 4 hours | 6 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Medium mixing bowl
* 1-quart mold
* Wire whisk

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 package (3 oz) | [[Cherry Flavored Gelatin]] | powdered |
| 1 cup | [[Boiling Water]] | hot |
| 7 oz | [[Ginger Ale]] | chilled |
| 2 Tbsp | [[Lime Juice]] | fresh |
| 1 lb 2 oz | [[Fruit Cocktail]] | drained well |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Base
1. In a medium bowl, combine the **1 package (3 oz) cherry flavored gelatin** and **1 cup boiling water**, stirring until the powder is completely dissolved.
2. Allow the mixture to cool to room temperature.

### Step 2: Incorporate and Chill
3. Gently stir in the **7 oz ginger ale** and **2 Tbsp lime juice**.
4. Place the bowl in the refrigerator and chill until the mixture thickens to the consistency of unbeaten egg whites.
5. Fold in the **1 lb 2 oz fruit cocktail** and pour the mixture into a 1-quart mold.
6. Chill for at least 4 hours, or until the gelatin is completely firm and set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This salad must be made at least 4 hours in advance to set properly; keep refrigerated until ready to serve.
