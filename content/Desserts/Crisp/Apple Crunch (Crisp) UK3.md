## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
| 1/2-3/4 Cup             | [[Sugar]]              |
| 1 Teaspoon             | [[Cinnamon]]         |
| 4 Cups                   | [[Jonathan Apples]], Peeled & Sliced          |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Sugar]] |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Butter]]          |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Flour]]          |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Uncooked Oatmeal]]          |

___
### Instructions
1. Combine sugar & cinnamon with apples & place in greased 9x9 pan.
2. Blend together with pastry blender the 1/2 Cup sugar, 1/2 Cup flour, 1/2 Cup butter & 1/2 Cup oatmeal until the mixture resembles small peas.
3. Sprinkle on apples.
4. Bake at 350° for 30-45 min. or until apples are tender & top is lightly browned.
5. Serve hot with ice cream/whipped cream.
