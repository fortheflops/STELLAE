## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 1/2 cup                    | [[Butter]]                                |
| 1/2 cup                    | [[Sugar]]                                 |
| 1/2 cup                    | [[Brown Sugar]]                           |
| 1 cup                      | [[Flour]]                                 |
| 2 1/2 cups                 | [[Apples]], sliced and drained            |
| 1/2 teaspoon               | [[Cinnamon]]                              |

___
## Instructions
1.  Preheat the oven to 375°F.
2.  Place the drained, sliced apples in a deep baking dish and sprinkle with the 1/2 cup of white sugar.
3.  In a separate bowl, combine the dry ingredients for the topping: brown sugar, flour, and cinnamon.
4.  Cut in the butter with a pastry blender or your fingers until the mixture is crumbly.
5.  Sprinkle the crumbly topping evenly over the apples.
6.  Bake for 45 minutes, or until the apples are done and the topping is crunchy.
