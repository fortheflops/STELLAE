## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
| 1 Can (or 3 Cups)             | [[Apples]]                  |
| 2 Teaspoon          | [[Cinnamon]]                 |
| 1 Cup             | [[White Sugar]]              |
| 1 Tablespoon          | [[Flour]]                    |
| 1/4 Teaspoon         | [[Salt]]                     |
### Topping
| Measurements      | Ingredients             |
| :----------------- | :---------------------- |
| 1 Cup             | [[Nuts]]                |
| 1 Stick           | [[Butter]]              |
| 3/4 Cup           | [[Brown Sugar]]         |
| 1/4 Cup           | [[Flour]]               |
| 1/4 Teaspoon      | [[Baking Powder]]         |
| 1/4 Teaspoon      | [[Baking Soda]]         |
___
### Instructions
1. Mix together apples, cinnamon, white sugar, flour, and salt.
2. **Topping:** Combine nuts, butter, brown sugar, flour, baking powder, and baking soda.
3.  Bake in 350 degrees Fahrenheit oven for 30 to 40 minutes.
