## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| As Needed                  | [[Apples]], sliced                        |
| As Needed                  | [[Butter]], cut into pats                 |
| 1 box                      | [[Jiffy Yellow Cake Mix]]                 |
___
## Instructions
1.  Preheat the oven to 350°F.
2.  Place the sliced apples in a baking dish.
3.  Scatter pats of butter over the top of the apples.
4.  Sprinkle the dry Jiffy yellow cake mix evenly over the top.
5.  Bake for around 25 minutes, or until the topping is golden and the apples are tender.
