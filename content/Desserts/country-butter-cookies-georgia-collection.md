# Country Butter Cookies

> 📜 **Collection:** Georgia Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A delightful historic recipe for country butter cookies featuring simple pantry staples. Perfect for baking up a sweet treat with a comforting homemade touch.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 15 mins | 30 mins | 24 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Baking sheet

---
## Ingredients

### Dough Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 cup | [[Butter]] | or oleo |
| 3/4 cup | [[Lite B Sugar]] | finely packed |
| 1 | [[Egg]] | beaten |
| 1 tsp | [[Vanilla]] | extract |
| 2 cups | [[Flour]] | sifted |
| 1/2 tsp | [[B.P.]] | baking powder |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Salt]] | fine |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing
1. Mix **1/2 cup butter** or oleo and **3/4 cup lite b sugar** until fluffy.
2. Beat in **1 egg** and **1 tsp vanilla** until well incorporated.
3. Combine dry ingredients including **2 cups flour**, **1/2 tsp b.p.**, and **1/4 tsp salt**, then mix into the butter mixture.

### Step 2: Chilling and Baking
4. Form into a shape and refrigerate for 30 minutes until firm.
5. Cut or roll the dough as desired.
6. Bake at 350 degrees F for 8 to 15 minutes until lightly golden.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The dough can be formed and refrigerated for up to 24 hours before cutting and baking.
