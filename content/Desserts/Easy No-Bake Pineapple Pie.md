# Easy No-Bake Pineapple Pie

> 📜 **Collection:** General Archive | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A wonderfully simple no-bake pie with a magic filling. The dry Dream Whip powder acts as a stabilizer, creating a firm, creamy, and sweet pineapple cream cheese filling as it chills.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | None | 4 hours | One 9-inch pie |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Medium bowl
* Electric mixer
* 9-inch pie pan

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | [[Vanilla Wafer Crust]] | or pre-baked crust, 9-inch |
| 1 small can (8 oz) | [[Pineapple]] | crushed, well-drained |
| 3/4 cup | [[Sugar]] | granulated |
| 1 (8 oz) package | [[Cream Cheese]] | softened to room temperature |
| 1 envelope | [[Dream Whip]] | dry, unwhipped powder from a 2-pouch box |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mix the Filling
1. In a medium bowl, beat the softened **8 oz package cream cheese** until smooth and creamy.
2. Add the well-drained **small can crushed pineapple** and the **3/4 cup sugar**. Mix until thoroughly combined.

### Step 2: Add Stabilizer and Chill
3. Sprinkle the **1 envelope dry Dream Whip powder** over the mixture and fold it in until everything is evenly incorporated.
4. Pour the filling into the prepared **9-inch vanilla wafer crust** and spread evenly.
5. Refrigerate for at least **4 hours**, or until the pie is completely firm and set, before slicing and serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store covered in the refrigerator for up to 3 days.
