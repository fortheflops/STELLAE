# Apple Dumplings

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A comforting traditional baked apple dumpling recipe featuring a flaky homemade crust and a sweet syrup poured over the top before baking. Perfect for a cozy dessert after a hearty meal.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 30 mins | None | 4 servings |

* Mixing bowl

## Ingredients

### Crust
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3/4 cup | [[Sweet milk]] | Liquid |
| 2 cups | [[Flour]] | Sifted |
| 4 tsp. | [[Baking powder]] | Leavening |
| 1 pinch | [[Salt]] | To taste |
| 3 T. | [[Lard]] | For shortening |

### Syrup & Filling
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 1/2 cups | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 3 t. | [[Butter]] | Softened |
| 2 cups | [[Water]] | Fresh |
| As needed | [[Apples]] | Boiled, peeled, and sliced |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Making the Crust and Prep
1. Combine **2 cups flour**, **4 tsp. baking powder**, **salt**, **3 T. lard**, and **3/4 cup sweet milk** to form the crust.
2. Boil, peel, and slice the **apples**.

### Step 2: Syrup and Baking
3. Boil together **1 1/2 cups sugar**, **3 t. butter**, and **2 cups water** to create a syrup, then pour over the dumplings.
4. Bake for **30 min.** until golden brown and bubbly.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the dumplings in the baking dish ahead of time, but pour the hot syrup mixture right before baking.
