# Old-Fashioned Buttermilk Marbled Pound Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** UK15 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Denny White | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic, tender buttermilk pound cake with a rich chocolate marble swirl, flavored with vanilla and almond extracts. This traditional recipe yields a dense, flavorful cake with a beautiful crumb.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 25 mins | 1 hour | 15 mins | 1 10-inch tube cake |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 10-inch tube pan
* Large bowl
* Small bowl
* Electric mixer
* Microwave-safe bowl or small saucepan
* Wire rack

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 cup | [[Butter]] | softened |
| 1/2 cup | [[Shortening]] | divided |
| 2 cups | [[Sugar]] | granulated |
| 4 large | [[Eggs]] | room temperature |
| 3 cups | [[Flour]] | all-purpose |
| 1/2 teaspoon | [[Soda]] | baking soda |
| 1 cup | [[Buttermilk]] | cold |
| 1/8 teaspoon | [[Salt]] | fine |
| 2 teaspoons | [[Vanilla Extract]] | pure |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Almond Extract]] | pure |

### For Marble
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 ounce | [[Chocolate]] | unsweetened, 1 square |
| 1 tablespoon | [[Shortening]] | for melting |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Chocolate Mixture
1. Preheat the oven to **350°F**. Grease and flour a **10-inch tube pan**.
2. In a small saucepan or microwave-safe bowl, melt the **1 tablespoon shortening** and the **1 ounce unsweetened chocolate** together until smooth, then set aside to cool.

### Step 2: Batter Preparation
3. In a large bowl, cream the **1/2 cup butter** and **1/2 cup shortening** together. Gradually add the **2 cups sugar**, beating well with an electric mixer until light and fluffy.
4. Add the **4 large eggs** one at a time, beating thoroughly after each addition.
5. In a small bowl, dissolve the **1/2 teaspoon baking soda** in the **1 cup buttermilk**. In another bowl, combine the **3 cups flour** and **1/8 teaspoon salt**.
6. Add the flour mixture to the creamed mixture alternately with the buttermilk, beginning and ending with the flour. Mix on low speed until just blended.
7. Stir in the **2 teaspoons vanilla extract** and **1 teaspoon almond extract**.

### Step 3: Assembly and Baking
8. Remove **2 cups** of the plain batter and place it in a separate bowl. Stir the cooled chocolate mixture into this portion until thoroughly blended.
9. Spoon one-third of the remaining plain batter into the prepared tube pan, top with half of the chocolate batter, and repeat layers, ending with the plain batter on top.
10. Gently swirl a knife through the batters once or twice to create a marble effect until you see a distinct pattern without overmixing.
11. Bake for **1 hour**, or until a wooden pick inserted in the center comes out clean and the top springs back lightly to the touch.
12. Let the cake cool in the pan for **10-15 minutes** before turning it out onto a wire rack to cool completely.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the cooled pound cake tightly wrapped in plastic wrap or kept in a cake keeper at room temperature for up to 4 days.
