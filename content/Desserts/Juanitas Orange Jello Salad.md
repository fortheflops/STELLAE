# Juanita's Orange Jello Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Juanita | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic fruit-filled Jello salad featuring mandarin oranges, pineapple, and pecans in an orange Jello base with a fun marshmallow layer. This nostalgic dessert is perfect for potlucks and family gatherings.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 5 mins | 6 hours | 8-10 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large mixing bowl
* Measuring cup
* Whisk
* Serving dish

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 6 oz | [[Orange Jello]] | 1 large package |
| 15 oz | [[Mandarin Oranges]] | 1 can, drained |
| 15 oz | [[Pineapple]] | 1 can, crushed, drained |
| 1 Cup | [[Pecans]] | chopped |
| 2-3 Cups | [[Marshmallows]] | tiny |
| To taste | [[Whipped Cream]] or [[Cool Whip]] | for topping |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Base
1. Drain the juice from the **15 oz mandarin oranges** and **15 oz crushed pineapple** into a measuring cup. Add enough cold water to the juice to equal **2 cups** of liquid.
2. In a large bowl, dissolve the **6 oz orange jello** in **2 cups** of boiling water, stirring for **2 minutes** until fully dissolved.
3. Stir the **2 cups** of reserved cold juice/water mixture into the hot **orange jello**.

### Step 2: Chill and Assemble
4. Chill the **orange jello** in the refrigerator until it is partially set, reaching the consistency of unbeaten egg whites.
5. Gently fold the drained **15 oz mandarin oranges**, **15 oz crushed pineapple**, and **1 cup pecans** into the partially set **orange jello**.
6. Pour the mixture into a large serving bowl. Sprinkle the **2-3 cups tiny marshmallows** over the top; they will float to create a top layer.
7. Refrigerate for at least **4-6 hours**, or until completely firm to the touch.
8. Just before serving, top with **whipped cream** or **cool whip**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This salad can be made up to 24 hours in advance; keep refrigerated until ready to serve.
