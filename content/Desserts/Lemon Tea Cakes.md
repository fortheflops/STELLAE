# Lemon Tea Cakes

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare Batter
1. Preheat oven to **350°F**. In a small bowl, stir **1/2 teaspoon vinegar** into **1/2 cup milk** to make 'sour milk' and set aside.
2. In a separate bowl, whisk together the **1 3/4 cups flour**, **1 teaspoon baking powder**, and **1/4 teaspoon salt**.
3. In a large bowl, cream the **1/2 cup butter** and **3/4 cup sugar** until fluffy. Add the **1 large egg** and **1 tablespoon lemon peel**; beat well.
4. Alternately add the dry ingredients and the soured milk to the creamed mixture, beating until smooth after each addition.

### Step 2: Bake and Glaze
5. Drop the dough by rounded teaspoons **2 inches** apart onto an ungreased cookie sheet.
6. Bake for **12 to 14 minutes**, or until the edges are lightly golden and set.
7. While the tea cakes bake, prepare the glaze by mixing the **3/4 cup sugar** and **1/4 cup lemon juice**.
8. As soon as you remove the tea cakes from the oven, immediately brush the tops with the lemon glaze. Let cool on the cookie sheet for a minute before transferring to a wire rack.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store baked tea cakes in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 3 days.
