# Ting-a-Lings

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic, crunchy no-bake treat featuring chow mein noodles and salted peanuts coated in rich melted semi-sweet chocolate. These nostalgic, sweet-and-salty clusters are incredibly easy to whip up and perfect for holiday cookie trays.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 5 mins | 30 mins | 36 pieces |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Double boiler
* Cookie sheet
* Wax paper
* Teaspoon

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 (6 oz) package | [[Semi-Sweet Chocolate Pieces]] |  |
| 1/2 cup | [[Peanuts]] | canned, salted |
| 1 (3 oz) can | [[Chow Mein Noodles]] |  |

---

## Instructions

### Preparation
1. In a **double boiler** over hot, not boiling water, melt **1 (6 oz) package semi-sweet chocolate pieces** until completely smooth and glossy.
2. Remove from heat, then gently stir in **1/2 cup canned salted peanuts** and **1 (3 oz) can chow mein noodles** until they are well coated with chocolate.
3. Drop the mixture by heaping teaspoonfuls onto a **wax paper**-lined **cookie sheet**.
4. Refrigerate until firm and set. If they begin to soften at room temperature, return them to the refrigerator again until firm.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 2 weeks. Keep chilled until ready to serve to prevent softening.
