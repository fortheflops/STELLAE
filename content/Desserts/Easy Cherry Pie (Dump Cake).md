# Easy Cherry Pie (Dump Cake)

> 📜 **Collection:** GailCollection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *An incredibly simple 4-ingredient dessert featuring a sweet cherry pie filling base and a buttery, crunchy cake mix and walnut topping.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5 mins | 45 mins | None | 6-8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 8x8 or 9x9 inch square pan
* Oven

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 can (21 oz) | [[Cherry Pie Filling]] | Standard size |
| 1 1/2 cups | [[Cake Mix]] | White cake mix or 1 box Jiffy cake mix, dry |
| 1 stick (1/2 cup) | [[Butter]] | Melted |
| 1/2 cup | [[Walnuts]] | Chopped, or to taste |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Layer
1. Preheat oven to **350°F** (**175°C**). Grease a square pan (e.g., **8x8 or 9x9 inch**).
2. Spread the **one can cherry pie filling** evenly in the bottom of the pan.
3. Sprinkle the dry **1 1/2 cups white cake mix** evenly over the cherry filling.
4. Sprinkle the **1/2 cup chopped walnuts** over the top of the cake mix.

### Step 2: Bake
5. Drizzle the **1 stick melted butter** as evenly as possible over the entire surface.
6. Bake for **45 minutes**, or until the topping is golden brown and the filling is bubbly. Let cool slightly before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftover cake covered in the refrigerator for up to 3 days.
