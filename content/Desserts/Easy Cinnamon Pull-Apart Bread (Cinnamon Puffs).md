# Easy Cinnamon Pull-Apart Bread (Cinnamon Puffs)

> 📜 **Collection:** General Archive | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic and incredibly easy pull-apart bread made with canned biscuits coated in cinnamon-sugar and baked in a rich buttery glaze. Perfect for a sweet breakfast treat or comforting dessert.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 30 mins | 5 mins | 10-12 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Bundt pan
* Medium bowl
* Large plastic bag
* Knife
* Serving platter

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3/4 cup | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 2 teaspoons | [[Cinnamon]] | Ground |
| 2 cans | [[Biscuits]] | 10 count each, refrigerated |
| 1 stick (1/2 cup) | [[Butter]] or [[Margarine]] | Melted |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation
1. Preheat your oven to **350°F**. Generously grease a **Bundt pan**.

### Step 2: Coating the Biscuits
2. In a medium bowl or a large plastic bag, combine the **3/4 cup sugar** and **2 teaspoons cinnamon**.
3. Separate the **2 cans biscuits** and cut each biscuit into quarters.
4. Toss the biscuit pieces in the cinnamon-sugar mixture until loosely coated, then arrange the coated pieces evenly in the prepared **Bundt pan**.

### Step 3: Baking and Serving
5. Combine the **1 stick (1/2 cup) butter or margarine** with any remaining cinnamon-sugar mixture and pour it evenly over the biscuits in the pan.
6. Bake for **30 minutes**, or until the top is golden brown and the biscuits are cooked through.
7. Let the pan cool for about **5 minutes**, then place a serving platter over the pan and carefully invert it until golden brown and fragrant. Serve warm.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Best served warm immediately after baking, but leftovers can be stored in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 2 days.
