## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Mix Cake
1. Preheat oven to **350°** and prepare two **8-inch pans**.
2. Combine **2 1/4 cups flour**, **1 1/2 cups shortening**, **1 1/2 cups white sugar**, **2 eggs**, **2 oz red food coloring**, **1 tbsp vanilla**, **2 tbsp cocoa**, and **1 tsp salt**.
3. In a separate small container, mix **1 cup buttermilk**, **1 tsp soda**, and **1 tbsp vinegar** together, let sit aside, then incorporate into the batter.

### Step 2: Bake Cake
4. Pour batter into the prepared pans and bake at **350°** for **30 minutes**, or until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean and the edges pull slightly away from the pan.

### Step 3: Make Frosting
5. In a saucepan, cook **1 cup milk** and **3 tbsp flour** while stirring constantly until thick, then set aside to cool.
6. In a separate bowl, cream **1 cup white sugar**, **1 cup cold butter**, and mix a lot.
7. Mix all the frosting components together until smooth and fluffy, then spread over the cooled cake.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Bake the cake layers a day in advance, wrap tightly, and store at room temperature before frosting.
