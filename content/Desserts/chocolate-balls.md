# Chocolate Balls

> *Delightful chilled chocolate balls coated in rich cocoa. A classic sweet treat that requires resting overnight.*

### Finishing
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and Cooking
1. Mix together **1 egg yolk**, **1/2 C. sugar**, and **1 rounding tbsp flour**.
2. Heat **2/3 C. milk** and add slowly to the above mixture.
3. Cook like a white sauce until thickened, stirring constantly.
4. Add **1 cake melted sweet chocolate** and stir until smooth.

### Step 2: Chilling and Shaping
5. Set the mixture away until the next day to firm up completely.
6. Shape the chilled mixture into balls.
7. Roll each ball in **ground cocoa** until fully coated.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The chocolate mixture must be refrigerated overnight before shaping into balls.
