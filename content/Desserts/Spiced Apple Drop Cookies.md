# Spiced Apple Drop Cookies

> *A classic recipe for soft, cake-like apple drop cookies. This old-fashioned treat is loaded with warm spices and packed with chopped apples, raisins, and nuts.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and Creaming
1. Preheat your oven to 400°F and grease your cookie sheets.
2. In a large bowl, cream the **1/4 cup soft butter** and **2/3 cup brown sugar** together with an electric mixer. Beat in the **1 large egg** and **1/4 cup apple juice or cider** until well combined.

### Step 2: Mixing and Baking
3. In a separate medium bowl, sift together the **2 cups flour**, **1 teaspoon baking soda**, **1/2 teaspoon salt**, **1 teaspoon cinnamon**, **1/2 teaspoon cloves**, **1/2 teaspoon allspice**, and **1/2 teaspoon nutmeg**.
4. Gradually add the dry ingredients to the creamed mixture, mixing on low speed until just combined.
5. By hand, stir in the **1 cup chopped apple**, **1 cup raisins**, and **1 cup chopped nuts**.
6. Drop the dough by rounded teaspoonfuls onto the prepared cookie sheets.
7. Bake for **8-10 minutes**, or until the cookies are set and the edges are lightly browned and fragrant.
