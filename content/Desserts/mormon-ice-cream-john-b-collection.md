# Mormon Ice Cream

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A rich and creamy homemade ice cream recipe featuring a cooked milk and flour base thickened to perfection. Churned to a smooth frozen consistency in a standard ice cream freezer.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 30 mins | 15 mins | 4 hours | 12 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Saucepan
* Whisk
* Mixing bowl
* 6-quart ice cream freezer

---
## Ingredients

### Ice Cream Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 qts. | [[Milk]] | Cold |
| 1 cup | [[Flour]] | None |
| 4 to 6 | [[Eggs]] | None |
| 3 cups | [[Sugar]] | None |
| 1 tsp. | [[Salt]] | None |
| 2 tbsp. | [[Vanilla]] | None |
| 1 tsp. | [[Lemon extract]] | None |
| 1 qt. | [[Heavy cream]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Cooking the Base
1. Combine a small amount of cold **2 qts. milk** with **1 cup flour** and stir smooth.
2. Heat remaining **milk** & stir flour mixture into it, stirring & cooking until slightly thickened.

### Step 2: Mixing and Freezing
3. Beat **4 to 6 eggs** & **3 cups sugar** together, then stir into hot **milk** mixture.
4. Cool, and add **1 tsp. salt**, **2 tbsp. vanilla**, **1 tsp. lemon extract**, & **1 qt. heavy cream**.
5. Freeze in 6-quart freezer until firmly set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the prepared ice cream in an airtight container in the freezer for up to 2 weeks.
