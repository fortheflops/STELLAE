# Mom's Marble Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** JillCollection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic, tender marble cake made with Swansdown cake flour, featuring a vanilla-scented white batter swirled with a rich chocolate mixture. Light and fluffy, it is finished with folded egg whites for the perfect crumb.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 25 mins | 55 mins | None | 1 loaf or 2 8-inch layers |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Loaf pan or two 8-inch round layer pans
* Small saucepan
* Large bowl
* Separate mixing bowl
* Wooden skewer

## Ingredients

### For the White Cake Batter
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3/4 cup | [[Butter]] | softened |
| 2 cups | [[Sugar]] | divided |
| 4 | [[Eggs]] | separated |
| 3 cups | [[Swansdown Cake Flour]] | sifted |
| 4 teaspoons | [[Baking Powder]] | None |
| 1/2 teaspoon | [[Salt]] | None |
| 1 cup | [[Milk]] | None |

### For the Chocolate Mixture
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3 oz (3 squares) | [[Unsweetened Chocolate]] | None |
| 1/4 cup | [[Water]] | boiling |
| 4 teaspoons | [[Sugar]] | None |
| 1/2 teaspoon | [[Baking Soda]] | None |

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Chocolate Mixture
1. Preheat your oven to **350°F**. Grease and flour your chosen pan(s) thoroughly.
2. In a small saucepan over low heat, melt the **3 oz (3 squares) unsweetened chocolate**. Remove from heat and stir in the **1/4 cup water**, **4 teaspoons sugar**, and **1/2 teaspoon baking soda**. Mix until smooth and set aside to cool completely.

### Step 2: White Cake Batter
3. In a large bowl, cream the **3/4 cup butter**. Gradually add **2 cups sugar**, beating until light and fluffy. Beat in the **4 eggs** yolks one at a time.
4. In a separate bowl, sift together the **3 cups Swansdown cake flour**, **4 teaspoons baking powder**, and **1/2 teaspoon salt**.
5. Add the flour mixture to the creamed butter mixture in parts, alternating with the **1 cup milk**. Stir in the **1/2 teaspoon vanilla extract**.
6. In another clean bowl, beat the egg whites until stiff peaks form. Gently fold the beaten egg whites into the batter until just incorporated.

### Step 3: Assemble and Bake
7. Divide the white cake batter equally into two bowls. Gently stir the cooled chocolate mixture into one half of the batter until fully blended.
8. Drop alternating spoonfuls of the white and chocolate batters into the prepared pan(s).
9. To create the marbled effect, run a knife through the batters once or twice in a swirling motion until just patterned.
10. Bake for **50 to 55 minutes** for a loaf pan, or about **30 minutes** for two layer pans, until a wooden skewer inserted into the center comes out clean and the cake is fragrant.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the cooled cake wrapped tightly in plastic wrap at room temperature for up to 3 days, or freeze for up to 2 months.
