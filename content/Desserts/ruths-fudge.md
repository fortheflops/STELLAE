# Ruth's Fudge

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Ruth | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic homemade fudge recipe featuring rich chocolate, marshmallow cream, and nuts. Perfect for the holidays or any sweet craving.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 10 mins | 2 hours | 24 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Saucepan
* Greased pan
* Wooden spoon

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 4 1/2 cups | [[Sugar]] | None |
| 1 large can | [[Pet Milk]] | None |
| 1/3 lb | [[Margarine]] | None |
| 1 jar | [[Marshmallow Cream]] | None |
| 3 pkgs | [[Chocolate Bits]] | 1/3 lb dot choc |
| 1 lb | [[Nuts]] | Chopped |
| 1 tsp | [[Vanilla]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Boiling
1. Mix **4 1/2 cups sugar**, **1 large can pet milk**, and **1/3 lb margarine** in a saucepan. Bring to a boil, stirring constantly, boil for **7 minutes**.

### Step 2: Mixing and Pouring
2. Remove from heat, add **1 jar marshmallow cream**, **3 pkgs chocolate bits**, **1 lb nuts**, and **1 tsp vanilla**. Mix until completely blended, then pour into a greased pan and let set until firm.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature or in the refrigerator for up to two weeks.
