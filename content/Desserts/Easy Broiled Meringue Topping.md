# Easy Broiled Meringue Topping

> 📜 **Collection:** Jill Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A quick and simple recipe for a classic meringue pie topping that's toasted to golden-brown perfection in minutes under the broiler. This airy, glossy topping is the perfect finish for any fruit or cream pie.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 2 mins | None | Topping for one 9-inch pie |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Electric mixer
* Mixing bowl
* Broiler

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 4 | [[Egg Whites]] | at room temperature |
| 1/2 teaspoon | [[Cream of Tartar]] | none |
| 4 tablespoons | [[Sugar]] | none |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Meringue
1. In a clean, dry bowl, beat the **4 egg whites** until foamy.
2. Add the **1/2 teaspoon cream of tartar** and continue beating until soft peaks form.
3. Gradually add the **4 tablespoons sugar**, one tablespoon at a time, beating continuously until the meringue is stiff, glossy, and the sugar has dissolved.

### Step 2: Broil and Serve
4. Spread the meringue over a prepared pie, ensuring it touches the crust on all sides to prevent shrinking.
5. Place the pie under the preheated broiler and toast for 1-2 minutes, watching constantly until the peaks are golden brown and fragrant.
6. Remove from the oven immediately and let cool before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Meringue is best served immediately after broiling as it can weep or lose volume if stored for long periods.
