# Mocka Tort

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Batter
1. Separate the **6 eggs** into yolks and whites.
2. Beat the **egg yolks** until thick and pale, then add **1 cup sugar** and **1 dsp mocha essence**.
3. In a separate clean bowl, beat the **egg whites** until stiff peaks form, then gently fold them into the yolk mixture.
4. When partly folded in, gently fold in the sifted **1 cup flour** and **1 tsp baking powder** until just combined and no flour pockets remain.

### Step 2: Bake and Assemble
5. Pour the batter into greased layer cake pans and bake in a **350° oven** until the layers are golden brown and spring back lightly when touched in the center.
6. Allow the layers to cool completely before frosting.
7. Frost with **whipped cream** or **rum butter icing** and decorate with **almonds**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The cake layers can be baked a day in advance, wrapped tightly in plastic wrap, and stored at room temperature. Assemble with whipped cream shortly before serving.
