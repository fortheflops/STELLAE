# Chocolate Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Anna Merritt | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic, rich chocolate cake recipe from Anna Merritt featuring a moist crumb and three large round layers. Perfect for celebrations or a decadent sweet treat.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 25 mins | 30 mins | None | 12 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Electric mixer
* Three round cake pans

---
## Ingredients

### Cake Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 cube | [[Butter]] | Softened |
| 1 c | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 1 c | [[Brown Sugar]] | Packed |
| 2 squares | [[Chocolate]] | Melted |
| 3 | [[Eggs]] | Whole |
| 2 1/2 c | [[Cake Flour]] | Sifted |
| 2 1/2 t | [[Baking Powder]] | Standard |
| 1 1/4 c | [[Milk]] | Room temperature |
| 1 1/2 t | [[Vanilla]] | Extract |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and Mixing
1. Preheat your oven to 350° and grease three large round cake pans.
2. Cream together the **1 cube butter**, **1 c sugar**, and **1 c brown sugar** until light and fluffy.
3. Beat in the **3 eggs** one at a time, then thoroughly mix in the **2 squares chocolate**.
4. Alternate adding the **2 1/2 c cake flour** and **2 1/2 t baking powder** with the **1 1/4 c milk**, mixing just until smooth.
5. Stir in the **1 1/2 t vanilla**.

### Step 2: Baking
6. Divide the batter evenly among the three prepared round cake pans.
7. Bake at 350° for 30 minutes, or until a wooden toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean and the cakes spring back lightly to the touch.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Baked cake layers can be wrapped tightly in plastic wrap and stored at room temperature for up to 2 days, or frozen for up to 1 month before frosting.
