# Pistachio Pineapple Delight

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic, super-easy recipe for a fluffy dessert salad. This no-bake potluck staple combines pistachio pudding, pineapple, marshmallows, and nuts in a creamy Cool Whip base.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 0 mins | 1 hour | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* large mixing bowl
* silicone spatula

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 8 oz | [[Cool Whip]] | thawed |
| 1 pkg (3.4 oz) | [[Pistachio Instant Pudding]] | dry |
| 2 Cups | [[Crushed Pineapple]] | undrained with juice |
| 1 Cup | [[Mini Marshmallows]] | none |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Chopped Nuts]] | none |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Base
1. In a large bowl, sprinkle the **1 pkg (3.4 oz) Pistachio Instant Pudding** over the **8 oz Cool Whip** and stir until the powder is fully incorporated and the mixture is smooth.

### Step 2: Combine and Chill
2. Gently stir in the **2 Cups Crushed Pineapple**, **1 Cup Mini Marshmallows**, and **1/2 Cup Chopped Nuts** until evenly combined.
3. Cover and chill in the refrigerator for at least 1 hour until the mixture is set and the flavors have melded.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This salad can be made up to 24 hours in advance; keep covered in the refrigerator until ready to serve.
