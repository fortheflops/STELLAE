## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
| 1 Box                 | Cheerios                           |
| 1 Box                 | Wheat Chex                         |
| 1 Box                 | Rice Chex                          |
| 1 Box                 | Stick Pretzels                         |
| 1 Bag       | Pretzel Goldfish (Or Oyster Crackers) |
| 2 Pounds             | Pecans                            |
| 2 Sticks              | Butter                        |
| 2 Cups    | Bacon Fat                       |
| 4 Tablespoon          | Worcestershire Sauce|
| 3 Tablespoon          | Garlic Salt                         |
| 4 Tablespoon          | Liquid Smoke                      |
| 1 Tablespoon | Salt|
___
### Instructions
1. In a large, clean plastic bag, toss together the Cheerios, Wheat Chex, Rice Chex, stick pretzels, pretzel Goldfish (or oyster crackers), and pecans.
2. In a separate bowl, melt together, the butter, and bacon fat,. Add the Worcestershire sauce, garlic salt, liquid smoke, and salt.
3. Pour the sauce over the dry ingredient mixture in the plastic bag. Seal the bag and toss thoroughly to coat everything evenly.
4. Pour the coated mixture into a roaster pan.
5. Bake in roaster pan at 250 degrees for 2 hours.
6. Cook for 20 minutes. Turn the mixture.
7. Continue cooking, turning every 15 minutes, for up to 2 hours total baking time.
