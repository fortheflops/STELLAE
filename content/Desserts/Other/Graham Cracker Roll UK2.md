## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
| 1 Pound           | [[Graham Crackers]], rolled as fine as possible |
| 1 Pound           | [[Dates]], cut fine               |
| 1 Pound           | [[Marshmallows]], cut fine        |
| 1 Cup             | [[Walnuts]]                        |
| 1/2 Pint          | [[Cream]]                          |
___
### Instructions
1. Keep part of crackers to roll above ingredients in.
2. When lightly mixed, let stand for 3 days in refrigerator.
3. Serve with whipped cream.
