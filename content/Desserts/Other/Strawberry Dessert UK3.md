## Recipe
### Crust
| Measurements | Ingredients                 |
| :----------- | --------------------------- |
| 1 cup        | [[Flour]]                   |
| 1/4 cup      | [[Brown Sugar]]             |
| 1/2 cup      | [[Pecans]]                  |
| 1/2 cup      | Melted [[Butter]]           |
### Filling
| Measurements | Ingredients                 |
| :----------- | --------------------------- |
| 2            | [[Egg Whites]]              |
| 1 cup        | [[Sugar]]                   |
| 10 oz package | [[Strawberries]]            |
| 2 tablespoons | [[Lemon Juice]]             |
| 1 pint       | [[Whipped Cream]]           |
___
## Instructions
### Crust
  1. Spread 2/3 mixture in bottom of pan.
### Filling 
1. Beat in mixer at high speed for 10 minutes.
2. Fold in 1/2 pint whipped cream.
3. Sprinkle remaining topping.
4. Freeze 6 hours at least.
5. Serves 12.
