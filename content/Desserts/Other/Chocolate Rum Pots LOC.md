## Recipe
| Measurements      | Ingredients                               |
| :---------------- | :---------------------------------------- |
| $\frac{1}{2}$ Cup | [[Sugar]]                                 |
| $\frac{1}{4}$ tsp | [[Salt]]                                  |
| 6 Tbsp            | [[Cocoa]], not instant mix                |
| 2 Cups            | [[Cream]] or [[Half & Half]]              |
| 6                 | [[Egg Yolks]]                             |
| 2 Tbsp            | [[Rum]]                                   |
| Optional          | [[Whipped Cream]] (for garnish)         |
| Optional          | [[Shaved Chocolate]] or [[Chocolate Sprinkles]] (for garnish) |
___
### Instructions
1.  Blend sugar, salt & cocoa thoroughly in a saucepan, then stir in cream.
2.  Cook over low heat, stirring now & then until tiny bubbles form at the edge of the mixture.
3.  Meanwhile, beat egg yolks thoroughly.
4.  Gradually stir cream mixture into egg yolks!
5.  Stir in rum.
6.  Pour into small pots de creme or custard cups.
7.  Set cups in a shallow pan of hot water and bake at 325 degrees 15 minutes or until a knife inserted near centers of custards comes out clean.
8.  Cool thoroughly.
9.  If wished, top with whipped cream and shaved chocolate or chocolate sprinkles.
___

>[!Note]
>Servings 6 to 8
