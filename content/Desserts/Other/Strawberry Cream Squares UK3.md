## Recipe
| Measurements | Ingredients                 |
| :----------- | --------------------------- |
| 2 packages   | [[Strawberry Jello]]        |
| 2 cups       | Boiling [[Water]]           |
| 2 10 oz pkg  | Frozen [[Strawberries]]      |
| 1 cup        | [[Sour Cream]] + 1 pint [[Whipping Cream]] |
| 1/2 - 2 cups | Crushed [[Pineapple]]       |
| 2 or 3 lg.   | [[Bananas]], diced          |
| 1 cup        | [[Nuts]], coarsely chopped  |
___
### Instructions
1. Dissolve jello in water - add berries -
2. Stir until thawed.
3. Add other ingredients.
4. Pour mixture into 8x8x2 pan.
5. Chill until firm.
6. Pour sour cream over jello - allow to set.
7. Pour remaining jello over sour cream.
