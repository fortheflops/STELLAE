# Pecan Pie

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Eighteen | ✍️ **Attribution:** Gail Peters | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic homemade pecan pie recipe with a rich, nutty filling and flaky crust. This heirloom recipe offers proportions for both a single pie and double pie batch.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing and Baking
1. Preheat oven to 325 degrees.
2. In a mixing bowl, combine **2 eggs**, **1/2 cup sugar**, **1/4 cup dark syrup**, **1/4 cup light syrup**, **2 tbsp soft oleo or butter**, **1 tsp vanilla**, and **1 cup pecans**.
3. Pour the mixture into a prepared pie crust.
4. Bake at 325 degrees until set and golden brown.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Pie can be baked a day ahead and stored in the refrigerator; bring to room temperature before serving.
