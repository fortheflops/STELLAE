# Chocolate Chambord Truffles

> 📜 **Collection:** Al Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *An elegant and decadent no-bake dessert featuring rich bittersweet chocolate infused with raspberry-flavored Chambord liqueur. These velvety truffles are rolled in cocoa powder for a classic, sophisticated finish.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 25 mins | 5 mins | 2 hours | 24 truffles |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Food processor
* Saucepan
* Microwave-safe bowl or double boiler
* Baking sheet
* Parchment paper
* Small cookie scoop

---
## Ingredients

### Truffle Mixture
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 1/2 lbs. | [[Bittersweet Chocolate]] | melted |
| 2 | [[Egg Yolks]] | room temperature |
| 1 cup | [[Confectioners' Sugar]] | sifted |
| 4 tbsp | [[Butter]] | unsalted |
| 1/2 cup | [[Heavy Cream]] | none |
| 1/4 cup | [[Chambord Liqueur]] | or other raspberry liqueur |

### Coating
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 cup | [[Cocoa Powder]] | for rolling |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Base
1. Combine **2 egg yolks** and **1 cup confectioners' sugar** in a food processor and process until smooth.
2. In a small saucepan, combine **1/2 cup heavy cream** and **4 tbsp butter**. Heat over low heat until the butter is completely melted, but **DO NOT BOIL**.

### Step 2: Mix the Truffle Dough
3. Add the warm cream and butter mixture to the food processor and purée until smooth and combined.
4. Add **1/4 cup Chambord liqueur** and pulse to incorporate.
5. Melt **1 1/2 lbs. bittersweet chocolate** gently in a double boiler or microwave, then add it to the food processor and mix until the mixture is completely smooth and glossy.
6. Transfer the mixture to a bowl and refrigerate until firm and scoopable, about 2 hours.

### Step 3: Shape and Coat
7. Scoop out tablespoonfuls of the chilled chocolate mixture and roll them between your palms into neat balls.
8. Roll each ball in **1/2 cup cocoa powder** until fully coated, then gently shake off any excess.
9. Store the finished truffles in the refrigerator until ready to serve.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the truffles in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 1 week, or freeze them for up to 1 month. Serve slightly chilled or at room temperature.
