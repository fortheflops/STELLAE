# Vintage Chocolate Cookies

> 📜 **Collection:** John B Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic, rich chocolate drop cookie featuring brown sugar, chopped nuts, and a subtle hint of nutmeg. These soft, vintage-style cookies are perfect for any occasion.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 10 mins | None | 2-3 dozen |

* mixing bowl

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |

| 1 C | [[Brown Sugar]] | packed |
| 1 | [[Egg]] | room temperature |
| 2 sqs | [[Chocolate]] | melted or chopped |
| 1 1/2 C | [[Flour]] | sifted |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Baking Soda]] | sifted |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Baking Powder]] | sifted |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Salt]] | sifted |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Nutmeg]] | sifted |
| 3/4 C | [[Nuts]] | chopped |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Dough
1. Preheat your oven to 350°F.
2. In a large bowl, cream the **1/2 C butter** and **1 C brown sugar** together until light and fluffy.
3. Beat in the **1 egg** and the **2 sqs chocolate** (melted if creating a chocolate base, or chopped if using as chips).
4. In a separate bowl, sift together the **1 1/2 C flour**, **1/4 tsp salt**, **1/4 tsp nutmeg**, **1/4 tsp baking soda**, and **1/4 tsp baking powder**.
5. Gradually stir the dry ingredients into the wet mixture until just combined, then fold in the **3/4 C nuts**.

### Step 2: Bake
6. Drop the dough by spoonfuls onto a prepared baking sheet.
7. Bake at 350°F for approximately **10 minutes** until the edges are set and the cookies are fragrant.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 5 days, or freeze the unbaked dough balls for up to 3 months.
