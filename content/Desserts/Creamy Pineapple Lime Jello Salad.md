# Creamy Pineapple Lime Jello Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic molded Jello salad featuring the tangy combination of lime Jello and cream cheese, packed with crushed pineapple, crunchy pecans, and diced celery. This vintage recipe offers a delightful contrast of textures and flavors perfect for festive gatherings.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 5 mins | 6 hours | 8-10 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* saucepan
* large bowl
* electric mixer
* whisk
* mold or serving dish

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 package (3 oz) | [[Lime Jello]] | powdered |
| 2 small packages (6 oz total) | [[Cream Cheese]] | softened |
| 1/2 pint (1 cup) | [[Coffee Cream]] | or half-and-half |
| 1 #2 can (20 oz) | [[Crushed Pineapple]] | drained, juice reserved |
| 1 cup | [[Pecans]] | chopped |
| 1 cup | [[Diced Celery]] | finely diced |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Base
1. Drain the juice from the **20 oz crushed pineapple** into a saucepan; if you have less than 1 cup of juice, add water to reach the 1 cup mark.
2. Bring the **1 cup pineapple juice** to a boil, then pour over the **3 oz lime jello** in a large bowl, stirring for 2 minutes until completely dissolved; let cool to room temperature.

### Step 2: Create the Creamy Mixture
3. In a separate bowl, use an electric mixer to beat the **6 oz cream cheese** and **1 cup coffee cream** until the mixture is smooth and well combined.
4. Whisk the **cream cheese mixture** into the cooled **lime jello** until the texture is uniform and smooth.

### Step 3: Combine and Set
5. Fold in the reserved **crushed pineapple**, **1 cup pecans**, and **1 cup diced celery** until evenly distributed.
6. Pour the mixture into a mold or serving dish and refrigerate for at least 6 hours, or overnight, until the salad is completely firm and set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This salad must be made at least 6 hours in advance to set properly; it can be stored covered in the refrigerator for up to 3 days.
