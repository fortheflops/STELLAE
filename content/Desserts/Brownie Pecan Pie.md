# Brownie Pecan Pie

> 📜 **Collection:** UK5 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Lisa Stephens | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A decadent dessert that combines the best of two classics: a rich, fudgy brownie and a sweet, crunchy pecan pie baked into a single pie. This delightful treat features a chocolate filling studded with pecans inside a flaky pie crust.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 50 mins | 10 mins | One 9-inch pie |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Medium saucepan
* Mixing bowl
* Wire whisk
* Wire cooling rack
* 9-inch pie pan

| 2/3 cup | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 1/8 teaspoon | [[Salt]] | None |
| 1 cup | [[Corn Syrup]] | Light |
| 4 ounces | [[Chocolate Chips]] | Semi-sweet |
| 3 tablespoons | [[Margarine]] | Or butter |
| 3 | [[Eggs]] | Slightly beaten |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Vanilla]] | Extract |
| 1 cup | [[Pecans]] | Coarsely chopped |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Make the Chocolate Base
1. Preheat the oven to **350°F** (175°C).
2. In a medium saucepan, combine the **2/3 cup sugar**, **1/8 teaspoon salt**, and **1 cup light corn syrup**. Bring the mixture to a boil over medium heat, stirring until the **2/3 cup sugar** dissolves. Boil for **2 minutes**.
3. Remove the pan from the heat. Add the **4 ounces semi-sweet chocolate chips** and **3 tablespoons margarine or butter**, stirring until everything is melted and smooth. Let the mixture cool for about **10 minutes**.

### Step 2: Combine and Bake
4. In a separate bowl, have the slightly beaten **3 eggs** ready. To temper the eggs, slowly pour the cooled chocolate mixture into the **3 eggs** while whisking constantly.
5. Stir in the **1 teaspoon vanilla** and the **1 cup coarsely chopped pecans** until well incorporated.
6. Pour the filling into the unbaked **1 9-inch pie shell**.
7. Bake for **50 minutes**, or until the center is just set and may have a slight jiggle.
8. Let the pie cool completely on a wire rack before slicing until the filling sets firmly.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the baked pie covered in the refrigerator for up to 3 days, and let come to room temperature or serve chilled.
