# Spice Sugar Cookies

> 📜 **Collection:** Jill Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A simple, oil-based sugar cookie with a soft, chewy texture, spiced with cinnamon or nutmeg and rolled in sugar. These vintage-style cookies are quick to prepare and perfect for any occasion.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Dough
1. Preheat your oven to 375°F and lightly grease a baking sheet.
2. In a medium bowl, sift together the **2 1/2 cups all-purpose flour**, **1 1/2 teaspoons baking powder**, **3/4 teaspoon salt**, and your choice of **1 teaspoon cinnamon** or **1/2 teaspoon nutmeg**.
3. In a large bowl, combine the **1 cup granulated sugar** and **3/4 cup cooking oil**.
4. Add the **2 eggs** to the oil mixture one at a time, beating well after each addition, then stir in the **1 teaspoon vanilla extract**.
5. Add the flour mixture to the wet ingredients and beat until a smooth dough forms.

### Step 2: Shape and Bake
6. Pour the additional **granulated sugar** into a shallow bowl.
7. Shape the dough into 1-inch balls and roll each in the **granulated sugar** until coated completely.
8. Place the balls on the prepared baking sheet and bake for **10 to 12 minutes** until the edges are lightly golden and set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 5 days or freeze the unbaked dough balls for up to 3 months.
