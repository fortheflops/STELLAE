# Rosettes Or Timbales

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *Crispy, delicate traditional rosettes and timbales fried to golden perfection and dusted with confectioners' sugar or cinnamon sugar. These versatile pastries can be filled with sweet or savory fillings.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Making the Batter
1. Mix **2 slightly beaten eggs**, **2 tsp granulated sugar**, and **1 cup milk** together.
2. Stir in **1 cup all-purpose flour** and **1/4 tsp salt**, beating until smooth. The batter will be like heavy cream (if batter is too thick and should be thinned with milk).

### Step 2: Frying
3. Heat oil to 350°F in deep saucepan or skillet (increase temp to 400°F for rosettes).
4. Heat iron in hot oil, drain excess oil on paper toweling.
5. Dip heated iron into batter until mold is 2/3 covered.
6. Dip coated iron quickly into hot oil, cooking until golden brown, about 1 minute.
7. Remove tumble or rosette from iron, drain on paper toweling.

### Step 3: Finishing
8. If using for dessert, dip in **confectioners' sugar** while still warm.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Timbales can be made a day ahead and stored, then dipped in confectioners' sugar before serving.
