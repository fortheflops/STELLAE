# No-Bake Minted Chocolate Cream Torte

> 📜 **Collection:** General Archive | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A show-stopping, 6-layer no-bake freezer torte utilizing two refrigerator cream pie kits. Features repeating layers of chocolate crust, chocolate cream, and mint-peppermint cream, finished with freshly whipped cream.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 25 mins | 0 mins | 4 hours | 6–8 generous servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 7-inch springform pan
* Small mixing bowls
* Medium mixing bowl
* Electric mixer
* Piping bag with No. 5 tip

---
## Ingredients

### Torte Layers
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 (9 3/4 oz.) pkgs. | [[Refrigerator Devil's Cream Pie]] | kits |
| 6 T. total | [[Sugar]] | divided, 3 T. per kit |
| 2/3 Cup total | [[Margarine]] | melted and divided, 1/3 C. per kit |
| 6 Cups total | [[Milk]] | divided, 3 C. per kit |
| 1/2 tsp. total | [[Peppermint Extract]] | divided, 1/4 tsp. per kit |

### Topping
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 Cup | [[Heavy Cream]] | cold |
| As needed | [[Mint Sprigs]] | for garnish |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and First Layers
1. Lightly grease a **7-inch springform pan** and set aside.
2. For Kit 1 Layer 1 (Crust): In a small bowl, blend the cracker crumb packet from the first pie kit with **3 T. sugar** and **1/3 Cup melted margarine**. Press crumbs firmly over the bottom of the prepared springform pan.
3. For Kit 1 Layer 2 (Chocolate): In another small bowl, blend the chocolate filling packet from the first kit with **1 1/2 Cups milk**. Beat at low speed for **2 mins.** until thickened. Spread evenly over the cracker crumb layer.
4. For Kit 1 Layer 3 (Mint): In a medium bowl, combine the white filling packet from the first kit with **1 1/2 Cups milk** and **1/4 tsp. peppermint extract**. Beat at low speed, then at medium speed for **3 mins.** until thickened. Spread evenly over the chocolate layer.

### Step 2: Second Layers
5. For Kit 2 Layer 4 (Crust): Repeat the crust step using the second pie kit, **3 T. sugar**, and **1/3 Cup melted margarine**. Sprinkle and press this crumb mixture gently over the white peppermint layer.
6. For Kit 2 Layer 5 (Chocolate): Repeat the chocolate layer step using the chocolate filling packet and **1 1/2 Cups milk**. Spread evenly over the second crumb layer.
7. For Kit 2 Layer 6 (Mint): Repeat the mint layer step using the white filling packet, **1 1/2 Cups milk**, and **1/4 tsp. peppermint extract**. Spread evenly over the second chocolate layer.

### Step 3: Freeze and Garnish
8. Freeze the torte for **4 hours**, or until firm and set.
9. Before serving, remove the outside rim of the springform pan. Beat **1 Cup heavy cream** until very stiff.
10. Pile prettily or pipe using a No. 5 tip the whipped cream in swirls around the edge of the freezer cake, and decorate with **mint sprigs** until festive and fragrant.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the torte wrapped tightly in the freezer for up to 1 week before serving.
