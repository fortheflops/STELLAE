# Meringues

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown One | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *Delicate and crispy homemade meringues folded with chocolate chips. These sweet treats offer a light texture and delightful chocolate flavor.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 25 mins | None | 12 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Electric mixer
* Baking sheets

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 | [[Egg whites]] | Room temperature |
| 1/8 tsp. | [[Salt]] | None |
| 1/8 tsp. | [[Cream of tartar]] | None |
| 1/4 tsp. | [[Vanilla]] | None |
| 3/4 c. | [[Sugar]] | None |
| 1 sm. pkg. | [[Chocolate chips]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Beat and Fold
1. Beat the **2 egg whites**, **1/8 tsp. salt**, and **1/8 tsp. cream of tartar** together until stiff peaks form.
2. Fold in **1/4 tsp. vanilla** and add **3/4 c. sugar** gradually, stirring carefully until well dissolved.
3. Stir in **1 sm. pkg. chocolate chips**.

### Step 2: Bake
4. Drop the mixture onto cooking sheets lined with typing paper.
5. Bake at 300° for **25 min**. (Note: May have to lower temp. to 250° for **20 min**).

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 3 days to maintain crispness.
