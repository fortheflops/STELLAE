# Faye's Graham Cracker Crumb Cake with Pineapple Frosting

> 📜 **Collection:** General Archive | ✍️ **Attribution:** Faye | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A rich, dense, and unique layer cake made with graham cracker crumbs and coconut instead of flour, topped with a sweet crushed pineapple frosting.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 40 mins | None | One 2-layer, 9-inch cake |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Two 9-inch layer pans
* Large mixing bowl
* Medium mixing bowl
* Electric mixer

---
## Ingredients

### For the Cake
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 lbs. (2 sticks) | [[Butter]] | Softened |
| 2 C. | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 5 | [[Eggs]] | Room temperature |
| 1 pkg. | [[Graham Crackers]] | Crushed |
| 1 (7oz.) pkg. | [[Coconut]] | Flaked |
| 2 tsp. | [[Baking Powder]] | None |
| 2 tsp. | [[Vanilla]] | None |
| 1 Cup | Nut Meats | Optional |

### For the Pineapple Frosting
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 stick | [[Margarine]] | Softened |
| 1 box (approx. 16 oz) | [[Confectioner's Sugar]] | None |
| 1 small can | [[Pineapple]] | Crushed, with juice |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Bake the Cake
1. Preheat your oven to **350°F**. Grease **two 9-inch layer pans**.
2. In a large bowl, cream the **1/2 lbs. (2 sticks) butter** and **2 C. sugar** until light and fluffy.
3. Add the **5 eggs**, one at a time, and beat well after each addition until fully incorporated.
4. Fold in the **1 pkg. graham crackers**, **1 (7oz.) pkg. coconut**, **2 tsp. baking powder**, and **2 tsp. vanilla**.
5. Add the **1 cup nut meats** last, if using.
6. Pour the batter evenly into the **two 9-inch layer pans**.
7. Bake at **350°F** for **30 to 40 minutes**, or until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean and golden brown.
8. Let the cakes cool completely on a wire rack before frosting.

### Step 2: Prepare the Frosting and Assemble
9. In a medium bowl, cream the **1 stick margarine** and the **1 box confectioner's sugar** until smooth.
10. Blend in the **1 small can crushed pineapple** (with its juice) until the frosting reaches a spreadable consistency.
11. Pour or spread the frosting onto the cooled cake layers until evenly coated and luscious.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The frosted cake stores well, and the frosting itself freezes nicely for future use.
