# White Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Five | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic homemade white cake recipe featuring cake flour, shortening, and vanilla. This traditional dessert bakes into a light, tender treat perfect for any celebration.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 25 mins | None | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Sifter
* Mixing bowl
* Electric mixer
* Baking pan

---
## Ingredients

### Dry Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 cups | [[Cake Flour]] | Sifted |
| 3/4 tsp. | [[Salt]] | None |
| 1 3/4 teas. | [[Calumet Baking Powder]] | None |
| 1 1/4 cups | [[Sugar]] | None |

### Wet Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 c. | [[Vegetable Shortening]] | None |
| 1 c. minus 2 tbls. | [[Milk]] | Total liquid amount |
| 1 tsp. | [[Vanilla]] | None |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] | Unbeaten |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing
1. Stir **1/2 c. vegetable shortening** just to soften.
2. Stir in dry ingredients, then add **3/4 c. of milk** and mix until all flour is dampened.
3. Beat for **2 minutes**.
4. Add remaining **milk** and **2 unbeaten eggs**, then beat for **1 minute** longer on low speed until smooth.

### Step 2: Baking
5. Bake at **375 degrees** for **25 minutes** or until golden brown and a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The cake layers can be baked ahead of time, wrapped tightly in plastic wrap, and stored at room temperature for a day or frozen for up to a month.
