# Chocolate Almond Raisin Clusters

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *These delightful chocolate almond raisin clusters are a clever no-fuss treat made by using the residual heat of toasted nuts to melt the chocolate. Perfect for a quick sweet fix, they require minimal effort and yield a satisfying, crunchy texture.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 10 mins | 20 mins | 24 clusters |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* baking pan
* medium bowl
* cookie sheet
* waxed paper
* spoon

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 Cup | [[Almonds]] | whole, unskinned |
| 2 Cups (12-oz pkg) | [[Chocolate Chips]] | semi-sweet |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Raisins]] | none |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Toast and Melt
1. Preheat your oven to 350°F and line a cookie sheet with waxed paper.
2. Spread the **1 cup almonds** in a single layer on a baking pan and toast for **10 minutes** until fragrant.
3. Immediately transfer the hot **almonds** to a medium bowl and add the **2 cups chocolate chips**. Stir continuously until the **chocolate chips** are completely melted and the **almonds** are glossy and coated.

### Step 2: Assemble and Chill
4. Stir in the **1/2 cup raisins** until evenly distributed.
5. Drop the mixture by spoonfuls onto the prepared cookie sheet.
6. Chill in the refrigerator for **20 minutes**, or until the clusters are firm to the touch.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 2 weeks to maintain freshness and snap.
