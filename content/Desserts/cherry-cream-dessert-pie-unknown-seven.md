# Cherry Cream Dessert Pie

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Seven | ✍️ **Attribution:** Jinnie Lou | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A delightful layered vintage no-bake dessert featuring a creamy sweetened cream cheese layer, chopped pecans, fluffy whipped topping, and almond-kissed cherry pie filling in a graham cracker crust. Originating from Jinnie Lou's recipe box in October 1968.*

## Ingredients

### Pie Assembly
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | [[Graham Cracker Crust]] | 9-inch ready-made crust |
| 8 oz. | [[Cream Cheese]] | softened |
| 1/2 cup | [[Confectioners' Sugar]] |  |
| 1 tbsp. | [[Milk]] |  |
| 1 tsp. | [[Almond Extract]] | divided (1/2 tsp each layer) |
| 1/2 cup | [[Pecans]] | chopped |
| 1/2 cup | [[Dream Whip]] | or prepared heavy whipping cream |
| 2 tbsp. | [[Confectioners' Sugar]] | for whip layer |
| 2 cups | [[Cherry Pie Filling]] | or blueberry pie filling |

---

## Instructions

### Assembly & Chilling
1. In a mixing bowl, combine **8 oz. cream cheese**, **1/2 cup confectioners' sugar**, **1 tbsp. milk**, and **1/2 tsp. almond extract**; mix well until smooth and creamy.
2. Spread the cream cheese mixture evenly over the bottom of the **1 Graham Cracker Crust**.
3. Sprinkle **1/2 cup chopped pecans** evenly over the cream cheese layer.
4. In a clean bowl, combine **1/2 cup Dream Whip** and **2 tbsp. confectioners' sugar**; whip until stiff peaks form. Spread the whipped cream gently over the **1/2 cup chopped pecans**.
5. In a small bowl, combine **2 cups cherry pie filling** and the remaining **1/2 tsp. almond extract**. Spread the cherry mixture over the whipped cream layer.
6. Chill in the refrigerator for at least 2 hours until cold and set before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the pie up to 24 hours in advance and store covered in the refrigerator until ready to slice and serve.
