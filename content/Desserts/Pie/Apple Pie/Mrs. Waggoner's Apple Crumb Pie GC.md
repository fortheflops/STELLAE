## Recipe
### Crust Ingredients
| Measurements        | Ingredients |
| :----------------- | :---------- |
| 1                  | [[Pie Crust]] |
### Filling Ingredients
| Measurements        | Ingredients                           |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------ |
| 4 Cups             | [[Sliced Apples (Gravenstein suggested)]] |
| 1/2 Cup            | [[Sugar]]                             |
| 1 Teaspoon         | [[Cinnamon]]                          |
| 1 Tablespoon       | [[Flour]]                             |
### Topping Ingredients
| Measurements        | Ingredients   |
| :----------------- | :------------ |
| 1/2 Cup            | [[Sugar]]     |
| 3/4 Cup            | [[Flour]]     |
| 1/3 Cup            | [[Margarine]] |
___
## Instructions
### For the Filling 
1. Mix the sliced apples, 1/2 cup of sugar, cinnamon, and 1 tablespoon of flour.
2.  Put the apple mixture into the pie crust.
### For the Topping
1. Mix 1/2 cup of sugar, 3/4 cup of flour, and margarine 'til crumbly.
2.  Put the crumbly topping over the apple filling.
3.  Bake for one hour at 400°F.
