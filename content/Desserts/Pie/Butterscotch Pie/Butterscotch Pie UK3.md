## Recipe
### Filling
| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
| 1 cup        | [[Brown Sugar]]             |
| 1/3 cup      | [[Flour]]                   |
| 1/2 teaspoon | [[Salt]]                    |
| 2 cups       | [[Milk]]                    |
| 2            | [[Egg Yolks]]               |
| 1/2 teaspoon | [[Vanilla]]                 |
| 2 tablespoons | [[Butter]]                  |
| 1            | [[Whipping Cream]]          |
### Crust
| Measurements | Ingredients                 |
| :----------- | --------------------------- |
| 1 stick      | Melted [[Butter]]           |
| 1 cup        | [[Flour]]                   |
| 1/4 cup      | [[Brown Sugar]]             |
| 1/2 cup      | [[Pecans]]                  |
___
## Instructions
1. Mix dry ingredients.
2. Add to milk.
3. Stir over medium heat until thick.
4. Add beaten egg yolks and cook and mix.
5. Stir in butter and vanilla.
6. Pour in crust and top with whipped cream.
### Crust
1. Mix and press in buttered pan.
2. Bake at 400°F for 10 minutes.
