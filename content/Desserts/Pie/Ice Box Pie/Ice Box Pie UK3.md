## Recipe
| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
| 1 can        | [[Eagle Brand Milk]]        |
| 1 large container | [[Cool Whip]] (or other whipped topping) |
| 1 large       | [[Lemon]], Juice (2 are better) |
| 1 cup        | [[Pecans]]                  |
___
### Instructions
1. Mix first 3 ingredients well.
2. (Have crust baked - or use vanilla wafer crust or graham cracker crust.)
3. This is enough filling for 2 crusts.
4. Divide pecans equally between 2 crusts, sprinkle on bottom of crust.
5. Then pour filling on top of pecans.
6. Put in refrigerator.
7. Keeps well.
