## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
| 25 Large             | [[Marshmallows]]              |
| 6                   | [[Hershey Bars]]         |
| 1/2 Cup             | [[Milk]]          |
| 1/2 Pint             | [[Whipped Whipping Cream]]          |
|                     | [[Nuts]] or Grated [[Hershey]] for topping|

___
### Instructions
1. Melt in 1/2 cup milk in double boiler, cool.
2. Add to 1/2 pint whipped whipping cream.
3. Add nuts or grated Hershey for topping.
