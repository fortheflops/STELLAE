### Instructions
1. Melt 8 squares German Sweet Chocolate and 1 stick oleo.
2. Mix 1 cup sugar, 1/2 cup flour, 1 teaspoon vanilla, and 3 eggs.
3. Pour in melted chocolate and butter. 
4. Mix in 1 cup pecans.
5. Pour in greased pie pan.
6. Bake at 300°F for 30 minutes. No more.
