## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
|   1 small can   |   Crushed [[Pineapple]] - Well Drained |
|   3/4 cups   |   [[Sugar]]   |
|   1 - 8 oz. pkg   |   [[Cream Cheese]] - Soften To Room Temperature |
|   1 pkg.   |   [[Dream Whip]] That Has Not Been Whipped |
___
### Instructions
1.  Drain One Small Can Of Crushed Pineapple - Well.
2.  Add 3/4 Cups Sugar.
3.  1 - 8oz. Pkg Cream Cheese - Soften To Room Temperature. (Add This To The First Two Ingredients).
4.  Fold In 1 Pkg. Dream Whip That Has Not Been Whipped.
5.  Put Into Vanilla Wafer Crust Or Previously Baked Crust.
6.  Put In Refrigerator.
