## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 4 pounds                   | [[Pie Dough]]                             |
| 1 1/2                      | #10 cans Canned Sliced [[Apricots]] (3 3/4 qts)|
| 3 cups                     | Juice from apricots + [[Water]]           |
| 3 ounces                   | [[Cornstarch]]                            |
| 1 1/4 cups                 | Cold [[Water]]                            |
| 1 pound 4 ounces           | [[Sugar]]                                 |
| 1 1/2 teaspoons            | [[Salt]]                                  |
| 3/4 teaspoon               | [[Mace]]                                  |
| 1 1/2 teaspoons            | [[Cinnamon]]                              |
| 2 tablespoons              | [[Lemon Juice]]                           |
___
## Instructions
1.  Divide the 4 pounds of pie dough in half. From one half, cut four 8-ounce pieces. Roll these out and place them in pie tins to form the bottom crusts.
2.  Drain the canned apricots, reserving the juice.
3.  Combine the reserved apricot juice with enough water to make 3 cups of liquid. Bring this juice mixture to a boil.
4.  In a separate bowl, dissolve the cornstarch in 1 1/4 cups of cold water to make a slurry.
5.  Add the cornstarch slurry to the boiling juice mixture and cook until thick, stirring constantly.
6.  Add the sugar, salt, mace, cinnamon, and lemon juice to the thickened mixture. Bring back to a full boil.
7.  Let the mixture cool completely.
8.  Fill each pie crust with 3 pounds of the apricot mixture.
9.  Divide the remaining half of the pie dough into four 8-ounce pieces.
10. Roll out each piece and cut into 1/2-inch wide lattice strips.
11. Weave the strips over the filling to create a lattice top.
12. Trim and seal the edges.
13. Bake at 425 for 40 minutes
