## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
| 3                   | Egg Whites                    |
| 3/4 Cup             | Sugar                         |
| 3/4 Cup             | Pecans                        |
| 3/4 Cup             | Graham Cracker Crumbs         |
| 1 Teaspoon          | Vanilla                       |
___
### Instructions
1. Beat egg whites until stiff.
2. Add other ingredients
3. Bake at 325 degrees for 35 minutes.
4. Cover with whipped cream at least 6 hours before serving.
