## Recipe
| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
| 22             | [[Ritz Crackers]] (crushed)              |
| 1 Cup             | [[Pecans]]         |
| 1/2 Cup                   | [[Sugar]]          |
| 1 Teaspoon | [[Vanilla]]          |
| 3      |[[Egg Whites]] (stiff whipped)|
| 1 Cup | [[Whipping Cream]]|
___
### Instructions
1. Add crackers, pecans, sugar & vanilla together.
2. Then add egg whites into mixture.
3. Put in greased pie pan.
4. Bake 350° for 20 min.
5. Allow to set one day.
6. Add whip cream before serving.
