# Chocolate Dump Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** Georgia Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Warner | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A delightfully light and airy chocolate dump cake with a unique whipped-cream texture. Baked slowly at a low temperature, this vintage favorite rises beautifully as long as you don't peek!*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Mix
1. Melt **1 1/2 squares chocolate** and **3 tbsp butter** together, then place in a mixing bowl.
2. Add **1 cup sugar** and **1/2 cup milk** to the bowl.
3. Sift **1 cup flour** and **2 tsp baking powder** together 3 times, then add to the mixture.
4. Add **2 to 3 eggs** and **1 tsp vanilla extract**.
5. Beat all ingredients together thoroughly until the batter is completely smooth and looks like whipped cream.

### Step 2: Bake
6. Pour the batter into a greased oblong baking pan.
7. Bake in a preheated **325°F** oven for **40 minutes**.
8. Do not open the oven door during baking to ensure the cake rises perfectly and remains very light and tasty.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This cake is best enjoyed fresh, but can be covered and stored at room temperature for up to 3 days.
