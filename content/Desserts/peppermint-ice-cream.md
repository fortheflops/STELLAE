# Peppermint Ice Cream

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Five | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A delightful homemade peppermint ice cream recipe featuring whipped egg whites, rich cream, and a refreshing peppermint flavor. Perfect for hot summer days or special family gatherings.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 30 mins | None | 4 hours | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Ice cream maker
* Mixing bowl
* Whisk

---
## Ingredients

### Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 4 | [[Eggs]] | separated |
| 2 cups | [[Sugar]] | divided |
| 1 dash | [[Salt]] | none |
| 1 can | [[Canned Milk]] | none |
| 1 quart | [[Cream]] | none |
| to taste | [[Peppermint Flavoring]] | none |
| as needed | [[Crushed Peppermint]] | crushed |

---

## Instructions

### Preparation
1. Beat the **4 eggs** whites till stiff, then add a dash of **1 dash salt**, gradually add **2 cups sugar** (yolks then rest of sugar).
2. Put **1 can canned milk** in freezer, **1 quart cream**, **peppermint flavoring**, and **crushed peppermint**.
3. Add meringue of whites, etc., then finish with milk and churn in an ice cream freezer until set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container in the freezer for up to 1 week for best texture.
