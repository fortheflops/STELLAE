## Instructions

### Cake Batter & Baking
1. Cream **1/2 cup shortening** and **1 1/2 cups sugar** together, then add **2 beaten eggs**.
2. Make a paste of **1 tbsp cocoa powder** and **2 oz red food coloring**, and add to the creamed mixture.
3. Sift **1/2 tsp salt** and **2 1/2 cups flour** together. Add to the mixture alternately with **1 cup buttermilk**, starting and ending with the **flour**.
4. Mix **1 tsp baking soda** and **1 1/2 tsp vinegar** together and add last.
5. Bake at 350°F in 2 greased and floured cake pans until done and springy to the touch.

### Icing & Assembly
6. Mix **5 tbsp flour** and **1 cup sugar** in a saucepan. Gradually add **1 cup canned milk** and mix well.
7. Cook and stir over medium heat until thick. Blend in **1 cup butter** and **1 tsp vanilla extract**.
8. Cool completely before icing the cake.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The cake layers can be baked and cooled a day ahead if kept wrapped. Store the frosted cake covered in the refrigerator.
