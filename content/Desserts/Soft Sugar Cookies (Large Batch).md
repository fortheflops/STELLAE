# Soft Sugar Cookies (Large Batch)

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic, large-batch recipe for soft, cake-like sugar cookies. This tender dough is enriched with buttermilk or sour cream and spiced with nutmeg for a perfect roll-and-cut treat.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 15 mins | 1 hour | 6-7 dozen cookies |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* large mixing bowl
* electric mixer
* sifter
* rolling pin
* cookie cutters
* ungreased cookie sheet

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 Cup | [[Butter]] | softened |
| 1 1/2 Cups | [[Sugar]] | granulated |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] | large |
| 1/4 Cup | [[Buttermilk]] or [[Sour Cream]] | room temperature |
| 4 Cups | [[Flour]] | sifted, all-purpose |
| 1 Tablespoon | [[Baking Powder]] | None |
| 1/8 Teaspoon | [[Baking Soda]] | None |
| 1 Teaspoon | [[Salt]] | None |
| 2 Teaspoons | [[Nutmeg]] | optional |
| As needed | [[Sugar]] | for sprinkling |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Dough
1. In a large bowl, cream the **1 cup butter** and **1 1/2 cups sugar** together with an electric mixer until fluffy.
2. Beat in the **2 eggs** until well combined.
3. In a separate bowl, sift together the **4 cups flour**, **1 tablespoon baking powder**, **1/8 teaspoon baking soda**, **1 teaspoon salt**, and **2 teaspoons nutmeg**.
4. On low speed, add the dry ingredients to the creamed mixture in three parts, alternating with the **1/4 cup buttermilk or sour cream**, beginning and ending with the dry ingredients until a soft dough forms.

### Step 2: Chill and Roll
5. Cover the dough and chill for at least **1 hour**, or until firm enough to handle.
6. Preheat your oven to **375°F**.
7. On a lightly floured surface, roll the chilled dough out to your desired thickness and cut into shapes with cookie cutters.

### Step 3: Bake
8. Place the cookies on an ungreased cookie sheet and sprinkle the tops with **sugar**.
9. Bake for **10-15 minutes**, or until the edges are lightly browned and the centers are set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The dough can be kept chilled for up to 2 days before rolling, or frozen for up to 3 months.
