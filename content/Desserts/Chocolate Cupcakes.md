# Chocolate Cupcakes

> *A classic vintage recipe for rich chocolate cupcakes baked to fluffy perfection. These delightful treats are easily prepared and topped off with a simple boiling water finish for an exceptionally moist crumb.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Mix
1. Preheat oven to **350°F**. Line muffin tins with cupcake papers.
2. In a mixing bowl, combine **1 cup [[Sugar]]**, **1/2 cup [[Cocoa Powder]]**, **1/2 cup [[Shortening]]**, **1 1/2 cups sifted [[Flour]]**, **3/4 teaspoon [[Salt]]**, **1 teaspoon [[Soda]]**, **1 teaspoon [[Vanilla Extract]]**, **1 [[Egg]]**, and **1/2 cup [[Milk]]**.

### Step 2: Finish Batter and Bake
3. Add **1/2 cup boiling [[Water]]** last to the batter.
4. Beat the mixture for **3 minutes** with an electric beater until smooth and well incorporated.
5. Fill cupcake papers about two-thirds full with the batter.
6. Bake at **350°F** for **25 minutes or less**, checking for doneness with a toothpick until it comes out clean.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store frosted or unfrosted cupcakes in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 3 days or freeze for up to 2 months.
