# Apple Nut Dessert

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A delightful old-fashioned apple and nut dessert baked to golden perfection. Serve warm with ice cream or whipped cream for a cozy treat.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 35 mins | None | 6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Shallow pan

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | [[Egg]] | Well beaten |
| 3/4 cup | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 2 rounded tbsp | [[Flour]] | All-purpose |
| 1 heaping tsp | [[Baking Powder]] | Leavening agent |
| 1/2 cup | [[Nut Meat]] | Finely chopped |
| 1 large or 2 small | [[Apples]] | Diced |
| 1 tsp | [[Vanilla]] | Extract |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing and Baking
1. Combine the **1 well beaten egg**, **3/4 cup sugar**, **2 rounded tablespoons flour**, **1 heaping teaspoon baking powder**, **1/2 cup nut meat**, **1 large or 2 small diced apples**, and **1 teaspoon vanilla** in a mixing bowl.
2. Pour the batter into a shallow pan.
3. Bake in a preheated oven at **350 degrees Fahrenheit** for about **35 minutes** until golden and set.

### Step 2: Serving
4. Serve warm with ice cream or whipped cream.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Best served fresh on the day it is baked.
