# Osgood Pie

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Betty Eakins | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A delightful traditional pie featuring a rich spiced filling with pecans and raisins. Topped with a fluffy meringue-like whipped egg white finish, it bakes to golden perfection.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation
1. Separate the **4 eggs**, setting the egg whites aside in a clean bowl.
2. Mix together the remaining ingredients: **2 cups sugar**, **1 cup pecans**, **3 tsp vinegar**, **1 tsp cloves**, **1 tsp cinnamon**, **1 cup butter**, **1 1/2 tbsp flour**, and **1 cup raisins** along with the egg yolks.

### Step 2: Meringue and Baking
3. Beat the set-aside **4 egg whites** until they are stiff.
4. Gently fold the beaten egg whites into the mixture.
5. Pour the filling into the **2 pie shells**.
6. Bake at 350°F for **30 to 45 minutes** until set and golden brown.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Bake completely and store at room temperature for a day, or cover and refrigerate for up to 3 days.
