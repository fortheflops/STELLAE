## Instructions

### Making the Filling
1. Combine the **2 cups dates**, **3/4 cup sugar**, **3/4 cup water**, and optional **1/2 cup nuts** in a saucepan.
2. Cook slowly, stirring constantly until thickened.
3. Remove from heat and let cool completely.

### Making the Dough and Baking
4. Mix thoroughly the **1 cup oleo**, **2 cups brown sugar**, **2 eggs**, **1/2 cup water**, and **1 tsp vanilla**.
5. Add the dry ingredients: **3 1/2 cups flour**, **1/2 tsp salt**, **1 tsp baking soda**, and **1/8 tsp cinnamon**, mixing well.
6. Drop by **1 tsp** of dough onto a cookie sheet.
7. Place **1/2 tsp** date filling in the center of the dough, then cover with another **1/2 tsp** dough.
8. Bake at 375 degrees F for **10-12 minutes** until light brown.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store baked cookies in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 5 days, or freeze the unbaked filled cookies.
