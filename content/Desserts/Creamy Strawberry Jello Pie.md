# Creamy Strawberry Jello Pie

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A fantastically simple no-bake pie featuring a creamy, fluffy filling made from strawberry Jello, melted vanilla ice cream, and fresh strawberries. Chill until firm for a retro dessert that always delights.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 0 mins | 2 hours 25 mins | One 9-inch pie |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large bowl
* Mixing spoon
* 9-inch pie pan
* Refrigerator

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | [[Pie Shell]] | Baked and cooled, 9-inch |
| 1 Package | [[Jello]] | Strawberry, regular size, 3 oz |
| 1 1/2 Cups | [[Water]] | Hot |
| 1 Pint | [[Ice Cream]] | Vanilla |
| 1 Cup | [[Strawberries]] | Drained and sliced |
| As needed | [[Whipped Cream]] | Optional, for serving |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mix and Melt
1. In a large bowl, dissolve **1 package strawberry jello** in **1 1/2 cups hot water**, stirring for 2 minutes until fully dissolved.
2. Add **1 pint vanilla ice cream** by the spoonful to the hot mixture and stir until it is completely melted and smooth.

### Step 2: Chill and Fold
3. Refrigerate the mixture for **15-25 minutes**, or until it has thickened to the consistency of unbeaten egg whites but is not fully set.
4. Gently fold in **1 cup drained, sliced strawberries** until evenly distributed.

### Step 3: Assemble and Set
5. Pour the filling into the prepared **9-inch baked and cooled pie shell**.
6. Chill the pie in the refrigerator for at least **2 hours**, or until the filling is completely firm and set.
7. Slice and serve with **whipped cream**, if desired.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the pie covered in the refrigerator for up to 3 days.
