# Caramel Apple Salad (Taffy Apple Salad)

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic potluck favorite, this creamy and crunchy no-bake dessert salad combines fresh apples, pineapple, marshmallows, and nuts in a rich butterscotch pudding base. It is a nostalgic treat perfect for gatherings and easy to prepare ahead of time.*

---

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large mixing bowl
* Silicone spatula

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 package (3.5 oz) | [[Butterscotch Instant Pudding Mix]] | dry powder |
| 1 Cup | [[Dry Roasted Nuts]] | cashews or pecans |
| 1 can (20 oz) | [[Pineapple]] | crushed, undrained |
| 1 Cup | [[Marshmallows]] | miniature |
| 3 Cups | [[Apples]] | chopped, unpeeled |
| 1 container (8 oz) | [[Whipped Topping]] | thawed |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Combine Ingredients
1. In a large bowl, combine the **1 package (3.5 oz) butterscotch instant pudding mix**, **1 cup dry roasted nuts**, **1 can (20 oz) crushed pineapple** (undrained), **1 cup miniature marshmallows**, **3 cups chopped unpeeled apples**, and **1 container (8 oz) whipped topping**.

### Step 2: Mix and Chill
2. Stir gently but thoroughly until all ingredients are well combined and the mixture is uniform.
3. Cover the bowl and chill in the refrigerator for at least **1-2 hours** until the pudding mix has dissolved and the salad is chilled and set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This salad is best when chilled for at least 2 hours; keep refrigerated and consume within 24 hours for the best texture.
