# Herr Hofmann's Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Eighteen | ✍️ **Attribution:** Herr Hofmann | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic German-style red wine cake dating back to 1977, featuring a rich blend of chocolate, cinnamon, and red wine. This moist and aromatic cake is perfect for afternoon tea or holiday gatherings.*

## Ingredients

### Cake Batter
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 200 g | [[Butter]] | softened |
| 200 g | [[Sugar]] | None |
| 375 g | [[Flour]] | all-purpose |
| 4 | [[Eggs]] | room temperature |
| 1/8 L | [[Red Wine]] | dry or semi-dry, approx. 125 ml |
| 1 tbsp | [[Cinnamon]] | ground |
| 1 tbsp | [[Cocoa powder]] | unsweetened |
| 100 g | [[Chocolate]] | very finely chopped |
| 1 packet | [[Baking powder]] | approx. 15g |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Creaming
1. Preheat your oven to **175°C (350°F)** and generously grease a Bundt pan, dusting it lightly with flour to prevent sticking.
2. In a large mixing bowl, beat the **200 g butter** and **200 g sugar** together using a hand mixer until the mixture is light, fluffy, and pale in color.
3. Add the **4 eggs** one at a time, beating thoroughly after each addition to achieve a smooth, emulsified batter.

### Step 2: Mixing the Batter
4. In a separate bowl, whisk together the **375 g flour**, **1 packet baking powder**, **1 tbsp cinnamon**, and **1 tbsp cocoa powder**.
5. Alternately fold the dry flour mixture and the **1/8 L red wine** into the creamed butter mixture, starting and ending with the dry ingredients, mixing until just combined to keep the cake tender.
6. Gently fold in the **100 g chocolate** (very finely chopped) with a spatula until evenly distributed throughout the batter.

### Step 3: Baking
7. Pour the batter into the prepared Bundt pan and smooth the top with a spatula.
8. Bake for **50 to 60 minutes**, or until a wooden skewer inserted into the center of the cake comes out clean with no wet batter clinging to it.
9. Let the cake cool in the pan for **10 to 15 minutes** before carefully turning it out onto a wire rack to cool completely.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This cake tastes even better the next day as the red wine and spice flavors deepen. Store wrapped tightly in foil at room temperature for up to 4 days.
