# Fluffy Orange and Browned Butter Icings

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A vintage duo of classic cake toppings featuring a bright, citrusy Fluffy Orange Icing and a rich, nutty Browned Butter Icing. Perfect for pairing with chiffon cakes, cupcakes, or traditional yellow cakes.*

## Instructions

### Fluffy Orange Icing
1. Cream the **1 1/2 (3 oz) packages cream cheese** until light and fluffy.
2. Gradually add the **2 1/2 cups sifted confectioners' sugar** and beat well until smooth.
3. Stir in the grated rind of **2 oranges**.
4. If the icing is too thick, add **a few drops of orange juice** to reach a spreadable consistency.

### Browned Butter Icing
5. Melt **1/4 cup butter** in a small saucepan, then keep over low heat until it turns a fragrant golden brown.
6. Remove from heat and blend in **2 cups sifted confectioners' sugar**, **2 tbsp cream**, **1 tsp cooking oil**, and **1 1/2 tsp vanilla**.
7. Stir in **1 tbsp hot water**.
8. Stir vigorously until the mixture cools and reaches a spreadable consistency. If it becomes too thick to spread, warm slightly over hot water.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Both icings can be made ahead and stored in airtight containers in the refrigerator for up to 3 days; bring to room temperature and stir well before using.
