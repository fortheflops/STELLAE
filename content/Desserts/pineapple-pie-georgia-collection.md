# Pineapple Pie

> 📜 **Collection:** Georgia Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *This classic Pineapple Pie features a rich, creamy custard base folded with whipped egg whites for a light, airy texture. Baked in a flaky crust, it delivers a perfect balance of tropical sweetness and buttery richness.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Filling
1. In a mixing bowl, beat **1/3 cup butter**, **2/3 cup sugar**, and **2 egg yolks** until smooth and creamy. Stir in **1 1/2 cups crushed pineapple**.
2. In a separate small bowl, dissolve **2 tsp cornstarch** in **2/3 cup cream**, then add to the pineapple mixture and mix well.
3. In a clean bowl, beat the **2 egg whites** until stiff peaks form, then gently fold them into the pineapple mixture until just combined.

### Step 2: Bake
4. Pour the mixture into a large **1 unbaked pie shell**.
5. Bake at **450°F** for **10 minutes**, then reduce the heat to **350°F** and bake for an additional **30 minutes** until the filling is set and the top is golden brown.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This pie can be baked a day in advance and stored in the refrigerator. Serve chilled or at room temperature.
