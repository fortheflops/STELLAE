# Mince Meat Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A traditional spiced Mince Meat Cake featuring rich dark molasses and aromatic spices. This classic baked dessert brings together sweet milk, butter, and mincemeat for a deeply comforting treat.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing the Base
1. Stir to a cream **2 eggs**, **1 c. sugar**, and **1/2 c. butter** until light and smooth.

### Step 2: Adding Liquids and Mincemeat
2. Add **1/2 c. dark molasses** and **1 c. sweet milk**, then stir thoroughly.
3. Mince fine **1 package mincemeat** and fold into the wet mixture.

### Step 3: Combining Dry Ingredients and Baking
4. Sift together **3 c. flour**, **2 tsp. baking powder**, and **1 tsp. salt**.
5. Stir the dry mixture into the liquid mixture until just combined.
6. Bake until golden brown and a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the baked cake in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 3 days, or wrap tightly and freeze for up to 2 months.
