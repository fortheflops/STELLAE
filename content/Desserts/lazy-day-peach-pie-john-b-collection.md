# Lazy Day Peach Pie

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A delightful no-bake dessert featuring a creamy peach filling set in a crunchy crumb crust. Perfect for warm days when you want something sweet without turning on the oven.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 0 mins | 2 hours | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Fork
* Electric mixer
* 9-inch pie plate

---
## Ingredients

### Filling and Crust
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 pkg | [[Orange flavored gelatine]] | Dry |
| 1/2 cup | [[Hot water]] | Hot |
| 1 pkg (12 oz) | [[Frozen peaches]] | Frozen |
| 1/4 cup | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 1 cup | [[Whipping cream]] | Unwhipped |
| 1 Tbsp | [[Lemon juice]] | Fresh |
| 1 | [[Crumb pie shell]] | 9 inch |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing and Chilling
1. Dissolve **1 pkg orange flavored gelatine** in **1/2 cup hot water**.
2. Add **1 pkg (12 oz) frozen peaches** and with a fork break peach slices apart as they thaw.
3. Stir in **1/4 cup sugar**, **1 cup whipping cream**, and **1 Tbsp lemon juice**.
4. Chill until congealed.
5. Beat with an electric mixer until fluffy.

### Step 2: Assembly and Final Chill
6. Pour into **1 crumb pie shell** and chill until firm.
7. Garnish with additional peach slices if desired.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Keep refrigerated until ready to serve; best enjoyed within 24 hours.
