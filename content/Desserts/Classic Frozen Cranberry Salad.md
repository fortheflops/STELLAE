# Classic Frozen Cranberry Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A nostalgic and festive frozen cranberry salad, combining whole berry cranberry sauce, pineapple, marshmallows, bananas, and pecans with whipped cream. This classic fruit salad is perfect for holiday gatherings and offers a delightful balance of tart and sweet flavors.*

---

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 0 mins | 8 hours | 8-10 Servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* large mixing bowl
* fork
* freezer-safe dish
* spatula

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 can (14 oz) | [[Cranberry Sauce]] | whole berry |
| 1 small can (8 oz) | [[Pineapple]] | crushed, drained |
| 1 package (10 oz) | [[Mini Marshmallows]] | none |
| 3 | [[Bananas]] | mashed |
| 1/2 cup | [[Pecans]] | chopped |
| 1 cup | [[Heavy Cream]] | whipped to stiff peaks (or 2 cups whipped topping) |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Combine Base
1. In a large bowl, break up the **1 can (14 oz) cranberry sauce** with a fork.
2. Add the **1 small can (8 oz) pineapple**, **1 package (10 oz) mini marshmallows**, **3 mashed bananas**, and **1/2 cup pecans**. Stir until well combined.

### Step 2: Fold and Freeze
3. Gently fold in the **1 cup heavy cream** until just incorporated, ensuring a light texture.
4. Spoon the mixture into a freezer-safe dish and cover tightly.
5. Freeze for at least **8 hours**, or overnight, until firm to the touch.
6. Let sit at room temperature for **10-15 minutes** to soften slightly before slicing and serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This salad is designed to be made ahead and can be kept in the freezer for up to 2 weeks if tightly covered.
