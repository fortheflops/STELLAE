# Easy No-Roll Pie Crust

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A foolproof and incredibly easy pie crust made with vegetable oil that requires no rolling. Simply mix the ingredients directly in the pie pan and press into place.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5 mins | 15 mins | None | One 9-inch pie crust |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 9-inch pie pan
* Small cup
* Fork

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 ½ cups | [[Flour]] | All-purpose |
| ¾ tsp | [[Salt]] | Standard |
| 1 tsp | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| ½ cup | [[Vegetable Oil]] | Liquid |
| 2 Tbsp | [[Milk]] | Cold or room temperature |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mix Dry Ingredients
1. Preheat your oven to 425°F (220°C) if pre-baking the shell.
2. Sift or whisk the **1 ½ cups flour**, **¾ tsp salt**, and **1 tsp sugar** together directly in a 9-inch pie pan until well combined.

### Step 2: Combine Liquids
3. In a small cup, mix the **½ cup vegetable oil** and **2 Tbsp milk** together.

### Step 3: Press and Shape
4. Pour the liquid mixture over the dry ingredients in the pan.
5. Mix with a fork until all the **flour** is moistened and the dough comes together with no dry spots showing.
6. Using your fingers or the back of a spoon, press the dough evenly and firmly onto the bottom and up the sides of the pan until it forms a uniform crust.
7. Either fill and bake according to your pie recipe, or prick the crust all over with a fork and pre-bake at 425°F for **15 minutes** until golden and set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The unbaked pressed crust can be covered and refrigerated for up to 24 hours before filling and baking.
