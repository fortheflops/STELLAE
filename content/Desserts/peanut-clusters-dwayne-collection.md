# Peanut Clusters

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic, effortless no-bake confection featuring crunchy salted peanuts coated in a rich blend of melted chocolate and almond bark. Perfect for holiday gifting or a quick sweet treat.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 10 mins | 30 mins | 36 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Double boiler
* Wax paper
* Baking sheet
* Spoon

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 24 oz | [[Almond Bark]] |  |
| 12 oz | [[Chocolate Chips]] | semi-sweet |
| 12 oz | [[Milk Chocolate Chips]] |  |
| 1 1/2 lbs | [[Salted Peanuts]] |  |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Melting
1. In a double boiler over low heat, combine the **24 oz almond bark**, **12 oz chocolate chips**, and **12 oz milk chocolate chips**, stirring frequently until the mixture is completely melted, glossy, and smooth.

### Step 2: Mixing and Portioning
2. Remove the melted chocolate mixture from the heat and stir in the **1 1/2 lbs salted peanuts** until they are thoroughly coated.
3. Drop the mixture by spoonfuls onto sheets of **wax paper** and let stand at room temperature until firm and completely set, about 30 minutes.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature or in the refrigerator for up to 2 weeks.
