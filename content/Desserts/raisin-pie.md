# Raisin Pie

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A delightful traditional raisin pie featuring a rich spiced filling and a naturally formed meringue top. This classic dessert bakes into a set, flavorful treat with warm notes of cinnamon and allspice.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 45 mins | None | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Pie plate
* Electric mixer

---
## Ingredients

### Filling Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 4 | [[Whole Eggs]] | separated |
| 2 cups | [[Sugar]] | granulated |
| 2 Tablespoons | [[Butter]] | softened |
| 3 tablespoons | [[Vinegar]] | standard |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Cinnamon]] | ground |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Allspice]] | ground |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Cloves]] | ground |
| 1 cup | [[Raisins]] | whole |
| 1 | [[Pie Crust]] | unbaked |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing and Preparation
1. Cream the **2 Tablespoons butter** and **2 cups sugar** until light and fluffy.
2. Add beaten egg yolks and **3 tablespoons vinegar**, then add **1 teaspoon cinnamon**, **1 teaspoon alspice**, **1 teaspoon cloves**, and **1 cup raisins**.
3. Beat well and fold in separately beaten egg whites.

### Step 2: Baking
4. Pour filling into an **1 unbaked pie crust**.
5. Cook slowly until thoroughly set, noting that this pie forms its own meringue while cooking.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The pie can be baked ahead of time and stored in the refrigerator for up to 2 days before serving.
