# Easy Chocolate Wheaties Clusters (Geng-a-lings)

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A simple and crunchy no-bake treat featuring chocolate-coated cereal clusters. These classic Geng-a-lings are the perfect quick dessert for any occasion.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 5 mins | 30 mins | 2-3 dozen clusters |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* baking sheet
* double boiler or microwave-safe bowl
* large mixing bowl
* spoon

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 4 Cups | [[Wheaties]] | cereal |
| 1 (12 Ounce) Package | [[Chocolate Chips]] | semi-sweet or milk |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare and Melt
1. Line a baking sheet with **wax paper**.
2. Melt the **12 ounce package chocolate chips** in a double boiler or in the microwave in 30-second intervals until smooth and glossy.

### Step 2: Combine and Set
3. In a large bowl, pour the melted **chocolate chips** over the **4 cups wheaties**. Stir gently until the cereal is evenly and completely coated.
4. Drop spoonfuls of the mixture onto the prepared **wax paper**.
5. Allow the clusters to cool and harden at room temperature until firm, or place in the refrigerator to speed up the setting process.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 1 week or in the refrigerator for up to 2 weeks.
