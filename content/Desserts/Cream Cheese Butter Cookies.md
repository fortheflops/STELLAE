# Cream Cheese Butter Cookies

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic 4-ingredient butter cookie dough enriched with cream cheese for an incredibly tender, soft texture. This versatile base is perfect for roll-and-cut shapes, thumbprints, or traditional rugelach.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 15 mins | 1 hour | 3-4 dozen cookies |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* large mixing bowl
* electric mixer
* plastic wrap
* rolling pin
* cookie cutters
* ungreased cookie sheet
* wire cooling rack

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 Pound (1 Cup) | [[Butter]] | softened |
| 1 (8-ounce) package | [[Cream Cheese]] | softened |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Sugar]] | granulated |
| 2 Cups (scant) | [[Flour]] | all-purpose, remove 1-2 tablespoons |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Dough
1. In a large bowl, cream the **1/2 pound butter**, **1 package cream cheese**, and **1/2 cup sugar** together with an electric mixer until the mixture is light and fluffy.
2. On low speed, gradually add the **2 cups flour** to the creamed mixture, mixing until a soft dough forms.
3. Gather the dough into a ball, wrap it in plastic wrap, and chill in the refrigerator for at least 1 hour.

### Step 2: Roll and Bake
4. Preheat your oven to 350°F.
5. On a lightly floured surface, roll the chilled dough out to your desired thickness (1/8 to 1/4 inch).
6. Cut the dough into shapes with cookie cutters and place them on an ungreased cookie sheet.
7. Bake for **12-15 minutes**, or until the edges are just beginning to turn golden.
8. Let the cookies cool on the baking sheet for a few minutes before transferring them to a wire rack to cool completely.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The dough can be prepared and kept chilled for up to 2 days before rolling, or frozen for up to 1 month.
