# Pineapple Drop Cookies

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic recipe for soft, cake-like pineapple drop cookies. This old-fashioned treat features brown sugar and well-drained crushed pineapple for a moist, chewy texture.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and Mixing
1. Preheat your oven to 400°F and grease your cookie sheets.
2. In a medium bowl, sift together the **2 cups flour**, **1 1/2 tsp baking powder**, **1/4 tsp baking soda**, and **1 tsp salt**.
3. In a large bowl, cream the **2/3 cup shortening**. Gradually add the **1 1/4 cups brown sugar** and beat until the mixture is light and fluffy.
4. Add the **2 eggs**, one at a time, beating well after each addition.
5. Stir in the **3/4 cup pineapple** and **1 tsp vanilla**.
6. Gradually add the dry ingredients to the wet mixture, mixing until just combined.

### Step 2: Baking
7. Drop by rounded teaspoonfuls onto the prepared cookie sheets.
8. Bake for **8 to 10 minutes**, or until the edges are lightly browned and the centers are set.
