# Creamy Cottage Cheese and Pineapple Jello Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic no-bake fluff salad featuring a clever shortcut of mixing dry gelatin powder directly into a creamy base of cottage cheese and pineapple. This tangy, sweet treat sets up in the refrigerator for a refreshing dessert.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 0 mins | 2-4 hours | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large mixing bowl
* Silicone spatula

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 Cups | [[Cottage Cheese]] | Standard |
| 2 Cups | [[Pineapple Bits]] | Well-drained |
| 2 Cups | [[Cool Whip]] | Thawed |
| 1 Pkg. (3 oz) | [[Dry Jello]] | Any flavor like orange, strawberry, or lime |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Combine Base
1. In a large bowl, combine the **2 Cups cottage cheese**, **2 Cups pineapple bits**, and **2 Cups Cool Whip**. Stir until the mixture is uniform and creamy.

### Step 2: Incorporate Gelatin
2. Sprinkle the **1 Pkg. dry jello** powder directly over the mixture.
3. Stir thoroughly until the **dry jello** is evenly distributed and no dry spots remain.

### Step 3: Chill and Set
4. Cover the bowl and refrigerate for at least **2-4 hours** until the mixture is set, chilled, and the flavors have melded together.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This salad is best made at least 4 hours in advance to allow the gelatin to dissolve and the texture to set properly. Store covered in the refrigerator for up to 3 days.
