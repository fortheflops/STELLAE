# Svarale's Refrigerator Cookies

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Svarale | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic, large-batch recipe for rich, buttery slice-and-bake refrigerator cookies. This simple shortbread-style dough is loaded with pecans and chilled overnight for perfect slicing.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 12 mins | Overnight | 8-10 dozen cookies |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* large mixing bowl
* electric mixer
* wax paper
* sharp knife
* cookie sheet
* wire cooling rack

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 Pound (4 sticks) | [[Butter]] | softened |
| 1 1/2 Cups | [[Sugar]] | granulated |
| 3 | [[Eggs]] | large |
| 5 Cups | [[Flour]] | all-purpose |
| 1 Cup | [[Pecans]] | chopped |
| 1 Teaspoon | [[Vanilla]] | extract |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Dough
1. In a very large bowl, cream the **1 pound (4 sticks) butter** and **1 1/2 cups sugar** together with an electric mixer until light and fluffy.
2. Add the **3 eggs** one at a time, beating well after each addition.
3. On low speed, gradually add the **5 cups flour**, mixing until a very stiff dough forms.
4. By hand, stir in the **1 cup pecans** and **1 teaspoon vanilla**.

### Step 2: Chill and Shape
5. Divide the dough into 3 or 4 equal portions. On a piece of wax paper, shape each portion into a log about 1 1/2 to 2 inches in diameter.
6. Wrap the logs tightly in the wax paper and chill overnight, or until very firm.

### Step 3: Slice and Bake
7. Preheat your oven to 350°F.
8. Unwrap one chilled dough log and use a sharp knife to slice it into 1/8-inch thick rounds.
9. Place the slices on an ungreased cookie sheet and bake for **10-12 minutes**, or until the edges are lightly golden and fragrant.
10. Let the cookies cool on the sheet for a minute before transferring them to a wire rack to cool completely.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The dough logs can be kept in the refrigerator for several days or frozen for up to one month before slicing and baking.
