# A Cake for All Seasons (Bourbon Fruitcake)

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A rich, classic fruitcake featuring walnuts, cherries, and raisins soaked in bourbon, baked into a dense, buttery cake, and aged in a bourbon-soaked cheesecloth.*

## Instructions

### Day 1: Soak the Fruit
1. In a large bowl, combine the **chopped walnuts or pecans**, **candied red cherries**, and **light or dark raisins**. Pour **1/2 cup of the bourbon** over them, mix well, cover, and let stand at room temperature overnight.

### Day 2: Bake the Cake
2. Preheat your oven to **325°F**. Generously grease and flour a 10-inch Bundt or tube pan.
3. In a medium bowl, sift together the **sifted all purpose flour**, **baking powder**, **salt**, and **nutmeg**. Pour this over the soaked fruit mixture and toss with your hands until all the fruit and nuts are well coated with flour.
4. In a separate large mixer bowl, beat the **butter or margarine**, **sugar**, and **vanilla** until light and fluffy.
5. Add the **eggs** one at a time, beating well after each addition. After the last egg is added, continue to beat at medium speed for **4 minutes**.
6. By hand, using a sturdy wooden spoon, fold the butter and egg batter into the floured fruit mixture. Combine well until thoroughly mixed; the final batter will be very thick.
7. Turn the batter into the prepared pan and smooth the top.
8. Bake in the center of the oven for **1 hour and 20 minutes**, or until a long wooden skewer inserted into the center comes out clean.
9. Cool the cake on a rack for **20 minutes** before carefully removing it from the pan.
10. While the cake is still warm, soak a large piece of **cheese cloth** in the remaining **1/2 cup of bourbon** and wrap the cake tightly in it.
11. Wrap the cheesecloth-covered cake in foil. Store in the refrigerator for several days to mellow before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the wrapped cake in the refrigerator for several weeks. It needs to mellow for several days before slicing and serving.
