# French Chocolate Silk Pie

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic, rich French-style chocolate silk pie featuring a smooth, whipped filling set in a crisp crust. This decadent dessert is perfect for special occasions and requires simple pantry staples.*

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 0 mins | 3 hours | 8 servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Electric mixer
* Mixing bowl
* Pie dish

---
## Ingredients

### Filling Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 cube | [[Margarine]] | Softened |
| 3/4 cup | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 1 square | [[Unsweetened Chocolate]] | Melted and cooled |
| 1 tsp | [[Vanilla Extract]] | None |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] | None |
| 1 | [[Pie Shell]] | Baked or graham cracker crust |

---

## Instructions

### Preparation
1. Cream the **1 cube margarine** and **3/4 cup sugar** together until light and fluffy.
2. Blend in the **1 square unsweetened chocolate** and **1 tsp vanilla extract**.
3. Add **2 eggs** one at a time, beating for 5 minutes after each addition until the mixture is light and airy.
4. Pour the filling into the **1 baked pie shell** and refrigerate for at least 3 hours until set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This pie must be refrigerated for at least 3 hours before serving to ensure the filling sets properly.
