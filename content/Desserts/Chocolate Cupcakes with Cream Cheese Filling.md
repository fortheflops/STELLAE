# Chocolate Cupcakes with Cream Cheese Filling

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *Indulge in easy chocolate cupcakes made from a cake mix, featuring a surprise hidden filling of sweet cream cheese, coconut, and chocolate chips. These delightful treats bake into a moist, impressive dessert that yields about 35 cupcakes.*

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

### For the Filling
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |

| 1 large | [[Egg]] | beaten |
| 1/2 cup | [[Sugar]] | granulated |
| 1 (6 oz) package | [[Chocolate Chips]] | semi-sweet |
| 1 cup | [[Coconut]] | flaked |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Batter
1. Preheat oven to **350°F**. Line cupcake pans with paper liners.
2. Prepare the **1 package chocolate cake mix** according to the package directions.
3. Fill the cupcake liners only about **halfway** full with the cake batter.

### Step 2: Make the Filling and Assemble
4. **Prepare the Filling:** In a separate bowl, beat together the **1 (8 oz) package cream cheese**, **1 large egg**, and **1/2 cup sugar** with an electric mixer until smooth.
5. By hand, stir in the **1 (6 oz) package chocolate chips** and **1 cup coconut**.
6. Drop about **one heaping teaspoon** of the filling mixture on top of the batter in each cupcake cup.

### Step 3: Bake
7. Bake for **20-25 minutes**, or until the cake portion is cooked through and a toothpick inserted into the cake (avoiding the filling) comes out clean and fragrant.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftover cupcakes in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 4 days due to the cream cheese filling.
