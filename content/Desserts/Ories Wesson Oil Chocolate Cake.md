# Orie's Wesson Oil Chocolate Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Orie | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A wonderfully simple and incredibly moist chocolate cake from Orie, made with oil and buttermilk. This classic recipe comes together easily for a crowd-pleasing dessert.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 35 mins | None | 1 9x13 inch cake |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 9x13 inch baking pan
* Large bowl
* Medium bowl
* Whisk
* Spatula

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3 cups | [[Flour]] | All-purpose |
| 10 Tbsp | [[Cocoa]] | Cocoa powder |
| 2 cups | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 2 tsp | [[Salt]] | None |
| 2 tsp | [[Baking Soda]] | None |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] | Large |
| 1 cup | [[Wesson Oil]] | Or other vegetable oil |
| 3 cups | [[Buttermilk]] | None |
| 4 tsp | [[Vanilla]] | Extract |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation
1. Preheat your oven to **350°F**. Grease and flour a **9x13 inch baking pan**.

### Step 2: Mixing Batter
2. In a large bowl, sift or whisk together the dry ingredients: **3 cups flour**, **10 Tbsp cocoa**, **2 cups sugar**, **2 tsp salt**, and **2 tsp baking soda**.
3. In a separate medium bowl, whisk together the **2 eggs**, **1 cup Wesson oil**, **3 cups buttermilk**, and **4 tsp vanilla**.
4. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients and mix with a spatula or whisk until just combined.

### Step 3: Baking
5. Pour the batter into the prepared pan.
6. Bake for **30-35 minutes**, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean and the cake springs back slightly to the touch. Let cool completely before frosting.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the cooled cake tightly covered at room temperature for up to 3 days, or refrigerate for up to 5 days.
