# 14 Karat Brownies

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Five | ✍️ **Attribution:** Nancy Hunt | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *Indulge in these rich 14 Karat Brownies featuring layers of German chocolate cake, gooey melted caramel, and chocolate chips. Perfect for chocolate lovers seeking a decadent and comforting treat.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 26 mins | None | 12 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 9x13 pan
* Saucepan
* Mixing bowl
* Spoon

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 14 oz | [[Caramels]] | Pkg. |
| 2/3 cup | [[Evaporated Milk]] | Divided |
| 1 pkg. | [[German Chocolate Cake Mix]] | Dry |
| 3/4 cup | [[Margarine]] | Melted (or butter) |
| 1 cup | [[Pecans]] | Chopped |
| 6 oz | [[Chocolate Chips]] | Pkg. |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Caramel Sauce
1. Melt **14 oz caramels** with **1/3 c. evap. milk**. Stir and cook until smooth. Keep warm.

### Step 2: Base and First Bake
2. Mix **1 pkg. German Choc. Cake mix** with **1/3 c. evap. milk** and **3/4 c. margarine melted** with a spoon.
3. Stir in **1 c. chopped pecans**.
4. Put 1/2 of the batter in a **9x13 pan**.
5. Bake 6 min. at **350°**. Take out.

### Step 3: Assembly and Final Bake
6. Sprinkle with **6 oz pkg. choc. chips**.
7. Spread warm carmel mixture over chips.
8. Then put rest of cake mix over carmel (real thick -- spread with hands).
9. Bake at **350°** for **20 min** (looks underdone -- but don't overbake). Cool.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 3 days or refrigerate for longer shelf life.
