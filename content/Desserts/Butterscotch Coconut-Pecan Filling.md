# Butterscotch Coconut-Pecan Filling

> 📜 **Collection:** General Archive | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A rich, chewy, and gooey butterscotch filling or frosting, packed with coconut and nuts. This cooked topping is similar in style to the classic frosting for a German chocolate cake.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5 mins | 10 mins | None | Topping for one 9x13 inch cake |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* medium saucepan
* wire whisk

| 1/3 Cup | [[Water]] | none |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Butterscotch Morsels]] | none |
| 1 | [[Egg Yolk]] | beaten |
| 2 Tablespoons | [[Butter]] | none |

| 1 Cup | [[Nuts]] | chopped |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Base
1. In a medium saucepan, whisk together the **1/2 cup sugar** and **1 tablespoon cornstarch**. Gradually whisk in the **1/2 cup evaporated milk** and **1/3 cup water** until the mixture is smooth.

### Step 2: Cook and Temper
2. Stir in the **1/2 cup butterscotch morsels**. Cook over medium heat, stirring constantly, until the mixture thickens and begins to bubble.
3. Temper the **1 beaten egg yolk** by slowly whisking a small amount of the hot mixture into it. Pour the tempered **egg yolk** mixture back into the saucepan, stirring constantly, and cook for **1-2 minutes** until the filling is very thick.

### Step 3: Finish and Cool
4. Remove the saucepan from the heat and immediately stir in the **2 tablespoons butter**, **1 cup coconut**, and **1 cup nuts** until the **butter** is melted and the mixture is well combined.
5. Let the filling cool completely until it reaches a spreadable consistency before applying to your cake.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days; bring to room temperature before spreading.
