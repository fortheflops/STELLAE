# Classic Fruit Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Eighteen | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A traditional rich fruit cake loaded with candied peel, raisins, and a touch of rum. Baked to golden perfection with a fragrant crumb.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing the Batter
1. Put in **250 gr. soft butter** into a mixing bowl.
2. Keep mixing, add **200 gr. sugar**, **4 eggs**, and **4 tbsp rum**. Don't stop mixing for approximately 5 minutes until fluffy and pale.
3. Add **0.125 liter milk**, **1 lb. flour**, and **1 pk. Backin (yeast)**. Mix well, adding a spoon of **1 lb. flour** and a spoon of **0.125 liter milk** alternately.

### Step 2: Adding Fruit and Baking
4. Add **40 gr. candied peel** and **300 gr. raisins**. Toss the raisins in flour first to avoid sticking.
5. Put the batter into a greased bake pan.
6. Bake at 175 to 195 Centigrade for 65 to 75 minutes until golden brown and a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to a week, or wrap tightly and refrigerate.
