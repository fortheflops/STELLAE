# Margarite's Quick Pineapple Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Margarite | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A decadent and super-moist pineapple sheet cake featuring a baked-on brown sugar and pecan topping, saturated with a hot, buttery buttermilk glaze.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Mix Cake
1. Preheat your oven to **350°F**. Grease and flour a **9x13 inch pan**.
2. In a large bowl, mix the **2 cups flour**, **1 ½ cups sugar**, **2 tsp baking soda**, **2 eggs**, and **1 (20 oz) can crushed pineapple** together until just combined.
3. Pour the batter into the prepared pan.

### Step 2: Add Topping and Bake
4. Sprinkle the **1 cup brown sugar** and **1 cup pecans** evenly over the top of the raw batter.
5. Bake for **25 to 30 minutes**, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.

### Step 3: Prepare Glaze and Finish
6. While the cake is baking, prepare the glaze. In a saucepan, combine the **1 ½ cups sugar**, **¾ cup buttermilk**, and **½ stick (¼ cup) oleo**.
7. Bring to a rolling boil, then remove the pan from the heat and stir in the **1 tsp vanilla**.
8. As soon as the cake comes out of the oven, pour the hot glaze evenly over the hot cake until fully absorbed. Let the cake cool completely in the pan before serving.
