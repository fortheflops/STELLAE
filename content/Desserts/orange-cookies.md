# Orange Cookies

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *Deliciously bright citrus treats flavored with fresh orange juice and grated rind. These homemade cookies offer a delightful burst of sweet fruit flavor in every bite.*

## Ingredients

### Dough Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3 c. | [[Flour]] | Sifted |
| 1 c. | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] | Whole |
| 1 1/2 tsp | [[Baking Powder]] | Leavening agent |
| 3/4 c. | [[Butter]] | Softened |
| 1 | [[Orange]] | Juiced |
| As needed | [[Orange Rind]] | Grated |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and Mixing
1. Cream together the **3/4 c. butter** and **1 c. sugar** until smooth.
2. Beat in the **2 eggs** one at a time, then stir in the **juice of 1 orange** and **grated rind** until well combined.

### Step 2: Baking
3. Sift together the **3 c. sifted flour** and **1 1/2 tsp b. powder**, then gradually mix into the wet ingredients to form a soft dough.
4. Drop spoonfuls onto a prepared baking sheet and bake until edges are lightly golden and set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The dough can be prepared in advance, wrapped tightly, and refrigerated for up to 2 days before baking.
