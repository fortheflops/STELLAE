# Impossible Pumpkin Pie

> 📜 **Collection:** PatsyCollection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A magical crustless pie from Patsy's collection that creates its own golden crust as it bakes! This clever 'Impossible Pie' uses Bisquick to form a seamless base for a smooth, richly spiced pumpkin custard.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Blend
1. Preheat your oven to **350°F (175°C)**. Grease a **9 or 10-inch pie plate** thoroughly.
2. Place **3/4 cup sugar**, **1/2 cup Bisquick**, **2 Tbsp oleo or butter**, **1 can (13oz) evaporated milk**, **2 eggs**, **1 can (16oz) pumpkin**, **2 tsp vanilla**, and **2 tsp pumpkin pie spice** into a blender or a large mixing bowl.
3. Blend for **1 minute**, or beat with a hand mixer for **2 minutes**, until the batter is completely smooth and uniform.

### Step 2: Bake and Cool
4. Pour the smooth batter into the prepared **pie plate**.
5. Bake for **50 to 55 minutes**, or until a knife inserted near the center comes out clean and the edges are set.
6. Let the pie cool completely on a wire rack for at least **2 hours** until firm. Serve chilled or at room temperature.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftover pie covered in the refrigerator for up to 4 days.
