# Ina Marris's Chocolate Angel Pie

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Ina Marris | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A heavenly dessert featuring a crisp, sweet meringue shell layered with a rich coating of melted chocolate and filled with light, fluffy whipped cream. This recipe makes two stunning pies.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 25 mins | 1 hour | Several hours or overnight | 2 9-inch pies |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Two 9-inch pie pans
* Large mixing bowl
* Chilled bowl
* Electric mixer

### Meringue Shells

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 pkg (12 oz) | [[Chocolate Chips]] | semi-sweet |
| 2 pints (4 cups) | [[Whipping Cream]] | heavy |

---

## Instructions

### Make the Meringue Shells
1. Preheat oven to **275°F**. Generously spray two **9-inch pie pans** with **cooking spray**.
2. In a large, clean bowl, beat the **8 egg whites** until foamy.
3. Gradually add the **2 cups sugar**, beating on high speed until the meringue is very stiff, glossy, and holds its shape.
4. Divide the meringue between the **two pans**, spreading it evenly to form two shells across the bottom and sides.
5. Bake for **1 hour** until set.
6. Turn the oven **off** and let the shells cool completely in the closed oven for several hours or overnight without opening the door.

### Assemble the Pies
7. Melt the **semi-sweet chocolate chips** and let the chocolate cool completely to room temperature.
8. In a chilled bowl, whip the **4 cups heavy whipping cream** until stiff peaks form.
9. Carefully spread the cooled, melted chocolate over the bottom of each cooled meringue shell until coated.
10. Fill each shell with the whipped cream until mounded.
11. Refrigerate for at least **2-4 hours** until set before slicing and serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the finished pies in the refrigerator for up to 2 days; leaving the shells to cool slowly in the oven is the key to preventing cracks.
