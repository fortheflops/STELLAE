# No-Bake Peanut Butter Fruit Squares

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A simple, no-bake confection featuring a chewy blend of ground dates, raisins, and currants bound with creamy peanut butter. These wholesome fruit squares are chilled until firm and finished with a delicate dusting of confectioners' sugar.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 0 mins | 2 hours | 1 1/3 lbs |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 8x8x2 inch pan
* food processor
* medium bowl
* sturdy spoon

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 Cup | [[Dates]] | pitted |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Raisins]] | seedless |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Currants]] | none |
| 1 Cup | [[Peanut Butter]] | none |
| 1/4 Cup | [[Milk]] | sweetened condensed |
| As needed | [[Sugar]] | confectioners' for dusting |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Base
1. Butter an 8x8x2 inch pan and sprinkle the bottom and sides with **confectioners' sugar**.

### Step 2: Process the Fruit
2. Using a food processor, process the **1 cup pitted dates**, **1/2 cup seedless raisins**, and **1/2 cup currants** until they form a thick, uniform paste.

### Step 3: Combine and Press
3. In a medium bowl, combine the fruit paste with **1 cup peanut butter** and **1/4 cup sweetened condensed milk**, mixing until thoroughly combined.
4. Press the mixture firmly and evenly into the prepared pan and smooth the surface.

### Step 4: Chill and Serve
5. Sprinkle the top generously with more **confectioners' sugar**.
6. Chill in the refrigerator for at least **2 hours** until the mixture is firm to the touch.
7. Once firm, cut into squares to serve.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to one week.
