# Chocolate Sheet Cake (Sheath Cake)

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic Texas-style Chocolate Sheet Cake topped with a warm, fudgy pecan frosting poured over the cake right out of the oven.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Batter
1. Preheat your oven to **400°F**. Grease a large cookie sheet or jelly roll pan (approx. 11x16 inches).
2. In a large mixing bowl, combine the **2 cups [[Flour]]** and **2 cups [[Sugar]]**.
3. In a saucepan, combine the **1 stick (1/2 cup) [[Oleo]]**, **1/2 cup [[Crisco]]**, **1 cup [[Water]]**, and **4 tbsp. [[Cocoa]]**. Bring this mixture to a boil.
4. Pour the hot chocolate mixture over the flour and sugar, and mix well.
5. In a separate small bowl, combine the **1/2 cup [[Buttermilk]]**, **1 tsp [[Baking Soda]]**, **2 [[Eggs]]**, and **1 tsp [[Vanilla]]**. Add this to the chocolate batter and stir until well combined.

### Step 2: Bake the Cake
6. Pour the batter into the prepared pan and bake for **20 minutes** until set and golden brown around the edges.

### Step 3: Make the Icing and Finish
7. About 5 minutes before the cake is done, begin the icing. In a saucepan, bring the **4 tbsp [[Cocoa]]**, **6 tbsp [[Milk]]**, and **1 stick (1/2 cup) [[Oleo]]** to a boil.
8. Remove from the heat and immediately stir in the **1 box (1 lb) [[Powdered Sugar]]**, **1 tsp [[Vanilla]]**, and **1 cup [[Pecans]]** until the icing is smooth.
9. As soon as the cake comes out of the oven, pour the hot icing over the hot cake and spread evenly until glossy and set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the cooled cake covered at room temperature for up to 3 days, or refrigerate for up to 5 days.
