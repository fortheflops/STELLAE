# Chocolate Cream Pudding

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A rich and velvety homemade chocolate cream pudding finished with whipped egg whites. This classic chilled dessert offers a deeply satisfying cocoa flavor and a wonderfully smooth texture.*

## Ingredients

### Pudding Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 cups | [[Milk]] | Scalded |
| 5 tbsp | [[Cornstarch]] | None |
| 1/2 cup | [[Sugar]] | None |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Salt]] | None |
| 1/3 cup | [[Cold milk]] | None |
| 1 1/2 oz | [[Chocolate]] | None |
| 3 tbsp | [[Hot water]] | None |
| 1 tsp | [[Vanilla]] | None |
| 3 | [[Egg whites]] | Beaten stiff |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Making the Base
1. Mix **5 tbsp cornstarch**, **1/2 cup sugar**, and **1/4 tsp salt** together, then dilute with **1/3 cup cold milk**.
2. Add the mixture to **2 cups scaled milk** and cook over hot water until thickened, stirring constantly until it coats the back of a spoon.

### Step 2: Adding Chocolate and Finishing
3. Melt **1 1/2 oz chocolate**, add **3 tbsp hot water**, stir until smooth, and add to the warm pudding mixture along with **1 tsp vanilla**.
4. Fold in the **3 egg whites** that have been beaten stiff with a pinch of salt until fully incorporated and light.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Chill in individual ramekins covered with plastic wrap for up to 2 days before serving.
