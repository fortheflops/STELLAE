# Japanese Fruit Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A delightful two-layer spiced fruit cake featuring a rich lemon-coconut filling. Perfect for special occasions and traditional baking enthusiasts.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 30 mins | 35 mins | None | 10 servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowls
* Saucepan
* Two 9-inch cake pans

## Ingredients

### Cake Batter
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 cup | [[Butter]] | Softened |
| 2 cups | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 3 1/4 cups | [[Flour]] | Sifted |
| 1 cup | [[Milk]] | Whole milk |
| 4 | [[Eggs]] | Whole |
| 1 tsp | [[Baking Powder]] | None |
| 1 tbsp | [[Vanilla]] | Extract |

### Spiced Batter Additions (for second layer)
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 tsp | [[Cinnamon]] | Ground |
| 1 tsp | [[Allspice]] | Ground |
| 1/2 tsp | [[Cloves]] | Ground |
| 1/4 lb | [[Raisins]] | Seeded and chopped |

### Lemon Coconut Filling

| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Juice of 2 | [[Lemons]] | Freshly squeezed |
| Rind of 1 | [[Lemon]] | Grated |
| 1 | [[Coconut]] | Grated |
| 2 cups | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 1 cup | [[Water]] | Boiling |
| 2 tbsp | [[Cornstarch]] | Dissolved in 1/3 cup cold water |

## Instructions

### Step 1: Making the Batter
1. Cream together the **1 cup butter** and **2 cups sugar**.
2. Add the **4 eggs**, **1 tbsp vanilla**, **1 cup milk**, and **3 1/4 cups flour** mixed with the **1 tsp baking powder**, beating well to form a smooth batter.

### Step 2: Dividing and Baking
3. Divide the batter into two equal parts.
4. In one portion of the batter, stir in **1 tsp cinnamon**, **1 tsp allspice**, **1/2 tsp cloves**, and **1/4 lb seeded raisins (chopped)**.
5. Bake the plain and spiced batters in two separate cake layers until golden and a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.

### Step 3: Preparing the Filling
6. Combine the **juice of 2 lemons**, **grated rind of 1 lemon**, **1 grated coconut**, **2 cups sugar**, and **1 cup boiling water** in a saucepan.
7. When it boils, add the **2 tbsp cornstarch** (previously dissolved well in **1/3 cup cold water**).
8. Cook the mixture, stirring constantly until thick.
9. Cool and spread between the layers of the cake.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the frosted cake in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 2 days, or refrigerate for up to 5 days.
