# Jill's Tender Pie Crust

> 📜 **Collection:** JC | ✍️ **Attribution:** Jill | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *Discover a classic recipe for an exceptionally tender and flaky pie crust crafted with vegetable shortening, an egg, and a hint of lemon juice. This versatile dough yields enough for three single 9-inch crusts, ensuring golden, melt-in-your-mouth results.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 0 mins | 30 mins | Three single 9-inch crusts |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large bowl
* Small bowl
* Pastry blender
* Fork
* Plastic wrap

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 cups | [[All-Purpose Flour]] | Sifted |
| 2/3 cup | [[Vegetable Shortening]] | Chilled |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Baking Powder]] | None |
| 1/2 teaspoon | [[Salt]] | None |
| 1 | [[Egg]] | Beaten |
| 2 tablespoons | [[Lemon Juice]] | None |
| 3 tablespoons | [[Water]] | Cold |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Dry Ingredients and First Cut
1. In a large bowl, sift together the **2 cups all-purpose flour**, **1 teaspoon baking powder**, and **1/2 teaspoon salt**.
2. Add one-third of the **2/3 cup vegetable shortening** to the flour mixture. Using a pastry blender or your fingers, cut in the shortening until the mixture resembles fine cornmeal.

### Step 2: Combining and Mixing
3. Add the remaining **vegetable shortening** and continue cutting it in until the particles are the size of small peas.
4. In a separate small bowl, beat the **1 egg**, **2 tablespoons lemon juice**, and **3 tablespoons water** together until combined.
5. Pour the liquid ingredients over the flour mixture and mix lightly with a fork until a dough just comes together, ensuring you do not overmix.

### Step 3: Shaping and Chilling
6. Divide the dough into three equal portions, shape each into a disk, wrap tightly in plastic wrap, and chill in the refrigerator for at least **30 minutes** before rolling out into a smooth, pliable pastry.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Wrap shaped dough disks tightly in plastic wrap and store in the refrigerator for up to 3 days, or freeze for up to 3 months before thawing overnight in the refrigerator.
