# Brown Sugar Nut Tea Cakes

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A unique and delicious tea cake with a rich, chewy, brownie-like texture. This simple recipe gets its lift entirely from beaten eggs and is loaded with brown sugar and nuts.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 15 mins | None | 2-3 dozen cookies |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Cookie sheets
* Electric mixer
* Large bowl
* Spatula
* Wire rack

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1½ cups | [[Cake Flour]] | Sifted |
| ½ tsp | [[Salt]] | None |
| 3 | [[Eggs]] | Large |
| 1 cup | [[Brown Sugar]] | Packed light |
| ¾ cup | [[Nuts]] | Chopped |
| ⅓ cup | [[Butter]] | Melted |
| ½ tsp | [[Vanilla]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Batter
1. Preheat your oven to **350°F**. Grease your cookie sheets.
2. Sift the **1½ cups cake flour**, measure it, then sift it again with the **½ tsp salt**.
3. In a large bowl, beat the **3 eggs** with an electric mixer on high speed until they are thick and lemon-colored, about **3 to 5 minutes**.
4. Gradually beat in the **1 cup brown sugar**.
5. On low speed, mix in the **¾ cup nuts**, **⅓ cup butter**, and **½ tsp vanilla**.
6. Gently fold in the **cake flour** and **salt** mixture with a spatula until just combined.

### Step 2: Bake and Cool
7. Drop by rounded teaspoons far apart on the prepared cookie sheets, as they will spread.
8. Bake for **12 to 15 minutes**, or until the edges are set and the centers are just barely firm.
9. Let them cool on the pan for a minute before transferring to a wire rack until completely cooled.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 5 days to maintain their chewy, dense texture.
