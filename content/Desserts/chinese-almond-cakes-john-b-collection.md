# Chinese Almond Cakes

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *Traditional Chinese almond cookies made with simple pantry staples and topped with a whole blanched almond. These buttery, crumbly treats are lightly scented with almond extract and baked until golden.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing the Dough
1. Sift into a large bowl **2 1/2 cups flour**, **3/4 cup sugar**, **1/4 tsp salt**, and **1 tsp baking powder**.
2. Blend in with a pastry cutter **3/4 cup lard**.
3. Beat together in a separate small container **1 egg**, **2 tbsp water**, and **1 1/2 tsp almond extract**, then add to the flour mixture.
4. Mix as you would pastry and when thoroughly blended form into balls about the size of a walnut.

### Step 2: Shaping and Baking
5. Place balls on a cookie sheet and press with the heel of the hand to flatten.
6. Press one **whole blanched almond** into the top of each cookie.
7. Brush cookies with **slightly beaten egg white**.
8. Bake at 350°F for **20-25 minutes** until set and lightly golden around the edges.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store baked cookies in an airtight container at room temperature for up to one week.
