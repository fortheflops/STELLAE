# Texas Sheet Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic Texas-style sheet cake with a rich, boiled cocoa frosting that's poured over the cake while it's still hot.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Bake the Cake
1. Preheat your oven to **400°F**. Grease an **11x16 inch sheet pan**.
2. In a large mixing bowl, combine the **2 cups [[Flour]]** and **2 cups [[Sugar]]**.
3. In a saucepan, combine the **1 stick (1/2 cup) [[Oleo]]**, **1/2 cup [[Shortening]]**, **1 cup [[Water]]**, and **3 1/2 tbsps [[Cocoa]]**. Bring this mixture to a boil.
4. Pour the hot chocolate mixture over the flour and sugar, and mix well.
5. In a separate small bowl, combine the **1/2 cup [[Buttermilk]]**, **1 teaspoon [[Baking Soda]]**, **2 [[Eggs]]**, and **1 teaspoon [[Vanilla]]**. Add this to the chocolate batter and stir until well combined.
6. Pour the batter into the prepared pan and bake for **20 minutes** until the center springs back and is done.

### Step 2: Prepare the Icing and Finish
7. About **5 minutes** before the cake is done, begin the icing.
8. In a saucepan, bring the **3 1/2 tbsps [[Cocoa]]**, **1/3 cup [[Milk]]**, and **1 stick (1/2 cup) [[Oleo]]** to a boil.
9. Remove from the heat and immediately stir in the **1 box (1 lb) [[Powdered Sugar]]**, **1 teaspoon [[Vanilla]]**, and **1 cup [[Nuts]]** until the icing is smooth.
10. As soon as the cake comes out of the oven, pour the hot icing over the hot cake and spread evenly.
11. Let the cake cool completely in the pan before cutting and serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the cake covered at room temperature or in the refrigerator for up to 4 days.
