# Boysenberry Dessert

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Mrs. Allen High | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A delightful layered boysenberry dessert featuring a graham cracker crust, a rich marshmallow cream filling, and a spiced berry topping. Perfect for serving a crowd, this chilled treat offers a wonderful contrast of textures.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 25 mins | 10 mins | 2 hours | 15 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Saucepan
* Mixing bowl
* Baking pan

---
## Ingredients

### Berry Layer
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 1/2 cups | [[Boysenberry]] | and juice |
| 1/4 cup | [[Sugar]] | None |
| 2 rounded tbsp | [[Cornstarch]] | None |

### Crust & Filling
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15-20 | [[Graham crackers]] | Crushed into crumbs |
| 1/2 cup | [[Butter]] | Melted |
| 1/2 lb | [[Marshmallows]] | Divided |
| 1 cup | [[Milk]] | None |
| 1 cup | [[Whipped cream]] | Or 1 package Lucky Whip |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Making the Berry Layer
1. Bring the **1 1/2 cups boysenberry and juice** to a boil.
2. Dissolve the **2 rounded tbsp cornstarch** and **1/4 cup sugar** in a small amount of **water**, then add to the boiling berries and juice.
3. Cook until thick, about **4-5 minutes**, stirring constantly until the mixture coats the back of a spoon, then let cool completely.

### Step 2: Preparing the Marshmallow Filling
4. Cook the **1 cup milk** and **1/2 lb marshmallows** together in a saucepan until the marshmallows are completely melted and smooth.
5. Let cool, then fold in the **1 cup whipped cream** (or **1 package Lucky Whip**).

### Step 3: Assembly and Chilling
6. Mix the **15-20 graham cracker crumbs** and **1/2 cup butter** together, and press most of the mixture into the bottom of a pan.
7. Pour in half of the **marshmallow mixture** over the crumbs and cool until set.
8. Pour over all of the **berry mix**, cover with the remaining **marshmallows mix**, and top with the remaining **graham cracker crumbs**.
9. Place in the refrigerator (or ice box) until completely set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Keep refrigerated until ready to serve; this dessert tastes even better when chilled for at least 4 hours.
