## Instructions
1.  Preheat oven to 400°F.
2.  Melt $\frac{1}{2}$ cube (4 tablespoons) of margarine in a baking pan (e.g., an 8x8 inch or similar size).
3.  Add one cup of cut-up peaches to the pan with the melted margarine.
4.  Sprinkle "some spice" (such as cinnamon or allspice, to taste) over the peaches.
5.  In a separate bowl, combine one cup of flour, $\frac{1}{4}$ teaspoon of salt, one teaspoon of baking powder, one cup of sugar (use less if using canned peaches), and some nutmeg (to taste).
6.  Stir in $\frac{1}{2}$ cup of milk to form a dough/batter.
7.  Pour the dough over the peaches in the baking pan.
8.  Bake at 400°F for about 25 minutes, or until the topping is golden brown and cooked through.
___

>[!Note]
>The amount of sugar can be reduced if using canned peaches. The card mentions adding "some spice" to the peaches.
