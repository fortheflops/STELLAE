___
### Instructions
1. Melt together: 1 Can fruit, 1 Cup sugar, 4 Tablespoons margarine.
2. Mix: 3/4 Cup flour, 3/4 Cup sugar, 3/4 Cup milk, 1 1/2 Teaspoons baking powder, salt.
3. Stir up & pour over fruit mixture.
4. Bake 350° until golden brown.
