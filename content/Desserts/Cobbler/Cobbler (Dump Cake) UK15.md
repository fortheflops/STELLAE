## Instructions
(The following instructions are inferred from the ingredient list, as only layering hints are provided.)
1.  Preheat the oven to 350°F.
2.  Spread the crushed pineapple (with its juice) in the bottom of a 9x13 inch pan.
3.  Arrange a layer of peaches over the pineapple.
4.  Sprinkle the dry cake mix evenly over the fruit.
5.  Arrange another layer of peaches on top of the cake mix.
6.  Sprinkle pecans and the 1/4 cup of sugar over the top.
7.  Drizzle melted butter evenly over everything.
8.  Bake for 35 to 45 minutes, or until the top is golden brown and the fruit is bubbly.
