## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 9 1/2 ounces               | [[Flour]]                                 |
| 2 1/2 tablespoons          | [[Sugar]]                                 |
| 1 tablespoon               | [[Lemon Zest]]                            |
| 1 teaspoon                 | [[Salt]]                                  |
| 4 1/2 ounces               | Chilled [[Butter]]                        |
| 1 1/2 ounces               | [[Lard]]                                  |
| 1 to 3 tablespoons         | Ice [[Water]]                             |
| As Needed                  | Frozen [[Fruit]] (for filling)            |

___
## Instructions
### For the Crust
1.  In a food processor, combine the flour, sugar, lemon zest, and salt. Pulse a few times to combine.
2.  Cut the chilled butter and lard into small pieces and add them to the flour mixture.
3.  Pulse until the mixture gets crumbly.
4.  Using a spritzer or adding one tablespoon at a time, add 1 to 3 tablespoons of ice water, pulsing until the dough just comes together.
5.  Form the dough into a disk, wrap it in plastic, and refrigerate for 30 minutes.
### Assembly (Inferred)
1.  Preheat the oven to 375°F.
2.  Place your desired amount of frozen fruit in a 9-inch baking dish.
3.  Roll out the chilled dough and place it on top of the fruit, either as a solid sheet or torn into rustic "cobbles."
4.  Bake for 30-40 minutes, or until the crust is golden brown and the fruit filling is bubbly.
