# Easy Mincemeat Fruit Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *An incredibly simple and moist fruitcake that uses the convenience of prepared mincemeat and sweetened condensed milk. This dump cake style recipe is packed with candied fruit and walnuts.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 2 hours | None | 1 9-inch tube cake |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 9- or 10-inch tube pan
* Parchment or wax paper
* Very large bowl
* Separate bowl
* Wire rack
* Long skewer

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 1/2 Cups | [[Flour]] | Sifted, all-purpose |
| 1 Teaspoon | [[Baking Soda]] | None |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] | Large, slightly beaten |
| 1 (14 oz) Can | [[Eagle Brand Milk]] | Sweetened condensed milk |
| 1 (28 oz) Jar | [[Mincemeat]] | Prepared |
| 2 Cups | [[Candied Fruit]] | Mixed |
| 1 Cup | [[Walnuts]] | Chopped |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and Dry Ingredients
1. Preheat your oven to **300°F**. Grease a 9- or 10-inch tube pan and line it with greased parchment or wax paper.
2. In a separate bowl, sift together the **2 1/2 cups flour** and **1 teaspoon baking soda**.

### Step 2: Mixing the Batter
3. In a very large bowl, combine the **2 eggs**, **1 (14 oz) can eagle brand milk**, **1 (28 oz) jar mincemeat**, **2 cups candied fruit**, and **1 cup walnuts**. Mix well.
4. Fold the sifted dry ingredients into the fruit mixture until just combined. The batter will be very thick.
5. Turn the batter into the prepared, lined pan and spread evenly.

### Step 3: Baking and Cooling
6. Bake for **2 hours**, or until the center springs back when lightly touched and a long skewer inserted into the center comes out clean.
7. Let the cake cool in the pan for **5 minutes**.
8. Turn the cake out onto a wire rack to cool completely before slicing.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the cooled fruitcake tightly wrapped in plastic wrap or foil in an airtight container at room temperature or in the refrigerator for up to several weeks.
