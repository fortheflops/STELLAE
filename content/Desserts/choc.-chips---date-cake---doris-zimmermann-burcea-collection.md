# Choc. Chips - Date Cake - Doris Zimmermann

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Cream
1. Mix **1 1/4 cups chopped dates** and **1 cup hot water** together and set aside to cool.
2. In a separate bowl, cream **3/4 cup butter** and **1 cup sugar** together until light and fluffy.
3. Beat in **2 eggs** to the creamed mixture.

### Step 2: Combine and Bake
4. Sift together **2 cups sifted flour**, **1 tsp baking soda**, and **1/2 tsp salt**; add to the creamed mixture alternately with the cooled date mixture.
5. Stir in **1 tsp vanilla extract** and **1/2 cup chocolate chips** (save some for topping).
6. Spread the batter evenly in a **9x13 pan**.
7. Sprinkle the remaining **1/2 cup chocolate chips** and **1/2 cup chopped nuts** over the top.
8. Bake at 350°F for about **35 minutes**, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean and the top is golden brown.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the baked cake in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 3 days or freeze for up to 2 months.
