## Instructions

### Crust Instructions
1. Mix **1 stick oleo**, **1 c flour**, and **1/2 c pecans** together and press into a 9x13 pan.
2. Bake at 350° for **10-12 minutes**, then remove and let cool completely until golden and set.

### Second Layer Instructions
3. Mix **8 oz cream cheese**, **1 c powdered sugar**, and **1 c cool whip** together.
4. Spread evenly over the cooled crust and cool in the refrigerator for 3 hours.

### Third Layer Instructions
5. Mix **2 pkgs chocolate lemon instant pudding**, **3 c milk**, and **1 tsp vanilla** together until smooth.
6. Pour over the cream cheese layer and cool for 1 hour until set.

### Fourth Layer Instructions
7. Put **1 1/2 c cool whip** on top of the third layer and return to the refrigerator to chill.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Keep refrigerated until ready to serve; can be prepared a day in advance.
