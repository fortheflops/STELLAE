## Recipe
| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| Three Baskets | Fresh [[Strawberries]] |
| One Envelope | [[Gelatin]] (Unflavored) |
| One or Two | [[Lemons]] (Juice of) |
| $\frac{1}{4}$ Cup | [[Sugar]] |
| $\frac{1}{2}$ Cup + 2 Tbsp | [[Water]] |
___
## Instructions
1.  Wash, trim, and puree the **strawberries**.
2.  In a small bowl, sprinkle the **gelatin** over the **lemon juice** and let it soften for 5 minutes.
3.  In a saucepan, boil the **sugar** and **water** for 5 minutes to create a syrup.
4.  Add the softened gelatin to the hot syrup and stir until dissolved.
5.  Once the syrup has cooled, stir in the strawberry puree.
6.  Freeze the mixture until solid.
7.  Remove from the freezer, break into chunks, and beat with a mixer until fluffy.
8.  Return to the freezer and freeze again until firm.
___

>[!Note]
