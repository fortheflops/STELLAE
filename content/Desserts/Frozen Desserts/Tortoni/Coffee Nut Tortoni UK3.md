## Recipe
| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 1 Cup | Heavy Cream |
| $\frac{1}{4}$ Cup | [[Sugar]] |
| 1 Tbsp | [[Instant Coffee]] |
| 1 tsp | [[Vanilla Extract]] |
| Few Drops | [[Almond Extract]] |
| 1 | [[Egg White]] |
| 2 Tbsp | [[Sugar]] |
| $\frac{1}{4}$ Cup | Finely Chopped [[Almonds]], Toasted |
| $\frac{1}{4}$ Cup | [[Coconut]], Toasted and Crumbled |
| Optional Garnish | [[Maraschino Cherry]] |
___
## Instructions
1.  In a chilled bowl, whip the **heavy cream** until thickened. Gently fold in **$\frac{1}{4}$ cup of sugar**, **instant coffee**, **vanilla extract**, and **almond extract**.
2.  In a separate clean bowl, beat the **egg white** until soft peaks form. Gradually add **2 tablespoons of sugar** and continue beating until a stiff, glossy meringue forms.
3.  Gently fold the meringue and *half* of the toasted **almonds** and **coconut** mixture into the whipped cream mixture.
4.  Spoon the mixture into 8 individual soufflé cups or paper-lined muffin cups.
5.  Sprinkle the remaining half of the almond/coconut mixture over the tops.
6.  Freeze until firm.
7.  Garnish with a **cherry** before serving, if desired.
___

>[!Warning]
>This recipe contains a raw egg white. For those with health concerns, consider using pasteurized egg whites.
