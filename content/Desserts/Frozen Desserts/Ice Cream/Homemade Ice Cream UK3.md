*A classic and simple no-cook recipe for creamy homemade ice cream, perfect with fresh bananas or strawberries.*
### Author
Unknown
___
## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 6 Cups | [[Half & Half]] |
| 2 Cups | [[Whole Milk]] |
| 1 Can | [[Sweetened Condensed Milk]] |
| 1 1/2 Cups | [[Sugar]] |
| 3 Tablespoons | [[Vanilla Extract]] |
| 4-6 | Mashed [[Bananas]] |
| *or* 1 Quart | Mashed [[Strawberries]] |
___
## Instructions
1.  In a large bowl, whisk together the **Half & Half**, **whole milk**, **sweetened condensed milk**, **sugar**, and **vanilla** until the sugar is dissolved.
2.  Stir in the mashed **bananas** or **strawberries**.
3.  Pour the mixture into an ice cream freezer.
4.  Freeze according to the manufacturer's directions.
___

>[!Note]
>Makes 4 Quarts.
