## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 Gallon | [[Chocolate Milk]] |
| 1 (9-12 Ounce) Carton | [[Refrigerated Whipped Cream Topping]] |
| 1 Can | [[Condensed Milk]] |
___
## Instructions
1.  In a large bowl, mix the **chocolate milk**, **whipped topping**, and **condensed milk** until well combined.
2.  Pour the mixture into an ice cream freezer.
3.  Freeze according to the manufacturer's directions.
