## Recipe
| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 7 Cups | [[Whipping Cream]] |
| 2 Cups | [[Sugar]] |
| 2 Tbsp | [[Vanilla]] |
| 2 Tbsp | [[Lemon Juice]] |
| 2 (13 oz) cans | [[Evaporated Milk]] |
___
## Instructions
1.  In a large bowl, stir together the **sugar**, **vanilla**, & **lemon juice**.
2.  Gradually whisk in the **evaporated milk** until smooth.
3.  Stir in the **whipping cream**.
4.  Refrigerate the mixture until well chilled.
5.  Pour into an ice cream freezer container.
6.  Churn and freeze according to the manufacturer's directions.
