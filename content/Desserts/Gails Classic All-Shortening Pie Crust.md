# Gail's Classic All-Shortening Pie Crust

> 📜 **Collection:** GC | ✍️ **Attribution:** Gail | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *Discover Gail's classic all-shortening pie crust recipe, designed for achieving an exceptionally flaky texture in any sweet or savory pie. This simple preparation uses chilled ingredients for optimal pastry results.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 0 mins | 30 mins | One double-crust 9-inch pie |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large bowl
* Pastry blender
* Fork
* Wax paper or plastic wrap
* Rolling pin

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 Cups | [[Flour]] | All-purpose |
| 1 Cup | [[Shortening]] | Chilled |
| 1/2 Teaspoon | [[Salt]] | None |
| 1/4 Cup (or more) | [[Water]] | Ice cold |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Combine Dry Ingredients
1. In a large bowl, combine the **2 cups flour** and **1/2 teaspoon salt**.

### Step 2: Cut in Shortening
2. Add the chilled **1 cup shortening** to the flour. Using a pastry blender, two knives, or your fingertips, cut the shortening into the flour until the mixture resembles coarse crumbs with some pea-sized pieces remaining.

### Step 3: Add Water and Form Dough
3. Sprinkle the **1/4 cup (or more) ice water** over the mixture, one tablespoon at a time, tossing gently with a fork until the dough just begins to hold together.

### Step 4: Shape and Chill
4. Gather the dough into a ball, divide it in half, and shape each half into a flat disk. Wrap each disk in wax paper or plastic wrap, and chill the dough for at least 30 minutes until firm before rolling it out on a lightly floured surface.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The wrapped dough disks can be refrigerated for up to 3 days or frozen for up to 3 months before thawing and rolling.
