# Classic New York-Style Cheesecake

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A rich, dense, and creamy crustless cheesecake that uses a slow oven cooldown method to help prevent cracks. Chill overnight for the smoothest texture.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 1 hour 10 mins | 5 hours | 12-16 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 9-inch springform pan
* Electric mixer
* Large mixing bowl

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 1/2 lbs | [[Cream Cheese]] | three 8-ounce packages, softened |
| 1 1/2 cups | [[Sugar]] | granulated |
| 3 tablespoons | [[Flour]] | all-purpose |
| 1/8 teaspoon | [[Salt]] | none |
| 4 | [[Eggs]] | large, room temperature |
| 1/4 cup | [[Milk]] | none |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Vanilla]] | extract |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Mix Base
1. Preheat your oven to **375°F** and ensure all ingredients are at room temperature.
2. In a large bowl, beat the **1 1/2 lbs cream cheese** and **1 1/2 cups sugar** with an electric mixer on low speed until very smooth and creamy.

### Step 2: Incorporate Dry Ingredients and Eggs
3. Mix in the **3 tablespoons flour** and **1/8 teaspoon salt** on low speed.
4. Add the **1 teaspoon vanilla**, then add the **4 eggs** one at a time, mixing on low speed after each addition just until combined without overbeating.

### Step 3: Bake and Cool
5. Stir in the **1/4 cup milk**, then pour the batter into a 9-inch springform pan.
6. Bake at **375°F** for **1 hour and 10 minutes** until the center still has a slight wobble.
7. Turn the oven **OFF** and let the cheesecake sit in the closed, warm oven for another hour to cool down slowly and prevent cracking.
8. Remove from the oven, cool completely, and chill in the refrigerator for at least 4 hours before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store sealed in the refrigerator for up to 5 days, or freeze for up to a month.
