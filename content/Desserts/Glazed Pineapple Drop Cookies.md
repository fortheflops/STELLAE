# Glazed Pineapple Drop Cookies

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A soft, cake-like drop cookie bursting with tropical flavor. This recipe features crushed pineapple in the batter and is finished with a sweet, buttery pineapple glaze and a sprinkle of nuts.*

## Ingredients

### For the Cookies

| 3 cups | [[Flour]] | all-purpose |
| 1 tsp | [[Baking Soda]] | None |
| 1/2 tsp | [[Salt]] | None |
| 1/2 cup | [[Butter]] | softened |
| 1 1/2 cups | [[Sugar]] | None |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] | large |
| 1 tsp | [[Almond Extract]] | None |

### For the Glaze & Topping
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/4 cup | [[Butter]] | soft |
| 2 cups | [[Confectioners' Sugar]] | None |
| 3 tbsp | [[Pineapple Juice]] | reserved from canned pineapple |
| 1/2 cup | [[Nuts]] | chopped |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare and Mix
1. Preheat your oven to 350°F and grease your cookie sheets. Drain the **1 (20 oz) can crushed pineapple** through a sieve, reserving the **3 tbsp pineapple juice** for the glaze.
2. In a medium bowl, sift together the **3 cups flour**, **1/2 tsp salt**, and **1 tsp baking soda**.
3. In a large bowl, cream the **1/2 cup butter** and **1 1/2 cups sugar** until light and fluffy. Beat in the **2 eggs**.
4. Gradually add the dry ingredients to the creamed mixture, beating until just blended, then fold in the drained **crushed pineapple** and **1 tsp almond extract**.

### Step 2: Bake and Glaze
5. Drop by tablespoonfuls 2 inches apart onto the prepared sheets and bake for **10-12 minutes** until the edges are lightly golden. Cool completely on a wire rack.
6. In a small bowl, combine the **1/4 cup butter**, **2 cups confectioners' sugar**, and **3 tbsp pineapple juice**. Beat until smooth.
7. Spread the glaze over the cooled cookies and immediately sprinkle with **1/2 cup nuts**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 3 days or freeze unglazed cookies for up to 1 month.
