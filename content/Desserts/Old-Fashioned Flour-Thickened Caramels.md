# Old-Fashioned Flour-Thickened Caramels

> 📜 **Collection:** JohnBCollection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A unique vintage caramel recipe featuring a distinct technique where flour is kneaded into butter to create a chewy, rich texture. This archival confection is perfect for those seeking a traditional, old-fashioned candy-making experience.*

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* large pot
* greased pan
* mixing bowl

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 Cups | [[Sugar]] | granulated |
| 1 1/3 Cups | [[Cream]] | divided |
| 2/3 Cup | White [[Corn Syrup]] | light |
| 1/6 Pound | [[Butter]] | softened |
| 1 tsp | [[Vanilla]] | extract |
| As needed | [[Flour]] | all-purpose |
| Optional | [[Nuts]] | chopped |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Thickener
1. Knead as much **[[Flour]]** into the **1/6 pound [[Butter]]** as it will hold until the mixture forms a wad about the size of a small orange.

### Step 2: Boil the Syrup
2. In a large pot, combine the **2 cups [[Sugar]]**, **2/3 cup [[Corn Syrup]]**, and **2/3 cup [[Cream]]** (half of the total amount). Cook for **5 minutes** over medium heat.

### Step 3: Final Cook
3. Add the remaining **2/3 cup [[Cream]]** and continue to cook until the mixture reaches the firm-ball or soft-crack stage, testing by dropping a small amount into cold water until it cracks.
4. Stir in the **[[Butter]]** and **[[Flour]]** mixture until the caramel is completely smooth and incorporated.
5. Remove from heat and stir in the **1 tsp [[Vanilla]]** and optional **[[Nuts]]**.

### Step 4: Set and Serve
6. Pour the mixture into a greased pan and allow to cool completely until firm, then cut into squares.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store finished caramels in an airtight container at room temperature for up to two weeks, or wrap individually in wax paper.
