# Peggie's Almond Oatmeal Macaroons

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Five | ✍️ **Attribution:** Peggie | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *Delightful almond oatmeal macaroons made with thin flaked oats and brown sugar, left to stand overnight for the best texture. These cookies bake up golden brown with a sweet nutty aroma.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 12 mins | 8 hours | 24 cookies |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Cookie sheet
* Spoon

## Ingredients

### Mix Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 4 cups | [[Thin flaked oats]] | Dry |
| 2 cups | [[Brown sugar]] | Packed |
| 1 cup | [[Vegetable oil]] | None |

### Add in AM
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] | Beaten |
| 1/2 tsp | [[Salt]] | None |
| 1 tsp | [[Almond extract]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing and Resting
1. In a large bowl, mix **4 cups thin flaked oats**, **2 cups brown sugar**, and **1 cup vegetable oil** together.
2. Let the mixture stand overnight.

### Step 2: Adding and Baking
3. In the morning, add **2 eggs**, **1/2 tsp salt**, and **1 tsp almond extract** to the oat mixture and stir well.
4. Drop the batter from a spoon onto a greased cooky sheet.
5. Bake at 350°F to 375°F until the edges are golden brown and set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The initial oat, brown sugar, and oil mixture can be left to stand overnight before adding the remaining ingredients.
