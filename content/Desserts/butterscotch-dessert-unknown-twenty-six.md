## Ingredients

### Crust
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 cup | [[Flour]] | All-purpose |
| 1/2 cup | [[Margarine]] | Softened |
| 1/3 cup | [[Nuts]] | Chopped |

### Second Layer
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 pkg (8 oz) | [[Cream Cheese]] | Philadelphia brand, softened |
| 1 cup | [[Powdered Sugar]] | None |
| 1 cup | [[Cool Whip]] | None |

### Third Layer & Topping
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 pkg | [[Instant Butterscotch Pudding]] | None |

| 3 cups | [[Milk]] | None |
| As needed | [[Cool Whip]] | For topping |
| As needed | [[Nuts]] | For garnish |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Crust Preparation
1. Preheat the oven to 350°F.
2. Mix together the **1 cup flour**, **1/2 cup margarine**, and **1/3 cup nuts**.
3. Press the mixture into a baking dish and bake at 350° for **15 mins** until lightly golden. Allow to cool completely.

### Step 2: Second Layer
4. In a bowl, beat the **1 pkg (8 oz) cream cheese** and **1 cup powdered sugar** together until smooth.
5. Fold in the **1 cup cool whip** and spread this mixture evenly over the cooled crust.

### Step 3: Third Layer and Assembly
6. Whisk together the **1 pkg instant butterscotch pudding**, **1 pkg vanilla pudding**, and **3 cups milk** until thickened.
7. Pour the pudding mixture evenly over the second layer.
8. Top with additional **cool whip** and sprinkle with **nuts**. Chill in the refrigerator until set before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Keep refrigerated and best served within 24 hours of preparation.
