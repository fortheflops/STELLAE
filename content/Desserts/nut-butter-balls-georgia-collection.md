# Nut Butter Balls

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare and Chill the Dough
1. In a large mixing bowl, mix well the **1 cup softened butter**, **1/4 to 1/2 cup sugar**, **1/2 teaspoon salt**, and **1 teaspoon almond extract (or 2 teaspoons vanilla extract)**.
2. Gradually blend in the **2 cups sifted flour** and **1 to 2 cups chopped walnuts, pecans, almonds, or hazelnuts** until a cohesive dough forms.
3. Place the dough in the refrigerator and chill until it is firm enough to be easily handled.

### Step 2: Shape, Bake, and Roll
4. Preheat your oven to **350°F**.
5. Shape the chilled dough into 1-inch balls or small rolls and arrange them on an ungreased cookie tin.
6. Bake at **350°F** for **12 to 15 minutes** until they are set and light brown on the bottom.
7. While still warm, roll the baked cookies in **additional sugar** to coat them thoroughly.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The cookie dough can be prepared, wrapped tightly, and refrigerated for up to 3 days, or frozen for up to 3 months before shaping and baking.
