# Date-Nut Rocks

> 📜 **Collection:** General Archive | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic, old-fashioned drop cookie packed with an incredible amount of dates, raisins, and nuts. These dense, chewy treats are full of warm spice flavor and perfect for holiday gifting.*

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and Mixing
1. Preheat oven to **375°F** and grease cookie sheets.
2. In a medium bowl, sift together **1 1/2 cups flour**, **1/4 tsp baking soda**, **1/2 tsp baking powder**, **1/4 tsp salt**, **1/2 tsp cloves**, **1 tsp cinnamon**, and **1/2 tsp allspice**.
3. In a large bowl, cream **2/3 cup shortening** and **1 cup brown sugar** until light and fluffy. Add **2 eggs** and mix well.

### Step 2: Combining and Chilling
4. Add the dry ingredients to the creamed mixture alternately with **2 tbsp sherry or brandy**, mixing until just combined.
5. Fold in **2 cups dates**, **2 cups raisins**, and **2 cups nuts** by hand.
6. Cover the dough and chill for at least one hour until firm to the touch.

### Step 3: Baking
7. Drop by teaspoonfuls 2-3 inches apart onto prepared sheets. Bake for **12 to 15 minutes** until the edges are set and the cookies are fragrant.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The dough can be chilled for up to 24 hours before baking, or frozen in pre-portioned balls for up to 3 months.
