# Cherry Chip Loaf or Bundt

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Nine | ✍️ **Attribution:** Judy Cardields | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A moist and flavorful loaf cake featuring sweet maraschino cherries and rich chocolate chips. This versatile recipe can be baked as two loaves or in a decorative bundt pan.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 75 mins | None | 2 loaves |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Loaf pans or Bundt pan
* Electric mixer

## Ingredients

### Batter Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 tsp | [[Baking soda]] | none |
| 16 oz | [[Sour cream]] | none |
| 3 | [[Eggs]] | none |
| 2 sticks | [[Butter]] | softened |
| 2 cups | [[Sugar]] | none |
| 3 cups | [[Flour]] | none |
| 1 tsp | [[Vanilla extract]] | none |
| 1 cup | [[Maraschino cherries]] | chopped |
| 1 cup | [[Chocolate chips]] | none |
| 1 cup | [[Nuts]] | optional, chopped |

---

## Instructions

### Preparation
1. In a small bowl, mix **1 tsp baking soda** into **16 oz sour cream** and set aside.
2. In a large mixing bowl, cream together **2 sticks butter**, **2 cups sugar**, and **3 eggs** until light and fluffy.
3. Add **3 cups flour** and **1 tsp vanilla extract** to the butter mixture and mix well.
4. Fold in the **sour cream mixture** and combine thoroughly.
5. Gently fold in **1 cup maraschino cherries**, **1 cup chocolate chips**, and optional **1 cup nuts**.
6. Pour the batter into prepared loaf pans or a bundt pan.
7. Bake at 350°F for 1 hour and 15 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This cake stays moist for several days if wrapped tightly in plastic wrap at room temperature.
