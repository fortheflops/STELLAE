# Choc. Ice Box Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** Burcea Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A delightful chilled dessert featuring rich chocolate, whipped cream, and ground macaroons. This classic icebox cake sets smoothly in the refrigerator for an effortless, decadent treat.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 30 mins | 5 mins | 4 hours | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Double boiler
* Ice box

---
## Ingredients

### Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 4 | [[Eggs]] | Separated |
| 1/2 pt. | [[Whipping Cream]] | Whipped |
| 2 bars | [[German Sweet Chocolate]] | Or 2 pkg chocolate chips |
| 2 tbsp. | [[Powdered Sugar]] | Divided |
| 1/2 lb. | [[Macaroons]] | Ground |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation
1. Melt **2 bars German Sweet Chocolate** over hot water.
2. Add **2 tbsp. water** to chocolate.
3. Well beat in **4 egg yolks**, 1 at a time, slightly beaten.
4. Add **2 tbsp. powdered sugar**.
5. Add **4 egg whites**, beaten.
6. Last add **1/2 pt. whipped cream**.
7. Line dish with crumbs. Save a few crumbs for top.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Keep refrigerated in the ice box for at least 4 hours or overnight until fully set before serving.
