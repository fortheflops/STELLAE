# M. Field's Apple Torte

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** M. Field | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A wonderfully simple and rustic apple torte from a recipe by M. Field, featuring a dense, moist, fruit-and-nut-packed interior with a crisp, sugary crust.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 45 mins | None | 8-10 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large bowl
* 8x12x2 inch baking pan
* Sifter
* Electric mixer or spoon

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| ⅔ Cup | [[Flour]] | Sifted all-purpose |
| 3 Teaspoons | [[Baking Powder]] | None |
| ½ Teaspoon | [[Salt]] | None |
| 1 Cup | [[Sugar]] | None |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] | Large |
| 3 Teaspoons | [[Vanilla Extract]] | None |
| 1 Cup | [[Nuts]] | Chopped |
| 2 Cups | [[Apple]] | Cubed |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation
1. Preheat your oven to **350°F**. Grease an **8x12x2 inch baking pan** well.

### Step 2: Make the Batter
2. In a large bowl, sift together the **⅔ cup flour**, **3 teaspoons baking powder**, and **½ teaspoon salt**. Stir in the **1 cup sugar**.
3. Add the **2 eggs** and **3 teaspoons vanilla extract** to the dry ingredients and beat the mixture well until it forms a thick batter.
4. By hand, stir in the **1 cup chopped nuts** and **2 cups cubed apple** until they are evenly distributed.

### Step 3: Bake and Serve
5. Pour the batter into the prepared pan and spread evenly.
6. Bake for **45 minutes** until the torte rises and falls slightly, creating a wonderfully crisp top.
7. Serve warm or at room temperature with ice cream or cream.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store covered at room temperature or in the refrigerator for up to 3 days.
