# Strawberry Chiffon Pie

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A light and airy Strawberry Chiffon Pie featuring a delicate fruit filling folded into stiff egg whites, all housed within a baked pastry shell. Topped with whipped cream and fresh strawberries, this refreshing dessert is perfect for spring and summer gatherings.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparing the Base
1. Soften **1 tbsp gelatine** in **1/4 cup cold water**.
2. Beat **3 egg yolks**, add **1/2 cup sugar**, **1 tbsp lemon juice**, and **1/2 tsp salt**.
3. Cook over hot water until thick, stirring constantly.

### Step 2: Mixing and Congealing
4. Add the softened **1 tbsp gelatine**, **1 cup strawberry pulp and juice**, and a few drops of **red coloring** to the hot egg mixture.
5. Cool the mixture until it is just beginning to congeal.

### Step 3: Folding and Assembling
6. Whip the remaining egg whites and beat in the remaining **1/4 cup sugar** until stiff peaks form.
7. Fold the beaten whites into the congealing strawberry mixture.
8. Pile the mixture into the **1 baked pastry shell**.

### Step 4: Serving
9. Chill until completely set.
10. Before serving, cover with a thin layer of **1 cup whipped cream** and garnish with **1/2 cup strawberries**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Chill the pie for at least 2 hours until set. Add whipped cream and fresh strawberries just before serving for the best texture.
