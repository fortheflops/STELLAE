# Frozen Strawberry Mousse Dessert

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A light and airy no-bake frozen dessert featuring a fluffy strawberry mousse made with whipped egg whites, set atop and below a crunchy brown sugar and pecan crust.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 0 mins | 6 hours | 12 servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 9x13 inch pan
* Electric mixer
* Large mixing bowl

## Ingredients

### Crust
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 cup | [[Flour]] | all-purpose |
| 1/4 cup | [[Brown Sugar]] | packed |
| 1/2 cup | [[Pecans]] | chopped |
| 1/2 cup | [[Butter]] | melted |

### Filling
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 | [[Egg Whites]] | large, pasteurized recommended |
| 1 cup | [[Sugar]] | granulated |
| 1 (10 oz) package | [[Strawberries]] | frozen, partially thawed |
| 2 tablespoons | [[Lemon Juice]] | fresh |
| 1 pint | [[Heavy Cream]] | whipped or 16 oz whipped topping |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Crust
1. In a bowl, combine the **1 cup flour**, **1/4 cup brown sugar**, **1/2 cup chopped pecans**, and **1/2 cup melted butter**. Mix until crumbly.
2. Reserve **1/3** of the mixture for the topping and press the remaining **2/3** firmly into the bottom of a **9x13 inch pan**.

### Step 2: Prepare the Filling
3. In a large mixing bowl, combine the **2 egg whites**, **1 cup sugar**, **10 oz partially thawed strawberries**, and **2 tablespoons lemon juice**.
4. Beat with an electric mixer at high speed for **10 minutes** until the mixture is very thick, fluffy, and holds stiff peaks.
5. Gently fold in the **1 pint whipped heavy cream** until just combined.

### Step 3: Assemble and Freeze
6. Pour the strawberry filling over the prepared crust and spread evenly.
7. Sprinkle the reserved **1/3 crust mixture** evenly over the top.
8. Cover and freeze for at least **6 hours**, or overnight, until firm to the touch.
9. Let sit at room temperature for a few minutes before slicing to serve.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This dessert must be frozen for at least 6 hours; it can be kept in the freezer for up to 2 weeks if tightly covered.
