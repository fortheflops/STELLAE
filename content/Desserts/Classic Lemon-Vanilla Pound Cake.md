# Classic Lemon-Vanilla Pound Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** UK15 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic, dense pound cake featuring a rich, buttery flavor and tender crumb. Brightly flavored with vanilla and lemon extracts.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 1 hour 20 mins | 15 mins | 1 10-inch tube cake |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 10-inch tube pan
* Large mixing bowl
* Electric mixer
* Separate mixing bowl
* Wire cooling rack
* Wooden skewer

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 sticks (1 cup) | [[Butter]] | softened |
| 1/2 cup | [[Crisco]] | Shortening |
| 3 cups | [[Sugar]] | granulated |
| 5 | [[Eggs]] | large |
| 3 cups | [[Flour]] | all-purpose |
| 1/2 teaspoon | [[Baking Powder]] |  |
| 1/2 teaspoon | [[Salt]] |  |
| 1 cup | [[Milk]] |  |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Vanilla]] | flavoring |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Lemon]] | flavoring |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Cream
1. Preheat your oven to **325°F**. Generously grease and flour a **10-inch tube pan**.
2. In a large bowl, cream the **2 sticks (1 cup) butter**, **1/2 cup Crisco**, and **3 cups sugar** together with an electric mixer until light, fluffy, and pale.
3. Add the **5 eggs** one at a time, beating well after each addition until incorporated.

### Step 2: Combine Dry and Wet Ingredients
4. In a separate bowl, whisk together the **3 cups flour**, **1/2 teaspoon baking powder**, and **1/2 teaspoon salt**.
5. On low speed, add the dry ingredients to the creamed mixture in three parts, alternating with the **1 cup milk**, beginning and ending with the dry ingredients. Mix only until just combined.
6. Stir in the **1 teaspoon vanilla** and **1 teaspoon lemon** flavorings until evenly distributed.

### Step 3: Bake and Cool
7. Pour the batter into the prepared tube pan and spread evenly to level the top.
8. Bake for **1 hour and 20 minutes**, or until a long wooden skewer inserted into the center comes out clean.
9. Let the cake cool in the pan for **15-20 minutes** before turning it out onto a wire rack to cool completely.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the cooled cake tightly wrapped in plastic wrap at room temperature for up to 3 days or freeze for up to 2 months.
