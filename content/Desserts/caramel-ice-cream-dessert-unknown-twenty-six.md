# Caramel Ice Cream Dessert

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A delightful frozen dessert featuring a baked nutty crust layered with softened ice cream and rich caramel topping. Perfect for serving a crowd at any gathering.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 15 mins | 4 hours | 12-15 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Cookie sheet
* 9"x13" pan

---
## Ingredients

### Crust and Toppings
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 cup | [[Butter or Margarine]] | Melted |
| 1 cup | [[Flour]] | Standard |
| 1/4 cup | [[Brown Sugar]] | Packed |
| 1/2 cup | [[Chopped Nuts]] | Chopped |
| 1/2 gallon | [[Ice Cream]] | Slightly softened (Praline & Cream or Melt Caramel Cone recommended) |
| 8 oz | [[Caramel Ice Cream Topping]] | Ready to use |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Baking the Crust
1. Melt the **1/2 cup butter or margarine**.
2. Mix together **1/2 cup butter or margarine**, **1 cup flour**, **1/4 cup brown sugar**, and **1/2 cup chopped nuts**.
3. Spread the mixture evenly on a greased cookie sheet.
4. Bake at 325°F for **15 minutes** until golden and fragrant, then chop into small pieces.

### Step 2: Assembling and Freezing
5. Spread half of the baked crumb mixture on the bottom of a 9"x13" pan.
6. Spread **1/2 gallon ice cream** on top of the mixture.
7. Sprinkle the remaining crumb mixture on top of the ice cream.
8. Drizzle **8 oz caramel ice cream topping** over the top.
9. Place in the freezer until firm and ready to serve.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store covered in the freezer for up to a week before serving.
