# Easy Dessert

> 📜 **Collection:** Elizabeth Knee Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A delightful and refreshing retro dessert featuring orange jello, creamy sherbet, marshmallows, and mandarin oranges. This simple chilled treat sets up in the refrigerator for a light, fruity finish to any meal.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 0 mins | 2 hours | 6 servings |

## Ingredients

### Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 package | [[Orange Jello]] | small |
| 1 pt. | [[Orange Sherbert]] | softened |
| 2 cups | [[Marshmallows]] | little |
| 1 cup | [[Mandarin Oranges]] | canned |

---

## Instructions

### Preparation
1. Dissolve **1 package orange jello** in **1 c. hot water**.
2. Immediately add **1 pt. orange sherbert** and stir until completely dissolved.
3. Refrigerate until slightly set.
4. Fold in **2 cups marshmallows** and **1 cup mandarin oranges**.
5. Return to the refrigerator and let it jell completely until firm.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Keep refrigerated until ready to serve; can be made up to a day in advance.
