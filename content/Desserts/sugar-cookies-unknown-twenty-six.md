# Sugar Cookies

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Jean | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A delightful old-fashioned recipe for classic sugar cookies shared by Jean. These soft and sweet treats are baked at 375 degrees for a comforting homemade dessert.*

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

## Ingredients

### Cookie Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 cup | [[Margarine]] | Softened |
| 1 cup | [[Oil]] | Vegetable oil |
| 1 cup | [[White Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 1 cup | [[Powdered Sugar]] | Confectioners' sugar |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] | Whole eggs |
| 1 tsp | [[Vanilla]] | Vanilla extract |
| 1 tsp | [[Soda]] | Baking soda |
| 1 tsp | [[Cream of Tartar]] |  |
| 4 1/2 cups | [[Flour]] | All-purpose flour |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing and Baking
1. Preheat your oven to 375 degrees.
2. In a large mixing bowl, combine **1 cup margarine**, **1 cup oil**, **1 cup white sugar**, and **1 cup powdered sugar** until smooth.
3. Add **2 eggs** and **1 tsp vanilla**, mixing well.
4. Stir in **1 tsp soda**, **1 tsp cream of tartar**, and gradually add **4 1/2 cups flour** until a dough forms.
5. Drop by spoonfuls onto a baking sheet and bake until lightly golden and set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The dough can be prepared ahead of time and stored in the refrigerator for up to 2 days before baking.
