# Refrigerator Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Katherine Dick | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A delightful no-bake refrigerator cake layered with coconut, chocolate, and vanilla wafer crumbs. Created by Katherine Dick, it requires chilling for 24 hours to set into a sliceable treat.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparing the Base and Filling
1. Cream **1/3 c butter** and blend in **1 c powdered sugar**.
2. Stir in the well beaten **4 egg yolks** and **1 t vanilla**.
3. In a separate bowl, beat **4 egg whites** until stiff, then gradually beat in **1/4 c sugar**.
4. Fold the beaten whites into the **butter** mixture, then divide it into two equal portions.

### Step 2: Flavoring the Batches
5. Add **1/2 c coconut** and **1/4 c nut meats** to one half of the mixture.
6. Add **4 T cocoa** to the other half of the mixture.

### Step 3: Layering and Chilling
7. Roll **28 vanilla wafers** to fine crumbs.
8. Line a loaf pan with waxed paper and cover the bottom with **1/3** of the wafer crumbs.
9. Pour in the **coconut** mixture, then cover with another **1/3** of the crumbs.
10. Pour in the **cocoa** mixture, and cover the top with the remaining **crumbs**.
11. Chill in refrigerator **24 hours** until set.
12. Serve with **whipped cream**, cut in slices, or use **ice cream**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This cake must be made ahead and chilled in the refrigerator for 24 hours before slicing and serving.
