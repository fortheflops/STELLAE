# Mincemeat Coffee Cake

## Instructions

### Step 1: Making the Batter
1. Sift together **1 1/2 cups flour**, **2 tsp. baking powder**, and **1/2 cup sugar**.
2. Cut in **1/3 cup shortening** until the mixture resembles coarse meal.
3. Combine **1 egg**, **1/2 cup milk**, **3/4 cup mincemeat**, **1/2 cup nuts**, and **1/2 cup Kellogg Concentrate Cereal**, then add to the flour mixture.

### Step 2: Baking and Glazing
4. Pat dough into a greased and floured 8 1/2" ring pan or **8x8" pan**.
5. Bake at 375° for **30 minutes** or until golden brown and baked through.
6. Remove from pan while still warm and spread with sugar glaze made by combining **1 cup confectioner sugar** and **1 Tbsp. milk**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Best served warm, but can be baked ahead of time and gently reheated before applying the glaze.
