# Svarale's Thumbprint Cookies

> *A classic recipe featuring a rich, buttery brown sugar dough rolled in crunchy nuts. These thumbprint cookies are finished with a generous dollop of creamy, vanilla-scented cream cheese icing.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Dough
1. Preheat oven to 350°F.
2. In a medium bowl, cream together **1/2 cup butter**, **1/4 cup brown sugar**, **1 egg yolk**, and **1/2 teaspoon vanilla**.
3. Stir in **1 cup flour** and **1/4 teaspoon salt** until a soft dough forms.

### Step 2: Shape and Bake
4. Roll dough into 1-inch balls, dip in **1 egg white**, and roll in **3/4 cup nuts**.
5. Place on an ungreased baking sheet and press a thumbprint into the center.
6. Bake for **10-12 minutes** until edges are lightly golden and set.

### Step 3: Icing and Assembly
7. Beat **8 ounces cream cheese**, **1/4 cup oleo**, and **1 teaspoon vanilla** until smooth.
8. Gradually add **1 lb powdered sugar** until creamy.
9. Once cookies are cool, fill each indentation with the icing.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The dough can be prepared and chilled for up to 24 hours before baking. Store finished cookies in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 5 days.
