# Cherry Upside Down Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic homemade cherry upside down cake featuring a buttery fruit topping and tender crumb. Baked to golden perfection and served warm with a sweet cherry sauce.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparing the Topping
1. Drain **1 no.2 can cherries**, reserving the juice for later.
2. Melt **1/3 cup margarine** in the bottom of an **8-inch square pan**.
3. Add **1/2 cup sugar** and stir over low heat to dissolve the sugar partially.
4. Sprinkle the **cherries** evenly over the sugar and margarine mixture.

### Step 2: Making the Batter
5. Cream **1/2 cup butter** and **1 cup sugar** together until fluffy, then add **2 well beaten eggs** and blend.
6. Sift together **2 cups cake flour**, **2 tsp baking powder**, and **1/2 tsp salt**.
7. Add the dry ingredients alternately to the creamed mixture with **3/4 cup milk**, stirring well.
8. Stir in **1/2 tsp almond extract** and **1/2 tsp vanilla extract**, beating until smooth.
9. Pour the batter over the **cherries** in the pan.

### Step 3: Baking and Serving
10. Bake in a moderate oven at **350°F** for **50-60 minutes** until golden brown and a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.
11. Cool for **5 minutes** in the pan, then carefully turn out upside down onto a deep plate.
12. To make the sauce: blend **1 tbsp cornstarch**, **1/4 cup sugar**, and **1 cup cherry juice**, cooking until thickened.
13. Pour the warm sauce over the cake and serve warm.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Best served warm shortly after baking, but can be stored covered at room temperature for up to 2 days.
