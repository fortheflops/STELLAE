# Classic Cream Puffs

> 📜 **Collection:** LOC | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic recipe for light and airy cream puffs made with a traditional pâte à choux dough. These hollow pastries are perfect for filling with whipped cream, custard, or ice cream.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 55 mins | None | 12 to 14 cream puffs |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 3-quart saucepan
* Wooden spoon
* Baking sheet
* Wire rack

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Butter]] | 1 Stick |
| 1 Cup | [[Boiling Water]] | Hot |
| 1 Cup | [[Flour]] | All-purpose |
| 4 | [[Eggs]] | Unbeaten |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Make the Pâte à Choux Dough
1. Preheat the oven to **450°F**. Grease a baking sheet.
2. In a saucepan, pour the **1 cup boiling water** over the **1/2 cup butter** and heat until the butter melts and the water returns to a boil.
3. Add the **1 cup flour** all at once. Stir constantly with a wooden spoon over low heat until the mixture forms a ball and pulls away from the sides of the pan. Remove from heat.
4. Add the unbeaten **4 eggs**, one at a time, beating vigorously after each addition until the dough is smooth. Continue beating until the mixture is smooth and velvety.

### Step 2: Bake and Fill
5. Drop heaping tablespoons of dough onto the baking sheet, about 2 inches apart.
6. Bake at **450°F for 15 minutes**, or until well puffed and lightly browned.
7. Reduce the heat to **300°F and bake for 30 to 40 minutes longer** to thoroughly dry out the centers without further browning.
8. Remove to a wire rack to cool completely. When cold, cut off the tops with a sharp knife and fill with whipped cream, custard, or ice cream as desired.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Unfilled cream puffs can be stored in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 2 days or frozen for up to 1 month.
