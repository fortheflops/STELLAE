# Frozen Strawberry Jello Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Cheryl Spears | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic frozen dessert salad featuring a fluffy base of strawberry gelatin, whipped cream, bananas, and crunchy pecans. This versatile treat can be frozen in pans or individual muffin cups for easy serving.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 5 mins | 6 hours | 24 servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* large mixing bowl
* whisk
* 8-inch pans
* muffin tins
* paper muffin liners
* electric mixer

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 (3 ounce) package | [[Strawberry Jello]] | powdered |
| 1 cup | [[Boiling Water]] | hot |
| 1 (10 ounce) package | [[Partially Thawed Strawberries]] | with juice |
| 2 small or 1 large | [[Banana]] | sliced |
| 1 cup | [[Heavy Cream]] | whipped until stiff peaks |
| 1 cup | [[Chopped Pecans]] | chopped |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Base
1. In a large bowl, pour **1 cup boiling water** over the **1 (3 ounce) package strawberry jello** and stir for 2 minutes until completely dissolved.
2. Break up the **1 (10 ounce) package partially thawed strawberries** with a fork and stir them into the hot mixture along with their juice.
3. Stir in the **2 small or 1 large banana** and **1 cup chopped pecans**.
4. Chill in the refrigerator for 20-30 minutes until the mixture has thickened slightly but is not fully set.

### Step 2: Fold and Freeze
5. In a separate bowl, whip the **1 cup heavy cream** until stiff peaks form.
6. Gently fold the **whipped cream** into the thickened **jello mixture** until just combined.
7. Pour into two 8-inch pans or spoon into paper-lined muffin tins.
8. Cover and freeze for at least 6 hours until firm to the touch.
9. Remove from the freezer 5-10 minutes before serving to allow the salad to soften slightly to a creamy consistency.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This salad can be frozen for up to 2 weeks; keep covered tightly to prevent freezer burn.
