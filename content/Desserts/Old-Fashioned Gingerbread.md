# Old-Fashioned Gingerbread

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic, dark, and moist gingerbread cake rich with molasses and a warm blend of aromatic spices. This traditional recipe yields a perfect, comforting dessert that pairs beautifully with whipped cream.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 40 mins | None | 1 9x13 inch cake |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* large mixing bowl
* electric mixer
* sifter
* 9x13 inch baking pan

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/3 Cup | [[Fat]] | Shortening or butter, softened |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 1 Cup | [[Molasses]] | Dark |
| 1 | [[Egg]] | Beaten |
| 3 Cups | [[Flour]] | All-purpose |
| 1 Cup | [[Milk]] | Room temperature |
| 1/2 Teaspoon | [[Baking Soda]] | None |
| 1/2 Teaspoon | [[Salt]] | None |
| 1 Teaspoon | [[Ginger]] | Ground |
| 1/2 Teaspoon | [[Cloves]] | Ground |
| 1/2 Teaspoon | [[Cinnamon]] | Ground |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation
1. Preheat your oven to 350°F. Grease and flour a 9x13 inch pan.

### Step 2: Creaming
2. In a large bowl, cream the **1/3 cup fat** and **1/2 cup sugar** together with an electric mixer until light and fluffy. Beat in the **1 cup molasses** and **1 beaten egg** until well combined.

### Step 3: Dry Ingredients
3. In a separate bowl, sift together the **3 cups flour**, **1/2 teaspoon baking soda**, **1/2 teaspoon salt**, **1 teaspoon ginger**, **1/2 teaspoon cloves**, and **1/2 teaspoon cinnamon** three times to ensure even distribution.

### Step 4: Combining
4. On low speed, add the dry mixture to the creamed ingredients in three parts, alternating with the **1 cup milk**, beginning and ending with the dry ingredients. Mix until just combined.

### Step 5: Baking
5. Pour the batter into the prepared pan and bake for **30 to 40 minutes**, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean and the cake springs back lightly to the touch.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the cake in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 3 days or freeze for up to 2 months.
