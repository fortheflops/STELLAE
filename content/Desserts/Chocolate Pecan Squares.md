# Chocolate Pecan Squares

> 📜 **Collection:** John B Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A rich, brownie-like bar cookie loaded with finely chopped pecans. These decadent treats are baked in a square pan and cut into small, bite-sized squares while still warm.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 30 mins | None | 25 squares |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 8x8x2 inch baking pan
* mixing bowls
* saucepan
* waxed paper

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 oz | [[Chocolate]] | melted |
| 1/4 C | [[Butter]] | melted |
| 2 Large | [[Eggs]] | beaten |
| 1 C | [[Sugar]] | granulated |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Salt]] | None |
| 1 1/2 tsp | [[Vanilla]] | extract |
| 1/4 C | [[Milk]] | None |
| 1 C | [[Flour]] | all-purpose |
| 1 C | [[Pecans]] | chopped fine |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Batter
1. Preheat your oven to 350°F.
2. In a small saucepan, melt the **1 oz chocolate** and **1/4 C butter** together, stirring until smooth.
3. In a separate bowl, beat the **2 large eggs** until they are light and lemon-colored, then gradually beat in the **1 C sugar**.
4. Stir in the **1/4 tsp salt**, **1 1/2 tsp vanilla**, and **1/4 C milk** until well combined.
5. Gradually add the **1 C flour** to the mixture, then fold in the **1 C pecans** and the melted chocolate-butter mixture.

### Step 2: Bake and Serve
6. Line a buttered 8x8x2 inch pan with waxed paper and butter the surface of the paper.
7. Pour the batter into the prepared pan and bake at 350°F for **30 minutes** until set.
8. While still warm, cut the bars into **1 1/2 inch squares** before removing them from the pan.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the cooled squares in a tightly covered container at room temperature for up to 5 days.
