# Coconut Cream Cheese Pound Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** UK15 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *An incredibly rich and moist pound cake, made tender with cream cheese and packed with tropical coconut flavor. This dense, buttery dessert is a true classic.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 1 hour 15 mins | 25 mins | 1 10-inch tube cake |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large bowl
* Electric mixer
* Separate bowl
* 10-inch tube pan
* Long wooden skewer
* Wire rack

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 cup | [[Butter]] | softened |
| 1/2 cup | [[Shortening]] | none |
| 1 (8 ounce) package | [[Cream Cheese]] | softened |
| 3 cups | [[Sugar]] | none |
| 6 | [[Eggs]] | large |
| 3 cups | [[Flour]] | all-purpose |
| 1/4 teaspoon | [[Soda]] | baking |
| 1/4 teaspoon | [[Salt]] | none |
| 1 (6 ounce) package | [[Coconut]] | frozen, thawed |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Vanilla Extract]] | none |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Coconut Extract]] | none |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Creaming
1. Preheat the oven to **350°F**. Generously grease and flour a **10-inch tube pan**.
2. In a large bowl, cream the **1/2 cup butter**, **1/2 cup shortening**, and **1 (8 ounce) package cream cheese** together with an electric mixer.
3. Gradually add the **3 cups sugar**, beating at medium speed until the mixture is light and fluffy.
4. Add the **6 eggs**, one at a time, beating well after each addition.

### Step 2: Mixing the Batter
5. In a separate bowl, combine the **3 cups flour**, **1/4 teaspoon soda**, and **1/4 teaspoon salt**.
6. With the mixer on low speed, add the dry ingredients to the creamed mixture, stirring just until blended.
7. Stir in the **1 (6 ounce) package coconut**, **1 teaspoon vanilla extract**, and **1 teaspoon coconut extract** until incorporated into the thick batter.

### Step 3: Baking and Cooling
8. Spoon the thick batter into the prepared pan and spread evenly.
9. Bake for **1 hour and 15 minutes**, or until a long wooden skewer inserted into the center comes out clean.
10. Let the cake cool in the pan for **10 to 15 minutes**.
11. Remove the cake from the pan and let it cool completely on a wire rack until set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the cooled cake tightly wrapped at room temperature for up to 3 days or refrigerate for up to a week.
