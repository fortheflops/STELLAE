# Carrot Cake

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Cake Batter
1. Preheat your oven to **350°F** and grease a **13x9-inch baking pan**.
2. In a large mixing bowl, combine **1 1/2 cups vegetable oil** and **2 cups sugar**.
3. Add **5 eggs** one at a time, beating well after each addition until the mixture is smooth and slightly pale.
4. Add **2 1/4 cups flour**, **2 tsp baking soda**, **1 tsp salt**, and **2 tsp vanilla extract** to the bowl. Mix until just combined.
5. Gently fold and blend in **2 cups grated carrots** and **1 cup crushed pineapple** until evenly distributed.

### Step 2: Bake and Cool
6. Pour the batter into the greased **13x9-inch baking pan**.
7. Bake in the preheated **350°F** oven for **1 hour**, or until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean and the cake springs back lightly when touched.
8. Remove from the oven and let the cake cool completely in the pan before icing.

### Step 3: Ice and Serve
9. In a medium bowl, beat together **1 (8 oz) package cream cheese**, **1/2 stick margarine**, **1 box powdered sugar**, and **2 tsp vanilla extract** until smooth, fluffy, and spreadable.
10. Spread the icing evenly over the cooled cake directly in the pan.
11. Leave the cake in the pan, and keep it refrigerated unless serving immediately.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Leave the cake in the pan, ice it, and keep it refrigerated unless served immediately. It stores beautifully in the fridge for up to 3-4 days.
