# Incredible Pudding Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** UK15 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Moonlite | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *Indulge in a magical self-saucing chocolate pudding cake featuring a tender top layer and a rich, hot fudge sauce underneath. This easy dessert transforms in the oven into a decadent treat perfect with ice cream.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 45 mins | 15 mins | 12 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 9x13 inch baking pan
* Wire whisk
* Mixing bowls

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 box | [[Betty Crocker Devil's Food Cake Mix]] or [[Yellow Cake Mix]] | plus ingredients to prepare it |
| 1 cup | [[Brown Sugar]] | packed |
| 1/2 cup | [[Cocoa Powder]] | unsweetened |
| 2 cups | [[Water]] | hot |
| As needed | [[Cream]] or [[Ice Cream]] | for serving suggestion |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Sauce
1. Preheat your oven to **350°F**.
2. Prepare the **[[Betty Crocker Devil's Food Cake Mix]]** or **[[Yellow Cake Mix]]** batter according to the directions on the box and set it aside.
3. In an ungreased **13x9x2 inch baking pan**, combine the **1 cup brown sugar**, **1/2 cup cocoa powder**, and **2 cups hot water**. Whisk with a wire whisk until the sugar and cocoa are completely dissolved to form the sauce layer.

### Step 2: Assemble and Bake
4. Gently pour or spoon the prepared **cake batter** over the liquid sauce mixture in the pan. **Do not stir or mix the layers together.**
5. Carefully place the pan in the oven and bake for about **45 minutes** until the cake top is set and springs back lightly when touched.
6. Let the cake cool in the pan for about **15 minutes** before serving.

### Step 3: Serve
7. Serve warm by spooning portions of the cake and the rich chocolate sauce from the bottom of the pan into bowls.
8. Top with **[[Cream]]** or **[[Ice Cream]]**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Best served warm shortly after baking, but leftovers can be covered and refrigerated for up to 3 days.
