# Oatmeal Drops

> *A delightful vintage recipe for chewy oatmeal drop cookies packed with raisins and nuts. Perfect for an afternoon treat with a glass of milk.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing and Dropping
1. Cream the **1 C. butter** and **1 C. sugar** together until light and fluffy.
2. Mix in the **2 eggs**, **5 tbsp. milk**, **2 C. flour**, **3 C. oatmeal**, **1 C. raisins**, **1 C. nuts**, **1/2 tsp. soda**, **1/2 tsp. salt**, and **1 tsp. cinnamon** until a cohesive dough forms.
3. Drop by spoonfuls from the tip of a spoon onto a prepared baking sheet.
4. Bake until golden brown and firm around the edges with a soft center.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store baked cookies in an airtight container at room temperature for up to a week.
