# Oatmeal Drops

> **Collection:** John B. Collection | **Original Attribution:** Unattributed
> *A vintage cookie recipe featuring wholesome oats, raisins, and nuts dropped from a spoon. These classic drop cookies are simple to mix and bake.*

![Original Handwritten Card](/assets/scans/oatmeal-drops.webp)

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 10 mins | 24 |

## Ingredients
- [ ] 1 C. butter
- [ ] 1 C. sugar
- [ ] 2 C. flour
- [ ] 3 C. oatmeal
- [ ] 5 tbsp. milk
- [ ] 2 eggs
- [ ] 1 C. raisins
- [ ] 1 C. nuts
- [ ] 1/2 tsp. soda
- [ ] 1/2 tsp. salt
- [ ] 1 tsp. cinnamon

## Instructions
1. Cream the butter and sugar together.
2. Add the eggs and milk, beating well.
3. Mix in the dry ingredients including flour, oatmeal, soda, salt, and cinnamon.
4. Stir in the raisins and nuts.
5. Drop from the tip of a spoon onto a baking sheet and bake until done.
