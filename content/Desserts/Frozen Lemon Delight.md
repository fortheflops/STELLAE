# Frozen Lemon Delight

> 📜 **Collection:** UK15 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A light and tangy no-bake frozen dessert featuring a fluffy, mousse-like lemon filling layered between crunchy cookie crumbs. This refreshing treat is perfect for warm days and can be easily adapted for a refrigerated mousse texture.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 0 mins | 4-6 hours | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* electric mixer
* deep mixing bowl
* 8x8 inch baking dish

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 Large Can (12 oz) | [[Chilled Evaporated Milk]] | well-chilled, whipped |
| 2 | [[Lemons]] | juice and zest |
| 1 Cup | [[Sugar]] | granulated |
| 1 1/2 Cups | [[Graham]] or [[Vanilla Wafer Crumbs]] | crushed |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Base
1. Place the **1 large can evaporated milk** and a deep mixing bowl in the freezer for at least 30 minutes to ensure it is well-chilled.

### Step 2: Whip the Filling
2. Pour the **1 large can evaporated milk** into the chilled bowl and whip with an electric mixer on high speed until thick and stiff peaks form.
3. With the mixer on low, gradually add the **1 cup sugar**, then fold in the **2 lemons (juice and zest)** until just combined.

### Step 3: Assemble and Freeze
4. Line the bottom of an 8x8 inch baking dish with half of the **1 1/2 cups graham or vanilla wafer crumbs**.
5. Pour the lemon filling over the crumb base and spread evenly, then sprinkle the remaining **1 1/2 cups graham or vanilla wafer crumbs** on top.
6. Cover and freeze for at least 4-6 hours until the mixture is firm to the touch.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This dessert is best kept frozen; however, you can double the recipe to serve it as a softer, refrigerated mousse-style dessert.
