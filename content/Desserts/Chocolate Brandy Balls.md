# Chocolate Brandy Balls

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic no-bake holiday confection featuring rich chocolate brandy balls. Made with crushed vanilla wafers, cocoa, walnuts, and brandy, they are rolled in sugar and aged for peak flavor.*

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large mixing bowl
* Measuring cups and spoons
* Airtight storage container

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 1/2 Cups | [[Vanilla Wafers]] | finely crushed (approx. 60 wafers) |
| 1 Cup | Confectioners' [[Sugar]] | sifted |
| 2 Tablespoons | [[Cocoa Powder]] |  |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Walnuts]] | finely chopped |
| 1/4 Cup | [[Brandy]] |  |
| 1/4 Cup | Light [[Corn Syrup]] |  |
| 1 1/2 Teaspoons | [[Water]] | optional, if needed |
| As needed | Granulated [[Sugar]] | for rolling |

---

## Instructions

### Prepare the Dough
1. In a large bowl, combine the **2 1/2 cups finely crushed vanilla wafers**, **1 cup sifted confectioners' sugar**, **2 tablespoons cocoa powder**, and **1/2 cup finely chopped walnuts** until evenly distributed.
2. Stir in the **1/4 cup brandy** and **1/4 cup light corn syrup**. Mix well until a stiff, sticky dough forms. If the mixture seems too dry, add up to **1 1/2 teaspoons water** to help it come together.

### Shape and Coat
3. Shape the mixture into **3/4-inch balls**, rolling them firmly between your palms.
4. Roll each ball in **granulated sugar** until coated completely and sparkling.
5. Store in a tightly covered container. For the best flavor, let them age for **1 to 2 days** before serving to allow the brandy flavor to mellow and mature.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in a tightly covered container at room temperature. For the best flavor, let them age for 1 to 2 days before serving.
