# Classic Buttermilk Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic, tender buttermilk layer cake made with separated eggs and a touch of vinegar for an extra fine crumb. This delightful vintage recipe yields a light and fluffy texture perfect for any celebration.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Dry Ingredients
1. Preheat your oven to **375°F**. Grease and flour two 8-inch round cake pans or one loaf pan.
2. In a medium bowl, sift together the **2 cups cake flour**, **1 tsp baking soda**, and **½ tsp salt**.
3. In a small bowl, combine the **½ cup buttermilk** and **3 Tbsp white vinegar**.

### Step 2: Creaming and Mixing
4. In a large bowl, cream the **⅔ cup shortening**, **1 cup sugar**, **1 tsp vanilla extract**, and **½ tsp lemon extract** thoroughly with an electric mixer until light and fluffy.
5. Add the **3 egg yolks**, one at a time, beating well after each addition until the mixture is light and fluffy.
6. Add the dry ingredients to the creamed mixture in three parts, alternating with the buttermilk-vinegar mixture in two parts, beginning and ending with the dry ingredients. Beat thoroughly after each addition.

### Step 3: Folding and Baking
7. In a separate, clean bowl, beat the **3 egg whites** until they are stiff but not dry.
8. Gently fold the beaten **3 egg whites** into the batter until just combined.
9. Pour the batter into the prepared pans.
10. Bake for **25 minutes** for round layers, or **40-45 minutes** for a loaf, until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the cooled cake wrapped tightly in plastic wrap at room temperature for up to 2 days, or refrigerate for up to 4 days.
