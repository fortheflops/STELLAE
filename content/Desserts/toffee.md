# Toffee

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic homemade toffee recipe featuring rich molasses and butter, cooked to a brittle stage and studded with chopped nuts. This traditional confection sets into a crunchy treat that easily breaks into shareable pieces.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 15 mins | 30 mins | 12 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Saucepan
* Greased pan
* Wooden spoon

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 cup | [[Powdered sugar]] | None |
| 1/2 cup | [[Molasses]] | None |
| 1/4 lb | [[Butter]] | None |
| As needed | [[Nuts]] | Chopped |
| 1 pinch | [[Soda]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Cooking and Setting
1. Cook the **1 cup powdered sugar**, **1/4 lb butter**, and **1/2 cup molasses** in a saucepan until brittle, stirring constantly until the mixture reaches the hard-crack stage.
2. Remove from heat and quickly add the **1 pinch soda** and chopped **nuts**, stirring well until combined.
3. Pour the hot mixture into a greased pan and let it cool completely until hardened.
4. Break the cooled toffee into pieces when cool.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature with parchment paper between layers for up to 2 weeks.
