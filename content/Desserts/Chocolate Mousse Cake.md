# Chocolate Mousse Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** UK | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A decadent no-bake dessert featuring a rich chocolate mousse flavored with Grand Marnier, nestled in a crust made from slices of pound cake.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 25 mins | 0 mins | 2 hours | 10–12 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* springform pan
* double boiler
* microwave
* chilled bowl
* electric mixer

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 (10-16 oz) loaf | [[Pound Cake]] | Frozen |
| 1 (12 oz.) pkg. | [[Chocolate Chips]] | Semi-sweet |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] | Large |
| 1 Pint (2 Cups) | [[Heavy Cream]] | Whipped |
| 2 Tbsp | [[Grand Marnier]] | None |
| 1 tsp | [[Vanilla]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Crust
1. Slice the **1 (10-16 oz) loaf frozen pound cake** into ¼-inch thick slices. Completely line the bottom and sides of a springform pan with the cake slices, pressing them to fit snugly and create a crust.

### Step 2: Melt the Chocolate
2. Melt the **1 (12 oz.) pkg. chocolate chips** in a double boiler or in the microwave in 30-second intervals, stirring until smooth and glossy. Let it cool for a few minutes so it is not piping hot.

### Step 3: Mix Base and Eggs
3. Stir the **2 Tbsp Grand Marnier** and **1 tsp vanilla** into the melted chocolate.
4. Beat in the **2 large eggs**, one at a time, until completely incorporated and smooth.

### Step 4: Whip Cream and Fold
5. In a separate chilled bowl, whip the **1 pint (2 cups) heavy cream** until stiff peaks form.
6. Gently fold about one-third of the whipped cream into the chocolate mixture to lighten it. Then, fold in the remaining whipped cream until just combined and no white streaks remain.

### Step 5: Assemble and Chill
7. Pour the mousse mixture into the pound cake crust until level.
8. Place in the freezer for **1 hour** to set firmly, then transfer to the refrigerator to chill until ready to serve.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store covered in the refrigerator for up to 3 days, or keep frozen for up to 1 week.
