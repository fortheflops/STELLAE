# Molded Mints

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *Delicate, melt-in-your-mouth molded mints perfect for parties or gifts. These simple confections require no baking and are shaped into elegant forms.*

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Candy molds

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 cup | [[Margarine]] | Softened |
| 1 lb | [[Powdered Sugar]] | Sifted |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Mint Flavoring]] | Extract |
| 1 to 2 tbsp | [[Warm Water]] | As needed for consistency |
| As desired | [[Food Coloring]] | Optional |

---

## Instructions

### Preparation and Molding
1. In a mixing bowl, combine the **1/2 cup margarine**, **1 lb powdered sugar**, **1/4 tsp mint flavoring**, and **1 to 2 tbsp warm water** until a smooth dough forms.
2. Add a drop of **food coloring** if desired to achieve your preferred shade.
3. Form the mixture into balls about the size of a walnut, then roll each in additional **powdered sugar**.
4. Press the balls into candy molds, pop them out, and place on a tray.
5. Let the mints stand for 48 hours until they are firm and set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** These mints must be made at least 48 hours in advance to allow them to set properly. Store in an airtight container at room temperature.
