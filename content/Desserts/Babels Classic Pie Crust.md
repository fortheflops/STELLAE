# Babel's Classic Pie Crust

> 📜 **Collection:** LOC | ✍️ **Attribution:** Babel | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A simple and reliable recipe for a classic, flaky pie crust made with shortening. Includes a clever tip for using tapioca to prevent a soggy bottom in fruit pies.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 0 mins | 30 mins | One double-crust 9-inch pie |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large bowl
* Pastry blender
* Fork
* Plastic wrap
* Pie pan

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 Cups | [[Flour]] | Standard |
| 2/3 Cup | [[Crisco]] | Shortening |
| 6 Tablespoons | [[Water]] | Ice cold |
| 1/2 Teaspoon | [[Salt]] | Fine |
| 2 Tablespoons | [[Tapioca]] | Optional, for fruit pies |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mix Dry Ingredients
1. In a large bowl, whisk together the **2 cups flour** and **1/2 teaspoon salt**.

### Step 2: Cut in Shortening
2. Add the **2/3 cup Crisco** to the flour mixture. Using a pastry blender or your fingertips, cut the shortening into the flour until the mixture resembles coarse crumbs with some pea-sized pieces remaining.

### Step 3: Add Water
3. Sprinkle the **6 tablespoons ice water** over the mixture, one tablespoon at a time, tossing gently with a fork until the dough just begins to come together without overmixing.

### Step 4: Chill
4. Divide the dough in half and shape each half into a flat disk. Wrap each disk in plastic wrap and refrigerate for at least **30 minutes** before rolling out.

### Step 5: Assemble and Bake Prep
5. To use for a fruit pie, roll out one disk and place it in your pie pan. Sprinkle the optional **2 tablespoons tapioca** over the bottom of the crust to help absorb excess juice before adding your filling until ready to bake.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Wrap dough disks tightly in plastic wrap and refrigerate for up to 3 days, or freeze for up to 3 months before thawing.
