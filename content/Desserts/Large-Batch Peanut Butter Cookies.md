# Large-Batch Peanut Butter Cookies

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic, large-batch recipe for soft and chewy peanut butter cookies. This simple, old-fashioned dough is easy to make and creates dozens of cookies perfect for any occasion.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Dough
1. Preheat your oven to 350°F.
2. In a large bowl, cream the **1 cup shortening**, **1 cup granulated sugar**, and **1 cup brown sugar** together with an electric mixer.
3. Beat in the **1 cup peanut butter** until well combined.
4. Add the **2 beaten eggs** and **1 teaspoon vanilla extract**; mix well.
5. In a separate bowl, sift together the **3 cups flour**, **2 teaspoons baking soda**, and **1/4 teaspoon salt**.
6. Gradually add the sifted dry ingredients to the wet ingredients, mixing until a stiff dough forms.

### Step 2: Shape and Bake
7. Shape the dough into 1-inch balls and place them on an ungreased baking sheet.
8. Press each ball down with the tines of a fork in a criss-cross pattern.
9. Bake for **10-12 minutes**, or until lightly browned and fragrant.
10. Let the cookies cool on the baking sheet for a few minutes before transferring them to a wire rack.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The dough can be prepared in advance and refrigerated for up to 2 days before shaping and baking.
