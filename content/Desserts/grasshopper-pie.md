# Grasshopper Pie

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic, refreshing Grasshopper Pie featuring a chocolate cookie crust and a creamy, mint-infused marshmallow filling. This chilled dessert is perfect for elegant gatherings and mint lovers alike.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 0 mins | 6 hours | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Pie pan
* Electric mixer

---
## Ingredients

### Crust
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 14 | [[Chocolate Wafers]] | Crushed |
| 2 Tbsp | [[Butter]] | Melted |

### Filling
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 dozen | [[Marshmallows]] | Standard size |
| 1/2 cup | [[Milk]] | Hot |
| 1/2 pint | [[Heavy Cream]] | Whipped (or 1 package Dream Whip) |
| 4 Tbsp | [[Creme de Menthe]] | Liqueur |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Crust Preparation
1. Combine the **14 chocolate wafers** and **2 Tbsp butter**, then press into a pie pan.

### Step 2: Filling
2. Melt the **2 dozen marshmallows** in the **1/2 cup hot milk** and allow the mixture to cool.
3. Whip the **1/2 pint heavy cream** (or Dream Whip) until stiff peaks form.
4. Fold the **4 Tbsp creme de menthe** and whipped cream into the cooled **marshmallow mix**.
5. Pour the mixture into the prepared crust and refrigerate for at least 6 hours until set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This pie must be made at least 6 hours in advance to set properly in the refrigerator.
