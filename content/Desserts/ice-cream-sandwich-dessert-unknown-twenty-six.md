# Ice Cream Sandwich Dessert

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Nancy | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A deliciously simple frozen dessert featuring layers of ice cream sandwiches, liqueur, and whipped topping. Perfect for easy entertaining and warm summer days.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 0 mins | 4 hours | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 11x13 dish
* Mixing bowl

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 package | [[Ice cream bars]] | Sandwich style |
| 1/2 cup | [[Liqueur]] | Divided |
| 1 tub | [[Cool Whip]] | Thawed |
| 1/2 cup | [[Toasted almonds]] | Chopped, or substitute Heath bits |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Assembly
1. Place **ice cream bars** in an **11x13 dish**.
2. Pour **1/4 c. liqueur** over the **ice cream bars**.
3. Mix the **cool whip** with **1/4 c. liqueur** and cover the **ice cream bars**.
4. Sprinkle with **toasted almonds** or **Heath bits**, then freeze until set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Keep stored in the freezer until ready to slice and serve.
