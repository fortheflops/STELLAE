# Chocolate Mousse

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic and rich chocolate mousse with a light, airy texture, created by folding stiffly beaten egg whites into a smooth chocolate, butter, and egg yolk base. This elegant dessert is perfect for chilled serving and can be customized with whipped cream or ice cream.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 25 mins | 5 mins | 4 hours | 4–6 Servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* heavy-bottomed saucepan
* electric mixer
* mixing bowls
* serving glasses or mold

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 6-12 oz | [[Chocolate]] | semi-sweet, chopped |
| 2 tablespoons | [[Water]] | room temperature |
| 3 large | [[Egg Yolks]] | separated |
| 3 large | [[Egg Whites]] | stiffly beaten |
| 1/2 cup | [[Butter]] | cut into small pieces |
| As desired | [[Ice Cream]] or [[Whipped Cream]] | melted or prepared, for serving |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Chocolate Base
1. In a heavy-bottomed saucepan, combine the **6-12 oz semi-sweet chocolate** and **2 tablespoons water**. Place over low heat and stir occasionally until the **chocolate** is completely melted and smooth.
2. Remove the pan from the heat. Add the **3 large egg yolks** one at a time, stirring well after each addition until fully incorporated.
3. Add the **1/2 cup butter** to the warm **chocolate** mixture and stir until the **butter** is completely melted and the mixture is smooth and glossy.

### Step 2: Fold and Chill
4. In a separate clean, dry bowl, beat the **3 large egg whites** with an electric mixer until stiff peaks form.
5. Gently fold about one-third of the **3 large egg whites** into the **chocolate** mixture to lighten it. Carefully fold in the remaining **egg whites** in two additions until just combined, being careful not to deflate the mixture.
6. Pour the mousse into a well-buttered mold or divide it among individual serving glasses.
7. Chill in the refrigerator for at least 4 hours, or until firm and set.
8. Serve chilled, topped with **melted vanilla ice cream** or **whipped cream**, if desired.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The mousse must be chilled for at least 4 hours before serving; it can be stored in the refrigerator for up to 2 days.
