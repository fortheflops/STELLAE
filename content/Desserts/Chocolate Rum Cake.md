# Chocolate Rum Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A decadent and incredibly moist chocolate rum cake made with a Devil's Food cake mix base. The cake is soaked in a simple, buttery rum glaze for the ultimate rich dessert.*

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |

| 1/4 cup | [[Rum]] | Light |

## Instructions

### Step 1: Bake the Cake
1. Preheat oven to **325°F**. Generously grease and flour a Bundt pan. Sprinkle the **1/2 cup chopped pecans** evenly in the bottom of the pan.
2. In a large bowl, combine the **1 package Duncan Hines Devil Food Cake Mix**, **1 small package instant vanilla pudding**, **1/2 cup light rum**, **1/2 cup water**, **1/4 cup apple sauce**, and **4 large eggs**.
3. Beat with an electric mixer at medium speed for **2 minutes** until smooth.
4. Pour the batter into the prepared pan over the nuts.
5. Bake for **55 minutes to an hour**, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.
6. Let the cake cool in the pan on a wire rack for **15-20 minutes** before making the sauce.

### Step 2: Make Sauce and Assemble
7. In a saucepan, combine the **1/2 cup butter**, **1/4 cup water**, and **1 cup sugar**. Bring to a boil over medium heat, stirring constantly, and boil for **2 minutes**.
8. Remove from the heat and carefully stir in the **1/4 cup light rum** until fully incorporated.
9. Invert the warm cake onto its serving platter.
10. Gently poke holes all over the cake with a skewer or toothpick.
11. Slowly spoon or brush the hot sauce over the cake, allowing it to soak in completely until the surface shines and is rich and fragrant.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the cake covered at room temperature for up to 3 days or refrigerate for up to a week for best flavor absorption.
