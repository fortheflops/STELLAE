# Apple Pudding

## Instructions

### Step 1: Making the Cake
1. Mix **2 cups apples**, **1 egg**, and **1 cup sugar** together in a bowl.
2. Sift **1 cup flour**, **1 tsp soda**, **1 1/2 tsp cinnamon**, and **dash salt** together, then add to the apple mixture.
3. Add **1/2 cup nut meats** and stir well.
4. Pour the batter into an ungreased 9x9 pan and bake at 350°F for **40 min** until golden and cooked through.

### Step 2: Making the Topping
5. In a saucepan, mix **1/2 cup brown sugar**, **1/3 cup white sugar**, **2 tbsp cornstarch**, **1 cup water**, **1 tsp vanilla**, and **1/2 cup butter**.
6. Stir and cook over low flame until thick.
7. Pour the hot topping over the hot cake immediately when removed from the oven.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Best served warm right after adding the hot topping, but leftovers can be covered and stored at room temperature for up to 2 days.
