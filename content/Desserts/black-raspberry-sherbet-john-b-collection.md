# Black Raspberry Sherbet

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Marie Gowdy | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A delightful frozen dessert combining fresh citrus juices, cooked black raspberry juice, and rich dairy. Churned to perfection in a crank freezer for a traditional treat.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | None | 4 hours | 1 gallon |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Strainer
* Mixing bowl
* Crank freezer

---
## Ingredients

### Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Juice of 4 | [[Oranges]] | Strained |
| Juice of 4 | [[Lemons]] | Strained |
| 1 pint | [[Black Raspberry Juice]] | Cooked and strained |
| 4 cups | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 1 quart | [[Coffee Cream]] | Chilled |
| 1 quart | [[Milk]] | Do not use homogenized milk |

---

## Instructions

### Freezing and Preparation
1. Mix the **Juice of four oranges**, **Juice of four lemons**, and **4 c sugar** well until the sugar dissolves completely.
2. Add the **1 pint black raspberry juice**, **1 quart coffee cream**, and **1 quart milk** to the mixture and stir thoroughly.
3. Pour the combined mixture into a crank freezer and freeze according to the manufacturer's instructions until set to a smooth, frozen consistency.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store finished sherbet in the refrigerator or freezer until ready to serve.
