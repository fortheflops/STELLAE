# Sour Cream Coffee Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Five | ✍️ **Attribution:** Mrs. Oleta | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A delightful homemade sour cream coffee cake featuring a rich cinnamon, brown sugar, and nut swirl baked right into a moist yellow cake base.*

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing the Batter
1. Beat together **1 pkg. Duncan Hines yellow cake mix**, **1/2 cup Wesson oil**, **1/2 cup water**, **1/2 cup sugar**, **4 eggs**, and **1 (8 oz) carton sour cream** until smooth and well combined.
2. In a separate bowl, mix together **8 tablespoons brown sugar**, **1 cup chopped nuts**, and **1 tablespoon cinnamon**.

### Step 2: Layering and Baking
3. Pour half of the **batter** into a floured and greased Bundt pan.
4. Sprinkle half of the **sugar mixture** over the batter.
5. Put the rest of the **batter** on top, then sprinkle the rest of the **sugar mixture** on top of the cake.
6. Bake for **55 minutes** at **350°** until golden brown and a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store tightly covered at room temperature for up to 3 days or wrap well and freeze for longer storage.
