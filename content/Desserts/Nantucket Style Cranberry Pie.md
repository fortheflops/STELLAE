# Nantucket Style Cranberry Pie

> 📜 **Collection:** General Archive | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic crustless pie featuring a tart layer of fresh cranberries and apples, topped with a sweet, buttery batter that bakes into a tender, cake-like crust.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 45 mins | None | One 9-inch pie |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 9-inch pie plate
* Medium bowl
* Mixer or whisk

---
## Ingredients

### Fruit Layer
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 1/4 Cups | [[Cranberries]] | Fresh or frozen |
| 2 medium | [[Apples]] | Peeled and sliced |
| 1/4 Cup | [[Brown Sugar]] | None |
| 1/4 Cup | [[Walnuts]] | Chopped, optional |

### Batter Topping
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | [[Egg]] | None |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Granulated Sugar]] | None |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Flour]] | Sifted |
| 1/3 Cup | [[Butter]] | Or oleo, melted |
| To taste | [[Cinnamon]] | For sprinkling |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Fruit Layer
1. Preheat oven to **325°F**. Lightly grease a **9-inch pie plate**.
2. Spread the **1 1/4 cups cranberries** evenly on the bottom of the pie plate. Top with the **2 medium apples**, sliced.
3. Sprinkle the fruit with **1/4 cup brown sugar** and **1/4 cup walnuts** (if using).

### Step 2: Batter and Bake
4. In a separate bowl, beat the **1 egg** until thick and pale. Add the **1/2 cup granulated sugar** and beat well until combined.
5. Mix in the **1/2 cup flour** and **1/3 cup butter** (melted), beating until smooth.
6. Pour the batter evenly over the fruit in the pie plate.
7. Sprinkle lightly with **cinnamon**.
8. Bake for **45 minutes**, or until a toothpick inserted into the cakey topping comes out clean and the top is golden brown and fragrant.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftovers covered in the refrigerator for up to 3 days. Serve warm or at room temperature.
