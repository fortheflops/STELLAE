# Anne Bell's Holly Hermits

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Anne Bell | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic, soft, and chewy spice cookie packed with candied fruit, raisins, and nuts. This old-fashioned drop cookie features a warm blend of cinnamon, nutmeg, and cloves.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Base
1. Preheat your oven to **375°F** and grease your cookie sheets.
2. In a large bowl, cream the **1/2 cup shortening** and **1 cup brown sugar** together until light and fluffy.
3. Beat in the **1 egg** and **3-4 tablespoons water** until fully incorporated.

### Step 2: Combine and Bake
4. In a separate bowl, sift together the **1 1/2 cups flour**, **1/2 teaspoon baking soda**, **1/2 teaspoon cinnamon**, **1/4 teaspoon salt**, **1/4 teaspoon nutmeg**, and **1/4 teaspoon cloves**.
5. Gradually add the dry ingredients to the creamed mixture, stirring until well combined.
6. Fold in the **3/4 cup candied fruits**, **1/2 cup raisins**, and **1/2 cup walnuts or pecans** by hand.
7. Drop by rounded teaspoonfuls onto the prepared cookie sheets and bake for **8 to 10 minutes** until the edges are lightly browned and fragrant.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 5 days or freeze for up to 3 months.
