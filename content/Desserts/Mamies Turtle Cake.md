# Mamie's Turtle Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** UK15 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Mamie | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A rich, layered bar-style cake featuring a gooey caramel, chocolate chip, and nut filling sandwiched between a simple German Chocolate cake mix batter. This decadent treat brings all the classic flavors of turtle candy into an easy-to-bake pan dessert.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 35 mins | None | 24 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 9x13 inch baking pan
* Mixing bowl
* Saucepan
* Wooden spoon or spatula

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 box | [[Pillsbury German Chocolate Cake Mix]] | dry |
| 3/4 cup (1 1/2 sticks) | [[Butter]] | melted |
| 2/3 cup | [[Evaporated Milk]] | for batter |
| 14 ounces | [[Kraft Caramels]] | unwrapped |
| 1/3 cup | [[Evaporated Milk]] | for caramel layer |
| 1 cup | [[Chocolate Chips]] | standard |
| 1 cup | [[Nuts]] | chopped |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Base Batter and First Bake
1. Preheat your oven to **350°F**. Grease a **9x13 inch pan**.
2. In a bowl, mix the **1 box Pillsbury German Chocolate Cake Mix**, **2/3 cup evaporated milk**, and **3/4 cup (1 1/2 sticks) butter, melted** until combined into a thick batter.
3. Pour **half** of this batter into the prepared pan and spread evenly to form the bottom crust.
4. Bake for **15 minutes** until the layer begins to set.

### Step 2: Make the Caramel Filling
5. While the first layer is baking, combine the **14 ounces Kraft Caramels** and **1/3 cup evaporated milk** in a saucepan over low heat.
6. Stir frequently until the caramels are completely melted and the mixture is smooth.

### Step 3: Assemble and Finish Baking
7. Pour the melted caramel mixture evenly over the baked cake layer.
8. Sprinkle the **1 cup chocolate chips** and **1 cup nuts, chopped** over the caramel layer.
9. Carefully drop spoonfuls of the remaining half of the **batter** over the top and gently spread to cover the filling.
10. Return to the oven and bake for another **20 minutes**, or until the top layer is cooked through and set.
11. Let cool completely in the pan until set before cutting into squares.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the cooled cake tightly covered in the refrigerator for up to 4 days, or freeze in squares for up to 2 months.
