# Jon Bernd's Sweet Little Cheesecakes

> 📜 **Collection:** PatsyCollection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Jon Bernd | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *Individual soufflé-style cheesecakes baked in muffin tins with a graham cracker crumb coating and a tangy sour cream topping.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 25 mins | 20 mins | Chilling time required | 36-40 mini cheesecakes |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Tiny muffin pans
* Large paper or plastic bag
* Large bowl
* Clean bowl
* Electric mixer
* Small bowl
* Small spoon
* Knife

### For the Filling
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 (8 oz) Pkgs | [[Cream Cheese]] | softened |
| ¾ cup | [[Sugar]] | divided |
| 3 | [[Eggs]] | separated |

### For the Topping
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| ¾ cup | [[Sour Cream]] | none |
| 2 ½ Tbsp | [[Sugar]] | none |
| Dash | [[Vanilla]] | none |

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Pans and Filling
1. Preheat your oven to **350°F**. Butter tiny muffin pans heavily with **[[Butter]]**.
2. Place the **1 cup graham crackers** into a large paper or plastic bag, place the greased pans inside, and shake until the cups are well coated. Remove the pans and wipe off any excess crumbs from the top.
3. In a large bowl, beat the **2 (8 oz) pkgs cream cheese** with the **¾ cup sugar** until smooth. Add the **3 egg yolks** and beat well.
4. In a separate, clean bowl, beat the **3 egg whites** with an electric mixer until stiff peaks form.
5. Gently fold the beaten egg whites into the cream cheese mixture in three additions until just combined.

### Step 2: First Bake and Topping Preparation
6. Fill the prepared muffin cups almost to the top with the batter.
7. Bake for **15 minutes** until set.
8. While the cheesecakes are baking, mix the **¾ cup sour cream**, **2 ½ tbsp sugar**, and **dash of vanilla** in a small bowl until smooth.

### Step 3: Second Bake and Cooling
9. Remove the cheesecakes from the oven. With a small spoon, put a dab of the sour cream mixture on top of each one.
10. Return the pans to the oven and bake for another **5 minutes** until the topping is set.
11. Let the cheesecakes cool completely, then gently loosen the edges with a knife before chilling them in the refrigerator until thoroughly chilled.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store sealed in the refrigerator, but note that these cheesecakes do not freeze well.
