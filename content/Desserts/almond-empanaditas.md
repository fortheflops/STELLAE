## Instructions

### Step 1: Making the Dough
1. Sift the **2 C. flour**, **2 tsp. baking powder**, and **1 tsp. salt** together in a mixing bowl.
2. Cut in the **1/2 C. shortening** until the mixture resembles coarse crumbs.
3. Add the **1/2 to 2/3 C. ice water**, **1 TBS** at a time, tossing with a fork till all **flour** is moistened.
4. Shape the dough into a ball.

### Step 2: Rolling and Filling
5. On a lightly floured surface, roll out the dough to 1/8 inch thickness.
6. Cut into 2 1/2 inch circles.
7. Mix the **3/4 C. chopped almonds**, **1/2 C. sugar**, and **1 tsp. cinnamon** together.
8. Beat the **1 egg white** with the **1/4 tsp. almond extract** until frothy; stir in the almond and sugar mixture.
9. Place about **1 tsp. filling** on half of each circle.
10. Wet edges with a little water and seal tightly with a fork.

### Step 3: Frying and Finishing
11. Fry the empanaditas in deep fat heated to 375°F about 4 minutes, till golden brown, turning once.
12. Drain on paper towels and sprinkle with powdered sugar while warm.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Uncooked assembled empanaditas can be frozen on a tray before transferring to a freezer bag for up to 1 month; fry directly from frozen, adding a minute to the cooking time.
