# Mary Doll's Butterscotch Brownies

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Mary Doll | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic recipe for rich and chewy butterscotch blondies packed with butterscotch chips and black walnuts. This simple, one-pan dessert is a timeless treat perfect for any occasion.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 30 mins | None | 24 bars |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 9x13 inch pan
* medium saucepan
* mixing bowl
* wire rack

| 1/2 tsp | [[Salt]] | sifted |
| 1 tsp | [[Vanilla]] | none |
| 1 pkg (6-12 oz) | [[Butterscotch Bits]] | chips |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Black Walnuts]] | chopped |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Base
1. Preheat your oven to 350°F. Grease and line a 9x13 inch pan with parchment paper.
2. In a medium saucepan, melt the **10 Tbsp butter or oleo** over low heat. Remove from the heat and stir in the **1 1/2 cups brown sugar** until well combined. Let the mixture cool for about 5 minutes.

### Step 2: Combine and Bake
3. Stir in the **2 beaten eggs** and **1 tsp vanilla**.
4. In a separate bowl, sift together the **1 1/2 cups flour**, **1/2 tsp salt**, and **2 tsp baking powder**.
5. Add the dry ingredients to the wet mixture and stir until just combined.
6. Fold in the **1/2 cup black walnuts** and **1 pkg butterscotch bits**.
7. Pour the batter into the prepared pan and spread it evenly. Bake for **30 minutes**, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out with moist crumbs attached. Do not overbake.
8. Let the blondies cool completely in the pan on a wire rack before cutting into bars.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 3 days or freeze for up to 1 month.
