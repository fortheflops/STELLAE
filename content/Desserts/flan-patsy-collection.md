# Flan

> 📜 **Collection:** Patsy Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic, creamy baked flan featuring a rich homemade caramel coating and a silky custard base. This traditional dessert offers a delicate balance of sweet caramel and smooth vanilla flavor.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 1 hr | None | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Wide saucepan
* 2 qt metal mold
* Sieve
* Pan for water bath

---
## Ingredients

### Caramel and Custard Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 1/2 cups | [[Sugar]] | Divided (1 cup for caramel, 1/2 cup for custard) |
| 1/2 cup | [[Water]] | None |
| 1/8 tsp | [[Cr Tartar]] | Cream of tartar |
| 2 tsp | [[Vanilla]] | None |
| 6 | [[Whole eggs]] | None |
| 4 | [[Egg yolks]] | None |
| 4 cups | [[Milk]] | Hot |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Making the Caramel
1. Combine **1 cup sugar** and **water** in a wide saucepan or 2 qt metal mold and bring to boil over moderate heat.
2. Shake pan or stir occasionally, until **sugar** is dissolved.
3. Add **cr tartar** and continue to boil until syrup turns golden-brown.
4. Tilt mold to coat inside thoroughly with caramel syrup, or pour into a 2 qt porcelain mold which has been warmed by sitting in a pan of hot water.
5. Set prepared mold aside.

### Step 2: Preparing the Custard and Baking
6. Combine **whole eggs**, **egg yolks**, and remaining **1/2 cup sugar** and beat well.
7. Add **hot milk** in a thin stream and stir to mix well, then stir in **vanilla**.
8. Strain custard thru sieve into caramel-coated mold.
9. Set mold into pan of hot water and bake at 325 degrees for **1 hr**.
10. Done when knife inserted in center comes out clean.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the baked flan covered in the refrigerator for up to 3 days; invert onto a serving platter just before serving.
