# Classic Lemon Curd

> 📜 **Collection:** General Archive | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A rich, tangy, and sweet lemon curd prepared in a double boiler for a perfectly smooth, velvety texture. This versatile component is ideal for filling tarts, topping cakes, or spreading on scones.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 15 mins | 1 hour | Approx. 1 1/2 cups |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Double boiler
* Wire whisk
* Fine-mesh sieve
* Heatproof bowl

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3 | [[Eggs]] | Large |
| 8 oz (1 cup) | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 2 | [[Lemons]] | Rind and juice |
| 3 oz (6 Tbsp) | [[Butter]] | Cut into pieces |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Combine and Cook
1. In the top of a double boiler, whisk **3 eggs** and **8 oz (1 cup) sugar** until well combined.
2. Stir in the **rind and juice of 2 lemons** and **3 oz (6 Tbsp) butter**.
3. Cook over simmering water, stirring constantly, until the mixture thickens enough to coat the back of a spoon, about **10-15 minutes**; ensure it does not boil to prevent scrambling.

### Step 2: Finish and Cool
4. Remove from heat and strain through a fine-mesh sieve for an extra-smooth texture.
5. Cover the surface directly with plastic wrap to prevent a skin from forming and let cool completely until thickened.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 2 weeks. The curd will continue to thicken as it chills.
