# Nancy Rucker's Simple Apple Crisp

> 📜 **Collection:** The Loraine Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Nancy Rucker | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A wonderfully simple and classic apple crisp featuring a buttery brown sugar crumble over tender, cinnamon-spiced apples. This timeless recipe is perfect for a cozy, rustic dessert.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 30 mins | None | 6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* casserole dish
* medium bowl
* pastry blender

---
## Ingredients

### Filling
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 4 Cups | [[Apples]] | peeled, cored, and thinly sliced |
| To taste | [[Cinnamon]] | ground |

### Topping
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3/4 Cup | [[Brown Sugar]] | packed |
| 1/3 Cup | [[Flour]] | all-purpose |
| 1/3 Cup | [[Butter]] | chilled and cut into pieces |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation
1. Preheat your oven to **300°F (150°C)** and lightly grease a casserole dish.

### Step 2: Prepare the Topping
2. In a medium bowl, combine the **1/3 cup flour** and **3/4 cup brown sugar**.
3. Cut in the **1/3 cup chilled butter** using a pastry blender or your fingers until the mixture resembles coarse, buttery crumbs.

### Step 3: Assemble and Bake
4. Place the **4 cups thinly sliced apples** into the prepared casserole dish and sprinkle evenly with **cinnamon**.
5. Distribute the crumbly topping mixture over the **apples**.
6. Bake for **30 minutes**, or until the **apples** are fork-tender and the topping is golden brown and fragrant.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The topping can be prepared in advance and stored in the refrigerator until ready to assemble. Bake fresh for the best texture.
