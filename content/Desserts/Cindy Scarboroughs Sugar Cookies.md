# Cindy Scarborough's Sugar Cookies

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Cindy Scarborough | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic, reliable recipe for soft but sturdy roll-and-cut sugar cookies, perfect for decorating. This simple dough is ideal for festive shapes and finishes beautifully with a powdered sugar glaze.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |

| 20 mins | 10 mins | 1 hour | 3 dozen cookies |

| 1 Teaspoon | [[Baking Powder]] | sifted |
| 1/4 Teaspoon | [[Salt]] | fine |

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Dough
1. In a large bowl, cream the **1 cup oleo** and **1 cup sugar** together with an electric mixer until light and fluffy.
2. Beat in the **1 egg**, **1 tablespoon milk**, and **1 teaspoon vanilla** until well combined.
3. In a separate bowl, sift together the **2 1/2 cups flour**, **1 teaspoon baking powder**, and **1/4 teaspoon salt**.
4. Gradually add the dry mixture to the wet ingredients, mixing on low speed until a soft dough forms.

### Step 2: Chill and Roll
5. Divide the dough in half, wrap each portion in plastic wrap, and chill for at least 1 hour to firm up.
6. Preheat your oven to 350°F.
7. On a lightly floured surface, roll the dough out to 1/8 to 1/4 inch thickness and cut into desired shapes.

### Step 3: Bake
8. Place shapes on an ungreased cookie sheet and bake for **8-10 minutes**, or until the edges are just beginning to turn golden brown.
9. Cool on the baking sheet for one minute, then transfer to a wire rack to cool completely before glazing.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The dough can be prepared and chilled for up to 2 days, or frozen for up to 1 month if wrapped tightly in plastic.
