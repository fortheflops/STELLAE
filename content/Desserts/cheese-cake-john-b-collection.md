# Cheese Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic rich cheesecake featuring a base of farmer cheese and sour cream with a graham cracker crust. This traditional dessert bakes into a creamy treat that is gently cooled in the oven.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 30 mins | 1 hour | 1 hour | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Baking pan
* Mixer

---
## Ingredients

### Filling
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 1/2 lbs. | [[Farmer Cheese]] | Room temperature |
| 1 cup | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 4 teas. | [[Cornstarch]] | None |
| 5 | [[Egg Yolks]] | Separated from whites |
| 5 | [[Egg Whites]] | Separated, beaten until stiff |
| 1 pint | [[Sour Cream]] | Room temperature |
| 1 teas. | [[Vanilla]] | Extract |

### Crust
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 lb. | [[Graham Crackers]] | Crushed |
| 1/8 lb. | [[Butter]] | Melted |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and Mixing
1. Ensure all **ingredients** are at room temperature.
2. Mix all **ingredients** together except **egg whites** until smooth.
3. Beat the **5 egg whites** until stiff, then gently fold into the mixture.

### Step 2: Crust and Baking
4. Mix the **1/2 lb. graham crackers** with the **1/8 lb. butter** and line a baking pan, reserving some crumb mixture to sprinkle over the top.
5. Pour the batter into the prepared pan and sprinkle the remaining crumbs over the top.
6. Bake at 325°F for one hour until set.
7. Let cool completely in the oven.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the baked and cooled cheesecake in the refrigerator for up to 3 days before serving.
