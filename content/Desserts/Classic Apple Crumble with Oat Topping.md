# Classic Apple Crumble with Oat Topping

> 📜 **Collection:** Patsy Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic apple crumble featuring tender, cinnamon-spiced apples baked under a delicious, buttery rolled oat topping. This comforting dessert is perfect for serving warm with a scoop of vanilla ice cream.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 1 hour | None | 4 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Baking pan
* Mixing bowl
* Pastry blender

## Ingredients

### Filling
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 Cups | [[Apples]] | Sliced, peeled |
| 1 Tbsp | [[Flour]] | All-purpose |
| 1/4 Tsp | [[Cinnamon]] | Ground |
| 1/4 Cup | [[Sugar]] | White, or to taste |

### Topping
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Rolled Oats]] | Old-fashioned |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Brown Sugar]] | Packed |
| 1/4 Cup | [[Flour]] | All-purpose |
| 4 Tbsp | [[Butter]] | Chilled |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Filling
1. Preheat oven to 300-325°F and grease a small baking pan.
2. Place the **2 cups sliced, peeled apples** into the pan.
3. Sprinkle with **1 tbsp flour**, **1/4 tsp cinnamon**, and **1/4 cup sugar**, tossing gently to combine.

### Step 2: Prepare the Topping and Bake
4. In a separate bowl, combine **1/2 cup rolled oats**, **1/2 cup brown sugar**, and **1/4 cup flour**.
5. Cut in the **4 tbsp chilled butter** using a pastry blender until the mixture is crumbly.
6. Sprinkle the topping evenly over the apples and bake for **1 hour**, or until the topping is golden brown and the apples are tender when pierced with a knife.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The topping can be prepared in advance and stored in the refrigerator for up to 24 hours before assembling and baking.
