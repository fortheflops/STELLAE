# Mrs. Fields Cookies

> 📜 **Collection:** Elizabeth Knee Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Jo Spotts | 📂 **Category:** Desserts

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep the Oatmeal
1. Place small amounts of the **5 cups oatmeal** into a blender and process until it turns to a fine powder.

### Step 2: Mix the Dough
2. Cream together **2 cups butter**, **2 cups sugar**, and **2 cups brown sugar** in a large bowl until light and fluffy.
3. Add **4 eggs** and **2 tsp. vanilla extract** to the creamed mixture and beat well.
4. In a separate bowl, mix together **4 cups flour**, the powdered **5 cups oatmeal**, **1 tsp. salt**, **2 tsp. baking powder**, and **2 tsp. baking soda**.
5. Combine the wet and dry ingredients, mixing thoroughly until a cohesive dough forms.
6. Fold in **24 oz. semi-sweet chocolate chips**, one grated **8 oz. Hershey bar**, and **3 cups chopped nuts** until evenly distributed.

### Step 3: Scoop and Bake
7. Using an ice cream scooper with a release wire, portion the dough into golf-ball sized cookies.
8. Place the cookies **2 inches apart** on ungreased cookie sheets.
9. Bake at **375 F** for **6-9 minutes** until the edges are lightly golden but the centers remain soft. Do not overbake.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The cookie dough can be portioned into balls and frozen on baking sheets, then transferred to a freezer bag for up to 3 months. Bake directly from frozen, adding 1-2 minutes to the baking time.
