# Classic Ritz Cracker Pie

> 📜 **Collection:** General Archive | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A unique and delicious vintage dessert featuring a chewy, meringue-like base made from crushed Ritz crackers, pecans, and sweet whipped cream.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 20 mins | 24 hours | One 9-inch pie |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 9-inch pie pan
* Electric mixer
* Large mixing bowl
* Separate mixing bowl

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3 | [[Egg Whites]] | At room temperature |
| 22 | [[Ritz Crackers]] | Crushed |
| 1 Cup | [[Pecans]] | Chopped |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 1 Teaspoon | [[Vanilla]] | Extract |
| 1 Cup | [[Heavy Cream]] | For topping |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Meringue
1. Preheat oven to **350°F**. Generously grease a **9-inch pie pan**.
2. In a clean, dry bowl, beat the **3 egg whites** with an electric mixer on high speed until stiff, glossy peaks form.

### Step 2: Combine and Bake
3. In a separate bowl, combine the crushed **22 Ritz crackers**, chopped **1 cup pecans**, **1/2 cup sugar**, and **1 teaspoon vanilla**.
4. Gently fold the dry cracker mixture into the stiffly beaten egg whites until just combined without deflating the whites.
5. Spread the mixture evenly into the prepared pie pan.
6. Bake for **20 minutes** until the top is golden and crisp.

### Step 3: Rest and Garnish
7. Remove from the oven and let it cool completely. Cover and let the pie set at room temperature for one full day (24 hours) until the texture becomes delightfully chewy.
8. Just before serving, whip the **1 cup heavy cream** to stiff peaks and spread it evenly over the top of the pie.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the baked pie covered at room temperature for up to 24 hours to set, then refrigerate after adding the whipped cream topping.
