# Pflaumenkuchen

## Instructions

### Step 1: Making the Dough
1. Mix **2 c. flour**, **1/2 c. sugar**, and **1 stick butter** together.
2. Add the beaten **1 egg** mixed with **1 T. water** and **1 t. vanilla** to form a dough.

### Step 2: Assembly and Baking
3. Refrigerate the dough for at least **2 hrs**.
4. Spread the chilled dough on a baking sheet with hands.
5. Cover the dough completely with sliced **Italian plums**.
6. Dot the fruit with a little more **butter**, then sprinkle generously with a little **sugar** and **cinnamon**.
7. Bake in a preheated **350° oven** for **30 mins, or so**, until the crust is golden and fruit is tender.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The dough can be prepared and refrigerated up to 24 hours in advance before assembling and baking.
