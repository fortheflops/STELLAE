# Chocolate Roll

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic hand-written recipe for a light chocolate cake roll baked in a shallow pan and filled with mint frosting. Perfect for special occasions or afternoon tea.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and Mixing
1. Preheat oven to **400°**. Line a **10" x 15" shallow pan** with **waxed paper**.
2. Sift together the **6 tbsp cake flour**, **1/2 tsp baking powder**, and **1/4 tsp salt**.
3. Beat the **4 egg whites** until stiff peaks form, then gradually beat in the **3/4 cup sugar**.
4. Fold in the **4 egg yolks** (well beaten), add the **1 tsp vanilla**, then gradually fold in the flour mixture and finally the **2 sq. chocolate**.

### Step 2: Baking and Rolling
5. Pour batter into the prepared pan and bake at **400°** for **15 minutes** until set.
6. Spread with **mint frosting** while warm and roll up carefully.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Roll the cake while warm in a towel dusted with powdered sugar to prevent cracking, then fill and store in the refrigerator.
