# Trudy Templin's Blitz Torte

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Trudy Templin | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic German Blitz Torte featuring a tender cake base and a crisp meringue top baked together, then filled with sweet pineapple.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 25 mins | 40 mins | None | 1 2-layer torte |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Two 8-inch or 9-inch round cake pans
* Medium mixing bowl
* Small mixing bowl
* Clean, dry bowl for meringue
* Small saucepan

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation
1. Preheat your oven to **325°F**. Grease and flour two **8-inch or 9-inch round cake pans**.

### Step 2: Make the Cake Batter
2. In a medium bowl, cream the **¼ cup softened butter** and **½ cup sugar** together. Add the **4 beaten egg yolks**, **pinch of salt**, and **1 tsp vanilla extract**, and beat well.
3. In a separate small bowl, whisk together the **1 cup flour** and **1 tsp baking powder**.
4. Alternately add the dry ingredients and the **5 Tbsp milk** to the creamed mixture, creating a stiff batter.

### Step 3: Make the Meringue
5. In a clean, dry bowl, beat the **4 egg whites** with a **pinch of salt** until soft peaks form.
6. Gradually add the **½ cup sugar** and continue beating until stiff, glossy peaks form.

### Step 4: Assemble and Bake
7. Divide the cake batter evenly between the two prepared pans.
8. Carefully spread the meringue evenly over the top of the unbaked batter in both pans.
9. Sprinkle the top of the meringue with a few chopped **[[Nuts]]**.
10. Bake for **35 to 40 minutes**, or until the meringue is lightly golden and a toothpick inserted into the cake layer comes out clean. Let the layers cool completely in the pans.

### Step 5: Make the Filling
11. While the cakes are cooling, combine the **½ cup crushed pineapple** and **½ cup sugar** in a small saucepan.
12. Cook over medium heat, stirring constantly, until the mixture thickens and becomes fragrant. Let it cool completely.

### Step 6: Assemble the Torte
13. Carefully remove the cooled cake layers from their pans.
14. Place one layer on a serving plate with the meringue side down. Spread the cooled pineapple filling over the cake layer.
15. Place the second layer on top, with the meringue side up.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the assembled torte covered in the refrigerator for up to 2 days.
