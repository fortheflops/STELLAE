# Homestyle Apple Crunch

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic homestyle apple crisp featuring tender Jonathan apples topped with a buttery, golden oatmeal streusel. This comforting dessert is perfect for cool evenings and pairs beautifully with vanilla ice cream.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 40 mins | None | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 9x9 inch baking pan
* Large mixing bowl
* Pastry blender

---
## Ingredients

### Filling
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 4 Cups | [[Jonathan Apples]] | peeled & sliced |
| 1/2 to 3/4 Cup | [[Sugar]] | divided |
| 1 Teaspoon | [[Cinnamon]] | ground |

### Topping
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Flour]] | all-purpose |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Sugar]] | granulated |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Butter]] | chilled |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Uncooked Oatmeal]] | rolled oats |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Filling
1. Preheat your oven to 350°F and grease a 9x9 inch baking pan.
2. In a large bowl, combine the **4 cups Jonathan apples**, **1/2 to 3/4 cup sugar**, and **1 teaspoon cinnamon**. Toss well and spread evenly into the prepared pan.

### Step 2: Prepare the Topping
3. In a separate bowl, combine the **1/2 cup flour**, **1/2 cup sugar**, and **1/2 cup butter**. Use a pastry blender to cut the butter in until the mixture resembles coarse crumbs or small peas.
4. Stir in the **1/2 cup uncooked oatmeal** until combined.

### Step 3: Assemble and Bake
5. Sprinkle the topping mixture evenly over the apples.
6. Bake for 30-45 minutes until the apples are tender and the topping is lightly browned and bubbly. Serve hot.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The topping can be prepared in advance and stored in the refrigerator; assemble just before baking for the best texture.
