## Instructions

### Step 1: Jello Base
1. Dissolve **2 pkg lime jello**, **4 tbsp sugar** (portion of total), and **1 pinch salt** in **2 cups boiling water**.
2. Add **1 cup cold water**, **1 tsp mint extract**, **1 tsp vanilla**, and **1 tsp brandy**. Chill until slightly thickened.

### Step 2: Whipping and Assembly
3. Beat **1 egg white**, add **2 tbsp sugar**, and beat to peaks.
4. Prepare **1 cup whipped cream or dream whip**.
5. Measure out a little of the thickened jello and set aside.
6. Blend the **egg white** and **whipped cream** into the remaining jello and chill until thick.
7. Spoon the mixture into the **1 pie crust**.
8. Pour reserved jello over the pie and marbalize with a knife.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Keep refrigerated for up to 2 days before serving for best texture.
