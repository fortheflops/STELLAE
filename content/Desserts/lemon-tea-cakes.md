## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing and Baking
1. Combine the **1 1/2 tsp vinegar**, **1/2 cup milk**, **1/2 cup butter**, **3/4 cup sugar**, **1 egg**, and **1 tsp lemon peel** in a mixing bowl.
2. Stir in the **1 3/4 cups flour**, **1 tsp baking powder**, **1/4 tsp soda**, and **1/4 tsp salt** until a dough forms.
3. Drop the batter from a spoon onto ungreased cookie sheets.
4. Bake at 350 degrees F for 12 to 14 minutes until edges are lightly golden and centers are set.

### Step 2: Glazing
5. Mix together the **3/4 cup sugar** and **1/4 cup lemon juice** to make the glaze.
6. Drizzle the lemon glaze over the warm tea cakes until coated.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the baked tea cakes in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 3 days.
