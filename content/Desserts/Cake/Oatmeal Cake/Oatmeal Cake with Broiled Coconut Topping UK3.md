## Recipe

### Topping
| Measurements      | Ingredients                                        |
| :---------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 6 Tbsp            | [[Butter]], melted                                 |

| ¼ Cup             | [[Milk]] or [[Cream]]                              |
| 1 Cup             | [[Nuts]], chopped                                  |

### Instructions
1.  **Prepare the Cake:** Preheat your oven to 350°F. Grease and flour a 13x9 inch baking pan.
2.  In a medium bowl, pour the boiling water over the oatmeal. Stir and let it stand for 20 minutes.
3.  In a large bowl, cream together the 1 cup brown sugar, 1 cup white sugar, and shortening until light and fluffy.
4.  Beat the eggs into the creamed mixture one at a time.
5.  Stir the soaked oatmeal mixture into the wet ingredients.
6.  In a separate bowl, whisk together the flour, cinnamon, salt, and baking soda.
7.  Add the dry ingredients to the wet ingredients and mix until just combined.
8.  Pour the batter into the prepared pan and bake for **30 minutes**.
9.  **Prepare the Topping:** While the cake is baking, combine all the topping ingredients (melted butter, ½ cup brown sugar, milk/cream, nuts, coconut, and vanilla) in a small bowl. Stir well.
10. After 30 minutes, remove the cake from the oven. Immediately spread the topping mixture evenly over the hot cake.
11. Return the cake to the oven and bake for an additional **10 minutes**, or until the topping is bubbly and lightly golden.
12. Let the cake cool in the pan on a wire rack before cutting and serving.
