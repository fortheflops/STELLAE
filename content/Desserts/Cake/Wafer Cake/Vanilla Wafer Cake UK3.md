## Recipe
| Measurements | Ingredients                 |
| :----------- | --------------------------- |
| 2 sticks     | [[Oleo]]                    |
| 2 cups       | [[Sugar]]                   |
| 6            | [[Eggs]]                    |
| 12 oz box    | [[Vanilla Wafers]], crushed |
| 1-7 oz bag   | [[Coconut]]                 |
| 1/2 cup      | [[Milk]]                    |
| 1 cup        | [[Nuts]]                    |
___
### Instructions
1. Cream together: 2 sticks oleo, 2 cups sugar.
2. Add 6 eggs.
3. Crush with rolling pin 12 oz box vanilla wafers.
4. Add 1-7 oz bag coconut.
5. 1/2 cup milk.
6. 1 cup nuts.
7. Bake in greased tube pan at 275°F for 1 hour and 15 minutes.
