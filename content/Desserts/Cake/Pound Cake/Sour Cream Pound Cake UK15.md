## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| **For the Cake:** |                                           |
| 1 cup                      | [[Butter]]                                |
| 1/2 cup                    | [[Shortening]]                            |
| 3 cups                     | [[Sugar]]                                 |
| 5                          | [[Eggs]]                                  |
| 3 1/4 cups                 | Sifted [[Cake Flour]]                     |
| 1/2 teaspoon               | [[Baking Powder]]                         |
| 1/2 teaspoon               | [[Salt]]                                  |
| 1 cup                      | [[Milk]]                                  |
| 1 teaspoon                 | [[Vanilla Extract]]                       |
| 1 teaspoon                 | [[Lemon Extract]]                         |
| 1 teaspoon                 | [[Rum Extract]]                           |
| 1 teaspoon                 | [[Coconut Extract]]                       |
| **For the Glaze:** |                                           |
| 1/2 cup                    | [[Sugar]]                                 |
| 1/3 cup                    | [[Water]]                                 |
| 1/2 teaspoon               | [[Almond Extract]]                        |
___
## Instructions
**For the Cake:**
1.  Preheat oven to 325°F. Grease and flour a 10-inch tube pan.
2.  In a large bowl, cream the butter and shortening together.
3.  Add the sugar and beat well at medium speed.
4.  Add the eggs one at a time, beating well after each addition.
5.  In a separate bowl, combine the sifted cake flour, baking powder, and salt.
6.  Gradually add the dry ingredients to the creamed mixture, alternating with the milk.
7.  Stir in all four flavorings (vanilla, lemon, rum, coconut).
8.  Pour the batter into the prepared tube pan.
9.  Bake for 1 hour and 30 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.
10. Let the cake cool in the pan for 10 minutes before removing it to a wire rack.

**For the Glaze:**
1.  While the cake is cooling, combine the 1/2 cup of sugar and 1/3 cup of water in a small saucepan.
2.  Bring the mixture to a boil.
3.  Remove from the heat and stir in the almond extract.
4.  Drizzle the glaze over the warm cake.
