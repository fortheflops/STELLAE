## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 2 sticks                   | [[Butter]]                                |
| 1/2 cup                    | [[Crisco]]                                |
| 3 cups                     | [[Sugar]]                                 |
| 3 cups                     | [[Flour]]                                 |
| 1/2 teaspoon               | [[Baking Powder]]                         |
| 1/2 teaspoon               | [[Salt]]                                  |
| 5                          | [[Eggs]]                                  |
| 1 cup                      | [[Milk]]                                  |
| 1 teaspoon                 | [[Vanilla]] flavoring                     |
| 1 teaspoon                 | [[Lemon]] flavoring                       |
___
## Instructions
1.  Preheat oven to 325°F. Grease and flour a tube pan.
2.  In a large bowl, cream together the butter, Crisco, and sugar.
3.  Add the eggs one at a time, beating well after each addition.
4.  Beat in the 1 cup of milk.
5.  In a separate bowl, whisk together the dry ingredients (flour, baking powder, salt).
6.  Gradually add the dry ingredients to the wet mixture, then add the vanilla and lemon flavorings. Beat well until everything is combined.
7.  Pour the batter into the prepared tube pan.
8.  Bake at 325°F for 1 hour and 20 minutes.
