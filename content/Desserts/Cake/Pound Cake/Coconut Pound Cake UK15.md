*An intensely flavored and incredibly moist coconut pound cake. This classic recipe uses a full pound of butter and gets its rich, tropical flavor from both cream of coconut and coconut extract.*

## Recipe
| Measurements      | Ingredients                                        |
| :---------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 pound (4 sticks)| [[Butter]], softened                               |
| 3 cups            | [[Sugar]]                                          |
| 6                 | [[Eggs]], room temperature                         |
| 4 cups            | All-purpose [[Flour]]                              |
| 1 tsp (See Note)  | [[Baking Powder]]                                  |
| 1 tsp (See Note)  | [[Salt]]                                           |
| 1 cup             | [[Coco Lopez Cream of Coconut]]                    |
| 2 tablespoons     | [[Milk]]                                           |
| 2 teaspoons       | [[Coconut Extract]]                                |
___
## Instructions
1.  Preheat your oven to 325°F. Generously grease and flour a large 10-inch tube or Bundt pan.
2.  In a large bowl, cream the softened butter and sugar together with an electric mixer for 5-7 minutes, until very light and fluffy.
3.  Add the room-temperature eggs, two at a time, beating well after each addition.
4.  In a separate bowl, whisk together the flour, baking powder, and salt.
5.  In a small bowl, combine the cream of coconut, milk, and coconut extract.
6.  On low speed, add the dry ingredients to the creamed mixture in three parts, alternating with the coconut-milk mixture, beginning and ending with the dry ingredients. Mix only until just combined.
7.  Pour the batter into the prepared pan and spread evenly.
8.  Bake for 1 hour and 30 minutes, or until a long wooden skewer inserted into the center comes out clean.
9.  Let the cake cool in the pan for 15 minutes before turning it out onto a wire rack to cool completely.
