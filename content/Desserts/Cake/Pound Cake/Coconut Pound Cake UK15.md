## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 3 cups                     | [[Sugar]]                                 |
| 1 pound                    | [[Butter]], softened                      |
| 6                          | [[Eggs]], room temperature                |
| 4 cups                     | All-purpose [[Flour]]                     |
| 3/4 cup - 1 can            | [[Coco Lopez Cream of Coconut]]           |
| 2 teaspoons                | [[Coconut Extract]]                       |
| 2 tablespoons              | [[Milk]]                                  |
___
## Instructions
1.  In a large bowl, combine the sugar and softened butter. Cream until light and fluffy.
2.  Add the eggs, two at a time, beating well after each addition.
3.  (Inferred Step: In a separate bowl, whisk the flour).
4.  (Inferred Step: Gradually add the flour to the creamed mixture, alternating with the Cream of Coconut and milk, beginning and ending with the flour).
5.  (Inferred Step: Stir in the coconut extract).
6.  (Inferred Step: Pour batter into a large, greased and floured tube pan or Bundt pan).
7.  (Inferred Step: Bake at 325-350°F for 1 hour and 15-30 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean).
