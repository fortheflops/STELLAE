## Recipe
| Measurements               | Ingredients                               |
| :------------------------- | :---------------------------------------- |
| 1/2 cup                    | [[Butter]] or [[Margarine]], softened     |
| 1/2 cup                    | [[Shortening]]                            |
| 1 (8 ounce) package        | [[Cream Cheese]], softened                |
| 3 cups                     | [[Sugar]]                                 |
| 6                          | [[Eggs]]                                  |
| 3 cups                     | All-purpose [[Flour]]                     |
| 1/4 teaspoon               | Baking [[Soda]]                           |
| 1/4 teaspoon               | [[Salt]]                                  |
| 1 (6 ounce) package        | Frozen [[Coconut]], thawed                |
| 1 teaspoon                 | [[Vanilla Extract]]                       |
| 1 teaspoon                 | [[Coconut Extract]]                       |
## Instructions
1.  Preheat the oven to 350°F. Grease and flour a 10-inch tube pan.
2.  In a large bowl, cream the butter, shortening, and cream cheese together.
3.  Gradually add the sugar, beating until the mixture is light and fluffy.
4.  Add the eggs, one at a time, beating after each addition.
5.  In a separate bowl, combine the flour, soda, and salt.
6.  Add the dry ingredients to the creamed mixture, stirring just until blended.
7.  Stir in the thawed coconut and both the vanilla and coconut extracts.
8.  Spoon the batter into the prepared 10-inch tube pan.
9.  Bake for 1 hour and 15 minutes.
10. Let the cake cool in the pan for 10 to 15 minutes.
11. Remove the cake from the pan and let it cool completely on a wire rack.
