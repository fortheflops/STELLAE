## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 2 cups (1 lb)              | [[Butter]]                                |
| 2 cups (1 lb)              | [[Sugar]]                                 |
| 10                         | [[Eggs]], separated                       |
| 2 tablespoons              | [[Brandy]]                                |
| 1 teaspoon                 | [[Vanilla]]                               |
| 4 cups (1 lb)              | [[Flour]]                                 |
| 1/2 teaspoon               | [[Salt]] (optional)                       |
___
## Instructions
1.  Preheat the oven to 325°F. Grease and lightly flour two 9x5x3" loaf pans (or one 10" tube pan).
2.  In a large bowl, cream the butter and sugar together. Whip until light and fluffy.
3.  In a separate bowl, whip the egg yolks and add them to the creamed mixture, beating until very light.
4.  Stir in the flavorings (Brandy and Vanilla) and the flour. Blend well.
5.  In another clean bowl, beat the egg whites until they form soft peaks (but are not dry).
6.  Gently fold the beaten egg whites into the batter until just well blended.
7.  Pour the batter into the prepared pans.
8.  Bake for 1 hour, or until the center of the cake springs back when touched lightly.
9.  Let the cakes cool in their pans for 10 minutes before turning them out onto a wire rack to cool completely.
