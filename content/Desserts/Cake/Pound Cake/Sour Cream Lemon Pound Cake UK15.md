## Instructions
(No instructions were provided on the recipe card. The following is an inferred method.)
1.  Preheat the oven to 325°F. Grease and dust a 10-cup pan with cake flour.
2.  In a large bowl, cream the butter and sugar together until light and fluffy.
3.  Add the eggs one at a time, beating well after each addition.
4.  In a separate bowl, whisk together the flour, baking soda, and salt.
5.  In another small bowl, combine the sour cream, lemon juice, and lemon peel.
6.  Alternately add the dry ingredients and the sour cream mixture to the creamed butter mixture, beginning and ending with the dry ingredients. Mix until just combined.
7.  Pour the batter into the prepared pan.
8.  Bake for 1 hour and 30 minutes, or until a tester inserted into the center comes out clean.
