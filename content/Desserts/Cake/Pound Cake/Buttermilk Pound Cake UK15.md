## Recipe
| Measurements               | Ingredients                               |
| :------------------------- | :---------------------------------------- |
| 1 cup                      | [[Butter]]                                |

| 4                          | [[Eggs]]                                  |
| 3 cups                     | All-purpose [[Flour]]                     |
| 1/2 teaspoon               | Baking [[Soda]]                           |
| 1/4 teaspoon               | [[Salt]]                                  |
| 1 cup                      | [[Buttermilk]]                            |
| 1 teaspoon                 | [[Vanilla Extract]]                       |
| 1 teaspoon                 | [[Lemon Extract]]                         |
## Instructions
(The following instructions are inferred as none were provided on the recipe card.)
1.  Preheat oven to 325°F (Note: 375°F is high for a pound cake; 325°F is more standard and prevents a dry cake). Grease and flour a large tube or Bundt pan.
2.  In a large bowl, cream the butter and sugar together until light and fluffy.
3.  Add the eggs one at a time, beating well after each addition.
4.  In a separate bowl, whisk together the flour, baking soda, and salt.
5.  Alternately add the dry ingredients and the buttermilk to the creamed mixture, beginning and ending with the dry ingredients. Mix until just combined.
6.  Stir in the vanilla and lemon extracts.
7.  Pour the batter into the prepared pan.
8.  Bake for 1 hour, or until a tester inserted into the center comes out clean.

>[!Note]
>The recipe card only lists ingredients and the baking instruction "Bake 375 - 1 hr." The mixing steps have been inferred. Baking at 375°F for a pound cake is quite high; you may want to reduce the temperature to 325°F or 350°F and adjust the time accordingly to prevent the outside from burning before the inside is cooked.
