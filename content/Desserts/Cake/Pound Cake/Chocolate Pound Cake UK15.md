## Author
Mrs. Galloway (Rockwood, Tenn.)
___
## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| **For the Cake:** |                                           |
| 1 1/2 cups                 | [[Butter]] or [[Shortening]]              |
| 3 cups                     | [[Sugar]]                                 |
| 5                          | [[Eggs]]                                  |
| 3 cups                     | [[Flour]]                                 |
| 4 tablespoons              | [[Cocoa Powder]]                          |
| 1/2 teaspoon               | [[Baking Powder]]                         |
| 1/2 teaspoon               | [[Salt]]                                  |
| 1 cup                      | [[Milk]]                                  |
| 1 tablespoon               | [[Vanilla]]                               |
| **For the Chocolate Frosting:** |                                   |
| 2 squares                  | Unsweetened [[Chocolate]]                 |
| 4 tablespoons              | [[Butter]]                                |
| 1/2 teaspoon               | [[Salt]]                                  |
| 1 teaspoon                 | [[Vanilla]]                               |
| 1 1/2 cups                 | [[Sugar]]                                 |
| 1 cup                      | [[Milk]]                                  |
| 1 tablespoon               | [[Corn Syrup]]                            |
| 1 cup                      | Chopped [[Nuts]]                          |
___
## Instructions
### Cake
1.  Preheat the oven to 375°F. Grease and flour a tube pan.
2.  In a large bowl, cream the butter (or shortening) and sugar thoroughly.
3.  Add the eggs, one at a time, beating well after each addition.
4.  In a separate bowl, sift the dry ingredients (flour, cocoa, baking powder, salt) together 3 times.
5.  Add the dry ingredients to the creamed mixture alternately with the milk and vanilla.
6.  Pour the batter into the prepared tube pan.
7.  Bake for 80 minutes.
### Chocolate Frosting
1.  Melt the 2 squares of unsweetened chocolate.
2.  In a saucepan, combine all frosting ingredients EXCEPT the vanilla and nuts.
3.  Bring the mixture to a boil and cook for 2 minutes.
4.  Remove from the heat and beat until the frosting begins to thicken.
5.  Stir in the vanilla and nuts.
6.  Spread the frosting on the cake.
___

>[!Note]
>The frosting makes a thin, fudgy glaze. Mrs. Galloway says she sometimes leaves the glaze off and serves her cake with whipped cream combined with well-drained crushed pineapple.
