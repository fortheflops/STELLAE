## Instructions
(No instructions were provided on the recipe card. The following is an inferred method.)
1.  Preheat the oven to 350°F. Grease and flour an 8-inch square pan.
2.  In a large bowl, whisk together the flour, corn meal, baking powder, and salt.
3.  In a separate bowl, cream the butter/margarine and sugar. Beat in the almond extract and milk.
4.  Gradually add the dry ingredients to the wet ingredients and mix until just combined.
5.  In another clean bowl, beat the egg whites until stiff peaks form.
6.  Gently fold the stiffly beaten egg whites into the batter.
7.  Pour the batter into the prepared pan.
8.  Sprinkle the 2 tablespoons of almonds and 2 tablespoons of coconut over the top of the batter before baking.
9.  Bake for 45 to 50 minutes, or until a tester inserted into the center comes out clean.
