## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| **For the Cake:** |                                           |
| 5                          | [[Egg Whites]]                            |
| 2 cups                     | [[Sugar]]                                 |
| 1 stick                    | [[Margarine]]                             |
| 1/2 cup                    | [[Crisco]]                                |
| 1 tablespoon               | [[Vanilla Extract]]                       |
| 5                          | [[Egg Yolks]]                             |
| 2 cups                     | [[Flour]]                                 |
| 1 teaspoon                 | Baking [[Soda]]                           |
| 1/2 teaspoon               | [[Salt]]                                  |
| 1 cup                      | [[Walnuts]]                               |
| 2 cups                     | [[Coconut]]                               |
| **For the Icing:** |                                           |
| 8 ounces                   | [[Cream Cheese]]                          |
| 1 stick                    | [[Margarine]]                             |
| 1 teaspoon                 | [[Vanilla]]                               |
| 1 box                      | [[Powdered Sugar]]                        |

___
## Instructions
### For the Cake
1.  Preheat the oven to 350°F. Grease and flour three 8-inch or 9-inch round cake pans.
2.  In a clean bowl, beat the 5 egg whites until stiff peaks form. Set aside.
3.  In a separate large bowl, cream together the sugar, 1 stick of margarine, and Crisco.
4.  Beat in the vanilla extract.
5.  Add the 5 egg yolks to the creamed mixture.
6.  In another bowl, whisk together the flour, baking soda, and salt.
7.  Alternately add the dry ingredients to the creamed mixture.
8.  Gently stir in the beaten egg whites.
9.  Fold in the walnuts and coconut.
10. Divide the batter evenly among the three prepared pans.
11. Bake at 350°F. (Baking time is not specified, but a typical time for 3 layers is 25-30 minutes, or until a tester comes out clean).
### For the Icing
1.  In a large bowl, combine the cream cheese, 1 stick of margarine, vanilla, and powdered sugar.
2.  Beat until smooth and creamy.
3.  Frost the cooled cake layers.
