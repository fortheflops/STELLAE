## Recipe
| Measurements | Ingredients                 |
| :----------- | --------------------------- |
| 1 package    | Yellow Cake Mix             |
| 1/2 bag      | [[Coconut]]                 |
| 1-15 oz can  | [[Coconut Cream]] (Lopez)   |
| 1 can        | [[Eagle Brand Milk]]        |
___
### Instructions
1. Cook as directed in 9x13 pan.
2. While hot, pierce with meat fork.
3. Pour 1-15 oz can Coconut Cream (Lopez).
4. Pour 1 can Eagle Brand Milk.
5. Cool completely.
6. Ice with 1 large carton Cool Whip.
7. Sprinkle coconut on top.
8. Refrigerate.
