## Recipe

>[!Note]
>You can use a white or yellow cake mix.
>You can use your own recipe for same.

| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
|                 |[[Cake Mix]]           |
| 3                 | [[Eggs]]                    |
| 1 11 Ounce can            | [[Mandarin Oranges]], undrained                  |
| 1/2 Cup           | [[Oil]]                   |
| 1/2 Cup    | [[Coconut]]   |
| | [[Cream of Coconut]]|
|12oz| [[Cool Whip]]|
___
### Instructions
1. Mix well
2. Preheat oven to 350 degrees Fahrenheit
3. Cook for 30 to 35 minutes
4. Remove from oven and let cool for about 1 hour.
5. Put holes in cake about 1 or 1 1/2 apart and slowly saturate with cream of coconut. Saturating the cake slowly and completely is important.
6.  Use 12 ounces of Cool Whip and add 1 cup coconut, use as icing.
7. If desired, toast 1 1/2 cups of coconut and let cool, then garnish cake.
