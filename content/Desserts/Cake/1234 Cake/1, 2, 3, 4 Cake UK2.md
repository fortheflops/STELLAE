## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
| 1 Cup               | [[Butter]]                    |
| 2 Cups              | [[Sugar]]                     |
| 3 Cups              | [[Flour]]                     |
| 4                   | [[Eggs]]                      |
| 1 Cup               | [[Milk]]                      |
| 2 Teaspoons             | [[Baking Powder]]              |
| 1 Teaspoon            | [[Vanilla]]                   |
| ½ Teaspoon            | [[Almond Extract]]           |
| ½ Teaspoon           |[[Lemon Extract]]|
___
### Instructions
1. Grease loaf pan.
2.  Heat oven 275°.
3.  Takes at least one hour.
4.  Separate eggs, beat whites stiff.
5. Cream butter & sugar.
6. Sift Flour & B.P.
7.  Add yolks to butter & sugar.
8. add flour & milk alternately.
9. Add egg whites & flour.
10. Beat well & pour.
