## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 package (2-layer size)   | [[Yellow Cake Mix]]                       |

| 1 (8 ounce) package        | [[Cream Cheese]], softened                |
| 1 1/2 cups                 | [[Milk]]                                  |
| 1 (4-serving size) package | Jell-O Vanilla Flavor [[Instant Pudding]] |
| 1 (20 ounce) can           | Crushed [[Pineapple]], drained            |
| 1-2 pints                  | Fresh [[Strawberries]], sliced            |
| 3 medium                   | [[Bananas]], sliced                       |
| As Needed                  | Whipped Topping (e.g., Cool Whip)         |
| Optional                   | [[Nut Chips]]                             |
| Optional                   | [[Chocolate Shavings]]                    |
___
## Instructions
1.  **Prepare the Crust:** Prepare the yellow cake mix according to the package directions. (This seems to contradict the no-bake nature of the rest of the recipe, but it is the first listed ingredient. An alternative, more common crust for this type of dessert would be a graham cracker or vanilla wafer crust).
2.  **Prepare the Pudding:** In a bowl, combine the cream cheese and milk. Beat until smooth. Add the vanilla instant pudding mix and beat until well combined.
3.  **Assemble the Layers:**
    * Arrange the sliced bananas over the prepared crust. (The card notes to use an agent to stop browning, like a little lemon juice).
    * Spread the pudding mixture over the banana layer.
    * Top the pudding layer with sliced strawberries.
    * Spread a layer of whipped topping over the strawberries.
    * Spoon the drained, crushed pineapple on next.
    * Cover with the remaining whipped topping.
4.  **Garnish and Chill:**
    * Sprinkle the top with shredded coconut.
    * For added appearance and flavor, you can also add nut chips and chocolate shavings on top.
    * Chill thoroughly and serve.
___

>[!Note]
>The first ingredient is a yellow cake mix, which is unusual for a no-bake layered dessert. It's possible the author intended to bake a cake layer as the base, or they may have meant to use a different crust (like graham crackers) and the cake mix was listed in error or for another purpose.
