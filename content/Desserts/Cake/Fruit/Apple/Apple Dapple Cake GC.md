## Instructions
### Cake
1. Preheat oven to 350°F. Grease and flour a tube or bundt pan.
2.  In a large bowl, beat three eggs. Gradually add two cups of sugar and one and a half cups of oil, mixing well.
3.  In a separate bowl, sift together three cups of flour, one teaspoon of salt, and one teaspoon of baking soda.
4.  Add the dry ingredients to the wet ingredients and mix until just combined.
5.  Stir in one teaspoon of vanilla, three cups of diced apples, and one cup of chopped nuts.
6.  Pour the batter into the prepared tube or bundt pan.
7.  Bake at 350°F for 1 hour, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.
### Sauce
1. While the cake is baking, prepare the sauce. In a small saucepan, combine $\frac{1}{2}$ cup of brown sugar, $\frac{1}{4}$ cup of oleo (margarine/butter), and two tablespoons of milk.
2.  Bring the sauce mixture to a boil and boil for 3 minutes.
3. Pour the hot sauce over the hot cake (while the cake is still in the pan).
4. Let the cake cool in the pan before serving.
