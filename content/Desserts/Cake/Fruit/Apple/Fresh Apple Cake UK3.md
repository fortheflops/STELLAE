## Recipe
### Cake
| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
|1 1/2 cups|[[Wesson Oil]]|
|2 cups|[[Sugar]]|
|2|[[Eggs]]|
|3|[[Raw Apples (Grated or Blended)]]|
|3 cups|[[Flour]]|
|1/2 teaspoon|[[Salt]]|
|1 teaspoon|[[Soda (Baking Soda)]]|
|1 teaspoon|[[Cinnamon]]|
|2 teaspoons|[[Vanilla]]|
|1 cup|[[Chopped Nuts]]|
### Topping
|Measurements  | Ingredients|
|:-----------------|:---------------------------|
|1 cup|[[Sugar]]|
|1 stick|[[Oleo (Margarine)]]|
|1/2 cup|[[Coffee Cream]]|
___
## Instructions
1.  Mix oil and sugar.
2.  Add 1 egg at a time - beat well.
3.  Add apples and mix well.
4.  Add 3 cups flour, 1/2 teaspoon salt, 1 teaspoon soda, 1 teaspoon cinnamon.
5.  Mix well.
6.  Add 2 teaspoons vanilla and 1 cup chopped nuts.
7.  Bake at 300 degrees for 1 1/2 - 2 hours.
### Topping Instructions
1.  Cook 1 cup sugar, 1 stick oleo, and 1/2 cup coffee cream until sugar dissolves.
