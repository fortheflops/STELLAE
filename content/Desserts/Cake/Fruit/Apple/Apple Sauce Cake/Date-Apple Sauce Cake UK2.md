### Instructions
1.  Preheat your oven to 300°F. Grease and flour a 9-inch baking pan.
2.  In a single large bowl, combine all the ingredients: sugar, Crisco, egg, nuts, dates, diced apples, nutmeg, salt, cinnamon, baking soda, and flour.
3.  Mix thoroughly with a sturdy spoon or spatula until everything is well combined. The batter will be very thick.
4.  Spread the batter evenly into the prepared pan.
5.  Bake for 1 hour.
6.  Let the cake cool in the pan before slicing. Serve with whipped cream.
___

>[!Note]
>The original recipe card is titled "Date-Apple Sauce Cake," but the ingredients call for fresh **diced apples**, not applesauce, which creates a chunky, moist fruit cake rather than a typical applesauce cake.
