*A wonderfully moist and fragrant spice cake made with applesauce, brown sugar, raisins, and nuts. This simple loaf cake is packed with the warm flavors of cinnamon, nutmeg, and cloves.*

## Recipe
| Measurements      | Ingredients                                        |
| :---------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 1/2 Cup           | [[Shortening]]                                     |
| 1 Cup             | [[Brown Sugar]], packed                            |
| 2                 | [[Eggs]]                                           |
| 2 Cups            | [[Applesauce]]                                     |
| 2 1/4 Cups        | [[Flour]]                                          |
| 2 tsp             | [[Baking Soda]]                                    |
| 1 tsp             | [[Cinnamon]]                                       |
| 1/2 tsp           | [[Nutmeg]]                                         |
| 1/4 tsp           | [[Cloves]]                                         |
| 1 Cup             | [[Raisins]]                                        |
| 1/2 Cup           | Chopped [[Nuts]]                                   |
___
### Instructions
1.  Preheat your oven to 350°F. Grease and flour a standard loaf pan.
2.  In a large bowl, cream the sugar and shortening together with an electric mixer. Add the eggs one at a time, beating well after each addition.
3.  In a separate bowl, sift together the flour, baking soda, nutmeg, cinnamon, and cloves.
4.  Add the dry ingredients to the creamed mixture alternately with the applesauce, beginning and ending with the dry ingredients. Mix until just combined.
5.  Gently fold in the raisins and nuts.
6.  Pour the batter into the prepared loaf pan.
7.  Bake for 1 hour, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.
8.  Let the cake cool in the pan for 10-15 minutes before turning it out onto a wire rack to cool completely.
