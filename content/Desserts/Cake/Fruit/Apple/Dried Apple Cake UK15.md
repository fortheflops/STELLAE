## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 2 cups                     | [[Sugar]]                                 |
| 1 cup                      | [[Shortening]]                            |
| 4 cups                     | [[Flour]]                                 |
| 2 1/2 cups                 | Cooked [[Dried Apples]]                   |
| 2 teaspoons                | Baking [[Soda]]                           |
| 1 teaspoon each            | [[Cinnamon]], [[Allspice]], [[Cloves]]    |
| 1 pinch                    | [[Salt]]                                  |
| 1 cup                      | [[Nuts]] (Black Walnuts if possible)      |
| 1 cup                      | [[Raisins]]                               |
___
## Instructions
(The following instructions are inferred as none were provided on the recipe card.)
1.  Preheat oven to 325°F. Grease and flour a large cake pan or tube pan.
2.  In a large bowl, cream together the shortening and sugar until light and fluffy.
3.  In a separate bowl, whisk together the flour, baking soda, salt, and spices.
4.  In another bowl, toss the cooked dried apples, nuts, and raisins with a small amount of the flour mixture to prevent them from sinking.
5.  Gradually add the dry ingredients to the creamed mixture, mixing until just combined. The batter will be very stiff.
6.  Fold in the apple, nut, and raisin mixture.
7.  Spread the batter evenly into the prepared pan.
8.  Bake at 325°F for approximately 1 hour and 30 minutes to 1 hour and 45 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.
