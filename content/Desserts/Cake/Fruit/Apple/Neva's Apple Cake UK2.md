### Instructions
1.  Preheat your oven to 350°F. Grease and flour a 9x13 inch pan or a tube/Bundt pan.
2.  In a large bowl, mix the oil, sugar, and eggs until well combined.
3.  Stir in the vanilla and the grated apples.
4.  In a separate bowl, whisk together the flour, baking soda, and salt.
5.  Add the dry ingredients to the wet apple mixture and stir with a spatula until just combined. The batter will be very thick.
6.  Fold in the chopped nuts.
7.  Spread the batter evenly into the prepared pan.
8.  Bake for 1 hour to 1 hour and 15 minutes, or until a long wooden skewer inserted into the center comes out clean.
9.  Let the cake cool in the pan before slicing and serving.
