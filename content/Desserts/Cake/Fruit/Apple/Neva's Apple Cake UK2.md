## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
| 1 Cup             | [[Wesson Oil]]                   |
| 2 Cups            | [[Sugar]]                       |
| 2                 | [[Eggs]]                        |
| 3 Cups            | [[Flour]]                        |
| 2 Teaspoons        | [[Baking Soda]]                  |
| 1/2 Teaspoon       | [[Salt]]                         |
| 2 Teaspoons        | [[Vanilla]]                     |
| 3 Cups            | Grated [[Winesap Apples]]        |
| 1 Cup             | [[Nuts]]                         |
___
### Instructions
1. Mix oil, sugar, and eggs well.
2. Combine dry ingredients with wet ingredients.
