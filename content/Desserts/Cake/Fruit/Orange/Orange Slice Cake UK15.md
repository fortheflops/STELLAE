## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| **For the Cake:** |                                           |
| 1 cup                      | [[Crisco]] or [[Oleo]]                    |
| 2 cups                     | [[Sugar]]                                 |
| 4                          | [[Eggs]]                                  |
| 1/2 cup                    | [[Buttermilk]]                            |
| 1 teaspoon                 | Baking [[Soda]]                           |
| 2 tablespoons              | Grated [[Orange Rind]]                    |
| 3 1/2 cups                 | Sifted [[Flour]]                          |
| 1 (8 or 10 oz) package     | [[Dates]], chopped                        |
| 1 (29 oz) size             | [[Orange Slice Candy]], cut in small pieces |
| 1 cup                      | [[Coconut]]                               |
| 2 cups                     | [[Pecans]]                                |
| **For the Glaze:** |                                           |
| 1 cup                      | Fresh [[Orange Juice]]                    |
| 2 cups                     | [[Powdered Sugar]]                        |
___
## Instructions
1.  Preheat the oven to 325°F. Grease and flour an angel food cake pan (tube pan).
2.  In a large bowl, cream together the Crisco/oleo and sugar.
3.  Beat in the eggs one at a time.
4.  (Inferred Step: In a small bowl, dissolve the baking soda in the buttermilk).
5.  (Inferred Step: In a separate large bowl, whisk together the sifted flour, grated orange rind, chopped dates, orange slice candy, coconut, and pecans).
6.  (Inferred Step: Gradually add the dry ingredients to the creamed mixture, alternating with the buttermilk, until just combined).
7.  Pour the batter into the prepared angel food cake pan.
8.  Bake at 325°F for 1.5 hours. The card also has "2-2 1/2 hr" written, suggesting a longer bake time may be necessary.
9.  **For the Glaze:** While the cake is hot and still in the pan, mix together the 1 cup of fresh orange juice and 2 cups of powdered sugar.
10. Slowly pour the glaze over the hot cake in the pan, allowing it to soak in.
11. Let the cake cool completely in the pan before removing.
