## Recipe
| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
| 3/4 cups     | [[Water]]                   |
| 3/4 cups     | Salad [[Oil]]               |
| 4            | Whole [[Eggs]]              |
| 1 package    | [[Lemon Jello]]             |
| 1 package    | Yellow Cake Mix             |
| 1 teaspoon    | [[Lemon Extract]]           |

___
### Instructions
1. Beat all ingredients well - 4-5 minutes.
2. Bake 1 hour at 350°F in greased and floured tube pan.
3. Take out when cool.
4. While hot, spoon mixture of juice of 2 lemons and 1 cup powdered sugar on cake.
5. (Punch holes in top of cake with fork.)
