## Recipe
### Cake
| Measurements | Ingredients                       |
| :----------- | --------------------------------- |
| 1 package    | Lemon Cake Mix ([[Duncan Hines]]) |
| 1 cup        | [[Apricot Nectar]]                |
| 3/4 cup      | [[Crisco Oil]]                    |
| 4            | [[Eggs]]                          |
| 1/2 cup      | [[Sugar]]                         |
### Icing
| Measurements    | Ingredients         |
| :----------------- | --------------------------- |
| 1 cup           | [[Powdered Sugar]]          |
| Juice of 1      | [[Lemon]]                   |
___
## Instructions
### Cake
1. Mix Sugar, Oil, Nectar, and Cake Mix together.
2. Add 1 Egg at a time.
3. Bake in a tube pan for 1 hour at 325°F.
#### Icing
1. Pour Icing over cake while still warm to make a glaze.
