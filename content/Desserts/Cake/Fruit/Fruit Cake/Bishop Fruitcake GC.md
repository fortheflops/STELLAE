## Instructions
1.  Preheat oven to 325°F (moderate oven). Grease a 9.5 x 5.5 x 2.75 inch loaf pan (or several small loaf tins).
2.  In a large bowl, combine three well-beaten eggs and one cup of sugar, beating well.
3.  In a separate medium bowl, sift together one and a half cups of all-purpose flour, one and a half teaspoons of baking powder, and $\frac{1}{4}$ teaspoon of salt.
4.  Add the nuts, chocolate chips, and fruit to the flour mixture and toss to coat them well.
5.  Fold the flour, fruit, and nut mixture into the beaten egg mixture.
6.  Pour the batter into the prepared pan(s).
7.  Bake for one and a half hours in the moderate 325°F oven, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.
8.  Serve like fruitcake.
