## Author
Margaret Burnham (1971-1972)
___
## Recipe
1.  Preheat the oven to 250°F.
2.  In a large bowl, cream the butter and sugar together.
3.  Add the flour.
4.  Add the eggs, one at a time.
5.  Stir in the lemon extract and the rest of the "stuff" (pecans, cherries, and pineapple).
6.  Pour the batter into a tube pan.
7.  Cook for 1.5 to 2 hours.
___

>[!Note]
>The recipe card has a handwritten note: "Revenge given to me by Margaret Burnham - 1971 or 1972." There are also various scribbled baking times, including "3 1/2 hrs."
