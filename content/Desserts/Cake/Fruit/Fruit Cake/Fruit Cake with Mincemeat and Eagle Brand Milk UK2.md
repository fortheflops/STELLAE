## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
| 2 1/2 Cups        | Sifted [[Flour]]          |
| 1 Teaspoon           | [[Baking Soda]]          |
| 2                 | [[Eggs]], slightly beaten   |
| 1 Can             | [[Eagle Brand Milk]]            |
| 1 28 Ounce Jar    | [[Mincemeat]]                |
| 2 Cups            | Mixed [[Candied Fruit]]   |
| 1 Cup             | [[Walnuts]] |
___
### Instructions
1.  Sift dry ingredients. Set aside.
2.  Combine eggs, milk, mincemeat, fruits, and nuts. Mix well.
3.  Fold in sifted dry ingredients.
4.  Turn into greased, lined pan.
5.  Bake in slow oven at 200 degrees Fahrenheit for 2 hours, or until center springs back when touched.
6.  Cool in pan for 5 minutes.
7.  Turn out.
