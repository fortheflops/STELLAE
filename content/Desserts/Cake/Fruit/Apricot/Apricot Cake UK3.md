## Recipe
### Cake
| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
| 1 package    | Yellow Cake Mix             |
| 1 package    | [[Lemon Gelatin]]           |
| 1/2 cup      | [[Wesson Oil]]              |
| 5            | [[Eggs]]                    |
| 2/3 cup      | [[Apricot Nectar]]          |
| 2 teaspoons  | [[Lemon Extract]]           |
###  Icing
| Measurements | Ingredients                 |
| :----------- | --------------------------- |
| Juice of 1   | [[Lemon]]                   |
| 1/2 cup      | [[Apricot Nectar]], heated   |
|              | [[Powdered Sugar]] to spread |
___
## Instructions
### Cake
1. Mix cake mix and gelatin.
2. Add Wesson oil, then eggs one at a time.
3. Add apricot nectar and lemon extract and mix well.
4. Bake in tube pan at 325°F for 45 minutes or until done.
### Icing
1. Mix heated apricot nectar well with powdered sugar to spread.
