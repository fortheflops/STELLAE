## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 1/2 pound                  | [[Butter]], softened (not melted)         |
| As Needed                  | [[Sugar]]                                 |
| 1 package                  | [[Vanilla Sugar]]                         |
| 2 to 4                     | [[Eggs]]                                  |
| As Needed                  | [[Flour]]                                 |
| As Needed                  | [[Raisins]]                               |
| 1 package                  | [[Baking Powder]]                         |
| 1/3 to 2/3 cup             | [[Milk]]                                  |
| 1 teaspoon                 | [[Cardamom]]                              |
___
## Instructions
1.  Using a wooden spoon, cream the softened butter with the sugar and vanilla sugar in a large bowl.
2.  Beat in the eggs one at a time.
3.  In a separate bowl, whisk together the flour, baking powder, and cardamom.
4.  Gradually add the dry ingredients to the butter mixture, alternating with the milk, until a heavy dough forms.
5.  Stir in the desired amount of raisins.
6.  Spread the dough into a greased baking pan. (Traditionally, this type of cake would be topped with sliced plums before baking).
