## Recipe
| Measurements  | Ingredients                               |
| :------------ | :---------------------------------------- |
| 4             | [[Eggs]], separated                       |
| 3/4 cup       | [[Sugar]]                                 |
| 3 tablespoons | [[All-Purpose Flour]], sifted             |
| 1 cup         | [[Milk]], lukewarm                        |
| 1 teaspoon    | [[Lemon Juice]]                           |
| 1 teaspoon    | [[Orange Juice]]                          |
| 1 teaspoon    | [[Vanilla Extract]]                       |
| To Taste      | Grated [[Lemon Rind]] and [[Orange Rind]] |
___
## Instructions
1.  Preheat oven to 350°F. Generously butter about 8 custard cups or ramekins and place them inside a larger baking pan.
2.  In a large bowl, beat the egg yolks until pale. Gradually beat in the sugar.
3.  Stir in the lemon juice, orange juice, vanilla extract, and grated rinds.
4.  Mix in the sifted flour, then slowly whisk in the lukewarm milk until the batter is smooth.
5.  In a separate, clean bowl, beat the egg whites until stiff peaks form.
6.  Gently fold the beaten egg whites into the yolk batter until just combined. The batter will be thin and foamy.
7.  Pour the batter into the prepared cups.
8.  Place the baking pan in the oven and carefully pour boiling water into the pan to come about halfway up the sides of the cups, creating a water bath.
9.  Bake for about 30 minutes. The recipe suggests testing after 20 minutes. The cakes are done when the tops are lightly golden.
10. Carefully remove the cups from the water bath and let them cool before serving. They can be served warm or chilled.
___

>[!Note]
>This recipe makes about 8 individual custard cups. The instruction "grated orange, lemon and vanilla" likely refers to the grated rind of the fruits along with vanilla extract.
