## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| **For the Cupcakes:** |                                           |
| One Package                      | [[Chocolate Cake Mix]] (or Sour Cream Chocolate Mix) |
| Ingredients for Cake Mix         | As per package directions (e.g., [[Eggs]], [[Oil]], [[Water]]) |
| **For the Filling Mixture:** |                                           |
| One Large Package (e.g., 8 oz)   | Philadelphia [[Cream Cheese]], softened   |
| One                              | [[Egg]]                                   |
| $\frac{1}{2}$ Cup                | [[Sugar]] (Regular granulated)            |
| One Cup                          | Flaked [[Coconut]]                        |
| One Small Package (e.g., 6 oz)   | [[Chocolate Chips]]                       |
___
## Instructions
1.  Preheat oven to 350°F. Line cupcake pans with paper liners.
2.  Prepare the chocolate cake mix according to the package directions.
3.  Fill cupcake pans $\frac{1}{2}$ full with the cake batter.
4.  In a separate bowl, prepare the filling mixture: Beat together one large package of softened Philadelphia cream cheese, one egg, and $\frac{1}{2}$ cup of sugar until smooth.
5.  Stir in one cup of flaked coconut and one small package of chocolate chips into the cream cheese mixture.
6.  Put about one teaspoon of the filling mixture on top of the batter in each cupcake cup.
7.  Bake at 350°F for 20-25 minutes, or until the cupcakes are cooked through and a toothpick inserted into the cake portion comes out clean.
___

>[!Note]
> (The card notes this makes 35 cupcakes or can be baked in 3 (cup) cake tins, adjust baking time accordingly for larger tins).
