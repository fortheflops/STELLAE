## Instructions
1.  Preheat oven to 350°F. Line muffin tins with cupcake papers.
2.  In a mixing bowl, combine one cup of sugar, $\frac{1}{2}$ cup of cocoa powder, $\frac{1}{2}$ cup of shortening, one and a half cups of sifted flour, $\frac{3}{4}$ teaspoon of salt, one teaspoon of baking soda, one teaspoon of vanilla, one egg, and $\frac{1}{2}$ cup of milk. (The card implies creaming sugar and shortening first, then adding other ingredients, but lists them together before beating instructions).
3.  Add $\frac{1}{2}$ cup of boiling water last.
4.  Beat the mixture for 3 minutes with an electric beater.
5.  Fill cupcake papers (about two-thirds full).
6.  Bake at 350°F for 25 minutes or less. (The recipe yields 18 small cupcakes or 12 large ones).
7.  Check for doneness with a toothpick; it should come out clean.
