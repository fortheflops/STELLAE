*A classic, vintage torte with a unique, chewy texture. This flourless dessert gets its structure from crushed soda crackers and beaten egg whites, and is packed with chopped nuts.*

## Recipe
| Measurements      | Ingredients                                        |
| :---------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 3                 | [[Eggs]], Separated                                |
| 1 Cup             | [[Sugar]]                                          |
| ¾ Cup             | Chopped [[Nuts]] (e.g., Walnuts, Pecans)           |
| ¾ Cup             | Crushed [[Soda Crackers]] (about 18-20 crackers)   |
| ½ Teaspoon        | [[Baking Powder]]                                  |
| Dash              | [[Salt]]                                           |
| For Serving       | Tart [[Jam]], [[Whipped Cream]], or [[Fruit]]      |
___
## Instructions
1.  Preheat your oven to 350°F. Grease a 9-inch pie plate or round cake pan.
2.  In a medium bowl, combine the three egg yolks, sugar, chopped nuts, crushed soda crackers, baking powder, and salt. Mix well.
3.  In a separate, clean bowl, beat the three egg whites with an electric mixer until stiff peaks form.
4.  Gently fold the beaten egg whites into the yolk mixture in three additions until just combined and no white streaks remain.
5.  Pour the batter into the greased pan or pie plate.
6.  Bake for **30 minutes**. The torte will puff up and be golden brown.
7.  Let the torte cool completely in the pan.
8.  To serve, spread with tart jam or top with whipped cream and fresh fruit.
