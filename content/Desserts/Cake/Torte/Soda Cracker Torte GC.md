## Instructions
1.  Preheat oven to 350°F. Grease a pan or pie plate.
2.  Separate three eggs. Beat the three egg whites until stiff peaks form; set aside.
3.  In a separate bowl, mix together three egg yolks, one cup of sugar, $\frac{3}{4}$ cup of chopped nuts, $\frac{3}{4}$ cup of crushed soda crackers, $\frac{1}{2}$ teaspoon of baking powder, and a dash of salt.
4.  Gently fold the stiffly beaten egg whites into the yolk mixture.
5.  Pour the batter into the greased pan or pie plate.
6.  Bake for $\frac{1}{2}$ hour (30 minutes) at 350°F.
7.  Cool the torte.
8.  To serve, spread with tart jam, jelly, whipped cream, or serve with fruit.
