*A show-stopping, 6-layer no-bake freezer torte. This 'party' recipe uses two refrigerator cream pie kits to create repeating layers of chocolate crust, chocolate cream, and mint-peppermint cream, all topped with whipped cream.*

### Instructions
1.  Lightly grease a 7-inch spring form pan.
2.  **Prepare Kit 1 - Layer 1 (Crust):** In a small bowl, blend the "little bag of cracker crumbs" from one kit with **3 T. sugar** & **1/3 Cup melted margarine**. Press crumbs over the bottom of the spring form pan.
3.  **Prepare Kit 1 - Layer 2 (Chocolate):** In another small bowl, blend the "bag of choc. filling" from the first kit with **1 1/2 Cup milk**. Beat at low speed for 2 mins. to thicken. Spread evenly over the cracker crumb layer.
4.  **Prepare Kit 1 - Layer 3 (Mint):** In a medium bowl, combine the "white filling" from the first kit with **1 1/2 Cups milk** and **1/4 tsp. peppermint extract**. Beat at low speed, then at medium speed for 3 mins. to thicken. Spread evenly over the chocolate layer.
5.  **Prepare Kit 2 (Layers 4, 5, 6):** Repeat steps 2, 3, and 4 using the second pie kit and the remaining ingredients (3 T. sugar, 1/3 C. margarine, 3 C. milk, 1/4 tsp. peppermint extract) to form a second crumb layer, a second chocolate layer, and a final white peppermint layer.
6.  **Freeze:** Freeze for 4 hours, or until firm.
7.  **Serve:** Before serving, remove the outside rim. Beat 1 Cup of heavy cream until very stiff. Pile or pipe (using a No. 5 tip) in swirls around the edge of the freezer cake. Decorate with mint sprigs.
___

>[!Note]
>Recipe card 1 lists "4 Cups milk" as a total, but the step-by-step instructions on cards 2 & 3 call for 3 cups of milk *per kit*, for a total of 6 cups. This formatted recipe follows the step-by-step instructions.
>
>Serves 6-8 generously.
