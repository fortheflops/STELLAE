## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 1 Cup | [[Dates]], chopped |
| 1 Cup | [[Nuts]], chopped |
| 1 Tablespoon | [[Flour]] |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] |
| 1 Teaspoon | [[Baking Powder]] |
| 1 Cup | [[Sugar]] |
| 1 Teaspoon | [[Vanilla]] |
___
### Instructions (Inferred)
1.  Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Grease and flour an 8x8 inch baking pan.
2.  In a small bowl, toss the chopped **dates** and **nuts** with the **flour** and **baking powder**.
3.  In a separate bowl, beat the **eggs** and **sugar** together until thick and pale. Beat in the **vanilla**.
4.  Gently fold the date and nut mixture into the egg mixture.
5.  Pour the batter into the prepared pan and spread evenly.
6.  Bake for 30 minutes, or until golden brown and a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.
7.  Cool completely before cutting into squares.
