## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
| 1 Cup               | Pecans, Chopped         |
| 1 Cup               | Apples, Chopped          |
| 1 1/2 Cups          | Sugar                    |
| 4 Tablespoons        | Flour                    |
| 2 1/2 Teaspoons      | Baking Powder            |
| 2                   | Eggs                     |
___
### Instructions
1. Blend all the above ingredients together well.
2. Bake in a well-greased, shallow 12-inch pan at 350°F for about 35 minutes.
3. Remove from pan while still warm and cut into squares.
4. When the torte has cooled, serve with whipped cream.
___

>[!Note]
>Brown sugar or cinnamon may be added to suit your taste!
