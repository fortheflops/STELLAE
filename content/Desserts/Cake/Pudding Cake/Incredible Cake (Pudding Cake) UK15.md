## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 box                      | [[Betty Crocker Devil's Food Cake Mix]] or [[Yellow Cake Mix]] |
| 1 cup                      | [[Brown Sugar]]                           |
| 1/2 cup                    | [[Cocoa Powder]]                          |
| 2 cups                     | Hot [[Water]]                             |

___
## Instructions
1.  Prepare the cake mix according to the directions on the box and set the batter aside.
2.  In a 13x9x2 inch baking pan, mix together the brown sugar, cocoa powder, and hot water.
3.  Gently pour the prepared cake batter over the liquid mixture in the pan.
4.  **Do not stir.**
5.  Bake at 350°F for about 45 minutes.
___

>[!Note]
> - This is a self-saucing pudding cake. The liquid layer at the bottom creates a rich chocolate sauce as it bakes.
> - Serve warm with cream or ice cream.
