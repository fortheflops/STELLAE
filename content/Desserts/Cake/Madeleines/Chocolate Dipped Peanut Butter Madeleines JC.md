## Recipe
| Measurements      | Ingredients                   |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 1/2 cup (1 stick) | [[Unsalted Butter]], room temperature |
| 1 cup             | [[Granulated Sugar]]              |
| 2/3 cup           | [[Unsalted Peanut Butter]] (chunky or smooth) |
| 2 large           | [[Eggs]], room temperature        |
| 1 teaspoon        | [[Vanilla Extract]]             |
| 1 cup             | [[All-Purpose Flour]]           |
| 1/2 teaspoon      | [[Salt]]                        |
| 2 cups            | [[Semisweet Chocolate Chips]] (or 8 ounces chopped) |
| 2/3 cup           | [[Peanuts]] (honey roasted or plain), chopped |
| 4 tablespoons     | [[Butter]] or [[Baking Spray]], for pans |
___
## Instructions
1.  Place a rack in the center of the oven and preheat to 350°F.
2.  Thoroughly coat two 12-shell madeleine pans with butter or baking spray, brushing into each mold.
3.  Place the 1/2 cup of butter and the sugar in a 2-quart microwave-safe bowl. Microwave on low for 1-2 minutes, then stir with a whisk until smooth.
4.  Let the mixture cool for 3-4 minutes.
5.  Add the eggs one at a time, whisking well after each addition until blended.
6.  Whisk in the peanut butter until well blended. Add the vanilla, flour, and salt, whisking until just incorporated.
7.  Using a 1 1/2-inch scoop or a teaspoon, fill each mold with batter and gently press.
8.  Bake for 10-12 minutes, until the centers are puffed up with no shiny spots and the edges are light brown.
9.  Let the madeleines cool in the pans on a wire rack for 1-2 minutes, then carefully invert them onto the rack to cool completely.
10. To prepare the topping, line a cookie sheet with waxed paper. Place the chopped peanuts in a shallow bowl.
11. Melt the chocolate in the microwave for 1-2 minutes, stirring until smooth.
12. Hold each cooled madeleine by its narrow end and dip about two-thirds of it into the melted chocolate, scraping the excess off the bottom.
13. Immediately dip the chocolate-covered portion into the chopped nuts to coat.
14. Place the finished madeleines on the prepared cookie sheet and allow the chocolate to set completely before serving.
___

>[!Note]
>This recipe yields 24 madeleines.
