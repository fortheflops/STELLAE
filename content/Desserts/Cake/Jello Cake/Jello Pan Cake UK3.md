## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients         |
| :----------- | ------------------- |
| 2/3 cup      | [[Water]]           |
| 2/3 cup      | [[Wesson Oil]]      |
| 1 package    | [[Lemon Jello]]     |
| 1 package    | White Cake Mix      |
| 4            | [[Eggs]]            |
| 1 tablespoon | [[Lemon Flavoring]] |
___
### Instructions
1. Put in a mixing bowl: 2/3 cup Water, 2/3 cup Wesson Oil, 1 package Lemon Jello.
2. Mix until dissolved.
3. Add 1 package White Cake Mix.
4. Mix well.
5. Add 4 Eggs, one at a time and beat well.
6. Add 1 tablespoon Lemon Flavoring.
7. Cook in tube pan.
8. Bake at 350°F for 1 hour.
