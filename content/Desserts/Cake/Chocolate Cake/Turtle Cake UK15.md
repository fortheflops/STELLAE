## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 box                      | [[Pillsbury German Chocolate Cake Mix]]   |
| 2/3 cup                    | [[Evaporated Milk]] (for batter)          |
| 3/4 cup (1 1/2 sticks)     | [[Butter]], melted                        |
| 14 ounces                  | [[Kraft Caramels]]                        |
| 1/3 cup                    | [[Evaporated Milk]] (for caramel layer)   |
| 1 cup                      | [[Chocolate Chips]]                       |
| 1 cup                      | [[Nuts]]                                  |
___
## Instructions
1.  Preheat oven to 350°F. Grease a 9x13 inch pan.
2.  In a bowl, mix the German chocolate cake mix, 2/3 cup of evaporated milk, and the melted butter.
3.  Pour half of this batter into the prepared pan and spread evenly.
4.  Bake for about 15 minutes.
5.  While the first layer is baking, combine the Kraft caramels and 1/3 cup of evaporated milk in a saucepan. Heat on low, stirring frequently, until the caramels are completely melted and the mixture is smooth.
6.  Pour the melted caramel mixture over the baked cake layer.
7.  Sprinkle the 1 cup of chocolate chips and 1 cup of nuts over the caramel.
8.  Carefully spread the remaining half of the batter on top.
9.  Return to the oven and bake for another 20 minutes, or until the top layer is cooked through.
