## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| One                              | Three-Layer [[Chocolate Cake]], Baked and Cooled |
| As Needed                        | Whole [[Bananas]]                         |
| As Needed                        | [[Whipped Cream]] (Sweetened, e.g., "finger-lickin' good real whipped") |
| For Garnish (Optional)           | Shaved [[Chocolate]]                      |
___
## Instructions
1.  Make 3 layers of chocolate cake and let them cool completely.
2.  On the bottom layer, put whole bananas with space between them.
3.  Cover the bananas and the first layer with whipped cream (the card notes "finger lickin' good real whipped").
4.  Add the second layer of cake.
5.  Cover the second layer with just whipped cream.
6.  Put on the top (third) layer of the cake and frost the whole cake with whipped cream.
7.  Decorate the top with shaved chocolate, if desired.\
