## Instructions
### Cake
1. Dissolve soda in 1/4 cup buttermilk.
2. Beat sugar, shortening, and egg yolks together.
3. Add flour, buttermilk, soda, and chocolate.
4. Beat well.
5. Fold in beaten egg whites.
6. Bake at 350°F.
### Filling
 1. Mix together and cook until thickened.
