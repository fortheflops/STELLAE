*A wonderfully simple and incredibly moist chocolate cake from Orie, made with oil and buttermilk. This is a classic 'dump cake' style recipe that comes together in one bowl.*
### Author
Orie
___
## Recipe
| Measurements      | Ingredients                                        |
| :---------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 3 cups            | [[Flour]]                                          |
| 10 Tbsp           | [[Cocoa]]                                          |
| 2 cups            | [[Sugar]]                                          |
| 2 tsp             | [[Salt]]                                           |
| 2 tsp             | [[Baking Soda]]                                    |
| 2                 | [[Eggs]] (See Note)                                |
| 1 cup             | [[Wesson Oil]]                                     |
| 3 cups            | [[Buttermilk]]                                     |
| 4 tsp             | [[Vanilla]]                                        |
___
### Instructions
1.  Preheat your oven to 350°F. Grease and flour a 9x13 inch baking pan.
2.  In a large bowl, sift or whisk together the dry ingredients: flour, cocoa, sugar, salt, and baking soda.
3.  In a separate medium bowl, whisk together the eggs, oil, buttermilk, and vanilla.
4.  Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients and mix with a spatula or whisk until just combined.
5.  Pour the batter into the prepared pan.
6.  Bake for 30-35 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean. Let cool completely before frosting.
___

>[!Note]
>The original recipe card did not list eggs, which are an essential binder and leavener for this type of cake. Two eggs have been added, which is a standard amount for a cake of this size.
