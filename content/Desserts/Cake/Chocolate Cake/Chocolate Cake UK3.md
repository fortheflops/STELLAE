### Instructions
1. Mix in a bowl 2 Cups flour, 2 Cups sugar.
2. In a saucepan 1 Stick oleo, 1/2 Cup shortening, 1 Cup water, 3 1/2 Tablespoons cocoa.
3. Bring to a boil.
4. Pour over dry ingredients & mix well.
5. Add 1/2 Cup buttermilk 1 Teaspoon soda, 2 eggs, 1 Teaspoon vanilla.
6. Put in 11 X 16 pan at 400 for 20 min.
7. Five min. before cake is done make icing.
8. Put in saucepan 3 1/2 Tablespoons cocoa, 1/3 Cup milk, 1 stick oleo.
9. Bring to boil.
10. Add 1 box sifted powdered sugar, 1 Teaspoon vanilla, 1 Cup nuts.
11. Spread over hot cake & let cool.
___

>[!Note]
> To sour milk add 1 Tablespoon vinegar.
