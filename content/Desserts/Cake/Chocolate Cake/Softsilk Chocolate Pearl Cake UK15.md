## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 3 cups                     | [[Cake Flour]]                            |
| 1 cup                      | Unsweetened Dutch Process [[Cocoa Powder]]|
| 1 teaspoon                 | [[Salt]]                                  |
| 4 sticks                   | Unsalted [[Butter]]                       |
| 2 cups                     | [[Sugar]]                                 |
| 1 tablespoon               | [[Vanilla]]                               |
| 8 large                    | [[Eggs]]                                  |
| 2 tablespoons              | Instant [[Espresso Powder]]               |
| 1/4 cup                    | Warm [[Water]]                            |
___
## Instructions
1.  Preheat the oven to 350°F. Grease and flour your chosen pan(s).
2.  In a bowl, sift together the cake flour, cocoa powder, and salt.
3.  In a large bowl, beat the butter and sugar until light and fluffy.
4.  Beat in the vanilla, then beat in the eggs one at a time.
5.  On low speed, gradually beat in the flour mixture until just combined.
6.  In a small cup, dissolve the espresso powder in the warm water.
7.  Beat the espresso mixture into the batter.
8.  Pour the batter into the prepared pan(s).
9.  Bake according to pan size:
    * Large Bundt: 1 hour 20 minutes
    * Smaller pans: 40 minutes
10. Let the cake(s) cool in the pan(s) for 10 minutes before turning out onto a rack to cool completely.
