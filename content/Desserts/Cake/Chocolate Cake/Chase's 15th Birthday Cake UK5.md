## Instructions
1.  **Bake the Cake:** Bake your favorite chocolate cake recipe in two 8-inch round pans. The card suggests a bake time of 34-40 minutes at what appears to be 350-400°F. Cool the cakes completely.
2.  **Make the Filling:** In a medium bowl, beat the softened cream cheese and butter with an electric mixer on medium speed until fluffy. Beat in the powdered sugar and vanilla.
3.  **Assemble the Cake:** Cut each cake layer horizontally to make a total of 4 thin layers. Place the first layer on a serving plate, cut side up. Sprinkle with raspberries. Repeat with the second and third layers. Top with the fourth layer, cut side down.
4.  **Make the Topping:** In a small bowl, combine the corn syrup, whipping cream, powdered sugar, cocoa, and vanilla. Beat until stiff peaks form.
5.  **Finish the Cake:** Spread the topping over the top and sides of the cake. In a small saucepan, heat the cherry jam over medium heat until melted. Brush the melted jam over the raspberries that are showing between the layers. Store the cake, covered, in the refrigerator.
