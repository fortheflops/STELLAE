## Instructions
### Cake
1.  Preheat oven to 325°F. Generously grease and flour a Bundt pan. Sprinkle the ½ cup of pecans evenly in the bottom of the pan.
2.  In a large bowl, combine the cake mix, dry pudding mix, rum, water, oil (or applesauce), and eggs.
3.  Beat with an electric mixer at medium speed for 2 minutes.
4.  Pour the batter into the prepared pan over the nuts.
5.  Bake for 55 minutes to an hour, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.
6.  Let the cake cool in the pan on a wire rack for 15-20 minutes before making the sauce.

### Sauce & Assembly
1.  In a saucepan, combine the butter, water, and sugar. Bring to a boil over medium heat, stirring constantly, and boil for 2 minutes.
2.  Remove from the heat and carefully stir in the rum.
3.  Invert the warm cake onto its serving platter.
4.  Gently poke holes all over the cake with a skewer or toothpick.
5.  Slowly spoon or brush the hot sauce over the cake, allowing it to soak in. Let the cake cool completely before serving.
