## Recipe
### Cake
| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
| 1/2 cup         | Chopped [[Pecans]]         |        
| 1 package       | [[Duncan Hines]] Devil Food Cake Mix |        
| 1 small package  | Instant [[Vanilla Pudding]] (Dry) |        
| 1/2 cup         | Light [[Rum]]               |
| 4               | [[Eggs]]                    |
| 1/2 cup         | [[Water]]                   |
| 1/4 cup         | Crisco Oil (Apple Sauce)     |
### Sauce
| Measurements    | Ingredients         |
| :----------------- | --------------------------- |
| 1/4 cup         | Light [[Rum]]               |
| 1 cup           | [[Sugar]]                   |
| 1/2 cup         | [[Butter]]                  |
| 1/4 cup         | [[Water]]                   |
___
## Instructions
### Cake
1. Grease Bundt Pan. Sprinkle 1/2 cup Pecans in pan.
2. Blend mix, pudding, rum, water, oil, and eggs.
3. Mix 2 minutes at medium speed.
4. Bake about 55 minutes to an hour at 325°F until pick comes out clean.
### Sauce
1. Boil for 2 minutes.
2. Before Glazing, remove cake from pan.
3. Pour Glaze on cake.
4. Let cool 1/2 hour.
5. Remove from Pan.
