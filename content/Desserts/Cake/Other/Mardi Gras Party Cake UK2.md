## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
| 2/3 Cup           | [[Nestle's Butterscotch Morsels]]             |
| 1/4 Cup           | [[Water]]                                       |
| 2 1/4 Cups        | Sifted [[Flour]]                             |
| 1 Teaspoon        | [[Salt]]                                        |
| 1/2 Teaspoon        | [[Baking Powder]]                             |
| 1 Teaspoon       | [[Baking Soda]]                                     |
| 1 1/4 Cups        | [[Sugar]]                                       |
| 1/2 Cup           | [[Shortening]]                                  |
| 3                 | Unbeaten [[Eggs]]                               |
___
### Instructions
1. Bake at 375 degrees Fahrenheit for 25-30 minutes.
2. Melt butterscotch morsels in water in a saucepan. Cool.
3. Sift together flour, salt, baking powder, and baking soda.
4. Add sugar gradually to shortening. Cream well.
5. Blend in eggs; beat well after each.
6. Blend in melted morsels. Mix well.
