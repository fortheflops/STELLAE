## Recipe

| Measurements   | Ingredients                      |
| :------------- | -------------------------------- |
| 1 3 Ounce      | [[Vanilla Pudding]], Dry         |
| 1 Can          | Crushed [[Pineapple]]            |
| 1 Can 11 Ounce | [[Mandarin Oranges]], Cut up     |
| 1 4 Ounce Jar  | [[Maraschino Cherries]], Cut up, |
| 1 8 Ounce      | [[Cool Whip]]                    |
___
### Instructions
1. Mix pudding dry and pineapple, oranges, and cherries. Do not drain fruit.
2. Combine all of these and fold in cool whip. Mix well and spread over cake.
3. Refrigerate
