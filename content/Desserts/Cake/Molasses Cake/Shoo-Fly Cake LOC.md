## Author
F. Woodland (Old Lehigh County Recipe)
___
## Recipe
### Dry Ingredients
| Measurements           | Ingredients        |
| :--------------------- | :----------------- |
| 1 $\frac{1}{2}$ Cups      | [[Flour]]            |
| 1 Cup                     | [[Brown Sugar]]      |
| $\frac{1}{4}$ Cup         | [[Butter]]           |
| $\frac{1}{2}$ Cup         | [[Molasses]]         |
| $\frac{1}{2}$ Cup         | Hot [[Water]]        |
| $\frac{1}{2}$ Teaspoon    | [[Soda]] (Baking Soda) |
| Amount Not Specified      | [[Pastry]] (e.g., Betty Crocker pie crust mix) |
___
## Instructions
1.  Make crumbs by combining the flour, sugar, and shortening (butter).
2.  Line a pie pan with pastry. (The author notes using Betty Crocker pie crust mix).
3.  Dissolve the soda in the hot water.
4.  Combine the soda-water mixture with the molasses. (The author notes using Grandma's Molasses, as "it doesn't have any preservatives").
5.  Pour the molasses mixture into the pastry-lined pan.
6.  Top with the crumbs.
7.  Bake in a moderately hot oven (350°F / 175°C) until firm, approximately 45 minutes.
