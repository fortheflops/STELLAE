## Recipe
| Measurements      | Ingredients                   |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| **For the White Cake Batter**|                                   |
| 3/4 cup           | [[Butter]], softened              |
| 2 cups            | [[Sugar]]                       |
| 4                 | [[Eggs]], separated               |
| 3 cups            | [[Swansdown Cake Flour]]          |
| 4 teaspoons       | [[Baking Powder]]               |
| 1 cup             | [[Milk]]                        |
| 1/2 teaspoon      | [[Vanilla Extract]]             |
| 1/2 teaspoon      | [[Salt]]                        |
| **For the Chocolate Mixture**|                                   |
| 3 ounces (3 squares) | [[Unsweetened Chocolate]]       |
| 1/4 cup           | Boiling [[Water]]                 |
| 4 teaspoons       | [[Sugar]]                       |
| 1/2 teaspoon      | [[Baking Soda]]                 |
___
## Instructions
1.  Preheat oven to 350°F. Grease and flour your pan(s) (one loaf pan or two round layer cake pans).
2.  **To make the chocolate mixture:** In a small saucepan, melt the chocolate. Stir in the boiling water, 4 teaspoons of sugar, and the baking soda. Mix until smooth and set aside to cool completely.
3.  **To make the white cake batter:** In a large bowl, cream the softened butter. Gradually add the 2 cups of sugar, beating until light and fluffy. Beat in the egg yolks one at a time.
4.  In a separate bowl, sift together the cake flour, baking powder, and salt.
5.  Add the flour mixture to the creamed butter mixture in parts, alternating with the milk. Mix until just combined.
6.  In another clean bowl, beat the egg whites until stiff peaks form. Gently fold the beaten egg whites into the batter.
7.  **To assemble:** Divide the white cake batter equally into two bowls. Gently stir the cooled chocolate mixture into one half of the batter.
8.  Drop alternating spoonfuls of the white and chocolate batters into the prepared pan(s).
9.  Use a knife to gently cut through the batters once or twice to create a marbled effect. Do not overmix.
10. **Bake** according to your pan choice:
    * **Loaf Pan:** Bake for 50 to 55 minutes.
    * **Two Layer Pans:** Bake for about 30 minutes.
11. The cake is done when a wooden skewer inserted into the center comes out clean.
