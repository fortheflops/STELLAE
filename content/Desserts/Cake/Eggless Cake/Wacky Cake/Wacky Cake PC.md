*A classic 'Wacky Cake' or 'Depression Cake' that is magically moist and rich despite containing no eggs, butter, or milk. This simple chocolate cake is mixed directly in the baking pan.*

### Instructions
1.  Preheat your oven to 350°F.
2.  In an ungreased 9x9 inch cake pan, sift or whisk together the flour, sugar, cocoa, salt, and baking soda.
3.  Using the back of a spoon, make three depressions or "dents" in the dry ingredients.
4.  Pour the vinegar into the first dent, the vanilla into the second, and the salad oil into the third.
5.  Pour the 1 cup of cold water over everything.
6.  Mix with a fork directly in the pan until the batter is smooth and well-combined.
7.  Bake for 20-30 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean. Let cool and serve plain or with your favorite frosting.
