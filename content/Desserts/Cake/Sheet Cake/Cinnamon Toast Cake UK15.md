## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| **For the Cake:** |                                           |
| 2 cups                     | [[Flour]]                                 |
| 1 cup                      | [[Sugar]]                                 |
| 2 teaspoons                | [[Baking Powder]]                         |
| 1 teaspoon                 | [[Salt]]                                  |
| 1 cup                      | [[Milk]]                                  |
| 2 tablespoons              | Melted [[Butter]]                         |
| 1 teaspoon                 | [[Vanilla]]                               |
| 1/2 cup                    | [[Raisins]] (optional)                    |
| **For the Topping:** |                                           |
| 1/2 cup                    | Melted [[Butter]]                         |
| 1/2 cup                    | [[Sugar]]                                 |
| 1 1/2 teaspoons            | [[Cinnamon]]                              |
___
## Instructions
1.  Preheat the oven to 350°F. Well grease a 15x10x1 inch jelly roll pan.
2.  In a mixing bowl, sift together the 2 cups of flour, 1 cup of sugar, baking powder, and salt.
3.  Blend in the milk, 2 tablespoons of melted butter, and vanilla. Mix well.
4.  If desired, stir in the 1/2 cup of raisins.
5.  Pour the batter into the prepared jelly roll pan.
6.  Bake for 20 to 25 minutes, or until golden brown.
7.  While the cake is baking, prepare the topping. Drizzle the 1/2 cup of melted butter over the hot, baked cake.
8.  In a small bowl, combine the 1/2 cup of sugar and 1 1/2 teaspoons of cinnamon. Sprinkle this mixture evenly over the buttered cake.
9.  Return the cake to the 350°F oven and bake for another 10 minutes.
