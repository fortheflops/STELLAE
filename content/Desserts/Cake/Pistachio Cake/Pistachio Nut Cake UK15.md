## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 (18.5 ounce) package     | [[White Cake Mix]]                        |
| 1 (3.5 ounce) package      | [[Pistachio Instant Pudding]]             |
| 4                          | [[Eggs]]                                  |
| 1/2 cup                    | Salad [[Oil]]                             |
| 1/2 cup                    | [[Orange Juice]]                          |
| 1/2 cup                    | [[Water]]                                 |
| 1 teaspoon                 | [[Almond Extract]]                        |
| 3/4 cup                    | [[Chocolate Syrup]]                       |

___
## Instructions
1.  Preheat the oven to 350°F. Grease and flour a 10-inch tube pan.
2.  In a large bowl, combine all ingredients EXCEPT the chocolate syrup.
3.  Beat at medium speed for 5 minutes.
4.  Pour two-thirds of the batter into the prepared tube pan.
5.  Add the chocolate syrup to the remaining one-third of the batter, mixing well.
6.  Pour the chocolate batter over the plain batter already in the pan.
7.  Zig-zag a spatula through the batters to create a marble effect.
8.  Bake for 50 to 55 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.
