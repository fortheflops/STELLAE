## Author
Jane Mason (Feb 1996)
___
## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| **For the Cake:** |                                           |
| 2                          | [[Eggs]]                                  |
| 1 cup                      | [[Sugar]]                                 |
| 1 teaspoon                 | [[Vanilla]]                               |
| 1 cup                      | All-purpose [[Flour]]                     |
| 1/2 teaspoon               | [[Salt]]                                  |
| 1 teaspoon                 | [[Baking Powder]]                         |
| 1/2 cup                    | Hot [[Milk]]                              |
| 1 tablespoon               | [[Butter]] or [[Margarine]]               |
| **For the Broiled Topping:** |                                       |
| 5 tablespoons              | Melted [[Butter]]                         |
| 9 teaspoons                | [[Brown Sugar]]                           |
| 4 teaspoons                | [[Cream]]                                 |
| 1/2 cup                    | [[Coconut]]                               |
| 1/2 cup                    | [[Pecans]]                                |
___
## Instructions
### For the Cake
1.  Preheat the oven to 350°F. Grease an 8-inch or 9-inch square layer pan.
2.  In a large bowl, beat the eggs. Gradually add the sugar and continue beating.
3.  Stir in the vanilla.
4.  In a separate bowl, sift together the flour, salt, and baking powder.
5.  Add the dry ingredients to the egg mixture, beating well.
6.  Scald the milk (heat until just before boiling) and add the tablespoon of butter, allowing it to melt in the hot milk.
7.  Add the hot milk mixture to the batter and give it a dozen more beats.
8.  Pour the batter into the prepared pan.
9.  Bake for 30 to 35 minutes.
### For the Broiled Topping
1.  While the cake is baking, combine all topping ingredients (melted butter, brown sugar, cream, coconut, and pecans).
2.  Once the cake is finished baking, pour the topping mixture over the hot cake.
3.  Place the cake under the broiler.
4.  Broil until the topping bubbles and is slightly brown.
___

>[!Note]
>Cut and serve the cake either hot or cold, with ice cream on top if desired.
