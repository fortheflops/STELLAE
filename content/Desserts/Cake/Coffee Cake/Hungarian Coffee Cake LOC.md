## Instructions
### Sweet Dough
1.  Mix together 2 cups lukewarm milk, $\frac{1}{2}$ cup sugar, and 2 teaspoons salt.
2.  Crumble 2 cakes of compressed yeast into the mixture. Stir until yeast is dissolved.
    * _*Note from recipe: If using dry yeast, soak in $\frac{1}{2}$ cup lukewarm water for 5 minutes. Stir before adding. Leave out $\frac{1}{2}$ cup of milk from the main dough ingredients (i.e., use 1 $\frac{1}{2}$ cups milk instead of 2 cups)._
3.  Stir in $\frac{1}{2}$ cup soft shortening and 2 eggs.
4.  Mix in 7-7 $\frac{1}{2}$ cups sifted flour, first with a spoon, then with your hand.
5.  When dough begins to leave the sides of the bowl, turn it out onto a lightly floured board.
6.  Knead dough.
7.  Let rise in a bowl covered with a damp cloth for 1 $\frac{1}{2}$ - 2 hours. (The recipe also mentions "Turn over in bowl," which implies greasing the bowl or the dough lightly).
8.  Punch down and let rise again until double in volume (about 30-45 minutes).
9.  Punch down again.

### Assembly
1.  The recipe states "Sweet dough (make $\frac{1}{2}$)". So, use half of the prepared sweet dough for this coffee cake.
2.  Cut the (half portion of) dough into pieces and form into balls.
3.  Roll each ball in $\frac{1}{2}$ cup melted butter.
4.  Then, roll each buttered ball in a mixture of $\frac{3}{4}$ cup sugar, 1 teaspoon cinnamon, and $\frac{1}{2}$ cup finely chopped nuts.
5.  Place the coated dough balls in a well-greased tube pan.
6.  Let rise for 45 minutes before baking.
7.  Bake at 375°F (190°C) for 35-40 minutes.
