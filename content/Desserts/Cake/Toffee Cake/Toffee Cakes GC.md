## Instructions
1.  Preheat oven to 325°F (slow oven).
2.  Mix the first three ingredients ($\frac{3}{4}$ cup butter, $\frac{1}{2}$ cup brown sugar, and two cups flour) together.
3.  Press this mixture into an ungreased shallow pan (10x15x$\frac{1}{2}$ inch specified on card, though $\frac{1}{2}$ inch depth seems very shallow, likely 10x15x1 or 10x15x2 inch pan is intended).
4.  Bake in the slow oven (325°F) for 25 minutes.
5.  Remove from oven. Immediately sprinkle the chocolate bits over the hot base. Let them sit for a few minutes until they are melted.
6.  Spread the melted chocolate evenly over the base.
7.  Sprinkle with $\frac{1}{2}$ cup of chopped nuts.
8.  Cut into 1 & $\frac{1}{2}$ inch wide squares while still warm.
