*An incredibly moist and intensely flavorful lemon poke cake. This easy recipe uses a lemon cake mix and lemon pudding as a base, which is then poked and saturated with a tangy lemon glaze right out of the oven.*

## Recipe
### For the Cake
| Measurements      | Ingredients                                        |
| :---------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 Package         | [[Lemon Cake Mix]]                                 |
| 1 Package         | Instant [[Lemon Pudding Mix]]                      |
| ¾ Cup             | [[Oil]]                                            |
| 4                 | [[Eggs]]                                           |
| ¾ Cup             | [[Water]]                                          |
### For the Glaze
| Measurements      | Ingredients                                        |
| :---------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 2 Cups            | Sifted Powdered [[Sugar]]                          |
| 6 Tablespoons     | [[Lemon Juice]]                                    |
___
## Instructions
1.  Preheat your oven to 350°F. Grease and flour a 9x13 inch pan.
2.  **Cake:** In a large bowl, combine the lemon cake mix, instant lemon pudding mix, oil, eggs, and water. Blend with an electric mixer for about 2 minutes until smooth.
3.  Pour the batter into the prepared pan.
4.  Bake for 35 minutes.
5.  **Glaze:** While the cake is baking, prepare the glaze. In a small bowl, whisk together the sifted powdered sugar and lemon juice until runny and smooth.
6.  **Finish:** As soon as the cake comes out of the oven, immediately poke the top of the cake all over with a fork.
7.  Pour the glaze smoothly and evenly over the top of the hot cake. Let the cake cool completely in the pan.
___

>[!Note]
>The original card amusingly states "Don't test" for doneness. The 35-minute bake time is usually very reliable for this type of cake.
