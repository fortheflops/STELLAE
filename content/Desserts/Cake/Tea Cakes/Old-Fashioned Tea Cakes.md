### Instructions
1.  Preheat oven to 425°F. Grease your baking sheets.
2.  In a large bowl, sift the dry ingredients together (**flour**, **cream of tartar**, **baking soda**, **salt**, and **sugar**).
3.  Cut the **shortening** into the dry ingredients until crumbly.
4.  In a small bowl, beat the **eggs** and **vanilla**. Add this to the flour mixture and mix well.
5.  Drop the dough by rounded teaspoonfuls onto the greased baking sheets.
6.  Bake for 8-10 minutes, or until the edges are golden.
