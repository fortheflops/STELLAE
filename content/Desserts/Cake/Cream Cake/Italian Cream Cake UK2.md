### Instructions
1. Cream oleo and shortening.
2. Add sugar and beat until mixture is smooth.
3. Add egg yolks and beat well.
4. Combine flour and soda and add to creamed mixture alternately with buttermilk.
5. Stir in vanilla. Add coconut and nuts.
6. Fold in egg whites.
7. Pour in greased, floured 8" pans.
8. Bake 350 degrees Fahrenheit 25 minutes or until done.
9. Cool and Frost with[[Cream Cheese Frosting UK2| Cream Cheese Frosting]]
