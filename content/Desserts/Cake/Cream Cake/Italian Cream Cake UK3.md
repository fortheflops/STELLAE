## Recipe
### Cake
|Measurements|Ingredients|
|:-----------------|:---------------------------|
|2 cups|[[Sugar]]|
|1 stick|[[Oleo]]|
|1/2 cup|[[Crisco]]|
|5|[[Egg Yolks]]|
|2 cups|[[Flour]]|
|1 cup|[[Buttermilk]]|
|1 teaspoon|[[Soda]]|
### Frosting
|Measurements|Ingredients|
|:-----------------|:---------------------------|
|1 8 ounce|[[Cream Cheese]]|
|1 stick|[[Soft Oleo]]|
|1 bag|[[Powdered Sugar]]|
|1 teaspoon|[[Vanilla]]|
|1/2 cup|[[Nuts]]|
___
## Instructions
1.  Cream well sugar, oleo, and Crisco.
2.  Add egg yolks - add 1 at a time.
3.  Alternate sifted regular flour with buttermilk.
4.  Add soda.
5.  Fold in 1 can angel flake coconut and 5 egg whites beaten stiff.
6.  Bake in 3 layers.
7.  Cook at 350 degrees for 25 minutes.
### Frosting Instructions
1.  Cream together 1 8 oz. soft cream cheese and 1 stick soft oleo.
2.  Add 1 bag powdered sugar, 1 teaspoon vanilla, and 1/2 cup coarsely chopped nuts.
