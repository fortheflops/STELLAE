## Recipe
### Cake
| Measurements | Ingredients                 |
| :----------- | --------------------------- |
| 2 cups       | [[Sugar]]                   |
| 1 cup        | [[Oleo]]                    |
| 2            | [[Eggs]]                    |
| 1 cup        | [[Sour Cream]]              |
| 1 tablespoon | [[Butter Flavoring]]        |
| 2 cups       | [[Flour]]                   |
| 1 tablespoon | [[Baking Powder]]           |
| 1/4 teaspoon | [[Salt]]                    |
### Filling
| Measurements | Ingredients                 |
| :----------- | --------------------------- |
| 1 teaspoon    | [[Cinnamon]]                |
| 1 cup        | Chopped [[Nuts]]            |
| 1/4 cup      | [[Brown Sugar]]             |
___
## Instructions
### Cake
1. Cream sugar, oleo, and eggs.
2. Add other ingredients.
3. Place in layers with filling in between.
4. Bake in bundt pan at 325°F - 65 minutes. (Cover)
