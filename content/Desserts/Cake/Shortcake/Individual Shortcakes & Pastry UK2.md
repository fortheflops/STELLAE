## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
| 2 Cups              | All-Purpose [[Flour]]        |
| 3 Tablespoons               | [[Sugar]]                     |
| 2 Teaspoons             | [[Baking Powder]]              |
| ¾ Teaspoon            | [[Salt]]                      |
| ½ Cup               | [[Shortening]]                |
| ⅔ Cup               | [[Milk]]                      |
___
### Instructions
1.  Combine flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt in a large mixing bowl; cut in shortening with a pastry blender.
2.  Add milk to flour mixture until soft dough forms.
3.  Roll ½-inch thick.
4.  Cut with a 3-inch cookie cutter. Carefully place on a baking sheet
5. Bake at 450 for 12-15min.
