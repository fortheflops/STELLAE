# Fudge

> 📜 **Collection:** Burcea Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *Rich and creamy homemade fudge made with chocolate chips, marshmallow creme, and nuts. This classic confection sets into decadent squares perfect for sharing or gifting.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 5 mins | 2 hours | 3 lbs |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Heavy 2 1/2 qt. saucepan
* 13x9 inch pan
* Mixing spoon

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3 c | [[Sugar]] | None |
| 3/4 c | [[Parkay Margarine]] | None |
| 2/3 c (5 1/3 fl. oz. can) | [[Evaporated Milk]] | None |
| 1 12 oz. pkg | [[Semi-Sweet Chocolate Pieces]] | None |
| 1 7 oz. jar | [[Marshmallow Creme]] | None |
| 1 c | [[Chiffed Nuts]] | chopped |
| 1 t | [[Vanilla]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Boiling
1. Combine **3 c sugar**, **3/4 c Parkay margarine**, and **2/3 c evaporated milk** in heavy **2 1/2 qt. saucepan**; bring to a full rolling boil, stirring constantly.
2. Continue boiling **5 min.** over med. heat, stirring constantly to prevent scorching.

### Step 2: Mixing and Setting
3. Remove from heat; stir in **1 12 oz. pkg semi-sweet chocolate pieces** until melted.
4. Add **1 7 oz. jar marshmallow creme**, **1 c chiffed nuts**, and **1 t vanilla**; beat until well blended.
5. Pour into greased **13x9 " pan**.
6. Cool at room temp.; cut into squares.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to two weeks.
