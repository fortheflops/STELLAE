# Christmas Tree Cookies

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic, buttery almond-flavored cookie dough specifically formulated for use in a cookie press to create festive holiday shapes. These tender treats are a vintage staple perfect for decorating with sprinkles.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Dough
1. Preheat your oven to 375°F.
2. In a large bowl, cream the **1 3/4 cups shortening** and **1 cup sugar** together with an electric mixer until the mixture is light and fluffy.
3. Beat in the **1 egg** and **1 teaspoon almond extract** until well combined.
4. In a separate bowl, sift together the **2 3/8 cups flour** and **1/8 teaspoon baking powder**.
5. Gradually add the dry ingredients to the creamed mixture, mixing on low speed until a soft dough forms. If desired, mix in a few drops of **green food coloring**.

### Step 2: Press and Bake
6. Fill your cookie press with the dough and fit it with the Christmas tree disk.
7. Press the cookies onto an ungreased cookie sheet and decorate with **sprinkles** if desired.
8. Bake for **10-12 minutes** until the edges are set but not browned. Let the cookies cool on the baking sheet for one minute before transferring to a wire rack.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The dough can be prepared in advance and chilled; bring to room temperature before using in the cookie press for best results.
