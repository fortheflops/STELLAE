# Mom's Mini Cheesecakes

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Mom | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A cherished recipe from Mom for individual, crustless cheesecakes baked in a muffin tin. These delightful treats feature a tangy, sweet sour cream topping baked right on top.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 35 mins | Chilling time required | 12 cheesecakes |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 12-cup muffin tin
* Paper cup liners
* Mixing bowl
* Small bowl

## Ingredients

### Cheesecake
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 12 oz | [[Cream Cheese]] | softened |
| 1/2 cup | [[Sugar]] | for filling |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] | large |
| 1/2 teaspoon | [[Vanilla]] | extract |
| 1/4 teaspoon | [[Almond Extract]] | extract |

### Topping
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 cup | [[Sour Cream]] | for topping |
| 1/2 cup | [[Sugar]] | for topping |
| 1/2 teaspoon | [[Vanilla]] | extract |
| Optional | Cherry Pie Filling | for garnish |

---

## Instructions

### For the Cheesecake
1. Preheat your oven to **300°F**. Line a **12-cup muffin tin** with **paper cup liners**.
2. In a mixing bowl, beat the softened **12 oz cream cheese** and **1/2 cup sugar** together until smooth and creamy.
3. Add the **2 eggs** one at a time, beating well after each addition.
4. Stir in the **1/2 teaspoon vanilla** and **1/4 teaspoon almond extract**. Beat until the mixture is smooth.
5. Fill each paper liner 3/4 full with the cheese mixture.
6. Bake for **25 minutes** until set.
7. Remove from the oven and let the cheesecakes cool in the pan for **10 minutes**. They will sink slightly in the center.

### For the Topping & Final Bake
8. While the cheesecakes are cooling, mix together the **1 cup sour cream**, **1/2 cup sugar**, and **1/2 teaspoon vanilla** in a small bowl until smooth.
9. Spoon this topping onto each of the warm cheesecakes, spreading it evenly to the edges.
10. If desired, top with a small spoonful of cherry pie filling.
11. Return the muffin tin to the oven and bake for an additional **10 minutes** to set the topping.
12. Let the cheesecakes cool completely, then cover and refrigerate until well chilled before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Cover and store in the refrigerator for up to 4 days until well chilled before serving.
