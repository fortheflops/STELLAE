# Mrs. Galloway's Chocolate Pound Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** UK15 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Mrs. Galloway | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A rich, classic chocolate pound cake from a recipe by Mrs. Galloway of Rockwood, Tenn. This dense, moist cake is topped with an old-fashioned, homemade cooked chocolate frosting with nuts.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Bake the Cake
1. Preheat the oven to **350°F**. Generously grease and flour a 10-inch tube pan.
2. In a large bowl, cream the **1 1/2 cups butter** (or **shortening**) and **3 cups sugar** thoroughly with an electric mixer until light and fluffy.
3. Add the **5 large eggs**, one at a time, beating well after each addition.
4. In a separate bowl, sift the dry ingredients (**3 cups flour**, **4 tablespoons cocoa powder**, **1/2 teaspoon baking powder**, **1/2 teaspoon salt**) together 3 times.
5. On low speed, add the dry ingredients to the creamed mixture alternately with the **1 cup milk** and **1 tablespoon vanilla**, beginning and ending with the dry ingredients.
6. Pour the batter into the prepared tube pan.
7. Bake for approximately **1 hour and 20 minutes**, or until a long wooden skewer inserted into the center comes out clean and the cake is golden brown.

### Step 2: Make the Frosting and Assemble
8. Melt the **2 squares (2 oz) unsweetened chocolate** and set aside.
9. In a medium saucepan, combine the **4 tablespoons butter**, **1 1/2 cups sugar**, **1 cup milk**, **1 tablespoon corn syrup**, and **1/2 teaspoon salt**.
10. Bring the mixture to a boil, stirring occasionally. Once boiling, cook for **2 minutes** until thickened slightly.
11. Remove the pan from the heat and stir in the melted chocolate.
12. Beat the frosting with a mixer or by hand until it begins to cool, thicken, and lose its glossy sheen.
13. Stir in the **1 teaspoon vanilla** and **1 cup chopped nuts**, then immediately spread the warm frosting over the cooled cake.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the cooled cake wrapped tightly in plastic wrap at room temperature for up to 3 days, or refrigerate for up to a week.
