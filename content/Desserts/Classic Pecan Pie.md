# Classic Pecan Pie

> 📜 **Collection:** GailCollection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A timeless, classic pecan pie featuring a sweet and gooey corn syrup custard loaded with pecans, all baked in a flaky pie crust. This quintessential holiday favorite sets into a rich and indulgent dessert.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 45-55 mins | 2 hours | One 9-inch pie |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Medium bowl
* Whisk
* 9-inch pie shell
* Wire cooling rack
* Oven

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | Unbaked Pie Shell | 9-inch |
| 3 | [[Eggs]] | Lightly beaten |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 1 Cup | Karo White [[Corn Syrup]] | Liquid |
| 1/2 Teaspoon | [[Salt]] | Fine |
| 1 Teaspoon | [[Vanilla Extract]] | Pure |
| 1 Cup | [[Pecans]] | Halves or chopped |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Custard
1. Preheat oven to **325°F**.
2. In a medium bowl, lightly beat the **3 eggs**. Add the **1/2 cup sugar** and beat well until combined.

### Step 2: Combine and Assemble
3. Stir in the **1 cup Karo White corn syrup**, **1 teaspoon vanilla extract**, and **1/2 teaspoon salt** until the mixture is smooth.
4. Spread the **1 cup pecans** evenly over the bottom of the unbaked **9-inch pie shell**.
5. Carefully pour the egg mixture over the pecans in the pie shell.

### Step 3: Bake and Cool
6. Bake for **45-55 minutes**, or until the pie is set, golden brown, and a knife inserted near the center comes out clean with a slight jiggle.
7. Let cool completely on a wire rack for at least **2 hours** until the filling firms up before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the baked pie covered in the refrigerator for up to 3 days, or freeze for longer storage.
