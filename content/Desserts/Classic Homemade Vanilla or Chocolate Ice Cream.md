# Classic Homemade Vanilla or Chocolate Ice Cream

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Linda Phillips | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A rich and creamy homemade ice cream base featuring sweetened condensed milk that can be easily customized as vanilla or chocolate. This simple no-cook recipe yields approximately one gallon of delicious frozen treat.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 30 mins | Several hours | Approx. 1 Gallon |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large mixing bowl
* Electric mixer
* Ice cream freezer

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 6 | [[Eggs]] | Large, preferably pasteurized |
| 2 cups | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 1 can | [[Eagle Brand Milk]] | Sweetened condensed |
| 1 tablespoon | [[Vanilla]] | Extract |
| To fill | [[Milk]] | Homogenized, whole |

### Chocolate Variation
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 can | [[Hershey's Chocolate]] | Syrup |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Base
1. In a large bowl, beat the **6 eggs** and **2 cups sugar** together with a mixer until the mixture is well blended, thick, and pale.
2. Stir in the **1 can Eagle Brand Milk** and **1 tablespoon vanilla** until fully combined.

### Step 2: Churn
3. Pour the mixture into the canister of your ice cream freezer.
4. Slowly pour in enough **milk** to reach the manufacturer's fill line, stirring gently.
5. If making chocolate, add the **1 can Hershey's Chocolate** syrup and stir until incorporated.
6. Assemble the ice cream maker and churn according to the manufacturer's directions until the mixture is frozen and reaches a thick, creamy consistency.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Serve immediately for soft-serve texture or transfer to a freezer-safe container to ripen for several hours for a firmer scoopable texture.
