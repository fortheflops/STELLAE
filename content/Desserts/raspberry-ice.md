## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |

| 2 cups | [[Boiling Water]] | None |
| 2 | [[10 oz pkg Frozen Raspberries]] | partially thawed |
| 1 2/3 cups | [[Evaporated Milk]] | tall can |
| 4 cups | [[Half & Half]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Preparation
1. Dissolve **2 3 oz Raspberry Jello** in **2 cups boiling water**.
2. Add **2 cups sugar** & stir until dissolved.
3. Stir in **2 10 oz pkg frozen raspberries** & **1 2/3 cups evaporated milk**.
4. Chill, add **4 cups half & half** & freeze until set to a smooth, icy consistency.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Keep stored in the freezer until ready to serve, allowing it to soften slightly for easier scooping.
