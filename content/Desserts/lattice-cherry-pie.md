# Lattice Cherry Pie

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic, rustic lattice-topped cherry pie featuring a crunchy almond-infused filling. This traditional recipe is perfect for showcasing seasonal fruit with a beautiful presentation.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 30 mins | None | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Pie dish
* Cookie sheet
* Mixing bowl

---
## Ingredients

### Filling Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 4 cups | [[Pie Cherries]] | pitted |
| 1 1/2 cups | [[Sugar]] | granulated |
| 1/4 cup | [[Cornstarch]] | thickener |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Salt]] | fine |
| 2 tbsp | [[Butter]] | cut into small pieces |
| 1/2 cup | [[Blanched Almonds]] | chopped |

---

## Instructions

### Assembly and Baking
1. In a mixing bowl, blend the **1 1/2 cups sugar** with **1/4 cup cornstarch** and **1/4 tsp salt**.
2. Mix in the **1/2 cup chopped blanched almonds** and **4 cups pie cherries** until well combined.
3. Fill a prepared pie shell with the fruit mixture and dot the top with **2 tbsp butter**.
4. Construct a lattice top using pastry strips, then place the pie on a cookie sheet in the lower part of the oven.
5. Bake at 425°F for 30 minutes or until the crust is golden brown and the filling is bubbling.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The filling can be prepared and kept chilled for up to 2 hours before assembling the pie to prevent the crust from becoming soggy.
