# Refrigerator Cheesecake

> 📜 **Collection:** UK15 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic no-bake refrigerator cheesecake featuring a light and fluffy filling made with whipped evaporated milk, lemon gelatin, and cream cheese on a graham cracker crust. This vintage dessert sets up entirely in the refrigerator for a refreshing and firm texture.*

| 25 mins | 0 mins | 4 hours | 1 9-inch cheesecake |

* Freezer
* Large mixing bowl
* Electric mixer
* Medium bowl
* 9-inch pie plate or springform pan

| 1/4 tsp | [[Lemon Rind]] | Grated |

| 1/2 tsp | [[Salt]] | Standard |
| 1 (8-oz) pkg | [[Cream Cheese]] | Softened |

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Crust and Chill
1. Place the can of **1 large can (12 oz) milk** in the freezer for at least **30-60 minutes** until ice crystals begin to form around the edges. Chill your large mixing bowl and beaters.
2. In a medium bowl, combine the **2 cups graham crackers** with the **1/4 cup butter**. Press the mixture firmly into the bottom of a **9-inch pie plate or springform pan**, then chill the crust in the refrigerator.

### Step 2: Dissolve the Gelatin
3. In a small bowl, pour the **1/2 cup water** over the **1 pkg (3 oz) lemon gelatin**. Stir until the gelatin is completely dissolved, then set it aside to cool completely to room temperature.

### Step 3: Whip and Build Filling
4. Pour the ice-cold **1 large can (12 oz) milk** into your chilled mixing bowl and beat with an electric mixer on high speed until thick and stiff peaks form.
5. Add the **1/2 cup lemon juice** and **1/4 tsp lemon rind** and continue beating on high speed until very stiff.
6. Add the **3/4 cup sugar**, **1/2 tsp salt**, and **1 (8-oz) pkg cream cheese** to the whipped milk mixture. Beat on medium speed until smooth and well-blended.
7. Gently fold the cooled, dissolved gelatin mixture into the cream cheese mixture until fully incorporated.

### Step 4: Pour and Set
8. Pour the filling into the prepared **9-inch pie plate or springform pan**. Refrigerate for at least **4 hours**, or preferably overnight, until completely firm and set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store covered in the refrigerator for up to 3-4 days.
