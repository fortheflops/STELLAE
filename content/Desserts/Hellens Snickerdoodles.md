# Hellen's Snickerdoodles

> 📜 **Collection:** The Loraine Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Hellen | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic recipe for soft and chewy snickerdoodles featuring a signature tangy flavor from cream of tartar. These timeless cookies are finished with a generous, sweet cinnamon-sugar coating.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 10 mins | 1 hour | 4–5 dozen |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* large mixing bowl

* sifter

* cookie sheet

| 2 3/4 cups | [[Flour]] | all-purpose |
| 2 tsp | [[Cream of Tartar]] | None |
| 1 tsp | [[Baking Soda]] | essential for texture |
| 1 tsp | [[Salt]] | None |

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |

| 2 tbsp | [[Cinnamon]] | None |

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Dough
1. In a large bowl, cream the **1 cup shortening** and **1 1/2 cups sugar** together with an electric mixer until light and fluffy.
2. Add the **2 eggs** and beat well until fully incorporated.
3. In a separate bowl, sift together the **2 3/4 cups flour**, **2 tsp cream of tartar**, **1 tsp baking soda**, and **1 tsp salt**.
4. Gradually add the dry ingredients to the creamed mixture, mixing on low speed until a soft dough forms.

### Step 2: Chill and Shape
5. Cover the dough and chill for at least **1 hour** to ensure proper texture.
6. Preheat your oven to **400°F**.
7. In a small, shallow bowl, combine the **2 tbsp sugar** and **2 tbsp cinnamon**.
8. Roll the chilled dough into small balls the size of walnuts, then roll each ball in the cinnamon-sugar mixture until coated completely.

### Step 3: Bake
9. Place the balls about 2 inches apart on an ungreased cookie sheet.
10. Bake for **8 to 10 minutes** until the cookies are puffed and set, but still soft to the touch.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The dough can be prepared and chilled for up to 24 hours before baking. Baked cookies store well in an airtight container for up to 5 days.
