# Apple Crisp

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Maxine Surdahl | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic homemade apple crisp featuring a spiced apple filling and a buttery oatmeal crumb topping. Perfect for serving warm with ice cream.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 50 mins | None | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 9x13 Pan
* Mixing bowl

---
## Ingredients

### Filling
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 | [[Apples]] | Large, peeled and sliced |
| 1 cup | [[White Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 2 tbsp | [[Flour]] | All-purpose |
| 1 tsp | [[Cinnamon]] | Ground |

### Topping
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3/4 cup | [[Flour]] | All-purpose |
| 3/4 cup | [[Brown Sugar]] | Packed |
| 3/4 cup | [[Oatmeal]] | Rolled oats |
| 3/4 cup | [[Butter]] | Softened or melted |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Filling
1. Preheat your oven to 350°F and grease a **9x13 Pan**.
2. Place the **10 large apples** into the prepared pan.
3. In a small bowl, combine **1 cup white sugar**, **2 tbsp flour**, and **1 tsp cinnamon**.
4. Pour the dry mixture evenly over the **apples** and toss to coat.

### Step 2: Topping and Baking
5. In another bowl, combine **3/4 cup flour**, **3/4 cup br. sugar**, **3/4 cup oatmeal**, and **3/4 cup butter** until crumbly.
6. Put the topping mixture on top of the rest of the apple layer.
7. Bake at 350°F for **40-60 min.** until the apples are tender and the top is golden brown and bubbly.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the crisp in the pan, cover tightly, and refrigerate for up to a few hours before baking.
