# Classic Pumpkin Spice Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A wonderfully moist and deeply spiced pumpkin cake. This large, dense cake is packed with the warm flavors of cinnamon, nutmeg, and pumpkin pie spice, making it a perfect autumn treat.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and Mixing
1. Preheat your oven to **350°F**. Generously grease and flour a large tube or Bundt pan.
2. In a large bowl, cream the **2 sticks (1 cup) oleo** and **2 3/4 cups sugar** together with an electric mixer until light and fluffy.
3. Add the **3 eggs** one at a time, beating well after each addition.
4. Beat in the **1 #303 can (16 oz) pumpkin puree** and **1 teaspoon vanilla**.

### Step 2: Combining Wet and Dry
5. In a separate medium bowl, whisk together the **3 1/2 cups flour**, **1 teaspoon cinnamon**, **1 teaspoon nutmeg**, and **1/2 teaspoon pumpkin pie spice**.
6. In a small cup, dissolve the **1/2 teaspoon baking soda** in the **2 tablespoons water**.
7. Add the dry ingredients to the creamed mixture alternately with the soda-water mixture, beginning and ending with the dry ingredients. Mix on low speed until just combined.

### Step 3: Baking and Cooling
8. Pour the batter into the prepared pan and spread evenly until smooth.
9. Bake for **1 hour**, or until a long wooden skewer inserted deep into the center comes out clean and free of wet batter.
10. Let the cake cool in the pan for **15-20 minutes** before turning it out onto a wire rack to cool completely.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store wrapped tightly in plastic wrap at room temperature for up to 3 days or freeze for up to 2 months.
