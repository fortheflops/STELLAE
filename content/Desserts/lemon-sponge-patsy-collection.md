# Lemon Sponge

> 📜 **Collection:** Patsy Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Mrs. E. | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic Lemon Sponge pie featuring a bright lemon custard base topped with a light, delicate sponge layer baked in a tender pastry crust. Simple to prepare, it transforms everyday ingredients into an elegant citrus dessert.*

| 3 tbsp | [[Flour]] | None |

| 1 cup | [[Milk]] | None |
| 2 large | [[Eggs]] | separated |
| 1/4 cup | [[Lemon]] juice and rind | freshly squeezed juice and finely grated zest of 1 lemon |
| 1 crust | [[Pie Pastry]] | unbaked |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Batter Preparation
1. Preheat the oven to 450°F.
2. In a mixing bowl, blend together **1 cup sugar**, **3 tbsp flour**, and **2 tsp butter**.
3. Whisk in **2 egg yolks**, **1 cup milk**, and **1/4 cup lemon juice and rind** until evenly incorporated.
4. In a separate clean bowl, beat the **2 egg whites** until stiff peaks form.
5. Gently fold the stiffly beaten **2 egg whites** into the liquid batter mixture.

### Step 2: Baking
6. Pour the batter into a pan lined with unbaked **pie pastry**.
7. Bake at 450°F for 10 minutes, then lower the oven temperature to 350°F and bake for an additional 50 minutes until the top is lightly golden and the filling sets like a soft custard. (Alternatively, bake at 350°F for 50 minutes if prepared as cup custards without pastry crust).

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Best served slightly warm or chilled. Store leftover Lemon Sponge covered in the refrigerator for up to 3 days.
