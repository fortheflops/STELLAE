# Nut Squares

> *A classic layered bar cookie featuring a tender, buttery shortbread crust topped with a chewy, cinnamon-spiced nut meringue. These delightful treats are perfect for pairing with coffee or tea.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Crust
1. Preheat oven to 350°F and grease an 8x8x2 inch pan.
2. In a medium bowl, cream **1/2 cup butter or margarine**, **1/4 cup brown sugar**, **1 tsp vanilla**, and **1/8 tsp salt** until fluffy.
3. Beat in **1 egg yolk** and stir in **1 1/4 cups flour** until a soft dough forms.
4. Press the dough into the pan and bake on the lowest rack for **10 minutes**.

### Step 2: Prepare the Topping and Final Bake
5. In a small bowl, beat **1 egg white** and **1/4 cup brown sugar** until smooth and frothy.
6. Stir in **1 cup nuts** and **1/2 tsp cinnamon**.
7. Spread the mixture over the hot crust and bake on the middle rack for **15 minutes** until the top is firm to the touch.
8. Cool on a wire rack and cut into squares while warm.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 5 days or freeze for up to 3 months.
