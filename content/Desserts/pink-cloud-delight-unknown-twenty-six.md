# Pink Cloud Delight

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Ruth | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A delightful layered dessert featuring a nutty crust, sweet cream cheese and pineapple filling, and a fruity cherry topping. Chill or freeze until set for a refreshing treat.*

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 20 mins | 2 hours | 12 servings |

* Mixing bowl

## Ingredients

### Crust
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 1/2 sticks + 2 tbsp | [[Oleos]] | Softened |
| 1 1/2 cups | [[Flour]] | All-purpose |
| 2 tbsp | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 3/4 cup | [[Pecans]] | Chopped |

### Filling and Topping
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 can | [[Eagle Brand Milk]] | Sweetened condensed milk |
| 1 can | [[Crushed Pineapple]] | Drained |
| 1 can | [[Cherry Pie Filling]] | Ready-to-use |
| 1 large container | [[Cool Whip]] | Thawed |
| 1 cup | [[Pecans]] | For topping |

---

## Instructions

### Crust Preparation
1. Preheat oven to 350°F.
2. Cut **1 1/2 sticks + 2 tbsp oleos**, **1 1/2 cups flour**, and **2 tbsp sugar** together until crumbly.
3. Add **3/4 cup pecans** and mix well.
4. Spread mixture evenly on the bottom of a pan or baking dish.
5. Bake for 20 minutes at 350°F until lightly golden. Remove and let cool completely.

### Filling and Assembly
6. When cool, mix together **1 can eagle brand milk**, **1 can crushed pineapple**, and **1 large cool whip**.
7. Spread the mixture over the cooled crust.
8. Top with **1 can cherry pie filling** and sprinkle **1 c pecans** over the top.
9. Chill in the refrigerator or freeze until set before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Can be prepared a day in advance and kept refrigerated, or frozen for longer storage.
