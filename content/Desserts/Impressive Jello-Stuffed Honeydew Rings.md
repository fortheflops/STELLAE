# Impressive Jello-Stuffed Honeydew Rings

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Gelatin and Melon
1. Dissolve **1 pkg. raspberry flavored gelatin** in **2 cups boiling water** and set aside to cool.
2. Slice one end off the **1 large honeydew melon** and use a long-handled spoon to scoop out all seeds; peel the entire exterior.
3. Place the melon upright in a heavy bowl to stabilize it, then pour the cooled **gelatin** into the cavity until 3/4 full.

### Step 2: Fill and Chill
4. Refrigerate until the **gelatin** reaches the consistency of egg whites.
5. Drop the **1 cubed apple**, **1 cubed pear**, **1 cubed peach**, and **12 seedless grapes** into the cavity.
6. Top off with remaining **gelatin** if needed and chill until completely firm, preferably overnight.

### Step 3: Coat and Serve
7. Mash the **12 oz cream cheese** with a few drops of **light cream** until spreadable.
8. Dry the outside of the **honeydew melon** thoroughly with paper toweling, then spread the **cream cheese** mixture over the entire surface.
9. Roll the coated melon in **chopped pecans** and chill again until the coating is firm.
10. Line a platter with **1 head romaine lettuce**, slice the melon crosswise into 1-inch thick rings, and arrange on the platter.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This dish is best prepared the day before to ensure the gelatin and cream cheese coating are completely firm before slicing.
