# Mac's Flaky All-Shortening Pie Crust

> 📜 **Collection:** UK15 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Mac | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic, tender, and flaky pie crust from Mac, made with shortening and enriched with an egg and a touch of vinegar for an easy-to-handle dough that freezes beautifully.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 0 mins | 30 mins | One double-crust or two single 9-inch pie crusts |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large bowl
* Small bowl
* Pastry blender
* Fork
* Plastic wrap

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3 cups | [[Flour]] | All-purpose |
| 1 1/2 cups | [[Crisco]] | Chilled, shortening |
| 1 | [[Egg]] | Beaten |
| 1 tablespoon | [[Vinegar]] | None |
| 7 tablespoons | [[Water]] | Cold |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Salt]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Combine Dry Ingredients
1. In a large bowl, whisk together the **3 cups flour** and **1 teaspoon salt** until well combined.

### Step 2: Cut in Shortening
2. Add the chilled **1 1/2 cups Crisco** to the flour mixture. Using a pastry blender or your fingertips, cut the shortening into the flour until the mixture resembles coarse crumbs with some pea-sized pieces remaining.

### Step 3: Mix Wet Ingredients
3. In a separate small bowl, whisk together the beaten **1 egg**, **1 tablespoon vinegar**, and **7 tablespoons cold water**.

### Step 4: Form the Dough
4. Pour the wet ingredients over the flour mixture and mix with a fork until the dough just begins to come together into coarse clumps without being overworked.

### Step 5: Shape and Chill
5. Divide the dough in half and shape each half into a flat disk. Wrap each disk tightly in plastic wrap and refrigerate for at least **30 minutes** before rolling out.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This dough can be tightly wrapped and frozen at the disk stage for up to 3 months; thaw overnight in the refrigerator before rolling.
