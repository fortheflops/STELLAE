## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 3 | [[Egg Whites]] |
| 3/8 Teaspoon | [[Cream of Tartar]] |
| 6 Tablespoons | [[Sugar]] |
| 1/2 Teaspoon | Flavoring ([[Vanilla]]) |
___
### Instructions
1.  In a clean, dry bowl, beat **egg whites** with **cream of tartar** until frothy.
2.  Gradually beat in the **sugar**, one tablespoon at a time.
3.  Continue beating until the meringue is stiff and glossy.
4.  Beat in the **flavoring**.
5.  Pile the meringue onto a pie filling, carefully sealing the meringue to the edge of the crust.
6.  Bake at 400°F for 8 to 10 minutes, or until golden brown.
7.  Let cool before serving.
___

>[!Note]
>For the best volume, use room temperature egg whites and make sure your mixing bowl and beaters are completely free of any grease or oil.
