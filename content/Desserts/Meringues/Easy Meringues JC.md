## Recipe
| Measurements      | Ingredients                   |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 4                 | [[Egg Whites]]                    |
| 1/2 teaspoon      | [[Cream of Tartar]]             |
| 4 tablespoons     | [[Sugar]]                       |
___
## Instructions
1.  In a clean bowl, beat the egg whites until they are foamy.
2.  Add the cream of tartar and continue beating until soft peaks form.
3.  Gradually add the sugar, one tablespoon at a time, beating continuously until the meringue is stiff and glossy.
4.  Spread the meringue over a prepared pie, making sure it touches the crust on all sides to prevent shrinking.
5.  Place the pie under a broiler ("under flame") and toast until the peaks are golden brown. Watch it carefully as this happens very quickly.
___

>[!Note]
>The original typed recipe is titled "Merigue." The instruction to "put under flame" refers to using an oven's broiler.
