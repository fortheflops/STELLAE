# Pineapple Cake with Cooked Coconut-Nut Frosting

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *An incredibly moist and simple pineapple cake topped with a rich, warm, cooked coconut and nut frosting poured right over the hot cake.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 35 mins | None | 1 9x13 cake |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 9x13 inch baking pan
* Large bowl
* Medium saucepan
* Whisk
* Spatula
* Toothpick

---
## Ingredients

### For the Cake
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 Cups | [[Flour]] | All-purpose |
| 1 1/3 Cups | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 2 tsp | [[Baking Soda]] | None |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] | Large, standard addition for structure |
| 1 (20 oz) can | [[Crushed Pineapple]] | Undrained |

### For the Topping
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 Stick (1/2 cup) | [[Margarine]] | Or butter |
| 3/4 Cup | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 3/4 Cup | [[Milk]] | Canned or regular |
| 1 tsp | [[Vanilla]] | Extract |
| 1 Cup | [[Coconut]] | Shredded |
| 1 Cup | [[Nuts]] | Chopped |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Bake the Cake
1. Preheat your oven to **350°F**. Grease a **9x13 inch baking pan**.
2. In a large bowl, whisk together the **2 cups flour**, **1 1/3 cups sugar**, and **2 tsp baking soda**.
3. Add the **2 eggs** and the **1 (20 oz) can crushed pineapple** to the dry ingredients. Stir with a spatula until just combined.
4. Pour the batter into the prepared pan and spread evenly.
5. Bake for **30-35 minutes**, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.

### Step 2: Prepare the Topping and Finish
6. About **10 minutes** before the cake is done, begin making the topping.
7. In a medium saucepan, combine the **1 stick (1/2 cup) margarine**, **3/4 cup sugar**, and **3/4 cup milk**. Cook over medium heat, stirring, until the mixture comes to a simmer and thickens slightly.
8. Remove from the heat and stir in the **1 tsp vanilla**, **1 cup coconut**, and **1 cup nuts**.
9. As soon as the cake comes out of the oven, pour the hot topping over the hot cake.
10. Let the cake cool completely in the pan before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the cooled cake covered at room temperature or in the refrigerator for up to 3 days.
