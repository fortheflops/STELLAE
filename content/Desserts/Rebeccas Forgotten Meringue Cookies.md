# Rebecca's Forgotten Meringue Cookies

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Rebecca | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic 'forgotten' meringue cookie loaded with chocolate chips and nuts, baked overnight in a cooling oven for a light, crisp texture. These airy treats are simple to prepare and rely on residual heat to achieve their signature delicate crunch.*

| 1 teaspoon | [[Vanilla]] | extract |
| 1 cup | [[Nuts]] | chopped |
| 1 (6 oz.) package | [[Chocolate Chips]] | semi-sweet |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Meringue
1. Preheat your oven to **350°F** and line a cookie sheet with parchment paper.
2. In a clean, dry bowl, beat the **2 large egg whites** with an electric mixer on high speed until very stiff peaks form.
3. Gradually add the **2/3 cup sugar** and **1 teaspoon vanilla**, continuing to beat until the meringue is stiff, glossy, and holds its shape.

### Step 2: Fold and Bake
4. Gently fold in the **1 cup chopped nuts** and **1 (6 oz.) package chocolate chips** using a spatula.
5. Drop the mixture by rounded teaspoonfuls onto the prepared cookie sheet.
6. Place the cookie sheet into the preheated oven and **immediately TURN THE OVEN OFF**.
7. Leave the cookies in the closed oven overnight, or for at least **8 hours**, without opening the door, until the cookies are crisp, dry, and light to the touch.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** These cookies are designed to be made ahead by resting in the oven overnight; store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 1 week.
