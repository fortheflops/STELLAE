# Decorator Frosting

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A simple and smooth decorator frosting made with vegetable shortening, water, powdered sugar, and white vanilla. Perfect for piping and decorating cakes and cookies with a bright white finish.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | None | None | 1 batch |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Electric mixer

---
## Ingredients

### Frosting Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/3 cup | [[Crisco]] | Do not use liquid type |
| 1/4 cup | [[Water]] | Scant |
| 1 lb. box | [[Powdered Sugar]] | None |
| 1 tsp. | [[White Vanilla]] | Flavoring may be omitted for class |

---

## Instructions

### Mixing
1. Combine the **1/3 cup Crisco**, **1/4 cup water**, **1 lb. box powdered sugar**, and **1 tsp. white vanilla** in a mixing bowl.
2. Beat at medium speed until well blended and smooth.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in a covered container at room temperature or in the refrigerator until ready to use.
