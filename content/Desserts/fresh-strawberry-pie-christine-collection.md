# Fresh Strawberry Pie

> 📜 **Collection:** Christine Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A delightful and refreshing summer pie featuring fresh strawberries and a glossy gelatin filling. Serve chilled with a generous dollop of whipped topping for an easy crowd-pleasing dessert.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 6 mins | 2 hrs | 8 servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Bowl
* Microwave
* Pie pan

## Ingredients

### Pie Filling and Crust
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 small box | [[Vanilla pudding]] | not instant |
| 1 small box | [[Strawberry Jello]] |  |
| 1 1/4 cups | [[Water]] |  |
| 2 cups | [[Fresh strawberries]] | washed and halved |
| 1 | [[Baked pie shell]] | baked |
| As needed | [[Whipped topping]] | for serving |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Cooking the Filling
1. In a bowl, stir **1 small box vanilla pudding (not instant)**, **1 small box strawberry Jello**, and **1 1/4 cups water** together until combined.
2. Cook in microwave on high for **6 minutes**, stirring occasionally, until the mixture becomes clear and thickened.

### Step 2: Assembly and Chilling
3. Add **2 cups fresh strawberries** into the cooked mixture and pour everything into the **1 baked pie shell**.
4. Let chill in the refrigerator for **2 hrs** until set. Serve with **whipped topping**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Prepare and chill the pie up to 24 hours in advance, adding the whipped topping just before serving.
