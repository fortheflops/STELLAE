# Layered Strawberry Salad with Sour Cream Filling

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic multi-layered Jello salad featuring a sweet strawberry, banana, and pineapple base with a surprise tangy sour cream filling in the middle. This retro potluck favorite is perfect for gatherings and sets into a firm, refreshing treat.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 5 mins | 6 hours | 12-15 Servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large mixing bowl
* Wire whisk
* 9x13 inch pan
* Spatula

---
## Ingredients

### Fruit Jello Layers
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 packages (3 oz each) | [[Strawberry Jello]] | powdered |
| 1 cup | [[Boiling Water]] | freshly boiled |
| 1 large can (20 oz) | [[Pineapple]] | crushed, undrained |
| 3 | [[Bananas]] | ripe, mashed |
| 1 cup | [[Nuts]] | chopped |
| 2 packages (10 oz each) | [[Frozen Strawberries]] | thawed |

### Filling
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 pint (2 cups) | [[Sour Cream]] | room temperature |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Base
1. In a large bowl, dissolve the **2 packages (3 oz each) strawberry jello** in **1 cup boiling water**, stirring for 2 minutes until fully dissolved.
2. Stir in the **1 large can (20 oz) crushed pineapple**, **3 mashed bananas**, **1 cup chopped nuts**, and **2 packages (10 oz each) frozen strawberries** until well combined.

### Step 2: Layer and Chill
3. Pour half of the fruit mixture into a **9x13 inch pan** and refrigerate for 1-2 hours until the layer is firm to the touch.
4. Carefully spread the **1 pint sour cream** evenly over the set layer.
5. Gently pour the remaining fruit mixture over the **sour cream** layer.
6. Return to the refrigerator for at least 4 hours, or overnight, until the entire salad is completely firm and set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This salad is best made the day before to ensure the layers are completely firm; keep refrigerated until serving.
