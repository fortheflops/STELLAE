# Devil's Food Drop Cookies

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic recipe for soft, cake-like chocolate cookies. These Devil's Food drop cookies get their rich flavor from melted chocolate and a tender, moist crumb from sour cream.*

| 20 mins | 10 mins | None | 3–4 dozen cookies |

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |

| 1 Cup | [[Brown Sugar]] | packed |
| 1 | [[Egg]] | large |
| 1 Teaspoon | [[Vanilla]] | None |
| 2 oz. | [[Chocolate]] | unsweetened, melted and cooled |
| 2 Cups | [[Flour]] | sifted |
| 1/2 Teaspoon | [[Baking Soda]] | None |
| 1/4 Teaspoon | [[Salt]] | None |
| 3/4 Cup | [[Sour Cream]] | None |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Walnuts]] | chopped |
| As needed | [[Chocolate frosting]] | for topping |

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Batter
1. Preheat your oven to 350°F and grease a cookie sheet.
2. In a large bowl, cream the **1/2 cup butter or margarine** and **1 cup brown sugar** together with an electric mixer until light and fluffy.
3. Beat in the **1 egg** and **1 teaspoon vanilla**, then stir in the **2 oz. chocolate** until smooth.
4. In a separate bowl, sift together the **2 cups flour**, **1/2 teaspoon baking soda**, and **1/4 teaspoon salt**.
5. On low speed, add the dry ingredients to the chocolate mixture alternately with the **3/4 cup sour cream**, beginning and ending with the dry ingredients. Mix until just combined.
6. Stir in the **1/2 cup walnuts**.

### Step 2: Bake and Finish
7. Drop the dough by rounded teaspoonfuls, about 2 inches apart, onto the prepared cookie sheet.
8. Bake for **10 minutes** until the edges are set and the tops spring back when lightly touched.
9. Let the cookies cool on the sheet for a minute before transferring them to a wire rack to cool completely.
10. Once cooled, frost with your favorite **chocolate frosting**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store cooled, unfrosted cookies in an airtight container for up to 5 days; frost just before serving for best texture.
