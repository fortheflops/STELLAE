# Lemonade Cookies

> 📜 **Collection:** Georgia Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Irma Bartles | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A delightful vintage cookie recipe featuring bright citrus flavor from frozen lemonade concentrate. These soft, cake-like cookies are brushed with a sweet lemon glaze and sprinkled with sugar.*

| 2 | [[Eggs]] |  |
| 3 cups | [[Flour]] | sifted |
| 1 tsp | [[Baking Soda]] |  |
| 6 oz | [[Lemonade Concentrate]] | frozen, thawed, divided |
| As needed | [[Sugar]] | for creaming and sprinkling |

---

## Instructions

### Preparation & Baking
1. Preheat oven to **400°F**.
2. Cream **1 cup margarine**, **1 tsp butter flavoring**, **1 tsp lemon flavoring**, and **sugar** together in a mixing bowl.
3. Add **2 eggs** and beat until the mixture is light and fluffy.
4. Combine **3 cups sifted flour** and **1 tsp baking soda**, then add to the creamed mixture.
5. Add **3/4 can of lemonade concentrate** (approx. 4.5 oz) and mix well.
6. Drop the dough onto an ungreased cookie sheet.
7. Bake for **8 minutes** or until they are lightly browned around the edges.
8. While warm, brush the cookies with the remaining **lemonade concentrate** and sprinkle with **sugar**. Let cool.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container for up to 5 days. Cookies can be frozen for up to 3 months.
