# Classic Sour Cream Raisin Pie

> 📜 **Collection:** General Archive | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic, old-fashioned pie featuring a sweet and tangy cooked custard filling loaded with plump raisins and spices, all topped with a light and airy meringue.*

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |

| 5 Tbsp | [[Flour]] | None |
| ¼ tsp | [[Salt]] | None |
| ¼ tsp | [[Cinnamon]] | None |
| 1 Cup | [[Raisins]] | None |
| 1 ½ Cups | [[Sour Cream]] | None |
| 2 | [[Egg Yolks]] | Beaten, reserve whites for meringue |
| 2 | [[Egg Whites]] | For meringue |
| ¼ Cup | [[Sugar]] | For meringue |
| 2 Tbsp | [[Butter]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Filling
1. In a saucepan, combine **1 cup sugar**, **flour**, **salt**, **cinnamon**, **raisins**, and **sour cream**.
2. Cook over medium heat, stirring constantly, until the mixture thickens and begins to bubble.

### Step 2: Temper and Thicken
3. Temper the beaten **egg yolks** by slowly whisking in a small amount of the hot mixture, then pour the tempered yolks back into the saucepan.
4. Add the **butter** and continue to cook for **1-2 more minutes**, stirring constantly until thick.

### Step 3: Assemble Meringue and Bake
5. Pour the hot filling into a pre-baked 9-inch pie shell.
6. Preheat the oven to **350°F**. Beat the **2 egg whites** until soft peaks form.
7. Gradually add **¼ cup of sugar** and continue beating until stiff, glossy peaks form.
8. Spread the meringue over the hot filling, sealing it to the crust edges.
9. Bake for **12-15 minutes**, or until the meringue is golden brown and fragrant.
10. Cool completely on a wire rack, then chill thoroughly before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the baked and chilled pie in the refrigerator for up to 3 days.
