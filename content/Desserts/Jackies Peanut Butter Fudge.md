# Jackie's Peanut Butter Fudge

> 📜 **Collection:** UK15 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Jackie | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic, foolproof recipe for creamy peanut butter fudge featuring a smooth marshmallow creme base and crunchy salted peanuts. This no-fail method creates a rich, decadent confection perfect for gifting or holiday gatherings.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 5 mins | 2 hours | 42 squares |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 13x9x2 inch pan
* large heatproof bowl
* large heavy-bottomed saucepan
* sturdy spoon

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |

| 1 (12 ounce) jar | [[Peanut Butter]] | creamy |
| 1 (7 ounce) jar | [[Marshmallow Creme]] | None |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Vanilla Extract]] | None |
| 1 cup | [[Peanuts]] | chopped, salted |
| 2 cups | [[Sugar]] | granulated |
| 2 cups | [[Light Brown Sugar]] | firmly packed |
| 3/4 cup | [[Milk]] | None |
| 1/2 teaspoon | [[Salt]] | None |
| 1/4 cup | [[Peanut Halves]] | salted, for topping |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation
1. Butter a 13x9x2 inch pan and set aside.
2. In a large heatproof bowl, combine the **12 ounce jar peanut butter**, **7 ounce jar marshmallow creme**, **1 teaspoon vanilla extract**, and **1 cup chopped peanuts**. Set aside.

### Step 2: Cook the Syrup
3. In a large, heavy-bottomed saucepan, combine the **2 cups sugar**, **2 cups light brown sugar**, **3/4 cup milk**, and **1/2 teaspoon salt**.
4. Bring to a boil over medium heat, stirring until the sugar dissolves.
5. Once at a rolling boil, continue to boil for **exactly 4 minutes**, stirring occasionally.

### Step 3: Combine and Set
6. Immediately remove the pan from the heat and pour the hot syrup over the peanut butter mixture in the bowl.
7. Stir quickly and vigorously until the mixture is well blended and smooth.
8. Pour the fudge into the prepared pan and sprinkle the top with the **1/4 cup peanut halves**.
9. Allow the candy to cool for about **2 hours** until firm to the touch before cutting into squares.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to two weeks or refrigerate for longer shelf life.
