# Cowboy Fours

> 📜 **Collection:** Patsy Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *Rich and chewy homemade caramels loaded with fresh pecans. A classic old-fashioned confection made from scratch on the stovetop.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 45 mins | None | Makes about 5 lbs |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Kettle
* Baking pan
* Wooden spoon

---
## Ingredients

### Candy Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 cup | [[W sugar]] | Granulated |
| 1 cup | [[Brown sugar]] | Packed |
| 1 cup | [[Dark Karo]] | Corn syrup |
| 1/2 cup | [[Butter]] | Softened |
| 2 cups | [[Thick cream]] | Heavy cream, divided |
| 1 lb | [[Pecans]] | Chopped or mixed nuts |
| 1 tablespoon | [[Vanilla]] | Pure extract |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Boiling the Base
1. Place **1 cup w sugar**, **1 cup brown sugar**, **1 cup dark karo**, **1/2 cup butter**, and **one cup of the cream** in a kettle.
2. Bring to a boil, then add the **remaining one cup cream** slowly so boiling does not stop.

### Step 2: Cooking and Testing
3. Allow the candy to brown a little bit, preventing burning by stirring often.
4. Cook until it forms a firm ball when dropped in cold water.

### Step 3: Finishing and Cooling
5. Remove from fire and add **1 tablespoon vanilla** and the **1 lb pecans**.
6. Pour into a buttered pan, cool, and cut into squares.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the cut squares in an airtight container between layers of parchment paper at room temperature for up to 2 weeks.
