# Gum Drop Cookies

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Jean W. | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *Delightful chewy cookies packed with colorful gumdrops, coconut, nuts, and rolled oats. A cherished recipe from the John B. Collection.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 12 mins | 2 hours | 24 cookies |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Baking sheet
* Knife

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 cup | [[Shortening]] | Softened |
| 1 cup | [[White Sugar]] | None |
| 1 cup | [[Brown Sugar]] | None |
| 1 cup | [[Gum Drops]] | No black ones, chopped in pieces |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] | None |
| 2 cups | [[Oatmeal]] | None |
| 2 cups | [[Flour]] | None |
| 1 cup | [[Coconut]] | Shredded |
| 1 cup | [[Nuts]] | Chopped |
| 1 tsp | [[Vanilla]] | None |
| 1 tsp | [[Soda]] | Baking soda |
| 1 tsp | [[Salt]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Mixing and Shaping
1. Cream together the **1 cup shortening**, **1 cup white sugar**, and **1 cup brown sugar** in a large mixing bowl until smooth.
2. Beat in the **2 eggs** and **1 tsp vanilla** until well combined.
3. Fold in the **1 cup gum drops**, **2 cups oatmeal**, **2 cups flour**, **1 cup coconut**, **1 cup nuts**, **1 tsp soda**, and **1 tsp salt** until a dough forms.
4. Roll the dough into logs, wrap tightly, and chill in the refrigerator until firm.

### Baking
5. Preheat the oven and slice the chilled logs into uniform rounds.
6. Place on a baking sheet and bake until golden brown around the edges and set in the center.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The dough logs can be wrapped and refrigerated for up to 3 days or frozen for up to 1 month before slicing and baking.
