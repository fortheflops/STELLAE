# Peanut Brittle

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Seven | ✍️ **Attribution:** Grandma Beall | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic homemade peanut brittle recipe straight from Grandma Beall's kitchen, cooked to crunchy perfection in a skillet. This sweet treat combines sugar, Karo syrup, and peanuts for a delightful confection.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 20 mins | 30 mins | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Deep fryer skillet
* Cookie sheet

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 1/2 c. | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 1/2 c. | [[White Karo]] | Light corn syrup |
| 1/4 c. | [[Water]] | None |
| 2 tsp | [[Vanilla]] | Extract |
| 2 tsp | [[Soda]] | Baking soda |
| 1 pint | [[Peanuts]] | Raw or roasted |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Cooking
1. Combine **1 1/2 c. sugar**, **1/2 c. white Karo**, and **1/4 c. water** in a deep fryer skillet.
2. Cook over medium heat until the **1 pint peanuts** crack.

### Step 2: Finishing
3. Turn off the heat.
4. Add **2 tsp vanilla** and **2 tsp soda**.
5. Pour onto a greased cookie sheet fast and let cool until hardened.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 2 weeks.
