# German Plum Cake (Pflaumenkuchen)

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic German sheet cake featuring a rich, buttery, and dense crumb lightly spiced with cardamom. It is traditionally topped with fresh plum halves and baked to golden perfection.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 25 mins | 45 mins | None | 1 9x13 inch cake |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 9x13 inch baking pan
* Large bowl
* Separate bowl
* Electric mixer or wooden spoon
* Whisk

---
## Ingredients

### For the Cake Batter
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 pound (2 sticks) | [[Butter]] | softened |
| 1 1/2 cups | [[Sugar]] | divided |
| 1 packet (or 2 tsp) | [[Vanilla Sugar]] | or vanilla extract |
| 3 large | [[Eggs]] | room temperature |
| 3 cups | [[Flour]] | all-purpose |
| 1 packet (or 4 tsp) | [[Baking Powder]] | none |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Cardamom]] | none |
| 1/2 to 2/3 cup | [[Milk]] | none |
| 1 cup | [[Raisins]] | optional |

### For the Traditional Topping
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 pounds | [[Plums]] | fresh, pitted and halved |
| 2-3 tablespoons | [[Sugar]] | for sprinkling |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Cinnamon]] | for sprinkling |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Batter
1. Preheat your oven to **375°F**. Grease and flour a **9x13 inch baking pan**.
2. In a large bowl, cream the softened **1/2 pound (2 sticks) butter** with the **1 1/2 cups sugar** and **1 packet (or 2 tsp) vanilla sugar** using a wooden spoon or electric mixer until fluffy.
3. Beat in the **3 large eggs** one at a time until fully incorporated.
4. In a separate bowl, whisk together the **3 cups flour**, **1 packet (or 4 tsp) baking powder**, and **1 teaspoon cardamom**.
5. Gradually add the dry ingredients to the butter mixture, alternating with the **1/2 to 2/3 cup milk**, until a very heavy, thick dough-like batter forms. If using, fold in the **1 cup raisins**.

### Step 2: Assemble and Bake
6. Spread the thick **batter** evenly into the prepared pan.
7. Arrange the **2 pounds fresh plums** halves, skin-side down, in tight rows over the top of the batter, pressing them in slightly.
8. In a small bowl, mix **2-3 tablespoons sugar** with **1 teaspoon cinnamon**, and sprinkle the mixture evenly over the plums and batter.
9. Bake for **40-45 minutes**, or until the cake is golden brown and fragrant, and a toothpick inserted into the cake portion comes out clean.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the baked cake tightly covered at room temperature for up to 2 days, or refrigerate for up to 5 days.
