# Lemon Pineapple Dessert

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A light and breezy no-bake Lemon Pineapple Dessert combining bright lemon pie filling, crushed pineapple, and fluffy whipped topping. Perfect for quick prep and sunny afternoon potlucks.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 0 mins | 2 hours | 12-14 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large mixing bowl
* Rubber spatula
* 9x13-inch baking dish

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 can (20 oz) | [[Crushed Pineapple]] | drained |
| 1 can (15.75 oz) | [[Lemon Pie Filling]] |  |
| 1 can (14 oz) | [[Sweetened Condensed Milk]] |  |
| 1 carton (8 oz) | [[Whipped Topping]] | thawed |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Combine Ingredients
1. In a large mixing bowl, combine the **1 can (20 oz) drained crushed pineapple**, **1 can (15.75 oz) lemon pie filling**, and **1 can (14 oz) sweetened condensed milk** until smooth.
2. Gently fold in the **1 carton (8 oz) thawed whipped topping** using a rubber spatula until fully incorporated and streak-free.

### Step 2: Chill and Serve
3. Spread the mixture evenly into a 9x13-inch baking dish or serving bowl.
4. Cover and chill in the refrigerator for at least **2 hours** until well set and chilled before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This dessert can be made up to 24 hours ahead of time and kept covered in the refrigerator until serving.
