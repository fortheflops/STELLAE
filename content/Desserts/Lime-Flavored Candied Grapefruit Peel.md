# Lime-Flavored Candied Grapefruit Peel

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A unique and festive recipe for candied grapefruit peel that gets a vibrant green color and a zesty flavor from the addition of lime-flavored gelatin. This citrus treat is perfect for holiday gifting or as a bright, sweet snack.*

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 | [[Grapefruit]] | medium white, peel only |
| 1 1/2 Cups | [[Sugar]] | granulated |
| 3/4 Cup | [[Water]] | divided |
| 1/2 Teaspoon | [[Salt]] | none |
| 1 (3 oz) package | [[Gelatin]] | lime-flavored |
| As needed | [[Sugar Crystals]] | green, optional for coating |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Peel
1. Score the **2 medium white grapefruit** peels into four lengthwise sections and remove. Scrape away most of the white membrane and cut the peel into 1/4-inch strips.

### Step 2: Blanching
2. Place **2 cups of peel** in a large pan, cover with **water**, bring to a boil, and cook for **20 minutes**; drain.
3. Repeat the boiling and draining process two more times with fresh **water** to remove bitterness, draining thoroughly after the final boil.

### Step 3: Candying
4. In a medium saucepan, combine **1 1/2 cups granulated sugar**, **3/4 cup water**, and **1/2 teaspoon salt**. Cook and stir until the **sugar** dissolves.
5. Add the drained **peel** to the syrup, bring to a boil, and simmer for **20 minutes** until the peel becomes translucent.
6. Gradually add the **1 (3 oz) package lime-flavored gelatin**, stirring over low heat until completely dissolved.

### Step 4: Finishing
7. Remove the **peel** from the syrup and place on a wire rack to drain and cool for **20 minutes**.
8. Roll each piece of **peel** in **green sugar crystals** if using, and let dry thoroughly on the rack before storing.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the finished, dried peel in a covered jar at room temperature for up to two weeks.
