# Mary Haddow's Sugar Cookies

> 📜 **Collection:** UK15 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Mary Haddow | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic, large-batch sugar cookie recipe featuring a unique blend of butter and oil for a soft, tender crumb. These cookies are finished with a signature cinnamon-sugar press for a delightful crunch.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Dough
1. In a large bowl, cream together **1 cup powdered sugar**, **1 cup sugar**, **1 cup oleo**, and **1 cup cooking oil** until smooth.
2. Beat in **2 eggs** and **1 teaspoon vanilla** until well combined.
3. In a separate bowl, whisk together **4 1/2 cups plus 2 tablespoons flour**, **1 teaspoon salt**, **1 teaspoon soda**, and **1 teaspoon cream of tartar**.
4. Gradually incorporate the dry mixture into the wet ingredients until a soft dough forms.

### Step 2: Chill and Shape
5. Cover the dough and chill for at least **2 hours** until firm.
6. Preheat oven to **375°F**.
7. Roll dough into small balls and place on an ungreased cookie sheet.
8. Dip the bottom of a glass into the **cinnamon and sugar mixture** and press each ball flat.

### Step 3: Bake
9. Bake for **10 minutes** until the edges are lightly golden and fragrant.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The dough can be prepared and chilled for up to 24 hours before baking. Baked cookies store well in an airtight container for up to one week.
