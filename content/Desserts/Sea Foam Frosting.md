# Sea Foam Frosting

> 📜 **Collection:** General Archive | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic boiled icing featuring a delicate caramel flavor derived from brown sugar. This light, fluffy meringue frosting is created by whipping a hot sugar syrup into stiffly beaten egg whites.*

---

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5 mins | 10 mins | None | Frosting for one 8x8 inch cake |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* small heavy-bottomed saucepan
* candy thermometer
* electric mixer
* mixing bowl

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/3 Cup | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 1/3 Cup | [[Brown Sugar]] | Firmly packed |
| 1/3 Cup | [[Water]] | Room temperature |
| 1 Tablespoon | [[Corn Syrup]] | Light |
| 1 | [[Egg White]] | Large, room temperature |
| 1/4 Teaspoon | [[Cream of Tartar]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Syrup
1. In a small, heavy-bottomed saucepan, combine the **1/3 cup sugar**, **1/3 cup brown sugar**, **1/3 cup water**, and **1 tablespoon corn syrup**.
2. Bring to a boil over medium heat, stirring until dissolved, then boil without stirring until the syrup reaches the soft-ball stage (238-245°F) on a candy thermometer.

### Step 2: Whip the Meringue
3. While the syrup boils, beat the **1 egg white** with the **1/4 teaspoon cream of tartar** in a clean, dry mixing bowl on high speed until stiff, glossy peaks form.

### Step 3: Emulsify and Finish
4. With the mixer running on high, very slowly and carefully pour the hot syrup in a thin, steady stream into the **egg white** mixture.
5. Continue beating constantly until the frosting is thick, glossy, and holds its shape, then use immediately on a cooled cake.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This frosting is best used immediately after preparation as it sets quickly; it does not store well once whipped.
