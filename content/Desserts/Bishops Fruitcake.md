# Bishop's Fruitcake

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic, dense fruitcake-style loaf packed with walnuts, dates, candied cherries, pineapple, and chocolate chips.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 1 hour 30 mins | None | 1 large loaf |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 9x5 inch loaf pan
* Large bowl
* Medium bowl
* Wire rack
* Wooden skewer

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 1/2 cups | Sifted All-Purpose [[Flour]] | sifted |
| 1 1/2 teaspoons | [[Baking Powder]] | none |
| 1/4 teaspoon | [[Salt]] | none |
| 3 large | [[Eggs]] | well beaten |
| 1 cup | Granulated [[Sugar]] | none |
| 4 ounces | [[Chocolate Chips]] | about 2/3 cup |
| 2 cups | Coarsely Chopped [[Walnuts]] | chopped |
| 1 cup | Coarsely Cut [[Dates]] | cut |
| 1 cup | Candied [[Cherries]] | none |
| 1 cup | Cut Candied [[Pineapple]] | optional |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Mix Dry Ingredients
1. Preheat your oven to **325°F**. Grease a **9x5 inch loaf pan** thoroughly.
2. In a separate medium bowl, sift together the **1 1/2 cups sifted all-purpose flour**, **1 1/2 teaspoons baking powder**, and **1/4 teaspoon salt**.

### Step 2: Coat Fruits and Nuts
3. In a large bowl, combine the **4 ounces chocolate chips**, **2 cups coarsely chopped walnuts**, **1 cup coarsely cut dates**, **1 cup candied cherries**, and **1 cup cut candied pineapple**.
4. Pour the flour mixture over the fruit and nuts, tossing well to coat everything completely and prevent sinking.

### Step 3: Combine Batter and Bake
5. In another large bowl, beat the **3 large eggs** and **1 cup granulated sugar** together until well combined and slightly thickened.
6. Fold the flour-coated fruit and nut mixture into the beaten egg mixture until just combined, noting that the batter will be very thick and dense.
7. Spoon the batter into the prepared **9x5 inch loaf pan** and spread it evenly.
8. Bake for **1.5 hours**, or until a long toothpick or wooden skewer inserted into the center comes out clean.
9. Let the cake cool completely in the pan on a **wire rack** until set before slicing.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store tightly wrapped in plastic wrap or an airtight container in a cool, dry place for up to 1 week, or refrigerate for longer storage.
