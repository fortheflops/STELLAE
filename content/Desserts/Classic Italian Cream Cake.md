# Classic Italian Cream Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** UK5 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic Southern-style layer cake with a buttermilk batter full of coconut and walnuts, finished with a rich cream cheese frosting.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 25 mins | 30 mins | None | 1 3-layer cake |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Electric mixer
* Large mixing bowls
* Whisk
* Three 8- or 9-inch round cake pans
* Toothpick

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Batter Base
1. Preheat the oven to **350°F**. Grease and flour three 8-inch or 9-inch round cake pans.
2. In a clean bowl, beat the **5 eggs** (whites only) with an electric mixer until stiff peaks form. Set aside.
3. In a separate large bowl, cream together the **2 cups sugar**, **1 stick (1/2 cup) margarine**, and **1/2 cup Crisco** until light and fluffy.
4. Beat in the **1 tablespoon vanilla extract**, then add the **5 eggs** (yolks only) one at a time, beating well after each.
5. In another bowl, whisk together the **2 cups flour**, **1 teaspoon soda**, and **1/2 teaspoon salt**.
6. Alternately add the dry ingredients and the **1 cup buttermilk** to the creamed mixture, beginning and ending with the dry ingredients. Mix until just combined.
7. Gently fold in the beaten egg whites, followed by the **1 cup walnuts** and **2 cups coconut** until fully incorporated.

### Step 2: Bake and Ice
8. Divide the batter evenly among the three prepared pans and bake for **25-30 minutes**, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.
9. Let the layers cool completely before frosting.
10. For the icing, in a large bowl, beat the **8 ounces cream cheese**, **1 stick (1/2 cup) margarine**, and **1 teaspoon vanilla** until smooth and creamy.
11. Gradually beat in the **1 pound powdered sugar** until the frosting reaches a smooth, spreadable consistency.
12. Frost the cooled cake layers, spreading icing between each layer and over the top and sides of the cake until even.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the frosted cake covered in the refrigerator for up to 4 days; bring to room temperature before serving.
