# Coffee Drop Cookies

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *Delightful homemade coffee drop cookies infused with aromatic spices and sweet raisins. These tender spiced treats pair perfectly with an afternoon cup of coffee.*

| 2 | [[eggs]] | Whole |
| 3 tsp | [[baking powder]] | Standard |
| 3/4 tsp | [[salt]] | Standard |
| 2 tsp | [[cinnamon]] | Ground |
| 3 C. | [[flour]] | All-purpose |
| 2 C. | [[raisins]] | Standard |
| 1 C. | [[cold strong coffee]] | Brewed and chilled |

---

## Instructions

### Mixing and Baking
1. Cream together the **1 C. shortening** and **2 C. brown sugar** in a large mixing bowl until smooth.
2. Beat in the **2 eggs** one at a time until well combined.
3. In a separate bowl, whisk together the **3 C. flour**, **3 tsp baking powder**, **3/4 tsp salt**, and **2 tsp cinnamon**.
4. Alternately add the dry flour mixture and **1 C. cold strong coffee** to the creamed mixture, mixing just until combined.
5. Fold in the **2 C. raisins** gently until evenly distributed.
6. Drop the dough by spoonfuls onto prepared baking sheets.
7. Bake at 375 degrees for **12-15 minutes** until golden brown and set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store baked cookies in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 5 days, or freeze the dough dropped by spoonfuls for later baking.
