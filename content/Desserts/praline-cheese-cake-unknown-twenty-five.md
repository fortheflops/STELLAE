## Instructions

### Step 1: Crust Preparation
1. Combine **1 c. cinnamon crisp graham crackers**, **3 T. sugar**, and **3 T. melted margarine** in a bowl, then press firmly into the bottom of a springform pan.

### Step 2: Filling and Baking
2. Combine **3 (8 oz.) packages cream cheese** and **1 1/4 c. dark brown sugar**. Add **2 T. flour**, **3 eggs**, **1 1/2 t. vanilla**, and **1/2 c. chopped pecans**, mixing until smooth.
3. Pour filling into the pan and bake at 350° for 50 minutes until set. Cool completely before removing from the pan, noting that the cake may puff up and crack slightly but will be covered by the topping.

### Step 3: Finishing
4. Brush the top of the cooled cake several times with **maple syrup** and garnish all over with **pecan halves**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the cheesecake covered in the refrigerator for up to 2 days before serving.
