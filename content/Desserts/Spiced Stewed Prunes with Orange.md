# Spiced Stewed Prunes with Orange

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A simple and comforting recipe for stewed prunes, gently simmered with a cinnamon stick and fresh orange for a warm, fragrant, and classic dessert. This elegant fruit dish offers a perfect balance of spiced sweetness and citrus brightness.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5 mins | 45 mins | None | 4–6 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Medium saucepan
* Knife
* Peeler

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 cups | [[Prunes]] | pitted |
| 3 cups | [[Water]] | cold |
| 1 stick | [[Cinnamon]] | or 1 tsp ground |
| 1 | [[Orange]] | peeled |
| 1/2 teaspoon | [[Orange Peel]] | thinly sliced |
| 1 1/2 tablespoons | [[Sugar]] | granulated |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Combine and Simmer
1. In a medium saucepan, combine the **2 cups prunes**, **3 cups water**, **1 cinnamon stick**, **1 peeled orange**, **1/2 teaspoon orange peel**, and **1 1/2 tablespoons sugar**.
2. Bring the mixture to a gentle simmer over medium heat.
3. Reduce the heat to low, cover the saucepan, and simmer for 30 to 60 minutes until the **prunes** are very tender and the liquid has thickened into a fragrant, syrupy consistency.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 5 days; serve warm or chilled.
