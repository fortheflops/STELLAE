# Lemon Crumb Pie

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Margo Gordon | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A refreshing, no-bake lemon dessert featuring a light, whipped evaporated milk filling and a crunchy vanilla wafer crust. This classic 1964 recipe is perfect for warm weather gatherings.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 0 mins | 2 hours | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Electric mixer
* Shallow baking dish
* Jellyroll pan

---
## Ingredients

### Filling and Crust
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 large can | [[Evaporated milk]] | well chilled |
| 1 pkg. | [[Lemon Jello]] | prepared |
| 1/2 cup | [[Sugar]] | granulated |
| 1/4 cup | [[Lemon juice]] | freshly squeezed |
| to taste | [[Lemon rind]] | grated |
| 4 cups | [[Vanilla wafer crumbs]] | approx. 1 pkg. |
| 1 dash | [[Salt]] | fine |

---

## Instructions

### Preparation
1. Prepare the **1 pkg. lemon Jello** according to package directions using two cups of water; chill until the mixture is almost set.
2. In a separate bowl, whip the **1 large can evaporated milk** until light and fluffy.
3. Add the **1/4 cup lemon juice**, **grated lemon rind**, **1/2 cup sugar**, and **1 dash salt** to the whipped milk, then gently fold this mixture into the thickened **lemon Jello**.

### Assembly
4. Line a shallow greased baking dish or jellyroll pan with a portion of the **4 cups vanilla wafer crumbs**.
5. Pour the **lemon Jello mixture** over the crumb base, then sprinkle the remaining **vanilla wafer crumbs** over the top.
6. Chill until completely firm and set before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This pie must be made ahead to allow the gelatin to set properly; keep refrigerated until ready to serve.
