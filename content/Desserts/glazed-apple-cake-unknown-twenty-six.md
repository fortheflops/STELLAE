# Glazed Apple Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A delightful old-fashioned glazed apple cake featuring fresh diced apples and a rich, sweet caramel-like sauce poured hot over the warm cake. Perfect for serving with afternoon coffee or as a comforting after-dinner treat.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparing the Cake Batter
1. Mix **2 cups diced peeled apples** with **1 cup sugar**, let stand until the sugar is dissolved.
2. Add **1 egg** and beat well.
3. Sift together dry ingredients (**1 cup sifted flour**, **1 1/2 tsp cinnamon**, and **1/2 tsp baking soda**) and add to the apple mixture.
4. Add **1/2 cup chopped nuts** and stir to combine.

### Step 2: Baking
5. Pour the batter into a buttered 8-inch square baking dish.
6. Bake for **40 minutes** in a preheated oven at **375°F** until golden and a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.

### Step 3: Making the Sauce and Glazing
7. In a saucepan, cook **1/2 cup packed brown sugar**, **1/2 cup white sugar**, **2 tbsp flour**, and **1 cup water** together until clear.
8. Remove from heat and add **1/4 cup butter** and **1 tsp vanilla**, stirring until the butter is melted and the sauce is smooth.
9. Pour the hot sauce over the warm cake while the sauce is still hot.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The cake can be baked ahead of time and warmed slightly before pouring the freshly prepared hot sauce over the top.
