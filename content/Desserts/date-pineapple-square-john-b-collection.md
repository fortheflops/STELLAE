# Date-Pineapple Square

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A delightful date and pineapple square featuring a yellow sheet cake base topped with a creamy fruit and gelatin mixture. Chilled until set, it makes for a refreshing and sweet dessert portioned for a crowd.*

---

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 30 mins | 10 mins | 2 hours | 15 servings |

| 1 tbsp | [[Sugar]] | None |

| 1/2 cup | [[Cold water]] | None |

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparing the Fruit Mixture
1. Combine **1 1/2 cups dates**, **1 can (8 3/4 oz) crushed pineapple**, **1 tbsp sugar**, and **1 dash salt** in a saucepan.
2. Heat to boiling, stirring occasionally until the mixture is hot and bubbling.

### Step 2: Adding Gelatin and Cooling
3. Soften **1 tbsp unflavored gelatin** in **1/2 cup cold water**.
4. Add the softened gelatin to the hot date mixture and stir until completely dissolved.
5. Stir in **2 tbsp lemon juice**, then cool until slightly thickened to a syrupy consistency.

### Step 3: Assembling and Chilling
6. Fold **1 cup whipping cream** (whipped) into the cooled date and pineapple mixture until smooth.
7. Spread the date-pineapple topping evenly over the **1 yellow sheet cake**.
8. Chill in the refrigerator until thoroughly set, mark off into **15 servings**, and garnish with additional **dates** and **pecans** before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Cover and chill in the refrigerator for several hours or overnight before slicing and serving.
