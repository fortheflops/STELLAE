# Gena Morris's Pina Colada Poke Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Gena Morris | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *An incredibly moist and tropical poke cake from Gena Morris, saturated with cream of coconut and sweetened condensed milk. Topped with a fluffy Cool Whip and shredded coconut frosting for the ultimate island-inspired treat.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 35 mins | 1 hr 10 mins | 12–16 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 9x13 inch baking pan
* Meat fork or wooden skewer
* Medium mixing bowl
* Wire whisk
* Spatula

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 package | [[Cake Mix]] | Yellow, plus ingredients to prepare according to box |
| 1 (15 oz) can | [[Cream of Coconut]] | Such as Coco Lopez |
| 1 (14 oz) can | [[Eagle Brand Milk]] | Sweetened condensed milk |
| 1 large (12 oz) carton | [[Cool Whip]] | Thawed |
| 1/2 bag (3-4 oz) | [[Coconut]] | Shredded, for topping |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Bake the Cake
1. Preheat your oven to **350°F**.
2. Prepare and bake the **1 package yellow cake mix** in a **9x13 inch pan** according to the package directions until golden brown and a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.

### Step 2: Poke and Soak
3. As soon as you remove the cake from the oven, while it is still hot, use a meat fork or a wooden skewer to poke holes all over the top of the **1 package yellow cake mix**.
4. In a medium bowl, whisk together the **1 (15 oz) can cream of coconut** and the **1 (14 oz) can eagle brand milk**.
5. Slowly and evenly pour this mixture over the hot, poked cake, allowing it to soak completely into the holes.

### Step 3: Cool and Frost
6. Let the cake cool completely at room temperature.
7. Once cooled, spread the **1 large (12 oz) carton cool whip** evenly over the top of the cake to frost it.
8. Sprinkle the **1/2 bag shredded coconut** evenly over the Cool Whip until fully covered.
9. Refrigerate the cake until thoroughly chilled and ready to serve.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store covered in the refrigerator for up to 4 days; the flavors only deepen as it chills.
