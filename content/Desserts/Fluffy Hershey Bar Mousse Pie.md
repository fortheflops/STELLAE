# Fluffy Hershey Bar Mousse Pie

> 📜 **Collection:** General Archive | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A nostalgic and incredibly easy no-bake pie featuring a light and fluffy mousse filling made from melted Hershey bars and marshmallows, folded into whipped cream and set in a crumb crust. This wonderfully light dessert offers a rich chocolate flavor perfect for any gathering.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 10 mins | 4-6 hours | One 9-inch pie |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Double boiler
* Electric mixer
* Mixing bowls
* 9-inch pie pan
* Spatula

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 (9-inch) | [[Chocolate]] or [[Graham Cracker Crust]] | prepared |
| 25 Large | [[Marshmallows]] | whole |
| 6 | [[Hershey Bars]] | standard 1.55 oz size, broken up |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Milk]] | standard |
| 1/2 Pint (1 cup) | [[Heavy Cream]] | chilled |
| Optional Topping | [[Nuts]] or Grated [[Hershey]] | chopped or grated for garnish |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Melt the Base
1. In the top of a double boiler, combine the **25 large marshmallows**, **6 Hershey bars**, and **1/2 cup milk**. Heat gently, stirring frequently, until completely melted, smooth, and combined.

### Step 2: Cool Completely
2. Remove the mixture from the heat and let it cool completely to room temperature until it reaches a lukewarm, set consistency.

### Step 3: Whip the Cream
3. In a separate, chilled bowl, whip the **1/2 pint (1 cup) heavy cream** with an electric mixer until stiff peaks form and hold their shape.

### Step 4: Fold and Assemble
4. Gently fold the cooled chocolate-marshmallow mixture into the whipped cream until no white streaks remain and the texture is uniform.

### Step 5: Fill and Chill
5. Pour the filling into the prepared **chocolate or graham cracker crust** and spread evenly.
6. Sprinkle with chopped **nuts** or grated **Hershey bar**, if desired.
7. Chill in the refrigerator for at least 4-6 hours, or until firm and set, before slicing and serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store sealed in the refrigerator for up to 3 days. Ensure the chocolate mixture is fully cooled before assembly to prevent melting the whipped cream.
