# Creamy Chocolate Frosting

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A rich and smooth cream cheese chocolate frosting that easily spreads over any cooled cake. Perfectly balanced with sweet confectioners sugar and melted unsweetened chocolate.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 0 mins | None | 1 cake |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Electric mixer

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 pkg. | [[Cream Cheese]] | Softened |
| 3 tbsp. | [[Milk]] | Added one tablespoon at a time |
| 2 1/2 c. | [[Confectioners Sugar]] | Sifted |
| 2 sq. | [[Unsweetened Chocolate]] | Melted |
| 1/8 tsp. | [[Salt]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing and Frosting
1. Soften **1 pkg. cream cheese** by adding **3 tbsp. milk**, **1 tablespoon at a time**.
2. Add gradually **2 1/2 c. sifted confectioners sugar**, blending well after each addition.
3. Blend in **2 sq. unsweetened chocolate**, **melted**, and **1/8 tsp. salt**.
4. Beat until smooth and fluffy.
5. Frost top and sides of cooled cake.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the frosting covered in the refrigerator for up to 3 days; bring to room temperature and re-whip before using.
