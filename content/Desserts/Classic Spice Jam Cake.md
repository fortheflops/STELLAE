# Classic Spice Jam Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic, moist jam cake with a tender crumb from sour milk and a rich flavor from brown sugar and warm spices.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 30 mins | None | 1 loaf or 2 8-inch layers |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Loaf pan or two 8-inch round cake pans
* Large bowl
* Separate bowls
* Electric mixer
* Whisk
* Toothpick

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3/4 Cup | [[Butter]] | softened |
| 1 Cup | [[Brown Sugar]] | packed |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] | well beaten |
| 1 1/2 Cups | [[Flour]] | all-purpose |
| 1 tsp. | [[Baking Soda]] | none |
| 1 tsp. | [[Cinnamon]] | none |
| 1 tsp. | [[Allspice]] | none |
| 1 tsp. | [[Nutmeg]] | none |
| 1 Cup | [[Jam]] | blackberry or raspberry |
| 3 Tbsp. | [[Sour Milk]] | buttermilk |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Cream
1. Preheat your oven to **375°F**. Grease and flour one loaf pan or two 8-inch round cake pans.
2. In a large bowl, cream the **3/4 cup butter** and **1 cup brown sugar** together with an electric mixer until light and fluffy. Add the **2 eggs** and mix to combine.

### Step 2: Combine Dry and Wet Ingredients
3. In a separate bowl, sift or whisk together the **1 1/2 cups flour**, **1 tsp. baking soda**, **1 tsp. cinnamon**, **1 tsp. allspice**, and **1 tsp. nutmeg**.
4. In another small bowl, stir the **1 cup jam** and **3 tbsp. sour milk** together.

### Step 3: Mix and Bake
5. Add the dry ingredients to the creamed mixture in three parts, alternating with the jam mixture, beginning and ending with the dry ingredients. Mix on low speed until just combined.
6. Pour the batter into your prepared pan(s).
7. Bake for **20-30 minutes**, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean and the cake is fragrant.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store wrapped tightly at room temperature for up to 3 days or refrigerate for up to a week.
