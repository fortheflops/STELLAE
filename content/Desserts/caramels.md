# Caramels

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *Rich and chewy traditional caramels made with sugar, cream, and glucose. Cooked to the crack stage for the perfect classic confection texture.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 30 mins | 2 hours | 24 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Saucepan
* Candy thermometer
* Baking pan

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 lb | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 2/3 lb | [[Glucose]] | None |
| 1 1/3 cups | [[Cream]] | Heavy cream |
| 1/6 lb | [[Butter]] | Softened |
| As needed | [[Flour]] | For kneading |
| Optional | [[Nuts]] | Chopped |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and Cooking
1. Knead **1/6 lb butter** with a small amount of **flour**.
2. Add **1 1/3 cups cream** at a time to the mixture.
3. Cook the mixture with **1 lb sugar** and **2/3 lb glucose** until it reaches the hard-crack stage and cracks when tested.
4. Add the remaining **1/6 lb butter** at last and fold in **nuts** if desired before pouring to set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container between layers of parchment paper at room temperature for up to 2 weeks.
