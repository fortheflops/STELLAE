# Mary's Old-Fashioned Flour-Thickened Caramels

> 📜 **Collection:** JohnBCollection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Mary | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A unique vintage caramel recipe featuring a distinct technique where flour is kneaded into butter to create a chewy, rich texture. These old-fashioned confections are finished with a hint of vanilla for a classic flavor profile.*

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 30 mins | None | 1 pan |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* large pot
* mixing bowl
* candy thermometer
* greased pan

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 Cups | [[Sugar]] | granulated |
| 1 1/3 Cups | [[Cream]] | divided |
| 2/3 Cup | [[Corn Syrup]] | white |
| 1/6 Pound | [[Butter]] | softened |
| 1 tsp | [[Vanilla]] | extract |
| As needed | [[Flour]] | all-purpose, kneaded into butter |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Thickener
1. Knead as much **[[Flour]]** into the **[[Butter]]** as it will hold until the mixture forms a 'wad' about the size of a small orange.

### Step 2: Boil the Syrup
2. In a large pot, combine **2 cups [[Sugar]]**, **2/3 cup [[Corn Syrup]]**, and **2/3 cup [[Cream]]** (half of the total). Cook for **5 minutes**.
3. Add the remaining **2/3 cup [[Cream]]** and continue to cook until the mixture reaches the hard-crack stage (it 'cracks' when tested in cold water).

### Step 3: Finish and Set
4. Add the **[[Butter]]** and **[[Flour]]** mixture to the pot and stir constantly until the texture is smooth.
5. Remove from heat and stir in **1 tsp [[Vanilla]]**.
6. Pour into a greased pan and allow to cool completely before cutting into squares.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to two weeks, or wrap individually in wax paper.
