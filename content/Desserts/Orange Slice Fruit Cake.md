# Orange Slice Fruit Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** UK15 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A wonderfully rich and moist old-fashioned fruitcake packed with chewy orange slice candies, dates, coconut, and pecans. Baked low and slow in a tube pan, it is finished with a sweet fresh orange juice glaze.*

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and Coating
1. Preheat your oven to **325°F**. Generously grease and flour a **large tube pan**.
2. In a very large bowl, combine the **1 lb orange slice candy**, **1 (8-10 oz) package dates**, **2 cups pecans**, and **1 cup coconut**.
3. Add **1/2 cup of the flour** and toss until all the fruit and nuts are well coated.

### Step 2: Mixing the Batter
4. In a separate large bowl, cream together the **1 cup Crisco** and **2 cups sugar** until light and fluffy.
5. Add the **4 eggs** one at a time, beating well after each addition.
6. In a small bowl, stir the **1 teaspoon baking soda** into the **1/2 cup buttermilk**.
7. Alternately add the remaining **3 cups flour** and the buttermilk mixture to the creamed mixture, beginning and ending with the flour.
8. Stir in the **2 tablespoons grated orange rind**.

### Step 3: Combining and Baking
9. Pour the batter over the floured fruit and nuts. Using a sturdy spoon or your hands, mix thoroughly until everything is well combined and the very thick batter is uniform.
10. Spoon the batter into the prepared tube pan, packing it down gently.
11. Bake for **1 1/2 to 2 1/2 hours** until a long wooden skewer inserted deep into the center comes out clean.

### Step 4: Glazing and Cooling
12. While the cake is still hot and in the pan, whisk together the **1 cup fresh orange juice** and **2 cups powdered sugar** to make a smooth glaze.
13. Poke holes all over the top of the hot cake with a skewer and slowly pour the glaze over it, allowing it to soak in completely.
14. Let the cake cool completely in the pan before removing.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store tightly wrapped in plastic wrap or foil in an airtight container for up to 1 week at room temperature, or freeze for up to 3 months.
