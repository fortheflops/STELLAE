# Oatmeal Chocolate Chip Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Pat Blake | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A delightful and moist oatmeal chocolate chip cake topped with chopped walnuts and semi-sweet chocolate chips. This classic recipe from Pat Blake yields a rich 9x13 inch pan treat.*

---

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 40 mins | 10 mins | 12 servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment

* Mixing bowls

* Measuring spoons

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Oatmeal Soaking
1. Pour **1 3/4 cups boiling water** over **1 cup uncooked oatmeal**. Let stand at room temperature for **10 minutes**.

### Step 2: Mixing the Batter
2. Add **1 cup brown sugar**, **1 cup granulated sugar**, and **1/2 cup margarine** to the oatmeal mixture. Stir until **margarine** melts.
3. Add **2 extra large eggs** and mix well.
4. Sift together **1 3/4 cups unsifted flour**, **1 tsp soda**, **1/2 tsp salt**, and **1 tbsp cocoa**.
5. Add the flour mixture to the sugar mixture and mix well.
6. Add about half of the **12 oz pkg semisweet chocolate chips** to the batter.

### Step 3: Baking
7. Pour the batter into a greased and floured **9x13 inch pan**.
8. Sprinkle **3/4 cup chopped walnuts** and the remaining **semisweet chocolate chips** on top.
9. Bake in an oven preheated to **350 degrees** for about **40 minutes**, or until a wooden pick inserted in the center comes out clean.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the baked cake covered at room temperature for up to 3 days, or wrap tightly and freeze for up to 2 months.
