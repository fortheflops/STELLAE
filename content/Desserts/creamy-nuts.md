# Creamy Nuts

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Irene Franklyn | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic, rich confection featuring a creamy sugar base folded with crunchy nut meats. This simple recipe creates a smooth, spreadable candy perfect for holiday gifting or snacking.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 15 mins | 30 mins | 12 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Saucepan
* Wooden spoon
* Wax paper

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 cup | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 1 tbsp | [[Karo]] | Light corn syrup |
| 1/2 cup | [[Sour cream]] | Full fat |
| 1 pinch | [[Salt]] | Fine |
| 1 tbsp | [[Butter]] | Unsalted |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Vanilla]] | Or preferred spices |
| 2 to 2 1/2 cups | [[Nut meats]] | Chopped walnuts or pecans |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Cooking the Base
1. In a saucepan, combine **1 cup sugar**, **1 tbsp Karo**, **1/2 cup sour cream**, and a **pinch salt**.
2. Cook over medium heat, watching carefully, until the mixture reaches the soft ball stage.

### Step 2: Finishing and Cooling
3. Remove from heat and stir in **1 tbsp butter** and **1/4 tsp vanilla**.
4. Beat the mixture until it becomes creamy and opaque.
5. Fold in **2 to 2 1/2 cups nut meats**.
6. Spread the mixture onto wax paper to cool and set until firm.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to one week.
