# Classic Chocolate Fondue

> 📜 **Collection:** LOC | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A simple and elegant chocolate fondue featuring the unique honey-almond notes of Toblerone. This rich, smooth dip is perfect for parties when served with an assortment of fresh fruit and cake.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Melt and Combine
1. Break the **9 oz triangular shaped chocolate (toblerone)** into pieces.
2. In a small, heavy saucepan or the top of a double boiler, combine the **9 oz triangular shaped chocolate (toblerone)** and **1/2 cup heavy cream**.
3. Heat gently over low heat, stirring constantly, until the **9 oz triangular shaped chocolate (toblerone)** is completely melted and the mixture is smooth and glossy.

### Step 2: Finish and Serve
4. Remove from the heat and stir in the **2 tablespoons brandy, cointreau, coffee liqueur or peppermint liqueur**.
5. Transfer the mixture to a fondue pot set over a low flame or warmer to keep it at a smooth, dippable consistency.
6. Arrange the **sponge or angel food cake**, **marshmallows**, **grapes**, **pineapple chunks**, **dried apricots**, and **banana** on a platter and serve immediately.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Fondue can be prepared ahead and gently reheated over low heat or in a double boiler; add a splash of cream if it thickens too much.
