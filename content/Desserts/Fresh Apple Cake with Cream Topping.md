# Fresh Apple Cake with Cream Topping

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *An incredibly moist and dense fresh apple cake, made with oil for a tender crumb and packed with grated apple and chopped nuts. The warm cake is served with a simple, rich cream and sugar sauce.*

| 2 | [[Eggs]] | large |

| 3 cups | [[Flour]] | all-purpose |
| 1/2 teaspoon | [[Salt]] | fine |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Baking Soda]] | sifted |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Cinnamon]] | ground |

### For the Topping
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |

| 1/2 cup | [[Margarine]] | or butter, one stick |
| 1/2 cup | [[Coffee Cream]] | half-and-half |

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Cake
1. Preheat your oven to **300°F**. Grease and flour a 9x13 inch pan or a tube/Bundt pan.
2. In a large bowl, mix the **1 1/2 cups Wesson Oil** and **2 cups sugar**. Add the **2 eggs** one at a time, beating well after each addition.
3. Stir in the **3 cups raw apples** and **2 teaspoons vanilla**.
4. In a separate bowl, whisk together the **3 cups flour**, **1/2 teaspoon salt**, **1 teaspoon baking soda**, and **1 teaspoon cinnamon**.
5. Gradually add the dry ingredients to the wet mixture, stirring until just combined, then fold in the **1 cup chopped nuts**.
6. Pour the batter into the prepared pan and bake for **1 1/2 to 2 hours** until a long wooden skewer inserted into the center comes out clean.

### Step 2: Prepare the Topping
7. Shortly before the cake is done, combine the **1 cup sugar**, **1/2 cup margarine**, and **1/2 cup coffee cream** in a small saucepan.
8. Heat over medium-low heat, stirring constantly, until the **sugar** is completely dissolved and the mixture is warm and smooth.
9. Serve the warm cake with the warm topping drizzled over each slice.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The cake can be stored at room temperature for 2 days or refrigerated for up to 5 days; reheat slices slightly before serving with the topping.
