# Carlson Sweets

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A delightful no-bake confection made by grinding dried fruits and walnuts together, rolled and cut into sweet morsels. Preserved carefully in a tin box with oiled paper between layers for long-lasting freshness.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 30 mins | None | None | 24 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Meat chopper
* Cutting board
* Small biscuit cutter
* Tin box

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 lb | [[Figs]] | Stems removed |
| 1 lb | [[Dates]] | Stems removed |
| 1 lb | [[Walnuts]] | Shelled |
| As needed | [[Powdered Sugar]] | For dusting and rolling |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and Grinding
1. Remove stems from **1 lb figs** and **1 lb dates**.
2. Mix the fruit and **1 lb walnuts** together, then put them through a meat chopper.

### Step 2: Rolling and Cutting
3. Work the mixture thoroughly with your hands on a board drenched with **powdered sugar**.
4. Roll the mixture **1/4" thick** and cut with a small biscuit cutter until all dough is used.

### Step 3: Storing
5. Dip each piece in **powdered sugar** and pack tightly in a tin box with oiled paper between each layer.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in a tightly sealed tin box with oiled paper between layers in a cool, dry place for up to several weeks.
