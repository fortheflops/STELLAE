# Fruit Cookies

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A delightful old-fashioned recipe for spiced fruit cookies loaded with candied fruits, raisins, and nuts. These rich drop cookies can be stored for months.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing the Batter
1. Cream the **1 cup shortening** and **2 cups sugar** together until light and fluffy.
2. Beat in the **2 eggs** one at a time, mixing well after each addition.
3. Alternately add the dry ingredients (**3 cups flour**, **1 tsp baking powder**, **1/4 tsp salt**, **1/2 tsp cloves**, **2 tsp cinnamon**, **1/2 tsp nutmeg**) and the **3/4 cup milk**, mixing just until combined.
4. Stir in the **1/2 cup candied pineapple**, **1/4 cup citron**, and **1 cup each raisins, candied cherries & nuts** until evenly distributed.

### Step 2: Baking and Storing
5. Drop by spoonfuls onto a greased or parchment-lined baking sheet.
6. Bake at 375 degrees F until golden around the edges and set in the center, about 10 to 12 minutes.
7. Cool completely on a wire rack. Keep for months in an airtight container.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** These cookies actually improve in flavor as they sit and can be stored in an airtight container for up to several months.
