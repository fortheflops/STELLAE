# Butterfinger Ice Cream

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Cathy Tatz | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A rich, homemade 1-gallon ice cream featuring a creamy egg and evaporated milk base loaded with crunchy Butterfinger candy pieces. This classic frozen treat is perfect for gatherings and can be easily customized with a hint of peanut butter.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 30 mins | 4 hours | 1 Gallon |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large mixing bowl
* Electric mixer
* 1-gallon ice cream maker
* Freezer-safe container

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3 Cups | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] | Large |
| 3 Large Cans | [[Evaporated Milk]] | Standard size |
| 1 Teaspoon | [[Peanut Butter]] | Optional |
| 5 | [[Butterfingers]] | Crushed |
| To fill | [[Milk]] | Whole milk |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Base
1. In a large bowl, beat the **2 eggs** until light and frothy, then gradually beat in the **3 cups sugar** until the mixture is thick and pale.
2. Stir in the **3 large cans evaporated milk** and the optional **1 teaspoon peanut butter** until the mixture is well combined and the sugar has fully dissolved.

### Step 2: Churn and Freeze
3. Pour the mixture into the canister of a **1-gallon ice cream maker** and add enough **whole milk** to reach the manufacturer's fill line.
4. Churn the ice cream according to the machine's directions until it reaches a soft-serve consistency.
5. During the last 5 minutes of churning, add the **5 crushed Butterfingers** and continue to churn until evenly distributed.
6. Transfer the mixture to a freezer-safe container and freeze for at least 4 hours until firm and scoopable.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight, freezer-safe container for up to 2 weeks for best texture.
