# Peanut Coconut Brittle

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic stovetop peanut brittle with a delightful twist of shredded coconut for extra texture. This crunchy, sweet, and salty candy is perfect for holiday gifting or special treats.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 20 mins | 30 mins | Approx. 2 pounds |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* heavy-bottomed 3-quart saucepan
* candy thermometer
* greased baking sheet
* greased spatula

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 cups | [[Sugar]] | rounded |
| 1/2 cup | [[Water]] | None |
| 1/4 cup | [[Karo Syrup]] | light corn syrup |
| 2 tablespoons | [[Oleo]] | margarine or butter |
| 1 1/2 cups | [[Peanuts]] | raw preferred |
| 1 cup | [[Coconut]] | shredded |
| 2 1/2 teaspoons | [[Baking Soda]] | scant 3 |
| 2 tablespoons | [[Vanilla]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Syrup
1. Generously grease several pie pans or a large cookie sheet.
2. In a heavy-bottomed 3-quart saucepan, combine the **2 cups sugar**, **1/2 cup water**, and **1/4 cup Karo syrup**. Cook over medium heat, stirring constantly, until the **sugar** dissolves.

### Step 2: Reach Hard-Crack Stage
3. Continue to cook, without stirring, until the syrup reaches the **hard-crack stage (300°F to 310°F)** on a candy thermometer. The mixture should be a light tan color.

### Step 3: Add Mix-ins and Finish
4. Stir in the **1 1/2 cups peanuts** and cook for 1 more minute.
5. Remove the pan from the heat. Immediately stir in the **2 tablespoons oleo** until melted.
6. Add the **2 1/2 teaspoons baking soda**, **2 tablespoons vanilla**, and **1 cup coconut** all at once. Stir rapidly as the mixture foams up.
7. Immediately pour the hot, foamy mixture into your prepared pans or onto the cookie sheet, spreading it as thinly as possible with a greased spatula.
8. Let the brittle cool completely until hard, then break into pieces.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the finished brittle in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 2 weeks.
