# Ida's Sugar Crescents

> 📜 **Collection:** JohnBCollection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Ida | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *Delicate, melt-in-your-mouth crescent cookies featuring a buttery base and chopped nuts. These classic treats are baked until set and finished with a generous coating of powdered sugar while still warm.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 20 mins | None | 2 dozen |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* baking sheet
* mixing bowl
* electric mixer

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Butter]] or [[Margarine]] | softened |
| 3 Tablespoons | [[Powdered Sugar]] | plus extra for rolling |
| 1 Cup | [[Flour]] | all-purpose |
| 1 Cup | [[Nuts]] | chopped |
| As Desired | [[Flavoring]] | e.g., vanilla extract |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Dough
1. Preheat your oven to 350°F.
2. In a large bowl, cream the **1/2 cup butter or margarine** until smooth, then add the **3 tablespoons powdered sugar** and blend well.
3. Stir in your preferred **flavoring** if using.
4. Gradually add the **1 cup flour**, mixing thoroughly until a dough forms.
5. Gently fold in the **1 cup nuts** until evenly distributed.

### Step 2: Shape and Bake
6. Shape the dough into small crescents, roughly the size of a small finger.
7. Place the crescents onto a greased baking sheet.
8. Bake at 350°F for **20 minutes** until the cookies are set and lightly golden.
9. While the cookies are still warm, roll them in additional **powdered sugar** until thoroughly coated.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to one week. You can freeze the unbaked dough logs for up to 3 months.
