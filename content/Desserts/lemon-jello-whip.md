| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |

## Instructions

### Step 1: Gelatin Base
1. Dissolve **1 pkg Lemon Jello** and **1 cup sugar** in **1 cup boiling water**.
2. Stir in the **juice of 2 lemons**.
3. Let the mixture sit until it reaches a jelly-like consistency.

### Step 2: Whipping
4. Whip the **1 can chilled evaporated milk** until stiff peaks form.
5. Fold the whipped milk into the congealed **lemon jello mixture** until fully combined.

### Step 3: Assembly
6. Pour the mixture over a base of crushed **vanilla wafers** or **graham crackers** and chill until set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This dessert is best made at least 4 hours in advance to ensure it is fully set and chilled before serving.
