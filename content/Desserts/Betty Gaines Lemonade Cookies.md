# Betty Gaines' Lemonade Cookies

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Betty Gaines | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A bright and tangy classic 1965 recipe for soft, cake-like lemonade cookies. This treat features a double dose of citrus flavor using frozen lemonade concentrate in both the batter and the glaze.*

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 8-10 mins | None | 4 dozen cookies |

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Dough
1. Preheat your oven to 400°F.
2. In a large bowl, cream together the **1 cup butter or oleo** and **1 cup sugar** until light and fluffy.
3. Beat in the **2 eggs** until well combined.
4. In a separate bowl, sift together the **3 cups flour** and **1 tsp. baking soda**.
5. On low speed, add the dry ingredients to the creamed mixture alternately with **1/2 cup lemonade concentrate**, beginning and ending with the dry ingredients.

### Step 2: Bake and Glaze
6. Drop by rounded teaspoonfuls about 2 inches apart on an ungreased cookie sheet.
7. Bake for **8-10 minutes**, until the edges are lightly browned and the centers are set.
8. As soon as the cookies come out of the oven, immediately brush the hot cookies with the **remainder of the lemonade concentrate** and sprinkle with **sugar**.
9. Remove the cookies to a cooling rack to cool completely.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 3 days or freeze for up to 1 month.
