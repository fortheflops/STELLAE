## Instructions

### Making the Cake
1. Beat **2 eggs**, add **1 C. sugar**, and beat again until well combined.
2. Add **2 C. flour** and **1 C. sour cream**, then add the flavoring and mix thoroughly.
3. At last, stir in the **1 tsp soda** that has been dissolved in a little water.
4. Bake until golden and a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.

### Making the 7 Minute Icing
5. Combine **1 C. sugar**, **1/4 C. cold water**, **1/2 tsp vanilla**, **1/8 tsp cream of tartar**, **1/8 tsp salt**, and **1 egg white**.
6. Cook for **7 minutes** while beating continuously until stiff peaks form and glossy texture is achieved.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the baked cakes in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 2 days, or frost with the icing and serve fresh.
