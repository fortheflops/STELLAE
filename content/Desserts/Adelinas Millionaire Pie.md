# Adelina's Millionaire Pie

> 📜 **Collection:** General Archive | ✍️ **Attribution:** Adelina | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic no-bake Millionaire Pie featuring a light and fluffy filling of pineapple, pecans, and sweetened condensed milk in a creamy Cool Whip base. This easy recipe makes two delicious pies!*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 0 mins | 4 hours | Two 9-inch pies |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large mixing bowl
* Electric mixer
* Rubber spatula

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 | [[Graham Cracker Crusts]] | 9-inch, ready-made |
| 1 (20 oz) can | [[Crushed Pineapple]] | well-drained |
| 1 (9 oz) container | [[Cool Whip]] | thawed |
| 1 can | [[Sweetened Condensed Milk]] | standard size |
| 3 tablespoons | [[Lemon Juice]] | fresh or bottled |
| 1/2 cup | [[Pecans]] | chopped |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mix the Base
1. In a large bowl, beat together the thawed **1 (9 oz) container Cool Whip**, **1 can sweetened condensed milk**, and **3 tablespoons lemon juice** with an electric mixer until smooth, well combined, and fluffy.

### Step 2: Fold in Mix-ins
2. Gently fold in the well-drained **1 (20 oz) can crushed pineapple** and **1/2 cup pecans** until evenly distributed throughout the creamy mixture.

### Step 3: Assemble and Chill
3. Divide the filling mixture evenly between the **2 graham cracker crusts**, spreading the tops smoothly.
4. Refrigerate for at least **4 hours**, or overnight, until the pies are completely firm, well chilled, and ready to slice.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the pies covered in the refrigerator for up to 3 days before serving.
