# Classic Cherry Coke Jello Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** UK15 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A fun and fizzy vintage Jello salad featuring the unique combination of cherry Jello, Coca-Cola, cream cheese, and maraschino cherries. This nostalgic treat offers a delightful texture and a classic flavor profile perfect for retro gatherings.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 5 mins | 4 hours | 6-8 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* small saucepan
* medium mixing bowl
* wire whisk
* serving dish or mold

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 package (3 oz) | [[Cherry Jello]] | powder |
| 2 cups | [[Coca-Cola]] | divided into 1 cup hot and 1 cup cold |
| 1 package (3 oz) | [[Cream Cheese]] | softened |
| 1 small jar | [[Maraschino Cherries]] | drained and halved |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Base
1. In a small saucepan, heat **1 cup Coca-Cola** until hot but not boiling.
2. In a medium bowl, pour the hot **1 cup Coca-Cola** over the **1 package (3 oz) Cherry Jello** and stir for 2 minutes until completely dissolved.
3. Add the **1 package (3 oz) cream cheese** to the mixture and whisk until smooth and the **cream cheese** is mostly incorporated.

### Step 2: Chill and Set
4. Stir in the remaining **1 cup cold Coca-Cola**.
5. Place the bowl in the refrigerator and chill until the mixture reaches the consistency of unbeaten egg whites.
6. Gently fold in the **1 small jar maraschino cherries**.
7. Pour into a serving dish or mold and refrigerate for at least 4 hours, or until the salad is completely firm and set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This salad must be made at least 4 hours in advance to set properly. Keep refrigerated until ready to serve.
