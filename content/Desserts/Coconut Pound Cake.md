# Coconut Pound Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** UK15 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *An intensely flavored and incredibly moist coconut pound cake featuring a full pound of butter, cream of coconut, and coconut extract.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 1 hour 30 mins | 15 mins | 1 large tube cake |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 10-inch tube or Bundt pan
* Large bowl
* Electric mixer
* Separate mixing bowls
* Whisk
* Long wooden skewer
* Wire rack

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |

| 3 cups | [[Sugar]] | none |
| 6 | [[Eggs]] | room temperature |
| 4 cups | All-purpose [[Flour]] | none |
| 1 tsp | [[Baking Powder]] | See Note |
| 1 tsp | [[Salt]] | See Note |
| 1 cup | [[Coco Lopez Cream of Coconut]] | none |
| 2 tablespoons | [[Milk]] | none |
| 2 teaspoons | [[Coconut Extract]] | none |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Cream
1. Preheat your oven to **325°F**. Generously grease and flour a large **10-inch tube or Bundt pan**.
2. In a large bowl, cream the **1 pound (4 sticks) butter** and **3 cups sugar** together with an electric mixer for **5-7 minutes**, until very light and fluffy.
3. Add the **6 eggs**, two at a time, beating well after each addition until fully incorporated.

### Step 2: Combine Wet and Dry Mixtures
4. In a separate bowl, whisk together the **4 cups flour**, **1 tsp baking powder**, and **1 tsp salt**.
5. In a small bowl, combine the **1 cup Coco Lopez cream of coconut**, **2 tablespoons milk**, and **2 teaspoons coconut extract**.

### Step 3: Mix and Bake
6. On low speed, add the dry ingredients to the creamed mixture in three parts, alternating with the coconut-milk mixture, beginning and ending with the dry ingredients. Mix only until just combined.
7. Pour the batter into the prepared pan and spread evenly.
8. Bake for **1 hour and 30 minutes**, or until a long wooden skewer inserted into the center comes out clean.

### Step 4: Cooling
9. Let the cake cool in the pan for **15 minutes** before turning it out onto a wire rack to cool completely.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the cooled cake wrapped tightly in plastic wrap at room temperature for up to 3 days, or freeze for up to 2 months.
