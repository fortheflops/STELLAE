# Jello Fudge Divinity

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A delightful and traditional candy recipe featuring Jello, whipped egg whites, and sugar syrup whipped to perfection. This sweet treat offers a unique texture and flavor.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 20 mins | 30 mins | 24 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Saucepan
* Candy thermometer
* Electric mixer
* Mixing bowl

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3 cups | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 3/4 cup | [[White Karo]] | Corn syrup |
| 3/4 cup | [[Water]] | None |
| 2 | [[Egg whites]] | Separated |
| 1 package | [[Jello]] | Any flavor |
| 1 tsp | [[Vanilla]] | Optional |
| 1/2 cup | [[Nuts]] | Chopped, optional |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Boiling Syrup
1. Boil **3/4 cup water**, **3/4 cup white karo**, and **3 to cups sugar** to medium hard ball stage.

### Step 2: Whipping and Mixing
2. Beat **2 egg whites** until peaks form.
3. Add **1 package dry jello** and beat.
4. Add hot syrup mixture slowly, along with **1 tsp vanilla** and **1/2 cup nuts**, beating until mixture holds its shape and loses its glossy sheen.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature with wax paper between layers for up to a week.
