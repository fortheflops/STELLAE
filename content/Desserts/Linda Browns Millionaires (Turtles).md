# Linda Brown's Millionaires (Turtles)

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Linda Brown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A rich, classic candy featuring chewy caramel and pecan clusters dipped in smooth chocolate. This elegant homemade confection is a timeless holiday favorite.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare and Melt Caramel
1. Line a cookie sheet with buttered **waxed paper**. Unwrap **1 (14 oz) package caramels**.
2. In the top of a double boiler, melt the **1 (14 oz) package caramels** with **3 tablespoons evaporated milk** over low heat, stirring constantly until smooth and completely melted.

### Step 2: Form Clusters
3. Remove the caramel mixture from the heat and stir in **2 cups pecan pieces** until evenly coated.
4. Drop the mixture by teaspoonfuls onto the prepared **waxed paper**. Let the clusters cool and set for at least **30 minutes** until firm.

### Step 3: Melt Chocolate and Dip
5. In a clean double boiler, melt **18 ounces semi-sweet chocolate chips** with **1-2 tablespoons shortening or coconut oil** until smooth, glossy, and completely melted.
6. Using a spoon, dip one caramel-pecan cluster at a time into the melted **chocolate**. Use a toothpick to help lift it out, tapping the spoon on the side of the pan to knock off excess **chocolate**.
7. Return the chocolate-coated clusters to the **waxed paper**.
8. Let the candies cool completely until the **chocolate** is hard and set.
9. Store in airtight tin containers with **wax paper** between the layers.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in airtight tin containers with wax paper between the layers at room temperature for up to 2 weeks, or freeze for longer storage.
