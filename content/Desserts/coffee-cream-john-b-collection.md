# Coffee Cream

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A delightful and creamy coffee-flavored rice pudding infused with vanilla and topped with whipped cream, pecans, and chocolate. This comforting dessert offers a rich texture and a wonderful balance of coffee and sweetness.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Rice Base
1. Combine **1 1/3 cups water**, **1 tsp salt**, and **2/3 cup uncooked rice** in a saucepan and stir in **1 cup milk**.
2. Cover and cook until the rice absorbs the milk.
3. Stir in **1/2 cup sugar**, **1 cup marshmallows**, and **1 cup strong coffee**.

### Step 2: Simmer and Cool
4. Cook covered over low heat for **15 mins**, stirring frequently until the mixture thickens properly.
5. Remove from heat and let cool completely.

### Step 3: Assembly
6. When cool, whip **1 cup whipping cream** and fold it along with **1/2 tsp vanilla** into the cooled rice mixture.
7. Serve topped with **toasted pecans** and grated **unsweetened chocolate**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Can be prepared a few hours in advance and kept chilled in the refrigerator until ready to serve.
