# Mernie's Pecan Pie

> 📜 **Collection:** Elizabeth Knee Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Mernie | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic, sweet southern pecan pie from Mernie's family collection. This recipe features a rich custard base made with white Karo syrup and melted butter, baked to golden-brown perfection.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Mix
1. Preheat your oven to **400°F**.
2. In a medium mixing bowl, whisk together the **3/4 cup sugar**, **1/2 cup white Karo corn syrup**, **2 tablespoons flour**, **3 eggs**, **3 tbsp melted butter**, and **1 tsp. vanilla extract** until the mixture is smooth and completely integrated.
3. Gently fold in the **1 cup pecans**.

### Step 2: Bake
4. Pour the filling mixture into your unbaked **1 pie crust**.
5. Place in the preheated oven and bake at **400°F**.
6. Watch the pie closely; as soon as the filling starts to thicken and set around the edges, turn the oven temperature down to **350°F**.
7. Continue baking at **350°F** until the center is set and only slightly jiggly when nudged (about 35 to 40 minutes total baking time). Let cool completely for at least **2 hours** before slicing to allow the custard to set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This pie can be baked up to 24 hours in advance. Store covered at room temperature or refrigerate, bringing to room temperature before serving.
