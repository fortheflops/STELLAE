# Refrigerator Pumpkin Pie

> 📜 **Collection:** Elizabeth Knee Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Jeanne Jones, Karine Robin & Sally Abrams | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A delightful no-sugar-added refrigerator pumpkin pie adapted from the No Sugar Deluxe Dessert Cookbook. It features a spiced gelatin and evaporated milk filling that sets into a smooth, creamy texture.*

---

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 10 mins | 2 hours | 8 servings |

## Ingredients

| 1 tbsp | [[Unflavored gelatin]] | Unflavored |
| 1 tsp | [[Ginger]] | Ground |
| 1/2 tsp | [[Nutmeg]] | Ground |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Salt]] | None |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] | Beaten |
| 13 oz can | [[Evaporated milk]] | No-sugar-skin style |
| 1 lb can | [[Pumpkin]] | Canned pumpkin puree |
| 8 packets | [[Equal sweetener]] | Artificial sweetener |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Cooking the Base
1. Mix **1 tbsp unflavored gelatin**, **1 tsp ginger**, **1/2 tsp nutmeg**, and **1/4 tsp salt** in a saucepan.
2. Beat **2 eggs** and **13 oz can evaporated milk** together and pour into the dry ingredients.
3. Let stand, then stir over low heat until the gelatin is completely dissolved, about **10 mins**.

### Step 2: Assembling and Chilling
4. Blend in **1 lb can pumpkin** and **8 packets Equal sweetener**.
5. Pour the mixture into the **1 cooked pie crust**.
6. Chill in the refrigerator until firm to the touch.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Keep refrigerated until ready to serve; can be made a day in advance.
