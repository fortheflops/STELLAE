# Ice-Cream Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic frozen dessert featuring layers of ladyfingers, vanilla ice cream, orange, raspberry, and pineapple flavors. Perfectly refreshing for special gatherings and easily made ahead.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Assembly and Orange Layer
1. Line the bottom and sides of a 9" springform pan with **18-20 ladyfingers**, split in half.
2. Mix **1 quart vanilla ice cream** with the **1 frozen orange-juice concentrate**.
3. Pour into mold over the bottom layer of **ladyfingers**.
4. If ice cream becomes too soft, refreeze slightly before pouring. Freeze firm.

### Step 2: Raspberry and Pineapple Layer
5. Purée the **2 packages frozen raspberries** and **two 9-ounce cans crushed pineapple**—or use the blender—and add the **1 tablespoon frozen lemonade concentrate**.
6. Strain to remove seeds and freeze until partially frozen.
7. Place in a chilled bowl and beat slightly.
8. Spoon over orange layer and refreeze until firm.

### Step 3: Final Layer and Freezing
9. To the other **1 quart vanilla ice cream** add **1 teaspoon almond flavoring**, **1 teaspoon rum flavoring**, **6 maraschino cherries**, and **3 tablespoons blanched pistachio nuts**.
10. Pour over raspberry layer and freeze overnight until completely firm.

### Step 4: Serving
11. Remove cake from freezer and place in refrigerator about 2 hours before serving.
12. Remove from pan, and garnish with **sweetened whipped cream**, **sugared strawberries**, and **mint**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Cover and freeze overnight for up to several days. Transfer to the refrigerator about 2 hours before serving to soften slightly.
