# Elegant Black Cherry & Red Wine Jello Mold

> 📜 **Collection:** General Archive | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A sophisticated congealed dessert featuring dark sweet cherries suspended in a rich, red wine-infused cherry gelatin. This elegant mold is best served chilled with a dollop of creamy dressing.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 5 mins | 4 hours | 8 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* saucepan
* large mixing bowl
* jello mold
* platter

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 (1-pound) cans | [[Cherries]] | dark sweet, pitted, syrup reserved |
| 2 (3-ounce) pkgs. | [[Gelatin]] | cherry flavored |
| 1 Cup | [[Wine]] | red |
| As needed | [[Water]] | to reach 2 1/2 cups total liquid |
| Optional | [[Almonds]] | sliced, for stuffing |
| For serving | [[Sour Cream]] | dressing |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Base
1. Drain the **2 (1-pound) cans cherries**, reserving the syrup.
2. Optional: Stuff the pitted **cherries** with **almonds**.
3. Add enough **water** to the reserved syrup to reach **2 1/2 cups** of liquid.
4. Bring the liquid to a boil in a saucepan.

### Step 2: Combine and Set
5. In a large bowl, pour the boiling liquid over the **2 (3-ounce) pkgs. gelatin**, stirring until completely dissolved.
6. Stir in the **1 cup red wine**.
7. Chill the mixture until it reaches the consistency of raw egg whites.
8. Fold in the **cherries** and pour into a mold.
9. Chill for at least **4 hours** until firm, then unmold onto a platter and serve with **sour cream dressing**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This mold must be prepared at least 4 hours in advance to set properly; keep refrigerated until ready to serve.
