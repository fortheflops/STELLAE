# Carole's Oatmeal Cookies

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Carole | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A hearty and customizable oatmeal cookie recipe featuring a wholesome base of whole wheat flour, wheat germ, and oats. These cookies are easily adapted with your favorite mix-ins like raisins, coconut, and sunflower seeds.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Base
1. Preheat your oven to 350°F.
2. In a large bowl, cream together the **1 cup shortening**, **1 cup brown sugar**, and **1/2 cup honey** with an electric mixer.
3. Add the **2 eggs** and mix well.

### Step 2: Combine and Bake
4. In a separate bowl, whisk together the **1 1/2 to 2 cups flour**, **1/2 cup wheat germ**, **1/2 teaspoon soda**, and **4 tbsp carob powder**.
5. Add the dry ingredients to the creamed mixture and mix until just combined.
6. By hand, stir in the **3 cups oats**.
7. Fold in your desired amounts of **1 cup raisins**, **1 cup sunflower seeds**, and **1 cup coconut**.
8. Drop by rounded spoonfuls onto an ungreased baking sheet.
9. Bake for **8-10 minutes**, or until lightly browned and set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 5 days or freeze for up to 3 months.
