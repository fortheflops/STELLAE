# Mrs. Hayes' Spiced Prune Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** PatsyCollection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Mrs. Hayes | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A wonderfully rich and moist spiced cake packed with cooked prunes, flavored with hot coffee, mace, and cinnamon, and lightened with beaten egg whites.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 25 mins | 1 hour | None | 1 large tube or 9x13 cake |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Saucepan
* Large mixing bowl
* Separate bowls
* Large tube pan or 9x13 inch pan
* Toothpick
* Electric mixer

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 ¼ Cups | [[Prunes]] | Washed, cooked, pitted, and chopped |
| 2 ¼ Cups | [[Sugar]] | None |
| 1 ¾ Cups | [[Shortening]] | Crisco or other shortening |
| 1 ⅛ Cups | [[Coffee]] | Hot |
| 4 ¼ Cups | [[Flour]] | All-purpose |
| ¾ tsp | [[Salt]] | None |
| 1 ½ tsp | [[Mace]] | None |
| ½ tsp | [[Cinnamon]] | None |
| 2 ¼ tsp | [[Baking Soda]] | None |
| 4 | [[Eggs]] | Separated |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Prunes and Oven
1. Place the **2 ¼ cups prunes** in a saucepan and cover with water. Bring to a simmer and cook until they are soft and tender. Drain, remove the pits, and chop or mash the prunes.
2. Preheat your oven to **350°F**. Grease and flour a large tube pan or a **9x13 inch pan**.

### Step 2: Mix the Batter
3. In a large bowl, cream together the **1 ¾ cups shortening** and **2 ¼ cups sugar** until light and fluffy. Add the **egg yolks** one at a time, beating well after each addition.
4. In a separate bowl, whisk together the **4 ¼ cups flour**, **¾ tsp salt**, **1 ½ tsp mace**, **½ tsp cinnamon**, and **2 ¼ tsp baking soda**.
5. Add the dry ingredients to the creamed mixture in three parts, alternating with the **1 ⅛ cups coffee** in two parts, beginning and ending with the dry ingredients. Mix on low speed until just combined.
6. Stir in the prepared chopped **prunes**.

### Step 3: Fold and Bake
7. In a separate, clean bowl, beat the **egg whites** until stiff peaks form.
8. Gently fold the stiffened **egg whites** into the batter in two or three additions until no white streaks remain.
9. Pour the batter into the prepared pan. Bake for **50-60 minutes**, or until the cake begins to pull away from the sides of the pan and a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean and fragrant.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store tightly wrapped at room temperature for up to 3 days or refrigerate for up to a week.
