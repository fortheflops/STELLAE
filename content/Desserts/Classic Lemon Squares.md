# Classic Lemon Squares

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic recipe for sweet and tangy lemon squares featuring a buttery shortbread crust and a rich, gooey lemon custard filling. These bars are baked to perfection for a delightful treat.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 45 mins | 1 hour | 24 squares |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 9x9 inch pan
* medium bowl
* small bowl
* pastry blender
* wire rack

---
## Ingredients

### For the Crust
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 cup | [[Flour]] | all-purpose |
| 1/2 cup | [[Butter]] | cold |
| 1/4 cup | [[Powdered Sugar]] | sifted |

### For the Filling
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 cup | [[Sugar]] | granulated |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] | large |
| 2 tablespoons | [[Flour]] | all-purpose |
| 1/2 teaspoon | [[Baking Powder]] | none |
| 2 tablespoons | [[Lemon Juice]] | freshly squeezed |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Crust
1. Preheat your oven to 350°F.
2. In a medium bowl, combine **1 cup flour** and **1/4 cup powdered sugar**. Cut in the **1/2 cup cold butter** until the mixture resembles coarse crumbs.
3. Press the mixture firmly into the bottom of an ungreased 9x9 inch pan and bake for **20 minutes** until lightly golden.

### Step 2: Prepare the Filling
4. While the crust bakes, beat the **2 large eggs** in a medium bowl. Add the **1 cup granulated sugar** and beat well.
5. In a small bowl, whisk together the **2 tablespoons flour** and **1/2 teaspoon baking powder**, then stir into the egg mixture along with the **2 tablespoons lemon juice** until smooth.

### Step 3: Final Bake and Cooling
6. Pour the filling over the hot, baked crust.
7. Return to the oven and bake for an additional **20-25 minutes**, or until the filling is set and no longer jiggles in the center.
8. Let the pan cool completely on a wire rack before cutting into squares. Dust with additional powdered sugar if desired.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 5 days, or freeze for up to 3 months.
