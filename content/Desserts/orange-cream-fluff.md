# Orange Cream Fluff

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A delightful and airy retro dessert featuring gelatin whipped with orange juice, lemon juice, and evaporated milk. Perfect for a light, refreshing sweet treat after any meal.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 5 mins | 2 hours | 6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Electric mixer
* Measuring cups
* Measuring spoons

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 tbsp | [[Gelatine]] | Unflavored |
| 1/4 C. | [[Cold water]] | Cold |
| 1/4 C. | [[Boiling water]] | Boiling |
| 1/2 C. | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 3/4 C. | [[Orange juice]] | Fresh or bottled |
| 1/4 C. | [[Lemon juice]] | Freshly squeezed |
| 1 C. | [[Evaporated milk]] | Chilled |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparing the Base
1. Soak **1/2 tbsp gelatine** in **1/4 C. cold water**, then dissolve completely in **1/4 C. boiling water**.
2. Add **1/2 C. sugar** and stir until completely dissolved.

### Step 2: Whipping and Chilling
3. Add **3/4 C. orange juice** and **1/4 C. lemon juice**, and when slightly thick, whip until fluffy.
4. Add **1 C. evaporated milk** and continue whipping until light, airy, and fluffy, then chill until set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store covered in the refrigerator for up to 2 days before serving for the best texture.
