# Mickey Martin's Microwave Peanut Brittle

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Mickey Martin | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A quick and easy recipe for classic peanut brittle made entirely in the microwave. This simple method creates a perfectly crunchy, sweet, and salty candy in just a few minutes.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5 mins | 11 mins | 30 mins | Approx. 1.5 pounds |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 2-quart microwave-safe bowl
* Large cookie sheet
* Spatula
* Oven mitts

## Instructions

### Step 1: Initial Mix and Cook
1. Generously grease a large cookie sheet and set aside.
2. In a large microwave-safe bowl, combine **1 1/2 cups raw peanuts**, **1 cup sugar**, **1/2 cup white Karo syrup**, and **1/2 teaspoon salt**.
3. Microwave on HIGH for **4 minutes**.

### Step 2: Incorporate Fats
4. Carefully remove the bowl and stir the mixture well.
5. Microwave on HIGH for another **4 minutes**.
6. Remove carefully and stir in **1 tablespoon margarine**.

### Step 3: Final Cook and Aeration
7. Microwave on HIGH for **3 minutes** until the mixture is very hot and bubbly.
8. Working quickly, stir in **1-2 teaspoons vanilla** and **1 teaspoon baking soda** until the mixture becomes light, pale, and foamy.

### Step 4: Set and Cool
9. Immediately pour the foamy mixture onto the greased cookie sheet and spread as thinly as possible.
10. Allow to cool completely until hard and brittle, then break into pieces.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 2 weeks to maintain crunch.
