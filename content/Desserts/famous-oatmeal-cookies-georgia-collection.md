# Famous Oatmeal Cookies

> 📜 **Collection:** Georgia Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic, versatile recipe for chewy and delicious oatmeal cookies that can be customized with your favorite mix-ins. The dough can be conveniently made ahead and refrigerated or frozen for fresh-baked treats anytime.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mix the Wet Ingredients
1. In a large mixing bowl, beat the **3/4 cup shortening**, **1 cup brown sugar**, **1/2 cup granulated sugar**, **1 egg**, **1/4 cup water**, and **1 tsp vanilla extract** together until the mixture is smooth and creamy.

### Step 2: Add Dry Ingredients
2. Sift together the **1 cup all-purpose flour**, **1 tsp salt**, and **1/2 tsp baking soda**. Add this dry mixture to the creamy wet mixture and blend well until fully incorporated.
3. Stir in the **3 cups oats** until evenly distributed throughout the dough. (Optional: fold in variety mix-ins like nuts, raisins, chocolate chips, or coconut at this stage).

### Step 3: Bake
4. Drop the dough by teaspoonfuls onto a greased cookie sheet.
5. Bake at **350°F** for **12 to 15 minutes** until the edges are lightly golden brown and the centers are set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Make cookie dough in advance and refrigerate for up to 4 weeks, or freeze for up to 3 months.
