# Dark Devil's Food Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic, rich Devil's Food Cake made with cocoa and shortening, using a two-stage mixing method for a fine, moist crumb.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 40 mins | 10 mins | 2 8-inch layers |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Two 8-inch round cake pans
* Large mixing bowl
* Electric mixer
* Wire cooling rack

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 1/4 Cups | [[Cake Flour]] | Sifted, like Softasilk |
| 1 3/4 Cups | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 2/3 Cup | [[Cocoa Powder]] | Unsweetened |
| 1 1/3 tsp | [[Baking Powder]] | Double-acting |
| 1 1/4 tsp | [[Soda]] | Baking soda |
| 1 tsp | [[Salt]] | Standard |
| 2/3 Cup | [[Shortening]] | High-grade |
| 1 Cup | [[Water]] | Divided |
| 3 | [[Eggs]] | Large, unbeaten |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Mix Dry Ingredients
1. Preheat oven to **350°F**. Grease and flour two 8-inch round cake pans.
2. In a large bowl, sift together the **2 1/4 cups cake flour**, **1 3/4 cups sugar**, **1 1/3 tsp baking powder**, **1 1/4 tsp soda**, **1 tsp salt**, and **2/3 cup cocoa powder**.

### Step 2: Two-Stage Mixing
3. Add the **2/3 cup shortening** and **2/3 cup water** to the dry ingredients. Beat for **2 minutes** with an electric mixer at medium speed, scraping the bowl frequently.
4. Add the **3 eggs** and the remaining **1/3 cup water**. Beat for **2 more minutes** until smooth and velvety.

### Step 3: Bake and Cool
5. Pour the batter evenly into the prepared pans. Bake for **35-40 minutes**, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.
6. Cool in the pans for **10 minutes** before inverting onto a wire rack to cool completely.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the cooled cake layers wrapped tightly in plastic wrap at room temperature for up to 2 days or freeze for up to 2 months.
