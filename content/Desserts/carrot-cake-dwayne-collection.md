# Carrot Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic, incredibly moist carrot cake spiced with cinnamon and loaded with grated carrots. It is finished with a rich, textured cream cheese icing packed with shredded coconut and chopped pecans.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Cake Batter
1. Preheat your oven to **350°F**.
2. Sift together the **2 cups flour**, **2 cups sugar**, **2 tsp cinnamon**, **2 tsp baking soda**, and **2 tsp salt** into a large mixing bowl.
3. Add the **1 1/2 cups vegetable oil** to the dry ingredients and mix well.
4. Incorporate the **4 eggs** one at a time, beating thoroughly after each addition to ensure a smooth batter.
5. Gently fold in the **3 cups finely grated carrots** until they are evenly distributed throughout the batter.

### Step 2: Bake the Cake
6. Pour the batter into a prepared baking pan.
7. Bake at **350°F** and check for doneness at **30 minutes** by inserting a toothpick into the center; it should come out clean with no wet batter clinging to it.

### Step 3: Mix the Icing and Assemble
8. In a medium bowl, beat together the **1 package (8 oz) cream cheese**, **1 cup powdered sugar**, **1 stick margarine**, and **1 tsp vanilla extract** until completely smooth and creamy.
9. Fold in the **1 cup coconut** and **1/2 cup pecans** until evenly combined.
10. Spread the icing generously over the completely cooled cake before slicing.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This cake can be baked and iced up to 24 hours in advance. Keep it covered and refrigerated to maintain the freshness of the cream cheese icing.
