# Never Fail Pecan Pie

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty One | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A foolproof, classic southern-style pecan pie featuring a rich, sweet filling over toasted pecans. Guaranteed to satisfy your sweet tooth with its perfect balance of gooey syrup and nutty crunch.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 50 mins | None | 8 servings |

* Mixing bowl

* Whisk

## Ingredients

### Filling Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] | Beaten |
| 2 T | [[Butter]] | Melted |
| 2 T | [[Flour]] |  |
| 1/4 t | [[Vanilla]] |  |
| 1/2 c | [[White Sugar]] |  |
| 1 1/2 c | [[Dark Syrup]] |  |
| 1 1/2 c | [[Pecans]] | Whole or chopped |
| Dash | [[Salt]] |  |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing the Filling
1. Beat the **2 eggs**, then add the **2 T flour** and **2 T melted butter**, blending thoroughly.
2. Add the **1/4 t vanilla**, **dash salt**, **1/2 c white sugar**, and **1 1/2 c dark syrup**, mixing until smooth.

### Step 2: Assembling and Baking
3. Arrange the **1 1/2 c pecans** in the bottom of an unbaked pie shell, then pour the prepared filling mixture evenly over them.
4. Bake at **425 degrees** for **10 min**, then reduce temperature to **325 degrees** for **40 min** until the filling is set and golden brown.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Can be baked a day ahead; store covered at room temperature or in the refrigerator until ready to serve.
