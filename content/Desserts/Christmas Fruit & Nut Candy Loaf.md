# Christmas Fruit & Nut Candy Loaf

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic no-bake holiday confection, this candy loaf is loaded with nuts and candied fruit in a creamy, fudge-like base. It offers a rich, sweet flavor profile with a delightful chewy and crunchy texture.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 20 mins | PT2H | 1 loaf (about 3 pounds) |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Loaf pan
* Waxed paper
* Very large bowl
* Heavy saucepan
* Candy thermometer
* Electric mixer
* Sturdy spoon
* Sharp knife

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3 Cups | [[Sugar]] | None |
| 1 ½ Cups | [[Cream]] | Heavy or whipping cream |
| 1 Cup | [[Corn Syrup]] | White or light corn syrup |
| ½ tsp | [[Vanilla Extract]] | None |
| ½ Pound | [[Walnuts]] | About 2 cups |
| ½ Pound | [[Brazil Nuts]] | About 1 1/2 cups |
| ½ Pound | [[Pecans]] | About 2 cups |
| ½ Pound | [[Cherries]] | Candied |
| ½ Pound | [[Pineapple]] | Candied |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Combine Fruits and Nuts
1. Line a loaf pan with waxed paper, leaving some overhang on the sides to use as handles. In a very large bowl, combine all the **walnuts**, **Brazil nuts**, **pecans**, candied **cherries**, and candied **pineapple**.

### Step 2: Cook the Syrup
2. In a heavy saucepan, combine the **3 cups sugar**, **1 ½ cups cream**, and **1 cup corn syrup**.
3. Cook the mixture over medium heat, stirring constantly, until it reaches the soft-ball stage (**235-245°F** on a candy thermometer) until thick and bubbling.

### Step 3: Beat and Mix
4. Remove the syrup from the heat. Let it cool for a moment, then beat with an electric mixer or a sturdy spoon until it begins to thicken and lose its glossy sheen.
5. Quickly stir in the **½ tsp vanilla extract**, then add the fruit and nut mixture. Stir vigorously until everything is well coated.

### Step 4: Mold and Cool
6. Pour the candy mixture into the prepared loaf pan and pack it down firmly with the back of a spoon until compact.
7. Let it cool completely at room temperature until firm.
8. Once cool and firm, use the waxed paper handles to lift the loaf out of the pan. Slice with a sharp knife until desired portion sizes are reached.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store tightly wrapped like a fruitcake in a cool, dry place or in the refrigerator for up to 3 weeks.
