# Uncooked Fruit Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Five | ✍️ **Attribution:** Lynda Epperson | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A traditional no-bake fruit cake packed with graham crackers, marshmallows, raisins, and nuts. Molded and chilled to set into a delicious holiday treat.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 30 mins | None | 4 hours | 12 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Rolling pin
* Large mixing pan
* Knife
* Cutting board

---
## Ingredients

### Cake Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 lb. | [[Graham Crackers]] | Crushed |
| 1 lb. | [[Marshmallows]] | Cut into small cubes |
| 1 lb. | [[Seedless Raisins]] | Whole |
| 1 lb. | [[Candy Orange Slices]] | Cut into cubes |
| 1 1/2 cup | [[Strawberry Preserves]] | None |
| 1 lb. | [[Pecan Meats]] | Chopped |
| 1 lb. | [[Coconut]] | Shredded |
| 1 can | [[Eagle Brand Milk]] | Sweetened condensed |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation
1. Roll the **1 lb. graham crackers** into fine crumbs.
2. Cut the **1 lb. marshmallows** into small cubes.
3. Cut the **1 lb. candy orange slices** into cubes.

### Step 2: Mixing and Molding
4. Add the **1 lb. seedless raisins**, **1 lb. pecan meats**, **1 lb. coconut**, **1 can Eagle Brand milk**, and **1 1/2 cup strawberry preserves** to the crumbs, marshmallows, and orange slices.
5. Toss everything together in a large pan to mix thoroughly until well combined.
6. Mold the mixture into a cake shape and keep it in a cool place, or place in the refrigerator until firm, but do not freeze.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Keep refrigerated in an airtight container for up to 2 weeks. Do not freeze.
