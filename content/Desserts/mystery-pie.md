# Mystery Pie

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *An intriguing archival dessert recipe featuring a light, nutty meringue-like base made with crushed crackers. Top with your favorite gelatin, sherbet, or any topping of choice.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and Baking
1. Butter pie pan with **1 tbsp butter**. Mix all together: **3 stiffly beaten egg whites**, **1 tbsp baking powder**, **1 cup sugar**, **1 cup chopped nuts or coconut**, and **21 crushed Ritz crackers**.
2. Bake at 350 degrees for **25 minutes** until golden and set.

### Step 2: Topping
3. Top with jello, crushed berries, sherbet, or anything of your choice.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Bake the crust ahead of time and add toppings just before serving.
