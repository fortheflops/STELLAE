# Huguenot Torte

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic Southern dessert, the Huguenot Torte is a rich, moist, and crustless cake packed with chopped apples and pecans. With very little flour, its texture is dense, gooey, and utterly delicious, especially when served with whipped cream.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 35 mins | None | 12 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large bowl
* Small bowl
* Electric mixer
* Wire whisk
* Shallow 12-inch pan or 9x13 inch pan

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] | Large |
| 1 1/2 Cups | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 4 Tablespoons | [[Flour]] | All-purpose |
| 2 1/2 Teaspoons | [[Baking Powder]] | Standard |
| 1 Cup | [[Apples]] | Chopped |
| 1 Cup | [[Pecans]] | Chopped |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Mix Base
1. Preheat your oven to 350°F. Grease a shallow 12-inch pan or a 9x13 inch pan.
2. In a large bowl, beat the **2 eggs** with an electric mixer until light and frothy. Gradually beat in the **1 1/2 cups sugar** until well incorporated.

### Step 2: Add Dry Ingredients and Fruit
3. In a separate small bowl, whisk together the **4 tablespoons flour** and **2 1/2 teaspoons baking powder**. Add this dry mixture to the egg mixture and blend well.
4. By hand, gently stir in the **1 cup chopped apples** and **1 cup chopped pecans** until they are evenly distributed.

### Step 3: Bake and Serve
5. Pour the batter into the prepared pan and spread it evenly into a thin layer.
6. Bake for about **35 minutes** until the torte is golden and set, and a toothpick comes out with moist crumbs.
7. Let the torte cool slightly in the pan, then cut it into squares and serve warm or at room temperature with whipped cream.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftover torte covered at room temperature or in the refrigerator for up to 3 days.
