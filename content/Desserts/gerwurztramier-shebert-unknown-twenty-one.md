# Gerwurztramier Shebert

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty One | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A refreshingly simple dessert featuring tart sherbet infused with aromatic wine. Perfect for a quick yet elegant after-dinner treat.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5 mins | None | None | 4 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Dessert bowls
* Spoon

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 pint | [[Lemon or Lime Sherbet]] | Frozen |
| 1 cup | [[Gerwurztramier Wine]] | Chilled |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Assembly
1. Scoop the **1 pint lemon or lime sherbet** into individual dessert bowls.
2. Pour the **1 cup gerwurztramier wine** directly over the sherbet until fragrant and slightly slushy.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble just before serving to prevent the sherbet from melting completely.
