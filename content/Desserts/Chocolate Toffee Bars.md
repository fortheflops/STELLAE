# Chocolate Toffee Bars

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic and incredibly easy recipe for toffee bars featuring a rich, buttery brown sugar shortbread base topped with melted chocolate and crunchy nuts. These simple bar cookies are perfect for sharing and come together with minimal effort.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 25 mins | 30 mins | 4 dozen small squares |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* medium bowl
* pastry blender
* 10x15 inch jelly roll pan
* spatula

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| ¾ Cup | [[Butter]] | softened |
| ½ Cup | [[Brown Sugar]] | packed |
| 2 Cups | [[Flour]] | all-purpose |
| 1 Package (6-12 oz) | [[Chocolate Bits]] | semi-sweet chocolate chips |
| ½ Cup | [[Nuts]] | chopped |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Crust
1. Preheat your oven to 325°F.
2. In a medium bowl, combine the **¾ cup butter**, **½ cup brown sugar**, and **2 cups flour**. Mix with a pastry blender or your hands until a crumbly dough forms.
3. Press this mixture firmly and evenly into an ungreased 10x15 inch jelly roll pan to form the crust.

### Step 2: Bake and Top
4. Bake for **25 minutes** until the crust is set and lightly golden.
5. Remove the pan from the oven. Immediately sprinkle the **1 package chocolate bits** evenly over the hot base. Let them sit for 3-5 minutes until they appear glossy and melted.
6. Use a spatula to spread the melted chocolate into a smooth, even layer.
7. Sprinkle the **½ cup nuts** over the melted chocolate.

### Step 3: Cool and Serve
8. While the bars are still warm, cut them into 1 ½ inch squares.
9. Let them cool completely in the pan until firm before removing.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 5 days or freeze for up to 3 months.
