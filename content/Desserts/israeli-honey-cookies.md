## Instructions

### Preparation and Baking
1. Preheat your oven to **375°F**.
2. Combine **1 cup flour**, **1 tsp baking powder**, **1/4 tsp salt**, **1/2 cup shortening**, **1 egg**, **1 tsp vanilla**, **1/2 cup honey**, **1/2 cup rolled oats**, **1/2 cup walnuts**, **1/2 cup raisins**, and **1 cup chocolate chips** in a large mixing bowl until a cohesive dough forms.
3. Drop spoonfuls of dough onto a baking sheet, making about 5 dozen cookies.
4. Bake for **10 minutes** until golden brown and set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store baked cookies in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 5 days, or freeze the unbaked dough portions for later baking.
