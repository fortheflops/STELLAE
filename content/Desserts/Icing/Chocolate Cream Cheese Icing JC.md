## Recipe
| Measurements      | Ingredients                   |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 cup or more     | [[Confectioners Sugar]]           |
| Approx. 2-3 tbsp  | [[Butter]], softened              |
| 2 ounces          | [[Cream Cheese]], softened        |
| 1 ounce (1 square)| [[Chocolate]], melted             |
| 2 to 3 tablespoons| [[Cream]] or [[Milk]]               |
| 1/2 teaspoon      | [[Vanilla Extract]]             |
___
## Instructions
1.  In a mixing bowl, beat the softened butter and cream cheese together until smooth and creamy.
2.  Beat in the melted (and slightly cooled) chocolate and the vanilla extract.
3.  Gradually add the confectioners sugar and beat until well combined.
4.  Add the cream, one tablespoon at a time, mixing until the icing reaches a smooth, spreadable consistency.
5.  Adjust as needed: if the icing is too thin, add more confectioners sugar; if it is too thick, add a little more cream.
6.  Spread on a completely cooled cake.
___

>[!Note]
>The original recipe uses inexact measurements, calling for a "good lump of butter" and "1/4 of a cake of cream cheese." The card also reminds the baker to "only ice the cake after it is cool."
