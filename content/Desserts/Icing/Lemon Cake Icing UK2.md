## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
| 3/4 Cup          | [[Sugar]]                                                                                     |
| 1/4 Cup | [[Milk]]                                                                                      |
| 3 Tablespoons    | [[Spry]]                                         |
| 1/4 Teaspoon    | [[Salt]]                                   |
|3/4 Teaspoon| [[Lemon Rind]]|
___
### Instructions
1. Boil sugar, milk, Spry, and salt for 1 minute, stirring constantly.
2. Add lemon rind; beat until thick.
