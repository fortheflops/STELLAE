# Pastry-Style Strawberry Shortcake

> 📜 **Collection:** PatsyCollection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A unique, old-fashioned strawberry shortcake with a rich, tender, almost cookie-like texture. This pastry-style base is made with butter and powdered sugar, perfect for individual servings.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 12 mins | None | 8-10 individual shortcakes |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large bowl
* Pastry blender
* Cookie cutter
* Baking sheet
* Individual molds or muffin tins

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Mix Dry Ingredients
1. Preheat your oven to **400°F**. Grease individual molds or a baking sheet.
2. In a big bowl, sift together the **2 cups flour**, **1 cup icing sugar**, **1 tsp baking powder**, and **¼ tsp salt**.

### Step 2: Cut in Fats and Form Dough
3. Add the **1/2 cup sweet butter** and **1/2 cup shortening** (cold and cubed) to the flour mixture. Using two knives in a crisscross motion or a pastry blender, cut the fat into the flour until the mixture resembles coarse crumbs.
4. Make a well in the center. Add the **2 eggs** (beaten) and **1 tsp vanilla**.
5. Use a fork to gradually mix the ingredients until a soft dough forms.

### Step 3: Shape and Bake
6. Turn the dough out onto a lightly floured surface and gently bring it together.
7. Roll or pat the dough out and either cut into rounds with a cookie cutter or press into your prepared molds. Place on a baking sheet.
8. Bake for **10-12 minutes**, or until the edges are lightly golden and fragrant.
9. Let cool slightly, then serve warm by filling with whipped **cream** and fresh **strawberries**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Shortcake dough can be cut into rounds and frozen on a baking sheet before baking; store in a freezer bag for up to 1 month.
