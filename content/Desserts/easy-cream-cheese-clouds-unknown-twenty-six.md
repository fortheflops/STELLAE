# Easy Cream Cheese Clouds

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** N.S. | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *Indulge in these delightful, airy Easy Cream Cheese Clouds featuring a creamy whipped filling atop a wax paper-lined sheet. Topped with sweet cherry pie filling, they make a stunning and effortless treat for any gathering.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | None | 2 hours | 10 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Electric mixer
* Mixing bowl
* Cookie sheet
* Spoon

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 (8 oz) | [[Cream Cheese]] | Softened |
| 1/2 cup | [[Powdered Sugar]] | None |
| 1 tsp | [[Vanilla Extract]] | None |
| 1 cup | [[Heavy Cream]] | None |
| 1 can | [[Cherry Pie Filling]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing and Whipping
1. Mix **1 (8 oz) cream cheese**, **1/2 cup powdered sugar**, and **1 tsp vanilla extract** at medium speed on an electric mixer.
2. Gradually add **1 cup heavy cream** and mix well.
3. Whip until thickened.

### Step 2: Shaping and Freezing
4. Using the back of a spoon, shape into 10 (3 1/2") shells.
5. Place on wax paper-lined cookie sheet.
6. Freeze for 2 hours or overnight until firm and set.

### Step 3: Serving
7. When ready to serve, fill with **1 can cherry pie filling**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the frozen cream cheese clouds in the freezer overnight until ready to thaw slightly and top with cherry pie filling.
