# Caramel Nut Strudel Bars

> 📜 **Collection:** UK5 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A rich and decadent layered bar featuring a crunchy corn flake and nut streusel that serves as both the crust and the topping. The filling is a gooey, buttery caramel cooked on the stovetop.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 35 mins | 35 mins | 24–36 bars |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 9x13 inch baking pan
* large bowl
* pastry blender
* medium saucepan
* wire rack

---
## Ingredients

### For the Streusel Crust/Topping
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 cup | [[Flour]] | all-purpose |
| 2/3 cup | [[Sugar]] | granulated |
| 1 1/2 cups | [[Corn Flakes]] | crushed |
| 1 1/2 cups | [[Nuts]] | chopped |
| 4 ounces | [[Butter]] | cold, cubed |

### For the Filling
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 12 ounces | [[Butter]] | 3 sticks |
| 2 1/4 cups | [[Sugar]] | granulated |
| 2 teaspoons | [[Cornstarch]] | none |
| 1/2 cup | [[Corn Syrup]] | none |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare Crust
1. Preheat the oven to 350°F and grease a 9x13 inch baking pan.
2. In a large bowl, combine **1 cup flour**, **2/3 cup sugar**, **1 1/2 cups corn flakes**, and **1 1/2 cups nuts**.
3. Cut in the **4 ounces cold butter** until the mixture is crumbly.
4. Reserve 1/3 of the mixture for the topping and press the remaining 2/3 firmly into the bottom of the pan.

### Step 2: Prepare Filling and Bake
5. In a medium saucepan, combine **12 ounces butter**, **2 1/4 cups sugar**, **2 teaspoons cornstarch**, and **1/2 cup corn syrup**.
6. Cook over medium heat, stirring constantly, until the mixture comes to a boil and thickens.
7. Pour the hot filling over the crust and sprinkle the reserved streusel on top.
8. Bake for **30-35 minutes** until the edges are golden brown and the filling is set.
9. Cool completely on a wire rack before cutting.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 3 days or freeze for up to 1 month.
