# Jean Williams' Cold Pineapple Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Jean Williams | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A delightful and refreshing cold cake layered with crushed pineapple, instant vanilla pudding, and Cool Whip. This quick dessert features an airy angle food cake base frosted into a sweet, creamy treat.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 0 mins | 1 hour | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Spatula
* Knife

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 can (13.5 oz) | [[Crushed Pineapple]] | Cold, drained somewhat |
| 1 qt | [[Cool Whip]] | Thawed |
| 1 package | [[Instant Vanilla Pudding]] | Dry |
| 1 | [[Angle Food Cake]] | Cut in two |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and Mixing
1. Cut the **1 angle food cake** in two horizontal layers.
2. Drain the **1 can crushed pineapple** somewhat, ensuring the pineapple remains cold.

### Step 2: Frosting and Assembling
3. Mix the drained **1 can crushed pineapple** with the **1 package instant pudding**.
4. Add the **1 qt Cool whip** to the mixture and combine thoroughly.
5. Frost the cut layers of the **1 angle food cake** with the mixture until well covered, and chill until set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Keep refrigerated until ready to serve to maintain its cold, refreshing texture.
