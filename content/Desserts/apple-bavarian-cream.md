# Apple Bavarian Cream

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A delicate and creamy chilled dessert featuring sweetened applesauce and whipped cream. This elegant molded treat is perfect for a sophisticated finish to any meal.*

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Custard Base
1. Soak **2 tbsp gelatin** in **1/3 cup cold water**.
2. Beat **2 egg yolks** with **1/2 cup sugar**.
3. Pour **1 1/2 cups hot milk** over the **egg yolks** mixture, add **1/2 tsp salt**, and cook over a double boiler until the mixture coats the back of a spoon.
4. Stir in the soaked **gelatin** and beat until completely dissolved; set aside to cool.

### Step 2: Assembly
5. Stir **1 cup applesauce** and **2 tsp lemon juice** into the cooled custard.
6. Gently fold in the **2 beaten egg whites**, **1/2 - 1 cup whipped cream**, and **1/2 tsp vanilla extract**.
7. Pour into a mold and chill until set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This dessert must be made at least 4 hours in advance to allow the gelatin to set properly in the refrigerator.
