# Rice Dessert

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic creamy rice dessert cooked gently in a double boiler and folded with whipped cream and candied cherries. Perfectly sweet and comforting for any occasion.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 2 hours | 30 mins | 6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Double boiler
* Mixing bowl

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 cup | [[Rice]] | Uncooked |
| 3 cups | [[Hot milk]] | Warmed |
| 1 tsp | [[Salt]] | None |
| 1/2 cup | [[Sugar]] | None |
| 1 tsp | [[Flavouring]] | Extract of choice |
| 1 cup | [[Whipped cream]] | Whipped |
| 1/4 cup | [[Candied cherries]] | Cut up |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Cooking the Rice
1. Combine **1/2 cup rice** and **3 cups hot milk** in a double boiler.
2. Cook for **2 hours or more** until the rice is completely tender and creamy.

### Step 2: Flavoring and Cooling
3. Stir in **1 tsp salt**, **1/2 cup sugar**, and **1 tsp flavouring**.
4. Allow the mixture to cool completely.

### Step 3: Folding and Serving
5. When cold, gently fold in **1 cup whipped cream** with **1/4 cup candied cherries cut up** in it until fully incorporated.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Keep refrigerated until ready to serve; best enjoyed within 24 hours.
