# Jeree Cunningham's Shortbread

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Jeree Cunningham | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic, rich, and buttery shortbread recipe featuring instructions for both pressed cookies and traditional Scottish rounds. This archival recipe emphasizes the use of high-quality real butter for the best texture.*

---

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 35 mins | 1 hour | 6-8 dozen cookies |

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 pound (4 sticks) | [[Butter]] | Softened, real butter only |
| 5 cups | [[Flour]] | All-purpose |
| 1/4 cup | [[Powdered Sugar]] | Sifted |
| 1 tablespoon | [[Rice Flour]] | Optional, for sandier texture |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Dough
1. In a large bowl, mix the **1 pound (4 sticks) butter** and **1/4 cup powdered sugar** until well combined.
2. Gradually blend in the **5 cups flour** and optional **1 tablespoon rice flour** with your hands, kneading until a stiff dough forms.

### Step 2: Individual Cookies
3. Preheat oven to 350°F.
4. Press the dough through a cookie press or roll out and cut into shapes on an ungreased cookie sheet.
5. Bake for **10-12 minutes** until the edges are a very light golden brown and fragrant.

### Step 3: Traditional Scottish Round
6. Pat the dough into a round, flat disc about ½-inch thick in a pan or on a baking sheet.
7. Crimp edges, pierce the surface with a fork, and score into wedges.
8. Chill in the refrigerator for **1 hour**.
9. Preheat oven to 350°F and bake for **25-35 minutes** until slightly brown.
10. Cut through the scored lines while still hot from the oven.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The dough can be prepared and chilled for up to 24 hours before baking, or the baked cookies can be stored in an airtight container for up to 2 weeks.
