# Orange Cupcakes

> 📜 **Collection:** JC | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A quick and tender orange cupcake with a bright citrus flavor, made using a simple muffin-style mixing method and fresh orange juice.*

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Muffin tins
* Large mixing bowl

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |

| 2 teaspoons | [[Baking Powder]] | None |
| 1/2 teaspoon | [[Salt]] | None |
| 3/4 cup | [[Sugar]] | None |
| 1 | [[Egg]] | Large, lightly beaten |
| 1/3 cup | [[Shortening]] | Softened |
| 1/3 cup | [[Evaporated Milk]] | None |
| 1/3 cup | [[Orange Juice]] | Fresh |
| 1 Tbsp | [[Orange Rind]] | Grated, to taste |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare Batter
1. Preheat oven to **350°F**. Grease or line muffin tins.
2. In a large bowl, sift together the **1 1/3 cups cake flour**, **2 teaspoons baking powder**, **1/2 teaspoon salt**, and **3/4 cup sugar**. Make a well in the center of the dry ingredients.

### Step 2: Combine Wet Ingredients
3. In a separate bowl, lightly beat the **1 egg**. Whisk in the **1/3 cup shortening**, **1/3 cup evaporated milk**, **1/3 cup orange juice**, and **1 tablespoon grated orange rind**.
4. Pour the wet ingredients all at once into the well of the dry ingredients.
5. Stir until just combined, then beat for **1 minute** until the batter is smooth.

### Step 3: Bake and Cool
6. Fill the prepared muffin tins about two-thirds full with batter.
7. Bake for **15 to 25 minutes** until a wooden skewer inserted into the center comes out clean and they are fragrant.
8. Let the cupcakes cool in the tins for a few minutes before transferring them to a wire rack to cool completely.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store cooled cupcakes in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 3 days or freeze for up to 2 months.
