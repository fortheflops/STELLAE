# Strawberry Cheese Dessert

> 📜 **Collection:** Burcea Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Corrine | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A delightful layered dessert featuring a buttery pecan crust, a fluffy whipped cream cheese filling, and a sweet strawberry gelatin topping. Perfect for summer gatherings and make-ahead entertaining.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Crust
1. Preheat oven to **350°F**.
2. Cream together **1 cup flour**, **1/2 cup butter**, **1 tsp salt**, **2 tbsp sugar**, and **2 tbsp chopped pecans**.
3. Pat the mixture into a **9x13-inch pan** and bake at **350°F** for **10 to 15 minutes** until lightly golden. Set aside to cool completely.

### Step 2: Make the Cheese Filling
4. Beat **8 oz cream cheese** and **1/2 cup powdered sugar** together until smooth.
5. In a separate bowl, whip **2 packets Dream Whip** with **1 cup milk** and **1 tsp vanilla extract** until stiff peaks form.
6. Gently fold the whipped mixture into the cream cheese mixture until combined, then spread evenly over the cooled crust.

### Step 3: Prepare the Strawberry Topping & Chill
7. Dissolve **1 packet strawberry Jell-O** and **1 envelope plain gelatin** (previously softened in **1/3 cup cold water**) in **1 cup hot water**.
8. Stir in **2 packets frozen strawberries**.
9. When the mixture is partially set and slightly thickened, spread it carefully over the cheese filling.
10. Refrigerate and chill for at least **8 hours** before slicing and serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This dessert must be chilled for at least 8 hours before serving. It can be made up to 24 hours in advance and kept covered in the refrigerator.
