# Margaret Farley's Pistachio Nut Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** UK15 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Margaret Farley | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A wonderfully moist and flavorful doctored cake mix recipe featuring pistachio pudding and a rich chocolate swirl. This vintage Bundt-style cake delivers a delightful marble effect and nutty sweetness in every slice.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 55 mins | None | 1 10-inch tube cake |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 10-inch tube or Bundt pan
* Large bowl
* Electric mixer
* Spatula

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 (18.5 ounce) package | [[White Cake Mix]] | Dry |
| 1 (3.5 ounce) package | [[Pistachio Instant Pudding]] | Dry |
| 4 | [[Eggs]] | Large, beaten |
| 1/2 cup | [[Oil]] | Salad oil |
| 1/2 cup | [[Orange Juice]] | Fresh or bottled |
| 1/2 cup | [[Water]] | Room temperature |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Almond Extract]] | For flavor |
| 3/4 cup | [[Chocolate Syrup]] | Divided for swirl |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare Batter
1. Preheat the oven to **350°F**. Grease and flour a **10-inch tube or Bundt pan**.
2. In a large bowl, combine all ingredients **EXCEPT** the **3/4 cup chocolate syrup**, including the **1 (18.5 ounce) package white cake mix**, **1 (3.5 ounce) package pistachio instant pudding**, **4 large eggs**, **1/2 cup oil**, **1/2 cup orange juice**, **1/2 cup water**, and **1 teaspoon almond extract**.
3. Beat with an electric mixer at medium speed for **5 minutes** until smooth and slightly fluffy.

### Step 2: Swirl and Bake
4. Pour two-thirds of the pistachio batter into the prepared **10-inch tube or Bundt pan**.
5. Add the **3/4 cup chocolate syrup** to the remaining one-third of the batter in the bowl and mix well until thoroughly combined.
6. Pour the chocolate batter over the pistachio batter in the pan. Gently zig-zag a spatula through the batters once or twice to create a marble effect without overmixing.
7. Bake for **50 to 55 minutes**, or until a wooden toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean and the edges pull slightly away from the pan.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the cooled cake wrapped tightly in plastic wrap at room temperature for up to 3 days, or refrigerate for up to a week.
