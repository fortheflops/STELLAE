# Coconut Cream Pie

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty One | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic homemade coconut cream pie featuring a rich, thickened milk filling poured into a baked pie shell. Perfect for any gathering, this comforting dessert is both sweet and creamy.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 15 mins | 2 hours | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Sauce pan
* Pie pan
* Mixing bowl

---
## Ingredients

### Filling and Crust
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | [[Pie shell]] | Baked |
| 2 T | [[Sugar]] | None |
| 3 T | [[Flour]] | None |
| 2-2 1/2 C | [[Milk]] | None |
| As needed | [[Coconut]] | Shredded |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Making the Filling
1. Mix together **2 T sugar** and **3 T flour**; place in sauce pan.
2. Slowly add **2-2 1/2 C milk** while stirring continuously until smooth and thickened.

### Step 2: Assembling the Pie
3. Add **coconut** to the mixture and stir well until fully incorporated.
4. Spoon the hot filling into the cooled, baked **1 pie shell** until evenly distributed.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Chill the assembled pie in the refrigerator for at least 2 hours until completely set before slicing.
