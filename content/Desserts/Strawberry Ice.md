# Strawberry Ice

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic homemade strawberry sorbet stabilized with gelatin for an incredibly smooth, refreshing texture. This vintage recipe yields a quart of vibrant, fruit-forward frozen dessert.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 25 mins | 5 mins | 6 hours | 1 Quart |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* blender
* small bowl
* small saucepan
* electric mixer
* freezer-safe container

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3 baskets | [[Strawberries]] | fresh, hulled and pureed |
| 1 envelope | [[Gelatin]] | unflavored |
| 1 to 2 | [[Lemons]] | juiced |
| 1/4 cup | [[Sugar]] | granulated |
| 1/2 cup + 2 tbsp | [[Water]] | divided |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Base
1. Wash, hull, and puree the **3 baskets fresh strawberries** in a blender until smooth.
2. In a small bowl, sprinkle the **1 envelope unflavored gelatin** over the **juice of 1 to 2 lemons** and let stand for 5 minutes to soften.

### Step 2: Create the Syrup
3. In a small saucepan, combine **1/4 cup sugar** and **1/2 cup plus 2 tablespoons water**. Bring to a boil and cook for **5 minutes** to create a simple syrup.
4. Remove from heat, stir in the softened **gelatin mixture** until completely dissolved, and allow to cool to room temperature.

### Step 3: Freeze and Aerate
5. Stir the cooled syrup into the **strawberry puree** until well combined.
6. Pour into a freezer-safe container and freeze until solid, about 4-6 hours.
7. Break the frozen block into chunks and beat with an electric mixer until smooth and fluffy, then return to the freezer until firm.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This sorbet can be stored in the freezer for up to 2 weeks; for best texture, let it sit at room temperature for 5 minutes before serving.
