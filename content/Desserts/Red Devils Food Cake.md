# Red Devil's Food Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic Red Devil's Food Cake featuring a rich, moist crumb and deep chocolate flavor. This vintage recipe utilizes buttermilk and boiling water for a uniquely tender texture.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 30 mins | 10 mins | 2 8-inch layers |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Two 8-inch or 9-inch round cake pans
* Large mixing bowl
* Electric mixer
* Separate mixing bowl
* Wire cooling rack

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| ¾ cup | [[Butter]] | softened |
| 2 cups | [[Sugar]] | none |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] | unbeaten |
| 1 tsp | [[Vanilla]] | none |
| 2½ cups | [[Flour]] | all-purpose |
| ½ cup | [[Cocoa]] | powder |
| 2 tsp | [[Baking Soda]] | none |
| 1 tsp | [[Salt]] | none |
| 1 cup | [[Sour Milk]] | buttermilk |
| 1 cup | [[Boiling Water]] | none |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Mix Base
1. Preheat your oven to **350°F**. Grease and flour two **8-inch** or **9-inch** round cake pans.
2. In a large bowl, cream the **¾ cup butter**, softened and **2 cups sugar** together with an electric mixer until light and fluffy.
3. Add the **2 eggs** one at a time, beating well after each addition.

### Step 2: Combine Wet and Dry
4. In a separate bowl, sift together the **2½ cups flour**, **2 tsp baking soda**, **1 tsp salt**, and **½ cup cocoa**.
5. Stir the **1 tsp vanilla** into the **1 cup sour milk** (buttermilk).
6. Add the dry ingredients to the creamed mixture in three parts, alternating with the milk mixture, beginning and ending with the dry ingredients.

### Step 3: Bake and Cool
7. Pour the **1 cup boiling water** in all at once and stir quickly until the batter is well combined. The batter will be very thin.
8. Pour the batter into the prepared pans.
9. Bake for **30 minutes**, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean and the edges pull slightly away from the pan.
10. Let the cakes cool in the pans for **10 minutes** before turning them out onto a wire rack to cool completely before frosting.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Baked cake layers can be wrapped tightly in plastic wrap and stored at room temperature for up to 2 days, or frozen for up to 3 months before frosting.
