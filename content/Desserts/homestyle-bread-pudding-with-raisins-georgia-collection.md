# Homestyle Bread Pudding with Raisins

> 📜 **Collection:** Georgia Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic, comforting homestyle bread pudding baked in a warm water bath and studded with sweet raisins. Serve warm drizzled with a bright, zesty homemade lemon sauce.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Bread Pudding
1. Preheat oven to **350°F**. Add **3 tbsp butter** to **2 1/2 cups skim milk** and scald.
2. In a mixing bowl, beat **3 eggs**; add **1/2 cup white sugar**, **1/4 cup brown sugar**, **1/4 tsp salt**, **1/2 tsp cinnamon**, and **1 1/2 tsp vanilla extract**. Mix well.
3. Stir in **6 cups soft bread cubes** and **3/4 cup seedless raisins**.
4. Spray a **9x9 baking dish** with nonstick spray. Pour the bread mixture into the pan.
5. Place the baking dish inside a larger pan filled with **hot water** (1 inch deep) to create a water bath.
6. Bake at **350°F** for **50 to 55 minutes**, or until a knife inserted in the center comes out clean.

### Step 2: Prepare the Lemon Sauce & Serve
7. In a heavy saucepan, combine **1 cup water**, **1/3 cup sugar**, and **1 tbsp + 1 tsp cornstarch**.
8. Bring to a boil and cook until thick, stirring constantly.
9. Remove the sauce from the heat and stir in **1 tbsp butter**, **1/2 tsp grated lemon rind**, **1 1/2 tbsp lemon juice**, and **1/2 tsp salt**.
10. Serve the bread pudding warm, drizzled with the warm **lemon sauce** or cream.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The bread pudding can be assembled a few hours ahead and kept refrigerated before baking. Leftovers can be stored in the refrigerator for up to 3 days and reheated gently.
