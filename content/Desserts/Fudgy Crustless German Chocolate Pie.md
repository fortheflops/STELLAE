# Fudgy Crustless German Chocolate Pie

> 📜 **Collection:** General Archive | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A rich and decadent crustless pie that bakes up like a fudgy brownie in a pie pan. Loaded with German sweet chocolate and pecans, this dessert is incredibly easy and delicious.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 30 mins | None | One 9-inch pie |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 9-inch pie pan
* Small saucepan
* Mixing bowl
* Wire rack

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 8 squares | [[German Sweet Chocolate]] | one 4-oz bar |
| 1 stick | [[Oleo]] or Butter | 1/2 cup |
| 1 cup | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 1/2 cup | [[Flour]] | All-purpose |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Vanilla]] | Extract |
| 3 | [[Eggs]] | Whole |
| 1 cup | [[Pecans]] | Chopped |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Melt
1. Preheat oven to **300°F**. Grease a **9-inch pie pan**.
2. In a small saucepan, melt the **8 squares German Sweet Chocolate** and **1 stick oleo** or butter together until smooth and set aside to cool slightly.

### Step 2: Mix and Bake
3. In a separate bowl, mix the **1 cup sugar**, **1/2 cup flour**, **1 teaspoon vanilla**, and **3 eggs**.
4. Pour the melted chocolate mixture into the egg mixture and stir until combined.
5. Fold in the chopped **1 cup pecans**.
6. Pour the batter into the greased pie pan and bake for **exactly 30 minutes** until the center looks slightly unset for a dense, fudgy texture.
7. Cool completely on a wire rack before serving; the pie will set as it cools.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store sealed in the refrigerator for up to 4 days and serve chilled or at room temperature.
