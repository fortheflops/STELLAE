# Classic Chocolate Fluff Frosting

> 📜 **Collection:** Jill Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A light and airy frosting featuring a sweet meringue folded into a rich, unsweetened chocolate base. This recipe provides a delicate, fluffy texture perfect for a two-layer cake.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 5 mins | None | Frosting for one two-layer, 9-inch cake |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* mixing bowl
* electric mixer
* spatula
* saucepan or microwave-safe bowl

## Ingredients

### Chocolate Base
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 4 tablespoons | [[Butter]] | softened |
| 3/4 cup | [[Confectioners Sugar]] | sifted |
| 3 squares (3 oz) | [[Unsweetened Chocolate]] | melted and cooled |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Vanilla Extract]] | None |
| 1/2 teaspoon | [[Salt]] | None |

### Meringue
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 | [[Egg Whites]] | None |
| 3/4 cup | [[Confectioners Sugar]] | sifted |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Base
1. Melt the **3 squares (3 oz) unsweetened chocolate** and set aside to cool slightly.
2. In a bowl, cream the **4 tablespoons butter**. Gradually add the first **3/4 cup confectioners sugar** and blend well. Stir in the cooled **3 squares (3 oz) unsweetened chocolate**, **1 teaspoon vanilla extract**, and **1/2 teaspoon salt** until smooth.

### Step 2: Whip the Meringue
3. In a separate, clean bowl, beat the **2 egg whites** until stiff, glossy peaks form.
4. Gradually add the second **3/4 cup confectioners sugar** to the **2 egg whites**, beating well until the mixture is thick, glossy, and stands in firm peaks.

### Step 3: Combine and Finish
5. Gently fold the finished meringue into the chocolate base with a spatula until no white streaks remain and the mixture is uniform.
6. Use immediately to frost your cake while the texture is light and airy.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This frosting should be used immediately after preparation for the best texture. Due to raw egg whites, keep refrigerated if not serving right away.
