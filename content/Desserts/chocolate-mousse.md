# Chocolate Mousse

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A rich and creamy chocolate mousse made with pudding mix, milk, instant coffee, and cream cheese. Chill until set and serve garnished with whipped cream and chocolate curls.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 10 mins | 2 hours | 8-10 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Saucepan
* Whisk
* Mixer
* 1 qt mold

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 pkg (4 oz) | [[Chocolate Pudding Mix]] |  |
| 1 3/4 c. | [[Milk]] |  |
| 1 tsp | [[Instant Coffee]] |  |
| 1 pkg (8 oz) | [[Cream Cheese]] | cut in cubes |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Cook and Blend
1. Combine **1 pkg (4 oz) chocolate pudding mix**, **1 3/4 c. milk**, and **1 tsp instant coffee**.
2. Cook, stirring constantly, until mixture comes to a full boil.
3. Add **1 pkg (8 oz) cream cheese**, cut in cubes. Beat until blended.

### Step 2: Chill and Serve
4. Pour into **1 qt mold**, cover, and chill.
5. Serve with whipped cream and choc. curls.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Cover and refrigerate for at least 2 hours or until thoroughly set before serving.
