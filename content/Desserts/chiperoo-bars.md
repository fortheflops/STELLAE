# Chiperoo Bars

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Five | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic mid-century brown sugar bar cookie packed with chocolate chips and chopped nuts. These rich, chewy Chiperoo Bars are baked to golden perfection and sliced warm.*

## Instructions

### Preparation & Mixing
1. Melt **2/3 cup shortening** in a saucepan. Stir in **1 lb. brown sugar**, mix well, and let cool.
2. Sift together **2 3/4 cups sifted flour**, **2 1/2 tsp. baking powder**, and **1/2 tsp. salt**.
3. To the cooled shortening mixture, add **3 eggs** one at a time, beating thoroughly after each addition.
4. Gradually blend in the sifted dry ingredients until just combined.
5. Stir in **1 cup nuts** and **1 (6 oz) package chocolate chips**.

### Baking & Slicing
6. Spread the batter evenly into a greased and floured baking pan.
7. Bake at **350°F** for **25 to 30 minutes** until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean and the edges are golden brown.
8. When almost cool, cut into bars.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 5 days, or freeze for up to 3 months.
