# Perfect Flaky Cobbler Crust

> 📜 **Collection:** UK5 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A versatile and flaky cobbler crust recipe featuring a blend of butter and lard for superior texture, with a bright hint of lemon zest. This classic pastry is the perfect topping for any fruit cobbler.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 40 mins | 40 mins | 30 mins | 1 9-inch cobbler topping |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* food processor
* 9-inch baking dish
* rolling pin
* plastic wrap

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 9 1/2 ounces | [[Flour]] | all-purpose |
| 2 1/2 tablespoons | [[Sugar]] | granulated |
| 1 tablespoon | [[Lemon Zest]] | freshly grated |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Salt]] | fine |
| 4 1/2 ounces | [[Butter]] | chilled and cubed |
| 1 1/2 ounces | [[Lard]] | chilled |
| 1 to 3 tablespoons | [[Water]] | ice cold |
| 4-5 cups | [[Fruit]] | frozen, for filling |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Dough
1. In a food processor, combine the **9 1/2 ounces flour**, **2 1/2 tablespoons sugar**, **1 tablespoon lemon zest**, and **1 teaspoon salt**. Pulse a few times to combine.
2. Add the **4 1/2 ounces chilled butter** and **1 1/2 ounces lard** to the processor. Pulse until the mixture resembles coarse, crumbly sand.
3. Add the **1 to 3 tablespoons ice water** one tablespoon at a time, pulsing briefly after each addition until the dough just begins to come together.
4. Turn the dough out onto a clean surface, form it into a disk, wrap it in plastic, and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes.

### Step 2: Assemble and Bake
5. Preheat the oven to 375°F. Place the **4-5 cups frozen fruit** in a 9-inch baking dish.
6. On a lightly floured surface, roll out the chilled dough and place it on top of the fruit.
7. Bake for **30-40 minutes**, or until the crust is golden brown and the fruit filling is hot and bubbly.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The dough disk can be refrigerated for up to 2 days or frozen for up to 1 month before rolling.
