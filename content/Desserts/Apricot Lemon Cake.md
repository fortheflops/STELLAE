# Apricot Lemon Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** UK15 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A wonderfully moist and flavorful cake made easy with a yellow cake mix base, enriched with lemon gelatin, apricot nectar, and lemon extract, then finished with a sweet citrus glaze.*

### Cake

| 1/2 cup | [[Wesson Oil]] | Vegetable oil |
| 5 | [[Eggs]] | Large |
| 2/3 cup | [[Apricot Nectar]] | Standard |
| 2 teaspoons | [[Lemon Extract]] | Liquid |

### Icing
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |

| 1/2 cup | [[Apricot Nectar]] | Heated |

## Instructions

### Step 1: Bake the Cake
1. Preheat your oven to **325°F**. Grease and flour a tube or Bundt pan.
2. In a large bowl, combine the **1 package yellow cake mix** and the **1 (3 oz) package lemon gelatin** powder.
3. Add the **1/2 cup Wesson oil**, then add the **5 eggs** one at a time, mixing well after each addition until smooth.
4. Add the **2/3 cup apricot nectar** and **2 teaspoons lemon extract** and mix well until the batter is thoroughly incorporated.
5. Pour the batter into the prepared pan and bake for **45 minutes**, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.
6. Let the cake cool in the pan for **15 minutes** before inverting it onto a wire rack until completely cool.

### Step 2: Prepare and Apply Icing
7. While the cake is cooling, prepare the icing. In a medium bowl, combine the **juice of 1 lemon** and the **1/2 cup heated apricot nectar**.
8. Gradually whisk in the **2-3 cups powdered sugar** until you reach a smooth, spreadable consistency.
9. Drizzle or spread the icing over the warm or cooled cake until fully coated and glossy.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the glazed cake in an airtight cake container at room temperature for up to 3 days or refrigerate for up to 5 days.
