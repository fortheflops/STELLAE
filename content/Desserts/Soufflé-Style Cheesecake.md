# Soufflé-Style Cheesecake

> 📜 **Collection:** GailCollection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A light and airy soufflé-style cheesecake that achieves its incredible lift and delicate texture from folding stiffly beaten egg whites into a rich cream cheese base. This vintage recipe yields a smooth, cloud-like dessert with a delightful wobble in the center.*

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 9-inch springform pan
* Electric mixer
* Blender or large mixing bowl
* Clean mixing bowl

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 ½ lbs (3 blocks) | [[Cream Cheese]] | softened |
| 2 cups | [[Sugar]] | divided |
| 3 large | [[Eggs]] | separated |
| 1/4 cup | [[Milk]] | room temperature |
| 1/2 teaspoon | [[Vanilla Extract]] | None |
| Optional | [[Crystallized Melon]] or [[Ginger]] | for garnish |
| As needed | Your favorite crust | graham cracker or cookie crust |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and Base
1. Preheat your oven to **350°F**. Prepare your favorite crust in a **9-inch springform pan**. Ensure all ingredients are at room temperature.

### Step 2: Mixing the Batter
2. In a blender or a large bowl with an electric mixer, combine the **1 ½ lbs (3 blocks) cream cheese**, **2 cups sugar**, **1/2 teaspoon vanilla extract**, **1/4 cup milk**, and **3 egg yolks**. Blend for several minutes until the mixture is completely smooth.

### Step 3: Whipping the Whites
3. In a separate, clean bowl, beat the **3 egg whites** with a mixer on high speed until they form stiff, glossy peaks.

### Step 4: Folding and Baking
4. Gently fold the beaten **3 egg whites** into the **cream cheese mixture** in three additions until just combined and no white streaks remain.
5. Pour the filling into the prepared crust.
6. Bake for **45-50 minutes**, or until the edges are set but the center still has a slight wobble.
7. Turn the oven off, crack the door open, and let the cheesecake cool inside the oven for **1 hour** to prevent cracking.
8. Remove from the oven, let cool to room temperature, then chill in the refrigerator for at least **4 hours** before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the cheesecake tightly sealed in the refrigerator for up to 4 days before serving.
