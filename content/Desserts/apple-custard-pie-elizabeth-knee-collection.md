# Apple Custard Pie

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Apple Base
1. Preheat your oven to **400°F**.
2. In a mixing bowl, toss together the **2 cups thinly sliced fresh apples**, **1/4 tsp nutmeg**, **1/2 tsp cinnamon**, and **1 tbsp flour** until the fruit is evenly coated.
3. Spread the spiced apple mixture evenly into the bottom of a **1 pastry-lined pie pan**.

### Step 2: Blend and Pour Custard
4. Using a blender or electric mixer, whip together the remaining filling ingredients: **1 cup unsweetened applesauce**, **1 cup milk**, **2 tsp vanilla extract**, **3 eggs**, and **1/4 cup flour** until completely smooth and slightly frothy.
5. Gently pour the custard mixture into the pie pan directly over the arranged **sliced apples**.

### Step 3: Bake and Cool
6. Bake the pie in the preheated oven at **400°F** for **10 minutes**.
7. Reduce the oven temperature to **350°F** and continue baking for **50 to 60 minutes**, or until the custard has set in the center and is beautifully golden brown.
8. Remove from the oven and cool completely on a wire rack. Store refrigerated before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This pie is best served chilled and can be stored, covered, in the refrigerator for up to 3 days. For a delicious variation, substitute the apples with fresh sliced peaches or pears.
