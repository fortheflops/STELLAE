# Buttermilk Pound Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** UK15 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic, tender buttermilk pound cake with a fine crumb and a hint of lemon. This traditional recipe creates a rich, moist cake perfect for any occasion.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 1 hour 15 mins | 20 mins | 1 large Bundt or tube cake |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large tube or Bundt pan
* Electric mixer
* Large mixing bowl
* Separate bowl
* Whisk
* Wooden skewer
* Wire cooling rack

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 cup (2 sticks) | [[Butter]] | Softened |
| 2 cups | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 4 | [[Eggs]] | Large |
| 3 cups | [[Flour]] | All-purpose |
| 1/2 teaspoon | [[Soda]] | Baking |
| 1/4 teaspoon | [[Salt]] | None |
| 1 cup | [[Buttermilk]] | None |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Vanilla Extract]] | None |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Lemon Extract]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Cream
1. Preheat your oven to **325°F**. Generously grease and flour a large tube or Bundt pan.
2. In a large bowl, cream the **1 cup (2 sticks) butter**, softened, and **2 cups sugar** together with an electric mixer until light and fluffy.
3. Add the **4 eggs** one at a time, beating well after each addition.

### Step 2: Combine Wet and Dry
4. In a separate bowl, whisk together the **3 cups all-purpose flour**, **1/2 teaspoon baking soda**, and **1/4 teaspoon salt**.
5. On low speed, add the dry ingredients to the creamed mixture in three parts, alternating with the **1 cup buttermilk** in two parts, beginning and ending with the dry ingredients. Mix only until just combined.
6. Stir in the **1 teaspoon vanilla extract** and **1 teaspoon lemon extract**.

### Step 3: Bake and Cool
7. Pour the batter into the prepared pan.
8. Bake for 1 hour to 1 hour and 15 minutes, or until a wooden skewer inserted into the center comes out clean and golden brown.
9. Let the cake cool in the pan for **15-20 minutes** before turning it out onto a wire rack to cool completely.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store wrapped tightly in plastic wrap at room temperature for up to 3 days, or freeze for up to 2 months.
