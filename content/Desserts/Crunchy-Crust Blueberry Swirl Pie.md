# Crunchy-Crust Blueberry Swirl Pie

> 📜 **Collection:** UnknownFifteen | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A unique no-bake pie featuring a crunchy baked oatmeal-nut crust and a cool blueberry-lemon gelatin filling finished with a tangy sour cream swirl. Chill until firm for a refreshing, sweet-tart dessert.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 12 mins | 4 hours | One 9-inch pie |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* 9-inch pie pan
* Knife

---
## Ingredients

### Crust
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 cup | [[Flour]] | None |
| 3/4 cup | [[Oatmeal]] | None |
| 1/4 cup | [[Nuts]] | Chopped |
| 1/2 cup (1 stick) | [[Margarine]] | Melted |

### Filling
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 cup | [[Water]] | Hot |
| 1 (3 ounce) package | [[Lemon Jello]] | None |
| 1 can (21 oz) | [[Blueberry Pie Filling]] | None |
| Several spoonfuls | [[Sour Cream]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Make the Crust
1. Preheat the oven to **375°F (190°C)**. In a bowl, combine the **1 cup flour**, **3/4 cup oatmeal**, and **1/4 cup chopped nuts**.
2. Add the **1/2 cup margarine** and mix until combined. Press the mixture into the bottom and up the sides of a **9-inch pie pan**.
3. Bake for **10-12 minutes**, until lightly browned and fragrant. Let the crust cool completely.

### Step 2: Make the Filling
4. In a bowl, dissolve the **1 package lemon Jello** in **1 cup hot water**.
5. Chill the Jello mixture until it has thickened to the consistency of egg whites.

### Step 3: Assemble and Chill
6. Once thickened, stir in the **1 can blueberry pie filling**.
7. Pour the filling into the cooled, cooked crust.
8. Add several spoonfuls of **sour cream** on top of the filling and use a knife to gently create a swirled effect.
9. Chill the pie for at least **4 hours**, or until completely firm, before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the pie covered in the refrigerator for up to 3 days until ready to serve.
