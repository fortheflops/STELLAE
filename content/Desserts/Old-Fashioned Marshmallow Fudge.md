# Old-Fashioned Marshmallow Fudge

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic, cooked chocolate fudge recipe made with melted marshmallows for a wonderfully smooth and creamy texture. This traditional fudge is cooked to the soft-ball stage and beaten until it thickens.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 20 mins | 2 hours | Approx. 2 pounds |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 8x8 inch pan
* heavy-bottomed saucepan
* candy thermometer
* wooden spoon

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 cups | [[Sugar]] | granulated |
| 1 cup | [[Milk]] | whole |
| 2 oz | [[Bitter Chocolate]] | unsweetened, chopped |
| 3 Tbsp | [[Butter]] | unsalted |
| 1 tsp | [[Vanilla]] | extract |
| 10 large | [[Marshmallows]] | melted |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Cook the Base
1. Butter an 8x8 inch dish.
2. In a heavy-bottomed saucepan, combine **2 cups sugar**, **1 cup milk**, and **2 oz bitter chocolate**. Cook over low-medium heat, stirring constantly, until the **sugar** is dissolved and the **chocolate** is melted.
3. Stop stirring and attach a candy thermometer. Continue to cook until the mixture reaches the **soft-ball stage (234°F to 240°F)**.

### Step 2: Cool and Beat
4. Remove the pan from the heat. Add **3 Tbsp butter** to the top but do not stir. Let the mixture cool undisturbed until it reaches approximately 110°F.
5. Add **1 tsp vanilla** and beat vigorously with a wooden spoon until the mixture loses its glossy sheen and becomes thick and creamy.
6. Quickly stir in the **10 large marshmallows** (melted).

### Step 3: Set
7. Pour the fudge into the prepared dish and let it set completely at room temperature until firm before cutting into squares.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 2 weeks or refrigerate for longer shelf life.
