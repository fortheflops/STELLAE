# Chocolate Peanut Clusters

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *Indulge in these easy-to-make chocolate peanut clusters combining semi-sweet chocolate and salted peanuts. Perfect for a quick sweet treat or holiday candy tray.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 5 mins | 30 mins | 18 clusters |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Double boiler
* Spoon
* Waxed paper

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 pkg. | [[Semi-Sweet Chocolate Chips]] | Standard package |
| 1 1/3 cups | [[Peanuts]] | Shelled |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Melting and Mixing
1. Heat **1 pkg. semi-sweet chocolate chips** over boiling water until partly melted, then remove from boiling water and stir until blended and smooth.
2. Add **1 1/3 cups peanuts** and mix well to coat completely.

### Step 2: Shaping and Cooling
3. Drop mixture by teaspoonfuls onto waxed paper.
4. Cool until firm to the touch before serving or storing.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to one week.
