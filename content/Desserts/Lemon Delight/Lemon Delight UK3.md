## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
| 1 Large Can          | [[Chilled Evaporated Milk]], Whipped |
| 2                     | [[Lemons]] - Juice & Rind   |
| 1 Cup          | [[Sugar]]                   |
|                       | [[Graham]] or [[Vanilla Wafer Crumbs]] |
___
### Instructions
1. Whip milk, add sugar gradually, then lemon & rind.
2. Line dish with crumbs, put filling in, & sprinkle crumbs on top.
3. Freeze.
4. Double for fridge.
