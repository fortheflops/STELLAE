# Lemon Supreme Special Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A wonderfully moist and easy-to-make lemon cake using a doctored cake mix. Enriched with apricot nectar and soaked in a simple lemon glaze, this cake is baked in a tube pan for a beautiful presentation.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 1 hour | 10 mins | 1 tube cake (12-16 servings) |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Tube or Bundt pan
* Large bowl
* Small bowl
* Whisk
* Long wooden skewer

## Ingredients

### Cake
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 package | [[Duncan Hines]] Lemon Cake Mix | dry |
| 1/2 cup | [[Sugar]] | granulated |
| 3/4 cup | [[Crisco Oil]] | vegetable oil |
| 1 cup | [[Apricot Nectar]] | chilled or room temperature |
| 4 | [[Eggs]] | large, beaten one at a time |

### Icing
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 cup | [[Powdered Sugar]] | sifted |
| Juice of 1 | [[Lemon]] | freshly squeezed |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Mix Cake
1. Preheat your oven to **325°F**. Grease and flour a tube or Bundt pan thoroughly.
2. In a large bowl, mix the **1/2 cup sugar**, **3/4 cup Crisco oil**, **1 cup apricot nectar**, and **1 package Duncan Hines lemon cake mix** together until combined.
3. Add the **4 eggs** one at a time, beating well after each addition until the batter is smooth and well incorporated.

### Step 2: Bake the Cake
4. Pour the batter evenly into the prepared tube or Bundt pan.
5. Bake for **1 hour**, or until a long wooden skewer inserted into the center comes out clean and the top springs back lightly to the touch.

### Step 3: Glaze and Cool
6. Let the cake cool in the pan for about **10 minutes**.
7. While the cake is cooling, prepare the icing by whisking together the **1 cup powdered sugar** and **juice of 1 lemon** in a small bowl until completely smooth.
8. Pour the icing over the warm cake, allowing it to soak into the surface, and let cool completely in the pan before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the cooled cake tightly covered at room temperature for up to 3 days or refrigerate for up to 5 days.
