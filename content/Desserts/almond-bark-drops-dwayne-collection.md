# Almond Bark Drops

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic no-bake candy treat made with melted almond bark, crunchy Spanish peanuts, mini marshmallows, and crispy rice cereal. Quick to prepare and perfect for holiday cookie trays or easy sweet cravings.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 5 mins | 30 mins | 24 drops |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Double boiler
* Teaspoon
* Waxed paper
* Baking sheet

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 12 oz | [[Almond Bark]] | chopped for easier melting |
| 1 cup | [[Spanish Peanuts]] | whole |
| 1 cup | [[Miniature Marshmallows]] |  |
| 1 cup | [[Crispy Rice Cereal]] | such as Rice Krispies |

---

## Instructions

### Melt and Mix
1. Melt the **12 oz almond bark** in the top of a double boiler over gently simmering water, stirring occasionally until completely smooth.

### Combine and Set
2. Remove the melted chocolate from heat and stir in the **1 cup Spanish peanuts**, **1 cup miniature marshmallows**, and **1 cup crispy rice cereal** until everything is thoroughly coated.
3. Drop the mixture by teaspoonfuls onto a sheet of waxed paper.
4. Let stand at room temperature for about 30 minutes until fully hardened and firm to the touch.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the cooled drops in an airtight container at room temperature layered between sheets of waxed paper for up to 2 weeks.
