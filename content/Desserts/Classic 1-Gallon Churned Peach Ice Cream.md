# Classic 1-Gallon Churned Peach Ice Cream

> 📜 **Collection:** General Archive | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A rich, classic 1-gallon churned peach ice cream featuring a traditional uncooked egg base, evaporated milk, and fresh peach nectar. This vintage recipe provides a creamy, authentic texture perfect for summer gatherings.*

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 1-gallon ice cream churn
* Large mixing bowl
* Whisk or electric mixer

## Ingredients

### Ice Cream Base
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 6 | [[Eggs]] | beaten until light |
| 2 1/2 Cups | [[Sugar]] | granulated |
| 2 large cans | [[Evaporated Milk]] | chilled |
| 1 qt. | [[Peach]] Nectar | homemade or store-bought |
| 1 | [[Lemon]] | juiced |
| 1 qt. | Whole [[Milk]] | cold |

### For Churning
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| As needed | Crushed [[Ice]] | for packing |
| 2/3 Cup | [[Salt]] | ice cream salt |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Base
1. In a very large bowl, beat the **6 eggs** until light and fluffy.
2. Slowly whisk in the **2 1/2 cups sugar** and **2 large cans evaporated milk** until fully combined.

### Step 2: Churning
3. Pour the mixture into a **1-gallon ice cream churn canister**.
4. Stir in the **1 qt. peach nectar**, **juice of 1 lemon**, and **1 qt. whole milk**.
5. Fasten the churn top and operate for **2 minutes** to incorporate ingredients before adding ice.
6. Pack the churn bucket by adding **crushed ice** and **2/3 cup salt** in alternating layers around the canister.
7. Operate the churn for **25 to 40 minutes** until the cream is frozen to a thick, scoopable consistency.
8. Remove the dasher, pack the ice cream down, and let it ripen in the ice for **1 hour** before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The ice cream can be stored in an airtight container in the freezer for up to 2 weeks; for best texture, consume within 3 days.
