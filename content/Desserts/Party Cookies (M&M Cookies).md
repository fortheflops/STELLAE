# Party Cookies (M&M Cookies)

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic, crowd-pleasing recipe for soft and chewy M&M cookies. This simple drop cookie is packed with colorful candy-coated chocolates, making it a perfect treat for any party.*

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

## Instructions

### Preparation and Mixing
1. Preheat your oven to 375°F.
2. In a large bowl, cream together the **1 cup shortening**, **1 cup brown sugar**, and **1/2 cup granulated sugar** with an electric mixer until light and fluffy.
3. Beat in the **2 teaspoons vanilla extract** and **2 eggs**.
4. In a separate bowl, sift together the **2 1/4 cups all-purpose flour**, **1 teaspoon baking soda**, and **1 teaspoon salt**.
5. Gradually add the sifted dry ingredients to the creamed mixture and mix well.
6. By hand, stir in **1/2 cup M&M candies**.

### Baking
7. Drop by rounded spoonfuls onto an ungreased cookie sheet.
8. Press the remaining **1 cup M&M candies** onto the tops of the cookies for decoration.
9. Bake for **10-12 minutes**, or until golden brown and fragrant. Let cool on the pan for a few minutes before transferring to a wire rack to cool completely.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 5 days, or freeze the unbaked dough balls for up to 3 months.
