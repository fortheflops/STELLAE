## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
| A Roaster Full             | [[Popped Corn]]              |
| 2 Cups             | [[Brown Sugar]]         |
| 2 Sticks                   | [[Butter]]          |
| 1/2 Cup                   | [[Karo]] |
| 1 Teaspoon        | [[Salt]]           |
| 1 Teaspoon | [[Soda]]          |
| 1 Teaspoon      |[[Vanilla]]|
___
### Instructions
1. Stir the above constantly until it comes to a boil and then let it boil 5 minutes.
2. Then add 1 teaspoon soda, 1 teaspoon vanilla.
3. Pour over popcorn.
4. 250° turn every 15 minutes for 1 hour.
