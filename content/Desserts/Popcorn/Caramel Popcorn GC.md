## Recipe
| Measurements                     | Ingredients                               |
| :------------------------------- | :---------------------------------------- |
| Three Quarts                     | Popped [[Popcorn]]                        |
| One Cup                          | [[Brown Sugar]] (Packed)                  |
| $\frac{1}{2}$ Cup                | [[Butter]] / [[Margarine]]                |
| $\frac{1}{4}$ Cup                | Light [[Corn Syrup]]                      |
| $\frac{1}{2}$ Teaspoon           | [[Salt]]                                  |
| $\frac{1}{2}$ Teaspoon           | Baking [[Soda]]                           |

___
## Instructions
1.  Preheat oven to 200°F. You will need one large ungreased cookie sheet.
2.  Have your three quarts of popped popcorn ready in a large bowl.
3.  In a saucepan, melt the butter/margarine. Add the packed brown sugar, light corn syrup, and salt.
4.  Heat the mixture on low, stirring constantly, until it comes to a boil. Continue to boil for 5 minutes, still stirring constantly.
5.  Remove the sauce from the heat and stir in the one half teaspoon of baking soda. The mixture will foam up.
6.  Pour the hot caramel sauce over the popped popcorn. Stir well to ensure the popcorn is evenly coated.
7.  Spread the coated popcorn onto the large ungreased cookie sheet in a single layer.
8.  Bake in the preheated 200°F oven for one full hour.
9.  It is crucial to turn or stir the popcorn every 15 minutes while it's baking to prevent burning and ensure even crisping.
10. Once baked, remove from the oven and let it cool completely on the cookie sheet. It will become crispier as it cools. Break apart any large clumps.
