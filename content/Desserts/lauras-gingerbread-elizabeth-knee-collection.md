## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing the Base
1. Mix well **1 c. brown sugar** and **1/2 c. shortening**.
2. Add **1 c. molasses** and mix well.
3. Stir in **2 t. bak. soda**.
4. Add **1 c. hot water** (full after steam has run off) into the cake mix.

### Step 2: Adding Dry Ingredients and Baking
5. Mix in **3 c. flour**, **1 t. ginger**, **1 t. cinnamon**, **1 t. allspice**, **1 t. nutmeg**, **1 t. cloves**, and **1/2 t. salt**.
6. Add the last **2 well beaten eggs** and mix. The batter should be quite thin.
7. Bake in a moderate oven for **30 mins** until a rich golden brown and a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.
8. Optional: Add **raisins**, **candied fruit**, **nuts**, or serve with **chocolate frosting** if desired.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the baked gingerbread in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 3 days, or wrap tightly and freeze for up to 2 months.
