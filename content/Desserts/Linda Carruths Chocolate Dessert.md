# Linda Carruth's Chocolate Dessert

> 📜 **Collection:** UK15 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Linda Carruth | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A decadent layered dessert featuring a buttery pecan crust, a sweet cream cheese layer, rich chocolate pudding, and a light whipped topping. This classic treat is perfect for gatherings and sets beautifully in the refrigerator.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Crust
1. Preheat oven to 325°F. Mix the **1 cup flour**, **1 stick oleo**, and **1 cup pecans** until crumbly.
2. Pat the mixture into an 8x11 inch dish and bake for **30 minutes** until golden brown. Let the crust cool completely.

### Step 2: First Layer
3. Beat together the **8 ounces cream cheese**, **1 cup Cool Whip**, **1 cup powdered sugar**, and **1 teaspoon vanilla** until smooth.
4. Spread the mixture evenly over the cooled crust.

### Step 3: Second Layer
5. In a separate bowl, whisk the **1 large package chocolate pudding mix**, **2 cups milk**, and **1 teaspoon vanilla** for 2 minutes until the mixture thickens.
6. Pour and spread this over the cream cheese layer.

### Step 4: Third Layer and Chill
7. Top the pudding layer with the remaining **2 cups Cool Whip**, spreading it evenly to cover the dessert.
8. Refrigerate for at least 4 hours, or preferably overnight, until the layers are set and firm.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This dessert is best when prepared the day before to allow the layers to set firmly in the refrigerator.
