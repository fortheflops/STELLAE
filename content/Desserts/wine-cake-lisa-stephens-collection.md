# Wine Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** Lisa Stephens Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A fragrant, moist dessert cake made effortlessly using yellow cake mix, instant pudding, and rich cream sherry wine. Baked in a tube pan and spiced with nutmeg, it offers a warm, nostalgic flavor profile.*

---

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 45 mins | 10 mins | 12 servings |

* Mixing bowl
* Whisk or electric mixer

| 3/4 cup | [[Vegetable Oil]] |  |

| 1 tsp | [[Nutmeg]] |  |

---

## Instructions

### Mixing and Baking
1. Preheat your oven to 350°F and grease a tube pan.
2. In a large bowl, mix **1 box yellow cake mix** and **1 box instant pudding** together while dry.
3. Add **4 eggs**, **3/4 cup vegetable oil**, and **3/4 cup cream sherry wine**. Mix well until smooth and fully integrated.
4. Add **1 tsp nutmeg** to the batter and stir well.
5. Pour the batter into the prepared tube pan.
6. Bake **45 minutes** at 350°F or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean and the top springs back lightly when touched.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the cake wrapped tightly at room temperature for up to 3 days to allow the flavors to deepen.
