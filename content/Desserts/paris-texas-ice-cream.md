# Paris Texas Ice Cream

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Five | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A rich and creamy homemade ice cream recipe originating from Paris, Texas, featuring rich dairy and customizable fruit options. Yields a generous batch perfect for gatherings.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | None | 30 mins | 16 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Ice cream maker
* Mixing bowl
* Measuring cups
* Measuring spoons

---
## Ingredients

### Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3 cups | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 1 tbsp | [[Flour]] | All-purpose |
| 1/2 tsp | [[Salt]] | Standard |
| 2 qts | [[1/2 & 1/2]] | Half and half |
| 1 pt | [[Whipping cream]] | Heavy cream |
| 2 tbsp | [[Vanilla]] | Or other flavoring |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing and Freezing
1. Mix the **3 cups sugar**, **1 tbsp flour**, and **1/2 tsp salt** together in a large bowl.
2. Add the **2 qts 1/2 & 1/2**, **1 pt whipping cream**, and **2 tbsp vanilla** (or other flavoring), stirring until the sugar is fully dissolved and the mixture is smooth.
3. Pour the mixture into an ice cream maker and freeze according to the manufacturer's instructions until it reaches a thick, scoopable consistency.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store finished ice cream in an airtight container in the freezer for up to two weeks.
