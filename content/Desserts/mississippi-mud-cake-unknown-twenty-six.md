# Mississippi Mud Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Maxine | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A rich and decadent classic Mississippi Mud Cake topped with a gooey layer of marshmallow cream and a fudgy chocolate frosting. Perfect for potlucks, family gatherings, or any chocolate lover's craving.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Bake the Cake
1. Preheat your oven to **350°F** and grease a 9x13-inch baking pan.
2. In a large mixing bowl, beat the **4 eggs** and **2 cups sugar** together until thick and pale (beat for quite a while).
3. Add the **2 sticks margarine**, **2 tablespoons cocoa powder**, **1 teaspoon vanilla extract**, and **1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour** to the egg mixture. Beat well until fully blended and smooth.
4. Fold in the **1 cup shredded coconut** and chopped **pecans**.
5. Pour the batter into the prepared baking pan and bake at **350°F** for 30 to 35 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.
6. Immediately upon removing the hot cake from the oven, spread the **1 small jar marshmallow fluff** evenly over the top so it melts into a gooey layer.

### Step 2: Prepare the Frosting
7. In a bowl, combine **1 stick margarine**, **1/3 cup cocoa powder**, **1 box (approx. 3 cups) powdered sugar**, **1 teaspoon vanilla extract**, and **1/3 cup evaporated milk**.
8. Beat the frosting mixture well until smooth and spreadable. Stir in optional chopped **pecans** if desired.
9. Spread the chocolate frosting gently over the warm marshmallow layer. Let the cake cool completely before slicing so the layers set beautifully.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Can be made 1 day in advance. Store covered at room temperature; do not refrigerate as it may harden the marshmallow layer.
