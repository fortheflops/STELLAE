# Chocolate Angel Food Dessert

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Ida Kountz | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A decadent layered dessert featuring rich melted chocolate, fluffy whipped cream, and angel food cake. Chilled overnight, this make-ahead sweet treat is sliced into squares and garnished for a crowd-pleasing finish.*

---

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 30 mins | 10 mins | 24 hours | 8 servings |

## Instructions

### Step 1: Chocolate Base
1. Melt **1 (6 oz.) pkg. chocolate chips** in a double boiler, add **1 T. sugar** and well beaten **4 eggs** ( yolks only ), gradually stirring until smooth.
2. Let mixture cool.

### Step 2: Whipping and Folding
3. Add **1 T. sugar** to egg whites and beat until stiff.
4. Add **1 tsp. sugar** to **1 cup whipped cream** and whip.
5. Fold whites and cream into chocolate mixture until thoroughly incorporated.

### Step 3: Assembly and Chilling
6. Break **1 angel food cake** into bits.
7. Place a layer of broken cake in the bottom of a baking dish, and cover with a portion of the chocolate mixture.
8. Repeat, ending with a second layer of the chocolate mixture to completely cover the cake.
9. Cover with waxed paper and chill for 24 hrs until set.
10. Cut into squares and garnish with additional **whipped cream**, **maraschino cherries**, or **walnut meats**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Cover with waxed paper and refrigerate for a full 24 hours before serving to allow the layers to set properly.
