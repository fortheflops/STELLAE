# Welsh Cookies

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Elaine | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *Delightful traditional Welsh cookies packed with currants, raisins, or dates, and chopped nuts. Perfect for serving golden-brown straight from a dry frying pan.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 15 mins | None | 5 dozen |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Rolling pin
* Dry frying pan
* Biscuit cutter

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 4 cups | [[Flour]] | Sifted |
| 1/2 lb | [[Butter]] | Or shortening |
| 1 cup | [[Sugar]] | None |
| 1 tsp | [[Salt]] | None |
| 1 tsp | [[Soda]] | Dissolved in buttermilk |
| 1/4 cup | [[Buttermilk]] | None |
| 1 tsp | [[Nutmeg]] | None |
| 1 1/2 cups | [[Currants]] | Or raisins, dates |
| 1/2 cup | [[Chopped Nuts]] | Chopped |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing and Rolling
1. Mix all ingredients including **4 cups flour**, **1/2 lb butter**, **1 cup sugar**, **1 tsp salt**, **1 tsp soda**, **1/4 cup buttermilk**, **1 tsp nutmeg**, **1 1/2 cups currants**, **1/2 cup chopped nuts**, and **2 eggs** all at once.
2. Roll out like pie dough to desired thickness.

### Step 2: Cooking
3. Cut into circles with a cutter.
4. Fry in a dry frying pan until golden brown and cooked through.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store baked cookies in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 5 days.
