## Instructions

### Cook the Custard Base
1. In a saucepan over low heat, combine **1 cup strained ripe avocado**, **4 egg yolks**, **4 tsp butter**, **1 dash nutmeg**, **1 dash cinnamon**, **2 tbsp sugar**, and the **juice of 1/2 lemon**.
2. Cook slowly, stirring constantly until heated through and smooth.

### Soften Gelatin and Cool
3. In a small bowl, sprinkle **1 tbsp gelatin** over **1/4 cup rum** to soften.
4. Add the gelatin mixture directly into the hot avocado custard and stir thoroughly until completely dissolved.
5. Remove from heat and set aside to cool.

### Whip Meringue and Assemble
6. In a clean bowl, beat **4 egg whites** until soft peaks form, then gradually add **1/2 cup sugar** and beat until stiff peaks form.
7. Gently fold the stiffly beaten egg whites into the cooled avocado mixture until homogeneous.
8. Pour the chiffon filling into the cooled **1 baked 9-inch pie crust**.
9. Sprinkle top with **1/4 cup macadamia nuts** and chill until set. Garnish with whipped cream before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Chill the pie in the refrigerator for at least 3 hours or overnight before serving to allow the gelatin chiffon setting to complete.
