# Orange-Spice Cookies

> 📜 **Collection:** UK15 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A soft, cake-like drop cookie featuring the warm, nostalgic flavors of molasses, ginger, cloves, and fresh orange peel. These old-fashioned treats are perfect for holiday baking or a cozy afternoon snack.*

| 1/4 tsp | [[Cloves]] | ground |

| 2 Tbsp | [[Molasses]] | none |

| 1/2 Cup | [[Nuts]] | chopped |

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Dough
1. Preheat your oven to **350°F** and lightly grease a cookie sheet.
2. In a medium bowl, stir together the **1 1/4 cups flour**, **1 tsp ginger**, **1/4 tsp cloves**, **1/2 tsp baking soda**, and **1/8 tsp salt**.
3. In a large mixer bowl, cream the **1/2 cup butter**, **1/2 cup brown sugar**, and **2 tbsp molasses** until light and fluffy.
4. Add the **1/2 egg** and **grated orange peel**; beat well until combined.
5. Gradually stir in the flour mixture until just combined, then fold in the **1/2 cup nuts** by hand.

### Step 2: Bake and Cool
6. Drop the dough by rounded teaspoonfuls about 2 inches apart onto the prepared cookie sheet.
7. Bake for **12 minutes**, or until the edges are lightly browned and the centers are set.
8. Remove from the oven and transfer to a wire rack to cool completely.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 5 days, or freeze the unbaked dough balls for up to 3 months.
