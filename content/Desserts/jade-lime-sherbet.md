# Jade Lime Sherbet

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A refreshing and creamy jade lime sherbet made with gelatin, milk, and fresh lemon juice. This frozen treat offers a delightful citrus tang and smooth texture.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 0 mins | 4 hours | 6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Freezer tray
* Beater

---
## Ingredients

### Sherbet Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 pkg | [[Lime Jello]] | Dry gelatin |
| 1 c. | [[Hot Water]] | Boiling |
| 1/2 c. | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 2 c. | [[Milk]] | Regular |
| 1 c. | [[Evaporated Milk]] | Chilled (marked as tnf milk) |
| 1/4 c. | [[Lemon Juice]] | Freshly squeezed |
| 1 tsp | [[Grated Lemon Peel]] | Zested |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and Freezing
1. Dissolve the **1 pkg lime jello** in **1 c. hot water**.
2. Stir in **1/2 c. sugar**, **2 c. milk**, **1 c. tnf milk**, **1/4 c. lemon juice**, and **1 tsp grated lemon peel**.
3. Mix thoroughly until completely combined.
4. Pour into a tray and freeze firm.

### Step 2: Whipping and Final Freeze
5. Turn the frozen mixture into a bowl and beat thoroughly until smooth and fluffy.
6. Return to tray and freeze until set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Keep stored in the freezer for up to 2 weeks. Let soften slightly at room temperature before serving.
