# Marshmallow Pudding

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Diane Tesitor | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A delightful layered dessert featuring melted marshmallows, crushed pineapple, whipped cream, and pecans over vanilla wafers. This chilled treat sets in the refrigerator for 24 hours to develop a rich, cohesive texture.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 25 mins | 10 mins | 24 hours | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Double boiler
* Mixing bowl
* Shallow pan

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 lb | [[Marshmallows]] | Standard size |
| 1 large can | [[Crushed pineapple]] | Juice drained |
| 1 pint | [[Whipping cream]] | Chilled |
| 1/2 c | [[Pecans]] | Crushed |
| 1 lb | [[Vanilla wafers]] | Crushed for lining |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Melting and Mixing
1. Melt in double boiler, **1 lb marshmallows** with juice drained from **1 large can of crushed pineapple**. Cool.
2. Fold this mixture into the **1 pint of whipping cream**.
3. Then fold in crushed pineapple and **1/2 c pecans** (crushed).

### Step 2: Layering and Setting
4. Line bottom of shallow pan with crushed **1 lb. vanilla wafers**.
5. Pour **1/2** of mixture over crumbs.
6. Add second layer of wafers and pour remainder of mixture over this.
7. Set in refrigerator **24 hours** until thoroughly chilled and set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Must be made 24 hours in advance and stored in the refrigerator before serving.
