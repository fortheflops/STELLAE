## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| One Package (e.g., 6-12 oz)      | Semi-Sweet [[Chocolate]] (chips or bar)   |
| Two Tablespoons                  | [[Water]]                                 |
| Three                            | [[Egg Yolks]]                             |
| Three                            | [[Egg Whites]], Stiffly Beaten            |
| One Cube (or $\frac{1}{2}$ Cup)  | [[Butter]], Cut into small pieces         |
| For Serving (Optional)           | Melted Vanilla [[Ice Cream]] or [[Whipped Cream]] |
___
## Instructions
1.  Combine semi-sweet chocolate and two tablespoons of water in a heavy pan over low flame.
2.  Stir occasionally until the chocolate is melted.
3.  Remove from heat.
4.  Gradually stir in three egg yolks, one at a time.
5.  Melt one cube of butter (cut into small pieces) and add it to the chocolate mixture.
6.  Be sure that the mixture is very smooth.
7.  Gently fold in three stiffly beaten egg whites.
8.  Put the mousse into a well-buttered mold or individual glasses.
9.  Chill until firm.
10. Serve with melted vanilla ice cream or whipped cream, if desired.
