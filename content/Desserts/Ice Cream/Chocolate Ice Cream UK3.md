## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
| 7             | [[Eggs]]              |
| 2 Cups             | [[Sugar]]         |
| 1 Quart                   | [[Half & Half]]          |
| 1/4 Teaspoon                   | [[Salt]] |
| 1 Tablespoon        | [[Vanilla]]           |
| 1 Can | [[Hershey Chocolate (liquid)]]          |
|      |[[Milk]]|
___
### Instructions
1. Beat eggs for 10 min. at top speed.
2. Add all ingredients except milk & mix.
3. Put in ice cream freezer, add milk & freeze.
