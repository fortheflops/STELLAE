## Recipe
| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
| 7 Cups            | Whipping Cream      |
| 2 Cups            | Sugar               |
| 2 Tbsp            | Vanilla             |
| 2 Tbsp            | Lemon Juice         |
| 2 (13 oz) cans    | Evaporated Milk     |
___
### Instructions
1.  Stir together sugar, vanilla, & lemon juice.
2.  Gradually add evap. milk.
3.  Refrigerate until well chilled.
4.  Pour into ice cream freezer container.
5.  Churn & freeze.
6.  (Pro Scoop Ice Cream Balls.) *(As written on card)*
