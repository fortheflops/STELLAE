## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
| 6 Cups | [[Half & Half]] |
| 2 Cups | [[Whole Milk]] |
| 1 Can | [[Sweetened Condensed Milk]] |
| 1 1/2 Cups | [[Sugar]] |
| 3 Tablespoons | [[Vanilla Extract]] |
| 4-6 | Mashed [[Bananas]] or 1 Quart Mashed [[Strawberries]] |
___
### Instructions
1. Mix and pack into ice cream freezer.
2. Freeze
