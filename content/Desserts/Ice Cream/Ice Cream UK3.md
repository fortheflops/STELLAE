## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
| 1 can        | [[Eagle Brand Milk]]        |
| 6            | [[Eggs]]                    |
| 2 cups       | [[Sugar]]                   |
| 1 tablespoon | [[Vanilla]]                 |
|              | Homogenized Milk           |
| 1 can        | [[Hershey's Chocolate]] (for Chocolate Ice Cream) |
___
### Instructions
1. Beat eggs and sugar until blended well.
2. Add Eagle Brand and vanilla.
3. Mix well.
4. Pour into freezer can.
5. Fill to top with homogenized milk.
6. Add 1 can Hershey's Chocolate for chocolate ice cream.
