# Grasshopper Pie

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Marci Le | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A delightful no-bake Grasshopper Pie featuring a crushed chocolate cookie crust and a creamy, minty marshmallow filling. Perfect for chilling ahead of time and serving after dinner.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 0 mins | 24 hours | 8 servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 9-inch pie plate
* Mixing bowl
* Electric mixer

### Filling

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |

## Instructions

### Step 1: Crust Preparation
1. Crush **16 Oreo cookies** and mix with **5 T. melted butter**.
2. Line a **9-inch pie plate** with the mixture and chill.

### Step 2: Filling Preparation
3. Melt **25 marshmallows** with **2/3 cup milk**, then cool thoroughly.
4. Add **1 shot of Creme de Menthe (Green)** and **1 shot of Creme de Menthe (White)** (or use 2 green).
5. Whip **1/2 pint cream** and gently fold into the cooled marshmallow mixture.
6. Pour the filling into the prepared crust and chill for **24 hours** until set.

### Step 3: Garnish and Serve
7. Sprinkle with reserved cookie crumbs or decorate with **cherries** and **whipping cream** before serving.
8. Cut into small pieces; after dinner, suggest cutting into eighths.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Cover and chill in the refrigerator for up to 24 hours before serving for best results.
