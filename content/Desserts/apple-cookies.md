# Apple Cookies

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *Delight in these spiced homemade apple cookies featuring raisins, nuts, and warm autumn spices. Perfectly soft and aromatic, they make a comforting treat for any afternoon gathering.*

| 1/2 C | [[Shortening]] | Softened |
| 1 C | [[Nuts]] | Chopped |
| 1 t | [[Cinnamon]] | Ground |
| 1 t | [[Nutmeg]] | Ground |
| 1 t | [[Soda]] | Baking soda |
| 2 C | [[Apples]] | Chopped |
| 1 C | [[Raisins]] | Standard |
| 2 1/2 C | [[Flour]] | All-purpose |
| 1/4 t | [[Cloves]] | Ground |
| 1 | [[Egg]] | Beaten |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing the Dough
1. Cream together the **1 C sugar** and **1/2 C shortening** in a large mixing bowl until smooth.
2. Beat in the **1 egg** until fully incorporated.
3. Stir in the **2 C chopped apples**, **1 C raisins**, and **1 C chopped nuts**.

### Step 2: Adding Dry Ingredients and Baking
4. Sift together the **2 1/2 C flour**, **1 t cinnamon**, **1 t nutmeg**, **1/4 t cloves**, and **1 t soda**, then gently fold into the wet mixture until just combined.
5. Drop spoonfuls of dough onto a prepared baking sheet and bake until edges are lightly golden and centers are set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store baked cookies in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 3 days, or freeze the unbaked dough drops for future baking.
