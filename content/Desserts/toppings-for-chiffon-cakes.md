# Toppings For Chiffon Cakes

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A delightful pair of vintage homemade cake toppings featuring a rich Chocolate Fluff and a creamy Orange Fluff. These classic toppings elevate any chiffon cake to a decadent dessert.*

## Instructions

### Chocolate Fluff Preparation
1. In a chilled bowl, mix together **2 cups whipping cream**, **1 cup confectioners sugar**, **1/2 cup cocoa**, and **1/16 tsp salt**.
2. Beat the mixture until stiff enough to hold a point.
3. Serve on wedges of cake.

### Orange Fluff Preparation
4. In a double boiler, combine **3 egg yolks** and **1/2 cup sugar**, then stir in **1/3-1/2 cup orange juice**.
5. Cook over hot water, stirring constantly, until the mixture thickens, about 15 minutes.
6. Remove from heat, stir in **1 tbsp grated orange rind**, and allow to cool.
7. Fold in **1 cup heavy cream** (whipped) and **1/2 cup blanched almonds** (chopped, toasted, or alternatively **1/2 cup fresh grated flaky coconut**).
8. Serve in wedges of cake.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store toppings covered in the refrigerator for up to a few hours before serving on cake.
