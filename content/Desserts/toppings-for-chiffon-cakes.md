# Toppings For Chiffon Cakes

> *A delightful duo of vintage whipped toppings perfect for chiffon cakes, featuring a rich, quick-to-make Chocolate Fluff and a bright, custard-based Orange Fluff.*

## Instructions

### Chocolate Fluff
1. In a chilled bowl, mix together **2 c. whipping cream**, **1 c. sifted confectioners' sugar**, **1/2 c. cocoa**, and **1/16 tsp. salt**.
2. Beat the mixture until it is stiff enough to hold a point.
3. Serve on wedges of cake. Makes about **4 c. topping**.

### Orange Fluff
4. In a double boiler, thoroughly mix together **3 egg yolks** and **1/2 c. sugar**.
5. Stir in **1/3 to 1/2 c. orange juice**.
6. Cook over hot water, stirring constantly, until the mixture thickens, about **15 minutes**.
7. Remove from heat and stir in **1 tbsp. grated orange rind**, then let cool completely.
8. Fold in **1 c. heavy cream** (whipped) and either **1/2 c. chopped, toasted, blanched almonds** or **1/2 c. fresh grated packaged coconut**.
9. Serve on wedges of cake. Makes about **3 c. topping**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The Chocolate Fluff is best served immediately but can be chilled for a few hours. The cooked orange base for the Orange Fluff can be made ahead and chilled before folding in the whipped cream and nuts/coconut.
