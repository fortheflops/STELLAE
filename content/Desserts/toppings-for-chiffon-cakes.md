# Toppings for Chiffon Cakes

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A delightful duo of vintage toppings perfect for chiffon cakes, featuring a rich Chocolate Fluff and a bright, citrusy Orange Fluff. These classic recipes add a light, airy finish to any homemade cake.*

## Instructions

### Chocolate Fluff
1. In a chilled mixing bowl, combine **2 cups whipping cream**, **1 cup sifted confectioners' sugar**, **1/2 cup cocoa powder**, and **1/16 teaspoon salt**.
2. Beat the mixture until it is stiff enough to hold a point.
3. Serve on wedges of cake. Makes about **4 cups** of topping.

### Orange Fluff
4. In a double boiler, thoroughly mix together **3 egg yolks** and **1/2 cup sugar**.
5. Stir in **1/3 to 1/2 cup orange juice**.
6. Cook over hot water, stirring constantly, until the mixture thickens, about **15 minutes**.
7. Remove from heat and stir in **1 tablespoon grated orange rind**, then allow to cool completely.
8. Gently fold in **1 cup whipped heavy cream** and **1/2 cup chopped, toasted, blanched almonds** (or **1/2 cup fresh grated packaged coconut**).
9. Serve on wedges of cake. Makes about **3 cups** of topping.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The Chocolate Fluff is best served immediately but can be chilled for a few hours. The Orange Fluff custard base can be made a day ahead and folded with whipped cream and nuts just before serving.
