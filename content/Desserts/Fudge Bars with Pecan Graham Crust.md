# Fudge Bars with Pecan Graham Crust

> *A decadent, brownie-like bar featuring a crunchy, salty-sweet graham cracker and toasted pecan crust. The rich fudge layer is crafted with unsweetened chocolate and brown sugar for a deep, intense flavor.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Crust
1. Preheat the oven to 350°F.
2. In a medium bowl, combine **2/3 cup graham cracker crumbs**, **1/2 cup pecans**, **2 tablespoons sugar**, **6 tablespoons butter**, and **1/4 teaspoon salt**.
3. Press the mixture firmly and evenly into the bottom of a 7x11 inch baking pan.
4. Bake for **5 minutes** until set, then let cool completely.

### Step 2: Prepare the Fudge Layer
5. In a small saucepan, melt **4 ounces chocolate** and **1/2 cup butter** together until smooth; set aside to cool to room temperature.
6. In a large bowl, beat **2 large eggs**, **1/4 teaspoon vanilla**, **1/4 teaspoon salt**, **2 cups light brown sugar**, and **1 cup flour** with a mixer until smooth.
7. Beat in the cooled **chocolate and butter mixture** until well combined.
8. Spread the fudge layer evenly on top of the cooled crust.
9. Bake in the middle of the oven for **30 to 35 minutes**, or until the top is set and the edges pull away from the pan.
10. While hot, score and cut into bars, wiping the knife clean between cuts, then let cool completely on a wire rack.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** These bars can be stored in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 3 days or refrigerated for up to 1 week.
