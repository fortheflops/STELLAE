# Mother's Custard Pie

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Mother | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic homemade custard pie recipe featuring a rich egg and milk filling delicately flavored with vanilla and nutmeg. Baked first at a high heat then lowered until perfectly set, it delivers a comforting, traditional treat.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 45 mins | None | 8 servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Whisk
* Pie pan

---
## Ingredients

### Filling
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5 | [[Eggs]] | Beaten |
| 3 cups | [[Milk]] | Canned milk okay |
| 1/2 cup | [[Sugar]] | Scant |
| 1 small teaspoon | [[Vanilla]] | None |
| To taste | [[Nutmeg]] | None |
| 1 pinch | [[Salt]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and Baking
1. Combine the **5 eggs**, **3 cups milk**, **1/2 cup sugar**, **1 small teaspoon vanilla**, and **1 pinch salt** in a mixing bowl and whisk until smooth.
2. Pour the mixture into a pie crust and dust the top with **nutmeg**.
3. Bake at 400°F for **10 mins**, then reduce the temperature to 325°F and continue baking **until set**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the baked custard pie in the refrigerator for up to 3 days. Serve chilled or at room temperature.
