## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Sensation Cake
1. Preheat your oven to **325°F**. Grease and flour a large tube or Bundt pan.
2. In a large bowl, cream the **1 pound butter** and **1 pound confectioners' sugar** together until light and fluffy. Beat in the **8 eggs** one at a time.
3. Alternately add the **5 cups flour** and the **1 cup lemon juice** to the creamed mixture, beginning and ending with the flour until just combined.
4. Pour the batter into the prepared pan and bake for **1 hour and 15-30 minutes**, or until a wooden skewer inserted into the center comes out clean.

### Step 2: Mix Fillings and Glazes
5. For the Macaroon filling, combine the **2 cups coconut**, **1/2 cup almonds**, **1 stick butter**, **8 ounces sour cream**, and **2 eggs** in a bowl and mix well until thoroughly incorporated.
6. For the Lemon Glaze, whisk together the **1 cup butter**, **1 cup sour cream**, and **1/4 cup lemon juice** in a bowl until smooth and velvety.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Cakes and frostings can be stored separately in airtight containers in the refrigerator for up to 3 days before assembly.
