# Cheese Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Five | ✍️ **Attribution:** June Thompson | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic homemade cheesecake featuring a spiced walnut and graham cracker crust with a rich, creamy filling. Perfect for chilling overnight for an indulgent dessert experience.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 30 mins | 35 mins | 8 hours | 8-10 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Springform pan
* Mixing bowls
* Electric mixer

---
## Ingredients

### Crust
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 cups | [[Graham Cracker Crumbs]] | Crushed |
| 1/4 cup | [[Walnuts]] | Chopped |
| 1/2 tsp | [[Cinnamon]] | Ground |
| 1/2 cup | [[Butter]] | Melted |

### Filling
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3 | [[Eggs]] | Well beaten |
| 2 (8 oz) pkgs | [[Cream Cheese]] | Room temperature |
| 1 cup | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 3 tsp | [[Vanilla Extract]] | Pure |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Salt]] | Standard |
| 1/2 tsp | [[Almond Extract]] | Pure |
| 3 cups | [[Sour Cream]] | Room temperature |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Crust Preparation
1. Combine the **2 cups graham cracker crumbs**, **1/4 cup chopped walnuts**, **1/2 tsp cinnamon**, and **1/2 cup melted butter** in a bowl.
2. Reserve **3 tablespoons** of the crumb mixture for the topping if not using fruit.
3. Press the remaining mixture firmly into the bottom and up the sides of a **2 1/2-inch springform pan**.

### Step 2: Filling and Baking
4. Combine all filling ingredients except the sour cream: **3 well beaten eggs**, **2 8oz pkgs cream cheese**, **1 cup sugar**, **3 tsp vanilla**, **1/4 tsp salt**, and **1/2 tsp almond extract**; beat until smooth.
5. Gently blend in the **3 cups sour cream** by hand until fully incorporated.
6. Pour the mixture into the prepared **crust** and sprinkle the reserved **crumbs** over the top.
7. Bake at 350°F to 375°F for **30 min** until set and lightly golden.
8. Cool completely, then chill uncovered overnight in the refrigerator until firm.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Chill uncovered in the refrigerator overnight before slicing and serving.
