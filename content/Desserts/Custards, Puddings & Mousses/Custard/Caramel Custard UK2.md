## Instructions
1.  Preheat oven to 325°F (165°C).
2.  In a skillet, melt and caramelize the **1 cup of sugar** until it is a deep amber liquid.
3.  In a saucepan, heat the **1 quart of milk** to scalding (do not boil).
4.  Slowly and carefully add the hot milk to the caramelized sugar, stirring over low heat until all the caramel syrup has dissolved. Let the mixture cool.
5.  In a separate bowl, lightly beat the **2 eggs** with the **1/4 tsp of salt**.
6.  Gradually add the cooled caramel-milk mixture to the beaten eggs, stirring constantly to temper.
7.  Pour the mixture into individual custard cups.
8.  Place the cups in a larger pan and fill the pan with hot water to come halfway up the sides of the cups.
9.  Bake for 1 hour, or until the custards are set.
