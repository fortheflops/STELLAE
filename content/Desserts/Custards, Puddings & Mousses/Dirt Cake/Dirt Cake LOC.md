*A fun, no-bake dessert made with layers of crushed chocolate sandwich cookies and a creamy vanilla pudding mixture, often served in a flower pot to resemble dirt.*
### Author
Lorra Sare
___
## Recipe
| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 1 Package | [[Oreo Cookies]] (or similar chocolate sandwich cookies) |
| 1 Stick (1/2 Cup) | [[Butter]], softened |
| 8 Ounces | [[Cream Cheese]], softened |
| 1 Cup | [[Powdered Sugar]] (Confectioners' Sugar) |
| 3 $\frac{1}{2}$ Cups | [[Milk]] |
| 2 Small Packages | Instant [[Vanilla Pudding]] Mix |
| 12 Ounces | [[Cool Whip]] (or other whipped topping), Thawed |
| Optional | Silk [[Flowers]] (for decoration) |
| Optional | [[Gummy Worms]] (for decoration) |
___
## Instructions
1.  Crush the **Oreo cookies** in a food processor or by hand until fine.
2.  In a large bowl, cream together the **butter**, **cream cheese**, and **powdered sugar** until smooth.
3.  In a separate bowl, mix the **milk** and instant **vanilla pudding mix**. Let it set for a few minutes.
4.  Combine the prepared pudding, the **Cool Whip**, and the cream cheese mixture. Mix well.
5.  Layer the ingredients in an 8" plastic flower pot: start with a layer of crushed Oreos, then a layer of the cream mixture. Repeat, ending with a top layer of crushed Oreos.
6.  Refrigerate for several hours or overnight.
7.  Garnish with silk flowers and/or gummy worms before serving.
