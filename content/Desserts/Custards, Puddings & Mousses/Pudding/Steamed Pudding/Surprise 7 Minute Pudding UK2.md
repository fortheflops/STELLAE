## Instructions
1.  Sift together the **Flour**, **Sugar**, **Soda**, **Spices**, & **Salt**.
2.  Stir in the **Carrots**, **Raisins**, **Fruits**, & **Pecans**.
3.  Add the **Suet** & **Milk**; mix well.
4.  Spoon the mixture into a greased pudding mold.
5.  Steam for 4 hours over simmering water.
6.  Serve with [[Vanilla Sauce UK2| Vanilla Sauce]].
