## Instructions
1.  Preheat oven to 350°F. Sift **flour**, measure and re-sift with **baking powder** and **salt**.
2.  Cream the **oleo/shortening**, add **1 cup of sugar** and cream until fluffy.
3.  Beat in the **egg** and **vanilla**.
4.  Add dry ingredients alternately with **milk**, beginning and ending with dry ingredients.
5.  Pour the batter into a buttered pan. Scatter **cherries** over the batter.
6.  Prepare the sauce by dissolving **1/2 cup of sugar** in **1/2 cup of boiling water**. Pour this hot sauce over the batter and cherries. Do not mix.
7.  Bake for 35 to 45 minutes.
