## Instructions
1.  Combine **semi-sweet chocolate** and two tablespoons of **water** in a heavy pan over low flame.
2.  Stir occasionally until the chocolate is melted.
3.  Remove from heat.
4.  Gradually stir in three **egg yolks**, one at a time.
5.  Add one cube of **butter** (cut into small pieces) to the chocolate mixture and stir until melted and smooth.
6.  Gently fold in three stiffly beaten **egg whites**.
7.  Pour the mousse into a well-buttered mold or individual glasses.
8.  Chill until firm.
9.  Serve with melted vanilla ice cream or whipped cream, if desired.
