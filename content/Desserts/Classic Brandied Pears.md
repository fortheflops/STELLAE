# Classic Brandied Pears

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Ann Seranne | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A timeless home-preserving recipe for tender, sweet pears soaked in a rich, flavorful brandy syrup. This classic preparation is perfect for elegant desserts or thoughtful homemade gifts.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare and Poach
1. Wash, peel, and cut the **[[Pears]]** as desired, then weigh the prepared fruit.
2. In a large pot, combine **1 pound of sugar** and **1 cup of water** for every pound of fruit. Bring to a boil, stirring until the **[[Sugar]]** is completely dissolved.
3. Add the **[[Pears]]** in batches to the boiling syrup. Cook for **10-15 minutes** per batch until the fruit is tender and easily pierced with a straw.

### Step 2: Pack and Infuse
4. Using a slotted spoon, remove the cooked **[[Pears]]** and pack them into hot, sterilized jars.
5. Continue to boil the remaining syrup until it thickens or reaches **222°F** on a candy thermometer.
6. Remove the syrup from heat and let it cool completely. Once cool, stir in **1 pint of brandy** for every pint of syrup.
7. Pour the syrup over the **[[Pears]]** in the jars until overflowing and seal the jars immediately.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store sealed jars in a cool, dark place; allow the flavors to meld for at least two weeks before serving.
