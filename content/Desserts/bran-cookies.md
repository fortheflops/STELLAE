# Bran Cookies

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A delightful and wholesome archival cookie recipe featuring fiber-rich bran and sweet brown sugar. Perfect for a comforting afternoon treat or a nostalgic family snack.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 10 mins | None | 24 cookies |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Baking sheet
* Measuring cups
* Measuring spoons

---
## Ingredients

### Cookie Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 cup | [[Butter]] | Softened |
| 1 cup | [[Brown Sugar]] | Packed |
| 2 tsp | [[Baking Powder]] | None |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] | Beaten |
| 3 cups | [[Flour]] | All-purpose |
| 1 cup | [[Bran]] | See box |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing and Baking
1. Cream together the **1 cup butter** and **1 cup brown sugar** in a large mixing bowl until light and fluffy.
2. Beat in the **2 eggs** until fully incorporated.
3. Stir in the **2 tsp baking powder**, **3 cups flour**, and **1 cup bran**, mixing just until a cohesive dough forms and no dry streaks remain.
4. Drop rounded spoonfuls of dough onto a greased baking sheet.
5. Bake until the edges are golden brown and the centers are set, yielding a comforting, wholesome texture.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The dough can be prepared ahead of time, covered, and refrigerated for up to 48 hours before baking.
