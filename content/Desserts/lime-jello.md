# Lime Jello

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Fay | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic mid-century gelatin salad featuring tangy lime, cream cheese, and crushed pineapple. This refreshing dessert-salad hybrid is whipped to a light, foamy consistency.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 0 mins | 4 hours | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Electric mixer
* Gelatin mold

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 pkg | [[Lime Jello]] | standard size |
| 2 scant cups | [[Water]] | boiling |
| 1 pkg | [[Philadelphia Cream Cheese]] | softened |
| 1 can | [[Crushed Pineapple]] | drained |
| 1/2 cup | [[Whipped Cream]] | lightly sweetened |
| 1/2 cup | [[Salad Dressing]] | Miracle Whip or similar |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation
1. Dissolve the **1 pkg Lime Jello** in **2 scant cups water** and set aside to cool.
2. When the mixture begins to set, use an electric mixer to whip the **Lime Jello** until it becomes light and foamy.
3. Fold in the **1 pkg Philadelphia Cream Cheese**, **1/2 cup whipped cream**, and **1/2 cup salad dressing** until smooth.
4. Gently stir in the **1 can crushed pineapple** and pour into a mold to chill until firm.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This dish must be made at least 4 hours in advance to allow the gelatin to set properly in the refrigerator.
