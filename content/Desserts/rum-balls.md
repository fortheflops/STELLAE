# Rum Balls

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic, no-bake holiday treat made with ground nuts, chocolate, and rum, rolled in powdered sugar. These festive treats keep exceptionally well and develop deeper flavors over time.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 30 mins | None | None | 24 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Grinder or food processor
* Mixing bowl

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 pkg (8 oz.) | [[Chocolate bits]] |  |
| 1 lb. | [[Confectioners' sugar]] |  |
| 1 stick | [[Butter]] | or margarine, softened |
| 1 lb. | [[Almonds]] | or pecans (in shell), shelled |
| 1/3 cup or so | [[Rum]] | to moisten |

---

## Instructions

### Preparation and Mixing
1. Shell the **1 lb. almonds** or pecans yourself.
2. Soften the **1 stick butter** while grinding the **1 pkg (8 oz.) chocolate bits** and shelled **1 lb. almonds** together.
3. Add the **1 lb. confectioners' sugar** and **1/3 cup or so rum** to a consistency to mold into balls until it holds together nicely.

### Rolling and Storing
4. Roll the molded mixture in more powdered sugar and let dry.
5. Store in the fridge where they will keep for a long time; do not let them sit long in the heat!

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** They will keep for a year or so in the fridge, tasting even better as they mature.
