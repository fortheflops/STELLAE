# Pecan Macaroons

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A simple, classic recipe for chewy, flourless Pecan Macaroons. These delightful cookies rely on a stiff meringue base packed with rich brown sugar and toasted pecans.*

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |

| 1 Cup | [[Brown Sugar]] | packed |
| 1 Cup | [[Pecan Nuts]] | finely chopped |
| 1/4 Teaspoon | [[Salt]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Meringue
1. Preheat your oven to 325°F and grease and flour a cookie sheet.
2. In a clean, dry bowl, beat the **1 egg white** with an electric mixer on high speed until very stiff peaks form.
3. Gradually add the **1 cup brown sugar** while continuing to beat until the meringue is stiff and glossy.

### Step 2: Fold and Bake
4. In a small bowl, toss the **1 cup pecan nuts** with the **1/4 teaspoon salt**.
5. Gently fold the nut mixture into the meringue with a spatula.
6. Drop the mixture by rounded teaspoonfuls, well apart, onto the prepared cookie sheet.
7. Bake for about **10 minutes** until the cookies are set on the outside but still chewy inside. Let them cool on the pan for a few minutes before transferring to a wire rack.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 3 days or freeze for up to 1 month.
