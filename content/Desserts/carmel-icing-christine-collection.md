# Carmel Icing

> 📜 **Collection:** Christine Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A rich and traditional cooked caramel icing made with brown sugar, oleo, and powdered sugar. Perfect for topping cakes and cookies with a sweet, spreadable finish.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5 mins | 5 mins | 3 mins | 12 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Saucepan
* Mixing bowl
* Electric mixer

---
## Ingredients

### Icing Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 stick | [[Oleo]] | Margarine or butter |
| 2 tbsp | [[Milk]] | Plus extra if needed |
| 1/2 cup | [[Brown Sugar]] | Packed |
| 1 cup | [[Powdered Sugar]] | Sifted |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Cooking the Base
1. Melt **1/2 stick oleo** in a saucepan, then add **1/2 cup brown sugar**.
2. Boil on low heat for 2 minutes, stirring constantly until smooth.
3. Stir in **2 tbsp milk** and bring back to a boil, stirring constantly.
4. Remove from heat and let cool for 2 or 3 minutes.

### Step 2: Mixing and Beating
5. Add **1 cup pwd sugar** to the warm mixture.
6. Beat until stiff. If the icing is too stiff, add a little extra **milk** until desired spreading consistency is reached.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Best used immediately after beating while warm and spreadable.
