# Layered Lime, Pear, and Cherry Jello Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic two-layer molded Jello salad featuring pear halves and cherries in a clear lime base, topped with a light and fluffy whipped lime and cream cheese mousse. This vintage recipe uses a unique whipping technique to create an airy, decadent texture.*

| 1 can (15 oz) | [[Pears]] | halved and drained |

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Base
1. Prepare the **3 oz Lime Jello** according to package directions and stir in **1 teaspoon oil** until well combined.
2. Pour a thin layer (1/2 to 1 inch) of the liquid **Lime Jello** into a mold and place in the refrigerator.

### Step 2: Assemble Fruit Layer
3. Once the mold layer is thickened but not firm, arrange the **drained pear halves** (cut-side up) and **maraschino cherries** in the mold.
4. Return to the refrigerator until the fruit layer is set completely firm.

### Step 3: Create the Mousse
5. Allow the remaining **Lime Jello** in the bowl to set until very firm.
6. Beat the firm **Lime Jello** with an electric mixer until it becomes light and fluffy.
7. Add the **8 oz softened cream cheese** to the whipped **Lime Jello** and beat until the mixture is smooth and well blended.

### Step 4: Final Set
8. Gently spoon the fluffy **cream cheese mixture** on top of the firm fruit layer in the mold.
9. Refrigerate for at least 6 hours, or preferably overnight, until the entire salad is set and firm to the touch.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This salad must be made at least 6 hours in advance, but is best when chilled overnight to ensure the mousse layer sets properly.
