## Instructions

### Step 1: Whipping the Frosting
1. In the top of a double boiler, combine **2 unbeaten egg whites**, **1 1/2 cups sugar**, **5 tbsp water**, and **1 1/2 tsp white corn syrup**.
2. Beat constantly over rapidly boiling water until the frosting holds its shape and stands in a stiff peak, which should take about **5 to 7 minutes**.

### Step 2: Flavoring and Coloring
3. Remove the frosting from the hot water.
4. Add **1 1/2 tsp mint flavoring** and a **small amount of red food coloring** to tint it a delicate pink.
5. Beat the mixture until it is thick enough to spread easily.

### Step 3: Decorating
6. Separate and tint **3 tbsp of frosting** to a deeper pink shade using a bit more **red food coloring**.
7. Fill and frost your cake, making decorative swirls with the back of a spoon.
8. Fill the center of the swirls with the deeper pink shade of frosting for a beautiful two-tone effect.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This frosting is best used immediately after preparation while still warm and pliable. It will set as it cools, so do not make it ahead of time.
