# Date Nut Bars

> 📜 **Collection:** Jill Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *These chewy, moist date and nut bars offer a rich, brownie-like texture perfect for any occasion. A simple, classic recipe that highlights the natural sweetness of dates paired with crunchy nuts.*

---

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 30 mins | None | 16–24 bars |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 8x8 or 9x9 inch square baking pan
* Large mixing bowl
* Small mixing bowl
* Wire rack

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |

| 1/2 cup | [[Butter]] | melted |
| 1 cup | [[Sugar]] | granulated |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] | large |
| 3/4 cup | [[All-Purpose Flour]] | sifted |
| 1/2 teaspoon | [[Baking Powder]] | sifted |
| 1/8 teaspoon | [[Salt]] | sifted |
| 1 cup | [[Nuts]] | chopped walnuts or pecans |
| 1 cup | [[Dates]] | chopped |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation
1. Preheat your oven to 350°F. Grease and flour an 8x8 or 9x9 inch square baking pan.

### Step 2: Mixing
2. In a small bowl, sift together the **3/4 cup all-purpose flour**, **1/2 teaspoon baking powder**, and **1/8 teaspoon salt**.
3. In a larger bowl, beat the **1/2 cup butter**, **1 cup sugar**, and **2 eggs** together until well combined.
4. Add the sifted dry ingredients to the wet mixture and stir until just incorporated.
5. Fold in the **1 cup chopped nuts** and **1 cup chopped dates**.

### Step 3: Baking and Cooling
6. Spread the batter evenly in the prepared pan.
7. Bake for about **30 minutes**, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean and the top is set.
8. Let the pan cool completely on a wire rack before cutting into bars.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 3 days, or refrigerate for up to a week. The bars remain moist due to the dates.
