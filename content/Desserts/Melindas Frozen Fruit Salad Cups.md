# Melinda's Frozen Fruit Salad Cups

> 📜 **Collection:** UK15 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Melinda | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A delightful frozen fruit salad featuring a medley of strawberries, pineapple, apricots, and bananas held together by a sweet marshmallow syrup. These individual portions are perfect for easy serving and refreshing treats.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 5 mins | 6-8 hours | 24 individual servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* medium saucepan
* large mixing bowl
* two 12-cup muffin tins
* cupcake liners

---
## Ingredients

### Syrup Base
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 1/2 Cups | [[Sugar]] | granulated |
| 1 Cup | [[Water]] | room temperature |
| 10 large | [[Marshmallows]] | standard size |

### Fruit
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 packages (10 oz each) | [[Frozen Strawberries]] | thawed with juice |
| 1 large can (20 oz) | [[Crushed Pineapple]] | with juice |
| 1 large can (15 oz) | [[Apricots]] | diced and drained |
| 4 | [[Bananas]] | ripe, sliced |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare Syrup and Fruit
1. Line two 12-cup muffin tins with cupcake liners.
2. In a medium saucepan, combine **1 1/2 cups sugar** and **1 cup water**. Bring to a boil, stirring until the **sugar** is completely dissolved.
3. Add **10 large marshmallows** to the hot syrup and stir until completely melted and smooth. Remove from heat and let cool slightly.
4. In a large bowl, combine the **2 packages frozen strawberries**, **1 large can crushed pineapple**, **1 large can diced apricots**, and **4 sliced bananas**.

### Step 2: Assemble and Freeze
5. Pour the slightly cooled **marshmallow syrup** over the fruit mixture and stir gently to combine.
6. Spoon the mixture evenly into the prepared cupcake liners.
7. Freeze for at least 6-8 hours, or overnight, until the cups are completely firm and set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** These can be kept in the freezer for up to 2 weeks if stored in an airtight container or wrapped individually.
