# Marshmallow Whipped Cream Dessert

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A delightful chilled dessert featuring a fluffy marshmallow custard filling set inside a buttery graham cracker crust. Topped with grated chocolate and pecans for an elegant finish.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 30 mins | 15 mins | 2 hours | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Double boiler
* Mixing bowl
* Pie plate

---
## Ingredients

### Filling
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 24 | [[Marshmallows]] | Melted |
| 1/2 cup | [[Milk]] | None |
| 2 whole | [[Eggs]] | Beaten |
| To taste | [[Vanilla extract]] | None |
| 1 cup | [[Cream]] | Whipped |
| 1 ounce | [[Baking chocolate]] | Grated or shaved |
| As needed | [[Pecans]] | Chopped and halves for decoration |

### Graham Cracker Crust
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 1/2 cups | [[Graham crackers]] | Crushed |
| 1/3 cup | [[Butter]] | Melted |
| 1/4 cup | [[Sugar]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Make Crust
1. Combine the **1 1/2 cups crushed graham crackers**, **1/3 cup melted butter**, and **1/4 cup sugar**, then press firmly into a pie plate and set aside.

### Step 2: Prepare Custard
2. Melt the **24 marshmallows** in **1/2 cup milk** using a double boiler.
3. Add the **2 whole beaten eggs** and cook until the mixture reaches the consistency of custard.
4. Stir in **vanilla to taste** and set aside to cool completely.

### Step 3: Assemble and Chill
5. Whip the **1 cup cream** and gently fold it into the cooled custard mixture.
6. Pour the mixture into the prepared graham cracker crust.
7. Grate or shave **1 ounce baking chocolate** over the top, sprinkle with **chopped pecans**, and decorate with **pecan halves**.
8. Chill in the refrigerator until set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Can be prepared up to 24 hours in advance and kept refrigerated until ready to serve.
