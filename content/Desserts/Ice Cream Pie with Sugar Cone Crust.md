# Ice Cream Pie with Sugar Cone Crust

> 📜 **Collection:** Jill Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A frozen dessert featuring a unique, crunchy crust made from sugar cones, layered with softened ice cream and a simple Hershey bar chocolate sauce.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 10 mins | Several hours | One 9 or 10-inch pie |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 9 or 10-inch pie pan
* Mixing bowl
* Small saucepan
* Oven

---
## Ingredients

### Sugar Cone Crust
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 box (approx. 12) | [[Sugar Cones]] | Crushed into fine crumbs |
| 6 tbsp (3/4 stick) | [[Butter]] | Melted |
| 1/2 teaspoon | [[Sugar]] | None |
| 1/2 teaspoon | [[Cinnamon]] | None |

### Filling
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3 pints | [[Ice Cream]] | Flavor of choice, softened slightly |
| 7 ounces | [[Hershey's Milk Chocolate Bar]] | None |
| 1/4 cup | [[Milk]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Make the Crust
1. Preheat oven to **375°F**.
2. Crush the **1 box sugar cones** into fine crumbs.
3. In a bowl, combine the crumbs, **6 tbsp butter**, **1/2 teaspoon sugar**, and **1/2 teaspoon cinnamon**.
4. Press the mixture firmly into the bottom and up the sides of a 9 or 10-inch pie pan.
5. Bake for **10 minutes** until set, then let cool completely.

### Step 2: Assemble and Freeze
6. Allow the **3 pints ice cream** to soften slightly at room temperature.
7. In a small saucepan, melt the **7 ounces Hershey's milk chocolate bar** with the **1/4 cup milk** over low heat until smooth and let cool to room temperature.
8. Spread half of the softened ice cream in the cooled crust.
9. Drizzle a layer of the cooled chocolate sauce over the top.
10. Spread the remaining ice cream over the sauce.
11. Freeze for several hours until completely firm.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the pie tightly wrapped in the freezer for up to 1 week before serving for best texture.
