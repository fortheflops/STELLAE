## Author
Evelyn Sue Zlotkin
___
## Recipe
| Measurements      | Ingredients                   |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 6 medium (3 lbs)  | [[Sweet Potatoes]]                |
| 2 cups            | [[Applesauce]]                  |
| 3/4 teaspoon      | [[Cinnamon]]                    |
| 3/4 cup           | [[Honey]]                       |
| 3 tablespoons     | Melted [[Chicken Fat]] or pareve [[Margarine]] |
___
## Instructions
1.  Preheat oven to 350°F. Grease a 2-quart baking dish.
2.  Cook the whole sweet potatoes in boiling water until tender. Allow them to cool enough to handle, then peel and slice them.
3.  In a small bowl, mix the applesauce and cinnamon together.
4.  In the prepared baking dish, create alternating layers of sliced sweet potatoes and the spiced applesauce.
5.  Drizzle each layer with some of the honey and the melted chicken fat or margarine.
6.  Bake uncovered for 45 minutes, basting occasionally with the pan juices.
___

>[!Note]
>The recipe specifies using "pareve margarine," indicating it is a kosher recipe suitable for serving with a meat meal. The dish serves 6-8.
