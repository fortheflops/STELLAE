# Dainty Tea Cakes

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *Delicate and crumbly tea cakes flavored with bitter almond extract. These traditional cookies are made by creaming butter and sugar and gradually incorporating flour.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 15 mins | None | 24 cookies |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Electric mixer
* Cookie press
* Baking sheet

---
## Ingredients

### Dough Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 cup | [[Butter]] | Softened |
| 2/3 cup | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 3 | [[Egg yolks]] | Separated |
| 1 tsp | [[Bitter almond extract]] | Flavoring |
| 2 1/2 cups | [[Flour]] | All-purpose |

---

## Instructions

### Mixing and Baking
1. Cream **1 cup butter** and **2/3 cup sugar** until waxy.
2. Beat **3 egg yolks** until light, then add to the butter mixture along with **1 tsp bitter almond extract**.
3. Add **2 1/2 cups flour** 1 tablespoon at a time until all has been taken up.
4. Put into cookie press and press onto baking sheets.
5. Bake in a hot oven until light brown and fragrant.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Dough can be prepared and loaded into the cookie press a few hours in advance, or stored in an airtight container for up to 3 days.
