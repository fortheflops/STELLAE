# Chocolate Rum Balls

> 📜 **Collection:** Louise Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic, no-bake recipe for rich and potent chocolate rum balls. This simple mixture combines vanilla wafer crumbs, cocoa, nuts, and your choice of spirits, and gets even better as it ages.*

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| ½ Pound (~2 ½ cups) | [[Vanilla Wafers]] | ground |
| 1 Cup | [[Nuts]] | chopped |
| 2 Tablespoons | [[Cocoa]] Powder | None |
| ½ Cup | Light [[Corn Syrup]] | None |
| ¼ Cup | [[Rum]], [[Bourbon]], or [[Whiskey]] | None |
| As Needed | [[Powdered Sugar]] | for rolling |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare and Mix
1. Grind or finely crush the **½ pound vanilla wafers** until they form fine crumbs. Finely chop the **1 cup nuts**.
2. In a large bowl, combine the ground **½ pound vanilla wafers**, chopped **1 cup nuts**, and **2 tablespoons cocoa powder**.
3. Pour in the **½ cup light corn syrup** and your choice of **¼ cup rum, bourbon, or whiskey**.
4. Mix with a sturdy spoon until a thick, sticky paste forms and holds together.

### Step 2: Shape and Coat
5. Roll the mixture into small, **1-inch balls**.
6. Roll each ball in **powdered sugar** to coat completely and evenly.
7. Store in a tightly covered container. For best flavor, allow the rum balls to age for at least a day or two; they taste best in a week or so as the flavors meld.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in a tightly covered container. These no-bake treats improve significantly as they age, tasting best after a week of storage.
