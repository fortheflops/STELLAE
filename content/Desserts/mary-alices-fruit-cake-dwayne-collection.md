# Mary Alice's Fruit Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Mary Alice | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A rich, traditional holiday fruit cake packed with candied cherries, pineapple, and pecans, flavored with lemon extract. This heirloom recipe can be baked in one or two pans and wrapped in rum-soaked cloth for preservation.*

## Ingredients

### Cake Batter
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 lb | [[Butter]] | softened |
| 1 lb | [[Brown Sugar]] | packed |
| 6 | [[Eggs]] | room temperature |
| 3 1/2 cups | [[Flour]] | regular, divided |
| 2 tsp | [[Baking Powder]] | None |
| 1/2 lb | [[Candied Cherries]] | None |
| 1/2 lb | [[Pineapple]] | candied, or citron |
| 1 lb | [[Pecans]] | None |
| 1 (2 oz) bottle | [[Lemon Extract]] | None |
| As needed | [[Rum]] | or wine, for wrapping (optional) |

---

## Instructions

### Preparation & Baking
1. Preheat oven to **250°F**.
2. Roll the **1/2 lb candied cherries**, **1/2 lb pineapple (or citron)**, and **1 lb pecans** in a portion of the **3 1/2 cups regular flour** until well coated.
3. Cream the **1 lb butter** and **1 lb brown sugar** together until light and fluffy. Beat in the **6 eggs** one at a time. Sift the remaining **3 1/2 cups regular flour** with the **2 tsp baking powder**, then combine with the wet ingredients.
4. Stir in the **1 (2 oz) bottle lemon extract** and fold in the floured fruits and nuts.
5. Pour the batter into prepared cake pan(s).
6. If using 2 cake pans, bake at **250°F** for **2 hours**. If using 1 cake pan, bake at **250°F** for **2 1/2 hours**.
7. Try baking for **1 hour** first to check and see if the cake is drying out too fast. The cake is done when a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.
8. Once cooled, wrap the cake in a clean cloth saturated with **rum** or wine, if desired, to age and keep moist.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Wrap the cooled fruit cake in a rum- or wine-saturated cloth and store in an airtight container in a cool, dark place to age.
