# Surprise 7 Minute Pudding

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A traditional, rich steamed pudding featuring a surprise blend of grated carrots, candied fruits, and pecans. Despite the quick assembly time, this classic dessert is steamed for four hours to achieve a moist, flavorful texture.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and Mixing
1. Generously grease a pudding mold or heatproof bowl.
2. In a large bowl, sift together the **2 cups flour**, **1 1/2 cups sugar**, **1 tsp baking soda**, **1 tsp allspice**, **1 tsp nutmeg**, **1 tsp cinnamon**, **1 tsp cloves**, and **1 tsp salt**.
3. Stir in the **1 1/2 cups grated carrots**, **1 cup raisins**, **1/2 cup candied pineapple**, **1/2 cup candied cherries**, and **1 cup whole pecans**.
4. Add the **1/2 cup ground kidney suet** and **2 cups milk**, mixing until the batter is thoroughly combined.

### Step 2: Steaming
5. Spoon the batter into the prepared mold, filling it two-thirds full to allow for expansion.
6. Cover the mold tightly with a lid or double layer of greased aluminum foil, securing with string.
7. Place the mold on a rack in a large pot with simmering water reaching halfway up the sides. Steam for **4 hours**, checking the water level periodically until the pudding is firm and fragrant.
8. Carefully remove from the pot, let rest for a few minutes, and unmold. Serve warm with [[Vanilla Sauce]].

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This pudding can be made in advance and reheated by steaming for an additional 30-45 minutes before serving.
