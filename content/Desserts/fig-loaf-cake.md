# Fig Loaf Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A delightful spiced fig loaf cake packed with chopped figs, raisins, and aromatic spices. Bake slowly until golden and fragrant for a classic homemade treat.*

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing the Batter
1. Cream **1 cup butter** with **2 cups brown sugar** until smooth and fluffy.
2. Add **4 eggs** (well beaten) to the mixture and beat well.
3. Stir in **1 tsp cinnamon**, **1 tsp nutmeg**, **1/2 tsp cloves**, and **1 cup water**.
4. Add **3 cups flour** and **2 tsp baking powder**, mixing until just combined.
5. Fold in **1/2 lb figs** (finely cut) and **2 cups raisins** (chopped) until evenly distributed.

### Step 2: Baking
6. Pour the batter into a greased loaf pan.
7. Bake slowly at a low temperature for about **2 hours**, or until a tester inserted into the center comes out clean and the cake is deeply golden.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store tightly wrapped in plastic wrap at room temperature for up to 3 days, or slice and freeze for longer storage.
