## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing and Baking
1. Sift the dry **2 cups flour**, **2 cups sugar**, **1 1/2 teaspoons baking soda**, **2 teaspoons cinnamon**, and **Dash nutmeg** together in a bowl.
2. Add **1 1/3 cups oil** and **4 eggs** one at a time, mixing well.
3. Fold in **3 cups grated raw carrots** and mix thoroughly.
4. Bake at 375 degrees in layer pans for **25 to 35 minutes** until golden brown and a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.
5. Allow to cool completely, then frost.

### Step 2: Preparing the Frosting
6. Combine **Two 3-ounce cream cheese packages**, **3/4 stick butter**, **3/4 box powdered sugar**, **3/4 cup chopped nuts**, and **1 teaspoon vanilla** until smooth and spreadable.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the frosted cake in the refrigerator for up to 3 days. Bring to room temperature before serving for the best texture.
