# Candied Walnuts

> 📜 **Collection:** Patsy Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A quick stovetop method for making classic candied walnuts with a simple butter, brown sugar, and cinnamon glaze. These toasted nuts are perfect for snacking or as a crunchy topping for salads and desserts.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5 mins | 5 mins | 1 hour | 2 cups |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* large skillet
* slotted spoon
* baking sheet
* parchment paper

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 Tbsp | [[Butter]] | unsalted |
| 1/2 cup | [[Brown Sugar]] | packed |
| 2 tsp | [[Cinnamon]] | ground |
| 2 Cups | [[Walnuts]] | halves |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare and Glaze
1. Line a baking sheet with parchment paper or wax paper.
2. In a large skillet, melt the **2 Tbsp butter** over medium heat.
3. Add the **1/2 cup brown sugar** and **2 tsp cinnamon**, stirring until the sugar begins to dissolve.

### Step 2: Toast and Cool
4. Add the **2 cups walnuts** to the pan and stir continuously for 3-4 minutes, until the nuts are well-coated and fragrant.
5. Using a slotted spoon, quickly remove the **walnuts** from the pan and spread them in a single layer on the prepared baking sheet.
6. Allow them to cool for at least 1 hour until the sugar coating is hard and crisp. Break apart any clusters that may have formed.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 2 weeks, or freeze for up to 3 months.
