# Classic Seven-Minute Frosting

> 📜 **Collection:** General Archive | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic, light, and glossy boiled icing that creates a stable, marshmallow-like meringue topping. This recipe is perfect for finishing two-layer cakes with a professional, fluffy finish.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5 mins | 10 mins | None | Frosting for a two-layer, 8-inch cake |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* small heavy-bottomed saucepan
* candy thermometer
* electric mixer
* mixing bowl

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 Cup | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 1/3 Cup | [[Water]] | Room temperature |
| 1 tsp. | [[Vinegar]] | White distilled |
| 2 | [[Egg Whites]] | Room temperature |
| 3/4 tsp. | [[Vanilla]] | Pure extract |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Syrup
1. In a small saucepan, combine the **1 cup sugar**, **1/3 cup water**, and **1 tsp. vinegar**.
2. Bring to a boil over medium heat, stirring until dissolved, then boil without stirring until the syrup reaches the soft-ball stage (**235-245°F**), or until it spins a long thread when dropped from a spoon.

### Step 2: Whip and Combine
3. While the syrup boils, beat the **2 egg whites** in a clean bowl with an electric mixer until stiff peaks form.
4. With the mixer on high, very slowly and carefully pour the hot syrup in a thin, steady stream into the **egg whites**.
5. Continue beating constantly until the frosting is thick, glossy, and holds its shape.
6. Beat in the **3/4 tsp. vanilla** and spread onto a cooled cake immediately.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This frosting is best used immediately after preparation as it sets quickly; it does not store well for long periods.
