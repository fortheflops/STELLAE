# Millie's Fudgy Brownies With Mocha Frosting

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Millie | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic, fudgy brownie recipe featuring a rich cocoa base and a decadent mocha frosting made with hot coffee. These brownies offer a perfect balance of deep chocolate flavor and smooth, caffeinated sweetness.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 30 mins | None | 24 brownies |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 9x13 inch pan
* Electric mixer
* Large mixing bowl
* Medium mixing bowl
* Toothpick

---
## Ingredients

### For the Brownie
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 cup | [[Oleo]] | softened butter or margarine |
| 1 1/2 cups | [[Sugar]] | granulated |
| 4 | [[Eggs]] | large |
| 1/2 cup | [[Cocoa]] | unsweetened |
| 1 cup | [[Flour]] | all-purpose |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Vanilla]] | extract |
| 1 cup | [[Nuts]] | chopped |

### For the Frosting
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 cup | [[Oleo]] | softened butter or margarine |
| 1/4 cup | [[Cocoa]] | unsweetened |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Vanilla]] | extract |
| 1 pound | [[Powdered Sugar]] | sifted |
| 3-4 tablespoons | [[Coffee]] | hot, as needed |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Bake the Brownies
1. Preheat your oven to 350°F and lightly grease a 9x13 inch pan.
2. In a large bowl, cream the **1 cup oleo** and **1 1/2 cups sugar** together with an electric mixer until light and fluffy.
3. Add the **4 eggs** one at a time, beating well after each addition.
4. Beat in the **1/2 cup cocoa**, **1 cup flour**, and **1 teaspoon vanilla** until just combined.
5. By hand, stir in the **1 cup nuts**.
6. Pour the batter into the prepared pan and bake for **30 minutes**, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out with moist, fudgy crumbs. Let the brownies cool completely.

### Step 2: Prepare and Apply Frosting
7. In a medium bowl, cream the **1/2 cup oleo**.
8. Beat in the **1/4 cup cocoa** and **1 teaspoon vanilla**.
9. Gradually add the **1 pound powdered sugar**, alternating with just enough **3-4 tablespoons hot coffee** to reach a smooth, spreadable consistency.
10. Spread the frosting over the cooled brownies until smooth and glossy.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store frosted brownies in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 3 days or refrigerate for up to 1 week.
