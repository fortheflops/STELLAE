# Lemon Raisin Eggless Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A charming old-fashioned eggless cake featuring a tender, scone-like crumb. This recipe utilizes the classic rubbing-in method for a delicate texture infused with bright lemon and sweet raisins.*

| 15 mins | 45 mins | None | 1 8-inch cake |

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation
1. Preheat your oven to **320°F**. Grease and flour an **8-inch round or square cake pan**.

### Step 2: Mixing the Base
2. In a medium bowl, sift together the **1 1/3 cups flour**, **2 tsps baking powder**, and **1 pinch salt**.
3. Add the **5 Tbsps cold butter** to the flour. Using your fingertips, rub the butter into the flour until the mixture resembles coarse breadcrumbs.
4. Stir in the **1/2 cup sugar**, **1/2 cup raisins**, **1/2 grated lemon rind**, and **a few drops lemon extract**.

### Step 3: Final Batter and Baking
5. Make a well in the center of the mixture and pour in the **1/2 cup milk**. Mix with a fork until you have a smooth, moist batter.
6. Pour the batter into the prepared pan and spread evenly.
7. Bake for **45 minutes**, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean and the top is lightly golden.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 3 days or freeze for up to 1 month.
