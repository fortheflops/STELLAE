# Marjorie's Welsh Cookies

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Marjorie | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A delightful traditional recipe for Welsh Cookies from Marjorie. These lightly sweet treats are fried on a dry pan and packed with raisins, dates, and nuts.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 25 mins | 20 mins | None | 5 dozen |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Rolling pin
* Cookie cutter
* Dry frying pan

| 1 1/2 cups | [[Raisins]] | None |

| 1/2 cup | [[Nuts]] | Chopped |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] | Beaten |
| 1 tsp | [[Nutmeg]] | Ground |

---

## Instructions

### Mixing and Rolling
1. Mix **4 cups flour**, **1/2 lb butter**, **1 cup sugar**, **1 tsp salt**, **1 tsp soda**, **1/4 cup buttermilk**, **1 1/2 cups raisins**, **1 cup chopped dates**, **1/2 cup chopped nuts**, **2 eggs**, and **1 tsp nutmeg** all at once.
2. Roll out the dough like pie crust to desired thickness.
3. Cut with a cookie cutter.

### Cooking
4. Fry in a dry frying pan until golden brown and cooked through.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The dough can be prepared, wrapped tightly, and refrigerated for up to 24 hours before rolling and frying.
