# Butterscotch Nut Pie (Meringue Pie)

> 📜 **Collection:** General Archive | ✍️ **Attribution:** Svarale | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A unique and chewy crustless pie featuring a sweet meringue base baked with graham cracker crumbs and pecans, then topped with whipped cream and chilled for hours to set.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 35 mins | 6 hours | One 9-inch pie |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 9-inch pie pan
* Electric mixer
* Large bowl
* Wire rack

---
## Ingredients

### Pie Base
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3 | [[Egg Whites]] | at room temperature |
| 3/4 Cup | [[Sugar]] | granulated |
| 3/4 Cup | [[Pecans]] | chopped |
| 3/4 Cup | [[Graham Cracker Crumbs]] | fine crumbs |
| 1 Teaspoon | [[Vanilla]] | extract |

### Topping
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 pint (2 cups) | [[Heavy Cream]] | whipped |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare and Bake the Base
1. Preheat oven to **325°F (165°C)**. Generously grease a **9-inch pie pan**.
2. In a clean, dry bowl, beat the **3 egg whites** with an electric mixer until stiff, glossy peaks form.
3. In a separate bowl, combine the **3/4 cup sugar**, **3/4 cup chopped pecans**, **3/4 cup graham cracker crumbs**, and **1 teaspoon vanilla**.
4. Gently fold the dry ingredients into the stiffly beaten **3 egg whites** until just combined without deflating.
5. Spread the mixture evenly into the greased **9-inch pie pan**.
6. Bake for **35 minutes** until set and lightly golden, then let the pie cool completely on a wire rack.

### Step 2: Topping and Chilling
7. Once the pie is completely cool, whip the **1 pint (2 cups) heavy cream** to stiff peaks.
8. Spread the whipped **heavy cream** evenly over the top of the pie.
9. Cover and refrigerate for at least **6 hours**, or overnight, until the pie develops its signature chewy texture and is ready to slice.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Cover and refrigerate for at least 6 hours or overnight before serving to allow the pie to set and develop its chewy texture.
