# Grape Nut Pudding

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A delightful and unique heritage dessert featuring lemon gelatin, crunchy grape nuts, walnuts, raisins, and prunes with warm spices. This comforting archived recipe offers a wonderful blend of textures and nostalgic flavors.*

## Ingredients

### Pudding Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 pkg | [[Lemon Jello]] | Gelatin dessert |
| 1 3/4 C. | [[Boiling Water]] | Hot |
| 3/4 C. | [[Grape-Nuts]] | Cereal |
| 3/4 C. | [[Raisins]] | Plumped |
| 3/4 C. | [[Walnut Meats]] | Chopped |
| 3/4 C. | [[Stoned Prunes]] | Cooked and chopped |
| 1/4 C. | [[Citron]] | Diced |
| 1/2 tsp. | [[Cinnamon]] | Ground |
| 1/4 tsp. | [[Cloves]] | Ground |
| 1 pinch | [[Salt]] | Optional |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and Setting
1. Dissolve the **1 pkg lemon jello** in **1 3/4 C. boiling water** in a large mixing bowl, stirring until completely dissolved.
2. Stir in the **3/4 C. grape-nuts**, **3/4 C. raisins**, **3/4 C. walnut meats**, **3/4 C. stoned prunes**, **1/4 C. citron**, **1/2 tsp. cinnamon**, **1/4 tsp. cloves**, and a pinch of **salt**.
3. Pour the mixture into a mold or serving dish and chill in the refrigerator until fully set and firm to the touch.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Can be prepared up to 2 days in advance and kept covered in the refrigerator until chilled and set.
