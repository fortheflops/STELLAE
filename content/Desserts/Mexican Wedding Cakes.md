# Mexican Wedding Cakes

> 📜 **Collection:** Jill Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic, buttery, and nutty shortbread cookie, rolled twice in powdered sugar for a melt-in-your-mouth finish. Also known as Russian Tea Cakes or Snowballs.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 15 mins | None | 2-3 dozen cookies |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* large bowl
* medium bowl
* wire rack
* cookie sheet

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 cup (1 stick) | [[Butter]] or [[Margarine]] | softened |
| 2 tablespoons | [[Granulated Sugar]] | none |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Vanilla Extract]] | none |
| 1 cup | [[All-Purpose Flour]] | none |
| 1/8 teaspoon | [[Salt]] | none |
| 1 cup | [[Nuts]] | chopped, pecans preferred |
| As needed | [[Confectioners Sugar]] | for coating |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Dough
1. Preheat the oven to 350°F.
2. In a medium bowl, cream the **1/2 cup (1 stick) butter or margarine** and **2 tablespoons granulated sugar** together until light and fluffy.
3. Beat in the **1 teaspoon vanilla extract**.
4. In a separate bowl, whisk together the **1 cup all-purpose flour** and **1/8 teaspoon salt**.
5. Gradually add the flour mixture to the creamed butter, mixing until just combined, then stir in the **1 cup chopped nuts**.

### Step 2: Bake and Coat
6. Shape the dough into 1-inch balls and place them on an ungreased cookie sheet.
7. Bake for **12-15 minutes**, until the bottoms are lightly golden but the tops remain pale.
8. Let the cookies cool for a few minutes until just cool enough to handle. While still warm, roll the cookies in **confectioners sugar**.
9. Let the cookies cool completely on a wire rack, then roll them in **confectioners sugar** a second time for a thick, snowy coating.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 1 week or freeze for up to 3 months.
