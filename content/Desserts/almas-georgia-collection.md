# Almas

> 📜 **Collection:** Georgia Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A traditional recipe for Almas cookies, featuring a delicate lemon-vanilla flavor profile. These rolled cookies are perfect for holiday baking and gatherings.*

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

### 🔪 Key Equipment

## Ingredients

### Wet Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3/4 cup | [[Butter]] or [[Margarine]] | Softened |
| 1 cup | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] | Large |
| 1/2 tsp | [[Flavoring]] | Lemon or Vanilla extract |

### Dry Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 1/2 cups | [[Flour]] | Sifted |
| 1 tsp | [[Baking Powder]] | Sifted together with flour |
| 1 tsp | [[Salt]] | Sifted together with flour |

---

## Instructions

### Mixing and Chilling
1. In a mixing bowl, blend the **3/4 cup butter or margarine**, **1 cup sugar**, **2 eggs**, and **1/2 tsp flavoring** together.
2. Add the sifted **2 1/2 cups flour**, **1 tsp baking powder**, and **1 tsp salt** to the mixture and blend until combined.
3. Chill the dough for at least 1 hour to ensure it is firm enough to roll.

### Rolling and Baking
4. Preheat oven to 400°F.
5. Roll the dough out to 1/8 inch thickness on a lightly floured surface.
6. Cut into desired shapes and place on an ungreased cookie sheet.
7. Bake for 6-8 minutes until the edges are lightly golden and set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The dough can be prepared and kept in the refrigerator for up to 2 days before rolling and baking.
