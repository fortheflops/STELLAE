# Fantasy Fudge

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic, no-fail chocolate fudge recipe featuring a rich blend of marshmallow fluff and evaporated milk. This foolproof method creates a smooth, glossy texture perfect for gifting or holiday gatherings.*

---

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 5 mins | 4 hours | Approx. 3 pounds |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 9x13 inch pan
* Large heavy-bottomed pot
* Large heatproof bowl
* Wooden spoon or spatula
* Foil

| 1 large can (12 oz) | [[Evaporated Milk]] | Standard |
| 2 small packages (12 oz total) | [[Chocolate Chips]] | Semi-sweet |
| 1 (7 oz) jar | [[Marshmallow Fluff]] | Standard |
| 1 cup | [[Nuts]] | Chopped |
| 1 tablespoon | [[Vanilla Extract]] | Pure |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation
1. Line a 9x13 inch pan with foil, leaving an overhang on the sides, and butter the foil.
2. In a large, heatproof bowl, combine the **12 oz chocolate chips**, **7 oz marshmallow fluff**, **1 cup nuts**, and **1 tablespoon vanilla extract**. Set aside.

### Step 2: Cook the Syrup
3. In a large, heavy-bottomed pot, combine the **4 1/2 cups sugar**, **1/2 cup butter**, and **12 oz evaporated milk**.
4. Bring the mixture to a hard, rolling boil over medium heat, stirring constantly.
5. Once boiling, continue to boil for **exactly 5 minutes**, stirring the entire time to prevent scorching.

### Step 3: Combine and Set
6. Immediately remove the pot from the heat and pour the hot syrup over the **chocolate chip mixture** in the bowl.
7. Stir vigorously with a wooden spoon or spatula until the **chocolate chips** are completely melted and the fudge is smooth and glossy.
8. Pour the fudge into the prepared pan and spread it evenly. Let it cool completely at room temperature or in the refrigerator for at least 4 hours, or until firm.
9. Once firm, use the foil handles to lift the fudge out of the pan and cut it into small squares.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 2 weeks or refrigerate for up to 1 month.
