# Lemon Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Seven | ✍️ **Attribution:** Grandma Beall | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A delightful and bright citrus cake recipe featuring a moist lemon gelatin base topped with a tart lemon or orange glaze. Passed down from Grandma Beall's kitchen, it promises a burst of sunshine in every slice.*

---

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 45 mins | None | 8 servings |

## Ingredients

### Cake
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |

| 1 pkg | [[Lemon jello]] | Dry gelatin |
| 1/2 c. | [[Oil]] | Vegetable oil |
| 1 c. | [[Cold water]] | Chilled |
| 3 | [[Eggs]] | Added one at a time |

### Lemon Icing
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 c. | [[Powdered sugar]] | Sifted |
| 1/2 c. | [[Lemon juice]] | Or orange juice |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Batter Preparation
1. Mix **1 pkg lemon jello** with **1 c. cold water**, **1 pkg lemon cake mix**, and **1/2 c. oil**.
2. Add **3 eggs** one at a time, mixing thoroughly until a smooth batter forms.

### Step 2: Baking
3. Bake at **350°** for **45 min**, or until a golden crust forms and a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.

### Step 3: Icing
4. Mix **2 c. powdered sugar** and **1/2 c. lemon juice** (or orange juice) together to form a smooth glaze.
5. Pour the glaze over the **cake** while the **cake** is hot so it absorbs the citrus flavor.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The cake can be baked a day ahead, but for best results, apply the warm glaze immediately after baking while the cake is hot.
