# Dirt Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** LOC | ✍️ **Attribution:** Lorra Sare | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A fun and nostalgic no-bake dessert featuring layers of crushed chocolate sandwich cookies and a creamy vanilla pudding mixture, famously served in a flower pot to look like real dirt.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 0 mins | 4 hours | 10-12 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* food processor
* rolling pin
* large mixing bowl
* electric mixer
* 8-inch plastic flower pot

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 Package | [[Oreo Cookies]] | or similar chocolate sandwich cookies |
| 1 Stick (1/2 Cup) | [[Butter]] | softened |
| 8 Ounces | [[Cream Cheese]] | softened |
| 1 Cup | [[Powdered Sugar]] | confectioners' sugar |
| 3 1/2 Cups | [[Milk]] | cold |
| 2 Small Packages | [[Vanilla Pudding]] | instant mix |
| 12 Ounces | [[Cool Whip]] | thawed |
| Optional | [[Flowers]] | silk, for decoration |
| Optional | [[Gummy Worms]] | for decoration |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Base
1. Crush the **1 package Oreo cookies** in a food processor or by hand until they resemble fine crumbs.

### Step 2: Cream the Filling
2. In a large bowl, cream together the **1 stick butter**, **8 ounces cream cheese**, and **1 cup powdered sugar** until the mixture is smooth and fluffy.

### Step 3: Prepare the Pudding
3. In a separate bowl, whisk the **3 1/2 cups milk** and **2 small packages vanilla pudding mix** for 2 minutes; let stand for 5 minutes until set.

### Step 4: Combine and Assemble
4. Fold the **12 ounces Cool Whip** into the set pudding, then combine with the cream cheese mixture until uniform.
5. In an 8-inch plastic flower pot, alternate layers of crushed **Oreo cookies** and the cream mixture, ending with a final layer of cookies on top to resemble dirt.
6. Refrigerate for at least 4 hours or overnight until firm and set.
7. Garnish with **silk flowers** and **gummy worms** just before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This dessert is best made at least 4 hours in advance to allow the layers to set properly; it can be stored in the refrigerator for up to 3 days.
