# Raisin Bars

## Ingredients

### Filling & Base
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |

| 2 tbsp | [[Shortening]] | Softened |
| 1 | [[Egg]] | Beaten |
| 1 tsp | [[Baking Soda]] | None |
| 1 tsp | [[Nutmeg]] | Ground |
| 2 tsp | [[Cinnamon]] | Ground |
| 1/2 tsp | [[Salt]] | None |
| 1/2 tsp | [[Vanilla Extract]] | Liquid |
| 2 cups | [[Flour]] | All-purpose |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Simmering Raisins
1. Combine **1 cup raisins** and **2 cups water** in a saucepan. Simmer for 10 minutes.
2. Ensure you have **1 cup liquid** left; add more water if necessary.

### Step 2: Mixing the Batter
3. Cream together **1 cup sugar** and **2 tbsp shortening**, then add **1 egg**.
4. Stir in **1 tsp baking soda**, **1 tsp nutmeg**, **2 tsp cinnamon**, **1/2 tsp salt**, and **1/2 tsp vanilla extract**.
5. Mix in **2 cups flour**, then gently fold in the cooked **1 cup raisins** last.

### Step 3: Baking
6. Spread the batter evenly into a greased jelly roll pan or cookie sheet.
7. Bake until golden brown and set in the center, checking for a fragrant spiced aroma.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 3 days.
