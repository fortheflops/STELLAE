# Classic Frozen Fruit Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A nostalgic and easy-to-make frozen fruit salad featuring a medley of pineapple, fruit cocktail, bananas, and marshmallows in a light and creamy Dream Whip base. This refreshing treat is perfect for gatherings and can be prepared well in advance.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 0 mins | 8 hours | 10-12 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large mixing bowl
* Electric mixer
* 9x13 inch freezer-safe dish
* Rubber spatula

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 large can (20 oz) | [[Crushed Pineapple]] | undrained |
| 1 can (15 oz) | [[Fruit Cocktail]] | drained |
| 1 package (10 oz) | [[Small Marshmallows]] | none |
| 2 | [[Bananas]] | sliced |
| 1/2 bottle | [[Maraschino Cherries]] | with juice |
| 1 package | [[Dream Whip]] | prepared according to package directions |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare Base
1. In a large bowl, prepare the **1 package Dream Whip** according to the package directions until light and fluffy.

### Step 2: Combine Ingredients
2. Gently fold in the **1 large can crushed pineapple** (with juice), **1 can fruit cocktail**, **1 package small marshmallows**, **2 bananas**, and **1/2 bottle maraschino cherries** with their juice until just combined.

### Step 3: Freeze and Serve
3. Spoon the mixture into a **9x13 inch freezer-safe dish**, cover, and freeze for at least 8 hours until firm.
4. Let the salad sit at room temperature for 10-15 minutes to soften slightly before cutting into squares.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This salad must be frozen for at least 8 hours and can be kept in the freezer for up to one week if tightly covered.
