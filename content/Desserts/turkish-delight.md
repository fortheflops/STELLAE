# Turkish Delight

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A traditional confection flavored with citrus juice and zest, boiled slowly and rolled in powdered sugar. This classic recipe yields a chewy, delightful treat.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 20 mins | 4 hours | 24 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Saucepan
* Pan
* Knife

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 box | [[Gelatin]] | Soaked |
| 1 | [[Lemon]] | Juiced and grated rind |
| 1 | [[Orange]] | Large, juicy, juiced and grated rind |
| 1 lb | [[Granulated Sugar]] | Gran. sugar |
| 2/3 cup | [[Water]] | Plus additional for soaking and tin |
| As needed | [[Powdered Sugar]] | For rolling |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and Cooking
1. Soak the **1 box gelatin**.
2. Add **2/3 cup water** to **1 lb gran. sugar** and bring to a boil; when boiling, add the soaked **1 box gelatin**.
3. Boil slowly for **20 minutes**, stirring constantly until thickened.
4. Add the **grated rind** and **juice of 1 orange** and **1 lemon** to the mixture.

### Step 2: Setting and Cutting
5. Wet a tin with **cold water** and pour in the **mixture** about 1 inch deep.
6. Let set until firm, then cut into squares and roll thoroughly in **powdered sugar**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container layered with parchment paper and extra powdered sugar for up to a week.
