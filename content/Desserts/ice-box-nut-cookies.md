# Ice Box Nut Cookies

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *Discover a classic recipe for Ice Box Nut Cookies, featuring a rich blend of brown sugar, butter, and crunchy nuts. Perfect for slicing and baking fresh whenever a sweet craving strikes.*

## Ingredients

### Cookie Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 cups | [[Brown Sugar]] | Packed |
| 1 cup | [[Butter]] | Softened |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] | Beaten |
| 3 1/2 cups | [[Flour]] | Sifted |
| 1 tsp | [[Soda]] | Baking soda |
| 1 tsp | [[Cream of Tartar]] | None |
| 1 cup | [[Nuts]] | Chopped |
| 1 tsp | [[Vanilla]] | Extract (implied) |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing the Dough
1. Cream together the **1 cup butter** and **2 cups brown sugar** until light and fluffy.
2. Beat in the **2 eggs** and add a splash of **1 tsp vanilla**.
3. Sift in the **3 1/2 cups flour**, **1 tsp soda**, and **1 tsp cream of tartar**, mixing until a cohesive dough forms.
4. Fold in the **1 cup nuts** until evenly distributed throughout the dough.

### Step 2: Chilling and Slicing
5. Shape the dough into logs, wrap tightly in parchment paper or plastic wrap, and chill in the ice box or refrigerator for at least 2 hours until firm.
6. Slice the chilled logs into thin rounds using a sharp knife.

### Step 3: Baking
7. Preheat the oven and place slices on a greased baking sheet.
8. Bake at 350°F (175°C) for about 10 to 12 minutes until the edges are golden brown and set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The dough logs can be wrapped tightly and stored in the refrigerator for up to 5 days or frozen for up to 1 month before slicing and baking.
