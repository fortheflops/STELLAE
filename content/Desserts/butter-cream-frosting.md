# Butter Cream Frosting

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Jan Lutz | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic homemade butter cream frosting recipe from the kitchen of Jan Lutz. Perfect for spreading smoothly over cakes and cupcakes with a rich, fluffy texture.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 0 mins | None | 12 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Electric mixer

---
## Ingredients

### Frosting Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 cup | [[Shortening]] | Room temperature |
| 1 | [[Egg white]] | Unbeaten |
| 2 tbsp | [[Milk]] | Plus extra if needed |
| 1 tsp | [[Vanilla]] | Extract |
| 1 tsp | [[Almond]] | Extract |
| 1 1/2 lbs | [[Conf. sugar]] | Sifted |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing
1. Combine the **1 cup shortening**, **1 egg white**, **2 tbsp milk**, **1 tsp vanilla**, and **1 tsp almond** in a large mixing bowl and beat until smooth.
2. Gradually beat in the **1 1/2 lbs conf. sugar** until the mixture reaches a smooth, spreadable consistency, noting that **milk may vary a little** depending on desired thickness.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 1 week; bring to room temperature and re-whip before using.
