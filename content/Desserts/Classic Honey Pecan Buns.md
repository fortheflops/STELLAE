# Classic Honey Pecan Buns

> 📜 **Collection:** General Archive | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic recipe for soft, gooey honey-pecan sticky buns made with a rich yeast-risen dough, brown sugar filling, and caramel-pecan glaze. Baked in individual muffin cups for a delightfully sweet pull-apart treat.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Make the Dough
1. In a small bowl, dissolve **2 packages yeast** and **1 Tbsp sugar** in **1/2 cup lukewarm water**. Let stand for **5-10 minutes** until foamy.
2. In a saucepan, scald **1/2 cup milk**. Remove from heat and stir in **1/4 cup sugar**, **1/2 tsp salt**, and **3 Tbsp shortening** until melted. Cool to lukewarm.
3. In a large bowl, combine the yeast mixture, lukewarm milk mixture, and **1 beaten egg**. Gradually stir in **3 1/2 cups sifted flour** to form a soft dough.
4. Turn the dough onto a lightly floured board and knead until smooth and elastic for about **5-8 minutes**. Place in a greased bowl, cover, and let rise in a warm place until doubled in bulk, about **30-60 minutes**.

### Step 2: Prepare the Pans
5. While the dough rises, prepare the brown sugar syrup. In a small saucepan, melt **1/2 cup butter** with **1 cup brown sugar** and **1/4 cup honey or corn syrup**.
6. Divide this syrup evenly among **24 greased muffin pans**. Place **3 pecan halves** in each muffin cup.

### Step 3: Shape and Fill the Buns
7. Punch down the risen dough and divide it into two equal portions. On a floured surface, roll each portion into an 8x12-inch rectangle.
8. Brush each rectangle with melted **butter**. Sprinkle each with **3/8 cup brown sugar** and **3/8 cup chopped pecans**.
9. Roll up each rectangle lengthwise, like a jelly roll. Cut each roll into **12 one-inch slices**.

### Step 4: Final Rise and Bake
10. Place one slice, cut-side up, in each prepared muffin pan. Cover and let rise in a warm place until doubled, about **25-30 minutes**.
11. Preheat oven to **400°F (200°C)**. Bake the buns for about **30 minutes**, or until golden brown and bubbling.
12. Immediately turn the pans upside down onto a serving platter while hot. Serve immediately.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Unbaked assembled buns can be covered and refrigerated overnight for a second rise in the morning before baking.
