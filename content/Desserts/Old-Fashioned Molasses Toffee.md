# Old-Fashioned Molasses Toffee

> 📜 **Collection:** John B Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic, hard-crack toffee made with powdered sugar, butter, and molasses. Baking soda is added at the end for a light, brittle texture, and it's finished with chopped nuts.*

| As Needed | [[Nuts]] | chopped |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation
1. Grease a **baking pan** and set it aside.

### Step 2: Cook the Toffee
2. In a saucepan, combine **1 cup powdered sugar**, **1/4 lb butter**, and **1/2 cup molasses**.
3. Cook over medium heat, stirring occasionally, until the mixture reaches the hard-crack stage (approx. 300°F) and becomes brittle when dropped in cold water.

### Step 3: Finish and Cool
4. Remove from heat and immediately stir in a **pinch of baking soda** and **chopped nuts as needed** until foamy and well combined.
5. Quickly pour the mixture into the prepared greased pan, spreading it evenly. Allow to cool completely until hardened, then break into pieces.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 2 weeks. Keep in a cool, dry place to prevent the toffee from becoming sticky.
