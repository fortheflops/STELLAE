# Peanut Butter Clusters

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A simple and delightful no-bake candy recipe featuring crunchy wheat flakes coated in a smooth peanut butter and chocolate blend. These clusters are perfect for quick treats and set to a firm, satisfying finish.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 5 mins | 30 mins | 36 clusters |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Double boiler
* Mixing bowl
* Teaspoon
* Waxed paper

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 pkg (8 oz) | [[Candy Making Chocolate]] | Chopped |
| 3 tbsp | [[Peanut Butter]] | Creamy or crunchy |
| 2 cups | [[Wheat Flakes]] | Cereal |

---

## Instructions

### Preparation
1. Heat **1 pkg (8 oz) candy making chocolate** and **3 tbsp peanut butter** over hot water until the **chocolate** is completely melted, stirring constantly.
2. Remove from heat and add **2 cups wheat flakes**, mixing thoroughly until well coated.
3. Drop by the teaspoon onto waxed paper.
4. Allow to cool until firm to the touch.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container in a cool, dry place for up to one week.
