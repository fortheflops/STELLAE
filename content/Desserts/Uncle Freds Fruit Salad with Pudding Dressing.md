# Uncle Fred's Fruit Salad with Pudding Dressing

> 📜 **Collection:** UK15 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Uncle Fred | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic potluck fruit salad featuring a clever twist where instant banana pudding is prepared with reserved fruit juice. This creates a thick, flavorful dressing that perfectly coats the medley of canned and fresh fruits.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 0 mins | 1 hour | 10–12 Servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Measuring cup
* Large mixing bowl
* Wire whisk

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 (15 oz) cans | [[Apricots]] | Drained, juice reserved |
| 1 (20 oz) can | [[Pineapple Chunks]] | Drained, juice reserved |
| 1 (10 oz) jar | [[Maraschino Cherries]] | Drained |
| 2-3 | [[Bananas]] | Ripe, sliced |
| 1/2 cup | [[Pecans]] | Chopped |
| 1 (5.1 oz) package | [[Instant Banana Pudding]] | Large package |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Dressing
1. Drain the **2 (15 oz) cans apricots** and **1 (20 oz) can pineapple chunks** into a measuring cup, reserving the fruit; add water if necessary to reach the liquid volume required by the **1 (5.1 oz) package instant banana pudding**.
2. In a large bowl, whisk the **1 (5.1 oz) package instant banana pudding** with the reserved fruit juice for **2 minutes** until the mixture begins to thicken.
3. Allow the pudding to stand for **5 minutes** until it reaches a set, sauce-like consistency.

### Step 2: Assemble and Chill
4. Gently fold the reserved **apricots**, **pineapple chunks**, **1 (10 oz) jar maraschino cherries**, **2-3 bananas**, and **1/2 cup pecans** into the thickened pudding.
5. Cover and chill in the refrigerator for at least **1 hour** until cold and set before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This salad can be prepared up to 24 hours in advance; keep refrigerated and covered tightly to prevent the bananas from browning.
