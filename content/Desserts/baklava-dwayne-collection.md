# Baklava

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic, large-batch recipe for crispy, spiced Baklava layered with almonds and walnuts, topped with a fragrant honey syrup. Perfect for holidays and large gatherings, yielding 80 pieces.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 45 mins | 1 hour | Several hours | 80 pieces |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 11"x17" pan
* Mixing bowl
* Sharp knife

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and Nut Mixture
1. Defrost strudel leaves and melt the **1 1/2 pound unsalted butter**.
2. Combine the **1 pound unblanched almonds**, **1 pound walnuts**, **1/2 tsp. nutmeg**, **1/2 tsp. cloves**, **1 tsp. cinnamon**, and **1 tablespoon cream of wheat** in a bowl and mix well.

### Step 2: Layering the Baklava
3. Brush the bottom of an 11"x17" pan with **2 tablespoons unsalted butter**.
4. Begin to layer **1 sheet filo**, brush with **unsalted butter**, cover with another **sheet of filo**, brush with **unsalted butter**, and repeat the process until you have used about a dozen leaves.
5. Spread about **1/3 cup nuts** over the layers.
6. Cover with a **sheet of filo**, brush with **unsalted butter**, add another **sheet of filo**, brush with **unsalted butter**, cover with another **1/3 cup of nuts**, and repeat the process until all nuts are used.
7. Cover with the remaining **filo sheets** (about 6), brushing each sheet with **unsalted butter**.

### Step 3: Cutting, Baking, and Syrup
8. With a sharp knife, cut into diamond shapes. Insert a **whole clove** into the center of each piece.
9. Bake at 325°F for **1 hour** or until golden brown.
10. While baklava is baking, make the syrup: mix **4 cups sugar**, **2 cups water**, **3 tablespoons honey**, juice of **1/2 lemon**, and **2 cinnamon sticks**. Stir well till sugar is mostly dissolved, boil **3 minutes** till syrup is clear, then let cool.
11. Pour the cooled syrup over the hot baklava.
12. Cool several hours before serving. Freezes well!

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Baklava freezes very well; cool completely before storing or freezing.
