# Classic Cooked Chocolate Cream Pie

> 📜 **Collection:** General Archive | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A traditional, from-scratch chocolate cream pie featuring a rich, stovetop-cooked pudding filling made with unsweetened chocolate and egg yolks. Chilled overnight, this vintage dessert delivers a decadent and smooth texture in a flaky crust.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 15 mins | 8 hours | One 9-inch pie |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Medium saucepan
* Separate saucepan
* Wire whisk
* 9-inch pie pan
* Plastic wrap

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Base
1. In a saucepan, whisk together the **1/4 cup cornstarch**, **1/2 cup sugar**, and **1/4 teaspoon salt**.
2. In a separate pan, scald the **2 cups milk** with the **1 1/2 (1 oz) squares chocolate**, heating until the milk is very hot and the chocolate is completely melted and smooth.

### Step 2: Cook the Pudding
3. Gradually add the hot chocolate milk to the cornstarch mixture, whisking constantly until smooth.
4. Cook over moderate heat, stirring constantly, until the mixture thickens and comes to a robust boil. Continue boiling for **2 minutes** until glossy and thick.
5. Remove from heat. Temper the **3 egg yolks** by slowly stirring a small amount of the hot mixture into them. Then, add the egg yolk mixture back to the saucepan, stirring well.
6. Return to the heat and boil for **1 more minute**, stirring constantly until smooth and thick.

### Step 3: Finish and Chill
7. Remove from heat and stir in the **2 tablespoons butter** and **1/2 - 1 tsp vanilla** until fully incorporated.
8. Let the filling cool slightly, then pour it into the pre-baked **1 (9-inch) pie shell**.
9. Cover with plastic wrap pressed directly onto the surface of the filling and refrigerate for at least **8 hours** or overnight until very firm and set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store covered with plastic wrap in the refrigerator for up to 3 days. The long chilling time is essential for the filling to set up perfectly.
