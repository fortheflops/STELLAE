# Chocolate Banana Whipped Cream Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A decadent three-layer chocolate cake filled with whole bananas and a generous amount of real whipped cream, then frosted with more whipped cream and a sprinkle of shaved chocolate.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 30 mins | 35 mins | 1 hour | 1 3-layer cake |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* mixing bowl
* electric mixer
* serving platter
* offset spatula

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 recipe | [[Chocolate Cake]] | three-layer, baked and cooled |
| 3-4 | [[Bananas]] | whole, ripe but firm, peeled |
| 3-4 Cups | [[Whipped Cream]] | sweetened, freshly whipped |
| As needed | [[Chocolate]] | shaved, for garnish (optional) |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare Components
1. Bake your favorite **1 recipe** **three-layer chocolate cake** and allow the layers to cool completely. Prepare your sweetened **whipped cream** by beating heavy cream with powdered sugar and vanilla until stiff peaks form.

### Step 2: Assemble Layer 1
2. Place the bottom **chocolate cake** layer on your serving plate. Peel and arrange the **3-4 bananas** on top, leaving a little space between them.
3. Cover the **bananas** and the entire first **chocolate cake** layer with a generous amount of **whipped cream** until smooth and covered.

### Step 3: Assemble Layer 2 and 3
4. Carefully place the second **chocolate cake** layer on top. Cover this layer with just **whipped cream**.
5. Place the third and final **chocolate cake** layer on top. Frost the entire **chocolate cake**—top and sides—with the remaining **whipped cream** until evenly coated.

### Step 4: Garnish and Chill
6. Decorate the top with **shaved chocolate**, if desired. Keep refrigerated until chilled and ready to serve.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the assembled cake covered in the refrigerator for up to 2 days before serving.
