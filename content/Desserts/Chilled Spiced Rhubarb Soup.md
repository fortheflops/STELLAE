# Chilled Spiced Rhubarb Soup

> 📜 **Collection:** Patsy's Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A unique and refreshing chilled dessert soup from Patsy's collection, featuring tender rhubarb simmered with warm spices like cinnamon and cloves, and finished with a splash of white wine.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Simmer the Base
1. In a saucepan, combine the **4 cups diced rhubarb**, **4 cups water**, **3/4 cup sugar**, **1 tbsp orange rind**, and **1 cinnamon stick**. Bring the mixture to a boil, then reduce the heat, cover, and simmer for **20 minutes** until the rhubarb is very tender.

### Step 2: Thicken and Finish
2. In a separate small bowl, whisk the **2 tbsp cornstarch** and a pinch of **cloves** with the remaining **1/2 cup cold water** until smooth.
3. Slowly add the cornstarch mixture to the simmering rhubarb, whisking constantly, and cook for **1-2 minutes** until it thickens slightly and coats the back of a spoon.
4. Remove the soup from the heat and stir in the **1/2 cup white wine** and **1/2 tsp vanilla**. Remove the **cinnamon stick**.

### Step 3: Chill and Serve
5. Let the soup cool completely, then chill thoroughly in the refrigerator for several hours until ice-cold.
6. Serve cold, garnished with **orange slices**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This soup can be prepared ahead and stored in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days. Serve thoroughly chilled.
