# Oatmeal Cake with Broiled Coconut Topping

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A wonderfully moist, old-fashioned oatmeal cake featuring a rich flavor from brown sugar and spices. The single-layer spice cake is finished with a gooey, caramelized topping of coconut, nuts, and brown sugar.*

---

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 40 mins | 20 mins | 1 9x13 cake |

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Soak
1. Preheat your oven to **350°F**. Grease and flour a **13x9 inch baking pan**.
2. In a medium bowl, pour the **1 ⅓ cups boiling water** over the **1 cup oatmeal**. Stir and let it stand for **20 minutes**.

### Step 2: Mix the Batter
3. In a large bowl, cream together the **1 cup brown sugar**, **1 cup white sugar**, and **½ cup shortening** until light and fluffy.
4. Beat the **2 eggs** into the creamed mixture one at a time.
5. Stir the soaked oatmeal mixture into the wet ingredients.
6. In a separate bowl, whisk together the **1 ½ cups flour**, **½ tsp cinnamon**, **½ tsp salt**, and **1 tsp baking soda**.
7. Add the dry ingredients to the wet ingredients and mix until just combined.
8. Pour the batter into the prepared pan and bake for **30 minutes**.

### Step 3: Make the Topping and Finish Baking
9. While the cake is baking, combine all the topping ingredients: **6 tbsp melted butter**, **½ cup brown sugar**, **¼ cup milk**, **1 cup nuts**, **1 cup coconut**, and **1 tsp vanilla extract** in a small bowl. Stir well.
10. After **30 minutes**, remove the cake from the oven. Immediately spread the topping mixture evenly over the hot cake.
11. Return the cake to the oven and bake for an additional **10 minutes**, or until the topping is bubbly and lightly golden.
12. Let the cake cool completely in the pan on a wire rack before cutting and serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the cooled cake covered at room temperature for up to 3 days, or refrigerate for up to 5 days.
