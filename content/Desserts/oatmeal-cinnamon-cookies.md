# Oatmeal Cinnamon Cookies

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Five | ✍️ **Attribution:** Florin Young | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *These delightful oatmeal cinnamon cookies are packed with raisins, nuts, and chocolate chips for a sweet, chewy treat. Perfect for sharing, this recipe yields a generous batch of delicious homemade cookies.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 12 mins | None | 6 dozen |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Electric mixer
* Cookie sheet

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 cup | [[Butter]] | or oleo (2 sticks) |
| 2 cups | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] | Whole |
| 1 tablespoon | [[Molasses]] | Standard |
| 2 tsp | [[Vanilla]] | Extract |
| 2 tsp | [[Cinnamon]] | Ground |
| 1/2 tsp | [[Salt]] | To taste |
| 1 1/2 tsp | [[Baking Soda]] | Leavening |
| 2 cups | [[Flour]] | All-purpose |
| 2 cups | [[Quick Oatmeal]] | Rolled oats |
| 2/3 cup | [[Raisins]] | Dried |
| 1 cup | [[Nuts]] | Chopped |
| 1/2 cup | [[Chocolate Chips]] | Semi-sweet |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing the Dough
1. Cream the **1 cup butter** or oleo and **2 cups sugar** together. Add the **2 eggs**, **1 tablespoon molasses**, and **2 tsp vanilla**, and cream again until smooth.
2. Combine the **2 cups flour**, **2 tsp cinnamon**, **1/2 tsp salt**, and **1 1/2 tsp baking soda**, then gradually add this to the creamed mixture.
3. Stir in the **2 cups quick oatmeal**, **2/3 cup raisins**, **1 cup nuts**, and **1/2 cup chocolate chips** until thoroughly combined.

### Step 2: Baking
4. Drop the dough by teaspoonfuls onto ungreased cookie sheets.
5. Bake in a 350-degree oven for **12 minutes** or until light brown, ensuring they achieve a soft, chewy texture.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The dough can be prepared ahead of time, covered, and refrigerated for up to 24 hours before dropping onto cookie sheets and baking.
