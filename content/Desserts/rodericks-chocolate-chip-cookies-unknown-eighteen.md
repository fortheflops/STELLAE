# Roderick's Chocolate Chip Cookies

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing and Baking
1. Cream together **1/2 cup shortening**, **2/3 cup butter or margarine**, **1 cup granulated sugar**, and **1 cup brown sugar** until smooth and fluffy.
2. Beat in **2 eggs** and **2 teaspoons vanilla** until well combined.
3. Gradually blend in **3 cups all-purpose flour** and **1 teaspoon baking soda** until a dough forms.
4. Stir in **1 cup chopped nuts** and **2 packages (6 ounces each) semisweet chocolate pieces** until evenly distributed.
5. Drop spoonfuls onto a baking sheet and bake until golden brown around the edges with soft centers.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Dough can be shaped into balls and frozen for up to 1 month before baking.
