# Sesame Honey Candy

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Seven | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic homemade confection made with toasted sesame seeds, grated coconut, and butter bound together with honey and vanilla. Easy to prepare on the stovetop and chill into sweet, bite-sized candy balls.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 5 mins | 30 mins | 3 dozen |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large frying pan
* Measuring cups
* Measuring spoons
* Wooden spoon

---
## Ingredients

### Candy Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/4 cup | [[Butter]] | 1/2 stick |
| 1/2 cup | [[Sesame seeds]] | None |
| 1 cup | [[Coconut]] | grated |
| 1/2 tsp | [[Vanilla]] | None |
| 1/4 cup | [[Honey]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Cooking the Mixture
1. In a big frying pan over low heat, melt **1/4 cup butter**.
2. Stir in **1/2 cup sesame seeds** and **1 cup grated coconut**.
3. Stir the mixture continuously over low heat for 5 minutes until warm and lightly combined.

### Flavoring & Chilling
4. Take the pan off the stove.
5. Add **1/2 teaspoon vanilla** and **1/4 cup honey**.
6. Stir until the **honey** is fully mixed in.
7. Place the candy mixture in a cold place until it becomes stiff enough to shape into balls.
8. Shape into 3 dozen small balls and store in the refrigerator.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Keep the shaped candy balls stored in a sealed container in the refrigerator so they retain their firm texture.
