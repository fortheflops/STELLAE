# Layered Strawberry, Pineapple, and Banana Jello Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic layered Jello salad featuring a tangy sour cream center sandwiched between layers of strawberry Jello packed with strawberries, pineapple, and bananas.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 5 mins | 6 hours | 12-15 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large mixing bowl
* 9x13 inch baking dish
* Spatula
* Measuring cups

---
## Ingredients

### Fruit Jello Layers
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 packages (3 oz each) | [[Strawberry Jello]] | powder |
| 2 Cups | [[Boiling Water]] | hot |
| 2 packages (10 oz each) | [[Frozen Strawberries]] | thawed |
| 1 large can (20 oz) | [[Crushed Pineapple]] | undrained |
| 3 | [[Bananas]] | ripe, sliced |

### Filling
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 pint (1 cup) | [[Sour Cream]] | chilled |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Base Jello Mixture
1. In a large bowl, dissolve the **2 packages strawberry Jello** in the **2 cups boiling water**, stirring for 2 minutes until fully dissolved.

### Step 2: Combine Fruit and Chill First Layer
2. Add the **2 packages frozen strawberries** to the hot Jello and stir until they are thawed and the mixture is cool.
3. Stir in the **1 large can crushed pineapple** and the **3 bananas**.
4. Pour half of this Jello mixture into a large dish (a 9x13 inch pan works well).
5. Place the dish in the refrigerator and chill until the layer is firm and set (at least 1-2 hours).

### Step 3: Add Cream Layer and Final Chill
6. Once the first layer is set, carefully spread the **1/2 pint sour cream** evenly over the top.
7. Gently pour the remaining fruit and Jello mixture over the sour cream layer.
8. Return to the refrigerator and chill for at least 4 more hours, or overnight, until the entire salad is completely firm and set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store covered in the refrigerator for up to 3 days. Do not freeze.
