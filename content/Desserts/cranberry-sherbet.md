# Cranberry Sherbet

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A delightful and tart homemade cranberry sherbet whipped with egg whites for a light, refreshing texture. Perfect for holiday dinners or a cooling summer treat.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 10 mins | 4 hours | 6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Saucepan
* Refrigerator tray
* Mixer

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 4 cups | [[Cranberries]] | Fresh or frozen |
| 1 cup | [[Boiling Water]] | None |
| 2 cups | [[Sugar]] | None |
| 1/8 tsp | [[Salt]] | None |
| 1/2 cup | [[Orange Juice]] | Freshly squeezed |
| 2 | [[Egg Whites]] | Unbeaten |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Cooking and Sieve
1. Cook the **4 cups cranberries** and **1 cup boiling water** in a saucepan for **10 minutes**.
2. Add the **2 cups sugar**, **1/8 tsp salt**, and **1/2 cup orange juice**.
3. Run through a sieve to remove solids, and cool.

### Step 2: Freezing and Whipping
4. Turn into a refrigerator tray and partially freeze until slushy.
5. Remove, add the **2 egg whites**, and beat for **1 minute** until light and frothy.
6. Return to the freezer until completely set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Can be prepared up to a week in advance and stored tightly covered in the freezer.
