# Nutritious Poppin Fresh Cookies

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Five | ✍️ **Attribution:** Flani Young | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A delightful recipe for wholesome oatmeal cookies packed with brown sugar, chocolate chips, and nuts from the kitchen of Flani Young. These chewy cookies offer a customizable base with optional mix-ins like peanut butter, coconut, or wheat germ.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 15 mins | None | 6 to 7 dozen cookies |

* Large bowl

* Cookie sheet
* Spoons

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 1/4 cups | [[All-purpose flour]] | Unbleached, lightly spooned and leveled |
| 2 cups | [[Brown sugar]] | Firmly packed |
| 1 tsp | [[Baking soda]] | None |
| 2 tsp | [[Vanilla extract]] | None |
| 1 tsp | [[Salt]] | None |
| 1 cup | [[Butter]] | Or oleo, softened |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] | None |
| 2 cups | [[Quick cooking oatmeal]] | None |
| 6 oz (1 cup) | [[Chocolate chips]] | None |
| 1 cup | [[Nuts]] | Chopped (or sunflower seeds) |

### Optional Ingredients (Choose One)
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 cup | [[Peanut butter]] | Optional |
| 1 cup | [[Wheat germ]] | Optional |
| 1 cup | [[Coconut]] | Optional |
| 1 cup | [[Non-fat dry milk]] | Optional |

---

## Instructions

### Mixing the Dough
1. Lightly spoon **2 1/4 cups all-purpose flour** into a measuring cup and level off.
2. Into a large bowl, combine all ingredients except **1 cup flour**, **2 cups quick cooking oatmeal**, **6 oz (1 cup) chocolate chips**, and **1 cup nuts**.
3. Beat at medium speed until blended, about **1 to 2 minutes**.
4. By hand, stir in the remaining flour, oats, chocolate chips, and nuts, along with one optional ingredient if desired.

### Baking
5. Drop dough by rounded teaspoons, **2 inches apart**, onto an ungreased cookie sheet.
6. Bake at **350 degrees** for **10 to 15 minutes**, until golden brown.
7. Cool **1 minute** before removing from the sheet.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store baked cookies in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 5 days, or freeze the unbaked dough balls for later baking.
