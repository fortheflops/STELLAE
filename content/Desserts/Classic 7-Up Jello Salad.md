# Classic 7-Up Jello Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A nostalgic and fizzy molded Jello salad featuring the classic combination of lime Jello, 7-Up soda, cream cheese, crushed pineapple, and pecans. This retro dessert offers a creamy texture with a delightful crunch.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 5 mins | 6 hours | 8–10 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* electric mixer
* large mixing bowl
* mold or serving dish
* spatula

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3 oz | [[Lime Jello]] powder | 1 small box |
| 8 oz | [[Cream Cheese]] | softened |
| 1 Cup | [[Boiling Water]] | none |
| 8 oz | [[Pineapple]] | crushed, drained |
| 1 tsp | [[Vanilla]] | extract |
| 1 Tbsp | [[Sugar]] | none |
| 10 oz | [[7-Up]] | chilled soda |
| 1 Cup | [[Pecans]] | chopped |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Base Preparation
1. In a large bowl, beat the **8 oz cream cheese** with the **3 oz lime jello powder** using an electric mixer until smooth and well combined.

### Step 2: Dissolving
2. Gradually pour in the **1 cup boiling water** while mixing on low speed until the mixture is smooth and the **lime jello** is completely dissolved.

### Step 3: Flavoring and Fizz
3. Stir in the **1 tbsp sugar** and **1 tsp vanilla**.
4. Slowly pour in the **10 oz 7-up**; the mixture will fizz, so stir gently until combined.

### Step 4: Folding and Setting
5. Gently fold in the **8 oz crushed pineapple** and **1 cup chopped pecans**.
6. Pour the mixture into a mold or serving dish and refrigerate for at least 6 hours, or until completely firm and set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This salad must be made at least 6 hours in advance to set properly; it can be stored in the refrigerator for up to 3 days.
