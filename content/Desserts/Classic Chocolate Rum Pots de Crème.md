# Classic Chocolate Rum Pots de Crème

> 📜 **Collection:** LOC | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A rich and elegant baked chocolate custard infused with a hint of rum. This sophisticated dessert is baked in a water bath for a perfectly smooth and decadent texture.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Base
1. Preheat oven to 325°F (165°C) and prepare 6-8 custard cups.
2. In a medium saucepan, whisk together **1/2 cup sugar**, **1/4 tsp salt**, and **6 tbsp cocoa**.
3. Slowly stir in **2 cups cream or half & half** and cook over low heat until hot and tiny bubbles form at the edges; do not boil.

### Step 2: Temper and Bake
4. In a separate bowl, beat **6 egg yolks** thoroughly.
5. Gradually temper the eggs by stirring a small amount of the hot cream mixture into the yolks, then slowly pour the mixture back into the saucepan.
6. Stir in **2 tbsp rum** and pour the custard into the cups.
7. Place cups in a shallow baking pan filled with hot water halfway up the sides and bake for **15 minutes** until a knife inserted near the center comes out clean and the center has a slight jiggle.

### Step 3: Cool and Serve
8. Remove from the water bath and cool completely on a wire rack before chilling in the refrigerator.
9. Garnish with **whipped cream** and **shaved chocolate or chocolate sprinkles** before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The custards can be prepared and chilled for up to 2 days; add garnishes immediately before serving.
