# Cranberry Parfait Pie

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *Indulge in a refreshing and tart Cranberry Parfait Pie, featuring a creamy vanilla and lemon gelatin filling paired with spiced cranberry sauce. Housed in a prepared pie shell, this frozen delight is a perfect make-ahead treat for any gathering.*

---

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | None | 4 hours | 8 servings |

## Ingredients

### Filling and Crust
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 pkg | [[Lemon Gelatin]] | 3 oz package |
| 1 cup | [[Hot Cranberry Juice Cocktail]] | Hot |
| 1/2 cup | [[Cold Water]] | Chilled |
| 1 pt | [[Vanilla Ice Cream]] | Softened |
| 1 tsp | [[Grated Lemon Peel]] | Freshly grated |
| 3 tbsp | [[Lemon Juice]] | Freshly squeezed |
| 1 lb can | [[Cranberry Sauce]] | Jellied or whole berry (approx. 2 cups) |
| 1 | [[9" Pie Shell]] | Baked or crumb crust, prepared |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparing the Gelatin Base
1. Dissolve **1 pkg lemon gelatin** in **1 cup hot cranberry juice cocktail** in a large mixing bowl, stirring until completely dissolved.
2. Stir in **1/2 cup cold water**, **1 tsp grated lemon peel**, and **3 tbsp lemon juice**.

### Step 2: Blending and Chilling
3. Add **1 pt vanilla ice cream** to the warm gelatin mixture, stirring until the ice cream melts completely and the mixture is smooth.
4. Fold in **1 lb can cranberry sauce** until evenly distributed.
5. Pour the filling into the **1 9" pie shell**.
6. Chill in the refrigerator until set and firm to the touch, about 4 hours.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Keep refrigerated until ready to serve, or freeze for a firmer, ice-cream-pie consistency.
