# Chocolate Chip Date Torte

> 📜 **Collection:** The Loraine Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A wonderfully moist and rich torte from The Loraine Collection, featuring a tender date cake base topped with a crunchy layer of chocolate chips, walnuts, and sugar that's baked right on top.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 30 mins | None | 1 9x13 inch cake |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 9x13 inch baking pan
* Medium bowl
* Large bowl
* Separate bowl

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 cup | [[Dates]] | Chopped |
| 1 ½ cup | [[Water]] | Boiling |
| 1 tsp | [[Baking Soda]] | for dates |
| ½ cup | [[Butter]] | softened |
| 1 ½ cup | [[Sugar]] | divided |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] | well beaten |
| 1 ¼ cup | [[Flour]] | all-purpose |
| ½ tsp | [[Salt]] | none |
| ¾ tsp | [[Baking Soda]] | for flour |
| 1 pkg. (6-12 oz) | [[Chocolate Chips]] | none |
| ½ cup | [[Walnuts]] | none |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and Soak
1. Preheat your oven to 350°F. Grease a 9x13 inch baking pan.
2. In a medium bowl, combine the **1 cup [[Dates]]**, **1 ½ cup boiling [[Water]]**, and **1 tsp [[Baking Soda]]** (for dates). Stir and set aside to cool until room temperature.

### Step 2: Mix Batter and Bake
3. In a large bowl, cream together the **½ cup [[Butter]]** and **1 cup** of the **1 ½ cup [[Sugar]]** until light and fluffy. Beat in the **2 [[Eggs]]**.
4. In a separate bowl, sift together the **1 ¼ cup [[Flour]]**, **½ tsp [[Salt]]**, and the remaining **¾ tsp [[Baking Soda]]** (for flour).
5. Add the cooled date mixture to the creamed butter mixture and stir. Then, add the flour mixture and stir until just combined and smooth.
6. Pour the batter into the greased pan. Sprinkle the top evenly with the **1 pkg. (6-12 oz) [[Chocolate Chips]]**, **½ cup [[Walnuts]]**, and the **remaining ½ cup of [[Sugar]]**.
7. Bake for **25-30 minutes**, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean and the top is golden and bubbling.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the torte covered at room temperature for up to 3 days or refrigerate for up to a week.
