# Cinnamon Toast Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** UK15 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A simple and comforting sheet cake that tastes just like cinnamon toast. This tender, buttery cake is baked in a jelly roll pan and topped with a generous layer of melted butter and cinnamon-sugar.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 35 mins | None | 1 15x10 inch cake |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 15x10x1 inch jelly roll pan
* Large mixing bowl
* Separate bowl
* Small bowl
* Wire rack

---
## Ingredients

### For the Cake
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 cups | [[Flour]] | All-purpose |
| 1 cup | [[Sugar]] | Standard |
| 2 teaspoons | [[Baking Powder]] | None |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Salt]] | None |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] | Large, see note |
| 1 cup | [[Milk]] | None |
| 2 tablespoons | [[Butter]] | Melted |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Vanilla]] | None |
| 1/2 cup | [[Raisins]] | Optional |

### For the Topping
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 cup | [[Butter]] | Melted |
| 1/2 cup | [[Sugar]] | None |
| 1 1/2 teaspoons | [[Cinnamon]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Mix Batter
1. Preheat the oven to **350°F**. Well grease a **15x10x1 inch jelly roll pan**.
2. In a large mixing bowl, sift together the **2 cups [[Flour]]**, **1 cup [[Sugar]]**, **2 teaspoons [[Baking Powder]]**, and **1 teaspoon [[Salt]]**.
3. In a separate bowl, whisk together the **1 cup [[Milk]]**, **2 tablespoons melted [[Butter]]**, **1 teaspoon [[Vanilla]]**, and **2 [[Eggs]]**.
4. Blend the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients and mix well. If desired, stir in the **1/2 cup [[Raisins]]**.
5. Pour the batter into the prepared jelly roll pan and spread evenly.

### Step 2: Initial Bake
6. Bake for **20 to 25 minutes**, or until golden brown and springy to the touch.

### Step 3: Topping and Final Bake
7. While the cake is baking, prepare the topping by melting the **1/2 cup [[Butter]]**.
8. Remove the cake from the oven. Immediately drizzle the **1/2 cup melted [[Butter]]** for the topping over the hot cake.
9. In a small bowl, combine the **1/2 cup [[Sugar]]** and **1 1/2 teaspoons [[Cinnamon]]**. Sprinkle this mixture evenly over the buttered cake.
10. Return the cake to the oven and bake for an additional **10 minutes** until caramelized and bubbly.
11. Let the cake cool in the pan on a wire rack before cutting into squares.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store sealed in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 3 days or refrigerate for up to 5 days.
