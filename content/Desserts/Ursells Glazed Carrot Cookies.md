# Ursell's Glazed Carrot Cookies

> 📜 **Collection:** JohnBCollection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Ursell | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A delightful vintage cookie recipe featuring soft, cooked carrots and a bright, zesty orange icing. These tender treats are perfect for those seeking a unique, nostalgic dessert experience.*

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3/4 cup | [[Sugar]] | granulated |
| 3/4 cup | [[Shortening]] | room temperature |
| 1 cup | [[Carrots]] | cooked, chopped or strained |
| 1 | [[Egg]] | large |
| 1 tsp | [[Vanilla]] | extract |
| 2 cups | [[Flour]] | all-purpose |
| 2 tsp | [[Baking Powder]] | sifted |
| 1 dash | [[Salt]] | fine |
| To taste | [[Orange Icing]] | powdered sugar and orange juice |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Batter
1. Preheat your oven to 350°F.
2. In a large bowl, cream together the **3/4 cup shortening** and **3/4 cup sugar** until light and fluffy.
3. Beat in the **1 egg**, **1 tsp vanilla**, and **1 cup cooked carrots** until well incorporated.

### Step 2: Combine and Bake
4. Sift together the **2 cups flour**, **2 tsp baking powder**, and **1 dash salt**.
5. Gradually add the dry ingredients to the wet mixture, stirring until just combined.
6. Drop spoonfuls of dough onto a baking sheet and bake at 350°F for 10-12 minutes until the edges are set and the cookies are lightly golden.
7. While the cookies are still warm, spread with the **orange icing** until smooth and glossy.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 3 days or freeze for up to 1 month.
