# Coffee Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** Christine Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic homemade coffee cake featuring a tender crumb and a spiced crumb topping. Perfect for serving with morning coffee or as an afternoon treat.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 45 mins | None | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Sifter
* 8" x 8" square pan

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 cups | [[Flour]] | Sifted |
| 1 cup | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 1/2 cup | [[Shortening]] | Softened |
| 3/4 tsp. | [[Salt]] | None |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Baking Powder]] | None |
| 1/2 tbsp. | [[Cinnamon]] | None |
| 1/2 tsp. | [[Soda]] | None |
| 3/4 cup | [[Buttermilk or Sour Milk]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing and Topping
1. Sift the dry ingredients (**2 cups flour**, **1 cup sugar**, **3/4 tsp. salt**, **1/4 tsp baking powder**, **1/2 tbsp. cinnamon**, **1/2 tsp. soda**), then cut in **1/2 cup shortening**.
2. Take out **1/2 cup** crumbs to reserve for the top.

### Step 2: Assembly and Baking
3. Dissolve **1/2 tsp. soda** into **3/4 cup buttermilk or sour milk**, then add the milk mixture to the dry ingredients and mix until incorporated.
4. Pour batter into a greased **8" x 8" square pan** and sprinkle the reserved **1/2 cup** crumbs over the top.
5. Bake at 350° for **45 min** until golden brown and a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 2 days.
