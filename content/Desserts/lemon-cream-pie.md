## Instructions

### Step 1: Cooking the Filling
1. In a saucepan, combine **1 cup sugar**, **1 tbsp cornstarch**, **1 tbsp butter**, **1 cup hot water**, and the **juice and grated rind of 1 lemon**.
2. Cook the mixture for 4 minutes until thickened and smooth.

### Step 2: Assembly and Meringue
3. Pour the hot mixture over the **1 beaten egg yolk** while whisking constantly to temper.
4. Pour the custard into the **1 baked pie crust**.
5. Top with a prepared meringue and brown in the oven until the peaks are golden and toasted.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The pie can be assembled and chilled, but it is best served the same day to ensure the meringue stays light and crisp.
