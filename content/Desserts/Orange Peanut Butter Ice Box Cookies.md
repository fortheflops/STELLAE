# Orange Peanut Butter Ice Box Cookies

> 📜 **Collection:** John B Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic vintage ice box cookie recipe that pairs creamy peanut butter with bright orange zest. These slice-and-bake cookies are perfect for preparing ahead and keeping on hand for quick treats.*

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

| 1/2 tsp | [[Soda]] | baking soda |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Pecan]] | chopped nut meats |

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Dough
1. In a large bowl, cream together the **1/2 cup butter** and **1/2 cup peanut butter** until smooth.
2. Add the **1/2 cup brown sugar** and **1/2 cup white sugar**, mixing until light and fluffy.
3. Beat in the **1 egg**, **2 tbsp orange juice**, and **1 tbsp orange rind** until well combined.
4. Gradually stir in the **2 3/4 cups flour** (previously sifted with **1/4 tsp salt** and **1/2 tsp soda**) and fold in the **1/2 cup pecan nut meats**.

### Step 2: Chill and Bake
5. Form the dough into logs or rolls, wrap tightly in waxed paper, and chill in the refrigerator overnight until firm.
6. Preheat oven to 375°F. Slice the chilled dough into rounds and bake for 8-10 minutes until the edges are lightly golden and fragrant.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The dough logs can be kept in the refrigerator for up to 3 days or frozen for up to 1 month before slicing and baking.
