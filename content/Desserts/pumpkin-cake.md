# Pumpkin Cake

> *A delightful spiced pumpkin cake topped with a rich cream cheese frosting and nuts. Perfect for autumn gatherings and family treats.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Making the Cake
1. Combine the **1 pkg yellow/spice cake mix**, **2 eggs**, **1/4 c water**, **2 teas. soda**, **1 teas. cinnamon**, **1/2 teas. ginger**, **1/4 teas. cloves**, and **1 can (16 oz.) pumpkin** in order in a **large mixing bowl**.
2. Beat for **4 minutes** at medium speed until smooth and thoroughly incorporated.
3. Pour the batter into a greased and floured **bundt tube pan**.
4. Bake at **350°F** for **30 to 35 minutes** or until done when a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.
5. Cool for **10 minutes** in the pan before turning out to cool completely.

### Step 2: Making the Frosting
6. Combine the **1 pkg (3 oz.) cream cheese** and **1/2 c. butter** at room temperature.
7. Add the **1 box (1 lb.) powdered sugar** and **1 tsp. vanilla**, beating until smooth.
8. Stir in the **1/2 c. nuts** and spread over the cooled pumpkin cake.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the frosted cake covered in the refrigerator for up to 3 days. Bring to room temperature before serving.
