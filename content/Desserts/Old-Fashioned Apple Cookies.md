# Old-Fashioned Apple Cookies

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic recipe for soft, cake-like drop cookies, packed with fresh chopped apples, raisins, and nuts. These hearty cookies have a wonderful spiced flavor from the brown sugar.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 12 mins | None | 4 dozen cookies |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* large bowl
* electric mixer
* cookie sheets
* whisk

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Shortening]] | room temperature |
| 1 1/2 Cups | [[Brown Sugar]] | packed |
| 1 | [[Egg]] | large |
| 2 Cups | [[Flour]] | all-purpose |
| 1 tsp | [[Baking Soda]] | none |
| 1/2 tsp | [[Salt]] | none |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Milk]] | none |
| 1 Cup | [[Apples]] | peeled, cored, and diced |
| 1 Cup | [[Raisins]] | none |
| 1 Cup | [[Nuts]] | chopped |
| Optional | [[Spices]] | 1/2 tsp cinnamon, 1/4 tsp nutmeg |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Base
1. Preheat your oven to 400°F and grease your cookie sheets.
2. In a large bowl, cream the **1/2 cup shortening** and **1 1/2 cups brown sugar** together with an electric mixer. Beat in the **1 egg**.

### Step 2: Combine Ingredients
3. In a separate bowl, whisk together the **2 cups flour**, **1 tsp baking soda**, **1/2 tsp salt**, and optional **spices**.
4. Add half of the dry ingredients to the creamed mixture and blend well.
5. Stir in the **1/2 cup milk**.
6. Add the remaining dry ingredients and mix until just combined.

### Step 3: Fold and Bake
7. By hand, fold in the **1 cup apples**, **1 cup raisins**, and **1 cup nuts**.
8. Drop by rounded teaspoonfuls onto the prepared cookie sheets.
9. Bake for **10-12 minutes**, or until the edges are golden brown and the centers are set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 3 days or freeze for up to 1 month.
