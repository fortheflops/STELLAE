# Layered Strawberry Cream Jello Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic multi-layered Jello salad featuring a fluffy, fruit-packed strawberry base with a surprise tangy sour cream layer in the middle. This retro dessert is perfect for gatherings and can be prepared well in advance.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 25 mins | 5 mins | 6 hours | 9 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* large mixing bowl
* 8x8x2 inch pan
* whisk
* spatula

---
## Ingredients

### Jello Layers
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 packages (3 oz each) | [[Strawberry Jello]] | powdered |
| 2 cups | [[Water]] | boiling |
| 2 (10 oz) packages | [[Strawberries]] | frozen |
| 1 pint | [[Whipping Cream]] | whipped |
| 1/2 to 2 cups | [[Pineapple]] | crushed, drained |
| 2 or 3 large | [[Bananas]] | diced |
| 1 cup | [[Nuts]] | coarsely chopped |

### Filling
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 cup | [[Sour Cream]] | room temperature |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Base
1. In a large bowl, dissolve the **2 packages strawberry jello** in **2 cups boiling water**, stirring for 2 minutes until fully dissolved.
2. Add the **2 packages frozen strawberries** and stir until they are thawed and the mixture has cooled and begun to thicken.
3. Gently fold in the **1 pint whipped cream**, **1/2 to 2 cups crushed pineapple**, **2 or 3 diced bananas**, and **1 cup chopped nuts**.

### Step 2: Layer and Chill
4. Pour half of the mixture into an 8x8x2 inch pan and refrigerate for 1-2 hours until firm to the touch.
5. Carefully spread the **1 cup sour cream** evenly over the set Jello layer.
6. Gently pour the remaining Jello mixture over the **sour cream** layer.
7. Return to the refrigerator and chill for at least 4 hours, or overnight, until the salad is completely firm and set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This salad is best made the day before to ensure the layers are completely firm. Keep refrigerated until ready to serve.
