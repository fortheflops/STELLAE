# Nutty Caramels (Candy Fours)

> 📜 **Collection:** Patsy Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic recipe for rich, chewy, homemade caramels, loaded with chopped nuts. This traditional candy is made by cooking sugar, corn syrup, butter, and cream to the perfect firm-ball stage.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 30 mins | 2 hours | Approx. 5 lbs |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 8x8 or 9x9 inch pan
* Heavy-bottomed large saucepan
* Candy thermometer
* Buttered knife

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 Cup | [[White Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 1 Cup | [[Brown Sugar]] | Packed |
| 1 Cup | [[Dark Corn Syrup]] | None |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Butter]] | 1 stick |
| 2 Cups | [[Heavy Cream]] | Divided |
| 1 Tablespoon | [[Vanilla Extract]] | None |
| 1-2 Cups | [[Chopped Nuts]] | Pecans recommended |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and Base
1. Butter an 8x8 or 9x9 inch pan.
2. In a heavy-bottomed, large saucepan, combine the **1 Cup White Sugar**, **1 Cup Brown Sugar**, **1 Cup Dark Corn Syrup**, **1/2 Cup Butter**, and **1 Cup Heavy Cream**.

### Step 2: Boiling and Cooking
3. Place over medium heat and bring to a boil, stirring constantly.
4. Once at a rolling boil, slowly stream in the remaining **1 Cup Heavy Cream**, ensuring the mixture does not stop boiling.
5. Continue cooking, stirring frequently, until the mixture reaches the **firm-ball stage (245°F - 250°F)** on a candy thermometer.

### Step 3: Finishing and Cooling
6. Remove the saucepan from the heat and immediately stir in the **1 Tablespoon Vanilla Extract** and **1-2 Cups Chopped Nuts**.
7. Pour the hot caramel into the prepared pan and let cool completely at room temperature for several hours until firm.
8. Turn the caramel block out onto a cutting board, cut into squares with a buttered knife, and wrap in wax paper.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store wrapped caramels in an airtight container at room temperature for up to two weeks.
