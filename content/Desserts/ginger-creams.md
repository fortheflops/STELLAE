# Ginger Creams

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Maria | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A delightful historic recipe for spiced Ginger Creams featuring molasses and warm spices. Perfect for serving with afternoon tea or as a comforting homemade treat.*

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing the Batter
1. Cream together the **1/2 cup butter** and **1/2 cup sugar** in a large mixing bowl until smooth.
2. Beat in the **3 eggs**, **1/2 cup molasses**, **1/2 cup sour milk**, and **1 tbsp vinegar** until well combined.
3. Stir in the **1 tsp baking soda**, **1/2 tsp cloves**, **1/2 tsp nutmeg**, and **1 tsp ginger**.
4. Gradually mix in **about 2 cups flour** until a soft dough forms.

### Step 2: Baking
5. Drop spoonfuls of the dough onto a prepared baking sheet.
6. Bake until the edges are golden brown and the centers spring back when lightly touched.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The unbaked dough can be covered and refrigerated for up to 24 hours before dropping onto baking sheets and roasting.
