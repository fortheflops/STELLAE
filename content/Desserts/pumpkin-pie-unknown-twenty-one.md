# Pumpkin Pie

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty One | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic autumn pumpkin pie spiced with cinnamon, ginger, cloves, and nutmeg. Baked in a flaky 9-inch pie shell until perfectly set.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 55 mins | None | 8 servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large bowl
* 9-inch pie shell
* Knife

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing
1. Mix **3/4 cup sugar**, **1/2 tsp salt**, **1 tsp cinnamon**, **1/2 tsp ginger**, **1/4 tsp cloves**, and **1/4 tsp nutmeg** together.
2. Beat **2 eggs** in a large bowl.
3. Stir in **1 can pumpkin** and the spice/sugar mixture, then stir in **6 oz milk**.
4. Pour the filling into the **1 9" pie shell**.

### Step 2: Baking
5. Bake at 425°F for **15 minutes**.
6. Reduce temperature to 350°F and bake for an additional **40-50 minutes**, or until a knife inserted in the center comes out clean.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Bake completely and store in the refrigerator for up to 2 days before serving.
