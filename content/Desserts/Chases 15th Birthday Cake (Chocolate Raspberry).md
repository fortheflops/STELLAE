# Chase's 15th Birthday Cake (Chocolate Raspberry)

> 📜 **Collection:** UK5 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A decadent, multi-layered chocolate cake filled with a sweet cream cheese frosting and fresh raspberries, all covered in a rich chocolate whipped cream topping. A perfect celebration cake!*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Cake
1. Bake your **chocolate cake recipe** in **two 8-inch round pans** and let them cool completely on a wire rack until room temperature.

### Step 2: Make the Filling
2. In a medium bowl, beat the softened **1 (8 ounce) package cream cheese** and **1/2 cup butter** with an electric mixer until light and fluffy.
3. Gradually beat in **4 cups powdered sugar**, followed by **2 to 3 teaspoons milk** and **1 teaspoon vanilla**, mixing until smooth and creamy.

### Step 3: Make the Frosting
4. In a chilled mixing bowl, combine **1 cup whipping cream**, **1/2 cup baking cocoa**, **1/4 cup powdered sugar**, **1 tablespoon corn syrup**, and **1/2 teaspoon vanilla**.
5. Beat on high speed until stiff peaks form and the mixture holds its shape.

### Step 4: Assemble the Cake
6. Using a long serrated knife, carefully cut each cooled **chocolate cake** layer in half horizontally to create a total of **4 thin layers**.
7. Place the first layer, cut side up, on a serving plate. Spread with **one-third of the cream cheese filling** and sprinkle with about **1/3 of the 2 cups raspberries**.
8. Repeat with the second and third layers, then place the fourth layer on top, cut side down.

### Step 5: Finish the Cake
9. Frost the top and sides of the cake evenly with the **chocolate whipped cream**.
10. In a small saucepan, gently heat **1/4 cup cherry jam** until it is melted and brushable, then carefully brush the melted jam over the visible **raspberries** between the cake layers until glossy.
11. Sprinkle the top with **mini chocolate chips** if desired.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the finished cake covered in the refrigerator for up to 3 days. Bring to slightly cooler than room temperature before serving.
