# Easy Cream Cheese Crescent Bars

> 📜 **Collection:** UK | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A decadent and incredibly easy dessert made by sandwiching a rich, sweet cream cheese filling between two layers of flaky crescent roll dough, baked until golden and topped with a simple vanilla icing.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 30 mins | None | 12-16 bars |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 9x13 inch baking dish
* Medium mixing bowl
* Pastry brush
* Whisk

## Ingredients

### Bars
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 cans (8 oz each) | [[Crescent Rolls]] | Divided for top and bottom crusts |
| 2 packages (8 oz each) | [[Cream Cheese]] | Softened |
| 1 cup | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 1 | [[Egg]] | Separated into yolk and white |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Vanilla]] | Extract |

### Icing
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 cup | [[Powdered Sugar]] | Sifted |
| 2 tablespoons | [[Milk]] | Room temperature |
| Few drops | [[Vanilla]] | Extract |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Bottom Crust
1. Preheat oven to **350°F**. Butter a **9x13 inch dish** thoroughly.
2. Unroll one can of **crescent rolls** and press evenly into the bottom of the prepared dish, pinching the seams together to form a solid crust.

### Step 2: Filling and Top Crust
3. In a medium bowl, cream together the softened **cream cheese**, **sugar**, **egg yolk**, and **1 teaspoon of vanilla** until smooth and creamy.
4. Spread this **cream cheese mixture** evenly over the bottom crust.
5. Unroll the second can of **crescent rolls** and place it carefully on top, pinching the seams and sides to seal the filling completely.
6. Lightly beat the reserved **egg white** and brush it evenly over the top layer.

### Step 3: Bake and Ice
7. Bake for **30 minutes**, or until the top is golden brown and fragrant.
8. Let the dessert cool completely in the pan.
9. For the **icing**, whisk together the **powdered sugar**, **milk**, and a few drops of **vanilla** until smooth.
10. Drizzle the **icing** over the cooled bars and let it set completely before cutting and serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftover bars in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3-4 days.
