# Classic Nutty Thumbprint Cookies

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A rich and buttery shortbread cookie packed with finely chopped nuts for a dense, melt-in-your-mouth texture. These simple thumbprints are finished with a sweet jelly or candied cherry center.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 30 mins | None | 2-3 dozen |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* large mixing bowl
* electric mixer
* cookie sheet

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 Sticks (1 Cup) | [[Butter]] | softened |
| 1 Cup | [[Cake Flour]] | none |
| 8 Tbsp (1/2 Cup) | [[Sugar]] | none |
| 1 Tsp | [[Vanilla]] | none |
| 1 1/2 to 2 Cups | [[Nuts]] | finely chopped |
| As needed | [[Jelly]] or [[Cherries]] | for filling |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Dough
1. Preheat your oven to 350°F.
2. In a large bowl, cream the **2 sticks butter** and **8 tbsp sugar** together with an electric mixer until light and fluffy. Stir in the **1 tsp vanilla**.
3. On low speed, gradually add the **1 cup cake flour** and mix until a soft dough forms.
4. By hand, stir in the **1 1/2 to 2 cups nuts**.

### Step 2: Shape and Bake
5. Roll the dough into 1-inch balls and place them on an ungreased cookie sheet.
6. Use your thumb to press an indentation into the center of each ball of dough.
7. Bake for **20 to 30 minutes** until the cookies are set and the edges are lightly golden.
8. Let the cookies cool completely on the baking sheet, then fill the indentations with **jelly** or a piece of **candied cherry**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 5 days, or freeze the unbaked dough balls for up to 3 months.
