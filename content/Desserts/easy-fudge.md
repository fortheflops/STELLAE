# Easy Fudge

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Five | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *Indulge in this classic, simple-to-make homemade fudge packed with rich chocolate, marshmallows, and nuts. A timeless sweet treat perfect for gifting or holiday gatherings.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 5 mins | 2 hours | 16 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Saucepan
* 8x8 inch baking pan
* Stirring spoon

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 2/3 cups | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 1 small can (5 oz) | [[Evaporated Milk]] | Standard |
| 1/8 tsp | [[Salt]] | None |
| 16 large | [[Marshmallows]] | Whole |
| 1 pkg (6 oz) | [[Semi-Sweet Chocolate]] | Chips or chopped |
| 1 tsp | [[Vanilla]] | Extract |
| 1/2 cup | [[Chopped Nuts]] | Walnuts or pecans |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Cooking the Base
1. Mix **1 2/3 cups sugar**, **1/8 tsp salt**, **1 small can (5 oz) evaporated milk**, and **16 large marshmallows** together in a saucepan.
2. Cook over medium heat until it reaches a full boil.
3. Lower heat and stir continuously (boiling) for 5 minutes until the mixture thickens slightly.

### Step 2: Mixing and Setting
4. Remove from heat and immediately add **1 pkg (6 oz) semi-sweet chocolate**.
5. Stir continuously until fully melted and smooth.
6. Stir in **1 tsp vanilla** and **1/2 cup chopped nuts** until well combined.
7. Pour the mixture into an **8x8 pan**.
8. Allow to cool completely until set, then cut into squares.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature with parchment paper between layers for up to one week.
