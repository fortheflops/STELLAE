# Strawberry Pie

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Cream Cheese Base
1. In a medium bowl, beat **4 oz cream cheese**, **3 tbsp sugar**, and **1/2 tsp vanilla extract** until smooth and free of lumps.
2. Gently fold **1 cup heavy cream** (whipped) into the cream cheese mixture until light and fluffy.
3. Spread the mixture evenly over the bottom of the **1 pie crust**.

### Step 2: Prepare the Strawberry Filling
4. Cook **2 lbs strawberries** in a saucepan for **25 minutes** after they have thawed, stirring occasionally until they break down.
5. Stir in **2 cups strawberries**, **1 cup sugar**, and **1 pinch salt**.
6. In a small bowl, combine **1 tbsp gelatin**, **2 tbsp water**, and **1 tbsp lemon juice** to bloom.
7. Add the gelatin mixture to the warm strawberry mixture and put on low heat to melt the **gelatin** completely, ensuring no granules remain.
8. Remove from heat and let the filling cool for **30 minutes** until it is lukewarm but not yet set.

### Step 3: Assemble and Chill
9. Pour the cooled strawberry mixture over the cream cheese layer in the **1 pie crust**.
10. Refrigerate for at least **4 hours** until the gelatin is fully set and the pie is firm enough to slice cleanly.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The pie can be assembled and refrigerated up to 24 hours in advance. Keep chilled until ready to slice and serve.
