# Southern Pecan Pie

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty One | ✍️ **Attribution:** V. Bond | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic Southern pecan pie featuring a rich corn syrup and egg filling atop a bed of crunchy pecans. Baked to golden perfection with a clean-cutting center.*

---

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

* Mixing bowl

* Measuring spoons

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing and Assembly
1. Mix **3 beaten eggs**, **1/2 c sugar**, **1 c dark Karo corn syrup**, **1/4 t salt**, **1 t vanilla**, and **1/4# butter** (or oleo, melted) together in a bowl.
2. Spread **1 c pecans** in bottom of **1 9" pie shell** & pour filling over.

### Step 2: Baking
3. Bake 15 minutes at 425 degrees Fahrenheit; reduce temperature to 325 degrees Fahrenheit baking for 45 minutes or until knife comes out clean.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the baked pie covered in the refrigerator for up to 3 days. Bring to room temperature or warm slightly before serving.
