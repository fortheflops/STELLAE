# Cheesecake

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty One | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A delightfully simple and creamy no-bake cheesecake featuring a fluffy blend of cream cheese and whipped topping. Perfect for chilling ahead of time and garnishing with fresh fruit.*

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

## Ingredients

### Filling Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 8 oz. | [[Cream Cheese]] | Softened |
| 1/3 c | [[Sugar]] | Slightly less |
| 8 oz. | [[Cool Whip]] | Whipped topping |
| 1 | [[Graham Cracker Crust]] | Prepared |
| As needed | [[Fruit]] | For garnish |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing and Assembling
1. Mix **8 oz. cream cheese** and **1/3 c sugar** together until smooth.
2. Gently blend in **8 oz. cool whip** until fully incorporated and fluffy.
3. Spoon the mixture into the **1 graham cracker crust**.
4. Refrigerate for at least 3 hours or overnight until firmly set.
5. Garnish with fresh **fruit** before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Can be prepared up to 24 hours in advance and kept refrigerated until ready to serve.
