# The Murphys' Pecan Bon Bons

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** The Murphys | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A quick and easy recipe for chewy, nutty, bite-sized confections, similar to a blondie, baked in mini muffin tins. These delightful little cakes are loaded with brown sugar and pecans for the perfect treat.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 10 mins | None | 26 cookies |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mini muffin tins
* Paper liners
* Medium bowl
* Large bowl
* Whisk
* Wire cooling rack

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 cup | [[Flour]] | All-purpose |
| 1/4 teaspoon | [[Baking Powder]] | None |
| 1/4 teaspoon | [[Salt]] | None |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] | Large |
| 1 cup | [[Brown Sugar]] | Light, packed |
| 1 cup | [[Pecans]] | Chopped |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Mix Dry Ingredients
1. Preheat your oven to **350°F**. Line tiny muffin cups with paper liners.
2. In a medium bowl, whisk together the **1/2 cup flour**, **1/4 teaspoon baking powder**, and **1/4 teaspoon salt**.

### Step 2: Prepare Batter and Bake
3. In a separate large bowl, beat the **2 large eggs**. Gradually add the **1 cup light brown sugar** and beat until well combined.
4. Add the dry ingredients to the egg mixture and stir until just incorporated.
5. Fold in the **1 cup chopped pecans**.
6. Spoon the batter into the prepared mini muffin cups.
7. Bake for **8 to 10 minutes**, or until the centers are set and golden.

### Step 3: Cool and Store
8. Let the bon bons cool in the pan for a few minutes before transferring them to a wire rack to cool completely until firm to the touch.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 5 days.
