# Harvest Torte

> *A delightful autumn-inspired Harvest Torte packed with apples, dates, and walnuts. Perfect served warm or cold with a dollop of whipped cream or ice cream.*

## Instructions

### Preparation and Baking
1. Combine **4 cups apples**, **1/2 cup flour**, **1 egg**, **1 tsp vanilla**, **1 cup sugar**, **2 tsp baking powder**, **1 tbsp butter**, **1/2 cup walnuts**, and **1/2 cup dates** in a bowl.
2. Stir until thoroughly mixed, do not beat.
3. Turn into a greased 8x8x2-inch pan.
4. Bake in a hot oven at 400°F for **40 minutes** or until apples are tender.

### Serving
5. Serve hot or cold with whipped cream or ice cream.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Can be baked a day ahead and stored covered at room temperature or in the refrigerator; serve warmed or chilled.
