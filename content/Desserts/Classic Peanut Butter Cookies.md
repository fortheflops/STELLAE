# Classic Peanut Butter Cookies

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic, foolproof recipe for soft and chewy peanut butter cookies, featuring the iconic fork-tine criss-cross pattern. This recipe balances brown and white sugars for the perfect texture and flavor profile.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Dough
1. Preheat your oven to **375°F**.
2. In a large bowl, cream together the **1/2 cup shortening**, **1/2 cup peanut butter**, **1/2 cup white sugar**, and **1/2 cup brown sugar** with an electric mixer until light and fluffy.
3. Beat in the **1 egg** and **1 tsp vanilla extract** until well combined.
4. In a separate bowl, whisk together the **1 1/4 cups flour**, **3/4 tsp baking soda**, **1/2 tsp baking powder**, and **1/4 tsp salt**.
5. Gradually add the dry ingredients to the creamed mixture, mixing on low speed until a stiff dough forms.

### Step 2: Shape and Bake
6. Roll the dough into **1-inch balls**.
7. Place the balls on an ungreased baking sheet and press with a fork to create a signature crisscross pattern.
8. Bake for **10-12 minutes**, or until the edges are lightly browned and the centers are set.
9. Let the cookies cool on the sheet for a few minutes before transferring to a wire rack to cool completely.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 5 days, or freeze the unbaked dough balls for up to 3 months.
