# Pineapple Oatmeal Cookies

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A soft and chewy oatmeal drop cookie featuring a tropical twist of crushed pineapple and warm spices. These cookies are perfectly balanced with brown sugar and crunchy walnuts.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 15 mins | None | 3 dozen cookies |

### 🔪 Key Equipment

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 cup | [[Shortening]] | softened |
| 1/2 cup | Granulated [[Sugar]] | none |
| 1/2 cup | [[Brown Sugar]] | packed |
| 1 | [[Egg]] | large |
| 1 cup | [[Crushed Pineapple]] | well-drained |
| 1 1/2 cups | [[Oats]] | quick or old-fashioned |
| 1 cup | [[Flour]] | sifted all-purpose |
| 1/2 tsp | [[Baking Soda]] | none |
| 1/2 tsp | [[Salt]] | none |
| 1/2 tsp | [[Cinnamon]] | none |
| 1/8 tsp | [[Nutmeg]] | none |
| 1/2 cup | [[Walnuts]] | chopped |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and Creaming
1. Preheat your oven to 375°F and grease your cookie sheets.
2. In a large bowl, cream the **1/2 cup shortening** with the **1/2 cup granulated sugar** and **1/2 cup brown sugar** until light and fluffy.
3. Beat in the **1 egg** and the **1 cup crushed pineapple** until well incorporated.

### Step 2: Mixing and Baking
4. In a separate bowl, whisk together the **1 cup flour**, **1/2 tsp baking soda**, **1/2 tsp salt**, **1/2 tsp cinnamon**, and **1/8 tsp nutmeg**.
5. Gradually add the dry ingredients to the creamed mixture and stir until just combined.
6. By hand, fold in the **1 1/2 cups oats** and **1/2 cup walnuts**.
7. Drop by heaping teaspoonfuls onto the prepared cookie sheets and bake for **12 to 15 minutes**, or until the edges are golden brown and the centers are set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 5 days or freeze for up to 3 months.
