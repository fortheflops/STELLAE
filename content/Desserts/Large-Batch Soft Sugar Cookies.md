# Large-Batch Soft Sugar Cookies

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic, large-batch recipe for soft, cake-like sugar cookies. This simple dough is perfect for rolling out and cutting into shapes for any occasion, yielding a generous amount for parties or freezing.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Dough
1. In a large bowl, cream the **1 cup shortening** and **2 cups sugar** together with an electric mixer until light and fluffy.
2. Beat in the **3 eggs**, **2/3 cup milk**, and **1 tablespoon vanilla** until well combined.
3. In a separate bowl, whisk together the **5 cups flour**, **2 teaspoons baking powder**, **1 teaspoon baking soda**, and **1 pinch salt**.
4. Gradually add the dry ingredients to the wet ingredients, mixing on low speed until a soft, sticky dough forms.

### Step 2: Chill and Roll
5. Cover the dough and chill for at least **1 hour**, or until firm enough to handle.
6. Preheat your oven to **400°F**.
7. On a well-floured surface, roll the chilled dough out to approximately **1/4 inch** thickness.
8. Cut into desired shapes with cookie cutters and place them on an ungreased cookie sheet.

### Step 3: Bake
9. Bake for **6-8 minutes**. The cookies should be puffed and set, but not browned.
10. Let the cookies cool on the baking sheet for **1 minute** before transferring them to a wire rack to cool completely.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The dough can be prepared and chilled for up to 24 hours before rolling. Baked cookies freeze well in an airtight container for up to 3 months.
