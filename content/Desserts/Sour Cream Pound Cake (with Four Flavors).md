# Sour Cream Pound Cake (with Four Flavors)

> 📜 **Collection:** UK15 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic, dense pound cake featuring a complex flavor blend of vanilla, lemon, rum, and coconut extracts, finished with a simple almond glaze. This rich heritage recipe uses both butter and shortening for the ultimate crumb texture.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Bake the Cake
1. Preheat your oven to **325°F**. Generously grease and flour a **10-inch tube pan**.
2. In a large bowl, cream the **1 cup butter**, softened, and **1/2 cup shortening** together. Gradually add the **3 cups sugar** and beat with an electric mixer at medium speed until light and fluffy.
3. Add the **5 eggs** one at a time, beating well after each addition.
4. In a separate bowl, combine the **3 1/4 cups cake flour**, **1/2 teaspoon baking powder**, and **1/2 teaspoon salt**.
5. On low speed, add the dry ingredients to the creamed mixture alternately with the **1 cup milk**, beginning and ending with the flour mixture. Mix until just combined.
6. Stir in all four flavorings: **1 teaspoon vanilla extract**, **1 teaspoon lemon extract**, **1 teaspoon rum extract**, and **1 teaspoon coconut extract**.
7. Pour the batter into the prepared tube pan.
8. Bake for **1 hour and 30 minutes**, or until a long wooden skewer inserted into the center comes out clean and the top is golden brown and springy.
9. Let the cake cool in the pan on a wire rack for **10 minutes** before making the glaze.

### Step 2: Prepare and Apply Glaze
10. In a small saucepan, combine the **1/2 cup sugar** and **1/3 cup water**. Bring the mixture to a rolling boil.
11. Remove from the heat and stir in the **1/2 teaspoon almond extract**.
12. Drizzle the hot glaze over the warm cake while it is still in the pan. Let the cake cool completely before removing.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the cooled pound cake tightly wrapped in plastic wrap or stored in an airtight cake keeper at room temperature for up to 4 days.
