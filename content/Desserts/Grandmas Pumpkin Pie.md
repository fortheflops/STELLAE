# Grandma's Pumpkin Pie

> 📜 **Collection:** GailCollection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Grandma & Gail | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic, from-scratch pumpkin pie recipe featuring a smooth, spiced custard filling that sets up beautifully. Perfect for holiday gatherings with instructions for both single and double batches.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Mix
1. Preheat your oven to **425°F**.
2. In a large bowl, combine **2 cups pumpkin puree**, **1 cup sugar**, **2 tablespoons cornstarch**, **1 1/2 cups milk**, **1 beaten egg**, **1 teaspoon cinnamon**, **1 teaspoon salt**, and **1 tablespoon melted butter**. Whisk thoroughly until smooth and well incorporated.

### Step 2: Bake and Cool
3. Pour the smooth filling into your **uncooked pie crust**.
4. Bake at **425°F for 15 minutes**.
5. Reduce the oven temperature to **325°F and bake for an additional 45 minutes**, or until a knife inserted near the center comes out clean and the filling is nearly set with a slight jiggle.
6. Let the pie cool completely on a wire rack for at least **2 hours** until the filling firms up.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the baked pie covered in the refrigerator for up to 3 days. Serve chilled or at room temperature.
