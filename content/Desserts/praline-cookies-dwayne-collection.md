# Praline Cookies

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *Deliciously sweet homemade praline cookies featuring brown sugar and crunchy pecans. A simple drop cookie recipe baked to golden perfection.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 20 mins | None | 24 cookies |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Cookie sheet
* Measuring cups
* Measuring spoons

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 stick | [[Butter]] | Softened |
| 1 cup | [[Brown Sugar]] | Packed |
| 1 | [[Egg]] | Slightly beaten |
| 1 tsp | [[Vanilla]] | Extract |
| 1 cup | [[Flour]] | All-purpose |
| 1/2 cup | [[Pecans]] | Chopped |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing and Baking
1. Cream together the **1 stick butter** and **1 cup brown sugar**.
2. Mix in the **1 egg** (slightly beaten) and **1 tsp vanilla**.
3. Stir in the **1 cup flour** and **1/2 cup pecans** until just combined.
4. Drop by teaspoonfuls onto a cookie sheet.
5. Bake at 325 degrees for 20 mins or until set and lightly browned.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store baked cookies in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 5 days.
