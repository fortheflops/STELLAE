# Cheese Cake with Pastry Crust

> 📜 **Collection:** Georgia Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *An elegant, heirloom-style cheesecake featuring a delicate homemade pastry crust and a rich, lemon-scented cheese filling studded with sweet currants. Folded with whipped cream and whipped egg whites, it bakes to a beautifully light yet decadent texture.*

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

## Ingredients

### Pastry Crust
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 cup | [[Butter]] | softened |
| 1 | [[Egg]] | whole |
| 1/4 cup | [[Sugar]] | None |
| As needed | [[Flour]] | enough to roll |
| 1/2 tsp | [[Baking Powder]] | None |

### Cheese Filling
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Size of an egg (approx. 1/4 cup) | [[Butter]] | softened |
| 1 cup | [[Sugar]] | None |
| 1 | [[Lemon]] | juice and grated rind |
| 3 tbsp | [[Flour]] | None |
| 3 | [[Eggs]] | separated |
| 1/2 cup | [[Currants]] | None |
| 1 lb | [[Cheese]] | such as pot cheese or cottage cheese, rubbed through a sieve |
| 1/2 pint | [[Heavy Cream]] | whipped |

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare and Chill the Pastry
1. In a mixing bowl, combine the **1/2 cup butter**, **1 whole egg**, **1/4 cup sugar**, **1/2 tsp baking powder**, and **enough flour** to make a workable dough suitable for rolling.
2. Roll out the pastry dough on a lightly floured surface and press it into the bottom and sides of a springform pan.
3. Place the pastry-lined pan on ice (or in the refrigerator) for **1 hour** to chill thoroughly.

### Step 2: Prepare the Filling
4. Rub the **1 lb cheese** through a fine sieve into a bowl to ensure a perfectly smooth, lump-free texture.
5. In a separate large bowl, cream together the **egg-sized butter**, **1 cup sugar**, the juice and grated rind of **1 lemon**, and **3 tbsp flour** until light and fluffy.
6. Beat the **3 egg yolks** (from the **3 separated eggs**) into the creamed mixture, then stir in the sieved **1 lb cheese** and **1/2 cup currants**.
7. In a clean bowl, whip the **1/2 pint heavy cream** until stiff peaks form, then gently fold it into the cheese mixture.
8. In another clean bowl, beat the **3 egg whites** until stiff but not dry, and gently fold them into the batter until just incorporated.

### Step 3: Bake and Cool
9. Pour the filling into the chilled pastry-lined springform pan and smooth the top.
10. Bake in a preheated oven at **325°F** for **1 1/2 hours** until the edges are golden and the center is set but still retains a slight, elegant jiggle.
11. Turn off the oven, prop the door open slightly, and let the cheesecake cool slowly inside the oven to prevent cracking.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The pastry crust can be pressed into the pan and chilled overnight. For the best flavor and texture, refrigerate the baked cheesecake for at least 6 hours or overnight before slicing.
