# Quick Zucchini Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** Georgia Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *Discover a delightful Quick Zucchini Cake recipe featuring yellow cake mix, grated zucchini, and warm cinnamon. Perfect for a sweet treat, this moist cake is finished with a dusting of confectioners' sugar.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 50 mins | None | 16-20 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large bowl
* 10-inch tube pan
* Toothpick
* Wire rack

## Ingredients

### Cake Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |

| 1 box | [[Yellow cake mix]] | Standard size |
| 4 | [[Eggs]] | None |
| 1/2 cup | [[Vegetable oil]] | None |
| 1 tsp. | [[Cinnamon]] | None |
| 2 cups | [[Zucchini]] | Unpeeled, grated |
| 1/2 cup | [[Raisins]] | None |
| 1/2 cup | [[Nuts]] | Chopped |
| 1 Tbsp. | [[Vanilla]] | None |
| As desired | [[Confectioners' sugar]] | Optional |

---

## Instructions

### Mixing and Baking
1. Beat **1 box yellow cake mix**, **4 eggs**, **1/2 cup vegetable oil**, **1 tsp. cinnamon**, and **1 Tbsp. vanilla** together in a large bowl for 6-7 minutes until smooth.
2. Fold in **2 cups zucchini**, **1/2 cup raisins**, and **1/2 cup nuts** until evenly distributed.
3. Grease and flour a 10-inch tube pan, spoon in batter and bake at 350° for 40-50 minutes.
4. Test with toothpick at 40 minutes for doneness; retest at 5 min. intervals until the toothpick comes out clean.
5. Remove cake from pan and let cool completely on wire rack.
6. Sprinkle with **confectioners' sugar**, if desired, before slicing and serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the cooled cake covered at room temperature for up to 3 days, or wrap tightly and freeze for up to 2 months.
