# Thelma Block's Toll House Squares

> 📜 **Collection:** Jill Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Thelma Block | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A moist, brownie-like bar cookie featuring a unique marbled chocolate top. These classic Toll House-inspired squares are perfect for freezing and sharing.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Batter
1. Preheat your oven to 375°F and grease a 9x9x2 inch baking pan.
2. In a medium bowl, sift together the **1 cup + 2 tbsp all-purpose flour**, **1/2 teaspoon baking soda**, and **1/2 teaspoon salt**.
3. In a large bowl, blend the **1/2 cup shortening or margarine**, **6 tablespoons granulated sugar**, and **6 tablespoons brown sugar**.
4. Beat in the **1 egg**, **1/2 teaspoon vanilla extract**, and **1/2 teaspoon water** until well combined.
5. Add the flour mixture to the wet ingredients, mix well, and stir in the **1/2 cup nuts**.
6. Spread the batter evenly in the prepared pan.

### Step 2: Marble and Bake
7. Sprinkle the **1 cup nestle's semi-sweet chocolate morsels** over the top of the batter.
8. Place the pan in the oven for **1 to 2 minutes** just until the chocolate begins to soften.
9. Remove the pan from the oven and quickly run a knife through the batter and softened chips to create a marbled effect.
10. Immediately return the pan to the oven and bake for an additional **12 to 15 minutes** until set but still moist; do not overbake.
11. Let the pan cool on a wire rack before cutting into squares.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** These squares freeze beautifully; store in an airtight container for up to 3 months.
