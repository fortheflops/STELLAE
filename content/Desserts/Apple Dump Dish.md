# Apple Dump Dish

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic, effortless dessert featuring layers of spiced apple pie filling, cake mix, and buttery pecans. This warm, gooey cobbler-style treat bakes into a golden, bubbly delight perfect for any occasion.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 1 hour | None | 8–10 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 9x13 inch baking dish
* Oven

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 (21 oz) Can | [[Comstock Sliced Apples]] | Apple pie filling |
| 1/4 Cup | [[Brown Sugar]] | Packed |
| 1 Box | [[Cake Mix]] | Spiced, dry |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Pecans]] | Chopped |
| 2 Sticks (1 Cup) | [[Butter]] | Sliced into thin pats |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Assembly
1. Preheat your oven to 325°F and grease a 9x13 inch baking dish.
2. Pour the **1 (21 oz) can Comstock Sliced Apples** into the bottom of the dish and spread evenly.
3. Sprinkle the **1/4 cup brown sugar** over the apples.
4. Evenly distribute the **1 box spiced cake mix** over the apple layer.
5. Scatter the **1/2 cup chopped pecans** over the top of the cake mix.
6. Arrange the **2 sticks butter** slices evenly across the entire surface.

### Step 2: Bake
7. Bake for **1 hour**, or until the top is golden brown and the filling is bubbling around the edges. Let cool slightly before serving warm.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftovers in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days; reheat in the oven or microwave before serving.
