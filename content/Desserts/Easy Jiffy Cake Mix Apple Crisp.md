# Easy Jiffy Cake Mix Apple Crisp

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A simple and delicious shortcut apple crisp featuring a buttery Jiffy yellow cake mix topping. This foolproof dessert is perfect for quick cravings and uses minimal ingredients.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5 mins | 25 mins | None | 6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 8x8 inch baking dish
* knife

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 4-5 medium | [[Apples]] | peeled, cored, and sliced |
| 1/2 Cup (1 stick) | [[Butter]] | cut into pats |
| 1 box | [[Jiffy Yellow Cake Mix]] | dry |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Assembly and Baking
1. Preheat the oven to 350°F. Lightly grease your baking dish.
2. Arrange the **4-5 medium apples** in an even layer in the bottom of the dish.
3. Scatter the **1/2 cup (1 stick) butter** pats over the top of the **apples**.
4. Sprinkle the **1 box Jiffy yellow cake mix** evenly over the **apples** and **butter**.
5. Bake for **25 minutes**, or until the topping is golden brown and the **apples** are tender and bubbly.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftovers in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days; reheat in the oven to crisp the topping.
