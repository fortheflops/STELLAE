# Peanut Clusters

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Marcile | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *Indulge in these rich and easy homemade peanut clusters, featuring a melted blend of white bark and chocolates mixed with salted Spanish peanuts. Drop by spoonfuls onto wax paper and let set for a delightful sweet treat.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 5 mins | 1.5 hours | 24 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Double boiler
* Microwave
* Wax paper
* Spoon

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 lbs | [[White bark]] | Chopped |
| 12 oz | [[Milk chocolate chips]] | Hershey |
| 4 oz | [[Semi-sweet chocolate]] | Can use unsweetened |
| 1 1/2 lbs | [[Salted Spanish peanuts]] | Whole |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Melting and Mixing
1. Melt the **2 lbs white bark**, **12 oz milk chocolate chips**, and **4 oz semi-sweet chocolate** together in a double boiler or microwave until smooth and completely melted.
2. Stir in the **1 1/2 lbs salted Spanish peanuts** until fully coated in the melted chocolate mixture.

### Step 2: Shaping and Setting
3. Drop the mixture by the teaspoon onto sheets of wax paper.
4. Let set for **1 1/2 hours** until firm and completely hardened.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 2 weeks, separating layers with wax paper.
