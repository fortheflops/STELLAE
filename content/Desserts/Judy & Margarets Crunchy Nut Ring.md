# Judy & Margaret's Crunchy Nut Ring

> 📜 **Collection:** General Archive | ✍️ **Attribution:** Judy & Margaret | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A wonderfully easy pull-apart bread from Judy & Margaret, made with canned biscuits arranged in a fluted pan and baked in a simple, buttery caramel-nut glaze.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 30 mins | 3 mins | 10-12 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Small saucepan
* Fluted tube pan (Bundt pan)
* Wire rack
* Serving platter

---
## Ingredients

### Caramel Glaze
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| ½ cup | [[Oleo]] or Butter | Melted |
| ¾ cup | [[Brown Sugar]] | Packed |
| ¼ cup | [[Syrup]] | Light corn syrup |
| ½ to 1 cup | [[Nuts]] | Chopped |

### Bread
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 Cans | [[Biscuits]] | Refrigerated |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Pan and Glaze
1. Preheat oven to **350°F**. Generously grease a fluted tube pan (Bundt pan).
2. In a small saucepan, melt the **½ cup oleo or butter**, **¾ cup brown sugar**, and **¼ cup syrup** together. Stir in the **½ to 1 cup nuts** until well combined.
3. Pour about **¼ cup** of the caramel-nut mixture into the bottom of the prepared pan.

### Step 2: Assemble and Bake
4. Separate the **2 cans biscuits** from both cans. Arrange the whole **biscuits** on their edges, overlapping them, all around the pan.
5. Pour the remaining caramel-nut mixture evenly over the **biscuits**.
6. Bake for **20 to 30 minutes**, or until the **biscuits** are puffed and golden brown and fragrant.

### Step 3: Cool and Invert
7. Remove from the oven and let the pan cool on a wire rack for **exactly 3 minutes**. Do not let it cool longer.
8. Place a serving platter over the pan and carefully invert it to release the bread. Serve warm.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Best served warm immediately after baking; the critical 3-minute cooling time ensures the caramel sets without hardening permanently into the pan.
