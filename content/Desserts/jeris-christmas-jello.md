# Jeri's Christmas Jello

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Five | ✍️ **Attribution:** Jerr Bowden | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *Delight in Jeri's Christmas Jello, a festive holiday dessert featuring cherry jello, crushed pineapple, cooked rice, maraschino cherries, and whipped topping. This nostalgic recipe makes a wonderful sweet treat for family gatherings.*

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 pkg. | [[Cherry Jello]] | Gelatin |
| 1 cup | [[Boiling Water]] | Hot |
| 5 | [[Ice Cubes]] | For cooling |
| 1/4 cup | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 1 small can | [[Crushed Pineapple]] | Drained |
| 1/4 cup | [[Cooked Rice]] | Prepared |
| 20 | [[Maraschino Cherries]] | Cut up |
| 1 pint | [[Kool Whip]] | Thawed |
| 1/2 cup | [[Chopped Nuts]] | Optional |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Base Preparation
1. Dissolve **1 pkg. cherry jello** in **1 cup boiling water**.
2. Add **5 ice cubes** and stir until completely melted.

### Step 2: Mixing Fruits and Rice
3. Stir in **1/4 c. sugar**, **small can crushed pineapple**, and **1/4 c. cooked rice**.
4. Cut up **20 maraschino cherries** and add them to the mixture.

### Step 3: Setting and Finishing
5. Let the jello set up until firm and chilled.
6. Gently blend in **1 pint Kool Whip** and **1/2 c. chopped nuts** until smooth and fully incorporated.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Prepare the jello base and let it set in the refrigerator up to 24 hours in advance, folding in the whipped topping just before serving.
