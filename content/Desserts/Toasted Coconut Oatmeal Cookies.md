# Toasted Coconut Oatmeal Cookies

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A rich and chewy oatmeal drop cookie featuring a deep, nutty flavor profile achieved by toasting the oats and coconut. These classic cookies are finished with a signature sugar-dipped glass press for a perfect texture.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Toasting and Prep
1. Preheat oven to 400°F. Spread **2 cups quick oats** and **2/3 cup flaked coconut** on a baking sheet and toast until lightly golden and fragrant; set aside to cool.

### Step 2: Mixing the Dough
2. In a large bowl, cream **1 cup butter or margarine** and **1 cup sugar** until fluffy. Beat in **2 eggs**, **3 tablespoons milk**, and **1 1/2 teaspoons vanilla**.
3. In a separate bowl, sift together **1 1/2 cups flour**, **1/2 teaspoon baking soda**, and **1/2 teaspoon salt**. Gradually incorporate into the wet mixture.
4. Stir in the cooled **toasted oats** and **toasted coconut** by hand.

### Step 3: Shaping and Baking
5. Drop dough by rounded teaspoonfuls 2 inches apart on an ungreased cookie sheet. Dip the bottom of a glass in **sugar** and flatten each cookie.
6. Bake for **8 to 10 minutes** until golden brown. Cool on a wire rack until firm and crisp.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 5 days or freeze for up to 3 months.
