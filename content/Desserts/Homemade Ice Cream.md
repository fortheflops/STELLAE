# Homemade Ice Cream

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic, no-cook recipe for 4 quarts of creamy homemade ice cream, easily customizable with fresh bananas or strawberries. This simple base yields a rich, velvety texture perfect for warm weather gatherings.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 30 mins | 4 hours | 4 Quarts |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large mixing bowl
* Wire whisk
* 4-quart ice cream maker
* Freezer-safe container

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 6 Cups | [[Half & Half]] | cold |
| 2 Cups | [[Whole Milk]] | cold |
| 1 Can | [[Sweetened Condensed Milk]] | standard size |
| 1 1/2 Cups | [[Sugar]] | granulated |
| 3 Tablespoons | [[Vanilla Extract]] | pure |
| 4-6 | [[Bananas]] | mashed |
| 1 Quart | [[Strawberries]] | mashed, optional alternative |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Base
1. In a large bowl, whisk together the **6 Cups Half & Half**, **2 Cups Whole Milk**, **1 Can Sweetened Condensed Milk**, **1 1/2 Cups Sugar**, and **3 Tablespoons Vanilla Extract** until the sugar is completely dissolved and the mixture is smooth.

### Step 2: Add Fruit and Churn
2. Gently stir in your choice of **4-6 Mashed Bananas** or **1 Quart Mashed Strawberries**.
3. Pour the mixture into the canister of a 4-quart ice cream maker and churn according to the manufacturer's directions until the mixture is thick and frozen.

### Step 3: Serve or Ripen
4. Serve immediately for a soft-serve consistency, or transfer to a freezer-safe container and freeze for at least 4 hours until firm and scoopable.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight, freezer-safe container for up to 2 weeks; for best texture, let sit at room temperature for 5 minutes before scooping.
