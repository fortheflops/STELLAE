# Fresh Apple Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Five | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A delightful old-fashioned fresh apple cake packed with chopped cooking apples, pecans, and warm cinnamon. Baked to golden perfection in a tube pan for a moist, tender crumb.*

---

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 25 mins | 1 hr | 30 mins | 12 servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl

## Ingredients

### Cake Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3 cups | [[Flour]] | Sifted |
| 1 tsp | [[Salt]] | None |
| 1 tsp | [[Baking Soda]] | None |
| 1 tsp | [[Cinnamon]] | None |
| 3 cups | [[Cooking Apples]] | Chopped |
| 1 1/4 cups | [[Cooking Oil]] | None |
| 2 tsp | [[Vanilla Extract]] | None |
| 2 cups | [[Sugar]] | None |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] | None |
| 1/4 cup | [[Pecans]] | Chopped |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Batter Preparation
1. Sift together the **3 cups sifted flour**, **1 tsp salt**, **1 tsp soda**, and **1 tsp cinnamon**.
2. Add **1 1/4 c. cooking oil** and **2 tsp vanilla** to the dry mixture and beat until it forms a smooth batter.
3. In a separate bowl, beat the **2 eggs** until foamy, then gradually add **2 c. sugar** until well blended.
4. Fold the egg mixture thoroughly into the flour batter, then gently fold in **1/4 c. pecans** and **3 c. chopped cooking apples**.

### Step 2: Baking
5. Pour the batter into a greased and floured tube pan.
6. Bake at 350 degrees for 1 hour, or until a tester inserted near the center comes out clean.
7. Let set in the pan until cool before inverting and slicing.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store tightly wrapped at room temperature for up to 3 days, or freeze the baked cake without glaze for up to 2 months.
