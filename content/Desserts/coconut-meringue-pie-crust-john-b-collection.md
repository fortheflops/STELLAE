# Coconut Meringue Pie Crust

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A delicate and crispy coconut meringue pie crust made with flaked coconut, whipped egg whites, and sugar. This unique crust provides a sweet, tropical foundation for your favorite pie fillings.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 30 mins | None | 1 pie crust |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 9-inch pie pan
* Mixing bowl
* Electric mixer

---
## Ingredients

### Crust Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2/3 cup | [[Flaked Coconut]] | divided |
| About 3 tbsp | [[Confectioners Sugar]] | None |
| 2 | [[Egg Whites]] | None |
| 1/8 tsp | [[Salt]] | None |
| 2/3 cup | [[Granulated Sugar]] | None |
| 1/2 tsp | [[Vanilla]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation
1. Toss **2/3 cup flaked coconut** with **about 3 tbsp confectioners sugar** to coat lightly, set aside.

### Step 2: Mixing and Baking
2. Combine **2 egg whites** and **1/8 tsp salt** and beat until foamy.
3. Gradually add **2/3 cup granulated sugar**, beating well after each addition. Continue beating to stiff peaks.
4. Fold in **1/2 tsp vanilla** and the prepared coconut.
5. Spread in a greased 9-inch pie pan and bake at 325° for 30 minutes, or until the meringue feels dry and firm.
6. Cool completely before filling.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Bake the crust a few hours in advance and store in a dry place until ready to fill to maintain its crisp texture.
