# Butterscotch No-Bake Cookies

> 📜 **Collection:** The Loraine Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A wonderfully simple and quick no-bake cookie featuring a sweet and salty blend of butterscotch and peanut butter. These crunchy treats are easily prepared with Frosted Flakes and require minimal effort.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 5 mins | 30 mins | 65 cookies |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* double boiler
* cookie sheets
* wax paper
* mixing spoon

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 22 oz total | [[Butterscotch Chips]] | 2 small packages |
| 1 cup | [[Chunky Peanut Butter]] | room temperature |
| 3 cups | [[Sugar Frosted Flakes]] | cereal |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare and Melt
1. Line cookie sheets with wax paper.
2. In the top of a double boiler, melt the **22 oz butterscotch chips** and **1 cup chunky peanut butter** together over low heat, stirring until the mixture is smooth and glossy.

### Step 2: Combine and Chill
3. Remove the mixture from the heat.
4. Gently fold in the **3 cups sugar frosted flakes** until they are evenly coated, being careful not to crush the cereal flakes.
5. Drop by teaspoonfuls onto the prepared wax paper and chill in the refrigerator until the cookies are firm to the touch.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 1 week to maintain crunch.
