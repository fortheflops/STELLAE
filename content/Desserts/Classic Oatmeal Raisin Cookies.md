# Classic Oatmeal Raisin Cookies

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic recipe for soft and chewy oatmeal raisin cookies featuring a warm blend of cinnamon and nutmeg. These hearty drop cookies are perfect for any occasion.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare and Cream
1. Preheat your oven to **375°F** and grease your cookie sheets.
2. In a large bowl, cream the **3/4 cup shortening** and **1 cup brown sugar** together with an electric mixer until light and fluffy. Beat in the **2 eggs** one at a time.

### Step 2: Combine Dry Ingredients
3. In a separate medium bowl, sift or whisk together the **1 cup flour**, **1 tsp baking powder**, **1/2 tsp salt**, **1 tsp cinnamon**, and **1/4 tsp nutmeg**.

### Step 3: Mix and Bake
4. On low speed, add half of the dry ingredients to the creamed mixture and mix.
5. Beat in the **1/3 cup milk** until combined, then add the remaining dry ingredients and mix well.
6. By hand, stir in the **3 cups oats** and **1 cup raisins**.
7. Drop by rounded teaspoonfuls onto the prepared cookie sheets.
8. Bake for **10 to 15 minutes**, or until the edges are golden brown and the centers are set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 5 days or freeze for up to 3 months.
