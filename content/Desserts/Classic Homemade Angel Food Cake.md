# Classic Homemade Angel Food Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** General Archive | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic, light, and airy homemade angel food cake made from scratch using a precise method of separating sugars for a perfectly fluffy meringue.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 45 mins | 2 hours | One 10-inch cake |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 10-inch ungreased tube pan
* Large mixing bowl
* Electric mixer
* Sifter
* Cooling bottle or pan feet
* Knife

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |

| 1 1/2 C. | [[Egg White]]s | room temperature |
| 1 C. | [[Flour]] | sifted |
| 3/4 C. | [[Sugar]] | for flour mixture |
| 1/4 t. | [[Salt]] | none |
| 1 1/2 t. | [[Cream of Tartar]] | none |
| 1 1/2 t. | [[Vanilla]] | flavoring |
| 3/4 C. | [[Sugar]] | for egg white mixture |

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Sift
1. Preheat oven to **375°F**. Have a **10-inch ungreased tube pan** ready.
2. Sift the **1 C. flour** and the first **3/4 C. sugar** together **4 times**. Set this mixture aside.

### Step 2: Whip Meringue
3. In a large, clean bowl, pour the **1 1/2 C. egg whites**. Add the **1 1/2 t. cream of tartar**, **1/4 t. salt**, and **1 1/2 t. flavoring**.
4. Beat the egg white mixture until it is stiff enough to hold in soft peaks, but remains moist and glossy.

### Step 3: Add Sugar and Fold
5. Gradually add the second **3/4 C. sugar** to the egg whites, **2 tablespoons** at a time, beating well after each addition.
6. Sift about **1/4 of the flour-sugar mixture** over the beaten egg whites. Fold in gently by hand or beat on the lowest possible mixer speed.
7. Repeat sifting and folding in the flour mixture, **1/4 at a time**, until it is all incorporated. Do not overmix.

### Step 4: Bake and Cool
8. Pour the batter into the **ungreased 10" tube pan**.
9. Bake at **375°F** for **40-45 minutes**, or until the cake springs back when lightly touched.
10. Immediately invert the pan (placing the center tube over a bottle or on the pan's feet) and let it cool completely upside down for **1-2 hours** until cool to the touch before removing.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the cooled cake wrapped tightly in plastic wrap or stored in an airtight cake keeper at room temperature for up to 3 days.
