# Ritz Cracker Dessert

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A delightful and unique dessert made with crushed Ritz crackers, whipped egg whites, and pecans. Baked to a light, golden finish and best served with whipped cream or ice cream.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 25 mins | None | 6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Loaf pan
* Mixing bowl
* Electric mixer

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 30 | [[Ritz crackers]] | Crushed |
| 1 cup | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 3 | [[Egg whites]] | Separated |
| 1 tsp | [[Vanilla]] | Extract |
| 1/2 cup | [[Nuts]] | Chopped |
| 1/2 tsp | [[Baking powder]] | None |
| 3/4 cup | [[Pecans]] | Chopped |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and Mixing
1. Do not grease the **loaf pan**.
2. Beat the **3 egg whites** until stiff, then add the **1 cup sugar** gradually.
3. Fold in the crushed **30 ritz crackers**, **1 tsp vanilla**, **1/2 cup nuts**, **1/2 t. B.P.** (baking powder), and **3/4 cup c. pecan**.

### Step 2: Baking
4. Bake at 350 degrees for **20 to 25 min** until set and lightly golden.
5. Serve with whipped cream or ice cream.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Best served fresh on the day it is baked.
