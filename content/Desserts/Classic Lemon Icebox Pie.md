# Classic Lemon Icebox Pie

> 📜 **Collection:** General Archive | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A wonderfully simple no-bake pie, this classic Lemon Icebox Pie features a tangy, creamy filling made from just three main ingredients set in a crisp crust.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 0 mins | 4 hours | Two 9-inch pies |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large mixing bowl
* Wire whisk
* Rubber spatula
* Two 9-inch pie pans

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 can | [[Eagle Brand Milk]] | Sweetened condensed milk |
| 1 large container | [[Cool Whip]] | Thawed |
| Juice of 1 large | [[Lemon]] | 2 are better for a tarter pie |
| 1 cup | [[Pecans]] | Chopped |
| 2 (9-inch) | [[Pie Crust]] | Pre-baked, graham cracker or vanilla wafer recommended |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mix the Filling
1. In a large bowl, combine the **1 can Eagle Brand milk** and **juice of 1 large lemon**. Whisk well until the mixture begins to thicken on its own.

### Step 2: Fold and Assemble
2. Gently fold in the thawed **1 large container Cool Whip** until the mixture is smooth and no streaks remain.
3. Divide the chopped **1 cup pecans** equally and sprinkle them into the bottom of the **2 (9-inch) pre-baked pie crusts**.
4. Pour the filling mixture evenly over the pecans in each crust until smooth.

### Step 3: Chill and Set
5. Refrigerate the pies for at least 4 hours, or until completely firm and set before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store sealed in the refrigerator for up to 4 days.
