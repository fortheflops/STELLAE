# Soda Cracker Torte

> 📜 **Collection:** GC | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic, vintage torte with a unique, chewy texture and rich nutty flavor. This flourless dessert gets its structure from crushed soda crackers and beaten egg whites.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 30 mins | None | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 9-inch pie plate
* Round cake pan
* Medium bowl
* Clean bowl
* Electric mixer

| 3/4 cup | [[Soda Crackers]] | Finely crushed, about 18-20 crackers |

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Batter Base
1. Preheat your oven to **350°F**. Grease a **9-inch pie plate** or round cake pan.
2. In a medium bowl, combine the **3 large** **[[Eggs]]** (yolks only), **1 cup** **[[Sugar]]**, **3/4 cup** chopped **[[Nuts]]**, **3/4 cup** crushed **[[Soda Crackers]]**, **1/2 teaspoon** **[[Baking Powder]]**, and a **dash** of **[[Salt]]**. Mix well until thoroughly combined.

### Step 2: Whip and Fold
3. In a separate, clean bowl, beat the remaining **3 large** **[[Eggs]]** (whites only) with an electric mixer until stiff peaks form.
4. Gently fold the beaten egg whites into the yolk mixture in three additions until just combined and no white streaks remain.

### Step 3: Bake and Serve
5. Pour the batter into the greased pan or pie plate.
6. Bake for **30 minutes** until the torte will puff up and be golden brown.
7. Let the torte cool completely in the pan.
8. To serve, spread with tart **[[Jam]]** or top with **[[Whipped Cream]]** and fresh **[[Fruit]]**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the cooled torte tightly covered at room temperature for up to 2 days, or refrigerate for up to 4 days.
