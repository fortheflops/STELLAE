# Pecan Crunch Pie

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A delightful and crunchy pecan pie featuring a whipped meringue-like base loaded with crushed graham crackers and chopped pecans. Chilled to perfection and topped with freshly whipped cream.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 25 mins | 4 hours | 6 servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Pie pan
* Mixing bowl

## Ingredients

### Filling and Crust
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3 | [[Egg whites]] | Beaten stiff |
| 1/2 tsp | [[Baking powder]] | None |
| 1 cup | [[Sugar]] | None |
| 1 cup | [[Whipping cream]] | Divided |
| 11 | [[Graham crackers]] | Squares, crushed |
| 1 cup | [[Pecans]] | Chopped |
| 1 tsp | [[Vanilla]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing and Baking
1. Beat **3 egg whites** and **1/2 tsp baking powder**. Add **1 cup sugar** very slowly.
2. Beat until very stiff.
3. Crush **11 graham crackers** squares. Add them and **1 cup pecans** (by folding) to the **3 egg whites** mixture.
4. Add **1 tsp vanilla**, spread in a heavily buttered **10 inch pie pan**.
5. Bake at 350° for **25 min** until set and lightly golden.

### Step 2: Chilling and Serving
6. Chill at least **4 to 5 hours** until completely set.
7. Whip remaining **1 cup whipping cream** and spread on pie before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Keep refrigerated for up to 24 hours before serving for the best texture.
