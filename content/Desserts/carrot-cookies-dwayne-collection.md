## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Dough
1. Preheat your oven to 375°F (190°C) and line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
2. In a large mixing bowl, cream together **3/4 cup shortening** and **3/4 cup sugar** until the mixture is light and fluffy.
3. Beat in **1 egg**, **1 cup cooked mashed carrots**, **1 tsp vanilla extract**, and **1 tsp lemon extract** until thoroughly combined.
4. In a separate bowl, whisk together **2 cups flour**, **2 tsp baking powder**, and **1 tsp salt** (if using).
5. Gradually stir the dry ingredients into the wet carrot mixture until a soft, cohesive dough forms.

### Step 2: Bake the Cookies
6. Drop the dough by rounded tablespoonfuls onto the prepared baking sheet, leaving about 2 inches of space between each cookie.
7. Bake for **10 to 12 minutes** until the edges are lightly golden and the tops spring back when gently touched.
8. Let the cookies cool on the baking sheet for 5 minutes before transferring them to a wire cooling rack to cool completely.

### Step 3: Frost (Optional)
9. In a small bowl, combine **1 1/2 cups powdered sugar**, **1 tbsp melted butter**, and **orange rind as desired**.
10. Stir in **orange juice as needed**, a teaspoon at a time, until the frosting reaches a smooth, spreadable consistency.
11. Spread the glaze over the completely cooled cookies and let it set before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store frosted or unfrosted cookies in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 3 days. Unfrosted cookies can be frozen for up to 3 months; thaw and frost before serving.
