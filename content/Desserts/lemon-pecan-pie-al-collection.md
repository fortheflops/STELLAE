# Lemon Pecan Pie

> 📜 **Collection:** Al Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A delightful twist on classic pecan pie, featuring a bright splash of fresh lemon juice to balance the rich sweetness. Baked in a flaky pastry shell, it is a perfect dessert for any gathering.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 45 mins | None | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Hand mixer
* 9-inch pie pan
* Oven

---
## Ingredients

### Filling
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 1/2 cups | [[Sugar]] | None |
| 3 | [[Eggs]] | None |
| 1/4 cup | [[Butter]] | softened |
| Juice of 1 (or 1/3 cup) | [[Lemon Juice]] | None |
| 3/4 to 1 cup | [[Pecans]] | None |

### Crust
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | [[Pie Shell]] | 9-inch, unbaked |

---

## Instructions

### Preparation and Baking
1. In a mixing bowl, beat together the **1 1/2 cups sugar**, **3 eggs**, **1/4 cup butter**, and **1/3 cup lemon juice** until well combined and smooth.
2. Stir in the **3/4 to 1 cup pecans**.
3. Pour the mixture into the **9-inch unbaked pie shell**.
4. Bake at **350°F** for about **45 minutes** until the filling is set with a slight jiggle in the center and the crust is beautifully golden brown.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Can be baked a day ahead and stored at room temperature or refrigerated. Serve at room temperature or slightly warmed.
