# High Altitude Pound Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Seven | ✍️ **Attribution:** Ginger | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A delightful high-altitude pound cake recipe adapted for mountain baking. Rich and moist with a tender crumb.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 1 hour 15 mins | 30 mins | 12 servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large mixing bowl
* Measuring cup
* Electric mixer
* Rubber spatula
* 10-inch tube pan

## Ingredients

### Cake Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3 c. | [[Cake flour]] | Sifted (or regular flour minus 2 Tbsp per cup) |
| 1 3/4 c. | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 1 tspn. | [[Baking powder]] | None |
| 1 1/2 tspn. | [[Salt]] | None |
| 3/4 c. | [[Shortening]] | None |
| 4 | [[Eggs]] | Plus enough milk to make 1 cup total in measuring cup |
| 3/4 c. | [[Milk]] | Divided |
| 1 1/2 tspn. | [[Vanilla extract]] | Or rum flavoring, or 3 Tbsp extract of lemon |

---

## Instructions

### Mixing the Batter
1. Place sifted **3 c. cake flour**, **1 3/4 c. sugar**, **1 tspn. baking powder**, and **1 1/2 tspn. salt** in large mixing bowl, then add **3/4 c. shortening**.
2. Put **4 eggs** in measuring cup and fill with milk to the **1 c.** mark. Pour into dry ingredients.
3. Beat on medium speed for 3 minutes, or 300 strokes by hand. Keep sides of bowl clean with scraper.

### Adding Flavor and Baking
4. Pour in **3/4 c. milk** and **1 1/2 tspn. vanilla** and beat for 2 minutes, or 200 strokes.
5. Pour into **10" tube pan**, which has been greased and floured.
6. Bake for 1 1/4 hours at 350°F, until golden and a tester comes out clean.
7. Cool completely in pan before removing.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** If cake becomes stale before using, it can be sliced and toasted on broiler.
