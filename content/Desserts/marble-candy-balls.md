# Marble Candy Balls

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A delightful old-fashioned recipe for small marble-sized sweet treats rolled in powdered sugar. These handmade confections keep indefinitely and make wonderful gifts.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 5 mins | None | 6 balls |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Saucepan
* Wax paper

---
## Ingredients

### Syrup and Coating
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3 T | [[Sugar]] | plus extra for rolling |
| 3 T | [[Water]] | none |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Making the Syrup and Shaping
1. Boil **3 T sugar** and **3 T water** to a thick syrup.
2. Pour this over the above mixture.
3. Roll into small balls about the size of marbles.
4. Turn the balls into powdered sugar and roll individually on waxed paper until fully coated and set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Will keep indefinitely when stored properly on waxed paper.
