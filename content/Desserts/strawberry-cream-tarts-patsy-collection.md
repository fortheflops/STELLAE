# Strawberry Cream Tarts

> 📜 **Collection:** Patsy Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic handmade pastry shell recipe filled with smooth cream and fresh strawberries. Baked to perfection at 400°F, these delicate tarts make a delightful single-serve dessert.*

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Dough
1. Sift the **2 3/4 cups flour**, **1 cup icing sugar**, and **1/4 tsp salt** through a sieve into a big bowl and mix together.
2. Make a well in the middle of the dry ingredients.
3. In a separate bowl, beat the **2 eggs** together with **1 tsp vanilla extract**.
4. Add the **1/2 cup sweet butter**, **1/2 cup shortening**, and the egg mixture into the flour well. Cut into the dry mixture using 2 knives until a cohesive dough forms.

### Step 2: Shape and Bake
5. Roll out dough on a lightly floured surface and press fit into tart molds.
6. Bake in a preheated oven at 400°F for **10 to 12 minutes** until pale golden brown.
7. Remove from oven and allow the shells to cool completely.

### Step 3: Assemble
8. Fill each cooled pastry shell with **cream**, then top generously with fresh **strawberries**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Baked tart shells can be stored in an airtight container for up to 2 days before filling with cream and strawberries.
