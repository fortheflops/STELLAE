# Baked-In Lemon Pudding Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A delightful layered dessert featuring a lemon or yellow cake base, a rich pineapple-lemon pudding filling, and a toasted coconut topping. This unique baked-in pudding cake is served chilled for a refreshing treat.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Filling
1. In a saucepan, combine the **2 reserved egg yolks**, **1/4 cup granulated sugar**, **1 1/3 cups water**, **8 1/2 oz drained crushed pineapple**, and **1 package lemon pudding mix**.
2. Cook over medium heat, stirring constantly, until the mixture thickens. Set aside to cool slightly.

### Step 2: First Cake Layer
3. Preheat oven to **350 degrees F**.
4. In a mixing bowl, combine the **1 package lemon or yellow cake mix**, **1 cup water**, and **2 egg whites**.
5. Blend and beat the mixture until smooth.
6. Spread half of the batter into a greased and floured **9 by 13-inch baking pan**.
7. Bake for **15 minutes** in the preheated oven.

### Step 3: Assemble and Final Bake
8. Spread the prepared **lemon pineapple filling** evenly over the partially baked cake.
9. To the remaining cake batter, add the remaining **1/4 cup water** and mix well.
10. Spread this remaining batter carefully over the filling layer.
11. Sprinkle **1 cup flaked coconut** evenly over the top.
12. Return to the oven and bake for **25 to 30 minutes** until the coconut is toasted and golden.
13. Cool completely, then refrigerate before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This cake is best served chilled. It can be prepared, covered, and refrigerated up to 24-48 hours before serving.
