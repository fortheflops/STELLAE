# Impossible Pumpkin Pie (Crustless)

> 📜 **Collection:** Patsy Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A magical crustless pie from Patsy's collection that creates its own layers as it bakes! This Impossible Pie features a smooth, spiced pumpkin custard with a built-in graham cracker base.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 50 mins | 2 hours | One 9-inch pie |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 9-inch pie plate
* Large mixing bowl
* Blender or wire whisk
* Wire cooling rack

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Mix Custard
1. Preheat oven to **325°F (165°C)**. Spray a **9-inch pie plate** with non-stick cooking spray.
2. In a large bowl, combine the **1 (16 oz) can solid pack pumpkin**, **1 (12 oz) can evaporated skim milk**, **2 whole eggs**, and **2 egg whites**. Mix with a blender or whisk until smooth and well incorporated.

### Step 2: Add Dry Ingredients and Bake
3. Stir in the **3/4 cup sugar**, **1 tsp ground cinnamon**, **1/4 tsp ground allspice**, **1/4 tsp ground ginger**, **1/8 tsp salt**, and **1/2 cup graham-cracker crumbs** until just combined.
4. Pour the mixture into the prepared pie plate.
5. Bake for **45 to 55 minutes**, or until a knife inserted near the center comes out clean and the center is fully set.

### Step 3: Cool and Serve
6. Let the pie cool completely on a wire rack for at least **2 hours** until it firms up.
7. Serve chilled or at room temperature, topped with **whipped cream** if desired.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the cooled pie covered in the refrigerator for up to 3-4 days before serving.
