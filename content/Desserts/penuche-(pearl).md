# Penuche (Pearl)

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Pearl | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *Indulge in a classic homemade candy treat with this traditional Penuche recipe featuring rich corn syrup, crunchy pecans, and walnuts. Perfect for holiday gifting or satisfying your sweet tooth with old-fashioned charm.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 20 mins | 30 mins | 24 servings |

* Candy thermometer

## Ingredients

### Candy Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3 1/2 cups | [[Sugar]] | None |
| 3/4 cup | [[Water]] | None |
| 1 cup | [[Corn syrup]] | None |
| 3 | [[Egg whites]] | Beaten |
| 1 cup | [[Pecans]] | Chopped |
| 1 cup | [[Walnuts]] | Chopped |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Boiling the Syrup
1. Boil **3 1/2 cups sugar**, **3/4 cup water**, and **1 cup corn syrup** together until it threads (reaches the thread stage).

### Step 2: Mixing and Cooking
2. Pour half of the hot syrup over **3 egg whites** (slightly beaten) while continuing to cook the rest of the syrup until it reaches the hard ball test stage.
3. Add this remaining hot syrup to the egg whites which have been beaten meanwhile.
4. Beat well, fold in **1 cup pecans** and **1 cup walnuts**, and pour into a buttered dish until set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the cooled and sliced penuche in an airtight container at room temperature for up to one week.
