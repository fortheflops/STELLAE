## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Batter
1. Preheat your oven to **350°F**. Grease and flour three 8-inch or 9-inch round cake pans.
2. In a large bowl, cream together the **2 cups sugar**, **1 stick (1/2 cup) oleo**, and **1/2 cup Crisco** until light and fluffy.
3. Add the **5 eggs** ( yolks ) one at a time, beating well after each addition.
4. In a separate bowl, whisk together the **2 cups flour** and **1 teaspoon baking soda**.
5. Alternately add the flour mixture and the **1 cup buttermilk** to the creamed mixture, beginning and ending with the **2 cups flour**.
6. In a clean, dry bowl, beat the **5 eggs** ( whites ) until stiff peaks form.
7. Gently fold the beaten egg whites into the batter, followed by the **1 (7 oz) can coconut**.
8. Divide the batter evenly among the three prepared pans.

### Step 2: Bake and Frost
9. Bake for **25 minutes**, or until a toothpick inserted into the center of the cakes comes out clean. Let cool completely before frosting.
10. For the frosting, in a large bowl, beat the **1 (8 ounce) pkg cream cheese** and **1 stick (1/2 cup) oleo** together until smooth.
11. Gradually add the **1 box (1 lb) powdered sugar** and beat until creamy. Stir in the **1 teaspoon vanilla** and the **1/2 cup nuts**.
12. Spread the frosting between the cooled cake layers and over the top and sides of the cake until smooth and well covered.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the frosted cake covered in the refrigerator for up to 3 days, bringing to room temperature before serving.
