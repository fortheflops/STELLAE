# Lemon Jello Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *An incredibly moist and flavorful lemon cake made easy with a white cake mix and a package of lemon Jello. This simple, one-bowl batter bakes up into a wonderfully dense and tender tube cake.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 1 hour | None | 1 tube cake (12-16 servings) |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Tube or Bundt pan
* Large mixing bowl
* Whisk
* Long wooden skewer

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 package | [[Cake Mix]] | White |
| 1 package (3 oz) | [[Lemon Jello]] | Dry powder |
| 4 | [[Eggs]] | Large |
| 2/3 cup | [[Water]] | None |
| 2/3 cup | [[Wesson Oil]] | Or other vegetable oil |
| 1 tablespoon | [[Lemon Flavoring]] | Extract |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare Pan and Base
1. Preheat your oven to **350°F**. Grease and flour a tube or Bundt pan.

### Step 2: Mix the Batter
2. In a large mixing bowl, combine the **2/3 cup water** and **2/3 cup Wesson oil**.
3. Sprinkle the **1 package (3 oz) lemon Jello** powder on top and mix until it dissolves.
4. Add the **1 package white cake mix** and mix well.
5. Add the **4 large eggs**, one at a time, beating well after each addition.
6. Stir in the **1 tablespoon lemon flavoring**.

### Step 3: Bake and Cool
7. Pour the batter into the prepared tube pan.
8. Bake for **1 hour**, or until a long wooden skewer inserted into the center comes out clean and the cake is golden and fragrant.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the cooled cake wrapped tightly in plastic wrap at room temperature for up to 3 days or refrigerate for up to 5 days.
