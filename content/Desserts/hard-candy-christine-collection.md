# Hard Candy

> 📜 **Collection:** Christine Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic homemade hard candy recipe featuring a simple blend of sugar, corn syrup, and water boiled to the hard-crack stage. Customize with your favorite extracts and food coloring for a festive, nostalgic treat.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep the Pan
1. Generously coat a cookie sheet with **butter** and dust heavily with **powdered sugar** to prevent the hot candy from sticking.

### Step 2: Boil the Candy
2. In a heavy saucepan, combine **3 3/4 cups sugar**, **1 1/4 cups white Karo corn syrup**, and **1 cup water**.
3. Bring the mixture to a boil over medium-high heat, stirring until the sugar dissolves. Insert a candy thermometer and boil without stirring until it reaches **300°F** (hard-crack stage), where a small drop of syrup placed in cold water forms hard, brittle threads.

### Step 3: Flavor, Color, and Cool
4. Remove the saucepan from the heat immediately. Carefully stir in **2 tbsp flavoring extract** (or **1 tsp flavoring oil**) and **food coloring** as desired. Be cautious as the mixture may sputter and release hot steam.
5. Pour the hot mixture onto the prepared **buttered and powdered sugar-dusted cookie sheet**.
6. Allow the candy to cool completely at room temperature until hard and brittle. Once cold, crack into bite-sized pieces.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature, layered with parchment paper to prevent sticking. Do not refrigerate, as moisture will make the candy sticky.
