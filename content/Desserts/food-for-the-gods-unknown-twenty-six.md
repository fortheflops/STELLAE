# Food For The Gods

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A delightful historic dessert loaded with dates, nuts, and crackers, creating a uniquely textured sweet treat. Perfect for special gatherings and reminiscent of classic home baking.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 45 mins | None | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Baking dish

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 cup | [[Dates]] | Fined/minced |
| 1 cup | [[Nuts]] | Fined |
| 12 tbsp | [[White Crackers]] | Crumbed (approx 20 to 24 crackers) |
| 6 | [[Eggs]] | Separated |
| 1 cup | [[Sugar]] | Unspecified type |
| 1 tsp | [[Baking Powder]] | Level |
| 1 tsp | [[Vanilla]] | Extract |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and Mixing
1. Mix the **1 cup dates** and **1 cup nuts** with **1 cup sugar** and **6 egg yolks**.
2. Add the **12 tbsp white crackers** crumbs and **1 tsp baking powder** along with the **1 tsp vanilla**.

### Step 2: Baking
3. Fold in the beaten **6 egg whites** last until just incorporated.
4. Bake at 350°F for **45 mins** until set and golden brown.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 2 days.
