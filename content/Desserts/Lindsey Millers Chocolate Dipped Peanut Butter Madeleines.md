# Lindsey Miller's Chocolate Dipped Peanut Butter Madeleines

> 📜 **Collection:** JC | ✍️ **Attribution:** Lindsey Miller | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *Tender, shell-shaped peanut butter cakes are dipped in rich semisweet chocolate and coated with chopped peanuts for the perfect finish. A delightful twist on the classic French madeleine created by Lindsey Miller.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 12 mins | 18 mins | 24 madeleines |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Two 12-shell madeleine pans
* Microwave-safe bowls
* Wire cooling rack
* Cookie sheet
* Waxed paper
* Whisk
* Small scoop or spoon

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 cup (1 stick) | [[Unsalted Butter]] | room temperature |
| 1 cup | [[Granulated Sugar]] | divided |
| 2/3 cup | [[Unsalted Peanut Butter]] | chunky or smooth |
| 2 large | [[Eggs]] | room temperature |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Vanilla Extract]] | none |
| 1 cup | [[All-Purpose Flour]] | none |
| 1/2 teaspoon | [[Salt]] | none |

### For Dipping
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 cups | [[Semisweet Chocolate Chips]] | none |
| 2/3 cup | [[Peanuts]] | honey roasted or plain, chopped |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Batter
1. Place a rack in the center of the oven and preheat to **350°F**. Thoroughly coat two 12-shell madeleine pans with butter or baking spray, brushing into each mold.
2. In a microwave-safe bowl, combine the **1/2 cup (1 stick) unsalted butter** and the **1 cup granulated sugar**. Microwave on low in 30-second intervals until the butter is just melted, then stir with a whisk until smooth.
3. Let the mixture cool for **3-4 minutes**.
4. Add the **2 large eggs** one at a time, whisking well after each addition.
5. Whisk in the **2/3 cup unsalted peanut butter** until well blended. Add the **1 teaspoon vanilla extract**, **1 cup all-purpose flour**, and **1/2 teaspoon salt**, whisking until just incorporated.
6. Using a small scoop or a spoon, fill each mold with batter.

### Step 2: Bake and Cool
7. Bake for **10-12 minutes**, until the centers are puffed with their characteristic hump and the edges are light brown.
8. Let the madeleines cool in the pans on a wire rack for **1-2 minutes**, then carefully invert them onto the rack to cool completely until they reach room temperature.

### Step 3: Chocolate Dipping
9. Line a cookie sheet with waxed paper. Place the **2/3 cup peanuts** in a shallow bowl. Melt the **2 cups semisweet chocolate chips** in the microwave in 30-second intervals, stirring until smooth.
10. Hold each cooled madeleine by its narrow end and dip about two-thirds of it into the melted chocolate, allowing excess to drip off.
11. Immediately dip the chocolate-covered portion into the chopped nuts to coat.
12. Place the finished madeleines on the prepared cookie sheet and let the chocolate set completely until firm before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store finished madeleines in an airtight container at room temperature with waxed paper between layers for up to 3 days.
