# Graham Cracker Torte

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A delightful and classic graham cracker torte featuring chopped nuts and whipped egg whites for a light, airy texture. Perfectly sweet and baked to golden perfection.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 35 mins | None | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowls
* Pie tin

## Ingredients

### Torte Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 c. | [[Graham cracker crumbs]] | Fine crumbs |
| 1 c. | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 1/2 tsp. | [[Vanilla]] | Liquid extract |
| 1/2 c. | [[Nuts]] | Chopped |
| 3 | [[Eggs]] | Separated |
| 1 1/2 tsp. | [[Baking powder]] | Standard |
| 1 pinch | [[Salt]] | Standard |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and Mixing
1. Mix **1 c. graham cracker crumbs**, **1 c. sugar**, **1 1/2 tsp. baking powder**, and **1 pinch salt** together.
2. Separate the **3 eggs** into yolks and whites.
3. Beat the **3 egg yolks** and mix them into the dry crumb mixture.
4. Add **1/2 c. nuts** and **1/2 tsp. vanilla** to the mixture and stir well.

### Step 2: Folding and Baking
5. Whip the **3 egg whites** until stiff peaks form, then gently fold them into the batter until fully incorporated.
6. Pour the batter into a greased pie tin.
7. Bake at 350 degrees F for **30-40 minutes** until golden brown and set in the center.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Bake ahead and store covered at room temperature for up to 2 days.
