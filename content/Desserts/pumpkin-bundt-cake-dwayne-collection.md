# Pumpkin Bundt Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A moist and tender autumn cake featuring canned pumpkin, warm spices, and crunchy pecans baked in a decorated bundt pan. Instant vanilla pudding mix and spice cake mix make this flavorful classic simple to prepare for any fall gathering.*

## Instructions

### Mixing the Batter
1. In a large mixing bowl, combine the **1 package spice cake mix**, **1 cup canned pumpkin**, **1/2 cup salad oil**, **1 package instant vanilla pudding mix**, **3 eggs**, **1 tsp ground cinnamon**, and **1/2 cup water**.
2. Beat with an electric mixer for 5 minutes until thoroughly mixed and smooth.
3. Fold **1/2 cup broken pecans** into the batter.

### Preparing the Pan & Baking
4. Generously grease a bundt pan with **butter** and dust with **flour**.
5. Place **whole pecans** in the bottom of the prepared bundt pan, securing the nuts with small dots of **butter**.
6. Pour the cake batter evenly into the prepared bundt pan.
7. Bake at 350°F (175°C) for 40 to 45 minutes until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.
8. Cool in the pan for 10 to 15 minutes.
9. Loosen the center and outer edges of the cake with a plastic spatula, then invert onto a wire cooling rack to cool completely.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store wrapped tightly in plastic wrap at room temperature for up to 4 days, or freeze the whole baked cake for up to 3 months.
