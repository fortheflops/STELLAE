# Delicious Chocolate Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** Jill Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A rich, classic chocolate layer cake featuring a tender crumb and deep flavor from melted unsweetened chocolate. Made with buttermilk and finished with a light, fluffy texture.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 35 mins | 10 mins | 1 2-layer 9-inch cake |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Two 9-inch round cake pans
* Large bowl
* Sifter
* Wire cooling rack
* Wooden skewer

| 2 cups | [[Sugar]] | None |
| 5 | [[Eggs]] | Large, well beaten |
| 3 ounces | [[Unsweetened Chocolate]] | Melted and cooled |
| 1 cup | [[Sour Milk]] or [[Buttermilk]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Sift Dry Ingredients
1. Preheat your oven to **350°F**. Grease and flour two **9-inch round layer cake pans**.
2. Sift the **2 1/2 cups all-purpose flour** once, then measure. Add the **1 teaspoon baking soda** and **1/2 teaspoon salt** to the measured flour, and sift the entire mixture together three more times. Set aside.

### Step 2: Make the Batter
3. In a large bowl, cream the **1 cup butter**. Gradually add the **2 cups sugar** and continue beating until the mixture is light and fluffy.
4. Add the **5 eggs** to the creamed mixture and beat well.
5. Blend in the **3 ounces unsweetened chocolate** (melted and slightly cooled).
6. Add the flour mixture in parts, alternating with the **1 cup sour milk or buttermilk**. Beat after each addition until the batter is smooth.
7. Stir in the **2 teaspoons vanilla extract**.

### Step 3: Bake and Cool
8. Divide the batter evenly between the prepared cake pans.
9. Bake for about **35 minutes**, or until a wooden skewer inserted into the center comes out clean and the cake springs back lightly.
10. Let the cakes cool in the pans for **10 minutes** before inverting them onto a wire rack to cool completely.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the cooled layers wrapped tightly in plastic wrap at room temperature for up to 2 days, or freeze for up to 2 months.
