# Teens' Favorite Fudge

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Nancy Haven | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *Indulge in a classic batch of creamy fudge made with evaporated milk, beet sugar, and marshmallow creme. This timeless recipe from Nancy Haven yields about two pounds of rich, nutty sweetness.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 15 mins | 2 hours | 2 pounds |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Saucepan
* Candy thermometer
* 8x8x2 inch baking pan
* Stirring spoon

---
## Ingredients

### Fudge Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3/4 cup | [[Evaporated milk]] | None |
| 2 cups | [[Beet sugar]] | None |
| 1/4 cup | [[Shortening]] | None |
| 1/4 teaspoon | [[Salt]] | None |
| 1 cup | [[Marshmallow creme]] | None |
| 1 package | [[Chocolate chips]] | None |
| 1/2 teaspoon | [[Vanilla]] | None |
| 1/2 cup | [[Nut meats]] | Chopped |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Cooking the Base
1. Cook the **3/4 cup evaporated milk**, **2 cups beet sugar**, **1/4 cup shortening**, and **1/4 teaspoon salt** together until it reaches the soft ball stage (**234° F**).
2. Stir constantly to prevent sticking and scorching.

### Step 2: Mixing and Setting
3. Remove from heat and immediately stir in the **1 package chocolate chips**.
4. Add **1 cup marshmallow creme**, **1/2 teaspoon vanilla**, and **1/2 cup nut meats**.
5. Pour at once into a well greased flat pan, **8x8x2**, until set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store finished fudge in an airtight container at room temperature with wax paper between layers for up to two weeks.
