# Chewy Date and Nut Torte

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A wonderfully simple and chewy baked dessert, almost like a torte, packed with dates and nuts and held together by a light egg and sugar batter. The minimal flour content gives it a wonderful torte-like texture.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 30 mins | None | 9-12 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 8x8 inch baking pan
* Small bowl
* Large bowl
* Electric mixer
* Toothpick

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 Cup | [[Dates]] | chopped |
| 1 Cup | [[Nuts]] | chopped |
| 1 Tablespoon | [[Flour]] | none |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] | none |
| 1 Teaspoon | [[Baking Powder]] | none |
| 1 Cup | [[Sugar]] | none |
| 1 Teaspoon | [[Vanilla]] | none |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Mix
1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Grease and flour an 8x8 inch baking pan.
2. In a small bowl, toss the chopped **1 cup dates** and **1 cup nuts** with the **1 tablespoon flour** and **1 teaspoon baking powder** to prevent sinking.

### Step 2: Batter and Bake
3. In a separate, larger bowl, beat the **2 eggs** and **1 cup sugar** together with an electric mixer until the mixture is thick, pale, and fluffy (about 3-5 minutes). Beat in the **1 teaspoon vanilla**.
4. Gently fold the date and nut mixture into the egg mixture until just combined.
5. Pour the batter into the prepared pan and spread evenly.
6. Bake for 30 minutes, until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean and the top is golden brown.
7. Let cool completely in the pan before cutting into squares.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store airtight at room temperature or in the refrigerator for up to 4 days.
