# Irene Shahnzarian's Nazook

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Irene Shahnzarian | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A traditional Armenian pastry featuring a rich, yeasted sour cream dough rolled with a sweet, buttery, vanilla-scented flour filling. These pinwheel cookies are baked until golden and offer a delightful, crumbly texture.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Dough
1. In a large bowl, stir together the **16-ounce container sour cream**, **3 eggs**, **1 package yeast**, and **1 teaspoon sugar**.
2. Pour in the **1 1/2 cups sweet butter** and gradually mix in the **4 cups flour** until a smooth, non-crumbly ball forms.
3. Cover and chill in the refrigerator for at least 3 hours.

### Step 2: Prepare the Filling
4. Melt the **1/2 cup sweet butter** and stir in the **1 cup sugar**, **1 1/2 cups flour**, and **2 teaspoons vanilla** to create a crumbly mixture.

### Step 3: Assembly and Baking
5. Preheat oven to 350°F and grease cookie sheets.
6. Divide dough and filling into four parts. Roll one part into a rectangle, spread with filling, and brush with a portion of the **1/4 cup sweet butter**.
7. Roll tightly like a jelly roll and cut into 1-inch slices.
8. Mix **2 egg yolks**, **1 tablespoon milk**, and **1 tablespoon sugar** to create an egg wash; brush over cookies.
9. Bake for **20 to 30 minutes** until golden brown and fragrant.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The dough can be prepared and chilled up to 24 hours in advance. Baked cookies store well in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 5 days.
