# Pumpkin Pie

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic, spiced pumpkin pie featuring a rich custard base of evaporated milk, whole milk, and warm autumn spices. Baked first at a high temperature to set the crust, then finished low and slow for a perfectly silky texture.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and Mixing
1. Preheat your oven to **450°F**.
2. In a large mixing bowl, lightly beat the **3 eggs**.
3. Add the **1 1/2 cups pumpkin**, **3/4 cup sugar**, **1/2 tsp salt**, **1/4 to 1/2 tsp ginger**, **1/4 to 1/2 tsp nutmeg**, **1/4 to 1/2 tsp cloves**, and **1 to 1 1/4 tsp cinnamon** to the beaten eggs and whisk until thoroughly combined.
4. Gradually stir in the **6 oz evaporated milk** and **1 cup milk** until the mixture is smooth and uniform.

### Step 2: Baking
5. Pour the prepared filling into an unbaked 9-inch pie shell.
6. Bake in the preheated oven at **450°F** for **10 minutes**.
7. Reduce the oven temperature to **325°F** and continue baking for **50 minutes**, or until a knife inserted near the center comes out clean and the custard is set with only a slight, gentle jiggle in the middle.
8. Allow the pie to cool completely on a wire rack before slicing and serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This pie can be baked up to 24 hours in advance. Once cooled completely, cover loosely and store in the refrigerator.
