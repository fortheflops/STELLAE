# Old-Fashioned Applesauce Cookies

> 📜 **Collection:** Patsy Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic recipe for soft, cake-like drop cookies made with applesauce, brown sugar, and warm spices. These old-fashioned treats are packed with raisins and nuts for a hearty, nostalgic flavor.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Dough
1. In a large bowl, thoroughly cream together the **1/2 cup shortening**, **1 cup brown sugar**, and **1 egg**.
2. Stir in the **1/4 cup cold coffee** and **1 cup apple sauce** until smooth.
3. In a separate bowl, whisk together the **1 3/4 cups flour**, **1/2 teaspoon baking soda**, **1/2 teaspoon salt**, **1/2 teaspoon nutmeg**, **1/2 teaspoon cloves**, and **1/2 teaspoon cinnamon**.
4. Gradually add the dry mixture to the wet ingredients, stirring until just combined, then fold in the **1/2 cup raisins** and **1/4 cup chopped nuts**.
5. Cover the dough and chill for at least 2 hours to allow the flavors to meld.

### Step 2: Bake
6. Preheat your oven to 375°F and lightly grease your baking sheets.
7. Drop rounded tablespoonfuls of the chilled dough 2 inches apart on the prepared baking sheets.
8. Bake for **9 to 12 minutes**, until the cookies are lightly browned, set, and leave almost no imprint when touched lightly.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The dough can be chilled for up to 24 hours before baking. Baked cookies store well in an airtight container for up to 5 days.
