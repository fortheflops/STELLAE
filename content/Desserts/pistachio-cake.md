# Pistachio Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Five | ✍️ **Attribution:** Norma | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A delightful and unique pistachio cake marbled with chocolate syrup for a sweet contrast. This classic recipe yields a moist, flavorful dessert baked to golden perfection in an angel cake pan.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 55-65 mins | 10 mins | 12 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Electric mixer
* Angel cake pan

---
## Ingredients

### Cake Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 package | [[White Cake Mix]] | Dry |
| 1 package | [[Pistachio Pudding]] | Royal brand, instant |
| 1 cup | [[Oil]] | Vegetable or canola |
| 4 | [[Eggs]] | Whole |
| 1 cup | [[Orange Juice]] | Fresh or bottled |
| 3/4 cup | [[Syrup]] | Hershey's chocolate syrup |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Batter Preparation
1. Mix **1 package white cake mix** and **1 package pistachio pudding** together.
2. Add **1 cup oil** and **4 eggs**, beating well after each egg is added.
3. Add **1 cup orange juice** and mix thoroughly until smooth.

### Step 2: Layering and Baking
4. Pour the batter into a buttered angel cake pan, reserving about **1/3** of the batter.
5. Add **3/4 cup syrup** to the remaining **1/3** of the batter and mix, then drizzle on top of the cake.
6. Bake at **350 degrees** for **55 to 65 minutes**, until a toothpick inserted near the center comes out clean.
7. Leave the cake in the pan for about **10 minutes** before carefully inverting to cool completely.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the cooled cake wrapped tightly in plastic wrap at room temperature for up to 3 days, or refrigerate for up to 5 days.
