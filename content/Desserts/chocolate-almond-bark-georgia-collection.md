# Chocolate Almond Bark

> 📜 **Collection:** Georgia Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A delightful and easy-to-make chocolate bark loaded with raisins and toasted almonds. Perfect for holiday gifting or a sweet homemade treat.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 10 mins | 30 mins | 12 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Double boiler
* 9x13 inch pan
* Waxed paper
* Spoon

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 11 1/2 oz. | [[Milk Chocolate Morsels]] |  |
| 1 T. | [[Vegetable Shortening]] |  |
| 1/2 cup | [[Raisins]] |  |
| 1/2 cup | [[Toasted Almonds]] | divided |
| 1/2 t. | [[Pure Vanilla Extract]] | X-tra Touch |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Melting and Mixing
1. Over hot water combine **11 1/2 oz. milk chocolate morsels** and **1 T. vegetable shortening**.
2. Stir till completely smooth.
3. Remove from heat and add **1/2 cup raisins** and **1/4 cup toasted almonds** (half of the divided nuts), along with **1/2 t. pure vanilla extract**.

### Step 2: Setting and Serving
4. Spread the mixture evenly in a waxed paper-lined 9x13 inch pan, ensuring the paper is long enough to lift the bark from the pan later.
5. Sprinkle the remaining **1/4 cup toasted almonds** over the top.
6. Chill until firm, then break into pieces before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store broken pieces in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to two weeks.
