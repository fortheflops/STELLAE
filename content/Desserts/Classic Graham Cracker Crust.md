# Classic Graham Cracker Crust

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A simple, classic no-bake graham cracker crust recipe made with just three ingredients. The perfect buttery, sweet, and crunchy base for cheesecakes and cream pies.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | None | 30 mins | One 8-inch pie crust |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Medium bowl
* Fork
* 8-inch pie plate

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 1/4 Cups | [[Graham Cracker Crumbs]] | Fine |
| 1/3 Cup | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 1/3 Cup | [[Melted Butter]] | Melted |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mix the Base
1. In a medium bowl, combine the **1 1/4 cups graham cracker crumbs** and **1/3 cup sugar**.

### Step 2: Add Butter and Press
2. Pour in the **1/3 cup melted butter** and mix with a fork until all the crumbs are evenly moistened.
3. Reserve 1/4 cup of the crumb mixture to use as a topping for your pie, if desired.
4. Press the remaining crumb mixture firmly and evenly onto the bottom and up the sides of an 8-inch pie plate until compact.

### Step 3: Chill
5. Chill the crust in the refrigerator for at least **30 minutes**, or until firm and set, before filling.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the unbaked or baked crust tightly wrapped in the refrigerator for up to 2 days or freeze for up to 1 month before filling.
