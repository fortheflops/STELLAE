# Banana Split Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Clarice | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic layered dessert inspired by a banana split, featuring a buttery pecan crust, a sweet cream cheese filling, and layers of bananas and pineapple, all topped with whipped cream. This nostalgic treat is perfect for gatherings and can be prepared ahead for easy serving.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare and Bake Crust
1. Preheat oven to 350°F. In a medium bowl, combine **1 1/2 cups flour**, **2 tablespoons sugar**, and **1 stick oleo**. Mix until crumbly, stir in **1/2 cup nuts** if using, and press into a 9x13 inch pan.
2. Bake for **20 minutes** until lightly golden brown, then let cool completely.

### Step 2: Prepare Filling and Assemble
3. In a large bowl, beat **1 lb confectioners sugar**, **2 eggs**, **1 cup oleo**, **1 tsp vanilla extract**, and **1 1/2 tsp butter flavoring** with an electric mixer for **5 minutes** until light and fluffy.
4. Spread the filling evenly over the cooled crust, then arrange **4 bananas** in a single layer followed by **20 oz crushed pineapple**.
5. Top with **16 oz Cool Whip**, spreading to cover the surface completely.
6. Garnish with **maraschino cherries** and **chopped nuts** if desired. Refrigerate for at least 2-3 hours until set and chilled before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This dessert is best when prepared the day before; keep refrigerated until ready to serve to ensure the layers set properly.
