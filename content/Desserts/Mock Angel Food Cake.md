# Mock Angel Food Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *An easier, alternative recipe for a light and airy cake with a texture similar to angel food cake, made with both chemical leaveners and beaten egg whites. This clever bake achieves a fluffy height without complex preparation methods.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 25 mins | None | 1 tube cake |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large bowl
* Electric mixer
* Tube pan

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 cups | [[Sugar]] | Divided |
| 2 cups | [[Cake Flour]] | Sifted (reduce by 4 tablespoons if using no-sift flour) |
| 2 tsp | [[Baking Powder]] | None |
| 1 tsp | [[Cream of Tartar]] | None |
| 5 | [[Egg Whites]] | Large, at room temperature |
| 1 cup | [[Hot Water]] | None |
| 1 tsp | [[Vanilla]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Batter
1. Preheat your oven to **425°F**. Do NOT grease your angel food or tube pan.
2. In a large bowl, whisk together the **2 cups sugar** and **2 cups cake flour**.
3. Add the **1 cup hot water** and **1 tsp vanilla**, and mix well to form a smooth batter.

### Step 2: Meringue
4. In a separate, clean large bowl, beat the **5 egg whites** with an electric mixer until they are fluffy.
5. Add the **1 tsp cream of tartar** and continue to beat.
6. Once soft peaks form, add the **2 tsp baking powder** and continue beating until the **egg whites** are stiff and glossy.

### Step 3: Combine and Bake
7. Gently fold the batter mixture into the stiffly beaten **egg whites** in three parts to avoid deflating them.
8. Pour the final batter into the ungreased tube pan.
9. Bake for **25 minutes**, or until the top is golden brown and springs back when you lightly touch it.
10. Immediately upon removing from the oven, invert the pan and let the cake cool completely upside down before removing.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the cooled cake wrapped tightly at room temperature for up to 3 days.
