# Classic Apple Crunch

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A simple and delicious apple crunch featuring tender, sweetened apples and a buttery brown sugar and cinnamon crumble topping. This classic dessert is perfect for any occasion.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 45 mins | None | 6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* deep baking dish
* mixing bowl
* pastry blender

---
## Ingredients

### Filling
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 1/2 cups | [[Apples]] | sliced and drained |
| 1/2 cup | [[Sugar]] | white |

### Topping
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 cup | [[Butter]] | chilled and cut into pieces |
| 1/2 cup | [[Brown Sugar]] | packed |
| 1 cup | [[Flour]] | all-purpose |
| 1/2 teaspoon | [[Cinnamon]] | ground |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation
1. Preheat the oven to 375°F. Lightly grease a deep baking dish.

### Step 2: Assemble the Filling
2. Place the **2 1/2 cups apples** in the baking dish and sprinkle evenly with the **1/2 cup sugar**.

### Step 3: Prepare the Topping
3. In a separate bowl, combine the **1/2 cup brown sugar**, **1 cup flour**, and **1/2 teaspoon cinnamon**.
4. Cut in the **1/2 cup butter** using a pastry blender or your fingers until the mixture is crumbly.

### Step 4: Bake
5. Sprinkle the topping evenly over the apples.
6. Bake for **45 minutes**, or until the apples are tender and the topping is golden brown and crunchy.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The topping can be prepared in advance and stored in the refrigerator for up to 24 hours before assembling and baking.
