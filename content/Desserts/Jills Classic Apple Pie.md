# Jill's Classic Apple Pie

> 📜 **Collection:** JillCollection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Jill | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic apple pie recipe from Jill, offering the choice between a traditional double crust or a sweet and simple crumb topping. Perfect for autumn gatherings and comforting family desserts.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 25 mins | 1 hour | None | One 9-inch pie |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 9-inch pie pan
* Large bowl
* Small bowl
* Pastry cutter

## Ingredients

### Filling
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 6 to 7 | [[Apples]] | Tart, peeled and thinly sliced |
| 3/4 to 1 cup | [[Sugar]] | Standard |
| 2 tablespoons | [[All-Purpose Flour]] | Standard |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Cinnamon]] | Ground |
| 1 dash | [[Nutmeg]] | Ground |
| 1 dash | [[Salt]] | Standard |
| 2 tablespoons | [[Butter]] | For dotting the filling |

### Crust & Topping
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 recipe | [[Pastry]] | For a double-crust 9-inch pie |
| 1/2 cup | [[All-Purpose Flour]] | For crumb topping |
| 1/4 cup | [[Sugar]] | For crumb topping |
| 1/4 cup | [[Butter]] | Cold, for crumb topping |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Filling
1. Preheat oven to **425°F**. Prepare your pie pastry and line a **9-inch pie pan** with the bottom crust.
2. To make the filling: In a large bowl, combine the **sugar**, **2 tablespoons of flour**, **cinnamon**, **nutmeg**, and **salt**. Add the sliced **apples** and toss until evenly coated.

### Step 2: Assembly and Topping
3. Pour the apple filling into the pastry-lined pan and dot the top with the **2 tablespoons of butter**.
4. Choose your topping: For a double crust, place the top crust over the filling, trim, fold, and crimp the edges to seal, and cut several slits in the top crust. For a crumb topping, in a small bowl, combine **1/2 cup flour** and **1/4 cup sugar**, and cut in **1/4 cup of cold butter** until crumbly, then sprinkle evenly over the **apples**.

### Step 3: Bake
5. Bake at **425°F for 15 minutes**.
6. Reduce the oven temperature to **375°F** and bake for another 35-45 minutes, or until the crust is golden brown and the filling is bubbly.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the baked pie covered at room temperature for up to 2 days or refrigerate for up to 5 days.
