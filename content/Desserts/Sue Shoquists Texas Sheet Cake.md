# Sue Shoquist's Texas Sheet Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** JillCollection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Sue Shoquist | 📂 **Category:** Desserts

## Instructions

### Step 1: Make the Cake
1. Preheat oven to **375°F**. Grease and flour a large **17x11 inch sheet pan**.
2. In a large mixing bowl, combine the **2 cups all-purpose flour**, **2 cups sugar**, and **1 teaspoon baking soda**.
3. In a saucepan, bring the **1 cup margarine**, **1 cup water**, **3 ½ tablespoons cocoa powder**, and **1 ½ teaspoons salt** to a boil.
4. Pour the hot cocoa mixture over the dry ingredients and mix well until incorporated.
5. In a small bowl, whisk together the **½ cup buttermilk**, **2 eggs**, and **1 teaspoon vanilla extract**.
6. Add the buttermilk mixture to the cake batter and mix until just combined.
7. Pour the batter into the prepared sheet pan and spread evenly.
8. Bake for **15 minutes**, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean and the edges pull away slightly.

### Step 2: Make the Icing
9. While the cake is baking, prepare the icing. In a saucepan, combine the **½ cup margarine**, **6 tablespoons milk**, and **3 ½ tablespoons cocoa powder**.
10. Bring the mixture to a boil, stirring constantly until everything is melted and smooth.
11. Remove the pan from the heat. Immediately stir in the **1 pound confectioners sugar**, **1 ½ teaspoons vanilla extract**, and **1 cup chopped nuts** until smooth.
12. Pour the warm icing over the cake while the cake is still hot from the oven, spreading it quickly and evenly until the surface is coated.
13. Let the cake cool completely and the icing set until firm before cutting into squares.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the cooled cake covered at room temperature for up to 3 days, or refrigerate to keep longer.
