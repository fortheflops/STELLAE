# Linda Brown's Classic Millionaire Pie

> 📜 **Collection:** General Archive | ✍️ **Attribution:** Linda Brown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A truly decadent Southern classic, this Millionaire Pie features a rich, buttery, no-bake filling layered in a pie crust and topped with a fluffy pineapple-pecan whipped cream.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 25 mins | 0 mins | 4 hours 30 mins | One 9-inch pie |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large bowl
* Electric mixer
* Chilled mixing bowl
* 9-inch pie crust

## Ingredients

### Filling
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | [[Pie Crust]] | Baked 9-inch, cooled |
| 1 cup (2 sticks) | [[Butter]] | Softened |
| 2 cups | [[Powdered Sugar]] | None |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] | Large |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Salt]] | None |
| 1 tsp | [[Vanilla Extract]] | None |

### Topping
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 pint (2 cups) | [[Heavy Cream]] | None |
| 1/4 cup | [[Powdered Sugar]] | None |
| 1 (8 oz) can | [[Pineapple]] | Crushed, well-drained |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Pecans]] | Chopped |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Make the Filling
1. In a mixing bowl, cream the softened **1 cup (2 sticks) butter** and **2 cups powdered sugar**. Add the **2 eggs**, **1/4 tsp salt**, and **1 tsp vanilla extract**.
2. Beat with an electric mixer on high speed for **5-7 minutes** until the mixture is very light, fluffy, and has increased in volume.

### Step 2: Chill the Base
3. Spread the filling evenly into the bottom of the cooled, pre-baked **1 baked 9-inch pie crust**.
4. Place the pie in the refrigerator to chill for at least **30 minutes** until slightly set.

### Step 3: Make the Topping
5. In a chilled mixing bowl, whip the **1 pint (2 cups) heavy cream** until soft peaks form.
6. Gradually add **1/4 cup powdered sugar** and continue whipping until stiff peaks form.
7. Gently fold in the well-drained crushed **1 (8 oz) can pineapple** and chopped **1/2 cup pecans** until well incorporated.

### Step 4: Assemble and Chill
8. Spread the pineapple-pecan whipped cream topping evenly over the chilled filling layer until smooth.
9. Chill the finished pie in the refrigerator for at least **4 hours**, or preferably overnight, until completely set before slicing and serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Chill the finished pie for several hours or overnight before serving. Store leftovers covered in the refrigerator for up to 3 days.
