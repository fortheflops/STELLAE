# Pots de Creme

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Michelle Ala | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *An incredibly simple and elegant no-bake chocolate pots de crème, made quickly in a blender for a rich, smooth, and decadent dessert. This classic French-inspired treat is perfect for effortless entertaining.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 0 mins | 3 hours | 4–6 Servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Saucepan
* Blender
* Ramekins

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 Cup | [[Chocolate Chips]] | Semi-sweet |
| 1 1/2 Cups | [[Half-and-Half]] | Scalded |
| 2 | [[Egg Yolks]] | Large |
| 3 Tablespoons | [[Brandy]] | Optional |
| To taste | [[Whipped Cream]] | For serving |
| To taste | [[Cookies]] | Crisp, not-too-sweet |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Base
1. In a saucepan, heat the **1 1/2 cups half-and-half** until scalded, ensuring bubbles form around the edges without reaching a boil.

### Step 2: Blend and Set
2. Combine the **1 cup chocolate chips**, **1 1/2 cups half-and-half**, **2 egg yolks**, and **3 tablespoons brandy** in a blender.
3. Secure the lid and process at high speed until the mixture is completely smooth and emulsified.
4. Pour the mixture into individual ramekins, filling them two-thirds full.
5. Cover and refrigerate for at least 3 hours, or until the custard is firm to the touch.

### Step 3: Serve
6. Top each ramekin with a dollop of **whipped cream** and serve alongside **crisp cookies**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The custard can be prepared and refrigerated overnight for a firmer set.
