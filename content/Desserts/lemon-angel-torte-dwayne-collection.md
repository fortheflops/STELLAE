# Lemon Angel Torte

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A delightful and airy lemon angel torte featuring a crisp meringue crust filled with luscious homemade lemon filling and whipped cream. Perfect for special occasions or a refreshing end to a summer meal.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 30 mins | 5 mins | 5 hours | 8 servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Small mixing bowl
* Electric mixer
* Pie plate
* Double boiler or saucepan

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparing the Torte Shell
1. Preheat oven to very hot (**450°F**).
2. Place **4 egg whites**, **3/4 cup sugar**, **1/4 tsp salt**, and **1/4 tsp cream of tartar** in a small mixing bowl.
3. Beat on high speed with electric mixer for **15 mins**, or until very stiff peaks form.
4. Spread evenly in a well-buttered pie plate and place in pre-heated oven.
5. Immediately turn off heat and let stand in oven for **5 hours or overnight** without peeking until dry and crisp.

### Step 2: Preparing the Lemon Filling
6. In top of double boiler, heat **4 egg yolks** until thick and lemon colored.
7. Gradually beat in **1/2 cup sugar**, **1 dash salt**, **1 tbsp grated lemon peel**, and **3 tbsp lemon juice**.
8. Cook over simmering water, stirring constantly, until thick—about **5 mins**. Remove from heat and let cool.

### Step 3: Assembly and Chilling
9. Fill torte shell with half of the **1 cup heavy cream** (whipped), then layer with prepared **Lemon Filling**.
10. Top with the rest of the whipped cream.
11. Chill in the refrigerator for **5 hours** before serving until set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the torte up to a day in advance and keep thoroughly chilled in the refrigerator until ready to serve.
