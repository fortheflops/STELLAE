# Easy Peach and Pineapple Dump Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** UK15 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Joy Phillips | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A super simple cobbler, also known as a dump cake, made by layering pineapple, peaches, and dry cake mix, then topping with pecans and butter before baking. This quintessential dessert is perfect for quick preparation and crowd-pleasing results.*

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 9x13 inch baking pan
* Oven

| 1-2 cans or bags | [[Peaches]] | sliced, drained if canned or frozen |
| 1 (13 ounce) box | [[Cake Mix]] | yellow or white, dry |
| 1/2 cup | [[Pecans]] | chopped |
| 1/4 cup | [[Sugar]] | granulated |
| 1/2 cup | [[Butter]] | melted |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Assembly and Baking
1. Preheat the oven to 350°F.
2. Spread the **1 (20 ounce) can crushed pineapple** (with juice) in the bottom of a 9x13 inch pan.
3. Arrange a layer of **1-2 cans or bags peaches** over the pineapple.
4. Sprinkle the **1 (13 ounce) box cake mix** evenly over the fruit.
5. Arrange another layer of **peaches** on top of the cake mix.
6. Sprinkle the **1/2 cup pecans** and **1/4 cup sugar** over the top.
7. Drizzle the **1/2 cup melted butter** evenly over everything.
8. Bake for **35 to 45 minutes**, until the top is golden brown and the fruit filling is bubbly.

---
