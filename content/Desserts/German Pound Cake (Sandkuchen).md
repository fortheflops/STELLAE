# German Pound Cake (Sandkuchen)

> 📜 **Collection:** GC | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic German Pound Cake, or Sandkuchen, featuring a rich, buttery flavor and a fine, sandy crumb perfumed with vanilla sugar and cardamom. This traditional treat bakes into a golden loaf with a tender texture.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 1 hour | 15 mins | 1 loaf cake |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Loaf pan
* Large bowl
* Separate bowl
* Electric mixer
* Wire rack
* Toothpick

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| ½ Pound (2 sticks) | [[Butter]] | softened |
| 1 ½ Cups | [[Sugar]] | See Note |
| 1 Package | [[Vanilla Sugar]] | or 2 tsp vanilla extract |
| 3 | [[Eggs]] | large |
| 2 ½ Cups | [[Flour]] | See Note, all-purpose |
| 1 Package (4 tsp) | [[Baking Powder]] | None |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Cardamom]] | ground |
| ⅔ Cup | [[Milk]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Cream
1. Preheat your oven to **350°F**. Grease and flour a standard loaf pan.
2. In a large bowl, cream the **½ pound (2 sticks) butter**, softened with the **1 ½ cups sugar** and **1 package vanilla sugar** with an electric mixer until light and fluffy.
3. Beat in the **3 eggs** one at a time, mixing well after each addition.

### Step 2: Combine and Bake
4. In a separate bowl, whisk together the **2 ½ cups flour**, **1 package baking powder**, and **1 teaspoon ground cardamom**.
5. On low speed, add the dry ingredients to the creamed mixture in three parts, alternating with the **⅔ cup milk**, beginning and ending with the dry ingredients. Mix only until just combined.
6. Pour the batter into the prepared loaf pan and spread evenly.
7. Bake for **50-60 minutes**, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean and the top is golden brown and fragrant.
8. Let the cake cool in the pan for **15 minutes** before turning it out onto a wire rack to cool completely.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the cooled cake wrapped tightly in plastic wrap or in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 4 days.
