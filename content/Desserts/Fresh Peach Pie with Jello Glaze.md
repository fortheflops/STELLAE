# Fresh Peach Pie with Jello Glaze

> 📜 **Collection:** Louise Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A beautiful no-bake pie featuring fresh, sliced peaches in a pre-baked shell, held together by a glossy peach Jello glaze.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 10 mins | 4 hours | One 9-inch pie |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 9-inch pie pan
* Medium saucepan
* Wire whisk

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | [[Pie Shell]] | 9-inch, pre-baked and cooled |
| 4-5 Cups | [[Peaches]] | Fresh, peeled and sliced |

### Glaze
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3/4 to 1 Cup | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 2 Tablespoons | [[Cornstarch]] | None |
| 1 Cup | [[Water]] | None |
| 2 Tablespoons | [[Corn Syrup]] | White |
| 3-4 Tablespoons | [[Jello]] | Peach flavor, half of a 3-oz package |
| Dash | [[Almond Flavoring]] | Optional |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Assemble the Fruit
1. Prepare a 9-inch pre-baked **pie shell** and let it cool completely.
2. Arrange the sliced fresh **peaches** evenly inside the cooled **pie shell**.

### Step 2: Make the Glaze
3. In a medium saucepan, whisk together the **sugar** and **cornstarch**. Stir in the **water** and **corn syrup** until smooth.
4. Bring the mixture to a boil over medium heat, stirring constantly, and boil for **5 minutes** until the mixture is thick and clear.
5. Remove the saucepan from the heat and immediately stir in the **peach Jello** powder and optional **almond flavoring** until the **Jello** is completely dissolved.
6. Let the glaze cool until it is lukewarm but still pourable, about 15 to 20 minutes.

### Step 3: Pour and Chill
7. Carefully pour the cooled glaze evenly over the **peaches** in the **pie crust**.
8. Refrigerate the pie for at least **4 hours**, or until the glaze is completely set.
9. Serve chilled, with whipped cream if desired.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the pie covered in the refrigerator for up to 2 days. Serve chilled for the best texture.
