## Recipe
| Measurements      | Ingredients                                        |
| :---------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 2 (15 oz) Cans    | [[Apricot]] Halves, drained                        |
| 2                 | [[Lemons]], juiced                                 |
| 1 cup             | Packed [[Brown Sugar]]                             |
| 1 ½ cups          | Crumbled [[Cheese Crackers]] (e.g., Cheez-Its)     |
| 2 Tbsp            | [[Butter]], cut into small pieces                  |
___
### Instructions
1.  In a bowl, combine the drained apricot halves, the juice of two lemons, and the brown sugar. Let this mixture sit for 30 minutes.
2.  Preheat your oven to 350°F.
3.  Pour the apricot mixture into a casserole dish.
4.  Cover the top of the apricots with the crumbled cheese crackers.
5.  Dot the top of the crackers evenly with the small pieces of butter.
6.  Bake for 30 minutes. Serve warm.
