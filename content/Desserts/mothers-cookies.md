## Instructions

### Preparation and Baking
1. Cream together **2 c. sugar** and **1 c. shortening** in a large mixing bowl until light and fluffy.
2. Add **3 eggs** and mix well.
3. Stir in **1 c. sour milk**.
4. In a separate bowl, sift together the required amount of **flour**, **1 tsp. soda**, **2 tsp. b. powder**, **1/2 tsp. salt**, and **nutmeg**.
5. Combine the dry ingredients into the wet mixture gradually until a soft dough forms.
6. Drop spoonfuls onto a greased baking sheet and bake until the edges are golden brown and centers are set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The dough can be prepared and chilled in the refrigerator for up to 24 hours before baking.
