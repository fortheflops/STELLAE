*A traditional Victorian holiday dessert featuring rich dried fruits and spices. This classic plum pudding is steamed to perfection for a deep, festive flavor.*

### Author
STELLAE Archive

---

## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 1 Cup | [[Dried Plums]] (prunes), chopped |
| 1 Cup | [[Raisins]] |
| 1 Cup | [[Suet]] (or beef tallow) |
| 1 Cup | [[Breadcrumbs]] |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Brown Sugar]] |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Flour]] |
| 3 | [[Eggs]] |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Brandy]] |

---

## Instructions
1. In a large bowl, combine the **1 cup chopped dried plums**, **1 cup raisins**, **1 cup suet**, **1 cup breadcrumbs**, **1/2 cup brown sugar**, and **1/2 cup flour**.
2. Beat the **3 eggs** and stir them into the mixture along with the **1/2 cup brandy** until well combined.
3. Spoon the mixture into a greased pudding basin and cover tightly with parchment paper and foil.
4. Steam the pudding in a large pot of boiling water for **6 hours**, ensuring the water level is maintained halfway up the basin.
