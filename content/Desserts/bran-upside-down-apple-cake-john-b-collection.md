# Bran Upside Down Apple Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A delightful and wholesome upside-down cake featuring a moist bran-and-flour batter layered over sweet, buttery sliced apples and brown sugar. Baked to a golden perfection, this traditional treat offers a wonderful rustic flavor and tender crumb.*

| 25 mins | 50 mins | None | 8 servings |

## Ingredients

### Cake Batter
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] | Separated |
| 1/3 cup | [[Water]] | Divided |
| 1 cup | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 1 tsp | [[Vanilla]] | Liquid extract |
| 1 1/4 cups | [[Bran]] | Uncooked |
| 1 cup | [[Flour]] | All-purpose |
| 2 1/2 tsp | [[Baking Powder]] | Leavening agent |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Salt]] | Fine |

### Fruit Topping
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 cup | [[Brown Sugar]] | Packed |
| 1/3 cup | [[Butter]] | Sliced or dotted |
| 5 to 6 | [[Apples]] | Thinly sliced |
| 1 tbsp | [[Water]] | Additional |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Pan Preparation
1. In a greased pan, spread the **1 cup brown sugar** and dot evenly with the **1/3 cup butter**.
2. Cover the sugar and butter mixture with **5 to 6 apples**, thinly sliced, and pour over the **1 tbsp water**.

### Step 2: Batter Preparation
3. Separate the **2 eggs** and beat the yolks.
4. Add half of the **1/3 cup water** to the beaten yolks, then alternate adding the dry ingredients (consisting of **1 1/4 cups bran**, **1 cup flour**, **2 1/2 tsp baking powder**, and **1/4 tsp salt**) and the remaining water, mixing until just combined.

### Step 3: Baking
5. Place the pan in the oven while mixing, then add the prepared batter over the apple layer.
6. Bake for **50 min** in a moderate oven until golden brown and a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Best served fresh and warm on the day it is baked, but can be covered and stored at room temperature for up to 1 day.
