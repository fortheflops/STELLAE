# Brazil Nut Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *Discover this classic, rustic Brazil Nut Cake packed with dates, walnuts, and whole Brazil nuts. A delightful treat rich in texture and traditional heritage flavors.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 45 mins | None | 10 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Baking pan
* Measuring cups

---
## Ingredients

### Cake Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 lb | [[Dates]] | Pitted and chopped |
| 1 lb | [[Walnuts]] | Chopped |
| 1/2 lb | [[Brazil Nuts]] | Whole or halved |
| 1 cup | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 4 | [[Eggs]] | Beaten |
| 1 cup | [[Flour]] | All-purpose |
| 1 tsp | [[Baking Powder]] | Level |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and Mixing
1. Preheat the oven and prepare your baking pan.
2. In a large mixing bowl, combine the **1 lb dates**, **1 lb walnuts**, and **1/2 lb brazil nuts**.
3. Add the **1 cup sugar**, **4 eggs**, **1 cup flour**, and **1 tsp baking powder** to the fruit and nut mixture, stirring until thoroughly incorporated and no dry flour remains.

### Step 2: Baking
4. Pour the batter into the prepared baking pan and bake until golden brown, fragrant, and a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 3 days or wrap tightly and refrigerate for up to a week.
