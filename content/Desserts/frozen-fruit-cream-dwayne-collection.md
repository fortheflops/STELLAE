## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing and Freezing
1. Mix the juice of the fruit (**4 oranges** and **4 lemons**), mash the **4 bananas**, and combine with the **4 cups sugar** and **4 cups cream**.
2. Put in freezer and fill to three-quarters full with **milk**, freezing until firm and slushy.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in the freezer for up to a week; let soften slightly before serving.
