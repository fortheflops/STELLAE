## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Filling
1. In a saucepan, combine **2 cups dates**, **3/4 cup sugar**, and **3/4 cup water**.
2. Cook over medium heat, stirring constantly, until the mixture is thick and fragrant.
3. Remove from heat, stir in **1/2 cup nuts**, and allow to cool completely.

### Step 2: Prepare the Dough
4. In a large bowl, cream together **1 cup shortening**, **2 cups brown sugar**, and **2 eggs** until smooth.
5. Stir in **1/2 cup liquid** (water, sour milk, or buttermilk) and **1 tsp vanilla**.
6. Gradually mix in **3 1/2 cups flour**, **1 tsp salt**, **1 tsp soda**, and **1/8 tsp cinnamon** until a soft dough forms.

### Step 3: Assembly and Baking
7. Drop **1/2 tsp dough** onto an ungreased cookie sheet.
8. Place a small amount of the cooled **date filling** on top of the dough.
9. Top with another **1/2 tsp dough** to seal the filling inside.
10. Bake at 350°F-375°F until the edges are golden brown and the centers are set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The date filling can be prepared up to 2 days in advance and stored in the refrigerator. Baked cookies stay soft when stored in an airtight container for up to 5 days.
