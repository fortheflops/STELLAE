# Margaret Farley's Texas Ranger Crispies

> 📜 **Collection:** UK15 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Margaret Farley | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic 'kitchen sink' oatmeal cookie packed with corn flakes, coconut, and nuts for a chewy, crispy texture. These hearty treats are a timeless favorite from the Margaret Farley collection.*

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 cup | [[Shortening]] | or 1/2 cup butter + 1/2 cup Crisco |

| 1/2 teaspoon | [[Salt]] | fine |

| 1 teaspoon | [[Baking Powder]] | leavening |

| 2 cups | [[Oatmeal]] | rolled oats |
| 1 cup | [[Coconut]] | shredded |
| 1 cup | [[Nuts]] | chopped |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare and Cream
1. Preheat your oven to 325°F and lightly grease your cookie sheets.
2. In a large bowl, cream together the **1 cup shortening**, **1 cup white sugar**, and **1 cup light brown sugar** with an electric mixer until light and fluffy.
3. Add the **2 eggs** and **1 teaspoon vanilla** and mix well.

### Step 2: Combine and Bake
4. In a separate bowl, combine the **2 cups flour**, **1/2 teaspoon salt**, **1 teaspoon baking powder**, and **2 teaspoons baking soda**.
5. Gradually add the dry ingredients to the creamed sugar mixture and mix well.
6. By hand, stir in the **2 cups corn flakes**, **2 cups oatmeal**, **1 cup coconut**, and **1 cup nuts**.
7. Drop by rounded teaspoonfuls onto the prepared cookie sheet.
8. Bake for **12 to 14 minutes**, or until lightly browned around the edges and fragrant.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 1 week, or freeze baked cookies for up to 3 months.
