# Neiman-Marcus Bars

> 📜 **Collection:** UK5 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A famously rich and decadent bar cookie featuring a chewy chocolate cake mix base topped with a sweet, gooey cream cheese layer. These classic treats are perfect for gatherings and bake to a delightful, fudgy consistency.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 45 mins | 1 hour | 24–36 bars |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 13x9 inch pan
* Large mixing bowl
* Electric mixer
* Wire rack

---
## Ingredients

### For the Base Layer
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 box | [[Devil's Food Cake Mix]] | dry |
| 1/2 cup | [[Margarine]] or Butter | softened |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] | large |
| 1 cup | [[Coconut Flakes]] | optional |
| 1 cup | [[Nuts]] | chopped, optional |

### For the Top Layer
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 8 ounces | [[Cream Cheese]] | softened |
| 1/2 cup | [[Margarine]] or Butter | softened |
| 1 pound | [[Powdered Sugar]] | about 4 cups |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Base
1. Preheat your oven to 350°F and grease a 13x9 inch pan.
2. In a large bowl, cream the **2 eggs** and **1/2 cup margarine** together.
3. Add the **1 box Devil's Food Cake Mix**, **1 cup nuts**, and **1 cup coconut flakes**; mix until a thick dough forms.
4. Press the dough evenly into the bottom of the prepared pan.

### Step 2: Prepare the Topping and Bake
5. In a separate bowl, beat the **8 ounces cream cheese** and **1/2 cup margarine** with an electric mixer until smooth.
6. Gradually beat in the **1 pound powdered sugar** until the mixture is creamy.
7. Pour the mixture over the base and spread evenly.
8. Bake for **40 to 50 minutes** until the edges are golden and the center is set but still gooey.
9. Cool completely on a wire rack before cutting into squares.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 3 days or refrigerate for up to 1 week.
