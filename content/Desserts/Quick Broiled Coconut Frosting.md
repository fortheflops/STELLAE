# Quick Broiled Coconut Frosting

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail's Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic, quick-to-prepare broiled frosting that creates a gooey, caramelized coconut and brown sugar topping. Perfect for finishing hot cakes with a bubbly, glazed texture.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5 mins | 10 mins | None | Frosting for one 8x8 or 9x9 inch cake |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* small saucepan
* wire whisk
* oven or broiler

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3/4 Cup | [[Brown Sugar]] | packed |
| 3/4 Cup | [[Coconut]] | shredded |
| 2 Tablespoons | [[Margarine]] or Butter | softened |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Milk]] | evaporated or regular |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Frosting
1. In a small saucepan, combine the **3/4 cup brown sugar**, **2 tablespoons margarine**, and **1/2 cup milk**. Heat over medium heat, stirring constantly until the **margarine** is melted and the **brown sugar** is fully dissolved.
2. Remove the saucepan from the heat and stir in the **3/4 cup coconut**. If the mixture appears too thick, add a splash of additional **milk** to reach a spreadable consistency.

### Step 2: Apply and Broil
3. Pour and spread the hot frosting immediately over your freshly baked, **hot cake**.
4. Return the frosted cake to the oven or place under the broiler for **5-7 minutes**. Watch carefully until the frosting is bubbly and looks golden-glazed, ensuring it does not burn.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This frosting is best prepared and applied immediately while the cake is hot; it does not store well once broiled.
