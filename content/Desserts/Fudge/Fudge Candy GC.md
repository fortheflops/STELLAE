## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 4 1/2 cups                 | [[Sugar]]                                 |
| 1 stick                    | [[Butter]]                                |
| 1 large can                | [[Evaporated Milk]]                       |
| 2 small packages           | [[Chocolate Chips]]                       |
| 1 tablespoon               | [[Vanilla Extract]]                       |
| 1 jar                      | [[Marshmallow Fluff]]                     |
| 1 cup                      | [[Nuts]], chopped                         |
___
## Instructions
1.  In a large, heavy-bottomed pot, combine the sugar, butter, and evaporated milk.
2.  Bring the mixture to a hard boil, stirring constantly for exactly 5 minutes.
3.  Remove the pot from the heat.
4.  Immediately add the chocolate chips and marshmallow fluff, stirring until both are completely melted and the mixture is smooth.
5.  Stir in the vanilla extract and chopped nuts.
6.  Pour the fudge mixture into a greased cookie sheet or baking pan.
7.  Let it cool completely at room temperature or in the refrigerator until firm.
8.  Once firm, cut into squares.
___

>[!Note]
>This is a classic, quick fudge recipe.
