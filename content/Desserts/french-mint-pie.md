# French Mint Pie

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Kay McMann | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A delightful 1964 recipe for French Mint Pie featuring a chocolate peppermint filling in a graham cracker crust. Perfect for chilling and serving with whipped cream dollops.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Crust Preparation
1. Combine **12 crushed graham crackers**, **1/4 cup melted butter**, and **1/4 cup sugar** stirred together.
2. Press into pie pan, bake **5 min.** at **325° F**.

### Step 2: Filling and Assembly
3. Beat in order given: **1 cup powdered sugar**, **1/2 cup soft butter**, **2 eggs** (add one at a time beating thoroughly), **2 squares melted chocolate**, **1 tsp. peppermint extract**, and **1/2 tsp. vanilla**.
4. Pour into shell, refrigerate for at least **6 hours** until set.
5. Top with **whip cream** dollops before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Keep refrigerated for at least 6 hours before serving to ensure the pie sets properly.
