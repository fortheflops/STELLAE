# Ginger Bread

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A traditional, comforting spiced gingerbread recipe featuring rich molasses and warming ginger. This old-fashioned treat bakes into a delightfully moist and flavorful cake.*

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Boiling and Mixing
1. Boil **1 cup molasses** and **1/2 cup butter** or shortening together.
2. Add **1 3/4 tsp soda** and beat well.
3. Beat in **1/2 cup sour milk**, **1 egg**, **2 cups flour**, **2 tsp ginger**, and **1/2 tsp salt** until smooth.

### Step 2: Baking
4. Pour the batter into a greased baking pan and bake until a deep golden brown and a rich aroma fills the kitchen, and a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 3 days to keep moist.
