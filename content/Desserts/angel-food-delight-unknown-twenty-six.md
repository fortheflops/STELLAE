# Angel Food Delight

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Marcile | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A delightful layered dessert featuring broken angel food cake, pistachio pudding, bananas, and Cool Whip. Topped with slivered almonds for a delightful crunch.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | None | 2 hours | 12 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 8x13 pan
* Mixing bowl

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | [[Angel Food Cake]] | Broken up |
| 2 pkgs | [[Pistachio Pudding Inst.]] | Instant |
| As needed | [[Bananas]] | Sliced on top |
| 1 large | [[Kool Whip]] | Thawed |
| As needed | [[Slivered Almonds]] | On top |

---

## Instructions

### Assembly
1. Layer the broken **1 angel food cake** in an 8x13 pan.
2. Prepare the **2 pkgs pistachio pudding inst.** and spread over the cake.
3. Arrange the **as needed bananas** sliced on top of the pudding layer.
4. Spread the **1 large kool whip** evenly over the bananas.
5. Sprinkle **as needed slivered almonds** on top until covered.
6. Chill in the refrigerator until set and chilled through.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Cover and refrigerate for at least 2 hours before serving to let the flavors meld together.
