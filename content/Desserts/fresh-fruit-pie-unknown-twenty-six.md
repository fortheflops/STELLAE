# Fresh Fruit Pie

## Instructions

### Step 1: Crust
1. Combine **1 1/2 cups flour**, **2 Tbsp sugar**, **2 Tbsp milk**, **1/2 tsp salt**, and **1/2 cup oil** together.
2. Press the mixture evenly into a pie tin and bake until set and lightly golden.

### Step 2: Filling and Assembly
3. In a saucepan, combine **4 Tbsp cornstarch**, **1 cup sugar**, **2 Tbsp white syrup**, and **1 cup water**.
4. Cook the mixture over medium heat until thick and translucent, stirring constantly.
5. Remove from heat and stir in **3 Tbsp Jell-O** until fully dissolved.
6. Allow the mixture to cool, then gently fold in the **fresh fruit** slices.
7. Pour the fruit mixture into the cooled pie shell and chill in the refrigerator until set.
8. Top with **cool whip** before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the completed pie in the refrigerator for up to 24 hours before serving for best results.
