# Simple Fruit Cobbler with Batter Topping

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic batter-style cobbler featuring a warm, syrupy fruit base topped with a soft, golden-brown crust. This simple recipe uses pantry staples for a quick and comforting homemade dessert.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 40 mins | None | 6-8 Servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* saucepan
* mixing bowl
* whisk
* 8x8 inch baking dish

## Ingredients

### Fruit Base
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 Can | [[Fruit]] | 15-20 oz, peaches or cherries |
| 1 Cup | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 4 Tablespoons | [[Margarine]] | Or butter |

### Batter
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3/4 Cup | [[Flour]] | All-purpose |
| 3/4 Cup | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 3/4 Cup | [[Milk]] | Whole or 2% |
| 1 1/2 Teaspoons | [[Baking Powder]] | None |
| 1 Pinch | [[Salt]] | Fine |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare Fruit Base
1. Preheat oven to 350°F.
2. In a saucepan, combine **1 can fruit**, **1 cup sugar**, and **4 tablespoons margarine**. Heat over medium heat, stirring until the **margarine** is melted and the **sugar** is dissolved. Pour the mixture into an 8x8 inch baking dish.

### Step 2: Prepare Batter and Bake
3. In a separate mixing bowl, whisk together the **3/4 cup flour**, **3/4 cup sugar**, **1 1/2 teaspoons baking powder**, and **1 pinch salt**.
4. Stir in the **3/4 cup milk** until you have a smooth batter.
5. Gently pour the batter evenly over the hot fruit mixture. Do not stir.
6. Bake for **35-40 minutes**, or until the topping is golden brown and fragrant, and a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store covered at room temperature for up to 2 days or refrigerate for up to 4 days; reheat in the oven before serving.
