# Flan

> 📜 **Collection:** Patsy Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic, rich flan recipe featuring a smooth egg custard made with whole eggs and extra yolks, baked over a golden-brown caramel syrup. This elegant dessert offers a silky texture and deep caramel flavor.*

| 2 Tsp | [[Vanilla]] | None |
| 6 | [[Whole Eggs]] | Large |
| 4 | [[Egg Yolks]] | Large |
| 4 Cups | [[Milk]] | Hot |

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare Caramel
1. In a wide saucepan, combine **1 cup of sugar** and **1/2 cup water**. Bring to a boil over moderate heat, stirring until the **sugar** is dissolved.
2. Add **1/8 tsp cream of tartar** and boil without stirring, shaking the pan occasionally, until the syrup turns a deep, fragrant golden brown.
3. Immediately pour the caramel into a **2-quart mold**, tilting quickly to coat the bottom and sides thoroughly.

### Step 2: Prepare Custard
4. In a large bowl, beat the **6 whole eggs**, **4 egg yolks**, and the remaining **1/2 cup sugar** until well combined.
5. Slowly pour the **4 cups hot milk** in a thin stream into the egg mixture while stirring constantly.
6. Stir in the **2 tsp vanilla** and strain the mixture through a fine-mesh sieve directly into the caramel-coated mold.

### Step 3: Bake and Serve
7. Place the mold into a larger baking pan and fill with hot water halfway up the sides of the mold.
8. Bake at 325°F for **1 hour**, or until a knife inserted near the center comes out clean.
9. Remove from the water bath and cool completely. Run a thin knife around the edge and invert onto a rimmed serving platter.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Flan is best made a day in advance and kept chilled in the refrigerator to allow the caramel to liquefy into a sauce.
