# Famous Millionaire Pie

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A decadent, two-layer chilled dessert featuring a creamy sugar-butter base topped with whipped cream, crushed pineapple, and crunchy pecans. This classic recipe yields two rich pies perfect for gatherings.*

## Ingredients

### Base Filling
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 cups | [[Powdered Sugar]] | sifted |
| 1/4 pound (1 stick) | [[Margarine]] or [[Butter]] | softened |
| 2 large | [[Eggs]] | fresh, whole |
| 1/8 teaspoon | [[Salt]] | None |
| 1/4 teaspoon | [[Vanilla Extract]] | None |

### Topping and Crust
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 | [[Pie Crusts]] | 9-inch, baked |
| 1 cup | [[Heavy Cream]] | None |
| 1/2 cup | [[Powdered Sugar]] | sifted |
| 1 cup | [[Pineapple]] | crushed, well drained |
| 1/2 cup | [[Pecans]] | chopped |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Base Preparation
1. Cream together **2 cups powdered sugar** and **1/4 pound butter** using an electric mixer.
2. Add **2 large eggs**, **1/8 teaspoon salt**, and **1/4 teaspoon vanilla extract**.
3. Beat until the mixture is light and fluffy, then spread evenly into the **2 baked 9-inch pie crusts** and chill.

### Step 2: Topping and Assembly
4. Whip **1 cup heavy cream** until stiff peaks form.
5. Blend in **1/2 cup powdered sugar**, then fold in **1 cup crushed pineapple** and **1/2 cup chopped pecans**.
6. Spread this mixture on top of the base layer and chill thoroughly until set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** These pies must be chilled thoroughly before serving; they can be prepared up to 24 hours in advance and kept refrigerated.
