# Date Nut Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Five | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic, dense fruit and nut cake loaded with rich dates and crunchy pecans. Perfectly spiced and baked low and slow for a moist, tender crumb.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 25 mins | 4 hours | None | 16 servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Baking pan

## Ingredients

### Cake Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 lb. | [[Oleomargarine]] | Softened |
| 1 lb. (2 cups) | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 6 | [[Eggs]] | Whole |
| 1 qt. | [[Pecan halves]] | Whole |
| 1 lb. | [[Dates]] | Pitted |
| 4 cups | [[Flour]] | All-purpose |
| 1 tsp. | [[Baking powder]] | Standard |
| 2 oz. | [[Lemon extract]] | Liquid |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing and Baking
1. Preheat the oven to **250°F**.
2. Cream together the **1 lb. oleomargarine** and **1 lb. sugar** until smooth.
3. Beat in the **6 eggs** one at a time.
4. Fold in the **1 qt. pecan halves** and **1 lb. dates**.
5. Sift together the **4 cups flour** and **1 tsp. baking powder**, then gently mix into the batter along with the **2 oz. lemon extract**.
6. Pour the batter into a greased pan and bake at **250°F** for **4 hours** if using 1 pan, or for **2 hours** if splitting the batter into 2 pans, until golden brown and firm to the touch.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This cake stores exceptionally well and can be wrapped tightly and aged for several days before serving.
