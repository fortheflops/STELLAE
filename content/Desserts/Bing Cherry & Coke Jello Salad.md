# Bing Cherry & Coke Jello Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic, vintage-style molded Jello salad featuring Bing cherries, pineapple, and nuts in a cherry Jello base made with Coca-Cola for a unique, fizzy sweetness. This retro dessert is a perfect centerpiece for potlucks and holiday gatherings.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 5 mins | 6 hours | 10–12 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Saucepan
* Large mixing bowl
* Measuring cup
* Jello mold or serving dish

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 #2 can | [[Bing Cherries]] | drained, juice reserved |
| 1 Cup | [[Crushed Pineapple]] | drained, juice reserved |
| 2 packages | [[Cherry Jello]] | 3 oz each |
| 2 Cups | [[Fruit Juice]] | reserved fruit juice supplemented with grape juice |
| 2 Cups | [[Coca-Cola]] | chilled |
| 1 Cup | [[Chopped Nuts]] | none |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Base
1. Drain the **[[Bing Cherries]]** and **[[Crushed Pineapple]]**, reserving the liquid. Add enough **grape juice** to the reserved juice to reach a total of **2 cups**.
2. In a saucepan, bring the **2 cups fruit juice** to a boil.
3. In a large bowl, combine the **2 packages cherry jello** with the boiling juice, stirring until completely dissolved. Allow to cool to room temperature.

### Step 2: Chill and Set
4. Stir the **2 cups chilled coca-cola** into the cooled **jello mixture**.
5. Place the bowl in the refrigerator until the mixture reaches the consistency of unbeaten egg whites.
6. Gently fold in the drained **[[Bing Cherries]]**, **[[Crushed Pineapple]]**, and **[[Chopped Nuts]]**.
7. Pour into a mold and refrigerate for 4-6 hours until firm and set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This salad must be made at least 6 hours in advance to ensure it is fully set; it can be stored in the refrigerator for up to 3 days.
