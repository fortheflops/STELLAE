# Plum Kuchen

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Eight | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A traditional German-style plum cake featuring a rich, buttery press-in shortcrust pastry topped with sweet Italian plums, cinnamon sugar, and dots of butter. Perfect for late summer baking when fresh plums are in abundance.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Dough
1. Mix the **1/2 cup sugar**, **2 cups flour**, **1/2 lb butter**, **1 egg**, and **1/2 tsp vanilla extract** with your hands until well blended.
2. Form the mixture into a cohesive ball, wrap in plastic, and refrigerate for **1 hour** to chill.

### Step 2: Assemble and Bake
3. Spread the chilled dough onto a cookie sheet using your hands, pressing it out evenly and making a raised lip of dough along the outer edge.
4. Fill the crust with halved **Italian plums**, arranging them tightly.
5. Sprinkle generously with **cinnamon** and **sugar**, then dot with small pieces of **butter**.
6. Bake at **350°F** for **1 hour** until the dough is slightly darkened and golden brown, and the plums are tender and bubbling.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The kuchen dough can be prepared, wrapped tightly, and refrigerated for up to 2 days or frozen for up to a month before assembling and baking.
