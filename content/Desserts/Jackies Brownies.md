# Jackie's Brownies

> 📜 **Collection:** PatsyCollection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Jackie | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A simple and wonderfully fudgy brownie recipe featuring Hershey's chocolate syrup for a rich, classic flavor. These brownies offer a perfectly chewy texture that is ideal for any occasion.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 30 mins | None | 16–24 brownies |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 9x13 inch pan
* Large mixing bowl
* Electric mixer
* Wire rack

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 cup (2 sticks) | [[Oleo]] | Margarine or butter, softened |
| 1 cup | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 4 | [[Eggs]] | Large |
| 1 cup + 1 Tbsp | [[Flour]] | All-purpose |
| 1 cup | [[Hershey Chocolate Syrup]] | Room temperature |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and Mixing
1. Preheat your oven to 350°F and grease a 9x13 inch pan.
2. In a large bowl, cream the **1 cup (2 sticks) oleo** and **1 cup sugar** together with an electric mixer until light and fluffy.
3. Add the **4 eggs** one at a time, beating well after each addition.
4. On low speed, gradually add the **1 cup + 1 Tbsp flour**, mixing until it is just combined.
5. Stir in the **1 cup Hershey chocolate syrup** until the batter is smooth and uniform in color.

### Step 2: Baking and Cooling
6. Pour the batter into the greased pan.
7. Bake for **30 minutes**, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out with moist, fudgy crumbs; do not overbake.
8. Let the brownies cool completely in the pan on a wire rack before cutting.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 3 days or freeze for up to 3 months.
