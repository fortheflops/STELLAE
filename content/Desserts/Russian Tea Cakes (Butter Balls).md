# Russian Tea Cakes (Butter Balls)

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic recipe for tender, buttery cookies also known as Russian Tea Cakes or Mexican Wedding Cookies. This simple shortbread-style dough is loaded with nuts, baked, and rolled in powdered sugar while still warm for a melt-in-your-mouth treat.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 18 mins | None | 3 dozen cookies |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large bowl
* Electric mixer
* Cookie sheet
* Wire cooling rack

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 cup (2 sticks) | [[Butter]] | softened |
| 1 tablespoon | [[Powdered Sugar]] | for dough |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Vanilla]] | liquid |
| 2 cups | [[Flour]] | sifted all-purpose |
| 1 cup | [[Nuts]] | finely chopped pecans or walnuts |
| 1 cup | [[Powdered Sugar]] | for rolling |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mix the Dough
1. Preheat your oven to **350°F**.
2. In a large bowl, cream the **1 cup (2 sticks) butter** and **1 tablespoon powdered sugar** together with an electric mixer until light and fluffy.
3. Beat in the **1 teaspoon vanilla** until well incorporated.
4. On low speed, gradually add the **2 cups sifted flour** and mix until a stiff dough forms.
5. Stir in the **1 cup finely chopped nuts** until evenly distributed.

### Step 2: Shape and Bake
6. Shape the dough into small, 1-inch balls and place them on an ungreased cookie sheet.
7. Bake for **15 to 18 minutes** until the bottoms are lightly golden and the cookies are set but the tops do not brown.

### Step 3: Sugar and Cool
8. Let the cookies cool on the baking sheet for just a minute or two until you can handle them.
9. While they are still very warm, gently roll each ball in the **1 cup powdered sugar** to coat completely, then place on a wire rack to cool.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store cooled cookies in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 1 week, or freeze unbaked dough balls for later use.
