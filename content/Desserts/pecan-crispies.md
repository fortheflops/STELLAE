# Pecan Crispies

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Maryou | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A delightful old-fashioned recipe for crisp pecan cookies from the kitchen of Maryou. These simple drop cookies feature a buttery base loaded with rich pecans.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 10 mins | None | 24 cookies |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Cookie sheet

---
## Ingredients

### Cookie Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 C. | [[Butter]] | Softened |
| 2/3 C. | [[Brown Sugar]] | Packed |
| 1 | [[Egg]] | Beaten |
| 1 C. | [[Flour]] | All-purpose |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Soda]] | Baking soda |
| 1 C. | [[Pecans]] | Chopped |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing and Baking
1. Cream the **1 C. butter** and **2/3 C. brown sugar** together until light and fluffy.
2. Add the beaten **1 egg**, followed by the **1 C. flour** and **1/4 tsp soda**.
3. Stir in the **1 C. pecans** until just combined.
4. Drop spoonfuls of the batter onto a buttered cookie sheet.
5. Cook at 350° until golden brown and set around the edges.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store baked cookies in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 5 days.
