# Mumies (Albanese) Rice Pudding

> 📜 **Collection:** Elizabeth Knee Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Rosalie | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic Albanese-style rice pudding recipe sourced from a New York restaurant, featuring a rich simmered milk and rice base finished with egg, sugar, and vanilla. Best served warm with cream or vanilla ice cream.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Simmer the Rice
1. Combine the **2 qts milk**, **1 cup rice**, and **3 oz or 2 tbsp butter** in a saucepan and simmer uncovered for **45 minutes** over low heat, stirring a few times to prevent sticking until the rice is tender.

### Step 2: Thicken and Finish
2. In a separate bowl, mix together the **1 egg**, **4 oz sugar**, **1/4 tsp salt**, and **3/4 tsp vanilla extract**.
3. Add this mixture to the hot rice mixture and cook together for a few minutes, stirring constantly, until the pudding thickens and coats the back of a spoon.
4. Serve warm with cream or vanilla ice cream. Do it slowly the first time!

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Can be made ahead and stored in the refrigerator for up to 3 days. Reheat gently on the stovetop with a splash of milk if it becomes too thick.
