# Lemon Sugar Cookies

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Dough
1. Preheat your oven to **400°F** and lightly grease your baking sheets.
2. In a large bowl, cream together the **1/2 cup shortening** and **1 cup sugar**.
3. Add the **1 unbeaten egg**, **2 tablespoons milk**, and **1 teaspoon grated lemon rind**; beat well.
4. In a separate bowl, sift together the **2 cups flour**, **1 teaspoon baking powder**, **1/2 teaspoon baking soda**, and **1 teaspoon salt**.
5. Gradually add the dry ingredients to the creamed mixture, mixing until a soft dough forms.

### Step 2: Shape and Bake
6. Drop by rounded teaspoonfuls onto the prepared baking sheet.
7. Dip the bottom of a glass in **granulated sugar** and use it to flatten each cookie.
8. Lightly sprinkle the tops of the cookies with **nutmeg**.
9. Bake for **8-10 minutes**, or until light golden and fragrant.
10. Let the cookies cool on the sheet for a minute before transferring to a wire rack.
