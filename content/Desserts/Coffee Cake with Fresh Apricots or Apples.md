# Coffee Cake with Fresh Apricots or Apples

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Mix Batter
1. Preheat oven to **350-375°F**. Grease and flour two **8-inch round or square cake pans**.
2. In a large bowl, sift together the **2 cups sifted flour**, **¾ cup sugar**, **2 tsps baking powder**, and **½ tsp salt**.
3. Cut in the **½ cup shortening** with a pastry blender or your fingers until the mixture resembles coarse crumbs.
4. In a separate small bowl, beat the **1 egg** and **¾ cup milk** together.
5. Add the egg and milk mixture to the dry ingredients. Use a table knife or spatula to gently chop and fold the liquid into the flour mixture until it is just combined, yielding a thick and lumpy batter.
6. Divide the batter evenly between the two prepared pans.

### Step 2: Add Toppings and Bake
7. Choose your topping: For the fruit version, arrange fresh **apricots** or **apples** slices thickly over the batter, sprinkle with **½ cup sugar** and a dash of **nutmeg**, then dot with small pieces of **butter**. For the plain version, combine **½ cup sugar**, **2 Tbsps flour**, and **1 tsp cinnamon**, sprinkle over the batter, and dot with **butter**.
8. Bake for **30-45 minutes**, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean and the edges are golden brown.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the baked coffee cakes tightly wrapped at room temperature for up to 2 days, or freeze for longer storage.
