# Silky No-Bake Chocolate Pie

> 📜 **Collection:** General Archive | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A wonderfully simple and rich no-bake chocolate pie with a unique cooking method that creates a silky, decadent filling from just three ingredients: dark chocolate, sweetened condensed milk, and water.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 10 mins | 4 hours | One 9-inch pie |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* medium saucepan
* spoon or spatula
* 9-inch pie pan

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 can | [[Sweetened Condensed Milk]] | e.g., Eagle Brand |
| 1 bar | [[Dark Chocolate]] | e.g., Hershey's, approx 4 oz |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Tap Water]] | cold |
| 1 (9-inch) | [[Baked Pie Shell]] | pre-baked |
| As needed | [[Whipped Cream]] | for topping, optional |
| As needed | [[Extra Dark Chocolate]] | for shaving, optional |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Melt and Cook the Base
1. Break the **dark chocolate** bar into pieces and melt it in a medium saucepan over low heat, stirring until smooth.

### Step 2: Thicken the Filling
2. Add the entire **1 can sweetened condensed milk** to the melted chocolate.
3. Cook over medium heat, stirring **constantly**, until the mixture thickens and becomes 'lumpy' or very thick.

### Step 3: Smooth and Pour
4. As soon as the mixture thickens, remove the saucepan from the heat and immediately pour in the **1/2 cup tap water**.
5. Stir the mixture vigorously until it becomes completely smooth and silky.
6. Pour the smooth chocolate filling into the pre-baked **9-inch baked pie shell** and spread evenly.

### Step 4: Chill and Serve
7. Chill the pie in the refrigerator for at least **4 hours**, or until the filling is completely set.
8. Top with **whipped cream** and **extra dark chocolate** shavings before serving, if desired, until presentation is complete.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store sealed in the refrigerator for up to 3 days.
