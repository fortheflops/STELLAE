# Snooks' Bacardi Rum Cake

> *A classic, award-winning Bacardi rum cake recipe featuring a moist yellow cake and pudding mix base soaked in a rich buttery dark rum glaze.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Bake the Cake
1. Preheat oven to **325°F**. Generously grease and flour a **10-inch Bundt pan**.
2. Sprinkle the **1 cup chopped pecans or walnuts** evenly over the bottom of the prepared pan.
3. In a large bowl, combine the **1 package yellow cake mix**, **1 package vanilla instant pudding mix**, **4 eggs**, **½ cup cold water**, **½ cup oil**, and **½ cup BACARDI dark rum**.
4. Beat with an electric mixer at medium speed for **2 minutes** until smooth.
5. Pour the batter over the nuts in the pan.
6. Bake for **60 minutes**, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean and the edges pull slightly away from the pan.
7. Let the cake cool in the pan on a wire rack for **15 minutes** while you prepare the glaze.

### Step 2: Prepare Glaze and Finish
8. In a saucepan, combine the **1 stick butter or margarine**, **¼ cup water**, and **1 cup sugar**.
9. Bring the mixture to a boil over medium heat, stirring constantly. Continue to boil for **5 minutes**, still stirring until thickened and syrupy.
10. Remove the pan from the heat and carefully stir in the **½ cup BACARDI dark rum**.
11. Generously prick the top surface of the warm cake (while it's still in the pan) with an ice pick or wooden skewer.
12. Slowly and evenly spoon the warm glaze over the cake, allowing it time to soak completely into the holes.
13. Let the cake cool completely in the pan before inverting it onto a serving plate.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store wrapped tightly in plastic wrap or under a cake dome at room temperature for up to 4 days; the flavor often improves on the second day.
