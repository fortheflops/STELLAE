# Plain Sugar Cookies

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic, sturdy roll-and-cut sugar cookie recipe featuring a unique technique of rolling the dough in sugar for a crisp, sweet crust. These cookies are perfect for holiday shapes and maintain their form beautifully during baking.*

| 2 Teaspoons | [[Baking Powder]] | none |
| Pinch | [[Salt]] | none |
| As needed | [[Sugar]] | for rolling |

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Dough
1. In a large bowl, cream the **1/2 cup butter or Crisco** and **1 cup sugar** together with an electric mixer.
2. Beat in the **3 eggs** and **1 teaspoon vanilla** until smooth.
3. In a separate bowl, whisk together the **3 cups flour**, **2 teaspoons baking powder**, and **pinch of salt**.
4. Gradually add the dry ingredients to the creamed mixture, mixing on low speed until a very stiff dough forms.

### Step 2: Chill and Roll
5. Cover the dough and chill for at least **30-60 minutes** to ensure it is easy to handle.
6. Preheat your oven to **375°F**.
7. Sprinkle your work surface generously with **extra sugar**. Roll the chilled dough out on the sugar to your desired thickness.

### Step 3: Bake
8. Cut the dough into shapes with cookie cutters and place them on an ungreased cookie sheet.
9. Bake for **8-10 minutes**, or until the edges are just beginning to turn golden and the cookies are set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The dough can be prepared and chilled for up to 24 hours before rolling. Baked cookies store well in an airtight container for up to one week.
