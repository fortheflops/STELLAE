# Nut Drop Cookies

> 📜 **Collection:** Burcea Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** McColl Family | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic mid-century recipe for tender drop cookies sweetened with brown sugar and Karo syrup, packed with chopped nuts. These quick-to-make treats bake up golden brown with a delightful crunch.*

## Ingredients

### Cookie Dough
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 1/2 cups | [[All-Purpose Flour]] | sifted |
| 1/2 tsp. | [[Salt]] | none |
| 2/3 cup | [[Shortening]] | such as Spry |
| 1/2 cup | [[Brown Sugar]] | none |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] | none |
| 1/2 cup | [[Karo Syrup]] | none |
| 1/2 tsp. | [[Vanilla Extract]] | none |
| 3/4 cup | [[Nuts]] | chopped |

### Topping (Optional)
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 24 | [[Pecans]] | halved, to place on top of each cookie |

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Sift
1. Preheat oven to **350°F** and grease a baking sheet.
2. Sift together the **1 1/2 cups sifted all purpose flour** and **1/2 tsp. salt**; set aside.

### Step 2: Mix the Dough
3. Cream the **2/3 cup shortening** and **1/2 cup brown sugar** together until light and fluffy.
4. Add the **2 eggs**, one at a time, beating well after each addition.
5. Add the **1/2 cup Karo syrup** and beat the mixture thoroughly.
6. Stir in the **1/2 tsp. vanilla extract**, **3/4 cup chopped nuts**, and the sifted dry ingredients until just combined into a soft dough.

### Step 3: Shape and Bake
7. Drop the dough by teaspoonfuls onto the greased baking sheet.
8. If desired, place **1/2 pecan** on each cookie before baking.
9. Bake at **350°F** for **10 to 12 minutes** until the edges are lightly golden and the centers are set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 5 days, or freeze baked cookies for up to 3 months.
