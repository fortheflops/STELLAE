# Applesauce Spice Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A wonderfully moist and fragrant spice cake made with applesauce, brown sugar, raisins, and nuts. This simple loaf cake is packed with the warm flavors of cinnamon, nutmeg, and cloves.*

| 2 1/4 Cups | [[Flour]] | all-purpose |
| 2 tsp | [[Baking Soda]] | None |
| 1 tsp | [[Cinnamon]] | None |
| 1/2 tsp | [[Nutmeg]] | None |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Cloves]] | None |
| 1 Cup | [[Raisins]] | None |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Nuts]] | chopped |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and Mixing
1. Preheat your oven to 350°F. Grease and flour a standard loaf pan.
2. In a large bowl, cream the **1 cup brown sugar** and **1/2 cup shortening** together with an electric mixer until light and fluffy. Add the **2 eggs** one at a time, beating well after each addition.
3. In a separate bowl, sift together the **2 1/4 cups flour**, **2 tsp baking soda**, **1/2 tsp nutmeg**, **1 tsp cinnamon**, and **1/4 tsp cloves**.
4. Add the dry ingredients to the creamed mixture alternately with the **2 cups applesauce**, beginning and ending with the dry ingredients. Mix until just combined.
5. Gently fold in the **1 cup raisins** and **1/2 cup nuts**.

### Step 2: Baking and Cooling
6. Pour the batter into the prepared loaf pan.
7. Bake for 1 hour, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean and the top is golden brown.
8. Let the cake cool in the pan for 10-15 minutes before turning it out onto a wire rack to cool completely.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 3 days or freeze for up to 2 months.
