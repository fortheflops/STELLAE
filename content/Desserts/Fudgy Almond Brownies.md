# Fudgy Almond Brownies

> 📜 **Collection:** Patsy Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic recipe for dense, fudgy brownies made with melted unsweetened chocolate and shredded almonds. These brownies feature a signature shiny, crackly top and a rich, chewy texture.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 25 mins | None | 16 brownies |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 9-inch square pan
* Double boiler
* Electric mixer
* Spatula
* Wire rack

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 oz | [[Unsweetened Chocolate]] | melted |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Sweet Butter]] | unsalted, melted |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] | large |
| 1 Cup | [[Sugar]] | granulated |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Unblanched Almonds]] | shredded or slivered |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Un-sifted All-Purpose Flour]] | none |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and Melting
1. Preheat your oven to 350°F and grease a 9-inch square pan.
2. Melt the **2 oz unsweetened chocolate** and **1/2 cup sweet butter** together over a double boiler until smooth; set aside to cool slightly.

### Step 2: Mixing the Batter
3. In a medium bowl, beat the **2 eggs** and **1 cup sugar** with an electric mixer on high speed until the mixture is thick, pale, and falls in a ribbon, about 3-5 minutes.
4. On low speed, gently mix in the cooled **chocolate** and **butter** mixture.
5. Using a spatula, fold in the **1/2 cup un-sifted all-purpose flour** and **1/2 cup unblanched almonds** until just combined, being careful not to overmix.

### Step 3: Baking and Cooling
6. Pour the batter into the prepared pan and spread evenly.
7. Bake for **25 minutes** until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out with moist crumbs.
8. Let the brownies cool completely in the pan on a wire rack before cutting into squares.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 3 days or freeze for up to 1 month.
