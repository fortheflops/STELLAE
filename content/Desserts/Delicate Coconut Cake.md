# Delicate Coconut Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** UK15 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A vintage 1918 recipe for a delicate coconut cake made with cornmeal for a unique texture and folded egg whites for lightness. Topped with toasted almonds and shredded coconut for a delightful finish.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Mix Dry Ingredients
1. Preheat the oven to **350°F**. Grease and flour an **8-inch square pan**.
2. In a medium bowl, whisk together the **1 1/2 cups flour**, **1/2 cup corn meal**, **2 teaspoons baking powder**, and **1/2 teaspoon salt**.

### Step 2: Cream Wet Ingredients and Combine
3. In a large bowl, cream the **1/4 cup butter** and **1 cup sugar** together until light and fluffy. Beat in the **1/2 teaspoon almond extract**.
4. Add the dry ingredients to the creamed mixture in three parts, alternating with the **2/3 cup milk**, beginning and ending with the dry ingredients. Mix until just combined.
5. Stir in the **1/2 cup coconut**.

### Step 3: Whip Whites and Fold
6. In a separate, clean bowl, beat the **3 large egg whites** with an electric mixer until stiff peaks form.
7. Gently fold the stiffly beaten **3 large egg whites** into the batter in three additions, until no white streaks remain.

### Step 4: Bake
8. Pour the batter into the prepared pan and spread evenly.
9. Sprinkle the **2 tablespoons almonds** and **2 tablespoons coconut** over the top of the batter.
10. Bake for **45 to 50 minutes**, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean and the top is golden brown.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the cooled cake wrapped tightly in plastic wrap at room temperature for up to 3 days or refrigerate for up to 5 days.
