## Recipe
| Measurements          | Ingredients                        |
| :-------------------- | :--------------------------------- |
| 1 cup                      | [[Powdered Sugar]]                        |
| As Needed                  | [[Canned Milk]] or [[Regular Milk]]       |
| 1 teaspoon                 | [[Vanilla Extract]]                       |
| 1 tablespoon               | [[Butter]], softened                      |
___
## Instructions
1.  Combine the powdered sugar, vanilla, and softened butter in a bowl.
2.  Gradually add a small amount of milk while mixing, until the frosting reaches your desired spreading consistency.
