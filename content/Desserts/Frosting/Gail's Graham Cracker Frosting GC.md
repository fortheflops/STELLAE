## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| $\frac{1}{2}$ Cup                | Condensed [[Milk]]                        |
| Two Tablespoons                  | Melted [[Butter]]                         |
| $\frac{1}{2}$ Cup                | Chopped [[Walnuts]]                       |
| One Cup                          | Powdered [[Sugar]] (Confectioners' Sugar) |
| Two Tablespoons                  | Hershey's [[Cocoa Powder]]                |
| $\frac{3}{4}$ Cup                | Rolled [[Graham Cracker Crumbs]]          |
| One Teaspoon                     | [[Vanilla Extract]]                       |
| Little (Optional)                | More [[Milk]] (if too dry)                |
| Little (Optional)                | More [[Graham Cracker Crumbs]] or Powdered [[Sugar]] (if too runny) |
___
## Instructions
1.  Put $\frac{1}{2}$ cup of condensed milk into a bowl.
2.  Add two tablespoons of melted butter, one cup of powdered sugar, and two tablespoons of Hershey's cocoa powder.
3.  Mix well. (The card notes "Mixer okay" for this step).
4.  Add $\frac{3}{4}$ cup of rolled graham cracker crumbs, one teaspoon of vanilla (flavoring), and $\frac{1}{2}$ cup of chopped walnuts.
5.  Stir these ingredients in by hand.
6.  Spreads easier on a warm cake, but don't have the frosting too stiff otherwise.
___

>[!Note]
>"I make 1 & $\frac{1}{2}$ recipe -- if too runny can add more crumbs or powdered sugar. Add little more milk if too dry."
