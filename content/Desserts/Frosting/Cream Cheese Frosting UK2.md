## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
| 1 Package (8oz)     | [[Cream Cheese]], softened           |
| 1/2 Stick         | [[Margarine]]                       |
| 1 Teaspoon       | [[Vanilla]]                       |
| 1 Box             | [[Powdered Sugar]]                   |
|                   | Chopped [[Pecans]]                   |
___
### Instructions
1.  Beat cheese and margarine until smooth.
2.  Add sugar and mix well.
3.  Add vanilla and beat until smooth.
4.  Spread between layers and on top and sides of cake.
5.  Sprinkle with pecans.
