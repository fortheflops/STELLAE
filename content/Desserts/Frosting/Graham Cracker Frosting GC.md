## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 1/2 cup                    | [[Condensed Milk]]                        |
| 2 tablespoons              | Melted [[Butter]]                         |
| 1/2 cup                    | Chopped [[Walnuts]]                       |
| 1 cup                      | Sifted [[Powdered Sugar]]                 |
| 2 tablespoons              | [[Hershey's Cocoa Powder]]                |
| 3/4 cup                    | [[Graham Cracker Crumbs]]                 |
| 1 teaspoon                 | [[Vanilla Extract]]                       |
___
## Instructions
1.  In a bowl, combine the condensed milk, melted butter, sifted powdered sugar, and cocoa powder.
2.  Mix well.
3.  Add the graham cracker crumbs and beat the mixture until it is creamy.
4.  Stir in the vanilla (flavoring) and chopped walnuts.
5.  Spread the frosting on a warm cake.
