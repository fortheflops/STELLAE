## Recipe
| Measurements      | Ingredients                   |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 1/4 cups        | [[Confectioners Sugar]], sifted   |
| 1/8 teaspoon      | [[Cream of Tartar]]             |
| 1                 | [[Egg White]]                     |
___
## Instructions
1.  In a small bowl, combine the sifted confectioners' sugar, cream of tartar, and egg white.
2.  Using an electric mixer on low speed, beat the ingredients until they are blended.
3.  Increase the mixer speed to high and continue beating until the frosting is very stiff. The mixture is ready when a knife drawn through it leaves a clean, defined path.
4.  Use immediately for piping or decorating. Keep the bowl covered with a damp cloth when not in use to prevent the frosting from hardening.
___

>[!Note]
>This recipe produces a stiff frosting similar to royal icing, which is ideal for piping detailed decorations that need to hold their shape and dry hard.
