## Recipe
| Measurements      | Ingredients                   |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| **Chocolate Base**|                                   |
| 4 tablespoons     | [[Butter]], softened              |
| 3/4 cup           | [[Confectioners Sugar]], sifted   |
| 3 squares (3 oz)  | [[Unsweetened Chocolate]]         |
| 1 teaspoon        | [[Vanilla Extract]]             |
| 1/2 teaspoon      | [[Salt]]                        |
| **Meringue** |                                   |
| 2                 | [[Egg Whites]]                    |
| 3/4 cup           | [[Confectioners Sugar]], sifted   |
___
## Instructions
1.  Melt the unsweetened chocolate over low heat or in a microwave and set it aside to cool slightly.
2.  **To make the chocolate base:** In a mixing bowl, cream the softened butter. Gradually add the first 3/4 cup of confectioners sugar and blend well. Stir in the cooled melted chocolate, vanilla, and salt, and mix until smooth.
3.  **To make the meringue:** In a separate, clean bowl, beat the egg whites with an electric mixer until they form stiff, but not dry, peaks.
4.  Gradually add the second 3/4 cup of confectioners sugar, about 2 tablespoons at a time, beating well after each addition.
5.  Continue beating until the meringue is thick, glossy, and stands in firm peaks.
6.  **To combine:** Add the meringue to the chocolate base. Fold together gently but thoroughly, just until no white streaks remain.
7.  Use immediately to frost the cake.
___

>[!Note]
>This recipe makes enough frosting to cover the top and sides of a two-layer, 9-inch cake.
