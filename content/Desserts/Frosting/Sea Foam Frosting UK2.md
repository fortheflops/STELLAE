## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
| ⅓ Cup               | [[Sugar]]                          |
| ⅓ Cup               | Firmly Packed [[Brown Sugar]]       |
| ⅓ Cup               | [[Water]]                          |
| 1 Tablespoon            | [[Corn Syrup]]                     |
| 1                   | [[Egg White]]                      |
| ¼ Teaspoon            | [[Cream of Tartar]]                |

___
### Instructions
1.  Combine in saucepan ⅓ Cup sugar, ⅓ cup firmly packed brown sugar, ⅓ Cup water.
2. Add 1 tbsp. Corn syrup. Cook until a little syrup dropped in cold water forms a soft ball.
3. Beat 1 egg white with ¼ tsp Cream of Tartar until stiff peaks form.
4. Add syrup to egg white in slow stream, beating until thick.
