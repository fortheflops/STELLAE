# Burnt Sugar Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A delightful traditional burnt sugar cake featuring homemade caramel syrup and whipped egg whites for a tender crumb. This classic baked treat brings rich, caramelized sweetness to any dessert table.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 30 mins | 45 mins | None | 12 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Cake pan
* Saucepan
* Electric mixer

## Ingredients

### Cake Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 cups | [[Powdered Sugar]] | Sifted |
| 3/4 cup | [[Butter]] | Softened |
| 1 tsp | [[Baking Powder]] | None |
| 2 1/2 cups | [[Flour]] | Sifted |
| 1 cup | [[Milk]] | Divided |
| 5 | [[Eggs]] | Separated into yolks and whites |
| 1/2 cup | [[Sugar]] | For caramelizing |
| 1/2 cup | [[Water]] | For caramelizing |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Caramel Syrup
1. Caramelize **1/2 cup sugar** and **1/2 cup water** in a saucepan until golden and aromatic. Add the mixture to **1 cup milk** (if too much, use 1/4 less cup).

### Step 2: Batter Preparation
2. Cream **3/4 cup butter**, then add **2 cups powdered sugar** and the egg yolks. Alternately add the prepared milk mixture and **2 1/2 cups flour**, mixing until smooth.

### Step 3: Folding and Baking
3. Fold in the stiffly beaten **5 egg whites** gently into the batter until just incorporated. Pour into a prepared baking pan and bake until a golden crust forms and a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The cake layers can be baked ahead of time, wrapped tightly, and stored at room temperature for a day or frozen before frosting.
