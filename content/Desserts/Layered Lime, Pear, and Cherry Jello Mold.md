# Layered Lime, Pear, and Cherry Jello Mold

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A beautiful two-layer vintage Jello mold featuring a decorative base of pears and cherries in clear lime Jello, topped with a smooth, tangy lime and cream cheese mousse.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 5 mins | 6 hours | 8-10 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Jello mold
* Measuring cup
* Large mixing bowl
* Electric mixer

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 (6 ounce) package | [[Lime Jello]] | Divided |
| 1 (8 ounce) package | [[Philadelphia Cream Cheese]] | Softened |
| 1 large can | [[Pears]] | Halved, juice reserved |
| 1 jar | [[Maraschino Cherries]] | Drained |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Oil]] | For greasing the mold |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep the Mold and Base
1. Lightly grease the inside of a jello mold with **1 teaspoon oil**.
2. Drain the juice from the **canned pears** into a measuring cup and add enough cold water to equal the amount of cold liquid required by the jello package directions.
3. In a large bowl, dissolve the **Lime Jello** powder in boiling water, stirring for **2 minutes**. Stir in the reserved cold pear juice mixture.

### Step 2: Layer the Fruit and Chill
4. Pour about one-third of the **Lime Jello** mixture into the prepared mold and refrigerate until it thickens but is not fully set, until tacky to the touch. Keep the remaining Jello at room temperature.
5. Arrange the **pear halves** and **maraschino cherries** decoratively in the thickened Jello layer. Return to the refrigerator to set completely firm.

### Step 3: Prepare and Add Cream Cheese Layer
6. While the fruit layer sets, allow the remaining **Lime Jello** to thicken at room temperature or in the fridge.
7. In a separate bowl, beat the softened **cream cheese** until smooth. Gradually add the remaining thickened **Lime Jello**, beating until the mixture is completely smooth and uniform.
8. Gently pour the cream cheese mixture over the firm fruit layer in the mold. Refrigerate overnight, or for at least **6 hours**, until the entire mold is firm and set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Keep the mold covered and refrigerated for up to 2 days before unmolding and serving.
