# Pennsylvania Dutch Apple Cobbler

> 📜 **Collection:** UK5 | ✍️ **Attribution:** J. Town | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A traditional Pennsylvania Dutch apple cobbler featuring a tender baked apple base, a moist sour cream cake layer, and a crunchy cinnamon-sugar topping. This comforting dessert is best served warm, optionally paired with a rich cream cheese topping.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Apple Base
1. Preheat the oven to 400°F.
2. Peel and slice the **8 apples** and spread them in a 9x13 inch glass casserole dish.
3. Sprinkle with **1 cup sugar** and **1 tablespoon cornstarch**, then place in the oven to bake while preparing the batter.

### Step 2: Prepare the Cobbler and Topping
4. In a large bowl with an electric mixer, cream together the **1/2 cup butter** and **1 cup brown sugar**.
5. Add the **2 eggs** and mix well, then incorporate the **2 cups self-rising flour**, **1 cup sour cream**, and **2 teaspoons rum** until combined.
6. In a separate small bowl, mix the **1/2 cup brown sugar**, **2 tablespoons butter**, **2 tablespoons flour**, and **1 teaspoon cinnamon** to create a crumble.

### Step 3: Assemble and Bake
7. Carefully remove the hot casserole dish from the oven. Dollop the cobbler batter over the hot apples and sprinkle the cinnamon crumble topping over everything.
8. Return to the oven and bake for 30-40 minutes until the cake part is brown, firm, and fragrant.
9. Serve warm, optionally with the prepared cream cheese topping.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the baked cobbler covered in the refrigerator for up to 3 days; reheat in a low oven before serving.
