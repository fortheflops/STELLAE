## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare and Bake Crust
1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C).
2. In a mixing bowl, mix **1/2 cup shortening** and **1/2 cup brown sugar** until well combined.
3. Stir in **1 cup flour** to form a soft dough.
4. Press and flatten the mixture evenly with your hands into an ungreased 9x13-inch pan.
5. Bake for **10 minutes** at 350°F until lightly set.

### Step 2: Add Topping and Finish Baking
6. In a bowl, mix **2 well beaten eggs**, **1 cup brown sugar**, **1 tsp vanilla extract**, **2 tbsp flour**, **1 tsp baking powder**, **1/2 tsp salt**, **1 cup coconut**, and **1 cup slivered almonds** until thoroughly combined.
7. Spread the topping evenly over the warm crust in the pan.
8. Bake for an additional **25 minutes** at 350°F until golden brown and set.
9. Cool completely in the pan before slicing into bars.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store sliced bars in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 5 days, or freeze layered with parchment paper for up to 3 months.
