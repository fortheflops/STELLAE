# Congo Squares (Blondies)

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic recipe for rich and chewy blondie bars, known as Congo Squares, packed with brown sugar, chocolate chips, and nuts. This simple, one-pan dessert is perfect for a crowd.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 30 mins | None | 24–36 bars |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 10x15 inch jelly roll pan
* Medium mixing bowl
* Saucepan
* Wire rack

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 3/4 Cups | [[Flour]] | Sifted, all-purpose |
| 2 1/2 Teaspoons | [[Baking Powder]] | None |
| 1/2 Teaspoon | [[Salt]] | None |
| 2/3 Cup | [[Shortening]] | None |
| 2 1/4 Cups | [[Brown Sugar]] | Packed |
| 3 | [[Eggs]] | Large |
| 1 Cup | [[Nutmeats]] | Broken, walnuts or pecans |
| 1 Package (6-12 oz) | [[Bits]] | Chocolate chips |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation
1. Preheat your oven to 350°F and grease a 10x15 inch jelly roll pan.
2. In a medium bowl, sift or whisk together the **2 3/4 cups flour**, **2 1/2 teaspoons baking powder**, and **1/2 teaspoon salt**.

### Step 2: Mixing the Base
3. In a saucepan, melt the **2/3 cup shortening** over low heat. Remove from heat and stir in the **2 1/4 cups brown sugar** until well combined; allow to cool slightly.
4. Add the **3 eggs** one at a time to the sugar mixture, beating well after each addition.
5. Gradually add the dry ingredients to the wet mixture and mix until just combined.

### Step 3: Bake and Cool
6. Fold in the **1 cup nutmeats** and **1 package chocolate bits** by hand.
7. Spread the batter evenly into the prepared pan and bake for **25-30 minutes** until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out with moist crumbs.
8. Let the pan cool completely on a wire rack before cutting into squares.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 5 days or freeze for up to 3 months.
