## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Mix Dry Ingredients
1. Preheat your oven to **350°F**.
2. In a medium bowl, combine **2 1/4 cups flour**, **1 1/4 tsp baking powder**, and **1 tsp salt**.

### Step 2: Mix Wet Ingredients
3. In a separate bowl, mix together **1 cup mashed bananas** and **1 cup buttermilk**.

### Step 3: Cream and Combine
4. In a large bowl, cream together **2/3 cup shortening** and **1 1/2 cups sugar** until light and fluffy.
5. Add **1/2 tsp banana flavoring** and **2 eggs** to the creamed mixture and beat well.
6. Alternately add the flour mixture and the banana mixture to the creamed shortening, mixing until just combined.

### Step 4: Bake
7. Pour the batter into a prepared baking pan and bake at **350°F** for **30 minutes** until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.

### Step 5: Frosting and Assembly
8. In a medium bowl, mix together **8 oz cream cheese**, **1/2 cup corn syrup**, and **1/2 cup peanut butter** until smooth and creamy.
9. Frost the cooled cake and top with **peanuts**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the frosted cake in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days. Bring to room temperature before serving.
