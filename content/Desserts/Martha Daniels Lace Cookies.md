# Martha Daniel's Lace Cookies

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Martha Daniel | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic recipe for thin, crispy, and buttery oatmeal lace cookies. This simple batter is chilled overnight and baked on foil for a delicate, see-through texture.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 15 mins | Overnight | 5-6 dozen |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* small saucepan
* large mixing bowl
* cookie sheet
* aluminum foil

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/4 Pound | [[Butter]] | 1 stick, melted |
| 1 | [[Egg]] | large |
| 2 Teaspoons | [[Vanilla]] | extract |
| 1 Cup | [[Old Fashioned Oats]] | none |
| 1 Cup | [[Sugar]] | none |
| 1 Teaspoon | [[Salt]] | none |
| 3 Tablespoons | [[Flour]] | sifted |
| 1/4 Teaspoon | [[Baking Powder]] | none |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Dough
1. In a small saucepan, melt the **1/4 pound butter** and let it cool slightly.
2. In a large bowl, beat the **1 egg** well, then stir in the **2 teaspoons vanilla** and the cooled, melted **butter**.
3. In a separate bowl, mix together the **1 cup old fashioned oats**, **1 cup sugar**, **1 teaspoon salt**, **3 tablespoons flour**, and **1/4 teaspoon baking powder**.
4. Add the dry ingredients to the wet mixture and stir until well combined.
5. Cover the bowl and refrigerate overnight.

### Step 2: Bake the Cookies
6. Preheat your oven to **325°F** and cover a cookie sheet with a thin layer of foil.
7. Drop the cold batter by **half-teaspoonfuls** onto the foil, leaving at least 3-4 inches of space for spreading.
8. Bake for **10-15 minutes** until the cookies are a uniform golden brown and fragrant.
9. Let the cookies cool completely on the foil until crisp, then gently peel the foil away from the back of the cookies.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The batter must be chilled overnight for best results; keep the batter in the refrigerator between baking batches to maintain the proper consistency.
