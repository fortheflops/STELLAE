## Recipe
| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 3/4 cup | [[Sugar]] |
| 2 teaspoons | [[Cinnamon]] |
| 2 cans | [[Biscuits]] (10 each) |
| 1 stick | [[Butter]] or [[Margarine]], melted |
___
## Instructions
1.  Preheat oven to 350°F. Grease a Bundt pan.
2.  In a bowl or large plastic bag, mix the **sugar** and **cinnamon**.
3.  Cut each **biscuit** into quarters.
4.  Roll the biscuit pieces in the cinnamon-sugar mixture to coat them.
5.  Arrange the coated pieces in the Bundt pan.
6.  Combine the melted **butter** with any remaining cinnamon-sugar mixture and pour it evenly over the biscuits.
7.  Bake for 30 minutes.
8.  Let cool for 5 minutes in the pan, then turn out onto a platter to serve.
___

>[!Note]
>Inverting the pan while the buttery glaze is still hot is key to ensuring it all pours over the bread.
