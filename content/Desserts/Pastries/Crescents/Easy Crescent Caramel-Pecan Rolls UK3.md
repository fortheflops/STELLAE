## Recipe
### Caramel Layer
| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 5 Tbsp | Margarine (or Butter) |
| $\frac{3}{4}$ Cup | Brown Sugar, packed |
| $\frac{1}{4}$ Cup | [[Water]] |
| $\frac{1}{2}$ Cup | Chopped [[Pecans]] |
### Rolls & Filling
| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 2 (8-oz) cans | Refrigerated [[Crescent Rolls]] |
| 3 Tbsp | [[Butter]] or Margarine, softened |
| $\frac{1}{4}$ Cup | [[Sugar]] |
| 2 tsp | [[Cinnamon]] |
___
## Instructions
1.  Preheat oven to 375°F. Place **5 Tbsp of margarine** in a 13x9 inch pan and put it in the oven to melt.
2.  Remove the pan from the oven. Stir in the **brown sugar** and **water**. Sprinkle the **pecans** evenly over the mixture.
3.  In a small bowl, combine the **$\frac{1}{4}$ cup sugar** and **cinnamon**.
4.  Unroll the **crescent dough** into 8 rectangles, pressing the seams to seal.
5.  Spread the rectangles with the **3 Tbsp softened butter** and sprinkle with the cinnamon-sugar mixture.
6.  Roll up each rectangle tightly from the short side and cut each log into 4 pieces.
7.  Place the 32 rolls cut-side down in the prepared pan.
8.  Bake for 20-25 minutes, or until golden brown.
9.  **Immediately** invert the pan onto a serving platter. Serve warm.
___

>[!Note]
>The key to this recipe is to invert the pan immediately after it comes out of the oven. This ensures the hot caramel sauce coats the rolls instead of setting in the pan.
