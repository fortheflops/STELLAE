## Recipe
### Crescent
| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
| 1 can        | Crescent Rolls              |
| 2 - 8 oz     | [[Cream Cheese]]            |
| 1 cup        | [[Sugar]]                   |
| 1            | [[Egg]] (Separated Yolk and White) |
| 1 teaspoon    | [[Vanilla]]                 |
### Icing
| Measurements | Ingredients                 |
| :----------- | --------------------------- |
| 1 cup        | [[Powdered Sugar]]          |
| 2 tablespoons | [[Milk]]                    |
| Few drops    | [[Vanilla]]                 |
___
## Instructions
### Crescent
1. Place in 9x13 dish.
2. Place crescent rolls in buttered dish.
3. Pinch seams together.
4. Cream together cream cheese, sugar, egg yolk, and vanilla. 
5. Top with another can of rolls.
6. Pinch seams and sides together.
7. Fork whip egg white and brush on top.
8. Bake 30 minutes at 350°F.
### Icing
1. Mix together powdered sugar, milk, and vanilla.
2. Ice when cool.
