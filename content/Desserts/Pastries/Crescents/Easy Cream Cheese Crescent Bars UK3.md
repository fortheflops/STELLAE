## Instructions
1.  Preheat oven to 350°F. Butter a 9x13 inch dish.
2.  Unroll one can of **crescent rolls** and press into the bottom of the dish, pinching the seams together.
3.  In a bowl, cream together the softened **cream cheese**, **sugar**, **egg yolk**, and **1 teaspoon of vanilla**. Spread this mixture over the bottom crust.
4.  Unroll the second can of **crescent rolls** and place it on top, pinching the seams and sides to seal.
5.  Lightly beat the reserved **egg white** and brush it over the top layer.
6.  Bake for 30 minutes, or until golden brown.
7.  Let the dessert cool completely.
8.  For the **icing**, mix the **powdered sugar**, **milk**, and a few drops of **vanilla**. Drizzle over the cooled bars.
___

>[!Note]
>The recipe requires two cans of crescent rolls, one for the bottom crust and one for the top.
