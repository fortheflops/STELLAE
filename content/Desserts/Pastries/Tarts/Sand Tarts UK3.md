## Recipe

| :----------- | ------------------------- |
| 1/2 pound    | [[Butter]] (2 sticks - Cream) |
| 3 tablespoons | [[Sugar]]                   |
| 2 3/4 cups   | [[Flour]] (Sift & Work In)   |
| 1 teaspoon    | [[Vanilla]]                 |
|              | [[Pecans]]                  |
___
### Instructions
1. Pinch off piece about size of marble and shape in shape of finger.
2. Sift powdered sugar.
3. Bake in slow oven about 10 minutes or until light brown.
4. Roll in powdered sugar.
5. Bake at 350°F.
6. Makes approximately 3 1/2 dozen.
