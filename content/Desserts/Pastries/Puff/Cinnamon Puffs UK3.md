## Recipe
| Measurements | Ingredients                 |
| :----------- | --------------------------- |
| 3/4 cup      | [[Sugar]]                   |
| 2 teaspoons  | [[Cinnamon]]                |
| 2 cans       | [[Biscuits]] (10 each)      |
| 1 stick      | [[Butter]] or [[Margarine]], melted |
___
### Instructions
1. Mix sugar and cinnamon.
2. Cut each biscuit into quarters.
3. Roll in cinnamon-sugar mixture loosely.
4. Fill bunt pan.
5. Combine melted butter with remaining cinnamon and sugar.
6. Pour over biscuits.
7. Bake at 350°F for 30 minutes.
8. Turn onto platter.
