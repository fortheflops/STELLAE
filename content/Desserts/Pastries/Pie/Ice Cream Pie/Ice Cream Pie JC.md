## Recipe
| Measurements      | Ingredients                   |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| **For the Sugar Cone Crust**|                                   |
| 1 box (approx. 12 cones) | [[Sugar Cones]]                   |
| 6 tbsp (3/4 stick)| [[Butter]], melted                |
| 1/2 teaspoon      | [[Sugar]]                       |
| 1/2 teaspoon      | [[Cinnamon]]                    |
| **For the Filling**|                                   |
| 3 pints           | [[Ice Cream]] (flavor of choice)  |
| 7 ounces          | [[Hershey's Milk Chocolate Bar]]  |
| 1/4 cup           | [[Milk]]                        |
___
## Instructions
**1. Make the Crust**
1.  Preheat oven to 375°F.
2.  Crush the sugar cones into fine crumbs. You can do this by placing them in a zip-top bag and using a rolling pin.
3.  In a bowl, combine the sugar cone crumbs, melted butter, sugar, and cinnamon. Mix until all the crumbs are moistened.
4.  Press the crumb mixture firmly onto the bottom and up the sides of a 9 or 10-inch pie pan.
5.  Bake for 10 minutes.
6.  Remove the crust from the oven and let it cool completely.

**2. Assemble the Pie**
1.  While the crust cools, remove the ice cream from the freezer to allow it to soften slightly.
2.  In a small saucepan over low heat, melt the Hershey's chocolate bar with the milk, stirring until smooth. Let the chocolate sauce cool to room temperature.
3.  Once the crust is cool and the ice cream is softened, spread half of the ice cream in an even layer in the crust.
4.  Drizzle a layer of the cooled chocolate sauce over the ice cream.
5.  Top with the remaining ice cream, spreading it evenly.
6.  Place the pie in the freezer for several hours, or until firm.
___

>[!Note]
>The first ingredient for the crust reads "Boy sugar cone crunch up," which has been interpreted as using one box of sugar cones.
