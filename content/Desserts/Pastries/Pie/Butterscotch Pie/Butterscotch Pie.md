### Filling
| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |

| 1/3 cup | [[Flour]] |
| 1/2 teaspoon | [[Salt]] |

| 1/2 teaspoon | [[Vanilla]] |
| 2 tablespoons | [[Butter]] |
| 1 cup | [[Heavy Cream]], for whipping |

## Instructions
### For the Crust
1.  Preheat oven to 400°F.
2.  In a bowl, mix the **flour**, **brown sugar**, and **pecans**. Add the melted **butter** and combine.
3.  Press the mixture into the bottom and up the sides of a buttered 9-inch pie pan.
4.  Bake for 10 minutes. Let cool completely.

### For the Filling
1.  In a saucepan, whisk together the **brown sugar**, **flour**, and **salt**. Gradually whisk in the **milk**.
2.  Stir constantly over medium heat until the mixture thickens and boils.
3.  Slowly stir a small amount of the hot mixture into the beaten **egg yolks**, then pour the yolks back into the saucepan, stirring constantly. Cook for 2 more minutes.
4.  Remove from heat and stir in the **butter** and **vanilla** until smooth.
5.  Pour the filling into the cooled crust. Chill for at least 4 hours until firm.
6.  Before serving, whip the **heavy cream** and top the pie.
