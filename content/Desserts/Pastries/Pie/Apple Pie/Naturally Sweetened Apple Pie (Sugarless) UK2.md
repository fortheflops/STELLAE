## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 1 lb. | [[Granny Smith Apples]] - peeled & sliced |
| 1/4 Cup | [[Raisins]] |
| 1 Small can | [[Frozen Concentrated Apple Juice]], thawed |
| 4 oz. (1/2 cup)| [[Heavy Cream]] |
| 2 Tbsp | [[Cornstarch]] |
| 2 Tbsp | [[Cinnamon]] |
| 1 recipe for a double | 9-inch [[Pie Crust]] |
___
### Instructions
1.  Preheat the oven to 450°F.
2.  Arrange the **apple slices** in the bottom **pie crust** and sprinkle the **raisins** over the slices.
3.  In a separate bowl, mix together the thawed **apple juice concentrate**, **heavy cream**, **cornstarch**, and **cinnamon**.
4.  Pour this mixture over the apples and raisins.
5.  Place the top crust on the pie, seal and crimp the edges, and cut a few slits for steam to escape.
6.  Bake at **450°F for 10 minutes**.
7.  Reduce the heat to **350°F and bake for an additional 30 minutes**, or until the crust is golden and the filling is bubbly.
