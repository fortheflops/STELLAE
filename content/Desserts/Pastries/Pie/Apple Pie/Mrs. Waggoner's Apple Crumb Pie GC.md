### Filling

### Topping
| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |

___
## Instructions
1.  Preheat oven to 400°F.
2.  **For the Filling:** In a large bowl, mix the **sliced apples**, **1/2 cup of sugar**, **cinnamon**, and **1 tablespoon of flour**. Pour this mixture into the unbaked **pie crust**.
3.  **For the Topping:** In a separate bowl, mix **1/2 cup of sugar** and **3/4 cup of flour**. Cut in the **margarine** until the mixture is crumbly.
4.  Sprinkle the crumbly topping evenly over the apple filling.
5.  Bake for one hour.
