## Recipe
| Measurements      | Ingredients                   |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| **For the Filling**|                                   |
| 6 to 7            | Tart [[Apples]], peeled and thinly sliced |
| 3/4 to 1 cup      | [[Sugar]]                       |
| 2 tablespoons     | [[All-Purpose Flour]]           |
| 1 teaspoon        | [[Cinnamon]]                    |
| 1 dash            | [[Nutmeg]]                      |
| 1 dash            | [[Salt]]                        |
| 2 tablespoons     | [[Butter]]                      |
| **For the Crust** |                                   |
| 1 recipe          | Pastry for a double-crust 9-inch pie |
| **Optional Crumb Topping** |                      |
| 1/2 cup           | [[All-Purpose Flour]]           |
| 1/4 cup           | [[Sugar]]                       |
| 1/4 cup           | [[Butter]], cold                  |
___
## Instructions
1.  Preheat oven to 425°F.
2.  Prepare your pie pastry. Line a 9-inch pie pan with the bottom crust.
3.  **To make the filling:** In a large bowl, combine the sugar, flour, cinnamon, nutmeg, and salt. Add the sliced apples and toss until the apples are evenly coated.
4.  Pour the apple filling into the pastry-lined pie pan and spread evenly. Dot the top with small pieces of the 2 tablespoons of butter.
5.  **Choose your topping:**
    * **For a double crust:** Place the top crust over the filling. Trim the edges, fold the top crust under the bottom, and crimp to seal. Cut several slits in the top crust to allow steam to escape.
    * **For a crumb topping:** In a small bowl, combine the 1/2 cup flour and 1/4 cup sugar. Cut in the 1/4 cup of cold butter with a pastry blender or your fingers until the mixture is crumbly. Sprinkle evenly over the apple filling.
6.  Bake at 425°F for 15 minutes. Then, reduce the oven temperature to 375°F and continue baking for another 35-45 minutes, or until the crust is golden and the filling is bubbly.
