## Recipe
### Base Filling
| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 2 Cups | [[Powdered Sugar]] |
| 1 Stick | [[Oleo]] or Butter, softened |
| 1 Large | [[Egg]] |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Salt]] |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Vanilla]] |
### Topping
| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 1 Cup | [[Heavy Cream]] |
| 1 Cup | Drained [[Crushed Pineapple]] |
| 1/2 Cup | Chopped [[Nuts]] (Pecans) |
### Crust
| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 2 | 8" baked [[Pie Crusts]] |
___
## Instructions
1.  **For the Base Filling:** In a bowl, cream together the **powdered sugar** and softened **oleo**. Add the **egg**, **salt**, and **vanilla**. Beat with an electric mixer on high speed for 5-7 minutes until light and fluffy.
2.  Divide this mixture evenly between the two cooled, baked **pie crusts**. Chill for at least 30 minutes.
3.  **For the Topping:** Whip the **heavy cream** until stiff peaks form. Gently fold in the drained **crushed pineapple** and chopped **nuts**.
4.  Spread the topping mixture over the chilled base layer in each pie.
5.  Chill thoroughly for several hours, or overnight, until set.
