*This is a classic no-bake "magic" pie. The secret is using the **dry** Dream Whip powder, which sets the filling as it chills.*

## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 1 (9-inch) | [[Vanilla Wafer Crust]] or pre-baked crust |
| 1 small can | Crushed [[Pineapple]], well-drained |
| 3/4 cup | [[Sugar]] |
| 1 (8 oz) pkg | [[Cream Cheese]], softened |
| 1 envelope | [[Dream Whip]] (dry, unwhipped powder) |
___
### Instructions
1.  In a medium bowl, beat the softened **cream cheese** until smooth.
2.  Add the well-drained **crushed pineapple** and the **sugar**. Mix well.
3.  Fold in the **dry Dream Whip powder** until completely combined.
4.  Pour the filling into a pre-made **vanilla wafer crust** or a previously baked pie crust.
5.  Refrigerate for at least 4 hours, or until firm, before serving.
