## Instructions
1.  In a bowl, dissolve one package of **strawberry jello** in one and a half cups of **hot water**.
2.  Add one pint of **vanilla ice cream** by the spoonful and stir until it is completely melted.
3.  Refrigerate the mixture for 15-25 minutes, or until it has thickened but is not fully set.
4.  Gently fold in one cup of drained, sliced **strawberries**.
5.  Pour the filling into a baked and cooled **pie shell**.
6.  Chill the pie for at least 2 hours, or until the filling is firm.
7.  Serve with whipped cream, if desired.
___

>[!Note]
>The note "bake at 425 until tan" on the recipe card refers to the instructions for pre-baking the pie shell before you begin making the filling.
