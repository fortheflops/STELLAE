## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 1                          | 8-inch Ready-made [[Graham Cracker Crust]]|
| 4 large                    | [[Egg Yolks]]                             |
| 1 (14 ounce) can           | Fat-free Sweetened Condensed [[Skim Milk]]|
| 1 teaspoon                 | Fresh grated [[Lime Peel]]                |
| 1/2 cup + 1 tablespoon     | [[Lime Juice]]                            |
___
## Instructions
1.  Preheat the oven to 350°F.
2.  In a large bowl, beat the egg yolks with an electric mixer on medium speed until thick and creamy, about 5 minutes.
3.  Add the remaining ingredients (sweetened condensed milk, lime peel, and lime juice) and beat until well blended.
4.  Pour the filling into the prepared graham cracker crust.
5.  Bake for 30 to 35 minutes, or until the filling is set.
6.  Let the pie cool on a wire rack for no longer than 30 minutes.
7.  Refrigerate the pie for at least 4 hours.
___

>[!Note]
>The pie can be stored, tightly covered, in the refrigerator for up to 4 days. Serve with whipped cream or Cool Whip.
