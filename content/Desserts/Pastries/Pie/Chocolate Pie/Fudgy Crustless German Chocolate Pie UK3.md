*This is a rich, fudgy, crustless pie that's incredibly simple to make. It has the texture of a dense, gooey brownie.*

## Recipe
| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 8 squares | [[German Sweet Chocolate]] (one 4-oz bar) |
| 1 stick | [[Oleo]] or Butter |
| 1 cup | [[Sugar]] |
| 1/2 cup | [[Flour]] |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Vanilla]] |
| 3 | [[Eggs]] |
| 1 cup | [[Pecans]], chopped |
___
### Instructions
1.  Preheat oven to 300°F. Grease a 9-inch pie pan.
2.  In a small saucepan, melt the **German Sweet Chocolate** and **oleo** together. Set aside to cool slightly.
3.  In a separate bowl, mix the **sugar**, **flour**, **vanilla**, and **eggs**.
4.  Pour the melted chocolate mixture into the egg mixture and stir until combined.
5.  Fold in the chopped **pecans**.
6.  Pour the batter into the greased pie pan.
7.  Bake for **exactly 30 minutes**.
8.  Cool completely before serving. The pie will set as it cools.
___

>[!Note]
>The instruction to bake for "30 minutes. No more." is crucial. This ensures the pie remains dense and fudgy in the center.
