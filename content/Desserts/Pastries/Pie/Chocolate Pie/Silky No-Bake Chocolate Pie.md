## Recipe
| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 1 can | Sweetened Condensed Milk (e.g., Eagle Brand) |
| 1 bar | Dark Chocolate (e.g., Hershey's, approx 4 oz) |
| $\frac{1}{2}$ Cup | Tap Water |
| 1 (9-inch) | Baked Pie Shell |
| Optional | Whipped Cream (for topping) |
| Optional | Extra Dark Chocolate (for shaving) |
___
## Instructions
1.  In a saucepan, melt the **dark chocolate** over low heat until smooth.
2.  Add the **sweetened condensed milk** and cook over medium heat, stirring **constantly** until the mixture thickens and becomes "lumpy."
3.  Remove the pan from the heat and immediately stir in the **tap water** until the mixture is smooth again.
4.  Pour the filling into the pre-baked **pie shell**.
5.  Chill for several hours, or until the filling is completely set.
6.  Top with **whipped cream** and **chocolate shavings** before serving, if desired.
