## Recipe
| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 1 (9-inch) | [[Chocolate]] or [[Graham Cracker Crust]] |
| 25 Large | [[Marshmallows]] |
| 6 | [[Hershey Bars]] (standard 1.55 oz size) |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Milk]] |
| 1/2 Pint (1 cup) | [[Heavy Cream]] |
| Optional Topping | [[Nuts]] or Grated [[Hershey]] |
___
## Instructions
1.  In the top of a double boiler, melt the **marshmallows** and **Hershey bars** in the **1/2 cup of milk**, stirring until smooth.
2.  Remove from heat and let the mixture cool completely.
3.  In a separate bowl, whip the **heavy cream** until stiff peaks form.
4.  Gently fold the cooled chocolate mixture into the whipped cream.
5.  Pour the filling into the prepared pie crust.
6.  Sprinkle with chopped **nuts** or grated **Hershey** for a topping, if desired.
7.  Chill for several hours until firm before serving.
___

>[!Note]
>It is very important to let the chocolate and marshmallow mixture cool completely to room temperature before folding it into the whipped cream.
