## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
|   1/4 cup   |   [[Cornstarch]]   |
|   1/2 cup   |   [[Sugar]]   |
|   1/4 teaspoon   |   [[Salt]]   |
|   2 cups   |   [[Milk]]   |
|   1 1/2 - 1 oz   |   Squares Unsweetened [[Chocolate]]   |
|   3   |   Slightly Beaten [[Egg Yolks]] (Save Whites)   |
|   2 tablespoons   |   [[Butter]] or [[Margarine]]   |
|   1/2 +   |   [[Vanilla]]   |
|   1   |   [[Pie Shell]]   |
___
### Instructions
1.  Salt. Gradually add Scalded Milk (Very Hot).
2.  Cook Over Moderate Heat, Stirring Constantly Until Mixture Thickens & Boils.
3.  Cook For 2 Min. At A Rolling Boil.
4.  Remove From Heat, Add Small Amt. Of Hot Mixture To A Bowl Containing The Beaten Egg Yolks; Then Add Egg Yolk Mixture To Hot Mixture Stirring Well.
5.  Boil 1 Min. Stirring Constantly.
6.  Add Marg & Vanilla. Cool Slightly.
7.  Pour Into Baked Pastry Shell. Cool.
8.  Cover With Plastic Wrap & Refrigerate Until Late Tomorrow.
