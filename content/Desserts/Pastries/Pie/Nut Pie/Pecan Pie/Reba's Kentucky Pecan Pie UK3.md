*A classic Kentucky-style pecan pie with a rich, dark brown sugar filling and a crunchy toasted pecan topping.*
### Author
Reba
___
## Recipe
| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 1 (9-inch) | Unbaked [[Pie Shell]] |
| 1 Cup | White [[Corn Syrup]] |
| 1 Cup | Dark [[Brown Sugar]], packed |
| $\frac{1}{3}$ tsp | [[Salt]] |
| $\frac{1}{3}$ Cup | Melted [[Butter]] or [[Margarine]] |
| 1 tsp | [[Vanilla Extract]] |
| 3 | [[Eggs]], slightly beaten |
| 1 heaping Cup | Shelled [[Pecans]] |
___
### Instructions
1.  Preheat oven to 350°F.
2.  In a large bowl, combine the **corn syrup**, **dark brown sugar**, **salt**, melted **butter**, and **vanilla**. Mix well.
3.  Stir in the slightly beaten **eggs**.
4.  Pour the filling into the unbaked **pie shell**.
5.  Sprinkle the **pecans** evenly over the top.
6.  Bake for approximately 45 minutes, or until the center is set but still has a slight jiggle.
7.  Let the pie cool completely on a wire rack before serving.
