*A classic pecan pie with a rich, sweet filling that's slightly thicker thanks to the addition of flour.*
### Author
Juanita
___
## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 1 | Unbaked 9-inch [[Pie Crust]] |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] |
| 1 Cup | [[Karo Syrup]] |
| ½ Cup | [[Sugar]] |
| 4 Heaping Tbsp. | [[Flour]] |
| 1 Cup | [[Pecans]] |
| 1 tsp | [[Vanilla]] |
| 2 Tbsp | [[Cream]] |
| 2 or 3 Tbsp | [[Butter]], melted |
___
### Instructions
1.  Preheat oven to 325°F.
2.  In a large bowl, beat the **eggs**.
3.  Add the **Karo syrup**, **sugar**, and **flour**. Mix well.
4.  Stir in the **pecans**, **vanilla**, **cream**, and melted **butter**.
5.  Pour the mixture into an unbaked 9-inch **pie crust**.
6.  Bake for 45-60 minutes, or until the center is set.
7.  Let cool completely before serving.
___

>[!Note]
>The flour in this recipe acts as a thickener, which will result in a slightly firmer, more opaque filling compared to pies that use only corn syrup and eggs.
