## Recipe
### Pie Base
| Measurements | Ingredients |

| 3/4 Cup | [[Sugar]] |
| 3/4 Cup | [[Pecans]], chopped |
| 3/4 Cup | [[Graham Cracker Crumbs]] |
| 1 Teaspoon | [[Vanilla]] |
### Topping
| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 1 pint (2 cups) | [[Heavy Cream]], whipped |
___
## Instructions
1.  Preheat oven to 325°F. Grease a 9-inch pie pan.
2.  In a clean, dry bowl, beat the **egg whites** until stiff peaks form.
3.  In a separate bowl, combine the **sugar**, chopped **pecans**, **graham cracker crumbs**, and **vanilla**.
4.  Gently fold the dry ingredients into the stiffly beaten egg whites.
5.  Spread the mixture into the greased pie pan.
6.  Bake for 35 minutes.
7.  Let the pie cool completely.
8.  Whip the heavy cream and spread it over the cooled pie.
9.  Cover and refrigerate for at least 6 hours before serving.
