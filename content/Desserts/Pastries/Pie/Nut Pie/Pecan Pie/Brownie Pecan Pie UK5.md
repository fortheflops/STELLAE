## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 2/3 cup                    | [[Sugar]]                                 |
| 1/8 teaspoon               | [[Salt]]                                  |
| 1 cup                      | Light [[Corn Syrup]]                      |
| 4 ounces                   | Semi-sweet [[Chocolate Chips]]            |
| 3 tablespoons              | [[Margarine]] or [[Butter]]               |
| 3                          | [[Eggs]], slightly beaten                 |
| 1 teaspoon                 | [[Vanilla]]                               |
| 1 cup                      | Coarsely chopped [[Pecans]]               |
| 1                          | Unbaked 9" [[Pie Shell]]                  |
___
## Instructions
**To Make the Soup Base:**
1.  Preheat the oven to 350°F.
2.  In a saucepan, combine the sugar, salt, and corn syrup.
3.  Bring the mixture to a boil over medium heat, stirring until the sugar dissolves.
4.  Boil for 2 minutes.
5.  Remove from the heat and add the chocolate chips and butter. Stir until melted. Let the mixture cool.
6.  In a separate bowl, have the slightly beaten eggs ready.
7.  Gradually pour the cooled chocolate mixture over the eggs, mixing well.
8.  Stir in the vanilla and pecans.
9.  Pour the filling into the unbaked pie shell.
10. Bake for 50 minutes.
11. Let the pie cool completely.
___

>[!Note]
>Serve topped with whipped cream or vanilla ice cream. Enjoy with friends at Thanksgiving or any time!!
