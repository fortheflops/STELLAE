## Recipe
| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 1 | Unbaked Pie Shell (9-inch) |
| 3 | [[Eggs]] |
| $\frac{1}{2}$ Cup | [[Sugar]] |
| One Cup | Karo White [[Corn Syrup]] |
| $\frac{1}{2}$ Teaspoon | [[Salt]] |
| One Teaspoon | [[Vanilla Extract]] |
| One Cup | [[Pecans]] |
___
## Instructions
1.  Preheat oven to 325°F.
2.  In a bowl, lightly beat the **eggs**. Add the **sugar** and beat well.
3.  Stir in the **corn syrup**, **vanilla**, and **salt**. Mix well.
4.  Spread the **pecans** evenly over the bottom of the unbaked **pie shell**.
5.  Pour the egg mixture over the pecans.
6.  Bake for 45-55 minutes, or until the pie is set and golden brown.
7.  Let cool completely on a wire rack before serving.
___

>[!Note]
>The pie is done when the center is nearly set but still has a slight jiggle. It will firm up completely as it cools.
