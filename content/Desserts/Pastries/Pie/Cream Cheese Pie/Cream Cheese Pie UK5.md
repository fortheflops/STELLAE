*A light and fluffy no-bake cream cheese pie with a simple, buttery nut crust.*
### Author
Carole Gunckel (3/97)
___
## Recipe
### Crust
| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 cup | [[Butter]] or [[Margarine]], softened |
| 1/4 cup | [[Brown Sugar]] |
| 1 cup | [[Flour]] |
| 1/2 cup | Chopped [[Nuts]] |
### Filling
| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 8 ounces | [[Cream Cheese]], softened |
| 3/4 cup | [[Powdered Sugar]] |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Vanilla]] |
| 1 pint (2 cups)| [[Heavy Cream]] |
___
## Instructions
### For the Crust
1.  Preheat the oven to 400°F.
2.  In a bowl, cream the **butter** and **brown sugar**. Add the **flour** and chopped **nuts** and mix until crumbly.
3.  Press the mixture into the bottom of a pie pan. Prick with a fork.
4.  Bake for 15 minutes. Let the crust cool completely.

### For the Filling
1.  In a chilled bowl, whip the **heavy cream** until stiff peaks form.
2.  In a separate large bowl, blend the softened **cream cheese**, **powdered sugar**, and **vanilla** until smooth.
3.  Gently fold the whipped cream into the cream cheese mixture.
4.  Pour the filling into the cooled shell.
5.  Chill for at least 4 hours, or until the pie is set.
