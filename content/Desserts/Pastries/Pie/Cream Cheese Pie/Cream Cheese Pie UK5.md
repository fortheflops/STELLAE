## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| **For the Crust:** |                                           |
| 1/2 cup                    | [[Butter]] or [[Margarine]]               |
| 1/4 cup                    | [[Brown Sugar]]                           |
| 1 cup                      | [[Flour]]                                 |
| 1/2 cup                    | Chopped [[Nuts]]                          |
| **For the Filling:** |                                           |
| 8 ounces                   | [[Cream Cheese]], softened                |
| 3/4 cup                    | [[Powdered Sugar]]                        |
| 1 teaspoon                 | [[Vanilla]]                               |
| 1 bottle                   | [[Whipping Cream]], whipped               |

___
## Instructions
**For the Crust:**
1.  Preheat the oven to 400°F.
2.  In a bowl, cream the butter and brown sugar together.
3.  Add the flour and chopped nuts and mix until a crumbly dough forms.
4.  Press the mixture into the bottom of a pie pan. Prick the crust with a fork.
5.  Bake for 15 minutes.
6.  Let the crust cool completely.

**For the Filling:**
1.  In a large bowl, blend together the softened cream cheese, powdered sugar, and vanilla.
2.  Gently fold in the whipped cream.
3.  Pour the filling into the cooled shell.
4.  Chill until the pie is set.
___

>[!Note]
>The final instruction on the card is to "top with topping," but the ingredients for the topping are not specified. A common topping for this type of pie would be fresh fruit, chocolate shavings, or a fruit pie filling.
