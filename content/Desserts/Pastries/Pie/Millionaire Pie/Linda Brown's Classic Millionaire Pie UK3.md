*A classic, rich, no-bake Millionaire Pie with a buttery, fluffy filling and a pineapple-pecan whipped cream topping.*

Linda Brown

## Recipe
### Filling
| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 1 | Baked 9-inch [[Pie Crust]], cooled |
| 1 cup (2 sticks) | [[Butter]], softened |
| 2 cups | [[Powdered Sugar]] |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Salt]] |
| 1 tsp | [[Vanilla Extract]] |
### Topping
| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 1 pint (2 cups) | [[Heavy Cream]] |
| 1/4 cup | [[Powdered Sugar]] |
| 1 (8 oz) can | Crushed [[Pineapple]], well-drained |
| 1/2 Cup | Chopped [[Pecans]] |
___
## Instructions
1.  **For the Filling:** In a mixing bowl, cream the softened **butter** and **2 cups of powdered sugar**. Add the **eggs**, **salt**, and **vanilla**. Beat with an electric mixer on high speed for 5-7 minutes until light and fluffy.
2.  Spread the filling into the bottom of the cooled, pre-baked **pie crust**. Chill for at least 30 minutes.
3.  **For the Topping:** Whip the **heavy cream** until stiff peaks form, gradually adding **1/4 cup of powdered sugar**. Fold in the well-drained **pineapple** and chopped **pecans**.
4.  Spread the topping over the chilled filling layer.
5.  Chill the finished pie for several hours, or overnight, until completely set.
