## Instructions
1.  Slice fresh peaches into shell.
2.  Mix 1 cup (or $\frac{3}{4}$ cup) sugar, 2 tablespoons cornstarch, 1 cup water, and 2 tablespoons white corn syrup.
3.  Boil 5 minutes.
4.  Remove from heat. Add 3-4 tablespoons (half package) peach jello. (Optional: Add a dash of almond flavoring here).
5.  Cool.
6.  Pour over peaches.
7.  Refrigerate.
8.  Serve with topping.
