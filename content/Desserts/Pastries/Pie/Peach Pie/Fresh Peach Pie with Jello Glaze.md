## Recipe
| Measurements | INGREDIENTS |
| :--- | :--- |
| 1 | 9-inch Pre-baked [[Pie Shell]] |
| 4-5 Cups | Fresh [[Peaches]], peeled and sliced |
| **Glaze:** | |
| $\frac{3}{4}$ to 1 Cup | [[Sugar]] |
| 2 Tablespoons | [[Cornstarch]] |
| 1 Cup | [[Water]] |
| 2 Tablespoons | White [[Corn Syrup]] |
| 3-4 Tablespoons | Peach [[Jello]] powder |
| Dash (Optional) | [[Almond Flavoring]] |
___
## Instructions
1.  Prepare a 9-inch pre-baked **pie shell** and let it cool.
2.  Arrange the sliced fresh **peaches** inside the cooled shell.
3.  **For the Glaze:** In a saucepan, whisk together the **sugar** and **cornstarch**. Stir in the **water** and **corn syrup**.
4.  Bring the mixture to a boil and cook for 5 minutes, stirring, until thickened and clear.
5.  Remove from the heat and immediately stir in the **peach Jello** powder (and optional **almond flavoring**) until completely dissolved.
6.  Let the glaze cool until lukewarm.
7.  Pour the cooled glaze evenly over the peaches in the crust.
8.  Refrigerate for several hours, or until the glaze is completely set.
9.  Serve chilled, with whipped cream if desired.
___

>[!Note]
>This pie must be made in a pre-baked and cooled pie shell, as the filling is not baked.
