*A classic, no-bake icebox pie that sets up in the refrigerator. The filling is light, tangy, and incredibly easy to make.*

## Recipe
| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 1 can | [[Eagle Brand Milk]] |
| 1 large container | [[Cool Whip]], thawed |
| Juice of 1 large | [[Lemon]] (2 are better) |
| 1 cup | [[Pecans]], chopped |
| 2 (9-inch) | Pre-baked [[Pie Crust]]s |
___
## Instructions
1.  Prepare two pre-baked pie crusts (graham cracker or vanilla wafer crusts work well).
2.  In a large bowl, mix the **Eagle Brand milk** and **lemon juice** together.
3.  Gently fold in the thawed **Cool Whip**.
4.  Divide the chopped **pecans** equally and sprinkle them into the bottom of the two crusts.
5.  Pour the filling mixture evenly over the pecans in each crust.
6.  Refrigerate for several hours, or until the pies are firm.
___

>[!Note]
>This recipe makes enough filling for two pies. It keeps well in the refrigerator.
