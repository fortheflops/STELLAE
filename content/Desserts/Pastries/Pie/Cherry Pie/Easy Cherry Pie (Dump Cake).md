## Instructions
1.  Preheat oven to 350°F. Grease a square pan (8x8 or 9x9 inch).
2.  Spread the **cherry pie filling** evenly in the bottom of the pan.
3.  Sprinkle the dry **white cake mix** evenly over the cherry filling.
4.  Sprinkle chopped **walnuts** over the top.
5.  Drizzle the melted **butter** over everything.
6.  Bake for 45 minutes, or until the topping is golden brown and the filling is bubbly.
