## Author
Mac (July 4, 1974)
___
## Recipe
| Measurements               | Ingredients                               |
| :------------------------- | :---------------------------------------- |
| 3 cups                     | [[Flour]]                                 |
| 1 1/2 cups                 | [[Crisco]]                                |
| 1                          | [[Egg]], beaten                           |
| 1 tablespoon               | [[Vinegar]]                               |
| 7 tablespoons              | Cold [[Water]]                            |

## Instructions
1.  In a large bowl, cut the Crisco into the flour until the mixture resembles coarse crumbs.
2.  In a separate small bowl, mix together the beaten egg, vinegar, and cold water.
3.  Add the wet ingredients to the flour mixture and mix until a dough forms.
4.  Divide and roll out the dough as needed.
___

>[!Note]
>This dough will freeze well!
