## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 1 1/2 Cups | [[Graham Cracker Crumbs]] |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Butter]], melted |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Powdered Sugar]] |
___
### Instructions
1.  In a bowl, combine the **graham cracker crumbs** and **powdered sugar**.
2.  Add the melted **butter** and mix well until all crumbs are moistened.
3.  Press the mixture firmly onto the bottom and up the sides of a pie pan.
4.  Chill for several hours until firm before filling.
