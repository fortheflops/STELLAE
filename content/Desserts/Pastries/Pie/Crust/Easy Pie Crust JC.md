## Recipe
| Measurements      | Ingredients                   |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 2 cups            | [[All-Purpose Flour]]           |
| 2/3 cup           | [[Vegetable Shortening]]        |
| 1 teaspoon        | [[Baking Powder]]               |
| 1/2 teaspoon      | [[Salt]]                        |
| 1                 | [[Egg]]                         |
| 2 tablespoons     | [[Lemon Juice]]                 |
| 3 tablespoons     | [[Water]]                       |
___
## Instructions
1.  Sift the flour, baking powder, and salt together into a large bowl.
2.  Add one-third of the shortening (about 1/4 cup) to the flour. Using a pastry blender or two knives, cut in the shortening until the mixture is as fine as cornmeal.
3.  Add the remaining shortening and continue cutting it in until the particles are the size of small peas.
4.  In a separate small bowl, beat the egg, lemon juice, and water together.
5.  Pour the liquid ingredients over the flour mixture and mix lightly with a fork until a dough just comes together.
6.  Shape the dough into a disk, wrap, and chill if desired.
7.  Roll out on a floured surface to use as a pie crust.
___

>[!Note]
>The original recipe specifies "Spry," a brand of vegetable shortening. An egg is mentioned in the instructions but was not included in the original ingredient list. The recipe is said to make "three half crust," likely meaning three single crusts.
