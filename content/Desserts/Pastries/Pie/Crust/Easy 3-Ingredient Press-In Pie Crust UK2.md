## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 1 cup | [[Flour]] |
| 2 Tbsp | [[Sugar]] |
| 1 cup | Melted [[Butter]] |
___
### Instructions
1.  Preheat the oven to 400°F.
2.  In a bowl, combine the **flour** and **sugar**.
3.  Add the melted **butter** and mix until a soft dough forms.
4.  Press the mixture into the bottom and up the sides of a pie pan, shaping the edges.
5.  Bake for about 10 minutes, or until golden brown.
6.  Cool completely before filling.
