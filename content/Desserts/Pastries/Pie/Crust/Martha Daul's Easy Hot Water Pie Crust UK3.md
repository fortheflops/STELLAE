Martha Daul
___
## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 3/4 Cup | [[Crisco]] |
| 1/4 Cup | Boiling [[Water]] |
| 1 Tablespoon | [[Milk]] |
| 2 Cups | [[Flour]] |
| 1 Teaspoon | [[Salt]] |
___
### Instructions
1.  In a mixing bowl, whip the **Crisco** with a mixer until fluffy.
2.  Carefully add the **boiling water** and **milk** to the whipped Crisco and stir until smooth.
3.  In a separate bowl, combine the **flour** and **salt**.
4.  Add the flour mixture to the wet ingredients and stir until a ball of dough forms.
5.  Divide the dough in half. Roll each half out between two sheets of wax paper to fit your pie pan.
___

>[!Note]
>This type of crust is known as a "hot water crust." It is very pliable and easy to work with, making it great for beginners.
