## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
| 3/4 Cup             | Crisco          |
| 1/4 Cup             | Boiling Water    |
| 1 Tablespoon       | Milk            |
| 2 Cups                | Flour            |
___
### Instructions
1. Whip Crisco in bowl.
2. Add 1/4 cup boiling water and 1 Tablespoon milk. Stir.
3. Add 2 cups flour. Stir and use hands to form ball.
4. Roll between wax paper.
