## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 1 1/4 C | Fine [[Graham Cracker Crumbs]] |
| 1/3 C | [[Sugar]] |
| 1/3 C | [[Melted Butter]] |
___
### Instructions
1.  In a bowl, combine the **graham cracker crumbs** and **sugar**.
2.  Add the **melted butter** and mix well until all crumbs are moistened.
3.  Reserve 1/4 cup of the mixture for a topping, if desired.
4.  Press the remaining mixture firmly onto the bottom and up the sides of an 8-inch pie plate.
5.  Chill until set before filling.
