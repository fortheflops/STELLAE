## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| **For the Crust:** |                                           |
| 1 cup                      | [[Flour]]                                 |
| 3/4 cup                    | [[Oatmeal]]                               |
| 1/4 cup                    | Chopped [[Nuts]]                          |
| 1/2 cup (1 stick)          | [[Margarine]], melted                     |
| **For the Filling:** |                                           |
| 1 cup                      | Hot [[Water]]                             |
| 1 (3 ounce) package        | [[Lemon Jello]]                           |
| 1 can                      | [[Blueberry Pie Filling]]                 |
| Several spoonfuls          | [[Sour Cream]]                            |
___
## Instructions
### For the Crust
1.  Preheat the oven to 375°F.
2.  In a bowl, combine the flour, oatmeal, and chopped nuts.
3.  Add the melted margarine and mix until all ingredients are combined.
4.  Press the mixture into the bottom and up the sides of a 9-inch pie pan (or just the bottom of an 8-inch square pan).
5.  Bake for 10-12 minutes, until lightly browned. Let the crust cool completely.

### For the Filling
1.  In a bowl, dissolve the lemon jello powder in 1 cup of hot water.
2.  Chill the jello mixture until it has thickened to the consistency of egg whites.
3.  Once thickened, stir in the can of blueberry pie filling.
4.  Pour the filling into the cooled, cooked crust.
5.  Add several spoonfuls of sour cream on top of the filling.
6.  Stir lightly with a knife or spatula to create a swirled effect.
7.  Chill the pie until firm, then serve.
___

>[!Note]
>You can substitute other pie fillings; the author notes that apple and cherry are also delicious. The author also suggests, "I have only used the sour cream with blueberries. You may want to use whipped cream with apple, etc."
