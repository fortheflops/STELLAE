## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| ¾ Cup | [[Sugar]] |
| ½ Cup | [[Bisquick]] |
| 2 Tbsp | [[Oleo]] or Butter, softened |
| 1 Can (13oz) | [[Evaporated Milk]] |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] |
| 1 Can (16oz) | [[Pumpkin]] |
| 2 tsp | [[Vanilla]] |
| 2 tsp | [[Pumpkin Pie Spice]] |
| OR: | |
| ½ tsp | [[Cinnamon]] |
| ½ tsp | [[Nutmeg]] |
| ⅛ tsp | [[Cloves]] |
___
### Instructions
1.  Preheat oven to 350°F. Grease a 9" or 10" pie plate.
2.  Combine all ingredients in a blender or a large bowl.
3.  Beat until smooth (about 1 minute in a blender or 2 minutes with a hand mixer).
4.  Pour into the greased pie plate.
5.  Bake for 50-55 minutes, or until a knife inserted near the center comes out clean.
6.  Cool completely before serving.
