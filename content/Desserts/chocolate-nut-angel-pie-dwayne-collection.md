# Chocolate Nut Angel Pie

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A delicate, gluten-free dessert featuring a crisp, slow-baked almond meringue shell filled with a rich, velvety chocolate whipped cream. Perfect for elegant gatherings or a special family treat.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Bake the Meringue Shell
1. Preheat oven to a slow **270°F**.
2. Sift together **1/2 cup sugar** and **1/8 tsp cream of tartar**.
3. Beat **2 egg whites** until stiff but not dry.
4. Add the sifted **1/2 cup sugar** and **1/8 tsp cream of tartar** mixture gradually to the **2 egg whites**, beating well after each addition.
5. Continue beating until the meringue is quite stiff and no sugar crystals are present when rubbed between your fingers.
6. Gently fold in **1/2 cup almonds** (chopped very fine).
7. Grease a **9-inch pie plate** with **butter** and fill with the meringue, making sure not to bring the mixture all the way to the edge of the plate.
8. Bake in the slow oven at **270°F** for about **1 hour**, or until delicately browned and dry to the touch. Cool thoroughly.

### Step 2: Prepare the Chocolate Filling
9. Melt **3/4 cup semi-sweet chocolate chips** in the top of a double boiler over simmering water.
10. Add **3 Tbsp hot water** and cook until thickened. Cool slightly; the mixture will become quite thick.
11. Stir in **1 Tbsp vanilla extract**.
12. Whip **1 cup heavy cream** until stiff peaks form, then gently fold it into the cooled chocolate mixture. Combine well, but do not beat to avoid deflating the cream.

### Step 3: Assemble and Chill
13. Pour the chocolate filling into the cooled meringue shell.
14. Chill in the refrigerator for **2 to 3 hours** until set before slicing and serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The meringue shell can be baked a day in advance and stored in a dry, airtight container. Once filled, the pie must be chilled for at least 2-3 hours and is best served the same day.
