# Flaky Vinegar and Egg Pie Crust

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *Discover a classic, large-batch recipe for a tender and flaky pie crust enhanced with vinegar and egg. This all-shortening dough rolls out easily and freezes beautifully for your favorite baked treats.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 0 mins | 1 hour | One double-crust or two single 9-inch pie crusts |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large mixing bowl
* Small bowl
* Pastry blender
* Fork
* Plastic wrap

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3 cups | [[Flour]] | All-Purpose |
| 1 cup | [[Shortening]] | Chilled |
| 6 Tbsp | [[Ice Water]] | Cold |
| 1 Tbsp | [[Vinegar]] | Standard |
| 1 | [[Egg]] | Beaten |
| 3/4 tsp | [[Salt]] | Fine |
| 1/2 tsp | [[Baking Powder]] | Standard |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Combine Dry Ingredients
1. In a large bowl, combine the **3 cups flour**, **3/4 tsp salt**, and **1/2 tsp baking powder** until well incorporated.

### Step 2: Cut in Shortening
2. Cut the chilled **1 cup shortening** into the flour mixture using a pastry blender until the mixture resembles pea-sized crumbs.

### Step 3: Prepare Wet Ingredients
3. In a separate small bowl, beat the **1 egg**, then combine it with the **6 Tbsp ice water** and **1 Tbsp vinegar**.

### Step 4: Form the Dough
4. Lightly mix the wet ingredients into the flour mixture with a fork until a shaggy dough forms without overworking.

### Step 5: Chill and Rest
5. Divide the dough, wrap it tightly in plastic wrap, and chill for at least **1 hour** until firm to the touch.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The dough disks can be tightly wrapped and frozen for up to 3 months; thaw overnight in the refrigerator before rolling.
