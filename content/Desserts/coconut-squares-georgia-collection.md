# Coconut Squares

> 📜 **Collection:** Georgia Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic, sweet dessert bar featuring a buttery shortbread crust topped with a rich, chewy layer of moist coconut, chopped walnuts, and brown sugar. Perfect for afternoon tea or holiday cookie platters.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare and Bake the Crust
1. Preheat your oven to **350°F**.
2. In a mixing bowl, mix **1/2 cup butter**, **1 cup flour**, and **4 tbsp confectioners' sugar** together until a soft dough forms.
3. Press the dough evenly into the bottom of an ungreased **9x9-inch square baking pan**.
4. Bake for **15 minutes** at **350°F** until the crust is lightly golden around the edges.

### Step 2: Prepare Topping and Final Bake
5. While the crust is baking, beat the **2 beaten eggs**, **1/2 cup brown sugar**, **2 tbsp flour**, **1/4 tsp baking powder**, **3/4 cup moist coconut**, a pinch of **salt**, **1 tsp vanilla extract**, and **1 cup chopped walnuts** together in a bowl until thoroughly combined.
6. Pour the coconut mixture evenly over the hot, pre-baked crust.
7. Return the pan to the oven and bake for an additional **30 minutes** at **350°F** until the topping is set, firm, and beautifully golden brown.
8. Allow to cool completely in the pan before slicing into squares.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store these squares in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 3 days, or freeze them layered between parchment paper for up to 3 months.
