# Naturally Sweetened Apple Pie (Sugarless)

> 📜 **Collection:** General Archive | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A delicious apple pie made without any added sugar, sweetened naturally with raisins and frozen concentrated apple juice for a wholesome twist on the classic dessert.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 minutes | 40 minutes | None | One 9-inch pie |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 9-inch pie pan
* Medium mixing bowl
* Whisk
* Paring knife

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 lb | [[Granny Smith Apples]] | peeled & sliced |
| 1/4 Cup | [[Raisins]] | whole |
| 1 Small can (6 oz) | [[Frozen Concentrated Apple Juice]] | thawed |
| 4 oz (1/2 cup) | [[Heavy Cream]] | cold |
| 2 Tbsp | [[Cornstarch]] | none |
| 2 Tbsp | [[Cinnamon]] | ground |
| 1 recipe | [[Pie Crust]] | double 9-inch |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Assembly
1. Preheat the oven to **450°F**. Place the bottom **9-inch pie crust** in a **9-inch pie pan**.
2. Arrange the **1 lb Granny Smith apples** (peeled & sliced) in the bottom crust and sprinkle the **1/4 cup raisins** evenly over the top.
3. In a separate medium bowl, mix together the thawed **1 small can frozen concentrated apple juice**, **4 oz heavy cream**, **2 Tbsp cornstarch**, and **2 Tbsp cinnamon** until smooth.
4. Pour this mixture evenly over the apples and raisins.
5. Place the top **pie crust** over the filling, seal and crimp the edges to secure, and cut a few slits in the top crust for steam to escape.

### Step 2: Bake
6. Bake the pie at **450°F for 10 minutes**.
7. Reduce the heat to **350°F and bake for an additional 30 minutes**, or until the crust is golden brown and the filling is bubbly.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the baked pie covered at room temperature for up to 2 days, or refrigerate for up to 5 days.
