# Mrs. Waggoner's Apple Crumb Pie

> 📜 **Collection:** GC | ✍️ **Attribution:** Mrs. Waggoner | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic apple crumb pie featuring a simple, sweet filling of spiced Gravenstein apples and a buttery, crumbly streusel topping. Perfect for autumn gatherings and comforting family dinners.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 1 hour | None | One 9-inch pie |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 9-inch pie pan
* Large bowl
* Separate bowl
* Pastry blender
* Baking sheet

---
## Ingredients

### Filling
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | [[Pie Crust]] | 9-inch, unbaked |
| 4 Cups | [[Apples]] | Sliced, Gravenstein suggested |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Sugar]] | None |
| 1 Teaspoon | [[Cinnamon]] | None |
| 1 Tablespoon | [[Flour]] | None |

### Topping
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Sugar]] | For topping |
| 3/4 Cup | [[Flour]] | For topping |
| 1/3 Cup | [[Margarine]] or Butter | Chilled |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation
1. Preheat oven to **400°F**. Place the unbaked **9-inch pie crust** in a **9-inch pie pan**.

### Step 2: Prepare the Filling and Topping
2. In a large bowl, mix the **4 cups sliced apples**, **1/2 cup of sugar**, **1 teaspoon cinnamon**, and **1 tablespoon of flour** until evenly coated. Pour this mixture into the unbaked **pie crust**.
3. In a separate bowl, mix **1/2 cup of sugar** and **3/4 cup of flour**. Cut in the **1/3 cup margarine or butter** using a pastry blender or your fingers until the mixture is crumbly.

### Step 3: Assemble and Bake
4. Sprinkle the crumbly topping evenly over the apple filling.
5. Bake for **1 hour** at **400°F**, or until the topping is golden brown and the filling is bubbly and tender. Place a baking sheet on the rack below to catch any drips until done.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the baked pie covered at room temperature for up to 2 days or refrigerate for up to 4 days.
