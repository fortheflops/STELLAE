# Pineapple Millionaire Pie

> 📜 **Collection:** General Archive | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A decadent, Southern-style no-bake pie featuring a rich, buttery base and a light pineapple-nut whipped cream topping. This vintage recipe yields two delicious pies.*

## Ingredients

### Crust
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |

### Base Filling
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 Cups | [[Powdered Sugar]] | None |
| 1 Stick | [[Oleo]] | or butter, softened (1/2 cup) |
| 1 Large | [[Egg]] | None |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Salt]] | None |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Vanilla]] | None |

### Topping
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 Cup | [[Heavy Cream]] | None |
| 1 Cup | [[Crushed Pineapple]] | Drained |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Nuts]] | Chopped pecans |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Make the Base Filling
1. In a mixing bowl, cream together the **2 cups powdered sugar** and **1 stick oleo** (or butter).
2. Add the **1 large egg**, **1/4 tsp salt**, and **1/4 tsp vanilla**.
3. Beat with an electric mixer on high speed for **5-7 minutes** until the mixture is very light and fluffy.

### Step 2: Assemble Base and Chill
4. Divide this mixture evenly between the **two 8-inch baked pie crusts** and spread into a smooth layer.
5. Chill in the refrigerator for at least **30 minutes** until set.

### Step 3: Make the Topping
6. In a separate, chilled bowl, whip the **1 cup heavy cream** until stiff peaks form.
7. Gently fold in the **1 cup crushed pineapple** and **1/2 cup chopped nuts** until combined.

### Step 4: Final Assembly and Chill
8. Spread the pineapple whipped cream topping evenly over the chilled base layer in each pie.
9. Refrigerate the pies for at least **4 hours**, or preferably overnight, until completely firm and chilled before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the finished pies covered in the refrigerator for up to 3 days.
