# Aunt Liz's Peach Cobbler

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Aunt Liz | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic and easy peach cobbler featuring a simple batter poured over fruit that bakes into a soft, cake-like topping. This vintage recipe is perfect for using fresh or canned peaches.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 25 mins | None | 6-8 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 8x8 inch baking pan
* mixing bowl
* wire whisk

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 cube (4 Tbsp) | [[Margarine]] | or butter |
| 1 Cup | [[Peaches]] | cut up, fresh or canned |
| To Taste | [[Spice]] | cinnamon or allspice |
| 1 Cup | [[Flour]] | all-purpose |
| 1/4 Teaspoon | [[Salt]] | none |
| 1 Teaspoon | [[Baking Powder]] | none |
| 1 Cup | [[Sugar]] | use less if using canned peaches |
| To Taste | [[Nutmeg]] | ground |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Milk]] | none |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Pan
1. Preheat oven to 400°F.
2. Place the **1/2 cube (4 Tbsp) margarine** in an 8x8 inch baking pan and place in the oven until melted.

### Step 2: Assemble the Cobbler
3. Carefully remove the pan from the oven, add the **1 cup peaches**, and sprinkle with **spice**.
4. In a separate bowl, whisk together the **1 cup flour**, **1/4 teaspoon salt**, **1 teaspoon baking powder**, **1 cup sugar**, and **nutmeg**.
5. Stir in the **1/2 cup milk** to form a smooth batter.
6. Pour the batter evenly over the **peaches** in the pan. Do not stir.

### Step 3: Bake
7. Bake for **25 minutes**, or until the topping is golden brown and fragrant.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store covered at room temperature for up to 2 days or refrigerate for up to 4 days. Reheat in a low oven before serving.
