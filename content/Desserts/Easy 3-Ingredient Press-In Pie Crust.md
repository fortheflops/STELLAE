# Easy 3-Ingredient Press-In Pie Crust

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *An incredibly simple, 3-ingredient press-in pie crust with a rich, buttery, shortbread-like texture. Perfect for cheesecakes or no-bake cream pie fillings.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5 mins | 10 mins | None | One 9-inch pie crust |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Medium bowl
* Fork
* 9-inch pie pan
* Glass (optional)

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 cup | [[Flour]] | All-purpose |
| 2 Tbsp | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 1 cup | [[Butter]] | Melted |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Mix
1. Preheat the oven to **400°F**.
2. In a medium bowl, combine the **1 cup flour** and **2 Tbsp sugar**.
3. Add the **1 cup melted butter** and mix with a fork until a soft, crumbly dough forms.

### Step 2: Press and Bake
4. Press the dough firmly and evenly into the bottom and up the sides of a **9-inch pie pan**, using your fingers or the bottom of a glass to create a compact layer.
5. Bake for about **10 minutes**, or until the edges are golden brown and fragrant.
6. Let cool completely before filling.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The crust can be prepped, baked, and stored covered at room temperature for up to 2 days before filling.
