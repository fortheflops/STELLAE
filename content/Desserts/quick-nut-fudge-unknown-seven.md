# Quick Nut Fudge

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Seven | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A simple and fast homemade fudge recipe featuring cocoa and crunchy nuts. This classic treat is prepared in a double boiler for a smooth, consistent texture.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 15 mins | 1 hour | 16 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Double boiler
* 9x9 pan

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 lb | [[Powdered Sugar]] | Sifted |
| 1/2 cup | [[Cocoa]] | Unsweetened |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Salt]] | Fine |
| 6 tbsp | [[Margarine]] | Softened |
| 4 tbsp | [[Milk]] | Whole |
| 1 tsp | [[Vanilla Extract]] | Pure |
| 1 cup | [[Nuts]] | Chopped |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation
1. Combine the **1 lb powdered sugar**, **1/2 cup cocoa**, **1/4 tsp salt**, **6 tbsp margarine**, **4 tbsp milk**, and **1 tsp vanilla** in a double boiler.
2. Heat over simmering water, stirring constantly until the mixture is smooth and glossy.
3. Fold in the **1 cup chopped nuts** until evenly distributed.
4. Spread the mixture into a 9x9 pan and allow to cool until firm before cutting into squares.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to one week or freeze for up to three months.
