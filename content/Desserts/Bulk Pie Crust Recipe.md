# Bulk Pie Crust Recipe

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A fantastic, large-batch recipe for a classic all-shortening pie crust. Enriched with egg and vinegar, this dough is tender, flaky, and easy to work with.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 0 mins | 30 mins | 5 single 9-inch crusts |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large mixing bowl
* Pastry blender
* 1-cup measuring cup
* Fork
* Plastic wrap

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 Pound Can (2 Cups) | [[Crisco]] | chilled |
| 5 Cups | [[Flour]] | unfiltered or all-purpose |
| 1 Tablespoon | [[Salt]] | none |
| 1 | [[Egg]] | beaten |
| 1 Tablespoon | [[Vinegar]] | none |
| As needed | [[Water]] | cold, to bring total liquid to 1 cup |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Combine Dry Ingredients
1. In a large bowl, whisk together the **5 cups** of **[[Flour]]** and **1 tablespoon** of **[[Salt]]** until thoroughly combined.

### Step 2: Cut in Shortening
2. Add the chilled **1 pound can (2 cups)** of **[[Crisco]]** to the flour mixture. Using a pastry blender or your fingertips, cut the shortening into the flour until the mixture resembles coarse crumbs with some visible pea-sized pieces.

### Step 3: Prepare the Liquid
3. In a 1-cup measuring cup, beat the **1** **[[Egg]]**. Add **1 tablespoon** of **[[Vinegar]]**, then add enough cold **[[Water]]** to bring the total liquid volume to **1 cup**. Mix well.

### Step 4: Form the Dough
4. Pour the liquid mixture over the flour mixture and toss gently with a fork until the dough just comes together into a shaggy ball without overmixing.

### Step 5: Divide and Chill
5. Divide the dough into 5 equal portions. Shape each portion into a flat disk and wrap individually in plastic wrap, ensuring they are sealed until firm.

### Step 6: Rest Before Rolling
6. Refrigerate for at least **30 minutes** until chilled and firm before rolling out into your desired pie plates.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This dough freezes very well for future use; simply thaw a wrapped disk of dough in the refrigerator before rolling out.
