## Instructions

### Step 1: Melting the Chocolate
1. Combine the **1/2 c. half & half** and **1 9oz bar milk choc.** in a saucepan over low heat, stirring constantly until completely melted and smooth.

### Step 2: Flavoring
2. Stir in the **2 T honey**, **1 teas. vanilla**, and **2 T Cointreau** until fully incorporated and aromatic.

### Step 3: Garnishing and Serving
3. Transfer the warm chocolate mixture to a fondue pot and sprinkle the **1/4 c. finely chopped pecans** over the top. Serve immediately with fresh **bananas**, **cantaloupe**, and **pineapple** for dipping until the chocolate achieves a glossy, clinging consistency.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The chocolate mixture can be prepared ahead of time and gently reheated in a double boiler before serving.
