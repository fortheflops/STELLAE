# Mom's Apple Pie

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Nine | ✍️ **Attribution:** TheBoop2 | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A cherished family recipe for homemade apple pie featuring a flaky pastry mix and a spiced apple filling. Baked until golden brown and bubbly for the ultimate comforting dessert.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Make Pastry Mix
1. Mix together **12 cups flour**, **2 Tbsp salt**, and **5 cups cisco shortening** and store in an air tight container. No need to refrigerate.
2. To make 2 pie crusts, measure **2 cups of the mix** with about **3 Tbsp** (maybe more) of ice cold water. Mix together well.

### Step 2: Prepare Filling and Assemble
3. Mix together all dry ingredients (**1/4 cup granulated sugar**, **1/4 cup brown sugar**, **1/4 tsp salt**, **3/4 tsp cinnamon**, **1/4 tsp nutmeg**, and **2 Tbsp flour**) except apples and set aside.
4. Peel and slice **1 small bag of apples**, then pour into pie pan.
5. Mix dry ingredients with apples and pour into pie pan. Dot with **butter**. Put on top crust, about **1 tsp of lemon juice** over apples.
6. Make vent holes in top crust. Rub top crust with **milk** (helps it brown evenly).

### Step 3: Bake
7. Bake at 375 until apples are done (sauce is bubbly) and crust is nicely brown (about 30 minutes or so, maybe 45).

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The dry pastry mix can be stored indefinitely in an airtight container at room temperature until ready to use.
