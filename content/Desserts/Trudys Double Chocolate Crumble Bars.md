# Trudy's Double Chocolate Crumble Bars

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Trudy | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A decadent multi-layered bar cookie featuring a rich brownie-like base, a gooey marshmallow center, and a crunchy chocolate-butterscotch topping. These indulgent treats are perfect for gatherings and store beautifully in the refrigerator.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Base
1. Preheat oven to 350°F and grease a 13x9 inch pan.
2. In a large bowl, cream the **1 1/2 cups butter or oleo** and **3/4 cup sugar**. Beat in the **2 eggs**.
3. Stir in the **3/4 cup flour**, **1/2 cup nuts**, **1 pinch salt**, **2 tablespoons cocoa**, and **1/4 teaspoon baking powder** until just combined.
4. Spread the batter evenly in the pan and bake for **15 minutes**.

### Step 2: Add Marshmallow Layer
5. Remove the pan from the oven and sprinkle the **3 cups tiny marshmallows** evenly over the hot base.
6. Return to the oven and bake for **3 more minutes** until the marshmallows are soft and puffy. Let cool completely.

### Step 3: Prepare and Apply Topping
7. In a saucepan over low heat, melt the **1/2 cup peanut butter**, **1/2 cup chocolate chips**, **1/2 cup butterscotch chips**, and **1/4 cup oleo** until smooth and glossy.
8. Remove from heat and stir in the **1/2 cup Rice Krispies cereal**.
9. Spread the topping over the cooled marshmallow layer and chill until firm before cutting into bars.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** These bars must be kept refrigerated to maintain the texture of the topping. They can be stored in an airtight container for up to 5 days.
