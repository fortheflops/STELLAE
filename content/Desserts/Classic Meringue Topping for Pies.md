# Classic Meringue Topping for Pies

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic and simple recipe for a light, airy, and glossy meringue topping. Perfect for piling high on your favorite cream or custard pies and baking until golden brown.*

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3 | [[Egg Whites]] | room temperature |
| 3/8 Teaspoon | [[Cream of Tartar]] | none |
| 6 Tablespoons | [[Sugar]] | none |
| 1/2 Teaspoon | [[Vanilla]] | flavoring |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Meringue
1. In a clean, dry bowl, beat **3 egg whites** with **3/8 teaspoon cream of tartar** until frothy and soft peaks form.
2. Gradually beat in the **6 tablespoons sugar**, one tablespoon at a time, until the meringue is stiff, glossy, and the sugar has dissolved.
3. Beat in the **1/2 teaspoon vanilla** until fully incorporated.

### Step 2: Assemble and Bake
4. Pile the meringue onto your prepared pie filling, spreading it to touch the edges of the crust to prevent shrinking.
5. Bake at 400°F for **8 to 10 minutes**, or until the peaks are golden brown and fragrant.
6. Cool on a wire rack away from drafts before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Meringue is best used immediately after whipping to ensure maximum volume and stability. Ensure your bowl and beaters are completely free of grease for the best results.
