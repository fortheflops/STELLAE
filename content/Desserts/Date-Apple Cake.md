# Date-Apple Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A dense, moist, and wonderfully simple cake packed with fresh diced apples, sweet dates, and crunchy nuts. This classic one-bowl recipe creates a chunky, fruit-forward dessert.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 1 hour | None | 1 9-inch cake |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 9-inch baking pan
* Large mixing bowl
* Sturdy spoon or spatula

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation
1. Preheat your oven to 300°F. Grease and flour a 9-inch baking pan.

### Step 2: Mixing
2. In a large bowl, combine the **1 cup (less 2 tbsp) sugar**, **1/4 cup Crisco**, **1 egg**, **1/2 cup nuts**, **1/2 cup dates**, **2 diced apples**, **1/2 tsp nutmeg**, **1/4 tsp salt**, **1/4 tsp cinnamon**, **1 tsp baking soda**, and **1 cup flour**.
3. Stir with a sturdy spoon until thoroughly combined; the batter will be very thick.

### Step 3: Baking
4. Spread the batter evenly into the prepared pan.
5. Bake for **1 hour**, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean and the top is set.
6. Let the cake cool completely in the pan before slicing.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 3 days or refrigerate for up to 1 week.
