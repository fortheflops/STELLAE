# Cheth Dawson's Sugar Cookies

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Cheth Dawson | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic soft, roll-and-cut sugar cookie made with sour cream for a tender, cake-like texture. This versatile dough is perfect for cutting into shapes and decorating for any occasion.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 10 mins | 1 hour | 3 dozen |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* large mixing bowl
* electric mixer
* small bowl
* rolling pin
* cookie cutters
* cookie sheet
* wire rack

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 cup | [[Butter]] | softened |
| 1 cup | [[Sugar]] | granulated |
| 1 | [[Egg]] | large |
| 1/2 cup | [[Sour Cream]] | full fat |
| 3 cups | [[Flour]] | all-purpose |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Baking Soda]] | fresh |
| Optional | [[Sugar]] | for sprinkling |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Dough
1. In a large bowl, cream the **1/2 cup butter** and **1 cup sugar** together with an electric mixer until light and fluffy.
2. Beat in the **1 egg** until fully incorporated.
3. In a separate small bowl, stir the **1 teaspoon baking soda** into the **1/2 cup sour cream**.
4. On low speed, add the **3 cups flour** to the creamed mixture in three parts, alternating with the **sour cream mixture**, beginning and ending with the **flour**. Mix until a soft dough forms.

### Step 2: Chill and Roll
5. Cover the dough and chill for at least **1 hour**, or until firm enough to roll.
6. Preheat your oven to **375°F**.
7. On a lightly floured surface, roll the chilled dough out to **1/4-inch thickness** and cut into shapes with a cookie cutter.

### Step 3: Bake
8. Place the cookies on an ungreased cookie sheet and sprinkle the tops with **sugar** if desired.
9. Bake for **8-10 minutes**, or until the edges are lightly golden. Let cool on a wire rack until firm.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The dough can be prepared and chilled for up to 24 hours before rolling. Baked cookies store well in an airtight container for up to 5 days.
