# Honey-Brandy Poached Pears

> 📜 **Collection:** The Loraine Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *An elegant and simple dessert of whole pears gently poached in a sweet honey syrup, finished with a touch of brandy for a warm, sophisticated flavor. This refined dish offers a perfect balance of sweetness and depth, ideal for a sophisticated dinner party finish.*

---

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 30 mins | None | 6 servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* deep skillet
* melon baller
* paring knife

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 6 | [[Pears]] | peeled, stems left on, cored from bottom |
| 1/3 cup | [[Sugar]] | granulated |
| 1/3 cup | [[Water]] | room temperature |
| 3/4 cup | [[Honey]] | liquid |
| 3 Tbsp | [[Brandy]] | added at end |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Pears
1. Peel the **6 pears**, leaving the stems on. Use a small spoon or melon baller to core them from the bottom, keeping the **pears** whole.

### Step 2: Create the Syrup
2. In a deep skillet, combine the **1/3 cup sugar** and **1/3 cup water**. Heat over medium heat, stirring until the **sugar** is completely dissolved.
3. Stir in the **3/4 cup honey** and carefully place the **6 pears** into the syrup.

### Step 3: Poach and Finish
4. Bring the syrup to a simmer, then reduce heat to low. Poach the **6 pears** gently, turning and basting them occasionally, for **20-30 minutes** or until they are tender when pierced with a knife.
5. Remove the skillet from the heat and let the **6 pears** cool completely in the syrup to absorb the flavors.
6. Just before serving, gently stir in the **3 Tbsp brandy**. Serve the **6 pears** with a generous drizzle of the honey-brandy syrup.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The pears can be poached and stored in their syrup in the refrigerator for up to 2 days; bring to room temperature before serving.
