# Blitz Kuchen

> *A delightful and quick-to-prepare traditional Blitz Kuchen topped with nuts, cinnamon, and sugar. Baked to a golden brown perfection.*

| 1 cup | [[Flour]] | All-purpose |

| 1/2 c. | [[Butter]] | Melted or softened |
| 1 tsp | [[Vanilla]] | Extract |

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |

| As needed | [[Cinnamon]] | Ground |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and Mixing
1. Combine the **1 cup sugar**, **4 eggs**, **1 cup flour**, **1 tsp B. Powder**, **1/2 c. butter**, and **vanilla** to form the batter.

### Step 2: Topping and Baking
2. Pour batter into a greased pan and sprinkle with **chopped nuts**, **cinnamon**, and **sugar**.
3. Bake in a moderate oven at **375 degrees** for **25 to 35 minutes** until browned and fragrant.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Best served fresh on the day it is baked, but can be stored covered at room temperature for up to 2 days.
