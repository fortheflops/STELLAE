# Apple Sauce Cookies

> 📜 **Collection:** Patsy Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic, spiced cookie recipe featuring a moist apple sauce base and a hint of coffee. These tender treats are perfect for tea time or a cozy afternoon snack.*

---

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

## Ingredients

### Wet Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 cup | [[Shortening]] | softened |
| 1 cup | [[Brown Sugar]] | packed |
| 1 | [[Egg]] | large |
| 1/4 cup | [[Coffee]] | cold |
| 1 cup | [[Apple Sauce]] | well-drained and thick |

### Dry Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 3/4 cups | [[Gold Medal Flour]] | sifted |
| 1/2 teaspoon | [[Baking Soda]] | none |
| 1/2 teaspoon | [[Salt]] | none |
| 1/2 teaspoon | [[Nutmeg]] | ground |
| 1/2 teaspoon | [[Cloves]] | ground |
| 1/2 teaspoon | [[Cinnamon]] | ground |
| 1/2 cup | [[Raisins]] | none |
| 1/4 cup | [[Nuts]] | coarsely chopped |

---

## Instructions

### Mixing and Chilling
1. Thoroughly mix the **1/2 cup shortening**, **1 cup brown sugar**, and **1 egg** in a large bowl.
2. Stir in the **1/4 cup cold coffee** and **1 cup apple sauce**.
3. In a separate bowl, combine the **1 3/4 cups flour**, **1/2 teaspoon baking soda**, **1/2 teaspoon salt**, **1/2 teaspoon nutmeg**, **1/2 teaspoon cloves**, and **1/2 teaspoon cinnamon**.
4. Stir the dry ingredients into the apple sauce mixture, then fold in the **1/2 cup raisins** and **1/4 cup nuts**.
5. Chill the dough for at least 2 hours.

### Baking
6. Preheat oven and drop rounded tablespoonfuls of dough 2 inches apart on a lightly greased baking sheet.
7. Bake for 9 to 12 minutes or until the cookies are set and no imprint remains when lightly touched.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The dough must be chilled for at least 2 hours, but can be kept in the refrigerator for up to 24 hours before baking.
