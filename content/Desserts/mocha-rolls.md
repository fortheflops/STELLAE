# Mocha Rolls

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *Delightful vintage no-bake mocha rolls combining vanilla wafer crumbs, pecans, and soluble coffee. Coated in powdered sugar, they make a wonderfully sweet and aromatic treat.*

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 C. | [[Vanilla wafer crumbs]] | Crushed |
| 1/4 C. | [[Pecans]] | Chopped |
| 1/4 C. | [[Powdered sugar]] | Plus extra for rolling |
| 1 tsp. | [[Soluble coffee]] | Instant coffee granules |
| 1 tbsp. | [[Corn syrup]] | None |
| 2 tbsp. | [[Water]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Combining
1. Roll wafers or crush them into **1 C. vanilla wafer crumbs**. Combine the crumbs with **1/4 C. chopped pecans**, **1/4 C. powdered sugar**, and **1 tsp. soluble coffee**.

### Step 2: Shaping and Rolling
2. Add **1 tbsp. corn syrup** and **2 tbsp. water**. For each measure, take **1 tbsp.** of the mixture and shape into small rolls about **1 1/2" long** until firm and cohesive.
3. Roll each finished piece thoroughly in **powdered sugar** until evenly coated.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature with layers separated by wax paper for up to 5 days.
