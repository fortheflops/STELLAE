## Instructions
1. Exclude newspaper clippings and blog posts to respect **1** **Copyright Law Compliance**.
2. Focus on recipes from old cookbooks and **Multiple** **Family Collection Recipes** to ensure legal and ethical sharing.
3. Monitor repeat recipes; once a recipe reaches **5 Variations** of **Apple Crisp Recipes**, create a dedicated history section.
4. Group these recipes in the new section with their added collection name, such as **Apple Crisp PC**.
