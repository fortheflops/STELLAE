# Wedding Cookies

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Nine | ✍️ **Attribution:** Audrey Kees | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic melt-in-your-mouth Mexican wedding cookie recipe made with rich butter, pecans, and powdered sugar. Perfect for holidays, celebrations, or an afternoon tea treat.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 15 mins | 20 mins | 48 cookies |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Electric mixer
* Baking sheet
* Mixing bowl
* Measuring cups and spoons

---
## Ingredients

### Cookie Dough
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 cup | [[Butter]] | softened |
| 1 cup | [[Powdered Sugar]] | divided (1/2 cup for dough, 1/2 cup for coating) |
| 1 tsp | [[Vanilla Extract]] | or 1/2 tsp almond extract |
| 2 cups | [[Flour]] | all-purpose |
| 1 cup | [[Pecans]] | finely chopped |

---

## Instructions

### Mixing & Shaping
1. Preheat oven to **350°F**.
2. Beat **1 cup softened butter**, **1/2 cup powdered sugar**, and **1 tsp vanilla extract** with an electric mixer until light and fluffy.
3. Add **2 cups flour** and **1 cup finely chopped pecans**, mixing until a cohesive dough forms.
4. Shape the dough into **1-inch balls** and place them **1 1/2 inches apart** on an ungreased baking sheet.

### Baking & Coating
5. Bake for **14 to 15 minutes** until set but not browned on top.
6. Allow cookies to cool completely on the baking sheet.
7. Once cooled, gently coat the cookies in the remaining **1/2 cup powdered sugar**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Cookies can be baked in advance and stored in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 1 week.
