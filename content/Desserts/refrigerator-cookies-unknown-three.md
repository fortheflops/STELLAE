# Refrigerator Cookies

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Three | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A delightful recipe for classic Refrigerator Cookies loaded with pecans and rich butter. These slice-and-bake cookies offer a wonderful crispy texture and are perfect to prep ahead.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 30 mins | 12 mins | 8 hours | 24 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Baking sheet
* Knife

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 lb | [[Butter]] | Softened |
| 5 cups | [[Flour]] | All-purpose |
| 1 1/2 cups | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 3 | [[Eggs]] | Whole |
| 1 cup | [[Pecans]] | Chopped |
| 1 tsp | [[Vanilla]] | Extract |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Dough Preparation
1. Cream **1 lb butter** and **1 1/2 cups sugar** together in a large mixing bowl until light and fluffy.
2. Add **3 eggs** to the mixture and beat well.
3. Add **5 cups flour** and **1 cup pecans**, mixing until just incorporated.
4. Add **1 tsp vanilla** and mix until fully combined.

### Step 2: Shaping and Baking
5. Shape the dough into rolls, wrap tightly, and chill in the refrigerator overnight until firm.
6. Slice the chilled dough about 1/8-inch thick using a sharp knife.
7. Place slices on a baking sheet and bake at 350°F until edges are lightly golden brown and set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The dough rolls can be wrapped and kept in the refrigerator overnight, or frozen for up to one month before slicing and baking.
