# Party Cookies

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Eighteen | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A delightful recipe for festive party cookies loaded with plain chocolate M&Ms. These sweet treats feature a classic soft-baked texture with a rich brown sugar base.*

## Ingredients

### Cookie Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 cup | [[Shortening]] | Softened |
| 1 cup | [[Brown sugar]] | Firmly packed |
| 1/2 cup | [[Granulated sugar]] | None |
| 2 tsp. | [[Vanilla]] | None |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] | None |
| 2 1/4 cups | [[All-purpose flour]] | Sifted |
| 1 tsp. | [[Baking soda]] | None |
| 1 tsp. | [[Salt]] | None |
| 1 1/2 cups | [[M&Ms]] | Plain chocolate |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing the Dough
1. Preheat your oven to 375°F.
2. In a large bowl, cream together the **1 cup shortening**, **1 cup firmly packed brown sugar**, and **1/2 cup granulated sugar** until smooth.
3. Beat in the **2 tsp. vanilla** and **2 eggs** until well combined.
4. In a separate bowl, combine the **2 1/4 cups sifted all-purpose flour**, **1 tsp. baking soda**, and **1 tsp. salt**.
5. Gradually blend the dry ingredients into the creamed mixture.
6. Fold in the **1 1/2 cups plain chocolate M&Ms** gently until evenly distributed.

### Step 2: Baking
7. Drop rounded spoonfuls of dough onto an ungreased baking sheet.
8. Bake at 375°F for about 10 minutes, or until the edges are golden brown and set.
9. Allow the cookies to cool on the baking sheet for a few minutes before transferring them to a wire rack to cool completely.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The dough can be prepared and stored in the refrigerator for up to 48 hours before baking.
