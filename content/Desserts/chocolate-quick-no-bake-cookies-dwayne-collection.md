# Chocolate Quick No-Bake Cookies

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Jan Batchelor | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic 1975 recipe for quick chocolate no-bake cookies featuring rich cocoa, creamy peanut butter, and hearty oats. This nostalgic treat is easily prepared on the stovetop and dropped onto waxed paper to set.*

## Instructions

### Version 1 Instructions (Preferred)
1. In a saucepan, combine **2 cups** of **sugar**, **1/4 cup** of **cocoa powder**, **1/2 cup** of **milk**, and **1/4 lb.** of **oleo**.
2. Bring the mixture to a rolling boil and boil rapidly for exactly **1 minute**.
3. Remove the saucepan from the heat and immediately stir in **1 tsp.** of **vanilla extract**, **1/2 cup** of **crunchy peanut butter**, a **pinch** of **salt**, and **3 cups** of **quick oats** until thoroughly combined and the peanut butter is fully melted.
4. Drop the mixture by teaspoonfuls onto **waxed paper** and allow to sit undisturbed until completely cooled and set firm.

### Version 2 Instructions
5. In a large bowl, combine **2 1/2 cups** of **quick cooking oats**, **1/2 cup** of **peanut butter**, and **1 tsp.** of **vanilla extract**.
6. In a separate saucepan, combine **1/2 cup** of **milk**, **2 cups** of **sugar**, a **dash** of **salt**, and **4 tbsp.** of **cocoa powder**.
7. Bring the liquid mixture to a boil and boil for **1 minute**.
8. Pour the hot boiled mixture directly over the oat mixture in the bowl and blend well until cohesive.
9. Drop by spoonfuls onto **waxed paper** and let cool until firm.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store cookies in an airtight container at room temperature with layers separated by waxed paper for up to 1 week, or freeze for up to 3 months.
