# Date Pudding

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A delightful old-fashioned Date Pudding made with sweet dates, chopped nuts, and whipped cream. Perfect for satisfying your sweet tooth after a hearty meal.*

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

## Ingredients

### Pudding Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 pkg. | [[Dates]] | Chopped |
| 4 tbsp | [[Sugar]] | None |
| 1 c. | [[Nuts]] | Finely chopped |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] | Beaten |
| 4 tbsp | [[Flour]] | None |

### Topping
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| As needed | [[Whipped Cream]] | For serving |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing and Baking
1. Stir all the prepared ingredients including **1 pkg. dates**, **4 tbsp sugar**, **1 c. nuts**, **2 eggs**, and **4 tbsp flour** together in a mixing bowl until well combined.
2. Transfer the mixture to a greased baking dish and bake until set and golden brown.
3. Serve warm or chilled with a generous dollop of **whipped cream**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Can be baked ahead of time and stored in the refrigerator for up to 2 days before serving with whipped cream.
