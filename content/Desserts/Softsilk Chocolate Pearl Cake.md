# Softsilk Chocolate Pearl Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** UK15 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A deeply rich and moist chocolate espresso cake, made with Dutch-processed cocoa and a generous amount of butter and eggs for a fine, velvety crumb.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 1 hour 20 mins | 15 mins | 1 large Bundt cake |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large Bundt pan
* Medium bowl
* Large bowl
* Electric mixer
* Small cup
* Wire cooling rack
* Wooden skewer

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3 cups | [[Cake Flour]] | Sifted |
| 1 cup | [[Cocoa Powder]] | Unsweetened Dutch Process |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Salt]] | None |
| 4 sticks (2 cups) | [[Butter]] | Unsalted, softened |
| 2 cups | [[Sugar]] | None |
| 1 tablespoon | [[Vanilla]] | None |
| 8 large | [[Eggs]] | None |
| 2 tablespoons | [[Espresso Powder]] | Instant |
| 1/4 cup | [[Water]] | Warm |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Dry Ingredients
1. Preheat the oven to **350°F**. Generously grease and flour a large Bundt pan.
2. In a medium bowl, sift together the **3 cups cake flour**, **1 cup unsweetened Dutch Process cocoa powder**, and **1 teaspoon salt** until well combined.

### Step 2: Batter Preparation
3. In a large bowl, beat the **4 sticks (2 cups) unsalted butter** and **2 cups sugar** with an electric mixer until light and fluffy.
4. Beat in the **1 tablespoon vanilla**, then add the **8 large eggs** one at a time, beating well after each addition until fully incorporated.
5. On low speed, gradually beat in the sifted flour mixture until it is just combined.

### Step 3: Espresso Addition and Baking
6. In a small cup, dissolve the **2 tablespoons instant espresso powder** in the **1/4 cup warm water**.
7. Beat the espresso mixture into the batter until thoroughly incorporated.
8. Pour the batter into the prepared Bundt pan and spread evenly.
9. Bake for **1 hour and 20 minutes**, until a wooden skewer inserted into the center comes out clean.

### Step 4: Cooling
10. Let the cake cool in the pan for **15 minutes** before carefully inverting it onto a wire rack to cool completely.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the cooled cake wrapped tightly in plastic wrap at room temperature for up to 3 days or freeze for up to 2 months.
