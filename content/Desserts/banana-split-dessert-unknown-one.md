# Banana Split Dessert

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown One | ✍️ **Attribution:** Beth Stungeon | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A delightful layered dessert featuring a graham cracker crust, sliced bananas, crushed pineapple, a rich egg and powdered sugar filling, and whipped topping. Perfect for gathering crowds, this chilled treat offers classic banana split flavors in every bite.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Crust Preparation
1. Combine **2 1/2 cups graham cracker crumbs**, **7 tbsp margarine**, and **1 tsp sugar** in a mixing bowl until moistened.
2. Press the mixture firmly into the bottom of a **13x9 pan**.

### Step 2: Fruit Layers
3. Slice **3 bananas** and arrange them evenly over the crust.
4. Spread **1 large can crushed pineapple** (drained) evenly over the sliced bananas.

### Step 3: Creamy Filling
5. Beat **2 eggs**, **2 cups powdered sugar**, and **1 tsp vanilla extract** together with an electric mixer for 15 minutes until light and fluffy.
6. Pour the creamy mixture gently over the pineapple layer.

### Step 4: Topping and Chilling
7. Spread **1 container Cool Whip** evenly over the top of the mixture.
8. Sprinkle **1/2 cup nuts** and add **1/2 cup maraschino cherries** over the whipped topping.
9. Chill in the refrigerator for at least 4 hours until fully set before slicing and serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Can be prepared up to 24 hours in advance and kept covered in the refrigerator until ready to serve.
