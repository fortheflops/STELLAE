# Chocolate Streamboats

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *Delightful homemade Chocolate Streamboats made with melting chocolate and crunchy nuts. Perfect for a quick sweet treat or holiday gift-giving.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 5 mins | 30 mins | About 32 pieces |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Double boiler
* Teaspoon
* Waxed paper

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 pkg. (8 oz.) | [[Candy Making Chocolate]] | Chopped or squares |
| 1 1/2 cups | [[Salted Cashew Nuts]] | Or salted peanuts, whole or broken |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Melting and Mixing
1. Melt **1 pkg. (8 oz.) candy making chocolate** over boiling hot water until partly melted.
2. Remove from heat and stir rapidly until completely melted.
3. Add **1 1/2 cups salted cashew nuts** (or salted peanuts) whole or broken and mix well.

### Step 2: Shaping and Setting
4. Drop from a teaspoon on waxed paper.
5. Place a whole nut meat on each.
6. Let stand in a cool place until hard.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container in a cool, dry place for up to 2 weeks.
