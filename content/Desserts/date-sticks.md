# Date Sticks

> **Collection:** John B. Collection | **Original Attribution:** Unattributed
> *A delightful vintage dessert featuring sweet dates, crunchy nuts, and a touch of vanilla. These baked date sticks make a comforting and classic treat.*

![Original Handwritten Card](/assets/scans/date-sticks.webp)

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 30 mins | 8 |

## Ingredients
- [ ] 2 eggs
- [ ] 1 c. sugar
- [ ] salt
- [ ] 1/2 c. water
- [ ] 1 c. nuts
- [ ] 1 c. dates
- [ ] 1 tsp vanilla
- [ ] 1 1/2 c. flour
- [ ] 1 tsp B. Powd.

## Instructions
1. Preheat the oven and prepare a baking pan.
2. In a bowl, mix together the eggs, sugar, and a pinch of salt.
3. Add the water, vanilla, dates, and nuts, stirring to combine.
4. Fold in the flour and baking powder until just incorporated.
5. Spread the mixture evenly into the prepared pan.
6. Bake until set and lightly golden, then cool and cut into sticks.
