# Date Sticks

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *Discover a vintage family recipe for Date Sticks, featuring a rich combination of sweet dates, crunchy nuts, and aromatic vanilla. These delightful treats bake up into chewy bars that are perfect for afternoon tea or a sweet snack.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and Mixing
1. Combine the **2 eggs** and **1 C. sugar**, beating until light and fluffy.
2. Stir in the **pinch salt**, **1/2 C. water**, **1 C. nuts**, **1 C. dates**, and **1 tsp. vanilla**.
3. Fold in the **1 1/2 C. flour** and **1 tsp. baking powder** until just combined into a smooth batter.

### Step 2: Baking
4. Pour the batter into a greased baking pan and bake until golden brown and a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.
