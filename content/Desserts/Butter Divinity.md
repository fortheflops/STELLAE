# Butter Divinity

> 📜 **Collection:** UK15 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic, light-as-air confection made by folding hot sugar syrup into stiffly beaten egg whites. This traditional candy offers a rich, melt-in-your-mouth texture perfect for holiday gifting.*

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 3 to 4-quart heavy saucepan
* Candy thermometer
* Electric mixer
* Large heatproof bowl
* Sturdy spoon

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare and Cook Syrup
1. Line two large baking sheets with waxed paper.
2. In a heavy 3 to 4-quart saucepan, combine **4 cups sugar**, **1/8 teaspoon salt**, **1 cup corn syrup**, and **1 cup water**. Cook over medium heat, stirring constantly until the **sugar** is completely dissolved.
3. Attach a candy thermometer and boil without stirring until the mixture reaches **262°F** (hard-ball stage).

### Step 2: Whip and Combine
4. While the syrup cooks, beat the **4 egg whites** in a large, heatproof bowl until stiff peaks form.
5. Remove the **syrup** from heat. With the mixer on high speed, very slowly pour the hot **syrup** in a thin, steady stream into the **egg whites**.
6. Continue beating on high speed for **5 minutes** until the mixture is very stiff and begins to lose its gloss.

### Step 3: Finish and Shape
7. Remove the bowl from the mixer. Using a sturdy spoon, quickly beat in the **1/2 cup butter** and **1 teaspoon vanilla** by hand. Fold in the optional **3 ounces melted chocolate** and **1 cup chopped nuts** if using.
8. Beat until the mixture is firm and holds its shape when dropped from a spoon.
9. Quickly drop by teaspoonfuls onto the prepared waxed paper and let stand until completely firm.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to two weeks. Avoid making on high-humidity days as the candy will not set properly.
