# Old Fashioned Strawberry Shortcake

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic, old-fashioned recipe for a single, large biscuit-style shortcake baked in a round pan. This rich cake is designed to be split and filled with fresh sweetened strawberries and whipped cream.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 15 mins | None | 6–8 servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 8-inch round cake pan
* Large bowl
* Pastry blender
* Measuring cup

| 1 | [[Egg]] | Large |

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Dough
1. Preheat your oven to **450°F**. Grease an **8-inch round cake pan**.
2. In a large bowl, sift or whisk together the **2 cups flour**, **1 tbsp baking powder**, **6 tbsp sugar**, and **½ tsp salt**.
3. Cut in the **⅓ cup butter** with a pastry blender or your fingertips until the mixture resembles coarse meal.
4. In a glass measuring cup, lightly beat the **1 egg**. Add enough **½ cup milk** to the cup to measure a total of ¾ cup of liquid.
5. Gradually stir the milk-egg mixture into the flour mixture with a fork until a soft dough forms.

### Step 2: Assemble and Bake
6. Turn the dough out onto a lightly floured surface and knead gently for about **20 seconds**.
7. Divide the dough in half. Pat half of the dough into the bottom of the prepared pan.
8. Brush the top of the dough in the pan with a little **melted butter**.
9. Pat out the remaining half of the dough into an **8-inch round** and place it on top of the buttered layer.
10. Bake for **12 to 15 minutes**, or until golden brown.
11. To serve, carefully split the warm cake along the buttered seam and fill and top with sweetened strawberries and whipped cream.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Best served fresh on the day it is baked, though baked shortcake layers can be stored tightly wrapped at room temperature for up to 1 day.
