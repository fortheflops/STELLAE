# Frozen Lemonade Treat

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A refreshingly simple frozen dessert made with lemonade concentrate, soda water, and whipped topping. This delightful sweet treat is easily prepared and frozen for a cool, creamy texture.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | None | 4 hours | 6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Freezer-safe container
* Spatula

---
## Ingredients

### Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 12 oz can | [[Frozen Lemonade]] | Concentrate |
| 1 can | [[Soda Water]] | Chilled |
| 9 oz container | [[Cool Whip]] | Thawed |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mix and Freeze
1. Combine the **12 oz can frozen lemonade**, **1 can soda water**, and **9 oz container cool whip** in a mixing bowl.
2. Mix together thoroughly until smooth and well incorporated, then pour into a freezer-safe container.
3. Place in the freezer until completely set and firm to the touch.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Keep stored in the freezer for up to 2 weeks for best taste and texture.
