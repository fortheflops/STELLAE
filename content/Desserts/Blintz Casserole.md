# Blintz Casserole

> 📜 **Collection:** Jill Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A rich and decadent brunch or dessert casserole featuring frozen cheese blintzes baked in a sweet, creamy sour cream custard. This crowd-pleasing classic is simple to assemble and perfect for special mornings.*

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment

* Whisk

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 12 to 18 | [[Cheese Blintzes]] | frozen |
| 1/4 pound (1 stick) | [[Butter]] | melted |
| 4 large | [[Eggs]] | room temperature |
| 1 1/2 cups | [[Sour Cream]] | full fat |
| 1/4 cup | [[Sugar]] | granulated |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Vanilla Extract]] | pure |
| 1/4 teaspoon | [[Salt]] | fine |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Orange Juice]] | optional |

### For Serving
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| As needed | [[Powdered Sugar]] | for dusting |
| As needed | [[Strawberries]] | fresh, sliced |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation
1. Preheat your oven to 350°F.
2. Melt the **1/4 pound butter** and pour it evenly into the bottom of a 9x13 inch casserole dish.
3. Arrange the **12 to 18 frozen cheese blintzes** in a single layer on top of the melted butter.

### Step 2: Custard and Bake
4. In a separate bowl, whisk together the **4 large eggs**, **1 1/2 cups sour cream**, **1/4 cup sugar**, **1 teaspoon vanilla extract**, **1/4 teaspoon salt**, and optional **1 teaspoon orange juice** until smooth.
5. Pour the custard mixture evenly over the **cheese blintzes**.
6. Bake for **45 minutes**, or until the custard is set and the top is golden brown and fragrant.
7. Serve warm, dusted with **powdered sugar** and topped with **fresh strawberries**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** For a smaller batch using 6 to 8 blintzes, cut all other ingredients in half and use an 8x8 inch baking dish.
