# California Fruit Fudge

> 📜 **Collection:** General Archive | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A rich chocolate fudge loaded with candied fruits. This vintage recipe features chopped cherries, pineapple, figs, raisins, and coconut folded into a smooth chocolate base.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 20 mins | None | 18 large squares |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* saucepan
* candy thermometer
* 8x4x3 inch loaf pan
* wooden spoon

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 sq. | [[Chocolate]] | Unsweetened |
| 3/4 c. | [[Milk]] | Room temperature |
| 2 c. | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| Dash | [[Salt]] | Fine |
| 2 Tbsp. | [[Butter]] | Softened |
| 1 tsp. | [[Vanilla]] | Extract |
| 1/4 c. | [[Cherries]] | Candied, chopped |
| 1/4 c. | [[Pineapple]] | Candied, chopped |
| 1/4 c. | [[Figs]] | Dried, chopped |
| 1/4 c. | [[Raisins]] | Seedless, chopped |
| 1/4 c. | [[Coconut]] | Shredded |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Base
1. Add **2 sq. [[Chocolate]]** to **3/4 c. [[Milk]]** in a saucepan. Cook over low heat, stirring constantly, until the mixture is smooth and blended.
2. Add **2 c. [[Sugar]]** and **dash [[Salt]]**, stirring until the sugar is dissolved and the mixture begins to boil.

### Step 2: Boil and Cool
3. Continue boiling, without stirring, until the temperature reaches 232°F or until a small amount of the mixture forms a very soft ball when dropped into cold water.
4. Remove from heat and stir in **2 Tbsp. [[Butter]]** and **1 tsp. [[Vanilla]]**. Allow the mixture to cool until lukewarm.

### Step 3: Beat and Set
5. Beat the mixture vigorously until it begins to thicken and loses its glossy sheen.
6. Fold in the **1/4 c. [[Cherries]]**, **1/4 c. [[Pineapple]]**, **1/4 c. [[Figs]]**, **1/4 c. [[Raisins]]**, and **1/4 c. [[Coconut]]**.
7. Turn the mixture at once into a greased 8x4x3 inch loaf pan. Once the fudge is completely cold, cut into squares.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to one week or refrigerate for longer shelf life.
