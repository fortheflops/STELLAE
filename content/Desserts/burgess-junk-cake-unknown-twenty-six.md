# Burgess' Junk Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Burgess Family | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A delightfully unique vintage cake featuring a rich chocolate pudding base mixed with sour cream white cake mix. Topped with crunchy nuts and sweet butterscotch chips, it bakes into an incredibly moist and flavorful dessert.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 35 mins | None | 12 servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 9x13-inch baking pan
* Medium saucepan
* Stirring spoon

## Ingredients

### Cake Batter
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 regular size package | [[Chocolate Pudding Mix]] | not instant (cook-and-serve) |
| 1 stick | [[Margarine]] | or butter |
| 1 box | [[Sour Cream White Cake Mix]] | dry mix |

### Topping
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| To taste | [[Nuts]] | chopped |
| To taste | [[Butterscotch Chips]] | for sprinkling |

---

## Instructions

### Preparation and Baking
1. Preheat your oven to **350°F** and lightly grease a **9x13-inch baking pan**.
2. In a medium saucepan, prepare the **1 regular size package chocolate pudding** according to its package directions. Bring it to a boil on the stovetop.
3. As soon as the pudding comes to a boil, remove it from the heat and add **1 stick margarine**. Stir continuously until the margarine is completely melted and incorporated.
4. Add **1 box sour cream white cake mix** directly into the hot pudding mixture. Mix well together until a cohesive batter forms.
5. Pour the batter evenly into the prepared **9x13-inch baking pan**.
6. Sprinkle the top of the batter generously with **chopped nuts** and **butterscotch chips**.
7. Bake at **350°F** for **30-35 minutes** until the cake is set and a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This cake can be made a day in advance. Store covered at room temperature for up to 3 days, or freeze slices individually wrapped for up to 1 month.
