# Easy Orange Cranberry Jello Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *An incredibly simple and festive Jello salad, perfect for the holidays. This recipe combines orange Jello with a whole jar of cranberry relish and orange juice for a sweet and tangy dessert.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 0 mins | 4 hours | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* medium bowl
* wire whisk
* serving dish or mold

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 package (3 oz) | [[Orange Jello]] | powdered |
| 1 jar (10 oz) | [[Ocean Spray Cranberry Relish]] | whole jar |
| 3/4 cup | [[Orange Juice]] | chilled |
| 1 cup | [[Water]] | boiling |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Base
1. In a medium bowl, pour the **1 cup boiling water** over the **1 package orange jello**. Stir for 2 minutes or until the gelatin is completely dissolved and clear.
2. Stir in the **3/4 cup orange juice** until fully incorporated.

### Step 2: Combine and Set
3. Add the **1 jar ocean spray cranberry relish** and stir until it is well combined with the **jello mixture**.
4. Pour the mixture into a serving dish or mold.
5. Refrigerate for at least 4 hours, or until the **jello** is completely firm and set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This salad can be prepared up to 2 days in advance; keep covered in the refrigerator until ready to serve.
