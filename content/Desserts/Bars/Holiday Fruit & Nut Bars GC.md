## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 9                          | [[Eggs]]                                  |
| 3 cups                     | [[Sugar]]                                 |
| 4 1/2 cups                 | [[Flour]]                                 |
| 4 1/2 teaspoons            | [[Baking Powder]]                         |
| 3/4 teaspoon               | [[Salt]]                                  |
| 3/4 pound                  | [[Chocolate Chips]]                       |
| 3 cups                     | [[Dates]], chopped                        |
| 3 cups                     | [[Pineapple]], crushed or tidbits         |
| 3 cups                     | [[Cherries]], likely maraschino or candied|
| 6 cups                     | [[Nuts]], chopped                         |
___
## Instructions
1.  Preheat your oven to 325-350°F. Grease and flour a large baking pan (e.g., a 13x18 inch sheet pan or multiple smaller pans).
2.  In a very large bowl, beat the 9 eggs with the sugar until light and fluffy.
3.  In a separate large bowl, whisk together the flour, baking powder, and salt.
4.  In another bowl, toss the chocolate chips, dates, pineapple, cherries, and nuts with a small amount of the flour mixture to prevent them from sinking to the bottom of the batter.
5.  Gradually add the dry ingredients to the egg and sugar mixture, mixing until just combined. Do not overmix.
6.  Fold the fruit, nut, and chip mixture into the batter.
7.  Spread the very thick batter evenly into your prepared pan(s).
8.  Bake for 45-60 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean. The baking time will vary significantly based on the size and depth of the pan used.
