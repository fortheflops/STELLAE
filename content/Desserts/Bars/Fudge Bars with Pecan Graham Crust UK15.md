## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| **For the Crust:** |                                           |
| 2/3 cup                    | Crudely crushed [[Graham Cracker]] crumbs |
| 1/2 cup                    | [[Pecans]], lightly toasted               |
| 2 tablespoons              | [[Sugar]]                                 |
| 3/4 stick (6 tbsp)         | Melted [[Butter]]                         |
| 1/4 teaspoon               | [[Salt]]                                  |
| **For the Fudge Layer:** |                                       |
| 4 ounces                   | Unsweetened [[Chocolate]], chopped        |
| 1 stick                    | Unsalted [[Butter]], cut in pieces        |
| 2 large                    | [[Eggs]], lightly beaten                  |
| 1/4 teaspoon               | [[Vanilla]]                               |
| 1/4 teaspoon               | [[Salt]]                                  |
| 2 cups                     | Firmly packed [[Light Brown Sugar]]       |
| 1 cup                      | All-purpose [[Flour]]                     |
___
## Instructions
### For the Crust
1.  Preheat the oven to 350°F.
2.  Combine all crust ingredients and press the mixture into a 7x11 inch baking pan.
3.  Bake for 5 minutes. Let it cool.
### For the Fudge Layer
1.  Melt the chopped chocolate and butter together, then let it cool to room temperature.
2.  In a separate bowl, beat the remaining fudge layer ingredients (eggs, vanilla, salt, brown sugar, flour) with a mixer until smooth.
3.  Beat in the cooled chocolate and butter mixture.
4.  Spread the fudge layer on top of the cooled crust.
5.  Bake in the middle of the oven for 30 to 35 minutes, or until the top is set and the layer begins to pull away from the sides of the pan.
6.  While the fudge is still hot, score and cut it into bars, wiping the knife clean after each cut.
7.  Let the bars cool completely in the pan on a wire rack.
___

>[!Note]
>Yields 24 bars. The bars will be somewhat soft even after cooling.
