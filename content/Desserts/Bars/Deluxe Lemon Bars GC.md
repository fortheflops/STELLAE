## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| **For the Crust:** |                                           |
| Two Cups                         | Sifted All-Purpose [[Flour]]              |
| $\frac{1}{2}$ Cup                | Sifted Confectioners' [[Sugar]] (Powdered Sugar) |
| One Cup                          | [[Butter]]                                |
| **For the Filling:** |                                           |
| Four                             | Beaten [[Eggs]]                           |
| Two Cups                         | Granulated [[Sugar]]                      |
| $\frac{1}{3}$ Cup                | [[Lemon Juice]]                           |
| $\frac{1}{2}$ Cup                | All-Purpose [[Flour]]                     |
| $\frac{1}{2}$ Teaspoon           | [[Baking Powder]]                         |
| For Dusting                      | Additional Confectioners' [[Sugar]]       |

## Instructions
1.  Preheat oven to 350°F.
2.  Sift together two cups of all-purpose flour and $\frac{1}{2}$ cup of confectioners' sugar for the crust.
3.  Cut in one cup of butter until the mixture clings together.
4.  Press this crust mixture into a 13x9x2 inch baking pan.
5.  Bake in the 350°F oven for 20 to 25 minutes, or until lightly browned.
6.  While the crust is baking, beat together four beaten eggs, two cups of granulated sugar, and $\frac{1}{3}$ cup of lemon juice for the filling.
7.  In a separate small bowl, sift together $\frac{1}{2}$ cup of all-purpose flour and $\frac{1}{2}$ teaspoon of baking powder.
8.  Stir this dry mixture into the egg mixture.
9.  Pour the filling over the hot baked crust.
10. Bake in the 350°F oven for 25 minutes longer. (The card notes: "will seem runny but once cool should congeal").
11. Sprinkle with additional confectioners' sugar while warm.
12. Cool completely before cutting into bars.
