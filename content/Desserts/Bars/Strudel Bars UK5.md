## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 5 ounces                   | [[Flour]]                                 |
| 2/3 cup                    | [[Sugar]]                                 |
| 1 1/2 cups                 | Crushed [[Corn Flakes]] or cereal         |
| 1 1/2 cups                 | Chopped [[Nuts]]                          |
| 4 ounces                   | [[Butter]], cubed                         |
| 12 ounces                  | [[Butter]]                                |
| 2 1/4 cups                 | [[Sugar]]                                 |
| 2 teaspoons                | [[Cornstarch]]                            |
| 1/2 cup                    | [[Corn Syrup]]                            |
___
## Instructions
(The following instructions are inferred as none were provided on the recipe card.)
1.  Preheat the oven to 350°F. Grease a baking pan.
2.  **For the streusel/crust:** In a bowl, combine the flour, 2/3 cup sugar, crushed corn flakes, and nuts.
3.  Cut in the 4 ounces of cubed butter until the mixture is crumbly.
4.  Press about two-thirds of this mixture into the bottom of the prepared pan to form a crust.
5.  **For the filling:** In a saucepan, combine the 12 ounces of butter, 2 1/4 cups of sugar, cornstarch, and corn syrup. Cook over medium heat, stirring, until the mixture thickens.
6.  Pour the filling over the crust.
7.  Sprinkle the remaining streusel mixture on top.
8.  Bake for 30-35 minutes, or until golden brown.
9.  Let it cool completely before cutting into bars.
