## Instructions
### For the Base Layer
1.  Preheat the oven to 350°F.
2.  In a large bowl, cream the 2 eggs and 1 stick of margarine together.
3.  Add the dry cake mix, nuts, and coconut. Mix well.
4.  Press this mixture evenly into the bottom of a 13x9 inch pan.
### For the Top Layer
1.  In a separate bowl, mix the cream cheese, 1 stick of margarine, and powdered sugar until smooth.
2.  Pour this mixture on top of the base layer in the pan.
3.  Bake at 350°F for 1 to 1 1/2 hours.
