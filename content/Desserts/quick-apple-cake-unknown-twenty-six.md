# Quick Apple Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** N. Lake | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A delightful and speedy apple cake featuring a yellow cake mix base, apple pie filling, and a crunchy brown sugar nut glaze. Perfect for a quick homemade treat that bakes to golden perfection.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 35 mins | None | 10-12 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 13x9-inch baking pan
* Mixing bowls

---
## Ingredients

### Cake Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 (18g) pkg | [[Yellow Cake Mix]] | Dry |
| 3 | [[Eggs]] | Beaten well |
| 1 can | [[Apple Pie Filling]] | Standard size |

### Glaze
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/3 c. | [[Brown Sugar]] | None |
| 1 T. | [[Flour]] | None |
| 1 tsp. | [[Cinnamon]] | None |
| 1 T. | [[Butter]] | None |
| 1 c. | [[Chopped Nuts]] | Chopped |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and Mixing
1. Add **1 (18g) pkg yellow cake mix** to **3 beaten eggs**. Add **1 can apple pie filling** and mix until just combined.
2. Spread the batter evenly in a greased **13x9-inch pan**.

### Step 2: Glaze and Baking
3. Combine **1/3 c. brown sugar**, **1 T. flour**, **1 tsp. cinnamon**, **1 T. butter**, and **1 c. chopped nuts** to form the glaze mixture.
4. Sprinkle the glaze mixture evenly on top of the batter.
5. Bake at **350°** for **35 minutes** until golden brown and set in the center.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Can be stored covered at room temperature for up to 2 days or refrigerated for up to 4 days.
