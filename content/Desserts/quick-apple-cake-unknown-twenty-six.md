# Quick Apple Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** N. Sate | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A deliciously simple semi-homemade cake featuring yellow cake mix and spiced apple pie filling. Topped with a crumbly brown sugar, cinnamon, and nut glaze, it bakes up golden and fragrant.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 35 mins | None | 12 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* 13x9-inch baking pan

---
## Ingredients

### Cake Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 (18 oz) | [[Yellow cake mix]] | Dry |
| 1 can | [[Apple pie filling]] | Ready-to-use |
| 3 | [[Eggs]] | Beaten well |

### Glaze
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/3 c. | [[Brown sugar]] | Packed |
| 1 tsp | [[Cinnamon]] | Ground |
| 1 T. | [[Flour]] | All-purpose |
| 1 c. | [[Chopped nuts]] | Pecans or walnuts |
| 1 T. | [[Butter]] | Softened or melted |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Batter Preparation
1. Add **1 (18 oz) yellow cake mix** to **3 beaten eggs** in a large mixing bowl.
2. Add **1 can apple pie filling** and mix until fully combined into a thick batter.

### Step 2: Assembly and Baking
3. Spread the batter evenly into a greased 13x9-inch pan.
4. In a small bowl, combine **1/3 c. brown sugar**, **1 tsp cinnamon**, **1 T. flour**, **1 c. chopped nuts**, and **1 T. butter** to make the topping; sprinkle the glaze mixture evenly on top of the batter.
5. Bake at 350°F for 35 minutes until the cake is golden brown and springs back lightly to the touch.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store covered at room temperature for up to 2 days, or refrigerate for up to 5 days.
