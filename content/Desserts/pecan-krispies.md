# Pecan Krispies

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *Delight in these classic Pecan Krispies, a simple yet charming cookie recipe loaded with rich buttery flavor and nutty pecans. Perfect for serving with afternoon tea or as a sweet homemade treat.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 10 mins | None | 24 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Baking sheet
* Measuring cups
* Measuring spoons

---
## Ingredients

### Cookie Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 cup | [[Butter]] | Softened |
| 2/3 cup | [[Brown Sugar]] | Packed |
| 1/2 tsp | [[Baking Soda]] | None |
| 3/4 cup | [[Pecans]] | Chopped |
| 1 cup | [[Flour]] | All-purpose |
| 1 | [[Egg]] | Beaten |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing the Dough
1. Cream together the **1 cup butter** and **2/3 cup brown sugar** until smooth.
2. Add the **1 egg** and mix well.
3. Stir in the **1/2 tsp baking soda**, **1 cup flour**, and **3/4 cup pecans** until just combined into a soft dough.

### Step 2: Baking
4. Drop spoonfuls of dough onto a prepared baking sheet.
5. Bake at 350°F until edges are golden brown and centers are set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Dough can be prepared and refrigerated in an airtight container for up to 2 days before baking.
