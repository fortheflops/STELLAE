# Butterscotch Pie

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic, rich butterscotch pie featuring a smooth, thickened filling and a light meringue topping. This traditional recipe relies on simple pantry staples for a comforting, nostalgic dessert.*

## Ingredients

### Filling Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 cup | [[Brown Sugar]] | Soft |
| 1 cup | [[Water]] | Cold |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] | Separated, yolks for filling, whites for meringue |
| 2 tbsp | [[Flour]] | All-purpose |
| 1 tsp | [[Vanilla Extract]] | Added at end |
| 2 tbsp | [[Butter]] | Softened |
| 1 | [[Pie Crust]] | Baked |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Filling Preparation
1. In a saucepan, mix **1 cup brown sugar**, **2 tbsp flour**, and the **2 egg yolks** into a smooth paste.
2. Gradually add **1 cup cold water** and **2 tbsp butter** to the mixture.
3. Cook over medium heat, stirring constantly until the mixture becomes thick and coats the back of a spoon.
4. Remove from heat and stir in **1 tsp vanilla extract**.

### Step 2: Assembly
5. Pour the thickened filling into the **1 baked pie crust**.
6. Whip the reserved **2 egg whites** to create a meringue and spread over the top of the pie.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The filling can be made a day in advance, but the meringue should be added and browned just before serving to prevent weeping.
