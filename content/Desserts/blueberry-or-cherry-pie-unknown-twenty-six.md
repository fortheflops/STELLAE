# Blueberry or Cherry Pie

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A delightful no-bake fruit pie featuring a creamy cream cheese and Dream Whip filling layered with fresh bananas and topped with your choice of prepared cherry or blueberry pie filling. This easy recipe yields two delicious pies perfect for family gatherings.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 0 mins | 1 hour | 2 pies |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Electric mixer
* Pie pans

## Ingredients

### Pie Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 8 oz | [[Cream cheese]] | Softened |
| 1 cup | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 1 large pkg | [[Dream Whip]] | Prepared according to package directions |
| 4 | [[Bananas]] | Sliced |
| 2 | [[Baked pie shells]] | Cooled |
| 1 can | [[Prepared pie filling]] | Cherry or Blueberry (B.B.) |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparing the Filling
1. Softened **8 oz cream cheese** and **1 cup sugar** together until smooth and creamy.
2. Prepare **1 large pkg Dream Whip** according to directions on the package.
3. Add the cream cheese mixture to the prepared **Dream Whip** and blend well until fluffy.

### Step 2: Assembling the Pies
4. Slice **4 bananas** directly into the **2 baked pie shells**.
5. Pour the whipped mixture evenly over the **bananas**.
6. Top with **1 can prepared pie filling** until covered and chill until set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Keep refrigerated until ready to serve; best enjoyed within 24 hours of assembly.
