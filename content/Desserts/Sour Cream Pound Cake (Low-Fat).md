# Sour Cream Pound Cake (Low-Fat)

> 📜 **Collection:** UK15 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown (from Cooking Light Desserts) | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A lighter version of a classic sour cream pound cake, made with egg substitute and low-fat margarine for a lighter but still moist and tender crumb.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 1 hour 35 mins | None | 16 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 10-inch tube pan
* Large bowl
* Small bowl
* Mixing bowls
* Electric mixer
* Long wooden skewer
* Wire rack

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3 cups | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 3/4 cup | [[Margarine]] | Low-fat |
| 1 1/3 cups | [[Egg Substitute]] | Frozen, thawed (or 8 egg whites) |
| 1 1/2 cups | [[Sour Cream]] | Low-fat |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Soda]] | Baking soda |
| 4 1/2 cups | [[Cake Flour]] | Sifted |
| 1/4 teaspoon | [[Salt]] | Standard |
| 2 teaspoons | [[Vanilla]] | Extract |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Cream
1. Preheat the oven to **325°F**. Coat a **10-inch tube pan** with cooking spray.
2. In a large bowl, cream the **3 cups sugar** and **3/4 cup margarine** at medium speed with an electric mixer until well combined.
3. Gradually add the **1 1/3 cups frozen egg substitute**, beating well until incorporated.

### Step 2: Mix Bases
4. In a small bowl, combine the **1 1/2 cups low-fat sour cream** and **1 teaspoon baking soda**; stir well and set aside.
5. In another bowl, combine the **4 1/2 cups sifted cake flour** and **1/4 teaspoon salt**.

### Step 3: Combine and Bake
6. With the mixer running at low speed, add the flour mixture to the creamed mixture in three parts, alternating with the sour cream mixture, beginning and ending with the flour.
7. Stir in the **2 teaspoons vanilla**.
8. Spoon the batter into the prepared pan.
9. Bake for **1 hour and 35 minutes**, or until a long wooden skewer inserted into the center comes out clean and golden brown and fragrant.
10. Let the cake cool in the pan for **10 minutes** before removing it to a wire rack to cool completely.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the cooled cake tightly wrapped at room temperature for up to 3 days or freeze for longer storage.
