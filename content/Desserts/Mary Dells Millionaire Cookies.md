# Mary Dell's Millionaire Cookies

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Mary Dell | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic, large-batch recipe for rich, dense, fruitcake-style drop cookies loaded with pecans, cherries, raisins, and a hint of whiskey. These spiced cookies are perfect for holiday gifting and large gatherings.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Dough
1. In a very large bowl, cream the **1/2 cup butter** and **2 cups brown sugar** together with an electric mixer until light and fluffy.
2. Beat in the **3 eggs**, one at a time, until fully incorporated.
3. Stir in the **3 tbsp whiskey** and **1 tsp vanilla**.
4. In a separate bowl, sift together the **2 1/2 cups flour** and **1 tsp baking soda**. In another small bowl, whisk the **1 tsp baking powder** into the **3/4 cup buttermilk**.
5. On low speed, add the dry ingredients to the creamed mixture alternately with the buttermilk mixture, beginning and ending with the dry ingredients.
6. By hand, fold in the **4 cups pecans**, **1 cup candied cherries**, and **15 oz golden seedless raisins** until evenly distributed.

### Step 2: Chill and Bake
7. Cover the dough and refrigerate overnight to allow the flavors to meld and the dough to firm up.
8. The next day, preheat your oven to 350°F and grease your baking sheets.
9. Drop the cold, stiff dough by rounded teaspoonfuls onto the prepared baking sheets.
10. Bake for **10-12 minutes**, or until the edges are golden brown and the centers are set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The dough must be refrigerated overnight before baking. Baked cookies can be stored in an airtight container for up to 2 weeks.
