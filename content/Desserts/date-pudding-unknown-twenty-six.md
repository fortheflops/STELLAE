## Instructions

### Part I & II: Preparing and Baking the Cake
1. Combine **1 cup hot water** and **1/2 lb dates** in a bowl, stir in **1 tsp soda**, and set aside on the back of the stove.
2. In a separate bowl, cream together **1 cup sugar** and **1 tbsp butter**, then beat in **1 unopened egg**.
3. Add Part I into the creamed mixture, then gently stir in **1 1/2 cups flour** and **1/2 tsp B. powder**.
4. Bake in a preheated oven at **350 degrees F** until golden brown and a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.

### Part III: Preparing and Applying the Sauce
5. While the cake is baking, combine **3/4 cup hot water**, **1/2 cup sugar**, **1/4 lb dates** (stoned and cut up), and **1/2 cup nuts** in a saucepan.
6. Boil the sauce mixture until it becomes thick.
7. Pour the hot Part III sauce evenly over the cake immediately when it comes out of the oven.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Best served warm fresh out of the oven, but leftovers can be covered and stored at room temperature for up to 2 days.
