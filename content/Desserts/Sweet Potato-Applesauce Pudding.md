# Sweet Potato-Applesauce Pudding

> 📜 **Collection:** Jill Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Evelyn Sue Zlotkin | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic layered casserole featuring tender sweet potato rounds and spiced applesauce, finished with a honey and fat glaze. This traditional dish is perfect for holiday gatherings and pairs beautifully with savory meals.*

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 6 medium (3 lbs) | [[Sweet Potatoes]] | whole |
| 2 cups | [[Applesauce]] | unsweetened |
| 3/4 teaspoon | [[Cinnamon]] | ground |
| 3/4 cup | [[Honey]] | divided |
| 3 tablespoons | [[Chicken Fat]] or [[Margarine]] | melted, pareve |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Potatoes
1. Place **6 medium (3 lbs) sweet potatoes** in a large pot of water, bring to a boil, and cook for 30-40 minutes until tender when pierced with a fork.
2. Drain the **sweet potatoes** and allow them to cool enough to handle, then peel and slice them into rounds.

### Step 2: Assemble and Bake
3. Preheat the oven to 350°F and grease a 2-quart baking dish.
4. In a small bowl, combine **2 cups applesauce** and **3/4 teaspoon cinnamon**.
5. Create alternating layers of sliced **sweet potatoes** and the spiced **applesauce** in the baking dish, drizzling each layer with **3/4 cup honey** and **3 tablespoons chicken fat or margarine**.
6. Bake uncovered for 45 minutes, basting occasionally with the pan juices, until the pudding is hot, bubbly, and fragrant.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The sweet potatoes can be boiled and peeled up to 24 hours in advance; store in the refrigerator until ready to assemble and bake.
