# Lemon Poppy Seed Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** JC | ✍️ **Attribution:** Mae Enders / Linda Coleman | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *An easy and moist Bundt cake made with a yellow cake mix, lemon pudding, and poppy seeds. Developed by Mae Enders and Linda Coleman.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 45 mins | 20 mins | 1 Bundt cake (12-16 servings) |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Bundt pan
* Large bowl
* Electric mixer
* Wire rack
* Wooden skewer

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 package | [[Duncan Hines Yellow Cake Mix]] | Dry |
| 1 small package | [[Lemon Pudding]] | Instant mix, dry |
| 4 | [[Eggs]] | Large |
| 1/2 cup | [[Vegetable Oil]] | Standard |
| 1 cup | [[Water]] | Hot |
| 1/4 cup | [[Poppy Seeds]] | Whole |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Mix
1. Preheat oven to **350°F**. Generously grease and flour a **Bundt pan**.
2. In a large bowl, combine the **1 package Duncan Hines Yellow Cake Mix**, **1 small package lemon pudding mix**, **4 eggs**, **1/2 cup vegetable oil**, and **1 cup hot water**.
3. Using an electric mixer, beat the mixture on medium speed for **3 minutes** until smooth.
4. Stir in the **1/4 cup poppy seeds** until evenly distributed.

### Step 2: Bake and Cool
5. Pour the batter into the prepared **Bundt pan**.
6. Bake for **45 minutes**, or until a wooden skewer inserted near the center comes out clean and fragrant.
7. Let the cake cool in the pan for **15-20 minutes** before inverting it onto a wire rack to cool completely.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the cooled cake wrapped tightly at room temperature for up to 3 days or refrigerate for up to 5 days.
