# Tea Cakes

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *Traditional sweet tea cakes rolled in powdered sugar while hot. A classic baked treat perfect for afternoon tea.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 12 mins | None | 24 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Cookie sheet
* Sifter

---
## Ingredients

### Dough Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 cup | [[Butter]] or Crisco | Softened |
| 1/2 cup | [[Powdered Sugar]] | Plus extra for coating |
| 1 tsp | [[Vanilla]] | Liquid |
| 2 cups | [[Flour]] | Sifted |
| 1 to 2 cups | [[Nuts]] | Chopped |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing and Shaping
1. Cream together the **1 cup butter or Crisco** and **1/2 cup powdered sugar**.
2. Mix in the **1 tsp vanilla**.
3. Fold in the **2 cups flour** and **1 to 2 cups chopped nuts**.
4. Roll the dough into balls and place on an ungreased cookie sheet.

### Step 2: Baking and Coating
5. Bake at 350°F until set and lightly golden.
6. After baking while still hot, roll the cookies in **powdered sugar**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store baked tea cakes in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 5 days.
