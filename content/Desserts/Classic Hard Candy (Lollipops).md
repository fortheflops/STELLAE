# Classic Hard Candy (Lollipops)

> 📜 **Collection:** LOC | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A foundational recipe for homemade hard candies cooked to the precise hard-crack stage. This versatile base allows for endless customization with your favorite flavorings and colors.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 20 mins | 30 mins | 12-24 lollipops |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* heavy-bottomed saucepan
* candy thermometer
* candy molds or cookie sheet

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 Cup | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 1/4 Cup | [[Water]] | Room temperature |
| 1/4 Cup | [[Corn Syrup]] | White |
| 1/2 tsp | [[Flavoring]] | Cherry, lemon, or preferred extract |
| 1/2 tsp | [[Food Coloring]] | Liquid or gel |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and Dissolving
1. Lightly oil your candy molds or a cookie sheet; if making lollipops, place the sticks in the molds.
2. In a heavy-bottomed saucepan, combine **1 cup sugar**, **1/4 cup water**, and **1/4 cup corn syrup**. Stir over medium heat until the **sugar** is completely dissolved.

### Step 2: Boiling to Hard-Crack
3. Once clear, stop stirring and attach a candy thermometer to the side of the pan.
4. Bring the mixture to a boil and cook without stirring until the thermometer reaches the **hard-crack stage (300°F to 310°F)**.

### Step 3: Flavoring and Setting
5. Remove the pan from the heat and let it sit until the bubbling subsides.
6. Working quickly, stir in the **1/2 tsp flavoring** and **1/2 tsp food coloring**.
7. Carefully pour the hot syrup into molds or onto the greased sheet and let cool at room temperature until completely hard and brittle.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store finished candies in an airtight container at room temperature; do not refrigerate as moisture will cause the candy to become sticky.
