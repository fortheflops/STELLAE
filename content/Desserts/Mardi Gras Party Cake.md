# Mardi Gras Party Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A festive and flavorful butterscotch layer cake made with melted butterscotch morsels mixed directly into the batter for a rich, candy-like flavor. This vintage dessert yields two tender 8-inch cakes.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 30 mins | None | 2 8-inch layers |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Two 8-inch round cake pans
* Small saucepan
* Large bowl
* Separate bowl
* Electric mixer

## Instructions

### Step 1: Melt and Prepare
1. Preheat your oven to **375°F**. Grease and flour two **8-inch round cake pans**.
2. In a small saucepan, melt the **⅔ cup Nestle's butterscotch morsels** with the **¼ cup water** over low heat. Stir until smooth and set aside to cool.

### Step 2: Mix the Batter
3. In a separate bowl, sift together the **2 ¼ cups flour**, **1 tsp salt**, **½ tsp baking powder**, and **1 tsp baking soda**.
4. In a large bowl, cream the **½ cup shortening** and **1 ¼ cups sugar** together with an electric mixer.
5. Add the **3 unbeaten eggs** one at a time, beating well after each addition.
6. Blend in the cooled **butterscotch mixture** until fully incorporated.

### Step 3: Combine and Bake
7. Alternately add the dry ingredients and the **1 cup milk** to the creamed mixture, beginning and ending with the dry ingredients. Mix until just combined.
8. Pour the batter evenly into the prepared pans and bake for **25-30 minutes**, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean and the edges are golden brown.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the frosted or unfrosted cake layers tightly wrapped in plastic wrap at room temperature for up to 2 days, or refrigerate for up to 5 days.
