# Fluffy Blueberry Banana Jello Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** UK15 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic, large-batch Jello salad featuring a light and fluffy lemon Jello base folded with whipped cream, blueberries, and mashed bananas. This retro dessert is perfect for feeding a crowd.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 5 mins | 6 hours | 1 9x13 inch pan |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* saucepan
* large mixing bowl
* whisk
* electric mixer
* 9x13 inch pan

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 4 packages (3 oz each) | [[Lemon Jello]] | powdered |
| 2 cans (15 oz each) | [[Blueberries]] | drained, juice reserved |
| 1 can (20 oz) | [[Pineapple Juice]] | No. 2 size |
| 1 Cup | [[Mashed Bananas]] | ripe |
| 2 cartons (1 pint total) | [[Heavy Whipping Cream]] | chilled |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Base
1. Drain the juice from the **2 cans blueberries** into a saucepan and add the **1 can pineapple juice**. Bring the combined juices to a boil.
2. In a large bowl, empty the **4 packages lemon jello**. Pour the boiling juice over the **jello** powder and stir until completely dissolved.

### Step 2: Chill and Whip
3. Place the **jello mixture** in the refrigerator and chill until it is partially set, reaching a consistency where it mounds when dropped from a spoon.
4. While the **jello** is chilling, whip the **2 cartons heavy whipping cream** in a separate, chilled bowl until stiff peaks form.

### Step 3: Assemble and Set
5. Gently fold the **whipped cream**, **1 cup mashed bananas**, and the reserved **blueberries** into the partially set **jello mixture** until just combined.
6. Pour the mixture into a 9x13 inch pan, spread evenly, and refrigerate for at least 4-6 hours until completely firm and set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This salad is best made at least 6 hours in advance to ensure it is fully set. Keep refrigerated until ready to serve.
