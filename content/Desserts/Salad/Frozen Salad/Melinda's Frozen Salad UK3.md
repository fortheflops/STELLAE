## Recipe
| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
|1 1/2 C.|[[Sugar]]|
|1 C.|[[Water]]|
|10 lg.|[[Marshmallows]]|
|2 pkg.|[[Frozen Strawberries (Soften with juice, may liquefy in blender)]]|
|1 lg. can|[[Crushed Pineapple (with juice)]]|
|1 lg. can|[[Apricots (Diced & Drained)]]|
|4|[[Sliced Bananas]]|
___
### Instructions
1. Boil sugar and water together to dissolve sugar.
2. Add marshmallows and melt.
3. Add frozen strawberries (soften with juice, may liquefy in blender).
4. Add crushed pineapple with juice.
5. Add apricots, diced and drained.
6. Add sliced bananas.
7. Freeze in cupcake liners.
