## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
|1 can|[[Cherry Pie Filling]]|
|16 oz. can|[[Crushed Pineapple with juice]]|
|1/2 cup|[[Sugar]]|
|1/2 pkg.|[[Mini Marshmallows]]|
|1 cup|[[Pecans]]|
| |[[Bananas (Thick sliced)]]|
___
### Instructions
1.  Mix cherry pie filling, crushed pineapple with juice, and sugar.
2.  Marinate overnight.
3.  Add mini marshmallows, pecans, and thick-sliced bananas.
4.  Mix well at the last minute.
