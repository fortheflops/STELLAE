___
## Instructions
1.  In a large bowl, mix all ingredients together: one cup drained pineapple, one cup drained mandarin oranges, one cup miniature marshmallows, one cup coconut, and one cup sour cream.
2.  Keep in the refrigerator overnight to allow the flavors to meld together.
3.  (The card notes you can add any other fruit, but this first combination will last for a week, okay.)
