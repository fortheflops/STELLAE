## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
|1 3 ounce package|[[Strawberry Jello]]|
|1 cup|[[Boiling Water]]|
|1 10 ounce package|[[Partially Thawed Strawberries]]|
|2 small or 1 large|[[Banana]]|
|1 8 ounce carton|[[Whipping Cream - Whipped]]|
|1 cup|[[Chopped Pecans]]|
___
### Instructions
1. Add boiling water to jello and dissolve well.
2. Add strawberries and sliced bananas.
3. Add pecans.
4. Add whipped cream.
5. Freeze in 2 8-inch flat pans.
6. Take out of freezer 5-10 minutes before serving.
___

>[!Note]
>Mom, I used muffin tins with the paper muffin cups to freeze this in. It makes about 2 dozen.
