## Author
Uncle Fred
___
## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 2 (15 ounce) cans          | [[Apricots]]                              |
| 1 large can                | [[Pineapple Chunks]]                      |
| 1 small jar                | [[Maraschino Cherries]]                   |
| 2 or 3                     | [[Bananas]], sliced                       |
| To Taste                   | [[Pecans]]                                |
| 1 large package            | [[Instant Banana Pudding]]                
___
## Instructions
1.  Drain the juice from the cans of apricots and pineapple chunks.
2.  In a large bowl, prepare the instant banana pudding according to the package directions, but use the reserved fruit juice instead of milk.
3.  Once the pudding has set into a sauce, gently fold in the apricots, pineapple chunks, maraschino cherries, sliced bananas, and pecans.
4.  Chill until ready to serve.
