## Recipe

| :---------------------------------- | :---------------------- |

| 1 Slice                             | [[Honeydew Melon]]      |
| As Needed                           | [[Lettuce]]             |
| 1 Scoop                             | [[Cottage Cheese]]      |
| As Needed                           | Sliced [[Strawberries]] |
___
### Instructions
1.  On a bed of lettuce, place a slice of cantaloupe and a slice of honeydew melon. (The recipe notes: "each cut along rind for easy eating").
2.  In the center (of the melon slices or plate), place a scoop of cottage cheese.
3.  Sprinkle with slices of strawberries.
4.  Serve as individual servings.
