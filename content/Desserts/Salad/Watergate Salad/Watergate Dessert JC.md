## Recipe
| Measurements      | Ingredients                   |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 large (20-oz) can | [[Crushed Pineapple]], well drained |
| 1 large (16-oz) container | [[Cool Whip]], thawed             |
| 1 package (3.4-oz) | Jello Instant [[Pistachio Pudding]] Mix |
| 1 cup             | Chopped [[Pecans]]                |
___
## Instructions
1.  In a large bowl, combine the dry pistachio pudding mix and the thawed Cool Whip. Stir until the powder is fully incorporated and the mixture is smooth.
2.  Gently fold in the well-drained crushed pineapple and the chopped pecans.
3.  Cover and chill for at least one hour before serving.
___

>[!Note]
>This simple dessert is also known as "Pistachio Delight" or "Green Fluff." Many variations also include one to two cups of mini marshmallows, folded in with the pineapple and nuts.
