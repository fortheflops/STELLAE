## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 large package            | [[Pistachio Instant Pudding Mix]]         |
| 1 (15 ounce) can           | Crushed [[Pineapple]], undrained          |
| 1 small carton             | [[Cool Whip]]                             |
| 1/4 cup                    | Chopped [[Pecans]]                        |
| 1 cup                      | Tiny [[Marshmallows]]                     |
| 1/2 cup                    | Shredded [[Coconut]]                      |
___
## Instructions
1.  In a large bowl, blend the dry pistachio pudding mix with the entire can of undrained crushed pineapple.
2.  Gently stir in the Cool Whip.
3.  Add the remaining ingredients (chopped pecans, tiny marshmallows, and shredded coconut) and stir to combine.
4.  Chill until ready to serve.
