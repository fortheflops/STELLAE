## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
|1 - 3 oz. pkg.|[[Strawberry Jello]]|
|1 - 3 oz. pkg.|[[Raspberry Jello]]|
|2 C.|[[Boiling Water]]|
|1 - 20 oz. can|[[Crushed Pineapple in own juice]]|
|1 - 12 oz. bag|[[Frozen Blueberries]]|
|1 - 8 oz. pkg.|[[Sour Cream]]|
| |[[Sugar to taste]]|
___
### Instructions
1. Dissolve jello in 2 C. boiling water.
2. Add can of crushed pineapple and package of blueberries.
3. Stir and add blueberries.
4. Blend softened cream cheese and sour cream and sugar to taste. (Vanilla may also be added, if desired.)
5. Spread mixture over jello as icing.
6. Serve on lettuce.
