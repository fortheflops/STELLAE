## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
|4 pkg.|[[Lemon Jello]]|
|2 cans|[[Blueberries]]|
|1 C.|[[Mashed Bananas]]|
|1 can|[[Pineapple Juice (No. 2)]]|
|2 cartons|[[Whipping Cream]]|
___
### Instructions
1. Drain juice from berries and combine with pineapple juice.
2. Heat to boiling.
3. Pour over the 4 pkg. Jello. Mix well.
4. Put in refrigerator until mounds when dropped from spoon.
5. Fold into whipped cream and bananas.
6. Makes 9x13 pan full.
