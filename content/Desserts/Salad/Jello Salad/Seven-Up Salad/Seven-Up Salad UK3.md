## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
| 1 sm. box (~3 oz)     | Lime Jello powder                      |
| 1 Cup                 | Boiling Water                          |
| 1 sm. can (~8 oz)     | Crushed Pineapple, drained             |
| 1 tsp                 | Vanilla                                |
| 1 Tbsp                | Sugar                                  |
| 1 (10 oz) bottle      | 7-Up Soda                              |
| 1 Cup                 | Pecans *(Chopped recommended)* |
| 1 (8 oz) Pkg          | Cream Cheese, softened                 |
___
### Instructions
1.  Mix softened Cream cheese, jello & boiling water.
2.  Add other ingredients & pour into mold.
3.  Chill until firm.
4.  *(Variation Note from card):* May sub orange jello & man. oranges [Mandarin Oranges].
