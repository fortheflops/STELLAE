## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
|2 pkg.|[[Strawberry Jello]]|
|2 pkg.|[[Frozen Strawberries]]|
|2 C.|[[Boiling Water]]|
|1 lg. can|[[Crushed Pineapple]]|
|3|[[Bananas]]|
|1/2 pt.|[[Sour Cream]]|
___
### Instructions
1. Dissolve Jello in boiling water.
2. Add frozen strawberries and let thaw.
3. Add crushed pineapple and sliced bananas.
4. Pour 1/2 mixture into mold and let congeal.
5. Spread sour cream and add remaining Jello mixture.
