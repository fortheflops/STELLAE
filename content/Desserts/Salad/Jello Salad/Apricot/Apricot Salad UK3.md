## Recipe
| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
|1 pkg.|[[Lemon Jello (Dry)]]|
|1 sm. pkg.|[[Cream Cheese]]|
|1 C.|[[Boiling Water]]|
|1 can|[[Apricots (#2 or 303)]]|
|1/2 C.|[[Apricot Juice]]|
|1/2 C.|[[Chopped Pecans]]|
___
### Instructions
1. Pulverize apricots in blender.
2. Blend gelatin from mix and cheese with a fork.
3. Add water. Mix well.
4. Add apricots in blender. Add juice.
5. Stir in pecans.
6. Mold.
