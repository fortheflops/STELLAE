## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
|1 #2 can|[[Bing Cherries]]|
|2|[[Coca Colas]]|
|1 C.|[[Crushed Pineapple]]|
|1 C.|[[Chopped Nuts]]|
|2 pkg.|[[Cherry Jello]]|
|2 C.|[[Fruit Juice (Grape)]]|
___
### Instructions
1. Drain juice from cherries and pineapple.
2. Boil fruit juice and pour over 2 pkg. Jello.
3. Then cool, add 2 cokes.
4. Stir and chill until partially congealed.
5. Add drained cherries, pineapple, and nuts and pour in mold.
