## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
|1 pkg.|[[Cherry Flavored Gelatin]]|
|1 cup|[[Boiling Water]]|
|1 (7 oz.) bottle|[[Ginger Ale]]|
|2 T.|[[Lime Juice]]|
|1 (1 lb. 2 oz.) can|[[Fruit Cocktail]]|
___
### Instructions
1. Dissolve gelatin in boiling water.
2. Cool.
3. Stir in ginger ale and lime juice.
4. Chill until mixture begins to thicken.
5. Drain fruit cocktail and stir into gelatin.
6. Pour into a 1 quart mold.
7. Chill until firm.
___

>[!Note]
>Serves Six
