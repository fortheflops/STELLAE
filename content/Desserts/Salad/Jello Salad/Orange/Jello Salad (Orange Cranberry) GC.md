## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 package                  | [[Orange Jello]]                          |
| 1 jar                      | [[Ocean Spray Cranberry Relish]]          |
| 3/4 cup                    | [[Orange Juice]]                          |
| 1 cup                      | [[Water]] (boiling)                       |
___
## Instructions
1. In a bowl, add 1 cup of boiling water to the orange jello powder and stir until completely dissolved.
2. Instead of the usual cold water, stir in 3/4 cup of orange juice.
3. Mix in the entire jar of Ocean Spray Cranberry Relish.
4. Pour the mixture into a serving dish or mold.
5. Refrigerate for several hours, or until the jello is completely firm.
___

>[!Note]
>This is a simple Jello salad recipe, great for holidays.
