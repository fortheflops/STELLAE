## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
|2 C.|[[Cottage Cheese]]|
|2 C.|[[Pineapple Bits]]|
|2 C.|[[Cool Whip]]|
| |[[Dry Jello]]|
___
### Instructions
1. Mix cottage cheese, pineapple bits, and Cool Whip well.
2. Sprinkle dry jello over and mix.
3. Chill well.
