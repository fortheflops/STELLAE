## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 (16 ounce) can           | [[Cherry Pie Filling]]                    |
| 3/4 cup                    | [[Pineapple]], drained                    |
| 1 can                      | [[Eagle Brand Sweetened Condensed Milk]]  |
| 1 (14 ounce) container     | [[Cool Whip]]                             |
| 1/2 cup                    | [[Pecans]]                                |
___
## Instructions
1.  In a large bowl, combine the cherry pie filling, drained pineapple, Eagle Brand milk, Cool Whip, and pecans.
2.  Mix well until everything is incorporated.
3.  Pour into an attractive serving dish.
4.  Chill thoroughly before serving.
