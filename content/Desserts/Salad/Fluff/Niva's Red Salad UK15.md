## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 cup                      | [[Coconut]]                               |
| 1 can                      | [[Eagle Brand Sweetened Condensed Milk]]  |
| 1 can                      | [[Cherry Pie Filling]]                    |
| 1 large container          | [[Cool Whip]]                             |
| 1 (15 ounce) can           | [[Pineapple Chunks]], drained             |
___
## Instructions
1.  In a large bowl, mix together the coconut, Eagle Brand milk, cherry pie filling, Cool Whip, and drained pineapple chunks.
2.  Cover and let it set in the refrigerator overnight.
___

>[!Note]
>The recipe card emphasizes to "Make one day ahead and let set in Refrig.
