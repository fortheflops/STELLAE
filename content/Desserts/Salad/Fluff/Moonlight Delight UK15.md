## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 can                      | [[Peaches]]                               |
| As Needed                  | [[Dream Whip]] or [[Cool Whip]]           |
| A handful                  | Chopped [[Pecans]]                        |
| A few                      | [[Maraschino Cherries]] (optional)        |
___
## Instructions
1.  Drain the can of peaches.
2.  Gently smash the peaches with your fingers.
3.  Fold the smashed peaches into the Dream Whip or Cool Whip.
4.  Stir in a handful of chopped pecans.
5.  If you have them, add a few maraschino cherries.
6.  Serve in a pretty glass bowl.
