# Pineapple Upside-Down Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** Jill Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic Pineapple Upside-Down Cake featuring a buttery brown sugar topping, pineapple rings, and pecans, baked in a cast-iron skillet with a light, fluffy batter.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 35 mins | 10 mins | 1 9-inch cake |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 9-inch oven-safe iron skillet
* Mixing bowls
* Electric mixer
* Sifter
* Wire cooling rack

## Ingredients

### For the Topping
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/3 cup | [[Butter]] | Melted |
| 1 cup | [[Brown Sugar]] | Packed |
| 1 (20-ounce) can | [[Sliced Pineapple]] | Drained, juice reserved |
| 2 tablespoons | [[Pecans]] | Large whole |

### For the Cake Batter
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 cup | [[Cake Flour]] | Sifted |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Baking Powder]] | None |
| 1/8 teaspoon | [[Salt]] | None |
| 3 large | [[Eggs]] | Separated |
| 1 cup | [[Granulated Sugar]] | None |
| 5 tablespoons | [[Pineapple Juice]] | Reserved from canned pineapple |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Skillet and Topping
1. Preheat your oven to 375°F.
2. In a **9-inch oven-safe skillet**, melt the **1/3 cup butter** over low heat.
3. Sprinkle the **1 cup brown sugar** evenly over the melted butter.
4. Arrange the **1 (20-ounce) can sliced pineapple** and **2 tablespoons pecans** attractively on top of the brown sugar mixture.

### Step 2: Mix the Batter
5. In a bowl, sift together the **1 cup cake flour**, **1 teaspoon baking powder**, and **1/8 teaspoon salt**.
6. In a separate large bowl, beat the **3 large eggs** (yolks only) until light and pale, then gradually beat in the **1 cup granulated sugar** until thick.
7. Stir the **5 tablespoons pineapple juice** into the yolk mixture, then gently mix in the sifted dry ingredients until just combined.
8. In a clean bowl, beat the remaining egg whites until stiff peaks form, and gently fold them into the cake batter in three additions.

### Step 3: Bake and Invert
9. Carefully pour the cake batter over the pineapple and pecans in the skillet, spreading it evenly.
10. Bake for **30 to 35 minutes**, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean and the cake is golden brown and fragrant.
11. Let the cake cool in the skillet for **5 to 10 minutes** before carefully inverting it onto a serving plate.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Best served fresh on the day it is baked, but leftovers can be stored covered at room temperature for up to 2 days.
