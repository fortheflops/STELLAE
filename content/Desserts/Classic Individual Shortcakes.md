# Classic Individual Shortcakes

> 📜 **Collection:** UK | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *Discover how to bake tender, biscuit-style individual shortcakes from scratch, ideal for layering with fresh fruit and whipped cream. These lightly sweetened pastries bake up golden and flaky in just 15 minutes.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 15 mins | None | 8–10 shortcakes |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large bowl
* Pastry blender
* Fork
* 3-inch biscuit cutter
* Baking sheet

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 Cups | [[All-Purpose Flour]] | Standard |
| 3 Tablespoons | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 2 Teaspoons | [[Baking Powder]] | Fresh |
| ¾ Teaspoon | [[Salt]] | Fine |
| ½ Cup | [[Shortening]] or Butter | Cold |
| ⅔ Cup | [[Milk]] | Chilled |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Mix Dry Ingredients
1. Preheat your oven to **450°F**.
2. In a large bowl, whisk together the **2 cups all-purpose flour**, **3 tablespoons sugar**, **2 teaspoons baking powder**, and **¾ teaspoon salt** until well combined.

### Step 2: Cut in Fat and Add Milk
3. Cut in the **½ cup cold shortening or butter** using a pastry blender or your fingertips until the mixture resembles coarse crumbs.
4. Add the **⅔ cup milk** all at once and stir with a fork until a soft, shaggy dough forms.

### Step 3: Shape, Cut, and Bake
5. Turn the dough out onto a lightly floured surface and gently knead 5-6 times, just until it comes together.
6. Pat or roll the dough to a **½-inch thickness**.
7. Cut out rounds with a **3-inch cookie or biscuit cutter** and place them on an ungreased baking sheet.
8. Bake for **12-15 minutes**, or until golden brown and fragrant.
9. To serve, split the warm shortcakes and fill with sweetened fruit and whipped cream.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Baked shortcakes can be stored in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 2 days, or frozen for up to 1 month.
