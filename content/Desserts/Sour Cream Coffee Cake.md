# Sour Cream Coffee Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic, rich, and moist sour cream coffee cake featuring a sweet cinnamon-nut filling swirled through the middle. This buttery cake is baked to perfection in a Bundt pan.*

## Ingredients

### For the Cake

| 2 cups | [[Sugar]] | None |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] | Large |
| 1 cup | [[Sour Cream]] | None |
| 2 cups | [[Flour]] | None |
| 1 tablespoon | [[Baking Powder]] | None |
| 1/4 teaspoon | [[Salt]] | None |
| 1 tablespoon | [[Butter Flavoring]] | None |

### For the Filling
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 cup | [[Nuts]] | Chopped |
| 1/4 cup | [[Brown Sugar]] | None |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Cinnamon]] | None |

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Filling
1. Preheat your oven to **325°F**. Generously grease and flour a **Bundt pan**.
2. In a small bowl, combine the **1 cup chopped nuts**, **1/4 cup brown sugar**, and **1 teaspoon cinnamon** for the filling. Set aside.

### Step 2: Batter and Assembly
3. In a large bowl, cream the **1 cup oleo** and **2 cups sugar** together with an electric mixer until light and fluffy.
4. Add the **2 eggs** one at a time, beating well after each addition.
5. In a separate bowl, whisk together the **2 cups flour**, **1 tablespoon baking powder**, and **1/4 teaspoon salt**.
6. On low speed, add the dry ingredients to the creamed mixture in three parts, alternating with the **1 cup sour cream**, beginning and ending with the dry ingredients.
7. Stir in the **1 tablespoon butter flavoring**.
8. Pour half of the cake batter into the prepared **Bundt pan**, and sprinkle the **cinnamon-nut filling** evenly over the batter.
9. Carefully spoon the remaining batter over the filling layer.

### Step 3: Bake and Cool
10. Bake for **65 minutes** until a long wooden skewer inserted into the center comes out clean.
11. Let the cake cool in the pan for **15-20 minutes** before turning it out onto a wire rack to cool completely.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the cooled cake tightly wrapped at room temperature for up to 3 days, or freeze for longer storage.
