# Walnut Hermits

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Jean | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *These delightfully spiced Walnut Hermits are packed with walnuts, raisins, and dates. Perfect for a cozy afternoon treat.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 12 mins | None | 36 cookies |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowls
* Electric mixer
* Cookie sheet

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 cup | [[Shortening]] | Softened |
| 2 cups | [[Brown Sugar]] | Packed |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] | Whole |
| 3 1/2 cups | [[Flour]] | All-purpose |
| 1 tsp | [[Baking Powder]] | None |
| 1 tsp | [[Baking Soda]] | None |
| 1/2 tsp | [[Salt]] | None |
| 2 tsp | [[Cinnamon]] | Spices can be halved |
| 1 tsp | [[Nutmeg]] | Ground |
| 1/2 cup | [[Sour Milk]] | Or buttermilk (can use 1/2 cup milk plus 2 tsp vinegar) |
| 1 cup | [[Walnuts]] | Broken (pecans may be substituted) |
| 2 cups | [[Seeded Raisins]] | Plumped |
| 1 cup | [[Dates]] | Cut up |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing the Dough
1. Cream the **2 cups brown sugar** and **1 cup shortening** together until smooth.
2. Add the **2 eggs** and beat well.
3. Add dry ingredients alternately with the **1/2 cup sour milk**.
4. Stir in the **1 cup broken walnuts**, **2 cups seeded raisins**, and **1 cup dates**.

### Step 2: Baking
5. Drop the dough by heaping teaspoons onto a greased cookie sheet.
6. Bake at 375°F for **10-12 minutes** until edges are lightly browned and set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container for up to 6 days to keep moist.
