# Simple Poppy Seed Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A simple and delicious lemon poppy seed cake made with the convenience of a lemon cake mix. This moist cake is baked in an angel food cake pan for a beautiful presentation.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 50 mins | 25 mins | 12–16 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large tube pan
* Angel food cake pan
* Large bowl
* Electric mixer
* Wire rack
* Long wooden skewer

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 Box | [[Cake Mix]] | Lemon flavor |
| 4 | [[Eggs]] | Large |
| 1 Cup | [[Water]] | Or substitute with [[Orange Juice]] |
| ½ Cup | [[Oil]] | Vegetable oil |
| ¼ Cup | [[Poppy Seeds]] | Small box |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Mix Batter
1. Preheat oven to **350°F**. Grease and flour a large tube or angel food cake pan.
2. In a large bowl, combine the **1 box lemon [[Cake Mix]]**, **4 large [[Eggs]]**, and **1 cup [[Water]]** (or **[[Orange Juice]]**).
3. Beat with an electric mixer on medium speed for **4 minutes** until smooth.
4. Add the **½ cup [[Oil]]** and **¼ cup [[Poppy Seeds]]** to the batter. Blend on low speed until just combined.

### Step 2: Bake and Cool
5. Pour the batter evenly into the prepared pan.
6. Bake for **45-55 minutes**, or until a long wooden skewer inserted into the center comes out clean and the top is springy to the touch.
7. Let the cake cool in the pan for **20-25 minutes** before carefully inverting it onto a wire rack to cool completely.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the cooled cake wrapped tightly in plastic wrap at room temperature for up to 3 days, or refrigerate for up to a week.
