# Apricot Casserole

> 📜 **Collection:** Patsy Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A unique and nostalgic fruit casserole featuring sweet apricots topped with a savory, buttery cheese cracker crust. This simple baked dessert balances tart citrus notes with a rich, crunchy topping.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 30 mins | 30 mins | 6-8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Casserole dish

---
## Ingredients

### Base Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 cans | [[Apricots]] | Halves, drained |
| 2 | [[Lemons]] | Juiced |
| 1/2 box | [[Brown Sugar]] | Generous cupful |

### Topping Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 box | [[Cheese Crackers]] | Crumbled |
| To taste | [[Butter]] | Dotted over top |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Maceration
1. In a casserole dish, combine the **2 cans apricots** with the juice of **2 lemons** and **1/2 box brown sugar**.
2. Let the mixture sit for 30 minutes to allow the flavors to meld.

### Step 2: Baking
3. Preheat oven to 350°F.
4. Sprinkle the **1/2 box cheese crackers** evenly over the apricot mixture.
5. Dot the top with small pieces of **butter**.
6. Bake for 30 minutes until the topping is golden brown and the fruit is bubbling.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The apricot base can be prepared and left to sit in the refrigerator for up to 2 hours before adding the cracker topping and baking.
