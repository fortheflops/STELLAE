# Doris Sanderson's Blueberry Yum Yum

> 📜 **Collection:** General Archive | ✍️ **Attribution:** Doris Sanderson | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic layered dessert, also known as a 'Lush' or 'Yum Yum,' featuring a graham cracker crust, a rich and fluffy cream cheese filling, and a sweet blueberry pie topping.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 10 mins | 4 hours | 8-10 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* 8x8 or 9x9 inch pan
* Electric mixer

---
## Ingredients

### Crust
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 16 | [[Graham Crackers]] | crushed (about 1 1/2 cups) |
| 1/4 cup | [[Oleo]] | or butter, melted |

### Filling & Topping
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 pkg (8 oz) | [[Philadelphia Cream Cheese]] | room temperature |
| 1/2 cup | [[Powdered Sugar]] | none |
| 1/2 cup | [[Sugar]] | granulated |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] | none |
| 1 can | [[Blueberry Pie Filling]] | none |
| As needed | [[Whipped Cream]] | for topping |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Make the Crust
1. Preheat oven to **375°F (190°C)**. In a bowl, combine the **16 graham crackers** crumbs with the **1/4 cup oleo** or butter. Press the mixture into the bottom of an 8x8 or 9x9 inch pan. Bake for **8-10 minutes** until set, then let the crust cool completely.

### Step 2: Make the Filling
2. In a mixing bowl, beat the **1 pkg (8 oz) Philadelphia cream cheese** until smooth. In a separate bowl, beat the **2 eggs** with the **1/2 cup powdered sugar** and **1/2 cup sugar** for about **5 minutes**, until thick, pale, and fluffy. Add the cream cheese to the egg mixture and beat until well combined and smooth.

### Step 3: Assemble and Chill
3. Spread the cream cheese filling evenly over the cooled crust. Carefully spread the **1 can blueberry pie filling** over the cream cheese layer. Top with a layer of **whipped cream**. Chill in the refrigerator for at least **4 hours**, or until completely set and firm to the touch, before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store covered in the refrigerator for up to 3-4 days.
