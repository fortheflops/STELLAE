# Oatmeal Cookies

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic handwritten recipe for traditional drop oatmeal cookies featuring brown sugar, raisins, and cinnamon. These rustic cookies bake up tender with wholesome oats and warm spices.*

## Ingredients

| 1/2 tsp | [[Baking Soda]] | None |
| 1 cup | [[Raisins]] | None |
| 1/2 cup | [[Nuts]] | Chopped |

| 1 1/2 cups | [[Oatmeal]] | Rolled oats |
| 1/2 tsp | [[Salt]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing and Baking
1. Cream together the **1/2 cup shortening** and **1 1/4 cups brown sugar** until smooth.
2. Beat in the **1 egg** and mix well.
3. Stir in the **1/2 cup milk**.
4. In a separate bowl, combine the **1 1/2 cups flour**, **1/2 tsp baking soda**, **1/2 tsp salt**, and **2 tsp cinnamon**, then blend into the wet mixture.
5. Fold in the **1 cup raisins**, **1/2 cup nuts**, and **1 1/2 cups oatmeal** until evenly distributed.
6. Drop by spoonfuls onto a prepared baking sheet and bake until golden brown around the edges and set in the center.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store baked cookies in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 5 days, or freeze the unbaked drop dough mounds for future baking.
