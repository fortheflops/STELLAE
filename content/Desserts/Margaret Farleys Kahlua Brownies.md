# Margaret Farley's Kahlua Brownies

> 📜 **Collection:** UK15 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Margaret Farley | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A sophisticated and fudgy brownie recipe featuring Kahlua-soaked raisins and a sweet coffee-infused glaze. These rich treats offer a perfect balance of deep chocolate and aromatic coffee notes.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Batter
1. Preheat oven to 350°F and grease an 8-inch square pan.
2. In a small bowl, combine **1/2 cup raisins** and **1/4 cup Kahlua**; let stand for **15 minutes**.
3. In a medium saucepan, melt **1/2 cup butter or margarine** and **1 1/2 ounces unsweetened chocolate** over low heat until smooth; remove from heat.
4. Stir in **3/4 cup sugar** and **1/2 teaspoon vanilla**; let cool slightly.
5. Beat in **2 large eggs**, one at a time, until well incorporated.
6. In a separate bowl, whisk together **1 cup all-purpose flour**, **1 teaspoon baking powder**, **1/4 teaspoon salt**, and **1 1/2 teaspoons instant coffee granules**.
7. Add dry ingredients to the chocolate mixture, stirring until just combined.
8. Fold in the **raisin-Kahlua mixture** and **1/2 cup finely chopped pecans**.
9. Spread batter into the pan and bake for **30 minutes**.

### Step 2: Frost and Finish
10. Combine **1 1/3 cups powdered sugar**, **1 dash salt**, **2 teaspoons warm water**, **1 teaspoon milk or cream**, and **1/4 cup Kahlua** in a small bowl until smooth.
11. Let brownies cool for **15-20 minutes** until firm to the touch.
12. Spread the frosting over the warm brownies and let cool completely before cutting.
