# Easy Cherry Pineapple Fluff

> 📜 **Collection:** UK15 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A quick and easy no-bake dessert salad combining cherry pie filling, pineapple, pecans, and sweetened condensed milk in a creamy whipped topping base. This classic fluff is perfect for potlucks and parties.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 0 mins | 4 hours | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* large mixing bowl
* silicone spatula
* serving dish

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 (16 ounce) can | [[Cherry Pie Filling]] | none |
| 3/4 cup | [[Pineapple]] | drained |
| 1 can | [[Eagle Brand Sweetened Condensed Milk]] | none |
| 1 (14 ounce) container | [[Cool Whip]] | thawed |
| 1/2 cup | [[Pecans]] | chopped |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Combine and Chill
1. In a large bowl, gently fold together the **1 (16 ounce) can cherry pie filling**, **3/4 cup pineapple**, **1 can Eagle Brand Sweetened Condensed Milk**, **1 (14 ounce) container Cool Whip**, and **1/2 cup pecans** until just combined and uniform in color.
2. Transfer the mixture to a serving dish, cover, and chill in the refrigerator for at least **4 hours** until the texture is set and the flavors have melded.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This fluff can be prepared up to 24 hours in advance; keep it covered and refrigerated until ready to serve.
