# Traditional Biscochitos

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic New Mexican holiday cookie, these crisp, tender shortbreads are made with lard and flavored with anise and whiskey. They are finished with a signature dusting of cinnamon-sugar for a traditional treat.*

| 2 | [[Eggs]] | Large |

| 4 teaspoons | [[Anise Seed]] | None |
| 6 cups | [[Flour]] | All-purpose |

## Instructions

### Step 1: Creaming the Base
1. In a very large bowl, beat the **1 lb (2 cups) lard** with an electric mixer for **15 minutes**.
2. Add half of the **1 1/4 cups sugar** and beat for **5 minutes**, then add the remaining sugar and beat for another **5 minutes**.
3. Add the **2 eggs** one at a time, beating for **5 minutes** after each addition, then stir in the **4 teaspoons anise seed** and **1/2 cup whiskey**.

### Step 2: Forming the Dough
4. In a separate bowl, sift together **2 cups flour**, **1 teaspoon salt**, and **1 1/2 teaspoons baking soda**.
5. Alternately add the flour mixture and **1/2 cup cold water** to the lard mixture.
6. Gradually mix in the remaining **4 cups flour** until a stiff dough forms, then cover and refrigerate overnight.

### Step 3: Rolling and Baking
7. Preheat the oven to 350°F. Roll the chilled dough out to 1/4-inch thickness on a floured surface and cut into desired shapes.
8. Place on an ungreased cookie sheet and bake for **15 minutes** until the edges are lightly golden.
9. While the cookies are still warm, dust them with the **1/2 cup sugar** and **2 teaspoons cinnamon** mixture.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The dough must be chilled overnight to ensure proper texture. Baked cookies can be stored in an airtight container for up to one week.
