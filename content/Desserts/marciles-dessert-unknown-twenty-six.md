# Marcile's Dessert

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Marcile | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A delightful and fluffy dessert featuring a light cake combined with creamy pudding and whipped topping. Perfect for serving a crowd with minimal effort.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | None | 2 hours | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Spatula
* Serving dish

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 can (13 3/4 oz) | [[Pears]] | Medium, canned, drained and chopped |
| 1 qt | [[Cool Whip]] | Thawed |
| 1 package | [[Instant Vanilla Pudding]] | Prepared according to package directions or dry mix as specified |
| 1 | [[Angel Food Cake]] | Prepared |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing and Assembly
1. Mix the **1 can pears**, **1 package instant vanilla pudding**, and **1 qt cool whip** together in a large mixing bowl until smooth and well combined.
2. Cut the **1 angel food cake** into bite-sized pieces.
3. Fold the cake pieces gently into the mixture until all pieces are evenly coated and fluffy.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Chill in the refrigerator for at least 2 hours before serving until well set.
