# Homemade Vanilla Ice Cream

> 📜 **Collection:** LOC | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A rich, no-cook homemade vanilla ice cream featuring whipping cream and evaporated milk with a hint of lemon for brightness. This classic recipe yields approximately one gallon of creamy, churned perfection.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 30 mins | 2 hours | Approx. 1 Gallon |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large mixing bowl
* Wire whisk
* Ice cream freezer

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 7 Cups | [[Whipping Cream]] | cold |
| 2 Cups | [[Sugar]] | granulated |
| 2 Tbsp | [[Vanilla]] | extract |
| 2 Tbsp | [[Lemon Juice]] | freshly squeezed |
| 2 (13 oz) cans | [[Evaporated Milk]] | chilled |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Base
1. In a large bowl, stir together the **2 cups sugar**, **2 tbsp vanilla**, and **2 tbsp lemon juice** until the **sugar** begins to dissolve.
2. Gradually whisk in the **2 (13 oz) cans evaporated milk** until the mixture is smooth.
3. Stir in the **7 cups whipping cream** until fully incorporated.

### Step 2: Chill and Churn
4. Cover the bowl and refrigerate the mixture for at least 2 hours, or until thoroughly chilled.
5. Pour the chilled mixture into the canister of an ice cream freezer.
6. Churn and freeze according to the manufacturer's directions until the ice cream is thick, creamy, and holds its shape.
7. Serve immediately or transfer to a freezer-safe container to ripen in the freezer for a firmer texture.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The base can be prepared and refrigerated for up to 24 hours before churning. Once churned, store in an airtight container in the freezer for up to 2 weeks.
