# Holiday Fruit & Nut Bars

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A festive, large-batch recipe for rich and chewy fruitcake-style bars. This batter is packed with dates, pineapple, cherries, chocolate chips, and nuts, making it perfect for holiday gift-giving.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 25 mins | 55 mins | None | 5-6 dozen bars |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 13x18 inch sheet pan
* Large mixing bowls
* Whisk
* Sturdy spoon

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 9 | [[Eggs]] | large |
| 3 cups | [[Sugar]] | granulated |
| 4 1/2 cups | [[Flour]] | all-purpose, divided |
| 4 1/2 teaspoons | [[Baking Powder]] | None |
| 3/4 teaspoon | [[Salt]] | None |
| 6 cups | [[Nuts]] | walnuts or pecans, chopped |
| 3 cups | [[Dates]] | chopped |
| 3 cups | [[Pineapple]] | crushed, well-drained |
| 3 cups | [[Cherries]] | maraschino or candied, halved |
| 3/4 pound | [[Chocolate Chips]] | approx 1 1/2 cups |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Fruit and Nut Mixture
1. Preheat oven to 325°F and grease/flour a 13x18 inch sheet pan.
2. In a very large bowl, toss the **6 cups nuts**, **3 cups dates**, **3 cups pineapple**, **3 cups cherries**, and **3/4 pound chocolate chips** with **1 cup flour** until well coated.

### Step 2: Create the Batter
3. In a separate large bowl, beat the **9 eggs** and **3 cups sugar** together until the mixture is light and fluffy.
4. In a third bowl, whisk together the remaining **3 1/2 cups flour**, **4 1/2 teaspoons baking powder**, and **3/4 teaspoon salt**.
5. Add the dry mixture to the **egg and sugar mixture** and stir until just combined.

### Step 3: Combine and Bake
6. Pour the batter over the fruit and nut mixture and mix thoroughly until everything is evenly incorporated.
7. Press the thick batter into the prepared pan and bake for 45-60 minutes until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.
8. Allow to cool completely in the pan before cutting into bars.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** These bars store well in an airtight container at room temperature for up to one week or can be frozen for up to three months.
