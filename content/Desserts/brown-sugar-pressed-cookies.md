# Brown Sugar Pressed Cookies

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *Delightful classic brown sugar pressed cookies that require chilling for optimal texture. Bake to a delicate golden finish for a sweet treat.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 8 mins | Overnight or 2 hours | 24 cookies |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Electric mixer
* Cookie sheet

---
## Ingredients

### Dough Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3/4 cup | [[Butter]] | Softened |
| 1 cup | [[Brown Sugar]] | Packed |
| 1 | [[Egg]] | Standard size |
| 1 tsp | [[Vanilla]] | Extract |
| 1/8 tsp | [[Salt]] | Fine |
| 1 tsp | [[Baking Powder]] | Standard |
| 2 1/4 cups | [[Flour]] | All-purpose |

---

## Instructions

### Mixing and Chilling
1. Cream together the **3/4 cup butter** and **1 cup brown sugar**, then beat in the **1 egg** and **1 tsp vanilla** until smooth.
2. In a separate bowl, combine the **2 1/4 cups flour**, **1 tsp baking powder**, and **1/8 tsp salt**, then mix into the wet ingredients.
3. Cover the dough and chill overnight or for at least 2 hours until firm.

### Baking
4. Preheat oven to 375 degrees.
5. Form the chilled dough onto ungreased sheet.
6. Bake for **6-8 minutes** until lightly golden and set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Dough can be prepared and kept refrigerated for up to 24 hours before shaping and baking.
