# Don Q Pineapple Rum Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Seven | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A moist and flavorful tropical Bundt cake infused with crushed pineapple and rich rum glaze. Perfect for gatherings, this crowd-pleasing dessert features a warm homemade rum syrup poured right over the warm cake.*

## Instructions

### Prep
1. Drain the **1 can (1 lb. 4 oz.) crushed pineapple** well, saving the **1/4 cup pineapple syrup** for the glaze.

### Mix & Bake Cake
2. Beat **1 cup sugar**, **3/4 cup sour cream**, **1/2 cup butter**, and **2 tsp. vanilla extract** together in a mixing bowl for 2 minutes.
3. Add **2 large eggs** and beat for 1 minute longer.
4. Stir together **2 cups buttermilk baking mix**, **1 cup sifted all-purpose flour**, and **1 tsp. baking soda**, then add to the wet mixture.
5. Mix in the drained **crushed pineapple** and **3 tbsp. rum** until combined.
6. Turn the batter into a well-greased 9-inch Bundt pan.
7. Bake at 375°F for about 45 minutes until golden brown and a toothpick inserted into the center tests done.

### Glaze & Serve
8. To make the glaze, combine **3/4 cup sugar**, **1/4 cup butter**, and **1/4 cup pineapple syrup** in a small saucepan. Stir over low heat until the sugar is fully dissolved and the butter is melted. Remove from heat and stir in **3 tbsp. rum**.
9. Remove the cake from the oven and spoon about half of the warm glaze over the cake while still in the pan.
10. Let stand for 10 minutes, then invert onto a serving plate.
11. Spoon the remaining glaze evenly over the cake. Allow to cool completely before slicing.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store covered at room temperature for up to 3 days. The flavor deepens as the rum glaze absorbs.
