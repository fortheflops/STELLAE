# Lemon Pound Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Five | ✍️ **Attribution:** Georgia Treadwell | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A delightful lemon pound cake made with pineapple supreme cake mix and lemon Jello, finished with a bright lemon glaze. Perfectly moist and bursting with citrus flavor.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 45 mins | 15 mins | 10 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Angel food pan
* Mixing bowl

---
## Ingredients

### Cake Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 box | [[Pineapple Supreme Cake Mix]] | Hines |
| 1 small box | [[Lemon Jello]] | Dry |
| 2/3 cup | [[Oil]] | None |
| 2/3 cup | [[Water]] | H2O |
| 4 | [[Eggs]] | Beaten |

### Glaze Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/4 cup | [[Lemon Juice]] | None |
| 1 cup | [[Powdered Sugar]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Baking
1. Preheat oven to **350°**.
2. Mix together **1 box pineapple supreme cake mix**, **1 small box lemon jello**, **2/3 cup oil**, **2/3 cup water**, and **4 eggs**.
3. Pour batter into a floured and greased angel pan.
4. Bake for **45 min.** until golden and springy to the touch.

### Step 2: Glazing
5. Cool in pan for **15 min.**
6. Pierce the warm cake and pour over the glaze made from **1/4 cup lemon juice** and **1 cup powdered sugar** until absorbed.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store tightly wrapped at room temperature for up to 3 days or freeze the unglazed cake for longer storage.
