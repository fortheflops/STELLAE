# Easy Watergate Salad (Pistachio Fluff)

> 📜 **Collection:** UK15 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Mae White | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic, creamy no-bake dessert featuring pistachio pudding, pineapple, and marshmallows. This nostalgic potluck favorite is also known as Pistachio Fluff or Green Stuff.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 0 mins | 1 hour | 8–10 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large mixing bowl
* Silicone spatula

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 large package (5.1 oz) | [[Pistachio Instant Pudding Mix]] | dry |
| 1 can (15 oz) | [[Pineapple]] | crushed, undrained |
| 1 small container (8 oz) | [[Cool Whip]] | thawed |
| 1/4 cup | [[Pecans]] | chopped |
| 1 cup | [[Marshmallows]] | tiny |
| 1/2 cup | [[Coconut]] | shredded |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Combine Base
1. In a large bowl, stir together the **1 large package (5.1 oz) pistachio instant pudding mix** and the **1 can (15 oz) crushed pineapple** (undrained) until well blended and smooth.

### Step 2: Fold and Chill
2. Gently fold in the **1 small container (8 oz) Cool Whip** until no white streaks remain.
3. Stir in the **1/4 cup chopped pecans**, **1 cup tiny marshmallows**, and **1/2 cup shredded coconut** until evenly distributed.
4. Cover and chill in the refrigerator for at least **1 hour** until the mixture is set and flavors have melded.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This salad can be prepared up to 24 hours in advance; keep covered in the refrigerator until ready to serve.
