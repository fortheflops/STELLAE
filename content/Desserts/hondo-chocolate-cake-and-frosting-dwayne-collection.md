# Hondo Chocolate Cake and Frosting

> *A rich and moist homemade chocolate cake topped with a decadent boiled chocolate frosting. This classic recipe yields a large sheet pan portion, perfect for sharing.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Making the Cake Batter
1. Mix **2 cups flour**, **1 1/2 tbsp salt**, and **2 cups sugar** together and set aside.
2. In a saucepan, combine **1 stick oleo**, **1/2 cup oil** (or up to **3/4 cup**), **3 1/2 tbsp cocoa**, and **1 cup water**. Bring to a boil, then remove from heat.
3. Pour the hot chocolate mixture over the dry ingredients and mix well.
4. Add **1/2 cup sour milk**, **2 beaten eggs**, **1 tsp baking soda**, and **1 tsp vanilla**, mixing thoroughly until smooth.

### Step 2: Baking
5. Grease and flour an extra large pan (or combination of a 9x13 and 9x9 pan).
6. Pour the batter into the prepared pans and bake for **20 minutes at 400°F** until a golden crust forms and the center springs back when lightly touched.

### Step 3: Frosting and Finishing
7. Five minutes before the cake is done, prepare the frosting: in a saucepan, bring **1 stick oleo**, **3 1/2 tbsp cocoa**, and **1/3 cup milk** to a boil, stirring constantly.
8. Remove from heat and add **1 box sifted powdered sugar**. Put back over heat and bring just barely to a boil.
9. Pour the hot frosting evenly over the hot cake while still in the pan, allowing it to soak slightly into the surface.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the frosted cake covered at room temperature for up to 2 days, or refrigerate for up to 5 days.
