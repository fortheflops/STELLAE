# Beatriz Mares' Cherry Winks

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Beatriz Mares | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic mid-century drop cookie featuring a chewy center of dates and pecans, finished with a signature crunchy corn flake coating and a bright maraschino cherry. These nostalgic treats are perfect for holiday baking and cookie exchanges.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Dough
1. Preheat your oven to 375°F.
2. In a large bowl, cream the **3/4 cup shortening** and **1 cup sugar** together until light and fluffy.
3. Beat in the **2 eggs**, **2 tbsp milk**, and **1 tsp vanilla** until well combined.
4. In a separate bowl, sift together the **2 1/4 cups flour**, **1 tsp baking powder**, **1/2 tsp baking soda**, and **1/2 tsp salt**.
5. Gradually add the dry mixture to the creamed ingredients, mixing until a stiff dough forms.
6. Fold in the **1 cup pecans**, **1 cup dates**, and **1/3 cup maraschino cherries** by hand.

### Step 2: Shape and Bake
7. Place the **2-3 cups crushed corn flakes** into a shallow bowl.
8. Shape level tablespoons of dough into balls and roll each in the **corn flakes** until completely coated.
9. Place balls on an ungreased cookie sheet, top each with a **1/4 piece of maraschino cherry**, and flatten slightly.
10. Bake for **10-12 minutes** until the edges are set and lightly golden.
11. Let cool on the sheet for one minute before transferring to a wire rack to cool completely.
