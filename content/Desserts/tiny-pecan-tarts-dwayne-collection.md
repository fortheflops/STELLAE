# Tiny Pecan Tarts

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Sara Brown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *These delicate, bite-sized pecan tarts feature a rich cream cheese pastry crust filled with a sweet, nutty brown sugar and pecan filling. Perfect for holiday dessert platters or afternoon tea.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 25 mins | 1 hr | 20 tarts |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl

* Measuring cups
* Measuring spoons

## Ingredients

### Pastry Crust
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 (3 oz) package | [[Cream Cheese]] | softened |
| 1/2 cup | [[Butter]] | or oleo, softened |
| 1 cup | [[Flour]] | sifted |

### Pecan Filling
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | [[Egg]] | None |
| 3/4 cup | [[Brown Sugar]] | None |
| 1 tbsp | [[Butter]] | or oleo, melted |
| 1 tsp | [[Vanilla Extract]] | None |
| 2/3 cup | [[Pecans]] | coarsely chopped |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Pastry Crust
1. Combine the **1 (3 oz) package cream cheese** and **1/2 cup butter** in a bowl until smooth and well blended.
2. Blend in the **1 cup flour** until a soft dough forms.
3. Chill the dough in the refrigerator for about **1 hour** to make it easier to handle.
4. Shape the chilled dough into balls about 1 inch in diameter.
5. Press each ball evenly into the cups of ungreased tiny muffin tins to form pastry shells.

### Step 2: Prepare the Filling and Bake
6. Preheat your oven to **325°F**.
7. In a medium bowl, beat the **1 egg** with a wooden spoon.
8. Add the **3/4 cup brown sugar**, **1 tbsp melted butter**, and **1 tsp vanilla**; mix well until smooth.
9. Stir in the **2/3 cup coarsely chopped pecans**.
10. Pour the pecan mixture into the prepared pastry shells.
11. Bake at **325°F** for **25 minutes** until the filling is set and the pastry edges are lightly golden.
12. Cool completely in the pans before carefully removing the tarts.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The pastry dough can be made ahead and kept refrigerated for up to 2 days. Baked tarts store well in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 3 days or can be frozen.
