# Simple Oatmeal Raisin Cookies

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic, soft, and chewy oatmeal raisin cookie recipe lightly spiced with cinnamon. These straightforward drop cookies are perfect for a quick, comforting treat.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 12 mins | None | 4 dozen cookies |

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 cup | [[Shortening]] | softened |
| 1 cup | [[Sugar]] | granulated |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] | beaten |
| 1/4 cup | [[Milk]] | regular |
| 2 cups | [[Flour]] | all-purpose |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Baking Powder]] | None |
| 1/2 teaspoon | [[Baking Soda]] | None |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Cinnamon]] | None |
| 1/4 teaspoon | [[Salt]] | None |
| 2 cups | [[Oats]] | quick or old-fashioned |
| 1 cup | [[Raisins]] | flour-coated |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Dough
1. Preheat your oven to 350°F.
2. In a large bowl, cream the **1 cup shortening** and **1 cup sugar** together with an electric mixer. Add the **2 eggs** and mix well.
3. In a separate medium bowl, sift together the **2 cups flour**, **1 teaspoon baking powder**, **1/2 teaspoon baking soda**, **1 teaspoon cinnamon**, and **1/4 teaspoon salt**.
4. In a small bowl, toss the **1 cup raisins** with a tablespoon of the flour mixture to coat.
5. On low speed, add the dry ingredients to the creamed mixture alternately with the **1/4 cup milk**, beginning and ending with the dry ingredients.
6. By hand, stir in the **2 cups oats** and the flour-coated **1 cup raisins**.

### Step 2: Bake
7. Drop by rounded teaspoonfuls onto an ungreased cookie sheet.
8. Bake for **10-12 minutes**, or until the edges are lightly browned and the centers are set.

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 5 days or freeze for up to 3 months.
