# Angel Food Cake Dessert

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A light and airy chilled dessert featuring cubed angel food cake folded into a rich, gelatin-stabilized whipped cream and egg custard. Perfect for serving chilled with sweet, thawed strawberries on top.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep the Cake and Gelatin
1. In a first bowl, cut **1 small or medium angel food cake** into cubes and set aside.
2. In a second bowl, soften **1 Tbsp gelatin** in **1 Tbsp cold water** and **1/2 cup hot water**. Let the mixture cool completely.

### Step 2: Whip the Eggs and Cream
3. In a third bowl, beat **3 egg yolks** with **1/2 cup sugar** until they are a pale, lemon yellow color.
4. In a fourth bowl, beat **3 egg whites** until stiff peaks form.
5. In a fifth bowl, beat **1 pint whipping cream** until thick.

### Step 3: Fold and Assemble
6. Gently fold the beaten **3 egg yolks** and sugar mixture into the whipped **1 pint whipping cream**.
7. Fold this mixture into the beaten **3 egg whites**.
8. Mix in the cooled **1 Tbsp gelatin** mixture and **1/2 tsp vanilla extract**.
9. Finally, fold in the cubed **1 small or medium angel food cake** until evenly coated.

### Step 4: Mold and Chill
10. Place the mixture into a greased mold.
11. Cover tightly with **aluminum foil** and let stand in the refrigerator overnight to set.
12. Serve chilled with thawed **strawberries** on the side so each person can spoon them over their slice of cake.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This dessert must be made at least 12 hours in advance to allow the gelatin to set properly. Keep refrigerated until ready to serve.
