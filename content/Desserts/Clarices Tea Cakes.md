# Clarice's Tea Cakes

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Clarice | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic Southern-style tea cake featuring a soft, rolled cookie with a rich butter dough and cake-like texture. These traditional sweets are finished with a hint of vanilla and baked until just golden.*

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |

| 1 ½ Cups | [[Sugar]] | Standard |

| 3 Cups | [[Flour]] | All-purpose |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] | Large |
| 1 Teaspoon | [[Baking Soda]] | None |
| 1 Teaspoon | [[Vanilla]] | None |
| Pinch | [[Salt]] | None |
| 2 Tbsp | [[Milk]] or [[Buttermilk]] | See note |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Cream
1. Preheat your oven to **375°F**.
2. In a large bowl, cream the **1 cup (2 sticks) butter or oleo** and **1 ½ cups sugar** together with an electric mixer until light and creamy.

### Step 2: Combine Wet and Dry
3. Add the **2 eggs** one at a time, beating well after each addition. Stir in the **1 teaspoon vanilla**.
4. In a separate bowl, whisk together the **3 cups flour**, **1 teaspoon baking soda**, and **pinch salt**.
5. Gradually add the dry ingredients to the creamed mixture, along with the **2 tbsp milk or buttermilk**, mixing on low speed until a stiff dough forms.

### Step 3: Roll, Cut, and Bake
6. Turn the dough out onto a lightly floured surface and knead gently a few times until it comes together.
7. Roll the dough out to about **¼-inch** thickness.
8. Cut out rounds with a cookie cutter and place them on an ungreased cookie sheet.
9. Bake for **8-10 minutes**, or until the edges are just beginning to turn golden.
10. Let cool on the cookie sheet for a minute before transferring to a wire rack.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store cooled tea cakes in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 5 days, or freeze the unbaked cut-out dough rounds for later use.
