# Classic Lemon Cheesecake Pie

> 📜 **Collection:** General Archive | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic baked lemon cheesecake pie featuring a rich and creamy filling with a bright, tangy flavor from fresh lemon juice and peel, all set in a simple graham cracker crust.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 35 mins | 4 hours | 6-8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Medium bowl
* Electric mixer
* 8-inch pie pan
* Wire cooling rack

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 (8-inch) | [[Classic Graham Cracker Crust UK2]] | Pre-made |
| 3 (3 oz) pkgs | [[Cream Cheese]] | Softened |
| 2 Tbsp | [[Butter]] | Softened |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Sugar]] | None |
| 1 | [[Egg]] | Whole |
| 2 Tbsp | [[Flour]] | None |
| 2/3 Cup | [[Milk]] | None |
| 1/4 Cup | [[Fresh Lemon Juice]] | None |
| 2 Tbsp | [[Grated Lemon Peel]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Filling
1. Preheat oven to **350°F**.
2. In a medium bowl, cream the **3 (3 oz) pkgs cream cheese** and **2 Tbsp butter** together until smooth and creamy.

### Step 2: Mix and Pour
3. Add the **1/2 cup sugar** and whole **1 egg** and mix well until fully combined.
4. Beat in the **2 Tbsp flour**, then gradually add the **2/3 cup milk** and continue beating until smooth.
5. Stir in the **1/4 cup fresh lemon juice** and **2 Tbsp grated lemon peel**.
6. Pour the filling into the unbaked **1 (8-inch) graham cracker crust** shell. Sprinkle with extra crust crumbs if you have them.

### Step 3: Bake and Chill
7. Bake for **35 minutes**, or until the center is nearly set and has a slight jiggle.
8. Let the pie cool completely on a wire rack, then chill thoroughly in the refrigerator for at least **4 hours** before serving until firm.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store sealed in the refrigerator for up to 3-4 days; extra good when topped with sour cream just before serving.
