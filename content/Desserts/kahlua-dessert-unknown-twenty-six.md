# Kahlua Dessert

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Maxine | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *Indulge in this delightful frozen Kahlua Dessert featuring layers of ice cream sandwiches, coffee liqueur, and toffee chips. It is an easy-to-make, no-bake treat perfect for parties and gatherings.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | None | 4 hours | 12 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 9x13-inch pan
* Mixing bowl
* Spatula

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 12 | [[Ice cream sandwiches]] | Unwrapped |
| 1/4 cup | [[Kahlua]] | None |
| 5 oz | [[Hershey Skor chips]] | Half of a 10 oz bag |
| 1 (12 oz) container | [[Cool Whip]] | Thawed |
| 1/4 cup | [[Kahlua]] | For mixing with Cool Whip |
| As needed | [[Toasted almonds]] | Optional, for topping |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Layering the Base
1. Arrange the **12 ice cream sandwiches** in a single layer in a **9x13-inch pan**.
2. Drizzle the **1/4 cup Kahlua** evenly over the ice cream sandwiches.

### Step 2: Topping and Freezing
3. Sprinkle the **5 oz Hershey Skor chips** evenly over the soaked sandwiches.
4. In a separate bowl, mix the remaining **1/4 cup Kahlua** with the **12 oz Cool Whip** until well combined.
5. Spread the **Cool Whip** mixture evenly over all.
6. Top with **toasted almonds** if desired.
7. Place in the freezer until firm, then thaw for **20 minutes** before you serve.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Keep stored in the freezer for up to a week; thaw for 20 minutes before serving.
