## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing the Base
1. Cream together **1/2 cup shortening**, **1 1/2 cups brown sugar**, and **1/2 cup white sugar** until smooth.
2. Add **2 eggs**, **1 tsp vanilla extract**, and **1 tsp lemon extract** to the mixture and beat well.

### Step 2: Combining Ingredients
3. Sift 3 times together: **2 1/2 cups flour**, **3/4 tsp soda**, **2 tsp baking powder**, **1/2 tsp salt**, **1 tsp ginger**, and **1 tsp cinnamon**.
4. Add the dry ingredients alternately with **1 1/2 cups pumpkin** to the creamed mixture and beat well.
5. Fold in **3/4 cup raisins** and **1/2 cup walnuts**.

### Step 3: Baking
6. Drop spoonfuls onto a well greased pan.
7. Bake in a **350° oven** for **15 to 20 minutes** until golden and set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 3 days to keep them soft.
