# Classic Cherries Jubilee

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A dramatic and elegant dessert featuring pitted Bing cherries simmered in a rich currant jelly sauce, flambéed with brandy, and served over cold vanilla ice cream. This classic recipe offers a sophisticated finish to any meal with its signature tableside flame.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5 mins | 10 mins | None | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* chafing dish
* wide skillet
* small saucepan
* long match
* fireplace lighter

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 (17 oz) can | [[Bing Cherries]] | pitted, drained |
| 3/4 cup | [[Currant Jelly]] | none |
| 1/2 cup | [[Brandy]] | none |
| As needed | [[Vanilla Ice Cream]] | for serving |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Sauce
1. Drain the **1 (17 oz) can pitted Bing cherries** well.
2. In a chafing dish or wide skillet, melt the **3/4 cup currant jelly** over medium-low heat, stirring frequently until the mixture is smooth and glossy.
3. Add the **drained cherries** to the melted jelly and heat through until the fruit is warmed and coated.

### Step 2: Flambé and Serve
4. Gently warm the **1/2 cup brandy** in a small saucepan (do not boil) and pour it into the center of the cherry mixture.
5. Carefully ignite the **brandy** using a long match or fireplace lighter, standing back as the mixture will flame up.
6. Allow the flames to burn out naturally, gently shaking the pan to incorporate the **brandy** into the sauce.
7. Immediately spoon the hot **cherries and sauce** over scoops of **vanilla ice cream** and serve at once while the sauce is still warm.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The cherry mixture can be prepared ahead of time and kept warm, but the flambé process must be performed immediately before serving for safety and effect.
