# Spry Lemon Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** UK15 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic, vintage quick-mix lemon cake made with Spry shortening for a wonderfully tender and moist crumb. This simple one-bowl method delivers bright citrus flavor and a golden finish.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 35 mins | None | 1 8-inch square cake |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 8-inch square cake pan
* Large mixing bowl
* Electric mixer

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Mix
1. Preheat your oven to **375°F**. Grease and flour an **8-inch square cake pan**.
2. In a large mixing bowl, sift together the **1 1/4 cups cake flour**, **3/4 cup sugar**, **2 teaspoons baking powder**, and **1/2 teaspoon salt**.
3. Add the **1/3 cup Spry**, **1 teaspoon lemon rind**, **1/2 cup milk**, and **1 teaspoon vanilla** to the bowl.
4. Beat for about **2 minutes** with an electric mixer on medium speed (or **150 vigorous strokes** by hand), scraping the bowl and spoon frequently until smooth and well blended.

### Step 2: Bake and Finish
5. Add the unbeaten **1 egg** and beat for **2 more minutes** until light and fluffy.
6. Pour the batter into the prepared pan.
7. Bake for **25-35 minutes**, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean and the top springs back lightly.
8. Let the cake cool completely before frosting with **Lemon Cake Icing**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the cooled, frosted cake covered at room temperature for up to 2 days, or freeze unfrosted cake layers wrapped tightly for up to 1 month.
