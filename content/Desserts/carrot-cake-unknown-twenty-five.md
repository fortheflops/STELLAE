## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Dry Ingredients
1. Preheat your oven to **350°F** and grease a baking pan.
2. Sift together the **2 cups flour**, **2 tsp baking powder**, **1 1/2 tsp baking soda**, **1 tsp salt**, and **2 tsp cinnamon** into a bowl and set aside.

### Step 2: Mix the Batter
3. In a large mixing bowl, combine the **1 1/2 cups vegetable oil** and **2 cups sugar**.
4. Add the **4 eggs** one at a time, beating thoroughly after each addition.
5. Gradually stir the sifted dry flour mixture into the wet ingredients until just combined.
6. Gently fold in the **2 cups grated carrots**, **1 small can crushed pineapple**, and **3/4 cup nuts** until evenly distributed.

### Step 3: Bake
7. Pour the batter into the prepared baking pan.
8. Bake at **350°F** for **35 minutes**, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean and the cake is springy to the touch.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This cake can be baked, cooled, and wrapped tightly in plastic wrap to store at room temperature for up to 2 days before frosting and serving.
