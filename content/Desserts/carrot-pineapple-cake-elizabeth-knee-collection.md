# Carrot-Pineapple Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** Elizabeth Knee Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic, incredibly moist carrot cake enhanced with crushed pineapple and warm spices. Perfect for any gathering, this simple scratch-made cake is traditionally finished with your favorite cream cheese frosting.*

## Instructions

### Preparation and Baking
1. Preheat your oven to **350 degrees**.
2. In a large mixing bowl, thoroughly combine the **2 cups sugar**, **1 1/4 cups salad oil**, and **4 eggs**.
3. Stir in the dry ingredients: **2 cups plus 1 tablespoon flour**, **2 teaspoons salt**, **1 teaspoon cinnamon**, and **2 teaspoons baking soda** until a smooth batter forms.
4. Gently fold in the **2 cups carrots** and **8 oz. pineapple** until evenly distributed throughout the batter.
5. Pour into a greased baking pan and bake at **350 degrees** for **1 hour** until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.
6. Cool completely on a wire rack, then frost with your favorite frosting.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This cake can be baked, cooled, and frosted up to 24 hours in advance. Store covered in the refrigerator to keep it moist.
