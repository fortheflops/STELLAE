# Molly's Toffee (Saltine Toffee)

> 📜 **Collection:** Patsy Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Molly | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *An easy and addictive vintage toffee candy made with a base of saltine crackers, boiled butter-sugar toffee, melted chocolate, and chopped pecans. Often called 'Christmas Crack,' this sweet and salty treat is perfect for holiday gifting and quick preparation.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 12 mins | 25 mins | Approx. 40 pieces |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Cookie sheet
* Aluminum foil
* Medium saucepan
* Spatula

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 Sleeve | [[Saltines]] | salt side up |
| 1 Cup (2 sticks) | [[Butter]] | None |
| 1 Cup | [[Brown Sugar]] | packed |
| 1 (12 oz) bag | [[Chocolate Chips]] | None |
| 1 Cup | [[Pecans]] | chopped |

---

## Instructions

### Preparation and Baking
1. Preheat your oven to **350°F**. Line a cookie sheet completely with foil, making sure the foil goes up the sides.
2. Arrange a single layer of **1 sleeve saltines** on the foil, salt-side up.
3. In a medium saucepan, combine the **1 cup (2 sticks) butter** and **1 cup brown sugar**. Bring to a boil over medium heat, then boil for exactly **3 minutes**, stirring constantly until the mixture is bubbling vigorously and turns a rich caramel color.
4. Immediately pour the hot toffee mixture over the **saltines** and spread it evenly to cover all the crackers.
5. Bake for **8 minutes** until the toffee is bubbling and golden brown.
6. Remove the pan from the oven and immediately sprinkle the **1 (12 oz) bag chocolate chips** over the hot toffee. Let them sit for **3-5 minutes** until they are soft, shiny, and melted.
7. Use a spatula to spread the melted **chocolate chips** into a smooth, even layer.
8. Sprinkle the **1 cup pecans** over the top, pressing down gently so they stick.
9. Chill in the refrigerator for about **25 minutes** until the chocolate is completely firm and set.
10. Once firm, break the toffee into irregular pieces. Store in an airtight container.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 2 weeks, or freeze for up to 3 months.
