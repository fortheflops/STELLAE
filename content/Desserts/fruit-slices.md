## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing
1. Mix **3 egg yolks** and **1 cup brown sugar**.
2. Add **1 cup flour** and **1 tsp baking powder** to egg-sugar mixture.
3. Add **1 tsp vanilla**, then the **3 stiffly beaten egg whites**.
4. Add **3 cups assorted candied fruits**, **1/2 cup chopped prunes**, **1 cup raisins**, and **1 cup walnuts**.

### Step 2: Baking
5. Bake for **40 minutes** in a **6.5 x 10.5-inch pan** until golden and set.
