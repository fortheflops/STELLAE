| 1 cup | [[Black Walnuts]] | Chopped |

---

## Instructions

### Mixing and Baking
1. Mix **2 eggs well beaten**, **1 cup white granulated sugar**, **3 cups Kellogg's Corn Flakes**, **1/2 cup cocoanut**, and **1 cup black walnuts** in order as they are given.
2. Ensure the mixture is not too moist; if necessary, add more **Kellogg's Corn Flakes** to achieve the proper consistency.
3. Bake in a slow oven until golden and set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store baked cookies in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 5 days.
