# Cobbler Pie

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic, comforting fruit cobbler pie featuring a rich, buttery batter baked to golden perfection. Perfect for showcasing fresh seasonal berries, peaches, or apples.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 45 mins | None | 6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large casserole dish
* Mixing bowl
* Whisk

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/4 lb. | [[Butter]] | melted |
| 2 tsp. | [[Baking Powder]] | None |
| 1 pinch | [[Salt]] | None |
| 1 cup | [[Sugar]] | None |
| 3/4 cup | [[Milk]] | None |
| 3-4 cups | [[Fruit]] | such as berries, peaches, or sliced apples |
| 1 cup | [[Flour]] | all-purpose |

---

## Instructions

### Preparation and Baking
1. Preheat your oven to a moderate **350°F**.
2. Melt the **1/4 lb. butter** directly inside a large casserole dish.
3. In a separate mixing bowl, combine the **1 cup flour**, **1 cup sugar**, **2 tsp. baking powder**, **1 pinch salt**, and **3/4 cup milk** (all ingredients except the fruit). Beat well until smooth and thoroughly combined.
4. Pour this batter mixture directly into the melted **1/4 lb. butter** in the casserole dish. Do not stir them together.
5. Evenly pour the **3-4 cups fruit** over the top of the batter.
6. Bake for **45 mins** in the preheated **350°F** oven until the crust rises to the top, turning a beautiful golden brown, and the fruit is bubbling around the edges.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Best served warm fresh from the oven. Leftovers can be covered and stored in the refrigerator for up to 3 days; reheat gently in a 350°F oven for 10 minutes before serving.
