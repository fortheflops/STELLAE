# Cheese Cookies

## Instructions

### Step 1: Make the Dough
1. In a large mixing bowl, cream together **1 large package (8 oz) cream cheese** and **1 lb butter** until smooth and well combined.
2. Add **1 cup sugar** and **4 cups flour** to the creamed mixture.
3. Mix all together thoroughly until a cohesive, soft dough forms.

### Step 2: Chill and Shape
4. Chill the dough in the refrigerator for at least **1 hour** until firm enough to handle.
5. Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C).
6. Roll the chilled dough into balls the size of a walnut.
7. Place the dough balls onto an ungreased cookie sheet.
8. Mash down each ball with a fork to flatten slightly, creating a classic crisscross pattern.
9. Sprinkle the tops lightly with extra **sugar**.

### Step 3: Bake
10. Bake at 350°F for **20 minutes** or until the edges are lightly golden brown and the bottoms are set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The dough can be made ahead, wrapped tightly in plastic wrap, and refrigerated for up to 3 days or frozen for up to 1 month before shaping and baking.
