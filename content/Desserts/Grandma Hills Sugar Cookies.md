# Grandma Hill's Sugar Cookies

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Dough
1. In a large bowl, mix **1 cup powdered sugar** and **1 cup granulated sugar**. Add **1 cup softened butter** and **1 cup cooking oil**, mixing until fluffy.
2. Beat in **2 large eggs** one at a time.
3. In a separate bowl, whisk together **4 1/2 cups plus 2 tablespoons flour**, **1 teaspoon salt**, **1 teaspoon baking soda**, and **1 teaspoon cream of tartar**.
4. Stir **1 teaspoon almond extract** into the wet ingredients, then gradually add the dry mixture until a soft dough forms.

### Step 2: Chill and Bake
5. Cover and chill the dough for at least **2 hours** until firm.
6. Preheat oven to **375°F**. Roll dough into small balls and coat in the **cinnamon and sugar mixture**.
7. Place on a cookie sheet and press flat with the bottom of a glass.
8. Bake for **10 to 12 minutes** until the edges are lightly golden and set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The dough can be prepared and chilled for up to 24 hours before baking. Baked cookies store well in an airtight container for up to one week.
