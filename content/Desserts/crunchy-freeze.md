# Crunchy Freeze

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A delightful frozen dessert featuring a creamy custard base folded with whipped topping and crunchy cereal. This make-ahead sweet treat sets firmly in the freezer for easy slicing and serving.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 5 mins | 4 hours | 6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Saucepan
* 8-inch square pan
* Mixing bowl

---
## Ingredients

### Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 pkg (3 oz) | [[Jello Custard Mix]] | Standard package |
| 1 tbsp | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 2 cups | [[Whipped Topping]] | Thawed |
| 1 1/2 cups | [[Milk]] | Whole or low-fat |
| 1 pinch | [[Salt]] | To taste |
| 1/2 cup | [[Grapenuts]] | Cereal |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparing the Custard
1. Blend the **1 pkg (3 oz) jello custard mix** with **1 1/2 c. milk**, a pinch of **salt**, and **1 T sugar**.
2. Bring the mixture to a boil, stirring constantly until thickened.
3. Pour the hot custard into an 8" square pan and refrigerate until completely cool and set.

### Step 2: Combining and Freezing
4. Gently blend **2 c. whipped topping** into the cooled custard until smooth.
5. Fold in **1/2 c grapenuts** cereal until evenly distributed.
6. Freeze until firm, yielding a cold, contrasting texture with a smooth custard and crunchy cereal.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store covered in the freezer for up to a week; let soften slightly at room temperature for 5 minutes before serving.
