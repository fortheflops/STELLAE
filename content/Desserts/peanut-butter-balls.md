# Peanut Butter Balls

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *Indulge in these classic no-bake peanut butter balls, combining rich peanut butter, sugar, and syrup into bite-sized sweet treats. Perfect for quick gatherings or an easy homemade confection.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 5 mins | 30 mins | 12 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Saucepan
* Mixing spoon
* Baking sheet

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 cup | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 1/2 cup | [[Syrup]] | Karo or honey |
| 3/4 cup | [[Peanut Butter]] | Smooth or crunchy |
| 2 1/2 cups | [[Rice Krispies]] | Crispy cereal |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Melting and Mixing
1. Combine **1/2 cup sugar** and **1/2 cup syrup** (Karo or honey) in a saucepan, cook until melted.
2. Remove from heat and stir in **3/4 cup peanut butter** until smooth.
3. Stir in **2 1/2 cups Rice Krispies** until evenly coated.

### Step 2: Shaping and Chilling
4. Shape the mixture into small balls and place them on a tray.
5. Refrigerate until firm and set before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to one week.
