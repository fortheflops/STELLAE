# Moonlight Delight (Easy Peach Fluff)

> 📜 **Collection:** UK15 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A light and airy no-bake dessert salad featuring smashed peaches and crunchy pecans folded into a creamy whipped topping. This simple 'Moonlight Delight' is the perfect quick treat for any gathering.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 0 mins | 1 hour | 6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* medium mixing bowl
* fork
* serving dish

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 can (15 oz) | [[Peaches]] | well-drained |
| 1 container (8 oz) | [[Dream Whip]] or [[Cool Whip]] | thawed |
| 1/2 cup | [[Pecans]] | chopped |
| A few | [[Maraschino Cherries]] | optional, for garnish |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Base
1. Drain the **1 can (15 oz) peaches** thoroughly to ensure the fluff does not become watery.
2. In a medium bowl, gently smash the **peaches** with your fingers or a fork until broken into bite-sized pieces.

### Step 2: Combine and Chill
3. Fold the smashed **peaches** into the **1 container (8 oz) Dream Whip or Cool Whip** until just combined and uniform in color.
4. Stir in the **1/2 cup pecans** and the optional **maraschino cherries**.
5. Transfer to a serving dish and chill in the refrigerator for at least **1 hour** until set and cold.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This dessert is best served chilled; store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 2 days.
