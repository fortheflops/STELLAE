# Old Fashioned Butter Cookies

## Instructions

### Dough Preparation
1. Sift together the **3 cups sifted flour**, **1 tsp baking powder**, and **1/2 tsp salt** into a bowl and set aside.
2. In a large bowl, cream the **1 cup butter or margarine**. Gradually add **3/4 cup sugar**, continuing to cream until light and fluffy.
3. Add the **1 unbeaten egg**, **2 tbsp milk**, and **1 1/2 tsp vanilla extract** to the creamed mixture; beat well until smooth.
4. Gradually blend in the sifted dry ingredients until a soft dough forms.

### Chilling, Shaping & Baking
5. Cover the dough and chill in the refrigerator for **1 hour** until firm enough to roll.
6. Preheat your oven to 400°F (200°C).
7. On a lightly floured surface, roll out the dough to 1/8-inch thickness and cut into desired shapes with cookie cutters.
8. Place cutouts on ungreased baking sheets and bake at 400°F for **5 to 8 minutes** until lightly golden around the edges.
9. Cool completely on wire racks, then frost as desired.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The dough can be prepared and chilled up to 24 hours in advance before rolling and baking.
