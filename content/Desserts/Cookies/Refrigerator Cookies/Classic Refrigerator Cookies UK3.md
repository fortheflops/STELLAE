## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
| 1 Cup               | [[Butter]]              |
| 1/2 Cup             | [[Brown Sugar]]         |
| 1/2 Cup             | [[White Sugar]]          |
| 1                   | [[Egg]], Slightly Beaten |
| 2 Cups        | [[Flour]]           |
| 1/2 Teaspoon             | [[Soda]]          |
| 1/4 Teaspoon            | [[Salt]]                |
| 1/2 Teaspoon             | [[Vanilla]]             |
| 1/2 Cup            | [[Nuts]]             |
___
### Instructions
1.  Cream butter, add sugar, egg, flour, soda, flavoring & nuts.
2.  Form in rolls, chill, slice & bake.
3.  In hot oven. (400°)
