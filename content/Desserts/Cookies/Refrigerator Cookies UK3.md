## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
| 1 Pound            | Butter           |
| 5 Cups              | Flour            |
| 1 1/2 Cups           | Sugar            |
| 3                   | Eggs             |
| 1 Cup               | Pecans           |
| 1 Teaspoon          | Vanilla          |
___
### Instructions
1. Cream butter and sugar, add eggs.
2. Add flour and pecans.
3. Add vanilla.
4. Make into rolls and chill overnight.
5. Slice about 1/8" thick and bake at 350°F.
