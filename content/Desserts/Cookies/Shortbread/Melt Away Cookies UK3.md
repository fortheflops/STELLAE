## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
| 1/2 cup      | [[Butter]]                  |
| 1/2 cup      | [[Oleo]]                    |
| 1 teaspoon    | [[Vanilla]]                 |
| 1/2 cup      | [[Powdered Sugar]]          |
| 1/4 cup      | [[Corn Starch]]             |
| 1 1/2 cups   | [[Flour]] (or a bit more)   |
| 1 teaspoon    | [[Almond Extract]]          |
___
### Instructions
1. Mix all together well.
2. Drop by small teaspoons on ungreased cookie sheet.
3. Bake 325°F for 14 minutes.
4. Frost with powdered sugar frosting - to which a small amount of vanilla & almond added for flavoring.
