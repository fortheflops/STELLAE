## Author
Margaret Farley (9/10/87)

## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 cup                      | [[Shortening]] (or 1/2c Butter + 1/2c Crisco)|
| 1 cup                      | White [[Sugar]]                           |
| 1 cup                      | Light [[Brown Sugar]]                     |
| 2                          | [[Eggs]]                                  |
| 1 teaspoon                 | [[Vanilla]]                               |
| 1 cup                      | [[Flour]]                                 |
| 1/2 teaspoon               | [[Salt]]                                  |
| 2 teaspoons                | Baking [[Soda]]                           |
| 1 teaspoon                 | [[Baking Powder]]                         |
| 2 cups                     | [[Corn Flakes]]                           |
| 1 cup                      | [[Coconut]]                               |
| 2 cups                     | [[Oatmeal]]                               |
| 1 cup                      | [[Nuts]], chopped                         |
___
## Instructions
1.  Preheat oven to 325°F.
2.  In a large bowl, cream together the shortening and both sugars.
3.  Add the eggs and vanilla and mix well.
4.  In a separate bowl, combine the flour, salt, baking powder, and baking soda.
5.  Add the dry ingredients to the creamed sugar mixture and mix well.
6.  Stir in the corn flakes, oatmeal, coconut, and nuts. Mix well.
7.  Drop by teaspoonfuls onto a lightly greased cookie sheet.
8.  Bake for 12 to 14 minutes, or until lightly browned.
___

>[!Note]
>Recipe Notes: This recipe makes 4 to 5 dozen cookies.
