## Recipe
| Measurements | Ingredients                 |
| :----------- | --------------------------- |
| 1 cup        | [[Shortening]]              |
| 1 cup        | [[Sugar]]                   |
| 2 cups       | [[Flour]]                   |
| 2            | [[Eggs]], beaten            |
| 2 cups       | [[Oats]]                    |
| 1/4 cup      | Sweet [[Milk]]              |
| 1 teaspoon    | [[Baking Powder]]           |
| 1/2 teaspoon  | [[Soda]]                    |
| 1 teaspoon    | [[Cinnamon]]                |
| 1/4 teaspoon  | [[Salt]]                    |
| 1 cup        | [[Raisins]]                 |
___
### Instructions
1. Cream shortening and sugar.
2. Add eggs.
3. Mix other dry ingredients.
4. Sprinkle over raisins.
5. Combine the 2 mixtures, adding milk.
6. Bake at 350°F for 10-12 minutes.
