## Instructions
1. Cream the shortening with the white and brown sugars.
2. Add beaten eggs and mix until smooth.
3. Stir in the peanut butter until well combined.
4. Add the flour and mix to form a stiff batter.
5. Stir in the vanilla extract.
6. Roll the dough into balls.
7. Place on a baking sheet and press with a fork to create a crisscross pattern.
