## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 6 cups                     | Sifted [[Flour]]                          |
| 3 teaspoons                | [[Baking Powder]]                         |
| 1 teaspoon                 | [[Salt]]                                  |
| 1 pound                    | [[Lard]] or [[Butter]]                    |
| 1 1/2 cups                 | [[Sugar]]                                 |
| 2 teaspoons                | [[Anise Seed]]                            |
| 2                          | [[Eggs]]                                  |
| 1/4 cup or more            | [[Brandy]] (Sherry can be substituted)    |
| **For the Topping:** |                                           |
| 1/4 cup                    | [[Sugar]]                                 |
| 1 teaspoon                 | [[Cinnamon]]                              |
___
## Instructions
1.  Preheat the oven to 350°F.
2.  Sift together the flour, baking powder, and salt.
3.  In a separate large bowl, cream the shortening (lard or butter) with the 1 1/2 cups of sugar and the anise seed.
4.  Beat the eggs until light and fluffy, then add them to the creamed mixture.
5.  Add the flour mixture and the brandy, mixing until well blended. Use only enough brandy to form a stiff dough.
6.  Knead the dough slightly and then pat or roll it out to 1/4 or 1/2 inch thickness.
7.  Cut the dough into fancy shapes.
8.  In a small bowl, mix together the 1/4 cup of sugar and 1 teaspoon of cinnamon for the topping.
9.  Dust the top of each cookie with the cinnamon-sugar mixture.
10. Bake for 10 minutes, or until very lightly browned.
___

>[!Note]
>Makes 15 Dozen
