## Recipe
| Measurements      | Ingredients                   |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 cup             | [[All-Purpose Flour]]           |
| 1/2 teaspoon      | [[Salt]]                        |
| 1/2 cup           | [[Butter]], softened              |
| 3 ounces          | [[Cream Cheese]], softened        |
| 1                 | [[Egg]]                         |
| 1/2 cup           | [[Sugar]]                       |
| 1 teaspoon        | [[Vanilla Extract]]             |
| 1 teaspoon        | Grated [[Orange Rind]]          |
| 1 teaspoon        | [[Orange Extract]]              |
| Optional          | [[Chocolate Chips]]             |
___
## Instructions
1.  Preheat oven to 350°F. Lightly grease a cookie sheet.
2.  Sift the flour once, measure it, and then sift it again with the salt into a bowl.
3.  In a large bowl, cream together the softened butter, cream cheese, and grated orange rind until smooth.
4.  Add the sugar, egg, vanilla, and orange extract. Beat until the mixture is light and fluffy.
5.  Stir in the flour mixture until well combined. If desired, fold in chocolate chips.
6.  Drop dough by the teaspoonful onto the prepared cookie sheet.
7.  Flatten each mound of dough slightly with the back of a spoon.
8.  Bake for 15 minutes.
9.  If the cookies are not browned, the recipe suggests you can "delicately brown" them under the broiler. Watch them very carefully as this will only take a few seconds.
10. Let the cookies cool on the sheet for a minute before transferring them to a wire rack.
