## Instructions
1.  Preheat oven to 375°F.
2.  In a large bowl, blend together one cup of shortening, one cup of brown sugar, and $\frac{1}{2}$ cup of granulated sugar until creamed.
3.  Beat in two teaspoons of vanilla and two eggs.
4.  In a separate bowl, sift together two and $\frac{1}{4}$ cups of sifted all-purpose flour, one teaspoon of baking soda, and one teaspoon of salt.
5.  Gradually add the sifted dry ingredients to the creamed mixture and mix well.
6.  Stir in $\frac{1}{2}$ cup of the M&M candies. Reserve the remaining one cup of candies for decorating.
7.  Drop spoonfuls of dough onto an ungreased cookie sheet.
8.  Decorate the tops of the cookies with the remaining M&M candies as desired.
9.  Bake at 375°F for 10-12 minutes, or until golden brown.
