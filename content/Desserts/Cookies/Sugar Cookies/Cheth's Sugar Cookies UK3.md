## Recipe
| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
| 3 cups       | [[Flour]]                   |
| 1 cup        | [[Sugar]]                   |
| 1/2 cup      | [[Butter]]                  |
| 1/2 cup      | [[Sour Cream]]              |
| 1 teaspoon    | [[Soda]]                    |
| 1            | [[Egg]]                     |
___
### Instructions
1. Cream together flour, sugar & butter.
2. Add sour cream, soda & egg.
3. Blend well.
4. Roll out dough 1/4" thick & cut with a cookie cutter.
5. (Yield about 3 dozen).
6. Bake at 375°F 10 minutes.
7. (Sprinkle with sugar before)
8. Bake if desired or ice afterwards.
___

>[!Note]
>- Good plain as well.
>- Have fun!
