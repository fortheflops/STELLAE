## Author
Patti Hill (from Grandma Hill's recipe)
___
## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 cup                      | [[Powdered Sugar]]                        |
| 1 cup                      | Granulated [[Sugar]]                      |
| 1 cup                      | [[Butter]] or [[Margarine]]               |
| 1 cup                      | [[Oil]]                                   |
| 2                          | [[Eggs]]                                  |
| 1 teaspoon                 | [[Almond Extract]]                        |
| 1 teaspoon                 | [[Salt]]                                  |
| 1 teaspoon                 | Baking [[Soda]]                           |
| 1 teaspoon                 | [[Cream of Tartar]]                       |
| 4 1/2 cups + 2 tablespoons | [[Flour]]                                 |
___
## Instructions
1.  In a large bowl, mix the powdered sugar and granulated sugar. Add the butter and blend in the oil, mixing to a fluffy consistency.
2.  Add the eggs and beat well.
3.  Stir in the almond extract and the dry ingredients (salt, soda, cream of tartar, flour).
4.  Chill the dough for 2 hours or more.
5.  Preheat the oven to 375°F.
6.  Roll the dough into balls.
7.  Roll the balls in granulated sugar.
8.  Place the sugar-coated balls on a cookie sheet and press them flat with the bottom of a glass.
9.  Bake for 12 minutes.
