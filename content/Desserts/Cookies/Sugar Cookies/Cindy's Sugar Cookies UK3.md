## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
| 1 Cup             | [[Oleo]]              |
| 1 Cup             | [[Sugar]]         |
| 1                   | [[Egg]]          |
| 1 Tablespoon                   | [[Milk]] |
| 1 Teaspoon        | [[Baking Powder]]           |
| 1 Teaspoon | [[Vanilla]]          |
| 1/4 Teaspoon      |[[Salt]]|
| 2 1/2 Cups | [[Flour]]|
___
### Instructions
1. Mix, roll out, cut out & bake at 350° til edges are slightly brown.
2. Glaze w/ powd. sugar icing.
___

>[!Note]
>Makes 3 dozen
