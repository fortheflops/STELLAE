## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
| 1 Cup             | Butter                        |
| 2                   | Eggs                          |
| 1 1/2 Cups          | Sugar                         |
| 1/4 Cup             | Buttermilk Or Sour Cream    |
| 2 Teaspoons         | Nutmeg (If Desired)          |
| 4 Cups              | Sifted Flour                  |
| 1 Tablespoon       | Baking Powder                  |
| 1/8 Teaspoon         | Baking Soda                   |
| 1 Teaspoon          | Salt                          |
___
### Instructions
1. Cream butter, sugar, and eggs.
2. Add liquid (buttermilk or sour cream), baking soda, salt, and nutmeg (if using).
3. Then add flour and baking powder.
4. Roll out dough, cut into shapes.
5. Sprinkle with sugar.
6. Bake at 375°F for 10-15 minutes.
___

>[!Note]
>Makes 6-7 Dozen
