## Instructions
1.  Heat oven to 400°F (200°C).
2.  Blend together $\frac{1}{2}$ cup shortening, 1 teaspoon salt, 1 teaspoon grated lemon rind, and 1 cup sugar.
3.  Beat in 1 unbeaten egg and 2 tablespoons milk.
4.  Stir in 2 cups flour, 1 teaspoon baking powder, and $\frac{1}{2}$ teaspoon soda.
5.  Drop by rounded teaspoonfuls on a lightly greased baking sheet.
6.  Grease the bottom of a glass. Dip the glass in sugar and use it to flatten each cookie.
7.  Sprinkle cookies with nutmeg.
8.  Bake for 8-10 minutes at 400°F (200°C), or until light golden.
9.  Cool on a rack.
