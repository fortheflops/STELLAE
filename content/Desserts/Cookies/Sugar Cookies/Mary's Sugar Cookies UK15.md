## Author
Mary Haddow
___
## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 cup                      | [[Powdered Sugar]]                        |
| 1 cup                      | [[Sugar]]                                 |
| 1 cup                      | [[Oleo]] (Margarine)                      |
| 1 cup                      | [[Cooking Oil]]                           |
| 2                          | [[Eggs]]                                  |
| 1 teaspoon                 | [[Vanilla]]                               |
| 4 cups + 5 tablespoons     | [[Flour]]                                 |
| 1 teaspoon                 | [[Salt]]                                  |
| 1 teaspoon                 | Baking [[Soda]]                           |
| 1 teaspoon                 | [[Cream of Tartar]]                       |
| As Needed                  | [[Cinnamon]] & [[Sugar]] for topping      |
___
## Instructions
1.  Preheat the oven to 375°F.
2.  In a large bowl, mix all ingredients together well to form a dough.
3.  Form the dough into small balls.
4.  Place the balls on a cookie sheet and mash them flat (with the bottom of a glass, for example).
5.  Sprinkle the tops of the cookies with a mixture of cinnamon and sugar.
6.  Bake for 10 minutes.
___

>[!Note]
>The final instruction on the card is simply: "Then eat."
