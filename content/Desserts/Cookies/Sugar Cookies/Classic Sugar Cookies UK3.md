## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
| 1 Cup               | Shortening                    |
| 2 Cups              | Sugar                         |
| 2/3 Cup             | Milk                          |
| 3                   | Eggs                          |
| 1 Tablespoon        | Vanilla                       |
| 2 Teaspoons         | Baking Powder                  |
| 1 Teaspoon         | Baking Soda                   |
| Pinch               | Salt                          |
| To Doughy Consistency | Flour|
___
### Instructions
1. Cream ingredients.
2. Add flour until doughy consistency.
3. Bake 6-8 minutes at 400°F
