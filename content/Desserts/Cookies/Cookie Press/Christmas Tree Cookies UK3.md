## Recipe
| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
| 1 cup        | [[Sugar]]                   |
| 1 cup        | [[Shortening]]              |
| 3/4 cup      | [[Shortening]]              |
| 1            | [[Egg]]                     |
| 2 1/4 cups + 1/8 cup | [[Flour]]               |
| 1/8 teaspoon  | [[Baking Powder]]           |
| 1 teaspoon    | [[Almond Extract]]          |
___
### Instructions
1. Cream sugar & shortening.
2. Add egg, sifted dry ingredients & flavoring.
3. Mix well.
4. Use cookie press.
5. Bake 375°F for 10-12 minutes.
