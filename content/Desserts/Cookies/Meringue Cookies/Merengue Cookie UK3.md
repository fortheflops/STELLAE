## Recipe
| Measurements | Ingredients                  |
| :----------- | ---------------------------- |
| 2            | [[Egg Whites]], beaten stiff |
| 2/3 cup      | [[Sugar]]                    |
| 1 teaspoon   | [[Vanilla]]                  |
| 1 cup        | Chopped [[Nuts]]             |
| 1-6 oz.      | [[Chocolate Chips]]          |

___
### Instructions
1. Beat eggs whites until very stiff.
2. Add sugar & vanilla.
3. Add nuts & choc. chips.
4. Drop on lightly greased cookie sheet.
5. Pre-heat oven to 350°F.
6. Turn off.
7. Put cookie into oven.
8. Leave overnight.
