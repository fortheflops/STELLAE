## Instructions
1.  Preheat oven to 325°F.
2.  Cream together $\frac{3}{4}$ cup of butter, $\frac{1}{3}$ cup of sifted confectioners' sugar, one teaspoon of vanilla, and $\frac{1}{4}$ teaspoon of salt until light and fluffy.
3.  Add two cups of sifted all-purpose flour and $\frac{1}{2}$ cup of finely chopped almonds; mix well.
4.  Use a teaspoon of dough to roll around each whole candied cherry, completely enclosing it.
5.  Place the dough-covered cherries on an ungreased baking sheet.
6.  Bake in the 325°F oven for 20 minutes.
7.  Cool slightly on the baking sheet.
8.  While still warm, roll each ball in additional sifted confectioners' sugar.
___

>[!Note]
>This recipe makes 4 dozen cookies.
