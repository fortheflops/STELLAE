## Recipe
### Cookies

| Measurements        | Ingredients                |
| :------------------ | -------------------------- |
| 1/2 Cup             | Crisco or Butter           |
| 1                   | Egg Yolk                   |
| 1/4 Cup             | Brown Sugar                |
| 1/2 Teaspoon        | Vanilla                    |
| 1 Cup               | Flour                      |
| 1/4 Teaspoon        | Salt                       |
| 1                   | Egg White, slightly beaten |
| 3/4 Cup             | Finely Chopped Nuts        |

### Icing
| Measurements        | Ingredients                |
| :------------------ | -------------------------- |
| 1 (8 Ounce) Package | Cream Cheese               |
| 1/2 Stick           | Oleo (Margarine)           |
| 1 Teaspoon.         | Vanilla                    |
| 1 Box               | Sifted Powdered Sugar      |
___
### Instructions
#### Cookies
1.  Mix thoroughly Crisco (or butter), egg yolk, brown sugar, and vanilla.
2.  Stir in flour and salt.
3.  Roll into 1-inch balls and dip in slightly beaten egg white.
4. Roll in finely chopped nuts
5.  Place 1 inch apart on a baking sheet.
6.  Press finger into the center of each cookie.
7.  Bake until set at 350°F.  (The original doesn't give a time, but thumbprint cookies typically bake for 8-12 minutes. Watch them carefully.)
#### Icing
1. Combine Ingredients
