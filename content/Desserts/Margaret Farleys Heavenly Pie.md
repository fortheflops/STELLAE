# Margaret Farley's Heavenly Pie

> 📜 **Collection:** UK15 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Margaret Farley | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic no-bake dessert featuring a light and fluffy filling of cream cheese, pineapple, coconut, and pecans in a creamy, tangy base. This delightful vintage recipe makes two pies.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | None | 4-6 hours | Two 9-inch pies |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large bowl
* Electric mixer
* Rubber spatula

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 | [[Graham Cracker Crusts]] | 9-inch |
| 8 ounces | [[Cream Cheese]] | softened |
| 1 can | [[Eagle Brand Sweetened Condensed Milk]] | standard size |
| 1 large can | [[Pineapple]] | 20 oz, crushed, well-drained |
| 1 (8 ounce) container | [[Cool Whip]] | thawed |
| 1/3 cup | [[Lemon Juice]] | fresh or bottled |
| 1 cup | [[Coconut]] | sweetened or unsweetened flakes |
| 1 cup | [[Pecans]] | chopped |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mix the Base
1. In a large bowl, beat the softened **8 ounces cream cheese**, **1 can Eagle Brand Sweetened Condensed Milk**, and **1/3 cup lemon juice** with an electric mixer until smooth and creamy.

### Step 2: Fold the Ingredients
2. Gently fold in the thawed **1 (8 ounce) container Cool Whip** until no streaks remain.
3. Fold in the well-drained **1 large can crushed pineapple**, **1 cup coconut**, and **1 cup chopped pecans** until just combined.

### Step 3: Assemble and Chill
4. Divide the filling mixture evenly between the **2 graham cracker crusts** and spread smoothly.
5. Refrigerate for at least 4-6 hours, or overnight, until the pies are firm and well chilled before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This no-bake pie is perfect for making ahead and can be stored in the refrigerator for up to 3 days until fully set and ready to serve.
