# Blueberry Banana Pie

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A delightful no-bake style pie featuring a rich cream cheese and whipped topping filling layered with fresh bananas and sweet fruit filling. Perfect for gatherings, this recipe yields two delicious pies with creamy textures and fruity finishes.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 0 mins | 2 hours | 2 pies |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Electric mixer
* Pie pans

---
## Ingredients

### Filling and Crust
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | [[8 oz pkg. Cream Cheese]] | Softened |
| 1 C | [[Sugar]] | None |
| 1 lg. box | [[Dream Whip]] | Prepared according to box directions |
| 4 | [[Banana]] | Sliced |
| 2 | [[Baked Pie Shells]] | Cooled |
| 1 can | [[Prepared Blueberry Pie Filling]] | Can also use cherry |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation of Filling
1. Soften the **1 pkg. cream cheese**, then cream together with **1 C sugar** until smooth.
2. Prepare the **1 lg. box Dream Whip** according to the directions on the package.

### Step 2: Assembly
3. Add the **cream cheese mixture** to the prepared **Dream Whip** and mix well.
4. Slice the **4 bananas** directly into the **2 baked pie shells**.
5. Pour the whipped mixture over the **bananas** until evenly distributed.
6. Top with **1 can prepared blueberry pie filling** and chill until set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Keep refrigerated until ready to serve; best enjoyed within 24 hours of assembly.
