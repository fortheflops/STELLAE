# Blueberry Banana Pie

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Mary Lanley | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A delightful no-bake style dessert featuring a creamy cream cheese and Dream Whip filling layered with fresh bananas and sweet blueberry pie filling. This recipe makes two wonderful pies to share with family and friends.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 0 mins | 2 hours | 2 pies |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Electric mixer
* Pie pans

---
## Ingredients

### Filling and Crust
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | [[Cream Cheese]] | 8 oz pkg, softened |
| 1 cup | [[Sugar]] | None |
| 1 large | [[Dream Whip]] | box, prepared according to package directions |
| 4 | [[Bananas]] | Sliced |
| 2 | [[Pie Shells]] | Baked and cooled |
| 1 can | [[Blueberry Pie Filling]] | Prepared |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparing the Filling
1. Soften the **1 cream cheese** and cream together with the **1 cup sugar** until smooth.
2. Prepare the **1 large Dream Whip** according to directions on the package.
3. Add the cream cheese mixture to the prepared Dream Whip and beat until light and fluffy.

### Step 2: Assembling the Pies
4. Slice the **4 bananas** directly onto the bottom of the **2 baked pie shells**.
5. Pour the whipped cream cheese mixture evenly over the sliced bananas.
6. Top each pie with the **1 can prepared blueberry pie filling**, spreading gently to cover the cream layer until set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Keep refrigerated until ready to serve. Best enjoyed within 24 hours of assembly.
