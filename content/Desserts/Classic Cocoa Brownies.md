# Classic Cocoa Brownies

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A rich and fudgy one-bowl brownie recipe featuring a simple cocoa powder base. These timeless treats are perfect for quick baking and deliver a deep chocolate flavor.*

### 🔪 Key Equipment

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |

| 1 cup | [[Sugar]] | granulated |
| 6 tbsp | [[Cocoa Powder]] | unsweetened |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] | large |
| 3/4 cup | [[Flour]] | all-purpose |
| 1/2 tsp | [[Baking Powder]] | none |
| Pinch | [[Salt]] | none |
| 1 tsp | [[Vanilla Extract]] | none |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation
1. Preheat your oven to **325°F**. Grease and flour a **9x9 inch square baking pan**.

### Step 2: Mixing the Batter
2. In a medium mixing bowl, combine the **1/2 cup + 2 tbsp margarine**, **1 cup sugar**, and **6 tbsp cocoa powder**. Stir until well combined.
3. Beat in the **2 eggs**, one at a time.
4. In a separate small bowl, whisk together the **3/4 cup flour**, **pinch salt**, and **1/2 tsp baking powder**.
5. Add the dry ingredients to the wet mixture and stir with a spatula until just combined; do not overmix.
6. Stir in the **1 tsp vanilla extract**.

### Step 3: Bake and Cool
7. Pour the batter into the prepared pan and spread it evenly.
8. Bake for **30 minutes**. The brownies are done when a wooden toothpick inserted near the center comes out with moist crumbs attached.
9. Let the brownies cool completely in the pan on a wire rack before cutting into squares.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 3 days or freeze for up to 2 months.
