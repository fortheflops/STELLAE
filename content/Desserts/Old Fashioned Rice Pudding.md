# Old Fashioned Rice Pudding

> 📜 **Collection:** UK15 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic baked rice pudding featuring cooked rice in a sweet egg custard flavored with lemon and raisins, topped with buttery bread crumbs. Perfect for a comforting homemade dessert.*

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 minutes | 45 minutes | None | 4-6 servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large bowl
* Small bowl
* 1-quart baking dish

| 1 1/2 cups | [[Milk]] | cold or room temperature |
| 1/4 cup | [[Sugar]] | granulated |
| 1 tablespoon | [[Butter]] | soft, or margarine |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Vanilla]] | extract |
| 1/8 teaspoon | [[Salt]] | fine |

| 1 teaspoon | [[Lemon Juice]] | freshly squeezed |

### For the Topping
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3 tablespoons | [[Bread Crumbs]] | soft |
| 2 tablespoons | [[Butter]] | melted, or margarine |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Mix Custard
1. Preheat the oven to **375°F** (**190°C**).
2. In a large bowl, combine the **2 large eggs**, **1 1/2 cups milk**, **1/4 cup sugar**, **1 tablespoon soft butter**, **1 teaspoon vanilla**, **1/8 teaspoon salt**, **2 cups cooked rice**, **1/3 cup raisins**, **1/2 teaspoon lemon rind**, and **1 teaspoon lemon juice**. Mix well until fully incorporated.

### Step 2: Assemble and Bake
3. Pour the rice mixture into a greased **1-quart baking dish**.
4. In a small bowl, toss the **3 tablespoons soft bread crumbs** with the **2 tablespoons melted butter** until thoroughly combined.
5. Sprinkle the buttered bread crumbs evenly over the top of the pudding.
6. Bake for **40 to 45 minutes**, or until a knife inserted near the center comes out clean and the pudding is set and golden brown.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftover baked rice pudding covered in the refrigerator for up to 3 days. Reheat gently before serving if desired.
