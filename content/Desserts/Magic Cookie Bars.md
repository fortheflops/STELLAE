# Magic Cookie Bars

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic, easy-to-make layered bar cookie featuring a buttery graham cracker crust topped with coconut, chocolate, and nuts. These bars are drenched in sweetened condensed milk and baked until golden and gooey.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 25 mins | 30 mins | 24–36 bars |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 13x9x2 inch baking pan
* Wire cooling rack
* Oven

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 Cup (1 stick) | [[Butter]] or [[Margarine]] | melted |
| 1 1/2 Cups | [[Graham Cracker Crumbs]] | none |
| 1 Can (3.5 oz) | [[Coconut Flakes]] | none |
| 1 pkg (6 oz) | [[Semi-Sweet Chocolate Chips]] | none |
| 1 Cup | [[Nuts]] | chopped |
| 1 Can (14 oz) | [[Sweetened Condensed Milk]] | none |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Crust
1. Preheat your oven to 375°F.
2. Place the **1/2 cup butter or margarine** in a 13x9x2 inch baking pan and place in the oven until melted.
3. Remove the pan from the oven and sprinkle the **1 1/2 cups graham cracker crumbs** evenly over the butter, pressing down firmly to form a crust.

### Step 2: Layer and Bake
4. Layer the **1 can coconut flakes**, **1 pkg semi-sweet chocolate chips**, and **1 cup chopped nuts** evenly over the crust.
5. Pour the **1 can sweetened condensed milk** evenly over the top of the layers.
6. Bake for **25 minutes** until the edges are lightly browned and the center is set.
7. Let the pan cool completely on a wire rack until firm before cutting into bars.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 5 days or freeze for up to 3 months.
