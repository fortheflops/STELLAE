## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing and Baking
1. Add **ingredients** to **creamed mixture**, blending well after each addition using low speed.
2. Divide and pour into two prepared **layer pans**.
3. Bake until golden and springy to the touch, then let cool completely before frosting.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Baked cake layers can be wrapped tightly and frozen for up to a month before thawing and frosting.
