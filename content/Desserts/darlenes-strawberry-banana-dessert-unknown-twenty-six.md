# Darlene's Strawberry Banana Dessert

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Darlene | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A delightful frozen dessert recipe featuring cream cheese, strawberries, and bananas from Darlene's kitchen. Perfect for a sweet treat that can be made ahead and frozen until ready to serve.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | None | Hours (freezing) | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Freezer-safe container

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 8 oz | [[Cream Cheese]] | Softened |
| 3/4 cup | [[Sugar]] | None |
| 10 oz | [[Strawberries]] | Undrained |
| 2 | [[Bananas]] | Diced |
| 1/2 cup | [[Nuts]] | Chopped |
| 1 (20 oz) can | [[Pineapple]] | Drained |
| 1 large tub (8 oz) | [[Cool Whip]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing and Freezing
1. Blend the **8 oz cream cheese** and **3/4 cup sugar** together until smooth.
2. Mix in the remaining ingredients: **10 oz strawberries**, **2 diced bananas**, **1/2 cup nuts**, **1 (20 oz) can pineapple**, and **1 large cool whip (8 oz)**.
3. Transfer to a container and freeze until set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Set out 15 minutes before serving for the best creamy texture.
