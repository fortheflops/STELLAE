# Chocolate Chip Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Seven | ✍️ **Attribution:** Mabel Nylund | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic layered chocolate chip cake folded with whipped egg whites and topped with a creamy egg yolk frosting and rich chocolate glaze. Perfect for traditional celebrations and dessert lovers.*

## Instructions

### Baking the Cake
1. Preheat oven to 350°F. Grease round cake pans and line the bottoms with wax paper.
2. In a large bowl, cream **1/2 cup butter** and **1/2 cup sugar** until light and fluffy.
3. Sift together **2 cups sifted flour** and **3 tsp baking powder**. Add to the creamed mixture alternating with **1 cup milk**.
4. Stir in **1 tsp vanilla extract** and **2/3 cup coarsely shaven bitter chocolate**.
5. In a separate clean bowl, beat **2 egg whites** until stiff peaks form, then beat in the remaining **1/2 cup sugar**. Gently fold the whipped egg whites into the cake batter.
6. Pour evenly into the prepared cake tins. Bake at 350°F for 30 minutes until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean. Turn out onto wire racks to cool completely.

### Frosting & Finishing
7. For the frosting, cream **1/2 cup butter** and work in **1 1/2 cups powdered sugar** until smooth and fluffy.
8. Beat **2 egg yolks** until light and thick, then thoroughly mix into the sugar and butter mixture.
9. Spread the creamy frosting evenly over the cooled cake layers.
10. In a small saucepan over low heat, melt **1/2 pkg chocolate chips** with **2 tbsp water** and **1 tbsp butter** until glossy and smooth.
11. Pour the warm melted chocolate glaze over the frosted cake before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the frosted cake covered in the refrigerator for up to 3 days; bring to room temperature before serving.
