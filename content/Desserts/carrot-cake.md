## Instructions

### Baking Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 350°F. Grease a loaf pan.
2. In a large bowl, combine **2 eggs**, **3/4 cup oil**, and **1 cup brown sugar** until smooth.
3. Stir in **2 cups raw grated carrots**, **3/4 cup raisins**, **1 tsp vanilla**, and **1/2 cup nutmeats**.
4. Fold in **2 cups flour**, **1 tsp baking soda**, and **2 tsp baking powder** until just combined.
5. Pour batter into the prepared loaf pan and bake for **1 hour**, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.
6. For the topping, beat **2-3 oz cream cheese**, **3 tbsp margarine**, **1 tsp vanilla**, **1 tbsp milk**, and **1/2 cup powdered sugar** until light and fluffy; spread over cooled cake.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The cake can be baked a day in advance and stored at room temperature; frost just before serving.
