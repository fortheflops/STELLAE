# Grandma Opie's Apple Pie

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Nine | ✍️ **Attribution:** Grandma Opie | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic homemade apple pie featuring tart Granny Smith apples and a rich spiced sugar syrup baked in a double crust. This comforting dessert offers a deliciously flaky pastry paired with tender, spiced fruit.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 25 mins | 1 hr | None | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Saucepan
* Pie pan

---
## Ingredients

### Filling and Syrup
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 cup | [[Unsalted butter]] | Unsalted |
| 3 tbsp | [[Flour]] | All-purpose |
| 1/4 cup | [[Water]] | None |
| 1/2 cup | [[White sugar]] | Granulated |
| 1/2 cup | [[Packed brown sugar]] | Packed |
| 8 | [[Granny Smith apples]] | Peeled, cored, and sliced |

### Pastry
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | [[9" double-crust pie]] | Prepared for a 9 inch pie |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Syrup Preparation
1. Melt **1/2 cup unsalted butter** in a saucepan. Stir in **3 tbsp flour** to make a paste.
2. Add **1/4 cup water**, **1/2 cup white sugar**, and **1/2 cup packed brown sugar** and bring to a boil. Reduce temp and let simmer.

### Step 2: Assembly and Baking
3. Place bottom crust in pie pan. Fill with **8 Granny Smith apples** - mound slightly. Cover with lattice top crust.
4. Gently pour sugar mixture over crust (pour slowly so it doesn't run out).
5. Bake at 425°F for **15 min**. Reduce oven temp to 350°F. Continue baking **35-45 min** until apples are tender.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the pie and refrigerate unbaked for up to a few hours before pouring the hot sugar syrup and baking.
