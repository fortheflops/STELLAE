# Noodle Pudding

> 📜 **Collection:** Jill Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic sweet noodle pudding (kugel) featuring a rich custard base of sour cream and cottage cheese, brightened with citrus and raisins, and finished with a golden cinnamon-sugar butter topping.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 25 mins | 1 hour | None | 10-12 servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large baking dish (9x13 inch)
* Large mixing bowl
* Electric mixer
* Saucepan
* Colander

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 package (approx. 12 oz) | [[Egg Noodles]] | Very fine, cooked and drained |
| 6 large | [[Eggs]] | Separated |
| 1 pint (2 cups) | [[Sour Cream]] | Cold |
| 1 pint (2 cups) | [[Cottage Cheese]] | Cold |
| 3/4 cup | [[Sugar]] | Divided |
| 1/2 | [[Lemon]] | Zested and juiced |
| 1/2 | [[Orange]] | Zested and juiced |
| 1/2 cup | [[Raisins]] | White raisins, or to taste |
| As needed | [[Butter]] | For buttering the dish and topping |
| As needed | [[Cinnamon]] | For topping |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Noodles
1. Preheat your oven to **375°F (190°C)**. Generously butter a large 9x13-inch baking dish.
2. Cook the **1 package egg noodles** according to package directions until al dente. Drain well in a colander.

### Step 2: Custard Base
3. In a large bowl, beat the **6 egg yolks**. Then, beat in the **3/4 cup sugar**, **lemon juice**, and **orange juice** until smooth.
4. Stir the **1 pint sour cream**, **1 pint cottage cheese**, **lemon zest**, and **orange zest** into the egg yolk mixture until fully combined.
5. Gently fold the cooked noodles and **1/2 cup white raisins** into the cheese mixture.

### Step 3: Egg Whites and Assembly
6. In a separate, clean bowl, beat the **6 egg whites** with an electric mixer until stiff peaks form.
7. Carefully fold the beaten **egg whites** into the noodle mixture in two or three additions until just combined.
8. Pour the final mixture into the prepared baking dish and spread evenly.

### Step 4: Topping and Baking
9. Dot the top with small pieces of **butter** and sprinkle generously with a mixture of **sugar** and **cinnamon**.
10. Bake for **1 hour**, or until the pudding is set firmly in the center and the top is golden brown and fragrant.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** You can assemble the pudding up to the point of folding in the beaten egg whites and keep it covered in the refrigerator until you are ready to bake.
