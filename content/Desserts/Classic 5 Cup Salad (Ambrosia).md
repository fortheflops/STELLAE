# Classic 5 Cup Salad (Ambrosia)

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A nostalgic and simple fruit salad featuring a perfect balance of five classic ingredients. This creamy, chilled dessert is a timeless potluck favorite that melds beautifully overnight.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 0 mins | 4 hours | 6–8 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large mixing bowl
* Silicone spatula

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 Cup | [[Pineapple]] | drained, chunk or crushed |
| 1 Cup | [[Mandarin Oranges]] | canned, drained |
| 1 Cup | [[Marshmallows]] | miniature |
| 1 Cup | [[Coconut]] | flaked |
| 1 Cup | [[Sour Cream]] | chilled |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Combine and Chill
1. In a large bowl, gently fold together the **1 cup pineapple**, **1 cup mandarin oranges**, **1 cup marshmallows**, **1 cup coconut**, and **1 cup sour cream** until evenly coated.
2. Cover the bowl tightly and refrigerate for at least **4 hours** or overnight to allow the flavors to meld and the **marshmallows** to soften into a creamy texture.
3. Stir gently once more before serving chilled.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This salad is best when made at least 4 hours in advance and will stay fresh in the refrigerator for up to one week.
