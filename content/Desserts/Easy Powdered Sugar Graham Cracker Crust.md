# Easy Powdered Sugar Graham Cracker Crust

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A quick and easy no-bake graham cracker crust made with powdered sugar for an extra-smooth texture. The perfect base for your favorite cream pies and cheesecakes.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 0 mins | 1 hour | One 9-inch pie crust |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Medium bowl
* Fork
* 9-inch pie plate

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 1/2 Cups | [[Graham Cracker Crumbs]] | Crushed |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Powdered Sugar]] | Sifted |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Butter]] | Melted |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mix the Crust
1. In a medium bowl, combine the **1 1/2 cups graham cracker crumbs** and **1/2 cup powdered sugar**.

### Step 2: Add Butter and Press
2. Pour in the melted **1/2 cup butter** and mix with a fork until all the crumbs are evenly moistened and the mixture holds together when pressed.
3. Press the mixture firmly and evenly onto the bottom and up the sides of a **9-inch pie plate**.

### Step 3: Chill
4. Chill the crust in the refrigerator for at least 1 hour, or until firm and set, before filling.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the prepared crust in the refrigerator for up to 2 days or freeze for up to 1 month before filling.
