# Margaret's Banana Nut Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Margaret | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A rich, moist, and large-batch banana nut cake featuring six ripe bananas and a tender buttermilk crumb. This dense, flavorful loaf is perfect for sharing and keeps exceptionally well.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 1 hour 30 mins | 20 mins | 1 large loaf or 2 standard loaves |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* large mixing bowl
* electric mixer
* sifter
* large loaf pan or tube pan
* wire cooling rack
* wooden skewer

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 Cup | [[Butter]] | softened |
| 3 Cups | [[Sugar]] | granulated |
| 4 | [[Eggs]] | large |
| 6 | [[Bananas]] | mashed, approx. 3 cups |
| 4 Cups | [[Flour]] | all-purpose |
| 2 teaspoons | [[Baking Soda]] | sifted |
| 1/2 cup | [[Buttermilk]] | room temperature |
| 1 Cup | [[Pecans]] | chopped |
| 2 teaspoons | [[Vanilla]] | extract |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and Mixing
1. Preheat your oven to 350°F. Grease and flour one large loaf/tube pan or two standard 9x5 inch loaf pans.
2. In a large bowl, cream the **1 cup butter** and **3 cups sugar** together with an electric mixer until light and fluffy. Add the **4 eggs** one at a time, beating well after each addition.
3. Stir in the **6 mashed bananas** until fully incorporated.

### Step 2: Combining Ingredients
4. In a separate bowl, sift together the **4 cups flour** and **2 teaspoons baking soda**.
5. Add the dry ingredients to the banana mixture alternately with the **1/2 cup buttermilk**, beginning and ending with the dry ingredients. Mix on low speed until just combined.
6. Fold in the **1 cup chopped pecans** and **2 teaspoons vanilla**.

### Step 3: Baking and Cooling
7. Pour the batter into the prepared pan(s). Bake for **1 1/2 hours** for a single large loaf, or about **1 hour** for two smaller loaves.
8. The cake is done when a long wooden skewer inserted into the center comes out clean and the top is golden brown.
9. Let the cake cool in the pan for **15-20 minutes** before turning it out onto a wire rack to cool completely.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This cake stays moist for several days when wrapped tightly in plastic wrap or stored in an airtight container at room temperature.
