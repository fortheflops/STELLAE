# Carrot Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Mix
1. Mix together the **1 1/2 cups vegetable oil**, **3 cups sugar**, and **5 medium eggs**.
2. Stir in the **3 cups grated carrots** and **12 oz crushed pineapple** into the wet mixture.
3. In a separate bowl, sift together the **4 1/2 cups flour**, **1 1/2 tsp salt**, **1 1/2 tsp baking soda**, **1 1/2 tsp baking powder**, and **1 1/2 tsp cinnamon**.

### Step 2: Combine and Bake
4. Blend the wet and dry mixtures together, then add the **3 tsp vanilla** and **1 1/2 cups walnuts**.
5. Pour the batter into a greased and floured 10x15 pan.
6. Bake at 350°F for **45 mins** until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean and the cake springs back lightly to the touch.

### Step 3: Frosting
7. Beat together the **6 oz cream cheese**, **8 oz margarine**, and **1 lb powdered sugar** until smooth and creamy.
8. Spread the frosting evenly over the cooled cake and sprinkle with the **2 oz grated walnuts**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store frosted cake in the refrigerator for up to 3 days. Bring to room temperature before serving.
