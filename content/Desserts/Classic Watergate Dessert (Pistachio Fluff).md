# Classic Watergate Dessert (Pistachio Fluff)

> 📜 **Collection:** Jill Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic no-bake dessert, also known as Pistachio Fluff, featuring a creamy blend of pistachio pudding, pineapple, and pecans. This simple, retro treat is a perfect crowd-pleasing potluck staple.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 0 mins | 1 hour | 8–10 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large mixing bowl
* Silicone spatula

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 large (20-oz) can | [[Crushed Pineapple]] | well drained |
| 1 large (16-oz) container | [[Cool Whip]] | thawed |
| 1 package (3.4-oz) | [[Pistachio Pudding]] Mix | dry |
| 1 cup | [[Pecans]] | chopped |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Base
1. In a large bowl, combine the **1 package (3.4-oz) pistachio pudding mix** and the **1 large (16-oz) container Cool Whip**. Stir until the powder is fully incorporated and the mixture is smooth and light green.

### Step 2: Fold and Chill
2. Gently fold in the **1 large (20-oz) can crushed pineapple** and the **1 cup pecans** until just combined.
3. Cover and chill for at least one hour before serving to allow the flavors to meld and the texture to set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store covered in the refrigerator for up to 3 days; the texture is best when served chilled.
