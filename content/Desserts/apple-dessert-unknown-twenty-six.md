# Apple Dessert

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A delightful baked apple dessert featuring a spiced apple base topped with a sweet, crumbly oat crust. Perfect served warm with a golden-brown finish.*

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 30 mins | None | 6 servings |

* Baking pan
* Mixing bowl

## Ingredients

### Apple Filling
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 4 cups | [[Apples]] | Sliced |
| 3/4 cup | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 1 tbsp | [[Flour]] | All-purpose |
| 1/2 tsp | [[Cinnamon]] | Ground |
| 1 pinch | [[Salt]] | None |

### Crumble Topping
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 tbsp | [[Dry milk]] | Powdered |
| 3/4 cup | [[Quick oat meal]] | None |
| 1 cup | [[Flour]] | All-purpose |
| 1 cup | [[Brown sugar]] | Packed |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Soda]] | Baking soda |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Baking powder]] | None |
| 3/4 tsp | [[Cinnamon]] | Or less to taste |
| 1/2 cup | [[Butter]] | Melted or softened |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep Filling
1. Put **4 cups sliced apples** in a buttered baking pan.
2. Sprinkle with **3/4 cup sugar**, **1 tbsp flour**, **1 pinch salt**, and **1/2 tsp cinnamon**.

### Step 2: Make Topping and Bake
3. Combine **2 tbsp powdered or dry milk**, **3/4 cup quick oat meal**, **1 cup flour**, **1 cup brown sugar**, **1/4 tsp soda**, **1/4 tsp baking powder**, **3/4 tsp cinnamon**, and **1/2 cup butter**. Mix until crumbly.
4. Pat the crumble mixture evenly on top of the **4 cups sliced apples**.
5. Bake at moderate temperature for about **30 minutes** or until the topping is golden and bubbling.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the dessert in the baking pan ahead of time and refrigerate for a few hours before baking.
