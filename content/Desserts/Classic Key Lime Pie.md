# Classic Key Lime Pie

> 📜 **Collection:** UK15 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic and straightforward recipe for a tangy and creamy baked Key Lime Pie, featuring a simple filling of egg yolks, sweetened condensed milk, and fresh lime juice in a ready-made graham cracker crust.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 35 mins | 4 hours 30 mins | One 8-inch pie |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large bowl
* Electric mixer
* Wire rack

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 (8-inch) | [[Graham Cracker Crust]] | ready-made |
| 4 large | [[Egg Yolks]] | None |
| 1 (14 ounce) can | [[Skim Milk]] | fat-free sweetened condensed |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Lime Peel]] | fresh grated |
| 1/2 cup + 1 tbsp | [[Lime Juice]] | fresh |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Filling
1. Preheat the oven to **350°F**.
2. In a large bowl, beat the **4 large egg yolks** with an electric mixer for about **5 minutes**, until thick and creamy.
3. Add the **1 (14 ounce) can fat-free sweetened condensed skim milk**, **1 teaspoon fresh grated lime peel**, and **1/2 cup + 1 tbsp lime juice**. Beat until well blended and smooth.

### Step 2: Bake and Chill
4. Pour the filling into the **1 (8-inch) ready-made graham cracker crust**.
5. Bake for **30 to 35 minutes**, or until the filling is set with only a slight jiggle in the very center.
6. Let the pie cool on a wire rack for **30 minutes**.
7. Refrigerate for at least **4 hours**, or until completely chilled and firm, before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The pie can be stored, tightly covered, in the refrigerator for up to 4 days.
