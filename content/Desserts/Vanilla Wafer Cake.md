# Vanilla Wafer Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A rich, dense, and wonderfully unique cake that uses an entire box of crushed vanilla wafers instead of flour. This buttery cake is loaded with coconut and nuts, and bakes low and slow for an incredibly moist texture.*

### 🔪 Key Equipment

* Large bowl

* Wire rack
* Wooden skewer

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 sticks (1 cup) | [[Oleo]] | Margarine or Butter, softened |
| 2 cups | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 6 | [[Eggs]] | Whole |
| 1 (12 oz) box | [[Vanilla Wafers]] | Finely crushed |
| 1 (7 oz) bag | [[Coconut]] | Shredded |
| 1 cup | [[Nuts]] | Chopped |
| 1/2 cup | [[Milk]] | Liquid |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and Creaming
1. Preheat your oven to a low **275°F**. Generously grease and flour a tube or Bundt pan. Finely crush the **1 (12 oz) box vanilla wafers** using a food processor or by placing them in a bag and crushing them with a rolling pin.
2. In a large bowl, cream the **2 sticks (1 cup) oleo** (or butter) and **2 cups sugar** together with an electric mixer until light and fluffy.
3. Add the **6 eggs** one at a time, beating well after each addition until smooth.

### Step 2: Mixing and Baking
4. By hand, stir in the crushed **vanilla wafers**, **1 (7 oz) bag coconut**, **1/2 cup milk**, and **1 cup chopped nuts** until everything is well combined and the batter is very thick.
5. Spoon the batter into the prepared pan and spread it evenly.
6. Bake for **1 hour and 15 minutes**, or until a long wooden skewer inserted into the center comes out clean and the cake is golden brown and fragrant.
7. Let the cake cool in the pan for **15-20 minutes** before turning it out onto a wire rack to cool completely.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the cooled cake wrapped tightly in plastic wrap at room temperature for up to 3 days or refrigerate for up to a week.
