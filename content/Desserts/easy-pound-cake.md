## Instructions

### Mixing and Baking
1. In a large mixing bowl, combine **3 cups flour**, **1 3/4 cups sugar**, **1 1/2 tsp salt**, **2 tsp baking powder**, **2 tsp vanilla**, **1 cup Crisco**, **3/4 cup milk**, and **2 eggs**.
2. Beat the mixture on speed #3 for 2 minutes until smooth.
3. Add the remaining **2 eggs** and beat for an additional 2 minutes until fully incorporated.
4. Pour batter into a greased loaf or tube pan and bake at 375°F for 1 hour, or until a tester comes out clean and the top is golden brown.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The batter can be divided into smaller loaf pans and frozen before baking. You may also cut the recipe in half for a smaller cake.
