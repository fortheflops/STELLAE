# Butterscotch Squares

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *Rich and chewy butterscotch squares loaded with walnuts and baked to golden perfection. A classic handheld treat that is simple to prepare and easy to slice.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 20 mins | 10 mins | 24 squares |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 9x13 pan
* Mixing bowl
* Saucepan

---
## Ingredients

### Batter Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 cups | [[Brown Sugar]] | Packed |
| 1/2 cup | [[Butter]] | Softened |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] | Whole |
| 1 1/2 cups | [[Flour]] | All-purpose |
| 2 tsp | [[Baking Powder]] | Level |
| 1 pinch | [[Salt]] | To taste |
| 1 tsp | [[Vanilla]] | Extract |
| 1 cup | [[Walnuts]] | Chopped |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing and Boiling
1. Mix **2 cups brown sugar** and **1/2 cup butter**, bring to a boil, then let cool slightly.

### Step 2: Combining
2. Add **2 eggs** one at a time, beating well after each addition.
3. Stir in the dry ingredients including **1 1/2 cups flour**, **2 tsp baking powder**, and **salt**, followed by **1 tsp vanilla** and **1 cup walnuts**.

### Step 3: Baking
4. Pour the batter into a lined 9x13 pan.
5. Bake at 375 degrees for **20 minutes** until golden and set.
6. Cool in pan before cutting into **1 1/2 inch squares**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 3 days.
