# Reba's Kentucky Pecan Pie

> 📜 **Collection:** General Archive | ✍️ **Attribution:** Reba | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic Kentucky Pecan Pie featuring a rich, deep caramel-like filling made with dark brown sugar and a crunchy, toasted pecan topping. It is an irresistible holiday dessert that sets into a decadent treat as it cools.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 45 mins | 2 hours | One 9-inch pie |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large mixing bowl
* Wire whisk
* 9-inch pie pan
* Baking sheet
* Wire cooling rack
* Aluminum foil

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 (9-inch) | [[Pie Shell]] | Unbaked |
| 1 Cup | [[Corn Syrup]] | White |
| 1 Cup | [[Brown Sugar]] | Dark, packed |
| 1/3 tsp | [[Salt]] | None |
| 1/3 Cup | [[Butter]] | Melted, or margarine |
| 1 tsp | [[Vanilla Extract]] | None |
| 3 | [[Eggs]] | Slightly beaten |
| 1 heaping Cup | [[Pecans]] | Shelled |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Mix Filling
1. Preheat oven to **350°F**. Place the unbaked **pie shell** on a baking sheet to catch any drips.
2. In a large bowl, combine the **white corn syrup**, **dark brown sugar**, **salt**, melted **butter** (or margarine), and **vanilla extract**. Mix well until smooth.
3. Stir in the slightly beaten **eggs** until thoroughly combined with the syrup mixture.

### Step 2: Assemble and Bake
4. Pour the filling directly into the unbaked **pie shell**.
5. Sprinkle the **shelled pecans** evenly over the top of the filling.
6. Bake for approximately **45 minutes**, or until the edges are set and the center is slightly jiggly. If the crust or pecans begin to over-brown, loosely tent the pie with aluminum foil.

### Step 3: Cool and Serve
7. Let the pie cool completely on a wire rack for at least **2 hours** before serving to allow the filling to fully set into a rich, decadent consistency.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the baked pie covered in the refrigerator for up to 3 days. Bring to room temperature or warm slightly before serving.
