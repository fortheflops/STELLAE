# Fool-Proof Cream Cheese Pie Crust

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *Discover a wonderfully simple and tender pie crust made with cream cheese and margarine instead of water. This creates an incredibly rich, flaky, and easy-to-handle dough.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | Varies | 30 mins | One double-crust 8-inch pie |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Medium bowl
* Fork
* Plastic wrap
* Rolling pin
* 8-inch pie pan

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 1/2 cups | [[Flour]] | All-purpose |
| 1/3 cup | [[Margarine]] | Room temperature |
| 3 Ounces | [[Cream Cheese]] | Room temperature |
| 1/4 teaspoon | [[Salt]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Blend Base Ingredients
1. In a medium bowl, use a fork to blend the room temperature **1/3 cup margarine** and **3 ounces cream cheese** together until they are well blended.

### Step 2: Form the Dough
2. Add the **1 1/2 cups flour** and **1/4 teaspoon salt** to the bowl and continue to mix with the fork until a soft dough forms.

### Step 3: Chill the Dough
3. Gather the dough into a ball, divide in half, and shape each half into a flat disk. Wrap in plastic and chill for at least **30 minutes** for easier rolling.

### Step 4: Roll and Bake
4. Roll out on a floured surface to fit your pie pan. For a filled, double-crust pie, place the top crust and slit it. Bake at **400°F** until the crust is quite brown, then reduce the oven heat to **350°F** and continue baking until the filling is cooked through and bubbling.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The dough can be made ahead, wrapped tightly in plastic, and stored in the refrigerator for up to 3 days or frozen for later use.
