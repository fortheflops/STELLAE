# Orange Slice Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** Elizabeth Knee Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic Southern holiday masterpiece, this dense and incredibly moist cake is packed with chopped orange slice candies, dates, nuts, and coconut, then soaked overnight in a bright citrus glaze.*

## Ingredients

### Cake Batter
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 cup (2 sticks) | [[Butter]] | softened |
| 2 cups | [[Sugar]] | None |
| 4 | [[Eggs]] | None |
| 1 tsp | [[Baking Soda]] | None |
| 1/2 cup | [[Buttermilk]] | None |

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 cup | [[Orange Juice]] | freshly squeezed |
| Juice of 1 | [[Lemon]] | freshly squeezed |
| 2 cups | [[Powdered Sugar]] | None |

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Batter Base
1. Cream **1 cup butter** and **2 cups sugar** together in a large mixing bowl until light, fluffy, and smooth.
2. Add **4 eggs** one at a time, beating thoroughly after each addition to ensure a stable emulsion.
3. Dissolve **1 tsp baking soda** directly into **1/2 cup buttermilk**, then stir this mixture into the creamed butter and sugar.

### Step 2: Fold in Fruits and Nuts
4. In a separate bowl, toss **1 lb. chopped dates**, **1 lb. chopped orange slice candies**, and **2 cups chopped nuts** with a small portion of the **3 1/2 cups flour** until evenly coated (this prevents them from sinking during baking).
5. Add the floured fruits and nuts, **1 can flaked coconut**, and the remaining **3 1/2 cups flour** to the creamed mixture, folding gently until just combined.

### Step 3: Bake
6. Grease and flour a large tube pan. Pour the thick batter evenly into the pan.
7. Bake at **250°F** for **2 1/2 to 3 hours** until a toothpick inserted deep into the center comes out clean and the top is golden brown.

### Step 4: Glaze and Set
8. As soon as the cake comes out of the oven, combine **1 cup fresh orange juice**, the **juice of 1 lemon**, and **2 cups powdered sugar** in a bowl, stirring until the sugar is completely dissolved.
9. Pour the warm glaze slowly and evenly over the hot cake while it is still in the pan.
10. Leave the cake in the pan overnight to allow it to fully absorb the glaze and set before slicing.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This cake must be made at least 24 hours before serving to allow the glaze to cure. It keeps beautifully wrapped tightly at room temperature for up to 2 weeks.
