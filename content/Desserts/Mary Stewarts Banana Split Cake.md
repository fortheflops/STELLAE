# Mary Stewart's Banana Split Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** UK5 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Mary Stewart | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A spectacular layered dessert featuring a yellow cake base topped with fresh fruit, creamy pudding, and whipped topping. This nostalgic treat captures all the classic flavors of a banana split in a crowd-pleasing cake.*

---

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

* 9x13 inch pan

* Mixing bowl

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |

| 1 (8-ounce) package | [[Cream Cheese]] | softened |
| 1 1/2 cups | [[Milk]] | cold |
| 1 (4-serving size) package | [[Instant Pudding]] | vanilla flavor |
| 3 medium | [[Bananas]] | sliced |
| 1-2 pints | [[Strawberries]] | fresh, sliced |
| 1 (20-ounce) can | [[Pineapple]] | crushed, drained |
| 1 large container | [[Whipped Topping]] | thawed |
| 1 1/3 cups | [[Coconut]] | shredded |
| Optional | [[Nut Chips]] & [[Chocolate Shavings]] | for garnish |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Base
1. Prepare and bake the **1 package yellow cake mix** in a 9x13 inch pan according to package directions; let cool completely until no longer warm to the touch.

### Step 2: Prepare the Pudding
2. In a medium bowl, beat the **1 package cream cheese** and **1 1/2 cups milk** with an electric mixer until smooth.
3. Add the **1 package instant pudding** and beat for 2 minutes until the mixture is thick and well combined.

### Step 3: Assemble the Layers
4. Arrange the **3 medium bananas** over the cooled cake.
5. Spread the pudding mixture evenly over the bananas.
6. Layer the **1-2 pints strawberries** over the pudding, followed by a layer of **whipped topping**.
7. Spoon the **1 can crushed pineapple** over the topping, then cover with the remaining **whipped topping**.

### Step 4: Garnish and Chill
8. Sprinkle the **1 1/3 cups coconut** and optional **nut chips** and **chocolate shavings** over the top.
9. Chill in the refrigerator for at least 3-4 hours until set and firm before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This cake is best when chilled for at least 4 hours; store leftovers covered in the refrigerator for up to 3 days.
