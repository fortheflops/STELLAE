# Lois' Strawberry Angel Food Dessert

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Lois | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A light and fluffy no-bake dessert featuring layers of crumbled angel food cake and a creamy strawberry gelatin mixture. This classic treat is perfect for gatherings and sets beautifully in the refrigerator.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 0 mins | 4 hours | 12–15 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large mixing bowl
* 9x13 inch pan
* Wire whisk
* Spatula

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 (3 oz) packages | [[Strawberry Jello]] | powdered |
| 3 Cups | [[Water]] | 2 cups hot, 1 cup cold |
| 1 (10 oz) package | [[Frozen Strawberries]] | thawed |
| 1 (8 oz) container | [[Cool Whip]] | thawed |
| 1 large | [[Angel Food Cake]] | store-bought or homemade |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Gelatin Base
1. In a large bowl, dissolve the **2 (3 oz) packages Strawberry Jello** in **2 cups hot water**.
2. Stir in **1 cup cold water** and the **1 (10 oz) package frozen strawberries** with their juice.
3. Refrigerate until the mixture reaches the consistency of unbeaten egg whites, about 1-2 hours.

### Step 2: Assemble and Chill
4. While the gelatin sets, tear or crumble the **1 large angel food cake** into bite-sized pieces and spread evenly in a 9x13 inch pan.
5. Once the gelatin is partially set, use a whisk to break it up, then gently fold in the **1 (8 oz) container Cool Whip** until the mixture is uniform and creamy.
6. Pour the mixture over the **angel food cake** pieces, stirring gently to coat, and smooth the top with a spatula.
7. Refrigerate for at least 3 hours, or until the dessert is completely firm and set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This dessert is best made at least 4 hours in advance to ensure it is fully set; keep refrigerated until ready to serve.
