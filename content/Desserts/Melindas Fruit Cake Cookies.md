# Melinda's Fruit Cake Cookies

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Three | ✍️ **Attribution:** Melinda | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic, large-batch recipe for fruitcake-style drop cookies from Melinda. This buttery, spiced dough is packed with dates and candied fruit, making it perfect for holiday baking and gift-giving.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Cream
1. Preheat your oven to **350°F**.
2. In a very large bowl, cream the **2 cups softened butter** and **3 cups white sugar** together with an electric mixer until light, fluffy, and pale.
3. Beat in the **4 eggs** one at a time, mixing well after each addition.

### Step 2: Mix Dry and Coat Fruit
4. In a separate large bowl, sift together the **5 cups flour** (reserving **1/2 cup**), **2 teaspoons salt**, **2 teaspoons baking soda**, and **2 teaspoons cinnamon**.
5. In another bowl, toss the **1 to 2 pounds chopped dates**, **1/2 cup candied pineapple**, and **1/2 cup candied cherries** with the reserved **1/2 cup flour** until well coated to prevent sinking.

### Step 3: Combine and Bake
6. On low speed, gradually add the remaining dry ingredients to the creamed mixture until a very stiff dough forms.
7. By hand, stir in the floured fruit until it is evenly distributed throughout the dough.
8. Drop by rounded teaspoonfuls onto an ungreased cookie sheet.
9. Bake for **10-12 minutes**, or until the edges are lightly browned and the centers are set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This is a very large recipe that can be halved for a standard batch. Store baked cookies in an airtight container for up to 2 weeks, or freeze for up to 3 months.
