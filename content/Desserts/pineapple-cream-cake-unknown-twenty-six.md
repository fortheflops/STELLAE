# Pineapple Cream Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A delightful and moist poke cake topped with crushed pineapple, creamy vanilla pudding, whipped topping, coconut, and nuts. This crowd-pleasing dessert actually tastes better when made a few days ahead.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 30 mins | 8 hours | 12 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 9x13 inch baking pan
* Mixing bowls
* Whisk

---
## Ingredients

### Cake and Toppings
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 box | [[Yellow or white cake mix]] | Prepared as directed on package |
| 1 (20 oz) can | [[Crushed pineapple]] | With juice |
| 1 (3 oz) box | [[Instant vanilla or coconut cream pudding mix]] | Dry mix |
| 1 cup | [[Milk]] | Cold |
| 1 cup | [[Coconut]] | Flaked |
| 1/2 cup | [[Nuts]] | Chopped |
| 1 (8 oz) tub | [[Cool Whip]] | Thawed |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Baking and Puncturing
1. Bake the **1 box yellow or white cake mix** in a **9x13 inch baking pan** as directed on the package until golden and a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.
2. Puncture the **cake** thoroughly all over as soon as it comes out of the oven while still piping hot.

### Step 2: Assembling and Chilling
3. Spread the **1 (20 oz) can crushed pineapple** with its juice evenly over the **cake** as soon as it is punctured.
4. In a mixing bowl, mix the **1 (3 oz) box instant vanilla or coconut cream pudding mix** and **1 cup milk** well until thickened, then fold in the **1 (8 oz) tub Cool Whip**.
5. Spread the creamy mixture over the cooled **cake**.
6. Sprinkle the top with **1/2 cup nuts** and **1 cup coconut**.
7. Let set overnight in the refrigerator until completely chilled and set; seems to get better if made 3-4 days ahead as it gets moister.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Freezes well, and tastes even better when made 3-4 days ahead of time as it becomes progressively moister.
