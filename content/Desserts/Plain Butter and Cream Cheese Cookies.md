# Plain Butter and Cream Cheese Cookies

> 📜 **Collection:** Jill Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *These tender, tangy drop cookies feature a delicate balance of butter and cream cheese infused with bright orange zest. They are a perfect, soft-textured treat that can be customized with optional chocolate chips.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 15 mins | None | 2-3 dozen cookies |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* large bowl
* electric mixer
* cookie sheet
* sifter
* wire rack

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 cup | [[Butter]] | softened |
| 3 ounces | [[Cream Cheese]] | softened |
| 1/2 cup | [[Sugar]] | granulated |
| 1 | [[Egg]] | large |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Vanilla Extract]] | none |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Orange Extract]] | none |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Orange Rind]] | grated |
| 1 cup | [[All-Purpose Flour]] | none |
| 1/2 teaspoon | [[Salt]] | none |
| 1/2 cup | [[Chocolate Chips]] | optional |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Dough
1. Preheat oven to 350°F and lightly grease a cookie sheet.
2. Sift the **1 cup all-purpose flour** and **1/2 teaspoon salt** together into a bowl.
3. In a separate large bowl, cream together the **1/2 cup butter**, **3 ounces cream cheese**, and **1 teaspoon grated orange rind** until smooth.
4. Add the **1/2 cup sugar**, **1 egg**, **1 teaspoon vanilla extract**, and **1 teaspoon orange extract**. Beat with an electric mixer until the mixture is light and fluffy.
5. Stir in the flour mixture until well combined. If desired, fold in **1/2 cup chocolate chips**.

### Step 2: Bake and Finish
6. Drop dough by the teaspoonful onto the prepared cookie sheet and flatten each mound slightly with the back of a spoon.
7. Bake for **15 minutes** until the edges are set.
8. Optional: If the cookies are not browned to your liking, place them under a preheated broiler for a few seconds, watching very carefully to prevent burning.
9. Let the cookies cool on the sheet for a minute before transferring to a wire rack to cool completely.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 3 days or freeze for up to 1 month.
