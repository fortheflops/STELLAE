# Frozen Passion

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *An incredibly simple, 2-ingredient frozen dessert made by combining any flavor of carbonated beverage with sweetened condensed milk. This refreshing treat is easily churned in an ice cream freezer for a quick, customizable dessert.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5 mins | 25 mins | None | 2 Quarts |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large mixing bowl
* Wire whisk
* 2-quart ice cream maker
* Freezer-safe container

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 (8 oz) bottles | [[Carbonated Beverage]] | Any flavor |
| 2 cans | [[Eagle Brand Sweetened Condensed Milk]] | Standard size |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Combine and Churn
1. In a large bowl, whisk together the **2 (8 oz) bottles carbonated beverage** and **2 cans sweetened condensed milk** until the mixture is smooth and fully incorporated.
2. Pour the mixture into the canister of a **2-quart ice cream maker**.
3. Freeze and churn according to the manufacturer's operating instructions until the mixture reaches a thick, soft-serve consistency.
4. Serve immediately for a soft texture, or transfer to a freezer-safe container and freeze for several hours until firm.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Transfer the churned mixture to a freezer-safe container and freeze for several hours to achieve a firmer, scoopable texture.
