# Cranberry Sauce

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A simple, refreshing frozen dessert salad featuring a blend of tart cranberries, sweet pineapple, and creamy sour cream. This easy-to-assemble treat is perfect for holiday gatherings and can be prepared well in advance.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 0 mins | 4 hours | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Freezer-safe container

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 cup | [[Nuts]] | chopped |
| 1 can | [[Cranberry Sauce]] | whole berry or jellied |
| 1 container | [[Sour Cream]] | standard size |
| 1 can | [[Crushed Pineapple]] | drained |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation
1. In a large mixing bowl, combine the **1 can cranberry sauce**, **1 container sour cream**, and **1 can crushed pineapple**.
2. Fold in the **1 cup nuts** until the mixture is uniform in color and texture.
3. Transfer the mixture to a freezer-safe container and freeze until firm, ensuring it is set throughout.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This dish must be made ahead as it requires freezing time; keep stored in the freezer until ready to serve.
