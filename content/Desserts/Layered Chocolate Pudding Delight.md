# Layered Chocolate Pudding Delight

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A decadent layered dessert featuring a buttery pecan crust, a sweet cream cheese layer, and a rich chocolate-vanilla pudding topping. This classic lush is a perfect make-ahead treat for any gathering.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Crust
1. Preheat oven to 350°F. Combine **1 cup flour**, **1 stick oleo**, and **1 cup ground pecans**. Pat firmly into a 9x13 inch pan and bake for **20 minutes** until lightly golden. Let cool completely.

### Step 2: Assemble Layers
2. In a bowl, beat **1 package cream cheese** and **1 cup powdered sugar** until smooth; fold in **1 cup Cool Whip**. Spread evenly over the cooled crust.
3. In a separate bowl, whisk **1 package chocolate pudding mix**, **1 package vanilla pudding mix**, and **3 cups cold milk** for **2 minutes** until the mixture begins to thicken. Pour over the cream cheese layer.
4. Spread the remaining **Cool Whip** evenly over the pudding layer.

### Step 3: Chill
5. Cover and chill in the refrigerator for at least **4 hours** until firm and set before slicing.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This dessert is best when chilled for at least 4 hours, but can be made up to 24 hours in advance. Keep covered in the refrigerator until ready to serve.
