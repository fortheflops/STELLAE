# Coffee Penuche

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *Indulge in a rich, traditional candy featuring aromatic coffee, brown sugar, and chopped nuts. This classic sweet treat melts in your mouth with comforting caramel-like coffee notes.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 20 mins | 30 mins | 24 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Saucepan
* Candy thermometer
* Baking pan

---
## Ingredients

### Candy Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 cup | [[Brown sugar]] | Packed |
| 1 cup | [[White sugar]] | Granulated |
| 1/4 cup | [[Cream or milk]] | Liquid |
| 1/2 cup | [[Coffee]] | Brewed |
| 1 tbsp | [[Butter]] | Softened |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Salt]] | Standard |
| 1 cup | [[Nuts]] | Chopped |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Combining and Boiling
1. In a heavy saucepan, combine **1 cup brown sugar**, **1 cup white sugar**, **1/4 cup cream or milk**, **1/2 cup coffee**, and **1/4 tsp salt**.
2. Cook over medium heat, stirring constantly until the sugars are completely dissolved.

### Step 2: Finishing and Setting
3. Continue cooking without stirring until the mixture reaches the soft-ball stage on a candy thermometer.
4. Remove from heat, add **1 tbsp butter**, and let it cool without stirring until lukewarm.
5. Beat vigorously until the mixture thickens and loses its gloss, then quickly stir in **1 cup nuts** before pouring into a greased pan to set until firm.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature with wax paper between layers for up to two weeks.
