# Frozen Fruit Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A refreshing, creamy frozen fruit salad featuring a blend of fruit cocktail, grapes, and crunchy walnuts. This simple chilled treat is perfect for warm days and easy to prepare ahead of time.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 0 mins | 4 hours | 6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Whisk
* Freezer container

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 cup | [[Sour Cream]] | Thick |
| 2/3 cup | [[Mayonnaise]] | None |
| 1 can (20 oz) | [[Fruit Cocktail]] | Drained |
| 1 can (8 oz) | [[Grapes]] | Spiced, drained |
| 1/2 cup | [[Walnuts]] | Coarsely broken |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation
1. Whip the **1 cup sour cream** in a bowl until stiff peaks form.
2. Gently fold in the **2/3 cup mayonnaise**, **1 can (20 oz) fruit cocktail**, **1 can (8 oz) spiced grapes**, and **1/2 cup walnuts** until well combined.
3. Pour the mixture into a refrigerator tray or freezer container.
4. Freeze until the mixture is firm to the touch.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This salad can be kept frozen for up to two weeks; ensure it is stored in an airtight container to prevent ice crystals.
