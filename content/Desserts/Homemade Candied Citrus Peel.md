# Homemade Candied Citrus Peel

> 📜 **Collection:** Patsy Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic recipe for transforming fresh orange and lemon peels into sweet, translucent candied strips. These sugary treats are perfect for snacking or as a flavorful addition to holiday baked goods.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 1 hour 30 mins | Overnight | Approx. 2 cups |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large pot
* Saucepan
* Spoon
* Knife
* Cutting board
* Wax paper

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3 | [[Oranges]] | Large, peeled in strips |
| 2 | [[Lemons]] | Large, peeled in strips |
| 1 Cup | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 3 Tbsp | [[Light Corn Syrup]] | None |
| 3/4 Cup | [[Water]] | For syrup |
| 1 Cup | [[Sugar]] | For rolling |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Peels
1. Remove the peel from the **3 large oranges** and **2 large lemons** in large strips.
2. Place the peels in a pot, cover with cold water, bring to a simmer, and cook for **30 minutes** to remove bitterness. Drain.
3. Return the peels to the pot, cover with fresh cold water, and simmer again until the peels are very tender.
4. Drain the tender peels and rinse under cold water. Once cool, use a spoon to scrape away any remaining white pith, then cut into 2-inch by 1/4-inch strips.

### Step 2: Candy and Cure
5. In a saucepan, combine **3/4 cup water**, **1 cup sugar**, and **3 Tbsp light corn syrup**. Stir over low heat until the sugar is completely dissolved.
6. Add the citrus peel strips to the syrup. Simmer gently, uncovered, until most of the syrup is absorbed and the peels look translucent.
7. Remove from heat and let the peels stand in the syrup overnight.
8. The next day, bring the mixture to a simmer again for a few minutes. Let it cool slightly, then drain the peels well.
9. Spread the remaining **1 cup sugar** on a plate. Roll the drained, sticky peels in the sugar until thoroughly coated.
10. Transfer the sugar-coated peels to wax paper to dry completely until firm and no longer tacky.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the dried candied peels in an airtight container at room temperature for up to two weeks.
