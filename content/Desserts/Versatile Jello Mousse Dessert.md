# Versatile Jello Mousse Dessert

> 📜 **Collection:** JC | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic, no-bake dessert featuring a light and fluffy Jello mousse made with whipped evaporated milk and fruit. This versatile recipe can be served as a molded dessert or as a pie in a graham cracker crust.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 5 mins | 2 hours | 8-10 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large mixing bowl
* Electric mixer
* Freezer
* Jello mold or serving bowl

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 package (3 oz) | [[Jello]] | any flavor |
| 3/4 cup | [[Water]] | boiling, or fruit juice |
| 1 can (12 oz) | [[Evaporated Milk]] | well-chilled |
| 3/4 cup | [[Sugar]] | none |
| 1 can (8-15 oz) | [[Crushed Pineapple]] | drained |
| Optional | [[Graham Cracker Crust]] | prepared |
| Optional | [[Fruit]] | additional |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Dissolve and Chill the Base
1. In a bowl, dissolve the **1 package Jello** powder in the **3/4 cup boiled liquid**. Let it cool and thicken slightly until it begins to thicken but is not fully set.

### Step 2: Whip the Milk
2. In a separate, chilled mixing bowl, beat the cold **1 can evaporated milk** with an electric mixer on high speed until thick and fluffy and it forms stiff peaks.

### Step 3: Combine and Set
3. Gradually add the **3/4 cup sugar** while continuing to beat until well combined.
4. Beat the cooled **Jello mixture** into the whipped milk mixture until smooth and blended.
5. Gently fold in the drained **1 can crushed pineapple** and any other desired fruit.
6. Pour into a serving bowl, mold, or a prepared **graham cracker crust**.
7. Refrigerate for several hours until completely firm and set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store sealed in the refrigerator for up to 3-4 days.
