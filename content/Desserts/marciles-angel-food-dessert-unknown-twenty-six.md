# Marcile's Angel Food Dessert

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Marcile | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A delightfully light and creamy dessert made with angel food cake, instant vanilla pudding, and cool whip. This chilled treat is easy to assemble and perfect for gatherings.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 0 mins | 2 hours | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Serving dish

---
## Ingredients

### Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 can (13.75 oz) | [[Crushed Pineapple]] | med, cold |
| 1 qt | [[Cool Whip]] | thawed |
| 1 package | [[Instant Vanilla Pudding]] | dry |
| 1 | [[Angel Food Cake]] | baked and torn or sliced |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing and Assembling
1. Mix the **1 can (13.75 oz) cold crushed pineapple** with the **1 package instant vanilla pudding**.
2. Gently fold in the **1 qt cool whip** until thoroughly combined and fluffy.
3. Layer or fold in the **1 angel food cake** pieces until fully coated, and chill until set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Keep refrigerated until ready to serve; best enjoyed within 24 hours.
