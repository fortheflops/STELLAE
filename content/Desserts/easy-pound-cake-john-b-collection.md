# Easy Pound Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic, simple pound cake recipe featuring a rich buttery flavor and tender crumb. Perfect for any occasion and easily adaptable with nuts or candied fruits.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 1 hour | None | 10 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large bowl
* Mixer
* Loaf pan
* Tube pan

---
## Ingredients

### Cake Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3 cups | [[Flour]] | Sifted |
| 1-3/4 cups | [[Sugar]] | None |
| 1 1/2 tsp | [[Salt]] | None |
| 2 tsp | [[Baking Powder]] | None |
| 2 tsp | [[Vanilla]] | None |
| 1 cup | [[Crisco]] | None |
| 3/4 cup | [[Milk]] | None |
| 4 | [[Eggs]] | Divided |

---

## Instructions

### Mixing and Baking
1. Combine all ingredients except 2 of the eggs in a large bowl of a mixer with **2 eggs**. Beat on #3 speed for 2 minutes.
2. Add the remaining **2 more eggs** and beat for another 2 minutes until smooth and fluffy.
3. Bake for **1 hour** at 375°F in a greased loaf or tube pan until golden brown and a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Can be divided into smaller loaf pans and one frozen for later use, or cut the recipe in half for a smaller cake.
