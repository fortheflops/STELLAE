# Norma's Dried Apple Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** UK15 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Norma | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A rich and dense old-fashioned spice cake packed with cooked dried apples, raisins, and black walnuts. This fruitcake-style loaf is a perfect, moist treat for autumn and winter gatherings.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Apples
1. Simmer **1 1/2 cups dried apples** in water until tender, about **15-20 minutes**. Drain well, mash, and set aside to cool completely.

### Step 2: Mix the Batter
2. Preheat oven to **325°F** and grease/flour a large tube or Bundt pan.
3. In a large bowl, cream **1 cup shortening** and **2 cups sugar** until smooth. Beat in **3 large eggs** one at a time.
4. In a separate bowl, whisk together **4 cups flour**, **2 tsp baking soda**, **1 pinch salt**, **1 tsp cinnamon**, **1 tsp allspice**, and **1 tsp cloves**.
5. Stir the **2 1/2 cups mashed dried apples** into the creamed mixture, then gradually add the dry ingredients until just combined.
6. Fold in **1 cup raisins** and **1 cup chopped nuts**.

### Step 3: Bake and Cool
7. Spread the thick batter into the pan and bake for **1 hour 30 minutes to 1 hour 45 minutes**, or until a **wooden skewer** comes out clean.
8. Cool in the pan for **20 minutes** before turning out onto a wire rack until completely cool and firm to the touch.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This cake improves with age; wrap tightly in plastic wrap and store in a cool, dry place for up to one week, or freeze for up to three months.
