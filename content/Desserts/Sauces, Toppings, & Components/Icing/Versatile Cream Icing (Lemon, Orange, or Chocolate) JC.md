## Recipe
### Base Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 4 tablespoons | [[Butter]], softened |
| 1 tablespoon | [[Cream]] |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Vanilla Extract]] |
| 3 cups | [[Confectioners Sugar]], sifted |
### Flavor Options
| Flavor | Additional Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Lemon** | 3 tablespoons [[Lemon Juice]] & Grated [[Lemon Rind]] |
| **Orange** | 3 tablespoons [[Orange Juice]] & Grated [[Orange Rind]] |
| **Chocolate** | 2 ounces [[Chocolate]], melted and cooled |
___
## Instructions
1.  In a mixing bowl, beat the softened **butter** until creamy.
2.  Stir in the **cream**, **vanilla**, and your chosen flavoring (**lemon juice**, **orange juice**, or melted **chocolate**).
3.  Gradually beat in the **confectioners' sugar** until the icing is smooth and spreadable. Add more liquid if too thick, or more sugar if too thin.
4.  If making the lemon or orange version, stir in the grated **rind**.
5.  Beat for another minute until fluffy, then spread on a cooled cake.
___

>[!Note]
>For the chocolate variation, use unsweetened or semi-sweet baking chocolate and ensure it has cooled to room temperature before beating it into the butter.
