### Instructions (Clarified Method)
1.  In the top of a double boiler (or a heatproof bowl set over a saucepan of simmering water), combine the **brown sugar**, **water**, **egg white**, and **cream of tartar**.
2.  Place over the simmering water and begin beating with a handheld electric mixer, starting on low and increasing to high.
3.  Beat constantly for about 7 minutes, or until the icing is thick, glossy, and holds stiff peaks.
4.  Remove from the heat and use immediately to frost a cooled cake.
___

>[!Note]
>This frosting must be used immediately after making, as it will start to set. The vinegar or cream of tartar helps to stabilize the egg white.

>[!Warning]
>This recipe contains lightly cooked egg whites. For those with health concerns, consider using pasteurized egg whites.
