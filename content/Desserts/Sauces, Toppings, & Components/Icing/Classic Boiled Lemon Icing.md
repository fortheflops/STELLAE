*This is a classic boiled icing. The process of beating the hot sugar mixture as it cools is what gives it a creamy, non-gritty texture.*

## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 3/4 Cup | [[Sugar]] |
| 1/4 Cup | [[Milk]] |
| 3 Tablespoons | [[Spry]] (Shortening) |
| 1/4 Teaspoon | [[Salt]] |
| 3/4 Teaspoon | [[Lemon Rind]] |
___
### Instructions
1.  In a saucepan, combine the **sugar**, **milk**, **Spry** (shortening), and **salt**.
2.  Bring to a rolling boil, stirring constantly, and cook for exactly 1 minute.
3.  Remove from the heat and add the **lemon rind**.
4.  Let the mixture cool until warm, then beat with an electric mixer until it becomes thick, creamy, and spreadable.
5.  Use immediately on a cooled cake.
___

>[!Note]
>"Spry" was a popular brand of vegetable shortening. Modern-day Crisco is a direct substitute.
