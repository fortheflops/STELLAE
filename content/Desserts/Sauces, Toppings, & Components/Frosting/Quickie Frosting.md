*This is a classic broiled frosting. The key is to spread it on a hot cake right out of the oven and then return it for a final toasting.*

## Recipe
| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| $\frac{3}{4}$ Cup | [[Brown Sugar]] |
| $\frac{3}{4}$ Cup | [[Coconut]] |
| 2 Tablespoons | [[Margarine]] or Butter |
| $\frac{1}{2}$ Cup | Canned [[Milk]] (Evaporated) or Regular Milk |
___
## Instructions
1.  As soon as your cake is baked, prepare the frosting.
2.  In a saucepan, combine the **brown sugar**, **margarine**, and **milk**. Heat and stir until the ingredients are melted and dissolved.
3.  Stir in the **coconut**. If the frosting is too thick, add a little more milk.
4.  Pour and spread the frosting over the **hot** cake.
5.  Return the cake to the oven (or place it under the broiler) for 5-7 minutes, or until the frosting is bubbly and glazed. Watch carefully to prevent burning.
___

>[!Warning]
>This type of frosting can go from bubbly to burnt very quickly under heat. Watch it closely during the final 5-7 minutes in the oven.
