## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 1 Package (8oz) | [[Cream Cheese]], softened |
| 1/2 Stick | [[Margarine]] or Butter, softened |
| 1 Teaspoon | [[Vanilla]] |
| 1 Box (1 lb) | [[Powdered Sugar]] |
| Optional Garnish | Chopped [[Pecans]] |
___
### Instructions
1.  In a large bowl, beat the softened **cream cheese** and **margarine** with an electric mixer until smooth.
2.  Gradually add the **powdered sugar** and mix well, starting on low speed and increasing to medium until the frosting is smooth.
3.  Add the **vanilla** and beat on high speed until the frosting is light and fluffy.
4.  Spread between layers and on the top and sides of a cooled cake.
5.  Sprinkle with chopped **pecans**, if desired.
