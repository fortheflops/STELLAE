## Recipe
| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 1 cup | [[Powdered Sugar]] |
| 1 tablespoon | [[Butter]], softened |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Vanilla Extract]] |
| 1-2 tablespoons | [[Canned Milk]] or [[Regular Milk]] |
___
## Instructions
1.  In a small bowl, combine the **powdered sugar**, softened **butter**, and **vanilla**.
2.  Gradually add a small amount of **milk** while mixing, until the frosting reaches your desired spreading consistency.
3.  Beat until smooth and spread on a cooled cake.
