## Recipe
| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 3 Tablespoons | [[Margarine]], softened |
| 1/3 Cup | [[Cocoa]] |
| 3 Cups | [[Powdered Sugar]] |
| 1/4 Cup | [[Hot Water]] |
| 1 Teaspoon | [[Vanilla]] |
___
### Instructions (Clarified Method)
1.  In a medium bowl, cream the softened **margarine** with an electric mixer until smooth.
2.  In a separate bowl, whisk together the **powdered sugar** and **cocoa**.
3.  Gradually add the dry ingredients to the margarine, alternating with the **hot water**, and beating until everything is incorporated and smooth.
4.  Stir in the **vanilla**.
5.  Beat on high speed for a minute until the frosting is light and fluffy.
___

>[!Note]
>This recipe makes enough frosting to cover the top and sides of a two-layer, 8-inch cake.
