## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 1 Cup | [[Sugar]] |
| 1/3 Cup | [[Water]] |
| 1 tsp. | [[Vinegar]] |
| 2 | [[Egg Whites]] |
| 3/4 tsp. | [[Vanilla]] |
___
### Instructions
1.  In a small saucepan, combine the **sugar**, **water**, and **vinegar**. Boil until the syrup "spins a long thread" when dropped from a spoon (this is the soft-ball stage, 235-245°F).
2.  While the syrup is boiling, beat the **egg whites** in a clean bowl until stiff peaks form.
3.  With your mixer on high, very slowly pour the hot syrup in a thin stream into the stiffly beaten egg whites.
4.  Continue beating constantly until the frosting is thick, glossy, and holds its shape.
5.  Beat in the **vanilla**. Spread on a cooled cake immediately.
___

>[!Note]
>This recipe makes enough frosting for two 8-inch cake layers.

>[!Warning]
>Use extreme caution when working with hot sugar syrup. Pouring the syrup slowly and carefully is crucial to prevent burns and to ensure the egg whites cook properly without scrambling.
