## Recipe
| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 cup | [[Condensed Milk]] |
| 2 tablespoons | Melted [[Butter]] |
| 1 cup | Sifted [[Powdered Sugar]] |
| 2 tablespoons | [[Hershey's Cocoa Powder]] |
| 3/4 cup | [[Graham Cracker Crumbs]] |
| 1/2 cup | Chopped [[Walnuts]] |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Vanilla Extract]] |
___
## Instructions
1.  In a bowl, combine the **condensed milk**, melted **butter**, sifted **powdered sugar**, and **cocoa powder**. Mix well until smooth.
2.  Add the **graham cracker crumbs** and beat the mixture until it is creamy.
3.  Stir in the **vanilla** and chopped **walnuts**.
4.  Spread the frosting on a warm cake.
