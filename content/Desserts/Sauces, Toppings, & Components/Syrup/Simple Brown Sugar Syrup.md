## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 1/3 Cup | [[Brown Sugar]] |
| 1/3 Cup | [[Honey]] |
| 3 Tbsp. | Melted [[Margarine]] or [[Butter]] |
___
### Instructions
1.  In a small saucepan, combine the **brown sugar**, **honey**, and melted **margarine** or **butter**.
2.  Stir over low heat until the sugar is dissolved and the ingredients are well combined.
3.  Use as directed in your recipe (e.g., pour into the bottom of a pan for sticky buns).
___
