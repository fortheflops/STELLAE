## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 1 ½ Cups | [[Powdered Sugar]] |
| 3 Tbsp | [[Sweet Butter]], Softened |
| 2 Tablespoons| [[Heavy Cream]] |
| ¾ Teaspoon | [[Peppermint Flavoring]] (Extract)|
| Optional | [[Green Food Coloring]] |
___
### Instructions
1.  In a medium bowl, combine the **powdered sugar**, softened **sweet butter**, **heavy cream**, and **peppermint flavoring**.
2.  Beat with an electric mixer until the filling is smooth and creamy.
3.  If desired, add a drop of **green food coloring** and mix until evenly colored.
4.  Spread the filling over a cooled layer of brownies or cookies.
5.  Cover and chill until firm, about 1 hour.
___

>[!Note]
>This filling is the classic center layer for desserts like crème de menthe brownies or homemade peppermint patties.
