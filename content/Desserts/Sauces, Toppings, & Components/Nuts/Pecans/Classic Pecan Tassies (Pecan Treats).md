## Recipe
### Crust
| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 1 Stick | [[Margarine]], softened |
| 1 (3 oz) Pkg | [[Cream Cheese]], softened |
| 1 Cup | [[Flour]] |
### Filling
| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 3 | [[Eggs]] |
| 2/3 Cup | [[Sugar]] |
| 1/4 Teaspoon | [[Salt]] |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Butter]], melted |
| 1 Cup | [[Light Corn Syrup]] |
| 1 Cup | [[Pecans]], finely chopped |
___
## Instructions
1.  **For the Crust:** In a bowl, blend the softened **margarine**, **cream cheese**, and **flour** until a dough forms. Wrap and chill for 1 hour.
2.  Preheat oven to 375°F.
3.  Divide the chilled dough into small balls and press them into the cups of a mini muffin tin to form tiny pie shells.
4.  **For the Filling:** In a separate bowl, mix the **eggs**, **sugar**, **salt**, melted **butter**, **corn syrup**, and finely chopped **pecans**.
5.  Pour the filling into the unbaked shells.
6.  Bake for 40-50 minutes, or until the filling is set.
7.  Let cool in the pan before carefully removing.
___

>[!Note]
>Using mini muffin tins is key for this recipe to create the classic "tassie" shape.
