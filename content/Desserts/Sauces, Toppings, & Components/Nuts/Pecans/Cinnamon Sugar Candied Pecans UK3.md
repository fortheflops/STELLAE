## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 1 | [[Egg White]] |
| 1 Tablespoon | [[Water]] |
| 1 Cup | [[Sugar]] |
| 1 Teaspoon | [[Cinnamon]] |
| 1 Teaspoon | [[Salt]] |
| 1 (10 Ounce) Package | Raw [[Pecans]] |
___
### Instructions
1.  Preheat oven to 300°F.
2.  In a medium bowl, beat the **egg white** and **water** together until frothy.
3.  In a separate bowl or a large plastic bag, mix together the **sugar**, **cinnamon**, and **salt**.
4.  Add the **pecans** to the frothy egg white mixture and toss to coat them thoroughly.
5.  Transfer the coated pecans to the sugar mixture and toss until they are completely covered.
6.  Spread the pecans in a single layer in a shallow baking pan.
7.  Bake for 45 minutes, **stirring every 15 minutes**.
8.  Let cool completely before storing.
___

>[!Note]
>Stirring every 15 minutes is crucial for this recipe. It prevents the sugar from burning and ensures the pecans get a perfectly crisp, even coating.
