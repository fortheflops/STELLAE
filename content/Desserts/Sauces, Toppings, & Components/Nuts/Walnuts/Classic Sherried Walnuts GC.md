## Instructions
1.  In a saucepan, combine the **sugars**, **honey**, **salt**, and **sherry**.
2.  Stir over medium heat until the sugars dissolve, then continue to boil without stirring until the mixture reaches the soft-ball stage (235-245°F).
3.  Remove the pan from the heat and immediately add the **cinnamon**, **nutmeg**, and **walnuts**.
4.  Beat the mixture with a wooden spoon as it starts to cool. Continue beating until the clear syrup turns opaque and creamy.
5.  Pour the coated nuts onto waxed paper and quickly separate them with forks before they harden. Let cool completely.
___

>[!Warning]
>Use extreme caution when working with hot sugar syrup, as it can cause severe burns.
