### Instructions (Clarified Method)
1.  In a saucepan, whisk together the **sugar** and **cornstarch**. Gradually whisk in the **evaporated milk** and **water** until smooth.
2.  Stir in the **butterscotch morsels**.
3.  Cook over medium heat, stirring constantly, until the mixture thickens and bubbles.
4.  Slowly stir a small amount of the hot mixture into the beaten **egg yolk**, then pour the yolk mixture back into the saucepan, stirring well. Cook for 1-2 more minutes.
5.  Remove from the heat. Immediately stir in the **butter**, **coconut**, and **nuts**.
6.  Let the filling cool completely before using.
___

>[!Note]
>This makes a fantastic frosting for a simple sheet cake or a rich filling between cake layers.
