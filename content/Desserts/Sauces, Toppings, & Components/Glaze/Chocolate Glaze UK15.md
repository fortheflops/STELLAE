## Recipe
| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 1/3 cup | [[Margarine]] or [[Butter]] |
| 2 cups | [[Powdered Sugar]] |
| 1 1/2 teaspoons | [[Vanilla]] |
| 2 (1 ounce) squares | Unsweetened [[Chocolate]], melted & cooled |
| 2 to 4 tablespoons | Hot [[Water]] |
___
## Instructions
1.  In a saucepan, melt the **margarine** or **butter** over low heat.
2.  Remove from heat and stir in the **powdered sugar**, **vanilla**, and the melted (and cooled) **unsweetened chocolate** until blended.
3.  Stir in the **hot water**, one tablespoon at a time, until the glaze reaches your desired consistency.
