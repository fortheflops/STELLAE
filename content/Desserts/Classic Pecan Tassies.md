# Classic Pecan Tassies

> 📜 **Collection:** General Archive | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *These classic pecan tassies are delightful two-bite miniature pecan pies featuring a rich, tender cream cheese pastry crust. Perfect for holiday gatherings and parties, they offer a sophisticated blend of buttery crust and sweet, nutty filling.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Crust
1. In a bowl, blend the **1 stick margarine**, **1 (3 oz) pkg cream cheese**, and **1 cup flour** until a soft dough forms.
2. Wrap the dough and chill for **1 hour**.

### Step 2: Shape and Fill
3. Preheat oven to **375°F**.
4. Divide the chilled dough into 24 small balls and press each into the cups of a mini muffin tin to form shells.
5. In a separate bowl, whisk together **3 eggs**, **2/3 cup sugar**, **1/4 teaspoon salt**, **1/2 cup melted butter**, **1 cup light corn syrup**, and **1 cup finely chopped pecans**.
6. Pour the filling into the unbaked shells.

### Step 3: Bake
7. Bake for **40-50 minutes**, or until the filling is set and the crust is golden brown.
8. Let cool in the pan before carefully removing to ensure they hold their shape.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The dough can be prepared and chilled up to 24 hours in advance. Baked tassies can be stored in an airtight container at room temperature for 3 days.
