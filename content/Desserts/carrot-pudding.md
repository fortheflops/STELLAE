## Instructions

### Mixing and Steaming
1. Combine **1 cup brown sugar**, **1 cup grated carrot**, **1 cup suet**, **1 tsp nutmeg**, **1 tsp cinnamon**, and **1 tsp baking soda** in a large mixing bowl.
2. Mix in **1 cup ground potato**, **1 cup raisins**, a pinch of **salt**, **1 tsp cloves**, **1 1/4 cups flour**, **1/4 cup hot water**, and **1 egg** until a uniform batter forms.
3. Transfer the mixture to a steamer and **steam for 3 hours** until set and cooked through.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Can be prepared ahead of time and reheated by steaming before serving.
