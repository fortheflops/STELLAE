# Jr. League's Italian Cream Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Jr. League | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic Southern-style Italian Cream Cake featuring a tender crumb packed with coconut and nuts, finished with a rich cream cheese frosting. This impressive three-layer cake is elevated by whipped egg whites for a light texture.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 25 mins | 25 mins | 10 mins | 1 3-layer cake |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Three 8-inch round cake pans
* Large mixing bowl
* Separate mixing bowls
* Whisk
* Electric mixer
* Wire cooling rack

## Ingredients

| 1/2 cup | [[Shortening]] | none |
| 2 cups | [[Sugar]] | none |
| 5 | [[Eggs]] | separated |
| 2 cups | [[Flour]] | all-purpose |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Baking Soda]] | see note |

| 1 cup | [[Nuts]] | chopped, pecans recommended |

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare Batter
1. Preheat your oven to **350°F**. Grease and flour three **8-inch round cake pans**.
2. In a large bowl, cream the **1 stick (1/2 cup) margarine** and **1/2 cup shortening** together. Gradually add the **2 cups sugar** and beat until the mixture is light and smooth.
3. Add the **5 egg yolks** one at a time, beating well after each addition.
4. In a separate bowl, whisk together the **2 cups flour** and **1 teaspoon baking soda**.
5. Add the flour mixture to the creamed mixture alternately with the **1 cup buttermilk**, beginning and ending with the flour. Mix until just combined.
6. Stir in the **1 teaspoon vanilla**, **1 small can (3.5 oz) coconut**, and **1 cup chopped nuts**.

### Step 2: Fold and Bake
7. In a separate, clean bowl, beat the **5 egg whites** until stiff peaks form.
8. Gently fold the beaten egg whites into the batter until fully incorporated.
9. Pour the batter evenly into the three prepared pans.
10. Bake for **25 minutes**, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean and the cakes are springy to the touch.
11. Let the cakes cool in the pans for **10 minutes** before turning them out onto a wire rack to cool completely.
12. Frost with your favorite [[Classic Cream Cheese Frosting|Cream Cheese Frosting]] once cooled.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The baked cake layers can be wrapped tightly and frozen for up to 1 month before frosting, or stored frosted in the refrigerator for up to 4 days.
