# Christmas Cane Cookies

> *These festive almond-vanilla butter cookies are twisted into charming candy cane shapes and finished with a crunchy peppermint sugar topping. Perfect for holiday gifting, this classic recipe yields a beautiful, professional-looking treat.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Dough
1. In a large bowl, beat the **1 cup butter or regular margarine** and **1 cup confectioners' sugar** until the mixture is light and fluffy.
2. Beat in the **1 egg**, **1 1/2 teaspoons vanilla extract**, and **1/2 teaspoon almond extract**.
3. In a separate bowl, whisk together the **2 1/2 cups flour** and **1/2 teaspoon salt**. Gradually add this to the creamed mixture, mixing until a soft dough forms.
4. Divide the dough in half. Blend the **1/2 teaspoon red food coloring** into one half. Chill both halves for 30 minutes if the dough is too soft to handle.

### Step 2: Shape and Bake
5. Preheat oven to 375°F. Working with **1 teaspoon** of each dough color, roll into 4-inch strips. Twist the strips together to form a spiral rope.
6. Place on an ungreased baking sheet and curve one end to form a cane shape. Repeat with remaining dough.
7. Bake for **10 minutes** until the edges are lightly golden and the cookies are set.
8. While baking, combine **1/4 cup crushed peppermint candy** and **1/4 cup granulated sugar**.
9. Remove from oven and immediately sprinkle the mixture over the hot cookies. Let cool on the pan before moving to a wire rack.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The dough can be prepared and chilled for up to 24 hours before shaping. Baked cookies store well in an airtight container for up to one week.
