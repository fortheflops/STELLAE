# Congo Squares

> 📜 **Collection:** Burcea Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *Delicious chewy blondie-style brownie bars packed with brown sugar, chocolate chips, and nuts. A classic, comforting treat perfect for sharing.*

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |

| 2 1/2 tsp | [[Baking powder]] | None |
| 1/2 tsp | [[Salt]] | None |
| 2/3 cup | [[Shortening]] | None |
| 1 1/4 cups | [[Brown sugar]] | None |
| 3 | [[Eggs]] | None |
| 1 tsp | [[Vanilla]] | None |
| 1 cup | [[Nut meats]] | chopped |
| 1 pkg. | [[Chocolate chips]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing and Baking
1. Preheat oven and grease **2 greased pans**.
2. Combine the **2 3/4 cups flour**, **2 1/2 tsp baking powder**, and **1/2 tsp salt**.
3. In a separate bowl, mix the **2/3 cup shortening**, **1 1/4 cups brown sugar**, **3 eggs**, and **1 tsp vanilla** until well blended.
4. Fold in the **1 cup nut meats** and **1 pkg. chocolate chips**.
5. Spread the mixture evenly into the **2 greased pans**.
6. Bake at 350 degrees F for **about 30 mins** until golden brown and baked through.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 3 days or freeze for longer storage.
