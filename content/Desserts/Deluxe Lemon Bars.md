# Deluxe Lemon Bars

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic recipe for rich and tangy lemon bars featuring a buttery shortbread crust and a sweet, gooey lemon custard filling. These bars are baked to perfection and finished with a delicate dusting of powdered sugar.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Crust
1. Preheat oven to 350°F.
2. In a large bowl, combine **2 cups flour** and **1/2 cup confectioners' sugar**.
3. Cut in **1 cup cold butter** until the mixture clings together to form a dough.
4. Press the mixture firmly into an ungreased 13x9x2 inch pan and bake for **20 to 25 minutes** until lightly browned.

### Step 2: Prepare the Filling
5. In a medium bowl, beat **4 large eggs**, **2 cups granulated sugar**, and **1/3 cup lemon juice**.
6. In a separate bowl, sift **1/2 cup flour** and **1/2 teaspoon baking powder**, then stir into the egg mixture until just combined.

### Step 3: Final Bake and Cooling
7. Pour the filling over the hot crust and bake for an additional **25 minutes** until set.
8. Sprinkle with **additional confectioners' sugar** while warm and cool completely on a wire rack before cutting.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 5 days; the bars will firm up significantly as they cool.
