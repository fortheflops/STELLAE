# Classic Divinity

> 📜 **Collection:** UK15 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic, light, and fluffy confection made by pouring hot sugar syrup into stiffly beaten egg whites. This traditional candy is prized for its melt-in-your-mouth texture and firm, cloud-like shape.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 20 mins | 30 mins | 3-4 dozen pieces |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 3-quart heavy-bottomed saucepan
* Candy thermometer
* Stand mixer
* Baking sheets
* Waxed paper

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |

| 3 cups | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 1 cup | [[Karo Syrup]] | Light corn syrup |
| 1/2 cup | [[Water]] | Room temperature |
| 1 dash | [[Salt]] | Fine grain |
| 2 | [[Egg Whites]] | Large, room temperature |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Vanilla]] | Pure extract |
| 1 cup | [[Nuts]] | Chopped, optional |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare and Cook Syrup
1. Line two large baking sheets with waxed paper.
2. In a **3-quart heavy-bottomed saucepan**, combine **3 cups sugar**, **1 cup Karo syrup**, **1/2 cup water**, and **1 dash salt**.
3. Cook over medium heat, stirring until the **sugar** is dissolved, then boil without stirring until the mixture reaches the **hard-ball stage (250°F to 260°F)** on a candy thermometer.

### Step 2: Whip and Combine
4. While the syrup cooks, beat **2 egg whites** in a large heatproof bowl until stiff peaks form.
5. Remove the **syrup** from heat and, with the mixer on high, slowly pour the hot liquid in a thin, steady stream into the **egg whites**.
6. Continue beating on high speed for 5-10 minutes until the mixture is thick, stiff, and loses its glossy sheen.

### Step 3: Finish and Set
7. Quickly stir in **1 teaspoon vanilla** and **1 cup nuts** by hand.
8. Drop by teaspoonfuls onto the prepared waxed paper and let stand until completely firm and set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 2 weeks; avoid humid environments as the candy may soften.
