# Classic Apricot Lattice Pie (Large Batch)

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic, large-batch recipe yielding four beautiful apricot lattice pies with a sweet and warmly spiced filling. Perfect for gatherings and celebrations, featuring a homemade crust and golden finish.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 30 mins | 40 mins | None | 4 9-inch pies |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large saucepan
* Mixing bowls
* Wire whisk
* Four 9-inch pie tins
* Rolling pin
* Baking sheet

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 4 pounds | [[Pie Dough]] | Divided |
| 1 1/2 | #10 cans Canned Sliced [[Apricots]] | Drained, juice reserved |
| 3 cups | Juice from apricots + [[Water]] | Combined liquid |
| 3 ounces | [[Cornstarch]] | None |
| 1 1/4 cups | Cold [[Water]] | For slurry |
| 1 pound 4 ounces | [[Sugar]] | None |
| 1 1/2 teaspoons | [[Salt]] | None |
| 3/4 teaspoon | [[Mace]] | Ground |
| 1 1/2 teaspoons | [[Cinnamon]] | Ground |
| 2 tablespoons | [[Lemon Juice]] | Fresh |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep Crust and Filling Base
1. Preheat oven to **425°F (220°C)**. Divide **2 lbs of pie dough** into four **8-ounce pieces**, roll out, and line **four 9-inch pie tins** to form the bottom crusts.
2. Drain the canned **apricots**, reserving the juice. Combine the reserved juice with enough **water** to make **3 cups of liquid** and bring this to a boil in a large saucepan.

### Step 2: Thicken and Spice the Filling
3. In a separate bowl, whisk the **cornstarch** into the **1 1/4 cups of cold water** to make a smooth slurry.
4. Pour the slurry into the boiling juice mixture, stirring constantly until the mixture thickens.
5. Stir in the **sugar**, **salt**, **mace**, **cinnamon**, and **lemon juice**. Bring the mixture back to a full boil, then remove from heat and let it cool completely.
6. Once cooled, gently fold in the drained sliced **apricots**.

### Step 3: Assemble and Bake
7. Divide the apricot filling evenly among the four prepared pie crusts.
8. Divide the remaining **2 lbs of pie dough** into four **8-ounce pieces**. Roll out each piece and cut it into **1/2-inch wide strips**. Weave the strips over the filling to create a lattice top, then trim and crimp the edges to seal.
9. Bake at **425°F** for **40 minutes**, or until the crust is golden brown and the filling is bubbly.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The filling can be prepared ahead and stored in the refrigerator for up to 2 days before assembling and baking the pies.
