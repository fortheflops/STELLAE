# Peanut Butter Chip Chocolate Cookies

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Nine | ✍️ **Attribution:** Cheri | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *Rich chocolate cookies packed with sweet peanut butter chips, crafted from Cheri's classic home recipe. Quick to prepare and bake in under 10 minutes for a delightful chocolate-peanut butter treat.*

| 2 cups | [[Peanut butter chips]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep & Creaming
1. Preheat oven to 350°F.
2. In a large bowl, cream together the **1 cup butter**, **1 1/2 cups sugar**, **2 eggs**, and **2 tsp vanilla extract** until smooth and blended.

### Step 2: Combine Dry Ingredients & Shape
3. In a separate bowl, combine the dry ingredients: **2 cups all-purpose flour**, **2/3 cup cocoa powder**, **3/4 tsp baking soda**, and **1/2 tsp salt**.
4. Stir the dry ingredient mixture into the wet sugar mixture until a cohesive dough forms.
5. Fold in the **2 cups peanut butter chips** until evenly distributed throughout the dough.
6. Shape the dough into small 1-inch balls and arrange on ungreased baking sheets.

### Step 3: Baking
7. Bake at 350°F for **8-10 minutes** until set around the edges but still soft in the center. Allow to cool on the baking sheet for 2 minutes before transferring to a wire rack.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Cookie dough balls can be shaped ahead of time and frozen for up to 3 months. Bake frozen dough balls directly at 350°F, adding 1-2 extra minutes to the baking time.
