## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| **For the Brownies:** |                                           |
| 1/2 cup                    | [[Raisins]]                               |
| 1/4 cup                    | [[Kahlua]]                                |
| 1/2 cup                    | [[Butter]] or [[Margarine]]               |
| 1 1/2 (1 oz) squares       | Unsweetened [[Chocolate]]                 |
| 3/4 cup                    | [[Sugar]]                                 |
| 1/2 teaspoon               | [[Vanilla]]                               |

## Instructions
### For the Brownies
1.  Preheat oven to 350°F. Grease an 8-inch square pan.
2.  In a small bowl, combine the raisins and 1/4 cup of Kahlua. Let stand for 15 minutes.
3.  In a saucepan, combine the butter and unsweetened chocolate. Melt over low heat, then let it cool slightly.
4.  Stir the sugar and vanilla into the melted chocolate mixture and set aside.
5.  In a separate bowl, combine the dry ingredients: flour, baking powder, salt, and instant coffee granules.
6.  Blend the chocolate mixture into the dry ingredients.
7.  Add the eggs, beating well.
8.  Stir in the raisin-Kahlua mixture and blend well.
9.  Spread the dough in the prepared 8-inch square pan.
10. Sprinkle the top with the chopped pecans.
11. Bake for 30 minutes.
### For the Frosting and Finishing
1.  While the brownies are baking, prepare the frosting by combining the powdered sugar, dash of salt, warm water, milk/cream, and 1/4 cup of Kahlua. Mix until smooth.
2.  Let the brownies cool slightly after removing them from the oven.
3.  Spread the Kahlua frosting over the warm brownies.
4.  Cut into 2-inch squares to serve.
