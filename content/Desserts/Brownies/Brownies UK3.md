## Recipe
| Measurements                 | Ingredients         |
| :--------------------------- | :------------------ |
| $\frac{1}{2}$ Cup + 2 Tbsp  | Margarine, melted   |
| 6 Tbsp                       | Cocoa Powder        |
| 1 Cup                        | Sugar               |
| $\frac{3}{4}$ Cup           | Flour               |
| 2                            | Eggs                |
| Pinch                        | Salt                |
| $\frac{1}{2}$ tsp           | Baking Powder       |
| 1 tsp                        | Vanilla Extract     |
___
## Instructions
1.  Preheat oven to $325^{\circ}F$ ($165^{\circ}C$).
2.  Grease and flour a 9x9 inch square baking pan.
3.  In a medium mixing bowl, lightly beat the 2 eggs.
4.  Stir in the melted margarine ($\frac{1}{2}$ Cup + 2 Tbsp).
5.  Stir in the 1 Cup of sugar and 6 Tbsp of cocoa powder until well combined.
6.  In a separate small bowl, whisk together the $\frac{3}{4}$ Cup flour, pinch of salt, and $\frac{1}{2}$ tsp baking powder.
7.  Add the dry ingredients to the wet mixture and stir until just combined. Be careful not to overmix.
8.  Stir in the 1 tsp of vanilla extract.
9.  Pour the batter into the prepared 9x9 inch pan and spread it evenly.
10. Bake for 30 minutes, or until a wooden toothpick inserted near the center comes out with moist crumbs attached (do not overbake).
11. Let cool completely in the pan on a wire rack before cutting into squares.
