## Recipe
### Brownie

| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
| 2 sticks     | [[Oleo]]                    |
| 1 1/2 cups   | [[Sugar]]                   |
| 4            | [[Eggs]]                    |
| 1/2 cup      | [[Cocoa]]                   |
| 1 cup        | [[Flour]]                   |
| 1 teaspoon    | [[Vanilla]]                 |
| 1 cup        | [[Nuts]]                    |
### Frosting

## Instructions
### Brownie 
1. Mix oleo and sugar.
2. Add eggs, cocoa, flour, vanilla, and nuts.
3. Bake in 9x13 lightly greased pan at 350°F for 30 minutes.
