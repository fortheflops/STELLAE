## Instructions

### Step 1: Fruit Prep
1. Preheat your oven to 350°F.
2. Place the **6 peeled and sliced apples or peaches** into a greased **9-inch deep baking dish**.
3. If desired, sprinkle a little **brown sugar** and **grated orange or lemon peel** between the layers of fruit for enhanced flavor.

### Step 2: Topping & Baking
4. In a mixing bowl, combine the dry ingredients: **1 cup flour**, **1 1/2 cups sugar**, **1 tsp baking powder**, and **1/4 tsp salt**.
5. Mix the **1 beaten egg** into the dry ingredients until the mixture becomes crumbly.
6. Sprinkle this crumbly mixture evenly over the top of the fruit.
7. Pour the **1/3 cup melted butter** evenly over the topping, then sprinkle generously with **cinnamon** and **nutmeg**.
8. Bake at 350°F for **50 to 60 minutes** until the top is a beautiful golden brown and the fruit is bubbling and tender.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Can be assembled a few hours ahead and kept refrigerated before baking. Best served warm, optionally with a scoop of vanilla ice cream.
