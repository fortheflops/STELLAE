# Bread Pudding

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Lois Shelton | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A traditional, large-batch bread pudding featuring a rich custard made from scalded milk and eggs, baked until golden brown and set. This classic recipe is perfect for serving a crowd with its simple, comforting flavors.*

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2/3 loaf | [[Bread]] | crusts removed |
| 8 to 10 | [[Eggs]] | large |
| 1/2 gallon + 1-2 cups | [[Milk]] | divided |
| 2 Cups | [[Sugar]] | granulated |
| 3 tsp | [[Vanilla]] | extract |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Salt]] | fine |
| To taste | [[Butter]] | for dotting |
| To taste | [[Nutmeg]] | ground, for sprinkling |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Base
1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C) and grease a large baking pan.
2. Remove crusts from the **2/3 loaf bread**. Arrange slices to cover the bottom of the pan, then cut the remaining bread into small pieces and distribute over the base.

### Step 2: Prepare the Custard
3. In a large bowl, beat the **8 to 10 eggs** well, then whisk in the **2 cups sugar** and **1/4 teaspoon salt**.
4. In a saucepan, heat the **1/2 gallon milk** until scalded (bubbles form around the edges), ensuring it does not boil.
5. Slowly pour the hot **milk** in a thin stream into the **egg** mixture, whisking constantly to temper the eggs, then stir in the **3 teaspoons vanilla**.

### Step 3: Assemble and Bake
6. Pour the custard mixture evenly over the **bread**. Let it sit for a few minutes to absorb; add the extra **1-2 cups milk** if the bread is not fully saturated.
7. Dot the top with small pieces of **butter** and sprinkle generously with **ground nutmeg**.
8. Bake for **45 minutes to 1 hour**, until the top is golden brown and a knife inserted into the center comes out clean.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The custard can be prepared and poured over the bread a few hours in advance to allow for better absorption before baking.
