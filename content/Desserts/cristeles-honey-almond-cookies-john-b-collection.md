# Cristele's Honey Almond Cookies

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Fern Best | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *Delightful crispy fried cookies scented with almond extract and coated in sweet honey syrup. This cherished recipe from the kitchen of Fern Best makes about 3 dozen treats.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 30 mins | 20 mins | 1 hour | 3 dozen cookies |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Electric mixer
* Mixing bowl
* Pastry wheel
* Deep fryer or heavy skillet
* Thermometer
* Paper towels
* Wire rack

## Instructions

### Step 1: Making the Dough
1. Sift together **2 cups all-purpose flour**, **1/2 teaspoon salt**, and **2 teaspoons baking powder**.
2. Using an electric mixer at high speed, beat **3 eggs** with **2 tablespoons sugar**, **3/4 teaspoon almond extract**, and **1 tablespoon salad oil** until thick.
3. Stir in the flour mixture until smooth, then knead until pliable.
4. Cover the bowl of dough with a towel and let stand an hour or so.

### Step 2: Shaping and Frying
5. On a lightly floured board, roll out half of the dough paper thin.
6. With a pastry wheel, cut into **9 x 1-inch strips**, then shape each strip into a bow or ring and repeat with the rest of the dough.
7. Heat **about 1 qt salad oil** to **370°F** and deep-fry cookies, turning once, until golden brown, then drain on paper toweling.

### Step 3: Honey Glaze
8. Combine **2 cups honey** and **3 tablespoons water**, bring to a boil, and dip cookies into the honey.
9. Drain on wax paper or a wire rack until set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store finished cookies in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 5 days.
