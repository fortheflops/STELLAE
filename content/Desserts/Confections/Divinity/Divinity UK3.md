### Instructions
1. Cook until it spins a thread.
2. Beat 2 egg whites in stiff peaks.
3. Pour at medium speed over egg slowly until blended.
4. Add 1 teaspoon vanilla.
5. Beat until it holds form.
6. Blend in 1 cup nuts.
