## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
| 1 1/2 cups   | Raw [[Peanuts]]             |
| 1/2 cup      | White [[Karo Syrup]]         |
| 1 tablespoon | [[Margarine]]               |
| 1 teaspoon    | [[Baking Soda]]             |
| 1 cup        | [[Sugar]]                   |
| 1/2 teaspoon  | [[Salt]]                    |
| 1-2 teaspoons | [[Vanilla]]                 |
___
### Instructions
1. Mix peanuts, sugar, syrup, and salt.
2. Cook on high for 4 minutes.
3. Stir and cook 4 more minutes on high.
4. Add margarine and cook 3 minutes.
5. Add vanilla and soda.
6. Stir until foamy.
7. Spread on greased cookie sheet.
8. Let set till cool.
9. Then break as desired.
