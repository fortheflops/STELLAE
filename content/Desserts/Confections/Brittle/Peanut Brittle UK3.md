## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
| 2 cups       | [[Sugar]] - Rounded         |
| 1/2 cup      | [[Water]]                   |
| 1/4 cup      | White [[Karo Syrup]]         |
| 3 scant teaspoons | [[Soda]]                    |
| 2 tablespoons | [[Vanilla]]                 |
| 1/4 stick    | [[Oleo]]                    |
| 1/2 cup      | [[Peanuts]]                 |
| 1 cup        | [[Coconut]]                 |
___
### Instructions
1.  Mix sugar, water & Karo.
2.  Boil until hairs put in peanuts & cook until light tan.
3.  Take off fire.
4.  Add butter.
5.  Stir.
6.  Add soda & vanilla.
7.  Stir well.
8.  Pour in about 6 pie pans.
