*A quick, easy, and foolproof recipe for classic Rocky Road fudge. This no-bake candy combines melted chocolate chips, sweetened condensed milk, marshmallows, and nuts for a decadent treat.*
### Author
Unknown
___
## Recipe
| Measurements      | Ingredients                                        |
| :---------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 2 pkgs (12 oz each)| [[Chocolate Chips]]                                |
| 1 can (14 oz)     | Sweetened Condensed [[Milk]] (e.g., Borden's, Eagle Brand) |
| 1 cup             | [[Nuts]], chopped                                  |
| 1 pkg (10 oz)     | [[Marshmallows]] (mini or quartered)               |
| 1 tsp             | [[Vanilla]]                                        |
___
### Instructions
1.  Line a 9x13 inch pan with wax paper or parchment paper, leaving an overhang on the sides to use as handles.
2.  Melt the chocolate chips in the top of a double boiler over simmering water, stirring occasionally until smooth.
3.  Remove the melted chocolate from the heat.
4.  Stir in the sweetened condensed milk and vanilla until well combined.
5.  Let the chocolate mixture cool for a few minutes so it doesn't melt the marshmallows.
6.  Fold in the marshmallows and nuts until they are evenly distributed.
7.  Pour the mixture into the prepared pan and spread it evenly.
8.  Let the candy cool completely, preferably in the refrigerator for at least 4 hours or overnight, until firm.
9.  Once firm, use the paper liner to lift the fudge out of the pan and cut it into squares.
