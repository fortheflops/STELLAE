## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
| 2 pkg        | Chocolate Chips                                     |
| 1 can        | Sweetened Condensed Milk (e.g., Borden's, Eagle Brand) |
| 1 Cup        | Nuts                                                |
| 1 pkg        | Marshmallows (quartered)                            |
| 1 tsp        | Vanilla                                             |
___
### Instructions
1.  Line a baking pan (e.g., 9x13 inch) with wax paper or parchment paper.
2.  Melt the chocolate chips in the top of a double boiler over simmering water, stirring occasionally until smooth.
3.  Remove the melted chocolate from the heat.
4.  Stir in the sweetened condensed milk and vanilla until well combined.
5.  Fold in the quartered marshmallows and nuts until evenly distributed.
6.  Pour the mixture into the prepared pan, spreading it evenly.
7.  Let cool completely, preferably overnight, until firm.
8.  Once firm, lift the candy out using the paper liner and cut into squares.
