## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 (12 ounce) package       | Crushed Keebler [[Vanilla Wafers]]        |
| 1/2 cup                    | [[Rum]]                                   |
| 1/2 cup                    | [[Brandy]]                                |
| 1/2 cup                    | [[Honey]]                                 |
| 1 pound                    | Ground [[Pecans]] or [[Walnuts]]          |
| As Needed                  | [[Powdered Sugar]] or [[Granulated Sugar]]|
___
## Instructions
1.  Mix together the crushed vanilla wafers, rum, brandy, honey, and ground nuts.
2.  Roll the mixture into small balls.
3.  Roll the balls in powdered or granulated sugar to coat.
