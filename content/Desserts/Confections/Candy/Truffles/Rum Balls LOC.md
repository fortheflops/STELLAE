## Instructions
1.  Grind $\frac{1}{2}$ lb. vanilla wafers. Chop 1 cup of nuts.
2.  Combine all ingredients: ground vanilla wafers, chopped nuts, 2 tablespoons cocoa, $\frac{1}{2}$ cup light corn syrup, and $\frac{1}{4}$ cup rum (or bourbon, cream, or whiskey).
3.  Mix to a thick paste.
4.  Roll into balls.
5.  Roll the balls in powdered sugar.
6.  (The recipe notes: "Taste best in a week or so.")
