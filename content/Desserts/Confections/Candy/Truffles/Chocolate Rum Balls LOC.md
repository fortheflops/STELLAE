*A classic, no-bake recipe for rich and potent chocolate rum balls. This simple mixture combines vanilla wafer crumbs, cocoa, nuts, and your choice of spirits, and gets even better as it ages.*

Unknown
___
## Recipe
| Measurements      | Ingredients                                        |
| :---------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| ½ Pound (~2 ½ cups)| [[Vanilla Wafers]], Ground                         |
| 1 Cup             | [[Nuts]], Chopped                                  |
| 2 Tablespoons     | [[Cocoa]] Powder                                   |
| ½ Cup             | Light [[Corn Syrup]]                               |
| ¼ Cup             | [[Rum]], [[Bourbon]], or [[Whiskey]]               |
| As Needed         | [[Powdered Sugar]] (for rolling)                   |
___
## Instructions
1.  Grind or finely crush the vanilla wafers. Finely chop the nuts.
2.  In a large bowl, combine the ground vanilla wafers, chopped nuts, and cocoa powder.
3.  Pour in the light corn syrup and your choice of rum, bourbon, or whiskey.
4.  Mix with a sturdy spoon until a thick, sticky paste forms.
5.  Roll the mixture into small, 1-inch balls.
6.  Roll each ball in powdered sugar to coat completely.
7.  Store in a tightly covered container.
___

>[!Note]
>The recipe notes: "Taste best in a week or so." The flavor of these no-bake treats improves significantly as they age and the flavors meld.
