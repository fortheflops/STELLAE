*A classic no-bake holiday confection. These rich chocolate brandy balls are made with a simple mixture of vanilla wafer crumbs, cocoa, walnuts, and brandy, all held together with corn syrup and rolled in sugar.*

## Recipe
| Measurements               | Ingredients                                        |
| :------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 2 ½ Cups                   | Finely Crushed [[Vanilla Wafers]] (approx. 60 wafers) |
| 1 Cup                      | Sifted Confectioners' [[Sugar]]                    |
| 2 Tablespoons              | [[Cocoa Powder]]                                   |
| ½ Cup                      | Finely Chopped [[Walnuts]]                         |
| ¼ Cup                      | [[Brandy]]                                         |
| ¼ Cup                      | Light [[Corn Syrup]]                               |
| 1 ½ Teaspoons (Optional)   | [[Water]] (if needed)                              |
| For Rolling                | Granulated [[Sugar]]                               |
___
## Instructions
1.  In a large bowl, combine the finely crushed vanilla wafer crumbs, sifted confectioners' sugar, cocoa powder, and finely chopped walnuts.
2.  Stir in the brandy and light corn syrup. Mix well until a stiff, sticky dough forms. If the mixture seems too dry, add a little water (up to 1 ½ teaspoons).
3.  Shape the mixture into ¾-inch balls.
4.  Roll each ball in granulated sugar to coat completely.
5.  Store in a tightly covered container. For the best flavor, let them age for a day or two before serving.
___

>[!Note]
>Makes approximately 4 dozen
