## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |

| Two & $\frac{1}{2}$ Cups         | Finely Crushed [[Vanilla Wafers]] (approx. 60 wafers) |
| One Cup                          | Sifted Confectioners' [[Sugar]] (Powdered Sugar) |
| Two Tablespoons                  | [[Cocoa Powder]]                          |
| $\frac{1}{2}$ Cup                | Finely Chopped [[Walnuts]]                |
| $\frac{1}{4}$ Cup                | [[Brandy]]                                |
| $\frac{1}{4}$ Cup                | Light [[Corn Syrup]]                      |
| One & $\frac{1}{2}$ Teaspoons (Optional) | [[Water]] (if needed)                   |
| For Rolling                      | Granulated [[Sugar]]                      |
___
## Instructions
1.  Combine two and a half cups of finely crushed vanilla wafer crumbs, one cup of sifted confectioners' sugar, two tablespoons of cocoa powder, and $\frac{1}{2}$ cup of finely chopped walnuts.
2.  Stir in $\frac{1}{4}$ cup of brandy and $\frac{1}{4}$ cup of light corn syrup.
3.  Add a little water (approximately one and a half teaspoons, as noted on the card) if necessary to form the mixture into $\frac{3}{4}$-inch balls.
4.  Roll the balls in granulated sugar.
5.  Store in a tightly covered container.
___

>[!Note]
>Makes approximately 4 dozen
