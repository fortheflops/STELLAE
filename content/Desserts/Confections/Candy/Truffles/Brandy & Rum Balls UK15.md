*A classic no-bake holiday treat. These potent and delicious balls are made with finely crushed vanilla wafers, nuts, honey, and a generous amount of both brandy and rum.*

Unknown

## Recipe
| Measurements      | Ingredients                                        |
| :---------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 (15 oz) pkg     | [[Vanilla Wafers]], finely rolled                  |
| 1/2 cup           | [[Honey]]                                          |
| 1/3 cup           | [[Brandy]]                                         |
| 1/3 cup           | [[Light Rum]]                                      |
| 1 pound           | Shelled [[Walnuts]] or [[Pecans]], cut small       |
| As Needed         | [[Granulated Sugar]] (for rolling)                 |
___
## Instructions
1.  Finely crush the vanilla wafers using a food processor or by placing them in a bag and crushing with a rolling pin. Finely chop the nuts.
2.  In a large bowl, combine the finely crushed vanilla wafers, chopped nuts, honey, brandy, and light rum.
3.  Mix well with a sturdy spoon or your hands until a sticky, firm dough forms.
4.  Shape the mixture into 1-inch balls.
5.  Roll each ball in granulated sugar to coat them completely.
6.  Wrap the balls individually in wax paper or store them in airtight containers.
___

>[!Note]
>The recipe card specifies to "use Keebler Vanilla Wafers" and notes that the balls will keep for about 1 month. The flavor improves significantly after they have aged for a few days.
