## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 15 ounces                  | [[Vanilla Wafers]], finely rolled         |
| 1/2 cup                    | [[Honey]]                                 |
| 1/3 cup                    | [[Brandy]]                                |
| 1/3 cup                    | [[Light Rum]]                             |
| 1 pound                    | Shelled [[Walnuts]] or [[Pecans]], cut small |
| As Needed                  | [[Granulated Sugar]]                      |
___
## Instructions
1.  Combine the finely crushed vanilla wafers, honey, brandy, light rum, and small-cut nuts.
2.  Mix well.
3.  Shape the mixture into round balls.
4.  Roll the balls in granulated sugar to coat them.
5.  Wrap in wax paper.
___

>[!Note]
>The recipe card specifies to "use Keebler Vanilla Wafers" and notes that the balls will keep for about 1 month.
