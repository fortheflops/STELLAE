*A potent and delicious no-bake holiday confection, packed with a full pound of ground nuts. This classic recipe uses both brandy and rum, along with honey and vanilla wafer crumbs, for a rich and flavorful treat.*
### Author
Unknown
___
## Recipe
| Measurements      | Ingredients                                        |
| :---------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 (12 oz) package | Crushed Keebler [[Vanilla Wafers]]                 |
| 1 pound           | Ground [[Pecans]] or [[Walnuts]]                   |
| 1/2 cup           | [[Honey]]                                          |
| 1/2 cup           | [[Rum]]                                            |
| 1/2 cup           | [[Brandy]]                                         |
| As Needed         | [[Powdered Sugar]] or [[Granulated Sugar]] for rolling |
___
## Instructions
1.  In a food processor, finely crush the vanilla wafers and grind the nuts.
2.  In a large bowl, combine the crushed vanilla wafers, ground nuts, honey, rum, and brandy.
3.  Mix well with a sturdy spoon or your hands until a sticky, firm dough forms.
4.  Shape the mixture into small, 1-inch balls.
5.  Roll each ball in powdered or granulated sugar to coat them completely.
6.  Store the finished balls in a tightly covered container.
___

>[!Note]
>The author notes, "I prefer gran. sugar" and says the balls will keep for 5 weeks. Like other recipes of this type, the flavor improves significantly after they have aged for a few days.
