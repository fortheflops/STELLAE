*A quick, easy, and clever recipe for chocolate almond raisin clusters. This simple method uses the heat from freshly toasted almonds to melt the chocolate chips, creating a delicious no-fuss candy.*
### Author
Unknown
___
## Recipe
| Measurements      | Ingredients                                        |
| :---------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 Cup             | Whole Unskinned [[Almonds]]                        |
| 2 Cups (12-oz pkg)| Semi Sweet [[Chocolate Chips]]                     |
| 1/2 Cup           | [[Raisins]]                                        |
___
### Instructions
1.  **Preparation:** Preheat your oven to 350°F. Line a cookie sheet with waxed paper.
2.  **Toast Almonds:** Spread the almonds in a single layer on a baking pan. Toast in the oven for **10 minutes**.
3.  **Melt Chocolate:** Immediately place the hot, toasted almonds in a medium bowl. Add the chocolate chips and stir continuously until the chips are completely melted and the almonds are coated.
4.  **Combine:** Stir in the raisins.
5.  **Form Clusters:** Drop by spoonfuls onto the waxed paper-lined cookie sheet.
6.  **Chill:** Chill in the refrigerator for 20 minutes, or until the clusters are firm.
___

>[!Servings]
>Makes about 24
