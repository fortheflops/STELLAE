*A rich chocolate fudge loaded with candied fruits. This vintage recipe features chopped cherries, pineapple, figs, raisins, and coconut folded into a smooth chocolate base.*

| Measurements | Ingredients |

| 3/4 c. | [[Milk]] |
| 2 c. | [[Sugar]] |
| Dash | [[Salt]] |
| 2 Tbsp. | [[Butter]] |
| 1 tsp. | [[Vanilla]] |
| 1/4 c. | Chopped Candied [[Cherries]] |
| 1/4 c. | Chopped Candied [[Pineapple]] |
| 1/4 c. | Chopped Dried [[Figs]] |
| 1/4 c. | Chopped Seedless [[Raisins]] |
| 1/4 c. | Shredded [[Coconut]] |
___
### Instructions
1.  Add chocolate to milk. Cook over low heat, stirring constantly, until mixture is smooth & blended.
2.  Add sugar & salt, stir until sugar is dissolved & mixture boils.
3.  Continue boiling, without stirring, to 232°F or until a small amount of the mixture forms a very soft ball in cold water.
4.  Remove from heat.
5.  Add butter & vanilla. Cool to lukewarm.
6.  Beat until mixture begins to thicken & loses its gloss.
7.  Add fruits & coconut, turn at once into greased 8x4x3 in. loaf pan.
8.  When fudge is cold, cut into squares. (Yields 18 large squares).
