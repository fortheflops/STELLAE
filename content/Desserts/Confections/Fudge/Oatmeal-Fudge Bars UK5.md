## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| **For the Oatmeal Layer:** |                                           |
| 1/2 cup                    | Soft [[Shortening]]                       |
| 1 cup                      | Packed [[Brown Sugar]]                    |
| 1                          | [[Egg]]                                   |
| 1/2 teaspoon               | [[Vanilla]]                               |
| 3/4 cup                    | Sifted [[Flour]]                          |
| 1/2 teaspoon               | Baking [[Soda]]                           |
| 1/2 teaspoon               | [[Salt]]                                  |
| 2 cups                     | Quick-cook [[Oats]]                       |
| 1/2 cup                    | Chopped [[Walnuts]]                       |
| **For the Fudge Layer:** |                                           |
| 1 (6 ounce) package        | Semi-sweet [[Chocolate Pieces]]           |
| 1 tablespoon               | [[Butter]] or [[Margarine]]               |
| 1/3 cup                    | Sweetened Condensed [[Milk]]              |
| 1/4 teaspoon               | [[Salt]]                                  |
| 1/2 cup                    | Chopped [[Walnuts]]                       |
| 1 teaspoon                 | [[Vanilla]]                               |
___
## Instructions
1.  **Make Oatmeal Layer:** Preheat the oven to 350°F. Grease a 9x9x1 3/4" pan. In a medium bowl, beat the shortening and sugar until fluffy. Beat in the egg and 1/2 teaspoon of vanilla. Sift the flour, baking soda, and 1/2 teaspoon of salt into the sugar mixture and mix well. Stir in the oats and 1/2 cup of walnuts. Remove 1 cup of this mixture and set it aside for the topping. Press the rest of the oat mixture into the bottom of the prepared pan.
2.  **Make Fudge Layer:** In a small saucepan, combine the chocolate pieces, butter, condensed milk, and 1/4 teaspoon of salt. Cook, stirring, over low heat until the chocolate and butter are melted. Remove from the heat and stir in the 1/2 cup of walnuts and 1 teaspoon of vanilla.
3.  **Assemble and Bake:** Spread the chocolate mixture over the oatmeal layer in the pan. Sprinkle the top with the reserved 1 cup of the oat mixture. Bake for 25 minutes, or until the surface is lightly browned. Let cool completely in the pan on a wire rack before cutting into bars.
