## Instructions
1.  **Make Oatmeal Layer:** Preheat the oven to 350°F. Grease a 9x9 inch pan.
2.  In a medium bowl, beat the shortening and sugar until fluffy. Beat in the egg and ½ teaspoon of vanilla.
3.  In a separate bowl, whisk together the flour, baking soda, and ½ teaspoon of salt. Add this to the sugar mixture and mix well.
4.  By hand, stir in the oats and ½ cup of walnuts.
5.  Reserve 1 cup of this oat mixture for the topping. Press the rest of the mixture firmly and evenly into the bottom of the prepared pan to form the crust.
6.  **Make Fudge Layer:** In a small saucepan over low heat, combine the chocolate pieces, 1 tablespoon of butter, condensed milk, and ¼ teaspoon of salt. Cook, stirring frequently, until the chocolate is melted and the mixture is smooth.
7.  Remove the pan from the heat and stir in the ½ cup of walnuts and 1 teaspoon of vanilla.
8.  **Assemble and Bake:** Spread the chocolate fudge mixture evenly over the oatmeal crust.
9.  Crumble the reserved 1 cup of the oat mixture over the top of the fudge layer.
10. Bake for **25 minutes**, or until the surface is lightly browned. Let cool completely in the pan on a wire rack before cutting into bars.
