## Instructions
1.  Prepare your favorite crust in a pan (or 6-8 individual crusts).
2.  In a blender or with a mixer, combine the sugar, vanilla, milk, cream cheese, and egg yolks.
3.  Blend for 7 minutes.
4.  In a separate, clean bowl, beat the 3 egg whites until they form stiff peaks (meringue).
5.  Gently fold the meringue into the blended cheese mixture.
6.  Pour the filling into the prepared crust(s).
7.  The card notes a baking temperature of 350°F and a time of "7 min." This is an unusually short baking time.
