*A classic, dense, and creamy New York-style cheesecake. This recipe uses a simple, crustless method and a slow oven cooldown to create a rich, smooth texture and prevent cracking.*

## Recipe
| Measurements         | Ingredients                                        |
| :------------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 1/2 lbs (3 blocks)| [[Cream Cheese]], softened                         |
| 1 1/2 cups           | [[Sugar]]                                          |
| 3 tablespoons        | [[Flour]]                                          |
| 1/8 teaspoon         | [[Salt]]                                           |
| 4                    | [[Eggs]]                                           |
| 1/4 cup              | [[Milk]]                                           |
| 1 teaspoon           | [[Vanilla]]                                        |
___
## Instructions
1.  **Preparation:** Preheat your oven to 375°F. For best results, use a 9-inch springform pan. It's not necessary to grease the pan. Ensure all your ingredients, especially the cream cheese and eggs, are at room temperature.
2.  **Mix:** In a large bowl, beat the softened cream cheese and sugar with an electric mixer on low speed until smooth and creamy.
3.  Add the salt and flour and mix lightly on low speed until just incorporated.
4.  Add the vanilla. Add the eggs one at a time, mixing on low speed just until each egg is blended in. Do not overbeat.
5.  Stir in the milk.
6.  **Bake:** Pour the batter into the springform pan. Bake for **1 hour and 10 minutes**. The center should still have a slight wobble.
7.  **Cool:** Turn the oven **OFF**. Leave the cheesecake in the closed, warm oven for another hour.
8.  After an hour, crack open the oven door and let the cake cool completely inside the oven.
9.  Once cooled, cover and refrigerate for at least 4 hours, or preferably overnight, before serving.
___

>[!Note]
>This is a crustless cheesecake. If you'd like a crust, press a mixture of 1 ½ cups graham cracker crumbs, ¼ cup sugar, and 6 tablespoons melted butter into the bottom of the pan before adding the filling.
