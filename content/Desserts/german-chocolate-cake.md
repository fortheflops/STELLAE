# German Chocolate Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic, rich German chocolate cake recipe featuring a moist crumb and a smooth, decadent glaze. This traditional recipe is baked in a tube pan for a beautiful presentation.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Cake Batter
1. Partially melt **1 bar German Chocolate** over hot water, then remove and stir until completely smooth.
2. Cream together **2 cups sugar** and **1 cup shortening** until light and fluffy.
3. Add **4 eggs**, **2 tsp vanilla extract**, **2 tsp butter flavoring**, and **1 cup buttermilk** to the creamed mixture.
4. Sift together **3 cups flour**, **1/2 tsp baking soda**, and **1 tsp salt**, then incorporate into the wet ingredients.
5. Blend in the melted **1 bar German Chocolate** and mix well.

### Step 2: Baking
6. Pour the batter into a well-greased and floured 9-inch tube pan.
7. Bake in a slow oven at 300°F for about 1 1/2 hours until a tester comes out clean.
8. Remove from the pan while still hot and place under a tightly fitting cover until thoroughly cooled to retain moisture.

### Step 3: Glaze
9. Melt **1 bar German Chocolate** and **1 tbsp shortening** in **1/4 cup water** over low heat.
10. Stir in **1 cup powdered sugar**, a pinch of **salt**, and **1/2 tsp vanilla extract** until smooth and glossy.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The cake can be baked and stored in an airtight container for up to 3 days; glaze should be applied just before serving.
