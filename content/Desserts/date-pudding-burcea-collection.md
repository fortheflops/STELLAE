# Date Pudding

> 📜 **Collection:** Burcea Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic refrigerated date pudding made with sweet marshmallows, dates, graham crackers, and nuts. Molded into a loaf and served chilled with whipped cream.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | None | 2 hours | 6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Loaf pan
* Mixing bowl

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 12 | [[Marshmallows]] | Whole or cut |
| 1 cup | [[Dates]] | Pitted and chopped |
| 18 | [[Graham crackers]] | Crushed |
| 1/2 cup | [[Nuts]] | Chopped |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Assembly and Cooling
1. Combine the **12 marshmallows**, **1 cup dates**, **18 graham crackers**, and **1/2 cup nuts** together.
2. Mold the mixture firmly into a loaf pan.
3. Let cool and set in the refrigerator until firm to the touch.
4. Slice and serve with **whipped cream**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Keep stored in the refrigerator in a loaf pan until ready to slice and serve.
