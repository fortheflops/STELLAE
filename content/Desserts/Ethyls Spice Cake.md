# Ethyl's Spice Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Ethyl | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic, old-fashioned spice cake from a recipe by Ethyl. This tender, buttery cake is flavored with a warm blend of nutmeg, ginger, and cinnamon, and studded with raisins and fresh lemon zest.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Mix Base
1. Preheat your oven to **350°F**. Grease and flour an **8-inch or 9-inch round or square cake pan**.
2. In a large bowl, cream the **6 Tbsp (3 oz) margarine** and **¾ Cup (6 oz) sugar** together with a mixer until light and fluffy. Beat in the **1 egg** until well combined.
3. In a separate bowl, sift together the **1 ⅓ Cups (6 oz) self-rising flour** with the **pinch of salt**, **¼ tsp nutmeg**, **¼ tsp ginger**, and **¼ tsp cinnamon**.
4. In a small cup, mix the **1 Tbsp water** and **¾ tsp lemon juice** together.

### Step 2: Combine and Bake
5. On low speed, add the dry ingredients to the creamed mixture alternately with the lemon-water, beginning and ending with the dry ingredients.
6. Stir in the **⅓ Cup (2 oz) raisins** and **grated rind of 1 lemon**.
7. Pour the batter into the prepared pan and smooth the top. If desired, add a simple streusel topping before baking.
8. Bake for **30-35 minutes**, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean and golden brown.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the cooled cake wrapped tightly in plastic wrap at room temperature for up to 3 days or in the refrigerator for up to 5 days.
