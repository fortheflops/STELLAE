# Swedish Slugs (Raisin Nut Cookies)

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic recipe for a hearty and chewy drop cookie, amusingly named 'Swedish Slugs'. This simple batter is loaded with walnuts and raisins for a timeless, delicious treat.*

| 1 tsp | [[Vanilla]] | extract |

| 1 cup | [[Raisins]] | none |

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Batter
1. Preheat your oven to 350°F and grease your cookie sheets.
2. In a large bowl, cream the **1 cup butter** and **1 1/2 cups sugar** together with an electric mixer until light and fluffy.
3. Add the **3 eggs** one at a time, beating well after each addition.
4. In a small cup, dissolve the **1 tsp baking soda** in the **1 Tbsp hot water**. Add this, along with the **1 tsp vanilla**, to the creamed mixture.

### Step 2: Combine and Bake
5. In a separate bowl, whisk together the **3 cups flour** and **1 tsp baking powder**.
6. Gradually add the dry ingredients to the wet ingredients, mixing on low speed until just combined.
7. By hand, stir in the **1 cup walnuts** and **1 cup raisins**.
8. Drop the dough by rounded teaspoonfuls onto the prepared cookie sheets and bake for **10 minutes**, or until the edges are lightly browned and the centers are set.

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 5 days or freeze for up to 3 months.
