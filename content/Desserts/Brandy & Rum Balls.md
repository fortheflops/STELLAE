# Brandy & Rum Balls

> 📜 **Collection:** UK15 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic no-bake holiday treat. These potent and delicious balls are made with finely crushed vanilla wafers, nuts, honey, and a generous amount of both brandy and rum.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 25 mins | 0 mins | None | 4–5 Dozen |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Food processor or rolling pin
* Large mixing bowl
* Sturdy spoon
* Airtight container

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |

| 1 (15 oz) pkg | [[Vanilla Wafers]] | finely rolled |
| 1/2 cup | [[Honey]] |  |
| 1/3 cup | [[Brandy]] |  |
| 1/3 cup | [[Light Rum]] |  |
| 1 pound | Shelled [[Walnuts]] or [[Pecans]] | cut small |
| As Needed | [[Granulated Sugar]] | for rolling |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Base
1. Finely crush the **1 (15 oz) pkg vanilla wafers** using a food processor or by placing them in a bag and crushing with a rolling pin. Finely chop the **1 pound walnuts or pecans**.

### Step 2: Mix, Shape, and Coat
2. In a large bowl, combine the finely crushed **vanilla wafers**, chopped **walnuts or pecans**, **1/2 cup honey**, **1/3 cup brandy**, and **1/3 cup light rum**.
3. Mix well with a sturdy spoon or your hands until a sticky, firm dough forms.
4. Shape the mixture into 1-inch balls.
5. Roll each ball in **granulated sugar** to coat them completely until sparkling and fully covered.
6. Wrap the balls individually in wax paper or store them in airtight containers.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container for up to 1 month. The flavor improves significantly after aging for a few days.
