# Classic Butter Cookies

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic butter cookie recipe using cake flour for an extra tender, melt-in-your-mouth texture. This versatile dough is perfect for a cookie press or as a roll-out cookie.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 10 mins | 30 mins | 3–4 dozen cookies |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* electric mixer
* large bowl
* sifter
* baking sheets
* wire rack

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 Cup (2 sticks) | [[Butter]] | softened |
| 2/3 Cup | [[Sugar]] | granulated |
| 1 | [[Egg]] | large |
| 1 tsp | [[Vanilla]] | extract |
| 2 1/4 Cups | [[Cake Flour]] | sifted |
| 1/2 tsp | [[Baking Powder]] | None |
| 1/8 tsp | [[Salt]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Dough
1. In a large bowl, cream together the **1 Cup (2 sticks) butter** and **2/3 Cup sugar** with an electric mixer until light and fluffy.
2. Add the **1 egg** and **1 tsp vanilla** and mix well.
3. In a separate bowl, sift together the **2 1/4 Cups cake flour**, **1/2 tsp baking powder**, and **1/8 tsp salt**.
4. Gradually add the dry ingredients to the wet ingredients, mixing on low speed until just combined.
5. Chill the dough in the refrigerator for at least **30 minutes**.

### Step 2: Bake
6. Preheat the oven to **375°F**.
7. Shape the dough as desired using a cookie press or by rolling and cutting, then place on ungreased baking sheets.
8. Bake for **8 to 10 minutes**, or until the edges are just beginning to turn golden.
9. Let the cookies cool on the baking sheet for a minute before transferring them to a wire rack to cool completely.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The dough can be prepared and chilled for up to 2 days before baking, or frozen for up to 1 month.
