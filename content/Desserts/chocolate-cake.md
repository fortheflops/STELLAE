# Chocolate Cake

> *A classic homemade chocolate cake recipe featuring a rich cocoa flavor and moist crumb. This simple traditional dessert comes together easily for any occasion.*

## Ingredients

### Cake Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 cup | [[Butter]] | Softened |
| 2 cups | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 1/2 cup | [[Buttermilk]] | Room temperature |
| 1 tsp | [[Soda]] | Baking soda |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] | Whole |
| 2 1/3 cups | [[Flour]] | All-purpose |
| 3 heaping tbsp | [[Cocoa]] | Unsweetened |
| 1 cup | [[Boiling Water]] | Hot |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing the Batter
1. Cream together the **1/2 cup butter** and **2 cups sugar** until light and fluffy.
2. Beat in the **2 whole eggs** one at a time, mixing well after each addition.
3. In a separate bowl, combine the **2 1/3 cups flour**, **3 heaping tbsp cocoa**, and **1 tsp soda**.
4. Alternate adding the dry mixture and **1/2 cup buttermilk** to the butter mixture, stirring just until combined.
5. Carefully stir in the **1 cup boiling water** until the batter is smooth and thin.

### Step 2: Baking
6. Pour the batter into a greased and floured baking pan.
7. Bake at 350 degrees F until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean and the cake springs back slightly to the touch.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The baked cake can be wrapped tightly and stored at room temperature for up to 2 days or frozen for up to 3 months before frosting.
