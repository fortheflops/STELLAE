# Cherry Upside-Down Pudding

> 📜 **Collection:** UK15 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A delightful self-saucing vintage dessert featuring a tender vanilla cake baked over a sweet cherry base. Perfect for a cozy family treat served warm.*

---

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 45 mins | None | 8 servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 8x8 inch baking pan
* Mixing bowls
* Sifter
* Electric mixer or wooden spoon
* Small bowl

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 1/3 C | [[Flour]] | Sifted |
| 1/2 tsp | [[Baking Powder]] | Sifted with flour |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Salt]] | Sifted with flour |
| 1/2 C | [[Oleo]] | Or shortening, softened |
| 1 C | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 1 | [[Egg]] | Large |
| 1 tsp | [[Vanilla]] | Extract |
| 1 C | [[Milk]] | Room temperature |

### For the Sauce and Cherries
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 C | [[Sugar]] | For the sauce |
| 1/2 C | [[Boiling Water]] | Hot |
| 1 Can | [[Cherries]] | Drained, approx. 15 oz |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Batter
1. Preheat oven to **350°F**. Sift **flour**, measure and re-sift with **baking powder** and **salt**.
2. Cream the **oleo/shortening**, add **1 cup of sugar** and cream until light and fluffy.
3. Beat in the **egg** and **vanilla**.
4. Add dry ingredients alternately with **milk**, beginning and ending with dry ingredients, mixing until just combined.

### Step 2: Assemble and Bake
5. Pour the batter into a buttered 8x8 inch baking pan. Scatter **cherries** evenly over the batter.
6. Prepare the sauce by dissolving **1/2 cup of sugar** in **1/2 cup of boiling water**. Pour this hot sauce over the batter and cherries. Do not mix.
7. Bake for **35 to 45 minutes**, or until the cake portion is golden brown and a toothpick inserted comes out clean with a bubbly sauce underneath. Let cool slightly before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Best served warm shortly after baking, but leftovers can be covered and refrigerated for up to 2 days.
