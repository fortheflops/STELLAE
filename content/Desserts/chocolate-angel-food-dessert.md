# Chocolate Angel Food Dessert

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A decadent layered chilled dessert featuring rich chocolate mousse folded with whipped cream and pieces of angel food cake. This classic no-bake treat is perfect for preparing ahead and serving in elegant squares.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 30 mins | 10 mins | 24 hours | 8 servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* double boiler
* mixing bowls
* whisk
* baking dish

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 (6 oz) pkg | [[Chocolate Chips]] | melted |
| 1 tbsp | [[Sugar]] | for chocolate mixture |
| 4 | [[Eggs]] | separated |
| 1 cup | [[Whipped Cream]] | plus extra for garnish |
| 1 tbsp | [[Sugar]] | for egg whites |
| 1 tsp | [[Sugar]] | for cream |
| 1 small | [[Angel Food Cake]] | broken into bite-sized pieces |
| to taste | [[Walnut Meats]] | chopped for garnish |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare Chocolate Base
1. Melt **6 oz chocolate chips** in a double boiler.
2. Add **1 tbsp sugar** and **4 well-beaten egg yolks** to the chocolate, stirring gradually until the mixture is smooth.
3. Remove from heat and let the mixture cool.

### Step 2: Whip and Fold
4. In a separate bowl, add **1 tbsp sugar** to the **4 egg whites** and beat until stiff peaks form.
5. In another bowl, add **1 tsp sugar** to the **1 cup whipped cream** and whip until thickened.
6. Gently fold the **egg whites** and **whipped cream** into the cooled **chocolate mixture**.

### Step 3: Assembly and Chilling
7. Place a layer of **broken angel food cake** in the bottom of a baking dish.
8. Cover with a portion of the **chocolate mixture**.
9. Repeat with a second layer of **broken angel food cake** and the remaining **chocolate mixture**, ensuring the cake is completely covered.
10. Cover with waxed paper and chill for 24 hours.
11. Cut into squares and garnish with additional **whipped cream** and **walnut meats** before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This dessert requires a minimum of 24 hours of chilling time to set properly, making it an ideal make-ahead recipe.
