# Creamy Apricot Jello Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic molded Jello salad featuring pureed apricots and crunchy pecans blended into a creamy lemon Jello and cream cheese base. This retro dessert offers a delightful balance of tangy fruit and rich, velvety texture.*

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment

* Medium mixing bowl
* Wire whisk

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 package (3 oz) | [[Lemon Jello]] | dry |
| 1 small package (3 oz) | [[Cream Cheese]] | softened |
| 1 Cup | [[Boiling Water]] | None |
| 1 can (15-16 oz) | [[Apricots]] | drained, juice reserved |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Apricot Juice]] | reserved from can |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Chopped Pecans]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Base
1. Drain the **1 can apricots**, reserving **1/2 cup apricot juice**. Place the drained **apricots** in a blender and pulverize until smooth.
2. In a medium bowl, use a fork to mash and blend the **1 package lemon jello** powder with the **1 small package cream cheese** until it forms a crumbly mixture.

### Step 2: Combine and Set
3. Carefully pour the **1 cup boiling water** over the **lemon jello** and **cream cheese** mixture. Whisk well until completely dissolved and smooth.
4. Stir in the pulverized **apricots** and the **1/2 cup apricot juice**.
5. Fold in the **1/2 cup chopped pecans**.
6. Pour the mixture into a mold or serving dish and refrigerate for at least 4 hours, or until completely firm and set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This salad must be made at least 4 hours in advance to set properly; keep refrigerated until ready to serve.
