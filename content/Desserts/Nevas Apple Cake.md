# Neva's Apple Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Neva | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A wonderfully moist and dense apple cake featuring grated Winesap apples and crunchy nuts. This classic dump-style recipe creates a rich, flavorful dessert perfect for any occasion.*

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 9x13 inch pan or tube pan
* Large mixing bowl
* Whisk
* Spatula

| 3 Cups | [[Flour]] | all-purpose |
| 2 Teaspoons | [[Baking Soda]] | leavening |
| 1/2 Teaspoon | [[Salt]] | fine |
| 2 Teaspoons | [[Vanilla]] | extract |
| 3 Cups | [[Winesap Apples]] | grated |
| 1 Cup | [[Nuts]] | chopped |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation
1. Preheat your oven to 350°F. Grease and flour a 9x13 inch pan or a tube/Bundt pan.

### Step 2: Mixing the Batter
2. In a large bowl, mix the **1 cup Wesson oil**, **2 cups sugar**, and **2 eggs** until well combined.
3. Stir in the **2 teaspoons vanilla** and the **3 cups grated Winesap apples**.
4. In a separate bowl, whisk together the **3 cups flour**, **2 teaspoons baking soda**, and **1/2 teaspoon salt**.
5. Add the dry ingredients to the wet apple mixture and stir with a spatula until just combined; the batter will be very thick.
6. Fold in the **1 cup nuts**.

### Step 3: Baking
7. Spread the batter evenly into the prepared pan.
8. Bake for **1 hour to 1 hour and 15 minutes**, or until a long wooden skewer inserted into the center comes out clean.
9. Let the cake cool in the pan until firm before slicing and serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This cake stays moist for several days when stored in an airtight container at room temperature.
