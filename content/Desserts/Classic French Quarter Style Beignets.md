# Classic French Quarter Style Beignets

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic recipe for light, airy, and pillowy beignets made with a simple choux pastry. These deep-fried doughnuts are generously dusted with powdered sugar and best served warm.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 15 mins | 10 mins | 12-18 beignets |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Heavy-bottomed pot
* Wooden spoon
* Stand mixer
* Deep fryer or large heavy pot
* Slotted spoon
* Paper towels

| 2 Teaspoons | [[Sugar]] | None |
| 1 Cup | [[Cake Flour]] | None |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] | Large |
| 1 | [[Egg Yolk]] | Large |
| 3-4 cups | [[Vegetable Oil]] or [[Shortening]] | For frying |
| As needed | [[Confectioners Sugar]] | For dusting |

## Instructions

### Step 1: Make the Dough
1. In a heavy pot, combine the **1 stick (1/2 cup) butter**, **1 cup water**, and **2 teaspoons sugar**. Bring to a rolling boil over medium-high heat.
2. Remove from heat and add the **1 cup cake flour** all at once. Stir vigorously with a wooden spoon until the mixture forms a smooth ball that pulls away from the sides of the pot. Let it cool for 5-10 minutes.

### Step 2: Incorporate Eggs and Heat Oil
3. Add the **2 eggs** and **1 egg yolk** one at a time, beating thoroughly after each addition until the dough becomes a smooth, glossy paste.
4. In a deep, heavy pot, heat **3-4 cups vegetable oil or shortening** to 375°F (190°C).

### Step 3: Fry and Serve
5. Drop rounded tablespoons of dough into the hot oil, being careful not to overcrowd the pot. Fry in batches for 2-3 minutes per side, turning as needed, until puffed and golden brown.
6. Remove the beignets with a slotted spoon and drain on paper towels. While still warm, dust them generously with **confectioners' sugar** and serve immediately.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Beignets are best enjoyed fresh and hot immediately after frying; the dough cannot be successfully made far in advance.
