# Jill's Perfect Pie Crust

> 📜 **Collection:** JC | ✍️ **Attribution:** Jill | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A simple, flaky pie crust recipe from Jill using shortening and ice water for a tender texture. This versatile recipe provides quantities for a single 9-inch crust, which can be easily doubled for a two-crust pie.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 0 mins | 30 mins | One single 9-inch crust |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Medium mixing bowl
* Wire whisk
* Pastry blender
* Fork
* Plastic wrap

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 cup | [[All-Purpose Flour]] | None |
| 1/2 teaspoon | [[Salt]] | None |
| 1/3 cup + 1 tbsp | [[Shortening]] | cold |
| 2 to 3 tablespoons | [[Water]] | ice cold |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mix Dry Ingredients
1. In a medium bowl, whisk together the **1 cup all-purpose flour** and **1/2 teaspoon salt** until well combined.

### Step 2: Cut in Shortening
2. Add the cold **1/3 cup + 1 tbsp shortening**. Using a pastry blender or your fingertips, cut the shortening into the flour until the mixture resembles coarse crumbs with some pea-sized pieces remaining.

### Step 3: Add Water and Form Dough
3. Sprinkle the **2 to 3 tablespoons ice water** over the mixture, one tablespoon at a time, tossing lightly with a fork after each addition until the dough starts to come together.

### Step 4: Shape and Chill
4. Gently gather the dough into a ball, handling it as little as possible. Wrap the dough in plastic and chill for at least **30 minutes** before rolling out on a lightly floured surface until smooth and pliable.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The wrapped dough can be stored in the refrigerator for up to 3 days or frozen for up to 3 months before rolling out.
