# Sunshine Orange Jello Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** UK15 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A bright and intensely flavorful Jello salad packed with triple the orange taste from orange Jello, mandarin oranges, and a boost of frozen orange juice concentrate.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 5 mins | 4 hours | 8–10 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 4-cup measuring cup
* Large mixing bowl
* Whisk
* Serving dish or mold

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 large package (6 oz) | [[Orange Jello]] | powder |
| 1 can (15 oz) | [[Mandarin Oranges]] | drained, juice reserved |
| 1 can (15 oz) | [[Crushed Pineapple]] | drained, juice reserved |
| 1 can (6 oz) | [[Frozen Orange Juice]] | thawed |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Liquid Base
1. Drain the juice from the **15 oz mandarin oranges** and **15 oz crushed pineapple** into a large measuring cup.
2. Add the **6 oz frozen orange juice** concentrate to the measuring cup.
3. Add enough cold water to the combined juices to reach a total of **2 cups** of liquid.

### Step 2: Dissolve and Combine
4. In a large bowl, dissolve the **6 oz orange Jello** in **2 cups** of boiling water, stirring for 2 minutes until fully dissolved.
5. Stir the **2 cups** of cold juice mixture into the hot **orange Jello**.

### Step 3: Chill and Assemble
6. Refrigerate the mixture until it reaches the consistency of unbeaten egg whites.
7. Gently fold in the drained **15 oz mandarin oranges** and **15 oz crushed pineapple**.
8. Pour into a mold or serving dish and refrigerate for at least 4 hours until completely firm and set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This salad can be prepared up to 24 hours in advance; keep covered in the refrigerator until ready to serve.
