# Molasses Cookies

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic heirloom recipe for spiced molasses cookies packed with warm ginger, cinnamon, and plump raisins. These soft-baked treats offer a nostalgic aroma and rich, comforting flavor.*

| 15 mins | 12 mins | None | 24 cookies |

## Ingredients

### Cookie Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 cup | [[Shortening]] | Softened |
| 1/2 cup | [[Molasses]] | Unsulfured |
| 1 cup | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 1 | [[Egg]] | Beaten |
| 3/4 cup | [[Evaporated milk]] | Combined with 3/4 tsp vinegar |
| 3/4 cup | [[Sour milk]] | Alternative or prepared |
| 3 cups | [[Flour]] | Sifted |
| 1 tsp | [[Salt]] | None |
| 1 tsp | [[Cinnamon]] | Ground |
| 1 tsp | [[Ginger]] | Ground |
| 1 tsp | [[Baking soda]] | Referred to as soda |
| As needed | [[Raisins]] | Optional mix-in |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing the Base
1. Cream together the **1/2 cup shortening**, **1/2 cup molasses**, and **1 cup sugar** in a large mixing bowl until smooth.
2. Beat in the **1 egg** until fully incorporated.

### Step 2: Combining Wet and Dry
3. In a separate bowl or measuring cup, prepare the milk mixture using the **3/4 cup evaporated milk** mixed with vinegar, or use **3/4 cup sour milk**.
4. In another bowl, sift together the **3 cups flour**, **1 tsp salt**, **1 tsp cinnamon**, **1 tsp ginger**, and **1 tsp baking soda**.
5. Alternately add the dry ingredients and the milk mixture to the creamed molasses base, mixing just until combined.
6. Fold in a handful of **raisins** if desired.

### Step 3: Baking
7. Drop spoonfuls of dough onto a prepared baking sheet.
8. Bake at 375 degrees F until the cookies are set in the center and golden brown around the edges, yielding a soft and tender texture.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The dough can be prepared and chilled in the refrigerator for up to 24 hours before dropping and baking.
