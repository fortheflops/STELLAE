# Classic Sand Tarts

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic recipe for buttery, melt-in-your-mouth shortbread cookies filled with chopped pecans and rolled in powdered sugar. These delicate treats are also known as Mexican Wedding Cookies or Russian Tea Cakes.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 10 mins | None | 3 1/2 dozen |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* large mixing bowl
* baking sheets
* parchment paper
* wire cooling rack

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 pound | [[Butter]] | 2 sticks, softened |
| 3 tablespoons | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Vanilla]] | Extract |
| 2 3/4 cups | [[Flour]] | Sifted |
| 1 cup | [[Pecans]] | Finely chopped |
| 1 cup | [[Powdered Sugar]] | For rolling |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare Dough
1. Preheat oven to 350°F and line baking sheets with parchment paper.
2. In a large bowl, cream the **1/2 pound butter** and **3 tablespoons sugar** together until light and fluffy.
3. Stir in the **1 teaspoon vanilla**, then gradually work in the **2 3/4 cups flour** and **1 cup pecans** until a stiff dough forms.

### Step 2: Shape and Bake
4. Pinch off marble-sized pieces of dough and shape into small fingers or crescents.
5. Place on baking sheets and bake for **10 minutes** until the bottoms are lightly browned and the tops remain pale.

### Step 3: Coat and Cool
6. While still warm, gently roll the cookies in **1 cup powdered sugar** to coat.
7. Transfer to a wire rack to cool completely; roll in sugar a second time if desired for a thicker coating.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to one week or freeze for up to three months.
