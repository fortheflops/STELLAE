# Yummy Bars

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *Indulge in these rich Yummy Bars featuring layers of graham crackers, chocolate, butterscotch, coconut, and nuts bound together with sweetened condensed milk. Baked to golden perfection, they make a delightful treat for any occasion.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 25 mins | None | 12 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 11x9-inch baking pan

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 stick | [[Margarine]] | Melted |
| 1 1/4 cups | [[Graham Crackers]] | Crushed (reserve 1/4 cup for top) |
| 1 cup | [[Chocolate Pieces]] | Choc pieces |
| 1 cup | [[Butterscotch Pieces]] | Butterscotch pieces |
| 1 cup | [[Coconut]] | Shredded |
| 1/2 cup | [[Nuts]] | Chopped |
| 1 can | [[Sweetened Condensed Milk]] | Eagle Brand |

---

## Instructions

### Assembly and Baking
1. Melt **1 stick margarine** in an **11x9-inch baking pan**.
2. Add **1 cup graham crackers** and press to form a crust.
3. Make layers of the **1 cup chocolate pieces**, **1 cup butterscotch pieces**, **1 cup coconut**, and **1/2 cup nuts**, then pour **1 can sweetened condensed milk** evenly over the top.
4. Pour the reserved **1/4 cup graham crackers** crumbs on top.
5. Bake in a **325° to 350° oven** for **20 to 25 minutes** until golden and bubbly.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 3 days or refrigerate to keep them firmer for longer.
