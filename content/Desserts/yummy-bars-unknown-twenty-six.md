# Yummy Bars

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Marcile | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *Indulgent layered magic cookie bars featuring a buttery graham cracker crust, chocolate and butterscotch chips, coconut, and nuts bound together with sweetened condensed milk. These rich, chewy bars are wonderfully simple to assemble and bake to golden perfection.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 25 mins | Cooling time | 12 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 11x9 baking pan

---
## Ingredients

### Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 stick | [[Margarine]] | melted |
| 1 1/4 cups | [[Graham crackers]] | crushed |
| 1 cup | [[Chocolate pieces]] | choc. chips |
| 1 cup | [[Butterscotch pieces]] | butterscotch chips |
| 1 cup | [[Coconut]] | flaked |
| 1/2 cup | [[Nuts]] | chopped |
| 1 can | [[Sweetened condensed milk]] | Eagle brand |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Layering
1. Melt the **1 stick margarine** directly in an **11x9 baking pan**.
2. Add the **1 1/4 cups graham crackers** and press into the bottom to form a crust.
3. Make subsequent layers with the **1 cup chocolate pieces**, **1 cup butterscotch pieces**, **1 cup coconut**, and **1/2 cup nuts**.
4. Pour the **1 can sweetened condensed milk** evenly over all the layers.

### Step 2: Baking
5. Bake in a preheated oven at 325-350 degrees F for **25 minutes** until edges are golden brown and bubbling.
6. Allow to cool completely before cutting into bars.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 3 days, or refrigerate for a firmer texture.
