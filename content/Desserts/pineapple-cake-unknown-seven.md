# Pineapple Cake

## Instructions

### Baking the Cake
1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C).
2. In a mixing bowl, combine by hand **1 1/2 cups sugar**, **2 cups flour**, **1 tsp baking soda**, **1/2 cup Wesson oil**, **1/2 tsp salt**, and **1 (#2) can crushed pineapple** including juice until fully mixed.
3. Pour batter into a greased loaf pan.
4. Bake at 350°F for **35 minutes** until set and golden brown.

### Preparing & Adding Icing
5. In a saucepan, combine **1 1/2 cups sugar**, **1 small can evaporated milk**, **1 stick oleo**, **1 tsp vanilla**, and **1 cup coconut**.
6. Cook over medium heat, stirring continuously, until thickened.
7. Pour the hot cooked icing directly over the hot cake immediately after removing it from the oven.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store tightly covered at room temperature for up to 3 days. The rich icing keeps the cake moist.
