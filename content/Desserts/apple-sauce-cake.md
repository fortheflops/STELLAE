## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing and Baking
1. Cream together the **1/2 C. butter** and **1 C. sugar** until light and fluffy.
2. Beat in the **1 egg** and **1 C. unsweetened applesauce** along with a splash of **vanella**.
3. Sift together the **2 C. flour**, **1 t. soda**, **1/4 t. baking powder**, and **spices**, then gently blend into the wet mixture.
4. Fold in the **1/2 C. nuts** and **1/2 C. raisins** until evenly distributed.
5. Pour batter into a greased baking pan and bake until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean and the top springs back lightly to the touch.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Can be baked a day ahead and stored in an airtight container at room temperature.
