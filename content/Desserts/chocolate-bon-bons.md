# Chocolate Bon Bons

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic no-bake confection featuring a rich peanut butter and date center dipped in a smooth chocolate coating. These bite-sized treats are perfect for holiday gift-giving or satisfying a sweet tooth.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 30 mins | 10 mins | 1 hour | 24 bon bons |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Double boiler
* Baking sheet
* Wax paper

## Ingredients

### Filling Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 cup | [[Peanut Butter]] | Creamy |
| 1 cup | [[Powdered Sugar]] | Sifted |
| 1 cup | [[Dates]] | Chopped |
| 1 cup | [[Nuts]] | Chopped |
| 1 TBS | [[Margarine]] | Softened |

### Coating Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 6 oz | [[Chocolate Chips]] | Semi-sweet |
| 2 squares | [[Baking Chocolate]] | Unsweetened |
| 1/2 cube | [[Paraffin Wax]] | Food grade |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare Centers
1. In a large bowl, combine **1 cup peanut butter**, **1 cup powdered sugar**, **1 cup chopped dates**, **1 cup chopped nuts**, and **1 TBS softened margarine**.
2. Mix thoroughly until well combined, then roll the mixture into small 1-inch balls.

### Step 2: Coating
3. Using a double boiler, melt together **6 oz chocolate chips**, **2 squares baking chocolate**, and **1/2 cube paraffin wax** until smooth and glossy.
4. Dip each ball into the chocolate mixture, ensuring even coverage, and place on wax paper to let harden completely.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to two weeks or freeze for longer storage.
