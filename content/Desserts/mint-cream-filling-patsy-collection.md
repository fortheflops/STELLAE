# Mint Cream Filling

> 📜 **Collection:** Patsy Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A smooth and creamy mint-flavored filling perfect for layering over cookies or desserts. Features sweet butter, heavy cream, and peppermint flavor with optional green coloring.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | None | 1 hour | 12 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Electric mixer

---
## Ingredients

### Filling Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 1/2 cups | [[Powdered sugar]] | Sifted |
| 3 tablespoons | [[Sweet butter]] | Softened |
| 2 tablespoons | [[Heavy cream]] | Chilled or room temperature |
| 3/4 teaspoon | [[Peppermint flavoring]] | Adjust to taste |
| 1 drop | [[Green food coloring]] | Optional |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing and Spreading
1. Beat together all the **1 1/2 cups powdered sugar**, **3 tablespoons sweet butter**, **2 tablespoons heavy cream**, and **3/4 teaspoon peppermint flavoring** until smooth and creamy.
2. If you like, gently mix or sprinkle a drop of **1 drop green food coloring** into this **mint cream** until your desired color is reached.
3. Spread the **mint cream filling** evenly over the cookie layer, cover, and chill until firm, about 1 hour.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the prepared filling covered in the refrigerator for up to 24 hours before spreading.
