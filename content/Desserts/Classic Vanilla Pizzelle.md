# Classic Vanilla Pizzelle

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic recipe for light, thin, and crispy Italian pizzelle cookies. This simple batter, flavored with a generous amount of vanilla, is baked in a special iron to create beautiful, decorative wafers.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 20 mins | None | About 40 pizzelles |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* pizzelle iron
* large mixing bowl
* electric mixer
* wire cooling rack
* sifter

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 6 | [[Eggs]] | extra-large |
| 1 1/4 cups | [[Sugar]] | granulated |
| 1 cup (2 sticks) | [[Butter]] or [[Margarine]] | melted and cooled |
| 2 tablespoons | [[Vanilla]] | extract |
| 3 1/2 cups | [[Flour]] | all-purpose |
| 4 teaspoons | [[Baking Powder]] | sifted |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Batter
1. Preheat your pizzelle iron for 10-15 minutes.
2. In a large bowl, beat the **6 extra-large eggs** with an electric mixer until frothy.
3. Gradually add the **1 1/4 cups sugar**, beating until the mixture is thick, smooth, and the sugar has dissolved.
4. Stir in the **1 cup melted butter or margarine** and **2 tablespoons vanilla extract**.
5. In a separate bowl, sift together the **3 1/2 cups flour** and **4 teaspoons baking powder**.
6. Gradually add the dry ingredients to the egg mixture, mixing until a thick, sticky batter forms.

### Step 2: Bake and Cool
7. Drop a heaping tablespoon of batter onto the center of the preheated pizzelle iron.
8. Close the lid and bake for 30-60 seconds, or until the **pizzelle** is lightly golden and steam is no longer visible.
9. Carefully remove the hot **pizzelle** and let it cool flat on a wire rack; it will crisp as it cools. Repeat with the remaining batter.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store cooled pizzelles in an airtight container at room temperature for up to two weeks. Discard the first two pizzelles when starting a new batch to ensure the iron is properly seasoned.
