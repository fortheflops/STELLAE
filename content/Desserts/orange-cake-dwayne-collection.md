# Orange Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A wonderfully moist and vibrant citrus cake featuring orange gelatin, shredded coconut, and a sweet fresh orange glaze. This classic crowd-pleaser is baked in a tube pan and finished while warm for maximum flavor absorption.*

---

| 15 mins | 50 mins | 10 mins | 12 servings |

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Batter
1. Dissolve **1 pkg. orange gelatin** in **2/3 cup boiling water**. Set aside to cool completely.
2. In a large mixing bowl, beat **4 eggs** well. Add **2/3 cup vegetable oil** and the cooled **orange gelatin** mixture to the beaten eggs, mixing to combine.
3. Add **1 pkg. orange cake mix** to the wet mixture. Beat well with an electric mixer for **3 minutes** until smooth and fully incorporated.
4. Gently fold in **1 can coconut** until evenly distributed throughout the batter.

### Step 2: Bake and Glaze
5. Pour the batter into a greased tube pan. Bake at **350°F** for **50 to 60 minutes** until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.
6. While the cake is baking, prepare the icing by squeezing **1 large or 2 small oranges** to yield fresh juice, then mix the juice with **1 cup sugar** until dissolved.
7. Cool the baked cake in the pan for **10 minutes**, then invert it onto a serving plate right side up.
8. Drip the prepared **orange icing** over the cake while it is still warm so it absorbs the sweet citrus glaze.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This cake can be baked and glazed a day in advance. Store covered at room temperature to keep it moist and flavorful.
