# Jumble Oatmeal Cookies

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A hearty and chewy oatmeal drop cookie loaded with raisins, nuts, and spices, with a tender crumb from buttermilk. These classic cookies are packed with a delicious jumble of textures and warm flavors.*

---

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 15 mins | None | 24 large cookies |

| 1 Cup | [[Raisins]] | plump |

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare and Mix
1. Preheat your oven to 375°F and grease your cookie sheets.
2. In a large bowl, blend the **1 cup shortening**, **1 1/2 cups brown sugar**, **1 tsp salt**, **1/2 tsp cinnamon**, **1/2 tsp cloves**, **1/4 cup buttermilk**, and **2 eggs** together with an electric mixer until well combined.
3. In a separate bowl, sift together the **1 1/3 cups flour** and **3/4 tsp baking soda**.
4. Add the flour mixture to the wet ingredients and beat until smooth.
5. By hand, stir in the **1 1/2 cups oats**, **1 cup nuts**, and **1 cup raisins**.

### Step 2: Bake
6. Drop by rounded tablespoonfuls about 2 inches apart onto the greased cookie sheets.
7. Bake for **15 minutes**, or until the edges are golden brown and the centers are set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 5 days, or freeze for up to 3 months.
