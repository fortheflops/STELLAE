# Copycat See's 175 Fudge

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic, foolproof recipe for a rich and creamy chocolate nut fudge. This recipe uses melted marshmallows and a boiled evaporated milk syrup to create a smooth, no-fail texture.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 10 mins | 4 hours | 2-3 pounds |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 8x8 inch dish
* double boiler
* heavy-bottomed saucepan
* heatproof mixing bowl
* foil

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 | [[Marshmallows]] | standard size |
| 1 Tablespoon | [[Water]] | for melting |
| 3/4 Cup | [[Milk]] | evaporated, large can |
| 2 Cups | [[Sugar]] | granulated |
| 18 ounces | [[Bits]] | semi-sweet chocolate chips |
| 1 Cup | [[Nut Meats]] | chopped |
| 1 Teaspoon | [[Vanilla Extract]] | pure |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Butter]] | 1 stick |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation
1. Line an 8x8 inch dish with foil and butter the foil.
2. In a large, heatproof mixing bowl, combine the **18 ounces chocolate bits**, **1 cup nut meats**, **1/2 cup butter**, and **1 teaspoon vanilla extract**.

### Step 2: Melt Marshmallows
3. In the top of a double boiler, combine the **10 marshmallows** and **1 tablespoon water**. Heat and stir until the **marshmallows** are completely melted and smooth; keep warm.

### Step 3: Boil Syrup
4. In a heavy-bottomed saucepan, combine the **3/4 cup evaporated milk** and **2 cups sugar**. Bring to a full, rolling boil over medium heat, stirring constantly, and boil for **exactly 6 minutes**.

### Step 4: Combine and Set
5. Pour the melted **marshmallows** over the **chocolate bits** mixture, then immediately pour the hot **sugar-milk syrup** over everything.
6. Stir until the **chocolate bits** and **butter** are completely melted and the fudge is smooth and glossy.
7. Pour into the prepared dish and refrigerate for at least 4 hours until firm.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The fudge can be made ahead and stored in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to two weeks.
