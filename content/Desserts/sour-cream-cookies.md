# Sour Cream Cookies

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *Delightfully soft and tender Sour Cream Cookies made with a touch of almond and nutmeg. These drop cookies bake up quickly for a comforting treat.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 8 mins | None | 24 cookies |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Baking sheet

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 c. | [[Butter]] | Softened |
| 1 1/2 c. | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 1/2 c. | [[Sour cream]] | Standard |
| 1/2 t. | [[Almond extract]] | Liquid |
| 1 t. | [[Baking soda]] | Leavening agent |
| 1/4 t. | [[Salt]] | Fine grain |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] | Whole |
| 1 t. | [[Vanilla extract]] | Liquid |
| 3 1/4 c. | [[Flour]] | All-purpose |
| 1 t. | [[Nutmeg]] | Ground |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing and Baking
1. Cream together the **1/2 c. butter** and **1 1/2 c. sugar**. Mix in **1/2 c. sour cream**, **2 eggs**, **1 t. vanilla**, and **1/2 t. almond** extract.
2. Stir in **1 t. soda**, **1/4 t. salt**, **3 1/4 c. flour**, and **1 t. nutmeg** until just combined into a soft dough.
3. Drop the dough by spoonfuls onto a greased baking sheet.
4. Bake at 375 degrees for **8 minutes** until lightly golden and set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store baked cookies in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 3 days.
