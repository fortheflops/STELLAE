# Nutty Brandy & Rum Balls

> 📜 **Collection:** UK15 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A potent and delicious no-bake holiday confection, packed with a full pound of ground nuts. This classic recipe uses both brandy and rum, along with honey and vanilla wafer crumbs, for a rich and flavorful treat that keeps for weeks.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Mix
1. In a food processor, finely crush the **1 (12 oz) package vanilla wafers** and grind the **1 pound pecans or walnuts** until finely textured.
2. In a large bowl, combine the **crushed vanilla wafers**, **ground nuts**, **1/2 cup honey**, **1/2 cup rum**, and **1/2 cup brandy**.
3. Mix well with a sturdy spoon or your hands until a sticky, firm dough forms and holds its shape.

### Step 2: Shape and Coat
4. Shape the mixture into small, **1-inch balls**.
5. Roll each ball in **powdered sugar or granulated sugar** until completely coated and sparkling.
6. Store the finished balls in a tightly covered container. The flavor improves significantly after they have aged for a few days.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in a tightly covered container. The flavor improves significantly after aging for a few days, and they will keep for up to 5 weeks.
