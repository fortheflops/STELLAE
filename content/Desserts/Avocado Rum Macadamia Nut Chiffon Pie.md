# Avocado Rum Macadamia Nut Chiffon Pie

> 📜 **Collection:** PatsyCollection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Hotel Hana-Maui | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A unique and elegant chiffon pie featuring a light and airy filling of creamy avocado, a hint of rum, and a crunchy macadamia nut topping. Originating from the historic Hotel Hana-Maui, this dessert offers a delightful tropical twist.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Custard Base
1. In the top of a double boiler, whisk together the **1 cup strained ripe avocado**, **4 egg yolks**, **1/4 cup butter**, **dash of nutmeg**, **dash of cinnamon**, **2 Tbsp sugar**, and **juice of 1/2 lemon**.
2. Cook over simmering water, stirring constantly, until the mixture thickens like a custard and coats the back of a spoon. Do not let it boil.

### Step 2: Bloom the Gelatin and Cool Custard
3. While the custard cooks, sprinkle the **1 Tbsp gelatin** over the **1/4 cup rum** in a small bowl and let it soften for **5 minutes**.
4. Once the custard has thickened, remove it from the heat and stir in the softened **gelatin** and **rum** mixture until completely dissolved. Set the custard aside to cool completely to room temperature.

### Step 3: Whip the Meringue
5. In a separate, clean mixing bowl, beat the **4 egg whites** with an electric mixer until soft peaks form.
6. Gradually add the **1/2 cup sugar** and continue beating on high speed until stiff, glossy peaks form.

### Step 4: Assemble and Chill
7. Gently fold the cooled **avocado custard** into the beaten **egg whites** until no white streaks remain and the mixture is light and airy.
8. Pour the chiffon filling into the cooled, baked **pie crust** and spread evenly.
9. Sprinkle the **1/4 cup chopped macadamia nuts** over the top.
10. Chill the pie in the refrigerator for at least **4 to 6 hours**, or until completely firm and set.
11. Garnish with **whipped cream** before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the finished pie covered in the refrigerator for up to 2 days.
