# Turtle Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** UK15 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A decadent layered chocolate cake featuring a gooey center of melted caramels, chocolate chips, and toasted pecans. Made effortlessly with a German Chocolate cake mix for maximum flavor.*

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 9x13 inch baking pan
* Saucepan
* Wooden spoon
* Toothpick

| 1 lb. | [[Caramels]] | unwrapped |
| 1 Can (5 oz) | [[Evaporated Milk]] | small |
| 1 Cup | [[Chocolate Chips]] | semi-sweet |
| 1 Cup | [[Pecans]] | chopped |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: First Bake
1. Preheat your oven to **350°F**. Grease and flour a **9x13 inch baking pan**.
2. Prepare the **1 package [[German Chocolate Cake Mix]]** according to the package directions.
3. Pour **half of the batter** into the prepared pan and spread it evenly.
4. Bake this first layer for **15 minutes** until slightly set.

### Step 2: Caramel Filling and Assembly
5. While the cake is baking, combine the **1 lb. [[Caramels]]** and **1 can (5 oz) [[Evaporated Milk]]** in a saucepan. Melt over low heat, stirring constantly until smooth.
6. Once the first layer is done, remove it from the oven. Pour the hot caramel mixture evenly over the cake.
7. Sprinkle the **1 cup [[Chocolate Chips]]** and **1 cup [[Pecans]]** over the caramel layer.
8. Carefully spoon the remaining cake batter over the top, spreading it to cover the caramel and nuts.

### Step 3: Final Bake and Cooling
9. Return the pan to the oven and bake for an additional **20 minutes**, or until a toothpick inserted into the top cake layer comes out clean.
10. Let the cake cool completely in the pan before slicing until set and firm.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store tightly covered at room temperature for up to 3 days or refrigerate for up to 5 days.
