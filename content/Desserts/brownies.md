## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing and Baking
1. Combine **1 c. sugar** and **1/2 c. melted shortening** in a mixing bowl.
2. Beat in **2 eggs**, a pinch of **salt**, and **1/4 c. milk** until smooth.
3. Stir in **1 c. flour** and **4 T. cocoa** until just incorporated.
4. Pour the batter into a greased baking pan and bake until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean and the edges pull slightly away from the pan.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store baked brownies in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 3 days.
