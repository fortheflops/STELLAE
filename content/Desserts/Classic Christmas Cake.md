# Classic Christmas Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** UK15 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A rich, dense, and buttery holiday cake packed with a pound of pecans and a pound of white raisins, flavored with whiskey. This traditional fruitcake-style dessert is a festive centerpiece for the holiday season.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 25 mins | 1 hour 30 mins | 30 mins | 1 tube cake |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large tube or Bundt pan
* Large mixing bowls
* Electric mixer
* Wire cooling rack
* Wooden skewer

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 lb (4 sticks) | [[Butter]] | softened |
| 2 Cups | [[Sugar]] | standard granulated |
| 6 | [[Eggs]] | large |
| 4 Cups | [[Flour]] | all-purpose |
| 1 tsp | [[Baking Powder]] | none |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Whiskey]] | none |
| 1 lb. | [[Pecans]] | chopped |
| 1 lb. | [[White Raisins]] | none |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Batter
1. Preheat your oven to **325°F**. Generously grease and flour a large tube or Bundt pan.
2. In a large bowl, cream the **1 lb (4 sticks) butter** and **2 cups sugar** together with an electric mixer until light and fluffy.
3. Add the **6 large eggs** one at a time, beating well after each addition until fully incorporated.

### Step 2: Dry Ingredients and Fruit Mix
4. In a separate bowl, sift together the **4 cups flour** and **1 tsp baking powder**.
5. In another bowl, toss the **1 lb. chopped pecans** and **1 lb. white raisins** with about **1/2 cup** of the flour mixture until well coated to prevent sinking.
6. Add the remaining flour mixture to the creamed butter mixture in three parts, alternating with the **1/2 cup whiskey** in two parts, beginning and ending with the flour.
7. Fold in the flour-coated **pecans** and **white raisins** gently until evenly distributed, noting that the batter will be very thick.

### Step 3: Bake and Cool
8. Spoon the batter into the prepared pan and spread evenly until smooth.
9. Bake for **1 hour and 30 minutes**, or until a long wooden skewer inserted into the center comes out clean and golden brown and fragrant.
10. Let the cake cool in the pan for **20-30 minutes** before turning it out onto a wire rack to cool completely.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the tightly wrapped cake in an airtight container in a cool, dark place for up to several weeks, feeding occasionally with additional whiskey if desired.
