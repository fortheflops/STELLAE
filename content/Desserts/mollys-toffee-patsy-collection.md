# Molly's Toffee

> 📜 **Collection:** Patsy Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Molly | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A deliciously simple and addictive homemade toffee made with saltine crackers, butter, sugar, and chocolate. This sweet and salty treat is easy to whip up and perfect for sharing.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 11 mins | 30 mins | 12 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Cookie sheet
* Foil
* Saucepan
* Spatula

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 sleeve | [[Saltines]] | Salt side up |
| 1 cup | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 1 cup | [[Butter]] | Unsalted or salted |
| 12 oz | [[Chocolate chips]] | Semi-sweet |
| 1/2 cup | [[Pecans]] | Chopped |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and Boiling
1. Line a cookie sheet with foil and completely cover the surface with **1 sleeve saltines**, placing them salt side up.
2. In a saucepan, bring **1 cup sugar** and **1 cup butter** to a boil, stirring constantly, and let boil for **3 minutes**.

### Step 2: Baking and Topping
3. Pour the hot sugar and butter mixture evenly over the **1 sleeve saltines**.
4. Bake in a preheated oven at 350° for **8 minutes** until bubbly and golden.
5. Remove from the oven and immediately cover with **12 oz chocolate chips**, letting them sit for a minute to melt before spreading evenly with a spatula.
6. Sprinkle **1/2 cup pecans** evenly over the melted chocolate.
7. Chill in the refrigerator until firm, then break apart into bite-sized pieces.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator or freezer for up to 2 weeks.
