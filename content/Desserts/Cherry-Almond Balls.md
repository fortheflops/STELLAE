# Cherry-Almond Balls

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic holiday cookie featuring a whole candied cherry wrapped inside a tender, buttery almond shortbread dough. These delightful little bites are baked and then rolled in powdered sugar while still warm.*

### 🔪 Key Equipment

* electric mixer

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |

| 1/3 Cup | Sifted Confectioners' [[Sugar]] | for dough |
| 1 Teaspoon | [[Vanilla Extract]] | None |
| 1/4 Teaspoon | [[Salt]] | None |
| 2 Cups | Sifted All-Purpose [[Flour]] | None |
| 1/2 Cup | Finely Chopped [[Almonds]] | None |
| 60 | Whole Candied [[Cherries]] | approx. 5 dozen |
| As needed | Additional Sifted Confectioners' [[Sugar]] | for rolling |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare Dough
1. Preheat your oven to 325°F.
2. In a large bowl, cream together the **3/4 cup butter**, **1/3 cup confectioners' sugar**, **1 teaspoon vanilla extract**, and **1/4 teaspoon salt** until light and fluffy.
3. On low speed, gradually add the **2 cups all-purpose flour** and **1/2 cup almonds**; mix well until a firm dough forms.

### Step 2: Assemble and Bake
4. Take a teaspoon of dough and flatten it in your hand. Place one **whole candied cherry** in the center.
5. Wrap the dough around the **whole candied cherry**, completely enclosing it, and roll it into a smooth ball.
6. Place the balls on an ungreased baking sheet and bake for **20 minutes** until the cookies are set but not browned.

### Step 3: Finish
7. Let the cookies cool on the baking sheet for a few minutes until handleable.
8. While still warm, roll each ball in **additional sifted confectioners' sugar** until well coated, then place on a wire rack to cool completely.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to one week or freeze for up to three months.
