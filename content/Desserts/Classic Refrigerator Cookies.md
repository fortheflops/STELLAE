# Classic Refrigerator Cookies

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic, versatile recipe for slice-and-bake refrigerator cookies. This rich, buttery dough with brown sugar and nuts is chilled in a log, then sliced and baked for perfect, uniform cookies every time.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 10 mins | 2 hours | 4–5 dozen cookies |

| 1/2 Cup | [[White Sugar]] | none |
| 1 | [[Egg]] | slightly beaten |
| 2 Cups | [[Flour]] | none |
| 1/2 Teaspoon | [[Baking Soda]] | none |
| 1/4 Teaspoon | [[Salt]] | none |
| 1/2 Teaspoon | [[Vanilla]] | none |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Nuts]] | chopped |

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Dough
1. In a large bowl, cream the **1 cup butter**, **1/2 cup brown sugar**, and **1/2 cup white sugar** until light and fluffy.
2. Beat in the **1 egg** and **1/2 teaspoon vanilla**.
3. In a separate bowl, whisk together the **2 cups flour**, **1/2 teaspoon baking soda**, and **1/4 teaspoon salt**.
4. Gradually add the dry mixture to the creamed ingredients until a stiff dough forms, then stir in the **1/2 cup nuts**.

### Step 2: Chill and Shape
5. Divide the dough in half and shape each into a log 1.5 to 2 inches in diameter.
6. Wrap tightly in wax paper and chill for at least 1-2 hours until very firm to the touch.

### Step 3: Bake
7. Preheat oven to **400°F**.
8. Slice the logs into **1/4-inch thick rounds** and place on an ungreased cookie sheet.
9. Bake for **8-10 minutes** until the edges are golden brown and fragrant, then cool on a wire rack.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The dough logs can be kept in the refrigerator for up to 3 days or frozen for up to 1 month before slicing and baking.
