# Classic Coffee Nut Tortoni

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *An elegant, no-bake frozen dessert featuring a light, airy mousse made from coffee-infused whipped cream and sweet meringue. This classic treat is studded with toasted almonds and coconut for a delightful crunch.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 25 mins | 0 mins | 6 hours | 8 servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* chilled mixing bowl
* electric mixer
* 8 small soufflé cups or muffin tin
* paper liners
* spatula

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 Cup | [[Heavy Cream]] | well-chilled |
| 1/4 Cup | [[Sugar]] | divided |
| 1 Tbsp | [[Instant Coffee]] | none |
| 1 tsp | [[Vanilla Extract]] | none |
| A few drops | [[Almond Extract]] | none |
| 1 | [[Egg White]] | large, pasteurized recommended |
| 2 Tbsp | [[Sugar]] | for meringue |
| 1/4 Cup | [[Almonds]] | finely chopped, toasted |
| 1/4 Cup | [[Coconut]] | toasted and crumbled |
| Optional | [[Maraschino Cherry]] | for garnish |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Mousse Base
1. In a chilled bowl, whip the **1 cup heavy cream** until it begins to thicken.
2. Gently fold in the **1/4 cup sugar**, **1 tbsp instant coffee**, **1 tsp vanilla extract**, and **a few drops almond extract** until combined. Set aside.

### Step 2: Prepare the Meringue
3. In a separate clean, dry bowl, beat the **1 egg white** with an electric mixer until soft peaks form.
4. Gradually add the **2 tbsp sugar** while continuing to beat until a stiff, glossy meringue forms.

### Step 3: Assemble and Freeze
5. Gently fold the meringue and half of the **1/4 cup almonds** and **1/4 cup coconut** mixture into the whipped cream mixture until just combined.
6. Spoon the mixture evenly into 8 prepared soufflé cups or paper-lined muffin cups.
7. Sprinkle the remaining half of the **almonds** and **coconut** over the tops.
8. Freeze for at least 4-6 hours until firm. Garnish with a **maraschino cherry** before serving if desired.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Tortoni can be kept frozen in an airtight container for up to 2 weeks. For best texture, remove from the freezer 5 minutes before serving.
