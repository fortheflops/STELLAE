# Apple Torte

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A wonderfully simple and rustic apple torte featuring a dense, moist, fruit-and-nut-packed interior with a crisp, sugary crust. This vintage recipe uses minimal flour for a unique and delicious texture.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 45 mins | None | 8-10 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 8x12x2 inch baking pan
* Large bowl
* Spoon or electric mixer

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| ⅔ Cup | [[Flour]] | Sifted all-purpose |
| 3 Teaspoons | [[Baking Powder]] | None |
| ½ Teaspoon | [[Salt]] | None |
| 1 ½ Cups | [[Sugar]] | None |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] | Large |
| 3 Teaspoons | [[Vanilla Extract]] | None |
| 1 Cup | [[Nuts]] | Chopped |
| 2 Cups | [[Apples]] | Diced |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Mix Dry Ingredients
1. Preheat oven to **350°F**. Grease an **8x12x2 inch baking pan** well.
2. In a large bowl, stir together the **⅔ cup sifted flour**, **3 teaspoons baking powder**, **½ teaspoon salt**, and **1 ½ cups sugar** until combined.

### Step 2: Add Wet Ingredients and Fruit
3. Add the **2 large eggs** and **3 teaspoons vanilla extract** to the dry ingredients. Beat the mixture well with a spoon or an electric mixer until it forms a thick batter.
4. By hand, gently stir in the **1 cup chopped nuts** and **2 cups diced apples** until evenly distributed.

### Step 3: Bake and Serve
5. Pour the batter into the prepared pan and spread evenly until smooth.
6. Bake for **45 minutes** until the torte rises, falls slightly, and develops a golden, crisp crust.
7. Serve warm with ice cream or fresh cream.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the torte covered at room temperature for up to 2 days or in the refrigerator for up to 4 days.
