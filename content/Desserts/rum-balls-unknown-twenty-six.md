# Rum Balls

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Rose Marie Paulin | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *Discover a delightful recipe for classic Rum Balls, featuring finely rolled vanilla wafers, cocoa, dates, walnuts, and a hint of rum or brandy. These no-bake treats are easily shaped with buttered hands and finished with a sweet coating of powdered sugar.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | None | 30 mins | 24 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Measuring cups
* Measuring spoons

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 package | [[Vanilla wafers]] | Rolled fine, approx. 50 cookies |
| 3 tbsp | [[Cocoa]] | None |
| 1/2 cup | [[Dates]] | Chopped |
| 1/2 cup | [[Walnuts]] | Chopped |
| 1/2 cup | [[White Karo]] | Light corn syrup |
| 1/4 cup | [[Liquor]] | Rum or brandy |
| As needed | [[Butter]] | For greasing hands |
| As needed | [[P. Sugar]] | Powdered sugar for rolling |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing and Shaping
1. In a mixing bowl, mix together **1 package vanilla wafers**, **3 tbsp cocoa**, **1/2 cup chopped dates**, **1/2 cup walnuts**, **1/2 cup white karo**, and **1/4 cup liquor** until thoroughly combined.
2. Grease hands with **butter** to prevent sticking, then roll the mixture into small, uniform balls.
3. Roll each shaped ball thoroughly in **p. sugar** until evenly coated and set on a tray until firm.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to one week to allow the flavors to meld.
