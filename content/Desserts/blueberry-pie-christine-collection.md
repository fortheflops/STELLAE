# Blueberry Pie

> 📜 **Collection:** Christine Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic homemade blueberry pie featuring a sweet, spiced berry filling thickened with quick tapioca and baked to golden perfection. Perfect for summer gatherings or a comforting dessert any time of year.*

| 25 mins | 50 mins | 20 mins | 8 servings |

### Filling

| 1 tsp | [[Cinnamon]] | ground |
| 1/3 cup | [[Tapioca]] | quick-cooking |
| 6 cups | [[Blueberries]] | fresh or frozen (thawed and drained) |
| 1 tbsp | [[Lemon Juice]] | fresh |
| 2 tbsp | [[Butter]] | cut into small pieces for dotting |

### Crust
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Filling
1. Preheat oven to **425°F**.
2. In a large mixing bowl, stir together **1 cup sugar**, **1/4 cup brown sugar**, **1 tsp cinnamon**, and **1/3 cup quick tapioca**.
3. Add **6 cups blueberries** and **1 tbsp lemon juice** to the sugar mixture. Toss gently to coat, then set aside for **20 minutes** to let the tapioca soften and absorb the juices.

### Step 2: Assembly and Baking
4. Roll out one of the **14.1 oz refrigerated pie crusts** and fit it into the bottom of a 9-inch pie dish.
5. Spoon the blueberry mixture into the bottom crust, then dot the top evenly with **2 tbsp butter**.
6. Cover with the second pie crust, crimp the edges to seal, and cut vents in the top to let steam escape.
7. Bake at **425°F** for **45 to 50 minutes** until the crust is a deep golden brown and the blueberry filling is bubbling thickly through the vents.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The pie can be baked a day ahead and stored at room temperature. Leftovers can be covered and refrigerated for up to 4 days.
