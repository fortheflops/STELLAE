## Instructions

### Baking the Cake
1. Preheat oven and prepare either **two 9-inch layer pans** or a **9x13 pan**.
2. Combine the **1 pkg. white cake mix**, **1 pkg. instant chocolate pudding**, **2 eggs**, and **2 cups milk**.
3. Bake as ordinary mix directs until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.

### Making the Frosting
4. Combine the first 5 ingredients (**1 cup canned milk**, **1 cup sugar**, **3 egg yolks**, **1/4 lb. oleo**, and **1 tsp. vanilla**) and place in a pan to cook over medium heat for **12 minutes**, stirring constantly until mixture thickens.
5. Remove from heat, add **1 1/3 cups coconut** and **1 cup nuts**.
6. Beat until cool and of spreading consistency, then spread over the cake.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the frosted cake in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 2 days or refrigerate for up to 5 days.
