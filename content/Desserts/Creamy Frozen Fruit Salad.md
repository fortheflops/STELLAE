# Creamy Frozen Fruit Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic frozen fruit salad featuring a rich, smooth custard base that is cooked on the stovetop before being folded with fruit cocktail and frozen. This versatile, nostalgic treat can be served as a sweet dessert or a savory-style side salad.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare Custard
1. In the top of a double boiler, combine **1 tablespoon butter**, **2 egg yolks**, **2 tablespoons cornstarch**, **1/4 cup sugar**, **1/4 teaspoon salt**, and **3/4 cup milk**.
2. Whisk constantly over simmering water until the mixture is thick and smooth like a custard.

### Step 2: Cool and Combine
3. Remove from heat and beat in **1/4 cup lemon juice**.
4. Allow the custard to cool completely to room temperature.
5. Once cool, fold in the **29 oz fruit salad** (and optional whipped cream if desired).

### Step 3: Freeze and Serve
6. Pour the mixture into a freezer-safe container and freeze until firm, at least 4 hours.
7. Serve with **whipped cream** for a dessert or **mayonnaise** for a salad.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This salad can be frozen for up to 2 weeks; keep tightly covered to prevent ice crystals.
