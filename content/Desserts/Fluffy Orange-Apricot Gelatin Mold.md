# Fluffy Orange-Apricot Gelatin Mold

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *An elegant and airy gelatin mold featuring the bright flavors of orange and apricot. Whipped egg whites are folded into the base to create a light, mousse-like texture, studded with fresh fruit.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 5 mins | 6 hours | 8-10 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Medium saucepan
* Large bowl
* Clean bowl
* Electric mixer
* 1 1/2 quart mold

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 4 ½ Cups | [[Orange Juice]] | divided |
| ⅓ Cup | [[Sugar]] | none |
| 3 envelopes | [[Unflavored Gelatin]] | none |
| ¼ Cup | [[Apricot Nectar]] | none |
| 2 large | [[Egg Whites]] | none |
| 1 Pint | [[Mixed Fruit]] | pears, peaches, apricots, grapes, or berries |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Dissolve Gelatin
1. In a saucepan, heat **1 cup of orange juice** with the **sugar** and **unflavored gelatin**. Stir constantly over low heat until the gelatin and sugar are fully dissolved.

### Step 2: Mix and Chill
2. Remove from heat and stir in the remaining **3 ½ cups of orange juice** and the **apricot nectar**.
3. Chill the mixture in the refrigerator until it is syrupy and has the consistency of unbeaten egg whites.

### Step 3: Whip and Fold
4. In a clean bowl, beat the **egg whites** until soft peaks form.
5. Gently fold the whipped egg whites into the chilled gelatin mixture until well incorporated.
6. Fold in the mixed **fruit** gently.

### Step 4: Mold and Set
7. Pour into a **1 ½ quart mold** and refrigerate for at least **6 hours**, or overnight, until completely firm and set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the gelatin mold covered in the refrigerator for up to 2 days before serving.
