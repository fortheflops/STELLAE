# Maple Nut Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A delightful old-fashioned maple nut cake topped with a rich maple icing and crunchy pecans. Perfect for serving alongside afternoon tea or as a sweet ending to family dinner.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Making the Batter
1. Cream **1/3 cup shortening** with **1 cup light brown sugar** until fluffy.
2. Add **2 egg yolks** and mix well.
3. Add **3/4 cup milk** to the mixture and stir to combine.
4. Sift together **1 1/2 cups flour**, **1/2 teaspoon salt**, and **2 teaspoons Royal Baking Powder**, then add to the wet ingredients.
5. Mix in **1 cup nuts** (preferably pecans) and **1 teaspoon vanilla**.

### Step 2: Baking and Icing
6. Bake in a greased loaf pan in a moderate oven for **35 minutes**, or until a golden brown color is achieved and a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.
7. To make the maple icing: add **1/2 teaspoon butter** to **2 tablespoons milk**, stir in **1 1/2 cups confectioner's sugar** to make a smooth paste, and add **1/2 teaspoon maple flavoring**.
8. Cover top and sides of the cake with the maple icing and spread evenly.
9. Sprinkle with additional nuts while the icing is still soft.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the baked cake in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 2 days, or frost just before serving.
