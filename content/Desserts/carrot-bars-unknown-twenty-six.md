## Instructions

### Baking the Bars
1. Preheat oven to 325°.
2. In a large bowl, mix **1 1/3 cups oil**, **4 eggs**, and **2 cups sugar** until well combined.
3. In a separate bowl, sift together **2 cups flour**, **2 tsp soda**, **1 tsp salt**, **2 tsp cinnamon**, and **1 tsp nutmeg**.
4. Gradually add the dry ingredients to the wet mixture, stirring just until combined.
5. Fold in **3 jars carrot baby food** until smooth.
6. Pour batter into a greased baking pan and bake at 325° until golden brown and a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean, about 30 minutes. Let cool completely.

### Making the Icing
7. In a mixing bowl, beat **1 stick oleo** and **8 oz cream cheese** together until creamy.
8. Add **2 tsp vanilla** and mix well.
9. Gradually add **1 box approx powdered sugar** until the icing reaches a smooth, spreadable consistency.
10. Stir in **1 cup chopped nuts**.
11. Spread evenly over the cooled carrot bars until fully covered, slice, and serve.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store frosted bars in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days.
