# Lemon Jello Poke Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A supremely moist and tangy lemon poke cake made easy with a yellow cake mix and lemon Jello. While still warm, the cake is poked and saturated with a sweet lemon juice glaze.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare and Bake the Cake
1. Preheat your oven to **350°F**. Grease and flour a tube or Bundt pan.
2. In a large bowl, combine the **1 package yellow cake mix**, **1 (3 oz) package lemon jello**, **3/4 cup water**, **3/4 cup salad oil**, **4 whole eggs**, and **1 teaspoon lemon extract**.
3. Beat with an electric mixer on medium speed for **4-5 minutes** until the batter is smooth and well blended.
4. Pour the batter into the prepared pan.
5. Bake for **1 hour**, or until a long wooden skewer inserted into the center comes out clean and golden brown.

### Step 2: Make the Glaze and Finish
6. While the cake is baking, prepare the glaze by whisking together the **juice of 2 lemons** and **1 cup powdered sugar** in a small bowl until completely smooth.
7. Let the cake cool in the pan for about **10-15 minutes** after baking.
8. While the cake is still warm, use a fork or wooden skewer to poke holes all over the top of the cake.
9. Slowly spoon the **lemon glaze** over the warm cake, allowing it to soak deeply into the holes until fully absorbed.
10. Let the cake cool completely in the pan until set before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftover cake tightly wrapped or covered in the refrigerator for up to 4 days.
