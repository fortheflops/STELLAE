# Janet's Bizcochitos

> 📜 **Collection:** UK15 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Janet Preston | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic large-batch recipe for traditional New Mexican bizcochitos featuring crisp, tender shortbread flavored with anise and brandy. These cookies are finished with a signature dusting of cinnamon-sugar for a perfect holiday treat.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Dough
1. Preheat your oven to 350°F.
2. In a medium bowl, sift together the **6 cups flour**, **3 teaspoons baking powder**, and **1 teaspoon salt**.
3. In a separate large bowl, cream the **1 pound lard or butter** with the **1 1/2 cups sugar** and **2 teaspoons anise seed** until light and fluffy.
4. Beat the **2 eggs** until light, then add them to the creamed mixture and mix well.
5. Gradually add the dry ingredients to the wet mixture, along with just enough **1/4 cup brandy** to form a stiff, workable dough.

### Step 2: Shape and Bake
6. Gently knead the dough a few times on a lightly floured surface, then roll it out to 1/4 or 1/2 inch thickness.
7. Cut the dough into desired shapes.
8. In a small bowl, mix together the **1/4 cup sugar** and **1 teaspoon cinnamon** for the topping.
9. Dust the top of each cookie with the cinnamon-sugar mixture.
10. Bake on an ungreased cookie sheet for **10 minutes**, or until very lightly browned around the edges and fragrant.
