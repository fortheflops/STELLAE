# O. Henry Bars

> 📜 **Collection:** Georgia Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic mid-century dessert bar featuring a chewy, caramelized oat crust topped with a rich, melted chocolate and chunky peanut butter glaze. Perfect for bake sales, potlucks, or an easy afternoon treat.*

### 🔪 Key Equipment

* Mixing bowl

## Instructions

### Step 1: Bake the Crust
1. Preheat your oven to **350°F** and grease a **9x13-inch baking pan**.
2. In a large bowl, thoroughly mix the **4 cups oats**, **1 cup oleo**, **1 cup brown sugar**, and **1/2 cup white corn syrup**.
3. Spread and press the mixture evenly into the greased **9x13-inch baking pan**.
4. Bake for **15 minutes** at **350°F** until lightly golden around the edges. Remove from the oven and let cool slightly.

### Step 2: Add the Topping
5. In a small saucepan over low heat (or in a microwave-safe bowl), melt the **12 oz chocolate chips** and **1/2 cup chunky peanut butter** together, stirring constantly until completely smooth and combined.
6. Spread the warm chocolate-peanut butter mixture evenly over the top of the baked oat crust.
7. Allow the bars to cool completely until the topping is firm and set, then cut into squares.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 5 days, or refrigerate for a firmer texture.
