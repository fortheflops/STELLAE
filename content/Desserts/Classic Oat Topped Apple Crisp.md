# Classic Oat Topped Apple Crisp

> 📜 **Collection:** PatsyCollection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A traditional apple crisp featuring a tender, cinnamon-spiced apple filling and a unique, buttery oat crust on the bottom. This classic recipe delivers a perfect balance of sweet fruit and crunchy, crumbly topping.*

---

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 30 mins | None | 4–6 Servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* baking dish
* mixing bowls
* pastry blender

## Ingredients

### For the Filling
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 Cups | [[Apples]] | peeled and cut |
| 1/4 Tbsp | [[Lemon]] juice | fresh |
| 1/4 Cup | [[Brown Sugar]] | packed |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Cinnamon]] | ground |

### For the Topping
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Flour]] | all-purpose |
| 3/4 Cup | [[Brown Sugar]] | packed |
| 6 Tbsp | [[Butter]] | chilled and cut into pieces |
| 1 Cup | [[Instant Oat Flakes]] | rolled |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Topping
1. In a bowl, combine **1/2 cup flour** and **3/4 cup brown sugar**. Cut in the **6 tbsp chilled butter** using a pastry blender until the mixture resembles coarse crumbs, then stir in the **1 cup instant oat flakes**.

### Step 2: Prepare the Filling
2. In a separate bowl, toss the **2 cups apples** with **1/4 tbsp lemon juice**, **1/4 cup brown sugar**, and **1/4 tsp cinnamon** until evenly coated.

### Step 3: Assemble and Bake
3. Preheat oven to 375°F. Press half of the topping mixture into the bottom of a buttered baking dish.
4. Spread the **apple mixture** evenly over the bottom crust, then sprinkle the remaining topping over the fruit.
5. Cover with foil and bake for **30 minutes**, or until the fruit is tender and bubbly, removing the foil for the last 10 minutes for a golden brown, crisp finish.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The topping can be prepared in advance and stored in the refrigerator for up to 2 days before assembling and baking.
