# Clarice's No-Bake Chocolate Oatmeal Cookies

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Clarice | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic, quick no-bake cookie recipe featuring a rich boiled cocoa-sugar syrup folded with oats and nuts. These chewy, fudgy treats set perfectly at room temperature for an effortless dessert.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5 mins | 5 mins | 30 mins | 3 dozen cookies |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* medium saucepan
* waxed paper
* teaspoon

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 Cup | [[Sugar]] | granulated |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Oleo]] | margarine or butter, 1 stick |
| 3 Tbsp | [[Cocoa]] | powder |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Milk]] | whole or 2% |
| Pinch | [[Salt]] | fine |
| 3 Cups | [[Oats]] | quick-cooking |
| 1 Cup | [[Nuts]] | chopped |
| 1 tsp | [[Vanilla]] | extract |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Syrup
1. Lay out sheets of waxed paper on your counter.
2. In a medium saucepan, combine the **1 cup sugar**, **1/2 cup oleo**, **3 tbsp cocoa**, **1/2 cup milk**, and **pinch of salt**.
3. Bring the mixture to a full, rolling boil over medium heat, stirring occasionally.
4. Once it reaches a rolling boil, cook for **exactly 1 minute**.

### Step 2: Combine and Set
5. Remove the pan from the heat and immediately stir in the **1 tsp vanilla**, **3 cups oats**, and **1 cup nuts** until well coated.
6. Working quickly, drop the mixture by teaspoonfuls onto the waxed paper.
7. Let the cookies cool and set completely at room temperature for about **30 minutes** until firm and glossy.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 5 days or refrigerate for a firmer texture.
