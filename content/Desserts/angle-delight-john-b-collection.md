# Angle Delight

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A delightful frozen dessert combining crushed pineapple, apples, coconut, pecans, and cherries folded into sweet whipped cream. Perfect for a refreshing and easy treat.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | None | 4 hours | 6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Electric mixer
* Freezer-safe dish

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 (8 oz) can | [[Crushed Pineapple]] | Drained |
| 2 | [[Apples]] | Peeled and cored |
| 1/4 cup | [[Flaked Coconut]] | None |
| 1/2 cup | [[Pecans]] | Chopped |
| 1/4 cup | [[Maraschino Cherries]] | Chopped |
| 1 cup | [[Heavy Cream]] | Chilled |
| 2 Tbsp | [[Powdered Sugar]] | None |
| 1/2 tsp | [[Vanilla Extract]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation
1. Ensure the **1 (8 oz) can crushed pineapple** is thoroughly drained, and peel and core the **2 apples**.
2. In a mixing bowl, whip the **1 cup heavy cream** until stiff peaks form.
3. Add the **2 Tbsp powdered sugar** and **1/2 tsp vanilla extract** to the whipped cream and gently combine.

### Step 2: Mixing and Freezing
4. Fold the drained **1 (8 oz) can crushed pineapple**, prepared **2 apples**, **1/4 cup flaked coconut**, **1/2 cup pecans**, and **1/4 cup maraschino cherries** into the whipped cream mixture until evenly incorporated.
5. Transfer the mixture to a freezer-safe dish and freeze until firm to the touch.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Can be prepared up to a week in advance and kept frozen until ready to serve.
