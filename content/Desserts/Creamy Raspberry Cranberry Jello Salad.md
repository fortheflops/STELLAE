# Creamy Raspberry Cranberry Jello Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** UK15 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A festive and creamy holiday salad featuring a tangy sour cream base, tart cranberry sauce, and sweet raspberry gelatin. This classic chilled dessert is packed with pineapple, mandarin oranges, and crunchy pecans for a perfect texture.*

| 1 (8 oz) can | [[Pineapple]] | crushed, drained |
| 1 (11 oz) can | [[Mandarin Oranges]] | drained |

| 1 cup | [[Sour Cream]] | light |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Base
1. In a large bowl, dissolve the **1 (3 ounce) package Raspberry Jello** in **1 cup boiling water**, stirring for 2 minutes until fully dissolved.
2. Stir in **1 cup cold water** (or reserved fruit juice) and place in the refrigerator until the mixture reaches the consistency of unbeaten egg whites.

### Step 2: Combine and Set
3. In a separate bowl, stir together the **1 cup light sour cream** and **1 (14 oz) can whole berry cranberry sauce**.
4. Gently fold the **sour cream mixture**, **1 (8 oz) can crushed pineapple**, **1 (11 oz) can mandarin oranges**, and **1/2 cup chopped pecans** into the partially set **Raspberry Jello**.
5. Pour the mixture into a serving dish or mold and refrigerate for at least 4-6 hours until completely firm and set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This salad can be prepared up to 24 hours in advance; keep covered in the refrigerator until ready to serve.
