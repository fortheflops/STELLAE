# Hungarian Coffee Cake (Monkey Bread)

> 📜 **Collection:** LOC | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic, pull-apart Hungarian coffee cake made from a rich, yeasted sweet dough. Balls of dough are dipped in melted butter and a cinnamon-sugar-nut mixture, then layered in a tube pan and baked into a gooey, delightful treat.*

## Ingredients

## Instructions

### Step 1: Make the Sweet Dough
1. If using active dry yeast, dissolve **2 pkgs active dry yeast** in **½ cup lukewarm water** and let stand for **5 minutes** until foamy. Use only **1 ½ cups milk** in the next step.
2. In a very large bowl, combine the **2 cups lukewarm milk**, **½ cup sugar**, and **2 tsps salt**. Stir to dissolve.
3. Stir in the **½ cup soft shortening**, **2 eggs**, and the proofed yeast mixture.
4. Gradually add the **7-7 ½ cups sifted flour**, first with a spoon and then with your hands, until a soft dough forms.
5. Turn the dough out onto a lightly floured board and knead for about **5-8 minutes** until smooth and elastic.
6. Place the dough in a greased bowl, cover with a damp cloth, and let rise in a warm place for **1 ½ - 2 hours** until doubled.
7. Punch the dough down and let it rise again for another **30-45 minutes**.

### Step 2: Assemble and Bake
8. Take **half** of the prepared sweet dough for this recipe.
9. Preheat your oven to **375°F**. Generously grease a tube pan.
10. In a small bowl, mix together the **¾ cup sugar**, **1 tsp cinnamon**, and **½ cup finely chopped nuts**. Have your **½ cup melted butter** ready in another small bowl.
11. Cut the dough into small, walnut-sized pieces and roll them into balls.
12. Roll each ball first in the **½ cup melted butter**, then in the cinnamon-sugar-nut mixture.
13. Place the coated dough balls in the prepared pan, layering them loosely.
14. Let the assembled cake rise for **45 minutes**.
15. Bake for **35-40 minutes** until golden brown and cooked through. Let it cool in the pan for **5-10 minutes** before inverting it onto a serving plate. Serve warm.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The remaining half of the sweet dough can be refrigerated or used for another coffee cake or rolls within 2-3 days.
