*A simple and wonderfully fudgy brownie recipe from Jackie. This easy recipe uses Hershey's chocolate syrup for a rich, classic chocolate flavor and a perfectly chewy texture.*
### Author
Jackie
___
## Recipe
| Measurements      | Ingredients                                        |
| :---------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 cup (2 sticks)  | [[Oleo]] (Margarine or Butter), softened           |
| 1 cup             | [[Sugar]]                                          |
| 4                 | [[Eggs]]                                           |
| 1 cup + 1 Tbsp    | [[Flour]]                                          |
| 1 cup             | [[Hershey Chocolate Syrup]]                        |
___
### Instructions
1.  Preheat your oven to 350°F. Grease a 9x13 inch pan.
2.  In a large bowl, cream the softened oleo/butter and sugar together with an electric mixer until light and fluffy.
3.  Add the eggs one at a time, beating well after each addition.
4.  On low speed, gradually add the flour, mixing until it is just combined.
5.  Stir in the Hershey's syrup until the batter is smooth and uniform in color.
6.  Pour the batter into the greased pan.
7.  Bake for **30 minutes**, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out with moist, fudgy crumbs. Do not overbake.
8.  Let the brownies cool completely in the pan on a wire rack before cutting.
