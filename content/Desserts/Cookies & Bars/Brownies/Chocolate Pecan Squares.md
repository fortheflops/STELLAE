### Instructions
1.  Melt chocolate + butter together - mix well.
2.  Beat eggs until light & lemon colored, then gradually beat in sugar.
3.  Stir in salt, vanilla, milk.
4.  Gradually add flour. Stir in pecans.
5.  Line a buttered 8x8x2 pan with wax paper. Butter paper.
6.  Bake 350° - 30 m.
7.  Cut into 1 1/2" squares before removing from pan.
8.  Store in tightly covered container.
