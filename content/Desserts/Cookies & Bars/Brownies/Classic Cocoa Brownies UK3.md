*A classic, one-bowl recipe for rich and fudgy cocoa brownies. This simple method uses melted margarine and cocoa powder to create a timeless, easy-to-make treat.*
### Author
Unknown
___
## Recipe
| Measurements      | Ingredients                                        |
| :---------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| ½ Cup + 2 Tbsp    | [[Margarine]], melted                              |
| 1 Cup             | [[Sugar]]                                          |
| 6 Tbsp            | [[Cocoa Powder]]                                   |
| 2                 | [[Eggs]]                                           |
| ¾ Cup             | [[Flour]]                                          |
| ½ tsp             | [[Baking Powder]]                                  |
| Pinch             | [[Salt]]                                           |
| 1 tsp             | [[Vanilla Extract]]                                |
___
### Instructions
1.  Preheat your oven to **325°F**. Grease and flour a 9x9 inch square baking pan.
2.  In a medium mixing bowl, combine the melted margarine, sugar, and cocoa powder. Stir until well combined.
3.  Beat in the eggs, one at a time.
4.  In a separate small bowl, whisk together the flour, salt, and baking powder.
5.  Add the dry ingredients to the wet mixture and stir with a spatula until just combined. Do not overmix.
6.  Stir in the vanilla extract.
7.  Pour the batter into the prepared pan and spread it evenly.
8.  Bake for **30 minutes**. The brownies are done when a wooden toothpick inserted near the center comes out with moist crumbs attached.
9.  Let the brownies cool completely in the pan on a wire rack before cutting into squares.
