### Instructions
1.  Cream butter & peanut butter together, add sugar.
2.  Add egg, juice & rind.
3.  Stir in flour, sifted with salt & soda.
4.  Stir in nuts.
5.  Form in rolls. Wrap in waxed paper & chill overnight.
6.  Slice & bake.
