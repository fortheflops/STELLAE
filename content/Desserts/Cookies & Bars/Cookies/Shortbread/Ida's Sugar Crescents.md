### Instructions
1.  Cream butter, add sugar & blend well.
2.  Add flavoring.
3.  Add flour slowly & mix thoroughly.
4.  Fold in nut meats.
5.  Shape into small crescents about size of small finger.
6.  Place on greased baking sheet.
7.  Bake 350° - 20 m.
8.  Roll in pwd sugar while warm.
