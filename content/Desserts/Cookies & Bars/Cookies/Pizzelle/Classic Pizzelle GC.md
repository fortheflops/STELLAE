*A classic, very large-batch recipe for traditional Italian pizzelle. This recipe creates an enormous quantity of thin, crisp, wafer-like cookies, flavored with either vanilla or anise and baked in a special pizzelle iron.*
### Author
Unknown
___
## Recipe
| Measurements      | Ingredients                                        |
| :---------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 Pound (4 sticks)| [[Butter]], Melted and cooled                      |
| 15                | [[Eggs]]                                           |
| 3 Cups            | [[Sugar]]                                          |
| 5 Pounds (~20 cups)| [[Flour]]                                          |
| 6 Level Teaspoons | [[Baking Powder]]                                  |
| To Taste (2 Tbsp) | [[Vanilla Extract]] (or Anise Flavoring)           |
___
## Instructions
1.  **Preparation:** Preheat your pizzelle iron according to the manufacturer's instructions.
2.  **Mix Wet Ingredients:** In a very large bowl, beat the fifteen eggs well. Add the sugar and the cooled melted butter. Beat thoroughly until well combined.
3.  **Combine:** In a separate, very large bowl, whisk together the flour and baking powder. Gradually add the dry ingredients to the wet ingredients, mixing with a sturdy spoon or mixer on low speed until a soft, non-sticky dough forms.
4.  Stir in the vanilla or anise flavoring to taste.
5.  **Bake:** Take a small piece of dough and roll it into a 1-inch ball.
6.  Place one ball of dough on the center of the hot pizzelle iron grid.
7.  Close the lid and bake for 30-60 seconds, or until the pizzelle is lightly golden and steam stops coming from the iron.
8.  Carefully remove the hot pizzelle with a fork or spatula and let it cool flat on a wire rack (it will crisp as it cools).
9.  Repeat with the remaining dough.
___

>[!Warning]
>This is an extremely large recipe that makes "an awful lot" of cookies (likely over 20 dozen). It is highly recommended to **cut the recipe by a quarter** for a more manageable batch (e.g., 1 stick butter, ~4 eggs, ¾ cup sugar, 5 cups flour, 1.5 tsp baking powder).
