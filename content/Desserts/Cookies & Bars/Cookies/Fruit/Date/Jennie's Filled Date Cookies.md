### Instructions
**Filling**
1.  Cook dates, sugar, and water until thick, stirring constantly.
2.  Remove from heat, add chopped nuts, and cool.

**Dough & Assembly**
1.  Mix well shortening, sugar + eggs.
2.  Add other ingredients (liquid, vanilla, dry ingredients).
3.  Drop with tsp. onto ungreased cookie sheet.
4.  Place 1/2 tsp dough, then Date filling, and Top with 1/2 tsp dough.
