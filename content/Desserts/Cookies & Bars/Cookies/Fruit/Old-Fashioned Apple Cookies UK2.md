*A classic recipe for soft, cake-like drop cookies, packed with fresh chopped apples, raisins, and nuts. These hearty cookies have a wonderful spiced flavor from the brown sugar.*

| 1                 | [[Egg]]                                            |
| 2 Cups            | [[Flour]]                                          |
| 1 tsp             | [[Baking Soda]]                                    |

| 1/2 Cup           | [[Milk]]                                           |

| 1 Cup             | Chopped [[Nuts]]                                   |

### Instructions
1.  Preheat your oven to 400°F. Grease your cookie sheets.
2.  In a large bowl, cream the shortening and brown sugar together with an electric mixer. Beat in the egg.
3.  In a separate bowl, whisk together the flour, baking soda, and salt (and any optional spices).
4.  Add half of the dry ingredients to the creamed mixture and blend well.
5.  Stir in the milk.
6.  Add the remaining dry ingredients and mix until just combined.
7.  By hand, fold in the chopped apples, raisins, and nuts.
8.  Drop by rounded teaspoonfuls onto the prepared cookie sheets.
9.  Bake for **10-12 minutes**, or until the edges are golden brown.
