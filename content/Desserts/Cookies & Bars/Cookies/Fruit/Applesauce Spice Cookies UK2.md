*A classic recipe for soft, cake-like applesauce cookies. These old-fashioned drop cookies are packed with warm spices, raisins, and nuts for a hearty and delicious treat.*

## Recipe
| Measurements      | Ingredients                                        |
| :---------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| ¾ cup             | [[Shortening]]                                     |
| 1 cup             | [[Brown Sugar]], Packed                            |
| 1                 | [[Egg]]                                            |
| 1½ cups           | [[Applesauce]]                                     |
| 2¼ cups           | Sifted [[Flour]]                                   |
| ½ tsp             | [[Baking Soda]]                                    |
| ½ tsp             | [[Salt]]                                           |
| ¾ tsp             | [[Cinnamon]]                                       |
| ¼ tsp             | [[Cloves]]                                         |
| 1 cup             | [[Raisins]]                                        |
| ½ cup             | Chopped [[Nuts]]                                   |
___
### Instructions
1.  Preheat your oven to 375°F. Grease your cookie sheets.
2.  In a large bowl, thoroughly cream together the shortening, brown sugar, and egg with an electric mixer.
3.  Stir in the applesauce.
4.  In a separate bowl, sift together the flour, baking soda, salt, cinnamon, and cloves.
5.  Add the dry ingredients to the wet mixture and stir until just combined.
6.  By hand, fold in the raisins and nuts.
7.  Drop the dough by rounded teaspoonfuls onto the prepared cookie sheets.
8.  Bake for **10 to 12 minutes**, or until the cookies are set and lightly browned around the edges.
___

>[!Servings]
>Makes 4 dozen.
