*A classic, dark, and moist gingerbread recipe. This old-fashioned spice cake is rich with molasses and flavored with a warm blend of ginger, cloves, and cinnamon, making it a perfect comfort dessert.*

### Instructions
1.  Preheat your oven to 350°F. Grease and flour a 9x13 inch pan (or two shallow 8-inch pans).
2.  In a large bowl, cream the softened fat and sugar together with an electric mixer. Add the molasses and the beaten egg and mix well.
3.  In a separate bowl, sift the flour, baking soda, salt, ginger, cloves, and cinnamon together three times.
4.  On low speed, add the dry ingredients to the creamed mixture in three parts, alternating with the milk, beginning and ending with the dry ingredients.
5.  Pour the batter into the prepared pan(s).
6.  Bake for **30 to 40 minutes**, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean. Serve warm, perhaps with whipped cream or lemon sauce.
