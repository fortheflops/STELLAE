*A classic, crowd-pleasing recipe for soft and chewy M&M cookies. This simple drop cookie is packed with colorful candy-coated chocolates, making it a perfect treat for any party.*

## Recipe
| Measurements               | Ingredients                                        |
| :------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 Cup                      | [[Shortening]]                                     |
| 1 Cup                      | Brown [[Sugar]], packed                            |
| ½ Cup                      | Granulated [[Sugar]]                               |
| 2                          | [[Eggs]]                                           |
| 2 Teaspoons                | [[Vanilla Extract]]                                |
| 2 ¼ Cups                   | Sifted All-Purpose [[Flour]]                       |
| 1 Teaspoon                 | Baking [[Soda]]                                    |
| 1 Teaspoon                 | [[Salt]]                                           |
| 1 ½ Cups                   | M&M [[Candies]], divided                           |
___
## Instructions
1.  Preheat your oven to 375°F.
2.  In a large bowl, cream together the shortening, brown sugar, and granulated sugar with an electric mixer until light and fluffy.
3.  Beat in the vanilla and the eggs.
4.  In a separate bowl, sift together the flour, baking soda, and salt.
5.  Gradually add the sifted dry ingredients to the creamed mixture and mix well.
6.  By hand, stir in **½ cup** of the M&M candies.
7.  Drop by rounded spoonfuls onto an ungreased cookie sheet.
8.  Press the remaining **1 cup** of M&M candies onto the tops of the cookies for decoration.
9.  Bake for **10-12 minutes**, or until golden brown. Let cool on the pan for a few minutes before transferring to a wire rack.
