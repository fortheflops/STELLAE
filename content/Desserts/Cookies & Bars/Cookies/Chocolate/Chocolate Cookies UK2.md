*A classic and quick recipe from Clarice for no-bake chocolate oatmeal cookies. This simple stovetop method combines a boiled cocoa-sugar syrup with oats and nuts to create chewy, fudgy cookies that set up on wax paper.*
### Author
Clarice
___
## Recipe
| Measurements      | Ingredients                                        |
| :---------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 Cup             | [[Sugar]]                                          |
| 1 Stick (½ Cup)   | [[Oleo]] (Margarine or Butter)                     |
| 3 Tbsp            | [[Cocoa]] Powder                                   |
| 1 tsp             | [[Vanilla]]                                        |
| ½ Cup             | [[Milk]]                                           |
| Pinch             | [[Salt]]                                           |
| 3 Cups            | [[Oats]] (quick-cooking)                           |
| 1 Cup             | [[Nuts]], chopped                                  |
___
### Instructions
1.  **Preparation:** Lay out sheets of waxed paper on your counter.
2.  **Cook Syrup:** In a medium saucepan, combine the sugar, oleo/butter, cocoa, milk, and salt.
3.  Bring the mixture to a full, rolling boil over medium heat.
4.  Once it reaches a rolling boil, cook for **exactly 1 minute**.
5.  **Combine:** Remove the pan from the heat. Immediately stir in the vanilla, oats, and nuts.
6.  **Drop Cookies:** Working quickly, drop the mixture by teaspoonfuls onto the waxed paper.
7.  Let the cookies cool and set completely at room temperature, about 30 minutes.
___

>[!Warning]
>Boiling sugar is extremely hot. Use caution when making this recipe, especially when the 1-minute timer starts.
