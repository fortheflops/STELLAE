*A simple recipe for soft, chewy sugar cookies made with oil instead of butter, and spiced with either cinnamon or nutmeg.*

## Recipe
| Measurements      | Ingredients                                        |
| :---------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 2 1/2 cups        | [[All-Purpose Flour]]                              |
| 1 1/2 teaspoons   | Double-Acting [[Baking Powder]]                    |
| 3/4 teaspoon      | [[Salt]]                                           |
| 1 teaspoon        | [[Cinnamon]] OR 1/2 tsp freshly grated [[Nutmeg]]  |
| 1 cup             | [[Granulated Sugar]]                               |
| 3/4 cup           | [[Cooking Oil]]                                    |
| 2                 | [[Eggs]]                                           |
| 1 teaspoon        | [[Vanilla Extract]]                                |
| For Coating       | Additional [[Granulated Sugar]]                    |
___
## Instructions
1.  Preheat your oven to 375°F. Lightly grease a baking sheet.
2.  In a medium bowl, sift together the flour, baking powder, salt, and your choice of cinnamon or nutmeg.
3.  In a large bowl, combine the 1 cup of sugar and the cooking oil.
4.  Add the eggs to the oil mixture one at a time, beating well after each addition. Stir in the vanilla.
5.  Add the flour mixture to the wet ingredients all at once and beat until a smooth dough forms.
6.  Pour the additional granulated sugar for coating into a small, shallow bowl.
7.  Shape the dough into 1-inch balls and roll each ball in the sugar to coat completely.
8.  Place the coated balls on the prepared baking sheet.
9.  Bake for **10 to 12 minutes**, until the edges are lightly golden.
