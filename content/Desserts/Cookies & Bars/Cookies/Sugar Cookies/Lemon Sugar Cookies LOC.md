*A classic recipe for soft, cake-like lemon sugar cookies. This simple drop cookie is flavored with fresh lemon rind and finished with a sprinkle of nutmeg for a warm, aromatic touch.*

## Recipe
| Measurements      | Ingredients                                        |
| :---------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| ½ Cup             | [[Shortening]], softened                           |
| 1 Cup             | [[Sugar]] (for dough)                              |
| 1                 | Unbeaten [[Egg]]                                   |
| 1 Teaspoon        | Grated [[Lemon Rind]]                              |
| 2 Tablespoons     | [[Milk]]                                           |
| 2 Cups            | [[Flour]]                                          |
| 1 Teaspoon        | [[Baking Powder]]                                  |
| ½ Teaspoon        | [[Baking Soda]]                                    |
| 1 Teaspoon        | [[Salt]]                                           |
| **For Topping:**|                                                    |
| As Needed         | [[Sugar]]                                          |
| As Needed         | [[Nutmeg]]                                         |
___
## Instructions
1.  Preheat your oven to 400°F. Lightly grease your baking sheets.
2.  In a large bowl, cream together the softened shortening and 1 cup of sugar.
3.  Add the unbeaten egg, milk, and grated lemon rind; beat well.
4.  In a separate bowl, sift together the flour, baking powder, baking soda, and salt.
5.  Gradually add the dry ingredients to the creamed mixture, mixing until a soft dough forms.
6.  Drop by rounded teaspoonfuls onto the prepared baking sheet.
7.  Dip the bottom of a glass in granulated sugar and use it to flatten each cookie.
8.  Lightly sprinkle the tops of the cookies with nutmeg.
9.  Bake for **8-10 minutes**, or until light golden.
10. Let the cookies cool on the sheet for a minute before transferring to a wire rack.
