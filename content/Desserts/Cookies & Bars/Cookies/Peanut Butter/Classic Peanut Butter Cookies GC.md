## Instructions
1.  Preheat your oven to 375°F.
2.  In a large bowl, cream together the shortening, peanut butter, white sugar, and brown sugar with an electric mixer until light and fluffy.
3.  Beat in the egg and vanilla.
4.  In a separate bowl, whisk together the flour, baking soda, baking powder, and salt.
5.  Gradually add the dry ingredients to the creamed mixture, mixing on low speed until a stiff dough forms.
6.  Roll the dough into 1-inch balls.
7.  Place the balls on an ungreased baking sheet and press with a fork to create a crisscross pattern.
8.  Bake for **10-12 minutes**, or until lightly browned. Let the cookies cool on the sheet for a few minutes before transferring to a wire rack.
