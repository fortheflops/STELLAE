*A fantastically simple 2-ingredient, no-bake treat. The original name for these crunchy chocolate-cereal clusters is "Geng-a-lings."*

## Recipe
| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 4 Cups | [[Wheaties]] |
| 1 (12 Ounce) Package | [[Chocolate Chips]] |
___
## Instructions
1.  Line a baking sheet with **wax paper**.
2.  Melt the **chocolate chips** in a double boiler or in the microwave until smooth.
3.  In a large bowl, pour the melted chocolate over the **Wheaties**. Stir gently until the cereal is completely coated.
4.  Drop spoonfuls of the mixture onto the wax paper.
5.  Let the clusters cool and harden at room temperature or in the refrigerator.
