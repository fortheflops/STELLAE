*A classic recipe for soft, cake-like orange spice cookies. This old-fashioned drop cookie is rich with the flavors of molasses, brown sugar, orange peel, and warm spices like ginger and cloves.*

## Recipe
| Measurements      | Ingredients                                        |
| :---------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 ¼ Cups          | [[Flour]]                                          |
| 1 tsp             | [[Ginger]]                                         |
| ¼ tsp             | [[Cloves]]                                         |
| ½ tsp             | [[Baking Soda]]                                    |
| ⅛ tsp             | [[Salt]]                                           |
| ½ Cup             | Packed [[Brown Sugar]]                             |
| ½ Cup             | [[Butter]] Or [[Margarine]], softened              |
| 2 Tbsp            | [[Molasses]]                                       |
| ½                 | [[Egg]] (See Note)                                 |
| ½ Cup             | [[Nuts]], chopped                                  |
| Grated Peel Of 1  | [[Orange]]                                         |
___
### Instructions
1.  Preheat your oven to 350°F. Lightly grease a cookie sheet.
2.  In a medium bowl, stir together the flour, ginger, cloves, baking soda, and salt.
3.  In a large mixer bowl, cream the softened butter, brown sugar, and molasses until light and fluffy.
4.  Add the half portion of a beaten egg and the grated orange peel; beat well.
5.  Gradually stir in the flour mixture until just combined.
6.  By hand, fold in the chopped nuts.
7.  Drop by rounded teaspoonfuls about 2 inches apart on the prepared cookie sheet.
8.  Bake for **12 minutes**, or until the edges are lightly browned.
___

>[!Note]
>**How to measure ½ an egg:** Crack one large egg into a small bowl and beat it well with a fork. Measure out approximately **1 ½ to 2 tablespoons** of the beaten egg to use in the recipe.
>
>The recipe makes about 42 cookies.
