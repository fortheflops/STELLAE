## Instructions
1.  **Preparation:** Preheat your oven to 325°F. Grease and flour a large baking pan (e.g., a 13x18 inch sheet pan).
2.  In a very large bowl, combine the chopped nuts, dates, drained pineapple, cherries, and chocolate chips. Sprinkle about 1 cup of the flour over this mixture and toss well to coat all the fruit and nuts.
3.  **Make the Batter:** In a separate large bowl, beat the 9 eggs with the sugar until light and fluffy.
4.  In another bowl, whisk together the remaining 3 ½ cups of flour, the baking powder, and the salt.
5.  Add the dry ingredients to the egg and sugar mixture and stir until just combined.
6.  **Combine:** Pour the batter over the floured fruit and nut mixture. Using a sturdy spoon or your hands, mix until everything is thoroughly combined. The "batter" will be very thick and dense.
7.  **Bake:** Spread and press the mixture evenly into your prepared pan. Bake for 45-60 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.
8.  Let the bars cool completely in the pan on a wire rack before cutting.
