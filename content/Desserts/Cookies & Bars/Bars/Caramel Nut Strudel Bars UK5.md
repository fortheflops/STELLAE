## Instructions
1.  **Preparation:** Preheat the oven to 350°F. Grease a 9x13 inch baking pan.
2.  **Make the Streusel:** In a large bowl, combine the flour, ⅔ cup sugar, crushed corn flakes, and nuts.
3.  Cut in the 4 ounces of cubed cold butter with a pastry blender or your fingers until the mixture is crumbly.
4.  Reserve about ⅓ of this mixture for the topping. Press the remaining ⅔ firmly into the bottom of the prepared pan to form a crust.
5.  **Make the Filling:** In a medium saucepan, combine the 12 ounces of butter, 2 ¼ cups of sugar, cornstarch, and corn syrup.
6.  Cook over medium heat, stirring constantly, until the mixture comes to a boil and thickens.
7.  **Assemble and Bake:** Pour the hot filling over the crust.
8.  Sprinkle the reserved streusel mixture evenly on top.
9.  Bake for 30-35 minutes, or until the edges are golden brown and the filling is set.
10. Let it cool completely in the pan on a wire rack before cutting into bars.
