## Instructions
### For the Base Layer
1.  Preheat your oven to 350°F. Grease a 13x9 inch pan.
2.  In a large bowl, cream the 2 eggs and 1 stick of softened margarine together.
3.  Add the dry cake mix, nuts, and coconut (if using). Mix well with a sturdy spoon or your hands until a thick dough forms.
4.  Press this mixture evenly into the bottom of the prepared pan.

### For the Top Layer
1.  In a separate bowl, beat the softened cream cheese and 1 stick of softened margarine with an electric mixer until smooth.
2.  Gradually beat in the powdered sugar until the mixture is smooth and creamy.
3.  Pour this mixture over the base layer in the pan and spread it evenly.
4.  Bake for **40 to 50 minutes**. The edges will be golden and the center should be set but still a bit gooey.
5.  Let the bars cool completely in the pan on a wire rack before cutting into squares.
___

>[!Note]
>The original recipe card suggests a baking time of 1 to 1.5 hours, which is likely too long and would result in a dry bar. A more standard time of **40-50 minutes** is recommended for a classic gooey texture.
