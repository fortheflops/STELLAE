*A classic and incredibly easy recipe for toffee bars. A rich, buttery brown sugar shortbread base is baked, then topped with chocolate chips that melt on the hot crust and are sprinkled with nuts.*

## Recipe
| Measurements      | Ingredients                                        |
| :---------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| ¾ Cup             | [[Butter]], softened                               |
| ½ Cup             | [[Brown Sugar]], packed                            |
| 2 Cups            | [[Flour]]                                          |
| 1 Package (6-12 oz)| [[Chocolate Bits]] (Chocolate Chips)               |
| ½ Cup             | Chopped [[Nuts]]                                   |
___
## Instructions
1.  Preheat your oven to 325°F.
2.  In a medium bowl, combine the softened butter, brown sugar, and flour. Mix with a pastry blender or your hands until a crumbly dough forms.
3.  Press this mixture firmly and evenly into an ungreased 10x15 inch jelly roll pan to form the crust.
4.  Bake for **25 minutes**.
5.  Remove the pan from the oven. Immediately sprinkle the chocolate chips evenly over the hot base. Let them sit for 3-5 minutes to melt.
6.  Use a spatula to spread the melted chocolate into a smooth, even layer.
7.  Sprinkle the chopped nuts over the melted chocolate.
8.  While the bars are still warm, cut them into 1 ½ inch squares. Let them cool completely in the pan before removing.
