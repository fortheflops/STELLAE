# Aunt Sadie's Yellow Angel Food Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** Jill Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Aunt Sadie | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A unique vintage yellow angel food cake utilizing whole eggs for a rich, moist texture, baked with a distinct multi-temperature sequence.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 25 mins | 1 hour | None | 1 tube cake |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large mixing bowls
* Electric mixer
* Sifter
* Ungreased tube pan
* Glass bottle (for cooling)

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 6 | [[Eggs]] | separated |
| 1 1/2 cups | [[All-Purpose Flour]] | sifted 3 times |
| 1 1/2 cups | [[Sugar]] | divided |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Baking Powder]] | none |
| 1/2 teaspoon | [[Cream of Tartar]] | essential |
| 1 pinch | [[Salt]] | none |
| 3 tablespoons | [[Water]] | cold |
| 1/2 cup | [[Water]] | boiled |
| 1 tsp | [[Flavoring]] | vanilla or to taste |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and Yolk Batter
1. Sift the **1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour** and **1 teaspoon baking powder** together three times and set aside. Separate the **6 eggs** into two large bowls.
2. In one bowl, beat the egg yolks with the **3 tablespoons cold water**. Gradually add the **1 1/2 cups sugar** and continue beating for **10 minutes or more**, until the mixture is very thick, pale, and ribbon-like.
3. Gently mix in the **1/2 cup boiled water**, then add the flour mixture, mixing only until just combined.

### Step 2: Meringue and Baking
4. In the second clean bowl, beat the egg whites with a **1 pinch salt** until foamy. Add the **1 tsp flavoring** and the **1/2 teaspoon cream of tartar**, and continue beating until stiff, glossy peaks form.
5. Gently fold the beaten egg whites into the yolk batter, one-third at a time, until just incorporated and no streaks of white remain.
6. Pour the batter into a large, ungreased tube pan. Bake at **300°F for 15 minutes**.
7. Increase temperature to **350°F and bake for 30 minutes**.
8. Reduce temperature back to **300°F and bake for another 15 minutes** until done and springy to the touch.
9. Immediately invert the pan placing the tube over the neck of a bottle and allow the cake to cool completely upside down before removing.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the cooled cake wrapped tightly at room temperature for up to 3 days.
