# Delicious Butter (White) Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** JC | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic, tender white butter cake made with separated eggs for a lighter texture. Can be flavored with vanilla, orange, or lemon.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 25 mins | 40 mins | 10 mins | 2 9-inch round layers |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* two 9-inch round cake pans
* large bowl
* separate bowl
* clean bowl
* wire rack
* wooden skewer

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3/4 cup | [[Butter]] | softened |
| 2 cups | [[Sugar]] | none |
| 4 | [[Eggs]] | separated |
| 3 cups | [[All-Purpose Flour]] | none |
| 4 teaspoons | [[Baking Powder]] | none |
| 1/2 teaspoon | [[Salt]] | none |
| 1 cup | [[Milk]] | none |
| 1 1/2 teaspoons | [[Vanilla Extract]] | none |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Orange Flavoring]] or [[Lemon Flavoring]] | optional |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Cream
1. Preheat oven to **350°F**. Grease and flour two **9-inch round cake pans**.
2. In a large bowl, cream the **3/4 cup butter**, softened, and **2 cups sugar** together until light and fluffy. Beat in the **4 egg yolks** one at a time.

### Step 2: Mix Batter
3. In a separate bowl, sift together the **3 cups all-purpose flour**, **1/2 teaspoon salt**, and **4 teaspoons baking powder**.
4. Add the dry ingredients to the creamed mixture alternately with the **1 cup milk**, beginning and ending with the flour mixture. Mix until just combined.
5. Stir in the **1 1/2 teaspoons vanilla extract** and optional **1 teaspoon orange flavoring or lemon flavoring**.

### Step 3: Fold and Bake
6. In a clean, dry bowl, beat the **4 egg whites** until stiff peaks form.
7. Gently fold the beaten **4 egg whites** into the cake batter in three additions, until no white streaks remain.
8. Divide the batter evenly between the prepared pans.
9. Bake for **35 to 40 minutes**, or until a wooden skewer inserted into the center comes out clean.
10. Let the cakes cool in the pans for **10 minutes** before inverting them onto a wire rack to cool completely.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the cooled cake layers wrapped tightly in plastic wrap at room temperature for up to 2 days or freeze for up to 2 months.
