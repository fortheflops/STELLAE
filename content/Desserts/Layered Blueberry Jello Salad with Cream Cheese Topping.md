# Layered Blueberry Jello Salad with Cream Cheese Topping

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic make-ahead Jello salad featuring a layer of strawberry and raspberry Jello packed with pineapple and blueberries, all topped with a sweet and tangy cream cheese frosting. This vintage recipe is perfect for potlucks and must be prepared in advance to ensure a firm set.*

## Ingredients

### Jello Layer
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 (3 oz) pkg | [[Strawberry Jello]] | powdered |
| 1 (3 oz) pkg | [[Raspberry Jello]] | powdered |
| 2 Cups | [[Boiling Water]] | hot |
| 1 (20 oz) can | [[Crushed Pineapple in own juice]] | undrained |
| 1 (12 oz) bag | [[Frozen Blueberries]] | frozen |

### Topping
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 (8 oz) pkg | [[Cream Cheese]] | softened |
| 1 (8 oz) container | [[Sour Cream]] | standard |
| To taste | [[Sugar]] | granulated |
| 1 tsp | [[Vanilla]] | optional |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Jello Base
1. In a large bowl, dissolve the **1 (3 oz) pkg strawberry Jello** and **1 (3 oz) pkg raspberry Jello** in **2 cups boiling water**, stirring until completely clear.
2. Stir in the **1 (20 oz) can crushed pineapple in own juice** and the **1 (12 oz) bag frozen blueberries** until evenly distributed.
3. Pour the mixture into a 9x13 inch pan and refrigerate until completely firm and set to the touch.

### Step 2: Prepare and Assemble Topping
4. Using an electric mixer, blend the **1 (8 oz) pkg cream cheese**, **1 (8 oz) container sour cream**, and **sugar to taste** until the mixture is smooth and creamy; stir in **1 tsp vanilla** if using.
5. Spread the cream cheese mixture evenly over the firm Jello layer like frosting.
6. Refrigerate for at least one hour before cutting into squares and serving on a bed of lettuce.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This salad must be prepared at least one day in advance to allow the Jello to set completely before adding the topping.
