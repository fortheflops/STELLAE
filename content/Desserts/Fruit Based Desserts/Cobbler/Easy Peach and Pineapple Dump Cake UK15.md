*This is the quintessential "dump cake" cobbler—a super easy dessert made by layering fruit and dry cake mix right in the pan.*
### Author
Joy Phillips
___
## Recipe
| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 1 (20 ounce) can | Crushed [[Pineapple]] |
| 1-2 cans or bags | [[Peaches]] (frozen or canned) |
| 1 (13 ounce) box | [[Cake Mix]] (yellow or white) |
| As Needed | [[Pecans]] |
| 1/4 cup | [[Sugar]] |
| As Needed | Melted [[Butter]] |
___
## Instructions
1.  Preheat the oven to 350°F.
2.  Spread the crushed **pineapple** (with juice) in the bottom of a 9x13 inch pan.
3.  Arrange a layer of **peaches** over the pineapple.
4.  Sprinkle the dry **cake mix** evenly over the fruit.
5.  Arrange another layer of **peaches** on top of the cake mix.
6.  Sprinkle **pecans** and **sugar** over the top.
7.  Drizzle melted **butter** evenly over everything.
8.  Bake for 35 to 45 minutes, until golden brown and bubbly.
