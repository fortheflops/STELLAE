| 3/4 Cup | [[Flour]] |

| 1 Pinch | [[Salt]] |
___
## Instructions
1.  Preheat oven to 350°F.
2.  In a saucepan, heat the **canned fruit** (with juice), **1 cup of sugar**, and **4 tablespoons of margarine** until the margarine is melted. Pour this mixture into a baking dish.
3.  In a separate bowl, mix together the **flour**, **3/4 cup of sugar**, **milk**, **baking powder**, and **salt** to form a batter.
4.  Pour the batter evenly over the fruit mixture in the pan.
5.  Bake for 35-40 minutes, or until golden brown.
