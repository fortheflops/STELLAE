*A classic and simple peach cobbler where the batter is poured over the fruit, creating a soft, cake-like topping as it bakes.*

Aunt Liz

| $\frac{1}{2}$ Cup | [[Milk]] |
| To Taste | Ground [[Nutmeg]] |

___
## Instructions
1.  Preheat oven to 400°F.
2.  Melt the **margarine** in a baking pan in the oven.
3.  Add the **peaches** to the pan with the melted margarine and sprinkle with **spice** (like cinnamon).
4.  In a separate bowl, combine the **flour**, **salt**, **baking powder**, **sugar**, and **nutmeg**.
5.  Stir in the **milk** to form a batter.
6.  Pour the batter over the peaches in the pan. Do not stir.
7.  Bake for about 25 minutes, or until the topping is golden brown.
___

>[!Note]
>The amount of sugar can be reduced if using canned peaches. The card mentions adding "some spice" to the peaches.
