## Recipe
### Filling
| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 2 Cups | Sliced, Peeled [[Apples]] |
| 1 Tbsp | [[Flour]] |
| ¼ Tsp | [[Cinnamon]] |
| ¼ Cup | [[Sugar]], or to taste |
### Topping
| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| ½ Cup | [[Rolled Oats]] |
| ½ Cup | [[Brown Sugar]] |
| ¼ Cup | [[Flour]] |
| 4 Tbsp | [[Butter]], chilled |
___
## Instructions
1.  Preheat oven to 300-325°F. Grease a baking pan.
2.  Place the sliced **Apples** into the pan.
3.  Sprinkle the apples with **1 Tbsp Flour**, **Cinnamon**, and **Sugar**.
4.  To make the topping, combine the **Rolled Oats**, **Brown Sugar**, and **¼ cup Flour**. Cut in the **Butter** until the mixture is crumbly.
5.  Sprinkle the topping evenly over the apples.
6.  Bake for 1 hour, or until the apples are tender.
7.  Best served warm.
