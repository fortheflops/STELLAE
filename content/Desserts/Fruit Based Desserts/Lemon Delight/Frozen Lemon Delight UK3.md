## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 1 Large Can | [[Chilled Evaporated Milk]], Whipped |
| 2 | [[Lemons]] - Juice & Zest |
| 1 Cup | [[Sugar]] |
| 1 1/2 Cups | [[Graham]] or [[Vanilla Wafer Crumbs]] |
___
## Instructions
1.  Pour the well-chilled **evaporated milk** into a chilled bowl. Whip with an electric mixer until thick and stiff peaks form.
2.  Gradually add the **sugar** while mixing, then fold in the **lemon juice** and **zest**.
3.  Line the bottom of a dish with half of the **crumbs**.
4.  Pour the lemon filling in and sprinkle the remaining crumbs on top.
5.  Cover and freeze until firm.
___

>[!Note]
>The recipe card notes "Double for fridge," which likely means that if you want to serve this as a softer, refrigerated dessert instead of a frozen one, you should double the recipe for the same size pan.
