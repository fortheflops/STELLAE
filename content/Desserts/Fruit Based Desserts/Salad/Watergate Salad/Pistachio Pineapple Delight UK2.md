## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 8 oz | [[Cool Whip]], thawed |
| 1 pkg | [[Pistachio Instant Pudding]] (dry) |
| 2 Cups | [[Crushed Pineapple]] & juice |
| 1 Cup | [[Mini Marshmallows]] |
| ½ Cup | [[Chopped Nuts]] |
___
## Instructions
1.  In a large bowl, sprinkle the dry **pudding** mix over the thawed **Cool Whip** and stir until well blended.
2.  Stir in the **mini marshmallows**, undrained **crushed pineapple**, and **chopped nuts**.
3.  Cover and chill thoroughly before serving.
