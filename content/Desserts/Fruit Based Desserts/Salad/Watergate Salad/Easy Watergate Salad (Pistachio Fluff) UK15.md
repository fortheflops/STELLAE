*A classic potluck favorite, this easy no-bake salad is also known as "Pistachio Fluff" or "Green Stuff."*
### Author
Mae White
___
## Recipe
| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 1 large package | [[Pistachio Instant Pudding Mix]] (dry) |
| 1 (15 ounce) can | Crushed [[Pineapple]], undrained |
| 1 small carton | [[Cool Whip]], thawed |
| 1/4 cup | Chopped [[Pecans]] |
| 1 cup | Tiny [[Marshmallows]] |
| 1/2 cup | Shredded [[Coconut]] |
___
## Instructions
1.  In a large bowl, stir together the dry **pistachio pudding mix** and the undrained **crushed pineapple**.
2.  Gently fold in the **Cool Whip**.
3.  Add the **chopped pecans**, **tiny marshmallows**, and **shredded coconut**, and stir to combine.
4.  Chill until ready to serve.
