| 1 pkg. | [[Dream Whip]], prepared |
___
## Instructions
1.  In a large bowl, prepare the **Dream Whip** according to package directions.
2.  Gently fold in the **crushed pineapple** (with its juice), the drained **fruit cocktail**, **small marshmallows**, sliced **bananas**, and the **cherries** with their juice.
3.  Combine everything gently.
4.  Pour into a freezer-safe container, cover, and freeze until firm.
