## Recipe
| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 1 can | [[Cranberry Sauce]] (whole berry) |
| 1 small can | [[Pineapple]] (crushed, drained) |
| 1 pkg. | [[Mini Marshmallows]] |
| 3 | [[Bananas]], mashed |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Pecans]], chopped |
| 1 Cup | [[Heavy Cream]], whipped (or 2 cups whipped topping) |
___
## Instructions
1.  In a large bowl, combine the **cranberry sauce**, drained **pineapple**, **mini marshmallows**, mashed **bananas**, and **pecans**.
2.  Gently fold in the **whipped cream** until everything is evenly mixed.
3.  Pour the mixture into a round container or freezer-safe dish.
4.  Cover and freeze until firm, preferably overnight.
5.  Let soften slightly before slicing and serving.
