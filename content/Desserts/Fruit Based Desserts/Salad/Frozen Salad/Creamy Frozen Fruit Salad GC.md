## Instructions
1.  In the top of a double boiler, cook the **butter**, **egg yolks**, **cornstarch**, **sugar**, **salt**, and **milk** together, stirring constantly until thick.
2.  Remove the custard from the heat and beat in the **lemon juice**.
3.  Cool the mixture completely.
4.  Fold in the drained **fruit salad**. (Whipped cream may also be folded in at this point if desired).
5.  Pour into a freezer-safe container and freeze until firm.
6.  Serve with **whipped cream** for dessert, or with **mayonnaise** as a salad.
