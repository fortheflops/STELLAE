## Recipe
### Syrup Base
| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 1 1/2 C. | [[Sugar]] |
| 1 C. | [[Water]] |
| 10 lg. | [[Marshmallows]] |
### Fruit
| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 2 pkg. | [[Frozen Strawberries]] (thawed, with juice) |
| 1 lg. can | [[Crushed Pineapple]] (with juice) |
| 1 lg. can | [[Apricots]] (diced & drained) |
| 4 | [[Bananas]], sliced |
___
## Instructions
1.  In a saucepan, boil the **sugar** and **water** together, stirring until the sugar dissolves.
2.  Add the **marshmallows** to the hot syrup and stir until they melt completely. Let the syrup cool slightly.
3.  In a large bowl, combine the thawed **strawberries** (with juice), **crushed pineapple** (with juice), drained **apricots**, and sliced **bananas**.
4.  Pour the marshmallow syrup over the fruit and stir to combine.
5.  Spoon the mixture into cupcake liners placed in muffin tins.
6.  Freeze until firm.
