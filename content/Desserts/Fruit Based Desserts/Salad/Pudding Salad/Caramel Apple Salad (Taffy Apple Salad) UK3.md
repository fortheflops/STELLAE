## Recipe
| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 1 (3.5 oz) pkg | Butterscotch Instant Pudding Mix *(dry powder)* |
| 1 Cup | Dry Roasted Nuts (or specifically Cashews) |
| 1 (20 oz) can | Crushed [[Pineapple]], undrained |
| 1 Cup | Miniature [[Marshmallows]] |
| 3 Cups | Chopped Unpeeled [[Apples]] |
| 1 (8 oz) container | Whipped Topping, thawed (e.g., Cool Whip) |
___
### Instructions
1.  In a large bowl, combine the **dry butterscotch instant pudding mix**, **nuts**, the entire can of **undrained crushed pineapple**, **miniature marshmallows**, chopped **unpeeled apples**, and the thawed **whipped topping**.
2.  Stir well until all ingredients are thoroughly combined.
3.  Cover the bowl and chill in the refrigerator for at least 1-2 hours, or until ready to serve. Keep chilled.
