*This is a classic potluck favorite, also known as Ambrosia Salad. Its name comes from the simple measurement: one cup of each of the five ingredients.*

## Recipe
| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| One Cup | Drained Chunk or Crushed [[Pineapple]] |
| One Cup | Canned [[Mandarin Oranges]], Drained |
| One Cup | Miniature [[Marshmallows]] |
| One Cup | [[Coconut]] (Flaked) |
| One Cup | [[Sour Cream]] |
___
## Instructions
1.  In a large bowl, mix all ingredients together: **drained pineapple**, **drained mandarin oranges**, **miniature marshmallows**, **coconut**, and **sour cream**.
2.  Cover and keep in the refrigerator overnight to allow the flavors to meld together.
___

>[!Note]
>You can add any other fruit you like, but this combination will last well for a week in the refrigerator.
