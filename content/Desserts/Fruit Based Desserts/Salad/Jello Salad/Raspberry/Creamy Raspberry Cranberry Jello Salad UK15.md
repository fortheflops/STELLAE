## Instructions (Inferred)
1.  Prepare the **Raspberry Jello** according to the package directions (usually 1 cup boiling water and 1 cup cold water).
2.  Chill the Jello until it is partially set (thickened, like egg whites).
3.  In a large bowl, gently fold together the partially set Jello, **whole berry cranberry sauce**, drained **crushed pineapple**, drained **mandarin oranges**, chopped **pecans**, and **sour cream**.
4.  Pour the mixture into a serving dish or mold.
5.  Chill for several hours, or until completely firm.
