### Instructions
1.  Dissolve gelatin in boiling water. Cool.
2.  Slice an end off the honeydew. With a long-handled spoon, scoop out the seeds.
3.  Peel the honeydew.
4.  To prevent tipping, place the melon in a heavy bowl in an upright position.
5.  Fill the melon cavity about 3/4 full with the cooled gelatin.
6.  Refrigerate until the filling has thickened to the consistency of egg white.
7.  Drop the cubed fruits (apple, pear, peach, grapes) into the cavity.
8.  If the cavity is not completely filled, add more gelatin. Chill until firm.
9.  Mash the cream cheese & dilute with a few drops of cream.
10. Dry the melon well with paper toweling, then spread the cream cheese over the entire outer surface.
11. Roll in chopped nuts.
12. Chill again until the cheese is very firm.
13. With a sharp knife, slice the melon crosswise 1 inch thick.
14. Line a platter with romaine lettuce & arrange the melon slices on it.
