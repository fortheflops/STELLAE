## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 2 Cups | [[Cottage Cheese]] |
| 2 Cups | [[Pineapple Bits]], well-drained |
| 2 Cups | [[Cool Whip]], thawed |
| 1 Pkg. | [[Dry Jello]] (any flavor) |
___
## Instructions
1.  In a large bowl, mix the **cottage cheese**, drained **pineapple bits**, and **Cool Whip** together until well combined.
2.  Sprinkle the **dry jello** powder over the mixture and stir well until fully incorporated.
3.  Cover and chill thoroughly for at least a few hours before serving.
___

>[!Note]
>You can use any flavor of Jello you like. Orange, strawberry, or lime are classic pairings with pineapple.
