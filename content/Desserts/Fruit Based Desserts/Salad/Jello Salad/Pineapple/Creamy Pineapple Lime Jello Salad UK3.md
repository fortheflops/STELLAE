*A classic creamy and crunchy Jello salad with the tangy combination of lime, pineapple, and cream cheese.*

## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 1 package | [[Lime Jello]] |
| 2 small packages | [[Cream Cheese]], softened |
| 1/2 pint | [[Coffee Cream]] (or half-and-half) |
| #2 can | [[Crushed Pineapple]] |
| 1 cup | [[Pecans]], chopped |
| 1 cup | [[Diced Celery]] |
___
### Instructions
1.  Drain the juice from the **pineapple** into a saucepan (if not enough juice, add water to make 1 cup). Bring the juice to a boil.
2.  Pour the boiling juice over the **lime Jello** powder in a large bowl and stir until dissolved. Let cool.
3.  In a separate bowl, beat the softened **cream cheese** and **coffee cream** together until smooth.
4.  Whisk the cream cheese mixture into the cooled Jello.
5.  Stir in the drained pineapple, chopped **pecans**, and diced **celery**.
6.  Pour the mixture into a mold and refrigerate until firm.
