## Instructions
1.  In a large bowl, dissolve the **strawberry Jello** powder in the **boiling water**.
2.  Add the **frozen strawberries** and stir until they thaw and the Jello cools.
3.  Stir in the **crushed pineapple** (with juice) and sliced **bananas**.
4.  Pour half of the mixture into a large dish (e.g., 9x13 inch pan) and refrigerate until it congeals.
5.  Carefully spread the **sour cream** over the set Jello layer.
6.  Gently pour the remaining Jello mixture on top of the sour cream.
7.  Refrigerate for several more hours, or until completely firm.
