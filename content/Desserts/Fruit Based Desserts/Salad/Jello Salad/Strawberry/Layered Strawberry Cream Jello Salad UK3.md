## Instructions
1.  In a large bowl, dissolve the **strawberry Jello** powder in the **boiling water**.
2.  Add the **frozen strawberries** and stir until they thaw and the mixture cools and begins to thicken.
3.  Gently fold in the **whipped cream**, drained **pineapple**, diced **bananas**, and chopped **nuts**.
4.  Pour half of this mixture into an 8x8x2 inch pan and refrigerate until firm.
5.  Once the first layer is set, carefully spread the **sour cream** over the top.
6.  Gently pour the remaining Jello mixture over the sour cream.
7.  Refrigerate for several more hours, or until completely firm.
