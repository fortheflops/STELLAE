*A classic orange Jello salad loaded with fruit and nuts, featuring a fun layer of marshmallows that floats to the top as it sets.*

Juanita

## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 1 Large Package | [[Orange Jello]] |
| 1 Can | [[Mandarin Oranges]], drained |
| 1 Can | [[Pineapple]], Crushed, drained |
| 1 Cup | [[Pecans]], chopped |
| 2-3 Cups | Tiny [[Marshmallows]] |
| For Topping | [[Whipped Cream]] or [[Cool Whip]] |
___
### Instructions
1.  Prepare the **Orange Jello** according to the package directions.
2.  Chill the Jello until it is partially set (thickened but not firm).
3.  Fold in the drained **mandarin oranges**, drained **crushed pineapple**, and chopped **pecans**.
4.  Pour the mixture into a serving bowl.
5.  Sprinkle the **tiny marshmallows** over the surface; they will float to the top.
6.  Refrigerate for several hours until completely firm.
7.  Before serving, top with **whipped cream** or **Cool Whip**.
