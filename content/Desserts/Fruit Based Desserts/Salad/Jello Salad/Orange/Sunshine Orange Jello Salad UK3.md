| *Note:* | You will need 4 cups total liquid |
___
## Instructions (Inferred)
1.  Drain the juice from the **mandarin oranges** and **crushed pineapple** into a large measuring cup.
2.  Add the thawed **frozen orange juice** concentrate to the measuring cup.
3.  Add enough cold water to the juices to make a total of 2 cups of cold liquid.
4.  In a large bowl, dissolve the **orange Jello** powder in 2 cups of boiling water.
5.  Stir the 2 cups of cold juice mixture into the Jello.
6.  Refrigerate until the Jello is partially set (thickened).
7.  Fold in the drained mandarin oranges and crushed pineapple.
8.  Pour into a mold or serving dish and refrigerate until firm.
