## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 1 sm. box (~3 oz) | [[Lime Jello]] powder |

| 1 Cup | [[Boiling Water]] |
| 1 sm. can (~8 oz) | Crushed [[Pineapple]], drained |
| 1 tsp | [[Vanilla]] |
| 1 Tbsp | [[Sugar]] |
| 1 (10 oz) bottle | [[7-Up]] Soda, chilled |
| 1 Cup | [[Pecans]], chopped |
___
### Instructions
1.  In a large bowl, beat the softened **cream cheese** with the dry **Jello** powder until combined.
2.  Gradually pour in the **boiling water**, mixing until smooth and the Jello is dissolved.
3.  Stir in the **sugar** and **vanilla**.
4.  Slowly pour in the chilled **7-Up**.
5.  Fold in the drained **pineapple** and chopped **pecans**.
6.  Pour into a mold and chill until firm.
___

>[!Note]
>The original recipe card suggests a variation: You can substitute orange Jello and mandarin oranges for the lime Jello and pineapple.
