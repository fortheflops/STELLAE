## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 1 pkg. | [[Cherry Flavored Gelatin]] |
| 1 cup | [[Boiling Water]] |
| 1 (7 oz.) bottle| [[Ginger Ale]], chilled |
| 2 T. | [[Lime Juice]] |
| 1 (1 lb. 2 oz.) can| [[Fruit Cocktail]], drained |
___
### Instructions
1.  In a bowl, dissolve the **gelatin** in the **boiling water**. Let it cool to room temperature.
2.  Gently stir in the **ginger ale** and **lime juice**.
3.  Chill the mixture in the refrigerator until it begins to thicken (partially set).
4.  Drain the **fruit cocktail** well and stir it into the thickened gelatin.
5.  Pour into a 1-quart mold.
6.  Chill until firm.
___

>[!Note]
>Serves Six.
