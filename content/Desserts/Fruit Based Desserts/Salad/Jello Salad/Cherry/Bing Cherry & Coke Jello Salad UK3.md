## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 1 #2 can | [[Bing Cherries]] |
| 1 Cup | [[Crushed Pineapple]] |
| 2 pkg. | [[Cherry Jello]] |
| 2 Cups | [[Fruit Juice]] (reserved from fruit + Grape Juice) |
| 2 | [[Coca Colas]] (2 cups total) |
| 1 Cup | [[Chopped Nuts]] |
___
## Instructions
1.  Drain the juice from the **cherries** and **pineapple** and reserve it. Add enough **grape juice** to the reserved juice to make 2 cups of liquid.
2.  Boil the 2 cups of fruit juice and pour it over the 2 packages of **Jello**. Stir until dissolved.
3.  Let the Jello mixture cool, then stir in the **2 Cokes**.
4.  Chill in the refrigerator until partially congealed.
5.  Fold in the drained cherries, pineapple, and **nuts**.
6.  Pour the mixture into a mold and refrigerate until firm.
