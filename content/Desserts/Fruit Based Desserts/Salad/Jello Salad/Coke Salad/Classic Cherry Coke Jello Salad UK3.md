## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 1 pkg. | [[Cherry Jello]] |
| 2 Cokes | (1 cup heated, 1 cup cold) |
| 1 sm. pkg. | [[Cream Cheese]], softened |
| 1 sm. bottle | [[Maraschino Cherries]], drained |
___
## Instructions
1.  In a saucepan, heat one **Coke** until hot.
2.  Pour the hot Coke over the **cherry Jello** powder in a bowl and stir until dissolved.
3.  Add the softened **cream cheese** and whisk until smooth.
4.  Stir in the second, cold **Coke**.
5.  Chill until partially set, then fold in the drained **cherries**.
6.  Pour into a mold and refrigerate until firm.
___

>[!Note]
>For the best results, make sure your cream cheese is very soft before whisking it into the Jello to prevent lumps.
