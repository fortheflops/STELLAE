## Recipe
| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 4 pkg. | [[Lemon Jello]] |
| 2 cans | [[Blueberries]] |
| 1 Cup | [[Mashed Bananas]] |
| 1 can | [[Pineapple Juice (No. 2)]] |
| 2 cartons | [[Whipping Cream]] |
___
## Instructions
1.  Drain the juice from the **blueberries** and combine it with the **pineapple juice** in a saucepan. Heat the juices to boiling.
2.  Pour the boiling liquid over the 4 packages of **Jello** in a large bowl. Stir until the Jello is completely dissolved.
3.  Refrigerate until the Jello is partially set (it should mound when dropped from a spoon).
4.  In a separate bowl, whip the **whipping cream** until stiff peaks form.
5.  Fold the whipped cream, mashed **bananas**, and the reserved blueberries into the partially set Jello.
6.  Pour into a 9x13 inch pan and refrigerate until firm.
