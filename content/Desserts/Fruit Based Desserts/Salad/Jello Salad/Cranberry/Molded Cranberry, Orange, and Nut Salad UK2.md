## Instructions
1.  Using a food chopper or processor, grind the **cranberries** and the whole **orange**.
2.  In a saucepan, add the **sugar** to the fruit mixture and bring it to a boil.
3.  In a separate small bowl, dissolve the **gelatin** in the cup of **cold water**.
4.  Remove the hot cranberry mixture from the heat and stir in the dissolved gelatin until well combined.
5.  Let the mixture cool down.
6.  Once cooled, stir in the chopped **nuts** and diced **celery**.
7.  Pour into a mold and refrigerate until set.
