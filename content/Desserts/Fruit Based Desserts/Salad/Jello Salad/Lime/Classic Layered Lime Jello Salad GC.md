| Measurements | Ingredients |

| (Reserved) | Liquid Lime Jello |
___
## Instructions
1.  Prepare the **Lime Jello** according to package directions. Divide the liquid Jello in half into two separate bowls.
2.  Chill one half of the Jello until partially set. Fold in the drained **pears**, **maraschino cherries**, and optional **marshmallows**. Pour this into a ring mold or serving dish and refrigerate to set.
3.  In a separate bowl, beat the softened **cream cheese** until smooth.
4.  Gradually beat the remaining, still-liquid Jello into the cream cheese until the mixture is smooth.
5.  Gently pour the cream cheese mixture over the set fruit layer.
6.  Refrigerate overnight, or until completely firm.
