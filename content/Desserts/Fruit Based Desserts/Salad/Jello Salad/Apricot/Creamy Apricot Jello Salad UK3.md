___
## Instructions
1.  Drain the **apricots**, reserving 1/2 cup of the juice. Pulverize the drained apricots in a blender.
2.  In a bowl, blend the dry **lemon Jello** mix and the softened **cream cheese** with a fork.
3.  Pour the **boiling water** over the mixture and stir until everything is dissolved and smooth.
4.  Stir in the pulverized apricots and the reserved apricot juice.
5.  Stir in the chopped **pecans**.
6.  Pour into a mold and refrigerate until firm.
