### Instructions
**Salad Base**
1.  Drain fruit; keep apricot and pineapple juice separate.
2.  Chop the apricots. Chill the chopped apricots and drained pineapple.
3.  Dissolve gelatin in hot water. Add 1 cup of the reserved apricot juice.
4.  Chill until partially firm.
5.  Fold in the chilled apricots, pineapple, & marshmallows.
6.  Pour into a 9x13 pan and chill until firm.

**Topping & Assembly**
1.  In a saucepan, combine sugar & flour.
2.  Blend in the slightly beaten egg & butter.
3.  Add 1 cup of the reserved pineapple juice & cook over low heat, stirring constantly, until thickened.
4.  Let the mixture cool thoroughly.
5.  Fold in 1 cup of whipped cream.
6.  Spread the topping over the congealed salad.
7.  Sprinkle with grated Cheddar cheese.
8.  Chill thoroughly. Cut in squares & serve on lettuce.
