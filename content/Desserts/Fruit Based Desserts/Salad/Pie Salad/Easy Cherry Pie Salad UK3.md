## Recipe
### Base (Marinate Overnight)
| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 1 can | [[Cherry Pie Filling]] |
| 1 (16 oz) can | [[Crushed Pineapple with juice]] |
| 1/2 cup | [[Sugar]] |
### Add-Ins (Last Minute)
| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 pkg. | [[Mini Marshmallows]] |
| 1 cup | [[Pecans]], chopped |
| 2-3 | [[Bananas]], thick sliced |
___
## Instructions
1.  **The Day Before:** In a large bowl, mix together the **cherry pie filling**, **crushed pineapple with its juice**, and **sugar**.
2.  Cover and let the mixture marinate in the refrigerator overnight.
3.  **Just Before Serving:** Add the **mini marshmallows**, chopped **pecans**, and thick-sliced **bananas** to the chilled fruit mixture.
4.  Fold gently to combine and serve immediately.
___

>[!Note]
>Adding the bananas and marshmallows at the last minute is key to keeping the bananas fresh and the marshmallows from getting too soft.
