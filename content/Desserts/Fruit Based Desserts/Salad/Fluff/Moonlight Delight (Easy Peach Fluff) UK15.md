*Aptly named "Moonlight Delight," this is a light, airy, and incredibly simple peach fluff salad that comes together in minutes.*

## Recipe
| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 1 can | [[Peaches]], well-drained |
| 1 container (8 oz) | [[Dream Whip]] or [[Cool Whip]], thawed |
| A handful (approx. 1/2 cup) | Chopped [[Pecans]] |
| A few | [[Maraschino Cherries]] (optional) |
___
## Instructions
1.  Drain the can of **peaches** thoroughly.
2.  In a medium bowl, gently smash the peaches with your fingers or a fork.
3.  Fold the smashed peaches into the **Dream Whip** or **Cool Whip**.
4.  Stir in a handful of chopped **pecans**.
5.  If desired, add a few **maraschino cherries**.
6.  Serve in a pretty glass bowl. Chill before serving for best results.
