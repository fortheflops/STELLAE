## Recipe
| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 1 (16 ounce) can | [[Cherry Pie Filling]] |
| 3/4 cup | [[Pineapple]], drained |
| 1 can | [[Eagle Brand Sweetened Condensed Milk]] |
| 1 (14 ounce) container | [[Cool Whip]], thawed |
| 1/2 cup | [[Pecans]], chopped |
___
## Instructions
1.  In a large bowl, combine the **cherry pie filling**, drained **pineapple**, **Eagle Brand milk**, thawed **Cool Whip**, and **pecans**.
2.  Mix gently until everything is well incorporated.
3.  Transfer to a serving dish and chill thoroughly before serving.
___

>[!Note]
>This recipe serves 8.
