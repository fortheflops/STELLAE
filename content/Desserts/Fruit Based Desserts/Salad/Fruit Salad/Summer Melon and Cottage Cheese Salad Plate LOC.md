___
### Instructions
1.  On a bed of **lettuce**, place a slice of **cantaloupe** and a slice of **honeydew melon**.
2.  In the center of the plate, place a scoop of **cottage cheese**.
3.  Garnish with slices of **strawberries**.
4.  Serve as an individual plate.
___

>[!Note]
>The recipe notes to "cut along rind for easy eating," meaning to make slices through the melon flesh down to the rind so it can be eaten with a fork.
