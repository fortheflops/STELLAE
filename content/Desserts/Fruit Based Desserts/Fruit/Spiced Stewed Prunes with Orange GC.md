## Recipe
| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 2 cups | [[Prunes]] |
| 3 cups | [[Water]] |
| 1 stick | [[Cinnamon]] (or 1 tsp ground) |
| 1 | [[Orange]], peeled |
| 1/2 teaspoon | Thinly sliced [[Orange Peel]] |
| 1 1/2 tablespoons | [[Sugar]] |
___
## Instructions
1.  Combine all ingredients (**prunes**, **water**, **cinnamon**, **peeled orange**, **orange peel**, and **sugar**) in a saucepan.
2.  Bring the mixture to a simmer over medium heat.
3.  Reduce the heat to low, cover, and let it simmer for 30 minutes to 1 hour, until the prunes are tender and the liquid has slightly thickened.
