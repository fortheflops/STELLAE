| 1 Cup | [[Sugar]] (For Rolling) |
___
## Instructions
1.  Remove the rind from the **oranges** and **lemons** in large strips.
2.  Simmer the rinds in water for 30 minutes and drain.
3.  Cover with fresh cold water and simmer again until tender. Drain and refresh under cold water.
4.  Scrape the white pith off the rinds and cut them into 2" x ¼" strips.
5.  In a saucepan, stir together **¾ cup Water**, **1 cup Sugar**, and **Corn Syrup** over low heat until the sugar is dissolved.
6.  Add the fruit peel and cook until most of the syrup is absorbed. Let stand overnight.
7.  The next day, bring the mixture to a simmer again. Cool slightly and drain.
8.  Spread **1 cup of sugar** on a surface. Roll the citrus peels in the sugar until well coated.
9.  Transfer to wax paper to dry, then store.
