## Instructions
1.  Score the peel of two **grapefruit** lengthwise into four sections and remove. Scrape away most of the white membrane and cut the peel into $\frac{1}{4}$-inch strips.
2.  Place two cups of the peel in a pan, cover with cold water, boil for 20 minutes, and drain. **Repeat this process two more times** (for a total of three boils).
3.  In a saucepan, combine **1 ½ cups of sugar**, **¾ cup of water**, and **½ teaspoon of salt**. Cook and stir until the sugar dissolves.
4.  Add the drained peel to the syrup, bring to a boil, and simmer for 20 minutes until translucent.
5.  Gradually stir in the **lime-flavored gelatin** over low heat until dissolved.
6.  Remove the peel from the syrup and drain on a rack for 20 minutes.
7.  Roll each piece of peel in **green sugar crystals** (if using).
8.  Let the peel dry thoroughly on a rack before storing in a covered jar.
