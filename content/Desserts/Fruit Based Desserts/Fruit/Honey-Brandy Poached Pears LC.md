### Instructions
1.  Peel the **pears**, coring them from the bottom while leaving them whole.
2.  In a deep skillet, combine the **sugar** and **water**. Heat until the sugar is dissolved.
3.  Stir in the **honey** and then add the pears.
4.  Poach the pears over low heat, basting occasionally, until they are tender (about 20-30 minutes).
5.  Let the pears cool completely in the syrup.
6.  Stir in the **brandy** and serve.
