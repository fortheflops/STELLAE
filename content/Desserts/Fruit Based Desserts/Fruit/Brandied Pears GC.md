*A classic preserving recipe for brandied pears, perfect for enjoying as a special dessert or giving as a homemade gift.*
### Author
"The Complete Book of Home Preserving" by Ann Seranne, Doubleday & Co., New York, 1995
___
## Recipe
| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Per pound of fruit:** | |
| 1 pound | [[Pears]] |
| 1 pound | [[Sugar]] |
| 1 cup | [[Water]] |
| **Per pint of syrup:** | |
| 1 pint | [[Brandy]] |
___
## Instructions
1.  Wash, peel, and cut the **pears** as desired. Weigh the prepared fruit.
2.  In a large pot, combine **1 pound of sugar** and **1 cup of water** for every pound of pears. Bring to a boil, stirring to dissolve the sugar.
3.  Cook the pears in the boiling syrup in batches, for 10-15 minutes per batch, until tender.
4.  Remove the cooked pears and pack them into hot, sterilized jars.
5.  Continue to boil the remaining syrup until it is thick (222°F on a candy thermometer).
6.  Let the syrup cool completely.
7.  Once cooled, add **1 pint of brandy** for every pint of syrup. Stir well.
8.  Fill the jars of pears to overflowing with the brandy syrup.
9.  Seal the jars at once.
