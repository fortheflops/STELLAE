*A classic and dramatic dessert, also known as Cherries Jubilee, where cherries are flambéed in brandy and served over ice cream.*

## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 1 (17 oz) can | Pitted [[Bing Cherries]] |
| 3/4 cup | [[Currant Jelly]] |
| 1/2 cup | [[Brandy]] |
| For serving | [[Vanilla Ice Cream]] |
___
## Instructions
1.  Drain the **cherries** well.
2.  Melt the **currant jelly** in a chafing dish or skillet over direct heat, stirring frequently.
3.  Add the cherries to the jelly and heat through.
4.  Gently warm the **brandy** and pour it into the center of the cherry mixture.
5.  Carefully ignite the brandy with a long match. Stand back from the flame.
6.  Once the flames die down, spoon the cherries and sauce over portions of **ice cream**.
7.  Serve immediately.
___

>[!Warning]
>Use extreme caution when flambéing. Never pour brandy directly from the bottle into a hot pan near a flame, and always keep a lid nearby to extinguish flames if necessary.
