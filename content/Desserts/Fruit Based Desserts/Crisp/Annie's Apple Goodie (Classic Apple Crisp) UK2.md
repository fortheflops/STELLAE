| Measurements | Ingredients |

## Instructions
1.  Preheat oven to 350°F.
2.  In a bowl, mix together the **apples**, **cinnamon**, **white sugar**, **1 tablespoon of flour**, and **salt**. Spread this mixture into a greased baking dish.
3.  For the **Topping**: In a separate bowl, combine the **nuts**, **butter**, **brown sugar**, **1/4 cup of flour**, **baking powder**, and **baking soda**. Mix until crumbly.
4.  Sprinkle the topping evenly over the apple mixture.
5.  Bake for 30 to 40 minutes, until the topping is golden and the fruit is bubbly.
