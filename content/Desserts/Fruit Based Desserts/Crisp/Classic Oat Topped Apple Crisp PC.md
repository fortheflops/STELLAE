## Instructions
1.  Preheat oven to 375°F.
2.  **For the Topping:** In a bowl, mix the **flour** and **3/4 cup brown sugar**. Cut in the chilled **butter** until crumbly. Stir in the **instant oat flakes**.
3.  **For the Filling:** In a separate bowl, mix the **apples**, **lemon juice**, **1/4 cup brown sugar**, and **cinnamon**.
4.  Press half of the topping mixture into the bottom of a buttered baking dish.
5.  Spread the apple mixture on top.
6.  Cover the apples with the rest of the topping mixture.
7.  Cover with foil and bake for 30 minutes.
