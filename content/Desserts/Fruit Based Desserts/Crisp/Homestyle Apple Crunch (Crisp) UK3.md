*A simple and comforting apple crisp (or 'crunch') with a classic oatmeal streusel topping, perfect for using Jonathan apples.*

## Recipe
### Filling
| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 4 Cups | [[Jonathan Apples]], Peeled & Sliced |
| 1/2-3/4 Cup | [[Sugar]] |
| 1 Teaspoon | [[Cinnamon]] |
### Topping
| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Flour]] |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Sugar]] |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Butter]], chilled |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Uncooked Oatmeal]] |

___
## Instructions
1.  Preheat oven to 350°F.
2.  In a bowl, combine the **apples**, **1/2 to 3/4 cup sugar**, and **cinnamon**. Place the mixture in a greased 9x9 inch pan.
3.  In a separate bowl, blend together the **1/2 cup sugar**, **1/2 cup flour**, **1/2 cup butter**, and **1/2 cup oatmeal** with a pastry blender until the mixture resembles small peas.
4.  Sprinkle the topping evenly over the apples.
5.  Bake for 30-45 minutes, or until the apples are tender and the top is lightly browned.
6.  Serve hot with ice cream or whipped cream.
