*A simple and classic apple crisp with a minimalist, buttery brown sugar topping.*
### Author
Nancy Rucker
___
## Recipe
### Filling
| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 4 Cups | [[Apples]], thinly sliced |
| To taste | [[Cinnamon]] |
### Topping
| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| $\frac{3}{4}$ Cup | [[Brown Sugar]] |
| $\frac{1}{3}$ Cup | [[Flour]] |
| $\frac{1}{3}$ Cup | [[Butter]], chilled |

___
## Instructions
1.  Preheat Oven to 300°F.
2.  In a bowl, combine the **flour** and **brown sugar** for the topping.
3.  Cut in the chilled **butter** until the mixture is crumbly. Set aside.
4.  Place the thinly sliced **apples** into a casserole dish and sprinkle with **cinnamon**.
5.  Top the apples with the crumbly mixture.
6.  Bake for 30 minutes.
