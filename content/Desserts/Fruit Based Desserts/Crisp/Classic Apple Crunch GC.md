## Recipe
### Filling
| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 2 1/2 cups | [[Apples]], sliced and drained |
| 1/2 cup | [[Sugar]] (white) |
### Topping
| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 cup | [[Butter]], chilled |
| 1/2 cup | [[Brown Sugar]] |
| 1 cup | [[Flour]] |
| 1/2 teaspoon | [[Cinnamon]] |

___
## Instructions
1.  Preheat the oven to 375°F.
2.  Place the drained, sliced **apples** in a deep baking dish and sprinkle with the **1/2 cup of white sugar**.
3.  In a separate bowl, combine the **brown sugar**, **flour**, and **cinnamon** for the topping.
4.  Cut in the chilled **butter** until the mixture is crumbly.
5.  Sprinkle the topping evenly over the apples.
6.  Bake for 45 minutes, or until the apples are done and the topping is crunchy.
