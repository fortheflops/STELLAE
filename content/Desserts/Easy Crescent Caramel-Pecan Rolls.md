# Easy Crescent Caramel-Pecan Rolls

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A super quick and easy recipe for delicious caramel-pecan rolls using refrigerated crescent roll dough. Perfect for a fast breakfast treat or dessert.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 25 mins | None | 32 small rolls |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 13x9 inch baking pan
* Small bowl
* Large serving platter

---
## Ingredients

### Caramel Layer
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5 Tbsp | [[Margarine]] | or butter |
| 3/4 Cup | [[Brown Sugar]] | packed |
| 1/4 Cup | [[Water]] | none |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Pecans]] | chopped |

### Rolls & Filling
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 (8-oz) cans | [[Crescent Rolls]] | refrigerated dinner rolls |
| 3 Tbsp | [[Butter]] | or margarine, softened |
| 1/4 Cup | [[Sugar]] | none |
| 2 tsp | [[Cinnamon]] | none |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Caramel Base
1. Preheat oven to 375°F. Place **5 Tbsp of margarine** (or butter) in a 13x9 inch baking pan and put it in the oven for a few minutes to melt.
2. Remove the pan from the oven. Stir the **3/4 cup brown sugar** and **1/4 cup water** into the melted margarine until combined. Sprinkle the **1/2 cup chopped pecans** evenly over the mixture.

### Step 2: Assemble the Rolls
3. In a small bowl, combine the **1/4 cup sugar** and **2 tsp cinnamon** for the filling.
4. Open both cans of crescent dough. Separate the **2 cans refrigerated crescent dinner rolls** into 8 rectangles, pressing the diagonal perforations to seal them.
5. Spread the **3 Tbsp softened butter** (or margarine) evenly over the 8 dough rectangles. Sprinkle the cinnamon-sugar mixture evenly over the butter.
6. Starting from the shortest side of each rectangle, roll it up tightly into a log, creating 8 short logs. Cut each log into 4 equal pieces, making 32 small rolls.

### Step 3: Bake and Serve
7. Place the **32 small rolls** cut-side down into the prepared pan on top of the caramel-pecan mixture.
8. Bake for **20-25 minutes**, until golden brown and fragrant.
9. Immediately invert the hot pan onto a large serving platter. Scrape any remaining caramel and pecans from the pan onto the rolls. Serve warm.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Best served warm immediately after baking to ensure the caramel coats the rolls properly.
