# Rice Raisin Pudding

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty One | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A comforting baked rice and raisin pudding spiced with nutmeg. Perfect for a cozy family dessert.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 50 mins | None | 4-6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 1 quart casserole
* Whisk
* Baking dish

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 1/2 C | [[Rice]] | cooked |
| 1/4 C | [[Raisins]] |  |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] |  |
| 1 1/2 C | [[Milk]] |  |
| 1/2 C | [[Sugar]] | less |
| 1/2 t | [[Nutmeg]] |  |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation
1. Place **1 1/2 C cooked rice** & **1/4 C raisins** in a greased 1 quart casserole.
2. Whisk **2 eggs**, **1 1/2 C milk**, **1/2 C sugar** & **1/2 t nutmeg**. Pour over rice.

### Step 2: Baking
3. Bake uncovered at 375 degrees for 45-50 minutes or until knife comes out clean.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Can be assembled ahead and refrigerated before baking, though bake time may need slight adjustment.
