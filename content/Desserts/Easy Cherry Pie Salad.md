# Easy Cherry Pie Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A delightful no-bake fruit salad featuring a marinated base of cherry pie filling and pineapple, finished with fresh bananas and crunchy pecans. This vintage-inspired treat is perfect for gatherings and offers a unique blend of textures.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 0 mins | 8 hours | 10–12 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large mixing bowl
* Silicone spatula
* Plastic wrap

---
## Ingredients

### Base (Marinate Overnight)
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 can (21 oz) | [[Cherry Pie Filling]] | Standard |
| 1 can (16 oz) | [[Crushed Pineapple with juice]] | Do not drain |
| 1/2 cup | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |

### Add-Ins (Last Minute)
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 pkg (approx. 5 oz) | [[Mini Marshmallows]] | Standard |
| 1 cup | [[Pecans]] | Chopped |
| 2-3 | [[Bananas]] | Thick sliced |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Marinate the Base
1. In a large bowl, combine the **1 can cherry pie filling**, **1 can crushed pineapple with juice**, and **1/2 cup sugar**.
2. Cover the bowl tightly and refrigerate overnight to allow the flavors to meld and the mixture to thicken.

### Step 2: Final Assembly
3. Just before serving, add the **1/2 pkg mini marshmallows**, **1 cup pecans**, and **2-3 bananas** to the chilled fruit mixture.
4. Fold everything together gently until just combined and serve immediately while the **bananas** are fresh and the **marshmallows** retain their texture.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The base must be marinated overnight for best results, but add the bananas and marshmallows immediately before serving to prevent sogginess.
