# Tomato Soup Spice Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** Patsy Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A surprisingly delicious and incredibly moist old-fashioned spice cake featuring condensed tomato soup for a tender crumb and rich flavor. Filled with warm spices and plump raisins, this vintage dessert is wonderfully paired with cream cheese frosting.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 35 mins | None | 1 2-layer 8-inch cake |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* two 8-inch round layer cake pans
* medium bowl
* small bowl
* large bowl
* electric mixer
* wire rack
* toothpick

| 1 Tbsp | [[Baking Powder]] | None |
| ½ tsp | [[Baking Soda]] | None |
| ½ tsp | [[Ground Clove]] | None |
| ½ tsp | [[Cinnamon]] | None |
| ½ tsp | [[Nutmeg]] | None |
| ½ Cup | [[Shortening]] | None |
| 1 Cup | [[Sugar]] | None |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] | Well-beaten |
| 1 ¼ Cups | [[Tomato Soup]] | Condensed |
| 1 Cup | [[Seedless Raisins]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Dry Ingredients
1. Preheat your oven to **375°F**. Grease and flour two 8-inch round layer cake pans.
2. In a medium bowl, sift together the **2 cups flour**, **1 tbsp baking powder**, **½ tsp baking soda**, **½ tsp ground clove**, **½ tsp cinnamon**, and **½ tsp nutmeg**.
3. In a small bowl, toss the **1 cup seedless raisins** with about a tablespoon of the flour mixture until they are well coated.

### Step 2: Mix the Batter
4. In a large bowl, cream the **½ cup shortening** with an electric mixer. Gradually add the **1 cup sugar** and continue beating until light and fluffy.
5. Add the **2 eggs** (well-beaten) and mix thoroughly.
6. Add the flour mixture to the creamed mixture in three parts, alternating with the **1 1/4 cups tomato soup**, beginning and ending with the dry ingredients. Mix until the batter is smooth.
7. Fold in the flour-coated **1 cup seedless raisins**.

### Step 3: Bake and Cool
8. Pour the batter evenly into the prepared pans.
9. Bake for **35 minutes**, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.
10. Cool in the pans for **10 minutes** before turning out onto a wire rack to cool completely. Frost as desired.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the unfrosted cake wrapped tightly at room temperature for up to 2 days, or seal tightly and refrigerate for up to 4 days.
