# Oatmeal Cookies

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A delightful old-fashioned recipe for chewy oatmeal cookies packed with sweet raisins. These comforting treats bake up golden and delicious in just minutes.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 12 mins | None | 24 cookies |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Baking sheet

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 cup | [[Shortening]] | Softened |
| 1 cup | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 1/2 cup | [[Sweet milk]] | Whole milk |
| 2 cups | [[Oatmeal]] | Rolled oats |
| 2 cups | [[Flour]] | All-purpose |
| 1/2 tsp | [[Salt]] | None |
| 1 to 2 | [[Eggs]] | Beaten |
| 1/2 tsp | [[Soda]] | Baking soda |
| 1 tsp | [[Cinnamon]] | Ground |
| 1 cup | [[Raisins]] | Optional |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing and Baking
1. Preheat your oven to **350°F**.
2. Combine the **1 cup shortening**, **1 cup sugar**, **1/2 cup sweet milk**, **2 cups oatmeal**, **2 cups flour**, **1/2 tsp salt**, **1 or 2 eggs**, **1/2 tsp soda**, **1 tsp cinnamon**, and **1 cup raisins** (if using) in a large mixing bowl, stirring until a cohesive dough forms.
3. Drop spoonfuls of the dough onto a baking sheet and bake for **12-15 mins** until the edges are golden brown and firm to the touch.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The dough can be prepared ahead of time and stored in the refrigerator for up to 2 days before baking.
