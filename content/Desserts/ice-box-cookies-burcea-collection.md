# Ice Box Cookies

> 📜 **Collection:** Burcea Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic heirloom recipe for crisp, buttery slice-and-bake ice box cookies. Richly textured with a blend of butter and lard, and studded with chopped nuts.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mix the Dough
1. In a large mixing bowl, combine **1 cup brown sugar**, **1 cup white sugar**, **3/4 cup butter**, and **3/4 cup lard**.
2. Mix with hand until thoroughly combined and creamy.
3. Incorporate the **3 eggs** and **1 tsp vanilla extract**, mixing well.
4. Sift together **4 1/2 cups flour**, **1 tsp baking soda**, **1/2 tsp salt**, and **1 tsp baking powder**.
5. Gradually blend the dry ingredients into the wet mixture, then fold in **1/2 cup nuts** until a stiff dough forms.

### Step 2: Shape and Chill
6. Divide the dough and roll into logs (like a jelly roll) using **wax paper** to wrap them tightly.
7. Put in a cold place (refrigerator) to chill overnight or until completely firm.

### Step 3: Slice and Bake
8. Preheat your oven to 375°F (190°C).
9. Using a sharp knife, slice the chilled dough logs into thin rounds.
10. Place slices on an ungreased baking sheet and bake for 8 to 10 minutes until the edges are lightly golden brown and set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The cookie dough logs can be kept wrapped in the refrigerator for up to 3 days, or frozen for up to 3 months before slicing and baking.
