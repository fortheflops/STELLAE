# Grandma's Pound Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic, buttery old-fashioned pound cake recipe yielding two delicious loaves. Baked to golden perfection with rich texture and delicate crumb.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 25 mins | 1 hour | None | 16 servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Electric mixer
* Loaf pans

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3 cubes | [[Butter]] | Softened |
| 1 1/2 cups | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 2 cups | [[Cake Flour]] | Sifted |
| 8 | [[Egg Yolks]] | Separated |
| 8 | [[Egg Whites]] | Separated |
| to taste | [[Flavoring]] | Extract of choice |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing the Batter
1. Cream the **3 cubes butter** and **1 1/2 cups sugar** together until light and fluffy.
2. Beat in the **8 egg yolks**.
3. Alternately add the sifted **2 cups cake flour** and your choice of **flavoring**, mixing just until combined.

### Step 2: Folding and Baking
4. In a separate bowl, beat the **8 egg whites** until stiff peaks form, then gently fold them into the batter.
5. Pour the batter into greased loaf pans.
6. Bake at 325°F for 1 hour, or until a wooden pick inserted in the center comes out clean and the top is golden brown.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store tightly wrapped at room temperature for up to 3 days, or freeze the baked loaves for up to 2 months.
