# Almond Jam Thumbprints

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Eighteen | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic European-style thumbprint cookie featuring a rich butter dough rolled in crunchy almonds and filled with sweet jam. Perfectly dusted with icing sugar, these delicate treats are beautiful on any dessert platter.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Dough
1. In a mixing bowl, stir together **1/2 pound butter**, **3 egg yolks**, and **150 g sugar** until smooth and well combined.
2. Add **350 g flour** and mix for about **2-3 minutes** until a soft, cohesive dough forms.

### Step 2: Shape and Coat
3. Form the dough into nut-sized balls.
4. Roll each ball first in the **3 beaten egg whites**, then roll in the **1/2 cup almonds** mixed with **2 tbsp sugar** until evenly coated.
5. Place the coated balls on a baking tray and punch a hole in the middle of each ball using your thumb or the end of a wooden spoon.

### Step 3: Bake and Finish
6. Bake (typically at 350°F / 180°C) until they are a light yellow/golden color and set.
7. Fill each hole with **1/2 cup jam** and dust **2 tbsp icing sugar** over the top before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 5 days. You can bake the cookies ahead of time and fill them with jam just before serving.
