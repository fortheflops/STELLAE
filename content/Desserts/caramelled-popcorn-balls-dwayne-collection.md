# Caramelled Popcorn Balls

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *Delight in these sweet and crunchy Caramelled Popcorn Balls, made with freshly popped corn and roasted peanuts bound together by rich caramel. Perfect for gatherings, holidays, or a comforting homemade treat.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 15 mins | 10 mins | 6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Saucepan
* Large mixing bowl
* Wax paper

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 qts | [[Popped Corn]] | Freshly popped |
| 1 lb | [[Caramel]] | Soft or unwrapped |
| 1/4 cup | [[Water]] | None |
| 1/4 cup | [[Peanuts]] | Roasted |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparing the Caramel
1. Combine the **1 lb caramel** and **1/4 cup water** in a saucepan over medium heat, stirring until completely melted and smooth.

### Step 2: Combining and Shaping
2. In a large mixing bowl, toss together the **2 qts popped corn** and **1/4 cup roasted peanuts**.
3. Pour the warm melted caramel mixture evenly over the popcorn and peanuts, stirring quickly until everything is well coated.
4. Shape the mixture firmly into balls while still warm, and place them on wax paper until completely set and firm to the touch.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store popcorn balls wrapped individually in plastic wrap or wax paper in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 3 days.
