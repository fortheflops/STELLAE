# Millionaire Pie

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A rich and creamy no-bake Millionaire Pie yielding two delicious pies loaded with pineapple, pecans, and coconut. Perfectly chilled and set until firm and fluffy.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | None | 4 hours | 16 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Electric mixer
* Pie pans

---
## Ingredients

### Pie Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | [[Tall can pineapple]] | Crushed |
| 4 oz | [[Dream Whip]] | 1 pkg. |
| 1 cup | [[Pecan]] | Chopped |
| 1 | [[Lemon]] | Juiced |
| 1 can | [[Eagle Brand milk]] | Sweetened condensed milk |
| 1 cup | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 1 cup | [[Angel Flake coconut]] | Optional |
| As needed | [[Vanilla wafers]] | Or graham crust |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing and Preparation
1. Mix the **juice of 1 lemon** with the **1 can Eagle Brand milk** in a large bowl.
2. Whip the **4 oz pkg. Dream Whip** according to directions on package.
3. Mix all the above ingredients together until well combined and fluffy.

### Step 2: Assembly and Chilling
4. Pour the mixture into pans that have been lined with **vanilla wafers** or a **graham crust** (may also be put in regular pie pans).
5. Refrigerate until thoroughly chilled and set, then serve.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Keep refrigerated until ready to serve; can be made a day ahead.
