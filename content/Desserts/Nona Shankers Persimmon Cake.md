# Nona Shankers' Persimmon Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Nona Shankers | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A wonderfully moist, pudding-like cake packed with two cups of sweet persimmon pulp, warm spices, raisins, and nuts. This simple one-bowl vintage recipe yields a rich tube cake that tastes even better the next day.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 1 hour | None | 1 tube cake (12-16 servings) |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Tube or Bundt pan
* Large bowl
* Medium bowl
* Whisk
* Wooden skewer

| 2 Cups | [[Flour]] | All-purpose |
| 1 Cup | [[Milk]] | None |
| 2 Cups | [[Persimmon Pulp]] | From very ripe persimmons |
| 1 Teaspoon | [[Lemon Juice]] | None |
| 3 ½ Teaspoons | [[Soda]] | Baking soda |
| 2 Teaspoons | [[Cinnamon]] | None |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] | Beaten |
| 1 Cup | [[Raisins]] | None |
| 1 Cup | [[Nuts]] | Chopped |
| 1 Teaspoon | [[Vanilla Extract]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and Dry Ingredients
1. Preheat your oven to **350°F**. Lightly grease a tube or Bundt pan.
2. In a large bowl, whisk together the **2 cups sugar**, **2 cups flour**, **3 ½ teaspoons baking soda**, and **2 teaspoons cinnamon**.

### Step 2: Wet Ingredients and Batter
3. In a separate medium bowl, combine the **1 cup milk**, **2 cups persimmon pulp**, **1 teaspoon lemon juice**, **2 beaten eggs**, and **1 teaspoon vanilla extract**.
4. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients and stir until just combined.
5. Fold in the **1 cup raisins** and **1 cup chopped nuts** until evenly distributed.

### Step 3: Baking and Cooling
6. Pour the batter into the prepared tube pan.
7. Bake for **1 hour**, or until a long wooden skewer inserted into the center comes out clean.
8. Let the cake cool completely in the pan before attempting to remove it, as it will be very moist. The cake's flavor will be even better the next day.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store wrapped tightly at room temperature or in the refrigerator; the flavor and texture improve significantly the next day.
