# Maxine Lindsey's Coconut Poke Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Maxine Lindsey | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A wonderfully moist and tropical cake from Maxine Lindsey featuring a simple cake mix base with mandarin oranges, saturated with creamy coconut, and topped with a fluffy coconut Cool Whip frosting.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 35 mins | 1 hour cooling | 1 9x13 cake (12-16 servings) |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 9x13 inch baking pan
* Large mixing bowl
* Medium mixing bowl
* Wooden spoon or skewer
* Dry skillet

---
## Ingredients

### For the Cake
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 box | [[Cake Mix]] | White or Yellow |
| 3 | [[Eggs]] | Large |
| 1 (11 oz) can | [[Mandarin Oranges]] | Undrained |
| 1/2 cup | [[Oil]] | Vegetable oil |

### For the Soak & Topping
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 (15 oz) can | [[Cream of Coconut]] | Not coconut milk |
| 1 (12 oz) container | [[Cool Whip]] | Thawed |
| 1 1/2 cups | [[Coconut]] | Shredded, divided |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Bake the Cake
1. Preheat your oven to **350°F**. Grease and flour a **9x13 inch pan**.
2. In a large bowl, combine the **1 box cake mix**, **3 eggs**, **1 (11 oz) can mandarin oranges**, and **1/2 cup oil**. Mix well with a spoon or electric mixer until smooth.
3. Pour the batter into the prepared pan and spread evenly.
4. Bake for **30 to 35 minutes**, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean and the edges are lightly golden.

### Step 2: Poke and Soak
5. Remove the cake from the oven and let it cool in the pan for about **1 hour** until room temperature.
6. Using the handle of a wooden spoon or a skewer, poke holes all over the cake, about **1 to 1 1/2 inches apart**.
7. Slowly pour the **1 (15 oz) can cream of coconut** over the entire surface of the cake, allowing it to saturate the cake and seep completely into the holes.

### Step 3: Frost and Garnish
8. In a medium bowl, fold **1 cup coconut** into the **1 (12 oz) container Cool Whip**.
9. Use this mixture as an icing, spreading it evenly over the top of the cooled, saturated cake.
10. If desired, toast the remaining **1/2 cup coconut** in a dry skillet over medium-low heat until golden brown and fragrant, then sprinkle it over the top of the cake as a garnish.
11. Refrigerate the cake until ready to serve.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the frosted cake sealed in the refrigerator for up to 4 days; the flavor often improves by the second day as the coconut fully saturates the cake.
