# Classic Butterscotch Pie with Pecan Crust

> 📜 **Collection:** General Archive | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A rich and creamy homemade butterscotch pie with a simple, press-in pecan and brown sugar crust, topped with a generous layer of whipped cream.*

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 20 mins | 4 hours | One 9-inch pie |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 9-inch pie pan
* Medium saucepan

## Ingredients

### Crust
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 stick (1/2 cup) | [[Butter]] | Melted |
| 1 cup | [[Flour]] | All-purpose |
| 1/4 cup | [[Brown Sugar]] | Packed |
| 1/2 cup | [[Pecans]] | Chopped |

### Filling
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 cup | [[Brown Sugar]] | Packed |
| 1/3 cup | [[Flour]] | All-purpose |
| 1/2 teaspoon | [[Salt]] | Standard |
| 2 cups | [[Milk]] | Whole milk preferred |
| 2 | [[Egg Yolks]] | Beaten |
| 1/2 teaspoon | [[Vanilla]] | Extract |
| 2 tablespoons | [[Butter]] | Unsalted |
| 1 cup | [[Heavy Cream]] | For whipping |

---

## Instructions

### For the Crust
1. Preheat oven to **400°F**.
2. In a bowl, mix the **1 cup flour**, **1/4 cup brown sugar**, and **1/2 cup pecans**. Add the **1 stick melted butter** and combine until crumbly.
3. Press the mixture firmly into the bottom and up the sides of a buttered 9-inch pie pan.
4. Bake for **10 minutes** until set. Let cool completely.

### For the Filling
5. In a saucepan, whisk together the **1 cup brown sugar**, **1/3 cup flour**, and **1/2 teaspoon salt**. Gradually whisk in the **2 cups milk** until smooth.
6. Stir constantly over medium heat until the mixture thickens and comes to a rolling boil.
7. Slowly stir a small amount of the hot mixture into the **2 beaten egg yolks**, then pour the yolks back into the saucepan, stirring constantly. Cook for **2 more minutes**.
8. Remove from heat and stir in the **2 tablespoons butter** and **1/2 teaspoon vanilla** until the butter is melted and everything is smooth.
9. Pour the hot filling into the cooled crust. Place plastic wrap directly on the surface and chill for at least **4 hours** until completely firm.
10. Before serving, whip the **1 cup heavy cream** to stiff peaks and spread over the chilled pie.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the baked and chilled pie covered in the refrigerator for up to 3 days.
