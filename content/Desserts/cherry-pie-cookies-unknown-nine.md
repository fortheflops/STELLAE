# Cherry Pie Cookies

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Nine | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *Delightful thumbprint cookies filled with sweet cherry pie filling and drizzled with melted chocolate. Perfect for gatherings and sweet cravings alike.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 15 mins | None | 2 doz |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Baking sheet
* Parchment paper

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 cup | [[Butter]] | Softened |
| 1/2 cup | [[Margarine]] | Softened |
| 2 cups | [[Flour]] | None |
| 3/4 cup | [[Powdered Sugar]] | None |
| 1 tsp | [[Vanilla]] | None |
| 1 can | [[Cherry Pie Filling]] | None |
| 1/2 cup | [[White or Milk Chocolate Chips]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Dough Preparation
1. Mix **1/2 cup butter** and **1/2 cup margarine** until fluffy.
2. Add **2 cups flour**, **3/4 cup powdered sugar**, and **1 tsp vanilla** and mix until just combined.

### Step 2: Shaping and Filling
3. Roll dough into 2" balls.
4. Place on parchment paper. Press thumb into center of cookie to make a well.
5. Place cherries from the **1 can cherry pie filling** into each well.

### Step 3: Baking and Drizzle
6. Bake at 325° for 12-15 minutes until edges are set.
7. Melt **1/2 cup white or milk chocolate chips** and drizzle over cooled cookies.
8. Let chocolate set and serve immediately or store in airtight container.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 3 days.
