# Juanita's Pecan Pie

> 📜 **Collection:** General Archive | ✍️ **Attribution:** Juanita | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic pecan pie from Juanita, featuring a rich, sweet filling made with Karo syrup and a touch of flour for a slightly thicker, more set texture.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 50 mins | 2 hours | One 9-inch pie |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large bowl
* Wire whisk or spoon
* 9-inch pie pan
* Wire cooling rack
* Aluminum foil

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | [[Pie Crust]] | unbaked 9-inch |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] | whole |
| 1 Cup | [[Karo Syrup]] | Light or Dark |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Sugar]] | standard |
| 4 Heaping Tbsp. | [[Flour]] | acts as a thickener |
| 1 Cup | [[Pecans]] | chopped or halves |
| 1 tsp | [[Vanilla]] | extract |
| 2 Tbsp | [[Cream]] | liquid |
| 2 to 3 Tbsp | [[Butter]] | melted |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Mix Filling
1. Preheat oven to **325°F**.
2. In a large bowl, beat the **2 eggs** until smooth.
3. Add the **1 cup Karo syrup**, **1/2 cup sugar**, and **4 heaping tbsp. flour**. Mix well until thoroughly combined and smooth.

### Step 2: Add Remaining Ingredients
4. Stir in the **1 cup pecans**, **1 tsp vanilla**, **2 tbsp cream**, and **2 to 3 tbsp melted butter** until fully incorporated.
5. Pour the filling mixture into the unbaked 9-inch **pie crust**.

### Step 3: Bake and Cool
6. Bake for **45-60 minutes**, or until the center is set (it may jiggle slightly but should not be liquid). If the crust browns too quickly, cover the edges with foil.
7. Let the pie cool completely on a wire rack for at least **2 hours** until the filling is fully set before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the cooled pie covered in the refrigerator for up to 3 days, or serve at room temperature on the day it is baked.
