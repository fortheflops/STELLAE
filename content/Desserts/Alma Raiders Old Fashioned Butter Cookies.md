# Alma Raider's Old Fashioned Butter Cookies

> 📜 **Collection:** PatsyCollection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Alma Raider | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic, versatile roll-and-cut butter cookie recipe perfect for festive shapes. This rich, tender dough yields a delicate, buttery treat ideal for any occasion.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 8 mins | 1 hour | 4–5 dozen cookies |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* large mixing bowl
* medium mixing bowl
* electric mixer
* rolling pin
* cookie cutters
* ungreased baking sheets
* wire cooling rack

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |

| 3 Cups | [[Flour]] | sifted |

| ½ Tsp | [[Salt]] | none |
| 1 Cup | [[Butter]] or [[Margarine]] | softened, 2 sticks |
| ¾ Cup | [[Sugar]] | none |
| 1 | [[Egg]] | unbeaten |
| 2 Tbsp | [[Milk]] | none |
| 1 ½ Tsp | [[Vanilla]] | none |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Dough
1. In a medium bowl, sift together the **3 cups flour**, **1 tsp baking powder**, and **½ tsp salt**.
2. In a large bowl, cream together the **1 cup butter or margarine** and **¾ cup sugar** with an electric mixer until light and fluffy.
3. Add the **1 unbeaten egg**, **2 tbsp milk**, and **1 ½ tsp vanilla** to the creamed mixture and beat well.
4. Gradually add the dry ingredients to the wet ingredients, mixing on low speed until a soft dough forms.

### Step 2: Chill and Roll
5. Divide the dough in half, wrap each in plastic wrap, and chill for at least **1 hour**.
6. Preheat your oven to **400°F**.
7. On a lightly floured surface, roll one portion of the dough out to **⅛-inch thickness**.
8. Cut into shapes with cookie cutters and place on an ungreased baking sheet.

### Step 3: Bake
9. Bake for **5-8 minutes**, or until the edges are just beginning to turn golden.
10. Let the cookies cool on the baking sheet for a minute before transferring them to a wire rack to cool completely.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The dough can be prepared and kept chilled for up to 2 days, or frozen for up to 1 month before rolling and baking.
