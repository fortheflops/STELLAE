## Recipe
### Crust
| Measurements        | Ingredients          |
| :------------------ | :------------------- |
| 1 Stick             | [[Margarine]]              |
| 1 Cup               | [[Flour]]         |
| 1 3 oz. Package      | [[Cream Cheese]]          |
### Filling
| Measurements        | Ingredients          |
| :------------------ | :------------------- |
| 3                   | [[Eggs]]              |
| 2/3 Cup               | [[Sugar]]         |
| 1/4 Teaspoon               | [[Salt]]          |
| 1/2 Cup               | [[Butter]]              |
| 1 Cup               | [[Light Corn Syrup]]         |
| 1 Cup               | [[Pecans]]          |
___
### Instructions
1. Blend & chill for 1 hr. ingredients for Crust.
2. Divide into sm. ball & press into smallest size muffin tins so that it forms a tiny pie shell in each space.
3. Chop pecans finely, mix with other ingredients, melted butter, eggs, salt & sugar.
4. Pour into cups.
5. Bake at 375 degrees for 40-50 minutes.
