## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| Three Cups                       | [[Walnuts]]                               |
| $\frac{3}{4}$ Cup                | [[Brown Sugar]]                           |
| $\frac{3}{4}$ Cup                | White [[Sugar]]                           |
| $\frac{1}{2}$ Cup                | California [[Sherry]]                     |
| $\frac{1}{4}$ Cup                | [[Honey]]                                 |
| $\frac{1}{4}$ Teaspoon           | [[Cinnamon]]                              |
| $\frac{1}{4}$ Teaspoon           | [[Nutmeg]]                                |
| Dash                             | [[Salt]]                                  |
___
## Instructions
1.  Combine sugars, honey, salt, and Sherry.
2.  Stir over flame until sugars dissolve and mixture reaches soft ball stage (235-245°F or 112-118°C).
3.  Remove from fire and add cinnamon, nutmeg, and nuts.
4.  Beat until creamy.
5.  Place on waxed paper and separate when hard.
