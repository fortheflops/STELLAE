# Lemon Bars

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Eighteen | ✍️ **Attribution:** Ann Plante | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *Delightfully zesty lemon bars featuring a buttery shortbread crust and a bright, frothy citrus filling. Perfect for community gatherings, bake sales, or a sunny afternoon treat.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 40 mins | 30 mins | 12 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 9x13 baking pan
* Mixing bowl
* Whisk
* Wire rack

## Ingredients

### Crust
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 1/2 cups | [[flour]] | all-purpose |
| 2/3 cup | [[powdered sugar]] | none |
| 3/4 cup | [[butter]] or [[margarine]] | softened |

### Filling
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3 | [[eggs]] | none |
| 1 1/2 cups | [[sugar]] | granulated |
| 3 tablespoons | [[flour]] | all-purpose |
| 1/4 cup | [[lemon juice]] | freshly squeezed |
| 1/3 cup | [[powdered sugar]] | for decoration |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Crust
1. Heat the oven to 375 degrees F.
2. Grease a 9x13 baking pan.
3. Combine the **1 1/2 cups flour**, **2/3 cup powdered sugar**, and **3/4 cup butter or margarine**.
4. Pat dough evenly into the prepared pan.

### Step 2: Baking and Topping
5. Bake for **20 minutes**, until slightly golden.
6. While the crust is baking, whisk together **3 eggs**, **1 1/2 cups sugar**, **3 tablespoons flour**, and **1/4 cup lemon juice** until frothy.
7. Pour this lemon mixture over the hot crust.
8. Return to oven for an additional **20-25 minutes**, or until light golden brown and set.
9. Cool completely on a wire rack.
10. Dust the top with **1/3 cup powdered sugar** before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftover bars in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days, dusted with fresh powdered sugar right before serving.
