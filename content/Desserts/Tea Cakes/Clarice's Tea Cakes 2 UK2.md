## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
| 1½ Cups              | [[Sugar]]                     |
| 1 Cup               | [[Butter]] or [[Oleo]]      |
| 3 Cups              | [[Flour]]                     |
| 2                   | [[Eggs]]                      |
| 1 Teaspoon            | [[Baking Soda]]               |
| 1 Teaspoon            | [[Vanilla]]                   |
| Pinch               | [[Salt]]                      |
___
### Instructions
1.  Cream butter & sugar until creamy.
2.  Add eggs one at a time.
3.  Add flour gradually, soda, salt, & vanilla.
4.  Dough will be stiff.  Knead gently, roll out about ¼".
5. Cut out & place on cookie sheet.
6. Bake 375° until done.
