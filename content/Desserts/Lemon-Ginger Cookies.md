# Lemon-Ginger Cookies

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic drop cookie featuring a delightful combination of warm, spicy ginger and bright, fresh lemon. These simple treats are studded with bits of candied ginger for an extra flavor kick.*

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Butter]] or [[Oleo]] | softened |
| 3/4 Cup | [[Sugar]] | plus extra for sprinkling |
| 1 | [[Egg]] | large |
| 1 tsp | [[Lemon Rind]] | grated |
| 1 1/2 Cups | [[Flour]] | all-purpose |
| 1/2 tsp | [[Ginger]] | ground |
| 1/2 tsp | [[Baking Soda]] | none |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Salt]] | none |
| 1/4 Cup | [[Candied Ginger]] | finely chopped |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare and Cream
1. Preheat your oven to 350°F and grease your cookie sheets.
2. In a large bowl, cream together the **1/2 cup butter or oleo** and **3/4 cup sugar** until light, fluffy, and pale.
3. Beat in the **1 large egg** and **1 tsp grated lemon rind** until well incorporated.

### Step 2: Combine and Bake
4. In a separate bowl, sift together the **1 1/2 cups flour**, **1/2 tsp ground ginger**, **1/2 tsp baking soda**, and **1/4 tsp salt**.
5. Gradually add the dry ingredients to the creamed mixture, mixing until just combined.
6. By hand, stir in the **1/4 cup finely chopped candied ginger**.
7. Drop by rounded teaspoonfuls onto the prepared cookie sheets and sprinkle the tops with a little extra **sugar**.
8. Bake for **8 to 10 minutes**, or until the edges are lightly browned and the centers are set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 5 days or freeze for up to 3 months.
