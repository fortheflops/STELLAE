# Wacky Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** Patsy Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic Depression-era chocolate cake that is magically moist and rich without the use of eggs, butter, or milk. This simple, one-pan recipe is mixed directly in the baking dish for effortless preparation.*

---

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 30 mins | None | 9 servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 9x9 inch cake pan
* Whisk
* Fork
* Spoon

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 1/2 cups | [[Flour]] | all-purpose |
| 1 cup | [[White Sugar]] | granulated |
| 3 tablespoons | [[Cocoa]] | unsweetened |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Salt]] | fine |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Baking Soda]] | none |
| 1 tablespoon | [[Vinegar]] | white |
| 1 tablespoon | [[Vanilla]] | extract |
| 5 tablespoons | [[Salad Oil]] | vegetable or canola |
| 1 cup | [[Water]] | cold |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare Dry Base
1. Preheat your oven to 350°F.
2. In an ungreased 9x9 inch cake pan, sift or whisk together the **1 1/2 cups flour**, **1 cup white sugar**, **3 tablespoons cocoa**, **1 teaspoon salt**, and **1 teaspoon baking soda**.

### Step 2: Create Wells and Mix
3. Using the back of a spoon, make three depressions in the dry ingredients.
4. Pour the **1 tablespoon vinegar** into the first dent, the **1 tablespoon vanilla** into the second, and the **5 tablespoons salad oil** into the third.
5. Pour the **1 cup cold water** over the entire mixture.
6. Mix with a fork directly in the pan until the batter is smooth and well-combined.

### Step 3: Bake
7. Bake for **20-30 minutes**, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean. Let cool completely before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the cooled cake in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 3 days or freeze for up to 1 month.
