# Cherry Chip Loaf

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Nine | ✍️ **Attribution:** Judy | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A delightful sweet loaf recipe filled with chopped maraschino cherries, chocolate chips, and nuts folded into a rich sour cream batter. Baked as either two classic loaves or a single festive Bundt cake.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 1 hour 15 mins | None | 2 loaves or 1 Bundt cake |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Two 9x5-inch loaf pans or one 10-inch Bundt pan
* Mixing bowl
* Electric mixer or hand whisk
* Spatula

## Instructions

### Batter Preparation
1. Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C) and grease **2 loaf pans** (or **1 Bundt pan**) thoroughly.
2. In a small bowl, mix **1 tsp baking soda** into **1 pint sour cream** and set aside.
3. In a large bowl, cream together **2 sticks margarine**, **2 cups sugar**, and **3 eggs** until smooth.
4. Add **3 cups flour**, **1 pinch salt**, and **1 tsp vanilla extract** to the creamed mixture and mix well.
5. Fold in the sour cream and baking soda mixture until fully combined.
6. Gently fold in **1 cup chopped maraschino cherries**, **1 cup chocolate chips**, and optional **1 cup chopped nuts** until evenly distributed.

### Baking
7. Pour the batter evenly into the greased **2 loaf pans** (or **1 Bundt pan**).
8. Bake at 350°F for **1 hour and 15 minutes**, until a toothpick or cake tester inserted in the center comes out clean and the top is golden brown.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Cool completely before wrapping tightly. Store at room temperature for up to 4 days or freeze wrapped loaves for up to 3 months.
