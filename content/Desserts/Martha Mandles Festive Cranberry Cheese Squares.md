# Martha Mandle's Festive Cranberry Cheese Squares

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Martha Mandle | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A festive, multi-layered dessert bar featuring an oat crust, creamy cheesecake filling, and rich mincemeat and cranberry toppings.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Crust
1. Preheat oven to **350°F**. In a mixer bowl, beat the **1 cup sifted flour**, **1 cup quick or old-fashioned oats**, **3/4 cup plus 1 tablespoon firmly packed brown sugar**, and **10 tablespoons margarine or butter** until crumbly.
2. Reserve **1/2 cup** of this mixture for the topping.
3. Press the remaining crumb mixture firmly onto the bottom of a **13x9 inch pan**. Bake for **15 minutes**.

### Step 2: Prepare the Filling and Assemble
4. While the crust bakes, in a small mixer bowl, beat the **1 (8-ounce) package cream cheese** until fluffy. Gradually beat in the **1 (14-ounce) can Eagle Brand Sweetened Condensed Milk** until smooth, then beat in the **2 large eggs**.
5. Spread the cheese mixture over the hot, baked crust and top with spoonfuls of the **1 (9-ounce) jar mincemeat**.

### Step 3: Prepare Fruit Layer and Final Bake
6. In a small saucepan, combine the **1 tablespoon sugar** and **2 tablespoons cornstarch**. Stir in the **1 (16-ounce) can whole cranberry sauce** and cook until thickened. Spoon this over the mincemeat layer.
7. Sprinkle the reserved **1/2 cup** of crumb mixture over the top.
8. Bake for **40 minutes**, or until the filling is set and the top is golden brown.
9. Chill completely in the refrigerator before cutting into squares.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the baked squares tightly covered in the refrigerator for up to 5 days.
