# Melt Away Cookies

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic, tender shortbread cookie made with cornstarch for a signature melt-in-your-mouth texture. These delicate treats are flavored with vanilla and almond for a rich, sophisticated finish.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 14 mins | None | 3–4 dozen cookies |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* large mixing bowl
* electric mixer
* wire rack
* cookie sheet

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 cup | [[Butter]] | softened |
| 1/2 cup | [[Oleo]] | margarine, softened |
| 1/2 cup | [[Powdered Sugar]] | sifted |
| 1 1/2 cups | [[Flour]] | all-purpose |
| 1/4 cup | [[Corn Starch]] | none |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Vanilla]] | extract |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Almond Extract]] | none |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Dough
1. Preheat your oven to **325°F**.
2. In a large bowl, cream the **1/2 cup butter**, **1/2 cup oleo**, and **1/2 cup powdered sugar** together with an electric mixer until light and fluffy.
3. Beat in the **1 teaspoon vanilla** and **1 teaspoon almond extract**.
4. In a separate bowl, whisk together the **1 1/2 cups flour** and **1/4 cup corn starch**.
5. Gradually add the dry ingredients to the creamed mixture, mixing on low speed until a soft dough forms.

### Step 2: Bake and Cool
6. Drop the dough by small teaspoonfuls onto an ungreased cookie sheet.
7. Bake for **14 minutes** until the cookies are set but not browned.
8. Let the cookies cool on the sheet for a minute before transferring them to a wire rack to cool completely.
9. Once cooled, frost with a simple powdered sugar frosting flavored with a little vanilla and almond extract.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 5 days or freeze unbaked dough balls for up to 3 months.
