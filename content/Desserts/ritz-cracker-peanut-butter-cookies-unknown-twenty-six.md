# Ritz Cracker Peanut Butter Cookies

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Marcile | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *Indulge in sweet and salty perfection with these easy Ritz Cracker Peanut Butter Cookies. Coated in melted white almond bark and chocolate chips, they offer a delightful crunch.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 0 mins | 30 mins | 24 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Microwave-safe bowl
* Baking sheet
* Wax paper

---
## Ingredients

### Cookies and Filling
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 sleeve | [[Ritz Crackers]] | Whole |
| 1/2 cup | [[Peanut Butter]] | Creamy |

### Coating
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 pkg. | [[White Almond Bark]] | Wh. |
| 1/2 large pkg. | [[Chocolate Chips]] | Choc |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Melting
1. Melt the **1 pkg. white almond bark** and **1/2 large pkg. chocolate chips** together in a microwave-safe bowl in 30-second intervals until smooth and completely melted.

### Step 2: Assembling and Dipping
2. Spread the top of each **[[Ritz Crackers]]** with a small amount of **1/2 cup peanut butter**, pressing two crackers together to form a sandwich.
3. Dip each peanut butter cracker sandwich into the above melted **1 pkg. white almond bark** and **1/2 large pkg. chocolate chips** mixture until fully coated.
4. Place on wax paper and let stand until the chocolate is set and firm.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to one week, or refrigerate to extend shelf life.
