# Jane Mason's Lazy-Dazy Cake with Broiled Topping

> 📜 **Collection:** UK15 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Jane Mason | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic hot milk cake topped with a rich, buttery mixture of brown sugar, coconut, and pecans that is broiled until bubbly and caramelized.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 35 mins | None | 1 8-inch or 9-inch square cake |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 8-inch or 9-inch square pan
* Large mixing bowl
* Separate bowl
* Small saucepan
* Toothpick
* Oven with broiler

---
## Ingredients

### For the Cake
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] | Large |
| 1 cup | [[Sugar]] | None |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Vanilla]] | None |
| 1 cup | All-purpose [[Flour]] | None |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Baking Powder]] | None |
| 1/2 teaspoon | [[Salt]] | None |
| 1/2 cup | [[Milk]] | None |
| 1 tablespoon | [[Butter]] or [[Margarine]] | None |

### For the Broiled Topping
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5 tablespoons | Melted [[Butter]] | Melted |
| 3 tablespoons | [[Brown Sugar]] | None |
| 4 teaspoons | [[Cream]] | None |
| 1/2 cup | [[Coconut]] | Shredded |
| 1/2 cup | [[Pecans]] | Chopped |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Make the Cake Batter
1. Preheat the oven to **350°F**. Grease an **8-inch or 9-inch square layer pan**.
2. In a large bowl, beat the **2 eggs**. Gradually add the **1 cup sugar** and continue beating until light and fluffy. Stir in the **1 teaspoon vanilla**.
3. In a separate bowl, sift together the **1 cup all-purpose flour**, **1/2 teaspoon salt**, and **1 teaspoon baking powder**.
4. Add the dry ingredients to the egg mixture and beat well.
5. In a small saucepan, heat the **1/2 cup milk** and the **1 tablespoon butter or margarine** until the butter is melted and the milk is hot (scalded).
6. Pour the hot milk mixture into the batter and beat for about a dozen more strokes until combined.
7. Pour the batter into the prepared pan.
8. Bake for **30 to 35 minutes**, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.

### Step 2: Prepare and Apply the Broiled Topping
9. While the cake is baking, combine all the topping ingredients (**5 tablespoons melted butter**, **3 tablespoons brown sugar**, **4 teaspoons cream**, **1/2 cup shredded coconut**, and **1/2 cup chopped pecans**) in a small bowl.
10. When the cake is finished baking, remove it from the oven and turn on the broiler.
11. Pour the topping mixture evenly over the hot cake.
12. Place the cake under the broiler, watching it carefully, until the topping bubbles and turns slightly brown.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Cut and serve the cake either hot or cold, with ice cream on top if desired. Store sealed in the refrigerator for up to 3 days.
