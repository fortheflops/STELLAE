# Pumpkin Pie Cake with Streusel Crust

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A delicious layered dessert featuring a simple cake mix crust, creamy pumpkin pie filling, and a crunchy cinnamon-sugar streusel topping. This crowd-pleasing treat combines the best flavors of pumpkin pie and coffee cake.*

---

| 15 mins | 50 mins | None | 1 9x13 pan |

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Crust
1. Preheat your oven to **350°F**. Grease a **9x13 inch pan**.
2. Empty the dry **yellow cake mix** into a bowl and reserve **1 cup** of it for the topping.
3. To the remaining cake mix, add **½ cup melted margarine** and **1 egg**. Mix until combined and press the mixture firmly into the bottom of the prepared pan until even.

### Step 2: Prepare the Filling
4. In a separate bowl, combine the **1 (30 oz) can pumpkin pie mix**, **2 eggs**, and **⅔ cup milk**.
5. Whisk until smooth and thoroughly combined.
6. Pour the filling evenly over the crust in the **9x13 inch pan**.

### Step 3: Prepare the Topping
7. In a small bowl, combine the **1 cup reserved yellow cake mix**, **¼ cup sugar**, and **1 teaspoon cinnamon**.
8. Cut in the **¼ cup cold oleo** with a pastry blender or your fingers until the mixture resembles coarse crumbs.
9. Sprinkle this streusel topping evenly over the pumpkin filling.

### Step 4: Bake
10. Bake for **45-50 minutes**, or until a knife inserted near the center comes out clean and the topping is golden and set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store covered in the refrigerator for up to 4 days.
