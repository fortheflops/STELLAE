# Miriam B. Fox's Prune Nut Cupcakes

> 📜 **Collection:** GC | ✍️ **Attribution:** Miriam B. Fox | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *Discover a wonderfully moist and spiced old-fashioned cupcake recipe packed with cooked prunes, raisins, and walnuts. These tender treats are finished with a rich buttermilk batter.*

---

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 25 mins | None | 24-30 small cupcakes |

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |

| 1 ½ Cups | [[Sugar]] | None |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] | Large |
| 2 Cups | [[Flour]] | All-purpose |
| 1 tsp | [[Soda]] | Baking soda |
| 1 ½ tsps | [[Salt]] | None |
| 1 tsp | [[Cinnamon]] | None |
| 1 Cup | [[Buttermilk]] | None |
| 1 Cup | [[Prunes]] | Drained, chopped, cooked |
| ½ Cup | [[Raisins]] | None |
| ½ Cup | [[Walnuts]] | Chopped |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Prune Cook
1. Preheat oven to **350°F**. Line small cupcake pans with paper liners.
2. If starting with dry prunes, cover them with water in a small saucepan, bring to a simmer, and cook for **10-15 minutes** until tender. Drain well and chop.

### Step 2: Mix the Batter
3. In a large bowl, cream together the **½ cup margarine** and **1 ½ cups sugar** until light and fluffy.
4. Beat in the **2 eggs**, one at a time, until fully incorporated.
5. In a separate bowl, sift or whisk together the **2 cups flour**, **1 ½ tsps salt**, **1 tsp cinnamon**, and **1 tsp soda**.
6. Alternately add the dry ingredients and the **1 cup buttermilk** to the creamed mixture, beginning and ending with the dry ingredients. Mix until just combined.
7. Fold in the **1 cup prunes**, **½ cup raisins**, and **½ cup walnuts** until evenly distributed.

### Step 3: Bake and Cool
8. Fill the prepared cupcake liners with batter.
9. Bake for **25 minutes**, or until a toothpick inserted into the center of a cupcake comes out clean and golden brown.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftover cupcakes in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 3 days or freeze for up to 2 months.
