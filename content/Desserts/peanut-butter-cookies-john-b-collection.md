# Peanut Butter Cookies

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic slice-and-bake recipe for rich and crumbly peanut butter cookies. These golden treats are perfect for holiday platters or everyday snacking.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 8 mins | 15 mins | 50 cookies |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Wax paper
* Cookie sheet

| 1/4 tsp. | [[Salt]] | None |

| 1/2 cup | [[Peanut butter]] | None |

| 1 cup | [[Granulated sugar]] | Divided |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] | Unbeaten |
| 1 tsp. | [[Vanilla]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Dough Preparation
1. Sift together **2 cups flour**, **2 tsp. baking powder**, and **1/4 tsp. salt** onto waxed paper.
2. Beat **1/2 cup shortening** in bowl until fluffy, add **1/2 cup peanut butter** & cream together until smoothly blended.
3. Add **1/2 cup brown sugar** and **1/2 cup granulated sugar**, gradually beating after each addition.
4. Add **1 tsp. vanilla** and **2 eggs**. Beat thoroughly.
5. Add flour mixture gradually, beating until dough is stiff.

### Step 2: Chilling and Slicing
6. Divide dough in half & roll in wax paper.
7. Place in refrigerator to chill until firm enough to slice, about **15 minutes**.

### Step 3: Baking
8. Preheat oven to **400°F** & cut rolls into slices, about **1/4 inch** thick, & place on ungreased cookie sheet.
9. Allow **1 inch** between cookies for spreading.
10. Bake at **400°F** for **7-8 minutes** until lightly golden.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Dough can be rolled, wrapped in wax paper, and refrigerated or frozen until ready to slice and bake.
