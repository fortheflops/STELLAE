# German Chocolate Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** UK15 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic layered German Chocolate Cake featuring a tender buttermilk-chocolate crumb and a rich, cooked coconut-pecan filling. This vintage masterpiece is a guaranteed crowd-pleaser for any special occasion.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Cake Batter
1. Preheat your oven to **350°F**. Grease and flour three 8-inch round cake pans.
2. Melt the **1 (4 oz) package German's Sweet Chocolate** in the **1 cup boiling water**. Set aside to cool completely.
3. In a large bowl, cream the **1 cup shortening** and **2 cups sugar** together until light and fluffy. Add the **4 eggs** (yolks) one at a time, beating well after each.
4. Stir in the cooled chocolate mixture and **1 teaspoon vanilla**.
5. In a separate bowl, whisk together the **2 1/2 cups cake flour** and **1 teaspoon baking soda**.
6. Add the flour mixture to the creamed mixture alternately with the **1 cup buttermilk**, beginning and ending with the flour.
7. In a clean, dry bowl, beat the remaining **4 eggs** (whites) until stiff peaks form.
8. Gently fold the beaten egg whites into the cake batter until just incorporated.
9. Pour the batter evenly into the prepared pans and bake for **30-35 minutes**, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean. Let cool completely.

### Step 2: Coconut-Pecan Filling and Assembly
10. In a medium saucepan, combine the **1 cup evaporated milk** (or cream), **1 cup sugar**, **3 egg yolks**, and **½ cup butter**.
11. Cook over medium heat, stirring constantly, until the mixture thickens, about **10-12 minutes**. Do not let it boil.
12. Remove from the heat and stir in the **1 cup coconut** and **1/2 cup pecans** until well combined.
13. Let the filling cool until it is thick enough to spread.
14. Spread the filling between the cooled cake layers and over the top of the cake, leaving the sides traditionally unfrosted until set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the frosted cake covered in the refrigerator for up to 3 days; bring to room temperature before serving for the best texture.
