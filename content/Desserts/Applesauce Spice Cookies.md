# Applesauce Spice Cookies

> 📜 **Collection:** UK15 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic recipe for soft, cake-like applesauce cookies. These old-fashioned drop cookies are packed with warm spices, raisins, and nuts for a hearty and delicious treat.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and Mixing
1. Preheat your oven to 375°F and grease your cookie sheets.
2. In a large bowl, thoroughly cream together the **3/4 cup shortening**, **1 cup brown sugar**, and **1 egg** with an electric mixer.
3. Stir in the **1 1/2 cups applesauce**.

### Step 2: Combining and Baking
4. In a separate bowl, sift together the **2 1/4 cups flour**, **1/2 tsp baking soda**, **1/2 tsp salt**, **3/4 tsp cinnamon**, and **1/4 tsp cloves**.
5. Add the dry ingredients to the wet mixture and stir until just combined.
6. By hand, fold in the **1 cup raisins** and **1/2 cup nuts**.
7. Drop the dough by rounded teaspoonfuls onto the prepared cookie sheets.
8. Bake for **10 to 12 minutes**, or until the cookies are set and lightly browned around the edges.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 5 days or freeze for up to 3 months.
