# Cola Congealed Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** General Archive | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A classic, vintage-style congealed salad featuring cherries, pineapple, and pecans in a creamy, fizzy base of cherry and strawberry gelatin and cola. This retro dessert is a perfect, crowd-pleasing addition to any potluck table.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 5 mins | 4-6 hours | 12-15 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* saucepan
* large mixing bowl
* wire whisk or electric mixer
* 13x9x2 inch pan

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 large can (20 oz) | [[Bing Cherries]] | drained, juice reserved |
| 1 large can (20 oz) | [[Crushed Pineapple]] | drained, juice reserved |
| 1 package (3 oz) | [[Cherry gelatin]] | powdered |
| 1 package (3 oz) | [[Strawberry gelatin]] | powdered |
| 1 package (8 oz) | [[Cream Cheese]] | softened |
| 1 Cup | [[Pecans]] | chopped |
| 14-16 oz | [[Cola Drink]] | cold |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Base
1. Drain the **[[Bing Cherries]]** and **[[Crushed Pineapple]]**, reserving the liquid. Add water to the reserved liquid to reach a total of **2 cups**.
2. In a saucepan, bring the **2 cups liquid** to a rolling boil.

### Step 2: Dissolve and Emulsify
3. In a large bowl, combine the **1 package cherry gelatin** and **1 package strawberry gelatin**. Pour the boiling **2 cups liquid** over the powders and stir for **2 minutes** until completely dissolved.
4. Add the **8 oz cream cheese** to the hot mixture and beat with a whisk or electric mixer until the mixture is smooth and fully incorporated.

### Step 3: Combine and Chill
5. Allow the mixture to cool slightly, then slowly stir in the **14-16 oz cola drink**; the mixture will fizz.
6. Gently fold in the drained **[[Bing Cherries]]**, **[[Crushed Pineapple]]**, and **1 cup pecans**.
7. Pour into a **13x9x2 inch pan** and chill in the refrigerator for **4-6 hours** until completely firm and set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This salad must be made at least 4-6 hours in advance to set properly; it can be stored covered in the refrigerator for up to 3 days.
