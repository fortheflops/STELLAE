# Marble Cookies

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *Delightful vintage marble cookies featuring swirled layers of vanilla and chocolate dough. Perfect for slicing and baking into crisp, eye-catching treats.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 25 mins | 12 mins | 1 hour | 24 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowls
* Baking sheet
* Rolling pin
* Knife

---
## Ingredients

### Dough Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/3 C. | [[Shortening]] | Softened |
| 1/3 C. | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 1 tsp. | [[Vanilla]] | Extract |
| 1 1/4 C. | [[Flour]] | All-purpose |
| 1 sq. | [[Chocolate]] | Melted |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing the Dough
1. Cream the **1/3 C. shortening** and **1/3 C. sugar** until smooth and fluffy.
2. Add **1 tsp. vanilla** and **1 1/4 C. flour**, mixing thoroughly to form a cohesive dough.
3. Divide the dough in half and add the **1 sq. melted chocolate** to one half, mixing well until thoroughly combined.

### Step 2: Shaping and Baking
4. Roll out the white dough, top with the chocolate dough, and roll like a jelly roll.
5. Fold in half and press to marble the colors together, then shape into 2 rolls.
6. Wrap in parchment or plastic wrap and chill in the refrigerator until firm.
7. Slice thin and bake for **12 minutes** at **350°F** until set and lightly golden.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The shaped dough rolls can be wrapped and stored in the refrigerator for up to 3 days or frozen for up to a month before slicing and baking.
