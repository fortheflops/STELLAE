# Marble Cookies

> **Collection:** John B. Collection | **Original Attribution:** Unattributed
> *Delicious homemade marble cookies with a swirling combination of vanilla and chocolate dough. Perfect for a vintage-style sweet treat.*

![Original Handwritten Card](/assets/scans/marble-cookies.webp)

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 12 mins | 24 |

## Ingredients
- [ ] 1/3 C. shortening
- [ ] 1/3 C. sugar
- [ ] 1 tsp. vanilla
- [ ] 1 1/4 C. flour
- [ ] 1 sq. chocolate

## Instructions
1. Cream 1/3 C. shortening and 1/3 C. sugar.
2. Add 1 tsp. vanilla and 1 1/4 C. flour.
3. Divide in half.
4. Melt 1 sq. chocolate and add to 1/2 of the dough, mixing well.
5. Roll out the white dough, top with the chocolate dough, and roll like a jelly roll.
6. Fold in half and press to marble.
7. Shape into 2 rolls, wrap, chill, slice thin, and bake 12 minutes at 350°.
