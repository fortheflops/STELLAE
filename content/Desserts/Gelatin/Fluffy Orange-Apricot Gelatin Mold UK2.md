*An elegant and light gelatin mold with a mousse-like texture from whipped egg whites and a bright orange-apricot flavor.*

## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 4½ Cups | [[Orange Juice]], divided |
| ⅓ Cup | [[Sugar]] |
| 3 envelopes | [[Unflavored Gelatin]] |
| ¼ Cup | [[Apricot Nectar]] |
| 2 | [[Egg Whites]] |
| 1 Pint | Mixed [[Pears]], [[Peaches]], [[Apricots]], [[Grapes]] or [[Berries]] |

___
## Instructions
1.  In a saucepan, heat **1 cup of orange juice** with the **sugar** and **gelatin**. Stir constantly over low heat until the gelatin and sugar are fully dissolved.
2.  Remove from heat and stir in the remaining **3½ cups of orange juice** and the **apricot nectar**.
3.  Chill the mixture in the refrigerator until it is syrupy and partially set.
4.  In a clean bowl, beat the **egg whites** until soft peaks form.
5.  Gently fold the whipped egg whites into the chilled gelatin mixture.
6.  Fold in the mixed **fruits**.
7.  Pour into a 1½ quart mold and refrigerate until firm.
___

>[!Warning]
>This recipe contains raw egg whites. For those with health concerns, consider using pasteurized egg whites.
