## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 (6 ounce) package        | [[Lime Jello]]                            |
| 1 (8 ounce) package        | [[Philadelphia Cream Cheese]], softened   |
| 1 large can                | [[Pears]], halved (juice reserved)        |
| 1 jar                      | [[Maraschino Cherries]]                   |
| 1 teaspoon                 | [[Oil]]                                   |
___
## Instructions
1.  Lightly oil the inside of your jello mold with the 1 teaspoon of oil.
2.  Following the package directions, dissolve the lime jello powder in boiling water, but replace the required amount of cold water with the reserved juice from the canned pears.
3.  Pour about one-third of the jello mixture into the prepared mold.
4.  Refrigerate until it begins to thicken but is not fully set.
5.  Arrange the pear halves and maraschino cherries in the thickened jello layer.
6.  Allow the remaining jello (not in the mold) to thicken.
7.  In a separate bowl, beat the softened cream cheese until smooth.
8.  Gradually add the remaining thickened jello to the cream cheese, beating until the mixture is completely smooth.
9.  Gently pour the cream cheese and jello mixture over the fruit layer in the mold.
10. Refrigerate until the entire mold is firm and fully set.
___

>[!Note]
>Cooking time about 15 minutes
