*A classic and simple recipe for firm, sliceable gelatin squares (Knox Blocks), also known as "Jigglers," a fun treat for kids and adults alike.*
### Author
Deb Hansen
___
## Recipe
| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 2 envelopes | [[Knox Unflavored Gelatine]] |
| 1 (3-ounce) package| Fruit flavored [[Gelatin]] (e.g., Jell-O) |
| 1 1/2 cups | Boiling [[Water]] |
___
## Instructions
1.  In a medium bowl, mix the **unflavored gelatine** powder with the **flavored gelatine** powder.
2.  Pour in the **boiling water** and stir until the gelatine is completely dissolved.
3.  Pour the mixture into an 8x8 inch dish.
4.  Allow it to cool, then refrigerate for several hours, or until very firm.
5.  Once set, cut into small blocks to serve.
