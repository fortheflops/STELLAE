## Author
Deb Hansen
___
## Recipe
| Measurements      | Ingredients                   |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 2 envelopes       | [[Knox Unflavored Gelatine]]      |
| 1 (3-ounce) package | Fruit flavored [[Gelatin]] (e.g., Jell-O) |
| 1 1/2 cups        | Boiling [[Water]]                 |
___
## Instructions
1.  In a medium bowl, mix the unflavored gelatine powder with the flavored gelatine powder.
2.  Pour in the boiling water and stir until the gelatine is completely dissolved.
3.  Pour the mixture into a dish or pan (an 8x8 inch pan works well).
4.  Allow it to cool to room temperature, then transfer to the refrigerator.
5.  Chill for several hours, or until very firm.
6.  Once set, cut into small blocks to serve.
