## Recipe
| Measurements      | Ingredients                   |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 package         | [[Jello]], any flavor             |
| 3/4 cup           | Boiled [[Water]] or [[Fruit Juice]] |
| 1 can             | [[Evaporated Milk]], chilled      |
| 3/4 cup           | [[Sugar]]                       |
| 1 can             | [[Crushed Pineapple]], drained    |
| Optional          | [[Graham Cracker Crust]]        |
| Optional          | Additional [[Fruit]]              |
___
## Instructions
1.  In a bowl, dissolve the Jello powder in the boiled liquid. Let it cool until it begins to thicken but is not fully set (the consistency of unbeaten egg whites).
2.  In a separate, chilled mixing bowl, beat the cold evaporated milk with an electric mixer until it becomes thick and fluffy.
3.  Gradually add the sugar while continuing to beat.
4.  Beat the cooled Jello mixture into the whipped milk mixture until everything is well blended.
5.  Fold in the drained crushed pineapple and any other fruit you wish to add.
6.  Pour the mixture into a serving bowl, mold, or a prepared graham cracker crust.
7.  If using a crust, you can sprinkle additional graham cracker crumbs on top.
8.  Refrigerate for several hours until firm.
___

>[!Note]
>The original recipe specifies "Milnot," a brand of evaporated milk. The author notes this is a "very versatile" dessert. For a layered version, you can prepare two batches using different colors of Jello and layer them in a springform pan, with or without a crust.
