# Old-Fashioned Tea Cakes

> 📜 **Collection:** General Archive | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A very, very old recipe for traditional tea cakes—a crisp, simple, and delicious cookie that isn't overly rich. Dropped by the spoonful and baked until the edges are golden.*

| 1 Cup | [[Sugar]] | None |

| 2 | [[Eggs]] | Lightly beaten |
| 1 tsp. | [[Vanilla Extract]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare Dough
1. Preheat your oven to **425°F (220°C)** and grease your baking sheets.
2. In a large bowl, sift together the **3 Cups flour**, **2 tsp. cream of tartar**, **1 tsp. baking soda**, **1/2 tsp. salt**, and **1 Cup sugar**.
3. Cut the **1 Cup shortening** into the dry ingredients using a pastry blender or your fingertips until the mixture resembles coarse crumbs.
4. In a small bowl, lightly beat the **2 eggs** and **1 tsp. vanilla extract**. Add this wet mixture to the flour mixture and mix well until a dough forms.

### Step 2: Bake and Cool
5. Drop the dough by rounded teaspoonfuls onto the greased baking sheets, leaving enough space between them for the cookies to spread.
6. Bake for **8-10 minutes**, or until the edges are golden brown and fragrant.
7. Let the cookies cool on the baking sheet for a minute before transferring them to a wire rack to cool completely.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store baked tea cakes in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 5 days.
