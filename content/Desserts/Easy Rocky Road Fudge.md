# Easy Rocky Road Fudge

> 📜 **Collection:** General Archive | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Desserts
> *A quick and foolproof no-bake recipe for classic Rocky Road fudge. This decadent treat combines melted chocolate, sweetened condensed milk, fluffy marshmallows, and crunchy nuts.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 5 mins | 4 hours | 24 squares |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 9x13 inch pan
* double boiler
* parchment paper
* spatula

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 pkgs (12 oz each) | [[Chocolate Chips]] | semi-sweet |
| 1 can (14 oz) | [[Milk]] | sweetened condensed |
| 1 tsp | [[Vanilla]] | extract |
| 1 pkg (10 oz) | [[Marshmallows]] | mini or large, quartered |
| 1 cup | [[Nuts]] | chopped |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare and Melt
1. Line a 9x13 inch pan with parchment paper, leaving an overhang.
2. In a double boiler over simmering water, melt the **2 pkgs (12 oz each) [[Chocolate Chips]]**, stirring until smooth and glossy.

### Step 2: Combine and Set
3. Remove from heat and stir in **1 can (14 oz) [[Milk]]** and **1 tsp [[Vanilla]]** until well combined.
4. Allow the mixture to cool slightly, then fold in **1 pkg (10 oz) [[Marshmallows]]** and **1 cup [[Nuts]]** until evenly distributed.
5. Spread the mixture into the prepared pan and refrigerate for at least 4 hours until firm to the touch.
6. Lift the fudge using the paper liner and cut into squares.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 2 weeks or freeze for up to 3 months.
