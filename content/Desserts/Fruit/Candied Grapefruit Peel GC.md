## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| Two                              | Medium White [[Grapefruit]]               |
| One & $\frac{1}{2}$ Cups         | Granulated [[Sugar]]                      |
| $\frac{3}{4}$ Cup                | [[Water]]                                 |
| $\frac{1}{2}$ Teaspoon           | [[Salt]]                                  |
| One (3 oz) Package               | Lime-Flavored [[Gelatin]]                 |
| For Coating                      | Green [[Sugar Crystals]] (Optional)       |
___
## Instructions
1.  Score grapefruit peel in four lengthwise sections with the point of a knife.
2.  Loosen the peel from the pulp with the bowl of a spoon.
3.  Remove most of the white membrane from the peel.
4.  Cut the peel into $\frac{1}{4}$-inch strips.
5.  Place two cups of the cut peel in a large pan. Cover generously with cold water.
6.  Boil for 20 minutes; drain.
7.  Repeat the boiling and draining process twice more with fresh water each time (for a total of three 20-minute boils). Drain thoroughly after the final boil.
8.  Combine one and a half cups of granulated sugar, $\frac{3}{4}$ cup of water, and $\frac{1}{2}$ teaspoon of salt in a medium saucepan.
9.  Cook and stir until the sugar dissolves.
10. Add the drained peel to the sugar syrup; bring to a boiling.
11. Simmer for 20 minutes, or until the peel is translucent.
12. Gradually add the one (3 oz) package of lime-flavored gelatin, stirring over low heat until the gelatin is dissolved.
13. Remove the peel from the syrup; drain on a rack. Let stand for 20 minutes.
14. Roll each piece of peel in green sugar crystals (if using).
15. Let the peel dry thoroughly on a rack.
16. Store in a covered jar.
