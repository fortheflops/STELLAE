## Author
"The Complete Book of Home Preserving" by Ann Seranne, Doubleday & Co., New York, 1995
___
## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| Per pound of fruit:        |                                           |
| 1 pound                    | [[Pears]]                                 |
| 1 pound                    | [[Sugar]]                                 |
| 1 cup                      | [[Water]]                                 |
| Per pint of syrup:         |                                           |
| 1 pint                     | [[Brandy]]                                |
___
## Instructions
1.  Wash and peel the pears. You can leave them whole, halve them, or cut them up as desired.
2.  Weigh the washed fruit to determine how much sugar and water you will need (1 pound of sugar and 1 cup of water per pound of fruit).
3.  In a large pot, combine the required amount of sugar and water and bring it to a boil, stirring to dissolve the sugar.
4.  Add a few pears at a time to the boiling syrup and cook them rapidly for 5 minutes.
5.  Continue to cook for another 5 to 10 minutes, or until the pears can be pierced easily with a straw.
6.  Remove the cooked pears and pack them into hot, sterilized jars.
7.  Continue this process until all the pears have been cooked and packed.
8.  Continue to boil the remaining syrup until it is thick, or until it registers 222°F on a candy thermometer.
9.  Let the syrup cool completely.
10. Once cool, add 1 pint of brandy for every pint of syrup. Stir well to combine.
11. Fill the jars containing the pears to overflowing with the brandy syrup.
12. Seal the jars at once.
