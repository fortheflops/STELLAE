## Instructions

### Assembly and Chilling
1. In a large mixing bowl, combine the **1 tbsp vinegar**, **1 clove minced garlic**, **2 cups cooked rice**, **1 package thawed peas**, **1/2 cup shredded carrots**, and **1/4 cup chopped green onion**.
2. Stir in the **1 cup mayonnaise** until the salad is evenly coated, creamy, and well combined.
3. Cover the bowl tightly and chill in the refrigerator for at least **1 hour** before serving to allow the flavors to meld beautifully.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This salad can be made up to 24 hours in advance. Keep covered and refrigerated until ready to serve; stir gently before presenting.
