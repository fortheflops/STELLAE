# Zesty Bell Pepper Slaw

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Eight | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A vibrant and crisp zesty bell pepper slaw combining crunchy cabbage, colorful bell peppers, and fresh basil tossed in a tangy yogurt-mayonnaise dressing. Perfect as a refreshing side dish for summer barbecues or light weeknight meals.*

---

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | None | 20 mins | 2 servings |

* Mixing bowl

* Cutting board

## Ingredients

### Slaw Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/4 | [[Cabbage]] | Medium head, coarsely chopped |
| 1/2 | [[Red Bell Pepper]] | Small, coarsely chopped |
| 1/2 | [[Yellow Bell Pepper]] | Small, coarsely chopped |
| 2 | [[Scallions]] | Coarsely chopped |
| 2 tablespoons | [[Fresh Basil Leaves]] | Shredded |

### Dressing and Seasoning
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3 tablespoons | [[Mayonnaise]] | None |
| 1 tablespoon | [[Plain Yogurt]] | None |
| 1 tablespoon | [[Cider Vinegar]] | None |
| A pinch | [[Cayenne]] | Or to taste |
| To taste | [[Salt]] | None |
| To taste | [[Pepper]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep Vegetables and Dressing
1. Coarsely chop the **1/4 medium head cabbage**, **1/2 small red bell pepper**, **1/2 small yellow bell pepper**, and **2 scallions**.
2. In a bowl, whisk together the **3 tablespoons mayonnaise**, **1 tablespoon plain yogurt**, **1 tablespoon cider vinegar**, and **a pinch of cayenne** until smooth.

### Step 2: Toss and Chill
3. Add the chopped vegetables, **2 tablespoons shredded fresh basil leaves**, **salt**, and **pepper** to the bowl with the dressing and toss well until evenly coated.
4. Cover and chill the slaw for **20 minutes** to let the flavors meld until crisp and refreshing.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the dressed slaw in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 4 hours before serving for the best texture.
