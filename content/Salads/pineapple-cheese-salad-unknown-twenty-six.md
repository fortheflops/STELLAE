# Pineapple Cheese Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A delightful layered molded salad featuring crushed pineapple, sharp cheddar cheese, and ripe bananas. Topped with a rich, creamy cooked custard and whipped cream.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 30 mins | 10 mins | 4 hours | 10-12 servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 13x9x2 pan
* Saucepan
* Mixing bowl

## Ingredients

### Salad Base
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 cups | [[Boiling Water]] | Hot |
| 2-3 oz | [[Lemon Jello]] | Dry |
| 2 cups | [[Cold Water]] | Cold |
| 1-20 oz | [[Crushed Pineapple]] | Drained (reserve 1 cup syrup) |
| 2 cups | [[Sharp Cheddar Cheese]] | Shredded |
| 6 | [[Ripe Bananas]] | Sliced |
| as needed | [[Salad Greens]] | For serving |

### Creamy Topping
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 cup | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 2 Tbsp | [[Flour]] | All-purpose |
| 1 cup | [[Pineapple Syrup]] | Drained from crushed pineapple |
| 1 tsp | [[Lemon Juice]] | Fresh |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] | Slightly beaten |
| 2 Tbsp | [[Butter]] | Softened |
| 1 cup | [[Whipping Cream]] | Whipped |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Salad Preparation
1. Dissolve the **2-3 oz lemon jello** in **2 cups boiling water**.
2. Stir in **2 cups cold water** and chill until partially set.
3. Fold in **1-20 oz crushed pineapple**, **2 cups shredded sharp cheddar cheese**, and **6 ripe bananas**.
4. Pour mixture into a **13x9x2 pan** and chill until completely firm.

### Step 2: Creamy Topping
5. Combine **1/2 cup sugar** and **2 Tbsp flour** in a saucepan.
6. Stir in **1 cup pineapple syrup**, **1 tsp lemon juice**, and **2 eggs**.
7. Cook over medium heat, stirring constantly, until thickened to a custard consistency.
8. Remove from heat and stir in **2 Tbsp butter**, then chill thoroughly.
9. Once cold, fold in **1 cup whipping cream** (whipped) and serve over the set salad on a bed of **salad greens**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The salad can be prepared up to a day in advance and kept refrigerated until ready to top and serve.
