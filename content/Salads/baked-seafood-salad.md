# Baked Seafood Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A delightful and warm Baked Seafood Salad loaded with shrimp, crabmeat, and vegetables. Perfect for gatherings and easily prepared for a crowd.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Assembly and Baking
1. Mix together **2 green peppers**, **2 onions**, **2 cups celery**, **12 oz shrimp**, **1/4 tsp pepper**, **2 cups mayonnaise**, **13 oz crabmeat**, **1 tsp salt**, and **2 tsp Worcestershire sauce** in a large mixing bowl until well combined.
2. Transfer the mixture to a baking dish and top evenly with **2 cups buttered crumbs**.
3. Bake at 350°F for **30 minutes** until bubbly and golden brown on top.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the salad in the baking dish without the crumb topping, cover, and refrigerate up to 4 hours before adding crumbs and baking.
