# Mike's Tomatoes, Onions, and Marinade

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Mike | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A refreshing summer salad featuring ripe slicing tomatoes and sweet Vidalia onions drizzled with a tangy balsamic-cider marinade. Topped with savory blue cheese crumbles, this simple dish delivers a steakhouse-quality flavor profile reminiscent of Del Frisco's.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | None | 15 mins | 4 servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Knife
* Cutting board
* Small bowl
* Whisk
* Serving platter

## Ingredients

### Salad Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2-3 large | [[Tomatoes]] | ripe, slicing type, sliced |
| 1 | [[Sweet Onion]] | Vidalia or Texas 1015, sliced |
| To taste | [[Blue Cheese]] | crumbled |

### Marinade
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 tbsp | [[Balsamic Vinegar]] | preferably high-quality aged balsamic |
| 1 tbsp | [[Cider Vinegar]] | such as Bragg's |
| 1 tsp | [[Sugar]] |  |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Salt]] |  |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Black Pepper]] |  |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Marinade
1. In a small bowl, whisk together the **2 tbsp balsamic vinegar**, **1 tbsp cider vinegar**, **1 tsp sugar**, **1/4 tsp salt**, and **1/4 tsp black pepper** until the sugar and salt are fully dissolved.

### Step 2: Assemble and Chill
2. Arrange **4-5 slices of tomatoes** and **1-2 slices of sweet onion** on a serving saucer or platter.
3. Drizzle the prepared marinade evenly over the tomato and onion slices.
4. Sprinkle **blue cheese crumbles** to taste over the marinated tomatoes and onions.
5. Refrigerate the assembled salad for **at least 15 minutes** or until thoroughly chilled and cold before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Can be prepared up to 2 hours in advance and kept chilled in the refrigerator. Add the blue cheese crumbles just before serving to maintain their texture.
