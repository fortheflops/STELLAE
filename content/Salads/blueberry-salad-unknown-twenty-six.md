# Blueberry Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A delightful layered jello salad featuring black raspberry and wild raspberry flavors combined with crushed pineapple, blueberries, and a creamy topping. This refreshing dish is a classic crowd-pleaser for potlucks and family gatherings.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 0 mins | 4 hours | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Measuring cup
* Salad mold or baking dish

---
## Ingredients

### Salad Base
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 pkgs | [[Black Raspberry Jell-O]] | Dry gelatin mix |
| 1 pkg | [[Wild Raspberry Jell-O]] | Dry gelatin mix |
| 2 cups | [[Hot Water]] | Steaming hot |
| 1 can | [[Crushed Pineapple]] | Juice reserved, 13 oz, drained |
| 1 can | [[Blueberries]] | Drained |
| As needed | [[Cold Water]] | Combined with fruit juices to make 4 cups |

### Topping
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 9 oz | [[Cool Whip]] | Thawed |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing the Jell-O
1. Dissolve the **2 pkgs black raspberry Jell-O** and **1 pkg wild raspberry Jell-O** in **2 cups hot water**.
2. Combine the juice from the **1 can crushed pineapple** and juice from the **1 can blueberries**, then add enough **cold water** to make **4 cups** of liquid total (6 total cups of mixture).
3. Reserve **2 cups** of the liquid mixture to use later.

### Step 2: Adding Fruit and Setting
4. To the remaining mixture, add the drained **1 can crushed pineapple** and **1 can blueberries**.
5. Let the **2 cups** of reserved jello mixture begin to congeal separately until slightly thickened.

### Step 3: Topping and Serving
6. Spread the **9 oz cool whip** over the top of the congealed salad as a frosting.
7. Chill until firmly set, slice into squares, and serve cold once fully set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Prepare up to 2 days in advance and keep refrigerated until ready to serve.
