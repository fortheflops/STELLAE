# Baked Chicken Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A delightful baked chicken salad featuring crunchy celery and walnuts, topped with potato puffs and fruit garnishes. Baked until lightly browned for a comforting and satisfying meal.*

---

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Assembly and Baking
1. Combine **10 oz canned chicken**, **1/2 cup celery**, **1/4 cup walnuts**, **1/4 tsp salt**, **black pepper**, **1 tbsp lemon juice**, and **1/3 - 1/2 cup mayonnaise**. Toss lightly to mix.
2. Heap the mixture into individual casseroles.
3. Top with **1/2 cup crushed potato puffs** and garnish with **canned cranberry sauce** and **peach slices**.
4. Bake until lightly browned and bubbling.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the individual casseroles up to a few hours in advance, cover, and refrigerate before topping with potato puffs and baking.
