# Mike's Balsamic Tomatoes Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Mike | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A vibrant and tangy salad featuring ripe tomatoes and sweet onions dressed in aged balsamic and cider vinegar, topped with blue cheese crumbles. Perfect as a refreshing side dish for any summer meal.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | None | None | 4 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Small bowl
* Whisk
* Serving platter

---
## Ingredients

### Dressing and Toppings
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 tbsp | [[Balsamic vinegar]] | 18 year aged |
| 1 tbsp | [[Cider vinegar]] | Bragg's is best |
| 1 tsp | [[White sugar]] | None |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Salt]] | None |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Black pepper]] | None |
| As needed | [[Tomatoes]] | Ripe, sliced or chopped |
| As needed | [[Sweet onions]] | Sliced |
| As needed | [[Blue cheese]] | Crumbles |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Dressing and Assembly
1. In a small bowl, whisk together the **2 tbsp balsamic vinegar**, **1 tbsp cider vinegar**, **1 tsp white sugar**, **1/4 tsp salt**, and **1/4 tsp black pepper** until the sugar dissolves.
2. Arrange the ripe **tomatoes** and **sweet onions** on a serving platter.
3. Sprinkle the dressing evenly over the **tomatoes** and **sweet onions**.
4. Top everything by sprinkling **blue cheese crumbles** over all until evenly distributed.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Best served immediately, but the dressing can be mixed ahead of time and stored in the refrigerator for up to 3 days.
