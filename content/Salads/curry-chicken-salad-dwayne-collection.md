# Curry Chicken Salad

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare and Chill Base
1. In a mixing bowl, combine the **1 1/2 cups cooked rice**, **1/2 tsp curry powder**, **1/4 cup minced onion**, **2 tsp vinegar**, and **9 tbsp mayonnaise**.
2. Mix the ingredients thoroughly until well combined and the rice is evenly coated, then cover and chill in the refrigerator until thoroughly cold.

### Step 2: Assemble and Serve
3. Just before serving, add the **2 cups chilled, chopped chicken**, **1 cup chopped celery**, **1/4 cup chopped green pepper**, **1/4 tsp salt**, **1 dash black pepper**, and **3/4 cup mayonnaise** to the chilled rice mixture.
4. Toss gently until everything is evenly coated, creamy, and well integrated. Serve immediately while cold and crisp.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The rice base can be prepared and chilled several hours or even a day in advance. Add the fresh vegetables, chicken, and final mayonnaise dressing just before serving to keep the celery and pepper crisp.
