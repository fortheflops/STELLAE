# Eastwood Spinach Salad

> *A vibrant Eastwood Spinach Salad tossed with mandarin oranges, artichoke hearts, Swiss cheese, and a homemade red wine vinaigrette. Perfect for gathering tables and potlucks.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Assembly
1. Tear **1 lb fresh spinach** and **1 head lettuce** into bite-size pieces.
2. Layer the **1 lb fresh spinach**, **1 head lettuce**, **16 oz mandarin oranges**, **1 (8.5 oz) artichoke hearts**, **1 (8 oz) ripe olives**, **1/4 lb. Swiss cheese**, **1 c. cashews**, and **green onions** in a large salad bowl.

### Step 2: Dressing and Serving
3. Combine **1/3 c. sugar**, **1/3 c. red wine vinegar**, **1 tsp dry mustard**, **1 c. oil**, **1 tsp salt**, **pepper**, **1 T. celery seed**, and **1 T. grated onion** in a blender; blend until smooth.
4. Pour dressing over salad just before serving and toss gently until well coated.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store dressing separately in the refrigerator for up to 3 days. Assemble the salad ingredients ahead of time, cover, and chill until ready to toss and serve.
