# Marinated Corn and Green Pepper Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Linda Brown | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A crisp, refreshing marinated vegetable salad featuring shoepeg corn, green beans, and peas tossed in a tangy sweet-and-sour dressing. This vibrant make-ahead dish absorbs flavors overnight for a delicious, crowd-pleasing side.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 5 mins | 8 hours | 2 quarts |

* Large bowl
* Small saucepan
* Whisk
* Colander

## Ingredients

### Salad
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 can | [[Shoepeg Corn]] | drained |
| 1 can | [[Cut Green Beans]] | drained |
| 1 small can | [[English Peas]] | drained |
| 4 stalks | [[Celery]] | chopped |
| 1 medium | [[Purple Onion]] | chopped, use less if desired |
| 1 medium | [[Green Pepper]] | chopped |
| 1 large jar | [[Diced Pimentos]] | drained |

### Marinade
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3/4 cup | [[Sugar]] | None |
| 1/2 cup | [[Vinegar]] | None |
| 1/4 cup | [[Oil]] | olive oil recommended |
| 1 Tbsp | [[Water]] | None |
| To taste | [[Salt]] | None |
| To taste | [[Pepper]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep the Vegetables
1. Thoroughly drain the **1 can shoepeg corn**, **1 can cut green beans**, **1 small can English peas**, and **1 large jar diced pimentos**.
2. Chop the **4 stalks celery**, **1 medium purple onion**, and **1 medium green pepper**.
3. In a large bowl, combine all of the prepared, drained vegetables and chopped aromatics until evenly distributed.

### Step 2: Make the Marinade
4. In a small saucepan, combine the **3/4 cup sugar**, **1/2 cup vinegar**, **1/4 cup oil**, **1 Tbsp water**, **salt**, and **pepper**.
5. Place the saucepan over low heat and stir constantly just until the **sugar** is completely dissolved, ensuring the mixture does not come to a boil.

### Step 3: Marinate and Serve
6. Carefully pour the warm marinade over the **vegetable mixture** in the large bowl.
7. Toss gently but thoroughly until all ingredients are evenly coated.
8. Cover the bowl and refrigerate for at least several hours, or preferably overnight, until the flavors are fully melded and the vegetables are tender-crisp. Serve chilled.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This salad can be kept sealed in the refrigerator for an extended period, tasting even better when allowed to meld overnight.
