# Tabouli from Co-op Mix

## Instructions

### Assembly and Chilling
1. Place **1 cup tabouli mix** in a mixing bowl.
2. Add **1/4 cup olive oil**, **1 cup cold water**, and **1 finely chopped tomato** to the bowl and stir well to combine.
3. Refrigerate for **1/2 to 1 hour** to allow the grains to fully absorb the water, soften, and let the flavors blend together beautifully.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This salad is excellent for making ahead as the flavors continue to develop. Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days.
