# Marcile's Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Marcile | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A crisp and refreshing salad featuring romaine lettuce, pecans, and mandarin oranges tossed with a sweet red wine vinegar dressing. This delightful recipe by Marcile makes a wonderful accompaniment to any meal.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | None | None | 4-6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Salad bowl
* Measuring cups
* Whisk

---
## Ingredients

### Salad Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 head | [[Romaine lettuce]] | Torn or chopped |
| 1/2 cup | [[Pecans]] | Chopped |
| 1 can | [[Mandarin oranges]] | Drained |
| 1/2 bunch | [[Green onions]] | Tops and all, chopped |

### Dressing Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/4 cup | [[Red wine vinegar]] | None |
| 1/4 cup | [[Sugar]] | None |
| 1/4 cup | [[Oil]] | Vegetable or salad oil |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparing the Salad
1. In a large salad bowl, combine the **Romaine lettuce**, **pecans**, **mandarin oranges**, and chopped **green onions**.

### Step 2: Mixing the Dressing and Serving
2. In a small bowl, whisk together the **1/4 cup wine (red) vinegar**, **1/4 cup sugar**, and **1/4 cup oil** until the sugar is fully dissolved and the mixture is well blended.
3. Pour the dressing over the salad just before serving and toss gently until all ingredients are evenly coated.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Prepare the salad ingredients and keep them chilled in the refrigerator, but do not add the dressing until right before serving to maintain crispness.
