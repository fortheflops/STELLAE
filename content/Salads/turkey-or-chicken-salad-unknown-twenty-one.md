# Turkey or Chicken Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty One | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A quick and hearty salad featuring cooked rice, tender turkey or chicken, and crisp celery tossed in a tangy Dijon mustard dressing. Perfect served over fresh lettuce leaves or stuffed into pitas for an easy lunch.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | None | None | 4 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Measuring cups
* Mixing spoon

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3 cups | [[Rice]] | cooked |
| 2 cups | [[Turkey]] or [[Chicken]] | diced, cooked |
| 1/2 cup | [[Celery]] | sliced |
| 2 oz. | [[Pimiento]] | diced, drained |
| 1/3 cup | [[Dijon mustard dressing]] |  |
| To serve | [[Lettuce]] leaves |  |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Combine and Serve
1. In a large mixing bowl, combine the **3 cups cooked rice**, **2 cups diced cooked turkey or chicken**, **1/2 cup sliced celery**, **2 oz. diced pimiento**, and **1/3 cup Dijon mustard dressing**.
2. Stir gently until all ingredients are evenly distributed and thoroughly coated with the dressing.
3. Arrange the **lettuce leaves** on plates and spoon the salad mixture over them, or serve stuffed inside sandwiches or pitas.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This salad can be prepared up to 24 hours in advance. Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator until ready to serve.
