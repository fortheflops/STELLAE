# Orange Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A classic, creamy orange gelatin salad featuring mandarin oranges and a fluffy marshmallow dressing. This retro side dish is perfect for potlucks and holiday gatherings.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 0 mins | 2 hours | 15 servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Whisk

## Ingredients

### Salad Base
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3 boxes | [[Orange Jello]] | dry |
| 2 cups | [[Boiling Water]] | hot |
| 2 cups | [[Orange Juice]] | canned |
| 2 tbsp | [[Lemon Juice]] | fresh |
| 2 cans | [[Mandarin Oranges]] | drained |
| 1 pkg | [[Orange Sherbet]] | softened |

### Dressing
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 3oz pkg | [[Cream Cheese]] | softened |
| 1 cup | [[Whip Cream]] | whipped |
| 1 cup | [[Marshmallows]] | cut into pieces |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Gelatin Base
1. Dissolve **3 boxes orange jello** in **2 cups boiling water**.
2. Stir in **2 cups orange juice** and **2 tbsp lemon juice**.
3. Add **2 cans drained mandarin oranges** and **1 pkg orange sherbet**, stirring until the sherbet is melted and incorporated.

### Step 2: Dressing and Setting
4. In a separate bowl, whip together **1 3oz pkg cream cheese** and **1 cup whip cream** until smooth.
5. Fold in **1 cup cut marshmallows**.
6. Combine the dressing with the gelatin mixture, then let stand for 1 hour until thickened and set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This salad can be prepared up to 24 hours in advance and kept refrigerated until ready to serve.
