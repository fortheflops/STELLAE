## Instructions

### Step 1: Making the Dressing and Combining
1. Combine **2 tsp Dijon mustard**, **4 tbsp lemon juice**, **3 tbsp soy sauce**, and **1 clove garlic**; whisk until blended.
2. Add **2/3 cup olive oil** slowly while whisking constantly until the mixture is creamy.
3. Add **1 tbsp minced jalapeño**, **1/2 cup diced red onion**, and **5 cups cooked black beans**. Fold gently and season with **salt and ground pepper**.
4. Garnish with **fresh cilantro leaves** and **fresh chopped tomato**. Chill for **2 hours** before serving until fully cold and infused.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Can be prepared up to a day in advance and kept refrigerated; add fresh cilantro and tomatoes just before serving for optimal color and texture.
