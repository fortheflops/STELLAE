# Cold Rice Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Jan Batchelor | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A classic 1970s cold rice salad featuring savory Chicken Rice-A-Roni tossed with marinated artichokes, stuffed green olives, and scallions in a creamy dressing. Perfect for potlucks, picnics, or an easy make-ahead side dish.*

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Cook and Cool
1. Cook **1 pkg. Chicken Rice-A-Roni** according to package directions, then let it cool completely to room temperature.

### Step 2: Assemble and Dress
2. In a large mixing bowl, combine the cooled rice with **1 small jar green stuffed olives** (chopped), **5 green scallions** (chopped), and **1 jar marinated artichokes** (chopped).
3. Add **2 to 3 tbsp artichoke oil** and **1/2 cup mayonnaise** to the bowl.
4. Mix thoroughly until all ingredients are evenly coated and combined. Chill in the refrigerator for at least 2 hours before serving to allow the flavors to meld.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This salad is best made ahead and chilled. Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days.
