# Orange Pineapple Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** Burcea Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A classic mid-century fruit salad featuring fresh orange slices layered over pineapple rings on crisp lettuce. Finished with a sprinkle of chopped raisins and a sweet, thinned mayonnaise dressing.*

### Salad Base
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 large | [[Orange]] | peeled and cut crosswise into 6 slices |
| 6 | [[Pineapple]] slices |  |
| 6 | [[Lettuce]] leaves | crisp |
| To taste | [[Raisins]] | chopped |

### Dressing
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3/4 cup | [[Mayonnaise]] |  |
| To taste | [[Pineapple juice]] | used for thinning |

---

## Instructions

### Assembly & Serving
1. Peel the **1 large orange** and cut crosswise into **6 slices**.
2. Put each **orange slice** on top of one of the **6 pineapple slices**.
3. Place each assembled fruit stack onto a crisp **lettuce leaf** on a serving platter.
4. Sprinkle the tops with chopped **raisins**.
5. In a small bowl, whisk the **3/4 cup mayonnaise** with a little **pineapple juice** until thinned to a smooth, drizzling consistency, then serve alongside the salad.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the fruit stacks up to 4 hours ahead, cover tightly, and refrigerate. Whisk the dressing just before serving to keep it fresh.
