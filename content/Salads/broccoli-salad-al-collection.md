| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep the Salad
1. In a skillet, cook the **10 slices bacon** until deeply browned and crispy, then drain on paper towels and crumble.
2. In a large mixing bowl, combine the chopped **4 cups broccoli**, sliced **1 cup grapes**, chopped **1/2 cup red onion**, **1/2 cup sunflower seeds**, and crumbled **10 slices bacon**.

### Step 2: Dress and Serve
3. In a separate small bowl, whisk together the **1 cup Miracle Whip**, **1/3 cup sugar**, and **2 tbsp vinegar** until the sugar is fully dissolved and the dressing is smooth.
4. Pour the dressing over the vegetables and stir well until everything is evenly coated.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Cover and refrigerate for at least 1 hour before serving to let the flavors meld. Can be made up to 24 hours in advance.
