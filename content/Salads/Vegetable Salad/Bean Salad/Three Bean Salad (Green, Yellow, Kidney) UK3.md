### Instructions
1. Place one can of drained french cut green beans and add to one can drained cut wax yellow beans and one can drained and rinsed kidney beans in a bowl.
2. Blend one small chopped onion, one small chopped green pepper, 1/2 cup vinegar, 3/4 cup sugar, 1/2 cup salad oil, 1/4 teaspoon salt, and 1/4 teaspoon pepper thoroughly.
3. Pour the blended mixture over the beans.
4. Cover and refrigerate for several hours before serving.
