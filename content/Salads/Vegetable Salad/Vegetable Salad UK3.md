## Recipe
| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
|4 pkg.|[[Frozen Mixed Vegetables (Cooked & Drained)]]|
|1 C.|[[Small Green Onions]]|
|1 C.|[[Stuffed Sliced Olives]]|
### Dressing
|Measurements|Ingredients|
|:-----------------|:---------------------------|
|6 T.|[[Wine Vinegar]]|
|2/3 C.|[[Oil]]|
|2 t.|[[Salt]]|
|1/4 t.|[[Pepper]]|
___
### Instructions
1. Mix together vegetables, green onions, and olives.
2. Marinate for 1 hour before serving.
___

>[!Note]
>Yields 16 Servings
