# Creamy Cucumber Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Eight | ✍️ **Attribution:** Judy | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A refreshing and crisp summer side dish featuring thinly sliced cucumbers and onions tossed in a tangy dill dressing. Perfect for picnics and barbecues, this classic salad is best served chilled.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 0 mins | 30 mins | 4 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Whisk
* Chef's knife
* Cutting board

## Ingredients

### Dressing
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 cup | [[Sour Cream]] or plain [[Yogurt]] | Judy recommends yogurt cheese for a thicker texture |
| 1 tbsp | [[Vinegar]] | None |
| 1 tsp | [[Sugar]] | None |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Dill Weed]] | None |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Salt]] | None |

### Vegetables
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 large (approx. 3 cups) | [[Cucumber]] | halved lengthwise and thinly sliced |
| 1 small | [[Onion]] | thinly sliced and separated into rings |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Dressing
1. In a medium mixing bowl, whisk together the **1/2 cup sour cream or plain yogurt**, **1 tbsp vinegar**, **1 tsp sugar**, **1/4 tsp dill weed**, and **1/4 tsp salt** until the dressing is completely smooth and well combined.

### Step 2: Toss and Chill
2. Add the **1 large sliced cucumber** and **1 small onion** to the bowl with the dressing.
3. Gently toss the vegetables until they are thoroughly and evenly coated with the creamy mixture.
4. Cover the bowl and refrigerate for at least **30 minutes** before serving to allow the flavors to meld and the cucumbers to become cold and crisp.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Can be made up to 4 hours in advance. Keep refrigerated until ready to serve, and give it a gentle stir just before presenting.
