# Broccoli Cauliflower Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A crisp and creamy salad featuring fresh broccoli florets, cauliflower, and smoky bacon tossed in a sweet tangy dressing. Perfectly chilled and garnished with sliced boiled eggs for a delightful gathering side dish.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 10 mins | 30 mins | 6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Whisk
* Skillet

---
## Ingredients

### Salad Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 cups | [[Fresh broccoli florets]] | Fresh |
| 1 cup | [[Cauliflower]] | Florets |
| 1/2 cup | [[Red onion]] | Diced |
| 1/2 cup | [[Sharp cheddar cheese]] | Grated |
| 6 slices | [[Bacon]] | Cooked and crumbled |

### Dressing
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3/4 cup | [[Mayonnaise]] | None |
| 3 tbsp | [[Sugar]] | None |
| 1 1/2 tbsp | [[Apple cider vinegar]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Make Dressing and Toss
1. Whisk together **3/4 cup mayonnaise**, **3 tbsp sugar**, and **1 1/2 tbsp apple cider vinegar** to make the dressing.
2. Combine the dressing with the **2 cups fresh broccoli florets**, **1 cup cauliflower**, **1/2 cup red onion**, **1/2 cup sharp cheddar cheese**, and **6 slices bacon**.

### Step 2: Chill and Serve
3. Chill the salad thoroughly until well-blended and cold.
4. Garnish with sliced boiled eggs before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Cover and refrigerate for up to 24 hours before serving for the best flavor infusion.
