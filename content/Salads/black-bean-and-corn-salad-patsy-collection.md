# Black Bean and Corn Salad

## Instructions

### Step 1: Combine Vegetables
1. In a large mixing bowl, combine **2 cans black beans**, **1 red bell pepper**, **3 green onions**, **2 ripe plum tomatoes**, and **1 can corn**. Mix in **black olives**, **cheese**, **pickle relish**, and additional **peppers** if using.

### Step 2: Make the Dressing
2. In a small bowl, whisk together **6 Tbs. virgin olive oil**, **3 Tbs. fresh lemon juice**, **5 Tbs. chopped cilantro**, **1 Tsp. black pepper**, and **3/4 Tsp. salt** until well combined.

### Step 3: Toss and Rest
3. Pour the dressing over the bean mixture and toss thoroughly to coat. Let rest for **1 hour** to allow the flavors to meld together; bring back to room temperature before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Can be prepared up to 24 hours in advance and refrigerated. Be sure to let it sit at room temperature for a bit before serving.
