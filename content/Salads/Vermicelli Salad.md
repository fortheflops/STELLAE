# Vermicelli Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A delightful chilled pasta salad combining broken vermicelli, crisp vegetables, and a creamy mayonnaise dressing. Perfect for potlucks, this make-ahead dish absorbs vibrant flavors overnight.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 30 mins | 10 mins | Overnight | 8–10 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large pot
* Colander
* Mixing bowls
* Refrigerator

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 lb. | [[Vermicelli]] | Break in 1/3's, Cook - Do Not Rinse |
| 2 T. | [[Lemon Juice]] | Fresh |
| 1 T. | [[Accent]] | MSG seasoning |
| 4 T. | [[Oil]] | Standard cooking oil |
| 1 lg. can | [[Black Olives]] | Sliced |
| 1 C. | [[Celery]] | Finely chopped |
| 1 med. | [[Bell Pepper]] | Chopped |
| 1 med. | [[Onion]] | Chopped, purple preferred |
| 1 sm. jar | [[Pimentos]] | Diced & drained |
| 2 C. | [[Mayonnaise]] | Creamy base |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Initial Marinade
1. Cook the **1 lb. vermicelli** according to package directions, drain without rinsing, and break into thirds. While still hot, mix the pasta with **2 T. lemon juice**, **1 T. Accent**, and **4 T. oil**.

### Step 2: Overnight Chill
2. Cover the seasoned pasta mixture and refrigerate overnight until fully chilled and the flavors have melded.

### Step 3: Combining Vegetables
3. Remove the pasta from the refrigerator and fold in **1 lg. can black olives** and **1 C. finely chopped celery**.

### Step 4: Final Dressing
4. Add the **1 med. bell pepper**, **1 med. onion**, **1 sm. jar pimentos**, and **2 C. mayonnaise**.

### Step 5: Final Mix
5. Mix thoroughly until all ingredients are evenly coated and chilled until ready to serve.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store sealed in the refrigerator for up to 3-4 days; flavors improve after resting overnight.
