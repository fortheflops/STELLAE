# Shrimp & Macaroni Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A classic chilled salad featuring tender elbow macaroni, cooked shrimp, hard-cooked eggs, and crisp vegetables tossed in a tangy dressing. Perfectly refreshing for warm days and easily made ahead.*

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 25 mins | 10 mins | 2 hours | 4 servings |

## Ingredients

### Salad Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 1/2 tablespoons | [[Lemon juice]] | Freshly squeezed |
| 1 tablespoon | [[Salad oil]] | Vegetable or canola |
| 1/2 cup | [[Elbow macaroni]] | Cooked |
| 1/2 cup | [[Cooked shrimp]] | Cleaned and prepared |
| 2 | [[Hard-cooked eggs]] | Chilled and peeled |
| 2 tablespoons | [[Green pepper]] | Diced |
| 1 tsp. | [[Onion]] | Chopped |
| 1/2 cup | [[Celery]] | Chopped |
| 1/2 cup | [[Fresh tomato]] | Diced (1 tomato) |
| 1/4 cup | [[Stuffed olives]] | Chopped |
| 2 tablespoons | [[Sour cream]] | Standard |
| 1/2 tsp. | [[Salt]] | To taste |
| 1/4 cup | [[Miracle Whip]] | Mayonnaise style dressing |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Marinating the Macaroni
1. Mix **1 1/2 tablespoons lemon juice** and **1 tablespoon salad oil**, then combine with **1/2 cup elbow macaroni**.
2. Chill overnight, stirring occasionally until flavors absorb.

### Step 2: Combining and Dressing
3. Fold in **1/2 cup cooked shrimp**, **2 hard-cooked eggs**, **2 tablespoons green pepper**, **1 tsp. onion**, **1/2 cup celery**, **1/2 cup fresh tomato**, and **1/4 cup stuffed olives**.
4. Blend **2 tablespoons sour cream**, **1/2 tsp. salt**, and **1/4 cup Miracle Whip** together.
5. Chill the completed salad for a couple of hours until thoroughly cold and set before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Can be prepared up to 24 hours in advance and kept refrigerated until ready to serve.
