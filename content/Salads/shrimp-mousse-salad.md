# Shrimp Mousse Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Peggy Meyer | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A classic mid-century molded salad featuring a savory blend of shrimp, tomato soup, and cream cheese. This elegant, chilled dish is perfect for luncheons or as a unique appetizer.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 25 mins | 10 mins | 4 hours | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Saucepan
* Whisk
* Mixing bowl
* Mold

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1-2 cans | [[Shrimp]] | drained |
| 1 can | [[Tomato Soup]] | condensed |
| 2 pkgs | [[Cream Cheese]] | softened |
| 1 1/2 cups | [[Celery]] | chopped fine |
| 1 1/2 cups | [[Onion]] | chopped fine |
| 1 1/2 cups | [[Green Pepper]] | chopped fine |
| 3 tbsp | [[Gelatine]] | unflavored |
| 1 1/2 cups | [[Cold Water]] | divided |
| 1 cup | [[Mayonnaise]] | plus extra for topping |

---

## Instructions

### Preparation
1. In a saucepan, boil the **1 can tomato soup** and whisk in the **2 pkgs cream cheese** until the mixture is smooth and creamy.
2. Dissolve the **3 tbsp gelatine** in **1 1/2 cups cold water** and stir into the soup mixture; remove from heat and let cool.
3. Fold in the **1 cup mayonnaise**, **1-2 cans shrimp**, **1 1/2 cups celery**, **1 1/2 cups onion**, and **1 1/2 cups green pepper**.
4. Pour the mixture into a mold and refrigerate until set and firm to the touch.
5. Top with a dressing made of equal parts whipped cream and **mayonnaise** before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This dish must be made at least 4 hours in advance to allow the gelatine to set properly in the refrigerator.
