# Savory Tuna Fish Mousse (Luncheon Salad)

> 📜 **Collection:** General Archive | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A perfect retro salad for a luncheon. This savory mousse features flaked white tuna, cottage cheese, and pimientos set in a lemon-gelatin base with chicken gumbo soup and mayonnaise.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 5 mins | 4 hours | 6-8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large bowl
* Measuring cups
* Measuring spoons
* Mold

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 Cup | [[Tuna]] | White, flaked |
| 1 Cup | [[Chicken Gumbo Soup]] | Undiluted |
| 2 T. | [[Pimiento]] | Chopped |
| 2 T. | [[Celery]] | Chopped |
| 1 T. | [[Onions]] | Chopped |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Mayonnaise]] | None |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Cottage Cheese]] | None |
| 1 small pkg. | [[Gelatin]] | Lemon flavored |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Water]] | Boiling |
| 1 T. | [[Lemon Juice]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Gelatin Base
1. In a large bowl, dissolve the **1 small pkg. lemon flavored gelatin** in **1/2 cup boiling water**.

### Step 2: Mix and Chill
2. Stir in the **1 T. lemon juice**.
3. Stir in all the remaining ingredients: **1 cup white tuna**, **1 cup chicken gumbo soup**, **2 t. pimiento**, **2 t. celery**, **1 t. onions**, **1/2 cup mayonnaise**, and **1/2 cup cottage cheese** until well combined.
4. Pour into an oiled mold and chill for at least **4 hours** until completely set and firm to the touch.
5. Unmold onto a bed of lettuce to serve.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store sealed in the refrigerator for up to 2 days before serving.
