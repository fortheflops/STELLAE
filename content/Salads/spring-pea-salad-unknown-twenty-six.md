# Spring Pea Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A crisp and creamy Spring Pea Salad combining fresh vegetables, cashews, and bacon tossed in a tangy dressing. Perfect for potlucks and warm-weather gatherings.*

---

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 0 mins | 30 mins | 6 servings |

## Ingredients

### Salad Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 oz | [[Peas]] | Fresh or frozen |
| 1 c | [[Celery]] | Diced |
| 1 c | [[Cauliflower]] | Fresh, chopped |
| 1/4 c | [[Green Onion]] | Diced |
| 2 T | [[Pimentos]] | Chopped |
| 1 c | [[Cashews]] | Chopped, or sunflower seeds |
| 1/4 c | [[Bacon]] | Crumbled |

### Dressing
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 c | [[Sour Cream]] | Can substitute buttermilk salad dressing |
| 1/3 tsp | [[Dijon Mustard]] | None |
| 1 sm. clove | [[Garlic]] | Minced |

---

## Instructions

### Preparation
1. Rinse the **10 oz fresh or frozen peas** in hot water and drain well.
2. Combine the **1 c diced celery**, **1 c fresh cauliflower**, **1/4 c diced green onion**, **2 T chopped pimentos**, **1 c chopped cashews**, and **1/4 c crumbled bacon** in a large mixing bowl.

### Dressing and Assembly
3. Mix the **1/2 c sour cream**, **1/3 tsp dijon mustard**, and **1 sm. clove minced garlic** together to form the dressing.
4. Pour the dressing over the salad mixture, toss thoroughly to coat, and chill until cold before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Cover and chill in the refrigerator for at least 30 minutes before serving to let the flavors meld.
