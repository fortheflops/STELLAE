# Bean Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A classic, colorful marinated bean salad featuring a medley of yellow wax, red kidney, green, and garbanzo beans tossed with crisp peppers and onions in a sweet and tangy vinaigrette. Perfect for picnics and gatherings, this make-ahead side dish only gets better with time.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Combine Vegetables
1. In a large mixing bowl, combine the **1 cup yellow wax beans**, **1 cup red kidney beans**, **1 cup green beans**, and **1 cup garbanzo beans**.
2. Gently toss in the **1 thin-ringed green pepper**, **1 crisp-ringed purple onion**, and **1 small jar pimientos**.

### Step 2: Dress and Marinate
3. In a separate bowl or jar, whisk together the **1/3 cup corn oil**, **1/2 cup sugar**, **3/4 cup vinegar**, **1 tsp salt**, and **black pepper** until the sugar is fully dissolved.
4. Pour the dressing over the bean mixture and toss thoroughly to coat.
5. Transfer the salad to a covered glass or pottery jar. Keep the mixture turned at intervals to marinate it thoroughly before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This salad is best made ahead of time to allow the flavors to meld. It will keep indefinitely when stored in a covered glass or pottery jar in the refrigerator. If doubling the recipe, simply use 1 full can of each bean type, well drained.
