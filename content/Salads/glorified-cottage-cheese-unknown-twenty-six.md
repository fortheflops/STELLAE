# Glorified Cottage Cheese

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Velma Holstean | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A delightful and fruity retro side dish featuring cottage cheese combined with pineapple, coconut, and cherries. Perfect for a refreshing addition to any family dinner or gathering.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | None | None | 6-8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl

---
## Ingredients

### Salad Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 12 oz | [[Cottage Cheese]] |  |
| 1 c | [[Mini Marshmallows]] | referred to as min. m. m. |
| 1 c | [[Tidbit Pineapple]] | drained |
| 1/2 c | [[Angel Flake Coconut]] |  |
| 1/8 c | [[Nuts]] | chopped |
| 1 | [[Banana]] | sliced, add just before serving |
| 10 | [[Maraschino Cherries]] | cut |
| 1 c | [[Whipping Cream]] | whipped |
| 1/4 c | [[Powdered Sugar]] |  |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing the Base
1. In a large mixing bowl, combine the **12 oz cottage cheese**, **1 c min. m. m.**, **1 c tidbit pineapple**, **1/2 c angel flake coconut**, and **1/8 c nuts**.

### Step 2: Adding Fruit and Cream
2. Gently fold in the **10 m. cherries cut** and the whipped **1 c w. cream** sweetened with **1/4 c p. sugar** until smooth and well incorporated.

### Step 3: Final Assembly
3. Chill until ready to serve, then fold in **1 banana sliced** just before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Mix all ingredients except the banana ahead of time and keep refrigerated until ready to serve.
