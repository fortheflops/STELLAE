# Dressing For Fruit Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A classic sweet and tangy homemade dressing perfect for enhancing any fresh fruit salad. This traditional recipe combines sugar, dry mustard, celery seed, paprika, onion juice, salad oil, and vinegar into a smooth emulsion.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | None | None | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Whisk
* Measuring cups
* Measuring spoons

---
## Ingredients

### Dressing Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 cup | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 1 tsp | [[Salt]] | Standard |
| 1 tsp | [[Dry Mustard]] | Ground |
| 1 tsp | [[Celery Seed]] | Whole |
| 1 tsp | [[Paprika]] | Ground |
| 1 tsp | [[Onion Juice]] | Freshly extracted |
| 1 cup | [[Salad Oil]] | Vegetable oil |
| 1/2 cup | [[Vinegar]] | Standard white or cider |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing and Emulsifying
1. Mix the **1 cup sugar**, **1 tsp salt**, **1 tsp dry mustard**, **1 tsp celery seed**, and **1 tsp paprika** together in a bowl, then add the **1 tsp onion juice**.
2. Add the **1 cup salad oil** and **1/2 cup vinegar** alternately, a small amount at a time, beating after each addition.
3. Beat for at least 15 minutes until the dressing becomes thoroughly emulsified and smooth.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in a sealed glass jar in the refrigerator for up to 1 week; shake or whisk well before serving.
