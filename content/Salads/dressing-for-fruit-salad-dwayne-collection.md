# Dressing For Fruit Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A tangy and sweet homemade dressing perfectly paired with fresh fruit salad. Features a smooth emulsion of oil, vinegar, and savory seasonings.*

| 1 tsp | [[Dry Mustard]] | None |
| 1 tsp | [[Celery Seed]] | None |
| 1 tsp | [[Paprika]] | None |
| 1 tsp | [[Onion Juice]] | None |
| 1 cup | [[Salad Oil]] | None |
| 1/4 cup | [[Vinegar]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing and Emulsifying
1. Mix the dry ingredients including **1 cup sugar**, **1 tsp salt**, **1 tsp dry mustard**, **1 tsp celery seed**, and **1 tsp paprika** in a mixing bowl.
2. Add the **1 tsp onion juice** and stir well.
3. Add the **1 cup salad oil** and **1/4 cup vinegar** alternately, a small amount at a time, beating continuously after each addition until thoroughly combined and smooth.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 1 week; shake or whisk well before serving.
