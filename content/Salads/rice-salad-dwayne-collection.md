# Rice Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A refreshing and simple cold rice salad tossed with peas, cucumbers, fresh herbs, and a zesty Italian dressing. Best assembled while the rice is still hot to maximize flavor absorption.*

## Ingredients

### Salad Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| As desired | [[Rice]] | cooked hot |
| As desired | [[Peas]] | cooked |
| To taste | [[Cucumbers]] | diced |
| To taste | [[Chives]] | chopped |
| To taste | [[Parsley]] | chopped |
| 1 cup | [[Italian Dressing]] |  |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Combine
1. Cook the **rice** and **peas** in a saucepan until tender and steaming hot.
2. Add the cooked **peas** to the hot **rice** in a large mixing bowl.
3. While the mixture is still hot, add the diced **cucumbers**, chopped **chives**, **parsley**, and **1 cup Italian dressing**.
4. Toss thoroughly until the **rice** absorbs the **1 cup Italian dressing** and the salad is evenly coated. Let cool, then chill before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This salad is best made a few hours ahead to let the flavors meld. Cover and refrigerate for up to 3 days, tossing with a splash of extra dressing if it dries out.
