# Molded Crabmeat Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** May Cowing | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A classic mid-century molded salad featuring flaked crabmeat suspended in a tangy lemon gelatin base. This refreshing dish is accented with crisp celery and pimento for a perfect texture contrast.*

## Ingredients

### Salad Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 pkg | [[Lemon Jello]] | standard size |
| 1 1/2 cups | [[Boiling Water]] | hot |
| 3 tbsp | [[Vinegar]] | white or cider |
| 1/2 tsp | [[Salt]] | fine |
| 2 cups | [[Crabmeat]] | shredded |
| 3/4 cup | [[Celery]] | chopped |
| 2 tbsp | [[Pimento]] | cut up |
| 1 tsp | [[Onion Juice]] | freshly squeezed |
| 1/2 cup | [[Mayonnaise]] | added when mixture is fairly stiff |

---

## Instructions

### Preparation
1. Dissolve **1 pkg lemon jello** in **1 1/2 cups boiling water**.
2. Stir in **3 tbsp vinegar** and **1/2 tsp salt**.
3. Allow the mixture to cool until it begins to thicken.
4. Fold in **2 cups shredded crabmeat**, **3/4 cup chopped celery**, **2 tbsp cut up pimento**, **1 tsp onion juice**, and **1/2 cup mayonnaise** once the mixture is fairly stiff.
5. Pour into a mold and refrigerate until completely set and firm to the touch before serving with your favorite dressing.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This salad must be made at least 4 hours in advance to allow the gelatin to set properly. It can be kept refrigerated for up to 2 days.
