# Macaroni Chicken Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** UK15 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A delightful vintage pasta salad combining prepared macaroni and cheese dinner with tender chicken, hard-cooked eggs, and crisp vegetables. Tossed in a creamy dressing and chilled thoroughly, it makes for a refreshing and hearty side or main dish.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 10 mins | 1 hour | 4–6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large mixing bowl
* Saucepan
* Stirring spoon

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 (7 1/4 ounce) package | [[Macaroni & Cheese Dinner]] | prepared according to package directions |
| 1 cup | [[Chicken]] | diced, cooked |
| 3 | [[Eggs]] | hard-cooked, chopped |
| 1/2 cup | [[Green Pepper]] | chopped |
| 1/2 cup | [[Green Peas]] | cooked |
| 1/4 cup | [[Celery]] | chopped |
| 1/4 cup | [[Radishes]] | sliced (water chestnuts can be used as a substitute) |
| 3 tablespoons | [[Onion]] | chopped |
| 1/3 cup | [[Salad Dressing]] | or [[Mayonnaise]] |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Base
1. Cook the **1 (7 1/4 ounce) package macaroni & cheese dinner** according to the package directions.

### Step 2: Combine Ingredients
2. In a large bowl, combine the prepared macaroni and cheese with **1 cup diced, cooked chicken**, **3 chopped hard-cooked eggs**, **1/2 cup chopped green pepper**, **1/2 cup cooked green peas**, **1/4 cup chopped celery**, **1/4 cup sliced radishes**, and **3 tablespoons chopped onion**.

### Step 3: Dress and Chill
3. Stir in **1/3 cup salad dressing or mayonnaise** last and toss gently until fully incorporated and creamy.
4. Chill thoroughly in the refrigerator before serving until completely cold and set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store sealed in the refrigerator for up to 2 days for the best texture and flavor.
