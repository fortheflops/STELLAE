# Cranberry-Pineapple Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Arlene | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A classic, creamy fruit salad featuring a sweet and tart blend of ground cranberries, crushed pineapple, and fluffy whipped cream. This nostalgic dish requires overnight preparation to allow the flavors to meld perfectly.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 0 mins | 24 hours | 12 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowls
* Food processor
* Electric mixer

---
## Ingredients

### Salad Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 packages | [[Fresh Cranberries]] | ground |
| 1 1/2 cups | [[Sugar]] | granulated |
| 2 pints | [[Whipping Cream]] | whipped stiffly |
| 1 package | [[Miniature Marshmallows]] | standard size |
| 1 tall thin tin | [[Crushed Pineapple]] | drained |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Initial Maceration
1. Combine the **2 packages fresh cranberries** and **1 1/2 cups sugar** in a bowl and let stand overnight to macerate.

### Step 2: Cream Base
2. In a separate bowl, combine the **2 pints whipping cream** (whipped stiffly) and **1 package miniature marshmallows**; let this mixture stand overnight.

### Step 3: Final Assembly
3. The next day, mix the cranberry mixture and the cream mixture together until well combined.
4. Fold in the **1 tall thin tin crushed pineapple** until evenly distributed and serve chilled.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This recipe requires an overnight rest for both the cranberry-sugar mixture and the cream-marshmallow mixture to achieve the correct consistency.
