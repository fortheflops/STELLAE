# Chafing Dish Spinach Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A warm and vibrant chafing dish spinach salad tossed with crispy bacon, green onions, and a tangy dressing. Perfectly wilted to tender perfection right at the table.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 10 mins | 30 mins | 4–6 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Paper towels
* Mixing bowl
* Chafing dish
* Electric skillet

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 lb. | [[Spinach]] | Fresh |
| 2 | [[Green Onions]] | Sliced, with tops |
| To taste | [[Pepper]] | Coarsely ground |
| 5 slices | [[Bacon]] | Diced |
| 2 Tbsp. | [[Wine Vinegar]] | None |
| 1 Tbsp. | [[Lemon Juice]] | None |
| 1 tsp. | [[Sugar]] | None |
| ½ tsp. | [[Salt]] | None |
| 1 | [[Egg]] | Hard-cooked, coarsely chopped |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep the Greens
1. Wash the **1 lb. fresh spinach**, discarding stems; pat dry on paper towels.
2. Tear into bite-sized pieces and place in a bowl.
3. Add the **2 sliced green onions** and sprinkle with **coarsely ground pepper to taste**.
4. Chill in the refrigerator until ready to use.

### Step 2: Cook the Bacon and Dress
5. Fry the **5 slices diced bacon** in a deep chafing dish on an electric skillet over low heat until crisp.
6. Add the **2 Tbsp. wine vinegar**, **1 Tbsp. lemon juice**, **1 tsp. sugar**, and **½ tsp. salt** to the skillet.
7. Add the chilled spinach mixture and toss just until the leaves are coated and slightly wilted.
8. Sprinkle with the **1 hard-cooked coarsely chopped egg** before serving immediately while warm.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Prep and chill the spinach and onions ahead of time, but do not combine with the warm bacon dressing until just before serving.
