# Chinese Cabbage Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Eight | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A delightful and crunchy Chinese cabbage salad tossed with broken ramen noodles, slivered almonds, and toasted nuts. Coated in a tangy homemade dressing infused with rice vinegar and the ramen seasoning packet.*

## Ingredients

### Salad Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 sm head | [[Cabbage]] | diced |
| 1 pkg | [[Ramen Noodles]] | chicken flavor, broken up |
| 1/2 c | [[Slivered Almonds]] | diced |
| To taste | [[Cashews]] | toasted, optional |
| To taste | [[Sesame Seeds]] | optional |

### Dressing
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/3 c | [[Rice Vinegar]] | none |
| 1/3 c | [[Vegetable Oil]] | none |
| 2 t | [[Sugar]] | none |
| 1 packet | [[Ramen Seasoning Packet]] | reserved from ramen noodles |
| 1 t | [[Soy Sauce]] | none |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep the Salad
1. In a large salad bowl, combine the **1 sm head cabbage**, **1 pkg ramen noodles**, **1/2 c slivered almonds**, and your choice of **toasted cashews or sesame seeds**.

### Step 2: Make the Dressing
2. In a small bowl or jar, whisk together the **1/3 c rice vinegar**, **1/3 c vegetable oil**, **2 t sugar**, **seasoning packet from ramen**, and **1 t soy sauce** until the sugar dissolves and the dressing is well combined.

### Step 3: Toss and Serve
3. Pour the dressing over the cabbage mixture just before serving, tossing thoroughly until all ingredients are evenly coated and crunchy.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Keep the dressing separate from the salad until ready to serve to maintain the crunchiness of the noodles and nuts.
