# Carrot Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** The Loraine Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Hazel | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A classic marinated carrot salad featuring a savory-sweet hot tomato soup dressing, crisp onions, and bell peppers. This vintage recipe chills overnight to absorb robust flavors before serving.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 15 mins | 8 hours | 6–8 Servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Saucepan
* Colander
* Mixing bowl
* Knife
* Cutting board

## Ingredients

### Hot Marinade
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 can | [[Tomato Soup]] | undiluted |
| 3/4 cup | [[Sugar]] | None |
| 1/2 cup | [[Oil]] | vegetable or canola |
| 1 tsp | [[Mustard]] | prepared |
| 1 tsp | [[Worcestershire Sauce]] | None |
| 1/2 cup | [[Vinegar]] | None |
| 1 tsp | [[Salt]] | None |
| 1 tsp | [[Pepper]] | ground |

### Salad
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 lbs | [[Carrots]] | peeled and sliced |
| 1 | [[Onion]] | medium, sliced |
| 1 | [[Bell Pepper]] | diced green pepper |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Cook Carrots
1. Peel and slice the **2 lbs carrots**. Cook the carrots in water without salt until fork tender.

### Step 2: Combine Salad Ingredients
2. Drain the cooked carrots well in a colander. In a large mixing bowl, combine the cooked carrots with **1 medium onion** (sliced) and **1 bell pepper** (diced green pepper).

### Step 3: Make the Hot Marinade
3. In a saucepan, combine the ingredients for the hot marinade: **1 can tomato soup**, **3/4 cup sugar**, **1/2 cup oil**, **1 tsp mustard**, **1 tsp Worcestershire sauce**, **1/2 cup vinegar**, **1 tsp salt**, and **1 tsp pepper**. Bring the mixture to a rolling boil.

### Step 4: Marinate and Chill
4. Pour the hot marinade evenly over the carrot mixture, ensuring everything is well coated. Cover and chill in the refrigerator overnight until thoroughly cold and flavorful.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This salad tastes best when prepared a day in advance and can be stored sealed in the refrigerator for up to 5 days.
