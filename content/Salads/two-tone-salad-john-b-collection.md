# Two Tone Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A delightful layered retro salad featuring lime gelatin, cream cheese, and diced pears. This classic recipe offers a refreshing and creamy texture profile.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 0 mins | 4 hours | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Loaf pan
* Mixing bowls
* Electric mixer

---
## Ingredients

### Salad Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 pkgs | [[Lime Jello]] | or lime gelatin |
| 1 1/2 cups | [[Boiling Water]] | hot |
| 1 tsp | [[Vinegar]] | none |
| 1/2 cup | [[Pear Juice]] | none |
| 1/2 tsp | [[Salt]] | none |
| 2 pkgs | [[Philadelphia Cream Cheese]] | softened |
| 2 cups | [[Diced Pears]] | diced |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Ginger]] | ground |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: First Layer
1. Dissolve **2 pkgs lime jello** in **1 1/2 cups boiling water**, add **pear juice**, **salt**, and **vinegar**.
2. Pour 1/2 of the mixture as a first layer in a loaf pan.
3. Chill until firm.

### Step 2: Second Layer & Assembly
4. Chill the remaining gelatin mixture until syrupy, then whip until fluffy and thick.
5. Cream the **2 pkgs Philadelphia cream cheese** with **1/4 tsp ginger**.
6. Fold in the whipped gelatin mixture gradually, then fold in the **2 cups diced pears**.
7. Pour over the firm first layer and chill until completely firm.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Prepare up to a day in advance and keep well-chilled in the refrigerator until ready to slice and serve.
