# Wild Rice Salad Clatter

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A delightful and refreshing wild rice salad tossed with herb and garlic dressing, crisp celery, and green onion. Served chilled over a bed of crisp lettuce and garnished with tomato wedges.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 25 mins | 30 mins | 6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Saucepan
* Shallow metal pan
* Mixing spoon

---
## Ingredients

### Salad Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 pkg (6 oz) | [[Long Grain and Wild Rice Mix]] | or rice mix |
| 1/2 cup | [[Bottled Herb and Garlic Salad Dressing]] | divided |
| 1 cup | [[Chopped Celery]] | chopped |
| 1/4 cup | [[Sliced Green Onion]] | sliced |
| As needed | [[Crisp Lettuce]] | for serving bed |
| As needed | [[Tomato Wedges]] | for garnish |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Rice Preparation & Chilling
1. Cook the **1 pkg long grain and wild rice mix** according to package directions, omitting any butter.
2. Turn the cooked rice into a shallow metal pan.
3. Toss the rice with **1/4 cup bottled herb and garlic salad dressing** (half of the total amount).
4. Place in the freezer to chill quickly for **30 minutes until cold**.

### Step 2: Assembly & Serving
5. Add the remaining **1/4 cup bottled herb and garlic salad dressing**, **1 cup chopped celery**, and **1/4 cup sliced green onion** to the chilled rice.
6. Toss until well blended and evenly coated.
7. Serve on a bed of **crisp lettuce** and garnish with **tomato wedges**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The rice can be cooked and chilled up to a day in advance before adding the fresh celery and final dressing.
