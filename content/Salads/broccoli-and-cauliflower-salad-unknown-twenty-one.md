# Broccoli and Cauliflower Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty One | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A crisp and refreshing cold salad featuring fresh broccoli, cauliflower, and carrots tossed with kidney beans and mozzarella in a simple herb vinaigrette. Perfect for summer picnics, potlucks, or as a healthy make-ahead side dish.*

## Ingredients

### Dressing
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 cup | [[White Vinegar]] | None |
| 1/2 cup | [[Oil]] | None |
| 1 tsp | [[Dried Oregano]] | None |

### Salad Base
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 cups | [[Broccoli]] florets | fresh |
| 2 cups | [[Cauliflower]] florets | fresh |
| 2 medium | [[Carrots]] | cut in thin strips |
| 16 oz | [[Kidney Beans]] | drained |
| 2 cups (8 oz.) | shredded [[Mozzarella Cheese]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Assembly and Chilling
1. In a large mixing bowl, whisk together the **1/2 cup white vinegar**, **1/2 cup oil**, **1 tsp dried oregano**, and **2 cloves minced garlic** until well combined.
2. Add the **2 cups broccoli florets**, **2 cups cauliflower florets**, and **2 medium carrots** cut in thin strips to the bowl, and toss thoroughly to coat the vegetables in the dressing.
3. Gently fold in the **16 oz drained kidney beans** and **2 cups shredded Mozzarella cheese**, tossing lightly to distribute evenly.
4. Cover the bowl tightly and chill in the refrigerator for **2 to 3 hours** to allow the flavors to meld, stirring occasionally to redistribute the dressing.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This salad is best made a few hours in advance so the vegetables can marinate. Store covered in the refrigerator for up to 3 days.
