# Shrimp And Macaroni Salad

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Marinating
1. Mix **1 1/2 T. lemon juice** and **1 T. salad oil** together, then combine with **1 1/2 cups elbow macaroni**.
2. Chill overnight in the refrigerator, stirring occasionally until the liquid is fully absorbed and the pasta is flavorful.

### Step 2: Assembly
3. Fold in **1 1/2 cups shrimp**, **2 hard cooked eggs**, **2 T. green pepper**, **1 tsp. onion**, **1/2 cup celery**, **1/2 cup fresh tomatoes**, and **1/4 cup stuffed olives**.
4. Blend **2 T. sour cream** and **1/4 cup miracle whip** together, then gently mix into the salad.
5. Add **1/2 tsp. salt** to taste.
6. Chill for a couple of hours before serving until thoroughly cold and set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Macaroni can be marinated overnight, and the fully assembled salad can be stored in the refrigerator for up to 24 hours.
