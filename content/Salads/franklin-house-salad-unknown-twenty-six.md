# Franklin House Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** N. Lake | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A delightful and hearty main dish salad featuring cooked wild rice, tender cubed turkey, crunchy water chestnuts, and sweet seedless grapes tossed in a creamy dressing. Perfect for a refreshing lunch or light dinner.*

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 40 mins | 30 mins | 6-8 servings |

## Ingredients

### Salad Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2/3 cup | [[Wild rice]] | Uncooked |
| 2 cups | [[Water]] | For cooking rice |
| 2 tsp | [[Salt]] | For cooking rice |
| 2 cups | [[Turkey]] | Cooked and cubed |
| 1/2 | [[Small onion]] | Grated |
| 8 oz | [[Water chestnuts]] | Chopped |
| 2 cups | [[Seedless grapes]] | Halved |
| 1 cup | [[Cashews]] | Or other nuts |

### Dressing Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2/3 cup | [[Mayonnaise]] | Standard |
| 1/4 cup | [[Milk]] | Standard |
| 1 tsp | [[Lemon juice]] | Fresh |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Rice and Dressing Prep
1. Cook **2/3 cup wild rice** in **2 cups water** and **2 tsp salt** until soft. Cool completely.
2. Mix **2/3 cup mayonnaise**, **1/4 cup milk**, and **1 tsp lemon juice** together in a small bowl for the dressing.

### Step 2: Combining and Chilling
3. Combine remaining ingredients including **2 cups cooked turkey**, grated **1/2 small onion**, **8 oz water chestnuts**, **2 cups seedless grapes**, and **1 cup cashews** with the cooked **wild rice** and prepared dressing.
4. Chill in the refrigerator until thoroughly cold and flavors are melded before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Prepare the salad up to a day in advance, keeping the cashews separate and adding them just before serving for optimal crunch.
