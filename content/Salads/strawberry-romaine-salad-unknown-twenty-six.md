## Instructions

### Step 1: Making the Dressing
1. Combine the first 7 ingredients (**1 cup oil**, **3/4 cup sugar**, **1/2 cup red wine vinegar**, **2 cloves garlic**, **1/2 tsp salt**, **1/2 tsp paprika**, **1/4 tsp pepper**, and the handwritten **1 tsp poppy seed**) in a jar or container, cover, and shake well until emulsified.

### Step 2: Assembling the Salad
2. Tear the **1 large head romaine lettuce** and **1 head Boston lettuce**, then combine lettuce, **1 pint strawberries**, **1 cup jack cheese**, and **1/2 cup walnuts** in a large salad bowl until thoroughly mixed.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The dressing can be refrigerated for up to one week before serving.
