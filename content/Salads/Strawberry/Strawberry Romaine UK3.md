## Recipe
### Salad Base
| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
|1 Bunch|[[Romaine Lettuce]]|
|1 Bunch|[[Bibb or Boston Lettuce]]|
|1 Pt.|[[Strawberries (1/2 into)]]|
|1/2 C.|[[Chopped Walnuts (I use more to what you desire)]]|
|1 C.|[[Monterey Cheese (Shredded)]]|
### Dressing
|Measurements|Ingredients|
|:-----------------|:---------------------------|
|1 C.|[[Oil (I use Olive Oil)]]|
|3/4 C.|[[Sugar]]|
|1/2 C.|[[Red Wine Vinegar]]|
|1/2 t.|[[Salt]]|
|1/2 t.|[[White Pepper]]|
|1/2 t.|[[Paprika]]|
___
### Instructions
1. Mix all ingredients - toss with just enough dressing to moisten.
2. Serve immediately.
