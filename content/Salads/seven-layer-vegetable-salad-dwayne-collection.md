# Seven Layer Vegetable Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A classic mid-century layered potluck salad featuring crisp lettuce, celery, red onion, green pepper, and sweet peas, topped with a creamy mayonnaise dressing, parmesan, and bacon. Perfect for making ahead, this salad develops its best flavors after chilling in the refrigerator.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 0 mins | 8 hours | 8 servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Long Pyrex bowl
* Measuring cups
* Measuring spoons
* Spatula

## Ingredients

### Salad Layers
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 head | [[Lettuce]] | shredded or chopped, enough to fill half of the bowl |
| 1/2 cup | [[Celery]] | chopped |
| 1/2 cup | [[Red Onion]] | chopped |
| 1/2 cup | [[Green Pepper]] | chopped |
| 1 can | [[Peas]] | drained, Le Sueur brand preferred |

### Dressing and Toppings
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 pt. | [[Mayonnaise]] | Hellmann's brand preferred |
| 2 tbsp | [[Sugar]] |  |
| 1 small jar | [[Parmesan Cheese]] | grated |
| To taste | [[Bacon]] | crumbled, or Bac'Os bacon bits |

---

## Instructions

### Assembly
1. Fill half of a long Pyrex bowl with the chopped **lettuce**.
2. Add the next ingredients in even layers over the lettuce: **1/2 cup chopped celery**, **1/2 cup chopped red onion**, **1/2 cup chopped green pepper**, and **1 can drained peas**.
3. Spread **1 pt. mayonnaise** evenly over the top layer of peas, sealing it to the edges of the bowl to lock in freshness.
4. Sprinkle **2 tbsp sugar** evenly over the mayonnaise layer, then sprinkle with **1 small jar grated parmesan cheese**.
5. Top the salad with **crumbled bacon** or Bac'Os.
6. Cover tightly and let sit in the refrigerator for at least **8 hours** to allow the flavors to meld and the salad to become thoroughly chilled before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This salad is best made 8 to 24 hours in advance. Keep covered and refrigerated until ready to serve.
