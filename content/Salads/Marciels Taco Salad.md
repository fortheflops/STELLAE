# Marciel's Taco Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Marciel | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A delightful and crunchy taco salad featuring seasoned hamburger, fresh vegetables, and tangy Thousand Island dressing. Perfectly tossed for a crowd-pleasing side or light entree.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 10 mins | None | 6–8 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Skillet
* Salad bowl
* Cutting board
* Chef's knife

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 Pound | [[Hamburger]] | Ground |
| 1 Package | [[Doritos]] | Crushed |
| 1 | [[Onion]] | Chopped |
| 1 Head | [[Lettuce]] | Shredded |
| 6 Ounces | [[Thousand Island Dressing]] | Ready-to-use |
| 4 | [[Tomatoes]] | Diced |
| 1 Can | [[Kidney Beans]] | Rinsed and drained |
| To taste | [[Hot Sauce]] | Optional |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Cook the Meat
1. In a skillet over medium-high heat, cook the **1 pound hamburger** until fully browned and no longer pink. Drain any excess grease.

### Step 2: Toss and Serve
2. In a large salad bowl, combine the cooked **hamburger**, shredded **1 head lettuce**, chopped **1 onion**, diced **4 tomatoes**, and rinsed **1 can kidney beans**.
3. Add the **6 ounces Thousand Island dressing** and **hot sauce** to taste, tossing gently until all ingredients are evenly coated and crisp.
4. Fold in the crushed **1 package Doritos** right before serving to maintain a satisfying crunch.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Combine the meat and vegetables without the Doritos and dressing ahead of time; add the dressing and chips just before serving to keep the salad crisp.
