# Spinach & Bacon Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** LOC | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A classic vintage salad featuring fresh spinach, crisp bacon, and a tangy homemade dressing. Tossed together right before serving for optimal texture and flavor.*

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 Pound | Fresh [[Spinach]] | Washed, drained, and torn |
| 1 Head | Red Leaf [[Lettuce]] | Or 1/2 head iceberg lettuce, washed, drained, and torn |
| 1/4 Pound | [[Bacon]] | Fried crisp, drained, cooled, and crumbled |

### Dressing
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/4 Cup | [[Sugar]] | None |
| 1 Teaspoon | [[Salt]] | None |
| 1 Teaspoon | Dry [[Mustard]] | None |
| 1 Teaspoon | [[Onion]] juice | Juice scraped from an onion |
| 1/3 Cup | [[Cider Vinegar]] | None |
| 1 Cup | Salad [[Oil]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep Greens and Bacon
1. Wash, drain, and tear the **1 pound fresh spinach** and **1 head red leaf lettuce** (or iceberg lettuce), then place them in a large salad bowl.
2. Fry the **1/4 pound bacon** until crisp, drain the fat, let cool, and crumble.

### Step 2: Make the Dressing
3. In a separate bowl, whisk together the **1/4 cup sugar**, **1 teaspoon salt**, **1 teaspoon dry mustard**, **1 teaspoon onion juice**, **1/3 cup cider vinegar**, and **1 cup salad oil** until well combined.

### Step 3: Toss and Serve
4. Add the crumbled **bacon** to the salad greens.
5. Pour the dressing over the salad greens and bacon just before serving, toss gently to combine, and serve immediately.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Keep the washed greens and prepared dressing stored separately in the refrigerator until ready to serve.
