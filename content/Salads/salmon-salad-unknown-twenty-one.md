# Salmon Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty One | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A creamy and refreshing salmon salad featuring cottage cheese, mayonnaise, and crisp vegetables. Perfect for serving over fresh greens with a garnish of eggs, tomatoes, and radishes.*

---

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | None | None | 4 servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Spoon

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 4 oz. | [[Cottage cheese]] | None |
| 1/4 c. | [[Mayo]] | None |
| 1 T. | [[Lemon juice]] | None |
| 1/2 c. | [[Celery]] | Chopped |
| 1/4 c. | [[Green pepper]] | Chopped |
| 7 3/4 oz. | [[Salmon]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing and Assembling
1. Mix all ingredients except the **7 3/4 oz. salmon** together in a bowl.
2. Gently fold in the **7 3/4 oz. salmon** lightly to maintain texture.
3. Add seasonoings to taste.
4. Serve the mixture on a bed of greens and garnish with eggs, tomatoes, and radishes until well presented.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the mixed salad in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 1 day before serving over fresh greens.
