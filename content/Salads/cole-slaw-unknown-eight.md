# Cole Slaw

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Eight | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A crisp and colorful coleslaw featuring fresh cabbage, red and yellow bell peppers, and scallions tossed in a creamy, tangy yogurt-mayo dressing with a hint of fresh basil.*

## Ingredients

### Salad Base
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/4 medium head | [[Cabbage]] | coarsely chopped |
| 1/2 small | [[Red Bell Pepper]] | coarsely chopped |
| 1/2 small | [[Yellow Bell Pepper]] | coarsely chopped |
| 2 | [[Scallions]] | coarsely chopped |
| 2 tablespoons | fresh [[Basil]] leaves | shredded |

### Dressing
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3 tablespoons | [[Mayonnaise]] | None |
| 1 tablespoon | plain [[Yogurt]] | yoghurt |
| 1 tablespoon | [[Cider Vinegar]] | None |
| 1 pinch | [[Cayenne Pepper]] | None |
| To taste | [[Salt]] and [[Black Pepper]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep
1. Coarsely chop the **1/4 medium head cabbage**, **1/2 small red bell pepper**, **1/2 small yellow bell pepper**, and **2 scallions** on a clean cutting board.

### Step 2: Dressing
2. In a mixing bowl, whisk together the **3 tablespoons mayonnaise**, **1 tablespoon plain yoghurt**, **1 tablespoon cider vinegar**, and **1 pinch of cayenne** until the dressing is smooth and fully emulsified.

### Step 3: Combine
3. Add the chopped vegetables, **2 tablespoons shredded fresh basil leaves**, and **salt and pepper to taste** to the bowl. Toss thoroughly until all ingredients are evenly coated and the cabbage is glistening.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Can be prepared up to 4 hours in advance and stored in the refrigerator. Toss again before serving to redistribute the dressing.
