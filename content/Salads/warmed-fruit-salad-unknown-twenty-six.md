# Warmed Fruit Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *Discover a comforting and unique Warmed Fruit Salad layered with mixed fruits, cinnamon, vanilla wafers, and a splash of sweet wine or brandy. This baked fruit dish offers a delightful blend of warm spices and tender fruits.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 1 hour 30 mins | None | 6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Casserole dish
* Can opener
* Measuring spoons

---
## Ingredients

### Fruit and Base
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 can | [[Canned cling peaches]] | Drained well |
| 1 can | [[Pears]] | Drained well |
| 1 can | [[Apricots]] | Drained well |
| 1 can | [[Chunk pineapple]] | Drained well |
| As needed | [[Butter]] | For greasing casserole |

### Topping and Sauce
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| As needed | [[Cinnamon]] | For sprinkling |
| 1 package | [[Vanilla wafers]] | Crumbled |
| 2 jiggers | [[Sweet wine or brandy]] | Cordial can be used |
| 2 teaspoons | [[Vanilla]] | Mixed with wine or brandy |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Assembly
1. Butter a **casserole** dish.
2. Arrange the **canned cling peaches**, **pears**, **apricots**, and **chunk pineapple** in layers in the casserole.
3. Between each layer, sprinkle with **cinnamon** and **crumbled vanilla wafers**, and ensure you also top the final dish with extra crumbs.

### Step 2: Baking
4. Mix the **2 jiggers sweet wine or brandy** (or cordial) with the **2 teaspoons vanilla**.
5. Pour the liquid mixture over all the ingredients in the casserole.
6. Bake uncovered for about **1 1/2 hours** in a **325° oven** until warm, bubbling, and fragrant.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the casserole ahead of time, cover, and refrigerate until ready to bake; add the liquid just before placing in the oven.
