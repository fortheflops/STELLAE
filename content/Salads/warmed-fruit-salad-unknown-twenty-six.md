# Warmed Fruit Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Sherry | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A delightful baked warm fruit salad featuring mixed canned fruits layered with cinnamon and crushed vanilla wafers. Finished with a sweet wine or brandy and vanilla drizzle, then baked until bubbling.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 1.5 hours | None | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Casserole dish
* Can opener
* Measuring spoons

---
## Ingredients

### Fruit Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 can | [[Cling Peaches]] | Canned, drained well |
| 1 can | [[Pears]] | Canned, drained well |
| 1 can | [[Apricots]] | Canned, drained well |
| 1 can | [[Chunk Pineapple]] | Canned, drained well |

### Assembly & Topping
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 box | [[Vanilla Wafers]] | Crumbled |
| to taste | [[Cinnamon]] | Ground |
| 2 jiggers | [[Sweet Wine]] | Or brandy/cordial |
| 2 tbsp | [[Vanilla]] | Liquid |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Layering the Casserole
1. Butter a **casserole** dish well.
2. Drain well the **cling peaches**, **pears**, **apricots**, and **chunk pineapple**.
3. Arrange the **fruit** in layers in the casserole.
4. Between each layer, sprinkle with **cinnamon** and crumbled **vanilla wafers**.
5. Top the entire dish with additional crumbled **vanilla wafers**.

### Step 2: Sauce and Baking
6. In a small container, mix together **2 jiggers of sweet wine** (or brandy/cordial) and **2 tablespoons of vanilla**.
7. Pour the liquid mixture evenly over all the ingredients in the casserole.
8. Bake uncovered at 325°F for about **1 1/2 hours** until hot and bubbling throughout.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the fruit and wafer layers in the casserole dish a few hours ahead, but pour the wine and vanilla mixture right before baking.
