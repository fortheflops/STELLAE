# Cranberry Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A classic, refreshing gelatin-based cranberry salad featuring bright citrus and pineapple notes. Perfect for holiday gatherings or as a sweet side dish.*

## Ingredients

### Salad Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 package | [[Raspberry Jello]] | Standard size |
| 1 cup | [[Hot Water]] | Boiling |
| 1/2 cup | [[Cold Water]] | Chilled |
| 1 small | [[Orange]] | Peeled and diced |
| 1/2 cup | [[Pineapple]] | Crushed and drained |
| 1 can | [[Ocean Spray Cranberry Sauce]] | Whole berry or jellied |
| 1/4 cup | [[Nuts]] | Chopped |

---

## Instructions

### Preparation
1. Dissolve **1 package raspberry jello** in **1 cup hot water**.
2. Stir in **1/2 cup cold water** and chill until partially set.
3. Fold in **1 small orange** (peeled and diced), **1/2 cup crushed pineapple** (drained), **1 can Ocean Spray cranberry sauce**, and **1/4 cup chopped nuts**.
4. Pour into a mold or serving dish and chill until firm.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This salad must be made at least 4 hours in advance to allow the gelatin to set firmly. It can be stored in the refrigerator for up to 3 days.
