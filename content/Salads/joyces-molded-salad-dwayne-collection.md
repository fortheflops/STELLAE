# Joyce's Molded Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Joyce | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A classic mid-century molded gelatin salad featuring vibrant cherry gelatin, frozen raspberries, whole cranberry sauce, crushed pineapple, and rich sour cream. Perfect as a festive side dish or a nostalgic dessert for holiday gatherings.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 0 mins | 4 hours | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Gelatin mold or 9x13 baking dish
* Spatula

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 package (3 oz) | [[Cherry Gelatin]] | such as Jell-O |
| 1 package | [[Raspberries]] | frozen |
| 1 can (14 oz) | [[Cranberry Sauce]] | whole berry |
| 1 can (20 oz) | [[Pineapple]] | drained |
| 1 container (8 oz) | [[Sour Cream]] | or 1 cup |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Dissolve and Thicken
1. Dissolve **1 package cherry gelatin** in hot water according to the package directions.
2. Stir in **1 package frozen raspberries** and **1 can whole cranberry sauce** into the hot gelatin mixture.
3. Place the mixture in the refrigerator to cool and thicken until it reaches a semi-set, syrupy consistency.

### Step 2: Fold and Chill
4. Once thickened, fold in **1 can drained pineapple** and **1 container sour cream** until thoroughly combined and streak-free.
5. Pour the mixture into a gelatin mold or serving dish.
6. Refrigerate for at least **4 hours** or until completely set, firm, and ready to unmold.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This salad can be prepared up to 2 days in advance. Keep refrigerated in the mold until ready to unmold and serve.
