# Shrimp and Black Bean Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** Georgia Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A refreshing and vibrant shrimp and black bean salad tossed with crisp vegetables, picante sauce, and a zesty lime dressing. Perfect for warm weather gatherings and easily made ahead.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 0 mins | 2 hours | 6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Serving platter

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 lb. | [[Shrimp]] | Cooked |
| 1 lb. | [[Black beans]] | Cooked, rinsed & drained |
| 1 sm. | [[Green pepper]] | In short, thin strips |
| 1/2 C. | [[Celery]] | Thinly sliced |
| 1/3 C. | [[Red onion]] | Thinly sliced small rings |
| 2/3 C. | [[Picante sauce]] | None |
| 2 T. | [[Cilantro]] | Chopped |
| 2 T. | [[Oil]] | None |
| 2 T. | [[Honey]] | None |
| 1/2 t. | [[Salt]] | None |
| 1 t. | [[Lime peel]] | None |
| 2 T. | [[Lime juice]] | None |
| As needed | [[Lettuce leaves]] | None |
| 1 cup | [[Cherry tomatoes]] | Halves |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing and Chilling
1. Combine **1 lb. cooked shrimp**, **1 lb. cooked black beans**, **1 sm. green pepper**, **1/2 C. thinly sliced celery**, and **1/3 C. thinly sliced small red onion rings** in a bowl.
2. Combine remaining ingredients except **1 cup cherry tomato halves** and **lettuce leaves**. Mix well.
3. Pour over shrimp mixture. Toss lightly to coat. Chill at least 2 up to 24 hrs.

### Step 2: Serving
4. Spoon salad on **lettuce leaves**-lined serving platter.
5. Garnish with **1 cup cherry tomato halves**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Chill the salad for at least 2 hours and up to 24 hours before serving to let the flavors meld.
