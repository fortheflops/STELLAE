## Instructions

### Step 1: Combine and Marinate
1. In a large mixing bowl, thoroughly mix together the **4 pkg. frozen mixed vegetables (cooked & drained)**, **1 C. small green onions**, and **1 C. stuffed sliced olives**.
2. In a separate small bowl, whisk together the **6 T. wine vinegar**, **2/3 C. oil**, **2 t. salt**, and **1/4 t. pepper**, then pour over the vegetable mixture.
3. Allow the salad to marinate in the refrigerator for **1 hour** before serving so the flavors fully develop until well-blended and chilled.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the marinated salad sealed in the refrigerator for up to 2 days before serving for the best flavor infusion.
