## Instructions

### Step 1: Making the Dressing
1. Combine **1 tsp paprika**, **1 tsp dry mustard**, **1/2 tsp salt**, **1/2 cup sugar**, **1 cup salad oil**, and **1/3 cup vinegar** in a blender or shaker.
2. Mix well until thoroughly combined, then stir in **1 tbsp poppy seeds**.

### Step 2: Assembling the Salad
3. In a large bowl, toss together **1 lb fresh spinach**, **1 pt strawberries**, **3 green onions**, and **1/2 cup toasted walnuts**.
4. Pour the dressing over the berries and greens, toss gently until well coated, and serve immediately.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the dressing in a sealed jar in the refrigerator for up to 3 days, but assemble the salad just before serving to keep the spinach crisp.
