# Tammy's Spaghetti Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** Georgia Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Tammy | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *Tammy's Spaghetti Salad is a refreshing, chilled pasta dish loaded with crisp vegetables and zesty Italian dressing. It is the ultimate make-ahead side dish for summer barbecues and family gatherings.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 10 mins | 8 hours | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large pot
* Colander
* Large mixing bowl

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 16 oz. | [[Spaghetti]] | Cooked according to package directions |
| 4 cups | [[Finely chopped veggies including cucumbers, radishes, carrots, celery, onion, black olives, tomato]] | Finely chopped |
| 10 oz. | [[X-tra Touch Italian dressing]] | Bottled |
| 4 tbsp | [[Schilling salad supreme seasoning]] | Seasoning blend |

---

## Instructions

### Preparation
1. Cook **16 oz. spaghetti** according to package directions until tender.
2. Drain, rinse with cold water, and cool completely.

### Assembly and Chilling
3. Add **4 cups finely chopped veggies including cucumbers, radishes, carrots, celery, onion, black olives, tomato**, **10 oz. X-tra Touch Italian dressing**, and **4 tbsp Schilling salad supreme seasoning** to the cooled pasta.
4. Mix well until all ingredients are evenly coated.
5. Refrigerate overnight before serving until well chilled and flavors are fully developed.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Refrigerate overnight before serving so the pasta absorbs the dressing and flavors meld completely.
