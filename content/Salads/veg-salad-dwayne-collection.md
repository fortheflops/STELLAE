## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Dressing
1. In a saucepan, combine the dressing ingredients: **3/4 cup cider vinegar**, **1/4 cup water**, **2 tbsp sugar**, **1 1/2 tsp celery salt**, **1 1/2 tsp mustard seed**, **1/4 tsp salt**, **1/8 to 1/4 tsp cayenne pepper**, and **1/8 tsp black pepper**.
2. Bring the mixture to a rolling boil over medium heat, and let it boil for exactly **1 minute** to dissolve the sugar and marry the spices.
3. Remove the saucepan from the heat and let the dressing cool completely to room temperature.

### Step 2: Combine and Marinate
4. In a large bowl, combine the prepared vegetables: **6 medium tomatoes**, **1 medium bell pepper**, **1 medium red onion**, and **1 medium cucumber**.
5. Once the dressing has cooled, pour it over the vegetables and toss thoroughly to ensure everything is evenly coated.
6. Cover the bowl tightly and refrigerate for at least **8 hours** or overnight to allow the vegetables to absorb the tangy marinade before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This salad is best prepared 8 to 24 hours in advance to allow the vegetables to fully marinate in the dressing.
