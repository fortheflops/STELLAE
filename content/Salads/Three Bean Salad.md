# Three Bean Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A classic, tangy three bean salad combining green beans, yellow wax beans, and kidney beans in a sweet and sour marinade. Chill for several hours to let the flavors meld into a crisp and refreshing dish.*

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | None | 4 hours | 6–8 servings |

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 can | [[French Cut Green Beans]] | Drained |
| 1 can | [[Cut Wax Yellow Beans]] | Drained |
| 1 can | [[Kidney Beans]] | Drained and rinsed |
| 1 small | [[Onion]] | Chopped |
| 1 small | [[Green Pepper]] | Chopped |

### Dressing
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 cup | [[Vinegar]] | Standard or cider vinegar |
| 3/4 cup | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 1/2 cup | [[Salad Oil]] | Vegetable or canola oil |
| 1/4 teaspoon | [[Salt]] | To taste |
| 1/4 teaspoon | [[Pepper]] | Black pepper |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Combine the Beans
1. Place **1 can drained french cut green beans**, **1 can drained cut wax yellow beans**, and **1 can drained and rinsed kidney beans** into a large mixing bowl.

### Step 2: Prepare the Dressing
2. In a separate bowl, blend **1 small chopped onion**, **1 small chopped green pepper**, **1/2 cup vinegar**, **3/4 cup sugar**, **1/2 cup salad oil**, **1/4 teaspoon salt**, and **1/4 teaspoon pepper** thoroughly until the sugar dissolves.

### Step 3: Marinate and Serve
3. Pour the blended dressing mixture evenly over the beans.
4. Cover the bowl and refrigerate for several hours until well chilled and marinated before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This salad tastes even better when prepared a day in advance; store covered in the refrigerator for up to 4 days.
