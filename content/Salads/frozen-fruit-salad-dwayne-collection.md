# Frozen Fruit Salad

> *A classic mid-century frozen salad featuring a rich cream cheese and sour cream base folded with sweet cherries, apricots, crushed pineapple, and miniature marshmallows. Perfect as a sweet side dish or a refreshing dessert.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Cream Base
1. Allow the **1 pkg (8 oz) cream cheese** to stand at room temperature until soft, then beat in a mixing bowl until fluffy.
2. Mix the **1/4 cup sugar** and **1/4 tsp salt** together, then add them to the beaten **cream cheese** along with **1 cup sour cream**, blending until smooth.

### Step 2: Fold in Fruits and Freeze
3. Gently fold the drained **1 can (16 oz) dark sweet cherries**, **1 can (16 oz) sliced apricots**, **1 large can crushed pineapple**, and **2 cups miniature marshmallows** into the cream cheese mixture.
4. If desired, fold in chopped **nuts** and a **few drops red food coloring** to tint the mixture a festive pink.
5. Pour the mixture into a **loaf pan** and freeze until completely firm, at least 4 hours.

### Step 3: Slice and Serve
6. Remove from the freezer and let the salad stand out at room temperature for a short while to soften slightly before slicing into clean portions.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This salad can be frozen several days in advance. Once fully frozen, wrap the loaf pan tightly in plastic wrap and aluminum foil to prevent freezer burn.
