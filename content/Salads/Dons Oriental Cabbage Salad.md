## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Dressing
1. The night before, combine the **3/4 cup olive oil** (or vegetable oil), **1 tablespoon sesame oil**, **1 package seasoning packet from ramen soup mix**, **1/3 cup sugar**, and **1/3 cup vinegar** in a jar or bowl. Stir occasionally until the sugar dissolves.

### Step 2: Assemble and Serve
2. Just before serving, in a large salad bowl, combine the **1 pound cole slaw mix**, **1 bunch green onions**, **1 package sliced almonds**, and **1/2 cup sunflower nuts**.
3. Break up the block of uncooked Ramen noodles and add them to the salad.
4. Pour the prepared dressing over the salad and toss to combine until evenly coated and crunchy.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This salad is best when the dressing is made the night before and the salad is tossed just before serving to maintain maximum crunch.
