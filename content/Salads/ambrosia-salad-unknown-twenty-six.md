# Ambrosia Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A classic sweet fruit salad combining mandarin oranges, pineapple tidbits, coconut, and miniature marshmallows in a creamy cottage cheese dressing. This refreshing side dish is easy to whip up and chilled to perfection.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | None | 30 mins | 4 to 6 servings |

* Mixing bowl
* Can opener
* Measuring cups
* Measuring spoons

## Ingredients

### Salad Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 small can | [[Mandarin oranges]] | Drained |
| 1 small can | [[Pineapple tidbits]] | Drained |
| 1 cup | [[Flaked coconut]] | None |
| 1 cup | [[Miniature marshmallows]] | None |
| 1 1/3 cups | [[Cottage cheese]] | None |
| Juice of 1/2 | [[Lemon]] | Freshly squeezed |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Salt]] | None |
| 1 tbsp | [[Mayonnaise]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Combining Fruit
1. Drain liquid completely from **1 small can mandarin oranges** and **1 small can pineapple tidbits**.
2. Mix together the **1 small can mandarin oranges**, **1 small can pineapple tidbits**, **1 cup flaked coconut**, and **1 cup miniature marshmallows** in a large mixing bowl.

### Step 2: Dressing and Chilling
3. Add the **juice of 1/2 lemon**, **1/4 tsp salt**, and **1 tbsp mayonnaise** to the **1 1/3 cups cottage cheese** and mix well.
4. Fold the cottage cheese mixture gently into the fruit mixture until thoroughly combined.
5. Chill in the refrigerator until cold and set before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Prepare the salad a few hours in advance and keep covered in the refrigerator until ready to serve for the best flavor.
