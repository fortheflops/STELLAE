# Rice Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Eighteen | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A sweet and retro fruit and rice salad featuring cherry jello, crushed pineapple, and a fluffy Dream Whip topping. Perfect for potlucks and gatherings.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Cooking the Rice
1. Cook in a heavy pan or iron skillet: **3 cups cold water**, **1/4 cup sugar**, **1/2 cup rice**, and **1/2 tsp. salt**.
2. Cook over high heat until all ingredients steam, then lower heat and cook **20 to 35 minutes** until tender.

### Step 2: Mixing and Chilling
3. Remove from heat, add **1/2 cup sugar**, and **1 pkg. red jello**, stirring well to dissolve.
4. Add **1 small can crushed pineapple** and mix well.
5. Put in refrigerator to chill until set.

### Step 3: Topping and Serving
6. Mix **1 package Dream Whip** according to package instructions and fold into the chilled mixture.
7. Serve cold.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Keep refrigerated until ready to serve; fold in the Dream Whip just before serving for best texture.
