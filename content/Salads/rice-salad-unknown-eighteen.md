# Rice Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Eighteen | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A delightful chilled rice salad featuring tender rice, sweet peas, crunchy celery, and tangy pickle relish. Perfectly seasoned and tossed with mellow cheddar cheese for a refreshing side dish.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 20 mins | 30 mins | 4 servings |

* Measuring spoons

## Ingredients

### Salad Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 cups | [[Rice]] | Cooked |
| 1 tsp | [[Salt]] | None |
| 1 cup | [[Peas]] | Cooked and drained |
| 1/3 cup | [[Celery]] | Chopped |
| 1 | [[Pimento]] | Or red pepper, diced |
| 4 tbsp | [[Pickle Relish]] | None |
| 3 tbsp | [[Cheddar Cheese]] | Mellow, grated or crumbled |
| As desired | [[Sugar]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing
1. In a large mixing bowl, combine the **2 cups cooked rice** and **1 tsp salt**.
2. Gently fold in the **1 cup cooked & drained peas**, **1/3 cup chopped celery**, and **1 diced pimento or red pepper** until evenly distributed.
3. Stir in the **4 tbsp pickle relish** and **3 tbsp mellow cheddar cheese**.
4. Add **sugar as desired** to balance the flavors, mixing thoroughly until well combined.

### Step 2: Chilling
5. Cover the bowl and chill in the refrigerator for at least 30 minutes to let the flavors meld before serving cold.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Prepare up to a day in advance and store tightly covered in the refrigerator until ready to serve.
