## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 2 cups                     | Cooked [[Bowtie Pasta]] or [[Rotini Pasta]], drained |
| 1 cup                      | [[Cherry Tomatoes]], quartered            |
| 1 package                  | Crumbled [[Feta & Basil Tomato]]          |
| 1/2 cup                    | [[Caesar Salad Dressing]] (or mix with Italian) |
| 1/2 teaspoon               | [[Basil]]                                 |
| 1 small can                | Sliced [[Olives]]                         |
| 1/4 cup                    | Chopped [[Red Onion]]                     |
| As Needed                  | Marinated, grilled [[Chicken Strips]], sliced into 1/4" strips |
| To Taste                   | [[Parmesan Cheese]]                       |
| To Taste                   | [[Salt]] and [[Pepper]]                   |
___
## Instructions
1.  Combine all ingredients in a large bowl and mix well.
2.  Serve warm or chilled.
___

>[!Note]
>The recipe card notes that if the salad is served chilled, the flavors will have more time to blend.
