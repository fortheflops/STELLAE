## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 box                      | [[Rotini]] (or other) pasta, cooked       |
| 8-16 ounces                | Block [[Cheddar Cheese]], shredded        |
| 2 large (or 10 baby)       | [[Carrots]], shredded                     |
| 1 can                      | Black [[Olives]], sliced                  |
| 4-5                        | [[Green Onions]], chopped                 |
| 1/2                        | English [[Cucumber]], shredded (optional) |
| 4-5 leaves                 | Fresh [[Basil]], chopped (optional)       |
| 1/2 - 3/4 bottle           | [[Italian Dressing]]                      |
| 1 teaspoon                 | [[Italian Seasoning]]                     |
| To Taste                   | [[Salt]] and [[Pepper]]                   |
___
## Instructions
1.  Combine all ingredients in a large bowl.
2.  Mix well.
___

>[!Note]
>Let refrigerate for half a day or longer for optimal flavor.
