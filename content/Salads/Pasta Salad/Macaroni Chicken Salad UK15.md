## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 (7 1/4 ounce) package    | [[Macaroni & Cheese Dinner]]              |
| 1 cup                      | Diced, cooked [[Chicken]]                 |
| 3                          | Hard-cooked [[Eggs]], chopped             |
| 1/2 cup                    | Chopped [[Green Pepper]]                  |
| 1/2 cup                    | Cooked [[Green Peas]]                     |
| 1/4 cup                    | Chopped [[Celery]]                        |
| 1/4 cup                    | Sliced [[Radishes]]                       |
| 3 tablespoons              | Chopped [[Onion]]                         |
| 1/3 cup                    | [[Salad Dressing]] or [[Mayonnaise]]      |
___
## Instructions
1.  Cook the macaroni and cheese dinner according to the package directions.
2.  In a large bowl, combine the prepared macaroni and cheese with all the other ingredients, except for the salad dressing.
3.  Stir in the salad dressing last and toss gently to combine.
4.  Chill thoroughly before serving.
___
