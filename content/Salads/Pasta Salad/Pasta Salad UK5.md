## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 (12-13 ounce) box        | [[Rotini]] or other pasta, cooked & cooled|
| 1/2 pound                  | [[Summer Sausage]], chopped               |
| 8 ounces                   | [[Cheddar Cheese]], shredded              |
| 2 large or 10 baby         | [[Carrots]], shredded                     |
| 1 can                      | Sliced [[Olives]]                         |
| 3/4 bottle                 | Zesty [[Italian Dressing]]                |
| To Taste                   | [[Salt]] and [[Pepper]]                   |
| **Optional:** |                                           |
| 1/2 cup                    | Shredded [[Cucumber]]                     |
| 4-5                        | Chopped [[Green Onions]]                  |
| 4-5 leaves                 | Fresh [[Basil]], chiffonaded              |

___
## Instructions
1.  Combine all ingredients in a large bowl.
2.  Mix well.
___

>[!Note]
>Let the salad refrigerate for at least half a day or longer to allow the flavors to meld.
