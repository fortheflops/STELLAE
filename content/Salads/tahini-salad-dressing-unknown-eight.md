# Tahini Salad Dressing

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Eight | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A creamy and flavorful tahini salad dressing featuring a citrusy twist of orange juice and soy sauce. Perfect for drizzling over your favorite greens.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5 mins | None | None | 4 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Blender
* Measuring cups
* Measuring spoons

---
## Ingredients

### Dressing Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/4 C. | [[Olive oil]] |  |
| 1/4 C. | [[Tahini]] |  |
| 1/4 C. | [[Orange juice]] |  |
| 2 tablespoons | [[Water]] |  |
| 1 tablespoon | [[Soy sauce]] |  |
| 1 tablespoon | [[Green onion]] | minced, optional |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Blending
1. Combine **1/4 C. olive oil**, **1/4 C. tahini**, **1/4 C. orange juice**, **2 tablespoons water**, and **1 tablespoon soy sauce** in a blender. Blend all ingredients together until smooth and emulsified.

### Step 2: Finishing
2. Stir in **1 tablespoon minced gr. onion** if desired, or sprinkle it over the dressing before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 5 days. Shake or whisk well before serving.
