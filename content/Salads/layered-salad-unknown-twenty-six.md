# Layered Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Marssile | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A delightful layered salad featuring crisp lettuce, cauliflower, and bacon, topped with a creamy mayonnaise dressing and parmesan cheese. Perfect for gatherings and best prepared the night before.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 10 mins | 8 hours | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Salad bowl
* Skillet
* Knife
* Cutting board

---
## Ingredients

### Salad Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 head | [[Lettuce]] | Cut up |
| 1 head | [[Cauliflower]] | Chawed/cut up |
| 12 slices | [[Bacon]] | Fried and crumbled |
| 1 bunch | [[Green Onions]] | Chopped |

### Dressing and Topping
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 cup | [[Hellmans Mayonnaise]] | None |
| 2 tbsp | [[Sugar]] | None |
| 1 cup | [[Parmesan Cheese]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Assembly and Refrigeration
1. Layer the **1 head lettuce**, **1 head cauliflower**, **12 slices bacon**, and **1 bunch green onions** in a large salad bowl.
2. In a separate small bowl, mix together the **1 cup hellmans mayonnaise** and **2 tbsp sugar**.
3. Dot the top of the ingredients with dressing.
4. Sprinkle with **1 cup parmesan cheese**.
5. Refrigerate overnight until chilled and set, then toss before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Refrigerate overnight before serving for the best flavor and texture.
