# Mexican Fiesta Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Jane Duncan | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A vibrant and crunchy Mexican Fiesta Salad featuring tuna, Fritos, and fresh vegetables tossed in a zesty avocado dressing. This easy-to-make dish is perfect for gathering crowds with its unique blend of textures and flavors.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | None | None | 6 servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Fork or whisk

## Ingredients

### Salad
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 small pkgs. | [[Fritos]] | Crushed or whole |
| 1/2 head | [[Lettuce]] | Chopped |
| 1 sm. can | [[Black Olives]] | Sliced |
| 2 cans | [[Tuna]] | Drained |
| 2 sm. | [[Tomatoes]] | Chopped |
| 1/4 cup | [[Green Onions]] | Chopped |
| 1 cup | [[Cheddar Cheese]] | Shredded |

### Dressing
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 | [[Avocado]] | Ripe |
| 1 t. | [[Lemon Juice]] | Fresh |
| 1/2 cup | [[Sour Cream]] | Standard |
| 1/3 cup | [[Salad Oil]] | Vegetable or canola |
| 1 clove | [[Garlic]] | Minced |
| 1/2 t. | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 1/2 t. | [[Chili Powder]] | Standard |
| dash | [[Tabasco]] | Hot sauce |
| to taste | [[Salt]] | Optional |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Dressing
1. Mash **1/2 avocado** in a bowl, add **1 t. lemon juice**, **1/2 cup sour cream**, **1/3 cup salad oil**, **1 clove garlic**, **1/2 t. sugar**, **1/2 t. chili powder**, **dash tabasco**, and **salt** to taste; beat well until smooth and creamy.

### Step 2: Salad Assembly
2. Combine **2 small pkgs. Fritos**, **1/2 head lettuce**, **1 sm. can black olives**, **2 drained cans tuna**, **2 sm. tomatoes**, **1/4 cup chopped green onions**, and **1 cup shredded cheddar cheese** in a large bowl and toss well.
3. Pour the prepared dressing over the salad just before serving and toss until thoroughly coated and crisp.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Keep the dressing stored in an airtight container and the salad ingredients separate until ready to serve to prevent the Fritos from getting soggy.
