# Spinach Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Joy | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A vibrant layered spinach salad featuring fresh bell peppers, hard-boiled eggs, and a warm garlic-sesame dressing. This vintage recipe from the Gail Collection delivers a delightful contrast of textures and flavors.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 5 mins | None | 4–6 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large bowl or platter
* Small skillet

---
## Ingredients

### For the Salad
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 ½ Bunches | [[Spinach]] | Fresh |
| 1 Bunch | [[Onions]] | Green, chopped |
| 4 | [[Eggs]] | Hard-boiled, sliced |
| 2 Tablespoons | [[Sesame Seeds]] | Unroasted |
| 1 | [[Pepper]] | Large red or green bell pepper, sliced |

### For the Dressing
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| ¼ Cup | [[Olive Oil]] | Standard |
| 3 to 4 Cloves | [[Garlic]] | Minced |
| 1 Tablespoon | [[Sesame Seeds]] | For dressing |
| 3 Tablespoons | [[Lemon Juice]] | Freshly squeezed |
| 3 Tablespoons | [[Soy Sauce]] | Or tamari |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Salad
1. In a large bowl or platter, put the ingredients in layers. Start with a bed of **1 ½ bunches fresh spinach**, followed by layers of the **1 sliced red or green bell pepper** and **1 bunch chopped green onions**.
2. Arrange the **4 sliced hard-boiled eggs** as the top layer over the other salad ingredients.
3. Sprinkle with **2 tablespoons sesame seeds**. Set aside.

### Step 2: Prepare the Dressing
4. In a small skillet, heat **¼ cup olive oil** over medium-high heat.
5. Add **3 to 4 cloves minced garlic** and **1 tablespoon sesame seeds** to the hot oil. Sauté briefly until fragrant.
6. Turn off the heat but leave the pan on the burner.
7. Add **3 tablespoons lemon juice** and **3 tablespoons soy sauce or tamari** to the pan. Stir to combine.

### Step 3: Combine and Serve
8. Immediately pour the hot dressing over the layered salad. Do not toss it. Serve at once.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Prep the salad layers ahead of time, but do not add the warm dressing until right before serving to maintain texture.
