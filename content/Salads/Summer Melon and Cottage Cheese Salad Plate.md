# Summer Melon and Cottage Cheese Salad Plate

> 📜 **Collection:** Louise Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A refreshing and classic summer fruit plate featuring slices of cantaloupe and honeydew melon served on a bed of lettuce with a scoop of cottage cheese and fresh strawberries. This light dish is perfect for a quick lunch or a cooling side.*

* Serving plate
* Knife

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 slice | [[Cantaloupe]] | cut along the rind |
| 1 slice | [[Honeydew Melon]] | cut along the rind |
| As needed | [[Lettuce]] | leaves for serving |
| 1 scoop | [[Cottage Cheese]] | chilled |
| As needed | [[Strawberries]] | sliced for garnish |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Assembly
1. Arrange a bed of **lettuce** on an individual serving plate.
2. Place the **1 slice cantaloupe** and **1 slice honeydew melon** on the **lettuce**, ensuring you have cut along the rind for easy eating.
3. Add **1 scoop cottage cheese** to the center of the plate.
4. Garnish with fresh **sliced strawberries** and serve immediately while crisp and cool.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This dish is best assembled fresh; however, the fruit can be pre-sliced and stored in an airtight container for up to 2 hours before serving.
