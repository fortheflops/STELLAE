# Classic Savory Molded Chicken Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** General Archive | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A classic molded salad recipe perfect for a luncheon, featuring diced cooked chicken, celery, olives, and water chestnuts suspended in a savory gelatin and mayonnaise base.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 25 mins | 5 mins | 4 hours | 6 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Small bowl
* Larger bowl
* Saucepan for hot water
* Oiled mold

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |

| 2 T. | Unflavored [[Gelatin]] | unflavored |
| 1/2 C. | Cold [[Water]] | cold |
| 3/4 C. | [[Mayonnaise]] | or salad dressing |
| To taste | [[Salt]], [[Pepper]], [[Paprika]], and [[Vinegar]] | to taste |
| 1 to 1 1/2 Cups | Diced Cooked [[Chicken]] | diced, cooked |
| 1 Cup | Finely Chopped [[Celery]] | finely chopped |
| 1/2 C. | Chopped [[Olives]] | chopped |
| 1/2 C. | Cooked, Diced [[Chestnuts]] | cooked, diced |
| 3 T. | Chopped [[Pimiento]] | chopped |
| Several sprigs | Chopped [[Parsley]] | chopped |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Gelatin Base
1. In a small bowl, soak the **2 T. unflavored gelatin** in **1/2 C. cold water**.
2. Dissolve the gelatin by placing the small bowl over hot water and stirring until completely dissolved.
3. Cool the gelatin slightly, then fold in the **3/4 C. mayonnaise**, **Salt**, **Pepper**, **Paprika**, and **Vinegar** to taste.

### Step 2: Combine and Mold
4. In a larger bowl, combine the **1 to 1 1/2 Cups diced cooked chicken**, **1 Cup finely chopped celery**, **1/2 C. chopped olives**, **1/2 C. cooked, diced chestnuts**, **3 T. chopped pimiento**, and **several sprigs chopped parsley**.
5. Pour the gelatin-mayonnaise mixture over the chicken mixture and mix well until evenly incorporated.
6. Turn the mixture into an oiled mold and refrigerate for at least 4 hours, or until firmly congealed.

### Step 3: Unmold and Garnish
7. Unmold the salad on a bed of fresh lettuce or other greens.
8. Garnish with carrot curls, radish roses, or other colorful vegetables until vibrant and presentable.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the molded salad covered in the refrigerator for up to 24 hours before serving for best results.
