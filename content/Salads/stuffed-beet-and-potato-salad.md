# Stuffed Beet and Potato Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A unique, classic salad featuring hollowed-out beets filled with a seasoned potato and nut mixture. This elegant dish is finished with a tangy boiled dressing for a perfect balance of flavors.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 45 mins | 45 mins | 1 hour | 4 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Pot
* Knife
* Mixing bowl

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 4-6 large | [[Beets]] | Cooked and peeled |
| As needed | [[Vinegar]] | For marinating |
| 2 cups | [[Potatoes]] | Cold, chopped |
| 1/2 cup | [[Nut meats]] | Chopped |
| 1/2 tbsp | [[Onion]] | Grated |
| To taste | [[Boiled dressing]] | Prepared |

---

## Instructions

### Preparation
1. Cook **large beets** until tender, drop into cold water, and peel.
2. Hollow out each **beet** to create a cup-like shape.
3. Fill the hollowed **beets** with **vinegar** and let stand in the refrigerator to marinate.
4. In a bowl, combine **cold potatoes**, **nut meats**, and **grated onion**; season to taste.
5. Moisten the mixture with **boiled dressing**.
6. Empty the **vinegar** from the **beets** and fill them with the **potato mixture**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The beets can be hollowed and marinated in vinegar up to 24 hours in advance. Keep the potato filling chilled separately and assemble just before serving.
