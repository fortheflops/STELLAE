# Artichoke White Bean Herb Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Eight | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A refreshing Sephardic-style salad featuring tender white beans, artichoke hearts, and crisp red peppers tossed in a vibrant herb vinaigrette. Perfect as a healthy make-ahead side dish or light lunch.*

## Ingredients

### Salad Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3 cups | [[White Beans]] | cooked |
| 8 oz | [[Artichoke Hearts]] | water-packed, drained, and sliced in quarters |
| 2/3 cup | [[Red Bell Pepper]] | diced sweet |
| 1/3 cup | [[Kalamata Olives]] | pitted and sliced |
| 1/4 cup | [[Red Onion]] | minced |
| 1/4 cup | [[Parsley]] | chopped |
| 2 tsp | [[Mint]] | minced fresh |
| 1 1/2 tsp | [[Basil]] | minced fresh |

### Dressing
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/3 cup | [[Olive Oil]] | None |
| 1/4 cup | [[Red Wine Vinegar]] | None |
| To taste | [[Salt]] | None |
| To taste | [[Black Pepper]] | freshly ground |

---

## Instructions

### Assembly and Marination
1. In a large bowl, combine the **3 cups cooked white beans**, **8 oz water-packed artichoke**, **2/3 cup diced sweet red pepper**, **1/3 cup pitted sliced Kalamata olives**, **1/4 cup minced red onion**, **1/4 cup chopped parsley**, **2 tsp minced fresh mint**, and **1 1/2 tsp minced fresh basil**.
2. In a small jar, combine the **1/3 cup olive oil**, **1/4 cup red wine vinegar**, and **salt & fresh ground pepper to taste**. Close the lid tightly and shake well until emulsified.
3. Pour the dressing over the vegetables and toss gently to coat all ingredients evenly.
4. Refrigerate for several hours to allow the flavors to meld and develop fully.
5. Toss again just before serving chilled.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This salad is ideal for making ahead as the flavors deepen over time. Store covered in the refrigerator for up to 3 days, tossing well before serving.
