# Salad Dressing

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Eighteen | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A classic homemade salad dressing featuring a sweet and tangy base thickened on the stove. Excellent when served over potato or fruit salads.*

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

## Ingredients

### Dressing Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 cup | [[Vinegar]] | None |
| 3/4 cup | [[Sugar]] | None |
| 1 | [[Egg]] | None |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Mustard]] | None |
| As needed | [[Cream]] | Sweet or sour |

---

## Instructions

### Cooking and Mixing
1. Mix **1/2 cup vinegar**, **3/4 cup sugar**, **1 egg**, and **1/4 tsp mustard** in a saucepan.
2. Heat and boil, stirring constantly until thick.
3. Remove from heat and add **cream** to taste.
