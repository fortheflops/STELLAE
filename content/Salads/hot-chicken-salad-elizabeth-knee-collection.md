# Hot Chicken Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** Elizabeth Knee Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A comforting baked hot chicken salad featuring tender chicken, crunchy almonds, celery, and topped with melted Swiss cheese and crushed potato chips. Perfect for a warm, savory lunch or dinner gathering.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 25 mins | None | 6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Baking dish

---
## Ingredients

### Salad Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3 cups | [[Cooked Chicken]] | Diced |
| 1 1/2 cups | [[Celery]] | Diced |
| 1/2 cup | [[Almonds]] | Chopped |
| 1 1/2 tbsp | [[Minced Onion]] | None |
| 1 1/2 tbsp | [[Lemon Juice]] | None |
| 1/8 tsp | [[Pepper]] | None |
| 1 cup | [[Salad Dressing]] | Mayonnaise style |
| 1 1/2 cups | [[Swiss Cheese]] | Shredded |
| 1 1/2 cups | [[Potato Chips]] | Crushed |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing and Baking
1. Preheat oven to 375 degrees F.
2. In a large bowl, combine the **3 cups cooked chicken**, **1 1/2 cups celery**, **1/2 cup almonds**, **1 1/2 tbsp minced onion**, **1 1/2 tbsp lemon juice**, **1/8 tsp pepper**, and **1 cup salad dressing**.
3. Transfer the mixture to a baking dish and top with **1 1/2 cups Swiss cheese** and **1 1/2 cups potato chips**.
4. Bake at 375 degrees F for **25 minutes** until bubbly and lightly browned.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the salad in the baking dish ahead of time, cover, and refrigerate. Add the potato chips right before baking.
