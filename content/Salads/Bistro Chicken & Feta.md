# Bistro Chicken & Feta

> 📜 **Collection:** UK5 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Lisa McFarland | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *Discover a delightful Bistro Chicken & Feta salad featuring tender pasta, grilled chicken, and fresh tomatoes. This versatile dish can be served warm or chilled for maximum flavor blending.*

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 10 mins | None | 4 Servings |

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 cups | [[Bowtie Pasta]] | Cooked and drained, or use [[Rotini Pasta]] |
| 1 cup | [[Cherry Tomatoes]] | Quartered |
| 1 package | [[Feta & Basil Tomato]] | Crumbled |
| 1/2 cup | [[Caesar Salad Dressing]] | Can mix with Italian dressing |
| 1/2 teaspoon | [[Basil]] | Dry or fresh |
| 1 small can | [[Olives]] | Sliced |
| 1/4 cup | [[Red Onion]] | Chopped |
| As Needed | [[Chicken Strips]] | Marinated, grilled, and sliced into 1/4" strips |
| To Taste | [[Parmesan Cheese]] | Grated |
| To Taste | [[Salt]] and [[Pepper]] | Adjust to taste |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Combine and Mix
1. In a large bowl, combine the **2 cups cooked bowtie pasta or rotini pasta**, **1 cup cherry tomatoes**, **1 package crumbled feta & basil tomato**, **1/2 cup caesar salad dressing**, **1/2 teaspoon basil**, **1 small can sliced olives**, **1/4 cup chopped red onion**, and **as needed marinated, grilled chicken strips**.
2. Season with **to taste salt and pepper** and **to taste parmesan cheese**.
3. Mix all components thoroughly until the dressing is evenly distributed and the salad is fragrant and well-coated.

### Step 2: Serve
4. Serve warm immediately, or chill in the refrigerator to allow the flavors to blend together until satisfyingly cool.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store sealed in the refrigerator for up to 2 days; chilling the salad allows the flavors to blend more deeply.
