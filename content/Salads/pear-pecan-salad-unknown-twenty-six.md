# Pear Pecan Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A delightful retro molded salad featuring sweet pears, tangy cream cheese, crunchy pecans, and whipped cream. Perfect for holiday gatherings or special luncheons.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 30 mins | 5 mins | 2 hours | 6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Saucepan
* Mixing bowl
* Salad mold

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 (No. 2 1/2) can | [[Pear Halves]] | Drained, juice reserved |
| 1 pkg (3 oz) | [[Lemon Jell-O]] | Gelatin dessert |
| 1 pkg (8 oz) | [[Cream Cheese]] | Softened |
| 1 cup | [[Chopped Pecans]] | Chopped |
| 1/2 pt | [[Whip Cream]] | Whipped |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparing the Gelatin
1. Drain juice from **[[Pear Halves]]**, reserve **1 cup of liquid**.
2. Boil juice, add **[[Lemon Jell-O]]** and stir until dissolved. Chill until partially set and syrupy.

### Step 2: Mixing and Molding
3. Blend **[[Pear Halves]]** and **[[Cream Cheese]]** together until smooth.
4. Stir in the partially set **[[Lemon Jell-O]]**, then add **[[Chopped Pecans]]**.
5. Fold in **[[Whip Cream]]** gently until well combined.
6. Pour into molds and chill until firmly set. Unmold and serve on lettuce leaves.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Can be prepared up to 24 hours in advance and kept refrigerated in the mold until ready to serve.
