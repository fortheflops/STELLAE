# Molded Crabmeat Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** May Cowing | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A classic retro molded salad featuring tender crabmeat, crunchy celery, and a vibrant lemon gelatin base. Perfect for retro gatherings or light summer lunches.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Base Preparation
1. Dissolve **1 pkg. lemon jello** in **1 1/2 cups boiling water**.
2. Add **3 T. vinegar** and **1/2 tsp. salt**, then let cool until the mixture begins to set.

### Step 2: Combining and Molding
3. Fold in **2 cups shredded crab meat**, **3/4 cup chopped celery**, **2 T. cut up pimento**, **1 tsp. onion juice**, and **1/2 cup mayonnaise** when the gelatin is fairly stiff.
4. Mold the mixture and chill until completely set and firm to the touch. Serve with dressing.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Prepare up to a day in advance and keep refrigerated in the mold until ready to serve.
