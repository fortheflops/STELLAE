## Instructions

### Step 1: Make the Dressing
1. In a small mixing bowl, combine **3 tbsp lime juice**, **1/2 tsp red chili flakes**, **1/2 tsp red chili powder**, **6 tbsp rice wine vinegar**, **1 tsp sugar**, and **6 tbsp olive oil**.
2. Season with **1 tsp salt** and **1 tsp pepper**, then whisk vigorously until the dressing is emulsified and well combined.

### Step 2: Assemble the Salad
3. In a large mixing bowl, combine **1 1/2 cups jicama**, **1 cup carrots**, **1 cup napa cabbage**, **1/2 cup red onion**, **1 cup English cucumber**, **1/2 cup red bell pepper**, and **1 tbsp cilantro**.
4. Toss the vegetables together gently until evenly distributed.

### Step 3: Marinate and Serve
5. Pour the prepared dressing over the vegetables and toss thoroughly to coat.
6. Let the salad sit for **15 minutes** to allow the flavors to meld and the vegetables to slightly soften, stirring 2 or 3 times during this period. Serve chilled or at room temperature.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This salad can be prepared a few hours in advance, but is best served the same day to maintain the crisp texture of the vegetables.
