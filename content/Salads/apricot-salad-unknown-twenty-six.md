# Apricot Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** N. L. | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A delightful and creamy gelatin-based fruit salad combining apricot, pineapple, and crunchy walnuts. Perfect for potlucks and family gatherings.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 10 mins | 4 hours | 12 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Saucepan
* Mixing bowl
* Electric mixer
* 13x9 Pan

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 small pkg | [[Apricot Jell-O]] | None |
| 2/3 C. | [[Sugar]] | None |
| 2/3 C. | [[Water]] | None |
| 1 (8 oz) | [[Cream Cheese]] | None |
| 2 jars | [[Strained Apricot Baby Food]] | None |
| 1 #2 can | [[Crushed Pineapple]] | drained |
| 1 lg can | [[Evaporated Milk]] | chilled |
| 1/2 C. | [[Chopped Walnuts]] | chopped |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Making the Base
1. Bring **2 small pkg apricot jell-o**, **2/3 C. water**, and **2/3 C. sugar** to a boil in a saucepan.
2. Remove from heat and dissolve **1 (8 oz) cream cheese** in it (off stove).
3. Add **2 jars strained apricot baby food** and **1 #2 can crushed pineapple**, then let cool completely.

### Step 2: Whipping and Assembling
4. Beat **1 lg can evaporated milk** until stiff.
5. Fold the cooled **apricot mixture** into the whipped milk.
6. Pour into a **13x9 pan** and sprinkle with **1/2 C. chopped walnuts** on top. Chill until firm.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Cover and refrigerate for at least 4 hours or overnight until set before serving.
