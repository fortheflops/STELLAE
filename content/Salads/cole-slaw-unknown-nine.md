# Cole Slaw

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Nine | ✍️ **Attribution:** Billie Kennedy | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A classic, creamy coleslaw recipe featuring a sweet and tangy dressing. Perfect for picnics, barbecues, and family dinners.*

## Ingredients

### Dressing and Slaw
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 bag | [[Coleslaw]] | Shredded cabbage mix |
| 2/3 cup | [[Mayo]] or [[Miracle Whip]] | Standard or light |
| 3 tbsp | [[Vegetable oil]] |  |
| 1/2 cup | [[White sugar]] |  |
| 1 tbsp | [[White vinegar]] |  |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Salt]] |  |
| 1/2-1 tsp | [[Onion powder]] | Optional, to taste |

---

## Instructions

### Mixing and Chilling
1. In a large mixing bowl, combine **2/3 cup mayo or Miracle Whip**, **3 tbsp vegetable oil**, **1/2 cup white sugar**, **1 tbsp white vinegar**, **1/4 tsp salt**, and optional **1/2-1 tsp onion powder** until smooth and the sugar begins to dissolve.
2. Add the **1 bag coleslaw** to the dressing mixture and toss thoroughly until the cabbage is evenly coated.
3. Cover and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes before serving to let the flavors meld and the cabbage slightly soften.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 2-3 days. Stir well before serving.
