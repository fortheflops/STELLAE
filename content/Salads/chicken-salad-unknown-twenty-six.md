# Chicken Salad

## Ingredients

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing
1. In a large mixing bowl, combine the **4 cups cooked chicken breasts**, **2 cups celery**, **1 cup green grapes**, **1/2 cup toasted almonds**, and **4 slices bacon** until well distributed.

### Step 2: Dressing
2. In a separate small bowl, whisk together the **1 cup mayonnaise**, **1 cup sour cream**, **1 1/2 tsp salt**, and **2 tbsp lemon juice** until smooth.
3. Pour the dressing over the chicken mixture and toss gently until everything is evenly coated and creamy.

### Step 3: Serving
4. Serve the chicken salad chilled on top of fresh **lettuce leaves** alongside slices of sweet **cantaloupe**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 2 days before serving.
