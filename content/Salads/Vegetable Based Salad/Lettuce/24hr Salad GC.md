## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| One                              | Large Head [[Lettuce]]                    |
| One Bunch                        | Green [[Onions]], chopped                 |
| One Large                        | Green Bell [[Pepper]], chopped            |
| One Can                          | [[Water Chestnuts]], sliced or chopped    |
| Two Cups                         | Chopped [[Celery]]                        |
| One Package                      | Frozen [[Peas]]                           |
| Two Cups                         | [[Mayonnaise]]                            |
| Three Tablespoons                | [[Sugar]]                                 |
| Four Ounces                      | Grated [[Parmesan Cheese]]                |
___
## Instructions
1.  In a large pyrex dish, break the lettuce into bite-sized pieces to form the bottom layer.
2.  Create subsequent layers with the chopped celery, green onions, green bell pepper, and water chestnuts.
3.  Add the package of frozen peas as the next layer; do not cook them.
4.  In a small bowl, mix three tablespoons of sugar with two cups of mayonnaise.
5.  Spread the mayonnaise and sugar mixture completely over the top of the layered vegetables, sealing the salad.
6.  Sprinkle four ounces of grated parmesan cheese over the top.
7.  Cover and let set in the refrigerator overnight or for 24 hours. Do not toss until ready to serve.
