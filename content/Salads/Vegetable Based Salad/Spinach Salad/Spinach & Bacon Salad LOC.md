## Instructions
1.  Prepare the salad greens: Wash, drain, and tear the fresh spinach and the red leaf (or iceberg) lettuce. Place them in a large salad bowl.
2.  Cook the bacon: Fry the bacon until crisp. Drain the fat, let the bacon cool, and then crumble it.
3.  Make the dressing: In a separate bowl, mix the sugar, salt, dry mustard, onion juice (juice scraped from an onion), cider vinegar, and salad oil. (These are the "last 6 ingredients" mentioned in the note).
4.  Combine the salad: Add the crumbled bacon bits to the salad greens.
5.  Dress the salad: Pour the dressing over the salad greens and bacon just before serving.
6.  Toss gently to combine and serve immediately.
