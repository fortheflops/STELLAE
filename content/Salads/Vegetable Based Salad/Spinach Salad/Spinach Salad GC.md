## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| **For the Salad:** |                                           |
| One & $\frac{1}{2}$ Bunches      | Fresh [[Spinach]]                         |
| One Bunch                        | Green [[Onions]], chopped                 |
| Four                             | Hard-boiled [[Eggs]], sliced              |
| Two Tablespoons                  | [[Sesame Seeds]]                          |
| One                              | Large Red or Green Bell [[Pepper]], sliced |
| **For the Dressing:** |                                           |
| $\frac{1}{4}$ Cup                | [[Olive Oil]]                             |
| Three to Four Cloves             | [[Garlic]], minced                        |
| One Tablespoon                   | [[Sesame Seeds]]                          |
| Three Tablespoons                | [[Lemon Juice]]                           |
| Three Tablespoons                | [[Soy Sauce]] or Tamari                   |
___
## Instructions
1. Prepare the Salad
	1. In a large bowl or platter, put the ingredients in layers. Start with a bed of fresh spinach, followed by layers of the sliced red or green pepper and chopped green onions.
	2.  The card notes the sliced hard-boiled eggs should be the top layer. Arrange them over the other salad ingredients.
	3.  Sprinkle with two tablespoons of sesame seeds. Set aside.
2. Prepare the Dressing
	1. In a small skillet, heat $\frac{1}{4}$ cup of olive oil over medium-high heat.
	2.  Add three to four cloves of minced garlic and one tablespoon of sesame seeds to the hot oil. Sauté briefly until fragrant.
	3.  Turn off the heat but leave the pan on the burner.
	4.  Add three tablespoons of lemon juice and three tablespoons of soy sauce or tamari to the pan. Stir to combine.
3. Immediately pour the hot dressing over the layered salad. Do not toss it. Serve at once.
