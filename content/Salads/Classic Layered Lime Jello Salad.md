# Classic Layered Lime Jello Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A nostalgic two-layer molded Jello salad featuring a fruity lime Jello base with pears and cherries, topped with a creamy lime and cream cheese layer. Perfect for vintage potlucks and family gatherings.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 25 mins | 5 mins | 6 hours | 8 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowls
* Electric mixer
* Ring mold or serving dish

---
## Ingredients

### Fruit Layer
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 Large (6 oz) Package | [[Lime Jello]] | divided |
| 1 Large Can | [[Pears]] | drained and diced |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Maraschino Cherries]] | whole or halved |
| 1 Cup | [[Miniature Marshmallows]] | optional |

### Cream Cheese Layer
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 Large (8 oz) Package | [[Cream Cheese]] | softened |
| Reserved | [[Lime Jello]] | liquid |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare and Divide Jello
1. Prepare the **Lime Jello** according to package directions using hot and cold water. Divide the liquid Jello in half into two separate bowls.

### Step 2: Create the Fruit Layer
2. Chill one half of the Jello in the refrigerator until it is partially set to the consistency of unbeaten egg whites.
3. Gently fold in the drained **pears**, **maraschino cherries**, and optional **marshmallows**.
4. Pour the fruit mixture into a ring mold or serving dish and return to the refrigerator until firm.

### Step 3: Prepare the Cream Cheese Layer
5. In a separate bowl, beat the softened **cream cheese** with an electric mixer until completely smooth.
6. Gradually beat the remaining still-liquid **Lime Jello** into the cream cheese until the mixture is thoroughly combined and smooth.

### Step 4: Assemble and Chill
7. Gently pour the cream cheese mixture evenly over the top of the set fruit Jello layer.
8. Refrigerate for at least 6 hours, or preferably overnight, until the entire salad is completely firm and set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Make this the day before serving to ensure it sets completely firm in the refrigerator.
