# Bean & Carrot Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A refreshing and tangy marinated salad combining crisp green beans, carrots, kidney beans, and fresh vegetables in a zesty dressing. Perfect for making ahead, the flavors meld wonderfully after chilling overnight in the refrigerator.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Assembly and Dressing
1. Place the **1 (16 oz) can drained green beans**, **1 (16 oz) can sliced carrots**, **1 (16 oz) can red kidney beans**, **1 small sliced thin onion**, **1/4 cup chopped green pepper**, **1/4 cup chopped celery**, and **2 tbsp snipped parsley** in a shallow dish.
2. In a screw-top jar, combine the **1/2 cup white vinegar**, **2 tbsp salad oil**, **1 tsp salt**, **1 tsp dry mustard**, and **1 no-calorie sweetener equivalent to 1/3 cup sugar**, shaking well until thoroughly mixed.
3. Pour the dressing evenly over the vegetable mixture.

### Step 2: Chilling and Serving
4. Refrigerate the salad for several hours or overnight to allow the flavors to fully develop and absorb.
5. When ready to serve, arrange the marinated salad in a serving bowl until crisp, tender-crisp, and well-chilled.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Cover and refrigerate several hours or overnight before serving to let the vegetables marinate thoroughly in the dressing.
