### Instructions
1.  Dissolve gelatin in boiling water; add lemon juice.
2.  Stir in all the remaining ingredients (tuna, soup, pimiento, celery, onions, mayonnaise, cottage cheese).
3.  Pour into a mold.
4.  Chill until set.
