# Marinated Cucumber Slices

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *Crisp cucumber slices marinated in a tangy vinegar and oil dressing with dill. A refreshing side dish that is best served well chilled.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | None | Several hours | 6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Knife
* Cutting board

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 cup | [[Vinegar]] |  |
| 1/4 cup | [[Salad oil]] |  |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Salt]] |  |
| A few grains | [[White pepper]] |  |
| 1 tsp | [[Dill weed]] |  |
| 1 | [[Unpared cucumber]] | Thinly sliced |
|  | [[Tomatoes]] | Optional addition |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and Marinating
1. Blend the **1/2 cup vinegar**, **1/4 cup salad oil**, **1/4 tsp salt**, **few grains white pepper**, and **1 tsp dill weed** in a bowl.
2. Add **1 unpared cucumber** (thinly sliced) and optional **tomatoes**, then mix lightly until well coated.
3. Chill for several hours until flavors meld and the cucumbers are cold and crisp.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Keep refrigerated in a covered container for up to 24 hours before serving.
