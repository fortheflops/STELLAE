## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
| 4                     | Cooked Potatoes             |
| 4                     | Hard-Boiled Eggs            |
| 1                     | Onion                       |
| 2-3                   | Sour Pickles                |
| To Taste              | Salt                        |
| To Taste              | Pepper                      |
| To Taste               | Celery Seed                 |
|                       | Mayonnaise or Mustard     |
___
### Instructions
1. Dice the cooked potatoes.
2. Chop the hard-boiled eggs.
3. Chop The onion
4. Chop the sour pickles
5. In a large bowl, combine the diced potatoes, chopped eggs, onion, and pickles.
6. Add salt, pepper, and celery seed to taste.
7. Add mayonnaise or mustard to taste.
8. Mix well until all ingredients are evenly coated.
9. Chill for at least 30 minutes to an hour.
