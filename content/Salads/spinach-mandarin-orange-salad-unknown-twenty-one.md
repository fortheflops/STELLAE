# Spinach Mandarin Orange Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty One | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A vibrant and refreshing Spinach Mandarin Orange Salad tossed with crisp red pepper and onion, finished with Honey Dijon Dressing. Perfect as a light main or a delightful side dish for any meal.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | None | None | 2 main or 4 salad servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Salad bowl

---
## Ingredients

### Salad Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 oz | [[Spinach]] | Fresh |
| 11 oz | [[Mandarin Oranges]] | Drained |
| 1/2 C | [[Onion]] | Chopped |
| 1 | [[Red Pepper]] | Sliced |
| To taste | [[Honey Dijon Dressing]] | As needed |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Assembly
1. In a large salad bowl, combine the **10 oz spinach**, **11 oz mandarin oranges**, **1/2 C chopped onion**, and **1 sliced red pepper**.
2. Add the **Honey Dijon Dressing** just before serving and toss gently until the greens are evenly coated and crisp.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the chopped vegetables and spinach separately in airtight containers in the refrigerator, and dress just before serving to maintain crispness.
