# Florentine Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Marcile | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A crisp and refreshing Florentine Salad featuring fresh spinach, tomatoes, and onions tossed in a homemade Roma dressing. Topped with hard-boiled eggs, this dish offers a wonderful balance of textures and flavors.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | None | Overnight (optional) | 6 servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Salad bowl
* Whisk
* Jar

## Ingredients

### Salad Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 6 cups | [[Fresh Spinach]] | Torn into bite-size pieces |
| 2 medium | [[Tomatoes]] | Cut into wedges |
| 1 medium | [[Onion]] | Sliced and separated into rings |
| 1/2 cup | [[Roma Dressing]] | Prepared |
| 3 | [[Hard-Cooked Eggs]] | Halved |

### Roma Dressing
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/3 cup | [[Vinegar]] | None |
| 2/3 cup | [[Oil]] | None |
| 1/2 tsp | [[Salt]] | None |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Paprika]] | None |
| 1 tsp | [[Sugar]] | None |
| 2 cloves | [[Garlic]] | Split, optional |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Salad
1. Combine **6 cups fresh spinach**, **2 medium tomatoes**, and **1 medium onion** in a large salad bowl.
2. Chill the mixture thoroughly in the refrigerator.

### Step 2: Make the Dressing
3. In a separate bowl or jar, combine **1/3 cup vinegar**, **2/3 cup oil**, **1/2 tsp salt**, **1/4 tsp paprika**, **1 tsp sugar**, and **2 cloves garlic** (if using).

### Step 3: Toss and Serve
4. Toss the chilled salad with **1/2 cup Roma dressing** until evenly coated.
5. Garnish the top with **3 hard-cooked eggs**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Let the dressing set overnight if you use garlic to allow the flavors to fully develop.
