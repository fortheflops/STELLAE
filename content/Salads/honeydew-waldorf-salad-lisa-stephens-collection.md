# Honeydew Waldorf Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** Lisa Stephens Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A refreshing twist on the classic Waldorf salad featuring sweet honeydew melon, crisp celery, and toasted walnuts. Tossed in a light honey-lime mayonnaise dressing, this chilled salad is light and perfect for summer.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 0 mins | 20 mins | 2 servings |

* Mixing bowl
* Small bowl
* Whisk
* Serving dishes

## Ingredients

### Salad Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/4 | [[Honeydew Melon]] | cut into bite-size pieces |
| 1 stalk | [[Celery]] | chopped |
| 1/4 cup | [[Walnuts]] | chopped, toasted lightly, and cooled |
| as needed | [[Lettuce]] | leaves for garnish |

### Dressing Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 tsp | [[Lime Juice]] | fresh, or to taste |
| 1 tsp | [[Honey]] | None |
| 1/4 cup | [[Mayonnaise]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Chill
1. In a bowl, combine the **1/4 honeydew melon** and **1 stalk chopped celery**, then chill the mixture in the refrigerator for **20 minutes**.
2. In a small bowl, stir together the **1 tsp fresh lime juice** and **1 tsp honey** until the honey is completely dissolved, then whisk in the **1/4 cup mayonnaise**.

### Step 2: Toss and Serve
3. Drain the melon mixture of any excess liquid, add the **1/4 cup toasted walnuts**, and toss the mixture with the dressing until evenly coated.
4. Arrange **lettuce leaves** on 2 serving dishes, spoon the salad onto the leaves, and serve immediately.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The melon and celery mixture can be chilled ahead of time. Combine with dressing and walnuts just before serving to preserve the crisp textures.
