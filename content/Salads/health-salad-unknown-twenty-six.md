# Health Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Maxine | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A classic mid-century gelatin salad featuring lemon Jell-O whipped with cottage cheese, mayonnaise, Dream Whip, carrots, and celery. Perfectly set in a 9x13 pan, this retro dish offers a unique blend of sweet, savory, and crunchy textures.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Gelatin Base
1. In a large mixing bowl, dissolve the **2 packages (3 oz each) lemon Jell-O** in **3 cups boiling water**, stirring until completely dissolved.
2. Allow the mixture to cool and partially set in the refrigerator until it is slightly thickened but not fully firm.
3. Using a hand mixer, whip the partially set **lemon Jell-O** until it is light, frothy, and doubled in volume.

### Step 2: Assemble and Chill
4. Gently fold in the **1/3 cup finely grated carrots**, **1/2 cup finely diced celery**, **1 cup large curd cottage cheese**, **1 cup prepared Dream Whip**, and **1 cup mayonnaise** until thoroughly combined.
5. Season with a pinch of **salt** and a squeeze of **lemon juice** to taste.
6. Pour the mixture into a **9x13-inch baking dish** and smooth the top.
7. Refrigerate for at least **4 hours**, or until completely set and firm to the touch before slicing and serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Can be made up to 24 hours in advance. Keep covered and refrigerated until ready to serve.
