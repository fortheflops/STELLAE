# Banana Split Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** N. Lab '13 | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A delightful and refreshing frozen Banana Split Salad loaded with cream cheese, pineapple, strawberries, bananas, and walnuts. Perfect for a crowd, this classic dessert salad sets up firm in the freezer for easy slicing and serving.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | None | 3 hours | 12-15 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* 9x13 pan

---
## Ingredients

### Salad Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 (8 oz) | [[Cream cheese]] | Softened |
| 1/3 cup | [[Sugar]] | None |
| 1 (20 oz) | [[Crushed pineapple]] | Drained |
| 1 (16 oz) | [[Frozen sliced strawberries in syrup]] | Thawed |
| 2 medium | [[Bananas]] | Firm, chopped |
| 1 (12 oz) | [[Cool Whip]] | None |
| 1 cup | [[Walnuts]] | Chopped |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing and Freezing
1. In a large mixing bowl, beat the **1 (8 oz) cream cheese** and **1/3 cup sugar** together until smooth.
2. Stir in the **1 (20 oz) crushed pineapple**, **1 (16 oz) frozen sliced strawberries in syrup**, and **2 medium bananas**.
3. Fold in the **1 (12 oz) Cool Whip** and **1 cup walnuts** until thoroughly combined.
4. Spread the mixture evenly into a greased 9x13 pan and freeze until firm, about **3 hrs**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Can be kept frozen for up to a week; cover tightly to prevent freezer burn.
