# Verlene's Poppy Seed Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** Georgia Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Verlene | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *Verlene's Poppy Seed Salad is a refreshing blend of fresh cut fruit tossed in a tangy orange and poppy seed dressing. Chill until ready to serve for a vibrant and crisp side dish.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | None | 30 mins | 4 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Glass bowl
* Measuring spoons
* Measuring cups

---
## Ingredients

### Salad Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 T. | [[Orange juice]] | Fresh |
| 1/2 C. | [[X-tra Touch Poppy Seed Dressing]] | Prepared |
| 1 1/2 t. | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 1/4 t. | [[Grated orange peel]] | Freshly grated |
| 4 C. | [[Fresh fruit]] | Cut up |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Dressing Preparation
1. Combine **2 T. orange juice**, **1/2 C. X-tra Touch Poppy Seed Dressing**, **1 1/2 t. sugar**, and **1/4 t. grated orange peel** in a bowl.
2. Refrigerate the dressing until chilled and ready to serve.

### Step 2: Assembling the Salad
3. Place **4 C. cut up fresh fruit** in a glass bowl.
4. Toss the fruit gently with the chilled dressing until evenly coated and glossy.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Keep the cut fruit and dressing stored separately in the refrigerator until ready to combine just before serving.
