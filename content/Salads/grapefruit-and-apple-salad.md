# Grapefruit and Apple Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A crisp and refreshing fruit salad featuring alternating slices of tart grapefruit and sweet red apples served on a bed of lettuce. This simple, elegant dish is perfectly complemented by a tangy French dressing.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 0 mins | None | 6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Knife
* Cutting board
* Serving platter

---
## Ingredients

### Salad Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | [[Grapefruit]] | Peeled and divided into sections |
| 2 | [[Red Apples]] | Cored and cut into wedges |
| 6 | [[Lettuce Leaves]] | Fresh, washed |
| to taste | [[French Dressing]] | Prepared |

---

## Instructions

### Assembly
1. Prepare the **1 grapefruit** by peeling and dividing it into sections.
2. Cut the **2 red apples** into wedges, ensuring they are cored but not peeled.
3. Arrange the **1 grapefruit** sections and **2 red apple** wedges alternately on top of the **6 lettuce leaves**.
4. Serve immediately with **French dressing** drizzled over the top.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Prepare the fruit and lettuce separately and store in the refrigerator; assemble just before serving to prevent the apples from browning and the lettuce from wilting.
