# Marilyn's Bibb Lettuce Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Marilyn | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A delightful and crisp salad featuring tender Bibb lettuce paired with sweet sliced pears and crunchy honey-toasted pecans. Dressed in a creamy yogurt-blue cheese blend, it offers a perfect balance of sweet, savory, and tangy flavors.*

| To taste | [[Parsley Flakes]] |  |

### Toppings
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 cup | [[Blue Cheese]] | crumbled |
| 1/2 cup | [[Pecans]] | toasted |
| 1 tbsp | [[Honey]] | for coating pecans |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Toast the Pecans
1. In a small skillet over medium heat, toss the **1/2 cup pecans** with **1 tbsp honey** until caramelized and fragrant, about 3 to 5 minutes. Remove from heat and spread on parchment paper to cool completely.

### Step 2: Prepare the Dressing
2. In a small bowl, whisk together **1/2 cup plain yogurt**, **1/2 cup blue cheese dressing**, and a pinch each of **mint**, **celery salt**, and **parsley flakes** until smooth and well combined.

### Step 3: Assemble and Serve
3. Arrange the torn **1 head Bibb lettuce** on a serving platter. Layer the sliced **3 pears** over the greens.
4. Drizzle the prepared dressing over the salad, then sprinkle with **crumbled blue cheese** and the cooled honey-toasted **pecans** just before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The dressing can be prepared and refrigerated up to 2 days in advance. Toast the pecans ahead of time, but assemble the salad and add toppings just before serving to maintain crispness.
