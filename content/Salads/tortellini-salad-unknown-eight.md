# Tortellini Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Eight | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A vibrant and refreshing cold pasta salad featuring tender cheese tortellini tossed with crisp garden vegetables and a rich pesto dressing. Perfect for picnics, potlucks, or an easy make-ahead lunch.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 10 mins | 8 hours | 4-6 servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large pot
* Colander
* Mixing bowl
* Chef's knife
* Cutting board

## Ingredients

### Salad Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 (12 oz.) package | [[Cheese Tortellini]] | cooked and cooled |
| 1/4 cup | [[Green Onion]] | chopped |
| 4 tbsp | [[Zucchini]] | finely diced |
| 4 tbsp | [[Red Bell Pepper]] | finely diced |
| 6 | [[Cherry Tomatoes]] | halved |
| 2/3 cup | [[Pesto Dressing]] |  |

---

## Instructions

### Preparation
1. Cook the **1 (12 oz.) package cheese tortellini** in a large pot of boiling salted water according to package instructions until al dente. Drain in a colander and let cool completely.
2. In a large mixing bowl, lightly toss together the cooled **cheese tortellini**, **1/4 cup chopped green onion**, **4 tbsp finely diced zucchini**, **4 tbsp finely diced red bell pepper**, and **6 halved cherry tomatoes**.
3. Pour the **2/3 cup pesto dressing** over the mixture and toss gently until all ingredients are evenly coated and glistening.
4. Cover the bowl tightly and refrigerate overnight to allow the flavors to meld and the salad to chill thoroughly before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This salad is ideal for making ahead as the overnight refrigeration allows the tortellini to absorb the herbaceous pesto flavors. Keep refrigerated for up to 3 days.
