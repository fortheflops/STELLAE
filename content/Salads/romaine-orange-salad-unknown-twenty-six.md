# Romaine Orange Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A refreshing salad featuring crisp romaine lettuce, pecans, and mandarin oranges tossed in a sweet red wine vinegar dressing. Perfect as a vibrant side dish for any meal.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | None | None | 4 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Salad bowl
* Whisk
* Measuring cups

---
## Ingredients

### Salad Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 head | [[Romaine Lettuce]] | Torn |
| 1/2 cup | [[Pecans]] | Chopped |
| 1/2 cup | [[Green Onions]] | Chopped |
| 1 can | [[Mandarin Oranges]] | Drained |

### Dressing
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/4 cup | [[Red Wine Vinegar]] | None |
| 1/4 cup | [[Sugar]] | None |
| 3/4 cup | [[Oil]] | Vegetable or canola |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Toss Salad
1. Combine the **1 head romaine lettuce**, **1/2 cup pecans**, **1/2 cup green onions**, and **1 can mandarin oranges** in a large salad bowl.

### Step 2: Prepare Dressing and Serve
2. Whisk together the **1/4 cup red wine vinegar**, **1/4 cup sugar**, and **3/4 cup oil** until well blended.
3. Pour the dressing over the salad just before serving and toss until evenly coated.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Keep the dressing stored in a separate jar in the refrigerator until ready to toss and serve.
