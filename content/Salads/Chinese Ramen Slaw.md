# Chinese Ramen Slaw

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Janine Porter - Cathie Purdy | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A crunchy, savory Chinese ramen slaw tossed with diced cooked chicken, toasted almonds, sesame seeds, and a tangy ramen-seasoned soy dressing. Perfect for quick weekday lunches or potlucks.*

---

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 8 mins | 30 mins (Chilling) | 4–6 Servings |

## Instructions

### Step 1: Toast the Ramen and Nuts
1. Preheat oven or toaster oven to **350°F (175°C)**. Crumble the **1 package dry Top Ramen noodles** inside the package (set aside the **seasoning packet** for dressing). Spread the crumbled **ramen noodles**, **1/4 cup sesame seeds**, and **1/4 to 1/2 cup slivered almonds** onto an ungreased baking sheet. Toast in the oven for **5-8 minutes**, stirring frequently, until lightly golden brown and fragrant. Be careful not to burn them. Remove from oven and let cool completely.

### Step 2: Prepare the Dressing
2. In a small bowl or jar, combine the **1/2 cup vegetable oil**, **1/4 cup rice vinegar**, the reserved **1 Top Ramen seasoning packet**, **3 tbsp soy sauce**, **1 tbsp sugar**, and **salt & pepper to taste**. Whisk or shake vigorously until well combined and the **sugar** is fully dissolved.

### Step 3: Assemble and Chill
3. In a large salad bowl, combine the **1 package coleslaw mix**, the **4 cooked and diced chicken breasts**, and the **1 bunch chopped cilantro**.
4. Add the prepared dressing and the cooled, toasted **ramen noodle, sesame seed, and almond mixture** to the bowl.
5. Toss all ingredients together gently until everything is evenly coated.
6. Cover and chill the salad in the refrigerator for at least **30 minutes** before serving to allow the flavors to meld. Toss again briefly before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The dressing and toasted noodle mixture can be prepared 1–2 days ahead. Combine with the fresh greens and chicken at least 30 minutes before serving.
