# Hot Potato Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A classic rustic hot potato salad layered with celery, parsley, and a tangy oil and vinegar dressing. Finished with a crown of crispy fried bacon for the ultimate savory crunch.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 30 mins | 15 mins | 4 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Saucepan
* Serving dish
* Knife
* Small pan

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 4 | [[Potatoes]] | Medium-sized, unpeeled |
| 1 pinch | [[Celery]] | Chopped |
| 1 pinch | [[Parsley]] | Chopped |
| 4 tbsp | [[Vinegar]] | None |
| 4 tbsp | [[Oil]] | None |
| 1 splash | [[Lemon juice]] | Fresh |
| 4 slices | [[Bacon]] | Fried and crisp |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep Potatoes
1. Wash and cook the **4 medium-sized potatoes** in their jackets until fork tender.
2. Allow them to cool, then pare and cut into thin slices.

### Step 2: Layer and Dress
3. Arrange a layer of the **sliced potatoes** in a serving dish, season, and sprinkle with **celery** and **parsley**.
4. In a small pan, mix the **4 tbsp vinegar**, **4 tbsp oil**, and a little **lemon juice**, then heat to a boiling point.
5. Pour the hot dressing over the **potatoes** and cover tightly to steam until flavors meld.

### Step 3: Finishing
6. Serve warm garnished with **crisp fried bacon**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Best served warm immediately after dressing, but potatoes can be boiled and sliced ahead of time.
