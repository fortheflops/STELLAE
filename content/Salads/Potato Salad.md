# Potato Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A classic, comforting potato salad featuring hard-boiled eggs, onions, and sour pickles bound with mayonnaise or mustard. Perfect for picnics, barbecues, and family gatherings.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | None | 30 mins | 4–6 Servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large bowl
* Knife
* Cutting board

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 4 | [[Potatoes]] | Cooked |
| 4 | [[Eggs]] | Hard-boiled |
| 1 | [[Onion]] | Whole |
| 2-3 | [[Sour Pickles]] | Whole |
| To Taste | [[Salt]] | None |
| To Taste | [[Pepper]] | None |
| To Taste | [[Celery Seed]] | None |
| As Needed | [[Mayonnaise or Mustard]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep Ingredients
1. Dice the **4 cooked potatoes**.
2. Chop the **4 hard-boiled eggs**.
3. Chop the **1 onion**.
4. Chop the **2-3 sour pickles**.

### Step 2: Combine and Chill
5. In a large bowl, combine the diced potatoes, chopped eggs, onion, and pickles.
6. Add **salt**, **pepper**, and **celery seed** to taste.
7. Add **mayonnaise or mustard** to taste.
8. Mix well until all ingredients are evenly coated and creamy.
9. Chill in the refrigerator for at least **30 minutes to an hour** before serving until cool and well-marinated.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store sealed in the refrigerator for up to 2-3 days for best flavor and texture.
