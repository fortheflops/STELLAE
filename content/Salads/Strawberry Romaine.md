## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Salad
1. In a large salad bowl, combine the **1 bunch romaine lettuce**, **1 bunch bibb or boston lettuce**, **1 pint strawberries**, **1/2 cup chopped walnuts**, and **1 cup monterey cheese**.

### Step 2: Mix the Dressing
2. In a separate bowl or jar, whisk together the **1 cup oil**, **3/4 cup sugar**, **1/2 cup red wine vinegar**, **1/2 teaspoon salt**, **1/2 teaspoon white pepper**, and **1/2 teaspoon paprika** until well combined.

### Step 3: Toss and Serve
3. Toss the salad mixture with just enough dressing to moisten until evenly coated, and serve immediately while crisp and vibrant.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Keep the dressing stored separately in a jar and toss with the salad just before serving to maintain crispness.
