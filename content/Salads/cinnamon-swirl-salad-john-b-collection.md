# Cinnamon Swirl Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A delightful and nostalgic molded salad featuring lemon jello, red cinnamon candies, applesauce, and walnuts, topped with a sweetened cream cheese swirl. Perfect for family gatherings and holiday spreads, this unique dish offers a sweet and tangy flavor profile.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 0 mins | 4 hours | 9 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 8x8x2 pan
* Mixing bowl
* Electric mixer

---
## Ingredients

### Salad Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 (6 oz) pkg | [[Lemon Jello]] | Dry |
| 3 cups | [[Boiling Water]] | Hot |
| 1/2 cup | [[Red Cinnamon Candies]] | Red |
| 2 cups | [[Applesauce]] | Unsweetened or sweetened |
| 1 tbsp | [[Lemon Juice]] | Fresh |
| 1 dash | [[Salt]] | Standard |
| 1/2 cup | [[Walnuts]] | Chopped |

### Topping Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 (6 oz) pkg | [[Cream Cheese]] | Softened |
| 1/4 cup | [[Milk]] | Liquid |
| 2 tbsp | [[Mayonnaise]] | Standard |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Making the Base
1. Dissolve the **1 (6 oz) pkg lemon jello** and **1/2 cup red cinnamon candies** in **3 cups boiling water**.
2. Stir in **2 cups applesauce**, **1 tbsp lemon juice**, and **1 dash salt**.
3. Chill until partially set.

### Step 2: Adding Walnuts and Topping
4. Fold in **1/2 cup walnuts** and pour the mixture into an **8x8x2 pan**.
5. Beat together **1 (6 oz) pkg cream cheese**, **1/4 cup milk**, and **2 tbsp mayonnaise** until smooth.
6. Spoon atop salad, swirl to marble, and chill until firm.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Cover tightly and refrigerate up to 24 hours before serving for best results.
