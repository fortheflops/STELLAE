# Barbara's Jello

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Five | ✍️ **Attribution:** Barbara Spurr | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A delightful retro congealed salad featuring lemon jello, crushed pineapple, grated cheese, and whipped topping. Perfect for vintage-themed potlucks and family gatherings.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 0 mins | 4 hours | 6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Measuring cups
* Jello mold or pan

---
## Ingredients

### Salad Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 pkg (3 oz) | [[Lemon Jello]] | or lemon gelatin |
| 1 sm can | [[Crushed Pineapple]] | drained, reserve juice |
| 1 pkg | [[Dream Whip]] | prepared |
| 1 cup | [[Grated Velveeta]] | grated |
| 1 sm pkg | [[Pecans]] | nuts and pineapple topping |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Making the Jello
1. Make the **1 pkg lemon jello** using **1 cup hot H2O** and **3/4 cup pineapple juice** (add cold H2O to make **3/4 cup** total liquid).

### Step 2: Combining and Setting
2. Allow the jello to set slightly, then fold in the **1 pkg Dream Whip**, **1 cup grated Velveeta** cheese, and **1 sm pkg pecans** along with the crushed pineapple until well combined.
3. Chill in the refrigerator until completely firm and set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Prepare up to a day in advance and keep refrigerated until ready to serve.
