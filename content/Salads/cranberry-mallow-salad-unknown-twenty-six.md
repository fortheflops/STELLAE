# Cranberry Mallow Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Marlene | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A delightful and sweet retro molded salad combining tart ground cranberries, crushed pineapple, raspberry gelatin, and fluffy miniature marshmallows. Perfect for holiday gatherings or traditional potlucks, served chilled on a crisp bed of lettuce.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 30 mins | 0 mins | 2 hours | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowls
* Measuring cups
* 9x13 inch pan
* Refrigerator

---
## Ingredients

### Fruit and Sugar Base
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 lb | [[Cranberries]] | Ground |
| 1 small can | [[Crushed Pineapple]] | Not drained |
| 2 cups | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |

### Gelatin and Topping
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 pkg | [[Raspberry Jell-O]] | Flavored gelatin |
| 2 cups | [[Hot Water]] | Boiling |
| 1 cup | [[Whipping Cream]] | Whipped |
| 1 cup | [[Miniature Marshmallows]] | Small |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Cranberry Mixture
1. Mix the **1 lb ground cranberries**, **1 small can crushed pineapple**, and **2 cups sugar** together in a large bowl.
2. Let the mixture stand for 1 hour or more to let the flavors meld and the sugar dissolve.

### Step 2: Gelatin and Assembly
3. In a separate bowl, dissolve the **2 pkg raspberry jell-o** in **2 cups hot water**.
4. Let the gelatin mixture cool until it starts to set.
5. Fold in all the other prepared ingredients including the **1 cup whipping cream**, **1 cup miniature marshmallows**, and the cranberry-pineapple mixture.
6. Pour the combined mixture into a pan and refrigerate until fully set. Serve in squares on fresh lettuce.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Prepare up to 24 hours in advance and keep refrigerated until ready to slice and serve.
