# Bible Study Chicken Salad

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mix Salad
1. In a large bowl, combine **1 (13 oz.) can chicken breast in water** (or **1 1/2 cups cooked, chopped chicken**), **1/3 cup orzo**, **2 ribs celery**, **1 cup whole red or green seedless grapes**, **8 oz. crushed pineapple**, **10 dried apricots**, **1/3 cup white or regular raisins**, and **1/3 cup dried cranberries**. Mix all of these ingredients well.

### Step 2: Prepare Dressing and Combine
2. In a separate bowl, mix together **1/2 cup mayonnaise**, **1/2 cup sour cream**, **2 T. reserved pineapple juice**, **2 tsp. dried tarragon leaves**, **1 tsp. chopped fresh or dried parsley leaves**, and **salt & pepper** to taste.
3. Add the dressing to the salad ingredients and mix well until evenly coated.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Refrigerate, preferably overnight, to allow the salad to thoroughly blend flavors before serving chilled.
