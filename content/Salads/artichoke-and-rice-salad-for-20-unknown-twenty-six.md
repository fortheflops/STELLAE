# Artichoke and Rice Salad For 20

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** M. Lake | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A flavorful and crowd-pleasing cold rice and artichoke salad featuring marinated artichoke hearts, curry dressing, and vegetables. Perfectly suited for large gatherings and chilled to perfection.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 30 mins | 20 mins | 4 hours | 20 servings |

* Mixing bowl

| As needed | [[Lettuce leaf]] | For serving |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and Mixing
1. Cook **1 pkg Uncle Ben's chicken flavored rice**, leaving out butter. Allow to cool completely.
2. Add **4 sliced green onions**, **2 stalks sliced celery**, **1/2 chopped fine green pepper**, **12 sliced pimento olives**, and **1 small can sliced mushrooms** (drained) to the cooled rice.
3. Drain **2 jars marinated artichoke hearts**, saving the marinade. Cut the artichoke hearts in half and add them to the mixture.

### Step 2: Dressing and Chilling
4. Prepare the dressing by mixing **1/2 cup mayonnaise**, **3/4 tsp curry powder**, and the saved artichoke marinade together.
5. Add **1 large cooked and cubed chicken** to the rice and vegetable mixture.
6. Mix in the dressing until thoroughly combined.
7. Chill in the refrigerator for several hours until cold and set.
8. Serve on a **lettuce leaf**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Prepare and mix the salad up to a day in advance, keeping it tightly covered in the refrigerator until ready to serve.
