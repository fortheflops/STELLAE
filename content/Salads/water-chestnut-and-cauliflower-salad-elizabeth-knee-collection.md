# Water Chestnut And Cauliflower Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** Elizabeth Knee Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A delightful layered vegetable salad featuring cauliflower, garbanzo beans, and water chestnuts topped with a creamy dressing. This dish is best prepared ahead of time to let the flavors meld together.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | None | 7 hours | 6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Salad bowl
* Measuring cups

---
## Ingredients

### Salad Base
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 can | [[Water Chestnuts]] | Sliced or chopped |
| 1 head | [[Lettuce]] | Broken or shredded |
| 2 cups | [[Cauliflower]] | Bite-size bits |
| 1 cup | [[Garbanzo Beans]] | Chickpeas, drained |
| 1/4 cup | [[Onion]] | Grated or chopped |
| 1/4 cup | [[Green Pepper]] | Chopped |

### Dressing and Topping
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 cups | [[Mayonnaise]] | Hellmann's preferred |
| 1 level tbsp | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 1/3 cup | [[Parmesan Cheese]] | Grated |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Layering the Salad
1. Layer the **1 head lettuce** (broken or shredded) in a large salad bowl.
2. Add the **2 cups cauliflower**, **1 can water chestnuts**, **1 cup garbanzo beans**, **1/4 cup onion**, and **1/4 cup green pepper** on top of the lettuce.
3. Cover the salad with foil.

### Step 2: Dressing and Chilling
4. In a separate bowl, mix the **2 cups mayonnaise** and **1 level tbsp sugar**.
5. Spread the mayonnaise mixture evenly over the layered vegetables.
6. Sprinkle **1/3 cup Parmesan cheese** over the top.
7. Let set for several hours or overnight in the refrigerator until thoroughly chilled.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Prepare the entire salad up to 24 hours in advance and keep covered in the refrigerator until ready to serve.
