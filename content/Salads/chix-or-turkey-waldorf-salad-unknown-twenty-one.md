# Chix Or Turkey Waldorf Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty One | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A classic, refreshing Waldorf salad made with chicken or turkey, crisp apples, celery, and walnuts bound in creamy mayonnaise. Perfectly chilled and ready for a light lunch or gathering.*

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |

| 1/4 c | [[Red Apple]] | Chopped |
| 1/2 c | [[Celery]] | Sliced |
| 1/4 c | [[Walnuts]] | Chopped |
| 2 T | [[Raisins]] |  |
| 1 c | [[Green Grapes]] | Halved, optional |
| 1/2 c | [[Dates]] | Chopped, optional |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing and Chilling
1. In a mixing bowl, combine the **1/2 c mayo**, **2 c chopped, cooked chix or turkey**, **1/4 c chopped red apple**, **1/2 c sliced celery**, **1/4 c chopped walnuts**, and **2 T raisins** (along with optional **1 c green grapes, halved** and **1/2 c chopped dates** if desired).
2. Mix thoroughly until all ingredients are evenly coated.
3. Cover and refrigerate until well chilled and flavors are melded.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 1 day before serving.
