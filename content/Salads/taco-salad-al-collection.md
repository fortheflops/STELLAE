# Taco Salad

## Instructions

### Step 1: Cook the Meat
1. In a medium skillet, sauté **1 lb ground chuck** and **1/2 clove garlic** until the meat is browned and cooked through, about **10 minutes**. Drain excess fat.
2. Add the **1 can (4 oz) green chiles**, **1 can (1 lb) tomatoes**, **1 teaspoon salt**, and **1/8 teaspoon pepper**; mix well. Cook over low heat, uncovered, for about **30 minutes** until the mixture is thickened and flavorful.

### Step 2: Assemble and Serve
3. Just before serving, in a large chilled salad bowl, arrange the **2 quarts salad greens**, **1 cup grated cheddar cheese**, **1 package (6 oz) corn chips**, and **1 cup chopped green onion**.
4. Add the warm cooked meat mixture and toss lightly until well combined. Garnish with the **1 medium tomato coarsely chopped** and serve immediately.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The meat mixture can be prepared up to 2 days in advance and reheated before assembling. Assemble the salad just before serving to keep the corn chips and greens crisp.
