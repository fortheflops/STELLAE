# Retro Apricot Jello Salad with Cheddar Topping

> 📜 **Collection:** General Archive | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A classic mid-century congealed salad featuring an orange gelatin base filled with apricots, pineapple, and marshmallows. It is finished with a creamy pineapple topping and a savory sprinkle of sharp cheddar cheese.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Salad Base
1. Drain the **1 (17-oz) can apricots** and **1 large can crushed pineapple**, reserving the juices separately.
2. Chop the **apricots** and chill them along with the **drained pineapple**.
3. In a large bowl, dissolve the **2 pkg. orange-flavored gelatin** in **2 cups hot water**.
4. Stir in the **1 cup apricot juice** and chill until the mixture is partially firm and syrupy.
5. Fold in the chilled **apricots**, **drained pineapple**, and **1 cup miniature marshmallows**.
6. Pour the mixture into a 9x13 pan and chill until completely firm.

### Step 2: Prepare the Topping and Assemble
7. In a saucepan, combine the **1/2 cup sugar** and **3 T flour**.
8. Blend in the **1 egg** and **2 T butter**.
9. Stir in the **1 cup pineapple juice** and cook over low heat, stirring constantly, until the mixture is thickened and smooth.
10. Remove from heat and let the mixture cool thoroughly.
11. Gently fold the **1 cup whipped whipping cream** into the cooled pineapple mixture.
12. Spread the topping evenly over the congealed **gelatin** base.
13. Sprinkle the **3/4 cup grated cheddar cheese** over the top and chill thoroughly until set.
14. Cut into squares and serve on a bed of **lettuce**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This salad is best prepared at least 4 hours in advance to ensure the gelatin is fully set. Keep refrigerated until ready to serve.
