## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
|1 lb.|[[Vermicelli (Break in 1/3's, Cook - Do Not Rinse)]]|
|2 T.|[[Lemon Juice (Fresh)]]|
|1 T.|[[Accent]]|
|4 T.|[[Oil]]|
|1 lg. can|[[Black Olives (Sliced)]]|
|1 C.|[[Finely Chopped Celery]]|
|1 med.|[[Bell Pepper (Chopped)]]|
|1 med.|[[Onion (Chopped, Purple-Good)]]|
|1 sm. jar|[[Pimentos (Diced & Drained)]]|
|2 C.|[[Mayonnaise]]|
___
### Instructions
1. Mix vermicelli, lemon juice, Accent, and oil while hot and refrigerate overnight.
2. Add black olives and celery.
3. Add chopped bell pepper, chopped onion, diced and drained pimentos, and mayonnaise.
4. Mix and refrigerate.
