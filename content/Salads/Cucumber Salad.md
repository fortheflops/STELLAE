## Instructions

### Step 1: Combine and Toss
1. In a mixing bowl, combine the **sliced cucumbers**, **sour cream**, **1 tablespoon minced onion**, **1 tablespoon fresh lemon juice**, **1/4 teaspoon dill weed**, and **1/4 teaspoon seasoned salt** until the cucumbers are evenly coated and crisp-tender.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Best served immediately, or chilled in the refrigerator for up to 1 hour before serving to let the flavors meld.
