# Hot Crab Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Gena | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A warm and savory baked seafood salad featuring green pepper, onion, celery, shrimp, and crab, topped with golden buttered bread crumbs. Perfect for serving hot with crackers or toast points.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 25 mins | None | 6–8 Servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 11x7 inch Pyrex baking dish
* Medium bowl
* Measuring cups and spoons
* Chef's knife
* Cutting board

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Topping
1. Preheat your oven to **350°F** (**175°C**). Lightly grease an **11x7 inch Pyrex dish** or similar baking dish.
2. Prepare the topping by tossing about **1/2 cup of plain bread crumbs** with **1-2 Tbsp of melted butter or margarine**. Set aside.
3. Finely chop the **1 large green pepper**, **1 medium onion**, and **1/4 cup celery**. Ensure the **1/4 cup fresh steamed shrimp** is cooled and chopped, and flake or chop the **1/4 cup artificial crab meat**.

### Step 2: Combine and Bake
4. In a medium bowl, combine the chopped **1 large green pepper**, **1 medium onion**, **1/4 cup celery**, **1/4 cup mayonnaise**, **1 tsp salt**, **1 Tbsp Worcestershire sauce**, chopped **1/4 cup fresh steamed shrimp**, and **1/4 cup artificial crab meat**.
5. Mix all ingredients together thoroughly until well combined.
6. Spread the mixture evenly into the prepared **11x7 inch baking dish**.
7. Sprinkle the prepared **buttered bread crumbs** evenly over the top of the crab mixture.
8. Bake for approximately **20-30 minutes**, or until the mixture is heated through, bubbly around the edges, and the bread crumb topping is **golden brown**.
9. Serve hot as an appetizer dip with crackers, toast points, or vegetable sticks.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the unbaked salad in the dish and store covered in the refrigerator for up to 24 hours before adding the bread crumbs and baking.
