## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare Dressing
1. Mix all dressing ingredients together (early): combine **1/4 cup vegetable oil**, **2 tbsp sugar**, **2 tbsp vinegar**, **1/2 tsp salt**, and **1 dash hot red pepper sauce**. Shake in a jar.

### Step 2: Toss Salad
2. In a large salad bowl, combine **1/4 head iceberg lettuce**, **1/4 bunch romaine**, **1/4 cup sliced almonds**, **2 green onions**, and **1 (11 oz) can mandarin oranges**. Pour dressing over remaining ingredients, toss gently until well coated, and add **bacon bits** if desired.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The dressing can be mixed ahead and stored in a jar in the refrigerator, but wait to dress the salad until just before serving to maintain crispness.
