# Cherry Jell-O Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A classic retro Jell-O salad featuring cherry gelatin, sweet pie filling, crisp apple, and crunchy celery. This delightful molded side dish or dessert offers a wonderful contrast of textures.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 0 mins | 4 hours | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Measuring cups
* Jell-O mold or 8x8 inch dish

---
## Ingredients

### Salad Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 large or 2 small | [[Cherry Jell-O]] | Dry gelatin powder |
| 1 can | [[Cherry Pie Filling]] | Hot |
| 1 | [[Red Apple]] | Unpeeled, cut up in small pieces |
| 1/3 cup | [[Celery]] | Cut up |
| 1/2 cup | [[Nuts]] | Ground |
| 3 cups | [[Boiling Water]] | Boiling |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing and Initial Set
1. Add **3 cups boiling water** to the **1 large or 2 small cherry jell-o** and stir until fully dissolved.
2. Add the **1 can cherry pie filling** while hot. Let set slightly until it begins to thicken.

### Step 2: Adding Mix-ins and Final Set
3. Stir in the **1/3 cup celery**, the **1 red apple** (with skin on, cut up in small pieces), and the **1/2 cup nuts**.
4. Let set completely in the refrigerator until firm and chilled through.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Can be prepared up to 24 hours in advance and kept refrigerated until ready to serve.
