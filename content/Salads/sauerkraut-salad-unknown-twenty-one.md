# Sauerkraut Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty One | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A crisp and tangy marinated salad featuring sauerkraut, crisp vegetables, and a sweet-and-sour dressing. This refreshing side dish develops its full flavor after resting in the refrigerator.*

## Ingredients

### Salad Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 16oz cans | [[Sauerkraut]] | Rinsed and drained |
| 1 | [[Green bell pepper]] | Chopped |
| 1/2-3/4 cup | [[Sugar]] | None |
| 1/2 cup | [[Oil]] | None |
| 1 Tbsp | [[Celery seed]] | None |
| 1 | [[Onion]] | Sliced thin |
| 1 stick | [[Celery]] | Sliced |
| 1/3 cup | [[Water]] | None |
| 2/3 cup | [[Vinegar]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Combine
1. Rinse **2 16oz cans sauerkraut** thoroughly with cold water in a colander, then drain well.
2. Combine the drained sauerkraut with **1 green pepper**, **1/2-3/4 c sugar**, **1/2 oil**, **1 T celery seed**, **1 onion**, **1 stick celery**, **1/3 c water**, and **2/3 c vinegar** in a mixing bowl, stirring until the sugar dissolves.

### Step 2: Marinate
3. Cover the bowl and marinate for 24 hours in the refrigerator until flavors are fully melded and crisp-tender.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This salad must be made at least 24 hours in advance to allow the flavors to properly marinate in the refrigerator.
