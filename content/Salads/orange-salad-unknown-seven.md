# Orange Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Seven | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A classic sweet whipped dessert salad featuring orange gelatin, creamy cottage cheese, whipped topping, mandarin oranges, and crushed pineapple. Perfect for potlucks and holiday gatherings.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 0 mins | 1 hour | 6 to 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Spatula
* Colander

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 box (3 oz) | [[Orange Gelatin]] | dry mix (such as Jell-O) |
| 1 pint | [[Cottage Cheese]] | None |
| 1 small carton (8 oz) | [[Whipped Topping]] | such as Cool Whip, thawed |
| 1 can (11 oz) | [[Mandarin Oranges]] | drained |
| 1 can (8 oz) | [[Crushed Pineapple]] | drained |

---

## Instructions

### Mixing & Chilling
1. In a large mixing bowl, fold together **1 box (3 oz) dry orange gelatin**, **1 pint cottage cheese**, and **1 small carton (8 oz) whipped topping** until uniformly blended.
2. Gently stir in **1 can (11 oz) drained mandarin oranges** and **1 can (8 oz) drained crushed pineapple** until all ingredients are evenly distributed.
3. Cover and chill in the refrigerator for at least 1 hour until the salad is set and chilled through before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Can be prepared up to 24 hours in advance and stored covered in the refrigerator.
