# Raspberry Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A refreshing and vibrant salad featuring fresh raspberries, mandarin oranges, and crisp water chestnuts. Tossed with a sweet raspberry dressing, this easy dish is ready in minutes.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 0 mins | None | 4 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Salad bowl
* Tongs

---
## Ingredients

### Salad Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 bag | [[Italian Blend Lettuce]] | Rinsed |
| 1 can | [[Mandarin Oranges]] | Drained |
| 1 box | [[Fresh Raspberries]] | Rinsed |
| 1 can | [[Water Chestnuts]] | Sliced |
| 1 bottle | [[Girard's Salad Dressing]] | Raspberry flavor |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Assembly
1. Combine the **1 bag Italian blend lettuce**, **1 can mandarin oranges**, **1 box fresh raspberries**, and **1 can water chestnuts** in a large salad bowl.
2. Toss gently until well mixed and vibrant, then drizzle with **1 bottle Girard's salad dressing** just before serving until lightly coated.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Keep ingredients unmixed and refrigerated until ready to serve to prevent the greens from becoming soggy.
