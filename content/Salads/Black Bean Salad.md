# Black Bean Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** Patsy Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A vibrant and refreshing black bean salad packed with crisp bell peppers, sweet corn, and a zesty cilantro-lemon dressing. Perfect for potlucks and warm-weather gatherings.*

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 Cans | [[Black Beans]] | Drained or rinsed |
| 1 | [[Red Bell Pepper]] | Chopped |
| 3 | [[Green Onions]] | Chopped |
| 2 | [[Ripe Plum Tomatoes]] | Chopped |
| 1 Can | [[Corn]] | Drained |
| As needed | [[Black Olives]] | Sliced |
| As needed | [[Cheese]] | Optional, cubed or crumbled |
| As needed | [[Pickle Relish]] | Optional |
| As needed | [[Peppers]] | Optional, diced |

### Dressing
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 6 Tbsp | [[Extra Virgin Olive Oil]] | High quality |
| 3 Tbsp | [[Lemon Juice]] | Freshly squeezed |
| 5 Tbsp | [[Cilantro]] | Chopped |
| 1 Tsp | [[Black Pepper]] | Freshly ground |
| ¾ Tsp | [[Salt]] | To taste |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep the Vegetables
1. In a large mixing bowl, combine the **2 cans black beans**, **1 red bell pepper**, **3 green onions**, **2 ripe plum tomatoes**, **1 can corn**, **black olives**, **cheese**, **pickle relish**, and **peppers** after ensuring all fresh vegetables are thoroughly chopped.

### Step 2: Make the Sauce and Combine
2. In a small bowl, whisk together the **6 tbsp extra virgin olive oil**, **3 tbsp lemon juice**, **5 tbsp cilantro**, **1 tsp black pepper**, and **¾ tsp salt** until fully emulsified.
3. Pour the dressing over the bean mixture and toss well to coat. Let the salad come to room temperature and marinate briefly until flavors meld.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store sealed in the refrigerator for up to 3 days; let sit at room temperature for 15 minutes before serving.
