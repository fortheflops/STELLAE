## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Dressing
1. In a large bowl, mix **¾ cup wine vinegar**, **½ cup olive oil**, **2 tablespoons sugar**, **1 teaspoon salt**, **½ teaspoon oregano**, and **¼ teaspoon pepper**.

### Step 2: Combine Vegetables and Simmer
2. Add **1 ¼ cups water** into a large saucepan along with the prepared dressing mixture.
3. Separate **½ small cauliflower** into florets, cut **2 carrots** into 2-inch strips, cut **2 stalks celery** into 1-inch strips, cut **1 small green pepper** into strips, and chop the drained **4 oz jar pimentos**, then add them to the saucepan along with the **1 3oz jar green olives**.
4. Simmer for **5 minutes** until tender-crisp.

### Step 3: Chill and Serve
5. Chill in the refrigerator for **24 hours** to fully develop flavors.
6. Drain well before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This salad must be made at least 24 hours in advance to marinate properly and can be stored in the refrigerator for up to 5 days.
