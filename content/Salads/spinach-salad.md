# Spinach Salad

## Ingredients

| 1 tbsp | [[Lemon Juice]] | Fresh |
| 1 pkg | [[Frozen Chopped Spinach]] | 10 oz, cooked and drained |
| 1 cup | [[Chopped Celery]] | Chopped |
| 1/4 cup | [[Chopped Onion]] | Chopped |
| 1 small carton | [[Cottage Cheese]] | Small curd |
| 1 cup | [[Mayonnaise]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Preparation
1. Mix **1 small pkg lemon Jell-O**, **1 cup boiling water**, **scant 1 tsp salt**, and **1 tbsp lemon juice** together, then set aside to cool slightly.
2. Cook and drain **1 pkg frozen chopped spinach (10 oz)**.
3. Stir all remaining ingredients including the **1 cup chopped celery**, **1/4 cup chopped onion**, **1 small carton cottage cheese**, and **1 cup mayonnaise** into the gelatin mixture until well combined.
4. Refrigerate overnight until fully set and firm to the touch.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This salad must be made the day before to allow the gelatin to set properly overnight in the refrigerator.
