# Vegetable Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A simple and hearty classic vegetable salad featuring peas, crunchy nuts, and hard-boiled eggs. This traditional recipe is perfect for gatherings and can be easily customized with your favorite dressing.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 10 mins | None | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Knife
* Cutting board

---
## Ingredients

### Salad Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 cans | [[Peas]] | Drained |
| 1 cup | [[Nuts]] | Chopped |
| 8 | [[Hard-boiled eggs]] | Peeled and chopped |
| To taste | [[Celery]] | Diced |

### Dressing
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| As needed | [[Dressing]] | Your choice of mayonnaise or vinaigrette |

---

## Instructions

### Assembly
1. In a large mixing bowl, combine the **2 cans peas**, **1 cup nuts**, **8 hard-boiled eggs**, and diced **celery**.
2. Gently fold in your preferred **dressing** until the salad reaches your desired consistency.
3. Serve chilled until the flavors are well incorporated and the salad is cool to the touch.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Prepare the salad components ahead of time, but add the dressing just before serving to maintain the crunch of the nuts and celery.
