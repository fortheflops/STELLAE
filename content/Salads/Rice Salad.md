# Rice Salad

## Instructions

### Step 1: Combine the Salad Base
1. In a large bowl, combine **3 cups cooked [[Rice]]**, **1 cup cooked [[Wild Rice]]**, **1 cup crumbled [[Feta Cheese]]**, **½ cup chopped [[Red Onion]]**, **⅓ cup chopped [[Green Bell Pepper]]**, **⅓ cup chopped [[Red Bell Pepper]]**, **⅓ cup chopped [[Yellow Bell Pepper]]**, **¼ cup chopped fresh [[Parsley]]**, **¼ cup sliced [[Black Olives]]**, **¼ cup sliced [[Green Olives]]**, **2 tbsp [[Capers]]**, and **1 15 oz can [[Cannellini Beans]]**.

### Step 2: Toss the Salad
2. Toss the mixture well until all ingredients are evenly distributed.

### Step 3: Prepare the Dressing
3. In a separate small bowl, combine **3 tbsp [[White Wine Vinegar]]**, **2 tbsp [[Water]]**, **1 tbsp [[Extra-Virgin Olive Oil]]**, **1 tsp [[Dijon Mustard]]**, **½ tsp [[Salt]]**, **1 clove minced [[Garlic]]**, and **¼ tsp [[Pepper]]**.

### Step 4: Whisk and Dress
4. Stir the dressing well with a wire whisk until emulsified, then pour it over the rice mixture.

### Step 5: Chill and Serve
5. Toss well until the dressing is fully incorporated, then cover and chill in the refrigerator until flavors meld.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store covered in the refrigerator for up to 3 days. Toss gently before serving.
