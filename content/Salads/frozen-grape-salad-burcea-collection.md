# Frozen Grape Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** Burcea Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A delightful and refreshing frozen grape salad made with cream cheese, marshmallows, and Tokay grapes. Perfect for warm weather gatherings or as a sweet side dish.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | None | 4 hours | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Electric mixer
* Refrig. tray

---
## Ingredients

### Salad Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 - 3 oz | [[Cream Cheese]] | Softened |
| 2 Tbsp | [[Mayonnaise]] |  |
| 2 Tbsp | [[Pineapple Syrup]] | Reserved from canned pineapple |
| 24 | [[Marshmallows]] | Quartered |
| 1 #2 can (2 1/2 cups) | [[Pineapple Bits]] | Drained |
| 1 cup | [[Heavy Cream]] | Whipped |
| 2 cups | [[Tokay Grapes]] | Halved and seeded |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing the Base
1. Soften the **2 - 3 oz cream cheese** and blend with **2 Tbsp mayonnaise**.
2. Beat in the **2 Tbsp pineapple syrup**.

### Step 2: Combining and Freezing
3. Add the **24 marshmallows** and drained **1 #2 can (2 1/2 cups) pineapple bits**.
4. Fold in the whipped **1 cup heavy cream** and halved **2 cups Tokay grapes**.
5. Pour into a quart refrig. tray. Freeze until firm.
6. Cut in squares. Makes 8 servings.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Keep frozen until ready to serve; can be prepared up to a day in advance.
