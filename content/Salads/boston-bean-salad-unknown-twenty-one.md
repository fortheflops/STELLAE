# Boston Bean Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty One | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A refreshing and tangy Boston bean salad featuring a mix of three beans tossed with celery, green onions, and a sweet molasses-vinegar dressing. Perfect for picnics and side dishes, this chilled salad develops its best flavors after resting in the refrigerator.*

| 1 c | [[Celery]] | sliced |
| 1/2 c | [[Green onion]] | sliced |

### Dressing Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 c | [[Vinegar]] | None |
| 1/4 c | [[Molasses]] | None |
| 1/4 c | [[Olive oil]] | None |
| 1 T | [[Dijon mustard]] | None |
| 1/4 t | [[Black pepper]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Making the Dressing and Salad
1. Mix **1/2 c vinegar**, **1/4 c molasses**, **1/4 c olive oil**, **1 T Dijon**, and **1/4 t pepper** together in a small bowl.
2. Combine the **15 oz. each navy, red & black beans**, **1 c celery**, and **1/2 c green onion** in a large mixing bowl.
3. Pour the dressing over the bean mixture and toss well to coat.

### Step 2: Chilling and Serving
4. Chill the salad in the refrigerator for **4 to 24 hours**, stirring occasionally until the flavors are fully blended and the beans have absorbed the dressing.
5. Serve chilled over crisp lettuce.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in the refrigerator for up to 24 hours, stirring occasionally, to allow the flavors to develop fully before serving.
