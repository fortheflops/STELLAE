# Four Bean Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** LOC | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A classic marinated four bean salad featuring green, wax, kidney, and garbanzo beans tossed in a tangy vinaigrette. Chill overnight for the best flavor development before serving.*

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |

| 1 Can | [[Green Beans]] | Cut, drained |
| 1 Can | [[Wax Beans]] | Cut, drained |
| 1 Can | [[Kidney Beans]] | Drained |
| 1 Can | [[Garbanzo Beans]] | Chickpeas, drained |
| 1 Medium | [[Onion]] | Chopped or sliced |

### Dressing
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/3 Cup | [[Oil]] | None |
| 2/3 Cup | [[Vinegar]] | White |
| 1 Teaspoon | [[Salt]] | None |
| Dash | [[Pepper]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Combine Beans
1. Drain all the canned **1 can green beans**, **1 can wax beans**, **1 can kidney beans**, and **1 can garbanzo beans** using a colander.
2. Chop or slice the **1 medium onion**.
3. In a large bowl, combine the drained beans and the chopped **1 medium onion**.

### Step 2: Make the Dressing and Marinate
4. In a separate bowl or jar, mix together the **1/3 cup oil**, **2/3 cup white vinegar**, **1 teaspoon salt**, **dash pepper**, and the **1 can sweet pepper flakes** (including their liquid, unless you prefer to drain them).
5. Pour the dressing mixture over the beans and onion, then mix well until evenly coated.
6. Chill in the refrigerator for several hours or overnight for best flavor before serving until the flavors are fully melded.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store sealed in the refrigerator for up to 5 days; the flavor actually improves as it marinates longer.
