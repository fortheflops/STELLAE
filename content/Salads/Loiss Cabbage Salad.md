# Lois's Cabbage Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Lois | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A crisp and tangy vintage cabbage salad layered with fresh onions and green peppers, marinated in a simple oil and vinegar dressing. This refreshing side dish can be easily adjusted for the perfect balance of flavor.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | None | 1 hour | 6–8 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large mixing bowl
* Cutting board
* Chef's knife
* Measuring cup

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| As needed | [[Cabbage]] | Chopped |
| As needed | [[Onions]] | Sliced |
| To taste | [[Salt]] | For layering |
| As needed | [[Green Peppers]] | Sliced or diced |
| ½ Cup | [[Vinegar]] | Divided |
| ½ Cup | [[Oil]] | Divided |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Layering and Salting
1. Salt the layers of **[[Cabbage]]** as you chop it, using quite a bit of **[[Salt]]**.
2. Top the cabbage with a layer of **[[Onions]]**, more **[[Salt]]**, and **[[Green Peppers]]**.

### Step 2: Dressing and Marinating
3. Stir in the **½ cup [[Oil]]** and **½ cup [[Vinegar]]**, ensuring a half oil and half vinegar ratio.
4. Leave the mixture out of the refrigerator, tossing and stirring several times until flavors meld before placing it into the refrigerator.
5. If the salad becomes too sour, add a splash of water and stir until balanced.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store sealed in the refrigerator for up to 3 days, tossing occasionally before serving.
