# Cranberry Sour Cream Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** Lisa Stephens Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A classic chilled gelatin salad blending raspberry jello, tangy sour cream, and whole cranberry sauce for a refreshing holiday side dish. Quick to assemble and ideal for making ahead of time.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 0 mins | 2 hours | 6 servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Measuring cups
* Whisk
* Serving dish

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 pkg | [[Raspberry Gelatin]] | None |
| 1 cup | [[Hot Water]] | or hot [[Cranberry Juice]] |
| 1 can | [[Whole Cranberry Sauce]] | None |
| 8 oz | [[Sour Cream]] | None |
| 1/2 cup | [[Nuts]] | optional, chopped |

---

## Instructions

### Assembly & Chilling
1. Dissolve the **1 pkg raspberry gelatin** in **1 cup hot water** (or hot **cranberry juice**) until completely dissolved.
2. Mix in the **1 can whole cranberry sauce** until evenly combined.
3. Allow the mixture to cool completely, then fold in the **8 oz sour cream** and optional **1/2 cup nuts**.
4. Pour into a mold or serving dish and refrigerate until firm and completely set, about 2 hours.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Can be made up to 24-48 hours ahead of time and kept covered in the refrigerator.
