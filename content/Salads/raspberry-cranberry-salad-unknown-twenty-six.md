# Raspberry-Cranberry Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** N. L. | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A delightful and fruity gelatin salad combining bright raspberry and jellied cranberry sauce. This refreshing side dish pairs wonderfully with poultry or pork.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 0 mins | 4 hours | 6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Bowl
* Mold
* Fork

---
## Ingredients

### Salad Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 (3 oz) | [[Jell-O]] | Red gelatin packages |
| 1 cup | [[Water]] | Boiling |
| 1 can | [[Cranberry Sauce]] | Jellied |
| 2 (10 oz) | [[Raspberries]] | Frozen, partially thawed |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation
1. Dissolve **2 (3 oz) Jell-O** packages in **1 cup water** (boiling).
2. Add **1 can cranberry sauce** which has been mashed with a fork.
3. Add **2 (10 oz) raspberries**, stirring gently until combined.
4. Pour into a mold, pan, or dish and chill until set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Can be prepared up to a day in advance and kept refrigerated until fully set and ready to serve.
