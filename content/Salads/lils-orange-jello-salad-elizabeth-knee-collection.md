# Lil's Orange Jello Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** Elizabeth Knee Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Lil | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A classic mid-century gelatin salad featuring orange jello, crushed pineapple, and chopped pecans, topped with whipped topping and a surprising sprinkle of parmesan cheese. This sweet and savory dish is a nostalgic crowd-pleaser perfect for family gatherings.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 5 mins | 4 hours | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Saucepan
* Pyrex dish
* Mixing bowl
* Spatula

---
## Ingredients

### Salad Base
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 large package | [[Orange Jello]] | dry gelatin mix |
| 2 cups | [[Cold Water]] | none |
| 1 tall can | [[Crushed Pineapple]] | undrained |

### Toppings & Mix-ins
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 8 oz | [[Cool Whip]] | regular size, thawed |
| 1 cup | [[Pecans]] | chopped |
| To taste | [[Parmesan Cheese]] | grated, for sprinkling |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Cook and Cool
1. In a medium saucepan, combine the **1 large package orange jello**, **2 cups cold water**, and **1 tall can crushed pineapple** with its juice.
2. Cook the mixture over medium heat for **five minutes**, stirring occasionally. Be careful to **do not boil**.
3. Remove the saucepan from the heat and let the mixture **cool completely** to room temperature before proceeding.

### Step 2: Assemble and Chill
4. Gently fold the **8 oz Cool Whip** and **1 cup chopped pecans** into the cooled jello mixture until well combined and uniform in color.
5. Pour the mixture into a **long pyrex dish**, smoothing the top with a spatula.
6. Sprinkle the top evenly with **parmesan cheese** to taste.
7. Place in the refrigerator and chill for at least **4 hours** until completely set and firm to the touch.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This salad can be prepared up to 24 hours in advance. Keep covered with plastic wrap in the refrigerator until ready to serve.
