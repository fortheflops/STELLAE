# Veg Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Marcile | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A crisp and flavorful vegetable salad featuring fresh cauliflower and broccoli tossed in a tangy mayonnaise dressing. Perfect for potlucks and family gatherings.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | None | 30 mins | 6 servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Knife
* Cutting board
* Whisk

## Ingredients

### Vegetables
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 head | [[Cauliflower]] | Fresh, chopped |
| 1 or 2 stalks | [[Broccoli]] | Fresh, raw, chopped |
| 1 | [[Green Pepper]] | Chopped |
| 1 small jar | [[Green Olives]] | Chopped |
| 1 can | [[Pitted Ripe Olives]] | Chopped |

### Dressing
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 cup | [[Miracle Whip]] | Can substitute mayonnaise |
| 2 Tbsp | [[Vinegar]] | None |
| 1 tsp | [[Salt]] | None |
| 2 tsp | [[Sugar]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep Vegetables
1. Chop the **1 head cauliflower**, **1 or 2 stalks broccoli**, and **1 green pepper** into bite-sized pieces.
2. Chop the **1 small jar green olives** and **1 can pitted ripe olives**.

### Step 2: Mix Dressing
3. In a large mixing bowl, whisk together the **1 cup miracle whip** (or mayonnaise), **2 Tbsp vinegar**, **1 tsp salt**, and **2 tsp sugar** until smooth.

### Step 3: Combine and Chill
4. Add all the chopped vegetables and olives to the dressing mixture and toss until evenly coated.
5. Chill in the refrigerator for at least 30 minutes to let the flavors meld before serving until crisp and well-chilled.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 2 days before serving for the best texture and flavor fusion.
