# The McFarland Family's Pasta Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** UK5 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A vibrant and versatile pasta salad featuring sharp cheddar, crisp vegetables, and a tangy Italian dressing. Best prepared in advance to let the flavors meld together.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 10 mins | 12 hours | 8–10 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large mixing bowl
* Colander
* Cheese grater
* Chef's knife
* Cutting board

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 box | [[Rotini]] (or other) pasta | cooked and cooled |
| 8-16 ounces | Block [[Cheddar Cheese]] | shredded |
| 2 large (or 10 baby) | [[Carrots]] | shredded |
| 1 can | Black [[Olives]] | sliced |
| 4-5 | [[Green Onions]] | chopped |
| 1/2 | English [[Cucumber]] | shredded, optional |
| 4-5 leaves | Fresh [[Basil]] | chopped, optional |
| 1/2 - 3/4 bottle | [[Italian Dressing]] | to taste |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Italian Seasoning]] | none |
| To Taste | [[Salt]] and [[Pepper]] | to taste |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Combine Ingredients
1. In a large bowl, combine the cooked **1 box rotini (or other) pasta**, **8-16 ounces block cheddar cheese**, **2 large (or 10 baby) carrots**, **1 can black olives**, **4-5 green onions**, **1/2 English cucumber**, and **4-5 leaves fresh basil**.

### Step 2: Toss and Season
2. Pour the **1/2 - 3/4 bottle Italian dressing** over the mixture. Sprinkle with **1 teaspoon Italian seasoning**, **to taste salt**, and **to taste pepper**.

### Step 3: Mix and Chill
3. Mix well until all ingredients are evenly coated. Refrigerate for half a day or longer until the pasta absorbs the flavors and the salad is chilled throughout.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Let refrigerate for half a day or longer for optimal flavor; can be stored in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days.
