## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation
1. Chop the **lettuce** and place in a salad bowl.
2. Toss the **lettuce** in **2/3 c. olive oil**, **1/4 c. lemon juice**, **salt**, **pepper**, **garlic salt**, and lots of **parmesan cheese**.

### Step 2: Making the Dressing
3. In a jar or small bowl, combine **1/4 c. lemon juice**, **2/3 c. olive oil**, a dash of **oregano**, a dash of **garlic salt**, **1/2 T. anchovy paste**, **1/4 tsp. basil**, and **1/4 tsp. parsley**.
4. Shake well until emulsified.

### Step 3: Assembly
5. Add **garbanzo beans**, **feta cheese**, **Calamata olives**, bottled **peppers**, and **garlic croutons**.
6. Shake **parmesan cheese** into the salad first, then toss everything together until well coated and serve.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Keep dressing separate and toss just before serving to maintain crispness.
