# Lillian's Potato Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** The Loraine Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Lillian | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *Discover a classic vintage potato salad recipe featuring hard-boiled eggs, crisp celery, and a tangy relish dressing from The Loraine Collection archives. This nostalgic side dish delivers authentic homemade flavor perfect for family gatherings.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 20 mins | 15 mins | 6-8 Servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Medium saucepan
* Knife
* Cutting board

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |

| 1 | [[Onion]] | Chopped |
| 1 tsp | [[Pickle Relish]] | None |
| 1 cap | Vinegar | None |
| 3 | [[Celery]] Stalks | Chopped |
| 1/2 tsp | Mustard | None |
| 5 | Hard Boiled [[Eggs]] | Boiled and cooled |
| As needed | [[Salt]] | To taste |
| As needed | [[Pepper]] | To taste |
| As needed | Mayonnaise | Added to desired creaminess |
| As needed | Paprika | For garnish |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep the Base Mixture
1. Mix together the **1 onion**, **1 tsp pickle relish**, **1 cap vinegar**, **salt**, and **pepper** in a small bowl and set aside.

### Step 2: Boil and Chop
2. Boil and cool the **4-5 medium potatoes** and **5 hard boiled eggs**. Cut the potatoes and place them in a large mixing bowl.

### Step 3: Combine and Dress
3. Add the onion and relish mixture from step one to the potatoes. Mix in **additional mayonnaise** to reach your desired consistency.

### Step 4: Incorporate Eggs and Garnish
4. Slice the **5 hard boiled eggs**. Set half of the egg slices aside, and gently fold the remaining half into the potato mixture until well combined. Place the rest of the egg slices on top of the salad and sprinkle lightly with **paprika** until visually appealing.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store covered in the refrigerator for up to 3 days. For best flavor, let it chill for at least an hour before serving.
