# Tropical Chicken Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A delightful and refreshing tropical chicken salad featuring tender chicken, fresh fruit, and a hint of curry. Perfect for warm weather lunches or light dinners.*

| As needed | [[Salad greens]] | Optional, for serving |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing and Chilling
1. Place **2 cups cubed cooked chicken** and **1 cup chopped celery** in a large bowl.
2. In a separate small container, combine **1 cup mayonnaise** and **1/2 to 1 tsp curry powder**.
3. Add the mayonnaise mixture to the chicken mixture and mix well.
4. Cover and chill in the refrigerator for at least **30 minutes** to allow the flavors to meld.

### Step 2: Assembly and Serving
5. Before serving, gently toss in **1 can chunk pineapple**, **2 large firm bananas**, **1 can mandarin oranges**, and **1/2 cup flaked coconut**.
6. Serve the mixture over a bed of **salad greens** if desired.
7. Sprinkle with **3/4 cup salted peanuts or cashew halves** right before serving for optimal crunch.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Combine and chill the chicken, celery, mayonnaise, and curry powder up to 24 hours in advance; fold in the fresh fruits, coconut, and nuts just before serving to maintain texture.
