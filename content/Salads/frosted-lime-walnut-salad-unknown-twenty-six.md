# Frosted Lime Walnut Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A delightful and refreshing retro molded salad featuring lime Jell-O, crushed pineapple, cottage cheese, and crunchy walnuts, topped with a creamy cheese frosting. Perfect for potlucks and holiday gatherings.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 0 mins | 4 hours | 6-8 servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* 9x5-inch loaf pan
* Electric mixer

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Making the Salad Base
1. Dissolve **1 pkg lime Jell-O** in **1 cup boiling water**.
2. Chill until syrupy, then stir in **1 can crushed pineapple**, **1 cup small curd cottage cheese**, **1/2 cup finely sliced celery**, **1 Tbsp chopped pimentos**, and **1/2 cup chopped walnuts**.
3. Turn mixture into a **9x5-inch loaf pan** rinsed in cold water.
4. Chill in the refrigerator until firm and set.

### Step 2: Preparing the Frosting and Serving
5. For the frosting, blend and beat until smooth **1 pkg (3 oz) cream cheese**, **1 Tbsp mayonnaise**, and **1 tsp lemon juice**.
6. When the salad is firm, frost the top and decorate with **walnut halves**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Prepare the salad a day in advance and keep refrigerated; apply the cream cheese frosting just before serving for the best appearance.
