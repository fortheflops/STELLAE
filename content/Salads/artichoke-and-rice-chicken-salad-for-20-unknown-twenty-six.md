# Artichoke And Rice Chicken Salad For 20

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A delightful and filling crowd-pleasing salad featuring chicken, rice, artichoke hearts, and a hint of curry. Perfect for large gatherings and chilled to perfection.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 30 mins | 20 mins | 2 hours | 20 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Saucepan
* Knife
* Cutting board

---
## Ingredients

### Salad Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 pkg. | [[Uncle Ben's Chicken Flavored Rice]] | Cooked without butter |
| 4 | [[Green Onions]] | Thinly sliced |
| 2 stalks | [[Celery]] | Sliced |
| 1/2 | [[Green Pepper]] | Choped fine |
| 1 jar | [[Pimento Stuffed Olives]] | Sliced |
| 1 small can | [[Mushrooms]] | Sliced, drained |
| 2 oz | [[Marinated Artichoke Hearts]] | Drained, saving marinade, cut in halves |
| 3/4 tsp | [[Curry Powder]] | None |
| 1/2 cup | [[Mayonnaise]] | None |
| 1 lb | [[Chicken]] | Cubed and cooked |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Mix Base
1. Cook the **1 pkg. Uncle Ben's chicken flavored rice** with no butter, then cool.
2. Add the **4 green onions**, **2 stalks celery**, **1/2 green pepper**, **1 jar pimento stuffed olives**, and **1 small can mushrooms** to the cooled rice.
3. Drain the **2 oz marinated artichoke hearts**, saving the marinade, and cut the hearts in halves. Add them to the mixture along with the **3/4 tsp curry powder**.

### Step 2: Dressing and Combining
4. Add the saved artichoke marinade to the **1/2 cup mayonnaise** dressing and combine thoroughly.
5. Add the **1 lb chicken** to the rice and chicken mixture.
6. Mix in the dressing and chill for several hours before serving.
7. Serve on a **lettuce leaf**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Chill in the refrigerator for several hours or overnight before serving to let the flavors meld together.
