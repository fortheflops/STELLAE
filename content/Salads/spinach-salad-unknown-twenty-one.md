# Spinach Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty One | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Salads
> *A delightful and unique spinach salad paired with crunchy pecans, creamy cottage cheese, and a zesty cider dressing. Perfect as a refreshing side dish for any family meal.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | None | None | 6 servings |

* Mixing bowl

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 lb | [[Spinach]] | Fresh |
| 1/2 cup | [[Pecans]] | Chopped |
| 1 lb | [[Cottage Cheese]] | Creamed |
| 1/2 cup | [[Sour Cream]] | None |
| 1/2 cup | [[Sugar]] | None |
| 3 tbsp | [[Cider Vinegar]] | None |
| 2 tsp | [[Horseradish]] | None |
| 1/2 tsp | [[Dry Mustard]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation
1. Mix **1 lb creamed cottage cheese** and **1 lb spinach** together in a large bowl until well combined.
2. Mix the remaining ingredients including **1/2 cup chopped pecans**, **1/2 cup sour cream**, **1/2 cup sugar**, **3 tbsp cider vinegar**, **2 tsp horseradish**, and **1/2 tsp dry mustard** together.
3. Combine the mixtures thoroughly and serve immediately.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Keep dressing and salad components separate until ready to serve to maintain crispness.
