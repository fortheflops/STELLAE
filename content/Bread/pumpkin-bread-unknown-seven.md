# Pumpkin Bread

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Seven | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Bread
> *A classic vintage pumpkin bread recipe packed with warm autumn spices and crunchy pecans. Baking two full loaves at once makes it ideal for holiday gifting or serving a crowd.*

## Instructions

### Preparation and Baking
1. Preheat your oven to 325°F (165°C) and grease **two loaf pans**.
2. In a large bowl, cream together **2 1/2 cups sugar**, **4 eggs**, and **1 cup salad oil** until smooth and combined.
3. Add **3 1/2 cups flour**, **2 tsp. soda**, **1 tsp. nutmeg**, **1 tsp. cinnamon**, and **1 cup water** to the bowl; mix until combined.
4. Fold in **1 can 303 Pumpkin** and **1 cup pecans** until evenly blended throughout the batter.
5. Pour the batter evenly into the two prepared pans and bake at 325°F for **1 hour, 15 minutes**, or until golden brown on top and a wooden toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Once completely cool, wrap loaves tightly in plastic wrap or foil. Store at room temperature for up to 4 days, or freeze for up to 3 months.
