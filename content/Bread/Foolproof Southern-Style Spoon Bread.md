# Foolproof Southern-Style Spoon Bread

> 📜 **Collection:** General Archive | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Bread
> *A foolproof and quick recipe for a classic, moist, and savory cornmeal spoon bread. Cooked first on the stovetop to thicken and then baked to golden perfection.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 25 mins | None | 4-6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Saucepan
* Whisk or spoon
* Baking dish

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 c. | [[Corn meal]] | sifted |
| 1 c. | [[Water]] | boiling H2O |
| 1/2 c. | [[Milk]] | cold or room temperature |
| 1 | [[Egg]] | well beaten |
| 1 hunk | [[Butter]] | approx. 1-2 Tbsp |
| 1 tsp | [[Salt]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Stovetop Base
1. Preheat your oven to **350°F** and grease a baking dish.
2. Slowly add **1 c. boiling H2O** to the **1/2 c. sifted corn meal**, stirring constantly to prevent lumps.
3. Place the saucepan on slow heat and stir well for several minutes until thickened to a smooth consistency.
4. Add the **1 hunk butter** and **1 tsp salt** to the mixture, stirring until the butter is completely melted and incorporated.
5. Remove the pan from the heat source.

### Step 2: Mixing and Baking
6. Stir in the **1/2 c. milk** and the **1 egg** (well beaten), mixing thoroughly until combined.
7. Pour the batter into the prepared greased baking dish.
8. Bake in the moderate oven at **350°F** for **20 minutes** until set and lightly golden brown on top.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Best served warm straight from the oven, but leftovers can be stored in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 2 days.
