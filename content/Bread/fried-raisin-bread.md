# Fried Raisin Bread

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Five | ✍️ **Attribution:** Judy | 📂 **Category:** Bread
> *A delightful handwritten recipe for fried raisin bread featuring a yeasted dough packed with sweet raisins. Perfect for a comforting breakfast treat with a golden, crispy exterior.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 30 mins | 15 mins | 2 hours | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large bowl
* Skillet

---
## Ingredients

### Dough Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 pkgs. | [[Dry Yeast]] | Active |
| 2 c. | [[Warm Water]] | Warmed |
| 1 tsp. | [[Sugar]] | For yeast activation |
| 1 1/2 tsp. | [[Salt]] | None |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] | Beaten |
| 2 tbsp. | [[Sugar]] | For dough |
| 1/2 box | [[Raisins]] | Approximate |
| As needed | [[Flour]] | To make a stiff dough |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Making the Dough
1. In a large bowl, mix **2 pkgs. dry yeast**, **2 c. warm water**, and **1 tsp. sugar**.
2. Let stand about 5 minutes until frothy.
3. Then add **1 1/2 tsp. salt**, **2 eggs**, **2 tbsp. sugar**, approx. **1/2 box raisins**, and enough **flour** to make a stiff dough.

### Step 2: Rising and Frying
4. Let rise to top of bowl until doubled in size.
5. Fry in hot oil until golden brown and cooked through.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Dough can be prepared ahead and refrigerated after the first rise.
