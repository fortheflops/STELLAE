# Barnett's Cranberry Orange Bread

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Barnett | 📂 **Category:** Bread
> *A festive and moist quick bread packed with fresh cranberries, bright orange zest and juice, and crunchy nuts. This classic recipe by Barnett delivers a delightful balance of tart and sweet flavors.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preheat and Prepare
1. Preheat your oven to **325°F**. Grease and flour a **9x5 inch loaf pan**.

### Step 2: Combine Dry Ingredients
2. In a large bowl, whisk together the **2 cups flour**, **1 cup sugar**, **1 1/2 tsp baking soda**, and **1/2 tsp salt**. Stir in the **1 cup chopped raw cranberries** and **1 cup chopped nuts** to coat them thoroughly.

### Step 3: Prepare Wet Ingredients
3. Grate the rind from the **1 large orange** into a medium bowl. Squeeze the juice from the orange into a **1/2 cup measuring cup**. Add the **2 Tbsp melted shortening** to the measuring cup, then top it off with enough boiling **water** to reach the **1/2 cup** line.

### Step 4: Mix Wet Components
4. Add the beaten **1 egg** and the liquid mixture from the measuring cup to the bowl with the orange rind. Stir until combined.

### Step 5: Combine Wet and Dry
5. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients and mix with a spatula only until moistened. Do not overmix to ensure a tender crumb.

### Step 6: Bake the Bread
6. Pour the batter into the prepared **9x5 inch loaf pan**. Bake for **1 hour**, or until a wooden skewer inserted into the center comes out clean and the top is golden brown and fragrant.

### Step 7: Cool and Serve
7. Let the bread cool in the pan for **10 minutes** before turning it out onto a wire cooling rack to cool completely before slicing.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Wrap tightly in plastic wrap and store at room temperature for up to 3 days, or freeze for up to 2 months.
