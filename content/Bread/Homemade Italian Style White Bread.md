# Homemade Italian Style White Bread

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Bread
> *A classic recipe for two loaves of homemade Italian-style white bread with a soft interior and crusty exterior. Perfect for sandwiches, toast, or dinner accompaniments.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 25 mins | 30 mins | 1 hour 30 mins | 2 loaves |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large mixing bowl
* Electric mixer
* Floured board
* Greased bowl
* Baking sheet

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 Pkgs | [[Yeast]] | active dry |
| 2 1/2 Cups | [[Water]] | lukewarm |
| 2 TBLS | [[Honey]] | none |
| 1 TBLS | [[Salt]] | none |
| 2 TBLS | [[Shortening]] | melted, or salad oil |
| 7 to 8 Cups | [[Flour]] | all-purpose, divided |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Activate the Yeast
1. In a large mixing bowl, sprinkle the **2 pkgs yeast** over the **2 1/2 cups lukewarm water**.

### Step 2: Mix the Base
2. Add the **2 tbls honey** and let stand for **5 minutes** until the yeast is foamy and mixed until dissolved.
3. Add the **1 tbls salt**, **2 tbls shortening**, and **3 cups flour**.

### Step 3: Knead the Dough
4. Mix thoroughly with an electric mixer on low speed, then switch to a dough hook or a sturdy spoon and mix in **3 more cups flour** until a shaggy dough forms.
5. Turn the dough out onto a well-floured board and knead for about **5-8 minutes**, adding more flour as necessary, until the dough is smooth and elastic.

### Step 4: First Rise
6. Place the dough in a greased bowl, turning it over once to grease the top.
7. Cover and let rise in a warm place until doubled in bulk, about **1 to 1.5 hours**.

### Step 5: Shape the Loaves
8. Punch the dough down and divide it in half.
9. Shape each half into a long Italian-style loaf by patting the dough into a rectangle, rolling it up tightly from the long edge, and pinching the seam to seal.

### Step 6: Second Rise
10. Place the loaves on a baking sheet sprinkled with **cornmeal**.
11. Cover and let them rise again for about **30-45 minutes** until nearly doubled.

### Step 7: Bake
12. Preheat your oven to **375°F**.
13. Optional: Just before baking, brush the loaves with a beaten egg for a shiny crust and cut a few diagonal slashes across the top with a sharp knife.
14. Bake for about **30 minutes**, or until the loaves are golden brown and sound hollow when tapped on the bottom.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store fully baked loaves in a paper bag or bread box at room temperature for up to 3 days, or freeze sliced bread for up to 3 months.
