# Melinda Ewing's Dinner Rolls

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Melinda Ewing | 📂 **Category:** Bread
> *A large-batch recipe for soft, rich, and slightly sweet yeasted dinner rolls. Enriched with butter and eggs, this dough yields dozens of fluffy rolls.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 25 mins | 20 mins | 2 hours | Approx. 42 rolls |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large heatproof bowl
* Small bowl
* Baking sheets
* Mixing spoon or electric mixer

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 Cup | [[Boiling Water]] | For melting butter |
| 1 Cup | [[Butter]] | Or margarine/oleo |
| 2/3 Cup | [[Sugar]] | None |
| 2 Teaspoons | [[Salt]] | None |
| 2 1/2 Packages | [[Yeast]] | Active dry, 1/4-ounce packages |
| 1 Cup | [[Warm Water]] | For dissolving yeast |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] | Beaten |
| 6 Cups | [[Flour]] | All-purpose, divided |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Base
1. In a large, heatproof bowl, place the **1 cup butter**, **2/3 cup sugar**, and **2 teaspoons salt**. Pour the **1 cup boiling water** over them and stir until the **1 cup butter** is melted. Let this mixture cool until it is lukewarm.

### Step 2: Proof the Yeast
2. In a separate small bowl, dissolve the **2 1/2 packages yeast** in the **1 cup warm water**. Let it stand for **5 minutes** until it becomes foamy.

### Step 3: Mix the Dough
3. Pour the yeast mixture into the cooled butter mixture. Add the **2 eggs** (beaten) and about **3 cups flour**. Beat well with a mixer or spoon until a smooth batter forms.

### Step 4: Knead and First Rise
4. Gradually add the remaining **flour**, mixing until a soft dough forms. Turn the dough out onto a lightly floured surface and knead for **5-8 minutes** until smooth and elastic. Place the dough in a large, greased bowl, cover, and let it rise in a warm place until doubled in bulk (about **1 to 1.5 hours**).

### Step 5: Shape and Second Rise
5. Punch the **dough** down. Pinch off pieces and shape them into rolls. Place the rolls on greased baking sheets. Cover the rolls and let them rise a second time until nearly doubled (about **30-45 minutes**).

### Step 6: Bake
6. Preheat your oven to **375°F**. Bake for **15-20 minutes**, or until golden brown and fragrant. Brush with melted butter while hot, if desired.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Shaped rolls can be frozen after the second rise, or baked rolls can be stored in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 3 days.
