# Banana Bread (3 Bowl Method)

> 📜 **Collection:** UK15 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Bread
> *A large-batch recipe for incredibly moist banana nut bread loaded with walnuts and raisins, utilizing a unique three-bowl method. Designed to be baked into a large Bundt cake or multiple loaves.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 25 mins | 1 hour | None | 1 large Bundt cake or 2-3 loaves |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 3 large mixing bowls
* Measuring cups and spoons
* Whisk
* Bundt pan or loaf pans

| 2 1/2 cups | [[Sugar]] | White |

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |

| 3 teaspoons | [[Soda]] | Baking soda |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Salt]] | None |
| 2 cups | [[Walnuts]] | Chopped |
| 1 to 2 cups | [[Raisins]] | Washed |

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and Mixing Bowls
1. Preheat oven to **375°F**. Thoroughly grease and flour your Bundt pan(s) or loaf pans.
2. In Bowl 1 (wet ingredients), combine the **1 1/2 cups canola oil**, **2 1/2 cups white sugar**, **5 large eggs**, and **2 teaspoons vanilla extract**.
3. In Bowl 2, mix the **8 mashed bananas** and **2 teaspoons lemon juice** together.
4. In Bowl 3 (dry ingredients), whisk together the **4 cups all-purpose flour**, **3 teaspoons baking powder**, **3 teaspoons baking soda**, and **1 teaspoon salt**, then stir in the **2 cups chopped walnuts** and **1 to 2 cups washed raisins** to coat.

### Step 2: Combining and Baking
5. Pour the **mashed bananas** mixture from Bowl 2 into the **canola oil and sugar** mixture in Bowl 1, stirring until well combined.
6. Pour the combined wet ingredients from Bowl 1 into the Bowl 3 dry ingredients, stirring gently just until combined; do not overmix.
7. Pour the resulting batter evenly into the prepared pan(s).
8. Bake for **1 hour** for a large Bundt pan (or about **45 to 55 minutes** for standard loaf pans), until a tester inserted into the center comes out clean and golden brown.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Wrap tightly in plastic wrap and store at room temperature for up to 3 days, or freeze for up to 2 months.
