# Applesauce Nut Bread

> 📜 **Collection:** UK15 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Bread
> *A moist and tender quick bread made with applesauce and pecans, finished with a sweet and crunchy brown sugar and cinnamon topping. Perfect for breakfast or as a snack.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Mix Wet Ingredients
1. Preheat the oven to **350°F**. Grease and flour an **8.5" x 4.5" x 2.5" loaf pan**.
2. In a large bowl, combine the **1 cup sugar**, **1 cup applesauce**, **1/3 cup oil**, **2 eggs**, and **3 tablespoons milk**.

### Step 2: Combine Dry Ingredients and Batter
3. In a separate bowl, mix the dry ingredients together: **2 cups flour**, **1 teaspoon baking soda**, **1/2 teaspoon baking powder**, **1/2 teaspoon cinnamon**, **1/4 teaspoon salt**, and **1/4 teaspoon nutmeg**.
4. Add the dry ingredients to the applesauce mixture and stir just until combined.
5. Stir in the **3/4 cup chopped pecans**.

### Step 3: Add Topping and Bake
6. Pour the batter into the prepared loaf pan.
7. In a small bowl, combine the topping ingredients: **1/4 cup brown sugar**, **1/2 teaspoon cinnamon**, and **1/4 cup chopped pecans**.
8. Sprinkle the topping evenly over the batter.
9. Bake for **1 hour and 15 minutes**, or until a tester inserted into the center comes out clean, covering loosely with foil after **30 minutes** to prevent over-browning.
