# Guacamole Quick Bread

> 📜 **Collection:** UK15 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Bread
> *A unique and delicious savory-sweet quick bread featuring the classic flavors of guacamole, including mashed avocado, onion, and green chile. Baked to golden perfection, it offers a tender and flavorful crumb.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 1 hour 10 mins | None | 1 loaf |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 9x5-inch loaf pan
* Small skillet
* Large bowl
* Separate bowl
* Spatula
* Wire rack

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 tablespoons | [[Onion]] | Chopped |
| 1/2 cup | [[Margarine]] | Softened |
| 2 cups | [[Flour]] | All-purpose |
| 1 1/2 teaspoons | [[Baking Powder]] | Standard |
| 1/2 teaspoon | [[Salt]] | Standard |
| 1/4 teaspoon | [[Cumin]] | Ground |
| 1 cup | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 1/2 cup | [[Avocado]] | Mashed, ripe |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] | Beaten |
| 1/4 cup | [[Green Chile]] | Roasted, peeled, and chopped |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Sauté
1. Preheat the oven to **375°F**. Grease a **9x5-inch loaf pan**.
2. In a small skillet, sauté the **2 tablespoons chopped onion** in a little **margarine** until soft. Set aside to cool slightly.

### Step 2: Mix Batter and Bake
3. In a large bowl, sift together the **2 cups flour**, **1 1/2 teaspoons baking powder**, **1/2 teaspoon salt**, and **1/4 teaspoon ground cumin**.
4. In a separate bowl, cream together the **1/2 cup softened margarine** and **1 cup sugar** until light and fluffy.
5. Beat in the **1/2 cup mashed avocado** and **2 beaten eggs**.
6. Stir in the sautéed **onion** and **1/4 cup chopped green chile**.
7. Add the dry ingredients to the wet ingredients and mix with a spatula until just combined.
8. Pour the batter into the prepared loaf pan.
9. Bake for **15 minutes at 375°F**.
10. Reduce the oven heat to **350°F** and continue to bake for another 55 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.
11. Cool in the pan for **10 minutes** before turning out onto a wire rack to cool completely.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the cooled bread wrapped tightly in plastic wrap at room temperature for up to 3 days, or refrigerate for up to a week.
