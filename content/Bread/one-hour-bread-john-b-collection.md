# One Hour Bread

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Bread
> *A quick and easy yeast bread recipe that rises in just 50 minutes and bakes up golden brown. Perfect for a warm, homemade loaf straight from the oven.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 30 mins | 50 mins | 1 loaf |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large mixing bowl
* Electric mixer
* 2 1-lb coffee cans

---
## Ingredients

### Dough Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 pkg | [[Active dry yeast]] | Dry |
| 1/4 cup | [[Hottest tap water]] | Hot |
| 1 tsp | [[Salt]] | None |
| 2 tbsp | [[Sugar]] | None |
| 2 1/2 cups | [[Flour]] | Divided |
| 1 cup | [[Dairy sour cream]] | None |
| 1/2 tsp | [[Baking soda]] | None |
| 1 | [[Egg]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing
1. In large mixing bowl, dissolve **1 pkg active dry yeast** in **1/4 cup hottest tap water**.
2. Add **1 1/2 cups flour**, **1 tsp salt**, **2 tbsp sugar**, **1 cup dairy sour cream**, **1/2 tsp baking soda**, and **1 egg**.
3. Blend 1/2 minute on low speed, scraping bowl constantly.
4. Beat 2 minutes on high speed, scraping bowl occasionally.
5. Stir in remaining **flour** thoroughly.

### Step 2: Rising and Baking
6. Divide batter between **2 greased 1 lb coffee cans**.
7. Let rise in warm place 50 minutes. Batter will rise slightly but not double.
8. Bake at 350 degrees until golden brown. Remove immediately from pan.
