# Stollen

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Mae | 📂 **Category:** Bread
> *A traditional holiday fruit bread filled with raisins, citron, and candied cherries, finished with a sweet confectionery glaze. This classic recipe yields a rich, spiced yeast dough folded and baked to golden perfection.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Making the Dough
1. Sprinkle **1 pkg. yeast** in **1/4 c. lukewarm water**, let stand 5 minutes. Stir until smooth.
2. Scald **3/4 c. milk**. Add **2 tbsp. shortening**, **1/4 c. sugar**, **1 1/2 tsp. salt**, and **1 tsp. grated lemon rind**. Cool to lukewarm.
3. Sift flour; measure. Add **1 pkg. yeast** to milk mixture, add **1 egg**, and **1 c. sifted flour**. Beat well.
4. Add **1/4 c. raisins**, **1/4 c. chopped citron**, and **1/4 c. candied cherries**. Add remaining **sifted flour** to make a soft dough.
5. Turn out lightly on a floured board, and knead in remaining **sifted flour** until smooth.

### Step 2: Shaping and Rising
6. Place dough in a greased bowl when smooth; let rise in a warm place until doubled in size, about 1 1/2 hours.
7. Punch down dough. Place on board and let rise 10 minutes.
8. Roll dough into an oval about 3/4 inch thick. Brush one half of the oval with **2 tbsp. melted butter**.
9. Fold over like a large parkerhouse roll. Do not press edges tightly together, place on a greased baking sheet.
10. Let rise until double in size, about 45 minutes.

### Step 3: Baking and Glazing
11. Bake in a 350°F oven for about 30 minutes until golden brown and hollow sounding when tapped.
12. Lightly frost or glaze with **confectioners sugar icing** while slightly warm.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The unbaked stollen can be assembled and frozen after shaping, then thawed, risen, and baked as directed.
