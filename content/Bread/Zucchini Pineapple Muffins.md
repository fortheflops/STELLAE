# Zucchini Pineapple Muffins

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Bread
> *An incredibly moist and flavorful muffin recipe packed with grated zucchini, crushed pineapple, nuts, and warm spices. This large-batch recipe yields approximately 40 muffins and freezes wonderfully.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 20 mins | None | Approx. 40 muffins |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Muffin tins
* Paper liners
* Large mixing bowl
* Separate mixing bowl
* Whisk
* Spatula
* Wire cooling rack

## Ingredients

### Wet Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3 | [[Eggs]] | Large |
| 1 Cup | [[Oil]] | Vegetable oil |
| 2 Cups | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 2 Tablespoons | [[Vanilla]] | Extract |
| 2 Cups | [[Zucchini]] | Grated |
| 1 (8.5 oz) Can | [[Pineapple]] | Crushed, with juice |
| 1 Cup | [[Nuts]] | Chopped |
| 1 Cup | [[Raisins]] | Optional |

### Dry Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3 Cups | [[Flour]] | All-purpose |
| ½ tsp | [[Salt]] | None |
| 2 tsps | [[Baking Soda]] | None |
| 1 ½ tsps | [[Baking Powder]] | None |
| 1 ½ tsps | [[Cinnamon]] | Ground |
| ¾ tsp | [[Nutmeg]] | Ground |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Wet Ingredients
1. Preheat your oven to **350°F (175°C)**. Line muffin tins with paper liners or grease them well.
2. In a large bowl, beat the **3 [[Eggs]]**, **1 cup [[Oil]]**, **2 cups [[Sugar]]**, and **2 tablespoons [[Vanilla]]** together until the mixture is thick and creamy.
3. Stir in the **2 cups [[Zucchini]]** (grated), the **1 (8.5 oz) can [[Pineapple]]** (crushed, with juice), and the **1 cup [[Nuts]]** (chopped).

### Step 2: Combine and Bake
4. In a separate bowl, whisk together the **3 cups [[Flour]]**, **½ tsp [[Salt]]**, **2 tsps [[Baking Soda]]**, **1 ½ tsps [[Baking Powder]]**, **1 ½ tsps [[Cinnamon]]**, and **¾ tsp [[Nutmeg]]**.
5. Gradually add the dry ingredients to the wet mixture, mixing with a spatula until just combined. Be careful not to overmix.
6. If using, gently fold in the **1 cup [[Raisins]]**.
7. Fill the muffin cups about two-thirds full.
8. Bake for **20 minutes**, or until a toothpick inserted into the center of a muffin comes out clean and golden brown.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Allow muffins to cool in the tins for a few minutes before transferring them to a wire rack to cool completely. These muffins freeze nicely for up to 3 months when stored in an airtight container.
