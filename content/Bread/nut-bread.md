# Nut Bread

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Bread
> *A classic, simple nut bread recipe featuring a hearty crumb and crunchy texture. Perfect for slicing and serving with butter or jam.*

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

* Mixing bowl
* Bread pan

## Ingredients

### Dry Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3 cups | [[Flour]] | All-purpose |
| 1/4 cup | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 4 tsp | [[Baking Powder]] | None |
| 1 tsp | [[Salt]] | None |
| 1 cup | [[Nuts]] | Chopped |

### Wet Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | [[Egg]] | Beaten |
| 1 cup | [[Milk]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Mixing and Baking
1. In a large bowl, mix the **3 cups flour**, **1/4 cup sugar**, **4 tsp baking powder**, and **1 tsp salt**.
2. Beat the **1 egg** in a separate bowl, add the **1 cup milk**, and stir into part of the dry ingredients.
3. Add the **1 cup nuts** and stir in the rest of the dry ingredients until just combined.
4. Pour the batter into a buttered bread pan and let rise for 30 minutes.
5. Bake until the loaf is golden brown and sounds hollow when tapped.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 3 days or freeze for up to 1 month.
