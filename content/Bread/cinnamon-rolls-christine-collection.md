# Cinnamon Rolls

> 📜 **Collection:** Christine Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Nellie | 📂 **Category:** Bread
> *A delightful handwritten recipe for traditional homemade cinnamon rolls from the John B. Collection. These soft, yeast-raised pastries make about two dozen fluffy rolls.*

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

## Ingredients

### Dough Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 cup | [[Sugar]] | None |
| 2 cups | [[Milk]] | Luke warm |
| 1/2 cup | [[Oleo]] | Margarine or butter |
| 3 tbsp | [[Yeast]] | None |
| 3 | [[Eggs]] | Beaten |
| 1 tsp | [[Salt]] | None |
| As needed | [[Flour]] | To make soft dough |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Making the Dough
1. Mix **1 cup sugar**, **1/2 cup oleo**, **1 tsp salt**, and **3 eggs** together.
2. Add **3 tbsp yeast** which has been dissolved in **2 cups milk** (luke warm).
3. Add enough **flour** to make a soft dough.

### Step 2: Rising and Shaping
4. Let the dough rise until doubled in size.
5. Make rolls as desired, then let rise again until light and puffy before baking until golden brown.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The shaped rolls can be covered and refrigerated overnight for a second rise in the morning before baking.
