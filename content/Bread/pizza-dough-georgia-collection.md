# Pizza Dough

> 📜 **Collection:** Georgia Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Bread
> *A quick and easy homemade pizza dough recipe that yields a wonderful crust. Featuring a hint of butter flavoring, this dough is ready to top and bake in no time.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 20 mins | 5 mins | 2 pizzas |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large bowl
* Cookie sheet

---
## Ingredients

### Dough Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 pkg. | [[Active dry yeast]] | Dry |
| 1 cup | [[Warm water]] | Warm |
| 2 1/2 cups | [[Flour]] | All-purpose |
| 2 T. | [[Vegetable oil]] | None |
| 1 t. | [[X-tra Touch butter flavoring]] | None |
| 1 t. | [[Sugar]] | None |
| 1 t. | [[Salt]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing and Resting
1. Dissolve **1 pkg. active dry yeast** in **1 cup warm water** in a large bowl.
2. Stir in remaining ingredients including **2 1/2 cups flour**, **2 T. vegetable oil**, **1 t. X-tra Touch butter flavoring**, **1 t. sugar**, and **1 t. salt**.
3. Beat vigorously **20 strokes**.
4. Let rest **5 minutes** until slightly puffed.

### Step 2: Shaping and Baking
5. Divide dough in half.
6. Pat each half into an **11-inch circle** on a cookie sheet.
7. Top as desired and bake at **425 degrees** for **15-20 minutes** until the crust is golden brown and crisp.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Dough can be wrapped tightly in plastic and refrigerated for up to 24 hours before shaping and baking.
