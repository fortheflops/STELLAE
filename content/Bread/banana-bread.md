# Banana Bread

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Five | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Bread
> *A classic, comforting homemade banana bread recipe featuring moist mashed bananas and chopped nuts. Baked slowly at 325 degrees until golden brown and tender.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 1 hr 10 mins | None | 1 loaf |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Loaf pan
* Mixing bowls

## Ingredients

### Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 cup | [[Butter]] | or oleo |
| 1 cup | [[Sugar]] |  |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] | well beaten |
| 3 | [[Bananas]] | ripe, mashed |
| 2 cups | [[Flour]] |  |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Baking Soda]] | soda |
| 1/2 teaspoon | [[Salt]] |  |
| 1 cup | [[Milk]] | sour milk or buttermilk |
| 1/2 cup | [[Nuts]] | chopped |

---

## Instructions

### Preparation and Mixing
1. Be sure to grease and flour a **loaf pan**.
2. Blend **1/2 cup butter** and **1 cup sugar**, then add **2 eggs** (well beaten) and beat again.
3. Beat in **3 bananas** (mashed).
4. Sift together **2 cups flour**, **1 teaspoon baking soda**, and **1/2 teaspoon salt**.
5. Add the dry mixture to the above wet mixture alternately with **1 cup milk** and **1/2 cup nuts**, ending with flour.

### Baking
6. Cook at 325 degrees for **1 hr 10 min** until golden brown and a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store tightly wrapped at room temperature for up to 3 days, or freeze the baked loaf for up to 2 months.
