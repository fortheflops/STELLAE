## Instructions

### Prep
1. Preheat oven to **400°F** (**200°C**). Line a muffin pan with **cupcake papers**.

### Make the Batter
2. In a large bowl, combine the **2 cups flour**, **2/3 cup sugar**, **1 tbsp baking powder**, and **1 tsp salt**.
3. Stir in the **3/4 cup chopped nuts** and **1/2 cup golden raisins**.
4. In a separate bowl, whisk together the **1/2 cup milk**, **1/3 cup oil**, **2 eggs**, and **2 tsp lemon flavoring**.
5. Pour the wet mixture into the dry flour mixture, add the **1 cup shredded zucchini**, and stir just until moistened.

### Bake and Glaze
6. Spoon the batter evenly into the prepared **muffin cups**.
7. Bake for **20 to 25 minutes** until the tops are golden brown and spring back when lightly touched.
8. Remove the muffins from the pan.
9. In a small bowl, combine the **1/2 cup powdered sugar** and **2-3 tsp fresh lemon juice** until smooth.
10. Drizzle the glaze over the **warm muffins**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 3 days. Best served warm or at room temperature.
