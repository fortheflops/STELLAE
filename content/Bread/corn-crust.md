# Corn Crust

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Bread
> *A rustic and traditional recipe for corn crust, made with simple pantry staples like cornmeal, boiling water, and butter. Baked thin and crisp in shallow pans for a delightful crunch.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 30 mins | None | 4 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Shallow pans
* Spatula

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 cups | [[Cornmeal]] | Dry |
| 2 tbsp | [[Butter]] | Softened |
| 1/2 tsp | [[Salt]] | None |
| 2 tsp | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 4 cups | [[Boiling water]] | Hot |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing and Baking
1. Mix **2 cups cornmeal**, **2 tsp sugar**, and **1/2 tsp salt**, add **2 tbsp butter**, pour over them **4 cups boiling water**.
2. Beat thoroughly, spread thinly on shallow pans.
3. Score before putting in oven.
4. When partly done, before beginning to brown, slide spatula under it.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Best prepared fresh, but mixture can be mixed just before spreading and baking.
