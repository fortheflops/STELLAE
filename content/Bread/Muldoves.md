# Muldoves

> 📜 **Collection:** JC | ✍️ **Attribution:** Jill Miller | 📂 **Category:** Bread
> *A simple cake or quick bread featuring a straightforward batter of flour, sugar, eggs, and oil. Enjoy this unique family recipe warm with your favorite morning beverage.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 30-35 mins | None | 1 loaf or 1 8x8 cake |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large bowl
* Separate bowl
* Whisk
* Loaf pan or 8x8 inch square pan
* Wooden skewer

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 4 | [[Eggs]] | Large, room temperature |
| 1 cup | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 3/4 cup | [[Vegetable Oil]] | Neutral flavored |
| 1/4 cup | [[Water]] | Lukewarm |
| 2 1/5 cups | [[All-Purpose Flour]] | Sifted |
| 2 teaspoons | [[Baking Powder]] | Fresh |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Mix Wet Ingredients
1. Preheat your oven to **350°F**. Grease and flour a loaf pan or an 8x8 inch square pan.
2. In a large bowl, beat the **4 large eggs** and **1 cup sugar** together until light and combined.
3. Stir in the **3/4 cup vegetable oil** and **1/4 cup water** until smoothly incorporated.

### Step 2: Combine and Bake
4. In a separate bowl, whisk together the **2 1/5 cups all-purpose flour** and **2 teaspoons baking powder**.
5. Add the dry ingredients to the wet ingredients and mix gently until just combined without overworking the batter.
6. Pour the batter into the prepared pan.
7. Bake for about **30-35 minutes**, or until golden brown and a wooden skewer inserted into the center comes out clean.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store tightly wrapped at room temperature for up to 3 days or freeze for up to 2 months.
