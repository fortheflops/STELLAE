# Spiced Plum Muffins

> *Moist and fragrant spiced muffins packed with warm spices, sweet purple plums, and crunchy pecans. Perfect for a cozy morning breakfast or afternoon treat.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Mix Dry Ingredients
1. Preheat your oven to **400°F**. Grease muffin tins or line them with paper liners.
2. In a large bowl, whisk together the **2 cups flour**, **1 3/4 cups sugar**, **1 teaspoon salt**, **1 teaspoon soda**, **1 teaspoon baking powder**, **1 teaspoon cinnamon**, **1 teaspoon nutmeg**, **1 teaspoon allspice**, and **1 teaspoon cloves**.

### Step 2: Combine Wet Ingredients and Batter
3. In a separate medium bowl, beat the **2 eggs**. Whisk in the **1 cup sour milk (buttermilk)** and **1 cup Wesson oil**.
4. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients and stir with a spatula until just moistened.
5. Gently fold in the **1 (15 oz) can purple plums** and the **1 cup pecans** until evenly distributed.

### Step 3: Bake
6. Fill the muffin tins about two-thirds full with batter.
7. Bake for **18-20 minutes**, or until a toothpick inserted into the center of a muffin comes out clean and golden brown.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftover muffins in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 3 days, or freeze for up to 2 months.
