# Pumpkin Bread

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Sharon | 📂 **Category:** Bread
> *A classic, spiced pumpkin bread recipe from Sharon's kitchen that yields three incredibly moist loaves. Perfect for autumn baking, this quick bread features a warm blend of cinnamon, nutmeg, and real pumpkin.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing the Batter
1. Sift together the dry ingredients: **3 1/3 cups flour**, **2 tsp. baking soda**, **1 1/2 tsp. salt**, **1 tsp. cinnamon**, and **1 tsp. nutmeg** into a large bowl.
2. Mix well, then add the remaining ingredients: **3 cups sugar**, **1 cup vegetable oil**, **4 eggs**, **2/3 cup water**, and **1 (1 lb.) can pumpkin purée**.
3. Mix until the batter is completely smooth and uniform in texture.

### Step 2: Baking and Cooling
4. Pour the batter evenly into **3 greased loaf pans**.
5. Bake for **1 hour** at **350°F** until a toothpick inserted into the center of the loaves comes out clean.
6. Cool for **10 minutes** in the pans before turning out onto a wire rack to cool completely.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Wrap cooled loaves tightly in plastic wrap and store at room temperature for up to 3 days, or freeze for up to 3 months.
