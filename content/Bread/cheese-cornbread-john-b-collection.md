# Cheese Cornbread

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Bread
> *Savory cheese cornbread loaded with smoky bacon, sauteed onions, and sharp cheddar cheese. This golden baked side dish pairs wonderfully with chili and comforting soups.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 35 mins | None | 24 squares |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Skillet
* 13x9x2 baking pan
* Mixing bowl

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/4 lb | [[Bacon]] | Sliced |
| 1/2 c | [[Onion]] | Chopped |
| 2 pkgs | [[Corn Muffin Mix]] | Standard package |
| 1 1/3 c | [[Milk]] | Plus additional if needed per package |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] | Beaten |
| 1 tsp | [[Pepper]] | Ground |
| 2 c | [[Cheddar Cheese]] | Shredded |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep Bacon and Onions
1. Dice the **1/4 lb bacon**, cook slowly in a skillet until crisp. Remove **bacon** from skillet.
2. Measure **1/4 c bacon fat** from skillet, add oil if needed to make **1/4 c**. Return **bacon fat** to skillet.
3. Saute the **1/2 c chopped onion** in the **bacon fat** until golden. Set aside.

### Step 2: Mix and Bake
4. Prepare the **2 pkgs corn muffin mix** according to directions, using the **2 eggs**, **1 1/3 c milk**.
5. Add the cooked **bacon**, sauteed **onion**, **bacon fat**, **1 tsp pepper**, and **1 c shredded cheddar cheese** (reserve the remaining **1 c shredded cheddar cheese**) into the batter and mix well.
6. Pour the batter into a well-greased 13x9x2 pan. Sprinkle the remaining **1 c shredded cheddar cheese** over the batter.
7. Bake at 400°F for 30-35 minutes until golden brown and the center springs back when lightly pressed.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftover cornbread tightly wrapped at room temperature for up to 2 days, or refrigerate for up to 5 days.
