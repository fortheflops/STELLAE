# Yeasted Buttermilk Biscuits

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Francis McDonald and Lois | 📂 **Category:** Bread
> *A unique refrigerator biscuit recipe utilizing yeast and a long chilling time to develop a delicious, tangy sourdough-style flavor. These tender biscuits bake up golden brown and fluffy after resting in the fridge.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 15 mins | 48 hours | 3-4 dozen biscuits |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Small bowl
* Very large bowl
* Spoon
* Floured surface
* Rolling pin
* Round biscuit cutter
* Greased baking sheet
* Oven

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 package (1/4-ounce) | [[Yeast]] | active dry |
| 1 cup | [[Water]] | warm |
| 2 cups | [[Buttermilk]] | cold |
| 3/4 cup | [[Corn Oil]] | or other vegetable oil |
| 1/4 cup | [[Sugar]] | granulated |
| 4 teaspoons | [[Baking Powder]] | fresh |
| 3 teaspoons | [[Salt]] | fine |
| 1/4 teaspoon | [[Baking Soda]] | fine |
| 6 to 7 cups | [[Flour]] | all-purpose, divided |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Dough
1. In a small bowl, dissolve the **1 package (1/4-ounce) active dry yeast** in the **1 cup warm water** and set aside.
2. In a very large bowl, whisk together **6 cups flour**, the **1/4 cup sugar**, **4 teaspoons baking powder**, **1/4 teaspoon baking soda**, and **3 teaspoons salt**.
3. Make a well in the center of the dry ingredients. Pour in the dissolved **yeast mixture**, **2 cups buttermilk**, and **3/4 cup corn oil**.
4. Mix well with a spoon or your hands until a soft dough forms. Add a little more flour from the remaining **1 cup flour** if the dough is too sticky to handle.

### Step 2: Chilling and Fermentation
5. Place the dough in a greased, covered bowl and refrigerate for at least **2 hours**, or ideally for up to **48 hours** to develop its signature tangy sourdough-style flavor.

### Step 3: Baking
6. When ready to bake, preheat your oven to **400°F**.
7. Turn the cold dough out onto a lightly floured surface. Gently pat or roll the dough to about **1/2 to 3/4 inch thickness**.
8. Cut out biscuits using a round biscuit cutter and place them on a lightly greased baking sheet.
9. Bake for **12-15 minutes**, or until golden brown and fragrant. Serve warm.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the unbaked dough sealed in the refrigerator for up to 48 hours to develop its signature tangy flavor before cutting and baking.
