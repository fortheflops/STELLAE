# Kim Maness's Refrigerator Bran Muffins

> 📜 **Collection:** UK5 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Kim Maness | 📂 **Category:** Bread
> *A fantastic large-batch bran muffin recipe from Kim Maness designed to be stored in the refrigerator for up to 6 weeks. Bake fresh, delicious muffins on demand whenever you like.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 18 mins | 2 days | 4-5 dozen muffins |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Very large bowl
* Separate large bowl
* Whisk
* Spatula
* Muffin tins
* Plastic wrap

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 4 | [[Eggs]] | Large, beaten |
| 5 cups | [[Flour]] | All-purpose |
| 5 teaspoons | [[Soda]] | Baking soda |
| 2 cups | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 1 quart (4 cups) | [[Buttermilk]] | Cold |
| 1 cup | [[Oil]] | Vegetable oil |
| 7 1/2 cups | [[Raisin Bran Flakes]] | Cereal |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mix the Dry and Wet Ingredients
1. In a very large bowl, whisk together the **5 cups flour**, **5 teaspoons baking soda**, and **2 cups sugar**.
2. In a separate large bowl, beat the **4 eggs**, then whisk in the **1 quart (4 cups) buttermilk** and **1 cup vegetable oil**.

### Step 2: Combine and Chill
3. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients and stir with a spatula until just combined.
4. Gently fold in the **7 1/2 cups Raisin Bran Flakes**.
5. Cover the bowl with plastic wrap and let the batter stand in the refrigerator for **2 days** before using it for the first time until fully chilled and rested.

### Step 3: Bake
6. When you are ready to bake, preheat the oven to **400°F**. Generously grease your muffin cups.
7. **Do not stir the batter.** Simply spoon the cold batter into the muffin cups, filling them generously.
8. Bake for **15-18 minutes**, or until a toothpick inserted into the center of a muffin comes out clean and golden brown.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The batter keeps covered in the refrigerator for up to 6 weeks, allowing you to bake fresh muffins on demand.
