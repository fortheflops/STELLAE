# Banana Bread

> 📜 **Collection:** Elizabeth Knee Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Bread
> *A wholesome, low-fat banana bread made with whole wheat flour, toasted wheat germ, and naturally sweetened with ripe bananas and raisins. Perfect for a healthy breakfast or snack slice.*

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 1 hour 15 mins | None | 20 servings |

* 9x5x3-inch loaf pan
* Mixing bowls
* Whisk
* Toothpick

## Ingredients

### Dry Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |

| 3/4 cup | [[Wheat Germ]] | toasted |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Baking Powder]] | None |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Baking Soda]] | None |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Cinnamon]] | None |

### Wet Ingredients & Mix-ins
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 | [[Egg Whites]] | beaten until stiff |
| 3 cups | [[Banana]] | puréed, using very ripe bananas |
| 1/4 cup | [[Fructose]] | granulated (honey may be used as an alternative) |
| 1 1/2 cups | [[Raisins]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Preparation & Baking
1. Preheat oven to **325°F**.
2. Combine the dry ingredients: **1 3/4 cups whole wheat flour**, **3/4 cup toasted wheat germ**, **1 teaspoon baking powder**, **1 teaspoon baking soda**, and **1 teaspoon cinnamon** in a large bowl.
3. In a separate bowl, combine the liquid ingredients, including the **3 cups banana purée**, **2 egg whites** (beaten until stiff), and **1/4 cup granulated fructose**.
4. Mix the dry and liquid ingredients together. Blend well until a cohesive batter forms.
5. Gently fold in the **1 1/2 cups raisins**.
6. Spray a **9" x 5" x 3" loaf pan** with cooking spray. Pour the mixture into the pan.
7. Bake for **60 to 75 minutes** or until a wooden toothpick inserted into the center of the bread comes out clean and the top is golden brown.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store wrapped tightly in plastic wrap at room temperature for up to 3 days, or freeze individual slices for a quick grab-and-go breakfast.
