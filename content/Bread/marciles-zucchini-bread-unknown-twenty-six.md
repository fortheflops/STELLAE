# Marcile's Zucchini Bread

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Marcile | 📂 **Category:** Bread
> *A moist and spiced zucchini bread recipe passed down from Marcile, featuring chopped nuts and warm cinnamon. Perfect for utilizing garden zucchini into a comforting homemade loaf.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 1 hr | 10 mins | 2 loaves or 1 bundt |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Loaf pans
* Bundt pan
* Mixing bowl
* Sifter

---
## Ingredients

### Batter Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 cups | [[Zucchini]] | Grated, include peel and seeds |
| 2 cups | [[Sugar]] | None |
| 3 | [[Eggs]] | None |
| 1 cup | [[Oil]] | None |
| 2 cups | [[Flour]] | Sifted |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Baking Powder]] | None |
| 2 tsp | [[Baking Soda]] | None |
| 3 tsp | [[Cinnamon]] | None |
| 1 tsp | [[Salt]] | None |
| 2 tsp | [[Vanilla]] | None |
| 1 cup | [[Nuts]] | Chopped, optional |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing
1. Grease pan real good (fits **2 loaf pans** or a **bundt pan**).
2. Mix all above together: **2 cups grated zucchini**, **2 cups sugar**, **3 eggs**, **1 cup oil**, **2 cups sifted flour**, **1/4 tsp baking powder**, **2 tsp baking soda**, **3 tsp cinnamon**, **1 tsp salt**, **2 tsp vanilla**, and **1 cup chopped nuts**.

### Step 2: Baking & Cooling
3. Bake at **350°** for **1 hr** until golden and a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.
4. Set **5-10 min**, then remove from pan.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Wrap cooled loaves tightly in plastic wrap and store at room temperature for up to 3 days, or freeze for longer storage.
