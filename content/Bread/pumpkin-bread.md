# Pumpkin Bread

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Five | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Bread
> *A classic homemade pumpkin bread recipe packed with warm autumn spices and a moist crumb. Perfect for cool weather baking and sharing with family.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Baking
1. Mix together **2 1/2 cups sugar**, **2/3 cup shortening**, **4 beaten eggs**, **2 cups pumpkin**, and **2/3 water**.
2. Stir in **3 1/2 cups flour**, **2 tsp bak. pow.**, **1 1/2 tsp soda**, **1 tsp salt**, **1 tsp cinnamon**, **1/2 tsp nutmeg**, and **1/2 tsp cloves** until just combined, folding in the optional **2/3 cup date, raisins or nuts** if desired.
3. Bake in **2 loaf pans** at 350 degrees for 1 hour until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean and the loaves are golden brown.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Wrap cooled loaves tightly in plastic wrap and store at room temperature for up to 3 days, or freeze for up to 3 months.
