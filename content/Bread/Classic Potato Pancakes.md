# Classic Potato Pancakes

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Bread
> *A simple and classic recipe for savory potato pancakes crafted with freshly grated potatoes and onion. Fried until perfectly crisp and traditionally served with a dollop of sour cream.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 15 mins | None | 8-10 pancakes |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Colander or cheesecloth
* Medium bowl
* Heavy skillet
* Paper towels

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 4 | [[Potatoes]] | medium, peeled and grated |
| 1/4 Cup | [[Flour]] | all-purpose |
| 1 | [[Egg]] | large, slightly beaten |
| 1 Teaspoon | [[Salt]] | standard |
| 1 Teaspoon | [[Onion]] | grated, optional |
| As needed | [[Vegetable Shortening]] | for frying |
| For Serving | [[Sour Cream]] | optional |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Drain
1. Peel and grate the **4 potatoes**. Place the grated potatoes in a clean kitchen towel or cheesecloth and squeeze firmly to remove as much excess liquid as possible.

### Step 2: Mix the Batter
2. In a medium bowl, combine the drained potatoes, **1/4 cup flour**, **1 egg**, **1 teaspoon salt**, and **1 teaspoon onion** (if using). Mix the ingredients thoroughly until just combined.

### Step 3: Fry to Golden Perfection
3. Shape the mixture into small, flat cakes or patties.
4. In a heavy skillet, heat a generous layer of **vegetable shortening** over medium-high heat until hot.
5. Carefully place the potato cakes into the hot shortening and fry for **3-4 minutes per side**, until crisp and golden brown.
6. Remove from the skillet, drain on paper towels, and serve at once with **sour cream**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Best enjoyed immediately while hot and crispy; leftovers can be stored in the refrigerator for up to 2 days and reheated in a skillet.
