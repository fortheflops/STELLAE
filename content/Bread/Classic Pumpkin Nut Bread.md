# Classic Pumpkin Nut Bread

> 📜 **Collection:** UK15 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Bread
> *A wonderfully moist and simple pumpkin nut bread made using the classic well mixing method for autumn baking. This large-batch recipe yields two aromatic loaves.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 1 hour | 10 mins (Cooling) | 2 loaves |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large mixing bowl
* Whisk
* Spatula
* Two 9x5 inch loaf pans
* Wire rack

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3 cups | All-purpose [[Flour]] | Sifted |
| 1 teaspoon | Baking [[Soda]] | None |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Salt]] | None |
| 3 teaspoons | [[Cinnamon]] | None |
| 2 cups | [[Sugar]] | None |
| 2 cups | Canned [[Pumpkin]] puree | None |
| 4 | [[Eggs]] | Large |
| 1 1/2 cups | Vegetable [[Oil]] | Or melted [[Shortening]] |
| 1/2 cup | [[Nutmeats]] | Chopped walnuts or pecans |
| 2 tablespoons | [[Applesauce]] | Optional, for reduced oil variation |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Mix Dry Ingredients
1. Preheat the oven to **350°F**. Grease and flour two **9x5 inch loaf pans**.
2. In a large mixing bowl, whisk together the **3 cups all-purpose flour**, **1 teaspoon baking soda**, **1 teaspoon salt**, and **3 teaspoons cinnamon**.
3. With a spoon, make a deep well in the center of the dry ingredients.

### Step 2: Combine and Bake
4. Into the well, add the **2 cups sugar**, **2 cups canned pumpkin puree**, **4 eggs**, **1 1/2 cups vegetable oil** (or melted shortening), **1/2 cup nutmeats**, and the optional **2 tablespoons applesauce** if desired.
5. Stir carefully with a spoon or spatula, mixing only enough to dampen the **3 cups all-purpose flour** and other dry ingredients. Do not overmix.
6. Pour the batter evenly into the two prepared **9x5 inch loaf pans**.
7. Bake at **350°F** for **1 hour**, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean and the loaves are fragrant and golden brown.
8. Cool in the pans for **10 minutes** before removing to a **wire rack** to cool completely.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the fully cooled loaves tightly wrapped in plastic wrap at room temperature for up to 3 days or freeze for up to 2 months.
