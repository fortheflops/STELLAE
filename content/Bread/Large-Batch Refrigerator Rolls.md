# Large-Batch Refrigerator Rolls

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Bread
> *A versatile, large-batch recipe for soft yeasted rolls. The dough can be refrigerated for up to two weeks, allowing you to bake fresh hot rolls whenever you like.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 30 mins | 15 mins | 2 hours | 5-6 dozen rolls |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large saucepan
* Very large mixing bowl
* Greased baking sheet
* Greased bowl

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 qt (4 Cups) | [[Milk]] | None |
| 1 Cup | [[Crisco]] | Solid vegetable shortening |
| 1 Cup | [[Sugar]] | None |
| 1 tsp | [[Salt]] | None |
| 2 pkgs (4 ½ tsp) | [[Yeast]] | Active dry yeast |
| 9 - 10 Cups | [[Flour]] | All-purpose recommended, divided |
| 1 tsp | [[Baking Powder]] | None |
| ½ tsp | [[Baking Soda]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Scald and Cool
1. In a large saucepan, combine the **1 qt (4 Cups) milk**, **1 cup Crisco**, **1 cup sugar**, and **1 tsp salt**. Heat over medium heat, stirring, until the mixture comes to a rolling boil.
2. Remove from heat and let the mixture cool until it is lukewarm (105-115°F).

### Step 2: Make the Sponge
3. Pour the lukewarm liquid into a very large mixing bowl. Sprinkle the **2 pkgs (4 ½ tsp) yeast** on top and let it dissolve for a few minutes.
4. Add **4-5 cups flour** to the bowl and beat well until you have a smooth batter (this is the "sponge").
5. Cover the bowl and let the sponge rise in a warm place until doubled in size (about 1-2 hours).

### Step 3: Form the Dough
6. Stir the sponge down. Add the **1 tsp baking powder** and **½ tsp baking soda**, then gradually mix in the remaining **4-5 cups flour** to form a soft dough.
7. Turn the dough out onto a floured surface and knead for **5-10 minutes** until smooth and elastic.

### Step 4: Bake or Refrigerate
8. To bake immediately, shape the dough into rolls, place them on a greased baking sheet, and let them rise for about 30 minutes. Bake at **375°F** for about 15 minutes, or until golden brown and fragrant.
9. To refrigerate, place the kneaded dough in a large, greased bowl, cover tightly, and store in the refrigerator for up to 2 weeks. When ready to bake, pinch off dough, shape into rolls, let rise for 30-60 minutes, and bake at **375°F** for about 15 minutes until golden brown.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The kneaded dough can be stored tightly covered in the refrigerator for up to 2 weeks, ready to be shaped and baked in small batches as desired.
