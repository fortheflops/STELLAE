# Hearty Whole Wheat Bread

> 📜 **Collection:** GailCollection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Bread
> *A simple, large-batch recipe for homemade whole wheat bread that yields two wholesome loaves. This straightforward yeasted dough creates golden, delicious loaves perfect for everyday sandwiches or toast.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 45 mins | 1 hour 5 mins | 2 loaves |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large mixing bowl
* Electric mixer
* Floured surface
* Two greased bread pans
* Oven

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 Tbsp (1 1/2 packets) | Dry [[Yeast]] | Active dry |
| 3 ½ Cups | Warm [[Water]] | Warm |
| 2 Tbsps | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 2 tsps | [[Salt]] | Standard |
| 8 to 9 Cups | Whole Wheat [[Bread Flour]] | One cup can be substituted with white flour |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Activate the Yeast
1. In a large mixing bowl, pour in the **3 ½ cups warm water**. Sprinkle the **1 tbsp dry yeast** and **2 tbsps sugar** over the water and let it stand for **5 minutes** until the yeast is activated and foamy.

### Step 2: Mix the Batter
2. Add the **2 tsps salt** and **4 cups whole wheat bread flour** (if using white flour, add it here). Beat well with an electric mixer for about **2 minutes** until a smooth batter forms.

### Step 3: Knead the Dough
3. Gradually stir in the rest of the **whole wheat bread flour**, a little at a time, until a stiff dough forms. Turn the dough out onto a floured surface and knead for **8-10 minutes** until it is smooth and elastic.

### Step 4: Rise and Bake
4. Divide the **dough** in half and shape into two loaves. Place the loaves in greased bread pans, cover, and let them rise in a warm place until the dough has risen well above the edge of the pans, about **1 hour**. Preheat your oven to **350°F**. Bake for **40-45 minutes**, or until the loaves are deeply golden brown and sound hollow when you tap the bottom.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** For a closer texture, let the dough rise once in the bowl before shaping it into loaves and letting it rise a second time in the pans. Store baked and cooled loaves tightly wrapped at room temperature for up to 3 days or freeze for up to 3 months.
