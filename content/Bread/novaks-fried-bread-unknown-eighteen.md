## Instructions

### Step 1: Mix the Dough
1. In a large mixing bowl, mix together **4 cups flour**, **1 tablespoon baking powder**, **1 teaspoon salt**, and **2 tablespoons sugar**.
2. Pour in **1 1/2 cups warm water** and mix with your hand until the mixture is soft like a ball of dough.

### Step 2: Shape and Fry
3. Take portions of the dough and stretch them until they form flat cakes.
4. Melt **1 cup lard** (or other shortening) in a frying pan over medium-high heat.
5. Place the dough cakes in the hot fat and fry until they are golden brown on both sides and cooked through.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Best enjoyed fresh and warm. Dough can be mixed ahead and kept covered in the refrigerator for up to 4 hours before frying.
