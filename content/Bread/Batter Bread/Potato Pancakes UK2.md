## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
| 4                  | [[Potatoes]], grated       |	
| 1/4 Cup               | [[Flour]]                   |	
| 1                  | [[Egg]], slightly beaten    |	
| 1 Teaspoon         | [[Salt]]                    |	
| 1 Teaspoon         | [[Onion]], grated, if desired|	
|                   |    [[Vegetable Shortening]]|	
___
### Instructions
1. Mix ingredients in order given.
2. Shape into cakes.
3. Fry in vegetable shortening until crisp on both sides.
4. Serve at once with sour cream.
