## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| **Wet Ingredients:** |                                           |

| One Cup                          | [[Yoghurt]] or [[Sour Cream]]             |

| **Dry Ingredients:** |                                           |
| Two Cups                         | All-Purpose [[Flour]]                     |

## Instructions
1.  In a large bowl, beat together the wet ingredients: two eggs, $\frac{1}{4}$ cup oil, and $\frac{1}{4}$ cup honey.
2.  Stir in one cup of yoghurt (or sour cream) and one cup of buttermilk.
3.  In a separate bowl, whisk together the dry ingredients: two cups flour, two teaspoons baking powder, one teaspoon baking soda, one teaspoon ground cardamom, and $\frac{1}{2}$ teaspoon salt.
4.  Combine the wet ingredients with the dry ingredients.
5.  Stir just until lightly mixed. A few lumps are okay.
6.  Heat a lightly oiled griddle or frying pan over medium-high heat.
7.  Pour or scoop the batter onto the griddle, using approximately $\frac{1}{4}$ cup for each pancake.
8.  Cook until bubbles appear on the surface and the edges are dry, then flip and cook until golden brown on the other side.
