*A unique recipe for cheese blintzes where sieved cottage cheese is mixed directly into the batter, creating a rich, thick pancake that is then filled with more cottage cheese and served with sour cream and strawberries.*

## Recipe
| Measurements      | Ingredients                                        |
| :---------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| **For the Batter:** |                                                  |
| 4                 | [[Eggs]]                                           |
| 1 cup             | Sieved [[Cottage Cheese]]                          |
| 1 cup             | [[Sour Cream]]                                     |
| 1 cup             | Sifted [[Flour]]                                   |
| 1 Tbsp            | [[Sugar]]                                          |
| ½ tsp             | [[Salt]]                                           |
| As needed         | [[Butter]] for the griddle                         |
| **For Filling & Topping:** |                                          |
| 1 cup             | [[Cottage Cheese]]                                 |
| As needed         | [[Sour Cream]]                                     |
| As needed         | Fresh [[Strawberries]]                             |
___
### Instructions
1.  In a large bowl, beat the eggs well.
2.  Add 1 cup of sieved (or blended) cottage cheese and 1 cup of sour cream. Beat until combined.
3.  Add the sifted flour, sugar, and salt. Beat the batter until it is smooth and thick.
4.  Heat a griddle or non-stick skillet over medium heat and melt a small amount of butter.
5.  Pour the batter to create pancakes (blintzes) of your desired size. Cook for 2-3 minutes per side, until golden brown and cooked through.
6.  As soon as you remove each blintz from the griddle, while it is still hot, place a heaping teaspoon of cottage cheese in the center.
7.  Roll it up and place on a serving plate.
8.  Top the warm, filled blintzes with sour cream and fresh strawberries to serve.
