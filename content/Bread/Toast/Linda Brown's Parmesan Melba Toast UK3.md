*A simple yet elegant recipe from Linda Brown for creating your own Parmesan Melba Toast. Thinly sliced bread is basted with margarine and baked low and slow until perfectly crisp.*
### Author
Linda Brown
___
## Recipe
| Measurements      | Ingredients                                        |
| :---------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 Loaf            | Favorite Bread (Roll, Bagel, Sour Dough, Wheat, etc.) |
| ½ Cup (1 stick)   | Margarine or Butter, melted                        |
| 1 Cup             | Parmesan Grated Cheese                             |
___
### Instructions
1.  **Freeze Bread:** For best results, freeze your loaf of bread until it is firm. This makes it much easier to slice thinly.
2.  **Preheat Oven:** Preheat your oven to a low temperature, between 225-250°F.
3.  **Slice Bread:** Using an electric knife or a very sharp serrated knife, slice the frozen bread as thinly as possible, aiming for 1/16" to 1/4" thick slices.
4.  **Prepare for Baking:** Arrange the paper-thin slices on a cookie sheet in a single layer.
5.  **Baste:** Baste both sides of each bread slice with the melted margarine.
6.  **Add Cheese:** Sprinkle a generous layer of grated Parmesan cheese on the top side of each slice.
7.  **Bake:** Bake for 45 minutes to 1 hour. The toasts are done when they are golden, completely dry, and crisp.
8.  **Store:** Let the toasts cool completely before storing them in an airtight container.
