### Instructions
1. Freeze bread (slices better if frozen).
2. After frozen, take out of freezer.  Slice paper thin, 1/16"-1/4" thick with an electric knife.
3. Place on a cookie sheet.
4. Melt margarine in microwave.
5. Baste both sides of bread with melted margarine.
6. Sprinkle Parmesan grated cheese on top sides of slices.
7. Bake in 225-250°F oven for 45 minutes to 1 hour.
8. Store in tight-lid container after cooled.
