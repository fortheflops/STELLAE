# Easy Beer Biscuits

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Bread
> *A remarkably simple and savory three-ingredient biscuit recipe using biscuit mix and beer. These drop-style biscuits bake up quickly in a muffin tin for any meal.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 15 mins | None | 12 biscuits |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 12-cup muffin tin
* Large mixing bowl
* Spoon or spatula

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 4 Cups | [[Biscuit Mix]] | like Bisquick |
| 3 Tablespoons | [[Sugar]] | none |
| 1 (12 oz) Can | [[Beer]] | room temperature |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Batter
1. Preheat oven to **350°F**. Grease a **12-cup muffin tin**.
2. In a large bowl, combine the **4 cups biscuit mix** and **3 tablespoons sugar**.
3. Pour in the **1 (12 oz) can beer** and stir with a spoon or spatula until just combined. The batter will be slightly lumpy and thick.

### Step 2: Bake and Serve
4. Spoon the batter into the prepared muffin tin, filling each cup about **⅔ full**.
5. Bake for **12-15 minutes**, or until the biscuits are golden brown and a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.
6. Let the biscuits cool in the muffin tin for a few minutes before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftover biscuits in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 2 days, or freeze for up to 1 month.
