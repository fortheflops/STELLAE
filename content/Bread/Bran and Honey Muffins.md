# Bran and Honey Muffins

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Bread
> *A wholesome and lightly sweetened muffin recipe based on a simple note for bran and honey muffins. Enjoy this tender, flavorful breakfast treat straight from the oven.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 20 mins | None | 12 muffins |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 12-cup muffin tin
* Paper liners
* Large mixing bowl
* Medium mixing bowl
* Wire whisk
* Spatula
* Wire cooling rack

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 cup | [[Flour]] | All-purpose |
| 1 cup | [[Bran]] | Wheat bran |
| 1 Tbsp | [[Baking Powder]] | Standard |
| 1/2 tsp | [[Salt]] | Standard |
| 1 large | [[Egg]] | Beaten |
| 1 cup | [[Milk]] | Standard |
| 1/3 cup | [[Honey]] | Liquid |
| 1/3 cup | [[Oil]] | Vegetable oil or melted butter |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Mix Dry Ingredients
1. Preheat your oven to **400°F (200°C)**. Grease a **12-cup muffin tin** or line it with paper liners.
2. In a large bowl, whisk together the **1 cup flour**, **1 cup wheat bran**, **1 tbsp baking powder**, and **1/2 tsp salt** until thoroughly combined.

### Step 2: Combine Wet Ingredients and Batter
3. In a separate medium bowl, lightly beat the **1 large egg**. Whisk in the **1 cup milk**, **1/3 cup honey**, and **1/3 cup oil or melted butter**.
4. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients and stir with a spatula until just combined. Do not overmix; the batter should be lumpy.
5. Fill the prepared muffin cups about two-thirds full with the batter.

### Step 3: Bake and Cool
6. Bake for **15 to 20 minutes**, or until a wooden toothpick inserted into the center of a muffin comes out clean.
7. Let the muffins cool in the pan for a few minutes before transferring them to a wire rack to cool completely.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftover muffins in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 3 days, or freeze for up to 2 months.
