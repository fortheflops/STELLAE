# Banana Bran Bread

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Bread
> *A delightful and wholesome banana bran bread recipe that combines ripe bananas and fiber-rich bran. This easy-to-make quick bread bakes into a moist, golden loaf.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 45 mins | None | 8 servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Loaf pan

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 4-5 | [[Banana]] | Medium, ripe, mashed (1 1/2 cups) |
| 1 | [[Egg]] | Well beaten |
| 1 cup | [[Bran]] | Shredded |
| 1/4 cup | [[Shortening]] | Melted |
| 1 1/2 cups | [[Flour]] | Sifted |
| 1/2 cup | [[Sugar]] | None |
| 2 1/2 tsp | [[Baking Powder]] | None |
| 1/2 tsp | [[Baking Soda]] | None |
| 1/2 tsp | [[Salt]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing and Baking
1. Mash **4-5 medium ripe bananas** to yield **1 1/2 cups**.
2. Stir in **1 well beaten egg**, **1 cup shredded bran**, and **1/4 cup melted shortening**.
3. Sift together and add **1 1/2 cups flour**, **1/2 cup sugar**, **2 1/2 tsp baking powder**, **1/2 tsp baking soda**, and **1/2 tsp salt**.
4. Pour the batter into a greased loaf pan.
5. Bake at 350 degrees for 45 minutes, or until golden brown and a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store tightly wrapped at room temperature for up to 3 days, or freeze the baked loaf for up to 2 months.
