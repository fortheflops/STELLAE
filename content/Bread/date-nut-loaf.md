# Date Nut Loaf

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Five | ✍️ **Attribution:** Flora Young | 📂 **Category:** Bread
> *A delightful and traditional date nut loaf recipe from the kitchen of Flora Young, featuring chopped walnuts and finely cut dates. Perfect for serving warm with butter or alongside afternoon tea.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 1 hour | None | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Loaf pan
* Sifter
* Mixing bowls

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3 cups | [[Flour]] | Sifted |
| 3 tsp | [[Baking Powder]] | Calumet brand |
| 3/4 tsp | [[Salt]] | None |
| 1/2 cup | [[Brown Sugar]] | Firmly packed |
| 1/2 cup | [[Walnuts]] | Chopped |
| 1 cup | [[Dates]] | Finely cut |
| 1 | [[Egg]] | Well beaten |
| 1 cup | [[Milk]] | 3/4 cup milk if using cake flour |
| 4 tbsp | [[Shortening]] | Melted oil or shortening |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Dry Ingredients
1. Sift **3 cups flour** once, measure, add **3 tsp baking powder** and **3/4 tsp salt**, and sift again.
2. Add **1/2 cup brown sugar** and mix well, then add **1/2 cup walnuts** and **1 cup dates**.

### Step 2: Wet Ingredients and Baking
3. Combine **1 egg**, **1 cup milk**, and **4 tbsp melted shortening or oil**, and add to the flour mixture, blending well.
4. Pour the batter into a greased loaf pan (9x5x3 inches)—putting waxed paper in the bottom of the pan makes it easier to remove.
5. Bake in a moderate oven at **350°** for **1 hour** until golden brown and a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store wrapped tightly in plastic wrap at room temperature for up to 3 days, or slice and freeze for longer storage.
