## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
| 3 Cups               | [[Flour]]                            |
| 1 Teaspoon           | [[Salt]]                             |
| 3 Heaping Teaspoons | [[Baking Powder]]                    |
| 1 Heaping Tablespoon  | [[Shortening]]                       |
| 1 Pint               | Lukewarm [[Water]]                   |
|                      | [[Fat]]                         |
___
### Instructions
1.  Sift flour, baking powder, and salt.
2.  Cut in shortening.
3.  Adding water to make dough consistency of biscuit dough.
4.  Roll out dough about 1/2" thick and cut into 3" squares.
5.  Cut hole in center.
6.  Fry in deep fat.
