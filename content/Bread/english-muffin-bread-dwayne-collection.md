# English Muffin Bread

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Bread
> *An easy, no-knead English muffin bread with a classic nooks-and-crannies texture perfect for toasting. Dusting the loaf pans with cornmeal yields an authentic bakery-style crust.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 35 mins | 45 mins | 2 loaves |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large mixing bowl
* Electric mixer
* Two 8.5x4.5x2.5-inch loaf pans
* Wire cooling racks

---
## Ingredients

### Bread Dough
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |

| 2 packages | [[Yeast]] | active dry or instant |
| 1 tbsp | [[Sugar]] |  |

| 1/4 tsp | [[Baking Soda]] |  |
| 2 cups | [[Milk]] | warm (120-130°F) |
| 1/2 cup | [[Water]] | warm (120-130°F) |
| As needed | [[Cornmeal]] | for dusting |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Make the Batter
1. In a large mixing bowl, combine **2 cups flour**, **2 packages yeast**, **1 tbsp sugar**, **2 tsp salt**, and **1/4 tsp baking soda**.
2. Add **2 cups warm milk** (120-130°F) and **1/2 cup warm water** (120-130°F).
3. Beat on low speed of an electric mixer for 30 seconds, scraping the bowl occasionally.
4. Beat on high speed for 3 minutes, then stir in the remaining **3 cups flour** (the batter will be stiff). Do not knead.

### Step 2: Proof and Bake
5. Grease two 8 1/2 x 4 1/2 x 2 1/2-inch loaf pans and sprinkle the insides generously with **cornmeal**.
6. Spoon the batter evenly into the prepared pans and sprinkle the tops with additional **cornmeal**.
7. Cover and let rise in a warm place until doubled in size, about 45 minutes.
8. Bake at 375°F for 35 minutes or until golden brown. Remove from pans immediately and cool on wire racks.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Bread can be sliced once completely cooled and stored in airtight bags for up to 3 days, or frozen for up to 2 months.
