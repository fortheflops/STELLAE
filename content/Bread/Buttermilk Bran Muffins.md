# Buttermilk Bran Muffins

> 📜 **Collection:** UK5 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Bread
> *A classic recipe for hearty and wholesome bran muffins made by soaking whole bran cereal in buttermilk for a moist texture. These tender muffins are finished with a delightful crunch of chopped nuts.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 20 mins | 5 mins | 18-24 muffins |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large mixing bowl
* Separate mixing bowl
* Muffin tin
* Paper liners
* Spatula
* Whisk

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 cups | [[Whole Bran Cereal]] | None |
| 2 cups | [[Buttermilk]] | None |
| 2 ½ cups | [[Flour]] | All-purpose |
| ¾ cup | [[Brown Sugar]] | Packed |
| 2 ½ tsps | [[Soda]] | Baking |
| 2 tsps | [[Salt]] | None |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] | Beaten |
| 1 stick (½ cup) | [[Butter]] | Melted |
| 1 cup | [[Nuts]] | Chopped |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Soak
1. Preheat the oven to **375°F**. Grease your muffin cups or line them with paper liners.
2. In a large bowl, combine the **2 cups whole bran cereal** and the **2 cups buttermilk**. Let it stand for at least **5 minutes** to allow the cereal to soften.

### Step 2: Mix the Batter
3. In a separate bowl, sift or whisk together the **2 ½ cups flour**, **¾ cup brown sugar**, **2 ½ tsps baking soda**, and **2 tsps salt**.
4. Add the **2 beaten eggs** and **1 stick (½ cup) melted butter** to the bran and buttermilk mixture and stir to combine.
5. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients and stir with a spatula until just combined. Do not overmix.
6. Fold in the **1 cup chopped nuts**.

### Step 3: Bake
7. Fill the prepared muffin cups about **⅔ full**.
8. Bake for **15-20 minutes**, or until golden and a toothpick inserted into the center of a muffin comes out clean and fragrant.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftover muffins in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 3 days, or freeze for up to 3 months.
