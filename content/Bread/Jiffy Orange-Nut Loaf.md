# Jiffy Orange-Nut Loaf

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Bread
> *A quick and easy orange nut loaf with a bright citrus flavor. This recipe uses a unique method of steeping the orange zest in hot butter and water to create a wonderfully aromatic bread.*

| 15 mins | 1 hour | 10 mins | 1 loaf |

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Steep
1. Preheat your oven to **350°F**. Grease and flour a **9x5 inch loaf pan**.
2. In a small, heatproof bowl, place the **2 tablespoons butter** and **2 tablespoons orange zest**. Pour the **1/2 cup boiling water** over them and let the mixture steep to infuse the flavors.

### Step 2: Mix Wet and Dry
3. In a large bowl, whisk together the dry ingredients: **2 cups flour**, **1 teaspoon baking soda**, **1/2 teaspoon salt**, and **1 teaspoon baking powder**.
4. In a separate medium bowl, beat the **1 large egg** with the **1 cup sugar**. Stir in the **1/3 cup orange juice** and **2 teaspoons vanilla extract**.
5. Add the steeped butter, zest, and water mixture to the egg mixture and stir to combine.

### Step 3: Combine and Bake
6. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients and mix with a spatula until just moistened. Do not overmix.
7. Fold in the **1/2 cup chopped walnuts**.
8. Pour the batter into the prepared loaf pan and bake for **1 hour**, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean and golden brown.
9. Let the bread cool in the pan for **10 minutes** before turning it out onto a wire rack to cool completely.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Wrap tightly in plastic wrap and store at room temperature for up to 3 days, or freeze for up to 2 months.
