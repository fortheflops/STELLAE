# Classic Flour Tortillas

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Bread
> *A simple, traditional recipe for soft and pliable homemade flour tortillas using lard and milk for an authentic texture and superior flavor.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 15 mins | 15 mins | 12-16 tortillas |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large bowl
* Cast-iron skillet or comal
* Rolling pin
* Clean kitchen towel

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3 Cups | [[Flour]] | All-purpose |
| 2 Heaping tsp | [[Baking Powder]] | About 2.5 tsp |
| 2 tsp | [[Salt]] | Standard |
| 3 Tbsp | [[Lard]] | Or shortening |
| 1 to 1 1/4 Cups | [[Milk]] | Warm, or water |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mix and Knead the Dough
1. In a large bowl, whisk together the **3 cups flour**, **2 heaping tsp baking powder**, and **2 tsp salt**.
2. Using your fingertips or a pastry blender, cut in the **3 tbsp lard** until the mixture resembles coarse crumbs.
3. Gradually add the **1 to 1 1/4 cups milk**, mixing with a fork until a shaggy dough forms.
4. Turn the dough out onto a lightly floured surface and knead for **1-2 minutes** until smooth and soft but not sticky.

### Step 2: Rest and Roll
5. Divide the dough into **12-16 equal pieces** and roll each into a ball. Cover with a damp cloth and let rest for **15 minutes**.
6. On a lightly floured surface, roll each dough ball out into a very thin round about 8 inches in diameter.

### Step 3: Cook and Serve
7. Heat a dry, ungreased cast-iron skillet or comal over medium-high heat.
8. Cook one tortilla at a time for about **30-60 seconds per side**, until it puffs up and develops golden-brown spots.
9. Stack the warm tortillas in a clean kitchen towel or a tortilla warmer to keep them soft as you cook them.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store cooked tortillas in a sealed bag in the refrigerator for up to 5 days, or freeze for up to 2 months.
