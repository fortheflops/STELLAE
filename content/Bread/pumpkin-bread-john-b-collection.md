# Pumpkin Bread

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Nancy L. Krenz | 📂 **Category:** Bread
> *Awarded first place at the 1974 New Mexico State Fair, this prize-winning pumpkin bread is perfectly spiced with cinnamon, nutmeg, ginger, and cloves. Richly moist and loaded with chopped nuts, it makes two fragrant loaves perfect for seasonal gatherings.*

## Instructions

### Mixing
1. In an electric mixer, combine the **3 1/2 cups flour**, **2 tsp baking soda**, **1 1/2 tsp salt**, **1 tsp cinnamon**, **1 tsp nutmeg**, **1 tsp ginger**, **1/4 tsp cloves**, **3 cups sugar**, **1 cup oil**, **4 eggs**, **2/3 cup water**, and **2 cups canned pumpkin**.
2. Beat all ingredients for **3 minutes**, reserving the nuts. Stir in the **1 cup chopped nuts** after beating.

### Baking
3. Pour batter into two greased medium size loaf pans (or one greased 10-inch tube pan).
4. Bake at 350°F for **1 hour** to **1 hour 10 minutes**, until golden and a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Loaves can be wrapped tightly in plastic wrap and stored at room temperature for up to 4 days, or frozen for up to 3 months.
