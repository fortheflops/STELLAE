# Carol Koenig's Poppy Seed Bread

> 📜 **Collection:** Jill Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Carol Koenig | 📂 **Category:** Bread
> *A simple and delicious quick bread featuring a tender crumb speckled with poppy seeds and enriched with evaporated milk. Perfect for breakfast or an afternoon treat.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 1 hour | 10 mins | 1 loaf |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 8.5 x 4.5 inch loaf pan
* Large mixing bowl
* Separate bowl
* Whisk
* Wire cooling rack
* Wooden skewer

---
## Ingredients

### Wet Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] | Large |
| 1/2 cup | [[Vegetable Oil]] | None |
| 1 cup | [[Sugar]] | None |
| 1 cup | [[Evaporated Milk]] | None |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Vanilla Extract]] | None |

### Dry Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 cups | [[All-Purpose Flour]] | None |
| 2 teaspoons | [[Baking Powder]] | None |
| 1/4 teaspoon | [[Salt]] | None |

### Add-In
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/4 cup | [[Poppy Seeds]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Mix Wet Ingredients
1. Preheat your oven to **350°F**. Grease and flour an **8.5 x 4.5 inch loaf pan**.
2. In a large bowl, beat the **2 large eggs**. Add the **1/2 cup vegetable oil**, **1 cup sugar**, and **1 cup evaporated milk**, mixing well until smooth.

### Step 2: Combine Dry Ingredients and Batter
3. In a separate bowl, whisk together the **2 cups all-purpose flour**, **2 teaspoons baking powder**, and **1/4 teaspoon salt**.
4. Add the dry ingredients to the wet ingredients and stir until just combined.
5. Fold in the **1/4 cup poppy seeds** and **1 teaspoon vanilla extract** until evenly distributed.

### Step 3: Bake and Cool
6. Pour the batter into the prepared loaf pan.
7. Bake for about **1 hour**, or until a wooden skewer inserted into the center comes out clean and the top is golden brown and fragrant.
8. Let the bread cool in the pan for **10 minutes** before transferring to a wire rack to cool completely.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the cooled loaf wrapped tightly in plastic wrap or in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 3 days, or freeze for up to 2 months.
