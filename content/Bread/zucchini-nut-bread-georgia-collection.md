## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Mixing
1. Sift together **2 cups sifted flour**, **1 teas. cinnamon**, **1/2 teas. baking soda**, **1/2 teas. salt**, and **1/4 teas. baking powder**; set aside.
2. Beat **2 eggs** in a bowl until blended, using an electric mixer at medium speed.
3. Gradually add **1 1/3 c. sugar** and **2/3 c. cooking oil**, mixing well after each addition, then blend in **2 teas. vanilla**.
4. Add dry ingredients all at once, using an electric mixer at low speed.
5. Stir in **1 1/3 c. shredded zucchini** and **1/2 c. chopped pecans**.

### Step 2: Baking and Finishing
6. Pour batter into a greased **9x5x3 in. loaf pan**.
7. Bake in a 350-degree oven for **1 hr. 15 min.** or until a cake tester inserted in the center comes out clean.
8. Cool in pan on a rack for **10 min.**, then remove from pan and cool completely on the rack.
9. Sprinkle the top generously with **confectioner's sugar** before slicing and serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store wrapped tightly in plastic wrap at room temperature for up to 3 days, or freeze for up to 2 months.
