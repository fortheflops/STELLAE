# Lisa's AP Pumpkin Bread

> 📜 **Collection:** UK5 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Lisa | 📂 **Category:** Bread
> *A classic, moist pumpkin bread loaded with warm spices, nuts, and raisins. This large-batch recipe yields two aromatic loaves perfect for sharing.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Mix Wet Ingredients
1. Preheat the oven to **350°F**. Grease the bottoms of **two 9x5 loaf pans**.
2. In a large bowl, cream the **2/3 cup shortening** and **2 2/3 cups sugar** together until well combined and fluffy.
3. Add the **4 eggs**, one at a time, beating well after each addition. Stir in the **1 (15 ounce) can pumpkin** and **2/3 cup water** until smooth.

### Step 2: Combine and Bake
4. In a separate bowl, whisk together the **3 1/3 cups flour**, **2 teaspoons baking soda**, **1 1/2 teaspoons salt**, **1/2 teaspoon baking powder**, **1 teaspoon cinnamon**, and **1 teaspoon cloves**.
5. Gradually add the dry ingredient mixture to the wet pumpkin mixture, stirring until just combined.
6. Fold in the **2/3 cup nuts** and **2/3 cup raisins**.
7. Pour the batter evenly into the prepared pans until smooth on top.
8. Bake for approximately **1 hour and 10 minutes**, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean and the loaves are fragrant and golden brown.
9. Let the loaves cool in the pans for about **10 minutes** before loosening the sides and turning them out onto a wire rack to cool completely.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Wrap cooled pumpkin bread tightly in plastic wrap and store at room temperature for up to 3 days, or freeze for up to 3 months.
