# Cornbread Muffins

> 📜 **Collection:** GailCollection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Bread
> *Enjoy light and tender cornbread muffins with this quick and classic recipe, ideal for serving alongside chili, soups, or barbecue. These slightly sweet muffins come together effortlessly for any meal.*

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Mix Dry Ingredients
1. Preheat oven to 375°F. Grease a **12-cup muffin pan** or line it with paper liners.
2. In a large bowl, sift the **¾ cup cornmeal**, **1 ½ cups flour**, **5 tsps baking powder**, **1 tsp salt**, and **¼ cup sugar** together until thoroughly combined.

### Step 2: Combine Wet Ingredients and Batter
3. In a separate bowl, whisk the **1 cup milk**, **1 beaten egg**, and **3 tbsps oil** together until smooth.
4. Pour the wet mixture into the dry ingredients and stir just until combined; do not overmix so the batter remains slightly lumpy.
5. Fill the prepared muffin cups about two-thirds full with the batter.

### Step 3: Bake
6. Bake for about **15 minutes**, or until golden brown and fragrant, and a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftover cooled muffins in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 2 days, or freeze for up to 3 months.
