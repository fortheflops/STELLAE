# Vintage Orange Peel Bread

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Boil the Rinds
1. Grind the **rinds of 3 oranges**.
2. Cover the rinds with **water** and add a **pinch of salt**.
3. Boil for **20-30 minutes** until tender, then drain very dry.

### Step 2: Make the Candied Peel and Batter
4. Add **1/4 C water** and **1 C sugar** to the drained rinds.
5. Boil until the mixture is quite thick and candied, then let cool completely.
6. Add **2 C flour**, **3 tsp baking powder**, **1 egg**, and **1 C (or less) milk** to the cooled mixture, mixing until just combined.

### Step 3: Bake the Bread
7. Pour the batter into a greased loaf pan and bake at **350°F** for **50-60 minutes** until golden brown and a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.
