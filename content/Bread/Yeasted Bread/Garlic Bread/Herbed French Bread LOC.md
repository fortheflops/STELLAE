*A simple and savory recipe for seasoned French bread. A fresh loaf is sliced and spread with a flavorful herb butter made with parsley, chives, scallions, and dill, then baked until warm and toasty.*

## Recipe
| Measurements      | Ingredients                                        |
| :---------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 Loaf            | [[French Bread]]                                   |
| ¼ Pound (½ Cup)   | [[Butter]], At room temperature                    |
| 2 Tablespoons     | Dried [[Parsley]]                                  |
| 2 Tablespoons     | [[Chives]]                                         |
| 1 Bunch           | [[Scallions]] (Green Onions)                       |
| 2 Tablespoons     | [[Dill Seed]] or [[Dill Weed]]                     |
| ½ Teaspoon        | [[Salt]]                                           |
___
## Instructions
1.  Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C).
2.  Cut the loaf of French bread into 1-inch slices, but do not cut all the way through the bottom crust.
3.  Finely chop the scallions (both white and green parts).
4.  In a bowl, combine the room-temperature butter, dried parsley, chives, finely chopped scallions, dill, and salt. Mix well to create a seasoned butter.
5.  Spread the seasoned butter generously between the slices of the French bread.
6.  Wrap the entire loaf in foil.
7.  Bake for 30 minutes.
8.  Serve warm.
