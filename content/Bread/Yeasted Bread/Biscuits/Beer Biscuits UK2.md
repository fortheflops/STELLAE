*A remarkably simple and savory three-ingredient biscuit recipe, perfect as a side for any meal. This recipe uses biscuit mix and a can of beer to create incredibly easy, drop-style biscuits.*
### Author
Unknown
___
## Recipe
| Measurements      | Ingredients                                        |
| :---------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 4 Cups            | [[Biscuit]] Mix (e.g., Bisquick)                   |
| 3 Tablespoons     | [[Sugar]]                                          |
| 1 (12 oz) Can     | [[Beer]] (Room Temp)                               |
___
### Instructions
1.  Preheat Oven to 350°F.
2.  Grease a 12-cup muffin tin.
3.  In a large bowl, combine the biscuit mix and sugar.
4.  Pour in the room-temperature beer and stir until just combined. The batter will be slightly lumpy.
5.  Spoon the batter into the prepared muffin tin, filling each cup about ⅔ full.
6.  Bake for 12-15 minutes, or until the biscuits are golden brown and a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.
7.  Let the biscuits cool in the muffin tin for a few minutes before serving.
___
