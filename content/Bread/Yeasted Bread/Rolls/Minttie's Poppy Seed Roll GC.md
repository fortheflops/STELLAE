*A simple and clever recipe from Minttie for making a classic poppy seed roll using the convenience of a hot roll mix. A sweet, rich poppy seed filling is spread over the dough, which is then rolled, shaped, and baked.*
### Author
Minttie
___
## Recipe
| Measurements      | Ingredients                                        |
| :---------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 box             | [[Hot Roll Mix]]                                   |
| 1                 | [[Egg]]                                            |
| As Needed         | Warm [[Water]]                                     |
| 1 can             | [[Poppy Seed Filling]]                             |
| As Needed         | [[Butter]], melted                                 |
___
## Instructions
1.  Prepare the hot roll mix according to the package directions, using the specified amount of warm water and one egg.
2.  Place the dough in a pre-warmed, greased bowl.
3.  Wring out a hot, damp cloth and place it over the top of the bowl.
4.  Let the dough rise in a warm, draft-free spot until it has doubled in size (about 1 hour).
5.  Turn the dough out onto a lightly floured surface.
6.  Using a rolling pin, roll the dough out into a large rectangle, about 1/4 inch thick.
7.  Spread the can of poppy seed filling over the entire surface of the dough.
8.  Roll up the dough tightly, like a jelly roll.
9.  Place the roll on a baking sheet, shaping it into a horseshoe or leaving it straight.
10. Brush the top of the roll with melted butter.
11. Bake according to the hot roll mix package directions (typically around 375°F for 20-25 minutes), or until golden brown.
