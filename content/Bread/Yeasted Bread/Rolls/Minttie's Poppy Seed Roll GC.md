## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 box                      | [[Hot Roll Mix]]                          |
| As Needed                  | [[Water]]                                 |
| 1                          | [[Egg]]                                   |
| 1 can                      | [[Poppy Seed Filling]]                    |
| As Needed                  | [[Butter]], melted                        |
___
## Instructions
1.  Prepare the hot roll mix according to the package directions, using water and an egg.
2.  Place the dough in a pre-warmed bowl.
3.  Wring out a hot, damp cloth and place it over the top of the bowl.
4.  Let the dough rise until it has doubled in size.
5.  Turn the dough out onto a floured surface.
6.  Using a rolling pin, roll the dough out until it is about 1/4 inch thick.
7.  Spread the can of poppy seed filling over the entire surface of the dough.
8.  Roll up the dough tightly, like a jelly roll.
9.  Place the roll on a baking sheet, shaping it into a horseshoe.
10. Brush the top with melted butter.
11. Bake according to the hot roll mix package directions, or until golden brown.
