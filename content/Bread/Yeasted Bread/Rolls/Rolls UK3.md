## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
| 1 Cup                 | Boiling Water               |
| 1 Cup                 | Butter or Oleo             |
| 2/3 Cup               | Sugar                       |
| 2 1/2 Package          | Yeast                       |
| 1 Cup                 | Warm Water                 |
| 2                     | Eggs                        |
| 2 Teaspoon            | Salt                        |
| 6 Cups                | Flour                       |
___
### Instructions
1.  Pour boiling water over butter, sugar, and salt.
2.  Let cool.
3. Add, Yeast, Warm Water, Eggs, and Flour.
___

>[!Note]
> Makes 3 1/2 dozen +
