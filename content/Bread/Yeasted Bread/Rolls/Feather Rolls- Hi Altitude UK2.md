## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
| 1 Cake              | [[Yeast]] or 1 pkg Fast Rising Dry Yeast |
| ¼ Cup               | Luke Warm [[Water]]                    |
| ¼ Cup               | [[Shortening]]                         |
| ¼ Cup               | [[Sugar]]                              |
| 2 Teaspoons             | [[Salt]]                               |
| 2 Cups              | Scalded [[Milk]]                        |
| 2                   | [[Eggs]], beaten                       |
| 5 to 6 Cups          | Sifted [[Flour]]                       |
___
### Instructions
1.  Soften yeast in lukewarm water.
2.  Measure shortening & sugar/salt in mixing bowl.
3.  Add scalded milk.
4.  Cool to lukewarm.
5.  Add softened yeast, eggs, & flour.
6.  Beat until smooth.
7.  Let rise until double in bulk.
8.  Stir down & with a spoon fill well-oiled muffin pans ½ full.
9. Let rise until double in bulk.
10. Bake at 375°-400° 15 to 20 min.
11. makes 30 medium rolls.
