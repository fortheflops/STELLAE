# Tex-Mex Biscuits

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Bread
> *Flavorful, cheesy biscuits packed with shredded cheddar and chopped green chilies. Quick to prepare using baking mix for a savory, golden-brown treat.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 10 mins | None | 1 dozen |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Baking sheet
* Floured surface
* Biscuit cutter

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 cups | [[biscuit/baking mix]] | Standard baking mix |
| 2/3 cup | [[milk]] | Liquid |
| 1 cup (4 oz) | [[Cheddar cheese]] | Finely shredded |
| 1 cup (4 oz) | [[green chilies]] | Chopped and drained |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparing the Dough
1. Combine **2 cups biscuit/baking mix** and **2/3 cup milk** until a soft dough forms.
2. Stir in **1 cup (4 oz) Cheddar cheese** and **1 cup (4 oz) green chilies**.

### Step 2: Shaping and Baking
3. Turn dough onto a floured surface; knead 10 times.
4. Roll out to 1/2-in thickness. Cut with a 2 1/2-in biscuit cutter.
5. Place on ungreased baking sheet.
6. Bake at 450° for 8-10 mins or until golden brown.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Unbaked cut biscuits can be frozen on a baking sheet and then stored in a bag until ready to bake from frozen.
