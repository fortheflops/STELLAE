# Minttie's Poppy Seed Roll

> 📜 **Collection:** GC | ✍️ **Attribution:** Minttie | 📂 **Category:** Bread
> *A simple and clever recipe from Minttie for making a classic poppy seed roll using the convenience of a hot roll mix. A sweet, rich poppy seed filling is spread over the dough, which is then rolled, shaped, and baked.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 25 mins | 1 hour | 1 large roll |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Damp cloth
* Rolling pin
* Baking sheet
* Pastry brush

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 box | [[Hot Roll Mix]] | 16-ounce box |
| 1 | [[Egg]] | Large |
| As Needed | [[Water]] | Warm, as per package directions |
| 1 can | [[Poppy Seed Filling]] | 12.5-ounce can |
| As Needed | [[Butter]] | Melted, for brushing |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare and Rise the Dough
1. Prepare the **1 box hot roll mix** according to the package directions, using the specified amount of warm **water** and **1 egg**.
2. Place the dough in a pre-warmed, greased bowl.
3. Wring out a hot, damp cloth and place it over the top of the bowl.
4. Let the dough rise in a warm, draft-free spot until it has doubled in size, about **1 hour**.

### Step 2: Shape and Fill
5. Turn the dough out onto a lightly floured surface.
6. Using a rolling pin, roll the dough out into a large rectangle, about **1/4 inch thick**.
7. Spread the **1 can poppy seed filling** evenly over the entire surface of the dough.
8. Roll up the dough tightly, jelly-roll style.

### Step 3: Bake
9. Place the roll on a baking sheet, shaping it into a horseshoe or leaving it straight.
10. Brush the top of the roll with **melted butter**.
11. Bake according to the hot roll mix package directions (typically around **375°F** for **20-25 minutes**), until the roll is golden brown and cooked through.
12. Let cool completely before slicing.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the baked and cooled roll wrapped tightly in plastic wrap at room temperature for up to 3 days, or freeze for up to 1 month.
