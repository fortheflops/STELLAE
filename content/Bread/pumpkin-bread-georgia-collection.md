# Pumpkin Bread

> 📜 **Collection:** Georgia Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Bread
> *A classic, spiced pumpkin bread loaded with warm autumn flavors and chopped nuts. Perfect for baking into one large or two small loaves that fill the kitchen with a cozy aroma.*

## Instructions

### Preparation & Mixing
1. Preheat your oven to **350°F** and grease one large loaf pan or two small loaf pans.
2. In a large mixing bowl, mix together **1 1/2 cups sugar** and **1/2 cup Wesson oil** until well combined.
3. Add **2 eggs**, **1 cup pumpkin**, **1/2 tsp cloves**, **1/2 tsp cinnamon**, and **1/2 tsp nutmeg**, mixing thoroughly.
4. Stir in **1 3/4 cups flour**, **1/2 cup water**, **1/4 tsp baking powder**, **1 tsp baking soda**, and **1/2 tsp salt**, mixing until the batter is smooth.
5. Gently fold in **1/2 cup nut meats**.

### Baking
6. Pour the batter into the prepared greased bread pan.
7. Bake for **1 hour** at **350°F** until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean and the top is beautifully golden brown.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store wrapped tightly in plastic wrap at room temperature for up to 3 days, or freeze for up to 3 months.
