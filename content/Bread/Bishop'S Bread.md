# Bishop'S Bread

> 📜 **Collection:** GailCollection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Bread
> *A rich, dense, and festive fruitcake-style loaf, packed with walnuts, dates, glacéed cherries, and chocolate chips. Perfect for holidays and special occasions.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Coat Mix-ins
1. Preheat your oven to **325°F**. Grease a **10x5x3 inch loaf pan** and line the bottom with greased wax paper.
2. In a medium bowl, sift together the **1 ½ cups flour**, **1 ½ tsps baking powder**, and **¼ tsp salt**.
3. Add the **⅔ cup semi-sweet chocolate bits**, **2 cups coarsely chopped walnuts**, **1 cup finely snipped dates**, and **1 cup halved glacéed cherries** to the flour mixture, stirring until all fruit and nuts are well coated to prevent sinking.

### Step 2: Mix Batter and Bake
4. In a separate large bowl, beat the **3 large eggs** well with an electric mixer at medium speed.
5. Gradually beat in the **1 cup sugar** until the mixture is light and fluffy.
6. Gently fold the flour, fruit, and nut mixture into the egg and sugar mixture until just combined into a thick batter.
7. Turn the batter into the prepared loaf pan and spread evenly.
8. Bake for **1.5 hours**, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean and the loaf is golden brown and fragrant.

### Step 3: Cool and Store
9. Cool the bread completely in the pan on a wire rack.
10. When cooled, remove from the pan, wrap tightly in foil, and store until ready to slice.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Wrap tightly in foil and store for at least a day before slicing for the best flavor and texture.
