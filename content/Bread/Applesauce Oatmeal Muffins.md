# Applesauce Oatmeal Muffins

> *Enjoy moist applesauce oatmeal muffins topped with a sweet, crunchy cinnamon-oat streusel. This wholesome recipe makes a wonderful quick breakfast or healthy afternoon snack.*

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Mix Dry Ingredients
1. Preheat oven to **400°F**. Line **12 medium muffin cups** with paper liners.
2. In a large bowl, combine **1 1/2 cups oats**, the **1 1/4 cups flour**, **3/4 teaspoon cinnamon**, **1 teaspoon baking powder**, and **3/4 teaspoon baking soda**.

### Step 2: Combine Wet Ingredients and Batter
3. In a separate bowl, whisk together the **1 cup applesauce**, **1/2 cup milk**, **1/2 cup brown sugar**, **3 tablespoons vegetable oil**, and **1 large egg**.
4. Add the wet ingredients to the dry ingredients and mix just until the dry ingredients are moistened. Do not overmix.
5. Fill the muffin cups almost full with the prepared batter.

### Step 3: Topping and Baking
6. In a small bowl, combine the **1/4 cup oats**, **1 tablespoon brown sugar**, **1/8 teaspoon cinnamon**, and **1 tablespoon melted margarine**.
7. Sprinkle the topping evenly over the batter in each muffin cup.
8. Bake for **20-22 minutes**, or until deep golden brown and a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store cooled muffins in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 3 days or freeze for up to 2 months.
