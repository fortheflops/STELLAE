# Savory Zucchini Torta

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Two | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Bread
> *A simple and savory crustless pie made with grated zucchini, onion, and Parmesan cheese held together with a Bisquick batter. Perfect as a side dish, appetizer, or light lunch.*

## Instructions

### Preparation and Baking
1. Preheat your oven to **350°F**. Grease a **9-inch Pyrex pie plate**.
2. Place the **2 cups grated zucchini** in a colander or clean kitchen towel and squeeze to remove excess water.
3. In a large bowl, mix together the squeezed **2 cups grated zucchini**, **1 cup Bisquick**, **½ cup Parmesan cheese**, **1 cup chopped onion**, **4 eggs**, **¼ cup oil**, **1 tbsp parsley**, and **salt and pepper** to taste.
4. Pour the mixture into the prepared **9-inch Pyrex pie plate**.
5. Sprinkle the top with a little extra **Parmesan cheese** and a dash of **paprika**.
6. Bake for **40 minutes**, or until golden brown and a knife inserted near the center comes out clean.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftovers covered in the refrigerator for up to 4 days. Reheat slices in a 350°F oven or toaster oven until warmed through to restore texture.
