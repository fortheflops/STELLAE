# Carrot Yogurt Bread

> 📜 **Collection:** Georgia Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Bread
> *A wholesome, low-fat, and sugar-free Carrot Yogurt Bread packed with whole wheat flour, wheat germ, bran, and sunflower seeds. Naturally sweetened with molasses and orange juice, this dense loaf is perfect for a healthy breakfast or snack.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mix Wet Ingredients
1. Place the **1 cup grated carrots**, **1 cup yogurt**, **1/2 cup orange juice**, **1/2 cup molasses**, **1/2 cup raisins**, **1/2 cup sunflower seeds**, **1/4 cup vegetable oil**, **3 slightly beaten egg whites**, and **1/2 tsp vanilla extract** in a large bowl.
2. Beat these ingredients together until well combined.

### Step 2: Combine Dry Ingredients
3. In a medium bowl, thoroughly combine the **2 cups whole wheat flour**, **1/2 cup wheat germ**, **1/2 cup bran**, **1 1/2 tsp baking soda**, **2 tsp cinnamon**, and **1/4 tsp nutmeg**.

### Step 3: Combine and Bake
4. Fold the dry ingredients gradually into the carrot mixture until just blended.
5. Place the batter in a lightly oiled bread pan.
6. Bake at **325°F** for about **1 hour** until a toothpick inserted into the center of the loaf comes out clean and the top is firm and golden brown.

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store wrapped tightly in plastic wrap at room temperature for up to 3 days, or freeze sliced portions for up to 3 months.
