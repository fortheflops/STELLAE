# Pecan Breakfast Bread

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Bread
> *A delightful pull-apart breakfast bread made with refrigerated crescent rolls, cinnamon-sugar, and a honey-vanilla glaze. This easy-to-assemble loaf is perfect for weekend brunches or morning gatherings.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 40 mins | None | 1 loaf |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 9x5-inch loaf pan
* saucepan

---
## Ingredients

### Bread Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 cans (8 oz. each) | [[Pillsbury Refrigerated Quick Crescent Dinner Rolls]] | separated into 16 triangles |
| 2 tablespoons | [[Butter]] | softened |
| 1/2 cup | [[Sugar]] | granulated |
| 1/4 cup | [[Pecans]] | chopped |
| 1 to 2 teaspoons | [[Cinnamon]] | ground |

### Topping
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/4 cup | [[Confectioners' sugar]] | sifted |
| 2 tablespoons | [[Butter]] | melted |
| 2 tablespoons | [[Honey]] | liquid |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Vanilla]] | extract |
| 1/4 cup | [[Pecan halves]] | whole |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Assembly
1. Separate the **2 cans Pillsbury Refrigerated Quick Crescent Dinner Rolls** into 16 triangles.
2. Spread each triangle with the **2 tablespoons butter**.
3. Combine the **1/2 cup sugar**, **1/4 cup chopped pecans**, and **1 to 2 teaspoons cinnamon**; sprinkle the mixture over the triangles.
4. Roll up each triangle starting at the wide end and rolling to the opposite point.
5. Place rolls, point side down, in a greased 9x5-inch loaf pan, forming 2 layers of 8 rolls each.

### Step 2: Baking
6. Bake at 375°F for 35 to 40 minutes or until the bread is golden brown and the center is fully cooked.
7. Remove from the pan at once and place right side up on a serving platter.

### Step 3: Topping
8. In a saucepan, combine the **1/4 cup confectioners' sugar**, **2 tablespoons butter**, **2 tablespoons honey**, and **1 teaspoon vanilla**.
9. Bring to a boil while stirring constantly, then stir in the **1/4 cup pecan halves**.
10. Cool slightly and drizzle over the warm bread.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The topping can be prepared in advance and reheated gently before drizzling over the freshly baked bread.
