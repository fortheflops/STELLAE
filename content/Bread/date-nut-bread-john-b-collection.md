# Date Nut Bread

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Soak
1. Combine **1 1/2 cups chopped dates** and **1 1/2 cups boiling water**, let stand till cool.

### Step 2: Mixing and Baking
2. Mix in **1 1/2 cups sugar**, **2 tbsp shortening**, and **1 egg**.
3. Stir in **2 3/4 cups flour**, a pinch of **salt**, **1 tsp cream of tartar**, **1 tsp baking soda**, **1 cup chopped nuts**, and **1 tsp vanilla**.
4. Mix well, makes **2 loaves**. Bake for **1 hour** at **375 degrees** until golden brown and a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Wrap tightly in plastic wrap and store at room temperature for up to 3 days, or freeze for longer storage.
