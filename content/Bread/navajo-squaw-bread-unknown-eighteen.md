# Navajo Squaw Bread

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Eighteen | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Bread
> *A traditional recipe for Navajo Squaw Bread, featuring a soft dough fried in shortening until golden brown and puffed. Perfect as a delicious side or base for meals.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 15 mins | None | 6 servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Frying pan

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3 cups | [[Flour]] | Standard |
| 1 tsp | [[Salt]] | None |
| 1 1/3 cups | [[Warm Water]] | Warm |
| 3/4 cup | [[Shortening]] | For frying |
| 1/2 tsp | [[Baking Powder]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Making the Dough
1. Mix together **3 cups flour**, **1 tsp salt**, and **1/2 tsp baking powder**.
2. Add **1 1/3 cups warm water**, then knead by hand until dough is soft.

### Step 2: Frying
3. Melt **3/4 cup shortening** in frying pan until hot.
4. Take a piece of dough, flatten, and drop it into the hot fat; dough should be round and thin, and after fried it should puff up and should be brown.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Best served freshly fried while warm and puffed.
