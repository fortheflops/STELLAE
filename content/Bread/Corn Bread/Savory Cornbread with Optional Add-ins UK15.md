## Recipe
| Measurements       | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| **Dry Ingredients:** |                                                    |
| 3/4 cup            | [[Quaker White Corn Meal]]                         |
| 1 cup              | [[Flour]]                                          |
| 1/4 tsp            | Baking [[Soda]]                                    |
| 1 tsp              | [[Salt]]                                           |
| 1 1/2 tsps         | [[Baking Powder]]                                  |
| **Wet Ingredients:** |                                                    |
| 2                  | [[Eggs]]                                           |
| 1 1/2 cups         | [[Buttermilk]]                                     |
| 1/3 cup            | [[Oil]]                                            |
| **Optional Add-ins:** |                                                    |
| 1 small can        | Whole Kernel [[Corn]], drained                     |
| 1 (4 oz) can       | Chopped [[Green Chiles]], drained                  |
| 1/2 cup            | Shredded [[Cheddar Cheese]]                        |
___
## Instructions
1.  Preheat oven to 425°F. Grease an 8x8-inch baking pan or a 12-cup muffin tin.
2.  In a large bowl, whisk together all the dry ingredients: corn meal, flour, baking soda, salt, and baking powder.
3.  In a separate bowl, beat the eggs, then whisk in the buttermilk and oil.
4.  Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients and mix until just combined. Do not overmix; a few lumps are fine.
5.  If using, gently fold in any of the optional add-ins like the drained corn, green chiles, and shredded cheese.
6.  Pour the batter into the prepared pan or muffin tins.
7.  Bake for 25-30 minutes (for a pan) or 15-20 minutes (for muffins), until golden brown and a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.
