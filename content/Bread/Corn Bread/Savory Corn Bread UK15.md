## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| **Dry Ingredients:** |                                           |
| 3/4 cup                    | [[Quaker White Corn Meal]]                |
| 1 cup                      | [[Flour]]                                 |
| 1/4 teaspoon               | Baking [[Soda]]                           |
| 1 teaspoon                 | [[Salt]]                                  |
| 1 1/2 teaspoons            | [[Baking Powder]]                         |
| **Wet Ingredients:** |                                           |
| 2                          | [[Eggs]]                                  |
| 1/3 cup                    | [[Oil]]                                   |
| As Needed                  | [[Buttermilk]]                            |
| **Optional Add-ins:** |                                           |
| 1 small can                | Whole Kernel [[Corn]]                     |
| 1 can                      | Chopped [[Green Chiles]]                  |
| 1/2 cup                    | Shredded [[Cheddar Cheese]]               |
| As Needed                  | [[Cream Cheese]]                          |
| As Needed                  | [[Creamed Pepper]]                        |

___
## Instructions
1.  Preheat oven to 425°F. Grease a baking pan or muffin tin.
2.  In a large bowl, whisk together all the dry ingredients: corn meal, flour, baking soda, salt, and baking powder.
3.  In a separate bowl, beat the eggs, then whisk in the oil and enough buttermilk to make a pourable batter (typically around 1 to 1.5 cups).
4.  Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients and mix until just combined. Do not overmix.
5.  If using, gently fold in any of the optional add-ins like corn, chiles, and cheese.
6.  Pour the batter into the prepared pan or muffin tins.
7.  Bake for 30 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.
