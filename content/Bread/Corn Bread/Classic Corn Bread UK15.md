## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 1/2 cups                 | [[Milk]]                                  |
| 1 1/2 cups                 | [[Buttermilk]]                            |
| 1/2 cup                    | [[Wesson Oil]]                            |
| 2                          | [[Eggs]]                                  |
| 1/2 cup                    | Melted [[Butter]]                         |
| 4 tablespoons              | [[Sugar]]                                 |
| 1/2 cup                    | Self-Rising [[White Flour]]               |
| 3 cups                     | Self-Rising [[Corn Meal]]                 |

___
## Instructions
1.  Preheat the oven to 475°F. Place a greased skillet or muffin tins in the oven to get hot.
2.  In a large bowl, beat the eggs.
3.  Add all of the liquid ingredients (milk, buttermilk, oil, melted butter) and the sugar to the beaten eggs. Mix well.
4.  Add the flour and corn meal to the wet ingredients. Mix well to combine.
5.  Pour the batter into the preheated, greased skillet or muffin tins.
6.  Bake for 20 minutes for muffins, or until the skillet cornbread is browned on each side.
___

>[!Note]
>This recipe makes 16 muffins or one skillet of cornbread.
