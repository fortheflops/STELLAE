## Instructions
1.  Preheat oven to 375°F. Grease a muffin pan or line it with paper liners.
2.  In a large bowl, sift the dry ingredients together: $\frac{3}{4}$ cup of cornmeal, one and a half cups of sifted white flour, five teaspoons of baking powder, one teaspoon of salt, and $\frac{1}{4}$ cup of sugar.
3.  Stir in one cup of milk, one beaten egg, and three tablespoons of oil. Mix until just combined; do not overmix.
4.  Put the batter into the prepared muffin pan, filling each cup about two-thirds full.
5.  Bake at 375°F for about 15 minutes, or until golden brown and a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.
