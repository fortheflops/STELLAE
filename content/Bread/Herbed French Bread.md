# Herbed French Bread

> 📜 **Collection:** LOC | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Bread
> *A simple and savory recipe for seasoned French bread featuring a flavorful herb butter made with parsley, chives, scallions, and dill. Baked until warm and toasty, it makes a wonderful pull-apart side for any meal.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 30 mins | None | 1 loaf (10-12 servings) |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Spoon or spatula
* Aluminum foil
* Baking sheet
* Chef's knife

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 Loaf | [[French Bread]] | Fresh |
| ¼ Pound (½ Cup) | [[Butter]] | At room temperature |
| 2 Tablespoons | [[Parsley]] | Dried |
| 2 Tablespoons | [[Chives]] | Fresh or dried |
| 1 Bunch | [[Scallions]] | Green onions, finely chopped |
| 2 Tablespoons | [[Dill Seed]] | Or dill weed |
| ½ Teaspoon | [[Salt]] | To taste |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Herb Butter and Loaf
1. Preheat your oven to **350°F (175°C)**.
2. Cut the **1 loaf French bread** into slices, ensuring you do not cut all the way through the bottom crust.
3. Finely chop the **1 bunch scallions**.
4. In a mixing bowl, combine the **¼ pound (½ cup) butter**, **2 tablespoons parsley**, **2 tablespoons chives**, **1 bunch scallions**, **2 tablespoons dill seed**, and **½ teaspoon salt**. Mix well until thoroughly blended into a rich seasoned butter.

### Step 2: Assemble and Bake
5. Spread the seasoned butter generously between each slice of the **1 loaf French bread**.
6. Wrap the entire buttered loaf securely in **aluminum foil**.
7. Bake in the preheated oven for **30 minutes** until warm, fragrant, and toasted.
8. Serve warm immediately, allowing guests to pull apart the buttery slices.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** You can stuff and wrap the loaf in foil up to 24 hours in advance; keep refrigerated until ready to bake.
