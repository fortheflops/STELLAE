# Jo's Whole Wheat Bread

> 📜 **Collection:** Elizabeth Knee Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Jo | 📂 **Category:** Bread
> *A classic, hearty homemade whole wheat bread recipe yielding two beautiful loaves. Sweetened naturally with honey, this traditional yeast bread is perfect for daily slicing, sandwiches, or toasting.*

---

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 45 mins | 2 hours | 2 loaves |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Kneading board

* Measuring spoons

## Ingredients

### Dough Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3 cups | [[Water]] | warm |
| 2 tbsp (or 2 packages) | [[Yeast]] | active dry |
| 1/2 cup | [[Honey]] | none |
| 6 cups | [[Whole Wheat Flour]] | divided |
| 1 tbsp | [[Salt]] | none |
| 2 cups | [[All-Purpose Flour]] | plus extra as needed for kneading |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mix and Knead
1. In a large mixing bowl, combine **3 cups warm water**, **2 tbsp yeast**, **1/2 cup honey**, and **3 cups whole wheat flour**. Mix well to combine.
2. Add the remaining **3 cups whole wheat flour** and mix well again.
3. Stir in **1 tbsp salt**, mix again, and then add **2 cups all-purpose flour**. Mix until a cohesive dough begins to form.
4. Turn the dough onto a floured bread board. Knead for **10 to 15 minutes**, adding extra **all-purpose flour** as needed, until the dough is smooth, elastic, and no longer sticky.

### Step 2: First Rise
5. Place the kneaded dough into a well-oiled bowl, turning once to coat.
6. Cover and let rise in a warm, draft-free place until it has doubled in size.
7. Gently punch down the risen dough to release air bubbles.

### Step 3: Shape, Second Rise, and Bake
8. Divide the dough in half and form into **2 loaves**.
9. Place the shaped dough into two greased loaf pans. Let rise in the pans for **30 minutes** (1/2 hour) until crowned slightly above the rims.
10. Bake at **350°F** for **45 minutes** until the crust is a deep golden brown and the loaves sound hollow when tapped on the bottom.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Baked loaves can be cooled completely, wrapped tightly in plastic wrap, and frozen for up to 3 months. Thaw at room temperature before slicing.
