# Sourdough Starter (from Yeast)

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Bread
> *Create a quick, tangy sourdough-style starter using commercial yeast and a 24-hour room temperature fermentation. This traditional method yields a bubbly, flavorful base ready for your favorite rustic breads and baked goods.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | None | 26 hours | Approx. 2 cups of starter |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Non-metallic bowl
* Measuring cups
* Measuring spoons
* Stirring spoon
* Cloth or plastic wrap

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 Package | [[Dry Yeast]] | 1/4-ounce package active dry yeast |
| 2 Cups total | [[Lukewarm Water]] | divided |
| 1 Teaspoon | [[Sugar]] | granulated |
| 1/2 Teaspoon | [[Salt]] | table salt |
| 2 Cups | [[Flour]] | all-purpose flour, divided |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Initial Mix and Rise
1. In a non-metallic bowl, dissolve **1 package** of **[[Dry Yeast]]** in **1 cup** of **[[Lukewarm Water]]**.
2. Stir in **1 teaspoon** of **[[Sugar]]**, **1/2 teaspoon** of **[[Salt]]**, and **1 cup** of **[[Flour]]** until smooth.
3. Cover the bowl and let it rise in a warm place for **1 to 2 hours** until the mixture bubbles up and then falls back down.

### Step 2: Second Feeding and Souring
4. After the mixture has fallen, stir in the remaining **1 cup** of **[[Lukewarm Water]]** and about **1 more cup** of **[[Flour]]** to recreate a thick paste consistency.
5. Cover loosely with a cloth or plastic wrap and let it stand at room temperature for **24 hours** to sour until bubbly and pleasantly tangy before using.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** To maintain this starter long-term, store it in the refrigerator, discard half regularly, and feed it with equal parts flour and water.
