# Russian Blinchiki (Thin Crepes)

> 📜 **Collection:** UK15 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Bread
> *A classic recipe for Russian blinchiki, featuring thin, delicate crepes filled with jam and baked until warm. Serve these delightful homemade crepes with a dollop of cool sour cream for an authentic treat.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 20 mins | None | 10-12 blinchiki |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Medium bowl
* Wire whisk
* Non-stick skillet or crepe pan
* Baking dish

| 2 Tbsp | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 1 Cup | [[Flour]] | All-purpose, sifted |
| 1-2 Tbsp | [[Melted Butter]] | Plus extra for greasing |

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Batter
1. In a medium bowl, beat the **2 eggs**, then whisk in the **1 cup milk** and **2 tbsp sugar** until fully incorporated.
2. Gradually add the **1 cup all-purpose flour**, whisking constantly to prevent lumps, until you have a smooth, thin batter with the consistency of heavy cream.
3. Stir in the **1-2 tbsp melted butter**. If the batter seems too thick, add a bit more [[Milk]].

### Step 2: Cook the Crepes
4. Heat a lightly greased non-stick skillet or crepe pan over medium heat.
5. Pour about **1/4 cup** of **batter** into the pan and swirl quickly to create a thin, even circle.
6. Cook for **1-2 minutes** until the edges lift and the bottom is lightly golden, then flip and cook for another minute until done.

### Step 3: Fill and Bake
7. Repeat with the remaining batter, stacking the finished [[Blinchiki]] on a plate.
8. Preheat your oven to **350°F (175°C)**. Spread a thin layer of your favorite **jam** on each [[Blinchiki]], roll it up tightly, and place it seam-side down in a baking dish.
9. Heat in the oven for **5-10 minutes** until warmed through and fragrant.
10. Serve warm with a generous dollop of **sour cream**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Unfilled crepes can be stacked, wrapped tightly in plastic wrap, and stored in the refrigerator for up to 3 days or frozen for up to 2 months.
