# Spiced Raisin Nut Bread

> 📜 **Collection:** UK15 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Bread
> *A wonderfully moist quick bread made with both brown and white sugar for a rich flavor, loaded with stewed raisins and chopped nuts. Although written on a recipe card titled 'Zucchini Bread', this delightful loaf focuses purely on sweet spices and fruit.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 1 hour | 10 mins | 2 loaves |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Two standard loaf pans
* Large mixing bowl
* Separate mixing bowl
* Wire whisk
* Spatula
* Wire cooling rack

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3 | [[Eggs]] | Large |
| 1 Cup | [[Brown Sugar]] | Packed |
| 1 Cup | [[White Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 1 Cup | [[Salad Oil]] | Vegetable oil |
| 2 Teaspoons | [[Vanilla]] | Extract |
| 3 Cups | [[Flour]] | All-purpose |
| 1 Teaspoon | [[Baking Soda]] | None |
| 1 Teaspoon | [[Salt]] | None |
| 1 Teaspoon | [[Cinnamon]] | Ground |
| ¼ Teaspoon | [[Baking Powder]] | None |
| 1 Cup | [[Raisins]] | Stewed and drained |
| ½ Cup | [[Nuts]] | Chopped |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Stew Raisins
1. Preheat your oven to **350°F**. Grease and flour **two standard loaf pans**.
2. To "stew" the **1 cup raisins**, place them in a bowl and cover with hot water. Let them soak for about **10 minutes** to plump up, then drain them well.

### Step 2: Mix Wet and Dry Ingredients
3. In a large bowl, beat the **3 eggs**. Add the **1 cup brown sugar**, **1 cup white sugar**, **1 cup salad oil**, and **2 teaspoons vanilla** and mix well.
4. In a separate bowl, whisk together the **3 cups flour**, **1 teaspoon baking soda**, **1 teaspoon salt**, **1 teaspoon cinnamon**, and **¼ teaspoon baking powder**.

### Step 3: Combine and Bake
5. Add the dry ingredients to the wet ingredients and stir with a spatula until just combined.
6. Fold in the **1 cup drained raisins** and the **½ cup nuts**.
7. Divide the batter evenly between the **two prepared loaf pans**.
8. Bake for **1 hour**, or until a toothpick inserted into the center of the loaves comes out clean. Cool in pans for **10 minutes** before removing to a wire rack to cool completely until ready to slice.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Wrap the cooled loaves tightly in plastic wrap and store at room temperature for up to 3 days, or freeze for up to 2 months.
