# Luncheon Bread

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Bread
> *A classic, tender yeast-leavened bread perfect for luncheons, designed to be pulled apart and served warm. This simple recipe yields a light, airy loaf that pairs beautifully with butter or jam.*

| 1/2 tsp | [[Salt]] | Fine |

## Instructions

### Preparation and Mixing
1. Beat **3 eggs** with **3 tbsp sugar** in a large mixing bowl.
2. Add **1/2 cup soft margarine**, **2 cups flour**, and **1/2 tsp salt** to the mixture.
3. Dissolve **2 cakes fresh yeast** in **3 tbsp warm milk** and add to the batter.
4. Beat the mixture well for 3 minutes until smooth.

### Rising and Baking
5. Place the dough into a greased bread pan or a 9-inch tube pan.
6. Let the dough rise in a warm spot until doubled in size, approximately 2 1/2 hours.
7. Bake in a preheated hot oven at 400°F for 20 minutes until golden brown.
8. Serve piping hot, pulling the bread apart at the table using two forks.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The dough can be prepared and placed in the pan, then refrigerated for a slower rise, though it is best baked fresh.
