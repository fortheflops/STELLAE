# Pumpkin Bread (Small Batch)

> 📜 **Collection:** UK15 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Bread
> *A simple, classic recipe for a single loaf of moist and spicy pumpkin nut bread. Perfect when you want a smaller batch of this autumn favorite.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 1 hour | 10 mins (Cooling in pan) | 1 loaf |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 9x5 inch loaf pan
* Large mixing bowl
* Medium mixing bowl
* Wire whisk
* Spatula
* Wire cooling rack

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 1/2 cups | [[All-purpose flour]] | Standard |
| 1 cup | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 1/2 teaspoon | [[Baking soda]] | Leavening agent |
| 1/2 teaspoon | [[Salt]] | Standard |
| 1 1/2 teaspoons | [[Cinnamon]] | Ground |
| 2 large | [[Eggs]] | Beaten |
| 1 cup | [[Pumpkin puree]] | Canned or fresh |
| 1/2 cup plus 2 tablespoons | [[Vegetable oil]] | Liquid |
| 1/2 cup | [[Nuts]] | Chopped |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Dry Ingredients
1. Preheat the oven to **350°F**. Grease and flour a standard **9x5 inch loaf pan**.
2. In a large bowl, whisk together the **1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour**, **1/2 teaspoon baking soda**, **1/2 teaspoon salt**, and **1 1/2 teaspoons cinnamon**.

### Step 2: Wet Ingredients and Combining
3. In a separate medium bowl, combine the **1 cup sugar**, **1 cup pumpkin puree**, **2 large eggs**, and **1/2 cup plus 2 tablespoons vegetable oil**. Mix well until fully incorporated.
4. Pour the wet pumpkin mixture into the dry ingredients and stir with a spatula until just combined. Do not overmix.
5. Gently fold in the **1/2 cup chopped nuts**.

### Step 3: Baking and Cooling
6. Pour the batter into the prepared loaf pan and bake for approximately **1 hour**, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean and the loaf is golden brown and fragrant.
7. Let the loaf cool in the pan for **10 minutes** before turning it out onto a wire rack to cool completely before slicing.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the cooled pumpkin bread wrapped tightly in plastic wrap or foil at room temperature for up to 3 days, or refrigerate for up to a week.
