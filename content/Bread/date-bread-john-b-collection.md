# Date Bread

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Bread
> *A delightful, moist date bread made with simple pantry staples and chopped nuts. This traditional recipe yields a comforting loaf that is perfect for tea time or breakfast.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep the Dates
1. Place **1 c. finely chopped dates** in bowl, sprinkle **1 tsp soda** and **1/2 tsp salt**, and cover with **1 c. boiling water**.

### Step 2: Mix the Batter
2. Allow to stand until cold.
3. Add **1 c. brown sugar** and **1 beaten egg**, stir well until blended.
4. Gradually add **2 c. flour**, **1 tsp vanilla**, and **1 c. chopped nuts**.

### Step 3: Baking
5. Bake in a moderate oven for **1 hr** until a rich golden brown and a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store tightly wrapped in plastic wrap at room temperature for up to 3 days or freeze for up to 2 months.
