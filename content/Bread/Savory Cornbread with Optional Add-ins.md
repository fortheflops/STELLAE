# Savory Cornbread with Optional Add-ins

## Ingredients

### Dry Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3/4 cup | [[Quaker White Corn Meal]] | None |
| 1 cup | [[Flour]] | All-purpose |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Soda]] | Baking soda |
| 1 tsp | [[Salt]] | None |
| 1 1/2 tsps | [[Baking Powder]] | None |

### Wet Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] | Large |
| 1 1/2 cups | [[Buttermilk]] | None |
| 1/3 cup | [[Oil]] | Vegetable oil |

### Optional Add-ins
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 small can | [[Corn]] | Whole kernel, drained |
| 1 (4 oz) can | [[Green Chiles]] | Chopped, drained |
| 1/2 cup | [[Cheddar Cheese]] | Shredded |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Mix Dry Ingredients
1. Preheat oven to **425°F**. Grease an **8x8-inch baking pan** or a **12-cup muffin tin**.
2. In a large bowl, whisk together all the dry ingredients: **3/4 cup Quaker White Corn Meal**, **1 cup flour**, **1/4 tsp baking soda**, **1 tsp salt**, and **1 1/2 tsps baking powder**.

### Step 2: Combine Wet and Dry
3. In a separate bowl, beat the **2 large eggs**, then whisk in the **1 1/2 cups buttermilk** and **1/3 cup oil**.
4. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients and mix until just combined. Do not overmix; a few lumps are fine.

### Step 3: Add-ins and Baking
5. If using, gently fold in any of the optional add-ins like the drained **corn**, **green chiles**, and **1/2 cup cheddar cheese**.
6. Pour the batter into the prepared pan or muffin tins.
7. Bake for **25-30 minutes** (for a pan) or **15-20 minutes** (for muffins), until golden brown and a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftover cornbread wrapped tightly at room temperature for up to 2 days, or refrigerate for up to 5 days.
