# Bran Bread

> *Discover this rustic, traditional Bran Bread featuring wholesome bran, sweet molasses, and sour milk. Perfectly baked to golden-brown perfection for a comforting treat.*

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing and Baking
1. Combine **1 qt bran**, **1 pt flour**, **1 pt sour milk**, **1 tsp soda**, **1/2 C molasses**, along with optional **nuts** and **raisins**, mixing well into a cohesive batter.
2. Transfer the mixture to a prepared pan and bake for about **3/4 hr**, until golden brown and a rich, fragrant aroma develops.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store tightly wrapped at room temperature for up to 3 days or slice and freeze for longer storage.
