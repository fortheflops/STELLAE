# Apricot Nut Bread

> 📜 **Collection:** GC | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Bread
> *A classic quick bread featuring soaked dried apricots and chopped nuts in a tender, orange-kissed crumb. Enjoy this moist loaf warm or cold with a spread of butter.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 55-65 mins | 50 mins (Soaking and Resting) | 1 loaf |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 9x5 inch loaf pan
* Parchment paper
* Large mixing bowl
* Separate mixing bowl
* Wire cooling rack

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 Cup | Dried [[Apricots]] | Plus extra warm water for soaking |
| 2 Cups | [[Flour]] | All-purpose |
| 1 Cup | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 2 tsps | [[Baking Powder]] | Standard |
| ¼ tsp | [[Soda]] | Baking soda |
| 1 tsp | [[Salt]] | Standard |
| ½ Cup | [[Nuts]] | Chopped (such as walnuts or pecans) |
| 1 | [[Egg]] | Large |
| 1 Tbsp | [[Butter]] | Soft, or shortening |
| ¼ Cup | [[Orange Juice]] | Fresh or bottled |
| ¼ Cup | [[Water]] | Standard |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Soak
1. Place the **1 cup dried apricots** in a bowl and cover them with warm water. Let them soak for **30 minutes** to soften.
2. Drain the **apricots** well and chop them into small pieces. Preheat your oven to **350°F**.
3. Grease a **9x5 inch loaf pan** and line the bottom with a piece of greased parchment paper.

### Step 2: Mix Wet and Dry Ingredients
4. In a large bowl, cream together the **1 cup sugar**, **1 tbsp soft butter**, and the **1 egg**.
5. Stir in the **¼ cup of water** and the **¼ cup of orange juice**.
6. In a separate bowl, sift together the **2 cups flour**, **2 tsps baking powder**, **¼ tsp baking soda**, and **1 tsp salt**.
7. Add the dry ingredients to the wet ingredients and stir until just combined.

### Step 3: Bake and Cool
8. Fold in the **½ cup chopped nuts** and the chopped **dried apricots**.
9. Pour the batter into the prepared loaf pan and let it stand at room temperature for **20 minutes**.
10. Bake for **55 to 65 minutes**, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean and golden brown.
11. Let the bread cool in the pan for **10 minutes** before turning it out onto a wire rack to cool completely.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store tightly wrapped in plastic wrap at room temperature for up to 3 days, or freeze for up to 2 months.
