# Pumpkin Bread

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Nine | ✍️ **Attribution:** Cheri | 📂 **Category:** Bread
> *Cheri's classic homemade pumpkin bread features aromatic warm spices like cinnamon, nutmeg, and cloves blended into a moist canned pumpkin batter. Yielding two loaves, it bakes up rich and flavorful with optional nuts, chocolate chips, or raisins.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 60 mins | 5 mins | 16 servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Sifter
* Large mixing bowl
* 2 Loaf pans
* Wire cooling rack

## Instructions

### Prep & Mix Dry Base
1. Preheat oven to 350°F and grease two loaf pans.
2. Sift together the **3 1/2 cups flour**, **1/4 tsp baking powder**, **2 tsp baking soda**, **1 1/2 tsp salt**, **1 tsp nutmeg**, **1 tsp cloves**, and **1 tsp cinnamon**.
3. Add **1 1/2 cups sugar** and **1 1/2 cups packed brown sugar** to the sifted dry mixture and mix well.

### Combine & Bake
4. Mix the dry ingredient mixture with **1 cup oil** and **1 can pumpkin** until well combined.
5. Add **4 eggs** one at a time, blending thoroughly after each addition.
6. Fold in **1 cup nuts, chips, or raisins** if using.
7. Pour evenly into the 2 prepared loaf pans.
8. Bake at 350°F for 50 to 60 minutes, until a tester inserted into the center comes out clean.

### Cool
9. Cool in pans on a wire rack for **5 minutes**.
10. Remove loaves from pans and allow to cool completely on the rack.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Loaves can be wrapped tightly in plastic wrap and stored at room temperature for up to 4 days or frozen for up to 3 months.
