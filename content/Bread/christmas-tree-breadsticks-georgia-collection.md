# Christmas Tree Breadsticks

> 📜 **Collection:** Georgia Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Bread
> *Festive and fun Christmas tree-shaped breadsticks made from refrigerated dough, brushed with egg wash, and decorated with seeds. Serve these warm as a delightful holiday appetizer or side.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 15 mins | None | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Cookie sheets
* Pastry brush

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 11 oz. | [[Refrigerated soft breadsticks]] | 1 can |
| 1 | [[Egg]] | Beaten |
| 1 T. | [[Water]] | None |
| As needed | [[Sesame seeds]] | None |
| As needed | [[Poppy seeds]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Shaping
1. Heat oven to **350°**. Lightly grease **cookie sheets**.
2. Separate **11 oz. can refrigerated soft breadsticks** dough into **8 pieces**. Unroll each into an **18" rope**.
3. Cut off **1"** of each rope. Zigzag the long **bread stick** to shape into a tree, and use the **1" piece** for the trunk.

### Step 2: Baking
4. Combine **1 egg** and **1 T. water**. Brush over trees.
5. Sprinkle with **sesame seeds** and **poppy seeds**.
6. Bake **14-16 minutes** until golden brown. Serve warm.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Best served fresh and warm straight from the oven for optimal texture.
