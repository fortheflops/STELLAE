# Feather Rolls (High Altitude)

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Bread
> *A classic recipe for light and fluffy feather rolls specifically adapted for high-altitude baking. This twice-risen yeasted dough is baked in muffin tins for perfectly shaped dinner rolls.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 25 mins | 20 mins | 2 hours | 30 medium rolls |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* small bowl
* small saucepan
* large mixing bowl
* muffin pans

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 pkg | [[Yeast]] | fast rising dry |
| 1/4 cup | [[Water]] | lukewarm |
| 2 cups | [[Milk]] | scalded |
| 1/4 cup | [[Shortening]] | softened |
| 1/4 cup | [[Sugar]] | None |
| 2 teaspoons | [[Salt]] | None |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] | beaten |
| 5 to 6 cups | [[Flour]] | sifted |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Activate Yeast and Prepare Liquid
1. In a small bowl, dissolve **1 pkg fast rising dry [[Yeast]]** in **1/4 cup lukewarm [[Water]]**. Let it stand for about **5 minutes** until it becomes foamy.
2. In a saucepan, heat **2 cups [[Milk]]** until it is scalded, just before boiling with small bubbles around the edge.
3. In a large mixing bowl, place **1/4 cup [[Shortening]]**, **1/4 cup [[Sugar]]**, and **2 teaspoons [[Salt]]**. Pour the hot scalded milk into the mixing bowl and stir until the shortening is melted. Let this mixture cool until it is lukewarm.

### Step 2: Mix the Dough and First Rise
4. Add the softened yeast mixture, **2 beaten [[Eggs]]**, and about half of the **5 to 6 cups sifted [[Flour]]** to the lukewarm milk mixture. Beat with a mixer or spoon until smooth.
5. Gradually stir in the remaining flour to make a soft dough.
6. Cover the bowl with a clean cloth and let the dough rise in a warm, draft-free place until it has doubled in bulk, about **1 to 2 hours**.

### Step 3: Shape and Second Rise
7. Stir the dough down. With a spoon, fill well-oiled muffin pans about halfway full.
8. Cover the pans and let the rolls rise again until they have doubled in bulk, about **30 to 45 minutes**.

### Step 4: Bake
9. Preheat your oven to **375°F to 400°F**.
10. Bake for **15 to 20 minutes**, or until the rolls are golden brown and fragrant.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The shaped unbaked rolls can be covered tightly and refrigerated overnight, then brought to room temperature and allowed to finish rising before baking.
