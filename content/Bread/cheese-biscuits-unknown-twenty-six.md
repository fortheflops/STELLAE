# Cheese Biscuits

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Bread
> *Fluffy, savory cheese biscuits brushed with garlic butter, yielding 12 delicious golden-brown treats. Quick to whip up and bake at a high heat for a perfect crust.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 10 mins | None | 12 biscuits |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Baking sheet
* Pastry brush

---
## Ingredients

### Biscuit Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 cups | [[Bisquick]] | Baking mix |
| 2/3 cup | [[Milk]] | None |
| 1/2 cup | [[Cheddar cheese]] | Shredded |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Garlic powder]] | None |
| 1/2 cup | [[Butter]] | Melted |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and Baking
1. Preheat oven to **450°F**.
2. Mix **2 cups Bisquick** and **2/3 cup milk**, then add **1/2 cup shredded Cheddar cheese** until a soft dough forms. Beat vigorously for **30 seconds**.
3. Drop by spoonfuls onto an ungreased cooking sheet.
4. Bake for **8-10 minutes** until golden brown.

### Step 2: Garlic Butter Finish
5. Mix **1/4 tsp garlic powder** and **1/2 cup melted butter** together.
6. Brush the mixture over the warm biscuits while still on the cookie sheet.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Best served warm straight from the oven, but baked biscuits can be stored in an airtight container for up to 2 days and reheated.
