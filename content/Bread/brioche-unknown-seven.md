# Brioche

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Seven | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Bread
> *A classic homemade brioche recipe featuring rich butter, milk, and eggs. Perfect for breakfast or as a delicious sweet bread base.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 30 mins | 30 mins | 24 hours | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Baking sheet
* Pastry brush

---
## Ingredients

### Brioche Dough
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 cup | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 1/2 tsp | [[Salt]] | None |
| 2 pkg | [[Yeast]] | Active dry |
| 3 1/2 cups | [[Flour]] | All-purpose |
| 2/3 cup | [[Butter]] | Softened |
| 1/2 cup | [[Milk]] | Lukewarm |
| 1 tsp | [[Lemon Peel]] | Grated |
| 5 | [[Eggs]] | Beaten |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing and Rising
1. Combine the **1/2 cup sugar**, **1/2 tsp salt**, **2 pkg yeast**, **3 1/2 cups flour**, **2/3 cup butter**, **1/2 cup milk**, **1 tsp lemon peel**, and **5 eggs** in a large mixing bowl.
2. Rise at **80°F** until doubled in size, then refrigerate for **24 hours**.

### Step 2: Shaping and Baking
3. Punch down the dough and shape as desired.
4. Brush with egg yolk and bake until golden brown and sound hollow when tapped.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The dough can be kept refrigerated for up to 24 hours before shaping and baking.
