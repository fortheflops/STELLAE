## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing and Baking
1. Combine **1 C. graham flour**, **1/2 C. white flour**, **1/8 C. sugar**, **1/2 t. soda**, **1/2 t. salt**, and **1/2 t. baking powder** in a mixing bowl.
2. Mix in **2 T. molasses**, **1/2 C. raisins**, and **3/4 C. sour milk** until a batter forms.
3. Pour into a greased pan and bake until golden brown and a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.
