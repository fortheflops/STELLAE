# Cheese Bread

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Bread
> *A simple and comforting savory cheese bread layered with whole wheat slices and a rich egg custard. This classic recipe is perfect for a quick breakfast or a light lunch.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 25 mins | None | 1 serving |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Baking dish
* Mixing bowl
* Whisk

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 slices | [[Whole Wheat Bread]] | Buttered |
| 1/2 cup | [[Cheese]] | Grated |
| 1 | [[Egg]] | Well beaten |
| 1 cup | [[Milk]] | Whole |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Salt]] | None |
| to taste | [[Pepper]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Assembly
1. Butter the **2 slices whole wheat bread**.
2. Place one slice in a buttered baking dish.
3. Cover the slice with **1/2 cup grated cheese**.
4. Place the other slice of bread on top of the cheese.

### Step 2: Custard and Baking
5. In a mixing bowl, combine the **1 well beaten egg**, **1 cup milk**, **1/4 tsp salt**, and **pepper** to taste.
6. Pour the mixture over the bread.
7. Bake in a hot oven until the center is firm to the touch.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Assemble the dish and pour the custard over the bread, then refrigerate for up to 1 hour before baking to allow the bread to soak up the liquid.
