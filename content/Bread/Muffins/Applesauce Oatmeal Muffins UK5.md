## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| **For the Muffins:** |                                           |
| 1 1/2 cups                 | [[Oats]]                                  |
| 1 1/4 cups                 | [[Flour]]                                 |
| 3/4 teaspoon               | [[Cinnamon]]                              |
| 1 teaspoon                 | [[Baking Powder]]                         |
| 3/4 teaspoon               | Baking [[Soda]]                           |
| 1 cup                      | [[Applesauce]]                            |
| 1/2 cup                    | [[Milk]]                                  |
| 1/2 cup                    | Packed [[Brown Sugar]]                    |
| 3 tablespoons              | Vegetable [[Oil]]                         |
| 1                          | [[Egg]]                                   |
| **For the Topping:** |                                           |
| 1/4 cup                    | Quaker [[Oats]]                           |
| 1 tablespoon               | Packed [[Brown Sugar]]                    |
| 1/8 teaspoon               | [[Cinnamon]]                              |
| 1 tablespoon               | Melted [[Margarine]]                      |
___
## Instructions
1.  Heat oven to 400°F. Line 12 medium muffin cups with paper liners.
2.  In a large bowl, combine 1 1/2 cups of oats, the flour, 3/4 teaspoon of cinnamon, baking powder, and baking soda.
3.  In a separate bowl, add the applesauce, milk, 1/2 cup of brown sugar, oil, and egg.
4.  Add the wet ingredients to the dry ingredients and mix just until the dry ingredients are moistened.
5.  Fill the muffin cups almost full.
6.  In a small bowl, combine the remaining ingredients for the topping (1/4 cup oats, 1 tablespoon brown sugar, 1/8 teaspoon cinnamon, and melted margarine).
7.  Sprinkle the topping evenly over the batter in each muffin cup.
8.  Bake for 20-22 minutes, or until deep golden brown.
