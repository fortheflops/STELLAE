## Recipe
| Measurements      | Ingredients                                        |
| :---------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 Cup             | [[Whole Wheat Flour]]                              |
| 1 Cup             | [[White Flour]] (Unbleached)                       |
| 3 tsp.            | [[Baking Powder]]                                  |
| 1/2 tsp           | [[Salt]]                                           |
| 2                 | Slightly Beaten [[Eggs]]                           |
| 1 (8 oz) Carton   | Spiced Apple-Flavored [[Yogurt]]                   |
| 1/3 Cup           | [[Honey]]                                          |
| 1/4 Cup           | [[Cooking Oil]]                                    |
___
### Instructions
1.  Preheat your oven to 400°F. Grease a 12-cup muffin pan or line it with paper liners.
2.  In a large mixing bowl, stir together the flours, baking powder, and salt.
3.  In a separate medium bowl, combine the beaten eggs, honey, and oil. Stir in the yogurt.
4.  Pour the yogurt mixture into the flour mixture. Stir with a spatula just until the dry ingredients are moistened; the batter will be lumpy.
5.  Fill the prepared muffin cups ⅔ full.
6.  Bake for 18 to 20 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.
