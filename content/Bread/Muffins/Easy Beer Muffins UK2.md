*A remarkably simple and savory three-ingredient muffin recipe. All you need is Bisquick, a little sugar, and a can of beer to make these quick and easy muffins.*
### Author
Unknown
___
## Recipe
| Measurements      | Ingredients                                        |
| :---------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 3 Cups            | [[Bisquick]]                                       |
| 3 Teaspoons       | [[Sugar]]                                          |
| 1 (12 oz) Can     | [[Beer]]                                           |
___
### Instructions
1.  Preheat your oven to 350°F. Grease a 12-cup muffin tin.
2.  In a medium bowl, combine the Bisquick and sugar.
3.  Pour in the can of beer and stir with a spoon or spatula until the ingredients are just blended. Do not overmix.
4.  Pour the batter into the prepared muffin tins, filling each cup about two-thirds full.
5.  Bake for 18-20 minutes, or until the muffins are golden brown and a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.
___

>[!Servings]
>Makes 12
