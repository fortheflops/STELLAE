# Zucchini Bread

> 📜 **Collection:** Georgia Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Bread
> *A delightful and moist homemade zucchini bread recipe yielding two delicious loaves with a sweet orange glaze. Perfect for using up garden zucchini with warm notes of cinnamon and vanilla.*

---

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 55 mins | None | 2 loaves |

## Ingredients

### Bread Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3 | [[Eggs]] | Beaten foamy |
| 1 cup | [[Oil]] | Vegetable oil |
| 1 tbsp | [[Cinnamon]] | Ground |
| 1 tbsp | [[Vanilla]] | Extract |
| 2 cups | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 1/4 cup | [[Nut meats]] | Chopped fine |
| 3 cups | [[Flour]] | All-purpose |
| 1 tsp | [[Salt]] | Standard |
| 1 tsp | [[Soda]] | Baking soda |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Baking Powder]] | Standard |
| 2 cups | [[Zucchini]] | With skins on, chopped fine |

### Glaze Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 cup | [[Powdered Sugar]] | Sifted |
| 1/4 cup | [[Frozen Orange Juice Concentrate]] | Thawed slightly |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Batter Preparation
1. Beat **3 eggs** until foamy, then add **2 cups sugar**, **1 cup oil**, **2 cups zucchini**, **1 tbsp vanilla**, and **1 tbsp cinnamon**.
2. Add the dry ingredients consisting of **3 cups flour**, **1 tsp salt**, **1 tsp soda**, and **1/4 tsp baking powder**. Mix well until just combined, then fold in **1/4 cup nut meats**.

### Step 2: Baking
3. Put the batter into greased loaf pans.
4. Bake at 325°F for **50-60 minutes**, or until a wooden toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean and the loaves are golden brown.

### Step 3: Glaze
5. Mix together **1 cup powdered sugar** and **1/4 cup frozen orange juice**.
6. Pour the glaze over the bread while it is still warm after baking.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the cooled loaves tightly wrapped in plastic wrap at room temperature for up to 3 days, or freeze for up to 2 months.
