### Instructions
1.  To a half c. sifted meal, add slowly while stirring, 1 C. boiling water.
2.  Place on slow heat and stir well for several minutes.
3.  Add butter & salt to mixture.
4.  Remove from fire, add 1/2 c. milk & 1 egg, well beaten.
5.  Put in greased baking dish & bake in moderate oven (350°F) 20 minutes.
