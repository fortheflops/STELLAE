# Banana Bread

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Bread
> *A classic homemade banana bread enhanced with a touch of vinegar and fragrant black walnut flavoring. Perfectly tender, moist, and simple to whip together for breakfast or snacking.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 50 mins | 10 mins | 1 loaf |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Sifter

* Measuring cups and spoons

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |

| 1/2 cup | [[Shortening]] | None |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] | unbeaten |
| 1 cup | [[Bananas]] | very ripe, mashed to liquid |
| 2 tsp | [[Vinegar]] | None |
| 1 tsp | [[Black Walnut Flavoring]] | None |
| 2 cups | [[Flour]] | None |
| 1 tsp | [[Baking Powder]] | None |
| 2/3 tsp | [[Baking Soda]] | None |
| 1/2 tsp | [[Salt]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Mixing & Baking
1. Cream together **1/2 cup shortening** and **1 cup sugar** until smooth. Add **2 unbeaten eggs**, **1 cup very ripe bananas**, **2 tsp vinegar**, and **1 tsp black walnut flavoring**.
2. Sift together **2 cups flour**, **1/2 tsp salt**, **2/3 tsp baking soda**, and **1 tsp baking powder**.
3. Add the sifted dry ingredients to the creamed mixture; mix well, but do not beat as for regular cake.
4. Transfer batter to a prepared loaf pan and bake for **50 minutes** at 350 degrees until golden brown and a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store wrapped tightly at room temperature for up to 4 days, or freeze the fully cooled loaf for up to 3 months.
