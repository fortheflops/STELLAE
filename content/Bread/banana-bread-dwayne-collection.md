# Banana Bread

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Bread
> *A classic and comforting homemade banana bread recipe featuring sweet mashed bananas and crunchy walnuts. Perfect for a cozy morning breakfast or an afternoon treat.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 1 hour | None | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Loaf pan

---
## Ingredients

### Batter Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 cup | [[Shortening]] | Softened |
| 3/4 cup | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] | Whole |
| 1 cup | [[Mashed Bananas]] | Ripe |
| 1 tsp | [[Lemon Juice]] | Fresh |
| 2 cups | [[Flour]] | All-purpose |
| 3 tsp | [[Baking Powder]] | Standard |
| 1/2 tsp | [[Salt]] | Standard |
| 1 cup | [[Walnuts]] | Chopped, if desired |

---

## Instructions

### Mixing and Baking
1. Preheat your oven to 350 degrees F and grease a loaf pan.
2. Beat **1/2 cup shortening** and **3/4 cup sugar** together until light and fluffy.
3. Add **2 eggs** and beat well.
4. Combine **1 cup mashed bananas** and **1 tsp lemon juice**, then mix into the creamed mixture.
5. Sift together **2 cups flour**, **3 tsp baking powder**, and **1/2 tsp salt**, then gently fold into the wet ingredients along with **1 cup walnuts** if desired.
6. Pour batter into the prepared greased loaf pan and bake for 1 hour until golden brown and a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store tightly wrapped at room temperature for up to 3 days or freeze for up to 2 months.
