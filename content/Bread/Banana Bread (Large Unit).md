# Banana Bread (Large Unit)

> 📜 **Collection:** UK15 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Bread
> *A moist, large-batch banana bread recipe that is incredibly versatile and can be made with chocolate chips, nuts, or butterscotch chips. Perfect for baking in loaf pans, mini pans, or a Bundt pan.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 45 mins | None | 2 loaves or 1 Bundt cake |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large mixing bowl
* Separate mixing bowl
* Whisk
* Loaf pans or Bundt pan

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 cup | [[Shortening]] | Softened |
| 2 cups | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 4 | [[Eggs]] | Large |
| 8 | [[Bananas]] | Ripe, mashed |
| 2 tablespoons | [[Buttermilk]] | Or substitute with lemon juice |
| 2 teaspoons | [[Vanilla]] | Extract |
| 4 cups | [[Flour]] | All-purpose |
| 2 teaspoons | [[Soda]] | Baking soda |
| 1/2 teaspoon | [[Salt]] | None |
| 1 heaping teaspoon | [[Cinnamon]] | Ground |
| 1 to 2 cups | [[Chocolate Chips]] | Or [[Nuts]], or [[Butterscotch Chips]] |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Wet Ingredients
1. Preheat the oven to **350°F**. Grease and flour your chosen pan(s).
2. In a large bowl, cream the **1 cup shortening** and **2 cups sugar** together until light and fluffy.
3. Beat in the **4 eggs**, **2 tablespoons buttermilk** (or lemon juice), **8 bananas** (mashed), and **2 teaspoons vanilla** until smooth and well combined.

### Step 2: Dry Ingredients and Combining
4. In a separate bowl, whisk together the dry ingredients: **4 cups flour**, **2 teaspoons soda**, **1/2 teaspoon salt**, and **1 heaping teaspoon cinnamon**.
5. Add the dry ingredient mixture to the wet mixture and stir gently until just combined until no flour pockets remain. Do not overmix.

### Step 3: Mix-ins and Baking
6. Fold in your choice of **1 to 2 cups chocolate chips**, **nuts**, or **butterscotch chips**.
7. Pour the batter into the prepared pan(s) and bake until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean and golden brown and fragrant.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store wrapped tightly in plastic wrap at room temperature for up to 3 days or freeze for up to 2 months.
