# Graham Muffins

> 📜 **Collection:** GC | ✍️ **Attribution:** Ruth Marshel | 📂 **Category:** Bread
> *A classic large-batch refrigerator bran muffin recipe from Ruth Marshel that yields delicious, fresh-baked muffins on demand. The versatile batter can be stored safely in the refrigerator for up to two weeks.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 25 mins | 20 mins | 1-2 weeks (refrigeration) | 5-6 dozen muffins |

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 6 Cups | All-Bran or Bran Buds [[Cereal]] | Divided |
| 2 Cups | Boiling [[Water]] | Hot |
| 1 Cup | Crisco or other vegetable [[Shortening]] | Softened |
| 2 ½ Cups | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 4 | [[Eggs]] | Large |
| 1 Quart (4 Cups) | [[Buttermilk]] | Cold |
| 5 Cups | [[Flour]] | All-purpose |
| 5 Teaspoons | Baking [[Soda]] | Leavening |
| 1 ½ Tablespoons | [[Salt]] | Standard |
| To Taste | [[Raisins]] or [[Dates]] | Optional |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Soak the Bran
1. In a medium bowl, pour the **2 cups boiling water** over **2 cups of the bran cereal**. Stir and set aside to cool completely.

### Step 2: Cream the Base
2. In a very large bowl, cream together the **1 cup vegetable shortening** and **2 ½ cups sugar**. Add the **4 eggs** one at a time, beating well after each addition until fluffy and smooth.

### Step 3: Combine Liquids and Dry Elements
3. In a separate bowl, whisk the **5 teaspoons baking soda** and **1 ½ tablespoons salt** into the **1 quart buttermilk**.
4. Add the buttermilk mixture to the creamed sugar mixture. Stir in the cooled, soaked bran cereal from the first step until incorporated.
5. Add the remaining **4 cups dry bran cereal** and the **5 cups flour** to the wet ingredients. Stir gently until just combined.

### Step 4: Add Mix-ins and Store
6. If desired, gently fold in **raisins or dates**.
7. Store the batter in a large, covered container in the refrigerator. It will keep fresh for **1-2 weeks**.

### Step 5: Bake
8. To bake, preheat the oven to **400°F**. Spoon the cold batter into greased or lined muffin tins, filling them about two-thirds full.
9. Bake for **15-20 minutes** until golden brown and a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the unbaked batter in a covered container in the refrigerator for up to 1-2 weeks, baking fresh batches as needed.
