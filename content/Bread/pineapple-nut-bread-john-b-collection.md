# Pineapple Nut Bread

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Bread
> *A tender quick bread loaded with crushed pineapple, chopped nuts, sweet raisins, and a touch of vanilla. Baked to perfection in a single loaf pan for an easy breakfast or afternoon treat.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep & Mix Dry Ingredients
1. Preheat oven to 350°F and grease a loaf pan.
2. Sift together **2 cups flour**, **1/2 tsp salt**, **1/2 cup sugar**, and **1 tsp baking powder** into a mixing bowl.
3. Add **1/2 cup chopped nuts** and **1/2 cup raisins** to the dry mix.

### Step 2: Combine Wet Ingredients & Batter
4. In a separate bowl, combine **1 egg**, **1 tsp vanilla**, and **2 tbsp melted shortening**.
5. Dissolve **1 tsp baking soda** into **1 flat can (8 oz) crushed pineapple** (with juice).
6. Stir all wet and dry ingredients together until fully blended. If the batter seems too stiff, add **a little extra juice or water** and stir in.

### Step 3: Bake
7. Pour batter into the greased loaf pan and bake at 350°F for **1 hour**, or until golden brown and a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.
