# Mexican Corn Bread

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Bread
> *A classic, savory Mexican cornbread layered with green chilies and sharp cheese. Baked in a hot skillet for a perfectly golden, crispy crust.*

## Instructions

### Preparation & Baking
1. Preheat oven to **375°F**.
2. Drain and chop the **1 small can green chili peppers**.
3. In a mixing bowl, mix all ingredients except the cheese and chilies: combine the **1 cup cornmeal**, **1/2 teaspoon salt**, **1 cup cream-style corn**, **2 eggs**, **2/3 cup buttermilk**, **1/2 teaspoon baking soda**, and **1/3 cup shortening**.
4. Pour half of the batter mixture into a hot skillet.
5. Sprinkle the **1 cup grated sharp cheese** and the chopped **green chili peppers** evenly over the batter layer.
6. Pour the remaining batter mixture over the cheese and chili layer.
7. Bake in the preheated oven at **375°F** for **30 to 40 minutes** until the top is beautifully golden brown and a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Best served warm fresh from the oven. Leftovers can be stored in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days and reheated before serving.
