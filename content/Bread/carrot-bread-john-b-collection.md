# Carrot Bread

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Bread
> *A classic, comforting homemade carrot bread recipe loaded with warm spices and freshly grated carrots. Perfectly moist and flavorful, this traditional loaf is wonderful for breakfast or an afternoon treat.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 1 hour | None | 8 servings |

* Measuring cups

## Ingredients

### Batter Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 4 | [[Eggs]] | Beaten |
| 2 cups | [[Sugar]] | None |
| 1 1/4 cups | [[Salad oil]] | None |
| 3 cups | [[Flour]] | None |
| 2 tsp | [[Baking powder]] | None |
| 1 1/2 tsp | [[Baking soda]] | None |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Salt]] | None |
| 2 tsp | [[Cinnamon]] | None |
| 2 cups | [[Grated raw carrots]] | Grated |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing the Batter
1. Beat **4 eggs**, add **2 cups sugar** gradually, beating until thick.
2. Add **1 1/4 cups salad oil** and beat until combined.
3. Stir in **3 cups flour**, **2 tsp baking powder**, **1 1/2 tsp baking soda**, **1/4 tsp salt**, and **2 tsp cinnamon**.
4. Stir in **2 cups grated raw carrots** until thoroughly incorporated into the batter.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Wrap tightly in plastic wrap and store at room temperature for up to 3 days, or freeze for up to 2 months.
