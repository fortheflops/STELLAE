## Recipe
| Measurements               | Ingredients                               |
| :------------------------- | :---------------------------------------- |
| **1st Bowl (Wet):** |                                           |
| 1 1/2 cups                 | [[Canola Oil]]                            |
| 2 1/2 cups                 | White [[Sugar]]                           |
| 5                          | [[Eggs]], well beaten                     |
| 2 teaspoons                | [[Vanilla]]                               |
| **2nd Bowl (Bananas):** |                                       |
| 2 teaspoons                | [[Lemon Juice]]                           |
| 8                          | Mashed [[Bananas]]                        |
| **3rd Bowl (Dry):** |                                           |
| 4 cups                     | [[Flour]]                                 |
| 3 teaspoons                | [[Baking Powder]]                         |
| 3 teaspoons                | Baking [[Soda]]                           |
| 1 teaspoon                 | [[Salt]]                                  |
| 2 cups                     | Chopped [[Walnuts]]                       |
| 1 to 2 cups                | Washed [[Raisins]]                        |
___
## Instructions
1.  Preheat the oven to 375°F. Grease and flour your Bundt pan(s).
2.  In a large bowl (1st Bowl), combine the canola oil, sugar, beaten eggs, and vanilla.
3.  In a separate bowl (2nd Bowl), mix the lemon juice and mashed bananas.
4.  In a third, very large bowl (3rd Bowl), whisk together the flour, baking powder, soda, and salt. Stir in the chopped walnuts and raisins.
5.  Pour the contents of the 2nd Bowl (bananas) into the 1st Bowl (wet ingredients) and stir.
6.  Pour the combined wet ingredients into the 3rd Bowl (dry ingredients).
7.  Stir until just mixed.
8.  Pour the batter into the prepared Bundt pan(s).
9.  Bake for 1 hour, or until a tester comes out clean.
