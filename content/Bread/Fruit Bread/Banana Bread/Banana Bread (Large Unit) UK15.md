## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 cup                      | [[Shortening]]                            |
| 2 cups                     | [[Sugar]]                                 |
| 4                          | [[Eggs]]                                  |
| 2 teaspoons                | [[Vanilla]]                               |
| 2 tablespoons              | [[Buttermilk]] or [[Lemon Juice]]         |
| 8                          | Mashed [[Bananas]]                        |
| 4 cups                     | All-purpose [[Flour]]                     |
| 2 teaspoons                | Baking [[Soda]]                           |
| 1/2 teaspoon               | [[Salt]]                                  |
| 1 heaping teaspoon         | [[Cinnamon]]                              |
| 1 cup or slightly more     | [[Chocolate Chips]], [[Nuts]], or [[Butterscotch Chips]] |

___
## Instructions
1.  Preheat the oven to 350°F. Grease your chosen pan(s).
2.  In a large bowl, cream the shortening and sugar together.
3.  Beat in the eggs, buttermilk (or lemon juice), mashed bananas, and vanilla.
4.  In a separate bowl, whisk together the dry ingredients (flour, soda, salt, cinnamon).
5.  Add the dry ingredients to the wet mixture and mix until just combined.
6.  Fold in the chocolate chips, nuts, or butterscotch chips.
7.  Pour the batter into the prepared pan(s) and bake according to the notes below.
___

>[!Note]
>This recipe can be baked in several ways:
>* Two standard loaf pans: Bake at 350°F for about 45 minutes.
>* 6 or 7 small mini aluminum pans: Bake for 45 minutes to 1 hour.
>* One Bundt pan: Bake for 30 to 35 minutes.
