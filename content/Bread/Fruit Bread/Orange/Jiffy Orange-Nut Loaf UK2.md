*A quick and easy orange nut loaf with a bright citrus flavor. This recipe uses a unique method of steeping the orange zest in hot butter and water to create a wonderfully aromatic bread.*

## Recipe
| Measurements      | Ingredients                                        |

| 2 Cups            | [[Flour]]                                          |
| 1 Cup             | [[Sugar]]                                          |
| 1 tsp             | [[Baking Soda]]                                    |
| 1 tsp             | [[Baking Powder]]                                  |
| ½ tsp             | [[Salt]]                                           |
| 1                 | [[Egg]]                                            |
| ⅓ Cup             | [[Orange Juice]]                                   |
| 2 Tbsp            | [[Orange Zest]]                                    |
| 2 Tbsp            | [[Butter]]                                         |
| ½ Cup             | [[Boiling Water]]                                  |
| 2 tsps            | [[Vanilla]] Extract                                |
| ½ Cup             | [[Walnuts]] (Chopped)                              |
___
### Instructions
1.  Preheat your oven to 350°F. Grease and flour a 9x5 inch loaf pan.
2.  In a small, heatproof bowl, place the butter and orange zest. Pour the boiling water over them and let the mixture steep to infuse the flavors.
3.  In a large bowl, whisk together the dry ingredients: flour, baking soda, salt, and baking powder.
4.  In a separate medium bowl, beat the egg with the sugar. Stir in the orange juice and vanilla.
5.  Add the steeped butter/zest/water mixture to the egg mixture and stir to combine.
6.  Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients and mix with a spatula until just moistened. Do not overmix.
7.  Fold in the chopped walnuts.
8.  Pour the batter into the prepared loaf pan.
9.  Bake for 1 hour, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.
10. Let the bread cool in the pan for 10 minutes before turning it out onto a wire rack to cool completely.
