*A classic, moist pumpkin bread packed with warm spices, sweet raisins, and crunchy nuts. This recipe rests for 20 minutes before baking to create a wonderfully tender loaf.*

## Recipe
| Measurements       | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| ⅔ Cup              | Dark [[Brown Sugar]]                               |
| ⅓ Cup              | Soft [[Shortening]]                                |
| 2                  | [[Eggs]]                                           |
| 3 Tablespoons      | [[Buttermilk]]                                     |
| 1 Cup              | Canned [[Pumpkin]]                                 |
| 2 Cups             | Sifted [[Flour]]                                   |
| 1 teaspoon         | [[Baking Powder]]                                  |
| ½ teaspoon         | Baking [[Soda]]                                    |
| ½ teaspoon         | [[Salt]]                                           |
| 1 teaspoon         | [[Cinnamon]]                                       |
| ½ teaspoon         | [[Nutmeg]]                                         |
| ½ Cup              | Chopped [[Nuts]]                                   |
| ½ Cup              | [[Raisins]]                                        |
___
## Instructions
1.  Preheat oven to 350°F. Grease a 9x5x3 inch loaf pan.
2.  In a large bowl, mix together the brown sugar, soft shortening, and eggs thoroughly.
3.  Stir in the buttermilk and canned pumpkin; mix well.
4.  In a separate bowl, sift together the flour, baking powder, baking soda, salt, cinnamon, and nutmeg.
5.  Fold the dry ingredients into the wet pumpkin mixture, mixing just until moistened.
6.  Fold in the chopped nuts and raisins.
7.  Pour the batter into the prepared loaf pan.
8.  Let the batter stand in the pan at room temperature for 20 minutes before baking.
9.  Bake for 50 to 60 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.
10. Cool in the pan for 10 minutes before turning out onto a wire rack to cool completely.
