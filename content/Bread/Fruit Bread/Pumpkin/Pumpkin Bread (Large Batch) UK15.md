## Instructions
(No instructions were provided on the recipe card. The following is an inferred method.)
1.  Preheat the oven to 325°F or 350°F depending on pan size. Grease and flour your loaf pans.
2.  In a large bowl, whisk together the flour, baking soda, salt, and cinnamon.
3.  In a separate, very large bowl, combine the sugar, pumpkin, eggs, and oil. Mix well.
4.  Gradually add the dry ingredients to the wet ingredients, mixing until just combined.
5.  Fold in the nuts.
6.  Divide the batter among your prepared pans.
