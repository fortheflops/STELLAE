## Instructions
1.  Preheat the oven to 350°F. Grease and flour a standard loaf pan.
2.  In a large bowl, whisk together the flour, baking soda, salt, and cinnamon.
3.  In a separate bowl, combine the sugar, pumpkin, eggs, and oil. Mix well.
4.  Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients and stir with a spatula until just combined.
5.  Fold in the nuts.
6.  Pour the batter into the prepared loaf pan.
7.  Bake for approximately 1 hour, or until a tester inserted into the center comes out clean.
8.  Let the loaf cool in the pan for 10 minutes before turning it out onto a wire rack to cool completely before slicing.
