# Pumpkin Bread (Large Batch)

> 📜 **Collection:** UK15 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Bread
> *A moist, spiced large-batch pumpkin bread recipe that yields multiple loaves, perfect for holiday gifts or freezing. Packed with classic autumn flavors and nuts.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 1 hour | 15 mins | 3 large loaves or 6-7 small loaves |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Very large mixing bowl
* Separate mixing bowl
* Whisk
* Spatula
* Loaf pans
* Wire cooling rack

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and Wet Ingredients
1. Preheat your oven to **325°F** for standard loaf pans or **340°F** for smaller pans. Thoroughly grease and flour your loaf pans.
2. In a very large bowl, combine **3 cups sugar**, **3 cups pumpkin puree**, **6 large eggs**, and **1 3/4 cups vegetable oil**. Whisk until completely smooth and well combined.

### Step 2: Dry Ingredients and Combining
3. In a separate large bowl, whisk together **4 1/2 cups all-purpose flour**, **1 1/2 teaspoons baking soda**, **1 1/2 teaspoons salt**, and **4 1/2 teaspoons cinnamon**.
4. Gradually add the dry ingredients to the wet ingredients, mixing with a spatula until just combined and no flour pockets remain. Do not overmix.
5. Gently fold in **1 cup chopped nuts** if you are using them.

### Step 3: Baking and Cooling
6. Divide the batter evenly among your prepared loaf pans.
7. Bake standard loaves at **325°F** for approximately **1 hour**, or small **5-inch** pans at **340°F** for **45 minutes**, until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.
8. Allow the loaves to cool in the pans for **10 to 15 minutes** before carefully turning them out onto a wire rack to cool completely.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Wrap cooled loaves tightly in plastic wrap and store at room temperature for up to 3 days, or freeze for up to 3 months for easy holiday gift-giving.
