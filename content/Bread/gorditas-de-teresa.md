# Gorditas de Teresa

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Five | ✍️ **Attribution:** Teresa | 📂 **Category:** Bread
> *Traditional homemade gorditas flavored with crispy bacon and cheese. These delightful masa cakes offer a comforting texture and savory filling.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 15 mins | None | 4 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Griddle
* Skillet

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 C | [[Masa Harina]] | None |
| 1 tsp. | [[B.P.]] | Baking powder |
| 2 tsp. | [[Salt]] | None |
| 1 C | [[Water]] | Warm |
| 2 pieces | [[Bacon]] | Crumbled |
| 2 tables. | [[Cheese]] | Shredded |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Dough Preparation
1. Combine the **2 C masa harina**, **1 tsp. B.P.**, and **2 tsp. salt** in a mixing bowl.
2. Gradually mix in the **1 C water** until a soft dough forms.
3. Knead in the **2 pieces bacon** and **2 tables. cheese** until evenly incorporated.

### Step 2: Cooking
4. Shape the dough into small balls and flatten them into thick patties.
5. Cook on a hot griddle or skillet until golden brown spots appear and they are cooked through until golden and slightly puffed.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Uncooked dough can be wrapped tightly and refrigerated for up to 24 hours before shaping and cooking.
