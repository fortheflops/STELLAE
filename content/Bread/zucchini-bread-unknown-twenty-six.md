# Zucchini Bread

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing
1. Combine the **3 beaten eggs**, **2 cups sugar**, **3 tsp vanilla**, **1 tsp salt**, **1 cup oil**, **3 tsp cinnamon**, **3 tsp baking powder**, **1 tsp soda**, and **3 cups flour** in a large mixing bowl and mix well.
2. Fold in the **3 cups shredded zucchini** and **1 cup nuts** until evenly incorporated.

### Step 2: Baking
3. Pour the batter into a greased loaf pan.
4. Bake at 350° for **1 hr 10 min** until golden brown and a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.
