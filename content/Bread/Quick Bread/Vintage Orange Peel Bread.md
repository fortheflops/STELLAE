### Instructions
1.  Grind rinds of 3 oranges.
2.  Cover with water, add pinch of salt - boil 20-30 m. (until tender).
3.  Drain very dry.
4.  Add 1/4 C. water - 1 C. sugar & boil until quite thick.
5.  Let cool & add 2 C. flour, 3 tsp B. Powd - 1 egg & 1 C. or less milk.
6.  Bake.
