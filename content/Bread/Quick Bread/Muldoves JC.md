## Recipe
| Measurements      | Ingredients                   |

| 4                 | [[Eggs]]                        |

| 3/4 cup           | [[Vegetable Oil]]               |
| 1/4 cup           | [[Water]]                       |
| 2 1/5 cups        | [[All-Purpose Flour]]           |
| 2 teaspoons       | [[Baking Powder]]               |
___
## Instructions
1.  Preheat oven to 350°F. Grease and flour a baking pan (a loaf pan or 8x8 inch square pan would be suitable).
2.  In a large bowl, beat the eggs and sugar together until light and combined.
3.  Stir in the oil and water.
4.  In a separate bowl, whisk together the flour and baking powder.
5.  Add the dry ingredients to the wet ingredients and mix until just combined.
6.  Pour the batter into the prepared pan.
7.  Bake for about 30 minutes, or until a wooden skewer inserted into the center comes out clean.
