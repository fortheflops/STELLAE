| 3 tablespoons              | [[Butter]] or [[Margarine]]               |

## Instructions
(No instructions were provided on the recipe card. The following is an inferred method.)
1.  Preheat oven to 375°F. Grease a round cake pan or a small cast-iron skillet.
2.  In a large bowl, whisk together the flour, brown sugar, baking powder, baking soda, and salt.
3.  Cut in the butter or margarine using a pastry blender or your fingers until the mixture resembles coarse crumbs.
4.  In a separate bowl, lightly beat the eggs, then whisk in the buttermilk.
5.  Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients and stir until just combined.
6.  Gently fold in the raisins.
7.  Turn the dough out onto a floured surface and knead lightly for about a minute, just until it comes together.
8.  Shape the dough into a round loaf and place it in the prepared pan.
9.  With a sharp knife, cut a cross on the top of the loaf.
10. Bake for 30-40 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.
