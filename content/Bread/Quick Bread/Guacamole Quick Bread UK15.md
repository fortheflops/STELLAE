## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 2 tablespoons              | Chopped [[Onion]]                         |
| 1/2 cup                    | [[Margarine]], softened                   |
| 2 cups                     | [[Flour]]                                 |
| 1 1/2 teaspoons            | [[Baking Powder]]                         |
| 1/2 teaspoon               | [[Salt]]                                  |
| 1/4 teaspoon               | Ground [[Cumin]]                          |
| 1 cup                      | [[Sugar]]                                 |
| 1/2 cup                    | Mashed [[Avocado]]                        |
| 2                          | [[Eggs]], beaten                          |
| 1/4 cup                    | Roasted, peeled, chopped [[Green Chile]]  |
___
## Instructions
1.  Preheat the oven to 375°F.
2.  Sauté the chopped onion in a little margarine until soft.
3.  In a large bowl, sift together all the dry ingredients (flour, baking powder, salt, cumin).
4.  In a separate bowl, cream together the soft margarine and sugar.
5.  Add the mashed avocado, beaten eggs, sautéed onion, and chopped green chiles to the creamed mixture.
6.  Add the dry ingredients to the wet ingredients and mix well.
7.  Pour the batter into a greased 9x5-inch loaf pan.
8.  Bake for 15 minutes.
9.  Reduce the oven heat to 350°F and continue to cook for another 55 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.
