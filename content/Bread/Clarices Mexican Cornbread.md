# Clarice's Mexican Cornbread

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Clarice | 📂 **Category:** Bread
> *A savory, moist cornbread featuring a hidden layer of melted cheese, packed with creamed corn, jalapeños, and bell peppers.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 28 mins | None | 8–10 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 10-inch cast-iron skillet
* Large mixing bowl
* Medium mixing bowl
* Whisk

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 1/2 Cups | [[Yellow Corn Meal]] | Dry |
| 3 tsps | [[Baking Powder]] | Leavening |
| 1 tsp | [[Salt]] | Seasoning |
| 1 Cup | [[Buttermilk]] | Chilled |
| 3 | [[Eggs]] | Large |
| 2/3 Cup | [[Cooking Oil]] | Liquid |
| 1 Cup | [[Cream Style Corn]] | Canned |
| 1 Cup | [[Cheese]] | Grated, Cheddar or Monterey Jack |
| 3 | [[Jalapeno Peppers]] | Finely chopped |
| 1 | [[Bell Pepper]] | Finely chopped |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Heat
1. Preheat your oven to **450°F**. Place a greased **10-inch iron skillet** in the oven to heat up until scorching hot.

### Step 2: Mix the Batter
2. In a large bowl, whisk together the **1 1/2 cups yellow corn meal**, **3 tsps baking powder**, and **1 tsp salt**.
3. In a separate bowl, beat the **3 eggs**, then stir in the **1 cup buttermilk**, **2/3 cup cooking oil**, **1 cup cream style corn**, **3 jalapeno peppers**, and **1 bell pepper**.
4. Pour the wet mixture into the dry ingredients and gently fold until just combined.

### Step 3: Assemble and Bake
5. Carefully remove the hot skillet from the oven. Pour half of the cornbread batter into the skillet.
6. Sprinkle the **1 cup cheese** evenly over the batter layer.
7. Pour the rest of the batter over the cheese layer, smoothing the top.
8. Bake for **28 minutes**, or until the top is golden brown and a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftover cornbread tightly wrapped at room temperature for up to 2 days, or refrigerate for up to 5 days.
