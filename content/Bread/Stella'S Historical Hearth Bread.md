# Stella'S Historical Hearth Bread

> 📜 **Collection:** STELLAE Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** STELLAE | 📂 **Category:** Bread
> *A simple, rustic historical hearth bread inspired by traditional open-fire baking methods. Perfect for experiencing the authentic, hearty flavors of the past with minimal ingredients.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 40 mins | 1 hour (Rising) | 1 Loaf |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Wooden spoon
* Cast iron Dutch oven

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3 Cups | [[Flour]] | stone-ground unbleached wheat flour |
| 1 Cup | [[Water]] | lukewarm |
| 1 tsp | [[Salt]] | fine sea salt |
| 1 tsp | [[Yeast]] | active dry yeast |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mix the Dough
1. In a large mixing bowl, combine the **1 tsp active dry yeast** with **1 cup lukewarm water** and let sit for **5 minutes** until frothy.
2. Stir in the **3 cups stone-ground flour** and **1 tsp fine sea salt** using a wooden spoon until a shaggy dough forms.

### Step 2: Rise and Bake
3. Knead the dough on a floured surface for **10 minutes** until smooth and elastic. Cover and let rise for **1 hour** until doubled in size.
4. Preheat your oven to **450°F** with a cast iron Dutch oven inside. Carefully transfer the dough to the hot pot, cover, and bake for **30 minutes**, then uncover and bake for **10 minutes** more until the crust is deeply golden brown and hollow-sounding when tapped.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Best enjoyed fresh on the day of baking. Store leftovers wrapped in a clean tea towel for up to 2 days.
