# Bran Muffins

> 📜 **Collection:** Elizabeth Knee Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Bread
> *A classic, hearty recipe for homemade bran muffins made with buttermilk and molasses. The batter can be stored in the refrigerator for up to two weeks, allowing you to bake fresh muffins whenever you like.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep the Bran
1. In a bowl, combine the **3 cups bran** and **1 cup boiling water**; let sit for **5 minutes** to soften.

### Step 2: Mix the Batter
2. In a separate large bowl, combine the liquid ingredients: **1/2 cup melted shortening**, **1 cup sugar**, **1/3 to 1/2 cup molasses**, **3 eggs**, and **1 pint buttermilk**.
3. Add the soaked bran mixture, then stir in the dry ingredients: **2 1/2 cups flour**, **2 1/2 tsp baking soda**, and **1 tsp salt**. Stir completely until well incorporated.

### Step 3: Bake
4. Spoon the batter into greased muffin tins and bake at **400 degrees F** for **15 minutes** until they are puffed and a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The prepared batter can be stored in a covered container in the refrigerator for up to 2 weeks. Bake fresh muffins directly from the fridge as needed.
