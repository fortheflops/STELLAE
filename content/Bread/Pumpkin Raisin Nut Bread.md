# Pumpkin Raisin Nut Bread

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Bread
> *A classic, moist pumpkin bread packed with warm spices, sweet raisins, and crunchy nuts. This recipe rests for 20 minutes before baking to create a wonderfully tender loaf.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 1 hour | 20 mins (Resting) | 1 loaf |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 9x5x3 inch loaf pan
* Large mixing bowl
* Separate mixing bowl
* Wire cooling rack

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |

| ⅓ cup | [[Shortening]] | Soft |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] | Large |
| 3 tablespoons | [[Buttermilk]] | None |
| 1 cup | [[Pumpkin]] | Canned puree |
| 2 cups | [[Flour]] | Sifted, all-purpose |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Baking Powder]] | None |
| ½ teaspoon | [[Soda]] | Baking soda |
| ½ teaspoon | [[Salt]] | None |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Cinnamon]] | None |
| ½ teaspoon | [[Nutmeg]] | None |
| ½ cup | [[Nuts]] | Chopped |
| ½ cup | [[Raisins]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Mix Wet Ingredients
1. Preheat oven to **350°F**. Grease a **9x5x3 inch loaf pan**.
2. In a large bowl, mix together the **⅔ cup dark brown sugar**, **⅓ cup soft shortening**, and **2 large eggs** thoroughly until well combined.
3. Stir in the **3 tablespoons buttermilk** and **1 cup canned pumpkin**; mix well until smooth.

### Step 2: Combine Dry Ingredients and Batter
4. In a separate bowl, sift together the **2 cups sifted flour**, **1 teaspoon baking powder**, **½ teaspoon baking soda**, **½ teaspoon salt**, **1 teaspoon cinnamon**, and **½ teaspoon nutmeg**.
5. Fold the dry ingredients into the wet pumpkin mixture, mixing just until moistened.
6. Fold in the **½ cup chopped nuts** and **½ cup raisins**.

### Step 3: Rest and Bake
7. Pour the batter into the prepared loaf pan and smooth the top.
8. Let the batter stand in the pan at room temperature for **20 minutes** before baking to develop a tender crumb.
9. Bake for **50 to 60 minutes**, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean and the loaf is golden brown and fragrant.
10. Cool in the pan for **10 minutes** before turning out onto a wire rack to cool completely.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the cooled bread wrapped tightly in plastic wrap or foil at room temperature for up to 3 days, or refrigerate for up to a week.
