# Crescents and Loaf

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Bread
> *A classic guide for shaping yeast dough into golden crescent rolls and a perfectly portioned mini loaf. Ideal for versatile baking from a single batch of dough.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 20 mins | None | 12 crescents and 1 loaf |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Pastry board
* Rolling pin
* Pastry brush
* Baking sheet
* Loaf pan (7 3/8 x 3 5/8 x 2 1/4 inches)

---
## Ingredients

### Dough & Prep
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 recipe | [[Dough]] | divided into two equal portions (1/4 of total batch each) |
| As needed | [[Flour]] | for dusting the board |
| To taste | [[Butter]] | melted, for brushing |

---

## Instructions

### Crescents
1. Roll **1/4 of the dough** on a lightly **floured** board into an **11-inch circle**.
2. Brush the surface evenly with **melted butter**.
3. Cut the circle into **12 wedges**.
4. Roll each wedge starting from the wide end toward the point to form crescents.
5. Place each crescent point-side down on a greased baking sheet to prevent unrolling.

### Loaf
6. Shape the remaining **1/4 of the dough** into a smooth loaf shape.
7. Place the shaped dough into a greased **loaf pan** measuring **7 3/8 x 3 5/8 x 2 1/4 inches**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Shaped crescents and loaf can be covered tightly and refrigerated overnight for a slow rise before baking the next morning.
