# Apple-Spiced Whole Wheat Muffins

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Bread
> *Wholesome whole wheat muffins made with spiced apple yogurt and sweetened with honey. These tender, flavorful treats are quick to whip up for a cozy morning breakfast.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 20 mins | None | 12 muffins |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 12-cup muffin pan
* Large mixing bowl
* Medium bowl
* Spatula
* Paper liners (optional)

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 Cup | [[Whole Wheat Flour]] | Standard |
| 1 Cup | [[White Flour]] | Unbleached |
| 3 tsp. | [[Baking Powder]] | Standard |
| 1/2 tsp | [[Salt]] | Standard |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] | Slightly beaten |
| 1 (8 oz) Carton | [[Yogurt]] | Spiced apple-flavored |
| 1/3 Cup | [[Honey]] | Liquid |
| 1/4 Cup | [[Cooking Oil]] | Standard |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Mix Dry Ingredients
1. Preheat your oven to **400°F**. Grease a **12-cup muffin pan** or line it with paper liners.
2. In a large mixing bowl, stir together the **1 cup whole wheat flour**, **1 cup white flour**, **3 tsp. baking powder**, and **1/2 tsp salt** until well combined.

### Step 2: Combine Wet Ingredients and Batter
3. In a separate medium bowl, combine the **2 slightly beaten eggs**, **1/3 cup honey**, and **1/4 cup cooking oil**. Stir in the **1 (8 oz) carton spiced apple-flavored yogurt**.
4. Pour the yogurt mixture into the flour mixture. Stir with a spatula just until the dry ingredients are moistened; the batter will be lumpy and should not be overmixed.
5. Fill the prepared muffin cups ⅔ full with the batter.

### Step 3: Bake
6. Bake for **18 to 20 minutes**, or until golden brown and a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftover muffins in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 3 days or freeze for up to 2 months.
