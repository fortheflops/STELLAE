*A classic smoothie-style Orange Julius. This simple recipe blends orange juice with a whole egg and banana for a creamy, frothy, and protein-rich drink.*

___
## Recipe
| Measurements | Ingredients        |
| :----------- | :----------------- |
| 1 Cup        | Orange Juice       |
| ½            | Banana             |
| 1            | Egg                |
| 4-6          | Ice Cubes          |
___
## Instructions
1.  Combine the orange juice, banana, egg, and ice cubes in a blender.
2.  Blend on high speed until the drink is smooth and frothy.
3.  Pour into a tall glass and serve immediately.
___

>[!Warning]
>This recipe contains a raw egg. Consuming raw or undercooked eggs may increase your risk of foodborne illness, especially for young children, the elderly, pregnant individuals, and those with weakened immune systems.
