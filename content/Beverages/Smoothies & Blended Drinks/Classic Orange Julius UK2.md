*A nostalgic and frothy blended drink, this classic Orange Julius recipe combines frozen orange juice concentrate, milk, and a hint of vanilla for a perfectly creamy and refreshing treat.*

## Recipe
| Measurements | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 3 oz         | Frozen [[Orange Juice]] concentrate (half a 6-oz can) |
| ½ cup        | [[Milk]]                                           |
| ½ cup        | [[Water]]                                          |
| ¼ cup        | [[Sugar]]                                          |
| ½ tsp        | [[Vanilla]]                                        |
| 5-6          | [[Ice Cubes]]                                      |
___
### Instructions
1.  Place all ingredients—the orange juice concentrate, milk, water, sugar, vanilla, and ice cubes—into a blender.
2.  Secure the lid and blend on high speed for 30 seconds, or until the drink is completely smooth and frothy.
3.  For a thicker drink, add a few more ice cubes and blend again.
4.  Pour into chilled glasses and serve immediately.
