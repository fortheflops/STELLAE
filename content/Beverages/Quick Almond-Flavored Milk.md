# Quick Almond-Flavored Milk

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Beverages
> *An incredibly simple recipe for a frothy, almond-flavored milk drink. This quick beverage blends whole milk, a few almonds, and honey for a lightly sweet and nutty treat.*

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 8 Ounces | [[Milk]] | Whole milk |
| 5 | [[Almonds]] | Whole |
| To taste | [[Honey]] | About 1 teaspoon recommended |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Blend and Froth
1. Combine the **8 ounces milk**, **5 almonds**, and a drizzle of **honey** in a blender.
2. Blend on high speed for **30 to 60 seconds**, or until the almonds are finely incorporated and the milk is smooth and frothy.
3. Serve immediately.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Best enjoyed immediately after blending for optimal frothy texture.
