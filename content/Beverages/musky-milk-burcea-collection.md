# Musky Milk

> 📜 **Collection:** Burcea Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Beverages
> *A delightful historic drink combining rum, orange juice, water, and grenadine for a sweet and refreshing cocktail experience. Perfect for an easy-to-mix archival beverage.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5 mins | None | None | 1 serving |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Glass
* Jigger

---
## Ingredients

### Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/3 | [[Rum]] | Standard measure |
| 1/3 | [[Orange Juice]] | Freshly squeezed |
| 1/3 | [[Water]] | Chilled |
| 1 dash | [[Grenadine]] | To taste |

---

## Instructions

### Mixing
1. Combine **1/3 rum**, **1/3 orange juice**, and **1/3 water** in a glass.
2. Add a splash of **grenadine** until well blended and visually uniform.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Best served immediately after mixing.
