## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| One Cup                          | Nondairy [[Creamer]] (like Pream)         |
| One Cup                          | [[Cocoa Mix]] (like Swiss Miss or "our receipe") |
| $\frac{2}{3}$ Cup                | Instant [[Coffee]]                        |
| $\frac{1}{2}$ Cup                | [[Sugar]]                                 |
| $\frac{1}{2}$ Teaspoon           | [[Cinnamon]]                              |
| $\frac{1}{4}$ Teaspoon           | [[Nutmeg]]                                |
___
## Instructions
1.  In a large bowl, mix together all the ingredients: one cup nondairy creamer, one cup cocoa mix, $\frac{2}{3}$ cup instant coffee, $\frac{1}{2}$ cup sugar, $\frac{1}{2}$ teaspoon cinnamon, and $\frac{1}{4}$ teaspoon nutmeg.
2.  Stir until thoroughly combined.
3.  Store the mix in an airtight container.
4.  To serve, use 3-4 heaping teaspoons of the mix per cup of hot water or milk.
