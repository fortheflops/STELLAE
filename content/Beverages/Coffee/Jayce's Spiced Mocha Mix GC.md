*A delightful instant spiced mocha mix recipe from Jayce. This blend combines the richness of cocoa and coffee with the warmth of cinnamon and nutmeg for a perfect cozy beverage.*
### Author
Jayce
___
## Recipe
| Measurements       | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 Cup              | Nondairy [[Creamer]] (like Pream)                  |
| 1 Cup              | [[Cocoa Mix]] (like Swiss Miss)                    |
| ⅔ Cup              | Instant [[Coffee]]                                 |
| ½ Cup              | [[Sugar]]                                          |
| ½ tsp              | [[Cinnamon]]                                       |
| ¼ tsp              | [[Nutmeg]]                                         |
___
## Instructions
1.  In a large bowl, mix together the nondairy creamer, cocoa mix, instant coffee, sugar, cinnamon, and nutmeg.
2.  Stir until thoroughly combined.
3.  Store the mix in an airtight container.
4.  **To serve**, use 3-4 heaping teaspoons of the mix per cup of hot water or milk, and stir until dissolved.
