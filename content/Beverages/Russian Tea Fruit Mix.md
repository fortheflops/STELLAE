# Russian Tea Fruit Mix

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Beverages
> *A classic vintage recipe for a fragrant 'Russian Tea' flavoring mix combining candied fruits, whole cloves, and lemon juice. Stir a spoonful into hot tea for a delightfully spiced, citrusy brew.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | None | None | Approx. 1/2 cup of mix (8-10 servings) |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Small bowl
* Knife
* Cutting board
* Airtight jar

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 Tbsp | [[Orange Peel]] | Candied, finely chopped |
| 1 slice | [[Pineapple]] | Candied, thick, finely chopped |
| 10 | [[Cherries]] | Candied, finely chopped |
| 1 Tbsp | [[Cloves]] | Whole |
| 3 Tbsp | [[Lemon Juice]] | Fresh or bottled |

---

## Instructions

### For the Mix
1. Finely chop the **2 Tbsp candied orange peel**, **1 slice candied pineapple**, and **10 candied cherries**.
2. In a small bowl, combine the chopped fruits with the **1 Tbsp whole cloves** and **3 Tbsp lemon juice**.
3. Stir well until thoroughly combined and aromatic. Transfer and store the mixture in a small, airtight jar in the refrigerator.

### To Serve
4. Brew a fresh cup of hot black tea until steaming and fragrant.
5. Add a heaping teaspoon of the fruit mixture into the hot tea, stir well, and serve.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the fruit mix in a sealed, airtight container in the refrigerator for up to several weeks.
