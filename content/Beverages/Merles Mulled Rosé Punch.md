# Merle's Mulled Rosé Punch

> 📜 **Collection:** LouiseCollection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Merle | 📂 **Category:** Beverages
> *A unique and festive warm punch from a recipe by Merle, combining rosé wine with a spiced tea base and fruit concentrates. This sweet, fruity, and aromatic beverage is perfect for holiday gatherings and parties.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Simmer the Spices
1. In a small saucepan, bring **2 cups water** to a boil.

### Step 2: Steep and Strain
2. Add **1 tbsp whole cloves**, **2 tbsp whole allspice**, and **2 cinnamon sticks** to the boiling water. Reduce the heat and let it simmer for **15 minutes** until aromatic.
3. Strain the liquid through a fine-mesh sieve to remove the solid spices, and reserve the spiced liquid.

### Step 3: Combine and Warm
4. In a large stockpot or slow cooker, combine the spiced liquid, **2 cups freshly brewed tea**, **1 cup sugar**, **1 (6 oz) can frozen orange juice concentrate**, **1 (12 oz) can frozen Hawaiian Punch concentrate**, and **1 qt (4 cups) rosé wine**.
5. Gently heat the punch over low heat until it reaches a warm serving temperature, stirring until the sugar is dissolved. **Do not let it boil**, as this will cook off the alcohol.

### Step 4: Garnish and Serve
6. Prepare the garnish by studding **thin lemon slices** with a few **whole cloves**.
7. Serve the warm punch in mugs, garnished with the clove-studded lemon slices until fragrant and inviting.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftover punch sealed in the refrigerator for up to 2 days, and gently reheat on the stovetop over low heat without boiling before serving.
