# Frozen Daiquiri

> 📜 **Collection:** Burcea Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Beverages
> *A refreshing frozen cocktail made with pineapple juice, rum, sugar, and lemon juice. Perfect for parties and keeps well in the freezer for weeks.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | None | Overnight | 10 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Freezer container
* Electric mixer
* Freezer

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 cups | [[Pineapple juice]] | Unsweetened |
| 1 cup | [[Rum]] | None |
| 2 tablespoons | [[Sugar]] | None |
| 1 | [[Lemon]] | Juiced |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Freezing
1. Combine **2 cups unsweetened pineapple juice**, **1 cup rum**, **2 tablespoons sugar**, and the **juice of 1 lemon** in a container and freeze overnight until slushy.

### Step 2: Mixing and Storing
2. Beat the slushy mixture until it gets a little foamy, then return to the freezer until ready to serve.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Will keep for weeks in the freezer, keep covered.
