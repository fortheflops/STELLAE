*A simple and classic recipe for an old-fashioned chocolate milkshake. This version uses a whole egg to create a rich, frothy texture without any ice cream.*

___
## Recipe
| Measurements | Ingredients        |
| :----------- | :----------------- |
| 1 Cup        | Chocolate Milk     |
| 1            | Egg                |
| 3            | Ice Cubes          |
___
## Instructions
1.  Combine the chocolate milk, egg, and ice cubes in a blender.
2.  Blend on high speed until the ice is crushed and the drink is smooth and frothy.
3.  Pour into a tall glass and serve immediately.
___

>[!Warning]
>This recipe contains a raw egg. Consuming raw or undercooked eggs may increase your risk of foodborne illness, especially for young children, the elderly, pregnant individuals, and those with weakened immune systems.
