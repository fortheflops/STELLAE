# Merry Berry Punch

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Beverages
> *A festive and fizzy party punch combining cranberry juice, lemonade, and a splash of grenadine, brought to life with bubbly raspberry soda.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | None | None | 24 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large punch bowl
* Stirring spoon
* Measuring cups
* Drinking glasses

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 Quarts | [[Cranberry Juice Cocktail]] | Chilled |
| 1 (6 Ounce) Can | [[Frozen Lemonade Concentrate]] | Thawed |
| 3 Cups | [[Cold Water]] | None |
| 1/3 Cup | [[Grenadine Syrup]] | None |
| 2 (12 Ounce) Cans | [[Raspberry Soda Pop]] | Chilled |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mix the Base
1. In a large punch bowl, prepare the lemonade by mixing the **1 (6 ounce) can frozen lemonade concentrate** with **3 cups cold water**. Stir until the concentrate is fully dissolved.

### Step 2: Combine Juices
2. Pour in the **2 quarts cranberry juice cocktail, chilled** and the **1/3 cup grenadine syrup**. Stir well to combine until the mixture is uniform.

### Step 3: Add Fizz and Serve
3. Just before serving, gently pour in the **2 (12 ounce) cans raspberry soda pop, chilled**. Stir briefly and serve immediately in glasses over ice until refreshingly cold.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Mix the juice and lemonade base ahead of time and keep chilled in the refrigerator, but do not add the raspberry soda until right before serving to preserve the fizz.
