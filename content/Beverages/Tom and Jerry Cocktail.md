# Tom and Jerry Cocktail

> 📜 **Collection:** GC | ✍️ **Attribution:** Joy | 📂 **Category:** Beverages
> *A classic warm winter cocktail featuring a rich, sweet, spiced egg batter mixed with hot milk and brandy for a festive treat.*

---

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 0 mins | None | 12–16 servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large bowl
* Separate bowl
* Electric mixer
* Mugs

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 6 | [[Eggs]] | Separated |
| 2 ½ cups | [[Powdered Sugar]] | Or honey |
| Pinch | [[Cream of Tartar]] | None |
| ½ tsp | [[Cinnamon]] | Ground |
| ½ tsp | [[Allspice]] | Ground |
| ¼ tsp | [[Cloves]] | Ground |
| As needed | [[Hot milk]] | For serving |
| As needed | [[Brandy or dark rum]] | For serving |
| As needed | [[Nutmeg]] | For garnish |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Make the Batter
1. Separate the **6 eggs**. In a large, clean bowl, begin beating the egg whites with an electric mixer until frothy.
2. Add a **pinch of cream of tartar** and continue beating until soft peaks form.
3. Gradually add the **2 ½ cups powdered sugar** and beat until the meringue is stiff, glossy peaks form.
4. In a separate bowl, beat the egg yolks for **5-10 minutes** until they are thick and lemon-colored.
5. Gently fold the beaten egg yolks into the stiff **egg white** mixture until just combined.
6. Fold in the **½ tsp cinnamon**, **½ tsp allspice**, and **¼ tsp cloves** until fully incorporated.

### Step 2: Serve the Drink
7. For each drink, place **2 heaping tablespoons** of the Tom and Jerry batter into a mug.
8. Add **1.5 ounces** of **brandy or dark rum**.
9. Fill the rest of the mug with **hot milk**, stirring constantly until the drink is smooth, frothy, and thoroughly combined.
10. Garnish with a sprinkle of freshly grated **nutmeg** and serve immediately until warm and comforting.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the prepared egg batter sealed in the refrigerator for up to 3-4 days before serving.
