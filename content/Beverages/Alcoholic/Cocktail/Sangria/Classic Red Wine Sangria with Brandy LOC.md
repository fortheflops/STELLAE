*A robust and fruity red wine sangria, fortified with a shot of brandy and flavored with fresh-squeezed orange and lemon juice. Garnished with fresh apple and orange slices.*
### Author
Unknown
___
## Recipe
| Measurements | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 bottle (750ml) | Burgundy Wine                                      |
| 1 shot (1.5 oz)  | Brandy or Cognac                                   |
| Juice of 2       | Oranges                                            |
| Juice of 1       | Lemon                                              |
| ½ - ¾ cup    | Sugar (adjust to taste)                            |
| **Garnish** |                                                    |
| As needed    | Wedges of cored, unpeeled Apple                    |
| As needed    | Slices of unpeeled Orange                          |
| As needed    | Ice                                                |
___
### Instructions
1.  In a large pitcher, combine the Burgundy wine, brandy, fresh juice from the oranges and lemon, and sugar.
2.  Stir well until the sugar is completely dissolved. Adjust sweetness to your taste.
3.  For the best flavor, cover and chill in the refrigerator for at least one hour.
4.  Just before serving, add the fresh apple wedges and orange slices to the pitcher.
5.  Serve in glasses over ice.
