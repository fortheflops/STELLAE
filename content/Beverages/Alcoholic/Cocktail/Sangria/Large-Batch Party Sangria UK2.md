*A simple and potent sangria recipe perfect for a crowd, made with a base of Chablis or Burgundy wine, apricot or peach brandy, and triple sec. Topped with ginger ale for a fizzy finish.*

## Recipe
| Measurements | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 3 Litres     | [[Chablis]] or [[Burgundy]] wine                   |
| 1 Cup        | [[Apricot Brandy]] or [[Peach Brandy]]             |
| 1 Cup        | [[Triple Sec]]                                     |
| 1 (12 oz) Can| Frozen [[Orange Juice]] concentrate, thawed        |
| 1 Litre      | [[Ginger Ale]], chilled                            |
| **Garnish** |                                                    |
| As Needed    | Sliced [[Oranges]], [[Lemons]], seasonal fruit     |
| As Needed    | Ice                                                |
___
### Instructions
1.  In a very large punch bowl or beverage dispenser, combine the wine, brandy, triple sec, and thawed orange juice concentrate.
2.  Stir well until the orange juice concentrate is fully dissolved and the mixture is uniform.
3.  For best results, chill the mixture for at least one hour to allow the flavors to combine.
4.  Just before serving, gently stir in the chilled ginger ale.
5.  Add plenty of ice and garnish with fresh orange slices, lemon slices, and any other seasonal fruit you like.
