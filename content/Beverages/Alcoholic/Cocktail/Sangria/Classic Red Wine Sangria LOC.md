*A timeless and easy-to-make red wine sangria. This recipe combines Burgundy wine with the bright flavors of orange and lemon juice for a simple, refreshing party punch.*
### Author
Unknown
___
## Recipe
| Measurements | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 bottle (750ml)| Burgundy Wine                                      |
| 1 cup        | Orange Juice                                       |
| 2 tbsp       | Lemon Juice                                        |
| ¼ cup        | Sugar                                              |
| **Garnish** |                                                    |
| As needed    | Lemon Slices                                       |
| As needed    | Orange Slices                                      |
| As needed    | Ice                                                |
___
## Instructions
1.  In a large pitcher, combine the Burgundy wine, orange juice, lemon juice, and sugar.
2.  Stir vigorously until the sugar is completely dissolved.
3.  For best flavor, cover and refrigerate for at least one hour to allow the flavors to meld.
4.  Just before serving, add a few fresh lemon and orange slices to the pitcher for garnish.
5.  Serve in glasses over ice.
