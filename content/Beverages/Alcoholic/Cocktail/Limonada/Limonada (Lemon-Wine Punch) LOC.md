*A refreshing and sophisticated party punch, this Limonada combines fresh lemon juice, sweet sherry, and two types of dry wine for a perfectly balanced drink. Serve chilled over ice.*
### Author
Unknown
___
## Recipe
| Measurements      | Ingredients                                        |
| :---------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 8                 | Lemons (for juice)                                 |
| 2                 | Lemons (for slices)                                |
| 1 cup             | Powdered Sugar                                     |
| ½ cup             | Sweet Sherry                                       |
| 1 bottle (750ml)  | Rosé Wine, dry                                     |
| 1 bottle (750ml)  | Chablis Wine, dry                                  |
| As needed         | Cracked Ice (for serving)                          |
___
## Instructions
1.  Squeeze the juice from 8 of the lemons into a large punch bowl or pitcher. Thinly slice the remaining 2 lemons and add them to the juice.
2.  Sprinkle the powdered sugar over the lemon juice and slices. Pour in the sweet sherry and stir gently until the sugar has completely dissolved.
3.  Gently pour in the chilled bottle of rosé and the chilled bottle of Chablis. Stir to combine.
4.  Refrigerate for at least 2 hours to allow the flavors to meld and chill thoroughly.
5.  Serve in glasses half-filled with cracked ice.
