*A luxurious and decadent hot beverage. This recipe combines melted semi-sweet chocolate with hot coffee, milk, and cream, seasoned with vanilla and your favorite liqueur for a perfect adult treat.*
### Author
Unknown
___
## Recipe
| Measurements      | Ingredients                                        |
| :---------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 oz              | Semi-sweet Chocolate                               |
| 2 Tbsp            | Sugar                                              |
| 1 Cup             | Boiling Water                                      |
| 1 Cup             | Freshly Made Hot Coffee                            |
| ½ Cup             | Hot Milk                                           |
| ½ Cup             | Hot Cream                                          |
| 1 tsp             | Vanilla Essence                                    |
| 1-2 Tbsp          | Liqueur of your choice                             |
| **For Garnish** |                                                    |
| As needed         | Whipped Cream                                      |
| Optional          | Cinnamon Stick Stirrer                             |
___
### Instructions
1.  In a saucepan, melt the semi-sweet chocolate with the sugar and boiling water over medium-low heat, stirring constantly until smooth.
2.  Remove from the heat and stir in the vanilla and 1-2 tablespoons of your preferred liqueur.
3.  Gently pour in the hot milk, hot cream, and hot coffee, stirring to combine.
4.  Pour the mixture into mugs.
5.  Top with whipped cream and, if desired, add a cinnamon stick as a stirrer.
