*A creamy and frothy classic fizz cocktail made in a blender. This recipe combines gin or vodka with milk, half-and-half, and a whole egg for a rich, smooth texture.*
### Author
Unknown
___
## Recipe
| Measurements       | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 6                  | [[Ice Cubes]]                                      |
| ½ cup              | [[Milk]]                                           |
| ½ cup              | [[Half-and-Half]]                                  |
| ⅔ cup              | [[Gin]] or [[Vodka]]                               |
| ⅔ cup              | [[Sweet & Sour Mix]]                               |
| 1                  | Whole [[Egg]]                                      |
___
## Instructions
1.  In a blender, combine the ice cubes, milk, half-and-half, gin or vodka, sweet & sour mix, and the whole egg.
2.  Secure the lid and blend on high speed until the mixture is smooth, frothy, and the ice is completely crushed.
3.  Pour into chilled glasses and serve immediately.
___

>[!Warning]
>This recipe contains a raw egg. Consuming raw or undercooked eggs may increase your risk of foodborne illness.
