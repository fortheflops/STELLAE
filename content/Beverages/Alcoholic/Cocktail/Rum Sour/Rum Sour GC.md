*A very simple two-ingredient cocktail from a recipe by Phil. This drink, called a Tom Collins, is a classic Rum Sour made with rum and sweet & sour mix, served on the rocks or blended as a daiquiri.*
### Author
Phil
___
## Recipe
| Measurements       | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 shot (1.5 oz)    | [[Rum]]                                            |
| 4-5 oz, to taste   | [[Sweet & Sour Mix]]                               |
| 2                  | [[Ice Cubes]] (for on the rocks)                   |
| 1 cup              | Crushed [[Ice]] (for blended)                      |
| **Garnish (Optional)**|                                                |
| 1 slice            | [[Pineapple Slice]]                                |
| 1                  | [[Cherry]]                                         |
___
## Instructions
### On the Rocks
1.  Place two large ice cubes in a highball or Collins glass.
2.  Pour one shot (1.5 ounces) of rum over the ice.
3.  Fill the remainder of the glass with sweet & sour mix.
4.  Stir gently and garnish with a pineapple slice or a cherry, if desired.

### Daiquiri Style (Blended)
1.  In a blender, combine one shot of rum, 4-5 ounces of sweet & sour mix, and about a cup of crushed ice.
2.  Blend on high until the mixture is smooth and slushy.
3.  Pour into a glass and garnish if you like.
___

>[!Note]
>This recipe is titled "Tom Collins," but its ingredients make it a classic **Rum Sour**. A traditional Tom Collins is made with gin, lemon juice, sugar, and club soda.
