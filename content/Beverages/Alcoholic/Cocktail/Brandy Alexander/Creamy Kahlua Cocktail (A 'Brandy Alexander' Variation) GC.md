*A simple and creamy three-ingredient after-dinner drink. This recipe card, titled "Brandy Alexander," offers a unique variation using Kahlua, white creme de cacao, and cream for a smooth, coffee-chocolate flavored cocktail.*
### Author
Unknown
___
## Recipe
| Measurements       | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 part (1 oz)      | [[Kahlua]]                                         |
| 1 part (1 oz)      | [[White Creme de Cacao]]                           |
| 1 part (1 oz)      | [[Cream]] (or Half-and-Half)                       |
___
## Instructions
1.  Combine Kahlua, white creme de cacao, and cream in a shaker filled with ice.
2.  Shake well until thoroughly chilled.
3.  Strain into a chilled champagne or coupe glass.
___

>[!Note]
>The original recipe card is titled "Brandy Alexander," but the ingredients listed create a different (but delicious!) creamy, coffee-liqueur based cocktail. A traditional Grasshopper uses crème de menthe, while a traditional Brandy Alexander uses brandy.
