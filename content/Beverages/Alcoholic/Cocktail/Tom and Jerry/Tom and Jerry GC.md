*A classic warm winter cocktail from a recipe by Joy. This recipe creates a sweet, spiced batter from eggs and sugar, which is then mixed with hot milk and brandy for a festive treat.*
### Author
Joy
___
## Recipe
| Measurements       | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 6                  | [[Eggs]], Separated                                |
| 2 ½ cups           | [[Powdered Sugar]] (or Honey)                      |
| Pinch              | [[Cream of Tartar]]                                |
| ½ tsp              | [[Cinnamon]]                                       |
| ½ tsp              | [[Allspice]]                                       |
| ¼ tsp              | [[Cloves]]                                         |
___
## Instructions
### Batter
1.  Separate the eggs. In a large, clean bowl, begin beating the egg whites with an electric mixer.
2.  When the whites become frothy, add a pinch of cream of tartar.
3.  Continue beating until soft peaks form, then gradually add the powdered sugar. Beat until the meringue is stiff and glossy.
4.  In a separate bowl, beat the egg yolks until they are thick and lemon-colored (this can take 5-10 minutes).
5.  Gently fold the beaten egg yolks into the stiff egg white mixture.
6.  Fold in the spices (cinnamon, allspice, and cloves), being generous with your measurements as the card suggests.

### To Serve
1.  For each drink, place two heaping tablespoons (or less, to taste) of the Tom and Jerry batter into a mug.
2.  Add one jigger (1.5 ounces) of brandy.
3.  Fill the rest of the mug with warm/hot milk, stirring constantly as you pour to dissolve the batter.
4.  Sprinkle the top of each drink with nutmeg and serve immediately.
