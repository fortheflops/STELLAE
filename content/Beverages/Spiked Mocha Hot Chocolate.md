# Spiked Mocha Hot Chocolate

> 📜 **Collection:** LOC | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Beverages
> *Indulge in a luxurious and decadent hot beverage featuring melted semi-sweet chocolate, hot coffee, and cream. Perfect for an adult treat, it is seasoned with vanilla and your choice of liqueur.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5 mins | 10 mins | None | 2-3 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Saucepan
* Whisk or spoon
* Mugs

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 oz | [[Semi-sweet Chocolate]] | Chopped or broken into pieces |
| 2 Tbsp | [[Sugar]] | None |
| 1 Cup | [[Boiling Water]] | Hot |
| 1 Cup | [[Freshly Made Hot Coffee]] | Hot |
| ½ Cup | [[Hot Milk]] | Heated |
| ½ Cup | [[Hot Cream]] | Heated |
| 1 tsp | [[Vanilla Essence]] | Or extract |
| 1-2 Tbsp | [[Liqueur]] | Of your choice, such as coffee, hazelnut, or Irish cream |

### For Garnish
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| As needed | [[Whipped Cream]] | For topping |
| Optional | [[Cinnamon Stick Stirrer]] | For garnish |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Melt and Combine Base
1. In a saucepan, melt the **1 oz semi-sweet chocolate** with the **2 tbsp sugar** and **1 cup boiling water** over medium-low heat, stirring constantly until smooth and glossy.

### Step 2: Add Flavorings
2. Remove from the heat and stir in the **1 tsp vanilla essence** and **1-2 tbsp liqueur** of your preferred choice until well incorporated.

### Step 3: Mix Liquids and Serve
3. Gently pour in the **½ cup hot milk**, **½ cup hot cream**, and **1 cup freshly made hot coffee**, stirring until completely combined and piping hot.
4. Pour the rich hot chocolate mixture into mugs, top with a generous amount of **as needed whipped cream**, and garnish with an **optional cinnamon stick stirrer** until ready to serve.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Best enjoyed immediately while hot, but the chocolate base can be prepared slightly ahead and reheated gently before adding dairy and liqueurs.
