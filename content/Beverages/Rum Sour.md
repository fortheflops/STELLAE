# Rum Sour

> 📜 **Collection:** GC | ✍️ **Attribution:** Phil | 📂 **Category:** Beverages
> *A quick and easy 2-ingredient cocktail featuring rum and sweet & sour mix, which can be served over ice or blended into a daiquiri.*

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 shot (1.5 oz) | [[Rum]] | Standard pour |
| 4-5 oz | [[Sweet & Sour Mix]] | To taste |
| 2 cubes | [[Ice Cubes]] | For on the rocks version |
| 1 cup | [[Ice]] | Crushed, for blended version |

### Garnish (Optional)
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 slice | [[Pineapple Slice]] | Optional |
| 1 | [[Cherry]] | Maraschino, optional |

---

## Instructions

### On the Rocks
1. Place **2 ice cubes** in a highball or Collins glass.
2. Pour **1 shot (1.5 ounces) rum** over the ice.
3. Fill the remainder of the glass with **4-5 oz sweet & sour mix**.
4. Stir gently and garnish with a **1 slice pineapple slice** or a **1 cherry**, if desired, until well-chilled and refreshing.

### Daiquiri Style (Blended)
5. In a blender, combine **1 shot (1.5 ounces) rum**, **4-5 oz sweet & sour mix**, and **1 cup ice** (crushed).
6. Blend on high until the mixture is smooth and slushy.
7. Pour into a glass and garnish if you like.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Best prepared fresh and served immediately to prevent dilution from the ice.
