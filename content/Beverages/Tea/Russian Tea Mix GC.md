## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| One (1 lb 2 oz) Container        | [[Tang]] (enough to make 4 qts)           |
| One Package                      | [[Lemonade Mix]] (enough to make 1 qt)    |
| $\frac{3}{4}$ Cup                | Instant [[Tea]]                           |
| One Teaspoon                     | Ground [[Cloves]]                         |
| Two Teaspoons                    | Ground [[Cinnamon]]                       |
| Two Cups (Optional)              | [[Sugar]]                                 |
___
## Instructions
1.  In a large bowl, mix together all the ingredients: Tang, lemonade mix, instant tea, ground cloves, ground cinnamon, and optional sugar.
2.  Stir until thoroughly combined.
3.  Store the mix in a tightly covered jar.
4.  To serve, use two teaspoons per cup of hot water, or to taste.
