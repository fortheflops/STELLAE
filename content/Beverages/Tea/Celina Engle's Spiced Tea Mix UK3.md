*A classic instant spiced tea mix from Celina Engle. This recipe combines the zesty citrus flavors of Tang and lemonade with instant tea and warm spices for a quick and comforting hot beverage.*
### Author
Celina Engle
___
## Recipe
| Measurements      | Ingredients                                        |
| :---------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 2 Cups            | Tang                                               |
| 2 Cups            | Sugar                                              |
| 1 (3 oz) package  | Lemonade Mix                                       |
| 1 tsp             | Cinnamon                                           |
| 1 tsp             | Ground Cloves                                      |
| 3/4 - 1 Cup       | Instant Tea                                        |
___
### Instructions
1.  In a large bowl, combine all ingredients: Tang, sugar, lemonade mix, cinnamon, ground cloves, and instant tea.
2.  Mix well with a whisk to ensure everything is evenly distributed and there are no clumps.
3.  Store the finished mix in a tightly sealed container.
4.  To serve, use 2 rounded teaspoons per average-sized mug of hot water. Stir until dissolved.
