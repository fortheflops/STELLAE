*A classic, comforting instant 'Russian Tea' mix recipe from Joyce. This tangy and spiced drink mix combines Tang, lemonade, instant tea, and warm spices for a quick and easy treat.*
### Author
Joyce
___
## Recipe
| Measurements       | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 (18 oz) container| [[Tang]] (or enough to make 4 qts)                 |
| 1 package          | [[Lemonade Mix]] (enough to make 1 qt)             |
| ¾ cup              | Instant [[Tea]]                                    |
| 2 tsp              | Ground [[Cinnamon]]                                |
| 1 tsp              | Ground [[Cloves]]                                  |
| 2 cups (Optional)  | [[Sugar]]                                          |
___
## Instructions
1.  In a large bowl, combine the Tang, lemonade mix, instant tea, ground cloves, and ground cinnamon. If you prefer a sweeter tea, add the optional sugar as well.
2.  Stir or whisk until all the ingredients are thoroughly combined.
3.  Store the mix in a tightly sealed jar or container.
4.  **To Serve:** Add two heaping teaspoons of the mix to a mug of hot water and stir until dissolved. Adjust the amount of mix to your taste.
