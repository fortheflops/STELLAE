*A traditional, warming Yogi Tea recipe. This naturally caffeine-free beverage is made by boiling whole spices like peppercorns, cardamom, cloves, cinnamon, and fresh ginger in water.*

## Recipe
| Measurements      | Ingredients                                        |
| :---------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 qt.             | Pure [[Water]]                                     |
| 16                | [[Black Peppercorns]]                              |
| 16                | Whole [[Cloves]]                                   |
| Seeds from 16     | [[Cardamom Pods]]                                  |
| 5 small sticks    | [[Cinnamon]], Crumbled                             |
| 6 slices          | Fresh [[Ginger Root]]                              |
| **Optional Add-ins** |                                                |
| 1 tsp             | [[Black Tea]]                                      |
| To taste          | Raw [[Milk]] or [[Cream]]                          |
| To taste          | [[Honey]] or [[Lemon Juice]]                       |
___
### Instructions
1.  To a medium saucepan, add the quart of water, black peppercorns, the contents of the cardamom pods, whole cloves, ginger root slices, and crumbled cinnamon sticks.
2.  Bring the mixture to a rolling boil.
3.  Reduce the heat to low, cover, and let it simmer for 20 minutes.
4.  **Optional:** If you'd like a caffeinated version, add the black tea during the last few minutes of simmering.
5.  Carefully strain the tea through a fine-mesh sieve into mugs.
6.  Serve the tea plain, or stir in honey, lemon juice, milk, or cream to your liking.
