*A classic recipe for a "Russian Tea" flavoring mix. This simple concoction combines finely chopped candied fruits with whole cloves and lemon juice to create a fragrant spoonful to stir into hot tea.*
### Author
Unknown
___
## Recipe
| Measurements | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 2 Tbsp       | Candied [[Orange Peel]]                            |
| 1 slice      | Candied [[Pineapple]]                              |
| 10           | Candied [[Cherries]]                               |
| 1 Tbsp       | Whole [[Cloves]]                                   |
| 3 Tbsp       | [[Lemon Juice]]                                    |
___
### Instructions
**For the Mix:**
1.  Finely chop the candied orange peel, candied pineapple, and candied cherries.
2.  In a small bowl, combine the chopped fruits with the whole cloves and lemon juice.
3.  Stir well. Store the mixture in a small, airtight jar in the refrigerator.

**To Serve:**
1.  Brew a cup of hot black tea.
2.  Add a heaping teaspoon of the fruit mixture into the hot tea and stir.
