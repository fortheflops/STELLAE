## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
| 2 Cups            | Tang               |
| 2 Cups            | Sugar              |
| 3 Ounces          | Lemonade Mix      |
| 1 Teaspoon       | Cinnamon           |
| 1 Teaspoon       | Ground Cloves     |
| 3/4 - 1 Cup      | Instant Tea       |
___
### Instructions
1. Mix All Ingredients Well.
2. Store In Tight Container.
3. Use 2 Rounded Teaspoons Per Average Cup.
