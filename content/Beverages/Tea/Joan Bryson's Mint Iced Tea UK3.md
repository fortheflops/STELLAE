*A refreshing and fruity iced tea punch with a cool mint finish. This large-batch recipe from Joan Bryson combines strong sweet tea with pineapple juice and lemonade for the perfect party drink.*
### Author
Joan Bryson
___
## Recipe
| Measurements      | Ingredients                                        |
| :---------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 2 Quarts          | Strong [[Tea]], brewed                             |
| 1/2 Cup           | [[Sugar]]                                          |
| 1 Cup             | [[Pineapple Juice]]                                |
| 1 (6 oz) Can      | Frozen [[Lemonade]] Concentrate, thawed            |
| 3 Cups            | [[Water]]                                          |
| 1/2 tsp           | Mint Or Peppermint Extract                         |
| **For Serving** |                                                    |
| As needed         | Ice                                                |
| As needed         | Orange Slices                                      |
___
### Instructions
1.  Brew 2 quarts of strong tea. While it is still hot, add the sugar and stir until it dissolves completely. Allow the sweet tea to cool to room temperature.
2.  In a large pitcher or punch bowl, combine the cooled sweet tea, pineapple juice, thawed lemonade concentrate, and water.
3.  Stir in the mint or peppermint extract.
4.  Chill thoroughly.
5.  Serve in glasses over ice, garnished with fresh orange slices.
