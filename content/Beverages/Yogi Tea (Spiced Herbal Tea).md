# Yogi Tea (Spiced Herbal Tea)

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Beverages
> *A traditional, warming Yogi Tea recipe made by boiling whole spices like peppercorns, cardamom, cloves, cinnamon, and fresh ginger in water. This naturally caffeine-free beverage can be served plain or customized with milk and honey.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Combine and Simmer
1. To a medium saucepan, add the **1 qt. pure water**, **16 black peppercorns**, seeds from **16 cardamom pods**, **16 whole cloves**, **6 slices fresh ginger root**, and **5 small sticks cinnamon**.
2. Bring the mixture to a rolling boil until bubbling vigorously.
3. Reduce the heat to low, cover, and let it simmer for **20 minutes** until the spices are deeply infused and fragrant.

### Step 2: Strain and Serve
4. **Optional:** If you'd like a caffeinated version, stir in the **1 tsp black tea** during the last few minutes of simmering.
5. Carefully strain the tea through a fine-mesh sieve directly into mugs.
6. Serve the tea plain, or stir in **honey**, **lemon juice**, **milk**, or **cream** to your liking until well combined.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Brewed tea can be stored in a sealed glass container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days and gently reheated before serving.
