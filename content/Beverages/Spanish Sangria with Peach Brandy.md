## Instructions

### Step 1: Macerate the Fruit
1. In a large pitcher, combine the **1 cup fresh peach slices** and **1 orange, sliced**. Sprinkle the **1/2 cup powdered sugar** over the fruit and stir gently to coat. Let the mixture sit for about **30 minutes** to draw out the juices.

### Step 2: Combine and Serve
2. Pour the **1/2 cup peach brandy** and the **1 bottle (750ml) dry red wine** into the pitcher. Stir everything together until well combined.
3. Just before you are ready to serve, gently pour in the **1 1/2 cups club soda or seltzer water**.
4. Serve immediately in glasses filled with **as needed cracked ice**. Garnish with the fruit slices from the pitcher and a **as needed mint sprigs** if desired.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Macerate the fruit and brandy base up to a day in advance, but add the chilled red wine and club soda just before serving to maintain effervescence.
