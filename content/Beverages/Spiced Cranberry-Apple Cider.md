# Spiced Cranberry-Apple Cider

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Beverages
> *A festive and aromatic spiced cider perfect for a crowd, combining apple cider and cranberry juice with brown sugar, cinnamon, cloves, and fresh lemon slices.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5 mins | 20 mins | None | 25 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large kettle or stockpot
* Slotted spoon

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 qts | [[Apple Cider]] | None |
| 1 1/2 qts | [[Cranberry Juice]] | None |
| 1 cup | [[Brown Sugar]] | Packed |
| 1 1/2 tsp | [[Whole Cloves]] | None |
| 4 | [[Cinnamon Sticks]] | 3-inch |
| 1 | [[Lemon]] | Thinly sliced |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Combine and Heat
1. Combine **2 qts apple cider**, **1 1/2 qts cranberry juice**, **1 cup brown sugar**, **1 1/2 tsp whole cloves**, **4 3" cinnamon sticks**, and **1 lemon slices** in a large kettle or stockpot.

### Step 2: Simmer and Serve
2. Heat over medium-high heat, stirring occasionally, until the mixture comes to a boil and the sugar is dissolved.
3. Reduce the heat to low, cover, and let the cider simmer for **15 to 20 minutes** to allow the flavors to meld until fragrant.
4. Use a slotted spoon to remove and discard the spices and cooked lemon slices.
5. Serve warm in mugs until comforting and piping hot. If desired, float a fresh lemon slice in each cup as a garnish.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Leftover cider can be stored in a sealed container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days and reheated gently on the stovetop.
