# Red Satin Punch

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Beverages
> *A festive and bubbly large-batch punch, perfect for parties. This simple recipe combines cranberry and apple juices with 7-Up, served with decorative cherry-filled ice cubes.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 0 mins | 4 hours | 40-50 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Ice cube trays
* Very large punch bowl
* Stirring spoon

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 Quarts | [[Cranberry Juice]] | chilled |
| 2 Quarts | [[Apple Juice]] | chilled |
| 2 (2-liter) bottles | [[7-Up]] | chilled |
| As needed | [[Maraschino Cherries]] | for ice cubes |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Ice Cubes
1. Place one **maraschino cherry** in each compartment of two or three ice cube trays. Carefully fill the trays with **7-Up** and place them in the freezer until frozen solid (at least **4 hours**).

### Step 2: Combine Juices
2. In a large punch bowl, combine the chilled **2 quarts cranberry juice** and chilled **2 quarts apple juice**.

### Step 3: Add Soda and Serve
3. Just before you are ready to serve, gently pour in the chilled **2 (2-liter) bottles 7-Up** and give it a brief stir until well incorporated and bubbly.
4. Add the prepared cherry ice cubes to the punch bowl and serve immediately.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The cherry ice cubes can be prepared up to a week in advance and stored in a freezer bag. Mix the juices and add the soda just before serving to maintain maximum fizz.
