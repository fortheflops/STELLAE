# Classic Mulled Wine

> 📜 **Collection:** General Archive | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Beverages
> *Warm up your winter evenings with this classic mulled wine infused with aromatic spices, fresh orange, and a splash of apple cider. Simmered gently to perfection, it offers a deeply comforting and fragrant holiday sip.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 15 mins | None | 4 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large saucepan
* Strainers
* Pre-warmed mugs

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 Bottle | [[Red Wine]] | Hearty, 750ml, like Cabernet Sauvignon or Merlot |
| 1 Cup | [[Apple Cider]] | Or pineapple juice |
| 1 Stick | [[Cinnamon]] | Whole |
| 6 | [[Cloves]] | Whole |
| 1 tsp | [[Allspice]] | Ground |
| ¼ tsp | [[Nutmeg]] | Grated |
| ½ tsp | [[Bitters]] | Standard |
| 1 | [[Orange]] | Thinly sliced, with a few reserved for garnish |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Combine and Heat
1. In a large saucepan or pot, combine the **1 bottle hearty red wine**, **1 cup apple cider**, **½ tsp bitters**, **1 tsp allspice**, **6 whole cloves**, **¼ tsp grated nutmeg**, and the **1 cinnamon stick**.

### Step 2: Simmer and Infuse
2. Add most of the **1 orange** slices to the pot, reserving a few for garnish.
3. Gently heat the mixture over medium-low heat until it is hot and steaming. **Do not let it boil**, as this will cook off the alcohol.
4. Reduce the heat to low, cover, and let the wine simmer gently for at least **15 minutes** until fragrant and well-infused.

### Step 3: Strain and Serve
5. Strain the mulled wine into **4** large, pre-warmed mugs.
6. Float a reserved **1 orange** slice on top of each mug for garnish and serve immediately while hot.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Leftover mulled wine can be stored in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days and gently reheated on the stove without boiling.
