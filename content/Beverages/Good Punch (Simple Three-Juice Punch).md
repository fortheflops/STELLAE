# Good Punch (Simple Three-Juice Punch)

> 📜 **Collection:** LOC | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Beverages
> *An incredibly easy and refreshing party punch made from a simple blend of orange juice, apple juice, and lemonade. A perfect non-alcoholic option for any gathering.*

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mix the Base
1. In a large punch bowl, prepare the lemonade by mixing the **1 (12-ounce) can frozen lemonade concentrate** with the amount of water specified on the can (usually 4 ⅓ cans, or about **6 cups**).

### Step 2: Combine Juices and Serve
2. Pour in the **1 (64-ounce) bottle apple juice** and **1 (64-ounce) bottle orange juice**, and stir well until fully combined and uniform.
3. For a sparkling punch, gently stir in the **1 (2-liter) bottle ginger ale or lemon-lime soda** just before serving for a fizzy kick.
4. Serve in glasses over ice, garnished with **sliced oranges & lemons** until visually appealing and ready to enjoy.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Combine the juices ahead of time and store sealed in the refrigerator; add any carbonated soda and ice just before serving.
