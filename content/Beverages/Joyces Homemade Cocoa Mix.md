# Joyce's Homemade Cocoa Mix

> 📜 **Collection:** GC | ✍️ **Attribution:** Joyce | 📂 **Category:** Beverages
> *A simple, large-batch recipe for a rich and creamy instant hot cocoa mix. This mix from Joyce is perfect for keeping on hand for a quick treat or giving as a gift.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 0 mins | None | Approx. 10 cups of mix (30-40 servings) |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Very large bowl
* Wire whisk
* Airtight container or jar

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 cup | [[Cocoa Powder]] | Unsweetened |
| 2 cups | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 5 ½ cups | [[Milk]] | Powdered |
| 1 cup | [[Creamer]] | Non-dairy, e.g., Coffeemate |
| 1 ½ tsp | [[Salt]] | Fine |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Combine and Whisk
1. In a very large bowl, combine the **1 cup cocoa powder**, **2 cups sugar**, **5 ½ cups powdered milk**, **1 cup non-dairy creamer**, and **1 ½ tsp salt**.
2. Whisk or stir thoroughly until all ingredients are combined and the mixture is uniform in color and free of clumps.

### Step 2: Store and Serve
3. Store the finished mix in a large, airtight container or glass jar until ready to use.
4. To serve, add **¼ to ⅓ cup of the mix** to a mug and stir in **one cup of boiling water** until completely dissolved and smooth.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the dry mix in a tightly sealed container in a cool, dry place for up to 6 months.
