# Hot Mocha

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Frances Finkle | 📂 **Category:** Beverages
> *Warm up with this rich and creamy Hot Mocha, combining hot coffee, chocolate bits, and heavy cream. Blended to smooth perfection, it makes for a delightful treat for two.*

## Ingredients

### Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 cup | [[Hot Coffee]] | Hot (Kava) |
| 1/4 cup | [[Chocolate Bits]] | None |
| 1/2 cup | [[Heavy Cream]] | Hot |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Blending
1. Put **1 cup hot coffee** and **1/4 cup chocolate bits** in **blender** and blend **10 seconds**.
2. With **blender** running, gradually pour in **1/2 cup hot heavy cream**.
3. When all the **cream** is added, stop **blender** until mixture is smooth and frothy.
