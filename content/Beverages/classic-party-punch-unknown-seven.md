# Classic Party Punch

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Seven | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Beverages
> *A refreshing and festive party punch featuring a blend of fruit juices and sparkling mixers. Perfect for large gatherings and celebrations.*

## Ingredients

### Punch Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 large bottle | [[Cranberry juice]] | chilled |
| 2 qts | [[Collins mix]] | chilled |
| 2 qts | [[Ginger ale]] | chilled |
| 1 qt | [[Seven-Up]] | chilled |
| 1 large can | [[Frozen orange juice]] | undiluted |
| 1 | [[Lemon]] | cut into pieces |
| 2 | [[Limes]] | cut into pieces |
| 1 can | [[Pineapple cubes]] | drained |

---

## Instructions

### Assembly
1. In a large punch bowl, combine the **1 large bottle cranberry juice**, **2 qts collins mix**, **2 qts ginger ale**, and **1 qt seven-up**.
2. Stir in the **1 large can frozen orange juice** until fully incorporated.
3. Gently add the **1 lemon cut up**, **2 limes cut up**, and **1 can pineapple cubes**.
4. Serve immediately while the mixture is effervescent and cold.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Combine the juices and fruit ahead of time, but add the carbonated beverages (ginger ale, Seven-Up, and Collins mix) just before serving to maintain fizz.
