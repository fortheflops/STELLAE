# Pineapple Squirt Punch

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Beverages
> *An incredibly simple and refreshing two-ingredient party punch. This recipe combines the sweet taste of pineapple juice with the fizzy, citrus flavor of Squirt soda.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5 mins | 0 mins | None | 10-12 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large punch bowl
* Punch ladle

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 (46 oz) can | [[Pineapple Juice]] | chilled |
| 3 (12 oz) cans | [[Squirt]] | soda, chilled |
| Optional | [[Ice Ring]] | made of juices or ginger ale |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Combine and Serve
1. In a large punch bowl, pour in the chilled **1 (46 oz) can pineapple juice**.
2. Just before serving, gently add the chilled **3 (12 oz) cans Squirt**.
3. Stir once or twice to combine.
4. Add an **ice ring** to the punch bowl to keep it cold.
5. Serve immediately while ice-cold and fizzy.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Keep the pineapple juice and soda well-chilled in the refrigerator until ready to assemble just before serving.
