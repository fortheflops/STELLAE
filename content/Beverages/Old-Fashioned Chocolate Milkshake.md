# Old-Fashioned Chocolate Milkshake

> 📜 **Collection:** LOC | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Beverages
> *A simple and classic recipe for an old-fashioned chocolate milkshake. This version uses a whole egg to create a rich, frothy texture without any ice cream.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 mins | 0 mins | None | 1 serving |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Blender
* Tall glass

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 cup | [[Chocolate Milk]] | chilled |
| 1 | [[Egg]] | large |
| 3 | [[Ice Cubes]] | large |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Blend the Ingredients
1. Combine the **1 cup chocolate milk**, **1 egg**, and **3 ice cubes** in a blender.

### Step 2: Froth and Serve
2. Blend on high speed for **20-30 seconds**, or until the ice is completely crushed and the mixture is smooth and frothy.
3. Pour into a tall, chilled glass and serve immediately until frothy and refreshing.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Best enjoyed immediately due to the raw egg and ice texture.
