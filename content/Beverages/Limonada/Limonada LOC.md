## Recipe
| Measurements      | Ingredients                 |
| :---------------- | :-------------------------- |
| 8                 | Lemons (for juice)          |
| 2                 | Lemons (for slices)         |
| 1 Cup             | Powdered Sugar              |
| $\frac{1}{2}$ Cup | Sweet Sherry                |
| 1 bottle (~750ml) | Rosé Wine, dry              |
| 1 bottle (~750ml) | Chablis Wine, dry           |
| As needed         | Cracked Ice (for serving)   |
___
## Instructions
1.  Squeeze juice of 8 lemons & slice 2 lemons.
2.  Sprinkle 1 C powdered sugar over juice & slices, along with 1/2 C sweet sherry.
3.  Add 1 bottle rose & chablis, both dry.
4.  Refrigerate until thoroughly chilled.
5.  Serve in glasses half filled with cracked ice.
