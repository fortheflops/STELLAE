# Orange Banana Julius Smoothie

> 📜 **Collection:** LOC | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Beverages
> *A classic smoothie-style Orange Julius. This simple recipe blends orange juice with a whole egg and banana for a creamy, frothy, and protein-rich drink.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5 mins | 0 mins | None | 1 serving |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Blender
* Tall glass

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 Cup | [[Orange Juice]] | Chilled |
| 1/2 | [[Banana]] | None |
| 1 | [[Egg]] | Large |
| 4-6 | [[Ice Cubes]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Blend and Serve
1. Combine the **1 cup orange juice**, **1/2 banana**, **1 egg**, and **4-6 ice cubes** in a blender.
2. Blend on high speed for **30-45 seconds**, or until the mixture is completely smooth and frothy.
3. Pour into a tall glass and serve immediately.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Best enjoyed immediately after blending due to the texture and raw egg content.
