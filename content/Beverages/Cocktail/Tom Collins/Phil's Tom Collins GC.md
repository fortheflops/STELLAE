## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| One Shot                         | [[Rum]]                                   |
| To Fill/Taste                    | [[Sweet & Sour Mix]]                      |
| Two                              | [[Ice Cubes]]                             |
| Optional                         | [[Pineapple Slice]] or [[Cherry]]         |
| For Daiquiri Version             | Crushed [[Ice]]                           |
___
## Instructions
1.  Pour one shot of rum over two ice cubes in a glass.
2.  Fill the remainder of the glass with sweet & sour mix to taste.
3.  If desired, add a pineapple slice or a cherry as a garnish.
### Daiquiri Version
1.  Alternatively, combine one shot of rum, sweet & sour mix (to taste), and crushed ice in a blender.
2.  Spin in the blender until smooth.
3.  If desired, add pineapple or cherry.
