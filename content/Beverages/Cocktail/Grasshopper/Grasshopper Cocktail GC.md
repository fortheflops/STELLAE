## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 1/3 part                   | [[Kahlua]]                                |
| 1/3 part                   | [[Cream]]                                 |
| 1/3 part                   | [[White Creme de Cacao]]                  |
___
## Instructions
1.  Combine Kahlua, cream, and white creme de cacao in a shaker with ice.
2.  Shake well until chilled.
3.  Strain into a chilled champagne glass.
