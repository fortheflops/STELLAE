# Pink Party Punch

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Beverages
> *A fun, fruity, and vibrant pink punch perfect for a large crowd. This recipe uses a Jello and Kool-Aid base, combined with pineapple and lemonade, and finished with fizzy ginger ale.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 5 mins | None | 60 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Very large bowl or stockpot
* Punch bowl
* Measuring cups

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 4 (3 oz) pkgs | Cherry [[Jello]] | powder |
| 3 cups | [[Sugar]] | none |
| 4 cups | Hot [[Water]] | hot |
| 3 pkgs | Strawberry [[Kool-Aid]] | unsweetened |
| 3 pkgs | Raspberry [[Kool-Aid]] | unsweetened |
| 2 (46 oz) cans | [[Pineapple Juice]] | chilled |
| 2 (12 oz) cans | Frozen [[Lemonade]] Concentrate | thawed |
| 6 cans | Cold [[Water]] | using lemonade can to measure |
| 12 bottles/cans | [[Ginger Ale]] | chilled |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Syrup Base
1. In a very large bowl or stockpot, combine the **4 (3 oz) pkgs Cherry Jello** powder and the **3 cups sugar**. Pour in the **4 cups hot water** and stir until the sugar and Jello are completely dissolved. Let this syrup base cool.

### Step 2: Mix the Punch Base
2. Once cooled, stir in the **3 pkgs strawberry Kool-Aid**, **3 pkgs raspberry Kool-Aid**, **2 (46 oz) cans pineapple juice**, and **2 (12 oz) cans frozen lemonade concentrate** until well combined.

### Step 3: Add Water and Chill
3. Fill an empty lemonade can with cold water and add it to the punch. Repeat for a total of **6 cans cold water**. Stir until everything is well combined, then chill this punch base in the refrigerator until thoroughly cold.

### Step 4: Serve
4. Just before serving, transfer the base to a large punch bowl and gently pour in the **12 bottles/cans ginger ale**. Stir briefly until bubbly and serve immediately in cups over ice until refreshing and cold.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The punch base can be prepared and kept chilled in the refrigerator for up to 2 days before adding the ginger ale.
