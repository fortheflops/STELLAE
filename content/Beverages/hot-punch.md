# Hot Punch

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Bernice | 📂 **Category:** Beverages

## Instructions

### Step 1: Infusion
1. In a large pot, combine **3 qt water**, **1 1/2 cups sugar**, **24 whole cloves**, and **2 cinnamon sticks**.
2. Bring the mixture to a boil and let it boil for **5 minutes** until the spices are fragrant.
3. Remove from heat, add **3 tea bags**, and steep for **5 minutes** until the tea is strong.

### Step 2: Finishing
4. Stir in **1 can frozen lemonade** and **1 can frozen orange juice** until fully melted and incorporated.
5. Transfer to a crock pot to keep warm for serving, or serve immediately.
6. If desired, stir in a splash of **rum** or **brandy** into individual mugs before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The base can be prepared in advance and reheated in a crock pot; add the spirits just before serving to maintain the flavor profile.
