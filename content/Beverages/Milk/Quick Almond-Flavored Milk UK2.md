*An incredibly simple recipe for a frothy, almond-flavored milk drink. This quick beverage blends whole milk, a few almonds, and honey for a lightly sweet and nutty treat.*

## Recipe
| Measurements | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 8 Ounces     | [[Milk]]                                           |
| 5            | [[Almonds]]                                        |
| To taste     | [[Honey]]                                          |
___
### Instructions
1.  Combine the milk, almonds, and a drizzle of honey in a blender.
2.  Blend on high speed until the drink is smooth and frothy.
3.  Serve immediately.
___

>[!Note]
>This quick recipe creates a single serving of almond-flavored dairy milk. For traditional, non-dairy almond milk, raw almonds are typically soaked in water, blended, and strained.
