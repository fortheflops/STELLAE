*A traditional recipe for Golden Milk, a warming and healthy beverage. This two-part recipe first creates a storable turmeric paste, which is then blended with milk, oil, and honey.*

Unknown

## Recipe
| Measurements | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| **Part 1: Turmeric Paste** |                                            |
| ¼ cup        | [[Turmeric Powder]]                                |
| ½ cup        | Pure [[Water]]                                     |
| **Part 2: Golden Milk (per serving)** |                            |
| 1 cup        | Raw [[Milk]] (or milk of your choice)              |
| ¼ tsp        | Turmeric Paste (from Part 1)                       |
| 1 tsp        | [[Almond Oil]] or any vegetable oil                |
| To taste     | [[Honey]]                                          |
| For garnish  | [[Cinnamon]]                                       |
___
### Instructions
**Part 1: Make the Turmeric Paste**
1.  In a small saucepan, combine the turmeric powder and water.
2.  Bring the mixture to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer, stirring constantly, until a thick paste is formed.
3.  Let the paste cool, then transfer it to a small jar. It can be stored in the refrigerator for later use.

**Part 2: Make the Golden Milk**
1.  In a saucepan, combine one cup of milk, ¼ teaspoon of the prepared turmeric paste, and 1 teaspoon of almond oil.
2.  Stir over low heat, bringing the mixture just to a boil.
3.  Remove from heat and stir in honey to your desired level of sweetness.
4.  For a frothy top, carefully pour the hot mixture into a blender and blend for a few seconds.
5.  Pour into a mug, sprinkle with cinnamon, and enjoy.
___

>[!Warning]
>This recipe suggests using raw milk. Consuming raw or unpasteurized milk may increase your risk of foodborne illness. Pasteurized milk is a safe alternative.
