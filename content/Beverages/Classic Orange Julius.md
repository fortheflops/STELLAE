# Classic Orange Julius

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Beverages
> *A nostalgic and frothy blended drink combining frozen orange juice concentrate, milk, and vanilla for a creamy treat.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 mins | None | None | 2 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Blender
* Measuring cups
* Measuring spoons
* Chilled glasses

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3 oz | [[Orange Juice]] concentrate | Frozen, half of a 6-oz can |
| ½ cup | [[Milk]] | Cold |
| ½ cup | [[Water]] | Cold |
| ¼ cup | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| ½ tsp | [[Vanilla]] | Vanilla extract |
| 5-6 | [[Ice Cubes]] | Fresh |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Blend the Ingredients
1. Place the **3 oz frozen orange juice concentrate**, **½ cup milk**, **½ cup water**, **¼ cup sugar**, **½ tsp vanilla**, and **5-6 ice cubes** into a blender.

### Step 2: Froth and Serve
2. Secure the blender lid and blend on high speed for **30 seconds**, or until the drink is completely smooth and frothy.
3. For a thicker drink, add a few more ice cubes and blend again until incorporated.
4. Pour the mixture into chilled glasses and serve immediately.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This beverage is best enjoyed immediately upon preparation to maintain its frothy texture.
