# Quick Kahlua

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Beverages
> *Learn how to make homemade coffee liqueur quickly and easily with this archival recipe. Perfect for cocktails or gifts.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 1 hour | 1 hour | 10 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Saucepan
* Bottles

---
## Ingredients

### Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3 cups | [[Water]] | None |
| 2 cups | [[Sugar]] | None |
| 15 tsp | [[Instant Yuban Coffee]] | None |
| 1 qt | [[Vodka]] | None |
| 3 tsp | [[Vanilla]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Simmer
1. Combine the **3 cups water**, **2 cups sugar**, and **15 tsp instant yuban coffee** in a saucepan. Simmer for one hour, taking care not to boil.

### Step 2: Mix and Bottle
2. Cool for one hour until room temperature. Add the **1 qt vodka** and **3 tsp vanilla**, stir well, and pour into bottles.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in clean glass bottles indefinitely in a cool, dark place.
