## Instructions

### Step 1: Blending
1. Combine **1 oz coconut cream**, **2 oz pineapple juice**, **1 1/2 oz rum**, and **1 cup crushed ice** in a blender.
2. Mix in blender for **30 seconds** until smooth and frothy.
3. Serve in a tall glass.

### Step 2: Pink Whale Variation
4. Alternatively, combine **1/2 peach**, **1/2 banana**, **1/2 can coco lopez**, **1 1/4 oz rum**, **1 oz rum**, and **4-5 dashes grenadine** in a blender.
5. Mix in blender until thoroughly combined and smooth.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Best served immediately after blending for optimal texture and temperature.
