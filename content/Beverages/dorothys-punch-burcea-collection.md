# Dorothy's Punch

> 📜 **Collection:** Burcea Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Dorothy | 📂 **Category:** Beverages
> *A delightful and refreshing party punch featuring a blend of citrus juices and ginger ale. Perfect for gatherings and celebrations.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | None | Chill time | 24 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Punch bowl
* Large pitcher
* Measuring cups

---
## Ingredients

### Punch Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 large cans | [[Pineapple Juice]] | Chilled |
| 6 oz | [[Lemon Juice]] | Freshly squeezed |
| 6 oz | [[Orange Juice]] | Freshly squeezed |
| 1/3 C | [[Lime Juice]] | Freshly squeezed |
| 1 C | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 4 qt | [[Ginger Ale]] | Chilled |
| 1 qt | [[Carbonated Water]] | Chilled |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing the Base
1. In a large punch bowl or pitcher, dissolve the **1 C sugar** into the mixed **6 oz lemon juice**, **6 oz orange juice**, and **1/3 C lime juice** until fully incorporated.

### Step 2: Adding Fruits and Fizz
2. Stir in the **2 large cans pineapple juice** until well blended.
3. Just before serving, gently pour in the chilled **4 qt ginger ale** and **1 qt carbonated water** to maintain maximum effervescence until bubbly and well combined.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Mix the juice and sugar base ahead of time and store in the refrigerator. Add the ginger ale and carbonated water right before serving to retain the fizz.
