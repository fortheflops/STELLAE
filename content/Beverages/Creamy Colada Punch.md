# Creamy Colada Punch

> 📜 **Collection:** UK15 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Beverages
> *A simple and delicious non-alcoholic tropical punch combining creamy coconut, sweet pineapple juice, and fizzy ginger ale. This easy three-ingredient beverage comes together in minutes for any gathering.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5 mins | 0 mins | None | 4-5 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 1-quart pitcher or punch bowl
* Wire whisk

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 (8.5 ounce) can | [[Cream of Coconut]] | Standard can |
| 3 cups | [[Pineapple Juice]] | Chilled |
| 1 (12 ounce) can | [[Ginger Ale]] | Chilled |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mix the Base
1. In a 1-quart pitcher or a punch bowl, stir or whisk the **1 (8.5 ounce) can cream of coconut** and **3 cups pineapple juice** together until they are completely smooth and well-mixed.

### Step 2: Add Fizz and Serve
2. Just before serving, gently stir in the **1 (12 ounce) can ginger ale** until just combined.
3. Serve immediately over ice until frosty and refreshing.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Combine the cream of coconut and pineapple juice ahead of time and refrigerate, but do not add the ginger ale until right before serving to maintain maximum fizz.
