# Orange Blossom Punch

> 📜 **Collection:** UK4 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Beverages
> *A bright and fruity party punch featuring a refreshing blend of citrus juices and ginger ale, topped with creamy sherbet. Perfect for celebrations and large gatherings.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 0 mins | None | 5 quarts (40-50 servings) |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large punch bowl
* Ladle
* Ice cream scoop

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 ½ qts (6 cups) | [[Orange Juice]] | chilled |
| 1 cup | [[Lemon Juice]] | chilled |
| ⅓ cup | [[Cherry Juice]] | from a jar of maraschino cherries, chilled |
| ½ cup | [[Sugar]] | none |
| 1 (2-liter) bottle | [[Ginger Ale]] | chilled |
| 2 qts | [[Sherbet]] | orange or lemon |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mix Base
1. In a large punch bowl, combine the **1 ½ qts (6 cups) orange juice**, **1 cup lemon juice**, **⅓ cup cherry juice**, and **½ cup sugar**.

### Step 2: Dissolve
2. Stir well until the **sugar** is completely dissolved and incorporated into the juices.

### Step 3: Fizz and Float
3. Just before you are ready to serve, gently pour in the chilled **1 (2-liter) bottle ginger ale** for a lively fizz.

### Step 4: Serve
4. Add large scoops of the orange or lemon **2 qts sherbet** to the punch bowl until creamy and frothy, then serve immediately while cold and fizzy.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Combine the juices and sugar ahead of time and chill; add the ginger ale and sherbet just before serving for optimal fizz and texture.
