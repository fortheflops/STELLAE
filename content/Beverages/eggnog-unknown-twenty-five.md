# Eggnog

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Five | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Beverages
> *A rich and creamy traditional eggnog made with sweetened condensed milk, whipped cream, and a hint of nutmeg. Perfect for holiday gatherings and festive celebrations.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | None | Chilling time | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Whisk

---
## Ingredients

### Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] | Well beaten |
| 1 can (15 oz) | [[Eagle Brand Condensed Milk]] | None |
| 1 tsp. | [[Vanilla]] | None |
| 1/4 tsp. | [[Salt]] | None |
| 1 qt. | [[Milk]] | None |
| 1/2 pt. (1 c) | [[Cream]] | Whipped |
| To taste | [[Nutmeg]] | For sprinkling |
| Optional | [[Rum]] | If desired |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing Base
1. Combine **2 eggs**, **1 can (15 oz) Eagle Brand Condensed Milk**, **1 tsp. vanilla**, and **1/4 tsp. salt** until blended.

### Step 2: Combining and Chilling
2. Gradually beat in **1 qt. milk**.
3. Fold in **1/2 pt. (1 c) cream**.
4. Add **rum** if you want.
5. Pour and sprinkle with **nutmeg**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in the refrigerator for up to 2-3 days before serving, giving it a gentle stir if any separation occurs.
