# Spice Tea

> *A comforting and aromatic spiced tea mix that combines instant tea, sugar, tang, and warming spices. Simply mix with hot water for a cozy drink.*

| 1 cup | [[Tang]] | or Start |

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing and Serving
1. Combine **1 cup instant tea**, **2 cups sugar**, **1 cup tang**, **1 pkg. lemon quik**, **1/2 tsp. cinnamon**, and **1/4 tsp. cloves** together in a large bowl and mix well until fully blended.
2. Store the dry mixture in an airtight container.
3. To serve, use **2 or 3 tsp.** of the mix per cup of **hot water** (H2O), stirring until completely dissolved and aromatic.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The dry spice tea mix can be stored in an airtight container at room temperature for several months.
