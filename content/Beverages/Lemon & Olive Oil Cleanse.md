# Lemon & Olive Oil Cleanse

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Beverages
> *A traditional morning cleanse combining fresh lemon juice, olive oil, and garlic for a revitalizing wellness tonic. Best consumed with a brief waiting period before eating.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5 mins | None | 30 mins | 1 Serving |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Citrus juicer
* Small glass

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | [[Lemon]] | Juiced |
| 1 tablespoon | [[Olive Oil]] | Cold-pressed recommended |
| As Needed | [[Garlic]] | Minced or crushed |
| Optional | [[Grapefruit Juice]] | For extra tartness |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mix and Rest
1. In a small glass, combine the juice of **1** **[[Lemon]]**, **1 tablespoon** **[[Olive Oil]]**, and **as needed** **[[Garlic]]** (along with optional **[[Grapefruit Juice]]** if desired), mixing well.
2. Wait for approximately **30 minutes** before consuming or eating anything else, as noted by the traditional cleanse guidelines.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Best prepared fresh immediately before consumption.
