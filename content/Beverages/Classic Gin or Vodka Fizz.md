# Classic Gin or Vodka Fizz

> 📜 **Collection:** GailCollection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Beverages
> *A creamy and frothy classic fizz cocktail made in a blender. This recipe combines gin or vodka with milk, half-and-half, and a whole egg for a rich, smooth texture.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5 mins | 0 mins | None | 2-4 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Blender
* Chilled glasses

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 6 | [[Ice Cubes]] | Cubed |
| 1/2 cup | [[Milk]] | Chilled |
| 1/2 cup | [[Half-and-Half]] | Chilled |
| 2/3 cup | [[Gin]] or [[Vodka]] | Preferred spirit |
| 2/3 cup | [[Sweet & Sour Mix]] | Standard |
| 1 | [[Egg]] | Whole, fresh |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Blend the Cocktail
1. In a blender, combine the **6 ice cubes**, **1/2 cup milk**, **1/2 cup half-and-half**, **2/3 cup gin or vodka**, **2/3 cup sweet & sour mix**, and the **1 whole egg**.

### Step 2: Froth and Serve
2. Secure the lid and blend on high speed until the mixture is smooth and frothy and the ice is completely crushed.

### Step 3: Pour
3. Pour into chilled glasses and serve immediately while frothy.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Best served immediately due to the frothy nature and fresh egg content.
