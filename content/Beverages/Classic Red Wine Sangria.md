# Classic Red Wine Sangria

> 📜 **Collection:** LOC | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Beverages
> *A timeless and easy-to-make red wine sangria. This recipe combines Burgundy wine with the bright flavors of orange and lemon juice for a simple, refreshing party punch.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 0 mins | 1 hour | 6–8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large pitcher
* Stirring spoon
* Glasses

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 bottle (750ml) | [[Burgundy Wine]] | Or other dry red wine |
| 1 cup | [[Orange Juice]] | None |
| 2 tbsp | [[Lemon Juice]] | Freshly squeezed |
| ¼ cup | [[Sugar]] | None |

### Garnish
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| As needed | [[Lemon Slices]] | For garnish |
| As needed | [[Orange Slices]] | For garnish |
| As needed | [[Ice]] | For serving |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Combine and Dissolve
1. In a large pitcher, combine the **1 bottle (750ml) Burgundy Wine**, **1 cup Orange Juice**, **2 tbsp Lemon Juice**, and **¼ cup Sugar**.
2. Stir vigorously until the sugar is completely dissolved and thoroughly incorporated.

### Step 2: Chill and Serve
3. For best flavor, cover and refrigerate for **at least one hour** to allow the flavors to meld into a rich, cohesive punch.
4. Just before serving, add a few fresh **Lemon Slices** and **Orange Slices** to the pitcher for garnish.
5. Serve in glasses over **Ice** until ice-cold and refreshing.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the mixed sangria covered in the refrigerator for up to 24 hours before adding the fresh citrus fruit garnish and serving.
