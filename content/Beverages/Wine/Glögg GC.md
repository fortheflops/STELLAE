## Instructions
1.  Combine the half gallon of red wine with the orange peelings (outer zest only), five or six cloves, a few slices of ginger, a few cardamom seeds, and a few cinnamon sticks.
2.  Soak this mixture overnight.
3.  The next day, bring the wine and spice mixture almost to a boil.
4.  Allow it to steep (let it sit in the hot liquid to infuse flavors).
5.  Add sugar to taste, starting with about $\frac{3}{4}$ cup, and stir until the sugar is completely dissolved.
6.  If desired, "spike" with Vodka to taste.
7.  Serve immediately (warm) with a few raisins and almonds in each glass.
