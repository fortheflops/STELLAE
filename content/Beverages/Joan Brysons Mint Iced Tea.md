# Joan Bryson's Mint Iced Tea

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Joan Bryson | 📂 **Category:** Beverages
> *A refreshing and fruity iced tea punch with a cool mint finish. This large-batch recipe from Joan Bryson combines strong sweet tea with pineapple juice and lemonade for the perfect party drink.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Brew and Sweeten
1. Brew **2 quarts strong tea**. While it is still hot, add the **1/2 cup sugar** and stir until it dissolves completely. Allow the sweet tea to cool to room temperature.

### Step 2: Combine and Chill
2. In a large pitcher or punch bowl, combine the cooled sweet tea, **1 cup pineapple juice**, **1 (6 oz) can frozen lemonade concentrate**, and **3 cups water**.
3. Stir in the **1/2 tsp mint or peppermint extract** until well incorporated. Chill thoroughly in the refrigerator until ice cold.

### Step 3: Serve
4. Serve in glasses over **ice**, garnished with fresh **orange slices** until refreshing and aromatic.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the mixed tea covered in the refrigerator for up to 3 days before serving.
