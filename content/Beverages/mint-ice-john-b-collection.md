# Mint Ice

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Beverages
> *A delightfully refreshing frozen mint ice infused with citrus and green coloring. Perfect for cooling down on warm days.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 5 mins | 2 hours | 6 servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Saucepan
* Ice cream freezer

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 qt | [[Water]] | None |
| 3 c. | [[Sugar]] | None |
| 4 | [[Lemons]] | Juiced |
| 1 | [[Orange]] | Juiced |
| 1 tsp | [[Mint Extract]] | None |
| 1 dash | [[Green Coloring]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Boiling and Mixing
1. Combine **1 qt water** and **3 c. sugar** and boil for **5 min**.
2. Cool the mixture, then add the juice of **4 lemons** and **1 orange**.
3. Add **1 tsp mint extract** and **green coloring** to the mixture.

### Step 2: Freezing
4. Cool completely and freeze in an ice cream freezer until set to a slushy, icy consistency.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Can be kept in the freezer until ready to serve; let soften slightly if frozen solid.
