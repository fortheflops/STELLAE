## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients                                 |
| :----------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| 3 oz                | Frozen [[Orange Juice]]         |
| ½ Cup               | [[Milk]]                        |
| ½ Cup               | [[Water]]                       |
| ¼ Cup               | [[Sugar]]                       |
| ½ Teaspoon            | [[Vanilla]]                     |
| 5-6                 | [[Ice Cubes]]                   |

___
### Instructions
1.  Blend Ingredients 30 seconds.
2.  Serve in Chilled glasses.
3.  You may add more Ice Cubes if you like.
