# Classic Iced Tea

> 📜 **Collection:** GailCollection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Beverages
> *A simple, classic recipe for brewing perfect iced tea. This method starts with freshly boiled water poured over tea bags to create a strong concentrate, which is then sweetened and served over ice.*

| 1 | [[Lemon]] | For juice and slices |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Boil and Steep
1. In a kettle or saucepan, bring the **4 cups (1 quart) cold water** to a full, rolling boil.
2. Place the **3 family-size tea bags** in a heatproof pitcher. Pour the boiling **cold water** over the **tea bags**.
3. Let the tea steep for **3-5 minutes** until it reaches a good dark color and fragrant aroma. Be careful not to steep for too long, or the tea may become bitter.

### Step 2: Sweeten and Serve
4. Remove and discard the **tea bags**.
5. While the tea is still hot, add the **½ to ¾ cup sugar** and stir until it is completely dissolved.
6. Fill serving glasses completely full of **lots of ice**.
7. Pour the hot, sweetened tea directly over the **ice** to flash-chill it.
8. Add a squeeze of **1 lemon** juice to each glass and garnish with a lemon slice. Serve immediately while ice cold.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the brewed and sweetened tea concentrate in the refrigerator for up to 3 days before serving over fresh ice.
