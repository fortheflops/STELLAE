# Cranberry Fizz Punch

> 📜 **Collection:** UK15 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Beverages
> *A sparkling and refreshing non-alcoholic punch. This simple recipe combines cranberry, grapefruit, and orange juices, sweetened and then topped with chilled ginger ale for a festive fizz.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | None | 1 hour | 12-14 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large pitcher
* Punch bowl
* Stirring spoon

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 quart | [[Cranberry Juice Cocktail]] | Chilled |
| 1 cup | [[Grapefruit Juice]] | Fresh or bottled |
| 1 cup | [[Orange Juice]] | Fresh or bottled |
| 1/2 cup | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 2 cups | [[Ginger Ale]] | Chilled |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mix and Dissolve
1. In a large pitcher or punch bowl, combine the **1 quart cranberry juice cocktail**, **1 cup grapefruit juice**, **1 cup orange juice**, and **1/2 cup sugar**.
2. Stir well until the **sugar** is completely dissolved and the mixture is well blended.

### Step 2: Chill and Serve
3. Chill the juice mixture thoroughly in the refrigerator for at least **1 hour** until cold.
4. Just before serving, gently stir in the **2 cups ginger ale** until combined for a festive fizz.
5. Serve immediately in glasses over ice until refreshing and bubbly.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The juice and sugar base can be mixed and stored in the refrigerator up to 24 hours in advance; do not add the ginger ale until just before serving to preserve the fizz.
