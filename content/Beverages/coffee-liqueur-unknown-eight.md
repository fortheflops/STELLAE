# Coffee Liqueur

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Eight | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Beverages
> *Craft your own rich and flavorful homemade coffee liqueur with this classic recipe. Perfect for gifting or enjoying after dinner.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 1 hour 30 mins | 4 weeks | 9 1-pint bottles |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large saucepan
* Bottles

---
## Ingredients

### Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 6 cups | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 3 quarts | [[Water]] | None |
| 3/4 cup | [[Instant coffee]] | None |
| 3 tablespoons | [[Pure vanilla extract]] | or 3 vanilla beans |
| 6 cups | [[Vodka]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Simmering
1. Combine the **6 cups sugar**, **3 quarts water**, **3/4 cup instant coffee**, and **3 vanilla beans** (if using) in a large saucepan.
2. Bring to a boil, watching carefully to prevent the mixture from boiling over.
3. Reduce heat to a simmer and cook for **1 1/2 hours** until thickened and well combined.

### Step 2: Bottling and Aging
4. Cool the syrup completely.
5. Add **3 tablespoons pure vanilla extract** (if not using beans earlier) and **6 cups vodka**.
6. Pour into bottles and drop a coffee bean into the bottom of each.
7. Allow to age in a cool, dark place for about **4 weeks** until mellowed and smooth.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in a cool, dark place for at least 4 weeks to age properly before serving.
