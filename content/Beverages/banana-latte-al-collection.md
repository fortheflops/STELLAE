# Banana Latte

> 📜 **Collection:** Al Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Beverages
> *A refreshing and creamy Banana Latte blending chilled strong-brewed coffee with soy milk, frozen bananas, and ice. Finished with a dusting of unsweetened cocoa powder and cinnamon for a guilt-free morning treat.*

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Blend the Base
1. Combine **1 cup soy milk** and **3/4 cup strong-brewed coffee** in a blender.
2. Add **2 frozen sliced bananas** and blend until completely smooth and creamy.

### Step 2: Chill and Serve
3. With the blender running, add **6-8 ice cubes** one by one until fully incorporated and the mixture reaches a thick, frosty consistency.
4. Pour into tall glasses and sprinkle the top with **1 tsp unsweetened cocoa powder** and **1/4 tsp cinnamon**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Best enjoyed immediately. You can freeze the sliced bananas up to 3 months in advance for quick preparation.
