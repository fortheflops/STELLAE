## Instructions

### Step 1: Infuse Overnight
1. In a large pot or bowl, combine the **½ gallon (2 litres) red wine** with the **1 orange** peelings, **5-6 whole cloves**, **3-4 slices ginger**, **1 tsp cardamom seeds**, and **2-3 cinnamon sticks**.

### Step 2: Steep and Heat
2. Cover and let the mixture soak at room temperature overnight to infuse the spices into the wine.
3. The next day, place the pot over medium-low heat and gently bring the mixture almost to a boil without letting it fully boil, until steaming hot and fragrant.
4. Let it steep at a hot, steaming temperature for about **20-30 minutes**.

### Step 3: Sweeten, Spike, and Serve
5. Stir in the **¾ cup sugar** until it dissolves completely, tasting and adding more if you prefer it sweeter.
6. If using, stir in the **vodka** just before serving.
7. To serve, place a small spoonful of **raisins** and **almonds** into the bottom of each mug and ladle the hot **Glögg** over them until piping hot.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The spiced wine can be infused overnight and reheated gently before serving; store leftovers covered in the refrigerator for up to 3 days.
