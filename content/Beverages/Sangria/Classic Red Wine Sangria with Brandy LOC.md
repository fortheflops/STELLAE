## Recipe
| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
| 1 bottle (~750ml)     | Burgundy Wine             |
| 1 shot (~1.5 oz)      | Brandy / Cognac           |
| Juice of              | 2 Oranges                 |
| Juice of              | 1 Lemon                   |
| $\frac{1}{2}$ - $\frac{3}{4}$ Cup | Sugar *(adjust to taste)* |
### Garnish
| Ingredients                             |
| :-------------------------------------- |
| Wedges of cored, unpeeled Apple         |
| Slices of unpeeled Orange               |
| Ice                                     |
___
### Instructions
- 1.  *(Implied)* Combine Burgundy wine, brandy/cognac, juice of 2 oranges, juice of 1 lemon, and $\frac{1}{2}$ - $\frac{3}{4}$ cup sugar in a pitcher. Stir well until sugar is dissolved. Chill if desired.
2.  float wedges of cored, unpeeled apple & of unpeeled orange
3.  serve over ice
