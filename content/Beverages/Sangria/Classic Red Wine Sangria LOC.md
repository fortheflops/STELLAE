## Recipe
| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
| 1 bottle (~750ml)   | Burgundy Wine       |
| 1 Cup               | Orange Juice        |
| 2 Tbsp              | Lemon Juice         |
| $\frac{1}{4}$ Cup  | Sugar               |
### Garnish
| Measurements | Ingredients           |
| :----------- | :-------------------- |
| As needed    | Lemon Slices        |
| As needed    | Orange Slices       |
| As needed    | Ice                 |
___
## Instructions
1. Combine Burgundy wine, orange juice, lemon juice, and sugar in a pitcher. Stir well until sugar dissolves. Chill if time allows.
2.  Float lemon & orange slices.
3.  Serve over ice.
