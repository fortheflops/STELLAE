# Fruity Party Punch

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Beverages
> *A simple and refreshing large-batch party punch featuring a blend of lemonade, pineapple juice, apricot nectar, and fizzy ginger ale. Perfect for gatherings and celebrations.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 0 mins | None | 40–50 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Very large punch bowl or beverage dispenser
* Stirring spoon
* Measuring cups

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5 (6 oz) cans | Frozen [[Lemonade]] Concentrate | Thawed |
| 60 ounces | Unsweetened [[Pineapple Juice]] | Chilled |
| 60 ounces | [[Apricot Nectar]] | Chilled |
| 2 (2-liter) bottles | [[Ginger Ale]] | Chilled |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mix the Juice Base
1. In a very large punch bowl or beverage dispenser, combine the **5 (6 oz) cans frozen lemonade concentrate**, **60 ounces unsweetened pineapple juice**, and **60 ounces apricot nectar**.
2. Stir well until all the juices are thoroughly combined and the mixture is uniform.

### Step 2: Chill and Serve
3. If making ahead, chill the juice base for at least an hour or for several hours until cold.
4. Just before serving, gently pour in the **2 (2-liter) bottles ginger ale** and stir briefly to mix without losing the fizz.
5. Serve immediately in individual glasses filled with ice until refreshing and bubbly.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The juice base can be mixed and chilled in the refrigerator for up to several hours before adding the ginger ale.
