# Golden Milk with Turmeric Paste

> 📜 **Collection:** UK15 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Beverages
> *A traditional, warming Ayurvedic beverage made by blending a storable turmeric paste with milk, oil, and honey. Enjoy this comforting drink hot, topped with a dash of cinnamon.*

| ¼ tsp | Turmeric Paste | Prepared from Part 1 |

| To taste | [[Honey]] | None |
| For garnish | [[Cinnamon]] | Ground |

---

## Instructions

### Part 1: Make the Turmeric Paste
1. In a small saucepan, combine the **¼ cup turmeric powder** and **½ cup water**.
2. Bring the mixture to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer, stirring constantly, until a thick paste is formed.
3. Let the paste cool, then transfer it to a small jar. It can be stored in the refrigerator for later use.

### Part 2: Make the Golden Milk
4. In a saucepan, combine **1 cup milk**, **¼ tsp turmeric paste**, and **1 tsp almond oil**.
5. Stir over low heat, bringing the mixture just to a boil.
6. Remove from heat and stir in **honey** to your desired level of sweetness.
7. For a frothy top, carefully pour the hot mixture into a blender and blend for a few seconds until smooth and foamy.
8. Pour into a mug, sprinkle with **cinnamon**, and serve warm.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The prepared turmeric paste can be stored in a sealed jar in the refrigerator for up to several weeks.
