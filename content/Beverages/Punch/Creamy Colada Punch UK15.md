## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 (8.5 ounce) can          | [[Cream of Coconut]]                      |
| 3 cups                     | [[Pineapple Juice]], chilled              |
| 1 (12 ounce) can           | [[Ginger Ale]], chilled                   |
___
## Instructions
1.  In a 1-quart pitcher or a punch bowl, stir the cream of coconut and pineapple juice together until they are well mixed.
2.  Gently stir in the chilled ginger ale.
3.  Serve immediately.
