## Recipe
| Measurements       | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 (8.5 ounce) can  | [[Cream of Coconut]]                               |
| 3 cups             | [[Pineapple Juice]], chilled                       |
| 1 (12 ounce) can   | [[Ginger Ale]], chilled                            |
___
## Instructions
1.  In a 1-quart pitcher or a punch bowl, stir or whisk the cream of coconut and pineapple juice together until they are smooth and well-mixed.
2.  Just before serving, gently stir in the chilled ginger ale.
3.  Serve immediately over ice.
