## Recipe
| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
| 1 Tbsp              | Whole Cloves                              |
| 2 Tbsp              | Whole Allspice *(Assumed whole)* |
| 2 Cups              | Water                                     |
| 2 Cups              | Freshly Brewed Tea                        |
| 1 can (3 oz)        | Frozen Orange Juice Concentrate, thawed   |
| 2                   | Cinnamon Sticks                           |
| 1 qt (4 Cups)       | Rosé Wine                                 |
| 1 Cup               | Sugar                                     |
| 1 can (~12 oz)      | Frozen Hawaiian Punch Concentrate, thawed |
### Garnish
| Measurements | Ingredients           |
| :----------- | :-------------------- |
| As needed    | Thin Lemon Slices     |
| As needed    | Whole Cloves          |
___
## Instructions
1.  Bring [2 Cups] water to a boil, add [1 Tbsp] cloves, [2 Tbsp] all spice, [and 2] cinnamon sticks.
2.  Simmer 15 min. & strain [discarding solids, reserving the spiced liquid].
3.  Put tea[,] sugar, spice liquid, orange juice [concentrate], Hawaiian punch [concentrate] + wine In deep fat fryer (or lg kettle)...
    * ***SAFETY WARNING: Heating alcoholic beverages like wine in a deep fat fryer can be extremely dangerous due to the risk of ignition. It is strongly recommended to use a large kettle on the stovetop over low heat or a slow cooker instead. Never leave heating alcohol unattended.***
4.  ...& heat to serving temperature - Do Not Boil.
5.  Garnish with thin lemon slices stuck with whole cloves.
___

>[!Note]
>I used 1/2 the sugar, a little more tea + wine." *(This suggests using 1/2 cup sugar and slightly increasing tea/wine amounts is a possible variation).
