*A simple and refreshing large-batch party punch. This recipe combines the sweet and tangy flavors of lemonade, pineapple juice, and apricot nectar, topped with fizzy ginger ale.*
### Author
Unknown
___
## Recipe
| Measurements | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 5 (6 oz) cans| Frozen [[Lemonade]] Concentrate, thawed            |
| 60 ounces    | Unsweetened [[Pineapple Juice]], chilled           |
| 60 ounces    | [[Apricot Nectar]], chilled                        |
| 2 (2-liter) bottles| [[Ginger Ale]], chilled                            |
___
### Instructions
1.  In a very large punch bowl or beverage dispenser, combine the thawed lemonade concentrate, pineapple juice, and apricot nectar.
2.  Stir well until the mixture is uniform.
3.  (Optional) Chill the juice base for at least an hour.
4.  Just before serving, gently pour in the chilled ginger ale and stir briefly.
5.  Serve immediately in cups over ice.
