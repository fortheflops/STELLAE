*A sparkling and refreshing non-alcoholic punch. This simple recipe combines cranberry, grapefruit, and orange juices, sweetened and then topped with chilled ginger ale for a festive fizz.*

## Recipe
| Measurements | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 quart      | [[Cranberry Juice Cocktail]]                       |
| 1 cup        | [[Grapefruit Juice]]                               |
| 1 cup        | [[Orange Juice]]                                   |
| 1/2 cup      | [[Sugar]]                                          |
| 2 cups       | [[Ginger Ale]], chilled                            |
___
## Instructions
1.  In a large pitcher or punch bowl, combine the cranberry juice cocktail, grapefruit juice, orange juice, and sugar.
2.  Stir until the sugar is completely dissolved.
3.  Chill the juice mixture thoroughly in the refrigerator for at least an hour.
4.  Just before serving, gently stir in the chilled ginger ale.
5.  Serve immediately over ice.
