*A simple, old-fashioned punch recipe featuring the classic taste of Coca-Cola mixed with the bright, fruity flavors of pineapple and fresh lemon juice.*
### Author
Unknown
___
## Recipe
| Measurements      | Ingredients                                        |
| :---------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 (2-liter) bottle| Coca-Cola, chilled                                 |
| 2 Cups            | Water                                              |
| 3/4 Cup           | Sugar                                              |
| 1 Cup             | Pineapple Juice, chilled                           |
| 3                 | Lemons, juiced                                     |
___
### Instructions
1.  First, create a simple syrup by combining the water and sugar in a small saucepan. Heat over medium heat, stirring, until the sugar has completely dissolved. Set aside to cool completely.
2.  In a large punch bowl or pitcher, combine the cooled sugar syrup, the chilled pineapple juice, and the fresh juice from the three lemons.
3.  Just before serving, gently pour in the chilled Coca-Cola.
4.  Stir briefly to mix and serve immediately in glasses over ice.
