*An incredibly simple and refreshing two-ingredient party punch. This recipe combines the sweet taste of pineapple juice with the fizzy, citrus flavor of Squirt soda.*

Unknown
___
## Recipe
| Measurements      | Ingredients                                        |
| :---------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 (46 oz) can     | [[Pineapple Juice]], chilled                       |
| 3 (12 oz) cans    | [[Squirt]] soda, chilled                           |
| Optional          | Ice ring made of juices or ginger ale              |
___
## Instructions
1.  In a large punch bowl, pour in the chilled pineapple juice.
2.  Just before serving, gently add the chilled Squirt.
3.  Stir once or twice to combine.
4.  Add an ice ring to the punch bowl to keep it cold.
5.  Serve immediately.
