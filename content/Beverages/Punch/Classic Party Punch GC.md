## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| $\frac{1}{3}$ Bottle or $\frac{1}{2}$ Package | Hawaiian [[Punch Concentrate]]            |
| One Quart                        | Pure [[Orange Juice]] or [[Pineapple Juice]] |
| One Quart                        | Dry [[Ginger Ale]]                        |
| One Bottle                       | [[Champagne]]                             |
| As Needed                        | [[Orange Sherbet]]                        |
| As Needed                        | [[Orange Slices]] (for garnish)           |
___
## Instructions
1.  In a large punch bowl, combine the Hawaiian punch concentrate, one quart of orange juice (or pineapple juice), and one quart of dry ginger ale.
2.  Stir well to combine.
3.  Just before serving, gently pour in one bottle of champagne.
4.  Float scoops of orange sherbet on top of the punch.
5.  Garnish with fresh orange slices.
