## Recipe
| Measurements       | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 (12 oz) can      | Frozen [[Lemonade]] Concentrate, thawed            |
| 1 (64 oz) bottle   | [[Apple Juice]], chilled                           |
| 1 (64 oz) bottle   | [[Orange Juice]], chilled                          |
| **Optional Add-ins**|                                                  |
| 1 (2-liter) bottle | Ginger Ale or Lemon-Lime Soda, chilled             |
| As needed          | Sliced Oranges & Lemons for garnish                |
___
## Instructions
1.  In a large punch bowl, prepare the lemonade by mixing the thawed concentrate with the amount of water specified on the can (usually 4 ⅓ cans, or about 6 cups).
2.  Pour in the chilled apple juice and orange juice.
3.  Stir well to combine all the juices.
4.  For a sparkling punch, gently stir in the chilled ginger ale or lemon-lime soda just before serving.
5.  Serve in glasses over ice, garnished with fresh fruit slices if you like.
