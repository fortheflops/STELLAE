## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 quart                    | [[Cranberry Juice Cocktail]]              |
| 1 cup                      | [[Grapefruit Juice]]                      |
| 1 cup                      | [[Orange Juice]]                          |
| 1/2 cup                    | [[Sugar]]                                 |
| 2 cups                     | [[Ginger Ale]], chilled                   |
___
## Instructions
1.  In a large pitcher or punch bowl, combine the cranberry juice cocktail, grapefruit juice, orange juice, and sugar.
2.  Stir until the sugar is completely dissolved.
3.  Chill the juice mixture thoroughly.
4.  Just before serving, gently stir in the chilled ginger ale.
