*A festive and fizzy party punch, this recipe combines cranberry juice, lemonade, and a splash of grenadine, all brought to life with bubbly raspberry soda.*
### Author
Unknown
___
## Recipe
| Measurements      | Ingredients                                        |
| :---------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 2 Quarts          | Cranberry Juice Cocktail, chilled                  |
| 1 (6 Ounce) Can   | Frozen Lemonade Concentrate                        |
| 3 Cups            | Cold Water                                         |
| 1/3 Cup           | Grenadine Syrup                                    |
| 2 (12 Ounce) Cans | Raspberry Soda Pop, chilled                        |
___
### Instructions
1.  In a large punch bowl, prepare the lemonade by mixing the thawed concentrate with 3 cups of cold water (or as directed on the can). Stir until dissolved.
2.  Pour in the chilled cranberry juice cocktail and the grenadine. Stir to combine.
3.  Just before serving, gently pour in the chilled raspberry soda.
4.  Stir briefly and serve immediately in glasses over ice.
___

>[!Note]
>Serves 24
