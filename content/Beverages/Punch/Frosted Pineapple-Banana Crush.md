### Instructions
1.  In a large bowl, mix the mashed **bananas**, **orange juice**, **lemon juice**, **pineapple juice**, and **crushed pineapple**.
2.  Freeze the mixture until it is slushy.
3.  Transfer the slushy mixture to a blender and blend until it becomes frothy.
4.  Strain the mixture, pressing as much juice through the strainer as possible.
5.  Chill the strained liquid thoroughly.
6.  Stir the drink before serving.
7.  Pour into six 8oz glasses. If desired, add 1 oz. of **light rum** to each glass.
8.  Garnish each glass with a **pineapple stick** and a **sprig of mint**.
