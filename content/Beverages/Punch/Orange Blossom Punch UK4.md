*A bright and fruity party punch perfect for any celebration. This recipe features a blend of orange, lemon, and cherry juices, topped with fizzy ginger ale and creamy sherbet.*
### Author
Unknown
___
## Recipe
| Measurements      | Ingredients                                        |
| :---------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 ½ qts (6 cups)  | [[Orange Juice]], chilled                          |
| 1 cup             | [[Lemon Juice]], chilled                           |
| ⅓ cup             | [[Cherry Juice]] (from a jar of maraschino cherries) |
| ½ cup             | [[Sugar]]                                          |
| 1 (2-liter) bottle| [[Ginger Ale]], chilled                            |
| 2 qts             | Orange or Lemon [[Sherbet]]                        |
___
### Instructions
1.  In a large punch bowl, combine the orange juice, lemon juice, cherry juice, and sugar.
2.  Stir well until the sugar is completely dissolved.
3.  Just before you are ready to serve, gently pour in the chilled ginger ale.
4.  Add large scoops of the sherbet to the punch bowl.
5.  Serve immediately.
___
