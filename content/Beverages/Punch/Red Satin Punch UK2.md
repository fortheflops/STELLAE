*A festive and bubbly large-batch punch, perfect for parties. This simple recipe combines cranberry and apple juices with 7-Up, served with decorative cherry-filled ice cubes.*

Unknown
___
## Recipe
| Measurements      | Ingredients                                        |
| :---------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 2 Quarts          | [[Cranberry Juice]], chilled                       |
| 2 Quarts          | [[Apple Juice]], chilled                           |
| 2 (2-liter) bottles| [[7-Up]], chilled                                  |
| As needed         | [[Maraschino Cherries]]                            |
___
### Instructions
1.  **Prepare the Ice Cubes:** Place one maraschino cherry in each compartment of two or three ice cube trays. Carefully fill the trays with 7-Up and place them in the freezer until frozen solid (at least 4 hours).
2.  **Combine Juices:** In a large punch bowl, combine the chilled cranberry juice and apple juice.
3.  **Add Soda:** Just before you are ready to serve, gently pour in the chilled 7-Up and give it a brief stir.
4.  **Serve:** Add the prepared cherry ice cubes to the punch bowl and serve immediately.
