## Recipe
| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
| 1 Pint (2 Cups)           | [[Cranberry Juice]]     |
| 12 Ounces                 | [[Apricot Nectar]]      |
| 2 Tablespoons             | [[Lemon Juice]]         |
| 2 Tablespoons             | [[Butter]]              |
| Optional                  | [[Lemon Slices]] (for garnish) |
| Optional                  | [[Spirits]] (e.g., Rum, Brandy) |

___
### Instructions
1.  Heat all ingredients (1 pint cranberry juice, 12 oz apricot nectar, 2 tablespoons lemon juice, 2 tablespoons butter) together.
2.  Serve in mugs.
3.  May garnish with lemon slices.
4.  Addition of spirits optional.
___

>[!Note]
>6 Servings
