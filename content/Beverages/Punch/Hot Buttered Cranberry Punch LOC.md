*A warm and comforting punch, perfect for the holidays. This simple recipe combines cranberry juice, apricot nectar, and lemon juice, enriched with butter for a smooth, classic flavor.*

Unknown
___
## Recipe
| Measurements      | Ingredients                                        |
| :---------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 Pint (2 Cups)   | [[Cranberry Juice]]                                |
| 12 Ounces         | [[Apricot Nectar]]                                 |
| 2 Tablespoons     | [[Lemon Juice]]                                    |
| 2 Tablespoons     | [[Butter]]                                         |
| Optional          | [[Lemon Slices]] (for garnish)                     |
| Optional          | [[Spirits]] (e.g., Rum, Brandy)                    |
___
### Instructions
1.  In a medium saucepan, combine the cranberry juice, apricot nectar, lemon juice, and butter.
2.  Heat over medium heat, stirring occasionally, until the butter is melted and the punch is hot. Do not allow it to boil.
3.  Ladle the hot punch into mugs.
4.  If desired, add a shot of your preferred spirit (like rum or brandy) to each mug and stir.
5.  Garnish with a fresh lemon slice and serve immediately.
