## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |
| 2 Cups | Bottled [[Cranberry-Apple Juice]], chilled |
| 1 Cup | Strong [[Iced Tea]], chilled |
| 1 (7-oz) bottle| [[Lemon-Lime Soda]], chilled |
| For Garnish | [[Mint Sprigs]] or [[Maraschino Cherries]] |
| For Serving | [[Ice Cubes]] |
___
### Instructions
1.  In a tall pitcher, combine the chilled **cranberry-apple juice**, **iced tea**, and **lemon-lime soda**.
2.  Pour the mixture over **ice cubes** in four 8-ounce glasses.
3.  Garnish with **mint sprigs** or **maraschino cherries**.
