# Classic Tequila Sunrise

> 📜 **Collection:** LOC | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Beverages
> *A classic and visually stunning cocktail, the Tequila Sunrise gets its name from its beautiful layered appearance. This simple recipe combines tequila and orange juice, with grenadine added at the end to sink and create a sunrise effect.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5 mins | None | None | 1 cocktail |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Highball glass
* Spoon

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1.5 oz (1 shot) | [[Tequila]] | Chilled or room temperature |
| 4-5 oz | [[Orange Juice]] | Freshly squeezed or bottled |
| 0.75 oz (1/2 shot) | [[Grenadine]] | Sweet syrup |
| As needed | [[Ice]] | Cubed |
| Optional | [[Orange Slice]] | For garnish |
| Optional | [[Cherry]] | Maraschino for garnish |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Assemble the Base
1. Fill a highball or Collins glass with **as needed ice**.

### Step 2: Add Spirits and Juice
2. Add **1.5 oz (1 shot) tequila** to the glass.
3. Fill the glass almost to the top with **4-5 oz orange juice** and stir briefly until well combined and chilled.

### Step 3: Create the Sunrise Effect
4. Slowly pour the **0.75 oz (1/2 shot) grenadine** over the back of a spoon or down the side of the glass until it sinks to the bottom.
5. Do not mix; allow the grenadine to settle and create the signature layered sunrise effect until visually striking.

### Step 4: Garnish and Serve
6. Garnish with an **optional orange slice** and an **optional cherry**, if you like, and serve immediately.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Best enjoyed immediately after preparation to maintain the distinct layered effect and prevent ice dilution.
