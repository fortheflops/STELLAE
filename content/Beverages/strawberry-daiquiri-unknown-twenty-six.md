# Strawberry Daiquiri

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Bettina | 📂 **Category:** Beverages
> *A refreshing and frosty classic cocktail featuring fresh strawberries, limeade, and rum. Blended to a smooth, icy consistency, it's the ultimate warm-weather refreshment.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5 mins | None | None | 4 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Blender
* Measuring cups

---
## Ingredients

### Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 can (6 oz) | [[Limeade]] | Frozen concentrate |
| Whole can (6 oz) | [[Rum]] | None |
| A few tbsp | [[Sugar]] | To taste |
| 8-10 | [[Fresh Strawberries]] | May use frozen |
| 10-12 | [[Ice Cubes]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Blending
1. Combine the **1/2 can (6 oz) limeade**, **whole can (6 oz) rum**, **a few tbsp sugar**, **8-10 fresh strawberries**, and **10-12 ice cubes** in a blender.
2. Blend on high speed until the mixture is completely smooth, frosty, and uniform in texture with no large ice chunks remaining.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Best served immediately while frozen and frothy.
