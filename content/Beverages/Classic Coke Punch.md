# Classic Coke Punch

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Beverages
> *A simple, old-fashioned party punch recipe featuring the classic taste of Coca-Cola mixed with the bright, fruity flavors of pineapple and fresh lemon juice.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 5 mins | None | 12-15 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* small saucepan
* large punch bowl or pitcher

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 Cups | [[Water]] | for simple syrup |
| 3/4 Cup | [[Sugar]] | for simple syrup |
| 1 Cup | [[Pineapple Juice]] | chilled |
| 3 | [[Lemons]] | juiced |
| 1 (2-liter) bottle | [[Coca-Cola]] | chilled |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Make the Simple Syrup
1. First, create a simple syrup by combining the **2 cups water** and **3/4 cup sugar** in a small saucepan. Heat over medium heat, stirring, until the **sugar** has completely dissolved and the mixture is clear. Set aside to cool completely until no longer warm to the touch.

### Step 2: Combine Base Ingredients
2. In a large punch bowl or pitcher, combine the cooled sugar syrup, the **1 cup pineapple juice**, and the fresh **juice from the 3 lemons**. Stir well until fully incorporated.

### Step 3: Add Carbonation and Serve
3. Just before serving, gently pour in the chilled **1 (2-liter) bottle Coca-Cola**. Stir briefly and gently to mix without losing the carbonation, and serve immediately in glasses over ice until refreshingly cold.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The simple syrup and fruit juice base can be prepared a day in advance and stored separately in the refrigerator; add the chilled Coca-Cola just before serving.
