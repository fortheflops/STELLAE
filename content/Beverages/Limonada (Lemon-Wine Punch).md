# Limonada (Lemon-Wine Punch)

> 📜 **Collection:** LOC | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Beverages
> *A refreshing and sophisticated party punch combining fresh lemon juice, sweet sherry, and dry wines. This large-batch drink is perfect for gatherings and chills beautifully.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | None | 2 hours | 12-16 servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large punch bowl
* Pitcher

* Citrus juicer

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 8 | [[Lemons]] | juiced |
| 2 | [[Lemons]] | sliced |
| 1 cup | [[Powdered Sugar]] | for sweetness |
| 1/2 cup | [[Sweet Sherry]] | fortified wine |
| 1 bottle (750ml) | [[Rosé Wine]] | dry, chilled |
| 1 bottle (750ml) | [[Chablis Wine]] | dry, chilled |
| As needed | [[Cracked Ice]] | for serving |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Combine the Base
1. Squeeze the juice from **8 lemons** into a large punch bowl or pitcher. Thinly slice the remaining **2 lemons** and add them to the juice.

### Step 2: Dissolve the Sugar
2. Sprinkle the **1 cup powdered sugar** over the lemon juice and slices. Pour in the **1/2 cup sweet sherry** and stir gently until the sugar has completely dissolved.

### Step 3: Add the Wines
3. Gently pour in the chilled **1 bottle (750ml) rosé wine** and the chilled **1 bottle (750ml) Chablis wine**. Stir to combine until fragrant and uniform.

### Step 4: Chill and Serve
4. Refrigerate for at least **2 hours** to allow the flavors to meld and chill thoroughly. Serve in glasses half-filled with **cracked ice** until refreshingly cold.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Prepare the punch base and add wines up to 2 hours in advance, keeping it sealed in the refrigerator until ready to ladle over ice.
