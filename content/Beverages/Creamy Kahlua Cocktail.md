# Creamy Kahlua Cocktail

> 📜 **Collection:** GC | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Beverages
> *A simple and creamy three-ingredient after-dinner drink featuring Kahlua, white creme de cacao, and cream for a smooth, coffee-chocolate flavor.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5 mins | 0 mins | None | 1 cocktail |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Cocktail shaker
* Chilled champagne or coupe glass
* Ice

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 part (1 oz) | [[Kahlua]] | None |
| 1 part (1 oz) | [[White Creme de Cacao]] | None |
| 1 part (1 oz) | [[Cream]] | Or half-and-half |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Combine and Shake
1. Combine **1 part (1 oz) Kahlua**, **1 part (1 oz) white creme de cacao**, and **1 part (1 oz) cream** in a shaker filled with **ice**.

### Step 2: Chill and Strain
2. Shake well for **15-20 seconds** until thoroughly chilled and frosty.
3. Strain into a chilled champagne or coupe glass until smooth and frothy.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Best enjoyed immediately after shaking while ice-cold.
