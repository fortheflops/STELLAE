# Bourbon Slush

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Sharon | 📂 **Category:** Beverages
> *A refreshing, frozen Southern classic featuring a blend of citrus juices, strong tea, and bourbon. Perfect for warm weather gatherings, this slush is frozen ahead of time and served topped with lemon-lime soda.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Base
1. In a saucepan, bring **4 cups water** and **1 cup sugar** to a boil. Boil for about **2 minutes** until the sugar is completely dissolved and a light syrup forms.
2. Remove from heat and stir in **12 oz frozen orange juice**, **12 oz frozen lemonade**, **1 cup strong tea**, and **1/2 cup lemon juice** until the frozen concentrates are fully melted and combined.
3. Allow the mixture to cool completely to room temperature, then stir in **1 cup bourbon**.

### Step 2: Freeze and Serve
4. Pour the mixture into a freezer-safe container and freeze for at least **8 hours** or overnight until it reaches a firm, scoopable slush consistency.
5. When ready to serve, scoop the frozen **bourbon slush** into a glass until it is about **3/4 full**.
6. Top off the glass with **lemon-lime soda** and stir gently to combine before enjoying.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This slush can be kept frozen in an airtight container for up to 3 months. Simply scoop and serve directly from the freezer as needed.
