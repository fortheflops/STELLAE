# Classic Red Wine Sangria with Brandy

> 📜 **Collection:** LOC | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Beverages
> *A robust and fruity red wine sangria fortified with brandy and fresh citrus juices. Perfectly chilled and garnished for parties and gatherings.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | None | 1 hour | 6-8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large pitcher
* Long-handled spoon
* Juicer
* Knife
* Cutting board

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 bottle (750ml) | [[Burgundy Wine]] | or other dry red wine |
| 1.5 oz | [[Brandy]] | or cognac, 1 shot |
| Juice of 2 | [[Oranges]] | freshly squeezed, about 1/2 to 2/3 cup |
| Juice of 1 | [[Lemon]] | freshly squeezed, about 2 tablespoons |
| 1/2 to 3/4 cup | [[Sugar]] | to taste |

### Garnish
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| As needed | [[Apple]] | wedges of cored, unpeeled apple |
| As needed | [[Orange]] | slices of unpeeled orange |
| As needed | [[Ice]] | for serving |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Combine and Dissolve
1. In a large pitcher, combine the **1 bottle (750ml) Burgundy wine**, **1.5 oz brandy or cognac**, **juice of 2 oranges**, **juice of 1 lemon**, and **1/2 to 3/4 cup sugar**.

### Step 2: Mix and Chill
2. Stir well until the sugar is completely dissolved and the mixture is well incorporated. Cover and chill in the refrigerator for at least one hour until thoroughly chilled and the flavors meld.

### Step 3: Garnish and Serve
3. Just before serving, add the fresh **apple wedges** and **orange slices** to the pitcher. Serve in glasses over **ice**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store covered in the refrigerator for up to 24 hours before adding the fresh fruit garnish.
