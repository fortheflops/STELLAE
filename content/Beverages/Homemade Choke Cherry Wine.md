# Homemade Choke Cherry Wine

> 📜 **Collection:** JohnBCollection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Ferma Best | 📂 **Category:** Beverages
> *A traditional large-batch fruit wine crafted from fresh chokecherries, warm water, yeast, and a progressive sugar addition. Fermented over three weeks until rich and sweet to taste.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 30 mins | 3 weeks | 3 weeks | 4 to 5 gallons |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large fermentation vessel
* Stirring paddle
* Coffee filter
* Bottles

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 Gallons | [[Choke Cherries]] | Fresh |
| 5 Gallons | [[Water]] | Warm |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Yeast]] | Standard |
| 5 Cups | [[Sugar]] | Initial amount |
| 1-2 Cups daily | [[Sugar]] | Additional, as needed |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and Initial Mix
1. Crush some of the **2 gallons choke cherries** to release their juices.
2. Place the crushed cherries into a large fermentation vessel with the **5 gallons warm water**, **1/4 tsp yeast**, and **5 cups sugar**.

### Step 2: Fermentation and Daily Feeding
3. Each morning, stir the mixture thoroughly and add **1 to 2 cups sugar**. Continue this daily until the mixture reaches your desired sweetness.
4. Continue stirring daily for about **3 weeks**, or until the **choke cherries** settle completely to the bottom and active bubbling subsides.

### Step 3: Bottling
5. Once fermentation is complete and the sediment has settled, siphon the liquid through a coffee filter into clean bottles until clear and sediment-free.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store bottled wine in a cool, dark place to age and mature for optimal flavor development.
