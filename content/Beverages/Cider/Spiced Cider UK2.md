*A festive and aromatic spiced cider perfect for a crowd, combining apple cider and cranberry juice with brown sugar, cinnamon, cloves, and fresh lemon slices.*
### Author
Unknown
___
## Recipe
| Measurements | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 2 qts        | [[Apple Cider]]                                    |
| 1 1/2 qts    | [[Cranberry Juice]]                                |
| 1 cup        | [[Brown Sugar]], packed                            |
| 1 1/2 tsp    | [[Whole Cloves]]                                   |
| 4            | 3" [[Cinnamon Sticks]]                             |
| 1            | [[Lemon]], thinly sliced                           |
___
### Instructions
1.  Combine all ingredients (apple cider, cranberry juice, brown sugar, whole cloves, cinnamon sticks, and lemon slices) in a large kettle or stockpot.
2.  Heat over medium-high heat, stirring occasionally, until the mixture comes to a boil and the sugar is dissolved.
3.  Reduce the heat to low, cover, and let the cider simmer for 15 to 20 minutes to allow the flavors to meld.
4.  Use a slotted spoon to remove and discard the spices and cooked lemon slices.
5.  Serve warm in mugs. If desired, float a fresh lemon slice in each cup as a garnish.
___

>[!Servings]
>Serves 25 at 1/2 cup each.
