*A simple and heartwarming recipe for classic mulled apple cider, simmered with cinnamon sticks and whole cloves for a perfectly spiced warm beverage.*

Unknown
___
## Recipe
| Measurements | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 1/2 Qts    | [[Apple Cider]]                                    |
| 2 Tbsp       | [[Sugar]]                                          |
| 4            | 3" [[Cinnamon Sticks]]                             |
| 10           | Whole [[Cloves]]                                   |
| Optional     | [[Lemon Slices]]                                   |
___
### Instructions
1.  In a large saucepan or stockpot, combine the apple cider, sugar, cinnamon sticks, and whole cloves.
2.  Bring the mixture to a boil over medium-high heat, stirring to ensure the sugar dissolves completely.
3.  Once it reaches a boil, reduce the heat to low, cover, and let it simmer for at least 30 minutes.
4.  Strain the cider through a fine-mesh sieve to remove the cinnamon sticks and cloves.
5.  Ladle the warm cider into mugs. Garnish with a thin lemon slice, if desired.
