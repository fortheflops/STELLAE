# Hot Buttered Cranberry Punch

> 📜 **Collection:** LOC | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Beverages
> *A warm and comforting punch, perfect for the holidays. This simple recipe combines cranberry juice, apricot nectar, and lemon juice, enriched with butter for a smooth, classic flavor.*

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 Pint (2 Cups) | [[Cranberry Juice]] | none |
| 12 Ounces | [[Apricot Nectar]] | none |
| 2 Tablespoons | [[Lemon Juice]] | freshly squeezed |
| 2 Tablespoons | [[Butter]] | none |
| Optional | [[Lemon Slices]] | for garnish |
| Optional | [[Spirits]] | e.g., rum, brandy, 1.5 ounces per serving |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Combine and Heat
1. In a medium saucepan, combine the **1 pint (2 cups) cranberry juice**, **12 ounces apricot nectar**, **2 tablespoons lemon juice**, and **2 tablespoons butter**.
2. Heat over medium heat, stirring occasionally, until the **2 tablespoons butter** is melted and the punch is hot and fragrant. Do not allow it to boil.

### Step 2: Serve and Garnish
3. Ladle the hot punch into mugs until warm and steaming.
4. If desired, add a shot of your preferred spirit, such as rum or brandy, to each mug and stir.
5. Garnish with a fresh **lemon slices** and serve immediately while hot.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Punch ingredients can be measured out ahead of time, but it is best heated and served fresh.
