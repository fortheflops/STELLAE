# Classic Mulled Cider

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Beverages
> *A simple and heartwarming recipe for classic mulled apple cider, simmered with cinnamon sticks and whole cloves for a perfectly spiced warm beverage. Enjoy this cozy drink during the crisp autumn and winter months.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5 mins | 30 mins | None | 6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large saucepan or stockpot
* Fine-mesh sieve
* Warm mugs

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 1/2 Qts | [[Apple Cider]] | None |
| 2 Tbsp | [[Sugar]] | None |
| 4 | [[Cinnamon Sticks]] | 3-inch |
| 10 | [[Cloves]] | Whole |
| Optional | [[Lemon Slices]] | For garnish |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Combine and Simmer
1. In a large saucepan or stockpot, combine the **1 1/2 qts [[Apple Cider]]**, **2 tbsp [[Sugar]]**, **4 [[Cinnamon Sticks]]**, and **10 whole [[Cloves]]**.
2. Bring the mixture to a boil over medium-high heat, stirring constantly until the **2 tbsp [[Sugar]]** dissolves completely.
3. Once it reaches a gentle boil, reduce the heat to low, cover with a lid, and let it simmer for at least **30 minutes** until fragrant and deeply spiced.

### Step 2: Strain and Serve
4. Carefully strain the **[[Apple Cider]]** through a fine-mesh sieve to remove the **4 [[Cinnamon Sticks]]** and **10 whole [[Cloves]]**.
5. Ladle the hot, spiced **[[Apple Cider]]** into warm mugs and garnish with **[[Lemon Slices]]**, if desired.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Leftover mulled cider can be stored in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days and gently reheated on the stovetop before serving.
