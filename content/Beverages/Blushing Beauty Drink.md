# Blushing Beauty Drink

> 📜 **Collection:** General Archive | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Beverages
> *A simple and refreshing non-alcoholic 'Blushing Beauty' drink, made by combining chilled cranberry-apple juice, strong iced tea, and lemon-lime soda. Served over ice.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5 mins | 0 mins | None | 4 8-ounce glasses |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Tall pitcher
* 4 8-ounce glasses

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 Cups | [[Cranberry-Apple Juice]] | Bottled, chilled |
| 1 Cup | [[Iced Tea]] | Strong, chilled |
| 1 (7-oz) bottle | [[Lemon-Lime Soda]] | Chilled |
| As needed | [[Ice Cubes]] | For serving |
| For Garnish | [[Mint Sprigs]] | Optional |
| For Garnish | [[Maraschino Cherries]] | Optional |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Combine and Serve
1. In a tall pitcher, combine the chilled **2 cups bottled cranberry-apple juice**, **1 cup strong iced tea**, and **1 (7-oz) bottle lemon-lime soda**.
2. Pour the mixture over **ice cubes** in four 8-ounce glasses until full and well-chilled.
3. Garnish with **mint sprigs** or **maraschino cherries** and serve immediately.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Combine juice and tea ahead of time in the refrigerator, but add the carbonated lemon-lime soda and ice just before serving to maintain fizz.
