## Instructions
1.  In a large bowl, mix together all the ingredients: one cup cocoa, two cups sugar, five and a half cups powdered milk, one cup nondairy creamer, and one and a half teaspoons of salt.
2.  Stir until thoroughly combined.
3.  Store the mix in a jar with a tight lid.
4.  To serve, use $\frac{1}{4}$ to $\frac{1}{3}$ cup of the mix per cup of boiling water.
