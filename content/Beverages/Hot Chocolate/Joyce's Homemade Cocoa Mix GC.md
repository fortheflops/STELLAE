*A simple, large-batch recipe for a rich and creamy instant hot cocoa mix. This mix from Joyce is perfect for keeping on hand for a quick treat or giving as a gift.*
### Author
Joyce
___
## Recipe
| Measurements       | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 cup              | [[Cocoa Powder]]                                   |
| 2 cups             | [[Sugar]]                                          |
| 5 ½ cups           | Powdered [[Milk]]                                  |
| 1 cup              | Nondairy [[Creamer]] (e.g., Coffeemate)            |
| 1 ½ tsp            | [[Salt]]                                           |
___
## Instructions
1.  In a very large bowl, combine the cocoa, sugar, powdered milk, nondairy creamer, and salt.
2.  Whisk or stir until all the ingredients are thoroughly combined and the mixture is a uniform color.
3.  Store the mix in a large jar or airtight container.
4.  **To Serve:** Use ¼ to ⅓ cup of the mix per mug of boiling water. Stir well until dissolved.
