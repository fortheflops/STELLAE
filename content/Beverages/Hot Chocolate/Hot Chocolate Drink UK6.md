## Recipe
| Measurements      | Ingredients                        |
| :---------------- | :--------------------------------- |
| 1 oz              | Semi-sweet Chocolate               |
| 2 Tbsp            | Sugar                              |
| 1 Cup             | Boiling Water                      |
| 1 tsp             | Vanilla Essence                    |
| 1-2 Tbsp          | Liqueur of your choice             |
| $\frac{1}{2}$ Cup | Hot Milk                           |
| $\frac{1}{2}$ Cup | Hot Cream                          |
| 1 Cup             | Freshly Made Hot Coffee            |
| As needed         | Whipped Cream *(Optional garnish)* |
| Optional          | Cinnamon Stick Stirrer             |
___
### Instructions
1.  Melt 1 oz. semi-sweet choc. with 2 T sugar & 1 C boiling water.
2.  Season with 1 t vanilla essence & 1-2 T of the liqueur of your choice.
3.  Add 1/2 C hot milk, 1/2 C hot cream & 1 C freshly made hot coffee.
4.  Pour into mugs & top with whipped cream.
5.  If desired, add a cinnamon stick stirrer.
