*A classic and refreshing orange float. This simple three-ingredient treat combines orange juice and creamy orange sherbet, all topped with fizzy ginger ale.*

Unknown
___
## Recipe
| Measurements      | Ingredients                                        |
| :---------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 5 Tablespoons     | [[Orange Juice]], chilled                          |
| 2-3 scoops        | [[Orange Sherbet]]                                 |
| 6-8 oz, as needed | [[Ginger Ale]], chilled                            |
___
## Instructions
1.  Pour the chilled orange juice into the bottom of a tall float glass.
2.  Add 2-3 scoops of orange sherbet.
3.  Slowly pour the chilled ginger ale over the sherbet until the glass is full and the float becomes foamy.
4.  Serve immediately with a straw and a long spoon.
