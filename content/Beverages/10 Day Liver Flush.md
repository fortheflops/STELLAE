# 10 Day Liver Flush

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Beverages
> *A classic cleansing elixir featuring fresh lemon juice, maple syrup, and a kick of cayenne pepper. Designed to be enjoyed as a refreshing and revitalizing tonic.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5 mins | None | None | 1 Serving |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 10-ounce glass
* Measuring spoons

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 Tablespoons | [[Lemon Juice]] | Fresh |
| 2 Tablespoons | [[Maple Syrup]] | Grade B |
| 1/10 Teaspoon | [[Cayenne Pepper]] | Ground |
| To Fill | [[Water]] | In a 10 oz glass |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mix the Elixir
1. In a **10-ounce glass**, combine the **2 tablespoons fresh lemon juice**, **2 tablespoons Grade B maple syrup**, and **1/10 teaspoon cayenne pepper**.

### Step 2: Fill and Serve
2. Fill the remainder of the **10-ounce glass** with **water** and stir well until fully incorporated, then drink when hungry.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Best prepared fresh immediately before drinking.
