# Orange Sherbet Float

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Beverages
> *A classic and refreshing orange float. This simple three-ingredient treat combines orange juice and creamy orange sherbet, all topped with fizzy ginger ale.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5 mins | 0 mins | None | 1 serving |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Tall glass
* Straw
* Long spoon

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5 Tablespoons | [[Orange Juice]] | chilled |
| 2-3 scoops | [[Orange Sherbet]] | none |
| 6-8 oz | [[Ginger Ale]] | chilled, as needed |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Assemble the Float
1. Pour the **5 tablespoons orange juice** into the bottom of a tall float glass.

### Step 2: Add Sherbet and Fizz
2. Add **2-3 scoops orange sherbet** on top of the juice.

### Step 3: Pour and Serve
3. Slowly pour the **6-8 oz ginger ale** over the sherbet until the glass is full and the float becomes delightfully foamy.
4. Serve immediately with a straw and a long spoon.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Best enjoyed immediately after assembly while the ginger ale is fizzy and the sherbet is creamy.
