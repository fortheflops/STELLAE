# Jayce's Spiced Mocha Mix

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Jayce | 📂 **Category:** Beverages
> *A delightful instant spiced mocha mix that combines the richness of cocoa and coffee with warm spices. Perfect for a cozy warm treat or a homemade gift.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | None | None | 35-45 servings (Approx. 3.5 cups of mix) |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large bowl
* Whisk
* Airtight container or jar

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 Cup | Nondairy [[Creamer]] | like Pream |
| 1 Cup | [[Cocoa Mix]] | like Swiss Miss |
| ⅔ Cup | Instant [[Coffee]] | granules |
| ½ Cup | [[Sugar]] | granulated |
| ½ tsp | Ground [[Cinnamon]] | none |
| ¼ tsp | Ground [[Nutmeg]] | none |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Combine the Mix
1. In a large bowl, mix together the **1 cup nondairy creamer**, **1 cup cocoa mix**, **⅔ cup instant coffee**, **½ cup sugar**, **½ tsp ground cinnamon**, and **¼ tsp ground nutmeg**.

### Step 2: Whisk and Store
2. Whisk or stir until all ingredients are thoroughly combined and the mixture is uniform.
3. Store the finished mix in an airtight container or jar until ready to use.

### Step 3: Serve
4. To serve, stir **3-4 heaping teaspoons** of the mix into one cup of hot water or hot milk until completely dissolved.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the dry mix in a large airtight container or jar at room temperature for up to several months.
