# Spiced Tea

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Beverages
> *A classic, large-batch spiced tea perfect for gatherings, featuring a fragrant blend of cinnamon and cloves. This comforting beverage is easily adjustable to your preferred level of sweetness.*

## Ingredients

### Spices and Base
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 tsp | [[Cinnamon]] | ground |
| 1 tsp | [[Cloves]] | ground |
| 10 cups | [[Water]] | hot |
| 5 tbsp | [[Tea]] | loose or bagged |

### Sweetener and Citrus
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3 cups | [[Sugar]] | adjust to taste |
| 3 | [[Lemons]] | juiced |
| 6 | [[Oranges]] | juiced |
| 20 cups | [[Water]] | cold |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Infusion
1. Place **1 tsp cinnamon** and **1 tsp cloves** in a tea bag and steep in **10 cups hot water** for 10 minutes.
2. Add **5 tbsp tea** to a bag and steep in the mixture for an additional 5 minutes.

### Step 2: Mixing
3. Remove tea bags and stir in **3 cups sugar** until completely dissolved.
4. Add the **juice of 3 lemons** and **6 oranges**, then stir in **20 cups water**.
5. Heat the mixture thoroughly until steaming before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The tea base can be prepared in advance and stored in the refrigerator; simply reheat on the stove before serving.
