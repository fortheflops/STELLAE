# Celina Engle's Spiced Tea Mix

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Celina Engle | 📂 **Category:** Beverages
> *A classic instant spiced tea mix from Celina Engle. This recipe combines the zesty citrus flavors of Tang and lemonade with instant tea and warm spices for a quick and comforting hot beverage.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | None | None | Approx. 6 cups of mix (100+ servings) |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Very large bowl
* Wire whisk
* Airtight container or jar

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 Cups | [[Tang]] | Drink mix |
| 2 Cups | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 3/4 to 1 Cup | [[Instant Tea]] | Unsweetened |
| 1 (3-ounce) package | [[Lemonade Mix]] | Sweetened powder |
| 1 tsp | [[Cinnamon]] | Ground |
| 1 tsp | [[Ground Cloves]] | Ground |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Combine and Mix
1. In a very large bowl, combine the **2 cups Tang**, **2 cups sugar**, **1 (3-ounce) package lemonade mix**, **3/4 to 1 cup instant tea**, **1 tsp cinnamon**, and **1 tsp ground cloves**.
2. Whisk or stir until all ingredients are thoroughly combined, uniform in color, and completely free of clumps.

### Step 2: Store and Serve
3. Store the finished mix in a large, airtight container or jar.
4. To serve, stir **2 rounded teaspoons** of the mix into **one cup of hot water** until dissolved until fully incorporated and fragrant. Adjust to taste.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the dry mix in a tightly sealed airtight container in a cool, dry place for up to 6 months.
