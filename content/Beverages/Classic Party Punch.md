# Classic Party Punch

> 📜 **Collection:** GC | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Beverages
> *A celebratory and easy-to-make party punch featuring a bubbly mix of champagne, ginger ale, and fruit juices, all topped with festive scoops of orange sherbet.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 0 mins | None | 20-25 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large punch bowl
* Long-handled spoon

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 (6 oz) can | Hawaiian [[Punch Concentrate]] | thawed |
| 1 Quart (4 cups) | [[Orange Juice]] or [[Pineapple Juice]] | chilled |
| 1 Quart (4 cups) | Dry [[Ginger Ale]] | chilled |
| 1 Bottle (750ml) | [[Champagne]] | chilled |
| 1 pint | [[Orange Sherbet]] | scooped |
| As needed | [[Orange Slices]] | for garnish |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mix the Base
1. In a large punch bowl, combine the **1 (6 oz) can Hawaiian punch concentrate** and **1 quart (4 cups) orange juice** (or pineapple juice). Stir until well mixed.

### Step 2: Add Bubbly and Serve
2. Just before serving, gently pour in the **1 quart (4 cups) dry ginger ale** and the **1 bottle (750ml) champagne**. Stir gently once or twice to combine.
3. Add several large scoops of **1 pint orange sherbet** to float on top of the punch.
4. Garnish with fresh **as needed orange slices** and serve immediately while it is cold and bubbly.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Combine juice and concentrate ahead of time and chill; add carbonated beverages and sherbet just before serving.
