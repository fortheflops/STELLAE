# Joyce's Russian Tea Mix

> 📜 **Collection:** GC | ✍️ **Attribution:** Joyce | 📂 **Category:** Beverages
> *A classic, comforting instant 'Russian Tea' mix recipe featuring a tangy and spiced blend of Tang, lemonade, instant tea, and warm spices.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 0 mins | None | Approx. 5 cups of mix (80 servings) |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Very large bowl
* Wire whisk
* Airtight jar or container

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 (18 oz) container | [[Tang]] | Or enough to make 4 quarts |
| 1 package | [[Lemonade Mix]] | Enough to make 1 quart |
| ¾ cup | Instant [[Tea]] | Unsweetened |
| 2 tsp | Ground [[Cinnamon]] | Standard grind |
| 1 tsp | Ground [[Cloves]] | Standard grind |
| 2 cups | [[Sugar]] | Optional, for a sweeter mix |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Combine the Mix
1. In a very large bowl, combine the **1 (18 oz) container Tang**, **1 package lemonade mix**, **¾ cup instant tea**, **1 tsp ground cloves**, and **2 tsp ground cinnamon**. If you prefer a sweeter tea, add the **2 cups sugar** as well.

### Step 2: Whisk and Store
2. Whisk or stir until all the ingredients are thoroughly combined and the mixture is uniform.
3. Store the finished mix in a tightly sealed jar or container until ready to use.

### Step 3: To Serve
4. Add two heaping teaspoons of the **tea mix** to a mug of hot water and stir until completely dissolved and fragrant. Adjust the amount of mix to your taste.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the dry mix in a tightly sealed container in a cool, dry place for up to 6 months.
