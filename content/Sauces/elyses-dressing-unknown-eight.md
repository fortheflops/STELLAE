# Elyse's Dressing

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Eight | ✍️ **Attribution:** Elyse | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A quick and tangy homemade dressing combining balsamic vinegar, grated ginger, and mustard. Perfect for elevating fresh green salads with zesty flavor.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5 mins | None | None | 4 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Whisk
* Small bowl

---
## Ingredients

### Dressing Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Mustard]] | Standard |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Ginger]] | Grated |
| 2 tablespoons | [[Balsamic Vinegar]] | None |
| 2 tablespoons | [[Olive Oil]] | None |
| To taste | [[Salt & Pepper]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing
1. Whisk together **1 teaspoon mustard**, **1 teaspoon grated ginger**, **2 tablespoons balsamic vinegar**, **2 tablespoons olive oil**, and **salt & pepper to taste** until fully emulsified.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in a sealed glass jar in the refrigerator for up to 5 days; shake well before serving.
