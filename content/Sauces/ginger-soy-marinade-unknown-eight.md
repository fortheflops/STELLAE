# Ginger Soy Marinade

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Eight | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A flavorful and versatile Asian-inspired marinade featuring soy sauce, sesame oil, and honey. Excellent for marinating kababs and proteins prior to cooking.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5 mins | None | 1 hour | 4 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Whisk

---
## Ingredients

### Marinade Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 cup | [[Soy sauce]] | None |
| 1/2 cup | [[White wine or rice vinegar]] | None |
| 3 Tbsp | [[Sesame oil]] | None |
| 3 Tbsp | [[Honey]] | None |
| 2 cloves | [[Garlic]] | Minced |
| 1 Tbsp | [[Fresh grated ginger]] | Grated |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing and Resting
1. Combine **1/2 cup soy sauce**, **1/2 cup white wine or rice vinegar**, **3 Tbsp sesame oil**, **3 Tbsp honey**, **2 cloves garlic**, and **1 Tbsp fresh grated ginger** in a bowl and blend well.
2. Refrigerate for at least an hour before using to allow the flavors to meld fully.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the mixed marinade in a sealed jar in the refrigerator for up to 5 days before using.
