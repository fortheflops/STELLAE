# Barbecue Sauce

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Gina Langle | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A rich and tangy homemade barbecue sauce packed with robust spices, Worcestershire, and a splash of beer. Perfect for slow-roasting or glazing your favorite meats until sticky and caramelized.*

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 Ounce | [[Tomato Paste]] | Unsalted |
| 4 Tablespoons | [[Worcestershire Sauce]] | Liquid |
| 1/4 Teaspoon | [[Garlic Powder]] | Fine powder |
| 1 Teaspoon | [[Tabasco]] | Hot sauce |

| 1/2 Cup | [[Ketchup]] | Base tomato |
| As needed | [[Beer]] | To fill can |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Combine and Bake
1. Combine the **1 ounce tomato paste**, **4 tablespoons worcestershire**, **1/4 teaspoon garlic powder**, **1 teaspoon tobasco**, **3 tablespoons dry mustard**, **1/2 teaspoon cayenne**, **1/2 teaspoon chili powder**, **3 tablespoons brown sugar**, **1 medium onion**, **2 tablespoons vinegar**, **1 tablespoon hickory sauce**, **2 chicken bouillon cubes**, and **1/2 cup ketchup** in a mixing bowl.
2. Fill the can with **beer** and incorporate into the mixture.
3. Optional: Bake at **350 degrees Fahrenheit** for **4-5 hours**, or at **450-475 degrees Fahrenheit** for **3-4 hours**, or at **300 degrees Fahrenheit** for **6 hours** until thick, bubbly, and fragrant.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the prepared barbecue sauce in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 2 weeks.
