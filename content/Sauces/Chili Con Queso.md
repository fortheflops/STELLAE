# Chili Con Queso

> 📜 **Collection:** LouiseCollection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A classic, creamy Chili Con Queso featuring melted cheese and sautéed aromatics. Perfect for parties and gatherings when served warm with crunchy tortilla chips.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 15 mins | None | 8–10 Servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Chef's knife
* Cutting board
* Skillet
* Double boiler
* Whisk

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 medium | [[Onion]] | finely chopped |
| 2 cloves | [[Garlic]] | minced |
| 1/2 cup | [[Chili]] | green chilies or jalapeños, finely chopped |
| 1 tbsp | [[Oil]] | for sautéing |
| 2 pounds | [[Velveeta]] | cubed |
| 1 can | [[Evaporated Milk]] | standard size |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare Aromatics
1. Finely chop the **onion**, **garlic**, and **chili**.
2. Heat the **oil** in a skillet over medium heat and sauté the **onion**, **garlic**, and **chili** until softened and fragrant.

### Step 2: Melt the Cheese
3. In a double boiler or heavy-bottomed saucepan, combine the **2 pounds Velveeta** and **1 can evaporated milk**.
4. Stir continuously over low heat until the **Velveeta** is completely melted and the mixture is smooth and velvety.

### Step 3: Combine and Serve
5. Fold the sautéed **onion**, **garlic**, and **chili** mixture into the cheese base.
6. Serve warm immediately with tortilla chips.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days; reheat gently on the stovetop, adding a splash of milk if the consistency becomes too thick.
