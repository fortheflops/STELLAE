# Romesco Sauce

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Eight | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A vibrant and flavorful Spanish Romesco sauce made with toasted almonds, roasted red peppers, and garlic. Perfect for serving alongside tapas, meatballs, or Tortilla Española.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 0 mins | None | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Food processor

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3/4 cup | [[Almonds]] | Toasted |
| 10 cloves | [[Garlic]] | Whole |
| 1/2 cup | [[Olive Oil]] | Quality extra virgin |
| 1/4 cup | [[Red Wine Vinegar]] | Standard |
| 2-3 | [[Dried Pequin Chiles or Chiles de Arbol]] | Dried |
| 2 cups | [[Roasted Red Bell Peppers]] | Peeled and chopped |
| To taste | [[Salt]] | Fine |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Blending
1. Combine the **3/4 cup toasted almonds**, **10 cloves garlic**, **1/2 cup olive oil**, and **1/4 cup red wine vinegar** in a food processor.
2. Add the **2-3 dried pequin chiles or chiles de arbol** into the processor bowl.
3. Blend the mixture until smooth.
4. Add the **2 cups roasted red bell peppers** and pulse a bit until the sauce is just a bit chunky.
5. Season with **salt to taste** and pulse briefly to combine until fully incorporated and textured.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 5 days, or freeze for up to 1 month.
