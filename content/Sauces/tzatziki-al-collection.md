# Tzatziki

> *A refreshing, traditional Greek tzatziki made with strained yogurt, grated cucumber, garlic, and fresh herbs. Perfect as a chilled starter, dip, or light cold soup.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Combine and Chill
1. In a medium mixing bowl, combine the **3 cups plain yogurt**, **2 cloves garlic** (peeled, crushed, and minced), **1 medium cucumber** (peeled and grated), **1 generous tbsp olive oil**, **juice of 1/2 lemon** (strained), and **salt to taste**. Do not add the herbs yet.
2. Mix together thoroughly until all ingredients are evenly distributed.
3. Seal the bowl tightly with plastic wrap and refrigerate for **8 hours** or overnight to allow the garlic and cucumber flavors to fully infuse the yogurt.

### Step 2: Garnish and Serve
4. Before serving, give the chilled mixture a thorough stir.
5. Spoon the mixture into chilled soup cups or serving bowls.
6. Sprinkle generously with chopped **fresh mint** or **dill** (which must be fresh and not dried) just before serving for a bright, aromatic finish.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This dish requires at least 8 hours of chilling to develop its signature flavor. Store sealed in the refrigerator for up to 3 days.
