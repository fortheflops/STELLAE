# Creamy Garlic Dressing

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Seven | ✍️ **Attribution:** Judy Beale | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A rich and velvety homemade dressing featuring a perfect balance of garlic and light cream. This versatile sauce is ideal for elevating fresh garden salads or as a flavorful dip.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5 mins | 0 mins | None | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Blender

---
## Ingredients

### Dressing Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/4 cup | [[Lemon Juice]] | freshly squeezed |
| 3/4 cup | [[Salad Oil]] | neutral oil |
| 2/3 cup | [[Light Cream]] | chilled |
| 2 cloves | [[Garlic]] | peeled |
| 1/2 tsp | [[Sugar]] | granulated |
| 1/4 tsp | [[White Pepper]] | ground |
| 1/2 tsp | [[Paprika]] | ground |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Blending
1. Combine the **1/4 cup lemon juice**, **3/4 cup salad oil**, **2/3 cup light cream**, **2 cloves garlic**, **1/2 tsp sugar**, **1/4 tsp white pepper**, and **1/2 tsp paprika** in a blender.
2. Whip the mixture on high speed until the texture is smooth and creamy.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days; whisk or shake before serving if separation occurs.
