## Recipe
### Slurry/Paste
| Measurements        | Ingredients       |
| :------------------ | :---------------- |
| 2 Tbsp              | Cornstarch        |
| $\frac{1}{2}$ Cup  | Chicken Broth     |
| 2 Tbsp              | Soy Sauce         |
### Main Sauce
| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
| 2 Tbsp                 | Butter                              |
| 1 Cup                  | Chicken Broth                       |
| $\frac{3}{4}$ to 1 Cup | Diced Green Pepper (Optional)       |
| 6 slices               | Diced Canned Pineapple              |
| $\frac{1}{2}$ Cup     | Vinegar                             |
| $\frac{3}{4}$ Cup     | Pineapple Juice *(likely from can)* |
| $\frac{1}{2}$ Cup     | Sugar                               |
| $\frac{1}{2}$ tsp     | Salt                                |
| $\frac{1}{4}$ tsp     | Ginger *(likely Ground)* |
___
### Instructions
1.  Have ready a paste of: 2 T Cornstarch, 1/2 C Chicken broth, 2 T soy sauce.
2.  Melt in a heavy saucepan: 2 T butter.
3.  Add: 1 C Chicken broth, 3/4 to 1 C diced green pepper (optional), 6 slices diced canned pineapple.
4.  Cover & simmer for 5 minutes.
5.  Add the cornstarch paste & the following ingredients to the peppers + pineapple: 1/2 cup vinegar, 3/4 cup pineapple juice, 1/2 cup sugar, 1/2 tsp salt, 1/4 tsp ginger.
6.  Simmer, stirring constantly until the mixture thickens.
