## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 2/3 cup                    | Firmly packed [[Brown Sugar]]   |
| 2/3 cup                    | [[Pineapple Juice]] (fresh if possible)|
| 1/4 cup                    | [[White Vinegar]]               |
| 1 teaspoon                 | [[Catsup]] (Ketchup)            |
| 1 teaspoon plus            | [[Soy Sauce]]                   |
| 1 dash                     | [[Garlic Salt]] (optional)      |
| 2 tablespoons              | [[Cornstarch]]                  |
| 1/4 cup                    | [[Water]]                       |
___
## Instructions
1. In a saucepan, mix the brown sugar, pineapple juice, white vinegar, catsup, soy sauce, and salt.
2. Bring the mixture to a boil.
3. In a small bowl, make a slurry by diluting the cornstarch with water.
4. Thicken the boiling sauce with the cornstarch slurry to your desired consistency.
5. Cook for one more minute after adding the cornstarch.
