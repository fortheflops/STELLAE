# Turkey Honey Dijon Gravy

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty One | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *Elevate your holiday roast with this savory Turkey Honey Dijon Gravy, combining pan drippings, tangy mustard, and a touch of sweetness. It cooks up quickly on the stovetop into a rich, bubbling sauce that yields 8 to 10 servings.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5 mins | 10 mins | None | 8-10 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Saucepan
* Whisk

---
## Ingredients

### Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/4 cup | [[Pan drippings from turkey]] | From roasted turkey |
| 1/4 cup | [[Flour]] | None |
| 1 1/2 cups | [[Chix broth]] | Combined with remaining drippings to equal 2 cups total liquid |
| 2 tsp | [[Dijon]] | Dijon mustard |
| 2 tsp | [[Honey]] | None |
| Dash | [[S&P]] | Salt and pepper to taste |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and Cooking
1. Measure **1/4 c drippings** into saucepan, stir in **1/4 c flour**.
2. Add enough broth to remaining drippings to equal **2 c**.
3. Add all at once to the flour mixture. Cook, stir over medium heat til thickened & bubbly.
4. Stir in **2 t Dijon** & **2 t honey**. Cook, stir **1 min**.
5. Season to taste w/**Dash S&P**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Gravy can be made ahead and reheated gently on the stovetop, whisking in a splash of broth if it thickens too much.
