# Yum-Yum Sauce

| 1/2 t. | [[Salt]] | None |
| 1/2 c. | [[Coffee]] | Prepared |
| 3 tbsp | [[Butter]] | None |
| 1/2 tsp | [[Vanilla]] | Extract |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Cooking
1. Combine **1/2 c. honey**, **1/2 c. sugar**, **1/2 t. salt**, and **1/2 c. coffee** in a saucepan.
2. Boil, stirring constantly, for **4 minutes**.

### Step 2: Finishing
3. Remove from heat, add **3 tbsp butter** and **1/2 tsp vanilla**, and mix well.
4. Makes **1 c. sauce**. Serve hot or cold.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 1 week; reheat gently before serving if desired warm.
