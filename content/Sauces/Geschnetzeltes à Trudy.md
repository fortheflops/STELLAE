# Geschnetzeltes à Trudy

> 📜 **Collection:** LOC | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A savory and comforting Swiss-inspired beef dish featuring tender cubed beef simmered in a rich brown gravy with onions and a touch of sour cream. Perfect for serving over a warm bed of noodles.*

---

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 Package | [[Brown Gravy Mix]] | e.g., Maggi, Knorr |
| 1 Pound | [[Beef]] | cut in small cubes, fat omitted |
| A Little | [[Crisco]] | or other shortening/oil |
| As Needed | [[Onion]] | chopped or sliced |
| To Taste | [[Pepper]] | none |
| To Taste | [[Paprika]] | none |
| 1 Tablespoon | [[Sour Cream]] | none |
| As Needed | [[Noodles]] | for serving |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Gravy and Meat
1. Prepare the **1 package brown gravy mix** according to the package directions.
2. Cut the **1 pound beef** into small cubes, omitting any fat.
3. In a pan, braise the **1 pound beef** and **onion** with a little **Crisco** (or other fat) until the beef is browned and fragrant.

### Step 2: Simmer and Finish
4. Pour the prepared gravy mix over the browned meat.
5. Bring the mixture to a boil.
6. Add **pepper** and **paprika** to taste.
7. Stir in the **1 tablespoon sour cream**.
8. Cook until the beef is tender and the sauce has reduced slightly.
9. Serve hot over **noodles**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftover beef and gravy sealed in the refrigerator for up to 3 days; reheat gently before serving over freshly cooked noodles.
