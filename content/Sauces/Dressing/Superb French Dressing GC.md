## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| One Cup                          | [[Catsup]] (or 8oz Tomato Sauce + Lemon Juice) |
| $\frac{1}{4}$ Cup                | [[Sugar]]                                 |
| $\frac{1}{2}$ Cup                | [[Vinegar]] (Herb flavored is nice)       |
| One Teaspoon                     | [[Salt]]                                  |
| One Small                        | [[Onion]], Quartered                      |
| $\frac{1}{2}$ Teaspoon           | [[Celery Seed]] (or Celery Salt)          |
| $\frac{1}{4}$ Teaspoon           | [[Paprika]]                               |
| $\frac{3}{4}$ Cup                | [[Salad Oil]], [[Olive Oil]], or a half-and-half mixture of both |
| Two or Three                     | Green [[Onions]]                          |
| One Clove                        | [[Garlic]]                                |
___
## Instructions
1.  Place the catsup (or tomato sauce/lemon juice), sugar, vinegar, salt, quartered small onion, celery seed, paprika, and salad/olive oil in a blender.
2.  Blend for about 30 seconds.
3.  Add two or three green onions and one clove of garlic.
4.  Blend again briefly just to combine.
5.  Store in a covered container in the refrigerator.
