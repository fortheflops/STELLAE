## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
| 1 Cup                 | Oil                         |
| 1/4 Cup               | Vinegar                 |
| 1/2 Cup                | Sugar                       |
| 1/4 Medium             | Onion, Cut Fine                |
| 1 Teaspoon                 | Salt              |
|1 Tablespoon| Dry Mustard|
|1 Tablespoon| Celery Seed|
___
### Instructions
1. Combine Oil, Vinegar, Sugar, Onion, Salt, Dry Mustard, and Celery seed.
2. Shake Well
3. Serve over spinach, orange sections, and Grapefruit
___

>[!note] 
>Makes one deep dish pie!
