### Instructions
1.  Combine a little sesame seed oil with a larger portion of safflower oil to create your oil base.
2.  In a bowl or jar, mix the oil base with cider vinegar, water, a little honey, and a little tamari.
3.  Add sesame seeds.
4.  Whisk or shake well to combine all ingredients.
5.  Taste and adjust ingredients as needed.
