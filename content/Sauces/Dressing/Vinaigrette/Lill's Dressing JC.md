## Recipe
| Measurements      | Ingredients                   |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 1/3 to 1/2 cup    | [[Catsup]] (Ketchup)            |
| 3 tablespoons     | [[Sugar]]                       |
| 1/4 cup           | [[Vinegar]] (estimated)           |
| 1/2 cup           | [[Oil]] (estimated)               |
| A small dash      | [[Salt]] and [[Pepper]]           |
___
## Instructions
1.  In a small bowl or a jar with a tight-fitting lid, combine the sugar, catsup, salt, pepper, vinegar, and oil.
2.  Whisk or shake vigorously until the sugar is dissolved and the dressing is well combined.
3.  Taste and adjust seasonings or the oil-to-vinegar ratio as desired.
4.  Serve over salad.
___

>[!Note]
>The original recipe does not specify the amounts for vinegar and oil, nor does it provide mixing instructions. A standard vinaigrette ratio and basic steps have been provided for a complete recipe. The card notes this dressing "can be used with Artichoke hearts or on French endive."
