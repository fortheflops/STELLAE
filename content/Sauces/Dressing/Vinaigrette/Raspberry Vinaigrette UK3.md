## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients              |
| :------------------ | :----------------------- |
| 1 Tbsp              | Raspberry Preserves      |
| $\frac{1}{2}$ tsp  | Salt                     |
| $\frac{1}{2}$ tsp  | Black Pepper             |
| $\frac{1}{4}$ tsp  | Minced Garlic            |
| $\frac{1}{4}$ Cup  | Raspberry Vinegar        |
| $\frac{1}{2}$ Cup  | Canola Oil (or similar)  |
___
### Instructions
1.  In a blender, combine the raspberry preserves, salt, black pepper, minced garlic, and raspberry vinegar. **Do not add the oil yet.**
2.  Blend these ingredients until well combined and smooth.
3.  Turn the blender on a low speed. While the machine is running, slowly drizzle the $\frac{1}{2}$ cup of canola oil through the opening in the lid until all the oil is added and the vinaigrette is emulsified and thoroughly blended.
4.  Transfer to a jar or container for storage. Keep refrigerated. Shake well before using.
___

>[!Note]
>- (Yields 2 1/2 Cups)
>- This vinaigrette pairs well with a salad made with spinach, red onion, fresh strawberries, and toasted almonds.
