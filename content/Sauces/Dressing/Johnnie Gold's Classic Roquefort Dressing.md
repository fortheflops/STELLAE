## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- |

| 1/2 tsp | [[Worcestershire Sauce]] |
| 1/2 cup | [[Sour Cream]] or Light [[Cream]] |
| 1/2 cup | [[Mayonnaise]] |
___
### Instructions
1.  In a medium bowl, start with the softened cream cheese.
2.  Add the dry ingredients (salt, garlic powder, dry mustard) and the Worcestershire sauce.
3.  Then add the sour cream and mayonnaise. Mix well.
4.  Crumble in the Roquefort or Blue Cheese and stir to combine.
5.  Chill before serving.
