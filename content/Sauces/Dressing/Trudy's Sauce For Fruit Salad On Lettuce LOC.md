### Instructions
1.  Bring milk to a boil.
2.  Mix 2 teaspoons cornstarch with cold milk or fruit juice.
3.  Add cornstarch mixture to boiling milk.
4.  Stir in marshmallow creme, orange juice, honey, and 1 or 2 egg yolks.
5.  Serve over fruit salad on lettuce.
