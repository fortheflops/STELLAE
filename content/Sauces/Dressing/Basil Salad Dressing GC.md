## Instructions
1.  In a jar with a tight-fitting lid, combine all the ingredients: one and a half cups finely chopped fresh basil, three ounces grated Parmesan cheese, $\frac{1}{4}$ cup olive oil, $\frac{1}{2}$ cup salad oil, $\frac{1}{4}$ cup ground pine nuts, $\frac{1}{3}$ cup crumbled feta cheese, and two crushed garlic cloves.
2.  Shake all together until well combined.
3.  Pour over a salad. (The card suggests a salad with tomatoes, cashews, celery, green onions, and lettuce).
