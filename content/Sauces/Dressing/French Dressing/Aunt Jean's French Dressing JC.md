## Author
Aunt Jean
___
## Recipe
| Measurements      | Ingredients                   |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 can (10.75-oz)  | [[Campbell's Tomato Soup]]      |
| 1 cup             | [[Vinegar]]                     |
| 1 cup             | [[Mazola Oil]] or other vegetable oil |
| 1/2 cup           | [[Sugar]]                       |
| 1 tablespoon      | Prepared [[Mustard]]            |
| 1 teaspoon        | [[Worcestershire Sauce]]        |
| 1/2 teaspoon      | [[Salt]]                        |
| 1 whole           | [[Onion]], peeled                 |
___
## Instructions
1.  In a blender or a mixing bowl, combine the tomato soup, sugar, vinegar, mustard, salt, and Worcestershire sauce.
2.  Beat or blend the mixture well.
3.  With the blender or mixer running on low, add the oil in a very slow, thin stream ("a drop at a time") until it is all incorporated and the dressing is thick and emulsified.
4.  Pour the dressing into a mason jar and add the whole, peeled onion for flavor.
5.  Seal and store in the refrigerator.
___

>[!Note]
>The recipe claims the dressing "Keeps indefinitely in refrigerator." The whole onion is added for flavor and can be removed before serving.
