# Fudgy Poured Chocolate Icing

> 📜 **Collection:** General Archive | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A classic, rich cooked chocolate icing designed to be poured over hot cake for a glossy, fudge-like finish. This traditional recipe is the perfect companion for Texas Sheet Cake.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5 mins | 5 mins | None | Frosting for one 9x13 inch cake |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* medium saucepan
* large mixing bowl
* electric mixer

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 stick | [[Oleo]] or [[Butter]] | softened |
| 4 Tbsp | [[Cocoa]] | unsweetened |
| 6 Tbsp | [[Canned milk]] | evaporated |
| 1 lb | [[Powdered sugar]] | sifted |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Pecans]] and/or [[Coconut]] | chopped |
| 1 tsp | [[Vanilla]] | extract |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Base
1. In a large mixing bowl, combine the **1 lb powdered sugar**, **1/2 cup pecans and/or coconut**, and **1 tsp vanilla**. Set aside.

### Step 2: Boil the Chocolate
2. In a medium saucepan, combine the **4 Tbsp cocoa**, **1 stick oleo or butter**, and **6 Tbsp canned milk**.
3. Bring the mixture to a rolling boil over medium heat, stirring constantly.

### Step 3: Combine and Finish
4. Immediately pour the boiling liquid over the **powdered sugar mixture** in the bowl.
5. Beat with an electric mixer until the icing is smooth and has a glossy sheen.
6. Pour the hot icing over the hot cake and spread quickly and evenly until it sets into a fudge-like layer.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This icing must be prepared and poured while both the icing and the cake are hot; it is not suitable for storage before application.
