# Hot Chocolate Sauce

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A simple and rich homemade hot chocolate sauce perfect for drizzling over ice cream or desserts. This quick recipe uses unsweetened chocolate and corn syrup for a smooth, glossy finish.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5 mins | 5 mins | None | 1 cup |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Saucepan
* Whisk

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 | [[Unsweetened Chocolate]] | 1-oz squares |
| 1 cup | [[Corn Syrup]] | Light or dark |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Vanilla Extract]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Preparation
1. Melt the **2 1-oz squares unsweetened chocolate** in a saucepan over low heat.
2. Add the **1 cup corn syrup** and stir until the mixture is well blended and smooth.
3. Stir in the **1/4 tsp vanilla extract**, heat through until warm, and serve immediately.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator and reheat gently on the stove or in the microwave before serving.
