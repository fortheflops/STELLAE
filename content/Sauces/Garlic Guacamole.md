# Garlic Guacamole

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A zesty and aromatic garlic-infused guacamole perfect for dipping or topping. This simple, fresh recipe balances creamy avocado with a spicy kick of green chili.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 0 mins | None | 4 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Fork or potato masher
* Chef's knife

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 | [[Avocado]] | Ripe, pitted and peeled |
| 2 cloves | [[Garlic]] | Minced |
| 1/4 cup | [[Onion]] | Finely chopped |
| 1 Tablespoon | [[Tomato]] | Diced |
| 1 Tablespoon | [[Lemon Juice]] | Freshly squeezed |
| 1/2 teaspoon | [[Salt]] | To taste |
| 1/4 teaspoon | [[Pepper]] | Freshly cracked |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Green Chili]] or [[Hot Sauce]] | Chopped |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Base
1. In a medium bowl, mash the **2 ripe avocados** with a fork until you reach your desired consistency, leaving some texture.

### Step 2: Combine Ingredients
2. Fold in the **2 cloves minced garlic**, **1/4 cup chopped onion**, **1 tablespoon diced tomato**, **1 tablespoon lemon juice**, **1/2 teaspoon salt**, and **1/4 teaspoon pepper** until well incorporated.
3. Stir in the **1 teaspoon chopped green chili or hot sauce** until evenly distributed and fragrant.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Press plastic wrap directly onto the surface of the guacamole to prevent oxidation and store in the refrigerator for up to 24 hours.
