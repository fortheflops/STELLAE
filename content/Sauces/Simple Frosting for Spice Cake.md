# Simple Frosting for Spice Cake

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail's Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A quick and easy classic frosting or glaze perfect for drizzling over a spice cake. This simple four-ingredient recipe comes together in minutes for a smooth, sweet finish.*

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Small mixing bowl
* Whisk or spatula

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 cup | [[Powdered Sugar]] | sifted |
| 1 tablespoon | [[Butter]] | softened |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Vanilla Extract]] | pure |
| 1-2 tablespoons | [[Canned Milk]] or [[Regular Milk]] | added gradually |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Combine and Mix
1. In a small bowl, combine the **1 cup powdered sugar**, **1 tablespoon butter**, and **1 teaspoon vanilla extract**.
2. Gradually add the **1-2 tablespoons canned milk or regular milk** one teaspoon at a time, stirring well after each addition until the frosting reaches your desired spreading or drizzling consistency.
3. Beat the mixture until smooth and glossy, then use immediately on a completely cooled cake.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This frosting is best used immediately; however, it can be stored in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days and re-whipped before use.
