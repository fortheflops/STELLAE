# Cream Gravy

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Susan | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A classic, easy-to-make cream gravy made with pan drippings and milk. Perfect for serving over biscuits, potatoes, or meat dishes.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 mins | 8 mins | None | 4 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Skillet
* Whisk

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 tbsp. | [[Fat]] | Left in skillet |
| 1 tbsp. | [[Flour]] | Unshifted |
| 1 cup | [[Milk]] | At room temperature |
| To taste | [[Salt]] | Optional |
| To taste | [[Pepper]] | Freshly ground recommended |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Make the Roux and Simmer
1. Leave **2 tbsp. fat** in skillet over medium-low heat.
2. Add **1 tbsp. flour** to the skillet and cook until brown and bubbly.
3. Gradually add **1 cup milk**, followed by **salt** and **pepper** to taste.
4. Stir constantly over medium-low heat until the gravy is smoothly thickened and bubbling gently.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Best served immediately, but leftovers can be stored in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 2 days and reheated with a splash of milk.
