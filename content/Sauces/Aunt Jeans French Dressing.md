# Aunt Jean's French Dressing

> 📜 **Collection:** Jill Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Aunt Jean | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A classic, tangy French dressing featuring a rich tomato soup base emulsified with vegetable oil. This vintage recipe is perfectly balanced with mustard and Worcestershire for a timeless salad accompaniment.*

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 can (10.75-oz) | [[Campbell's Tomato Soup]] | undiluted |
| 1 cup | [[Vinegar]] | standard |
| 1 cup | [[Mazola Oil]] | or vegetable oil |
| 1/2 cup | [[Sugar]] | granulated |
| 1 tablespoon | [[Mustard]] | prepared |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Worcestershire Sauce]] | none |
| 1/2 teaspoon | [[Salt]] | none |
| 1 whole | [[Onion]] | peeled |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Base Preparation
1. In a blender or mixing bowl, combine the **1 can (10.75-oz) Campbell's Tomato Soup**, **1/2 cup sugar**, **1 cup vinegar**, **1 tablespoon prepared mustard**, **1/2 teaspoon salt**, and **1 teaspoon Worcestershire sauce**.
2. Beat or blend the mixture until smooth and well combined.

### Step 2: Emulsification
3. With the blender or mixer running on low, slowly drizzle in the **1 cup Mazola oil** in a very thin, steady stream.
4. Continue blending until the dressing is thick, creamy, and fully emulsified.

### Step 3: Storage
5. Pour the dressing into a mason jar and add the **1 whole peeled onion** to infuse flavor.
6. Seal the jar and refrigerate until chilled and ready to serve.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This dressing keeps indefinitely in the refrigerator; remove the whole onion before serving if desired.
