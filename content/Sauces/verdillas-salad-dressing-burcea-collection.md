# Verdilla's Salad Dressing

## Instructions

### Step 1: Boil the Base
1. In a saucepan, combine the **1 tbsp cornstarch**, **4 tbsp sugar**, **1/2 cup dry mustard**, **1/2 tsp salt**, and **1/2 cup milk**.
2. Bring the mixture to a boil over medium heat, whisking constantly to prevent lumps, until it is smooth and slightly thickened.

### Step 2: Add the Acid
3. In a separate small bowl, combine the **1/4 cup vinegar** and **1/4 cup water**.
4. Bring this acid mixture to a boil, then add it to the thickened milk base, stirring well to combine.

### Step 3: Temper and Finish
5. Slowly temper and add the **2 beaten eggs** into the warm mixture, whisking vigorously to avoid scrambling the eggs.
6. Cook the mixture gently over low heat until it thickens to a creamy, coat-the-back-of-a-spoon consistency.
7. Remove from heat and stir in the **1 tbsp butter** until completely melted and incorporated.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Allow the dressing to cool completely before transferring to an airtight jar. Store in the refrigerator for up to 2 weeks.
