## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
| 1 Large Carton        | Sour Cream (16 Ounce)            |
| 1 Package            | Lipton Dry Onion Soup             |
| Juice of 1           | Lemon                             |
| Dash                  | Worcestershire Sauce              |
| 1 6 Ounce Can        | Deveined Shrimp (mashed with fork) |
| 1 6 Ounce Can        | Crab Meat                         |
___
### Instructions
1. Mix together sour cream, Lipton dry onion soup, lemon juice, and Worcestershire sauce.
2. Add the mashed shrimp and crab meat to the mixture.
3. Mix Thoroughly
___

>[!note] 
>Will keep in refrigerator about 4 days.
