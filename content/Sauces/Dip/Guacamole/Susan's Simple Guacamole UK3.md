## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
|3-5|[[Very Ripe Avocados]]|
|1/4 t.|[[Minced Garlic]]|
|1/4|[[Small Onion (Chopped)]]|
|1/2 - 3/4 t.|[[Salt]]|
|1-2|[[Tomatoes (Cut into Pieces)]]|
|1 T.|[[Lemon Juice]]|
|1/2 t.|[[Red Pepper]]|
___
### Instructions
1. Peel and mash avocados with lemon juice.
2. Add onion, garlic, tomatoes, salt, and red pepper.
3. Mix together with a fork.
4. Be sure to store salad with the avocado seed.
