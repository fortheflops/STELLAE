## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
| 1 Package             | Knorr Dry Vegetable Soup Mix                                                     |
| 1 1/2 Cup             | Sour Cream                                                                     |
| 1 Cup                 | Mayonnaise (Not Miracle Whip)                                                |
| 1 Package (10 Ounce)  | Chopped Spinach (thawed and squeezed dry)                                        |
| 3                     | Green Onions, Chopped Fine                                                     |
| 1 Loaf                | Rye Bread or King Hawaiian Bread (unsliced)                                   |
___
### Instructions
1. Mix by hand all of the above ingredients (except bread).
2. Chill.
3. Before serving, cut the center out of the bread and slice the center piece into bite-size squares.
4. Place spinach dip in the cut-out center of the bread.
5. To serve, dip bite-size bread pieces into dip.
