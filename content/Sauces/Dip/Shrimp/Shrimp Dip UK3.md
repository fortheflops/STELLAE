## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
| 1 8 Ounce            | Cream Cheese (soften)           |
| 1/2 Pint               | Sour Cream (cream)          |
| 1 Can                 | Cleaned Shrimp, Blue Plate |
| 3 or 4               | Green Onions              |
| 1 Tablespoon          | Worcestershire Sauce     |
| 1 1/2 Teaspoon         | Garlic Salt                |
___
### Instructions
1. Soften cream cheese.
2. Combine softened cream cheese, sour cream, cleaned shrimp, green onions, Worcestershire sauce, and garlic salt in a bowl.
3. Mix thoroughly until all ingredients are well combined.
4. Optional: Chill in the refrigerator for at least 30 minutes to allow flavors to meld.
5. Serve
