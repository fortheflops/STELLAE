# Lemon Nut Butter Sauce

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A simple and savory lemon-infused nut butter sauce perfect for drizzling over steamed vegetables. This quick recipe adds a delightful crunch and brightness to your favorite greens.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 mins | 3 mins | None | 4 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Saucepan

---
## Ingredients

### Sauce Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/4 cup | [[Butter]] | melted |
| 1/4 cup | [[Peanuts]] | chopped |
| 4 tsp | [[Lemon Juice]] | freshly squeezed |

---

## Instructions

### Preparation
1. Melt the **1/4 cup butter** in a saucepan over medium heat.
2. Add the **1/4 cup chopped peanuts** and cook for 1 minute until fragrant.
3. Remove from heat and stir in the **4 tsp lemon juice**.
4. Serve immediately over hot cooked broccoli until the sauce coats the vegetables evenly.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Best served immediately; reheating may cause the butter to separate.
