# Cheese Pasta Sauce

> 📜 **Collection:** Patsy Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A rich and creamy traditional cheese pasta sauce made with fresh egg yolk, butter, cream, and grated parmesan cheese. This velvety vintage sauce coats pasta perfectly for a comforting weeknight meal.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5 mins | 10 mins | None | 4 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Fork
* Saucepan
* Mixing bowl

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | [[Egg Yolk]] | Beaten |
| ¼ lb | [[Butter]] | Melted |
| ⅔ Cup | [[Cream]] | Room temperature |
| ½ Cup | Grated [[Parmesan Cheese]] | Divided |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Base
1. Beat **1 egg yolk** with a fork until smooth.
2. Add the beaten **egg yolk** to **⅔ cup cream** and mix well.

### Step 2: Combine and Melt
3. Melt **¼ lb butter** gently in a pan over low heat.
4. Pour the melted **¼ lb butter**, the **egg/cream mixture**, and **½** of the grated **parmasean cheese** over freshly cooked hot pasta, stirring constantly until fully incorporated and creamy.

### Step 3: Finish the Sauce
5. Add the remaining grated **parmasean cheese** slowly while continuing to stir until the sauce is velvety, rich, and coats the pasta completely.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Best served immediately upon preparation to maintain the ideal creamy texture and prevent separation.
