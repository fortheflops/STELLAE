# Teriyaki Sauce

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Rick & Joy Gay | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A classic, versatile homemade teriyaki sauce perfect for marinating meats. This simple blend of brown sugar, soy sauce, and aromatics delivers a deep, savory-sweet flavor profile.*

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 Cup | [[Brown Sugar]] | Packed |
| 1 Cup | [[Soy Sauce]] | Standard |
| 1 Cup | [[Water]] | Room temperature |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Oil]] | Vegetable |
| 1 Teaspoon | [[Lemon Juice]] | Freshly squeezed |
| 1/2 Teaspoon | [[Pepper]] | Black, ground |
| 1/2 Teaspoon | [[Ginger]] | Ground |
| 1/2 Teaspoon | [[Garlic Salt]] | Fine |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation
1. In a mixing bowl, combine the **1 cup brown sugar**, **1 cup soy sauce**, **1 cup water**, **1/2 cup vegetable oil**, **1 teaspoon lemon juice**, **1/2 teaspoon pepper**, **1/2 teaspoon ground ginger**, and **1/2 teaspoon garlic salt**.
2. Whisk the mixture vigorously until the **brown sugar** is completely dissolved and the sauce appears uniform.

### Step 2: Marinating
3. Use the prepared sauce as a marinade for chicken, steak, or pork.
4. For the best flavor penetration and tenderization, marinate your protein for at least a full day or overnight in the refrigerator.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight glass jar in the refrigerator for up to 2 weeks.
