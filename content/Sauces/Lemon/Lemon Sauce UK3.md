## Recipe

| Measurements   | Ingredients            |
| :------------- | ---------------------- |
| 3 Tablespoon          | Cornstarch                   |
| 1 Cup                 | Sugar                       |
| 2 Tablespoon          | Grated Lemon Rind            |
| 1/4 Cup               | Lemon Juice                 |
| 1/4 Cup               | Margarine                   |
| 2 Cups                | Water                       |
| 1/4 Teaspoon         | Salt                        |
___
### Instructions
1. Combine cornstarch, sugar, lemon peel, and salt in a 1-quart saucepan.
2. Add water slowly.
3. Stir over medium heat until thickened.
4. Remove from heat, add lemon juice and margarine.
___

>[!Note]
>Makes 3 cups. Serve on plain cake, gingerbread, and fruit cobbler.
