# Bar-B-Q Sauce

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Rick Gay | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A classic homemade bar-B-q sauce crafted by Rick Gay, featuring a rich blend of onions, vinegar, and spices simmered to perfection. This vintage recipe delivers a tangy, smoky flavor that elevates any grilled dish.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Combine Ingredients
1. Mix all ingredients together in a large saucepan: **two large diced onions**, **two cups vinegar**, **one tablespoon dry mustard**, **3/4 teaspoon salt**, **one teaspoon garlic salt**, **one tablespoon Worcestershire sauce**, **1 1/2 teaspoons pepper**, **1 1/8 cups brown sugar**, **one tablespoon liquid smoke**, **two cups catsup**, and **1/4 teaspoon red pepper** (or **1 tsp chili powder**).

### Step 2: Simmer and Thicken
2. Bring the mixture to a rolling boil over medium heat.
3. Reduce heat and simmer, without a lid, for an hour or so, until the sauce is slightly thickened and fragrant.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the cooled sauce in a sealed glass jar in the refrigerator for up to 3 weeks.
