### Instructions
1. Leave 2 tbsp. fat in skillet.
2. Add 1 tbsp. flour.
3. Cook until brown and bubbly.
4. Add 1 cup milk.
5. Add salt and pepper to taste.
6. Stir constantly until the gravy is thickened.
7. Serves 4.
8. Heat: Medium-Low.
9. Time: 10 min.
