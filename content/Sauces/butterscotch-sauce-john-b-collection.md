# Butterscotch Sauce

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A rich and classic homemade butterscotch sauce made with brown sugar, corn syrup, butter, and cream. Perfect for drizzling over ice cream and desserts.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5 mins | 15 mins | None | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Saucepan
* Measuring cups
* Measuring spoons
* Wooden spoon

---
## Ingredients

### Sauce Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 1/4 cups | [[Brown Sugar]] | Packed |
| 3 tbsp | [[Corn Syrup]] | Light or dark |
| 4 tbsp | [[Butter]] | Unsalted |
| 3/4 cup | [[Thin Cream]] | Or light cream |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Boiling and Combining
1. Put **4 tbsp butter**, **3 tbsp corn syrup**, and **1 1/4 cups brown sugar** in a pan.
2. Boil until it forms a soft ball when dropped into cold water, then remove from heat.
3. Add **3/4 cup thin cream** and stir until smooth and thoroughly combined.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in a tightly sealed glass jar in the refrigerator for up to 2 weeks. Gently reheat before serving.
