## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Paste and Vegetables
1. Have ready a paste by combining **2 Tbsp cornstarch**, **1/2 cup chicken broth**, and **2 Tbsp soy sauce**.
2. Melt in a heavy saucepan **2 Tbsp butter**.
3. Add **1 cup chicken broth**, **3/4 to 1 cup diced green pepper (optional)**, and **6 slices diced canned pineapple**.
4. Cover and simmer for **5 minutes** until tender.

### Step 2: Combine and Thicken
5. Add the prepared cornstarch paste along with **1/2 cup vinegar**, **3/4 cup pineapple juice**, **1/2 cup sugar**, **1/2 tsp salt**, and **1/4 tsp ginger** to the peppers and pineapple mixture.
6. Simmer, stirring constantly until the mixture thickens and becomes glossy.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the cooled sauce in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 5 days; reheat gently before serving.
