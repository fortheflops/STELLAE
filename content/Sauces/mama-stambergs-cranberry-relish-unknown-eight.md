# Mama Stamberg's Cranberry Relish

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Eight | ✍️ **Attribution:** Mama Stamberg | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A vibrant, tangy, and slightly spicy holiday classic featuring raw cranberries, onion, and a kick of horseradish. This traditional family recipe is frozen to meld the unique flavors and served partially thawed for a refreshing, slushy texture.*

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Food grinder or food processor
* Mixing bowl
* Freezer-safe container

## Ingredients

### Relish Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 cups | [[Cranberries]] | raw |
| 1 small | [[Onion]] |  |
| 3/4 cup | [[Sour Cream]] |  |
| 2 tablespoons | [[Horseradish]] | red is milder |
| 1/2 cup | [[Sugar]] |  |

---

## Instructions

### Preparation
1. Grind the **2 cups raw cranberries** and **1 small onion** together using a food grinder or food processor until finely and evenly minced.
2. In a mixing bowl, thoroughly stir in the remaining ingredients: **3/4 cup sour cream**, **2 tablespoons horseradish**, and **1/2 cup sugar** until the sugar is dissolved and the mixture is uniform.
3. Transfer the mixture into a freezer-safe container and freeze until completely solid.
4. Move the frozen relish from the freezer to the refrigerator at least **one hour** before serving, allowing it to soften to a thick, icy, and spoonable slushy consistency.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This relish can be prepared and frozen weeks in advance. Be sure to transfer it to the refrigerator at least one hour before dinner so it reaches the perfect semi-thawed state.
