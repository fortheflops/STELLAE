# Vegetable Dip

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A creamy and savory vegetable dip made with a classic ranch dry mix. This quick-to-prepare appetizer is perfect for serving with fresh crudités or crunchy chips.*

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 8-ounce package | [[Cream Cheese]] | Softened |
| 1 small carton | [[Yogurt]] | Plain or Greek |
| 1 small package | [[Ranch House Dressing]] | Dry mix |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Combine and Chill
1. In a medium mixing bowl, combine the **1 8-ounce package cream cheese**, **1 small carton yogurt**, and **1 small package ranch house dressing**.
2. Mix well until the texture is smooth and fully incorporated.
3. Refrigerate for at least **30 minutes** to allow the flavors to meld before serving with chips or fresh vegetables.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days; stir well before serving.
