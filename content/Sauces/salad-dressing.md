# Salad Dressing

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A rich and savory warm salad dressing featuring crispy bacon drippings and a tangy vinegar base. This classic recipe is perfect for wilting fresh greens into a comforting side dish.*

## Ingredients

### Dressing Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| As needed | [[Bacon]] | Sliced and browned |
| 3 | [[Eggs]] | Beaten |
| 1 cup | [[Cold water]] | None |
| 3 tbsp | [[Sugar]] | None |
| 3 tbsp | [[Vinegar]] | None |
| To taste | [[Cornstarch]] | For thickening |

---

## Instructions

### Preparation
1. Simmer **bacon** in a skillet until brown and crispy; remove from pan, reserving the **bacon drippings**.
2. In a mixing bowl, beat together the **3 eggs**, **1 cup cold water**, **3 tbsp sugar**, and **3 tbsp vinegar**.
3. Add the egg mixture to the **bacon drippings** and stir until smooth.
4. Thicken the mixture with **cornstarch** over low heat until it reaches a smooth, pourable consistency.
5. Stir in the **chopped bacon** and serve warm over your favorite salad greens.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This dressing is best served warm immediately after preparation. If made ahead, reheat gently over low heat, whisking constantly to prevent the eggs from scrambling.
