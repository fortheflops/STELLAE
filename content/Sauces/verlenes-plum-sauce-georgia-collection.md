# Verlene's Plum Sauce

> 📜 **Collection:** Georgia Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Verlene | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A sweet and savory plum sauce spiced with ginger, cloves, and allspice, sweetened with honey. Perfect for serving alongside ham, chicken, or lamb.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Combine and Simmer
1. In a medium saucepan, combine the **2 cups chopped plums**, **1/2 cup honey**, **2 tbsp minced onion**, **1 tsp ground ginger**, **1/4 tsp ground cloves**, **1 tsp vanilla extract**, **1/4 cup lemon juice**, **1 tsp grated lemon peel**, **1/4 tsp salt**, and **1/4 tsp ground allspice**.
2. Bring the mixture to a boil over medium-high heat. Once boiling, reduce the heat and simmer for **15 minutes** until the plums are completely softened and the mixture is fragrant.

### Step 2: Puree and Finish
3. Carefully transfer the hot mixture to a blender and puree until completely smooth.
4. Return the pureed sauce to the saucepan and simmer for an additional **5 minutes** until slightly thickened and glossy. Serve warm alongside ham, chicken, or lamb.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 1 week. Reheat gently on the stovetop before serving.
