# Mildred's Chili-Enchilada Sauce

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Mildred (Neighbor on 12th St. - 1943) | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A rich and savory vintage chili-enchilada sauce from 1943, featuring ground beef, aromatic spices, and a robust chili powder base. Perfect for layering into authentic, crispy enchiladas.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Brown the Meat and Aromatics
1. Melt **2 Tbsp. Crisco** in a frying pan over medium heat.
2. Pour in **2 lb. hamburger** and cook until browned.
3. Add **2 grated garlic buttons** and cook for another minute until fragrant.

### Step 2: Add Spices and Simmer
4. Stir in **10 Tbsp. plain red chili powder**, **1 level Tbsp. cumino seed**, **1 scant Tbsp. powdered cumino seed**, **1 Tbsp. oregano**, and **1 Tbsp. salt**.
5. Stir thoroughly to coat the meat evenly.
6. Add enough **water** to cover the mixture.
7. Stir in **1 can tomato paste** and sprinkle with **1 Tbsp. flour**.
8. Simmer for **20 to 30 minutes** until thickened and deeply flavorful.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the cooled sauce in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 5 days, or freeze for up to 3 months.
