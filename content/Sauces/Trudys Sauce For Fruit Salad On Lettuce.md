# Trudy's Sauce For Fruit Salad On Lettuce

> 📜 **Collection:** LouiseCollection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Trudy | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A vintage, creamy fruit salad dressing featuring a delicate blend of marshmallow creme and citrus. This versatile sauce perfectly complements fresh fruit served over crisp lettuce.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 10 mins | None | 4 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Saucepan
* Whisk
* Mixing bowl

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| To taste | [[Milk]] | For boiling |
| 2 Teaspoons | [[Cornstarch]] | Thickener |
| As needed | [[Milk]] or [[Fruit Juice]] | Cold, for mixing cornstarch |
| To taste | [[Marshmallow Creme]] | Room temperature |
| To taste | [[Orange Juice]] | Freshly squeezed |
| To taste | [[Honey]] | Liquid |
| 1 or 2 | [[Egg Yolk]] | Beaten |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Base
1. Bring the **[[Milk]]** to a gentle boil in a saucepan.
2. In a separate small bowl, whisk the **2 teaspoons [[Cornstarch]]** with enough **cold [[Milk]] or [[Fruit Juice]]** to create a smooth, lump-free slurry.
3. Slowly whisk the **cornstarch mixture** into the **boiling [[Milk]]**, stirring constantly until the mixture thickens slightly.

### Step 2: Emulsify and Finish
4. Remove from heat and stir in the **[[Marshmallow Creme]]**, **[[Orange Juice]]**, **[[Honey]]**, and **1 or 2 [[Egg Yolk]]s**.
5. Continue whisking until the sauce is smooth, glossy, and fully incorporated.
6. Allow to cool slightly before serving over your favorite fruit salad on a bed of lettuce.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 2 days; whisk well before serving as separation may occur.
