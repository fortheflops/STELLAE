# Sauce Remoulade

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A classic, tangy Sauce Remoulade perfect for dipping meat fondue. This simple, chilled condiment combines savory capers, pickles, and fresh herbs for a balanced flavor profile.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 0 mins | 30 mins | 4 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Spatula

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 tsp | [[Prepared Mustard]] | Standard yellow or Dijon |
| 2 tbsp | [[Capers]] | Well drained and minced |
| 2 tbsp | [[Sour Pickles]] | Minced |
| 1 tbsp | [[Parsley]] | Chopped |
| 1 cup | [[Mayonnaise]] | Full fat preferred |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation
1. In a medium mixing bowl, combine the **1 cup mayonnaise**, **1 tsp prepared mustard**, **2 tbsp minced capers**, **2 tbsp minced sour pickles**, and **1 tbsp chopped parsley**.
2. Stir until the mixture is uniform and the ingredients are evenly distributed.
3. Cover and chill in the refrigerator for at least 30 minutes to allow the flavors to meld before serving with meat fondue.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This sauce can be prepared up to 2 days in advance; keep refrigerated in an airtight container.
