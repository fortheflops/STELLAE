# Molded Strained Cranberry Sauce

> 📜 **Collection:** JillCollection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A classic recipe for a smooth, homemade jellied cranberry sauce made from fresh cranberries, perfect for holiday meals.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 20 mins | 2 hours | 6–8 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Saucepan
* Fine mesh sieve
* Clean pot
* Cold plate
* Molding dish

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 pound | [[Cranberries]] | Fresh, washed |
| 2 cups | [[Water]] | Divided |
| 2 cups | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Simmer and Strain
1. In a saucepan, boil the **1 pound fresh cranberries** with the **2 cups water** until the berries stop popping and are very soft.

### Step 2: Extract Pulp and Boil
2. Strain the mixture through a sieve into a clean pot, pressing firmly on the solids to extract as much pulp as possible. Discard the skins and seeds.
3. Bring the strained liquid to a rapid boil. Add the **2 cups sugar** and continue to boil for **8 to 10 minutes**, or until a drop of the sauce jells when placed on a cold plate and cooled slightly.

### Step 3: Mold and Chill
4. Immediately pour the hot mixture into a mold that has been rinsed with cold water (do not dry).
5. Chill in the refrigerator until completely firm and set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the molded cranberry sauce covered in the refrigerator for up to 5 days before serving.
