# Apricot Sauce

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Humphill Halls | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A sweet and smooth apricot sauce made with chilled fruit, honey, and powdered sugar. Perfect for spooning over sherbet or ice cream.*

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5 mins | None | None | 4 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Blender
* Can opener
* Measuring cups
* Measuring spoons

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 can (1 lb.) | [[Chilled Apricot Halves]] | Drained |
| 1/4 cup | [[Apricot Syrup]] | Reserved from canned apricots |
| 1 Tbsp | [[Honey]] | None |
| 1 Tbsp | [[Powdered Sugar]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Blend the Sauce
1. Drain **1 can (1 lb.) chilled apricot halves** thoroughly.
2. Place the drained apricot halves and **1/4 c. apricot syrup** into the blender container.
3. Process first at low speed, then increase to high until completely smooth.
4. Blend in **1 t. honey** and **1 t. powdered sugar** until fully incorporated and smooth.
5. Serve chilled over sherbet or ice cream.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days.
