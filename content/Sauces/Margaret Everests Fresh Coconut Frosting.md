# Margaret Everest's Fresh Coconut Frosting

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Margaret Everest | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A classic, uncooked meringue frosting featuring freshly grated coconut for a uniquely moist texture. This airy topping is designed to soak into cake layers for a deep, tropical flavor.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 0 mins | 6 hours | Frosting for one two-layer cake |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Electric mixer
* Mixing bowl
* Grater

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 small | [[Coconut]] | meat, finely grated |
| 2 | [[Egg Whites]] | fresh |
| 1 Cup | [[Sugar]] | granulated |
| 1 Teaspoon | [[Vanilla Extract]] | none |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Coconut
1. Drain the juice from the **1 small coconut**, crack open the shell, and remove the meat. Peel the brown skin and finely grate the white meat.

### Step 2: Whip the Meringue
2. In a clean, dry bowl, beat the **2 egg whites** with an electric mixer on high speed until stiff peaks form.
3. Gradually beat in the **1 cup sugar**, one tablespoon at a time, until the meringue is stiff and glossy.

### Step 3: Assemble
4. Gently fold in the **1 teaspoon vanilla extract** and the prepared **1 small coconut**.
5. Spread the frosting between cake layers and on top. Allow the cake to stand for at least 5-6 hours until the frosting has soaked into the cake and the texture is set.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Leftover frosting will last for up to a week if covered and refrigerated.
