# Fluffy Lemon Meringue Frosting

> 📜 **Collection:** General Archive | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A light and airy cooked meringue frosting featuring a delicate lemon flavor. This classic recipe uses a hot sugar syrup to stabilize whipped egg whites for a glossy, professional finish.*

| 2 large | [[Egg Whites]] | inferred, room temperature |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Lemon]] Flavoring | extract |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Syrup
1. In a small saucepan, combine the **1 cup sugar**, **1 pinch salt**, **1 tablespoon white Karo**, and **1/4 cup water**. Cook over medium heat, stirring until the **sugar** is fully dissolved.
2. Continue cooking without stirring until the syrup reaches the thread stage (**230-235°F**), where it spins a long thread when lifted.

### Step 2: Whip and Combine
3. While the syrup cooks, beat the **2 large egg whites** in a large, clean bowl until stiff, glossy peaks form.
4. Once the syrup reaches temperature, pour it in a thin, steady stream over the **stiffly beaten egg whites** while beating continuously with an electric mixer on high speed.
5. Continue to beat until the frosting is thick, glossy, and holds its shape firmly.
6. Beat in the **1 teaspoon lemon flavoring** until fully incorporated, then use immediately to ice the cake.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This frosting is best used immediately after preparation as it sets quickly. It is not recommended for long-term storage.
