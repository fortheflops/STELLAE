# Peach Melba Sauce

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Five | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A classic fruit sauce made with raspberries and red currant jelly, perfect for topping desserts. It cooks down until clear and thick before being strained and cooled.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5 mins | 10 mins | Cooling time | 4 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Saucepan
* Strainer
* Spoon

---
## Ingredients

### Sauce Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 (10 oz) pkg. | [[Raspberries]] | Frozen, thawed |
| 1 1/2 tsp. | [[Cornstarch]] | None |
| 1/2 cup | [[Red currant jelly]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and Cooking
1. Combine **1 (10 oz) pkg. thawed raspberries** and **1 1/2 tsp. cornstarch** in a saucepan.
2. Add **1/2 cup red currant jelly** to the mixture.
3. Bring the mixture to a boil, stirring constantly until it becomes clear and thick.
4. Strain the sauce to remove seeds, then set aside to cool.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 5 days.
