# Johnny's Dressing

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Johnny | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A robust and tangy homemade vinaigrette featuring a bold garlic and caper base. This large-batch recipe is incredibly versatile and stores exceptionally well for months.*

---

## Instructions

### Mixing and Storage
1. In a large mixing bowl or jar, combine the **6-7 cloves minced garlic**, **1 oz salt**, **12 oz water**, **16 oz oil**, **8 oz sugar**, **3 tbsp capers**, the juice of **1/2 lemon**, and **16 oz vinegar**.
2. Whisk or shake vigorously until the **8 oz sugar** and **1 oz salt** are completely dissolved and the dressing is well blended and emulsified.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in a sealed bottle or jar in the refrigerator. This dressing will keep well for up to 3 months.
