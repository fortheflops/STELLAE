# Vanilla Sauce

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A classic, silky vanilla sauce perfect for drizzling over warm puddings and desserts. This simple stovetop recipe uses cornstarch for a smooth, thickened consistency.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5 mins | 10 mins | None | 4 Servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Saucepan
* Whisk

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 Cup | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Nutmeg]] | Ground |
| 1 tsp | [[Vanilla]] | Extract |
| 2 Tbsp | [[Cornstarch]] | None |
| 2 Tbsp | [[Butter]] | None |
| 3 Cups | [[Water]] | Cold |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Combine and Cook
1. In a saucepan, combine the **1 cup sugar**, **1/4 tsp nutmeg**, **1 tsp vanilla**, **2 tbsp cornstarch**, **2 tbsp butter**, and **3 cups cold water**.
2. Bring the mixture to a boil over medium heat, whisking constantly until the sauce is smooth and thickened to a glossy, coat-the-back-of-a-spoon consistency.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days; reheat gently on the stovetop or microwave, adding a splash of water if it becomes too thick.
