# Classic Cream Cheese Frosting

> 📜 **Collection:** General Archive | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A rich, tangy, and sweet classic cream cheese frosting, perfect for carrot cake, red velvet cake, or any of your favorite desserts. This simple recipe is creamy and comes together in minutes.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 0 mins | None | Frosting for a 9x13 inch or two-layer cake |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Electric mixer
* Large mixing bowl
* Spatula

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 Package (8oz) | [[Cream Cheese]] | softened |
| 1/2 Stick | [[Margarine]] or [[Butter]] | softened |
| 1 Teaspoon | [[Vanilla]] | extract |
| 1 Box (1 lb) | [[Powdered Sugar]] | sifted |
| Optional | [[Pecans]] | chopped, for garnish |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Base
1. In a large bowl, beat the **1 package (8oz) cream cheese** and **1/2 stick margarine or butter** with an electric mixer until smooth and creamy.

### Step 2: Incorporate Sugar
2. Gradually add the **1 box (1 lb) powdered sugar**, about one cup at a time, beating on low speed until combined, then increasing to medium speed until the frosting is smooth.

### Step 3: Whip and Finish
3. Add the **1 teaspoon vanilla** and beat on high speed for another minute until the frosting is light and fluffy.
4. Spread the frosting on a completely cooled cake and sprinkle with **chopped pecans**, if desired.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 5 days; bring to room temperature before spreading.
