# Enchilada Sauce

> 📜 **Collection:** Patsy Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A rich and savory homemade enchilada sauce featuring pureed tomatoes, bell peppers, celery, and a blend of aromatic spices. This large-batch recipe yields enough flavorful sauce to coat twenty-four enchiladas.*

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 30 mins | None | 24 enchiladas |

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 Cup | [[Olive Oil]] | for cooking |
| 1 | [[Green Pepper]] | finely chopped |
| Few Stalks | [[Celery]] | from a large stock, very finely chopped |
| 1 ½ Cups | [[Flour]] | all-purpose |
| 3 Tbsp | [[Chili Powder]] | standard ground |
| 1 Tbsp | [[Salt]] | to taste |
| 5 Cups | [[Pureed Tomatoes]] | smooth or crushed |
| 2 Cups | [[Stock]] | chicken or vegetable |
| To Taste | [[Oregano]] | seasoning |
| To Taste | [[Rosemary]] | seasoning |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Sauté the Aromatics
1. Finely chop the **green pepper** and **celery stalks** until very fine.
2. In a heavy-bottomed saucepan, heat the **1 cup olive oil** and cook the chopped **green pepper** and **celery** until tender and fragrant.

### Step 2: Create the Roux
3. In a separate bowl, blend together the **3 tbsp chili powder** and **1 ½ cups flour**.
4. Add the dry flour and chili powder mixture to the saucepan with the cooked **olive oil** and vegetables, mixing well to form a smooth paste.

### Step 3: Simmer the Sauce
5. Gradually whisk in the **1 tbsp salt**, **5 cups pureed tomatoes**, and **2 cups stock**, along with a pinch of **oregano** and **rosemary**.
6. Simmer the mixture over medium-low heat, stirring frequently, until thickened and smooth to a rich, coating consistency.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the cooled sauce in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 5 days, or freeze for up to 3 months.
