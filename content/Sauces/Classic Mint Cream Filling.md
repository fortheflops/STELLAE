# Classic Mint Cream Filling

> 📜 **Collection:** Patsy Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A versatile, buttercream-style mint cream filling perfect for layering in brownies or sandwiching between cookies. This classic recipe provides a smooth, refreshing center for homemade candies and baked goods.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 0 mins | 1 hour | Filling for an 8x8 or 9x9 inch pan |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Medium bowl
* Electric mixer

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 1/2 Cups | [[Powdered Sugar]] | Sifted |
| 3 Tbsp | [[Sweet Butter]] | Softened |
| 2 Tablespoons | [[Heavy Cream]] | Room temperature |
| 3/4 Teaspoon | [[Peppermint Flavoring]] | Extract |
| Optional | [[Green Food Coloring]] | As desired |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing the Filling
1. In a medium bowl, combine the **1 1/2 cups powdered sugar**, **3 tbsp sweet butter**, **2 tablespoons heavy cream**, and **3/4 teaspoon peppermint flavoring**.
2. Beat with an electric mixer on low speed to combine, then increase to high speed and beat until the filling is smooth, creamy, and light in texture.
3. If desired, add a drop of **green food coloring** and mix until the color is uniform throughout.

### Step 2: Assembly and Chilling
4. Spread the filling evenly over your cooled brownie or cookie layer.
5. Cover and chill in the refrigerator until firm, about **1 hour**, before adding a top layer of chocolate or serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 1 week; bring to room temperature briefly if it becomes too stiff to spread.
