# El Palenque Green Salsa

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Eight | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A vibrant and creamy green salsa featuring fresh avocado, tangy tomatillos, and zesty jalapeños. Perfect for dipping or drizzling over your favorite dishes.*

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 large | [[Avocado]] | Ripe, diced |
| 1 to 2 | [[Garlic cloves]] | Minced |
| Juice of 1 | [[Lime]] | Freshly squeezed |
| 2 medium | [[Tomatillos]] | Diced |
| 6 to 8 | [[Green onions]] | Chopped finely, use half or so of the green part |
| 3 | [[Jalapeños]] | Minced, seeds removed |
| As desired | [[Cilantro leaves]] | For seasoning |
| As desired | [[Salt]] | To taste |
| As needed | [[Water]] | For thinning consistency |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and Mixing
1. Combine the **1 large avocado** (diced), **1 to 2 garlic cloves** (minced), and **juice of 1 lime** in a mixing bowl.
2. Add the **2 medium tomatillos** (diced), **6 to 8 green onions** (chopped finely), and **3 jalapeños** (minced, seeds removed) to the bowl.
3. Season as desired with **cilantro leaves** and **salt**.
4. Thin to desired consistency by adding **water** and stirring until smooth and well blended.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 2 days, pressing plastic wrap directly onto the surface to prevent browning.
