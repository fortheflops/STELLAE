## Instructions

### Preparation
1. In a mixing bowl, combine **1 c. mayonnaise** and **1 c. sour cream** until smooth.
2. Stir in **1 tbsp. onion flakes**, **1 tbsp. dried parsley**, **1 tsp. dill weed**, and **1 tsp. beau monde seasoning**.
3. Mix thoroughly until all ingredients are well incorporated, then chill in the refrigerator for at least 30 minutes to let the flavors meld before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3-4 days.
