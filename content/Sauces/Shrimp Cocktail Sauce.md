# Shrimp Cocktail Sauce

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A classic, zesty shrimp cocktail sauce featuring catsup, chili sauce, horseradish, and a splash of cooking sherry. Perfect for pairing with chilled seafood appetizers.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | None | 30 mins | 4 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Measuring spoons
* Whisk or spoon
* Cocktail glasses

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| ¼ cup | [[Catsup]] | Standard |
| ¼ cup | [[Chili Sauce]] | Standard |
| ¼ cup | [[Lemon Juice]] | Freshly squeezed recommended |
| 1 tsp | [[Horseradish]] | Grated |
| 6 drops | [[Tabasco Sauce]] | For heat |
| 1 tsp | [[Chives]] | Chopped |
| ½ tsp | [[Parsley]] | Chopped |
| ½ cup | [[Cooking Sherry]] | Optional, may be omitted |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mix and Chill
1. In a mixing bowl, combine the **¼ cup catsup**, **¼ cup chili sauce**, **¼ cup lemon juice**, **1 tsp grated horseradish**, **6 drops tabasco sauce**, **1 tsp chopped chives**, **½ tsp chopped parsely**, and optional **½ cup cooking sherry**.
2. Mix thoroughly until well blended, then place in the refrigerator to chill and marry the flavors until fully cold.

### Step 2: Serve
3. At serving time, arrange cooked shrimp in cocktail glasses and cover with **1 to 2 Tbsp.** of the prepared sauce per serving until well coated and appealing.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the sauce sealed in the refrigerator for up to 3 days before serving for the best flavor integration.
