## Instructions

### Preparation and Basting
1. Combine the **red wine vinegar**, **bourbon**, **mustard**, **br. sugar**, and **olive oil** in a mixing bowl and whisk until smooth.
2. Baste over **chix**, **burgers**, **steaks**, or **ribs** during grilling or cooking until caramelized and fragrant.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight jar in the refrigerator for up to 1 week before using.
