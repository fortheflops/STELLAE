# Classic Boiled Vanilla Frosting

> 📜 **Collection:** General Archive | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A light, fluffy, and glossy meringue-style boiled icing perfect for layering cakes. This classic seven-minute frosting is achieved by whipping hot sugar syrup into stiff egg whites.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5 mins | 10 mins | None | Frosts a 2-3 layer cake |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* small heavy-bottomed saucepan
* candy thermometer
* electric mixer
* large mixing bowl

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Syrup
1. In a small, heavy-bottomed saucepan, combine **1 1/2 cups sugar**, **1/2 cup water**, and **1/2 tsp vinegar**.
2. Bring to a boil over medium heat, stirring until the **sugar** dissolves.
3. Continue to boil without stirring until the syrup reaches the soft-ball stage (238-245°F) on a candy thermometer.

### Step 2: Whip the Meringue
4. While the syrup boils, combine **3 egg whites** and **few grains of salt** in a large, clean mixing bowl.
5. Beat with an electric mixer on high speed until stiff peaks form.

### Step 3: Combine and Finish
6. With the mixer running on high, very slowly and carefully pour the hot syrup in a thin, steady stream into the **egg whites**.
7. Continue beating constantly for about 7 minutes until the frosting is thick, glossy, and holds its shape.
8. Beat in **1 tsp vanilla** and use immediately on a cooled cake.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This frosting is best used immediately after preparation as it sets quickly. It is not recommended for long-term storage.
