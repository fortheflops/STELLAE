## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| Two                              | Large [[Onions]], Diced                   |
| Two Cups                         | [[Vinegar]]                               |
| One Tablespoon                   | Dry [[Mustard]]                           |
| $\frac{3}{4}$ Teaspoon           | [[Salt]]                                  |
| One Teaspoon                     | [[Garlic Salt]]                           |
| One Tablespoon                   | [[Worcestershire Sauce]]                  |
| One & $\frac{1}{2}$ Teaspoons    | [[Pepper]]                                |
| $\frac{1}{2}$ Box (1 & $\frac{1}{8}$ Cups) | [[Brown Sugar]]                   |
| One Tablespoon                   | Liquid [[Smoke]]                          |
| Two Cups                         | [[Catsup]]                                |
| $\frac{1}{4}$ Teaspoon           | Red [[Pepper]] (or 1 tsp Chili Powder)    |
___
## Instructions
1.  Mix all ingredients together in a large saucepan: diced onions, vinegar, dry mustard, salt, garlic salt, Worcestershire sauce, pepper, brown sugar, liquid smoke, catsup, and red pepper (or chili powder).
2.  Bring the mixture to a boil.
3.  Reduce heat and simmer, without a lid, for an hour or so, until the sauce is slightly thickened.
