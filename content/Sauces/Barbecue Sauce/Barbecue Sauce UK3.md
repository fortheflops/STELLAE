## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
| 1 Ounce             | Tomato Paste                 |
| 4 Tablespoon          | Worcestershire               |
| 1/4 Teaspoon         | Garlic Powder              |
| 1 Teaspoon         | Tobasco                |
| 3 Tablespoon        | Dry Mustard             |
| (1/2 Teaspoon)        | (Cayenne)              |
| 1/2 Teaspoon         | Chili Powder            |
| 3 Tablespoon      | Brown Sugar          |
| 1 Medium              | Onion                 |
| 2 Tablespoon          | Vinegar              |
| (1 Tablespoon) |(Hickory Sauce) |
|2|Chicken Bouillon Cubes|
|1/2 Cup| Ketchup |
|   | Beer to fill Can |
___
### Instructions
1. Fill can with beer.
2. (Optional)
	- Bake at 350 degrees Fahrenheit for 4-5 hours.
	- Or, bake at 450-475 degrees Fahrenheit for 3-4 hours.
	- Or, bake at 300 degrees Fahrenheit for 6 hours.
