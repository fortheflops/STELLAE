| 1 Teaspoon | [[Salt]] | Fine grain |
| 1 Tablespoon | [[Dry Mustard]] | Powdered |
| 1 Tablespoon | [[Celery Seed]] | Whole |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Emulsify
1. Combine the **1 cup oil**, **1/4 cup vinegar**, **1/2 cup sugar**, **1/4 medium onion**, **1 teaspoon salt**, **1 tablespoon dry mustard**, and **1 tablespoon celery seed** in a jar or bowl.
2. Shake or whisk vigorously until the **sugar** is fully dissolved and the mixture is emulsified and slightly thickened.
3. Serve immediately over fresh spinach, orange sections, and grapefruit until well coated and flavorful.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 2 weeks; shake well before each use.
