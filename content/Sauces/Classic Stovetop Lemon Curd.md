# Classic Stovetop Lemon Curd

> 📜 **Collection:** General Archive | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A classic recipe for a rich and tangy lemon curd made directly on the stovetop. A simple but decadent filling for tarts or a spread for scones.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 10 mins | None | Approx. 1 cup |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* medium heavy-bottomed saucepan
* wire whisk
* heatproof spatula
* fine-mesh sieve

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 6 oz (3/4 cup) | [[Sugar]] | granulated |
| 2 oz (4 Tbsp) | [[Margarine]] or [[Butter]] | cubed |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] | large |
| 2 | [[Lemons]] | grated rind and strained juice |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Combine and Cook
1. In a medium, heavy-bottomed saucepan, whisk together the **2 large eggs** and **6 oz sugar** until well combined.
2. Stir in the **grated rind of 2 lemons**, the **strained juice of 2 lemons**, and the **2 oz margarine or butter**.
3. Cook over **very low heat**, stirring constantly with a whisk or heatproof spatula, ensuring you scrape the bottom and corners of the pan.
4. Continue cooking for **8-10 minutes** until the mixture has thickened enough to coat the back of a spoon; do not allow the mixture to boil, or the **eggs** will scramble.

### Step 2: Finish and Cool
5. Once thickened, remove from the heat and optionally strain through a fine-mesh sieve for an extra-smooth texture.
6. Let the curd cool completely, covering the surface directly with plastic wrap to prevent a skin from forming.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in the refrigerator in an airtight container for up to 1 week; the curd will thicken further as it chills.
