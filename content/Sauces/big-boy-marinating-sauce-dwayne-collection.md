# Big Boy Marinating Sauce

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A versatile and savory marinating sauce featuring oil, soy sauce, and aromatic spices. Perfect for infusing deep flavor into bite-sized cuts of meat overnight.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | None | Overnight | 8 servings |

## Ingredients

### Marinade Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 1/2 cups | [[Wesson Oil]] | None |
| 3/4 cup | [[Soy Sauce]] | None |
| 1/4 cup | [[Worcestershire Sauce]] | None |
| 2 tsp | [[Dry Mustard]] | None |
| 2 1/2 tsp | [[Salt]] | None |
| 1 tsp | [[Course Black Pepper]] | None |
| 1/2 cup | [[Wine Vinegar]] | None |
| 1 1/2 tsp | [[Parsley Flakes]] | None |
| 2 cloves | [[Garlic]] | Crushed |
| 1/2 cup | [[Lemon Juice]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation
1. Cut your meat into bite-size pieces.
2. Combine **1 1/2 cups Wesson Oil**, **3/4 cup Soy Sauce**, **1/4 cup Worcestershire Sauce**, **2 tsp Dry Mustard**, **2 1/2 tsp Salt**, **1 tsp Course Black Pepper**, **1/2 cup Wine Vinegar**, **1 1/2 tsp Parsley Flakes**, **2 cloves Garlic**, and **1/2 cup Lemon Juice** in a mixing bowl.
3. Add the meat to the marinade and let it marinate overnight in the refrigerator until fully infused with flavor.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The sauce can be mixed ahead of time, and meat can marinate in the refrigerator overnight.
