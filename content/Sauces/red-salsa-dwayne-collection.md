# Red Salsa

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A quick and flavorful homemade red salsa featuring canned tomatoes, onion, garlic salt, and ground chili powder. Perfect as a dip for tortilla chips or a zesty topping for tacos.*

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 cups | [[Canned Tomatoes]] | drained and chopped |
| 1/2 cup | [[Onion]] | finely chopped |
| 1 tsp. | [[Garlic Salt]] | None |
| 2 tbsp. | [[Chili Powder]] | ground |

---

## Instructions

### Preparation
1. Drain and finely chop the **2 cups canned tomatoes**.
2. In a medium mixing bowl, combine the chopped **2 cups canned tomatoes**, **1/2 cup onion**, **1 tsp. garlic salt**, and **2 tbsp. chili powder**.
3. Stir the mixture thoroughly until the spices are evenly distributed. Let the salsa rest for at least **10 mins** at room temperature to allow the flavors to meld before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 5 days. The flavors will continue to deepen and intensify over time.
