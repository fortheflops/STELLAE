# Classic Chocolate Glaze

> 📜 **Collection:** UK15 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A quick and easy chocolate glaze made with melted unsweetened chocolate, perfect for drizzling over Bundt cakes, donuts, or cookies. This versatile recipe provides a smooth, glossy finish that sets beautifully on any baked good.*

---

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/3 cup | [[Margarine]] or [[Butter]] | none |
| 2 cups | [[Powdered Sugar]] | none |
| 1 1/2 teaspoons | [[Vanilla]] | none |
| 2 (1 ounce) squares | [[Chocolate]] | unsweetened, melted and cooled |
| 2 to 4 tablespoons | [[Water]] | hot |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Base
1. In a medium saucepan, melt the **1/3 cup margarine** or **butter** over low heat until completely liquefied.

### Step 2: Combine Ingredients
2. Remove the saucepan from the heat. Stir in the **2 cups powdered sugar**, **1 1/2 teaspoons vanilla**, and the **2 (1 ounce) squares unsweetened chocolate** until the mixture is smooth and glossy.

### Step 3: Adjust Consistency
3. Stir in the **2 to 4 tablespoons hot water**, one tablespoon at a time, until the glaze reaches your desired pouring or spreading consistency.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 2 days; re-warm gently if it thickens too much.
