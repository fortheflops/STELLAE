# Gail's Graham Cracker Frosting

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Gail | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A unique and delicious frosting with a fudgy, chewy texture, packed with the crunch of graham cracker crumbs and chopped walnuts. This versatile topping is perfect for a simple sheet cake.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 0 mins | None | Frosting for one 9x13 inch cake |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Medium mixing bowl
* Electric hand mixer
* Spatula

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Sweetened Condensed Milk]] | Standard |
| 2 Tablespoons | [[Butter]] | Melted |
| 1 Cup | [[Powdered Sugar]] | Sifted |
| 2 Tablespoons | [[Cocoa Powder]] | Hershey's brand |
| 3/4 Cup | [[Graham Cracker Crumbs]] | Rolled |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Walnuts]] | Chopped |
| 1 Teaspoon | [[Vanilla Extract]] | Pure |
| As needed | [[Milk]] | For consistency adjustment |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Create the Base
1. In a medium bowl, combine the **1/2 cup sweetened condensed milk**, **2 tablespoons melted butter**, **1 cup powdered sugar**, and **2 tablespoons cocoa powder**.
2. Beat with a mixer until the mixture is completely smooth and well combined.

### Step 2: Fold and Adjust
3. Switch to a spatula and stir in the **3/4 cup graham cracker crumbs**, **1 teaspoon vanilla extract**, and **1/2 cup chopped walnuts** by hand until evenly incorporated.
4. Check the consistency: if too dry, add **milk** one teaspoon at a time; if too runny, add more **powdered sugar** or **graham cracker crumbs** until it reaches a spreadable, fudgy consistency.

### Step 3: Application
5. Spread the frosting onto a warm cake for easier application, or apply to a cooled cake and allow it to set until firm and fragrant.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This frosting can be made ahead and stored in an airtight container in the refrigerator; if it stiffens, let it come to room temperature before spreading.
