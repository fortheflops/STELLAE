# Sala Di Pomodori

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Ida E | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A classic Italian tomato pasta sauce simmered with sweet onions, fresh basil, and rich plum tomatoes. Perfect for tossing with your favorite pasta or as a base for Italian dishes.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 50 mins | None | Makes about 1 1/2 cups |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 2-3 qt. enamel or stainless steel saucepan
* Fine mesh sieve

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 T | [[Olive Oil]] | None |
| 1/2 C | [[Onion]] | Finely chopped |
| 5 C | [[Italian Plum Tomatoes]] | Coarsely chopped, not drained |
| 3 T | [[Tomato Paste]] | None |
| 1 T | [[Sugar]] | None |
| 1 T (or 1 t dried) | [[Basil]] | Finely chopped fresh or dried |
| 1 t | [[Salt]] | None |
| To taste | [[Black Pepper]] | Freshly ground |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Sauté the Aromatics
1. Heat **2 T** of **[[Olive Oil]]** in a **2-3 qt. enamel or stainless steel saucepan** until a light haze forms over it.
2. Add **1/2 C** of **[[Onion]]** and cook over medium heat for **7-8 minutes** until soft but not browned.

### Step 2: Simmer the Sauce
3. Add **5 C** of **[[Italian Plum Tomatoes]]**, **3 T** of **[[Tomato Paste]]**, **1 T** of **[[Basil]]**, **1 T** of **[[Sugar]]**, **1 t** of **[[Salt]]**, and **[[Black Pepper]]** to the saucepan.
4. Reduce heat to very low and simmer for **40 minutes** partially covered, stirring occasionally until rich and well-blended.

### Step 3: Finish and Serve
5. Run the sauce through a sieve if a thinner consistency is desired.
6. Taste for seasoning and serve hot over your favorite pasta.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the cooled sauce in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 5 days, or freeze for up to 3 months.
