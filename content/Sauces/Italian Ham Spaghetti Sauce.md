# Italian Ham Spaghetti Sauce

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A savory and comforting Italian ham spaghetti sauce infused with fresh basil, garlic, and crushed red pepper flakes. Perfect for serving over your favorite pasta twists.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 25 mins | None | 4–6 Servings |

* Sauté pan

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | [[Onion]] | Chopped |
| 3 Cloves | [[Garlic]] | Chopped |
| As needed | [[Olive Oil]] | For cooking |
| A pinch | [[Crushed Red Pepper Flakes]] | To taste |
| 2 Cans | [[Italian Tomatoes]] | Chopped |
| 1 Can | [[Tomato Sauce]] | Standard |
| As needed | [[Ham]] | Rolled and cut into narrow ribbon strips |
| 1 Cup | [[Fresh Basil]] | Chopped |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Build the Base
1. Cook the **1 onion** and **3 cloves garlic** in a pan with **olive oil** until soft and fragrant.
2. Stir in the **crushed red pepper flakes**.

### Step 2: Simmer the Sauce
3. Add the **2 cans chopped Italian tomatoes** and **1 can tomato sauce** to the pan.
4. Cook for **20 minutes** until the sauce thickens and deepens in flavor.

### Step 3: Finish and Serve
5. While the sauce simmers, roll the **ham** and cut it into narrow ribbon strips.
6. Add the prepared **ham** and **1 cup chopped fresh basil** into the sauce.
7. Cook for a few minutes until heated through, then serve over pasta twists.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftover sauce in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 4 days or freeze for up to 3 months.
