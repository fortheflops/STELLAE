# Cilantro Pesto

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Eight | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A vibrant and versatile cilantro pesto loaded with garlic, parmesan, pine nuts, and jalapeños. Delicious tossed with pasta, potatoes, or potstickers, or brushed over French bread.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 0 mins | None | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Food processor
* Metal blade

---
## Ingredients

### Pesto Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 clove | [[Garlic]] | Whole |
| 4 oz | [[Parmesan]] | Grated or cut into 1-inch cubes |
| 2 cups | [[Cilantro]] | Roughly chopped |
| 1/4 cup | [[Pine nuts]] | Whole |
| 1 tbsp | [[Jalapeños]] | Diced |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Salt]] | None |
| 3/4 cup | [[Olive oil]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Processing the Pesto
1. Using a food processor fitted with the metal blade, add **1 clove garlic** and **4 oz parmesan** (either grated or cut into 1 inch cubes). Process until they are minced.
2. Add **2 cups cilantro**, **1/4 cup pine nuts**, **1 tbsp jalapeños**, and **1/4 tsp salt**, and process until finely minced.
3. With the motor running, slowly add **3/4 cup olive oil** until fully incorporated.

### Step 2: Serving and Usage
4. Toss with hot pasta, potatoes, vegetables, or pot stickers—only enough to barely coat.
5. Alternatively, drizzle a little on cooked cold meat or chicken, or cut a loaf of French bread lengthwise, brush on a little cilantro pesto, and bake or broil until it is heated and crisp.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 5 days, or freeze for longer storage.
