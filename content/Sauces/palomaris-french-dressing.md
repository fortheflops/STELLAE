# Palomaris French Dressing

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Five | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A classic homemade French dressing featuring a sweet and tangy blend of oil, brown sugar, catsup, and vinegar. Perfect for tossing with crisp green salads or serving as a flavorful condiment.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | None | None | 12 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Whisk
* Glass jar

---
## Ingredients

### Dressing Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 c. | [[Oil]] | Vegetable or canola |
| 1 c. | [[Brown Sugar]] | Packed |
| 1 c. | [[Catsup]] | Standard tomato ketchup |
| 1 c. | [[Vinegar]] | Standard or cider |
| 1 | [[Onion]] | Grated |
| 1 teas. | [[Salt]] | Standard |
| 1 teas. | [[Pepper]] | Ground |
| 1 pinch | [[Garlic Salt]] | To taste |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing
1. Combine the **1 c. oil**, **1 c. brown sugar**, **1 c. catsup**, and **1 c. vinegar** in a mixing bowl and whisk until smooth.
2. Stir in the **1 grated onion**, **1 teas. salt**, **1 teas. pepper**, and a pinch of **garlic salt** until fully incorporated.
3. Shake well before serving and keep stored in the refrigerator.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in a sealed glass jar in the refrigerator for up to 2 weeks. Shake well before each use.
