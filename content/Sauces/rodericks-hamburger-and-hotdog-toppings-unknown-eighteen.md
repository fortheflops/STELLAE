## Instructions

### Assembly
1. Put the **as needed carrots**, **as needed mustard**, **as needed cheese**, **to taste salt**, **to taste pepper**, **as needed onions**, **as needed soy sauce**, **as needed hot sauce**, **as needed lettuce**, **as needed orange juice**, **as needed water**, **as needed pickles**, **as needed catsup**, **as needed apples**, **as needed orange**, **as needed tea**, **as needed barbecue sauce**, **as needed tabasco sauce**, **as needed worcestershire sauce**, **as needed lemon**, **as needed relish**, **as needed mayonnaise**, and **as needed steak sauce** on hamburgers.
2. If you put it on a hotdog, cut the hotdog in half a little.
3. Put the toppings on in the exact same order as written on the page until well combined and layered.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Prepare and chop all solid toppings beforehand and store them in separate airtight containers in the refrigerator until ready to assemble.
