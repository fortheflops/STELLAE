# Coleslaw Salad Dressing

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Ruth | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A classic, creamy cooked dressing perfect for coleslaw. This traditional recipe uses a vinegar and water base thickened with flour and enriched with egg yolks.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 10 mins | 30 mins | 12 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Saucepan
* Whisk

---
## Ingredients

### Dressing Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 pt | [[Vinegar]] | White or cider |
| 1 pt | [[Water]] | Filtered |
| 3/4 cup | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 2 tbsp | [[Flour]] | All-purpose |
| 2 tbsp | [[Butter]] | Softened |
| 3 | [[Egg Yolks]] | Beaten |
| to taste | [[Cream]] | Heavy or light |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Cooking the Base
1. In a saucepan, heat **1 pt vinegar** and **1 pt water** until boiling.
2. Separately, mix **3/4 cup sugar**, **2 tbsp flour**, and **2 tbsp butter** until creamed together, then stir into the boiling liquid.
3. Remove from the stove and immediately beat in the **3 beaten egg yolks** until smooth.
4. Finish by thinning the dressing with **cream** to your desired consistency.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to one week.
