# Cocktail Sauce for Shrimp

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Five | ✍️ **Attribution:** Mary Ann Young | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A classic, tangy cocktail sauce recipe from the kitchen of Mary Ann Young. Perfect for pairing with chilled shrimp or other seafood favorites.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5 mins | None | 30 mins | 4 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Whisk
* Measuring spoons

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 tsp | [[Horseradish]] | prepared |
| 3 tbsp | [[Ketchup]] | written as catsup |
| 1 tsp | [[Salt]] |  |
| 2 tbsp | [[Vinegar]] |  |
| 4 tbsp | [[Lemon Juice]] | freshly squeezed |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Tabasco Sauce]] | or to taste |

---

## Instructions

### Preparation
1. In a small mixing bowl, combine the **2 tsp prepared horseradish**, **3 tbsp ketchup**, **1 tsp salt**, **2 tbsp vinegar**, **4 tbsp lemon juice**, and **1/4 tsp tabasco sauce**.
2. Whisk the ingredients together thoroughly until the **1 tsp salt** is completely dissolved and the sauce is smooth and uniform.
3. Cover and chill in the refrigerator for at least **30 mins** before serving to allow the sharp, tangy flavors to meld together beautifully.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 1 week. Stir well before serving.
