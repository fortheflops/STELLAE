# Raspberry Vinaigrette

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A vibrant and tangy homemade raspberry vinaigrette featuring fruit preserves and a smooth oil emulsion. Perfect for elevating fresh summer green salads.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | None | None | 2 1/2 Cups |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Blender
* Glass jar or storage container

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 Tbsp | [[Raspberry Preserves]] | None |
| 1/2 tsp | [[Salt]] | None |
| 1/2 tsp | [[Black Pepper]] | Freshly ground recommended |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Minced Garlic]] | Minced |
| 1/4 Cup | [[Raspberry Vinegar]] | None |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Canola Oil]] | Or similar neutral oil |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Blend the Base
1. In a blender, combine the **1 Tbsp raspberry preserves**, **1/2 tsp salt**, **1/2 tsp black pepper**, **1/4 tsp minced garlic**, and **1/4 cup raspberry vinegar**. **Do not add the oil yet.**
2. Blend these ingredients until well combined and completely smooth.

### Step 2: Emulsify the Oil
3. Turn the blender on a low speed. While the machine is running, slowly drizzle the **1/2 cup canola oil (or similar)** through the opening in the lid until all the oil is added and the vinaigrette is fully emulsified and thoroughly blended until smooth and thick.

### Step 3: Storage
4. Transfer the dressing to a jar or container for storage. Shake well before using.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store sealed in the refrigerator. Shake well before using, as natural separation may occur.
