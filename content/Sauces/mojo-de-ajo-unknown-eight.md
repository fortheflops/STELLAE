# Mojo De Ajo

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Eight | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A fragrant, slow-cooked Cuban garlic sauce infused with olive oil, lime, and red pepper flakes. Perfect for drizzling over seafood, meat, or yuca.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 35 mins | None | 8 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Small saucepan
* Chef's knife
* Glass jar

---
## Ingredients

### Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 large heads | [[Garlic]] | Choped into 1/8" bits |
| 1 cup | [[Olive Oil]] | None |
| 1/2 tsp | [[Salt]] | Plus more to taste |
| 1 tsp | [[Lime Juice]] | Freshly squeezed |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Red Pepper Flakes]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Simmering
1. Chop the **2 large heads garlic** into 1/8" bits.
2. Put the garlic into a small saucepan with **1 cup olive oil** and **1/2 tsp salt**.
3. Set over med-low flame stirring occasionally until it comes to barely a simmer (there should be just a hint of movement on the surface of the oil).
4. Adjust the heat to the lowest possible setting to keep the mixture at a gentle simmer and cook stirring occasionally until the garlic is soft and pale golden—about 30 minutes.

### Step 2: Finishing
5. Add the **1 tsp lime juice** and simmer until most of the juice is evaporated, about 5 minutes.
6. Stir in the **1 teaspoon red pepper flakes**, then taste and add more salt if you think it needs it.
7. Cool and put in a glass jar.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in a glass jar in the refrigerator for up to 2 weeks.
