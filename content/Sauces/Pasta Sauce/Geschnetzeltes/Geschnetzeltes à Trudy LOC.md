## Recipe
| Measurements              | INGREDIENTS             |
| :------------------------ | :---------------------- |
| 1 Package                 | [[Brown Gravy Mix]] (e.g., Maggi, Knorr) |
| 1 Pound                   | [[Beef]] (cut in small cubes, fat omitted) |
| A Little                  | [[Crisco]] (or other shortening/oil) |
| Amount Not Specified      | [[Onion]] (chopped or sliced) |
| To Taste                  | [[Pepper]]              |
| To Taste                  | [[Paprika]]             |
| 1 Tablespoon              | [[Sour Cream]]          |
| Amount Not Specified      | [[Noodles]] (for serving) |
___
## Instructions
1.  Prepare Brown Gravy Mix according to package directions
2.  Cut 1 pound of beef into small cubes, omitting any fat.
3.  In a pan, braise the beef and onion with a little Crisco (or other fat) until the beef is browned.
4.  Pour the prepared gravy mix over the meat.
5.  Bring the mixture to a boil.
6.  Add pepper and paprika to taste.
7.  Stir in 1 tablespoon of sour cream.
8.  Cook until the beef is tender.
9.  Serve with noodles.
