## Instructions

### Step 1: Make the Base
1. Combine **1 cup sugar**, **1/2 cup salad vinegar**, and **1/2 cup water** in a saucepan and bring to a boil.

### Step 2: Mix and Beat
2. Add **1 cup Mazola oil** and **1 tsp celery seed** to the mixture.
3. Beat well until thoroughly combined.

### Step 3: Combine and Serve
4. Prepare assorted fruits including **cantaloupe**, **watermelon**, **grapes**, **banana**, and **kiwi**.
5. Pour dressing over the fruit, let set for an hour, drain, and serve.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The dressing can be prepared ahead of time and stored in the refrigerator, but toss with fruit just before the resting period.
