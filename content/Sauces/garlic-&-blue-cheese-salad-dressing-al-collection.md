# Garlic & Blue Cheese Salad Dressing

> 📜 **Collection:** Al Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A rich and savory homemade salad dressing blending robust garlic, soy sauce, and apple cider vinegar with crumbled blue cheese or seasoned feta.*

## Ingredients

### Dressing Base
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 cup | [[Olive Oil]] | extra virgin |
| 1/4 cup | [[Apple Cider Vinegar]] | None |
| 1/8 cup | [[Soy Sauce]] | None |
| 3 | [[Garlic]] | medium to large cloves |
| 1/8 tsp | [[Black Pepper]] | None |

### Cheese & Finishers
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 oz | [[Blue Cheese]] | or seasoned Feta, crumbled |
| 3 tbsp | [[Parmesan Cheese]] | None |
| 1 tsp | [[Sugar]] | optional, to taste if dressing is too sharp |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Blend & Chill Base
1. Put the first 5 ingredients—**1 cup extra virgin olive oil**, **1/4 cup apple cider vinegar**, **1/8 cup soy sauce**, **3 cloves garlic**, and **1/8 tsp pepper**—into a blender.
2. Blend until completely smooth, then refrigerate the mixture directly in the blender jar or a container until it is slightly cloudy and chilled.

### Step 2: Add Cheese & Adjust
3. Once chilled, crumble half of the **2 oz blue cheese** (or feta) and add it to the blender along with the **3 tbsp parmesan cheese**.
4. Blend slightly, just until the cheeses are incorporated but the texture remains pleasantly crumbly.
5. Taste the dressing; if it seems too sharp, add **1 tsp sugar** and pulse briefly to combine.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight jar in the refrigerator for up to one week. Shake vigorously before using as the oil and vinegar may separate.
