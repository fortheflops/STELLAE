# Fluffy Brown Sugar Icing

> 📜 **Collection:** General Archive | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A classic boiled icing that gets a delicate caramel color and flavor from brown sugar. This light, fluffy meringue-style frosting is made by beating egg whites and sugar over a double boiler until stiff and glossy.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5 mins | 7 mins | None | Frosting for one 8-inch cake |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Double boiler
* Handheld electric mixer
* Heatproof bowl

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 Cup | [[Brown Sugar]] | packed |
| 3 Tbsp | [[Water]] | room temperature |
| 1 | [[Egg White]] | large |
| 1 Pinch | [[Cream of Tartar]] | stabilizer |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Combine and Whip
1. In the top of a double boiler, combine the **1 cup brown sugar**, **3 tbsp water**, **1 egg white**, and **1 pinch cream of tartar**.
2. Place the bowl over simmering water and begin beating with a handheld electric mixer, starting on low speed and increasing to high speed.
3. Beat constantly for approximately **7 minutes** until the icing is thick, glossy, and forms stiff peaks that hold their shape.
4. Remove from the heat and use immediately to frost a cooled cake before the icing sets.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This frosting must be used immediately after preparation as it sets quickly upon cooling. Do not store for later use.
