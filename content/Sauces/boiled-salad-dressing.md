# Boiled Salad Dressing

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A traditional, rich boiled salad dressing thickened with eggs and flour. This versatile sauce can be finished with either sweet or sour cream for a tangy, creamy texture.*

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

## Ingredients

### Base Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 heaping tbsp | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 1 heaping tsp | [[Flour]] | All-purpose |
| 1 rounded tsp | [[Mustard]] | Powdered or prepared |
| q.s. | [[Water]] | Enough to moisten |
| 1/2 cup | [[Vinegar]] | White or cider |
| 3 | [[Eggs]] | Beaten |
| 1 walnut-sized piece | [[Butter]] | Softened |
| 1/2 pt | [[Sweet Cream]] or [[Sour Cream]] | Added at the end |

---

## Instructions

### Preparation and Cooking
1. In a bowl, mix the **1 heaping tbsp sugar**, **1 heaping tsp flour**, and **1 rounded tsp mustard**. Moisten with a small amount of **water** to form a paste.
2. In a double boiler, scald the **1/2 cup vinegar** and pour it over the prepared **sugar**, **flour**, and **mustard** mixture.
3. Whisk in the **3 eggs** and the **1 walnut-sized piece butter**.
4. Cook over the double boiler, stirring constantly, until the mixture is thick enough to coat the back of a spoon.
5. Remove from heat and stir in the **1/2 pt sweet cream or sour cream** until smooth.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to one week.
