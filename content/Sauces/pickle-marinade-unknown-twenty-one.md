# Pickle Marinade

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty One | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A tangy and savory pickle marinade that tenderizes and infuses rich flavor into meat before grilling. Quick to whip up with pantry staples like olive oil and pickle juice.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5 mins | None | 2-4 hours | 4 servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Grill

## Ingredients

### Marinade Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/4 c | [[Olive oil]] | None |
| 1 t | [[Garlic powder]] | None |
| 1/2 t | [[Black pepper]] | None |
| 1/2 t | [[Basil]] or [[Rosemary]] | Optional herb choice |
| As needed | [[Pickle juice]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing and Marinating
1. Mix **1/4 c olive oil**, **1 t garlic powder**, **1/2 t black pepper**, **1/2 t basil or rosemary**, and **pickle juice** together in a bowl.
2. Pour the mixture over your meat and let it marinate for **2-4 hours**.
3. Remove the meat and **grill** until cooked to your desired doneness.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the mixed marinade in an airtight jar in the refrigerator for up to 3 days before using.
