# Stanley Sauce

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A rich and savory curry-infused cream sauce perfect for dipping meat fondue. This classic recipe features caramelized onions and a velvety texture.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 15 mins | None | 3 cups |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Saucepan
* Whisk
* Spatula

---
## Ingredients

### Sauce Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/4 cup | [[Butter]] or Margarine | melted |
| 2 cups | [[Onions]] | minced |
| 1 tbsp | [[Flour]] | all-purpose |
| 1 cup | [[Heavy Cream]] | room temperature |
| 1/2 tsp | [[Curry Powder]] | none |
| 1/2 tsp | [[Salt]] | none |

---

## Instructions

### Preparation
1. Melt the **1/4 cup butter or margarine** in a saucepan over medium heat.
2. Add the **2 cups onions** and cook until they are golden brown and fragrant.
3. Sprinkle in the **1/2 tsp curry powder** and **1 tbsp flour**, stirring well to create a roux.
4. Slowly stir in the **1 cup heavy cream** and **1/2 tsp salt** until smooth.
5. Bring the mixture to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer for 5 minutes until the sauce thickens to a velvety consistency.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days; reheat gently over low heat, adding a splash of cream if it thickens too much.
