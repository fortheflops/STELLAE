| 1/4 tsp. | [[Salt]] | None |
| 2 squares (2 oz) | [[Unsweetened Chocolate]] | Chopped |
| 2 tbsp. | [[Light Corn Syrup]] | None |
| 2 tbsp. | [[Butter]] | None |
| 1 tsp. | [[Vanilla]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Cooking the Syrup
1. Combine **2 c. sugar**, **1 c. water**, **1/4 tsp. salt**, **2 squares (2 oz) unsweetened chocolate**, and **2 tbsp. light corn syrup** in a saucepan.
2. Place over slow heat and stir until dissolved.
3. Cook until a little syrup dropped in cold water forms a soft ball.

### Step 2: Finishing and Spreading
4. Add **2 tbsp. butter** and **1 tsp. vanilla**.
5. Beat until thick and creamy.
6. Spread on cooled cake.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Best used immediately after beating while still spreadable.
