# Remoulade Sauce For Fish or Shrimp

> 📜 **Collection:** The Loraine Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A classic, zesty remoulade sauce featuring a blend of horseradish, tarragon vinegar, and fresh aromatics. Perfect for elevating fried fish or chilled shrimp dishes.*

| 1/2 Cup | [[Celery]] | finely chopped |

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Base
1. Using a garlic press, mince the **1 clove garlic** into a medium mixing bowl.
2. Whisk together the **1/2 cup tarragon vinegar**, **1 tbsp horseradish mustard**, **2 tbsp tomato catsup**, **1 tbsp paprika**, and **1/2 tsp cayenne pepper** until the spices are fully dissolved.

### Step 2: Emulsify and Finish
3. Slowly drizzle the **1 cup salad oil** into the spice mixture while whisking vigorously until the sauce is thoroughly combined and slightly thickened.
4. Fold in the **1/2 cup green onion** and **1/2 cup celery** until evenly distributed throughout the sauce.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 1 week; flavors improve after resting for 30 minutes.
