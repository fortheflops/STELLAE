## Instructions

### Making the Red Chile
1. Boil the **8-10 red chiles** (seeded and stems removed) in water for **30 minutes** until softened.
2. Allow the chiles to cool slightly, then transfer to a blender and blend until completely smooth.
3. Transfer the blended chile to a pot and cook for **2 hours** to develop deep flavor.

### Preparing Carne Adovada
4. Prepare the **1 pork butt** by cutting away excess fat and cubing the meat.
5. Cover the cubed pork completely with the prepared **red chile sauce** and marinate in the refrigerator overnight.
6. Preheat the oven to **425 degrees F**, cook the marinated pork for **30 minutes**, then reduce the temperature to **325 degrees F** and cook for **1 more hour** until the meat is tender and fully cooked.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The red chile sauce can be made ahead and stored in the refrigerator for up to 5 days or frozen for up to 3 months. The pork can marinate in the refrigerator overnight before baking.
