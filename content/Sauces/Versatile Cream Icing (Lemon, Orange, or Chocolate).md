# Versatile Cream Icing (Lemon, Orange, or Chocolate)

> 📜 **Collection:** Jill Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A classic and versatile cream icing featuring a simple base that adapts easily to lemon, orange, or chocolate flavors. This smooth, spreadable frosting is perfect for finishing cakes, cupcakes, and cookies.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 5 mins | None | Frosting for one 8 or 9-inch cake |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Electric hand mixer
* Sifter
* Rubber spatula

---
## Ingredients

### Base Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 4 tablespoons | [[Butter]] | softened |
| 3 cups | [[Confectioners Sugar]] | sifted |
| 1 tablespoon | [[Cream]] | none |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Vanilla Extract]] | none |

### Flavor Options
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3 tablespoons | [[Lemon Juice]] | for lemon variation |
| To taste | [[Lemon Rind]] | grated, for lemon variation |
| 3 tablespoons | [[Orange Juice]] | for orange variation |
| To taste | [[Orange Rind]] | grated, for orange variation |
| 2 ounces | [[Chocolate]] | melted and cooled, for chocolate variation |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Base
1. In a mixing bowl, beat the **4 tablespoons butter** until creamy and smooth.

### Step 2: Incorporate Flavors
2. Stir in the **1 tablespoon cream**, **1 teaspoon vanilla extract**, and your chosen flavoring: **3 tablespoons lemon juice**, **3 tablespoons orange juice**, or **2 ounces melted chocolate**.

### Step 3: Sweeten and Adjust
3. Gradually beat in the **3 cups confectioners sugar** until the icing is smooth and reaches a spreadable consistency; add more liquid if too thick, or more sugar if too thin.

### Step 4: Final Finish
4. If making the citrus version, stir in the grated **lemon rind** or **orange rind**.
5. Beat for another minute until the icing is light and fluffy, then spread immediately onto a cooled cake.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days; bring to room temperature and re-whip before using.
