# Hollandaise Sauce

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty One | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A quick and easy blender hollandaise sauce made with egg beaters, lemon juice, and melted oleo. Perfect for eggs benedict and vegetables, this foolproof recipe comes together in minutes.*

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 c | [[Egg Beaters]] |  |
| 2 T | [[Lemon Juice]] |  |
| 1/3 c | [[Oleo]] | melted |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Blending
1. Combine **1/2 c egg beaters** and **2 T lemon juice** in the blender.
2. While blending, slowly pour in **1/3 c oleo** until combined and smooth.

### Step 2: Holding
3. Mixture may be held over hot water (not boiling) for **10 minutes** before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Mixture may be held over hot water (not boiling) for up to 10 minutes.
