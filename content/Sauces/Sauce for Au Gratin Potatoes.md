# Sauce for Au Gratin Potatoes

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A rich and creamy homemade cheese sauce perfect for topping au gratin potatoes or roasted vegetables. This versatile recipe uses a classic roux base for a smooth, velvety consistency.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 15 mins | None | 4–6 Servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* saucepan
* wire whisk

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 Stick | [[Butter]] | unsalted |
| 2 Tablespoons | [[Flour]] | all-purpose |
| 2 Cups | [[Milk]] | whole |
| 1 Cup | [[Carnation Milk]] | evaporated |
| 1 Cup | [[Water]] | room temperature |
| A Good Size | [[Chunky Cheese]] | shredded or cubed |
| To Taste | [[Garlic Salt]] | none |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Roux
1. In a saucepan, melt **1/2 stick butter** over medium heat.
2. Add **2 tablespoons flour** to the melted butter and stir constantly for 1-2 minutes until a smooth, golden paste forms and the raw flour smell dissipates.

### Step 2: Create the Sauce
3. Slowly whisk in **2 cups milk**, **1 cup Carnation milk**, and **1 cup water**, whisking continuously to prevent lumps.
4. Season with **garlic salt** to taste.
5. Add the **chunky cheese** and stir until completely melted and the sauce is smooth.
6. Bring to a gentle simmer and cook, stirring occasionally, until the sauce thickens to a velvety consistency that coats the back of a spoon.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Leftover sauce can be stored in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 4 days; reheat gently on the stovetop.
