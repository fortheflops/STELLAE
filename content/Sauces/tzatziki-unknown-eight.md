# Tzatziki

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Eight | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A traditional, creamy homemade Tzatziki sauce featuring grated cucumber, garlic, and rich Greek yoghurt. Perfectly chilled and balanced with a splash of red wine vinegar and olive oil.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | None | 2 hours 15 mins | 4 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Fine mesh strainer
* Whisk
* Grater

---
## Ingredients

### Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 cup | [[Greek yoghurt]] | full fat |
| 1 | [[organic cucumber]] | small, 3-inch section used |
| 1/2 to 3/4 | [[garlic clove]] | large |
| 1 Tbsp | [[olive oil]] | None |
| 1 tsp | [[red wine vinegar]] | None |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Kosher salt]] | None |
| 3 grinds | [[black pepper]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Preparation
1. Cut a **3" section of washed and dried cuke**. Grate the **cucumber** and drain in a **fine mesh strainer** for **15 min**.
2. Grate the **1/2 to 3/4 large garlic clove** into the **1 cup Greek yoghurt**.
3. Add **1 Tbsp olive oil**, **1 tsp red wine vinegar**, **1/4 tsp Kosher salt**, and **3 grinds black pepper**.
4. Push the **cucumber** with fingers to release more liquid, then add the **cucumber** to the other ingredients and whisk for **1 minute** until everything is combined.
5. Refrigerate for **2 hrs** before using.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Refrigerate for at least 2 hours before serving to allow the flavors to meld properly.
