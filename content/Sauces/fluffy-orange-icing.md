# Fluffy Orange Icing

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A vintage, citrus-infused cream cheese frosting that yields a light and fluffy topping. Perfect for flavoring and decorating cakes like orange chiffon.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | None | None | 1 cake icing |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Electric mixer
* Microplane or grater

---
## Ingredients

### Fluffy Orange Icing
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 1/2 | [[3 oz. packages cream cheese]] | softened |
| 2 1/2 cups | [[confectioners sugar]] | sifted |
| 2 | [[oranges]] | grated rind of |
| As needed | [[orange juice]] | optional, for thinning |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing the Base
1. Cream the **1 1/2 3 oz. packages cream cheese** in a mixing bowl until light and fluffy.

### Step 2: Adding Sugar and Flavor
2. Gradually add the **2 1/2 cups confectioners sugar**. Beat well until smooth.
3. Stir in the grated rind of **2 oranges**.
4. If the mixture is too thick, add a few drops of **orange juice** until it reaches a perfect consistency for spreading.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Can be stored in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days; bring to room temperature and re-whip before using.
