## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 1/2 | [[3 oz. packages cream cheese]] | Softened |
| 2 1/4 C. | [[Confectioners sugar]] | Sifted |
| 2 | [[Oranges]] | Grated rind of |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing and Beating
1. Cream the **1 1/2 3 oz. packages cream cheese** until light and fluffy.
2. Gradually add **2 1/4 C. confectioners sugar** and beat well.
3. Stir in the **grated rind of 2 oranges**. If too thick, add a few drops of **orange juice** until it reaches a perfect spreading consistency.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days; bring to room temperature and stir gently before using.
