## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 Cup | [[Catsup]] | or 8oz tomato sauce + lemon juice |
| 1/4 Cup | [[Sugar]] | granulated |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Vinegar]] | herb flavored preferred |
| 1 Teaspoon | [[Salt]] | fine grain |
| 1 Small | [[Onion]] | quartered |
| 1/2 Teaspoon | [[Celery Seed]] | or celery salt |
| 1/4 Teaspoon | [[Paprika]] | sweet |
| 3/4 Cup | [[Salad Oil]] | or [[Olive Oil]], or half-and-half mixture |
| 2-3 | [[Onions]] | green, trimmed |
| 1 Clove | [[Garlic]] | peeled |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Initial Blend
1. Place the **1 cup catsup**, **1/4 cup sugar**, **1/2 cup vinegar**, **1 teaspoon salt**, **1 small onion**, **1/2 teaspoon celery seed**, **1/4 teaspoon paprika**, and **3/4 cup salad oil** into a blender.
2. Blend for **30 seconds** until the mixture is smooth and emulsified.

### Step 2: Final Incorporation
3. Add the **2-3 green onions** and **1 clove garlic** to the blender.
4. Blend again briefly until the aromatics are finely minced and fully incorporated into the dressing.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in a tightly covered glass jar in the refrigerator for up to 2 weeks; shake well before serving.
