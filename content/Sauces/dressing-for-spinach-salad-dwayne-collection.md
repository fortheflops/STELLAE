# Dressing for Spinach Salad

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A classic, sweet and tangy warm vinaigrette perfect for wilting fresh spinach. This quick microwave method dissolves the sugar effortlessly for a smooth, rich dressing.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 2 mins | None | 6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Microwave
* Microwave-safe bowl
* Whisk

---
## Ingredients

### Dressing Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3/4 cup | [[Oil]] | vegetable or canola |
| 1/2 cup | [[Sugar]] | granulated |
| 1/4 cup | [[Vinegar]] | white or cider |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Salt]] | fine |
| 2 tsp | [[Prepared Mustard]] | yellow or Dijon |
| To taste | [[Green Onions]] | chopped |
| To taste | [[Black Pepper]] | cracked |

### Salad Base
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 bunch | [[Spinach]] | chopped |
| 4 slices | [[Bacon]] | cooked and crumbled |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Heat the Dressing Base
1. In a microwave-safe bowl, combine the **1/2 cup sugar**, **3/4 cup oil**, and **1/4 cup vinegar**.
2. Heat the mixture in the microwave on high for 1 to 2 minutes, or until the **1/2 cup sugar** is completely dissolved and the liquid is hot.

### Step 2: Emulsify and Assemble
3. Whisk in the **1/4 tsp salt**, **2 tsp prepared mustard**, **chopped green onions**, and **cracked pepper** until the dressing is well blended and emulsified.
4. Pour the warm dressing over the **chopped spinach** and crumbled **bacon** in a large salad bowl, tossing gently to coat and slightly wilt the spinach before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The dressing can be prepared ahead of time and stored in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 1 week. Reheat gently in the microwave before tossing with fresh spinach.
