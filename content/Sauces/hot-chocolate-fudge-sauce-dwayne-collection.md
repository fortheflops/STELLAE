# Hot Chocolate Fudge Sauce

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *An incredibly rich and velvety chocolate fudge sauce slow-cooked to perfection in a double boiler. Perfect for drizzling over ice cream or cakes, this classic recipe yields an exceptionally creamy texture that stores beautifully.*

---

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 2 hours | None | 24 servings |

* Stirring spoon

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 cup | [[Butter]] |  |
| 6 oz. | [[Bitter Chocolate]] |  |
| 1 tall can | [[Evaporated Milk]] |  |
| 2 cups | [[Powdered Sugar]] | sifted |

---

## Instructions

### Preparation & Melting
1. Melt the **1/2 cup butter** and **6 oz. bitter chocolate** together in the top of a double boiler over gently simmering water, stirring until completely smooth and integrated.

### Mixing & Slow Cooking
2. Add the **1 tall can evaporated milk** and **2 cups powdered sugar** alternately to the melted chocolate mixture, stirring thoroughly after each addition to maintain a smooth, glossy consistency.
3. Let cook in the double boiler for **2 hours**, stirring occasionally. The sauce will become beautifully thick, rich, and creamy as it slow-cooks.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This sauce can be kept covered in the refrigerator indefinitely. Simply reheat the desired amount gently before serving.
