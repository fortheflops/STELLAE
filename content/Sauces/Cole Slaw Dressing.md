# Cole Slaw Dressing

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A simple and classic creamy dressing perfect for fresh coleslaw. This versatile recipe balances tangy vinegar with a touch of sweetness for a perfect crunch.*

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/4 Cup | [[Cream]] | Cold |
| 1 Tbsp | [[Vinegar]] | White or apple cider |
| 1-2 Tbsp | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| To Taste | [[Salt]] | Fine grain |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Whisk and Season
1. In a small mixing bowl, combine the **1/4 cup cream**, **1 tbsp vinegar**, **1-2 tbsp sugar**, and **salt**.
2. Use a wire whisk to blend the mixture until the **sugar** is fully dissolved and the dressing is smooth and creamy.
3. Taste the dressing and adjust the **sugar** and **salt** levels until the flavor profile is balanced to your preference.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days; whisk again before serving.
