## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
| 2                  | [[Tomatoes]]                        |
| 1 Small            | [[Onion]]                           |
| 2                  | Seeded, Canned [[Jalapeno Peppers]] |
| 1 Clove            | [[Garlic]]                          |
| 1/4 Cup           | [[Tomato Juice]]                    |
___
### Instructions
1. Chop tomatoes, onion, and peppers.
2. Mince garlic.
3. Stir in tomato juice.
4. Refrigerate until ready to serve.
