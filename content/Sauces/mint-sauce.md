# Mint Sauce

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A traditional, simple mint sauce recipe featuring a balanced blend of vinegar and brown sugar. Perfect for pairing with roasted meats or lamb dishes.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5 mins | 5 mins | 1 hour | 4 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Small saucepan
* Measuring spoons

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 Tbsp | [[Mint]] | Leaves, chopped fine |
| 2 1/2 Tbsp | [[Brown Sugar]] | Packed |
| 3 Tbsp | [[Vinegar]] | Standard |

---

## Instructions

### Preparation
1. Heat the **3 Tbsp vinegar** and **2 1/2 Tbsp brown sugar** in a small saucepan until the sugar is fully dissolved.
2. Remove from heat and add the **2 Tbsp mint**.
3. Let the mixture stand for 1 hour to allow the flavors to infuse before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This sauce can be stored in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days.
