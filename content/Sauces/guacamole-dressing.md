# Guacamole Dressing

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A fresh and creamy guacamole-inspired dressing perfect for salads or as a dip. This simple mixture combines mashed avocado with tomato, garlic, and lemon for a bright, flavorful finish.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 0 mins | None | 1 cup |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Fork
* Knife

---
## Ingredients

### Dressing Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3/4 cup | [[Avocado]] | Mashed |
| 1/3 cup | [[Tomato]] | Peeled and chopped |
| 1 clove | [[Garlic]] | Grated |
| 1 tsp | [[Lemon juice]] | Fresh |
| 1/2 tsp | [[Salt]] | To taste |

---

## Instructions

### Preparation
1. In a small bowl, combine the **3/4 cup mashed avocado**, **1/3 cup peeled chopped tomato**, **1 clove grated garlic**, **1 tsp lemon juice**, and **1/2 tsp salt**.
2. Mix all ingredients thoroughly until the dressing reaches a uniform, creamy consistency.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 24 hours; press plastic wrap directly onto the surface to prevent browning.
