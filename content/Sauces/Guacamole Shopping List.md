# Guacamole Shopping List

> 📜 **Collection:** UK15 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A creamy and zesty guacamole recipe featuring a unique blend of picante sauce and green chiles. This simple dip is perfect for parties and pairs excellently with tortilla chips.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 0 mins | None | 4–6 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Fork or potato masher
* Knife

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 large | [[Avocados]] | Ripe |
| 1 jar | [[Pace Picante Sauce]] | Mild or medium |
| As Needed | [[Sour Cream]] | For creaminess |
| 1 (4 ounce) can | [[Green Chiles]] | Drained |
| To Taste | [[Garlic Powder]] | None |
| To Taste | [[Salt]] | None |
| Juice of 1/2 | [[Lime]] | Freshly squeezed |
| 1 small | [[Tomato]] | Diced |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Base
1. In a medium bowl, mash the **2 large avocados** until they reach your desired level of chunkiness.

### Step 2: Season and Combine
2. Stir in the **juice of 1/2 lime**, **to taste garlic powder**, and **to taste salt** until well incorporated.
3. Gently fold in the **1 jar pace picante sauce**, **as needed sour cream**, **1 (4 ounce) can green chiles**, and **1 small tomato** until the mixture is uniform and fragrant.
4. Serve immediately while fresh to prevent oxidation.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container with plastic wrap pressed directly against the surface of the guacamole to prevent browning; consume within 24 hours.
