## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
| 2 Cups            | [[Onions]], diced            |
| 8 Cloves          | [[Garlic]] (if large - use 6)|
|                   | [[Grease]]                  |
| 3 Cans            | Chopped [[Chili]]           |
| 1 Can            | [[Tomatoes]], Drained       |
| 2 Pounds           | [[Velveeta Cheese]]         |
___
### Instructions
1. Fry onions and garlic in very little grease until clear.
2. Add chopped chili and drained tomatoes.
3. Add Velveeta cheese.
4. Melt, mix and serve.
