# Raw Chili Sauce

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A traditional, no-cook raw chili sauce recipe featuring a vibrant blend of garden-fresh tomatoes, peppers, and aromatic spices. This classic condiment is perfect for canning and adds a zesty depth to any meal.*

## Instructions

### Preparation and Storage
1. In a large bowl, combine the **1 peck ripe tomatoes**, **2 large cups celery**, **1 cup white mustard seed**, **1/2 cup salt**, **2 tsp ground cloves**, **2 tsp black pepper**, **4 tsp ground cinnamon**, **6 green peppers**, **1 quart vinegar**, **2 1/2 cups sugar**, and **2 large cups onions**.
2. Mix all the ingredients thoroughly and let the mixture stand overnight to allow the flavors to meld.
3. Pack the mixture into clean jars and seal tightly for storage.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This sauce must sit overnight before jarring to ensure proper flavor development.
