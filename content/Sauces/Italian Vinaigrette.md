# Italian Vinaigrette

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A simple, customizable Italian vinaigrette base enhanced with dried herbs and seasonings. This archival recipe offers a quick way to elevate a standard vinegar and oil dressing.*

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| As Needed | [[Greens]] | Fresh, washed |
| To Taste | [[Salt]] | Fine grain |
| To Taste | [[Pepper]] | Freshly cracked |
| As Needed | [[White Vinegar]] | Additional for acidity |
| As Needed | [[Italian Seasoning]] | Dried |
| As Needed | [[Thyme]] | Dried |
| 1 bottle | [[White Vinegar]] & [[Oil]] Dressing Base | Commercial or pre-mixed |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Season the Greens
1. In a large bowl, toss the **as needed greens** with **to taste salt** and **to taste pepper** until evenly coated.

### Step 2: Prepare the Vinaigrette
2. In a separate container, combine the **1 bottle white vinegar & oil dressing base** with **as needed white vinegar**, **as needed italian seasoning**, and **as needed thyme**.
3. Whisk thoroughly to incorporate the herbs, then let the mixture sit for **5 minutes** to allow the flavors to meld before bottling.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in a sealed glass bottle in the refrigerator for up to 2 weeks; shake well before each use.
