# Fruit Dressing

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Five | ✍️ **Attribution:** Mary Ann Young | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A delightful fruit dressing from the recipe file of Mary Ann Young, combining pineapple and lemon juice with whipped cream. Perfect for enhancing your favorite fruit salads.*

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

* Saucepan
* Mixing bowl

## Ingredients

### Dressing Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 cups | [[Pineapple juice]] | None |
| 1/4 cup | [[Lemon juice]] | Freshly squeezed |
| 1/4 cup | [[Sugar]] | None |
| 1 dash | [[Salt]] | None |
| As needed | [[Cornstarch]] | For thickening |
| 1 pint | [[Cream]] | Sweetened, whipped |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Cook and Chill
1. Combine **2 cups pineapple juice**, **1/4 cup lemon juice**, **1/4 cup sugar**, and **1 dash salt** in a saucepan. Bring to a boil and thicken with **cornstarch**.
2. Remove from heat and chill until cold and set.

### Step 2: Combine
3. Whip **1 pint cream (sweetened)** until stiff peaks form.
4. Gently fold the whipped cream into the chilled pineapple mixture until smooth and uniform.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Keep refrigerated in an airtight container for up to 2 days before serving.
