## Recipe
| Measurements        | Ingredients                                        |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| One Cup                          | [[Brown Sugar]]                           |
| One Cup                          | [[Soy Sauce]]                             |
| One Cup                          | [[Water]]                                 |
| $\frac{1}{2}$ Cup                | Vegetable [[Oil]]                         |
| One Teaspoon                     | [[Lemon Juice]]                           |
| $\frac{1}{2}$ Teaspoon           | [[Pepper]]                                |
| $\frac{1}{2}$ Teaspoon           | [[Ginger]] (Ground)                       |
| $\frac{1}{2}$ Teaspoon           | [[Garlic Salt]]                           |
___
## Instructions
1.  Mix all ingredients together in a bowl until the sugar is dissolved.
2.  Use as a marinade for chicken, steak, pork, or most anything else.
3.  Try to marinate for at least a full day or overnight for best results.
