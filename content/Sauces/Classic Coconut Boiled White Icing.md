# Classic Coconut Boiled White Icing

> 📜 **Collection:** General Archive | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A light, fluffy, and glossy boiled icing, perfect for a classic coconut cake. This stable meringue-style frosting is made by whipping a hot sugar syrup into beaten egg whites.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5 mins | 15 mins | None | Frosting for a three-layer cake |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* heavy-bottomed saucepan
* candy thermometer
* electric mixer
* large mixing bowl

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 1/2 cups | [[Sugar]] | granulated |
| 1 cup | [[Water]] | room temperature |
| 6 tablespoons | [[Karo Syrup]] | white |
| 6 | [[Egg Whites]] | large, room temperature |
| 1/3 cup | [[Powdered Sugar]] | sifted |
| 2-3 cups | [[Coconut]] | shredded, for layering and coating |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Syrup
1. In a heavy-bottomed saucepan, combine the **1 1/2 cups sugar**, **1 cup water**, and **6 tablespoons Karo syrup**. Bring to a boil over medium heat, stirring until the sugar dissolves.
2. Continue to boil, without stirring, until the syrup reaches the soft-ball stage (**238°F / 114°C**) on a candy thermometer.

### Step 2: Whip the Meringue
3. While the syrup is boiling, beat the **6 egg whites** in a large, clean mixing bowl on high speed until stiff peaks form.
4. With the mixer still running on high speed, very slowly and carefully pour the hot syrup in a thin, steady stream into the beaten **egg whites**.
5. Continue beating constantly until the icing is thick, glossy, and holds its shape.

### Step 3: Finish and Assemble
6. Add the **1/3 cup powdered sugar** and beat until just combined.
7. Spread the icing on the first layer of a cooled cake and sprinkle with **2-3 cups coconut**. Add the second layer and repeat. Place the third layer on top, then ice the top and sides of the entire cake and sprinkle generously with **coconut**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This icing is best used immediately while fresh and glossy. It does not store well for long periods as it may lose its structural integrity.
