# Classic Pesto

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Eight | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A vibrant and aromatic classic Italian pesto made with fresh basil, toasted pine nuts, and rich Parmesan cheese. Perfect for tossing with pasta, spreading on sandwiches, or drizzling over fresh tomatoes.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | None | None | 4 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Food processor
* Spatula

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 cups | [[Basil]] | fresh |
| 2/3 cup | [[Olive Oil]] | None |
| 1 cup | [[Parmesan Cheese]] | grated |
| 2 cloves | [[Garlic]] | None |
| 1/4 cup | [[Pine Nuts]] | roasted |

---

## Instructions

### Preparation
1. In a food processor, combine the **2 cloves garlic** and **1/4 cup roasted pine nuts**, pulsing several times until finely minced.
2. Add the **2 cups fresh basil** and **1 cup grated parmesan** to the bowl.
3. With the motor running, slowly drizzle in the **2/3 cup olive oil** and process until semi-smooth, vibrant green, and fully emulsified.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 5 days. Pour a thin layer of olive oil over the top surface before sealing to prevent oxidation and browning.
