# Prosciutto Spaghetti Sauce

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A savory and aromatic Italian pasta sauce featuring rich prosciutto, tomatoes, and garlic. Perfect for tossing with farfalle and finishing with freshly grated parmesan.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Sauté the Aromatics
1. In a skillet, sauté the **1 chopped onion** and **3 cloves garlic** in **olive oil** until fragrant and translucent.

### Step 2: Build the Sauce
2. Add the **1/2 tsp. crushed red pepper flakes**, **1 large or 2 small cans chopped tomatoes**, and **1 can tomato sauce** to the skillet. Simmer for **30 minutes** until rich and slightly thickened.

### Step 3: Finish and Serve
3. At the last minute, stir in the **1/4 lb. approx. prosciutto** (cut into ribbon strips) and **chopped basil** until heated through.
4. Serve the sauce over hot **cooked farfalle** and sprinkle generously with **parmesan**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftover sauce in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days, or freeze without the prosciutto for up to 2 months.
