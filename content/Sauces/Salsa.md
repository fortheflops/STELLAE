# Salsa

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A vibrant and fresh homemade salsa combining ripe tomatoes, onions, and jalapeños. Perfect as a quick condiment or dip for your favorite snacks.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | None | 30 mins | 4 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Cutting board
* Chef's knife
* Mixing bowl
* Measuring cup

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 whole | [[Tomatoes]] | Chopped |
| 1 small | [[Onion]] | Chopped |
| 2 whole | [[Jalapeno Peppers]] | Seeded and canned |
| 1 clove | [[Garlic]] | Minced |
| 1/4 cup | [[Tomato Juice]] | Fresh |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Mix
1. Chop the **2 whole tomatoes**, **1 small onion**, and **2 whole seeded, canned jalapeno peppers** on a cutting board.
2. Mince the **1 clove garlic** finely.
3. Combine all the chopped vegetables and minced garlic in a mixing bowl.
4. Stir in the **1/4 cup tomato juice** until well incorporated.

### Step 2: Chill and Serve
5. Refrigerate the mixture until ready to serve, allowing the flavors to meld together for at least **30 minutes** until fragrant and chilled.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store sealed in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days.
