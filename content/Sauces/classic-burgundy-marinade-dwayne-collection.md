# Classic Burgundy Marinade

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A rich and savory marinade featuring burgundy wine and soy sauce, perfect for tenderizing and flavoring meats. This classic blend balances acidity and salt for a deep, complex profile.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5 mins | 0 mins | 8 hours | Makes approx. 3 cups |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Whisk

---
## Ingredients

### Marinade Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 1/2 cups | [[Burgundy Wine]] | Dry red |
| 1 cup | [[Soy Sauce]] | Standard |
| 2 tbsp | [[Garlic Powder]] | None |
| 1/4 cup | [[Oil]] | Vegetable or canola |
| 1/2 cup | [[Catsup]] | Tomato ketchup |

---

## Instructions

### Preparation
1. In a medium bowl, combine the **1 1/2 cups burgundy wine**, **1 cup soy sauce**, **2 tbsp garlic powder**, **1/4 cup oil**, and **1/2 cup catsup**.
2. Stir the mixture thoroughly until the **garlic powder** is fully incorporated and the sauce is uniform.
3. Add your choice of protein to the bowl, ensuring it is fully submerged, and marinate overnight in the refrigerator for maximum flavor penetration.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The marinade can be prepared and stored in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 1 week before use.
