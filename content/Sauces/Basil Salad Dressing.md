# Basil Salad Dressing

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Pat Haelie | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A vibrant and aromatic herb-forward dressing featuring fresh basil, nutty pine nuts, and a blend of savory cheeses. This versatile sauce is perfect for elevating fresh garden salads with tomatoes and crisp greens.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 0 mins | None | 8 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Jar with tight-fitting lid
* Chef's knife
* Measuring cups

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 1/2 Cups | [[Basil]] | Fresh, chopped fine |
| 3 Ounces | [[Parmesan Cheese]] | Grated |
| 1/4 Cup | [[Olive Oil]] | Extra virgin |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Oil]] | Salad oil, soy oil suggested |
| 1/4 Cup | [[Pine Nuts]] | Ground |
| 1/3 Cup | [[Feta Cheese]] | Crumbled |
| 2 Cloves | [[Garlic]] | Crushed |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Combine and Emulsify
1. In a jar with a tight-fitting lid, combine the **1 1/2 cups fresh basil**, **3 ounces grated parmesan cheese**, **1/4 cup olive oil**, **1/2 cup salad oil**, **1/4 cup ground pine nuts**, **1/3 cup crumbled feta cheese**, and **2 crushed garlic cloves**.
2. Seal the jar tightly and shake vigorously until the mixture is well combined and emulsified into a thick, fragrant dressing.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 5 days; shake well before serving as the oils may separate.
