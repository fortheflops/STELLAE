# Chocolate Sauce

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A rich and velvety homemade chocolate sauce perfect for drizzling over your favorite desserts. This classic recipe combines melted chocolate, powdered sugar, and cream for a decadent finish.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5 mins | 10 mins | None | 4 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Saucepan
* Whisk

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3 oz | [[Chocolate]] | chopped |
| 5 tbsp | [[Powdered Sugar]] | sifted |
| 1 tbsp | [[Water]] | room temperature |
| 1/2 pt | [[Cream]] | heavy |
| 1 | [[Egg Yolk]] | beaten |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Cooking the Base
1. In a saucepan, combine the **3 oz chocolate**, **5 tbsp powdered sugar**, and **1 tbsp water**.
2. Cook over low heat, stirring constantly, until the mixture is completely smooth.

### Step 2: Finishing
3. Stir in the **1/2 pt cream** until well incorporated.
4. Remove from heat and slowly pour the hot mixture over the **1 beaten egg yolk**, whisking constantly to prevent curdling until the sauce is glossy and thickened.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days; gently reheat over low heat before serving.
