# Classic Cream Cheese Icing

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Five | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A rich and creamy homemade icing made with cream cheese, butter, and powdered sugar. Perfect for topping cakes or cookies, finished with a generous amount of nuts.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | None | None | 12 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Electric mixer

---
## Ingredients

### Icing Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 8-oz pkg. | [[Cream Cheese]] | Softened |
| 1 stick | [[Butter]] | Softened |
| 1 box | [[Powdered Sugar]] | Sifted |
| 1 cup | [[Nuts]] | Chopped |

---

## Instructions

### Mixing Instructions
1. Cream together the **1 8-oz pkg. cream cheese** and **1 stick butter** until smooth and fluffy.
2. Add the **1 box powdered sugar** gradually, mixing continuously until fully incorporated and smooth.
3. Blend in the **1 cup nuts** until evenly distributed throughout the icing.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days. Bring to room temperature and re-whip slightly before using.
