## Recipe
| Measurements      | Ingredients                   |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 pound           | Fresh [[Cranberries]]             |
| 2 cups            | [[Water]]                       |
| 2 cups            | [[Sugar]]                       |
___
## Instructions
1.  Wash the cranberries.
2.  In a saucepan, boil the cranberries with the water until the berries stop popping.
3.  Strain the mixture through a sieve into a clean pot, pressing on the solids to extract as much pulp as possible. Discard the skins and seeds.
4.  Bring the strained liquid to a rapid boil. Add the sugar and continue to boil for 8 to 10 minutes, or until a drop of the sauce jells when placed on a cold plate.
5.  Immediately pour the hot mixture into a mold that has been rinsed with cold water (do not dry).
6.  Chill in the refrigerator until firm.
