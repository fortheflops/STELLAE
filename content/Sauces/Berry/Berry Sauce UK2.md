## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
| 3/4 Cup            | [[Berry Juice]]              |
| 1/2 Cup            | [[Sour Cream]]              |
| 2 Teaspoons        | [[Cornstarch]]               |
___
### Instructions
1. Combine juice and cornstarch in saucepan, mixing until smooth.
2. Cook over medium heat, stirring constantly until mixture thickens and loses starchy taste.
3. Chill.
4. When ready to serve, fold juice into sour cream.
