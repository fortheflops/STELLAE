## Instructions

### Preparation & Simmering
1. In a medium saucepan, combine the **1 cup tomato ketchup**, **2 tbsp butter**, **1/4 cup molasses**, **2 tbsp vinegar**, **2 tbsp minced onion**, **1 tsp garlic salt**, **1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce**, **2 tsp dry mustard**, **1/2 cup brown sugar**, **1 tsp salt**, **1/4 tsp pepper**, **1/4 tsp chili pepper**, and **1 dash Tabasco sauce**.
2. Bring the mixture to a gentle simmer over low heat, stirring occasionally to ensure the **1/2 cup brown sugar** dissolves and the **2 tbsp butter** melts completely. Simmer for 10 to 15 minutes until the sauce has darkened slightly, thickened to a glossy consistency, and the flavors are fully married.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container or jar in the refrigerator for up to 2 weeks. The flavors will deepen and meld further after sitting overnight.
