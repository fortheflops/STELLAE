# Brandy Cream Sauce

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Ann | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A rich and velvety brandy-infused cream sauce perfect for drizzling over desserts. This vintage recipe offers a decadent finish with a delicate hint of nutmeg.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 10 mins | None | 4 Servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Whisk
* Saucepan

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3 | [[Eggs]] | Beaten |
| 1/4 cup | [[Cream]] | Heavy |
| 1/2 cup | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 1/2 cup | [[Brandy]] | High quality |
| 1 pint | [[Hot Cream]] | Heated |
| As needed | [[Nutmeg]] | Freshly grated |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Base
1. In a mixing bowl, beat the **3 eggs** until smooth.
2. Whisk in the **1/4 cup cream** and **1/2 cup sugar** until fully incorporated.
3. Slowly beat in the **1/2 cup brandy**.

### Step 2: Thicken and Serve
4. Transfer the mixture to a saucepan over low heat and slowly whisk in the **1 pint hot cream**.
5. Continue to whisk gently until the sauce thickens enough to coat the back of a spoon; do not let it boil to avoid curdling the **3 eggs**.
6. Remove from heat and finish by grating **nutmeg** over the top before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This sauce can be kept warm in a double boiler for up to 30 minutes, or refrigerated and gently reheated over low heat.
