# Frantz Company Salsa

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A quick and flavorful homemade salsa recipe preserved on Frantz Manufacturing Company stationery. Combining diced tomatoes, green chiles, celery, onion, and jalapeno, it makes the perfect vibrant dip or condiment.*

## Ingredients

### Salsa Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 (14 oz) cans | [[Hunts Diced Tomatoes]] | None |
| 1 (8 oz) can | [[Hunts Tomato Sauce]] | None |
| 1 (4 oz) can | [[Diced Green Chiles]] | None |
| 1/2 cup | [[Celery]] | diced |
| 1/2 cup | [[Onion]] | diced |
| 1-2 tsp | [[Jalapeno Peppers]] | whole, seeded, and diced |

---

## Instructions

### Assembly and Chilling
1. In a medium mixing bowl, combine the **2 (14 oz) cans Hunts Diced Tomatoes**, **1 (8 oz) can Hunts Tomato Sauce**, and **1 (4 oz) can Diced Green Chiles**.
2. Stir in the **1/2 cup diced Celery**, **1/2 cup diced Onion**, and **1-2 tsp Jalapeno Peppers** (ensuring they are seeded and finely diced).
3. Mix thoroughly until all ingredients are evenly distributed and the mixture is well-blended.
4. Cover the bowl tightly and refrigerate for at least **1 hour** before serving to allow the spicy jalapeno and fresh onion flavors to meld together beautifully.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 5 days. The flavors will continue to deepen and intensify overnight.
