# Angel Whip Dressing

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A light and airy sweet fruit dressing made with lemon, orange, butter, and whipped evaporated milk. Perfect for elevating fruit salads or serving as a delicate dessert sauce.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 10 mins | 30 mins | 1.5 cups |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Sifter
* Small saucepan
* Whisk
* Mixing bowl
* Hand mixer

## Ingredients

### Dressing Base
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/3 cup | [[Sugar]] | None |
| 1 tbsp | [[Flour]] | None |
| 1 | [[Egg]] | beaten |
| 1/4 cup | [[Water]] | None |
| Juice & grated rind of 1/2 | [[Lemon]] | None |
| Juice & grated rind of 1/2 | [[Orange]] | None |
| 2 tsp | [[Butter]] | None |
| 1/2 cup | [[Evaporated Milk]] | undiluted Carnation milk, chilled ice-crystal cold for whipping |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Cook the Cooked Base
1. Sift together the **1/3 cup sugar** and **1 tbsp flour** into a small saucepan.
2. Beat the **1 egg** and add it along with the **1/4 cup water**, **juice & grated rind of 1/2 lemon**, **juice & grated rind of 1/2 orange**, and **2 tsp butter** to the dry ingredients.
3. Cook over low heat, stirring constantly, until thickened and smooth.
4. Remove from heat and allow to cool completely.

### Step 2: Whip and Fold
5. In a chilled bowl, whip the **1/2 cup Carnation milk** (chilled ice-crystal cold) until stiff and frothy.
6. Beat the whipped milk into the cooled custard mixture until light and homogeneous.
7. Serve heavenly with any fruit salad or as a dessert.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Chilled thoroughly before serving. Best consumed the same day it is whipped.
