# Lemony Fruit Dressing

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A classic, creamy vintage dressing featuring a sweet and tangy lemon base perfect for fruit salads. This versatile sauce combines citrus notes with a marshmallow-thickened texture for a unique flavor profile.*

* Mixing bowl

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/3 Cup | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 1 Tbsp | [[Flour]] | All-purpose |
| 1/2 tsp | [[Lemon]] Peel | Grated |
| 1/4 Cup | [[Lemon Juice]] | Freshly squeezed |
| 1 | [[Egg]] | Well beaten |
| 1 Cup | [[Marshmallows]] | Tiny |
| 1 Cup | [[Mayonnaise]] or [[Salad Dressing]] | Room temperature |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Cook the Base
1. In a **1-quart saucepan**, combine the **1/3 cup sugar** and **1 tbsp flour**.
2. Stir in the **1/2 tsp lemon peel**, **1/4 cup lemon juice**, **1 egg**, and **1 cup marshmallows**.
3. Cook and stir over low heat for approximately **10 minutes** until the mixture thickens slightly and the **marshmallows** have completely melted.

### Step 2: Finish and Chill
4. Remove from heat and allow to cool slightly.
5. Gently fold in the **1 cup mayonnaise or salad dressing** until smooth.
6. Cover and chill thoroughly before serving over fruit salads.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days; stir well before serving.
