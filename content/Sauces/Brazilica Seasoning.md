# Brazilica Seasoning

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A flavorful and aromatic vintage seasoning blend made with grated cheese, fresh basil, and garlic, perfect for tossing with hot noodles.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 5 mins | None | 5 small jars |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Food processor or grinder
* Small jars

---
## Ingredients

### To Make Brazilica
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 4 Cups | [[Cheese]] | Grated (e.g., Parmesan, Romano, or a mix) |
| 1 1/2 Cups | [[Basil]] | Fresh, finely ground or processed |
| 9 Cloves | [[Garlic]] | Ground (noted as 'cubes' on card) |
| 1 Small Cupped Handful | [[Salt]] | Standard |

### For Serving with Noodles
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3 Large Tablespoons | Brazilica | Prepared seasoning |
| 1/3 Cube | [[Butter]] | Or margarine, approximately 2.5-3 tablespoons |
| Some | [[Water]] | Hot water from cooking noodles |
| 1 Pack | [[Noodles]] | Cooked |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Seasoning
1. In a bowl, cream the **4 cups grated cheese** into the **1 1/2 cups ground basil** along with a **small cupped handful of salt**.
2. Add the **9 ground garlic cloves** to the mixture.
3. Work the ingredients together until it becomes smooth looking.
4. Pack the mixture into **five small artichoke jars** (or similar small, clean jars) and seal for storage.

### Step 2: Serve with Noodles
5. For **1 pack of cooked noodles**, measure out **3 large tablespoons of the prepared Brazilica**.
6. Add **1/3 cube of margarine or butter**.
7. Pour **some hot water from cooking the noodles** into the Brazilica and butter mixture to emulsify.
8. Stir this sauce into the hot, drained noodles until well combined and glossy.
9. Stir in more Brazilica if desired for extra flavor.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the prepared Brazilica seasoning sealed in small jars in the refrigerator for up to several weeks.
