# Simple Brown Sugar Syrup

> 📜 **Collection:** General Archive | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A quick and easy three-ingredient brown sugar syrup perfect for creating gooey glazes for sticky buns or warm toppings for desserts. This versatile component balances sweetness with a rich, smooth consistency.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 mins | 5 mins | None | Approx. 3/4 cup |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* small saucepan
* whisk or spoon

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/3 Cup | [[Brown Sugar]] | packed |
| 1/3 Cup | [[Honey]] | liquid |
| 3 Tbsp. | [[Margarine]] or [[Butter]] | melted |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Combine and Heat
1. In a small saucepan, combine the **1/3 cup brown sugar**, **1/3 cup honey**, and **3 tbsp. melted margarine or butter**.
2. Stir over low heat until the **brown sugar** is fully dissolved and the mixture is smooth, glossy, and well combined.
3. Remove from heat and use immediately as a glaze for sticky buns or as a warm dessert topping.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 2 weeks; reheat gently on the stovetop or in the microwave before using.
