## Recipe

| :----------- | ------------------------- |

|1 can|[[Cherry Pie Mix]]|
___
### Instructions
1. Mix together in large (pyrex) bowl.
2. Add 1/2 C. liquid, water, wine juice.
3. Cook in oven at 350° for 1 hour.
4. Serve warm for really elegant serving - serve in chafing dish with flamed brandy & ice cream.
