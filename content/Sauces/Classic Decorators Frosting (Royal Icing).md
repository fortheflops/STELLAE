# Classic Decorator's Frosting (Royal Icing)

> 📜 **Collection:** Jill's Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A stiff, classic decorator's frosting that dries to a hard finish, perfect for intricate piping work. This recipe creates a glossy, stable icing ideal for detailed cookie and cake decorations.*

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 1/4 cups | [[Confectioners Sugar]] | sifted |
| 1/8 teaspoon | [[Cream of Tartar]] | none |
| 1 | [[Egg White]] | large |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Combine and Whip
1. In a small, clean, grease-free bowl, combine the **1 1/4 cups confectioners sugar**, **1/8 teaspoon cream of tartar**, and **1 egg white**.
2. Using an electric mixer on low speed, beat the ingredients until fully blended.
3. Increase the mixer speed to high and beat for 5-7 minutes until the frosting is very stiff, glossy, and holds a sharp peak; the mixture is ready when a knife drawn through it leaves a clean, defined path that does not close.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Keep the bowl covered with a damp cloth at all times when not in use to prevent the frosting from hardening prematurely.
