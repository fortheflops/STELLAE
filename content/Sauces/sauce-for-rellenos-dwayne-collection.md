# Sauce for Rellenos

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A flavorful classic sauce crafted specifically for layering over chiles rellenos. Sautéed onion, butter, and a hint of oregano create a rich, lightly thickened tomato sauce.*

## Instructions

### Making the Sauce
1. In a saucepan over medium heat, melt **1 tbsp butter** and sauté the **1/2 medium chopped onion** until softened.
2. Stir in **1 tsp all-purpose flour** to coat the onions.
3. Slowly pour in **1 (8 oz) can tomato sauce** and **4 oz water**, stirring constantly to blend smoothly.
4. Add **1 pinch salt**, **1 pinch sugar**, and **1 dash dried oregano**.
5. Reduce heat and cook slowly for about 10 minutes until the sauce simmers gently and thickens slightly.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Can be made up to 3 days in advance and kept refrigerated in an airtight container. Reheat gently over low heat before pouring over cooked rellenos.
