# Pimento Cheese Crock

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Karlene Jensen | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A classic, creamy pimento cheese dip made with melted Velveeta and tangy vinegar. This vintage recipe is perfect for parties and easily stored for later enjoyment.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5 mins | 15 mins | None | 8–10 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Double boiler
* Whisk

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 13-ounce can | [[Evaporated Milk]] | Full fat |
| 1/2 pound | [[Velveeta Cheese]] | Cubed |
| 1 large jar | [[Pimentos]] | Drained |
| 2 tablespoons | [[Vinegar]] | White or apple cider |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Melt and Combine
1. Bring the **1 13-ounce can evaporated milk** to a gentle boil using a double boiler.
2. Add the **1/2 pound Velveeta cheese**, **1 large jar pimentos**, and **2 tablespoons vinegar** to the milk.
3. Continue to heat and stir until the mixture reaches a second boil and the cheese is completely smooth and velvety.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 1 week; reheat gently on the stovetop.
