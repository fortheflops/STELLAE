# Bbq Sauce

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty One | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A flavorful and easy-to-make barbecue sauce featuring a unique blend of brewed coffee and spices. Perfect for brushing on ribs, steaks, or chicken during the final minutes of cooking.*

| 1/2 c | [[Brown sugar]] | Packed |
| 1/4 c | [[Vinegar]] | Standard |
| 1 t | [[Garlic powder]] | Dry |

| 2 T | [[Cayenne]] | Ground |
| 1/2 t | [[Italian seasoning]] | Dried |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing and Application
1. Combine **1 C ketchup**, **2/3 c brewed coffee**, **1/2 c br. sugar**, **1/4 c vinegar**, **1 t garlic powder**, **3 T Worchestershire**, **2 T cayenne**, and **1/2 t Italian seasoning** in a bowl and mix thoroughly.
2. Brush on ribs, steaks or chix the last **15 minutes** of cooking until bubbling and caramelized.
3. Serve with remaining sauce on the side.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 2 weeks.
