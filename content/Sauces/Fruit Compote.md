# Fruit Compote

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A rich and elegant fruit compote featuring a blend of dried fruits and cherries. This versatile dish is perfect served warm over ice cream or as a sophisticated dessert topping.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 60 mins | None | 8–10 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large Pyrex bowl
* Oven
* Chafing dish

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 package | [[Dried Prunes (Pitted)]] | pitted |
| 1 package | [[Dried Apricots]] | whole |
| 1 large can | [[Pineapple Chunks with liquid]] | do not drain |
| 1 can | [[Cherry Pie Mix]] | standard size |
| 1/2 cup | [[Liquid]] | water, wine, or fruit juice |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and Baking
1. In a large pyrex bowl, combine the **1 package dried prunes (pitted)**, **1 package dried apricots**, **1 large can pineapple chunks with liquid**, **1 can cherry pie mix**, and **1/2 cup liquid**.
2. Stir the mixture thoroughly until well combined.
3. Place in an oven preheated to 350°F and bake for **1 hour** until the fruit is tender and the sauce is thickened and fragrant.

### Step 2: Serving
4. Serve warm for an elegant presentation.
5. For a festive touch, serve in a chafing dish with flamed brandy and a scoop of ice cream.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This compote can be made ahead and stored in the refrigerator for up to 5 days; reheat gently before serving.
