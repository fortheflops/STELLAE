# Butter Cream Icing

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Fred Sanders of Detroit Mich. | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A classic, fluffy butter cream icing recipe originating from Fred Sanders of Detroit. Perfect for topping cakes and cupcakes with a rich, smooth finish.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 0 mins | 20 mins | 12 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Electric mixer
* Refrigerator

---
## Ingredients

### Icing Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 cup | [[Butter]] | Softened |
| 1/2 cup | [[Spry]] | Vegetable shortening |
| 3/4 cup | [[Milk]] | Scalded |
| 1 | [[Egg White]] | Unbeaten |
| 1 to 1 1/2 cups | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 1 tsp | [[Vanilla]] | Pure extract |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Base Preparation
1. Beat the **1 egg white**, slowly add the **1 to 1 1/2 cups sugar**, then add the **3/4 cup scalded milk** a tablespoon at a time.
2. Put the mixture in the refrigerator for **20 minutes** until chilled.

### Step 2: Combining and Whipping
3. Cream together the **1/2 cup butter** and **1/2 cup spry**.
4. Add the cooled milk and sugar mixture to the butter mixture a tablespoon at a time.
5. Add the **1 tsp vanilla** and beat until light and fluffy.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days; re-whip before using.
