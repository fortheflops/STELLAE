# Berry Sauce

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A smooth and tangy berry sauce thickened with cornstarch and folded into rich sour cream. Perfect for drizzling over desserts or breakfast treats.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5 mins | 10 mins | 30 mins | 4–6 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Saucepan
* Wire whisk

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3/4 Cup | [[Berry Juice]] | divided or as needed |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Sour Cream]] | chilled |
| 2 Teaspoons | [[Cornstarch]] | none |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Thicken the Juice
1. Combine **3/4 cup berry juice** and **2 teaspoons cornstarch** in a saucepan, mixing until completely smooth.

### Step 2: Cook and Chill
2. Cook over medium heat, stirring constantly until the mixture thickens and loses its starchy taste.
3. Remove from heat and chill thoroughly in the refrigerator.

### Step 3: Serve
4. When ready to serve, gently fold the chilled berry mixture into the **1/2 cup sour cream** until smooth and well combined.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the cooked and chilled berry base in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days before folding into the sour cream.
