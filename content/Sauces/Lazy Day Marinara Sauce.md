# Lazy Day Marinara Sauce

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A deliciously effortless slow-cooker marinara sauce loaded with crushed tomatoes, aromatic herbs, and Italian seasonings. Simmered all day until rich and fragrant.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 6-8 hours | None | 8–10 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Crock pot
* Can opener

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 Large Cans | [[Crushed Tomatoes]] | 14.5 oz., Italian Style |
| 1 Large Can | [[Tomato Paste]] | 2 - 6 oz. cans |
| 1 Small Can | [[Tomato Sauce]] | standard size |
| 2 Packages | [[American Beauty Spaghetti Seasoning]] | dry mix |
| 2 | [[Bay Leaves]] | whole |
| 2 Pinches | [[Oregano]] | dried |
| 2 Cloves | [[Garlic]] | minced or 1/4 teaspoon garlic powder |
| 1 Small Paste Can | [[Water]] | filled using the empty tomato paste can |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Slow Cook
1. Combine the **2 large cans crushed tomatoes**, **1 large can tomato paste**, **1 small can tomato sauce**, **2 packages american beauty spaghetti seasoning**, **2 bay leaves**, **2 pinches oregano**, **2 cloves garlic**, and **1 small paste can water** in a crock pot.
2. Cook all day on low heat until the sauce is deeply flavorful, dark red, and fragrant.

### Step 2: Finish and Serve
3. Remove the **2 bay leaves** before serving.
4. Sprinkle generously with fresh Parmesan cheese right before serving over your favorite pasta.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store leftover marinara sauce in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 5 days, or freeze for up to 3 months.
