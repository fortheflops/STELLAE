# Salad Dressing

> 📜 **Collection:** LOC | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A versatile and light homemade salad dressing featuring a nutty sesame and safflower oil base. This simple vinaigrette is easily customizable with tamari and honey for a perfect balance of savory and sweet.*

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 tbsp | [[Sesame Seed Oil]] | to taste |
| 1/2 cup | [[Safflower Oil]] | main portion |
| 1/4 cup | [[Cider Vinegar]] | to taste |
| 2 tbsp | [[Water]] | to thin |
| 1 tsp | [[Honey]] | to taste |
| 1 tsp | [[Tamari]] | or soy sauce |
| 1 tbsp | [[Sesame Seeds]] | toasted |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Emulsify
1. In a bowl or jar, combine the **1 tbsp sesame seed oil** and **1/2 cup safflower oil** to create the base.
2. Add the **1/4 cup cider vinegar**, **2 tbsp water**, **1 tsp honey**, and **1 tsp tamari** to the oil mixture.
3. Whisk or shake vigorously until the dressing is emulsified and slightly thickened.
4. Stir in the **1 tbsp sesame seeds** and taste, adjusting the acidity or sweetness as desired until the flavor is balanced and bright.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in a sealed glass jar in the refrigerator for up to 2 weeks; shake well before each use as the oil may separate.
