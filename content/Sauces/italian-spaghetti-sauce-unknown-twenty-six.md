# Italian Spaghetti Sauce

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty Six | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A rich and savory Italian spaghetti sauce recipe featuring ground beef, aromatic garlic, and rich tomato paste. Perfect for serving over long spaghetti noodles with a generous sprinkle of parmesan cheese.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 3 hours | 3 hours | 8 servings |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Saucepan
* Large pot
* Wooden spoon

## Ingredients

### Sauce Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |

| As needed | [[Onion]] | Chopped |
| As needed | [[Garlic]] | Minced |
| 1/4 c. | [[Butter]] | None |
| 1/4 c. | [[Olive Oil]] | None |
| 2 - 8 oz | [[Tomato Sauce]] | None |
| 2 - 6 oz | [[Tomato Paste]] | None |
| 2 T. | [[W. Sauce]] | Worcestershire sauce |
| 1 lb | [[Ground Beef]] | None |
| 1 lb | [[Long Spaghetti]] | None |
| As needed | [[Parmesan Cheese]] | For serving |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Simmering the Sauce
1. In a saucepan, melt the **1/4 c. butter** and **1/4 c. olive oil**, then sauté the **onion** and **garlic** until fragrant.
2. Add the **1 lb ground beef** and cook until browned completely.
3. Stir in the **2 T. parsley**, **2 - 8 oz tomato sauce**, **2 - 6 oz tomato paste**, and **2 T. W. sauce**.
4. Simmer and cool for **3 hrs** until rich, thick, and deeply flavorful.

### Step 2: Serving
5. Boil the **1 lb long spaghetti** in a large pot of salted water until tender and al dente.
6. Serve the rich meat sauce over the hot spaghetti and top with freshly grated **parmesan cheese**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This sauce tastes even better when made ahead and refrigerated for up to 3 days or frozen for up to 3 months.
