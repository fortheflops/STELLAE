# Johnnie Gold's Classic Roquefort Dressing

> 📜 **Collection:** General Archive | ✍️ **Attribution:** Johnnie Gold | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A rich, tangy, and classic homemade dressing featuring a creamy base of cream cheese, sour cream, and mayonnaise. This no-cook recipe is perfectly seasoned with garlic and dry mustard for a bold, savory finish.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Medium mixing bowl
* Wire whisk
* Rubber spatula

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 Package (3 oz) | [[Cream Cheese]] | softened |
| 3 oz | [[Roquefort]] or [[Blue Cheese]] | crumbled |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Salt]] | None |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Garlic Powder]] | None |
| 1/4 tsp | [[Dry Mustard]] | None |
| 1/2 tsp | [[Worcestershire Sauce]] | None |
| 1/2 cup | [[Sour Cream]] or [[Cream]] | light cream |
| 1/2 cup | [[Mayonnaise]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Base
1. In a medium bowl, place the **1 package (3 oz) cream cheese** and beat until smooth.

### Step 2: Season and Emulsify
2. Add the **1/4 tsp salt**, **1/4 tsp garlic powder**, **1/4 tsp dry mustard**, and **1/2 tsp worcestershire sauce** to the bowl, mixing well to combine.
3. Gradually incorporate the **1/2 cup sour cream** and **1/2 cup mayonnaise**, whisking until the mixture is completely smooth and uniform.

### Step 3: Finish and Chill
4. Gently fold in the **3 oz roquefort or blue cheese** until evenly distributed.
5. Cover and chill in the refrigerator for at least **1 hour** to allow the flavors to meld into a thick, tangy consistency.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 5 days; stir well before serving as separation may occur.
