# Chile Sauce

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A classic, rustic homemade chile sauce featuring ripe tomatoes, aromatic spices, and a touch of sweetness. Perfect for canning or serving alongside your favorite savory dishes.*

| 1 tbsp | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 1 | [[Onion]] | Chopped |
| 1 pinch | [[Celery Seed]] | Dried |
| 1 pinch | [[Cinnamon]] | Ground |
| 1 pinch | [[Allspice]] | Ground |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and Simmering
1. Chop the **12 large ripe tomatoes** and **1 onion** into small pieces.
2. Combine the **12 tomatoes**, **1 onion**, **3/4 cup vinegar**, **1 large pepper**, **1/2 tsp salt**, **1 tbsp sugar**, and the pinches of **celery seed**, **cinnamon**, and **allspice** in a large pot.
3. Simmer the mixture over medium-low heat, stirring occasionally, until the sauce thickens to your desired consistency and the vegetables are soft and broken down.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to two weeks or process in water bath cans for long-term storage.
