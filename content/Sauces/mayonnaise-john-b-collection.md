## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep Bowl Ingredients
1. Into a bowl put **2 egg yolks**, **1 1/2 tsp salt**, **3/4 tsp mustard**, **1 c olive oil**, **1 tsp sugar**, **1/8 tsp cayenne**, **2 tbsp vinegar**, and **2 tbsp lemon juice**.

### Step 2: Cook Sauce Base
2. Make a sauce of **1 c water**, **1/3 c flour**, and **1 tbsp butter**; cook in double boiler until thickened.

### Step 3: Combine and Beat
3. Pour sauce hot onto ingredients in bowl and beat briskly with eggbeater until it will give thick mayonnaise at once and keep well.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in a tightly sealed container in the refrigerator to keep well.
