# Quick and Easy Chocolate Frosting

> 📜 **Collection:** General Archive | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A simple, classic chocolate frosting made with cocoa powder, powdered sugar, and margarine. This quick and easy recipe makes enough to generously frost a two-layer, 8-inch cake.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 0 mins | None | Frosts one 8-inch two-layer cake |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* medium bowl
* electric mixer
* whisk

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3 Tablespoons | [[Margarine]] | softened |
| 3 Cups | [[Powdered Sugar]] | sifted |
| 1/3 Cup | [[Cocoa]] | unsweetened |
| 1/4 Cup | [[Hot Water]] | divided |
| 1 Teaspoon | [[Vanilla]] | extract |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Cream and Combine
1. In a medium bowl, cream the **3 tablespoons margarine** with an electric mixer until smooth.
2. In a separate bowl, whisk together the **3 cups powdered sugar** and **1/3 cup cocoa**.
3. Gradually add the dry mixture to the **margarine**, alternating with the **1/4 cup hot water**, beating until incorporated and smooth.
4. Stir in the **1 teaspoon vanilla**.
5. Beat on high speed for one minute until the frosting is light, fluffy, and holds a smooth peak.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 1 week; bring to room temperature and re-whip before using.
