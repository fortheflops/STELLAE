# Old Time Chocolate Sauce

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A rich and classic chocolate sauce prepared in a double boiler for a smooth, velvety consistency. This traditional recipe is perfect for drizzling over ice cream or your favorite desserts.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5 mins | 30 mins | None | 1 pint |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Double boiler
* Spoon

---
## Ingredients

### Sauce Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3 squares | [[Chocolate]] | Unsweetened or semi-sweet |
| 1/4 cup | [[Butter]] or [[Margarine]] | Softened |
| 1 1/2 cups | [[Powdered Sugar]] | Sifted |
| 5/8 cup (or 1 small can) | [[Evaporated Milk]] | Standard |

---

## Instructions

### Preparation and Cooking
1. Place the **3 squares chocolate**, **1/4 cup butter or margarine**, **1 1/2 cups powdered sugar**, and **5/8 cup evaporated milk** in the top of a double boiler.
2. Cover and heat over boiling water, stirring the mixture together well as the **chocolate** melts.
3. Cook for 30 minutes, stirring occasionally until the sauce is smooth and thickened.
4. Fill a pint container and store in the refrigerator, reheating as needed before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in a pint container in the refrigerator and reheat each time before serving.
