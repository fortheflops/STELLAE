# Fruit Salad Dressing

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Mrs. Easte | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A classic, rich fruit salad dressing made with eggs, lemon, and butter, finished with whipped cream. This elegant sauce provides a creamy, tangy contrast to fresh seasonal fruit.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 15 mins | None | 6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Double boiler
* Whisk
* Mixing bowl

---
## Ingredients

### Dressing Base
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 | [[Eggs]] | or 4 yolks |
| 5 tablespoons | [[Sugar]] | granulated |
| 1 | [[Lemon]] | juiced |
| 2 tablespoons | [[Butter]] | melted |

### Finishing
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 pint | [[Cream]] | whipped |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Cooking the Base
1. Combine the **2 eggs** (or **4 yolks**), **5 tablespoons sugar**, **juice of 1 lemon**, and **2 tablespoons melted butter** in a double boiler.
2. Cook over simmering water, whisking constantly, until the mixture is thick enough to coat the back of a spoon.

### Step 2: Finishing
3. Remove from heat and allow to cool slightly.
4. When ready to serve, fold in the **1/2 pint whipped cream** to thin and lighten the dressing.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The cooked base can be refrigerated for up to 2 days; fold in the whipped cream immediately before serving.
