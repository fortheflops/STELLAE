# Pink Mint Frosting

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A vintage boiled frosting recipe flavored with mint and tinted with a touch of pink food coloring. Perfect for topping and filling cakes with a decorative swirled finish.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 7 mins | None | 1 cake |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Double boiler
* Electric mixer or hand beater
* Spoon

---
## Ingredients

### Frosting Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 | [[Egg whites]] | Unbeaten |
| 1 1/2 cups | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 5 tbsp | [[Water]] | Standard |
| 1 1/2 tsp | [[White corn syrup]] | Standard |
| 1 1/2 tsp | [[Mint flavoring]] | Standard |
| 1 small amount | [[Red coloring]] | Food coloring |
| 3 tbsp | [[Frosting]] | Reserved for deeper pink tint |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Boiling and Beating
1. In the top of a double boiler, mix **2 unbeaten egg whites**, **1 1/2 cups sugar**, **5 tbsp water**, and **1 1/2 tsp white corn syrup**.
2. Beat constantly over rapidly boiling water until the frosting stands in a peak, about 5 to 7 minutes, achieving a fluffy texture.

### Step 2: Flavoring and Decorating
3. Remove from water, and add **1 1/2 tsp mint flavoring** and a small amount of **red coloring**.
4. Beat until thick enough to spread.
5. Tint **3 tbsp frosting** a deeper pink.
6. Fill and frost the cake, making swirls with the back of a spoon.
7. Fill the center of the swirls with the deeper shade of **frosting**.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Best used immediately after preparation while fluffy and pliable.
