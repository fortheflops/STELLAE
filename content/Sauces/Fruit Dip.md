# Fruit Dip

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Cathy | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A simple and creamy fruit dip made with just two ingredients. Perfect for pairing with fresh seasonal fruit for a quick dessert or snack.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5 mins | 0 mins | None | 4–6 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Electric hand mixer or spatula

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 8 Ounces | [[Cream Cheese]] | softened to room temperature |
| 7 Ounces | [[Marshmallow Creme]] | standard jar |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Combine and Serve
1. In a medium mixing bowl, combine the **8 ounces cream cheese** and **7 ounces marshmallow creme**.
2. Beat or stir until the mixture is smooth, light, and fully incorporated.
3. Serve immediately with fresh fruit until the dip is finished.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days; bring to room temperature before serving for best texture.
