# Herb Dressing

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Eight | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A vibrant and versatile herb dressing made with fresh chives, parsley, and garlic whisked with olive oil and white wine vinegar. Perfect for elevating green salads with a punch of aromatic flavor.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing and Whisking
1. Whisk together **3 Tbsps white wine vinegar**, **1/4 tsp dry mustard**, **1/2 tsp salt**, **1/8 tsp pepper**, and **1 pinch cayenne pepper** in a small bowl until combined.
2. Pour **2/3 cup olive oil** in gradually, beating constantly as you do until fully emulsified.
3. Stir in **1 Tbsp minced fresh chives**, **1 Tbsp minced fresh parsley**, **1 small clove minced garlic**, **1/2 tsp crushed basil**, **1/4 tsp crushed oregano**, and **1/4 tsp crushed tarragon**.
4. Note: If you can get fresh herbs, use them and double the quantities for a more robust flavor profile.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight jar in the refrigerator for up to 5 days. Shake well before serving.
