> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A classic homemade French dressing recipe featuring a tangy blend of catsup, sugar, and vinegar. Perfect for tossing with crisp garden salads.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Blending
1. Combine **1 c. catsup**, **1/4 c. sugar**, **1/2 c. vinegar**, **1 t. salt**, **1 small onion**, **1/2 t. celery seed**, **1/4 t. paprika**, and **3/4 c. salad oil** in a blender.
2. Blend for **30 seconds** until smooth, emulsified, and well combined.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight jar in the refrigerator for up to 2 weeks; shake well before serving.
