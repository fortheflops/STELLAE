# Cranberry Orange Apple Sauce

> 📜 **Collection:** Louise Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A delightful vintage fruit sauce combining tart cranberries, sweet apples, and a hint of citrus orange zest. Perfect as a holiday side dish or a sweet topping, this recipe comes from the Louise Collection archive.*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Cook the Cranberries
1. In a pot, combine **1 package cranberries**, **2 cups water**, and **2 cups sugar**. Cook for at least **one-half hour** until all the berries burst and the mixture becomes jammy and thick.
2. Ten minutes before the cranberries are done cooking, stir in the **grated rind of one-half orange** until fragrant.

### Step 2: Prepare the Applesauce
3. If making applesauce from scratch, cook **McIntosh apples** or **Stark's Delicious apples** separately. Add **1 cup water** (or as desired for consistency) and **one-half teaspoon cinnamon** to the apples, cooking on a low flame until they are soft, tender, and easily mashed.

### Step 3: Cool and Combine
4. Cool the cooked cranberries and the **applesauce** completely.
5. Once cooled, mix the cranberry sauce and **applesauce** together until fully incorporated and smooth.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Can be made ahead and stored in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 1 week. Flavors will deepen as it sits.
