## Recipe

| Measurements | Ingredients               |
| :----------- | ------------------------- |
|1 can (1 lb.)|[[Chilled Apricot Halves (Drained)]]|
|1/4 C.|[[Apricot Syrup]]|
|1 T.|[[Honey]]|
|1 T.|[[Powdered Sugar]]|
___
### Instructions
1. Drain 1 can (1 lb.) chilled apricot halves.
2. Place drained halves & 1/4 C. apricot syrup in blender container.
3. Process first at low, then at high until smooth.
4. Also use 1 T. honey & 1 T. powdered sugar.
5. Serve over sherbet or ice cream.
