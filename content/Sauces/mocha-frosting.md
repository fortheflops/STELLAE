# Mocha Frosting

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A rich and fluffy mocha frosting flavored with cocoa and strong black coffee. Perfect for topping cakes and cupcakes.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | None | None | 12 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Sifter

---
## Ingredients

### Frosting Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/4 c. | [[Shortening]] | Softened |
| 3 1/2 c. | [[Confectioners' Sugar]] | Sifted |
| 1/8 tsp. | [[Salt]] | None |
| 2 tbsp. | [[Cocoa]] | None |
| 1 | [[Egg]] | None |
| 1/4 c. | [[Strong Black Coffee]] | Cold |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing and Beating
1. Put **1/4 c. shortening** into mixing bowl.
2. Sift in **3 1/2 c. confectioners' sugar**, **1/8 tsp. salt**, and **2 tbsp. cocoa**.
3. Add **1 egg** and **1/4 c. strong black coffee (cold)**.
4. Beat until thoroughly fluffy, achieving a smooth and spreadable consistency.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days. Bring to room temperature and re-whip before using.
