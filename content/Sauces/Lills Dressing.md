# Lill's Dressing

## Instructions

### Step 1: Emulsify
1. In a small bowl or a jar with a tight-fitting lid, combine the **3 tablespoons sugar**, **1/3 to 1/2 cup catsup**, **1 small dash salt**, **1 small dash pepper**, **1/4 cup vinegar**, and **1/2 cup oil**.
2. Whisk or shake vigorously until the **3 tablespoons sugar** is completely dissolved and the dressing is smooth, emulsified, and well combined.
3. Taste the dressing and adjust the seasonings or the oil-to-vinegar ratio as desired until the flavor is balanced and bright.
4. Serve immediately over your favorite salad, such as artichoke hearts or French endive.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in a sealed jar in the refrigerator for up to 1 week; shake well before serving as the oil may separate.
