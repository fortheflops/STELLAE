# Chile Con Queso

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A classic, creamy Chile Con Queso dip featuring a savory blend of onions, garlic, chiles, and melted cheese. This vintage recipe is perfect for parties and gatherings.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 15 mins | None | 10–12 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large skillet
* Wooden spoon
* Chef's knife

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 Cups | [[Onions]] | diced |
| 8 Cloves | [[Garlic]] | minced (use 6 if large) |
| As needed | [[Grease]] | for frying |
| 3 Cans | [[Chili]] | chopped |
| 1 Can | [[Tomatoes]] | drained |
| 2 Pounds | [[Velveeta Cheese]] | cubed |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Sauté Aromatics
1. In a large skillet, heat a small amount of **grease** over medium heat.
2. Add the **2 cups onions** and **8 cloves garlic** and fry until the onions are clear and fragrant.

### Step 2: Combine and Melt
3. Stir in the **3 cans chili** and **1 can tomatoes** until well combined.
4. Add the **2 pounds Velveeta cheese** and stir continuously until the cheese is completely melted, smooth, and bubbling gently.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days; reheat gently on the stovetop with a splash of milk if needed.
