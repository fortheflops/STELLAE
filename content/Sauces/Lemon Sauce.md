# Lemon Sauce

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A bright and zesty lemon sauce perfect for drizzling over cakes, gingerbread, and fruit cobblers. This classic recipe uses simple pantry staples to create a smooth, thickened finish.*

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* 1-quart saucepan
* Wire whisk

## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3 Tablespoons | [[Cornstarch]] | None |
| 1 Cup | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 2 Tablespoons | [[Grated Lemon Rind]] | Freshly grated |
| 1/4 Cup | [[Lemon Juice]] | Freshly squeezed |
| 1/4 Cup | [[Margarine]] | None |
| 2 Cups | [[Water]] | None |
| 1/4 Teaspoon | [[Salt]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Combine and Thicken
1. In a **1-quart saucepan**, combine the **3 tablespoons cornstarch**, **1 cup sugar**, **2 tablespoons grated lemon rind**, and **1/4 teaspoon salt**.
2. Slowly whisk in the **2 cups water** until smooth.
3. Stir over medium heat until the mixture is thickened and translucent.
4. Remove from heat and stir in the **1/4 cup lemon juice** and **1/4 cup margarine** until fully melted and glossy.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 5 days; reheat gently on the stovetop before serving.
