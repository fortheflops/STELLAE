# Classic Boiled Lemon Icing

> 📜 **Collection:** General Archive | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A classic boiled icing featuring a bright lemon flavor, cooked on the stovetop and beaten until thick, glossy, and perfectly spreadable. This vintage recipe creates a smooth, non-gritty frosting ideal for finishing lemon cakes.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5 mins | 10 mins | 10 mins | Icing for one 8 or 9-inch cake |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* medium saucepan
* handheld electric mixer
* spatula

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3/4 Cup | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| 1/4 Cup | [[Milk]] | Whole |
| 3 Tablespoons | [[Spry]] | Vegetable shortening; Crisco is a modern substitute |
| 1/4 Teaspoon | [[Salt]] | Fine |
| 3/4 Teaspoon | [[Lemon Rind]] | Freshly grated |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Boil the Base
1. In a medium saucepan, combine the **3/4 cup sugar**, **1/4 cup milk**, **3 tablespoons Spry**, and **1/4 teaspoon salt**.
2. Bring the mixture to a rolling boil over medium heat, stirring constantly. Continue to boil for exactly **1 minute**.

### Step 2: Cool and Whip
3. Remove the saucepan from the heat and stir in the **3/4 teaspoon lemon rind**.
4. Allow the mixture to cool until it is warm to the touch but not hot.
5. Using a handheld electric mixer, beat the mixture on medium-high speed as it cools. Continue beating for several minutes until the icing loses its transparency, becomes creamy, and thickens to a spreadable consistency.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This icing should be used immediately upon reaching the correct consistency to ensure it spreads smoothly over the cake.
