# Garlic Relish on Pork Chop

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Relish
1. In a mixing bowl, combine the **1 tbsp garlic**, **2 tbsp basil**, **1 tbsp oregano**, **1/4 cup balsamic vinegar**, **2 tbsp olive oil**, **6 Roma tomatoes**, and **3/4 cup red onion**. Toss gently until well mixed and set aside to let the flavors meld.

### Step 2: Prepare the Marinade
2. In a separate bowl, whisk together the **1/2 cup soy sauce**, **2 tbsp dijon mustard**, **1 tbsp honey**, **1 tsp garlic**, **juice of 2 lemons**, **1 tbsp dried sage**, and **1/4 cup olive oil** until smooth.

### Step 3: Marinate and Cook
3. Coat your pork chops in the marinade, place them in a plastic bag over night in the refrigerator.
4. Cook the marinated pork chops to your desired doneness, then top generously with the fresh garlic relish before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The marinade and relish can be prepared up to 24 hours in advance and stored separately in the refrigerator.
