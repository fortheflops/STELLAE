# Royal Cream Salad Dressing

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A classic, simple-to-prepare Royal Cream Salad Dressing featuring a balanced blend of light cream and tarragon vinegar. This vintage recipe is perfect for elevating fresh garden greens with its tangy and smooth profile.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5 mins | 0 mins | None | 4 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Glass jar with lid

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3 parts | [[Light Cream]] | chilled |
| 1 part | [[Tarragon Vinegar]] | room temperature |
| 1 tsp | [[Mustard Powder]] | none |
| to taste | [[Salt]] | none |
| to taste | [[Pepper]] | freshly cracked |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Emulsify
1. Combine the **3 parts light cream**, **1 part tarragon vinegar**, **1 tsp mustard powder**, **salt**, and **pepper** in a glass jar.
2. Seal the jar tightly and shake vigorously until the mixture is well combined, thickened, and smooth in consistency.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in a sealed jar in the refrigerator for up to 3 days; shake well before serving as separation may occur.
