# Fudgy Graham Cracker Frosting

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail's Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A rich and textured frosting featuring a delightful crunch from graham crackers and walnuts. This unique recipe spreads perfectly over warm cakes for a decadent finish.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 0 mins | None | Frosting for one 9x13 inch cake |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* medium mixing bowl
* electric hand mixer
* silicone spatula

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 cup | [[Condensed Milk]] | standard |
| 2 tablespoons | [[Butter]] | melted |
| 1 cup | [[Powdered Sugar]] | sifted |
| 2 tablespoons | [[Hershey's Cocoa Powder]] | unsweetened |
| 3/4 cup | [[Graham Cracker Crumbs]] | fine |
| 1/2 cup | [[Walnuts]] | chopped |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Vanilla Extract]] | pure |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Base Preparation
1. In a medium bowl, combine the **1/2 cup condensed milk**, **2 tablespoons melted butter**, **1 cup sifted powdered sugar**, and **2 tablespoons cocoa powder**. Mix well until the texture is smooth and glossy.

### Step 2: Incorporate Texture
2. Add the **3/4 cup graham cracker crumbs** and beat the mixture with an electric mixer until it becomes thick and creamy.

### Step 3: Final Fold
3. Stir in the **1 teaspoon vanilla extract** and **1/2 cup chopped walnuts** by hand until just combined and evenly distributed.

### Step 4: Application
4. Spread the frosting immediately onto a warm cake for the easiest application; the frosting will set firmly as it cools.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days; bring to room temperature before spreading.
