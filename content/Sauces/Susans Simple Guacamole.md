# Susan's Simple Guacamole

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Susan | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A classic, creamy guacamole recipe featuring fresh avocados and zesty aromatics. This simple dip is perfect for parties and pairs beautifully with your favorite tortilla chips.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 0 mins | None | 4–6 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Fork
* Mixing bowl

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3-5 | [[Very Ripe Avocados]] | peeled |
| 1 T. | [[Lemon Juice]] | freshly squeezed |
| 1/4 | [[Small Onion (Chopped)]] | finely chopped |
| 1/4 t. | [[Minced Garlic]] | minced |
| 1-2 | [[Tomatoes (Cut into Pieces)]] | diced |
| 1/2 - 3/4 t. | [[Salt]] | to taste |
| 1/2 t. | [[Red Pepper]] | crushed |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Base
1. In a mixing bowl, peel and mash the **3-5 very ripe avocados** with the **1 T. lemon juice** until smooth but slightly chunky.

### Step 2: Combine and Season
2. Fold in the **1/4 small onion (chopped)**, **1/4 t. minced garlic**, **1-2 tomatoes (cut into pieces)**, **1/2 - 3/4 t. salt**, and **1/2 t. red pepper**.
3. Mix everything together with a fork until well incorporated and fragrant.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container with the avocado seed pressed into the center to prevent browning; consume within 24 hours.
