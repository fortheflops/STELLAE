# Chocolate Whiskey Sauce

> 📜 **Collection:** UK15 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Leonardo's Restaurant in Santa Fe | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A rich and decadent chocolate whiskey sauce from Leonardo's Restaurant in Santa Fe, featuring Irish whiskey and dual chocolates. Perfect for drizzling over your favorite desserts and easily made ahead.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 10 mins | None | 6–8 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Small saucepan
* Whisk

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3/4 cup | [[Whipping Cream]] | None |
| 1/3 cup | [[Sugar]] | None |
| 1/4 cup | [[Butter]] | Unsalted, 1/2 stick |
| 3 tablespoons | [[Irish Whiskey]] | None |
| 4 ounces | [[Semisweet Chocolate]] | Coarsely chopped |
| 2 ounces | [[Unsweetened Chocolate]] | None |
| 1 teaspoon | [[Vanilla Extract]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Simmer the Base
1. In a small saucepan, heat the **3/4 cup whipping cream**, **1/3 cup sugar**, and **1/4 cup unsalted butter** over medium heat until the sugar dissolves.

### Step 2: Thicken and Infuse
2. Bring the mixture to a simmer and cook until it has slightly thickened, about **4 minutes**.
3. Add the **3 tablespoons Irish whiskey** and simmer for **4 more minutes** until well integrated.

### Step 3: Melt and Smooth
4. Remove the saucepan from the heat.
5. Add the **4 ounces coarsely chopped semisweet chocolate**, **2 ounces unsweetened chocolate**, and **1 teaspoon vanilla extract**.
6. Mix until the sauce is completely smooth, glossy, and fragrant.
7. Let it cool slightly before serving.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This sauce can be prepared one day ahead. Cover and refrigerate, then rewarm gently over low heat before serving.
