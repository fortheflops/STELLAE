## Instructions

### Preparation and Notes
1. Prepare **1 pkg McCormick enchilada sauce** just as it states on the back. It may seem 'soupy', but it comes out fine.
2. Cook, cool, and heat before serving for better flavor. It can also be frozen and actually tastes better than cooked and served immediately.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Can be cooked, cooled, and frozen or refrigerated for later reheating to enhance the flavor.
