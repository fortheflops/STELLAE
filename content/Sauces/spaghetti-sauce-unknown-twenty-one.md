# Spaghetti Sauce

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Twenty One | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A rich and savory homemade spaghetti sauce simmered with herbs, beef, and a unique touch of Worcestershire. This classic recipe develops deep flavors as it slowly cooks on the stove.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 2 hours | None | 6 servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Saucepan
* Wooden spoon
* Measuring spoons

---
## Ingredients

### Sauce Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 can | [[Canned tomatoes]] | Whole or crushed |
| 1 can | [[Tomato sauce]] | Standard |
| 1 pinch | [[Gr. pepper]] | Ground |
| To taste | [[Onion & garlic]] | Chopped |
| 2 T | [[Parsley]] | Dried or fresh |
| 1 t | [[Oregano]] | Dried |
| 1/2 t | [[Basil]] | Dried |
| 1/4 t | [[Anise]] | Seeds |
| 1/4 c | [[Parmesan]] | Grated |
| As needed | [[Bread cubes]] | Optional for thickening |
| As needed | [[Beef]] | Ground or cubed |
| 1 T per cup | [[Worcestershire]] | Added for every cup of pasta sauce |

---

## Instructions

### Simmering
1. Combine the **canned tomatoes**, **tomato sauce**, **gr. pepper**, **onion & garlic**, **beef**, and **bread cubes** in a large pot.
2. Stir in the **2 T parsley**, **1 t oregano**, **1/2 t basil**, **1/4 t anise**, **1/4 c parmesan**, and **1 T Worcestershire** (calculated per cup of sauce).
3. Simmer gently for **1 1/2 - 2 hours** until the sauce thickens and reaches a rich, aromatic doneness.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 4 days, or freeze for up to 3 months.
