# Brandied Hard Sauce

> 📜 **Collection:** UK2 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A classic, rich, and creamy brandy-infused sauce perfect for drizzling over holiday puddings and cakes. This vintage recipe yields a velvety texture that elevates any traditional dessert.*

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | 0 mins | 30 mins | 2 Cups |

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Electric mixer
* Mixing bowl
* Rubber spatula

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Butter]] | softened |
| 2 Cups | [[Powdered Sugar]] | sifted |
| 1 | [[Egg Yolk]] | beaten |
| 2 Tbsp | [[Brandy]] | room temperature |
| 1/2 tsp | [[Vanilla]] | extract |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Creaming
1. In a mixing bowl, cream the **1/2 cup butter** until smooth.
2. Gradually add the **2 cups powdered sugar**, beating continuously until the mixture is light, fluffy, and pale in color.

### Step 2: Emulsifying
3. Add the **1 beaten egg yolk**, **2 tbsp brandy**, and **1/2 tsp vanilla** to the mixture.
4. Mix well until fully incorporated and smooth, ensuring no streaks remain.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 1 week; bring to room temperature before serving for best consistency.
