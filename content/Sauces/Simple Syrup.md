# Simple Syrup

> 📜 **Collection:** Gail Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A versatile and essential simple syrup base perfect for sweetening beverages or desserts. This clear, smooth syrup is easily customized with your favorite extracts.*

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Saucepan
* Whisk or spoon

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 4 Cups | [[Water]] | Boiling |
| 1 Cup | [[Sugar]] | Granulated |
| To Taste | [[Flavoring]] | Vanilla, almond, or mint extract |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Dissolve and Simmer
1. In a saucepan, bring **4 cups water** to a boil.
2. Stir in **1 cup sugar** while the water is boiling.
3. Continue to stir until the **sugar** is completely dissolved and the liquid becomes clear and syrupy.
4. Remove from heat and stir in **flavoring** to taste.
5. Allow the mixture to cool completely until it reaches room temperature before storing.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in a sterilized glass jar in the refrigerator for up to 2 weeks.
