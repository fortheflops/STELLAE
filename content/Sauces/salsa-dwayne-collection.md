# Salsa

> 📜 **Collection:** Dwayne Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A quick and flavorful fresh tomato salsa seasoned with garlic salt, chili, and MSG. Perfect for serving as a dip with crisp tostadas or chip side.*

## Ingredients

### Salsa Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 large | [[Tomato]] | chopped |
| 1 small | [[Onion]] | chopped |
| 1/2 tsp | [[Garlic Salt]] | None |
| To taste | [[Fresh Chili]] | chopped |
| 1/2 tsp | [[MSG]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Preparation
1. In a mixing bowl, combine the **1 large chopped tomato**, **1 small chopped onion**, **1/2 tsp garlic salt**, **chopped fresh chili to taste**, and **1/2 tsp MSG**.
2. Let the mixture marinate for at least **15 mins** to allow the flavors to blend.
3. Serve cold or at room temperature as a dip with tostadas.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This salsa freezes well. Refrigerate in an airtight container for up to 3 days or freeze for longer storage.
