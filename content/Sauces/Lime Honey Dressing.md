# Lime Honey Dressing

> 📜 **Collection:** UK3 | ✍️ **Attribution:** Gina Morris | 📂 **Category:** Sauces
> *A vibrant and sweet citrus dressing perfect for enhancing fresh fruit salads. This versatile glaze pairs exceptionally well with melon balls for a refreshing treat.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5 mins | 0 mins | None | 12 Servings |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Wire whisk

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 16 Ounce | [[Lime Aid]] | Undiluted |
| 3/4 Cup | [[Oil]] | Neutral |
| 1/2 Cup | [[Honey]] | Liquid |
| 1/4 Tablespoon | [[Salt]] | Fine |
| 2 Tablespoon | [[Celery Seed]] or [[Poppy Seed]] | Divided |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Combine and Serve
1. In a medium bowl, whisk together the **16 ounce lime aid**, **3/4 cup oil**, **1/2 cup honey**, **1/4 tablespoon salt**, and **2 tablespoon celery or poppy seed** until fully emulsified and smooth.
2. Pour the dressing over a fresh fruit salad or drizzle over melon balls until evenly coated and glossy.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in an airtight jar in the refrigerator for up to 1 week; shake well before serving as ingredients may separate.
