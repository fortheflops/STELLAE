## Instructions

### Step 1: Naming Conventions
1. Always name the recipes as close to the **original recipe names** as possible to preserve historical context and authenticity.

### Step 2: Categorization Rules
2. Categorize most recipes by **vibe-based categories** to capture the culinary spirit of the dish.
3. If vibes are unclear, defer to the **first ingredient** to help categorize (for example, **Curried Beef or Lamb** will be categorized under beef since **beef** comes first).
