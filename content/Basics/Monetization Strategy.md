# Monetization Strategy

> 📜 **Collection:** STELLAE Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** STELLAE Creator | 📂 **Category:** Basics
> *A clean, unobtrusive strategy for monetizing a historical culinary archive without disrupting the user experience. Focuses on video content and subtle product integrations.*

---

## Instructions

### Implementation Steps
1. Begin with **1 clean website**, ensuring it is completely free of **intrusive banners** and **pop-ups** until the user experience feels **seamless, uncluttered, and historically authentic**.
2. Incorporate **video content** such as **shorts**, **recipe videos**, or **mini documentaries** to enrich the archive until reader engagement is **vibrant and active**.
3. Blend in **subtle product links** (such as linking **dried plums** in a Victorian plum pudding recipe) to direct users to high-quality purveyors, enhancing the recipe naturally without disrupting the visual flow.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Review monetization strategies quarterly to ensure they remain unobtrusive and relevant to the user experience.
