# Mom's Pastry Mix

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Nine | ✍️ **Attribution:** Mom | 📂 **Category:** Basics
> *A versatile, shelf-stable pastry mix that makes preparing homemade pie crusts quick and easy. Simply combine the dry base with cold water to achieve a flaky, tender dough.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 mins | None | None | Makes approximately 17 cups of mix |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Large mixing bowl
* Pastry blender
* Airtight storage container

---
## Ingredients

### Pastry Mix Base
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 12 cups | [[Flour]] | All-purpose |
| 2 tbsp | [[Salt]] | None |
| 5 cups | [[Crisco Shortening]] | None |

### For 2 Pie Crusts
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 cups | [[Pastry Mix]] | Prepared mix |
| 3 tbsp | [[Iced Cold Water]] | Very cold |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparing the Mix
1. In a large bowl, combine the **12 cups flour**, **2 tbsp salt**, and **5 cups Crisco shortening**.
2. Use a pastry blender to cut the shortening into the dry ingredients until the mixture resembles coarse crumbs.
3. Store the finished mixture in an airtight container until ready to use.

### Step 2: Making the Crust
4. To make two pie crusts, place **2 cups pastry mix** into a bowl.
5. Add **3 tbsp iced cold water** and mix well until the dough just comes together and is no longer crumbly.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The dry pastry mix can be stored in an airtight container in a cool, dry place for several months.
