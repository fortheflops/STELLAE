# Eagle Brand Milk

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Basics
> *A simple, homemade alternative to store-bought sweetened condensed milk. This easy recipe uses basic pantry staples to create a rich, creamy base for your favorite desserts.*

## Ingredients

### Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3 cups | [[Warm Water]] | warm |
| 3/4 cup | [[Sugar]] | granulated |
| 1 cup + 2 tbsp | [[Dried Milk]] | powdered |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mixing
1. In a bowl, combine **3 cups warm water** and **3/4 cup sugar**, mixing until the sugar is fully dissolved.
2. Stir in **1 cup + 2 tbsp dried milk** until smooth and well incorporated.
3. Let the mixture stand overnight at room temperature until it thickens to the desired consistency.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** This mixture must sit overnight to thicken properly; store in the refrigerator once thickened.
