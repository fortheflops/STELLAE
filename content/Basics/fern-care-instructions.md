## Instructions

### Care Routine
1. Feed the plant every 2 to 4 weeks using one of the following: **Nitrate of soda**, **Ammonia water**, **Manure leachings**, **Prepared plant food**, or a mixture of **Ground bone** and **Wood ashes**.
2. Bathe the leaves occasionally with **weak suds**.
3. Maintain consistent moisture levels by keeping the soil damp at all times, but ensure you apply **water** sparingly to avoid over-saturation.
