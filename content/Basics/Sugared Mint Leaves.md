# Sugared Mint Leaves

> 📜 **Collection:** GC | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unknown | 📂 **Category:** Basics
> *Learn how to make delicate sugared mint leaves, perfect as a sweet edible candy or a charming garnish for your favorite hot and cold beverages. This classic technique uses fresh herbs, egg white, and fine sugar for a sparkling, crisp finish.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20 mins | 1 hour | 24 hours | Makes a batch |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Small bowl
* Small pastry brush
* Foil-lined sheet
* Tight jar

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| As Needed | [[Mint Leaves]] | Fresh, not too large |
| 1 | [[Egg White]] | Slightly beaten |
| As Needed | [[Sugar]] | Superfine or granulated |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep and Coat
1. Pick fresh **mint leaves** that are not too large, wash gently, and dry them completely on a towel.
2. In a small bowl, beat **one egg white** until it is slightly fluffy.
3. Dip each individual **fresh mint leaves** into the slightly beaten **egg white**, ensuring it is fully coated.

### Step 2: Sugar and Dry
4. Dip both sides of the coated leaf in **superfine or granulated sugar**.
5. Place the sugared leaves on a foil-lined sheet.
6. Put them in a low oven (e.g., **200°F** or the lowest setting) until they are completely dry and crisp, or let them air dry for **24-48 hours**.
7. Store the dried, sugared **mint leaves** in a tight jar.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store the dried, sugared mint leaves in a tight jar at room temperature for several weeks.
