## Ingredients

### General Household Cleaner
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 cup | [[Ammonia]] | Standard household strength |
| 1/2 cup | [[Baking Soda]] | Dissolved |
| 1/2 cup | [[Vinegar]] | White distilled |
| To fill | [[Water]] | To reach 1 gallon total |

### Window Cleaner
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Equal parts | [[Alcohol]] | Rubbing alcohol |
| Equal parts | [[Vinegar]] | White distilled |
| To fill | [[Water]] | To reach 1 gallon total |

---

## Instructions

### Mixing Instructions
1. For the general cleaner, combine **1 cup ammonia**, **1/2 cup baking soda**, and **1/2 cup vinegar** in a large container, then add **water** until the mixture reaches the 1 gallon mark.
2. For the window cleaner, mix **equal parts alcohol** and **equal parts vinegar**, then add **enough water** to reach a total volume of 1 gallon.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Store in a clearly labeled, airtight gallon container away from direct sunlight and heat.
