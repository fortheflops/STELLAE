# Pie Crust C Super

> 📜 **Collection:** John B. Collection | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Basics
> *A simple and reliable recipe for a classic flaky pie crust. This foundational pastry dough is perfect for both sweet and savory pies.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 mins | 0 mins | 30 mins | 2 crusts |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Mixing bowl
* Pastry cutter

---
## Ingredients

### Dough Ingredients
| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 cups | [[Flour]] | Sifted |
| 1 cup | [[Crisco]] | Chilled |
| 1 tsp | [[Salt]] | None |
| 1/3 cup | [[Sour Milk]] | None |
| 1 tbsp | [[Vinegar]] | None |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation
1. In a large bowl, combine the **2 cups flour**, **1 cup Crisco**, and **1 tsp salt** until the mixture resembles coarse crumbs.
2. In a separate small bowl, mix the **1/3 cup sour milk** and **1 tbsp vinegar** together.
3. Combine the liquid mixture with the dry ingredients, mix until just incorporated, and set aside to rest before rolling.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** The dough can be wrapped tightly in plastic wrap and refrigerated for up to 2 days or frozen for up to 3 months.
