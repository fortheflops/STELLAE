## Instructions

### Step 1: Discover and Collect
1. Combine **1 Part** **Food History** with **A Dash** of **Economics** to uncover the stories of how people lived, worked, and shared meals.

### Step 2: Build the Resource
2. Infuse with a **Generous Portion** of **Passion** to build an educational resource rich in historical context.

### Step 3: Share the Vision
3. Serve this accessible history to everyone, fostering a connection between culture and the modern table.
