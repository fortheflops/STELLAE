# Roasted Garlic for Terra Cotta Baker

> 📜 **Collection:** Unknown Eight | ✍️ **Attribution:** Unattributed | 📂 **Category:** Basics
> *Learn how to perfectly roast garlic using a terra cotta baker for a rich, buttery spread. This method caramelizes the cloves until tender and golden brown.*

---

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Inactive / Chill Time | Yield / Servings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5 mins | 70 mins | None | 1 bulb |

---

### 🔪 Key Equipment
* Terra cotta baker
* Knife
* Fork

---
## Ingredients

| Measurements | Ingredients | Prep / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 bulb | [[Garlic]] | Top cut off |
| 1/4-1/2 teaspoon | [[Olive oil]] | Or melted butter |
| To taste | [[Seasoning]] | Salt and pepper |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation and Baking
1. Cut off the top of the **1 bulb garlic** exposing the top of each clove.
2. Remove loose outer skins and puncture each clove with a fork.
3. Sprinkle with **1/4-1/2 teaspoon olive oil** or melted butter and season to taste.
4. Cover and bake at 300° for **65-70 min**, basting several times, or microwave at full power for **60-65 seconds** until soft, fragrant, and golden brown.

---

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Roasted garlic can be stored in an airtight container in the refrigerator covered in oil for up to one week.
