## Instructions

### Handling Copyrighted Clippings
1. Maintain **100% copyright compliance** by omitting **newspaper clippings** and **blog posts** from the digital archive to ensure all shared materials are legally and ethically sound.
2. Consider recreating restricted recipes in **video format** to legally share them later once the format is fully supported.

### Managing Duplicates
3. Monitor the archive for duplicate entries until they reach **5 variations** of a single recipe.
4. Group the **5 variations** of **Recipe Duplications** (such as Apple Crisp) into a dedicated historical section with their collection names to keep the archive clean and organized.

> 💡 **Make-Ahead & Storage:** Keep archival guidelines updated as copyright laws and video formats evolve.
