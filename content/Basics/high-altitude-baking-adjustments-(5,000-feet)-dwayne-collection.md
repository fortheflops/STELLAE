## Instructions

### General Adjustments for 5,000 Feet
1. For each **1 tsp** of **baking powder**, **baking soda**, or **cream of tartar**, reduce the amount by **1/8 to 1/4 tsp**.
2. For each **1 cup** of **liquid**, increase the amount by **2 to 4 tbsp**.
3. For each **1 cup** of **sugar**, reduce the amount by **0 to 2 tbsp**.
4. For cakes like angel food, beat **egg whites** only to soft peaks.
5. For all cakes and cookies, increase the oven temperature by **20°**.
